Semtilbu.
3.
Pakai Pathen thilsem jouse lah a gul hi alungthim thempen ahi. Nikhat chu aman numeinu adongin, “Pathen'in honsunga theiga jouse naneh thei lhon lou beh ding ahi tia asei mong em?” ati. 
Numei nu chun gul jah a keini'n “Eden honsunga theiga jouse kaneh thei lhon ding ahi nai.” 
Ahin Pathen'in hitin aseiye, “Hon lailung a theiga vangkhu naneh lhon louhel ding, lo jong nalo lhon louhel ding ahi. Ahin iti ham khat a nalo lhon a naneh lhon le nathi jeng lhon ding ahi,” tin aseiye. 
Hinlah Satan in numeinu jah a aseijin, “Nathi lhon loubeh ding ahi. 
Ajeh chu Pathen'in ahet ahi hichu naneh nini lhon le namit lhon hung hahdoh intin Pathen toh nabah lhon ding thilpha le thilse hetheiya naum lhon ding ahi ti Pathen'in ahet jeh a asei ahi bouve,” atitai. 
Hiche a chun numei nun theiga chu aven ahile neh dinga tui chule mitvet dinga jong vetnom nom um, chule mi achihsah jeng ding jeh chun aha duchat cheh tai. Hijeh chun numei nun theiga chu akilo in anetan chule a pasalpa jong them khat apen amapan jong ala in ane tai. 
Hiche apat chun amit lhon chu ahung hahdoh in tahsa sagoh keo ahilhon chu aki hechen lhon tai. Hichun thingna akilo lhon'in atahsa lhon chu akiselna lhon tan ahi. 
Chuin nisa dai lhah lamin honsunga Pakai Pathen awgin chu ajalhon'in hijeh chun hon lailung'a thinglah a chun amani tah Pakai Pathen masanga akisel mang lhon tai. 
Ahivangin Pakai Pathen'in mihempa chu akouvin, “Adam hoiya um nahi hitam?” atile, 
Adam in jong adonbut in “Keiman nangma honsung'a na hung chu kahen ahile ka sagoh keo kahi jeh a ka kichat a kisela kahi,” ati. 
Hichun Pakai Pathen'in jong aseiye, koi ham nangma sagoh keova na um'e tia na seipeh a chu? Honsung'a theiga nehih in kati chu naneh hitam? atile, 
Adam in adonbut in, “Keiman kaum khompi dinga nangman neipeh numeinu hin theiga chu themkhat eipen keiman kana netai,” ati. 
Chuphat in Pakai Pathen'in numeinu jah a aseiye, “Nangin ipi dinga hitobang pihi nabol hitam!” atile numei nun adonbut in, “Hiche gul hin eijol jol phat a kaneh ahi bouve,” ati. 
Hichun Pakai Pathen'in gul jah a, “Nangman hiche nana boldoh jeh in, ganhing jouse lah a nang hi sapset nachan ding na hinkho lhung keiya na oipoh a na kithol tho ding, chule na hinkho sunga leivui naneh ding ahi. 
Keiman nang le numeinu chilhah kah a kidouna kakoi ding, amapa chun nangma hi na luchang avohchip a, nangman amapa kengphang na chuh peh ding ahi,” ati. 
Pathen'in numeinu jah a chun hitin aseiye, “Keiman na naovop hahsatna kabe peh ding, naosonat gentheiya cha nahin doh ding ahi. Najipa chung'a vai nahom nom jing ding, ahi vang'a najipan nang chunga vai ahop ding ahi,” ati. 
Chuin Ama'n Adam jah a hitin ati, “Nangman naji thu na ngaiya naneh louhel ding ahi tia ka thupehsa theiga naneh tah jeh in nangma jeh a leiset hi sapset chang ahitai. Chule na hinkho damlai sungse a gimna le genthei thohpum pum'a neh na kihol a naneh ding ahi. 
Chule nang doudin ling le mi-at thei jong hung kehdoh intin, chule nangma anche louhing a nakivah ding ahi. 
Na ti-ul namaija long pum'a anneh na kihol'a na neh ding, chule nangma leiset a kisemdoh nahin leiset naleso kit ding ahiye,” ati. 
Hichun Adam in ajinu chu amin Eve asah tai. Ajeh chu amanu mijouse hung pen dohna minu ahi tan ahi. 
Hichun Pakai Pathen'in savun a kon'in Adam le Eve ding von akhui peh in amani chu asilpeh in ahi. 
Hichun Pathen'in aseiye, “Tu apat a hi Adam hi eiho lah a khat tobang ahung hitan, thilpha le thilse hetheijin aumtai, chule tunjong aman hinna thingphung ga khu kilo intin hin ne lhon leh tonsot a hing den ding ahitai ati.” 
Hiche a pat chun Pakai Pathen'in Amani chu Eden hon a kon'in anodoh in, Chuleh Adam chu ahung kisemdohna leiset loulhou dingin asoldoh tan ahi. 
Hiti chun Pathen'in Adam anodoh jouvin, Eden hon nisolam'a hinna thingphung ngah dingin vantil Cherub chu achen sah in, chule chemjam kou jeju chu akipei kimvel le sahtan ahi.
