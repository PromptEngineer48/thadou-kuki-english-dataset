Chujouvin Adam le Eve akitimat lhon'in ahile Eve in nao ahin vop in Cain chu ahung peng tai, hiche a chun amanu'n hitin aseiye, Pathen phatthei bohna jal in cha pasal khat kanei tai ati. 
Phat chomkhat jou chun Eve chun chapa Abel ahing kit tai, hichun khonungin chapang teni chu ahung khanglen lhon tan, Abel chu kelngoi ching in ahung pang in, Cain vang chu loubol mi ahung hitan ahi. 
Phat phabep jouvin Cain in alousohga alhun phat in alousohga phabep ahin pon Pathen a dingin kilhaina ahung sem tai. 
Hitobang ma chun Abel in jong akelngoi lah a apeng masa athao pen tah khat chu Pathen a dingin ahin pedoh tai, Hichun Pathen'in Abel lam joh aven athilto jong kipah tah in asanpeh tai. 
Hinlah Pathen'in Cain thilto maicham chu asanpeh tapoi, hijeh chun Cain chu hatah in alunghang in akaiku jengin ahi. 
Hiche phat chun Pathen'in Cain jah a, “Ibola nang le nang ki lunghannahi ham? Chule ibola namai thim jeng a ham? 
Thilpha bol dinga kilom ho chu nabol le na kisan pehda ding ham? Ahin thilpha bol dinga kilom ho chu na bolnom louleh kichih theiyin chonset nan naval lhum ding ahitai. Hiche ho hi na chinpha ding joh ahi,” ati. 
Nikhat chu Cain in asopipa Abel jah a “Eini thinglhang lam'a che hite atin, gamlang agei lhon phat in Cain chu asopipa Abel chunga achun athat tai.” 
Phat chomkhat jouvin, Pakaijin Cain jah a, “Nasopipa Abel hoiya um hitam?” atile Cain'in jong adonbut in, “Kahepoi, Keima hi ka sopipa Abel ching tup kahim?” ati. 
Hichun Pathen'in jong Cain jah a “Ipi nabol hitam? Na sopipa Abel thisan chu tol'a kon'in keima angsung gei in ahung peng in ahi. 
Hijeh chun tu apatna nangma hi leiset chunga sapsetna noija uma nahitai, na sopipa Abel thisan in na chup ahitai. 
Leiset a konna nangma dinga ga imacha pung ding ahi tapoi, itobanginnatong jong lechun nangman leiset a in mun neilou gamvah mangpa na kiti ding ahi.” 
Chuphat in Cain chun Pathen henga aseiye, “Keima gotna kachan hi kei thoh jou hoi ahipoi. 
Vetan tunin nangma a kon'in neidel mangtai, nangin lei chunga umna neilou nei hisah tai, tu apat a hi koi hijongleh eitoh khapen pen chun eitha ding ahitai.” 
Pathen'in adonbut in, “Ahipoi koi tobang nangma hinkho sumang dinga kigo a aum le keiman ama chunga sagi vei ka phulah ding ahi.” Hichea chun Pathen'in Cain chu koiman atha louna dingin Cain chunga melchihna khat akoipeh in ahi. 
Hichea kon chun Cain in Pathen kom adalhan Eden niso lam a Nod mun'a chun acheng tai. 
Cain chun ajinu aki timatpin ahile nao ahin vop kit tan hiche nao chu Enoch ahi. Chuin Cain in phat phabep jouvin khopi khat ahinsem doh in, achapa min aput sah in hiche khopi min chu Enoch asah tai. 
Enoch in chapa Irad ahingin, Irad chun Mehujael ahingin, Mehujael chun Methushael ahingin, Methushael chun Lamech ahing'e. 
Lamech chun ji ni aneijin amasa nu chu ahile Adah ahin chule anina nu chu Zillah ahiye. 
Adah in Jabal ahingin, ama hi ahiye, khoilupon in a chenga gancha chingho dinga apau chu. 
Chule asopi pa min chu Jubal ahin, ama chu lathem gosem mut them masa pen ahiye. 
Zillah jong chun Tubal-cain ahingin, ama chun manchah ding abonchan sum-eng jong thih jong ahin kheng'e. Chule Tubal-cain chun sopi numei khat aneijin amin Naamah ahi. 
Chujouvin nikhat chu Lamech chun ajite ni jah a hitin aseiye , “Vo Adah le Zillah ka aw kasei ngai lhon'in, ‘Ajeh chu pasal khat in eimavo jeh in ka that in gollhang khat in eiden jeh in ka that tai. 
Koi tobang mikhat in Cain chu atha le sagi vei gotna chang ding ahile keima hinkho lading koi tobang aum le som sagi vei gotna chang ding joh ahiye.” 
Chuin Adam chun ajinu akitimatpi kit in ahile amanun chapa khat ahing kit in, amin Seth asah tai; ijeh inem itile amanun hitin aseiye, “Pathen'in chapa eipeh kit hi Abel khel a ei kipe ah ahi, ajeh chu Cain thapa khel dingin eipe tai,” ati. 
Seth chu mipi lhing ahung hiphat in chapa khat aneijin amin Enosh asah e. Hiche apat chun mipi hon Pathen chu amin pan in ahou pan tauvin ahi.
