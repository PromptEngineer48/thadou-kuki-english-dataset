7.
Chuin ima jouse ahina ding bang ahijou phat in Pathen'in Noah chu ahoulimpi tai. “Nangma le na insung mite kongsunga lut tauvin ajeh chu leiset chunga mihem holah a michonpha thudih a chon nangbou nahi”ati. 
Nangman gancha theng lah a kon'in kop sagi, kop khat a achal khat le anu khat cheh, chule gancha thenglou lah a kon a jong kop sagi ma kop khat a achal khat le anu khat cheh ki kailut inlang, 
Chule chunga leng vachate lah a jong kop sagi cheh na puilut ding amaho lah a chu achal khat le anu khat akopto cheh a na puilut ding hichengse chu twisang let a konna nahinso theina dinga navah a chikhai dinga nahing hoi ding ahi. 
Nisagi jouva pat go hatah a leiset chunga ka juhsah pan ding ahitai, chule go chu ni somli le jan somli sunga ka juhsah ding leiset chunga um jouse le ahing thei jouse ka suhmang hel ding ahitai,”ati. 
Hiti chun Noah injong Pathen'in athupeh bang bang chun abonchan abolsohkei tan ahi. 
Leiset chunga twisang ahunglet chun Noah chu kum jagup ana lhing tan ahi. 
Hichun Noah chu achapa te toh, ajinu toh, chule amoute toh twisanglen na kon'a ahoidoh theina diuvin kong sunga alut tauve. 
Satheng ho lah a ahin, sa thenglou ho lah a kon'in jong tol'a kithol tho ho lah a kon'in jong, 
Akop kop a anu khat le achal khat cheh, Pathen'in Noah ahoulimpina bang chun kong sunga chun Noah kom'a avahlut cheh tauvin ahi. 
Chuin nisagi lhin jou chun twisanglen go ahung jupan tan, leiset chung chu gotwi in achup tai. 
Noah chu kum jagup alhin kum lhani lhinna ah, leinoija twi um jouse ahung put tan, chule vanthamjol apat in hatah in go alon tho tai. 
Ni somli le jan somli sungin go chu tang louhel'in hatah in ajujom jing tan ahi. 
Hicheni tah chun Noah ahin, Noah chapa te Shem, Ham, chule Japheth ahiuvin, Noah jinu ahin, amoute thum ahiuvin abonchauvin kong sunga alut tauve. 
Amaho ahiuvin, kong sunga um gamsa hang ahin insa gancha alen aneo chule tol'a aop'a kitholtho ho jong ama ama jat dungjui cheh in, vachate jong ama ama chilhah dungjui a vacha namkim a kon ahiuve. 
Akop akop in amaho chun kongsung ahin jon cheh un, hichun avetsah chu ahile hinna neiya tahsa hing ahiuve ti photchetna ahi. 
Hichu gancha ho lah a anu khat le achal khat ahung lut soh phat un Pathen'in Noah ateppeh bang chun, Pakaijin kot akhah tai. 
Chujouvin ni somli le jan somli sungin hatah in twisanglen chu akhang in leiset chung jouse achup tan, hichun kong chu twi in ahin potou cheh cheh tan leiset sanga sang jon alet dohtai. 
Twi chu nasa tah in akhang tul tul jeng tan ahile kong chu twi chunga akitol le len ahi. 
Achaina geiyin twi chun mol jouse lah a asangpen geiyin abonchan achup sohhel tai. 
Twi chu akhanbe jingin mol jouse lah a asangpen jong chu tong som ni jen thuh-in achup manghel tai. 
Hijeh chun leiset chunga um ahing thei ahaithei jouse, chunga leng vachate ahin, in na kivah gancha ho ahin, gam'a gamsa ho ahiuvin, ahon hon a kiloiya leiset chung'a kitol le ganhing namkim ahiuvin chule mihemte geiyin agam sohhel tai. 
Ima jouse leiset chunga um kiti pou anahhom a hinna hu neiya haithei jouse abonchan athigam soh tan ahi. 
Pathen'in leiset chunga thil ahing thei jouse asumang sohhel tai. Mihem ho jouse, gancha ho, ganhing gamsa ho, tolgo chunga lamjot thei jouse, chunga leng vachate jouse abonin athi sohhel tai. Ahin hichea sohcha a hing doh Noah le a insung mite chule kong sunga um ganhing anu khat le achal khat um ho bou ahiuve. 
Hiche twisanglen chun leiset chung ni jakhat le nisom nga sung achup e.
