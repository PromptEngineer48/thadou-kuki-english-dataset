Semtilbu.
8.
Hinlah Pathen'in Noah le gamsaho le gancha ho kong sunga um jouse ageldoh jingin, leiset chunga huipi hattah ahin nunsah in ahile twisanglen chu ahung kem lheh tai. 
Leinoi lam a twi ho jong along kang tan, chule chunglam'a van'a konna go nasa tah a ju jong athip tan ahi. 
Hichun twisanglen chu leiset chunga kon'in ni jakhat le ni somnga jouvin akang heihoi tan ahi. 
Hiti chun nisom le nisagi lhinni lha sagi channa chun kong chu Ararat molchunga ahung kinga tai. 
Lhani le akeh jouvin twi chu ahung kemtul tul'in mol dang dang ho jong molvum ahung kimu pan tan ahi. 
Chujouvin ni somli jou chun Noah in huikot kong sunga asem chu ahin hong doh tan, 
Hichun va-ah khat alha doh in ahile, leiset chung adung avaiyin aleng len, leiset chunga twi akan loulai jeh in ahung kinungle kit tai. 
Chuin Noah in jong leiset chunga twi kang soh tam ti vetna dingin vakhu khat alendoh sah kit in ahi. 
Hinlah vakhu chun kingah na ding mun amu hihlaiye, ajeh chu twijin leiset achup nahlai jeh in ahung kinungle kit in, hichun Noah in vakhu chu akhut a adomlhan kong sunga akoilut kit tai. 
Chuin nisagi akho ngaiyin, chujouvin kong'a kon chun vakhu chu alha doh kit'a ahile, 
Vakhu chu nilhah langin ama henglam'a ahung kile kit in, vetau amuh achun athah beh a kiloulha Olive thingna khat ahin pon, hijeh chun Noah in jong leiset chunga twipi akangsoh tai, ti ahedoh paijin ahi. 
Chujouvin Noah jong nisagi ma akho ngai kit in vakhu chu alha doh kit in ahile vakhu chu ahung kinung le kit tapoi. 
Noah hinkho chu kum jagup le kum khatna lha masapen, chu che lha nimasapen ni chun leiset chunga twipi abonchan akang sohhel tan, hijeh chun Noah in jong kong husin chu ahoh lhan agahven ahile leiset chung chu ana gohel tan ahi. 
Hiti chun lhani jou don chun leiset chung hoitah in agohel tai. 
Hichun Pathen'in ahoulimpin hitin ati, 
“Nabon chauvin kong sunga kon chun potdoh tauvin, nangma nahin, na jinu ahin, na chapa te ahiuvin, chule na moute ahiuvin kilhon dohpi'n. 
Tahsa a hing ganhing jouse, naum khompi ho vachate ahiuvin, gancha ho ahiuvin, leiset chunga a op'a kithol tho ho ahiuvin, hiche ho chu leiset letthe thua ahung chikhai uva, ahung hin phat uva chule ahung punjalna ding uvin hin puidoh tan,” ati. 
Hichun Noah ahin, ajinu ahin, achapa te ho ahin amoute toh konga kon chun ahung potdoh tai. 
Chuin gancha jouse alen aneo abonchauvin vacha ho ahiuvin ni ni in akop kop in anu khat le achal khat konga kon'in ahung potdoh un ahi. 
Hichun Noah in maicham Pathen henga asemin, hi a chun aman kipehdohna maicham pumgo thilto saho chule vacha ho amaho akipehdohna dung jui cheh in pumgo thilto dingin agouvam in ahi. 
Chuin anamtui maicham ahung kisem jeh chun Pathen alung lhai lheh jengin hitin ama akihoulim in aseiye, “Mihem lung ngaitona hi achapan laiya kipat a hung gilou pan hita jongleh mihem jeh in keiman leiset hi gaosap kit tapong'e; chule ganhing ka semsa ho jong hi sumang kit tapong'e. 
Iti nama jongleh leiset aum laisen muchi tuphat, chang aat phat, gam dap phat, gamsat phat, nipi lai le phalbi lai, sunlai le jan beilou ding ahi,” ati.
