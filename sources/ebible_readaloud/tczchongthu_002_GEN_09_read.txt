Semtilbu.
9.
Hichun Pathen'in Noah le achapa te phatthei aboh in hitin ahoulimpi in hitin aseiye, “Cha le nao hing uvin, pung uvin lang leiset lochan sohkei uvin ati. 
Leiset chunga um sa jouse ahin chunga leng vachate jouse ahin chule gamsa a op'a kithol tho ho ahiuvin chule twikhanglen'a nga ho ahiuvin abonchauva nangho naki chat cheh dingu ahi, abon chauva nangho khut a kapeh doh ahitai. 
Ahing theiya avahle thei jouse nangho dinga anneh a pang thei cheh ahiuve, anche louhing kapeh ma banga chu aboncha kapeh doh nahi tauve. 
Hinlah nangman naneh theilou ding chu sahing athisan pum pum a naneh loubeh ding ahi. 
Hinlah keiman kakhol chil ding chu koi tobang khat touvin mihem hinkho asuhbeiya thisan aso le, iti ham khat a gamsan mihem atha le hiche gamsa chu thadoh pai ding chule koitobanginmihem hinkho asuhbei leh ama hinkho hitobang ma a chu suhbei ding ahiye. 
Koitobanginmihem hinkho asuhbei le hiche mihem hinkho sumang pa chu ama hinkho jong hi tobang'a chu kisumang ding ahiye, ajeh chu Pathen'in mihem hi ama lim bang banga asem doh ahiye. 
Tuhin nangho hatah in pungnuvin lang leiset le asung dimset in cha le nao hing uvin lang, pung uvin chule hinlo chan sohkei uvin,” ati. 
Hichun Pathen'in Noah le achate ahoulim pin hitin aseiye. 
“Keiman kakitepna avel'in ka sudet nom lheh tai, nang toh na chilhah te chengse dingin jong, 
Chule gancha nam kim nangma toh kong sunga um chengse toh vacha ho ahin, insa ho ahin, chule gamsa hang ho jouse ahin chule leiset chunga atoi thei avah thei jouse dinga hi 
Keiman nangho toh ka kitepna ka phudohna ajeh pentah chu hiche hi ahi, tahsa a hingho twisanglen a kisumang kit louhel ding ahin, chule leiset suhmangna dinga jong twisang letna um kit louhel ding ahitai,” ati. 
Hichun Pathen'in aseiye, “Keiman ka kitepna ka phudoh hi nangho le nalhat khom piu ganhing nam kim ho ahiuvin chule tonsot a khangguija mite ding ahin, ikah uva amelchihna hiche hi ding ahi. 
Keiman ka semdohsa nisa vah emsel chu vantham jol'a ka koiye, hichu leiset le kei kikah a kitepna khat a um melchihna hi ding ahi. 
Keiman meibol lhang lei chunga kahin sol teng nisa vah emsel ho chu meilhang lah a hung kilang ding ahiye. 
Hiche phat le chun keiman nangho le ikah uva ka kitepna chu hin geldoh kit ingting, chutengleh gotwi chu tahsa hingte sumang dinga twi sanga hunglen kit louhel ding ahiye. 
Keiman nisa vah emsel meilhang lah a kamuji tengleh Pathen le leiset chunga tahsa a ahing thei jouse kah a tonsot kumkho a dinga kitepna chu kagel doh ding ahi,”ati. 
Hichun Pathen'in Noah ahoulim pin hitin aseiye, “Hiche hi leiset chunga tahsa ahing thei jouse toh kei kikah a kitepna ka phudoh chu amelchihna hi ding ahi,” ati. 
Noah chate ho kong sunga kon'a hung potdoh ho chu ahileh Shem, Ham chule Japheth ahiuve, Ham kiti pa hi Canaan pa ahiye. 
Noah chate thum ho a konna vannoi hi mihem hung dim ahi. 
Chuin twisanglet kichai jou chun Noah in tolgo lah achun lou abol pan tan hia chun lengpi phung aphut doh tai. 
Chujouvin nikhat chu Noah chun lengpitwi adonin ahile akham tan khoilupon buh sungah chun asagoh keovin alum tai. 
Ham Canaan pa chun apa Noah sagoh keova aum chu amun apotdoh paiyin asopi teni aga hetsah in ahile, 
Hichun Shem le Japheth in ponsil achoi lhon'in alengkouva adom gel lhon'in nunglam ngan anung toi lhon'in apa akeova um chu agakhu khum lhon'in, amai lhon vang ahei manggel lhon'in apa akeova um amulhon poi. 
Hichun alupna a kon'in Noah ahung khah doh phat in achapa neopen in ana limbol chu ahedoh tai. 
Hichun Noah in aseijin: “Canaan vang gaosap chang hen, Asopi teni henga soh jouse soh in hung pang hen,” atin, 
Hichun Noah in hitin aseiye, “Pathen'in Shem phatthei boh maihen, ahin Canaan vang asopi soh himai hen, 
Pathen'in Japheth gam chu hatah in kehlet peh hen, chule Japheth in Shem ponbuh sung dunga jong chen sah hen, ahin Canaan vang asopipa soh hijing maihen,” ati. 
Twisanglen kichai jouvin Noah chu kum jathum le kum somnga ahing nalaiye. 
Hiti chun Noah hin nikho abon bonin kum jako le kum somnga alhingin, chujouvin ama athi tai.
