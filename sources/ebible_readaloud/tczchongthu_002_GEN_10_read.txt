Semtilbu.
10.
Hiche thusim ho hi Noah chapa te Shem, Ham chule Japheth chilhah thusim chu ahiye; Twisang let jou chun amaho a kon'in cha tamtah ahung punbe in ahi. 
Japheth chilhah ho chu Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech chule Tiras ahiuve. 
Gomer chilhah ho chu Ashkenaz, Riphath chule Togarmath ahiuve. 
Javan chilhah ho chu Elishah, Tarshish, Kittim, chule Rodanim ahiuve. 
Hiche apat chun amaho chilhah chengse chun leiset ahin toupha pan tauvin ama ama hitheina leh aphung achang akaidol cheh in ama nam hina dol cheh in acheng tauvin ahi. 
Ham chilhah ho chu Cush, Mizraim, Put, chule Canaan ahiuve. 
Cush chilhah ho chu Seba, Havilah, Sabtah, Raamah, chule Sabteca ahiuve. Raamah chilhah ho chu Sheba le Dedan ahi. 
Cush kitipa hin leiset chunga mihat a hung pang masapen Nimrod chu ahing'in ahi. 
Hiche phat laiya chun vannoiya gamleng hat sakap them pen ahung hin, chule Pathen ging tah mi jong anahi e, hijeh chun mihon Nimrod banga gamlen hat sakap thempen ahi tin asei uve. 
Hichun Cush chun alenggam chu Babylon khopi sunga Babylonia tin ahin semdoh in, hiche khopi ho chu Erech, Akkad, chule Calneh ahiuve. 
Aman hiche a kon chun Assyria ajonin hikom achun Nineveh khopi aphut doh in aban'a Rehobothir khopi ahin Calah khopi jong ahin phut doh kit e. 
Chuban chun Nineveh le Calah khopi teni kikah achun Resen khopi aphut doh in khopi lentah ahi. 
Mizraim chu Ludim ho, Anamim ho, Lehabim ho, Naphtuhim ho, 
Pathrusim ho, Casluhim ho, chule Caphtorim ho chu Philistine te hung kondohna ho chu ahi. 
Canaan chapa tahpen chu Sidon, Sidon chilhah te hung kon dohna ho chu ahiuvin chule Hit chilhah ho jong Canaan akon ahiuve. 
Jebus ho, Amor ho, Girgash ho ahin, 
Hivi teho, Arki ho, Sin ho ahin, 
Arvad ho, Zemar ho, chule Hamath ho jouse hi ama hinsa cheh ahiuve, ahin khonungin Canaan insung kon mite chu gamtin'a akithe cheh sohkei uvin ahi. 
Hiche apat chun Canaan gamgi chu Sidon apat Gerar lang jonin Gaza geiyin aki lhung lut peh in, Sodom lang jonin aki lhungin, Gomorrah aphan, Admah ahin phan, chule Zeboiim ahop in Lasha geiyin akilhung lut peh-e. 
Amaho cheng hi Ham chilhah ho ahiuvin, ama ama insung dungjui le, apao dungjui le, agam dungjui chuleh ajat dungjui uva ahiye. 
Shem chate pasal ho jouse apunbe in ama chu Japheth upa ahin, Ebel chilhah ho jouse hi Shem'a konna hung kondoh cheh ahiuve. 
Shem chilhah ho chu ahile Elam, Asshur, Arphaxed, Lud chule Ram ahiuve. 
Ram chilhah ho chu ahile Uz, Hul, Gether, chule Mash ahiuve. 
Arphaxad chu Shelah pa ahin, chule Shelah chu Ebel pa ahiye. 
Ebel chun cha ni aneijin amasapa chu Peleg (Hichu kisuh khenna tina ahi) ahi, ijeh inem itile ama phatlai a chu vannoi chu ama ama paotho doh dungjuiya ahung umdoh laitah chu ahi, chule asopipa min chu Joktan ahi. 
Joktan chapa chu Almodad ahin, Sheleph ahin, Hazarmaveth ahin, Jerah ahin, 
Hadoram ahin, Uzal ahin, Diklah ahin, 
Obal ahin, Abimael ahin, Sheba ahin, 
Ophir ahin, Havilah ahin chule Jobab ahiuve. Amaho chengse hi Joktan chilhah cheh ahiuve. 
Ama hon gam kivaipohna atoupha uchu Mesha apat in Sephar lang jonin ahung kilhungin, nisolam'a thinglhang gamsung geiyin ahung kilhunglut peh in ahi. 
Amaho jouse hi Shem chilhah ahiuvin, aphung le achang, apao tho'u dungjui, agam dung jui le ajat dungjui uva Shem chilhah te ahiuve. 
Hiche phung le chang hung kimin phah se hi Noah chate a kon'a chi le nam hung kisem doh ama gui penna dungjuiya vannoiya nam jouse hi hiche phungpi a kon'a twisanglet jouva patna hung um doh ahi.
