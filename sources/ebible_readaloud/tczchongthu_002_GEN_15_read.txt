Semtilbu.
15.
Phat chomkhat jou nung hin, Pakaijin gaovin Abram akihoulimpin hitin aseipeh e. “Abram imacha kicha hih in, ajeh chu keiman kahuh ding, kipaman jong atam a tam na chan ding ahi,” ati. 
Abram in adonbut in, “O Thaneipen Pakai, chapa kaneilou vangin na phatthei bohna ho hi iti phat hitam? Nangman cha neipeh lou jeh in, Damascus a Eliezer, kasohpa hin, kagou jouse ahinlo ding ahitai. 
Nangman keima chilhah ding a chikhai ding, neipe tapon, hijeh a chu ka sohte lah a khat hin ka gou alo ding ahitai. 
Pakaijin ama chu ahoulim pin, “Ahipoi, nangma soh hon na gou alo ahi, nangma a konna chapa um ding ama chun na gou alo ding ahi.” 
Hichun Pakaijin Abram chu polama akoudoh in akihou pin, “Vanchung lam ven Ahsi ho khu simtem in nasim jou nah em. Hitobang chu ahi na chilhah atam ding dan chu,” ati. 
Chuin Abram in jong Pakai tahsan na dihtah in aneitan ahile hichu ama dingin dihchan nan akisimpeh tai. 
Chujouvin Pakaijin Abram ahoulimpi kit in, “Hiche gam hi nalo dingin nape ing'e tia Chaldea gam Ur khopia konna nangma nahin puidoh a Pakai chu keima kahi,” ati. 
Hichun Abram in adonbut in, “O Pakai hiche hi kalo ding ahi ti ipia iti kahet doh thei ding ham?” ati. 
Hichun Pakaijin Abram ahou lim pin ajah a, “Bongnou kum thum lhingsa khat toh, kelngoi chal kum thum lhingsa khat toh, chule vahui nou khat le vapal nou khat kaheng'a hin choi tan,” ati peh e. 
Hichun Abram in hichengse chu apedoh in athat in, hichun ahapsat cheh in, ahap lang ton alupsah in hinlah vacha ho vang chu asemkeh poi. 
Chuin muthongho ahung lenglhauvin hiche sa kikoi ho chu ane kigo uvin ahile Abram in muthongho chu anodoh jin ahi. 
Chuin nisa alhum ding kon'in Abram ahung imut soh tan, chujongleh tijat umtah muthim lhangkhal ahung jingin amapa chu ahin jinkhum tai. 
Hichun Pakaijin Abram ahoulimpi in, “Nangman thonlou dinga nahet ding chu na chilhah ho hi gam danga hung mah thah diu ahiye, amaho se chu kum jali sunga mi bol genthei thoh a ga um ding ahiuve. 
Ahin keiman hiche nam sugenthei ho chu ka phulah ding ahi, chule phat ahung sot a achaina teng amaho chun hatah a nei le gou ahin nei diu ahi. 
(Nangman vang lungmong sel'a na pute heng na jot ding, chule na kum lhingset a na hinkho kichaiya na kivui ding ahiye.) 
Chule amaho hi khangli ahung lhin phat le hiche gam'a hi hung kinung lekit diu ahi, ajeh chu tuchan gei hin Amor mite khan gitlouna hi pilhing loulai ahiye,” ati. 
Chuin nisa alhum phat in mu athim tan ahile, meikhu belsung khat ah mei al-lenglung khat le meivah tobang khat ahung kitol in, hiche pi chu sa kihapsat a kikoi teni kah achun ahung kitollelen ahi. 
Hichun Patkaiyin Abram chu hitin a kihoulimpi in, kinoptona anei lhon'in hiche nichun, “Keiman nason na chilhah te kapeh gam chu hiche hi ahi, Egypt gamgi apat luipi dung gei Euphrate vadung gei, 
Gamsung tutua Ken mite, Keniz mite chule Kadmon miten atoupha se, 
Hit mite, Periz mite chule Rephaim mite, 
Amor mite ahiuvin, Canaan mite ahiuvin, Girgash miho ahiuvin, chule Jebus miho gam ahiuve,” ati.
