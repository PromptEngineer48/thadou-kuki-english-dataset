Semtilbu.
18.
Mamre munna gangpi phung kom'a chun Pakai chu Abraham henga ahung kilah in ahi. Nikhat hi nichangsat pettah hin Abraham chu aponbuh maiya ana touvin ahi. 
Ama ahung dahdoh in ahile akom'a mithum ana din amun ahi. Amaho chu amu phat in ama alhai jelin amaho lemnan tol ah abohkhup in ahi. 
Chuin Abraham'in “Ka Pakai,” tin adonbut in, “Nang hon pha nasah uleh phat chomkhat naki ngah thei diu ham. 
Nakeng silna ding'u kahin choi kah in hiche thing limnoiya hin ana kicholdo tadiuvin. 
Chule nang hon na lhacha pa hi khotona neitah a nei hungvil ngal'u ahileh na kholjin na mano masangun na tha kidona dingun naneh ding'u them khat nahin gon peh unge, ama hon aphai, nasei bang chun hin boltan atiuve. 
Hichun Abraham chu aponbuh kom langa alhai jel'in Sarah kom'ah asei tan ahi, “Kinloi tah in changbong atena dim thum tedoh inlang twi toh mehnangin, chule changlhah hin kang loi temin,” aga tin ahi. 
Hichun Abraham'in gancha ho kivahna lang ajonin bongnou athao pen asohte ho apen, gangtah in ahin hon sah in ahi. 
Anneh ding ho amin phat in Abraham'in bongnoi ha le bongnoi toh chule bongnou sa ahon uchu amaho angsung'ah aneh diuvin aga luipeh in ama hon anneh laisen ama jong thinglim a chun ading jinge. 
Chuin ama hon Abraham jah a “Na loinu Sarah hoilam a um aham?” atiu leh Abraham'in ponbuh sunga aum nai, ati. 
Chuin amaho lah a khat chun aseijin, “Keima hi khovei tulai phat mama le kahung kinung lekit ding ahi, vetan hiche phat teng chule na inneipi Sarah khun chapa khat ana hin ding ahi,” ati chu anunga ponbuh sunglam a um Sarah chun angai lhin ahi. 
Abraham le Sarah chu atahsa kum lhon chu atam gel lhon tan, chule Sarah jong nao nei phat kum akhel ahitai. 
Hichun Sarah guhthim in ama le ama aki nuisat in hitin aseiye, “Iti hithei ding ham, kei tobang aching sa numeijin kipana thil phalou kamu ding ham, ka jipa jeng jong akum atam lheh tai,” ati. 
Pakaijin Abraham jah achun aseiye, “Ipi dinga Sarah khu nuiya ka teh nunga keiman cha kanei tahbeh mong ding hinam,” ati ham? 
Pakaiya dinga ipi ahahsa um ham? Keima khovei tulai phat na kahung kit ding chule Sarah in cha pasal khat anei ding ahi.” 
Chuphat in Sarah chu atija tan hichun Sarah in aseilep tan, “Keima ka nuipoi” atin ahinla, “Pakaijin adonbut in nangma na nui nai,” ati. 
Hichun pasal thum ho chu an aneh jouvun ading doh un Sodom lang chu agaldot un, ama hon adalha uvin, Abraham chu amaho thah doh ding tin lampi gei aga kilhonpin ahi. 
Pakai chun aseiye, “Keiman katoh gon ho hi Abraham kom'a kasel ding ham?” ati. 
Ajeh chu Abraham chu lentah le nam hattah chule vannoiya nam jouse ama jeh a phatthei chang ding ahi ngal'in, 
Chule achapa te le ama ban a a insung mi aman thu apeh a, chutia chu adih abol thei uva, thu adih a atan theina ding uva Pakai lampi ajui thei uva, chutengleh Pakaijin Abraham chung chang'a akitepnasa chu ahin molso theina dinga ama hi kilheng doh ahi tila kahenan,” ati. 
Hichun Pakaijin Abraham ahoulimpi in “Sodom le Gomorrah douna thu kisei asangval tan chule achonset nau jong aseval tai,” ati. 
Keima chesuh ing kating kaheng'a thu hung kisei dungjuiya hi abol cheh uham, hilou ham gapho chen inge,” ati. 
Hichun miho chu chukom'a kon'in akiheiyun Sodom lang chu ajonun ache tauve, ahinlah Abraham chu Pakai masanga chun ading chah den jingin ahi. 
Abraham'in anailut in hitin aseiye, “Nangman michonpha atheng chule mi phalou agilouho aboncha na suhmang tha ding ham,” ati. 
Ijem tin khopi sunga chun michonpha som nga bang um ta le, asunga michonpha somnga ho jal'a jong chu akhomun chu khen dehlou hel'a na suhmang jeng ding ham? 
Nang dinga hitobang thil nabol a, michonpha le migilou agoma natha cheh a, michonpha le migilou akibah ding chu bolda hel jeng le chun kati! Leiset thutan pan adih a atan louding ham?” atile. 
Hichun Pakaijin adonbut in, “Keiman mi somnga thudih thil phalou bol Sodom khopi a kamu doh thei le, amaho jal'a chu khopi ka huhdoh ding ahi.” 
Hichun Abraham'in aseikit in, “Ka Pakai nangman neiphal peh thei ding le kasei ban ho kajom nom in keima vang bohse vutvai tobang kahin, 
Ijem tin mi somli le nga tobang chonpha mitheng mi somnga sanga lhomjo namu le amaho jeh a chu khopi hi na suhmang ding ham, mi nga lhahsam jeh chun? Pakaijin adonbut in, Keiman hiche khopi a hi mi som li le nga kamu doh jouva ahile sumang ponge,” ati. 
Hichun Abraham'in adeichat angaichat avel in aseijin, “Ijem tin mi som li bou umta le,” atin chuin Pakaijin ahoulimpi kit in, “Keiman amaho mi somli jeh a chu ka suhmang louhel ding ahi,” ati. 
Ka Pakai, “Lungset tah in lunghanglou jenin,” Abraham atao kit leovin, “Nei phalpeh thei lechun kasei nom na laiye, Ijemtin mi som thum muta le chun,” atile Pakaijin ahoulimpi in “Keiman mi somthum hijongleh ka suhmang louding ahi,” ati. 
Hichun Abraham'in aseikit in, “Nangman nei phalpeh thei le ka thusei hi kajom nom'e, “Ijem tin mi somni hijongleh atile,” Pakaijin adonbut in, “Mi somni kamu jong le ka suhmang louding ahi,” ati. 
Abraham'in achaina in aseipeh tan, “Pakai, nei lunghan hihbeh inlang, khatvei neisei sah kit le tia katao ahi, mi som muta le chun” hichun Pakaijin aseiye, “Mi som kamu doh jong le ka suhmang louding ahi,” ati. 
Hichun Abraham henga athusei ding aboncha aseisoh phat in Pakai chu ache tan, Abraham vang chu a inmun lam'a ahung kile kittai.
