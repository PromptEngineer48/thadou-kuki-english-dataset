Semtilbu.
20.
Hiche akon chun Abraham chu Negev gam langa akichon'in Kadesh le Shur kikah a phat chomkhat ana chengin, chuin Gerar lang geijin aga chengin ahi. 
Abraham'in a inneipi Sarah chu “Ka sopinu ahi,” tin ana phongdoh in ahile Gerar lengpa Abimelech chun mi asol'in Sarah chu aga puisah tai. 
Hinlah hiche jan chun Pathen'in Abimelech amangin aki musah in hitin aseipeh e, “Nangma hi athisa nahi ahitai, ajeh chu numei na kipui hi ajinei numei ahi bouve!” 
Ahin Abimelech chun amanu (Sarah), chu akitimatpi loulai ahi, hijeh chun Abimelech in “Pakai, nangin hiche imacha hechenlou nam hi na suhmang mong ding hitam? 
Hitia chu Abraham tah in jong 'Ka sopinu ahi' tia eina houlimpi hilou ham? Chule amanun jong 'Ka sopipa ahi' atia, hijeh achu keiman ka lungthim'a jong themmona beihel a chule ka khut teni jong atheng ahi bouve” ati. 
Hijeh chun Pathen'in amangin adonbut in, “Ahinai, keiman nangma hetthem louna ho chu ka henai. Hiche ho jeh achu nangma keima douna a chonset a konna kasuh khen'a chule hiche ho jeh a chu numeinu to kaki timatsah lou ahi. 
Tun numeinu chu a inneipu kom'a soltan, a inneipun taona na manpeh ding ahi, ajeh chu ama themgao ahi, chuteng nangma nadam kit ding ahitai. Ahin nahet ding khat chu a inneipi nanung pehdoh louleh nangma chule na mite nathi gam diu ahi. 
Hijeh chun Abimelech jong jingkah matah in akithou doh in chuin asoh ho jouse gangtah in akoukhom tan, chuin Abimelech in thusoh jouse chu asohte ho lah achun aseijin tijatnan adim tauve. 
Hichun Abimelech in Abraham chu akouvin ajah a “Ipibol a hiti pia hi nei hung bol'u hitam? Itobang'a nang douna a kana chonset hitam, hitia kei le ka lenggam chunga chonset nasatah hi nahin pohlut ham? Bol louhel ding thil nangman neibol khum ahitai,” ati. 
Chuin Abimelech in Abraham kom'a aseiye, “Nangin ipi nabolna ham?” 
Abraham'in adonbut in, “Hiche mun hi Pathen ginglou mihem tamna mun ahi tin kagel in, ka loinu chu ka sopinu ahi kiti leh amanu jeh a chu eitha lou diu ham kati ahi.” 
Adihtah a sei dinga ka sopinu jong ahi nai, eihing lhonpa chu khat ahin kanu lhon vang achom chom ahi. Keiman kaji din kakipui tai. 
Pathen'in eina koudoh chun kapa insung kana dalhah chun mun khat a patna mun chom a kana vale lhon lai chun kana seiye, thil ngaichat kaneiye katin, hoilang hijongleh iche le nangho gamsunga kasopipa tin miho lah a nei phondoh ding ahi tin kana seipeh e.” 
Hichun Abimelech in akelcha ho le kelngoi hon ho chule abong hon chule numei pasal asoh ho chu Abraham apetai. Abimelech in a inneipi Sarah jong chu anungsol peh kit tai. 
Hichun Abimelech in adonbut in, “Dah inlang vetem in kagamsung nangin nadei penna mun'a nachen thei ahiye.” 
Abimelech in Sarah jah a aseiye, “Ven keiman na sopipa dangka sang khat kapen, hiche sum hi na kom'a um chengse mitmu a suhkhel kihetthem tona dinga kapeh ahi, chule japi angsunga nangma boina nanei tapoi,” ati. 
Hichun Abraham'in Pathen kom'a taona amangin Pathen'in Abimelech asudam tan, chule a inneipi le asoh numei ho jouse naobu jouse jong cha anei theina diuvin akihong gam tai. 
Ajeh chu Abraham jinu Sarah jeh a Pakaijin Abimelech insunga numei naobu jouse ana khatan peh ahiye.
