Semtilbu.
21.
Pakaijin ana seisa bang chun Sarah ahung villin Sarah chunga asuh bulhit peh tai. 
Hichun Sarah in nao ahin vop in, Abraham chu atehset nungin Pathen'in ateppeh phat dontah in chapa khat ahin petan ahi. 
Abraham'in achapa chu amin Isaac asah tan ahi. 
Chule Isaac pen jou niget alhin nin Abraham'in Pathen thupeh bangin achapa chu chep atanpeh tai. 
Isaac ahung pen chun, Apa Abraham chu kum jakhat ana lhingtai. 
Chuin Sarah in aphong doh in, “Pathen'in nuina eipei,” ati. 
Chule aman, “Sarah hin cha le nao noi hin chepsah inte tin koipen in Abraham kom'a ana sei ngam dem? Ahinlah keiman atehset nungin chapa khat kahinpeh tai,” ati. 
Isaac chu ahung khanglen deuvin anneh masapen Abraham'in kipanan kikhopna asem e. 
Hichun Sarah in jong achapa chu Egypt mi Hagar'in Abraham ahinpeh Ishmael to aki chep lhon chu amudoh tai. 
Hichun amanun Abraham kom'ah hitin aseiye, “Hiche sohnu le achapa chu solmang in ajeh chu hiche chapang hin keima chapa toh chanvou anei khom lhon ding aumpoi; Isaac toh kikomto ahi.” 
Hichun Abraham'in nopmo asa lheh jengin ajeh chu Ishmael jong chu achapa ahiye. 
Hinlah Pathen'in Abraham chu ahoulimpi in, “Nangman chapang chung chang chule na sohnu jeh in ima lung phatmona nei hih in, Sarah in bol in atiho chu bol in ajeh chu Isaac a konna bou na chilhah te hung kisim ding bou ahi.” 
Hinlah keiman Hagar chapa jong chu nam lentah khat kaso thou thou ding ahi. Ajeh chu amapa jong chu nangma chapa jong ahi thouve.” 
Hichun Abraham ajingin matah in akithouvin, neh ding bu asutup in chule twipohna ding twibom Hagar chu apen alengkouva aledoh peh tan ahi, hichun amanu le achapa chu asolmang in, chuin Hagar chu suhna ding helouvin Beersheba gamthip noiya avahlelei. 
Hichun twi ahung bei chun amanu (Hagar) chun naosen chu hampa nangboh limnoiyah akoitai. 
Hichun amanu (Hagar) apotdoh in atoulha tan achang seh in lamsom tobang kikah in “Keiman hiche chapang thi hi kamu nompoi,” atin amanu chu alhi alongin akaptai. 
Hinlah Pathen'in chapang ka awgin chu ajan chuin Pakai vantil in vanna kon'in Hagar chu ahin kouvin, “Hagar ipi boina nanei ham? Imacha kichatna nei hih in! Pathen'in aumna mun'a kon'in chapang ka awgin chu ajatai, ati. 
Kipatdoh inlang nao khu gakidop loiyin nakhut in kipomchah jon, ijeh inem itile keiman ama khu nam lentah kahisah ding ahi.” 
Hichun Pathen'in Hagar mit chu kho amusah in chua twi tamtah amusah tan, hichun amanun gangtah in twipai chu asudim in chapang chu adonsah tai. 
Chule chapang chu Pathen'in ahin umpi jingin neocha ahi apat gamthipnoi jenga ahung chengin, thalpi kap ahung them lheh jengin ahi. 
Chule Ishmael chu Paran gamthip noiya ana chengin, chule anun Egypt gam'a ama ji dingin numei khat aga puipeh in ahi. 
Hiche phat laitah chun Abimelech le asepai jalamkai Phicol chu Abraham vil din ahung'e. “Pathen'in nangma hi na umpi jingin, na thilbolna jouse ah na kithopi jinge,” tin ahung seiye. 
Pathen min'in kitemin, chule nei joulhep louhel ding, ka chate jong ahilouleh ka chilhah ho jong, keivang nangma adia thilpha bolnom jing, chule tuhin na kihahsel ding, thilpha nabol jing ding hiche gamsunga maljin gampam nahi laisen,” ati. 
Abraham'in adonbut in “Heng'e kakitem e,” ati. 
Hichun Abraham'in Abimelech kom'a lunglhai louna aphong doh in Abimelech sohten twikul hunam a Abraham sohte ana lahpeh u thu chu ahi. 
“Tuchung hi hiche thu kajah masah pen ahi,” tin Abimelech in adonbut'e. “Keiman hiche chung chang hi koi oimo a, koi ngoh ding ham kahepoi. Ajeh chu phat masan ima thu nana sei khapoi,” ati. 
Abraham'in hichun akelngoi ho, kelcha ho, chule bonghon ho Abimelech apen kinoptona anei lhon'e. 
Hichun Abraham'in kelngoi nou sagi ho chu achom a alhengdoh in ahi. 
Abimelech in Abraham chu adongin “Adang ho a konna hiche sagi ho hi ipi dia nachom khen ham?” ati. 
Abraham'in adonbut in, “Hiche twikhuh keiman kalaisa ahi, tia ahetoh a nei panpehna dinga hiche kelngoi sagi hi neisanpeh tei in” ati. 
Hijeh chun ama in hiche mun chun Beersheba asah tai. Ajeh chu mihem teni akihettona mun lhon ahiye. 
Beersheba munna kihettona mun'a akimuto jouvin, Abimelech le Phicol sepai lamkaipa chu Philistine mun lama akinung le lhon tan ahi. 
Abraham'in Beersheba mun achun chahthing phung aphutdoh in, hiche achun aman Pathen chu tonsot Pathen ahi tin aphong doh e. 
Hichun Abraham chu Phillistine mun'a chun phat sottah achengin ahi.
