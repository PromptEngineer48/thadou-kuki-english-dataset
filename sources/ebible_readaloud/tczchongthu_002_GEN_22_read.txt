Semtilbu.
22.
Thil hijatpi kichai jouvin Pathen'in Abraham chu apatep in aman hitin aseiye, “Vo Abraham!” atile, “Hikom'a kaum'e, Pakai” ati. 
Hichun Pathen'in aseiye, “Nangailut tah na chapa changkhat seh Isaac chu puijin lang Moriah gam'a chen keiman kahin seipeh ding molsang khat pen pen chunga chu pumgo thilto maicham adin pedoh tan,” ati. 
Chuti chun Abraham chu jingkah matah in athoudoh in a sangan teni akigon tup in chule asoh pasal ni akipuiyin achapa Isaac apuiyin, pumgo thilto govamna dingin thing akipoh in akipat doh in Pathen'in aseipehna mun chu ajontai. 
Ani thum lhinnin Abraham chu adah in aven ahile amun chu gal'a kon'in amutai. 
Abraham'in asoh teni jah a aseijin, “Nangni hikom'a hin sangan toh ana um tauvin, kei le ka chapa khulam akhun gache lhon tadinge, Pathen kaga hou lhon ding kahung kinung le vah lhon ding ahi.” 
Abraham'in thinglom maicham a pumgo thilto halna ding chu Isaac aputsah in chule achempai achoijin amani hiti chun akijot lhon'in achelhon tai. 
Chuin Isaac in apa Abraham jah a “Hepa” atile Abraham'in jong “Hoijo, kachapa” ati. Hichun Isaac in “Mei le thinglom la aum tan, pumgo thilto kelngoi hoiba ham?” 
Abraham'in adonbut in “Kachapa, Pathen ama tah in kelngoi pumgo maicham a ding hin gong nante” atin hichun amani achebe lhon'in ahi. 
Pathen'in Abraham aseipehna mun ahung lhun lhon phat in Abraham chun maicham khat asemin, thinglom chu agol chan ajam'in chuin achapa Isaac chu aheltum in maicham chunga alupsah tai. 
Hichun aman achem chu apaiya kon'in aladoh in, achapa chu sut agon akilam tai, 
Hinlah van'a kon'in Pakai vantil in “Abraham, Abraham” tin ahin kouvin ahile, Abraham'in “Pakai keima hikom'a kaum'e,” ati. 
Ama chun “Chapang pa chunga na khut nalha louhel ding, imacha jong nalo louding ahi, ajeh chu Pathen nagin jeh in nangman na chapa chang khat seh jeng jong keima adin na khut kihpoi, tihi tun kichen tah in kahedoh tai,” ati. 
Chuin Abraham adah doh in avet leh, “Vetan anung langa nanglom lah a kelngoi chal khat akia kihen amun ahi,” hichun Abraham jong achen kelngoi chal chu aga kaiyin achapa khel chun maicham thilto chun atoh in ahi. 
Hijeh chun Abraham'in hiche mun chu Yahweh-Yireh (Pathen'in hin ngaito nante) tin asahtai. Tuni geihin hiche “Pathen'in amolsang chung ah hin kingaito nante,” ti thuchih hi akisei jing nalaiye. 
Pakai vantil chun vanna kon'in Abraham tin aniveina a ahin koukit in ahi. 
Hitin aseiye, “Keima ka tahsa tah a kakihahsel ahitai, hitobang a thil hi nabol ngam jenga na chapa chang khat seh chunga khutkih louva napan jeng phat in, tin Pakaijin aseiye. 
Keiman kitah tah a phatthei kaboh ding nahi, chule na chilhah te ahsi jat ka phahsah ding chule vadung panga neldi jat ka sosah ding nahi, na chilhah ten amelma teu kelkot ahin toupha diu ahiye. 
Chule nangman ka thusei nangai jeh a leiset chunga namtin vaipi nason na chilhah te vanga phatthei hung kiboh ding ahiuve,” ati. 
Hichun Abraham asoh teni kom langa ahung kinungle in, chuin ama hon Beersheba lam ajon un, hichea chun Abraham chu Beersheba a aumtai. 
Phat chomkhat jou chun Abraham chu thu aseipeh un Milcah jong hin nasopi pa Nahor to cha pasal phachompi ahing lhone. 
Uz chu atahpen ahin, Buz hi ani channa ahin, Ram hingpa Kemuel ahin, 
Chesed ahin, Hazo ahin, Pildash ahin, Jidlaph ahin chule Bethuel ahiuve,” ati. 
Bethuel chun Rebekah ahing'e. Abraham sopipa Nahor chu Milcah cha hicheng get hi ana hinpeh e. 
Hicheng val in jong athaikemnu amin Reumah kiti nu jong chun Tebah toh, Gaham toh, Tahash toh chule Maacah ana hing'e.
