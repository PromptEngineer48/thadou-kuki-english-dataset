23.
Sarah chu kum jakhat le kum somni le kum sagi jen ahingin, 
Sarah chu Kiriath-arba kiti mun'a athi tan, hichu Hebron kiti Canaan gamsunga um ahi, hichun Abraham chu Sarah umlou jeh a lunghel le mitlhi longin achup in ahi. 
Hichun ajinu long adalhah ding jeh in Hit mite ho kom'a aseijin, 
“Keima gam chom mi kahin nangho lah a maljin bou kahi. Gambeh neo khat nei chohsah un, chuti le keiman ka jinu hi phaten kivui thei ing'e,” ati. 
Hit mite ho chun Abraham adonbut un, 
Ja umtah kapu thuseiyu ngaiyin, nangma Pathen a leng chapa banga keiho lah a uma keiho lhan khuh aphapen khat a khun na thilong chu vui jengin, kalah uva mihem khatcha jong hi alhankhuh na itpeh a na thilong na vuisah louding aumpoi,” atiuve. 
Hichun Abraham ading doh in agamsung mipite Hit miho chu salam bol din abohkhup tai. 
Abraham'in aseijin, “Nangman nei kithopi nom kahen ahithei ding le Ephron Zohar chapa kom'a nei seipeh uvin.” 
“Ajeh chu agamgi chin a um ama Machpelah songlhan keo khu eipeh theiya ahile ken jong lhan mun'a kaman jing dingin nangho umpet tah hin aman alhingset in peng kate,” ati. 
Ephron chu mipi lah a atoupet ahin, Abraham chu adonbut in midang hon jong athusei chu aja cheh un, Hit mite ho khopi sunga um jouse jah ah hitin aseiye. 
“Hithei ponte ka Pakai,” tin Abraham chu adonbut e, “Lungset tah in ka thusei hi ngaiyin, keiman ka gamsunga adamlai, atonglai, kapeh ding nahi. Tuhin mipi ho masanga nangma kapeh ding nahi, chule nangman najinu alongdamsa navui thei ahi.” 
Abraham chu agam mipi ho masang'a abohkhup kit in, 
Hichun Abraham'in Ephron le mipi atou cheng ho jah a aseikit in, “Hithei ponte, lungset tah in ngaiyun, nangho kom'a ka choh nom lheh e, keiman gam man chu kapeh lhing nom e, ka loinu longdamsa kavui theina dingin,” ati. 
Hichun Ephron in Abraham adonbut in, 
Ka Pakai lungset tah in ngaiyin hiche gam man hi dangka jali ahiye, amavang eini golcha kingailutna hi mantam ahi? Che inlang na loinu longdamsa chu gavui tan ati. 
Hichun Abraham chun Ephron in aseijat dangka jali chu apetan, hiche lai phat na kailhang thil man dungjuijin, thil kichoh dan ho chu Hit mite lah a tehse hon amu cheh uve. 
Abraham'in Ephron kom'a gam Machpelah kiti Mamre kom'a um chu achodoh tai. Hiche mun in ahop chu ahile atong, adam sung, chule akom'a thingphung um jouse jong ahi. 
Abraham dinga chu anei agou ahitan, hichea chun Hit mite kelkot kom'a cheng hon jong ahesoh un ahi. 
Phat chomkhat jouvin Abraham'in ajinu Sarah long Machpelah a Mamre kom'a Hebron kiti Canaan gamsunga avuitan ahi. 
Aleimun le aleilaiya songlhan keo chu Hit miten alhan mun dingin Abraham achansah tauve.
