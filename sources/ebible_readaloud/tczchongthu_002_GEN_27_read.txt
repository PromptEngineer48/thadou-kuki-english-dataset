Semtilbu.
27.
Ateh a kho amu chenlou nung hin nikhat hi Isaac in achapa alenjo pa chu akouve. 
“Ka chapa! Keima hi ateh kahitai, itih a thi ding kahim kasei thei tapoi, 
Hijeh chun tun nangma kigong inlang na manchah nathalpi kigot inlang gamlah a kei ding sa gakap in. 
Chutengleh keiman kadu lamtah in hin hon inlang hinchoi jin keiman kaneh ding chutengleh nangma hi kahinkho abei masanga phatthei kaboh ding nahi ati. 
Ahin Isaac le Esau kihouna chu Rebekah in guhthim chan ana ngaijin hichun Esau chu gamleng dinga akondoh phat in; 
Rebekah in Jacob jah a hitin aseiye, “Ngaijin, napa Isaac in na upa Esau hitin ahoulimpi kaja e,” ati. 
“Sa neiga delpeh inlang kadu lamtah in hin hon inlang hinchoijin keiman ne ingting chule Pathen masanga phatthei kaboh ding nahi, kahinkho abei masang'in,” ati. 
Hijeh chun tun kachapa ka thusei hi phate chan ngaiyin ka thusei ho hi najui ding ahi. 
“Nangma kelngoi hon lah a gachen lang ahoitah cheh anouni hin kaijin chutengleh keiman napa dulamtah a kahon peh ding ahi. 
Hiche teng chule nangman napa kom'a nachoi ding aman aneh a aneh jou teng phatthei naboh ding ahi, ama ahinkho adampet ahi,” ati. 
Ahin Jacob in anu Rebekah adonbut in ajah a, “Vetem in ka upa Esau a tichung amulin ahin keima vounem a timullou kahi. 
I kati ding ham? Kapa in eihin jut le ta le jou a kabol ahin hetdoh leh koi phatthei chan sanga sapsetna joh kachan joh ding ahi,” ati. 
Hinlah anu Rebekah in adonbut in, “Sapsetna ho aum le abon'in keiman kipoh tang kate ati, chapa bol in kati ho hi nabol angaiye, che inlang kelngoi chu kaheng'a hin kailut loiyin ati!” 
Hichun Jacob jong achen kelngoi chu anu kom'a ahin kailut tai. Chuin anun jong apa dulam tah in ahon peh tai. 
Hichun Esau vonho lah a aphapen chu insunga kon'in agala doh in achapa aneojo pa Jacob chu avonpeh tan ahi. 
Chule kelngoi nou teni mul chu akhut teni le angong amul bei lai achun atompeh in ahi. 
Hichun Rebekah in sa tuitah a ahon chule changlhah ama semsa chu achapa Jacob khut a apetan ahi. 
Hichun Jacob in changlhah le kelsa chu apa aga pen “Hepa” atileh aman jong “Hoiyo koipen nahim ka chapa?” 
Jacob in adonbut in, “Keima Esau na chapa apeng masa chu kahi,” ati. “Keiman kahin subulhit tai nei ngansena chu lungset tah in kithou inlang kahin choi sa tuitah a kihon hi ne inlang phatthei neiboh tan,” ati. 
Chuin Isaac in achapa chu adonbut in, “Iti dan ham? Man gangtah in nahin vaidoh loijen in ka chapa,” atile aman jong adonbut in “Pakai na Pathen'in gangtah a eihin lolhin sah ahi bouve,” ati. 
Hichun Isaac in Jacob henga, “Hung kikhin naijin katham phah theina din, nangma hi ka chapa Esau nahi mong hinam nahilou ham na jutle tem inge.” 
Hijeh chun Jacob in apa Isaac anailut in ahile ahin jutle tan ahi, “A awso hila Jacob awso ahin, akhut teni vang hi Esau khut ahi ngal in,” atin ahi. 
Amavang Jacob ahi chu ahetchen theilou ahitai, ajeh chu Jacob khut chu a upa Esau khut tobanga mul ahijeh in hichun Isaac chun Jacob chu phatthei abohtan ahi. 
Isaac in adong pha kit leuvin, “Nangma hi kachapa Esau nahi mong hinam?” atile, Jacob in adonbut in “Ka hinai,” ati. 
Chuphat in Isaac in aseiye, “Kaheng'a hin domnai tan nangma phatthei kaboh theina dingin ka chapan gangtah a naga matsa nahon chu ne ing kate,” ati, chuin Jacob in ahin doppeh phat in anen chule lengpitwi jong ahin doppeh in ahile Isaac chun adon tai. 
Hichun apa Isaac chun ajah a “Eihin nailut inlang neihin bengchop tem in ka chapa,” atile, 
Jacob in anai pheijin apa chu abengchop tai, chuin Isaac kivonna ho gimnam chu anah le len chuin achapa chu phatthei aboh in hitin aseiye, “Ah! Kachapa gimnam hi Pakai phatthei bohsa gamlah gimnam abang'e! 
Tun Pathen'in van'a kon'in daitwi na chap jing hen, leiset a leipha lailai nachan sah jing hen; chule chang le mim chule juning ninglhin sah jing hen. 
Nam simsen louvin naja hen chule mihem tamtah nangma noija kun cheh hen lang, nasopi ho chunga vai hin hom jingin lang; nanu sunga peng doh pasal jouse jong na henga kun cheh uhen. Nangma na gaosapte chu gaosap chang hen, chule nangma phatthei na bohte chu phattheiboh chang hen,” ati. 
Isaac in Jacob phatthei aboh chai chai jin, Jacob in apa angsung agah dalhah ding kon chet in a upa Esau chu agam lenna a kon'in ahunglhung pai jeng tai. 
Chuin Esau in jong neh ding a althet in ahonpeh in apa henga ahin doppeh in apa jah a aseitai. “Hepa, hung kithouvin lang, na hai hai pet a phatthei neiboh na dingin na chapa thasa hi hung ne o,” ati. 
Ahin Isaac in adonbut in, “Nang koi nahim?” Esau in adonbut in, “Na chapa apeng masa, Esau kahi,” ati. 
Isaac hatah in ati akithing tan aseiye, “Koiham ijo langa hin dom a hunga chu? Keiman ama hin dop chu kana ne tai. Nangma hung lhun masang jep chun phatthei jong ka boh joutai; phattheina jouse jong ama achang jou tai!” 
Chuin Esau in apa thusei jouse chu angai chai sohhel phat in Esau jong ha kan akap in hatah in alunghang in apa kom'a atao kit in, “Hepa, keima jong phatthei nei boh teitei in ong,” ati. 
Hinlah Isaac in aseiye, “Na sopipa ahungin eihung lhemlha tai, nang phatthei na chan ding jouse na lahpeh gamtai.” 
Esau in adonbut in, “Hiche jeh a chu amin Jacob kisah hitei jinte ong? Ajeh chu aman nivei jen eilhep lhah ahitai, phat masa in ka upat hina eina lahpeh in, tun ka phatthei chan ding ana kilah doh kit e, atin hichun keiya dinga phatthei khat beh jong neina khen pehlou hitam?” atile, 
Isaac in Esau adonbut in, “Keiman Jacob chu nangma chunga vaihom dingin kakoi tai, chule asopite jouse ama noija kun cheh diu, chule chang le mim ahin, juning ahin, haotheina jong kapeh ahitai, tua hijat nunga nangma ipi kabolpeh thei nahlai ding ham kachapa?” atitai. 
Ahi vang'in Esau in apa jah a “O hepa, phattheina khat chabeh jong umlou hitam? Phatthei neiboh teiteijin ong, hepa!” atin apengin akaptai. 
Chuin apa Isaac in ahoupin ajah a “Nangma leichung phalou lah lah a cheng ding nahi tan, chule van daitwi in jong hin chapkha taponte. 
Nangma na chemjam a naki vah ding chule na sopipa noija na um ding ahin nangma na hung ki-ondoh teng vang leh nangong chang'a konna ama namkol chu na pailhah ding ahitai,” ati. 
Hiche phat a pat a chu Esau in Jacob chu hatah a ahin vetda pan ahitai; ajeh chu apa Isaac in phattheina jouse Jacob aboh jeh ahi. Chule Esau in alungthim sunga hitin agel tan, “Kapa hin nikho jong hi sot tapon tin akichai tengleh keiman ka naopa Jacob hi katha ding ahi,” ati. 
Ahin achapa alenjo Esau lungthim tohgon jouse chu Rebekah jah a aki seidoh khah tan ahin Rebekah in mi asol'in achapa neojopa Jacob chu aga kousah in ajah a aseipehtai, “Ngaiyin, na upa Esau khun nang thana dingin lungthim tohgon anei tai. 
Chapa, chingthei tah in ngai jin, tun kigong inlang kasopipa Laban kom Haran langa jam tan. 
Hiche mun achun ni ijat hijongleh na upa Esau lungdam kahsen ga umtan. 
Ama ahung lungdam a na bolsetna ho asuhmil hel'a ahung lungdam mong mong tengleh keiman mi hinsol ing ting nahung holdoh sah nang'e. Nang ni ucha nikhat a na manthah lhon ding ka nompoi.” 
Hichun Rebekah in Isaac kom'a aseiye, “Keiman hiche Hit mite numei ho ka theichim behseh jeng tai! Ijem tin Jacob in hiche numei ho jin ahin kipui kit ding kagel theipoi kathilo tei ding ahi,” ati.
