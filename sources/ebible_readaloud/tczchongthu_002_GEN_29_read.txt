Semtilbu.
29.
Hichun Jacob akhol che chu abanjom in solam mite gam chu ahunglhung tai. 
Chuin aman agahven ahile loulaiya twisam amun akom'a chun kelngoi hon loi thum'a akol jong amun hiche gancha ho chu twikulla don ding twi ngah ahiuve, chule twisam chu songpi lentah in adal khum ahi. 
Hitia chu kelngoi hon ho abon'a ahung kisuhkim tengu leh kelngoi ching hon twikhuh kamsin songpi chu akhindoh jiuva kelngoi te twi achipsah jiuva achipva teng ule song chu akikoina ngai ngai twikhuh kam a chu ale khinlut kit jiu ahi. 
Chuin Jacob in amaho jah a “Sopite ho hoiya hung kon nahiu ham?” Atile ama hon adonbut un “Keiho Haran gam'a hung kon kahiuve,” ati. 
Amapan adong kit in “Nahor chapa Laban chu nahet khah hih uvem?” atile “Ama hon jong kahe nauve,” atiuve. 
Chuin Jacob in ajah uva “Ama chu dam dam a hinam?” tia adoh leh ama hon “Henge, adam jingnai; ven, tua hung kelngoihon toh hung kilhon khu achanu Rachel hija khu,” atiuve. 
Jacob in amaho jah a “Tuhin nisa sangtah hinalaija gancha hoi khom phat bon hilou lai, kelngoi hon chu twi chipsah uvin lang gavah pha uvin,” ati. 
Ama hon adonbut un, “Gancha ho jouse ahung kikim tokah in twi kipe thei ponte atiuve, hichun kelngoi chingho chun songpi chu twisam kot apat chun atangdoh un kelngoi hon jouse twija ka vahva ding ahi,” atiuve. 
Ama hon hitia akihoulim pet tah uchun Rachel chu apa kelngoi hon hotoh ahung kijot luttai, ajeh chu amanu jong kelngoi chinga pang ahiye. 
Hichun Jacob in anu sopipa Laban chanu Rachel le anu sopipa Laban kelngoite amu phat in anailut in twikhuh kamsin songpi chu alihdoh in apupa kelngoi hon chu twi achip sah in ahi. 
Hichun Jacob in Rachel chu abeng chop in awging tah in akap e. 
Jacob in Rachel jah a apa sopinu Rebekah chapa ahi thusim aseipeh tan ahile Rachel chu achen apa aga hetsah tan ahi. 
Laban in Jacob asopinu chapa ahung lhun ahet chun akimupin akoijin achop jengin chuin a inlama ahin puilut tai. Hichun Jacob in ima jouse thusim ho chu aseipeh tai. 
Chuin Laban in Jacob henga aseiye, “Nangma hi kagu ka chang chule kaphe kavou mong nahi,” ati. Hichun Jacob chu apu Laban kom'a lhakhat sung aumtai. 
Laban in Jacob henga aseiye, “Nangma hi ka insungmi nahi vang'in mohseh a tohman beiya naum ding chu adih theipoi, natoh man ipi hi ding ham nei seipeh temin tin adong tai.” 
Laban in chanu ni aneijin, alenjo nu min chu Leah ahin, chule aneojo nu min chu Rachel ahi. 
Ahin Leah mit chu aleng thimin amavang Rachel chu amel-hoi jin, amaiso jong lungset aum lheh jenge. 
Jacob in Rachel chu hatah a adeichat jeh in hitin aseiye, “Na chanu neojo nu Rachel khu kaji dia neipeh ding leh keiman kum sagi nana tong ing kate,” ati. 
Laban in adonbut in, “Hithei nante,” ati. “Ajeh chu keiman midang ho kapeh sanga nangma kapeh ding chu thilphajo ahi bouve, Hijeh chun keima kom'a um jingtan,” ati. 
Hichun Jacob in kum sagi sungin Rachel man toh kitoh dingin na atong tan, hinlah Jacob in Rachel angailut behset jeh chun ni chomcha toh abahsah in ahi. 
Chujouvin Jacob in Laban jah a hitin aseiye, “Keiman ka kitepna ho jouse ka subulhit tai, kajinu tun neipe tan kaluppi nom tai,” tin ahung thum tai. 
Hichun Laban in jet le veija cheng ule nao jouse akou khom sohkeiyin kichenna golvah chu aboltai. 
Hinlah hiche jan ahung thim phat chun Laban in Leah chu Jacob kom'a aga that lutpeh tan, Jacob in hichenu chu alupkhompi tai. 
Laban in asohnu Zilpah jong chu Leah kithopi a pang dingin apehtha tai. 
Ahin jingkah matah in Jacob ahung kitho leh Leah joh anahin, Jacob in Laban jah a aseijin, “Ipi iti a hitia hi neibol hitam? Kum sagi nangma noiya na kana toh chu Rachel man dinga katoh hilou ham! “Ibol a nei jou lhep ham?” tin aseiye. 
Laban in adonbut in “Keiho chondan a a unu umpet a hi anaonu jinei doh theilou ahi.” 
“Hiche hapta khat sung hin jinei dan hi subulhit tadin, hiche teng chule Rachel jong kapeh ding nahi, ahin nangman kum sagi ma kana natoh kit ding ahi” ati peh e. 
Chuin Jacob in Leah chu hapta khat lhingin akichenpin hiche jou chun Laban in Rachel jong ajinu dingin apetai. 
Laban'in asohnu, Bilhah jong chu achanu Rachel jenle a pang dingin apehtha tai. 
Hichun Jacob in Rachel jong alupkhompi tan, aman Rachel chu Leah sang'in hatah in adeijon, hichun Jacob in kum sagi ma Laban na atongkit in ahi. 
Ahin Pathen'in Leah kihot bol chu amudoh tan, hijeh chun anaobu ahondoh peh in, amavang Rachel chu achiingin chanei louvin aumtai. 
Leah vang chu achung agih tan; chapa khat ahingin amin Reuben asah tai, Ijeh inem itile, “Pathen'in kagimna ho eihetpeh tai, hiche jeh a hi kajipan eilungset ding ahitai,” ati. 
Chuin aman nao avop kit in chapa khat ahing kittai, ajeh chu “Keima eikihot bol hi Pakaijin ajah doh phat a chapa hiche jong hi eipeh kit ahiye” atin hijeh chun amin Simeon asah tai. 
Hichun Leah achung gih in, nao avop kittan amanun hitin aseiye, “Tu apat a hi kajipa toh thakhat tipum khat kahi lhon ding ahitai, ajeh chu keiman cha pasal thum kahinpeh tai hichun naosen chu amin chu Levi asah tai.” 
Chujouvin Leah in nao avop kit in chapa khat ahingin, “Tuchunga vang Pakai ka thangvahna ding ahi,” atin hijeh chun amin Judah asah tai, chujou apat in vang cha abang tan ahi.
