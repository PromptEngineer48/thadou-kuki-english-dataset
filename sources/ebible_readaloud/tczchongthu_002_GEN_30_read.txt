Semtilbu.
30.
Hichun Rachel'in Jacob cha ahinpeh lou akihetchet phat in asopinu athangset pin, hijeh chun Jacob kom'a atao tan, “Keijong cha le nao nei hinpeh in achuti louleh keima thi ding kanom jotai!” atin ahi. 
Jacob chu Rachel chunga alunghang jah jengin, “Keima Pathen kahipoi Ama ahi chanei louva nakoi, na naobu khah tan achu!” ati. 
Hichun Rachel in adonbut in, “Kasohnu, Bilhah hi, galup khompi tan, chutile amanun nao eihinpeh intin chua konna chu keiman jong ka chilhah insung kaki semdoh thei ding ahi,” ati. 
Rachel in Bilhah, asohnu chu, Jacob ji din apetai chuin Jacob in Bilhah chu akitimatpi tai. 
Bilhah in nao ahinvop in Jacob dingin chapa khat ahinpeh tai. 
Hichun Rachel in aseiye, “Pathen'in ka lunggel eisuh bulhitpeh tai! Chule kataona jong eisanpeh in chapa eipe tai” atin hijeh chun naosen min chu Dan asahtai.” 
Chuin Rachel sohnu Bilhah chun nao avopkit in Jacob chapa anina ahinpeh kittai. 
Hichun Rachel'in, “Keiman ka sopinu toh hatah a kaki set delna lhon a keiman kajou tai!” atin hichun naosen min chu Naphtali asah tai. 
Leah in nao aneikit tah louding ahin kihetdoh phat in asohnu Zilpah chu jidin apetai. 
Hijeh chun Leah sohnu Zilpah chun Jacob dingin chapa khat ahinpeh tai. 
Hichun Leah in hitin aseiye, “Hamphat aum bouve,” atin naosen min chu “Gad” asah tai. 
Hichun Leah sohnu Zilpah chun Jacob chapa anina chu ahinpeh kit in ahi. 
Leah chun aseiye, “Keiya din iti thanop um hitam! Tua hi numei dang jousen hatah a eikipapi diu ahitai,” atin hiche naosen min chu Asher asah tai. 
Nikhat Reuben chu suhlou chang vohlai in akondoh in ahile loulai achun *Joldei-Ai amun anu Leah ahin pohpeh in ahile, hichun Rachel in Leah kom'a “Lungset tah in na chapa Joldei-Ai hinpoh chu kei jong nei hom thei ding ham,” tin athum tai.” 
Hinlah Leah in Rachel jah a “Ka inneipu nei lahpeh jong thil neocha a nabol ham? Tua jong kachapa Joldei-Ai hinpoh jong lahpeh ding neigot kit ham?” atileh Rachel in adonbut in, “Chuti ahile na chapa Joldei-Ai poh man dingin tujan Jacob in nangma na lupkhompi thei nante,” ati. 
Hiche nilhah chun Jacob phailei lama konna ahung kinungle phat in Leah achen aga kimupin, “Tujan hi keima toh ilupkhom ding ahi, ajeh chu tujan a dinga ka chapan nao neitheina ahinpoh a tujana dinga ka choh nahitai,” hichun Jacob chu hiche jan chun Leah to ageh lhontai. 
Pathen'in Leah taona chu asanpeh tan, nao ahin voptan Jacob chapa nga lhinna ahinpeh tai. 
Hichun Leah in asei tan, “Ka sohnu chu kajipa ka pehdoh jeh in Pathen'in ka chohdoh man eipetai,” atin hichun naosen min chu Issachar asah tan ahi. 
Chujouvin jong Leah chun nao avop kit in Jacob dingin chapa gup lhinna ahinpeh kit tai. 
Hichun Leah in asei tan, “Pathen'in keima numei thilken phatah tah eikenpeh tai, tu apat a kajipan ngailutna sangtah einei ding ahitai, ajeh chu chapa gup kaneipeh tai,” hichun amanun naosen min chu Zebulun asah tai. 
Ahin phat chomkhat jouvin nao numei khat ahingin amin chu Dinah asah in ahi. 
Hichun Pathen'in Rachel chu asumil pon Pathen'in ataona asanpeh in anaobu chu ahonpeh tai. 
Hijeh chun Rachel in jong nao ahin vop in chapa khat ahin neitai; hichun Rachel in hitin aseiye, “Pathen'in ka jachatna jouse asubei tai,” ati. 
Rachel'in anao min chu Joseph asah in hitin aseiye, “Pakaijin chapa dang khat jong eihin sah kit teitei hen o!” ati. 
Rachel in Joseph ahin neijou phat chun, Jacob in Laban kom'a aseitai, “Nei nungsol tan kagam lang uva,” ati. 
Kajite kachate neipe dohtan phat sottah nangma in'ah lhachan kapang tan, tua hi keima ki nungle phat ahung hitai, chule itobanga nangma na katohpeh nahim nangman nakihet nai, ati. 
Ahin Laban in Jacob jah a aseiye, “Ahithei ding leh lungset tah in nei dalha hih in ajeh chu nangma ka insunga na hunglut apat in Pathen'in phatthei eiboh lheh jengin ahi. 
Chule Jacob in aseijin, “Natoh man ding ijat ham nei seipeh tan keiman kapeh ding nahi” ati. 
Chuin Jacob in adonbut in, “Nangman nahenai, itobanga nangma na katoh a na gancha ho hung pung thei ham?” ati. 
“Ajeh chu keima kahung masang chun themcha bou na neijin tun apung jal tan, Pathen'in hatah in phatthei na boh tan ahi. Ahile itih phat hi ding ham? Keima insunga ding katohna ding,” ati. 
Chule Laban in adong kit in, “Ipiba kapeh ding ham?” Jacob in aseiye, “Imacha neipe hih in, ahin thil khat nei bolpeh le chun keiman nagancha na vettup peh nang'e ati. 
Tun keiman na gancha kelngoi ho gakhol sohkei jing ting, amaho lah achun apol jouse hin lhengdoh inting, chule keltah ho lah a jong apol thim hihen hapol apol jouse hin lheng doh ingting, hichengse chu keima natoh man in nei bolpeh tan, ati. 
Khonung teng ka natoh man sutoh dinga nahung teng ka kitahna nahin muchen theina dingin, kelchate lah a apollou hihen, hapol a pollou akimu doh khah a, kelngoi lah a jong apollou khattou aum khah a ahile hiche ho se chu nangma a konna kaguh a kisim jeng thei ding ahi,” ati. 
Hichun Laban in adonbut in, “Apha lheh jeng'e nangman nasei bang chu himai tahen,” ati. 
Hinlah Laban in hiche nikho ma ma chun kelcha achal asandup apol, chule abeh beh a kang kelcha anu apol ho, chule kelngoi avom ho jouse chu achapa kom'a abon'in agathah doh sah sohkei in ahi. 
Hichun aman Jacob to nithum jen lampi a kigamlat in asolmang tai, chule Jacob in Laban gancha akithah lou jouse chu aching jom jing tan ahi. 
Chuin Jacob in Almond le Shiga phung bah ho chu amalngim in hiche thingbah avel akang lai ho chu kelcha ho amusah in ahi. 
Hichun Jacob in thingbah amalngim ho chu kelngoi ho twi donna konga akoiyin, kelngoite twichip dinga ahungji teng ule kijangmatji ahijeh uva akoi ahi. 
Kelngoi ho chu thingbah akang lai masanga chu kijangmatji, chuteng chule kelngoite chun akangpol jong, hapol a pol jong ahin hing doh doh tauve. 
Jacob in kelngoi ho chu ahoikhen in Laban kelngoi hon lah a akangpol ho leh avom ho chu ahoikhen in ama chan ding chengse chu atum in akhendoh in, Laban a lah achun ahoilut tapoi. 
Hiti chun kelngoi lah a asapha ho chu akijangmat teng ule thingbah ho amu uva, akijangmat theina ding uvin Jacob in jong thingbah ho amasang uva twi lonna kom laiya aga jampeh jin ahi. 
Hinlah Jacob in kelngoi atahsa phalou ho ahi tengleh aga jamle jipoi, ajeh chu Laban a chu asapha lou jeng ahin, Jacob a vang chu asapha jeng ahiuve. 
Hijeh chun Jacob chu ahung hao thei lheh tan, kelngoi tamtah ahin neitan, soh numei ahin soh pasal ahin tamtah aneijin, chule sangan le sangangsao jong tamtah anei doh tai.
