Semtilbu.
31.
Jacob in Laban chapa te ama chung'a alung damlou u thu ajatan ama hon aseiyun, “Jacob in ipau thil jouse akilah tan, chule anei agou athilkeo jouse khu ipauva konna akilamdoh ahi,” ati tauve. 
Chujongleh Jacob in Laban maiso aven ahile alung lhailou lungthim chom agel jong ahe chen tan ahi. 
Hichun Pathen'in Jacob chu ahoulimpin, “Nangma kinungle kit tan, napu napa chule na sopite chenna gam'a keiman kaumpi jing ding nahi,” ati. 
Hichun Jacob in mi asol in loulam a akelngoi chin nauva Rachel le Leah aga kousah tan ahi. 
Chuin Jacob in hitin aseiye, “Keiman ka hechen tai, napa lhon maiso kavet le lungthim chom chom aneitai, masang tobang ahi tapoi, hijongleh kapu kapate Pathen'in eiumpi jingnai. 
Nangnin jong nahe lhon nai, katha neisun kajo chan a napa lhon ding natoh katonge. 
Hinlah aman eijou lhep tan, kanatoh man jong somvei tobang eikhel pehtai, amavang Pathen'in keima chunga vang thil phalou imacha alhunsah pon ahi. 
Aman hitin aseiye, 'gancha apol ho jouse chu nangma nikho thaman hitante' atile gancha ho chu apol jeng hatah in ahung pung tul tul kit tan, hichun aman alunggel akhel kit in 'gancha availang jenga pol ho' atikit leh gancha anou jouse chu adung le avai langa pol bou apeng kit tai. 
Hitia hi Pa Pathen'in napa gancha ho jouse alah a keima eipehsoh ahitai. 
Chuin phat chomkhat jou kelngoite kijangmat laijin jan mang khat kaneijin, ka khodah in ahile kelpi chunga kaldoh kelchal ho chu apol ho leh adup ho aki timatpiu kamui. 
Chuin hitia mang kanei pet in Pakai vantil khat in eihin houpin 'Jacob!' eihin tin kenjong 'Hoijo hikom'a kaum'e,' kati. 
“Chuin Pakai vantil chun, 'Dah inlang gahve temin, kelpi ho chunga kaldoh kelchal ho khu apol, adup, akangpol, availanga pol jengin amat e, ijeh inem itile Laban in na bolsetna jouse keiman abonchan ka musoh keitai. 
Keima na Pathen Bethel mun'a song khom thao nanu a na kigahna Pathen chu kahi, tun kipatdoh in hiche gam hi dalhan lang na penna gam langa kile kit tan.'” 
Chuphat in Rachel le Leah in adonbut in, “Keinin imacha boina kanei lhonpoi; kapa lhon nei le gou chanvou ka chan lhon ding um beh ham?” 
Aman dalpam mi a eigel lhon hilou ham? Ajeh chu aman eijoh doh lhon in keini kichohna sum jouse jong eisuh mang peh tauve. 
Chule nei le gou jouse Pathen'in kapa lhona kon a alahmang peh ho jouse chu eiho le ichate ho a ahi bouve. Hijeh chun tun Pathen'in najah a asei jouse chu ipi hijongleh bol jengin,” ati lhon tai. 
Hichun Jacob akipat doh in ajite le achate jouse chu Sangan chunga atoudoh sah sohkei tan; 
chule aganchaho jouse amasang lam uva ahoiyin Paddan-aram gam'a anei agou jouse aki suhtup in Canaan gam'a apa Isaac henga che dingin aumtai. 
Chuin Laban chu kelngoi mulvou dinga akondoh phat in Rachel in apa insung'a semthu pathen chu ana kiguh mang tai. 
Jacob in jong Aramea mipa Laban chu alhem lhan, ajamdoh dingu jong aseipeh pon ahi. 
Hichun Jacob in anei agou jouse akipoh in Eupharate vadung apal galkai in Gilead thing lhang lam ahin jontai. 
Ahin nithum jouvin Jacob ajammang tai tithu chu Laban ahin seipeh un; 
Chuin Laban in asopi ho cheng akou khom in, Jacob chu ahin nung del tauvin ahile, nisagi lhin ni chun Gilead lhang gamsunga ahin juilut tan ahi. 
Hinlah Pathen'in Laban Syrea mipa chu janin ana kimupin hitin aseiye, “Kichih theijin Jacob chu imacha nalo theilou ding ahi,” ati. 
Hichun Laban'in Jacob chu ahin phatan, chule Jacob in thinglhang lah a chun aponbuh akison tauvin, chuin Laban le a-u anaoten jong Gelead thinglhang a Jacob to kigamlat louvin aponbuh u akison tauve. 
Chuin Laban in Jacob jah a aseiye, “Ibola hitobanga hi neijou lhep hitam ati, chule iti dana ka chanu teni jong gal a sohchang ho banga nabol jeng ham? 
Ibola guhthim cha a najam mang uham? Ibol'a neijou lhep ham ati, chule nache ding iti dan a nei hetsah loubeh ham? Neina hetsah le chun thupitah a kilhahna ankong le la ngaitah tah kisan tin, tumging to kithoa kivailhah tadinga ati. 
Na thilbol ho hi ngolna in na dimset e, ka chanu teni cha katute jong kabeng chop man tapon, kilhahna jong kana bol thei tapoi! ati. 
Tua hi nangma ka suhbei thei nahi, hinlah napu napate Pathen'in janhi nilhah in eina kimuto pin gihna jong eineiyin Jacob chu na suhboi theilou ding ahi eiti! 
Nangma lunggel na inlam a nache ding na thanopna chu ka hethem lheh e, kham thei jong nahipoi, napa insung miho toh kimuto kal na ngalel jong kahe nai, hinlah ibola ka pathen houna doiphung chu naguh ham? ati. 
Jacob in adonbut in, “Keiman nangma kana ging lheh in ahi, ajeh chu nangman, nachanu teho chu hunam a nei lahpeh ding ham tia kana gel ahi. 
Amavang na doibom vang chu ahile nangman hol inlang na mudoh thei leh namuna mi chu koi hile ahinkho subei jeng tan ati, chule nangma thil ho jong keima sopiho ana lakha aum leh kanung peh ding nahi; Jacob in vang Rachel in ana lah chu ana hetlou ahi. 
Laban chu doibom hol in Jacob ponbuh a ache masapen in , aban'in Leah kom'ah, hiche jou chun Leah soh teni kom'ah achen, hinlah imacha amudoh pon, achaina in Rachel ponbuh a alut in ahi. 
Rachel in apa insung doibom chu ana ladoh in Sangan pho kah a chu ana sel'a chule achunga chu ama ana tou ahi, hichun Laban in ponbuh sung aning akah aphet vang'in imacha amudoh tapon ahi. 
Amanun apa kom'a aseitai, “Lungsettah in nei ngaidam in ka pakai keima numei hina lhinna kaneijin kadin theilou ding ahi,” Laban vang ahol chu atang dehpon amavang insung doibom chu amudoh pon ahi. 
Hichun Jacob hatah in ahung lunghang tan Laban'in apupa jah a asei tan ahi, “Keima ipi ka guchatna ka jkonna umham?” atitan, “Chule ipi mong kabol khel a hichan geiya hi kanung neihin del a keima migucha banga nei bol hitam? 
Nangman kanei kathil koina jouse aning akah geiyin naphet in, tun nang thil nakimudoh ho chu neivet sah un, sopi ule nao ho masang laitah ahin hin podoh in, ama hon eini kahlah a thutanna nei mai uhen!” ati. 
“Nangma kom'a kum som ni na gancha ho kana vettup peh in, na gancha ho kelngoi ahin na kelcha ho ahin imacha boina ana umpon hiche kum hijat kana vettup sunga chun kelngoi khat jong kana kilah khapoi. 
Keiman gamsa in, gancha ho athasa jong kahin polut khapon, athi amang ho jong keima nikhotha toh suhto din kana boljin, gancha ho sun hileh jan hileh amang ho jouse chu keima lhalo toh suhto din nei kahpeh jin ahi. 
Hiche hi keima dinmun anahi. Hitipi chun sun teng nisan eihalvampai gon, jan tengleh dap kathoh jin, kamit tenin la imutna him him amupha pon ahi. 
Hitobanga chu kum somni nangma insunga kana kimanchah ahi. Keiman kum som le kum li nangma insunga lhacha in kana pangin, na chanu teni lona in kum gup kana tohbe in gancha ho lona in chule kana toh man somvei tobang nei khelpeh in ahi. 
Kapate Pathen, Abraham Pathen, kapa Isaac ginpa chun eipanpi lou hile tua hi nangman khut keohel tahbah a neina solmang ding ahitai. Pathen'in ka gentheina le kakhut tah a ka tohgimna hi amua jan nilhah a chu na gihsal ahiye,” ati. 
Hichun Laban in hitin Jacob adonbut in, “Hiche numei ho hi ka chanu ahin, hiche chapang ho jong katute ahi, chule gancha ho jouse jong abon'a keima gancha ahi. Chule ipi hitale namu phah chan hi aboncha keiya jeng ahi cheh nai. Amavang keiman tua hi ka chanute chule achate ho chunga ipi kabol ding hitam? ati. 
Hijeh chun hungin eini kikah a kitepna nei hite hichun eini eikan khom cheh lhon ding ahi.” 
Hichun Jacob in hetjingna dingin song khom khat aphutdoh tai. 
Hichun Jacob in asopi ho lah a aseijin, “Songho hin chom khom un,” ati, ama hon song chu khat chunga khat asethop un, hichun Jacob le Laban song kise thop kom'a atou lhon'in kitepna ankong chu anekhom lhon tan ahi. 
Songlhuh kise chu Laban in Jegar-sahadutha asah in (hichu Aramaic paova “hettohsah songthop” tina ahin), hiche ma ma chu Jacob in Galeed asah e (hichu Hebrew paova “hettohsah songthop” tina ahi). 
Hichun Laban in, “Tuni nikhoa hiche song lhuh hi kei le nang kah a sakhia panga ahitai,” tia, hiche jeh chun amin jong Galeed asah in ahi. 
Hiche mun jong hi Mizpah akiti tai, aman aseijin, “Ajeh chu nangma le keima jong khonung teng jongleh gamla tah a umjongleh hen Pathen'in eiumpi jing lhon hen.” 
Ka chanute ho nahin suh gentheija numei dang ho jia nahin kipuiya ahile, koima chan muhih jongleh abonchan Pathen'in amusoh keiye, Pathen hi nang le kei kikah a kitepna a hiche thua hi pang jing ding ahi. 
Hiche song athopma kise ho hi Jacob in Laban jah ah, “Vejingin kei le nang kah a kihet themna hijing ding ahitai. 
Hiche song kise thop hile songphu hi sakhi a pang ahin, ken jong nangma suse dinga hiche hi kahin khokhel louhel ding, nangin jong keima suse dinga hiche hi nahin khokhel louhel ding ahi. 
Abraham Pathen, Nahor Pathen chule amani Pa Pathen chu eini kah a thutan ding ahitai,” ati. Hijeh chun Jacob jong apa Isaac ginpa minin akihahsel tai. 
Hichun Jacob in hiche molchunga chun Pathen henga maicham asemin kitepna ki hetthemna ankong jong asemin, anneh anei jou uchun hiche jan machun molchunga chun ageh tauve. 
Laban ajing chun matah in athoudoh in achanute ho le atute ho jouse abonchan achop sohkeiyin, chule abonchauvin phatthei aboh sohkeiyin, hichun ama a inlam ma aki nungle kittai.
