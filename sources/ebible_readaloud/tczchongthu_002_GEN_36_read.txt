36.
Hiche hose hi Esau phunggui ahi (Edom tia jong kisei) . 
Esau in Canaan nungah ni aji din ana kipuijin khat nu chu Hit mi Elon chanu Adah ahi, anina nu chu ahile Anah chanu Oholibamah ahin Hiv mi Zibeon dinga atunu ahi. 
Chule Esau in asopinu Basemath jong jidin akipuiyin, Ishmael dia achanu, Nebaioth dia anaonu ahi. 
Adah chun Esau din chapa Eliphaz ahinpeh in, Basemath chun chapa Reul ahingin ahi. 
Oholibamath chun Canaan gam'a achen laiyin chapa thum Esau din ana hinpeh in, amaho min chu ahile Jeush, Jalam, chule Korah ahiuve. 
Esau in ajite le achate cheng abonchauvin, agancha ho jouse, abongchal ho jouse, chule anei agou thil jouse Canaan gam'a achen laiya alamdoh jouse toh mun chom ajon tan, asopipa Jacob chu adalha tan ahi. 
Ijeh inem itile anei agou lhon, agancha lhon chuleh adinmun lhon jeh chun aumkhom lhon theina dia gam chu ninglhing joulou ahitai. 
Hijeh chun Esau (Edom) chu thinglhang lam a Seir kiti mun'a acheng tai. 
Hiche hi Esau chilhah Seir thinglhang gam’a cheng Edom mite chu ahi. 
Hiche ho hi Esau chapa te min ahi: Eliphaz kitipa hi Esau loinu Adah in ahin, chule Reul kitipa hi Esau loinu Basemath in ahin ahiye. 
Eliphaz chilhah ho Teman, Zepho, Gatam chule Kenaz ahiuve. 
Timna, Eliphaz thaikemnu chapa khat ahingin amin chu Amalek ahi. Amaho se hi Esau jinu Adah in ahin ama hung kikhailha chilhah ho ahiuve. 
Reul chapa te ho chu Nahath, Zerah, Shammah chule Mizzah ahi. Amaho se hi Esau jinu Basemath chilhah ho ahiuve. 
Esau chate ho Oholibamah in ahin ho chu, Jeush, Jalam, chule Korah ahiuvin, Oholibamah hi Anah chanu Zibeon dinga atute ahung hitai. 
Hiche ho hi Esau chate chilhah te phung choma hung lamkai ho ahiuve, Esau chapa tahpen chilhah ho Eliphaz a hung kikhai lha ho, Teman, Omar, Zepho, Kenaz ahin, 
Korah, Gatam chule Amalek amaho se hi Eliphaz phung lamkai Edom gamsunga cheng ahiuve. Amaho jouse hi Esau jinu Adah chilhah a konna hung peng doh ahiuve. 
Esau chapa Reul a konna hung pengdoh phung lamkai ho chu, Nahath, Zerah, Shammah chule Mizzah ahiuve. Amaho cheng hi phung lamkai Edom gamsunga Reuel chate ahi. Hiche ho jouse hi Esau jinu Basemath chilhah a konna hung peng doh ahiuve. 
Esau chate ho Oholibamah toh ahin ho Jeush, Jalam chule Korah ahiuve. Amaho se hi phung lamkai cheh jong ahung hitauvin, hiche phung lamkai ho sese hi Esau loinu Oholibamah kitinu Anah chanu chilhah a hung kon doh ahiuve. 
Hiche phung ho hi Esau a konna hung kondoh (Edom mi a jong kiheu) phung lamkai hon ahetpeh ho chu ahiuve. 
Hor mi Seir kitipa chilhah chengse Edom gam'a ana cheng ho chu amaho hi ahiuve: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, 
Dishon, Ezer, chule Dishan ahi. Amaho hi Edom gam'a cheng Hor mi Seir chilhah a phung lamkaiho chu ahiuve. 
Lotan chilhah ho chu Hori le Hemam ahi. Lotan sopinu min chu Timna ahi. 
Shobal chilhah ho chu Alvan ahin, Manahath ahin, Ebal ahin, Shepho ahin, chule Onam ahi. 
Zibeon chilhah chu Aiah le Anah ahi lhon'e. (Anah kitipa hin apa gancha sangante achin pet a gamthip lah a twisamputna ana mudoh ahi). 
Anah chilhah chu achapa Dishon le achanu Oholibamah ahi lhone. 
Dishon chilhah ho chu Hemdan, Eshban, Ithran chule Keran ahiuve. 
Ezer chilhah ho chu Bilhan ahin, Zaavan ahin, chule Akan ahiuve. 
Dishan chilhah chu Uz le Aran ahi lhone. 
Hor mite upa lamkai ho chu Lotan ahin, Shobal ahin, Zibeon ahin, Anah ahin, 
Dishon, Ezer chule Dishan ahiuvin aphungmin uhi phung lamkaiho min dungjuiya kisah Seir gamsunga cheng ho chu ahiuve. 
Hiche lengho hi Israel mite lah a lengvaipo ana um masanga Edom gamsunga lengvaipo ho chu ahiuve. 
Beor chapa Bela chun Edom gamsunga lengvai anapon, ama khopi chu amin Dinhabah ahiye. 
Bela athi jou chun Zerah chapa Jobab chapa chu Bozrah gamsunga vaipon ahung pangin ahi. 
Jobab athi jou chun Teman mi Husham mipa chun lengvai ahin hom kittai. 
Husham thi jou chun Bedad chapa Hadah chun lengvai ahinpon, ama chenna khopi Avith a pat in Moab gamsunga Midian ho jouse gal'a ahin joutan ahi. 
Hadad thi jou chun Masrekah khopi a cheng Samlah in apanmun ahinlo kittai. 
Samlah thi jou chun vadung a um Rehoboth khopi a cheng Shaul chun apanmun alon ahi. 
Shaul thi jou chun Achor chapa Baal-hannan kitipa chun lengvai ahipo kit in ahi. 
Acbor chapa Baal-hannan thijou chun Hadar in Pau khopi apat in lengvai ahipo pantan, Hadar jinu min chu Mehetabel ahin Matred chanu Me-zahab dinga atunu ahi. 
Amaho phung lamkai jouse hi Esau chilhah a konna hung kondoh cheh ahiuvin, Timna gam'a cheng ahiuvin, mi upa Alvah ahin, mi upa Jetheth ahin, 
Mi upa Oholibamah ahin, mi upa Elah ahin, mi upa Pinon ahin, 
Mi upa Kenaz ahin, mi upa Teman ahin, mi upa Mibzar ahin, 
Mi upa Magdiel ahin, chule mi upa Iram ahi; hicheng hi leiset atoupha na gamsung uva akho mun dung jui uva Edom chilhah a kon a mi upaho chu ahiuve.
