Semtilbu.
40.
Hicheng thilsoh kichai nung chun Pharaoh lengpa natong ju vaihompa le an vaihompan apupa Egypt lengpa doumah abol lhon'in achonse lhontai. 
Hiche lamkai teni chunga hin Pharaoh nasatah in alunghang tan ahi. 
Chuin Pharaoh in amani chu Joseph umna lengpa songkul natong ho umna ah akoiyin ahi. 
Songkul ngahpan amani chu Joseph thaneina noiya a umsah in Joseph in jong amani chu vetkolna anei jingin ahi. 
Jan khat chun songkul sunga kihen teni Egypt lengpa ju vaihompa le an vaihompa chun mang khat cheh, amang ledohna toh kilhon cheh in anei lhon'in ahi. 
Ajing jingkah a Joseph in amani amu chun lungkham mel aput lhon chu ahedoh tai. 
“Ipi dia hibanga lhungkham nahi lhon ham?” tin adong’e. 
Amanin adonbut lhon'in, “Janlhah in keinin mang kanei gel lhon'in amavang koima chan kamang ipi kiseina ahi aledoh thei aumpoi,” ati lhon e, ahin Joseph in adonbut in, “Mang letheina kiti hi Pathen kin ahi.” Chule “Namang lhon chu neisei peh lhon'in” ati. 
Hichun khon dom le a pang lamkaipa chun Joseph chu amang ho chu aseipeh tai. “Ka mangin lengpi bah ka masanga kamun ahi. 
Hiche lengpi phunga chun abah thum ajam kamun, gang tah in ahung mum in chuin apahlha tai, hiche aga ho chu lengpi theiga ahung hitai. 
Pharaoh chakhon chu ka khut chunga ana um'in, hichun keiman lengpiga phabep chu atwi khon a chun kahehlhan ahi” ati. 
“Mangin avetsah chu hiche hi ahi,” tin Joseph in aseijin. “Thingbah thum chu nithum vetsahna ahi.” 
Nithum sunga hin Pharaoh in nadomtou intin, chule masanga na natohna a nanung koi kit ding ahi. 
Chule nangma kom'a katao ahi, chanvou pha nahung chan teng neihin sumil hihbeh in Pharaoh kom'a hitobang ka thilbol ho hi eina seipeh in, ajeh chu songkul'a konna eilha doh theina dingin, ati. 
Ajeh chu Hebrew gam a ka in apat a eikiman doh tua hiche songkul'a kaum hi mona neilou kahin bolkhel kaneipoi. 
Hichun changlhah hom'a lamkaipa chun mang aphalam a hoitah a Joseph in ale chu amu le ama jong atha anom lheh in; Joseph jah ah hitin aseiye, “Keiman jong janmang kaneiye, ka manga chun paipeh thum changlhah dimset kalu chunga kapun ahi. 
Pharaoh a dingin Paipeh achunga pa sunga chun changlhah ajat lhingsel in aum in, ahin vacha ho ahung lengun ka luchunga changlhah ho chu anedoh tauve.” 
Joseph in amang chu alepeh tan, “Paipeh thum chun nithum ma avetsah ahin, 
Nithum sunga hi Pharaoh in nalu adopsang ding thingphunga nakhai sanga, vachaten naphe anehchai ding'u ahi.” 
Pharaoh in nithum jouvin ama penni kipapina in anoiya a semang pachonga pang jouse akou khom in hiche a pang dinga chu ju vaihom lamkaipa le an vaihom lamkaipa chu alhadoh gel in, 
Ju vaihom pa chu aju vaihomna ma man apansah kit in, aman jong jukhon chu Pharaoh khut a agapetai. 
Ahin an vaihom lamkaipa vang chu Joseph in amani henga mang alepeh bang chun akhai lih tan ahi. 
Hichun ju vaihom lamkaipan Joseph chu ageldoh tapon asumil deltai.
