Semtilbu.
46.
Hichun Jacob chu Egypt gam jon din akipatdoh tan anei agou jouse jong apon Beersheba ahung lhungin hiche jan chun apa Isaac Pathen min'in maicham asemin ahi. 
Hiche jan chun Pathen'in gaova akihoulim pin, “Jacob! Jacob!” tin akouve. Aman jong, “Hikom'a kaume,” ati. 
“Keima napu napate Pathen kahi,” tin ahoulim pin, nangma Egypt gam langa chesuh ding kicha hihbeh in ajeh chu Keiman nangma hiche mun'a chu nam lentah kaso ding nahi. 
Keiman Egypt lama na chesuh teng ka kilhonpi ding nahi, chule keiman nangma ka hin nungpui kit ding Egypt a nathi ding ahi, ahin namit nasinna mun a chu Joseph in naumpi ding ahi, ati. 
Hichun Jacob in Beersheba adalhan chule achate sen Egypt lama apui tauvin, kangtalai chunga ama ahin chapang naosen ho jong chule ajite ho chengse jong Pharaoh in ima ma agontup peh bangin apuisuh tauvin ahi. 
Ama hon anei agou jouse chule agancha hou jouse Canaan gamsunga ana kilamdoh ho jouse jong apo tauvin ahi. 
Achate hou sese chule atute ho jouse jong chule achanute ho jong chule achanute chate jouse jong ama chilhah namkim jouse toh Egypt ahin jon tauve. 
Tua min hung kipe ho chengse hi Israel chilhah Jacob chate Egypt gam'a ana cheho chu ahiuve: Reuben chu Jacob chate lah a atahpen ahi. 
Reuben chapa te chu Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron chule Carmi ahiuve. 
Simeon chapa te chu Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jakin, Johar chule Shaul ahiuve. (Shual nu chu Canaan numei ahi). 
Levi chapa te chu Gershon, Kohath chule Merari ahiuve. 
Judah chapa te chu Er, Onan, Shelah, Perez chule Zerah ahiuve. (Er le Onan chu Canaan gam'a ana thi lhon tajongleh) Perez chapa te chu Hezron le Hamul ahi lhon'e. 
Issachar chapa te chu Tola, Puah, Jashub chule Simron ahiuve. 
Zebulun chapa te chu Sered, Elon chule Zahleel ahiuve. 
Hiche ho hi Paddan-aram gam'a achen lai uva peng Jacob le Leah chapa te ahiuve, chule amaho tilouva apenbe achanu, Dinah chu ahi; Jacob chate jouse numei le pasal abon'a Leah a konna hung peng doh jouse chu agoma somthum le thum ahiuve. 
Gad chapa te chu Zephon, Haggi, Shuni, Ezbon, Eri, Arodi chule Areli ahiuve. 
Asher chapa te chu Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi chule Beriah ahiuve. Serah chu asopinu u ahi. Beriah chapa te chu Heber chule Malkiel ahi lhon e. 
Hiche pasal ho sese hi Zilpah chate ho Leah sohnu akon a Jacob chilhah ho ahiuvin agom a som le gup ahiuve. 
Jacob chilhah Rachel in ahinpeh chu Joseph le Benjamin ahi. 
Joseph chate ni Egypt gam'a peng chu Mannaseh le Ephhraim ahi lhone, amani nu min chu Asenath Potiphera chanu On gam'a thempu pa chanu ahiye. 
Benjamin chapa te chu Bela, Beker, Ashbel, Gera, Namaan, Ehi, Rosh, Muppim, Huppim chule Ard ahiuve. 
Hiche ho hi Rachel le Jacob a konna hung peng achapa te ahiuvin abonchauvin som le li ahiuve. 
Dan chapa chu Hushim ahi. 
Napthali chapa te chu Jahzeel, Guni, Jezer chule Shillem ahiuve. 
Hiche pasal ho hi Bilhah in ahin peh Laban in Rachel soh dinga apeh nu akon ahiuve. Jacob in Bilhah toh ahin chu abon'un pasal sagi ahiuve. 
Jacob chilhah te Egypt ma ama toh cheho chu achate jiho sim louvin mihem somgup le gup ahiuve. 
Hiche tilou chun Jacob in Egypt ma cha pasal ni aneiben hijeh chun abonchauvin Jacob insung mite chu Egypt gam'a anachen laiyu chun mihem somsagi alhingset un ahi. 
Amaho alamjot nauva alhunna diu ahung naithim tan, Jacob in Judah chu Goshen jotna lamhil hedin asol in, phat chomkhat jouvin amaho alhung tauve. 
Joseph a sakol kangtalai chunga atou doh in apa Jacob chu Goshen lama aga kimuto pitai. Hichun Joseph in apa Jacob amu phat chun phat sottah a ngongkoi in akap tai. 
Hichun Jacob in ahin hou in, Joseph kom'a aseitai, “Keima tua hi thi thei kahi tai, ajeh chu nangma nana hing nalai in tuhin namai kamu tai,” ati. 
Hichun Joseph in asopite le apa insung mite jah a “Keima chetou ing ting Pharaoh jah a thu galhut ing ka ting ajah a, ‘Canaan gam'a cheng ka sopite le kapa insung mite ka heng ahunglhung tauve. 
Amaho hi kelngoi ching mi ahiuvin, chule ama hon anei thil jouse le agancha hou jong ahin kipui uve gati ing'e.'” 
“Ahin Pharaoh in ipi na natoh u nahin doh teng ule, 
na donbut dingu, “Keiho, nasohte hi, kapu kapa teu bangin kelngoi ching kahiuve,' nati diu ahi. Chuteng Pharaoh in Goshen lhanglam a nahin chen sah diu ahi; ajeh chu Egypt miten kelngoi chingho atheinom pouve,” ati.
