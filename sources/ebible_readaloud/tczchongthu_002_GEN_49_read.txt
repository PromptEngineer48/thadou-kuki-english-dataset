Semtilbu.
49.
Hichun Jacob in achate jouse akou khom in hitin aseiye, “Keima naivel a hung kikhom un, chule keiman nangho thuguh ka seipeh ding nahiuve, khonunga ipi hunglhung ding ham na seipeh uvinge,” ati. 
“Hungun chule ngaijun, nangho Jacob chate ho; napau, Israel thusei ngaiyun.” 
“Reuben, kachapa atahpen, ka thahatpen laiya kanei, ka khanghoi laitah a kahin chule nangma amasa penna jana chang chule nangma amasapen a thahat chang nahi. 
Nangma twi kimang banga phatna najou tapoi, ajeh chu napa lupna jalkhun nasuboh tai.” 
“Simeon le Levi sopi khat ahi lhon'e, chule amanchah lhon ho chu galsatna ding bou ahi. 
Keima lhagao vang amani komah jaoda maihen, chule amani tohgon jong jahda ding kanom e; ahin amani alunghan teng le mihem hinkho ahin suhbei lhon ding ahi, chule amanin golseh a bongchal akipalsah lhon ding ahi. 
Jacob chate ho lah a kahop khen ding keiman Israel sunga amani chu kathe cheh ding ahitai, alunghan nalhon sapset ahitai, ajeh chu tijat umtah, alunghan nalhon jong phalou tah ahi.” 
“Judah nangma nasopi hon nathang-at uve. Nangman na melma te angonga na matchah ding, nasopi ho jouse nangma masanga kun cheh diu ahi. 
Ka chapa Judah hi keipi bahkai nou in an anneh jou pet ahi. keipi bahkai banga kitaule a lumji tobang; keipi tobang chu kon athodoh jou ding ham? 
Leng tenggol chu Judah a konin kikhen kang pontin, vaihompa tenggol jong akhanggui mite chutoh kikhen ponte, aneipa akondohna mun ahung masangse a, nam jousen ajabol ding pa chu. 
Aman a sangan chu lengpi thei phunga ahen in, chule sangan nou chu abah hoipen lengpi bah a ahen in, chule apon ho chu lengpitwiya asop in chule ama von ho chu lengpitwiya asop ngim e. 
Amit teni chu lengpitwi sangin athimjon, chule ahatu jouse chu bongnoi sangin akangjon ahi.” 
“Zebulun chu twikhanglen panga cheng ding chule ama kom'a chu kongho kinga ding; Sidon geiya agamgi akeh ding ahi.” 
“Issachar vang chu ahile sangan hattah ahi, ama chu hahsatna molni kikah a um ahi. 
Aman ngahmun khat amudoh in chule agam nop dan jong chu dei chat um ahi; hijeh chun aman thilpo dingin alengkou adon, hunam in soh dingin akipelut tai.” 
“ Dan in Israel phungdang ho bang a amite chunga vai ahin hop ding ahi. 
Dan chu lampi lhanglang a gul tobang ahi, chule lampi a gulse kijam tobang ahin, ama chun sakol keng achuh le achunga toupa akinung le lhah tai. 
Keiman nangma katahsan e huhhingna kachan theina din, O Pakai!” 
“Gad chunga mipi hon lam ajot diu ahin achaina aman amaho chunga lam ahin jot ding ahi.” 
“Asher chu anneh neng ding chule lengho nehthei ding tah akhut a soh ding ahi.” 
“Napthali chu sajuhpi nou hoitah neiya kichamlhat sah ahi.” 
“Joseph hi lengpi thingga phatah tobang ahi, chule twinah phung thingbah ga phatah ahi, abah hon jong bang akhokhel gamtai. 
Thalpi kapthem hon avetda lheh un, amapa akap un chule abol genthei lheh jeng uve. 
Ahin amapa thalpi alhou lheh in, chule Kelngoi chingpa, Israel te Songpi, Jacob Thahatna chun aban thahat sah in ahi. 
Napa panpi Pathen'in van chunga phattheina tincheng, chule leithol noilanga twi dim bang phattheina, chule noitwi le naobu a phattheina jouse naboh tahen. 
Napan phatthei nabohna ho chu masanga napute phattheina ho sangin phatthei joh hi maihen, chule molsang pen ho sangin sangpen maihen. Hiche phattheina ho jouse hi Joseph chunga um jing tahen, ajeh chu ama hi asopite lah a dinga leng cha ahi.” 
“Benjamin chu Sial tobang ahi, ajeh chu jingkah lam le amelmate chu aval lhum jin nilhah lam teng alotup ho chu ahomkhen jenge.” 
Amaho se hi Israel phungsom le phungni ahiuve, hiche thucheng ho hi apan aseipeh u ahi; amaho jouse chu phatthei aboh in chule kilhahna thucheng aneijin, chule amaho jouse khat khat in ama hina cheh in phatthei aboh cheh in ahi. 
Hichun Jacob in ahilchah un, “Kapu kapate kom jon a keima thivah ding kahitai. Hit mi Ephron louva kapa le kapu kivuina songko a chun neihin vuikhom’un. 
Hiche songlhan chu Machpelah loumun Mamre nisolam Canaan gamsunga um ahin, Abraham'in lhan mun a man jing dinga Hit mipa Ephron henga ana choh ahi. 
Hiche mun'a chun Abraham le Sarah akivui lhon'in, chule hiche mun chu Isaac le aloinu Rabekah jong akivuijin chule keiman jong hilai mun a chu Leah kana vui ahi. 
Hiche gam chu phaicham kotong lam chu kapu Abraham'in Hit mipa kom'a ana choh doh ahi. 
Hichun Jacob chu achapa te athulhah chaijin jalkhun chunga akeng teni asep doh in ama ahaitan tai, chuin ama amite to akigom tan tai.
