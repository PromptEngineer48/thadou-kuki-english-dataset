Semtilbu.
50.
Joseph chu apa senan akineiyin kap pumin akoijin achop in ahi.. 
Chuin Joseph in asohte louthem ho chu apa longdamsa tom din thu apen, hichun louthem ho chun atom tauvin ahi. 
Hitobanga longdamsa kitom chu nisom li tobang lut jia ahi; chule Egypt mihon ama chu ni somsagi alunghem piuvin ahi. 
Hichun lunghem na nikho ho chu ahung kichai tan, Joseph in lengpa Pharaoh insung mite chu ahoulimpin, “ Lungset tah in kadei khat Pharaoh kom’a ka khelin nei seipeh uvin. 
Kapa in adamlai in eina kihahsel sah e. “Aman kajah a, “Ngaijin, keima ka hinkho beivah ding ahitai. Ka tahsa hi Canaan gam'a hin nung lhut teiyin lang, chule keima kivuina dia lhan ka kisemna mun'a neivui tei ding ahi.' Hijeh chun lungset tah in neisol inlang kapa ga kivui inge. Kavui chai tengleh kinomsot louva kahung kile pai ding ahitai.” 
Joseph ngehna chu Pharaoh in aphasah tai. “Napan nangma nakitep sahna bangin chen lang gavui tan,” ati. 
Hichun Joseph chu apa vui din achetan ahile Pharaoh in alhonpi dingin Egypt gamsunga alen alal ho ahin asohte ahin, asemang pachong ho jouse toh asol than ahi. 
Joseph in a insung mite ahin jouse apuijin ahi. Ahin chapang neocha ho leh gancha ho vang Goshen a adalhauvin ahi. 
Sakol kangtalai tampia mihonpi chun Joseph alhonpi uvin ahi. 
Jordan vadung koma Atad changphol ahunglhun un Joseph pa puldouna kin lentah nisagi sungin amangun ahi. 
Agamsung mi, Canaan miten Atad changvohna mun'a amaho lunghem tah a aumu chu amu uvin, hiche mun chu (Jordan toh kinai ahi) Abel- Mizraim, tin akhel uvin, hichu “Egypt mite dia nasatah a puldouna mun” tin asei uve. 
Hichun Jacob chaten apau thupeh bang bangin abolpeh un ahi. 
Ama hon apa longdamsa chu Canaan gam'a apolut tauvin Mamre kom'a Macphelah loumun a Abraham'in hiche mun chu Hit mipa Ephron kom'a atonsot a kivui theina dinga ana choh na mun achun avui tauvin ahi. 
Jacob avuijou chun Joseph ahin asopi te ho jouse chule midang akilhonpi jouse Egypt gam'a ahung kinungle tauvin ahi. 
Ahin tua hi amaho apao umtah lou jeh chun Joseph sopi ho chu hatah in ahung tija tauvin, ama hon aseiyun, “Joseph chun alunghanna ho eihon ina bolset nau jouse chu ahin phulah ding ahi,” atiuve. 
Hijeh chun ama hon hiche thucheng ho hi Joseph athot tauve: napan athi masang chun hitin eina thulhah uvin 
Na heng'a kasei diuvin: na sopi ten na chunga thil gilou abol uva suhkhel anei nau chu ngaidam in.” Hijeh chun, keiho napa Pathen sohten, ka suhkhel nau chu nei ngaidamun tia katao u ahi.” Hiche thu thot amu chun Joseph akap tai. 
Hichun asopi ho ahungun Joseph masanga akun tauvin ahi. “Ven, keiho hi nasohte kahiuve.” atiuve. 
Ahin Joseph in, “Nangho tija hih un, keima Pathen beh kahia nangho kagimbol ding ham?” tin adonbut e. 
Nang hon kachunga thil phalou nana gong un ahin Pathen'in aphalam tah in ahin gong e. Hiche panmun chan eilhut a tua ka mite mihem tamtah hinkho huhhing'a um thei ahi. 
Ahipoi, nangho kicha hih un. Keiman nangho nahiuvin nachate hou ahin kavetkol jom jing diu ahi,” ati. Chuti chun aman asopi ho chu nunnem tah in alhamon kit un ahi. 
Chuti chun Joseph ahin asopi te ho jouse Egypt a acheng jing uvin ahi. Joseph chu atahsa kum jakhat le som alhing tan ahi. 
Chuin Joseph in khang thum channa geiya Ephraim chilhah ho jong amun chujongleh Joseph thagui a peng Manasseh chapa Machir chate jong ana mupeh in ahi. 
Chuin Joseph in asopite jah a, “Keivang kathina ding anailam tai,” “ahivangin Pathen'in nangho nahungvil teiyun tin, Egypt gam a kon hin Abraham, Isaac chule Jacob henga ana kihahselna gama dingin nahin puitou vunte,” ati. 
Hichun Joseph in Israel chate chu aki tepsah in chuin aman hitin aseiye, “Pathen'in nahin kithopiuva hiche mun'a konna nahung pui teng ule hiche mun'a konna kagu kachang ho hi na nungpoh teitei diu ahi,” ati. 
Hichun Joseph kum jakhat le kum som ahin ahinkho abeitai. Hichun Egypt miten Joseph longdamsa chu atom un athikong chu Egypt gamsunga thingkong sunga akoi tauve.
