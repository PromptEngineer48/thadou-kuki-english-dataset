Potdohbu.
2.
Hiche phatlai hin, Levi insung'a kon pasal khat leh Levi insungmi numei khat ma akicheng lhon tan ahi. 
Numeinu chu ahung gaijin chapa khat ahing tai. Amanun achapa chu aven ahile lunglhai umtah naosen ahin, lhathum sung'in asel tan ahi. 
Ahivang'in amanun achapa chu asel joulou phat in, sinkhup Papyrus naang'a kisa khat agalan twilutna lou dingin avonglai jouse ahuhbit sohkeiye. Chuin sinkhup sung'a naocha chu akhum'in Nile Vadung pang phaipheng lah achun alha lut tai. 
Chuin naosen a unu jong gamla thim'a kon'in adingin naosen chu i-atidem tin agalvet in ahi. 
Chujouvin Pharaoh chanu vadung'a kisil dingin ahung suh tan, asottolle ho jong vadung pang achun avahle uvin ahi. Chuin amanun phaipheng phung lah a sinkhup chu ahin mudoh tan, aga ladin asohnu chu asol tan ahi. 
Lengpa chanun jong sinkhup chu ahongdoh in ahile, naocha chu amu in ahi. Chuin naocha chu akap in, amanun jong naocha chu avoujang natpi lheh in, “Hiche hi Hebrew mite cha khat hitei ding ahi,” ati. 
Hichun naosen sopinun jong lengpa chanu heng ahin naije. “Keima kache a Hebrew numei khat naosen don ding kagahol peh ding ham?” tin adong'e. 
Lengpa chanu in jong, “Henge, gapui jin!” tin adonbut in, numeinu jong ache in naosen hingnu mama chu aga puiye. 
Chuin lengpa chanu'n jong naosen hingnu jah a aseijin. “Naosen hi lanlang neidon peh in, Keiman nadon man kapeh ding nahi,” ati. Chuin numeinu in jong naosen chu a-inlam'a adom'in amapa chu adon tan ahi. 
Khonung'in, chapang pa jong akhanglen tan ahile, anu in amapa chu Pharaoh chanu henga ahung thahkit tan, achapa mong mong dingin Lengpa chanun amapa chu amin Mose asah tai, ajeh chu amanun aseijin, “Keiman amapa hi twi lah a kon kaloidoh ahi,” ati. 
Kum phabep ache nungin, Mose jong ahung khanglen tan, amapa jong asopiho vil din achetou tan, aman jong asopi Hebrew mite hunam'a na atohsah nau aga mudoh tai. Agavil pet chun, Egypt mikhat in asopi Hebrew mikhat chung'a akhut alha chu amupaijin ahi. 
Akimvel avet a koiman eivepoi, ti aphot chen phat in, Mose'n Egypt mipa chu athat in neldi lah a asel tan ahi. 
Ajing nikho in, asopi ho vildin Mose apotdoh kit tan ahile, Hebrew mini akinah lhon aga amun ahi. “Ipi jeh a nagolpa navoh ham?” tin kinah hin panpa heng'a chun Mose'n aseiye. 
Amapan adonbut in, “Koiham nangma keiho chunga leng'a na pansah le thutan dinga thaneina nape? Janhi a Egypt mipa natha banga chu keima jong tha ding neigot ham?” tin aseijin ahileh, Mose jong akichan “Ka thilbol mijousen ahetai,” tin angaiton ahi.” 
Mose thilbol chu Pharaoh in ahin jadoh tahbeh tan, chuin Mose chu tha ding ago tan ahi. Ahivang'in Mose jong Pharaoh angsung'a kon'in ajam'in Median gamsung'a cheng din achetai. Median alhun chun Mose jong twikul pang'a atou tan ahi. 
Tun Median thempupa chun chanu sagi aneijin amaho chengse chu apau ganchahon ho dingin twi kongho chu athal ngai bang'un ahung thal dim'un ahi. 
Ahinlah kelngoi ching midang phabep ahung uvin amaho chu anodoh un ahi. Hichun Mose jong achomdoh jengin kelngoi ching hoa kon chun numei chapang ho chu ahuhdoh tan ahi. Chuin amapan amaho ganchate a ding twi chu athal peh tan ahi. 
Numei chapang ho chu apau Reuel heng ale lhun uchun, amapan adongun, “Ipi jeh a tuni nahung kimale uham?” atin ahile, 
ama hon jong adonbut uvin, “Egypt mi khat in kelngoi ching hoa kon'a eihuhdoh uva, chule amapa chun keiho ding le igancha hon houva ding twi eithal peh jeh u ahi,” atiuve. 
“Ahile amapa hoiba ham?” tin apauvin adong'in, “Ipi dinga amapa chu chukom'a chu nahin dalhah uham? Ahung'a ankong isokhom diuvin gakouvun,” ati. 
Mose'n jong akounau chu asanpeh in, thempupa toh chun hiva chun acheng khom tan ahi. Hiche phatlai chun, Reuel in achanu Zipporah chu Mose ji dingin ape tan ahi. 
Phat chomkhat jouvin, amanun chapa khat ahing'in, Mose'n Gershom asah tai “Ajeh chu keima hi gamchom beh a khopem kahi,” tina ahi. 
Kum phabep ache nungin, Egypt lengpa athitan ahi. Hinlah Israel te chu soh a kihen ohna noiya pohgih poa khoisa jing nalai ahiuve. Panpi ngaiyin akap un, aka awgin'u chu Pathen angsung alhung tan ahi. 
Akhoisat nau Pathen'in aja tan, chuin Abraham, Isaac leh Jacob heng'a akitepna ahin geldoh tan ahi. 
Aman Israel mite chu avesuh in, chuin tunvang toh phat ahitai ti chu ahe tan ahi.
