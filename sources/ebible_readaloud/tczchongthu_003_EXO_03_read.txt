Potdohbu.
3.
Mose’n nikhat anupa pa Midian thempu pa Jethro kelngoi achin laijin, kelngoi hon chu gamla tah gamthip lam'a apuiyin, Pathen molsang, Sinai mol lam'a chun ahung tan ahi. 
Hiche laimun'a chun Pathen vantil meikong in hamboh lah a ahung kilah in, Mose in lungdong tah in aven ahi. Hamboh chu akong jengin hinlah akavam deh pon ahi. 
Chuin Mose ama leh ama akihoulim in, “Hiche hi datmah umtah thil ahiye, ipi jeh pentah a hiche hamboh khu kavam lou ahidem? kache a kavet tei angaiye” ati. 
Hichu vedingin Mose’n hilam chu anailut jep jep e ti Pakaijin amu phat in, Pathen'in hamboh lailunga kon'in, “Mose! Mose!” tin akouvin ahile, Mose in jong adonbut in “Keima hiva kaum'e,” ati. 
“Hin nailut hihbeh in” tin Pakaijin agih tan ahi. “Nakeng chot kisut in, ajeh chu nangma muntheng'a ding nahi. 
Keima napa dinga a Pathen kahi - Abraham Pathen, Isaac Pathen, leh Jacob Pathen kahi,” ati. Hichu Mose in ajah phat in amai akhutai, ajeh chu Pathen avet ngamlou ahi. 
Chuphat in Pakaijin ahenga aseijin, “Egypt a kamite kibol gimna ka muchen tai. Achung uva tohvai hom hon asuhgim nau aka awgin'u kaja tan ahi. Heng'e, athohgim nau abonchan ka hechen tai, ati. 
Hijeh a chu Egypt mite thaneina noiya kon'a amaho huhdoh ding le Egypt gam'a kon'a gampha tah, khoiju le bongnoi lonna gam hichu - Canaan mite, Hit mite, Amor mite, Periz mite, Hivi mite chule Jebus mite tua achenna'u gam'a puilut dinga Keima hikom'a hung kumlha kahi, ati. 
Vetan! Israel chate ka awgin ka angsung ahunglhung tai, chule Egypt miten asuhgim nau kichen tah in kamutai. 
Tun che jengin, ajeh chu Keiman nang hi Pharaoh henga kasol nahiye. Kami Israel te Egypt gam'a konna nahin puidoh ding ahi,” ati. 
Hinlah Mose Pathen heng'ah akiphin in, “Kei koi kahia Pharaoh henga kaga kilah ding ham? Kei koi ka hia Israel te Egypt gam'a kon'a ka hin puidoh ding ham?” ati. 
Pathen'in adonbut in, “Keiman kaumpi jing ding nahiye. Keiman kasol nahina nangma dinga melchihna hiche hi ahi: Nangman miho chu Egypt gam'a kon'a nahin puidoh tengleh, hiche mol tah a hi Pathen nahou ding'u ahi,” ati. 
Ahivang'in Mose akiphin'in, “Keima Israel chate henga kachea ajah uva, ‘Napu napa teu Pathen'in na heng uva eihinsol ahi,’ tin sei leng ‘Amin ipi ham?’ tia eidoh diu ahi, chuteng amaho chu iti ka donbut ding ham?,” ati. 
Pathen'in Mose adonbut in, “KEIMA HI KEI,A KAHI JENG'E. Hiche hi Israel te heng'a seipeh in: Keima chun naheng uva eihinsol ahi,” tin ati. 
Pathen'in jong Mose heng'a aseijin, “Hiche hi Israel te heng'a seipeh in: Yahweh,* napu napa teu Pathen - Abraham Pathen, Isaac Pathen, chule Jacob Pathen'in - naheng uva eihin sol ahi. Hiche hi ka tonsot min ahi, akhang akhang'a nageldoh jing diu kamin ahi. 
“Tun chenlang Israel sung'a upaho gakou khom tan. Aheng uva seipeh in 'Yahweh, napu napa teu Pathen - Abraham, Isaac, chule Jacob Pathen - kaheng'a akilah in ahi. Aman kajah a aseijin, “Keiman naichan kanave jing'un, chule Egypt miten na engbol nau keiman kamui. 
Keiman nangho Egypt a na genthei thoh nauva kon'a huhdoh dinga kakitep ahi. Keiman khoiju leh bongnoi lonna gam'a chu kapuilut ding nahiuve - hichu Canaan mite, Hit mite, Amor mite, Periz mite, Hivi mite chule Jebus mite tua achen nau gam khu ahi,” ati. 
“Israel upahon nathusei angai diu ahi. Chuteng nang leh Israel upa chengse Egypt lengpa heng'a nache tei uva, ‘Pakai, Hebrew mite Pathen chun, eikimupi uve. Hijeh chun lungset tah in nithum sung'a gamthip lam'a kakhol jin uva Pakai ka Pathen'u henga kilhaina kasem dingu ahi” tia aheng'a nagasei diu ahi. 
“Amavang Egypt lengpa chu khut thahat in ano kahse a nahin soldoh lou diu keiman kahei,” ati. 
“Hijeh a chu Keiman kakhut ka lhan a, chule Egypt mite kajep ding, alah uva thil kidang jouse kabol ding ahi. Ajona le Aman nahin soldoh ding'u ahi. 
Chule keiman Egypt mite mitvet a khotona kahin chan sah ding nahiuve. Ama hon thilpeh nahin peh dingu chuteng khut goh keova na dalhah lou diu ahiye. 
Israel numei jousen akinaipiu Egypt mite leh a in'uva um gamchom numei ho kom'a sana leh dangka chule ponsil akithum cheh ding'u ahi. Na chanu teu leh na chapa teu hiche ho chu navon peh dingu, Egypt mite haosatna jouse na holhah peh dingu ahi,” ati.
