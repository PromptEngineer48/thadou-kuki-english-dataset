Potdohbu.
6.
Hichun Pakaijin Mose henga aseijin, “Tua hi keiman Pharaoh chung'a ipi ka bol'am namu ding ahi. Aman kaban thahatna ahin hetdoh teng, aman mipi ho chu ahin soldoh ding ahi. Dihtah a, aman hunam a agamsung dalha dinga ahin soldoh ding'u ahi!” 
Chule Pathen'in Mose henga aseijin, “Keima Yahweh - 'Pakai' kahi. 
Abraham henga, Isaac henga, chule Jacob henga El-Shaddai - 'Hatchungnung Pathen' - aheng uva ka kilah in ahi, hinlah kamin, Yahweh, ahi ti amaho heng'a kaphong doh poi. 
Chule kholjin miho banga achen nau gam, Canaan gam peh dinga kakitepna keiman amaho toh avel'in namdetna kanei kit in ahi. 
Israel mite, tua Egypt mite soh a pang ho thohgimna kaja nai ti hi naginchat mong thei ahi. Chule amaho toh ka kitepna jong phatah in kahe jing nai. 
“Hijeh chun, Israel mite henga seiyin: 'Keima Pakai kahi. Egypt gam'a nasoh tan nauva kon'a ka huhdoh ding chule na thohgim nauva kon'a kalha ong ding nahiuve. Keiman nangho ban thahat tah le thutanna gimnei tah a kalhatdam ding nahiuve. 
Keima mite nahi mong uve tia kachu dingu, chule na Pathen'u kahi ding ahi. Chuteng keima hi nangho Pakai na Pathen'u Egypt gam'a na thohgim nauva kon'a nahin lha ong pau chu kahi nahin hetdoh dingu ahi. 
Keiman Abraham, Isaac, chuleh Jacob heng'a kaki hahselna gam'a chu ka puilut ding nahiuve. Nangho goulo ding mong monga kapeh ding nahiuve. Keima Pakai kahi!'” 
Pakaijin asei bangin Mose in jong Israel mite heng'a aseipeh in, ahinlah ama hon avel'in angainom tapouvin ahi. Ajeh chu amaho asoh chan nauva aki suhgim'u chun alungphatmo sah val tauvin ahi. 
Chuin Pakaijin Mose heng'a aseijin, 
“Egypt lengpa Pharaoh heng'a leche inlang, Israel miten na gamsung dalha tauhen tin aheng'a gaseijin.” 
“Ahinlah Pakai!” Mose anompon, “Ka miten ka thusei eingaipeh tapouvin tin. Iti Pharaoh in ka thu ngai mong nante tia kakinep ding ham? Keima mipaomo kahi.” 
Ahivangin Pakaijin Mose leh Aaron heng'a thu aseijin Israel te ding leh Egypt lengpa Pharaoh dinga abol lhon ding ho ape lhon'in, Pakaijin Israel te Egypt akon puidoh dingin Mose leh Aaron thupeh anei tan ahi. 
Hichengse hi apu apateu khang chedol'a Israel insung upaho ahi: Israel insung'a apeng masapen Reuben chapa te se chu: Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, chule Carmi. Amaho chilhahse hi Reuben insung kon ahi tauve. 
Simeon chapa te se chu: Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jakin, Zohar leh Shaul ahiuve. (Shaul hingnu vang chu Canaan numei ahi.) Amaho chilhahse hi Simeon insung kon ahi tauve. 
Levi chilhah te se amaho insung minbu dungjuiya achapa te chu: Gershon, Kohath leh Merari. (Levi chu kum ja le somthum le sagi ana hing'e). 
Gershon chihahte chu Libni leh Shimei ahi lhon'e Anigel lhon'in insung khangguiyin akisim lhon tan ahi. 
Kohath chilhah te chu Amram, Izhar, Hebron leh Uzziel ahi. (Kohath chu kum ja le somthum le thum ana hing'e.) 
Merari chilhah te chu Mahli leh Mushi ahi lhon'e. Ama ho chengse hi Levi insung chedol minbua amin cheh uchu ahi. 
Amram chun apa sopinu Jochebed chu jin aneijin, amanun chapa ahin peh in, Aaron leh Mose ahi lhon'e. (Amram chu kum ja le somthum le sagi ana hing'e.) 
Izhar chapa te chu Korah, Nepheg leh Zicri ahiuve. 
Uzziel chapa te chu Mishael, Elzaphan leh Sithri ahiuve. 
Aaron in Elisheba aki chenpin, ama chu Amminadab chanu chule Nahson dinga asopinu ahi. Amanun chapa ahindoh ho chu Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar leh Ithamar ahiuve. 
Korah chapa te ho chu Assir, Elkanah leh Abiasaph ahiuvin, amaho chilhah hi Korah insung mi ahi tauve. 
Aaron chapa Eleazar chun Putiel chanute lah a khat ji in aneijin, chuin amanun chapa khat ahing'in Phinehas ahi. Amaho cheng hi Levi insung'a pu le pate ho insung bahkai dungjuiya chengse chu ahiuve. 
Aaron leh Mose hiche laiya kiminphah teni mama hi ahi lhon'e Pakaijin ajah lhon'a, “Israel mite chu Egypt gam'a kon'in sepai te bangin hin lamkai lhon'in,” tia asei chu. 
Egypt lengpa, Pharaoh kom'a Israel mite Egypt gam'a konna puidoh dinga thu sei chu, Mose leh Aaron ahi lhon'e. 
Egypt gam'a Pakaijin Mose ahoulimpi achun, 
aheng'a aseijin, “Keima Pakai kahi! Egypt lengpa, Pharaoh henga seiyin, keiman naheng'a kasei jouse chu.” 
Ahinlah Mose in Pakai akinel pin, aseijin, “Hichu ka bolthei louding ahi! Ajeh chu keima mipaomo kahi! Ipi jeh a Pharaoh in ka thusei angai ding ham?” ati.
