Potdohbu.
7.
Hijeh chun Pakaijin Mose heng'a aseijin, “Hiche hi phate chan ngaiyin. Pharaoh lengpa kom'a Pathen tobang'a kasem ding nahiye, chule na sopipa, Aaron, hi nang dia themgaova pang ding ahi. 
Ka thupeh jouse hi Aaron jah a seijin lang, Aaron in Pharaoh heng'a Israel mite agamsung'a kon'a asol dohna dinga thu apeh tei ding ahi. 
Ahinlah Keiman Pharaoh lungthim ka suhtah ding, chuteng melchihna le thil kidang tampi Egypt gamsung'a kapun sah ding ahi. 
Hiche a jong chu Pharaoh in na thu angai loulai ding ahi. Keiman Egypt gamsung'a ka khut kahin lhanjah ding. Keiman ka thaneina a kon - kamite, Israel te Egypt gam'a kon thutanna gimnei tah pum'a ka huhdoh ding ahiuve. 
Keiman kaban thahat kahin lhan'a Israel te kahin puidoh teng, Egypt miten Keima hi Pakai kahi ahin hetdoh diu ahitai.” 
Chuin Pakai thupeh bangin Mose leh Aaron injong abol lhon tan ahi. 
Pharaoh heng'a ngehna aganei lai lhon chun Mose kum somget ahitan, chule Aaron chu kum som get leh kum thum ahitai. 
Chuin Pakaijin Mose leh Aaron heng'ah thu aseijin, 
“Pharaoh in ngehna ahin nei ding, ‘Thil kidang nei vetsah lhon'in.' Hitobang ahinsei teng, Aaron henga seiyin ‘Na tenggol chu la inlang Pharaoh masang laiya chun sep lhan, chuteng gul hung kisoh ding ahi.” 
Chuin Mose leh Aaron jong Pharaoh heng'a ache lhon'in Pakai thupeh bangin abol lhon tai. Aaron in tenggol chu Pharaoh le avaipipo te ho angsung'a chun asep lhan ahile, gul ahung kisoh tai! 
Hichun Pharaoh in ama miching ho le doithem ho akou khomin, Egypt gam'a mitphel doithem hon jong chuche tobang chu abol'un ahi. 
Atenggol'u tol'a aseplha cheh uvin ahile, amahoa jong gul akisoh tai! Ahinlah Aaron tenggol chun amaho tenggol ho chu avallhum soh tai. 
Hicheng nung jeng jong hin Pakaijin asei masat bang chun Pharaoh lungthim chu atah jing nalaiyin, thu ima ngaisah in anei hih laiye. 
Chuin Pakaijin Mose heng'a aseijin, “Pharaoh lungchang atah in, chule aman mipi ho soldoh ding anom naipoi. 
Hijeh chun jingkah teng Pharaoh vadung lam'a ahung suh teng nangma na che ding. Nile vadung pam'a nadin ding, hikom'a chu nakimupi ding ahi. Na tenggol gul kisoh chu kichoi teiyin. 
Chuteng akom'a hiti hin seiyin, 'Pakai, Hebrew mite Pathen'in nakom'a thuseiya eihin sol ahi, “Ka miten keima eihouna ding uvin gamthip noiya soldoh tan.” Tugeiyin, nangman ama thusei iman na ngaisah poi. 
Hijeh a chu hiche hi Pakaijin asei ahi: “Keima Pakai kahina ka kilah sah ding ahi.” Vetan! Ka khut a um tenngol hi mangcha a Nile twi kajep ding, chuteng vadung hi thisan kisoh ding ahi. 
Asung'a nga jouse thi ding, chule vadung uilhahel ding, Egypt miten Nile vadung'a kon twi adon thei louhel diu ahitai,” ati. 
Chujongleh Pakaijin Mose heng ah aseijin: “Aaron jah a seiyin, 'Na tenggol kimat inlang Egypt gam'a twi jouse chunga na khut jah inlang - vadung jouse, twinah jouse, luikhuh jouse chunga twi jouse thisan sosah tan. Egypt gam muntin'a twi jouse, thingtwikong le songkong jouse sunga twi jouse geiya thisan soh ding ahi.'” 
Chuin Mose leh Aaron in jong Pakai thupeh bangin abol lhon tan ahi. Pharaoh le athunei vaihomte hon avet laiyun, Aaron in jong atenggol ajahdoh in Nile luidung chu ajep jeng tan ahi. Phulou helouvin, vadung twi apumpin thisan akisoh hel tan ahi. 
Luidunga nga jouse athisohhel tan, chule twi anen behseh phat chun Egypt miten adon thei tapouvin ahi. Egypt gamsung pumpi mun jouse thisan dim ahitai. 
Ahivang'in Egypt gam'a mitphel doithem hon jong adoiyu mangchan ama hon jong twi thisan asosah uvin ahi. Hijeh chun Pharaoh lungthim chu atah jing nalaiye. Pakaijin asei bangin Mose leh Aaron thusei chu nahsah in anei hih laiye. 
Pharaoh jong aleng inpi lam'ah akilen, hicheng thilsoh ho jouse chu alungthim'a gelkham asa hih laiye. 
Ahin Egypt mite jousen luipang dung alai thuh un twi akihol le tauvin ahi, ajeh chu ama hon Nile luidunga twi chu adonthei tah louvu ahi. 
Pakaijin Nile luidung ajep kal nisagi alhing tan ahi.
