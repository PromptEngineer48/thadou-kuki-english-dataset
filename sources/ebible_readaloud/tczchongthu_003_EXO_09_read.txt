Potdohbu.
9.
Pakaijin Mose thupeh aneijin,“Pharaoh heng'a chekit inlang ajah a hiti hin seijin. Hiche hi ahi Hebrew mite Pakai, Pathen'in asei chu: Kamite che'u henlang, Keima kin gabol uhen. 
Na soldoh louva Israel mite hi na umsah jing dinga ahile, 
na loujaova na gancha jouse na sakolte, sangante, sangongsao te, gancha hon, kelngoite leh na kelchate chung'a gamna hise kahin lansah ding thigam kei ding ahiuve, ati. 
Ahinlah Pakaijin Isrrael te gancha ho leh Egypt mite gancha ho chung'a chom khenna ahin sem ding, Israel mite ganchaho vang chu khatcha jong thilou ding ahi! 
“Pakaijin hiche gamna hise ahin lansah ding ahitai. Aman aseije jing nikho teng gamsung pumpia ahin lansah ding ahi.” 
Pakaijin aseidoh bangin aboltai. Ajing jingkah in Egypt mite gancha athigam hel tan, ahinlah Israel mite gancha vang chu khatcha jong athi hihbeh jeng'e. 
Thilsoh umchan dol khol chil dingin Pharaoh in asohte ho chu asoltan ahile, Israel mite gancha ho chu khat cha jeng jong athi pon ahi! Amavang Pharaoh lung thim chu angaiya bangin atah jing nalaiyin* aman mipi ho chu asol doh dehpon ahi. 
Chuin Pakaijin Mose leh Aaron heng'a aseijin, “Lhum sung'a kon'in vutdi tampi kiham lhon'in, hichu Pharaoh mitmu chang tah a Mose'n vanlam'a athe tou ding ahi,” ati. 
“Egypt gamsung pumpi chu vutvai nelsel chun alodim jeng ding, chuteng mihem chung hile gancha chung hile uilut natna huoisen alhunden jeng diu ahi,” ati. 
“Chuin amanin jong lhumsung'a kon'in vutdi akiham lhon'in, Pharaoh angsung'a ading lhon tai,” Pharaoh in avet jing laiyin, Mose'n vutdi chengse chu vanlam angan athetou le, vutdi nel chun gampumpi alodim jeng tan mihem le gancha ho chung’a uilut natna hoise alang tai. 
Egypt gam’a doichoiya thilbol ho chung'a jong achutan Mose angsung'a ading ngam tapouve, ajeh chu uilut natna hoise chun amaho leh Egypt mite jouse alhun den jeng ahitai. 
Ahinlah Pakaijin Pharaoh lungthim asutah nalaijin, “Pakaijin Mose heng'a aseidohsa bangin, Pharaoh in ngai nahsah in anei hih laiye.” 
Pakaijin Mose heng'a aseijin, “Jingkah matah in thouvin Pharaoh angsung'a ga dingin lang, ajah a seijin. Hiche hi Pakai Hebrew mite Pathen'in asei chu ahi: “Ka mite chetau hen, chutileh keima eihou thei diu ahi.” 
“Na soldoh louva ahile, keiman natna hoise kahin lansah be nahlai ding nangma le nanung juiho chule namite chung'a kahin lansah cheh ding, chuteng nangman keima Pakai tailou leiset a Pathen dang aumpoi ti nahin hetdoh ding ahi,” ati. 
“Tu jenga jong keiman ka khut kalhan jah a nangma le namite chung'a gamna hoise ka lansah a leiset chung'a hi ka suhmang hel jeng thei nahiye.” 
“Ahinlah keiman leiset pumpia ka thaneina ka musah bou nahin hichu leiset pumpia kithe jal ding ahi.” 
“Ahinlah hicheng jouse nungin jong nangma leh nangma na kichoisang gunset in, kamite na soldoh hih laiye.” 
“Jing nikho tuphat dontah le keiman gelchang nasatah Egypt gamsung'a imatih channa analha khaloulai chu kahin lhahsah ding ahiye.” 
“Kino tan! Na loujaova gancha ho jouse hoitup dingin namite sol loijin, achuti louva ahile hitobang gelchang ahung kheh teng loujaova mihem hihen gancha hijongleh aki dalha chan chu gelchang'in ase a thi jeng ding ahi,” ati. 
Pharaoh nungjuiho mi phabep chu Pakaijin hitia asei jeh chun akicha tauve. Amaho acheloi jun loujaova gancha ho leh asohte ho'u chu aga puilut tauvin ahi. 
Ahinlah Pakai thusei ija sep louse vang chun aloujao dung uva kihong tah in gancha alha thang nalai uve. 
Chuin Pakaijin Mose heng'a aseijin, “Egypt gamsung loujao tin cheng'a gelchang ahung kheh a mihem ahin gancha ahin chule loujaova thingphung jouse hihen lang, Egypt gamsung pumpia thil um jouse ase khahna dingin vanlam nganlang na khut lhang jah tan,” tin aseitai. 
Chuin Mose'n jong vanlam asang'in atenggol chu agah jahdoh jeng le, Pakaijin vanthamjol aki thinsah in gelchang ahung khetan, leiset chung geijin meikong ahung lenglha tan, Egypt gam pumpia Pakaijin gelchang goju toh kibangchet in akhehsah tan ahi. 
Imatih chan a jong hitobang gelchang chu Egypt gamsunga ana khe khalou lai ahin, tanglou hel'in gelchang chu akhe tan meikou apahlha jengin ahi. 
Hitobang gelchang chun Egypt gamsung pumpi alodim lhajeng tan chule loujaova umphot gancha hihen mihem hijongleh aum jouse chu asep lhu jeng tan, gamsunga thingphung jouse jong asep thang deh tan ahi. 
Goshen mun Israel mite chenna mun bou chu ahoidoh tan ahi. 
Hitia thil aso phat chun, Pharaoh in jong mi asol loijin Mose leh Aaron agakou sah tan hiti hin aseiye, “Tun vang keima chonse kahitai,” tin kilhanna aneijin “Pakai hi thudih tanpa ahin, keima le kamite kadih tapouve,” ati. 
Pakai henga nei taopeh lhon tan, ajeh chu vanthamjol aging jeng jung'e! Gelchang nasatah in akhe jing nalaijin ahi. Ka gimval tauve. Keiman na soldoh tauving'e; um jong umsot tahih un, ati. 
Mose'n adonbut in, “Ahi le aphai.” Khopi sung hi ka dalhah lhah leh keima jong Pakai heng'a ka tao ding ahi. Vanthamjol gin hung thip ding, gelchang khe jong hung tang jeng ding, nangman jong leiset pumpi hi Pakaiya ahi ti nahet doh ding ahi,” ati. 
“Ahinlah keiman kahe jing nalaije, nangma leh nasoh ten tuchan'in Pakai na ging hih lai uve,” ati. 
Hiti chun gamsung'a Phaipheng jouse leh sakol chang abonchan amangthah hel jeng tai, sakol chang chu avui ado laitah ahin chule phaipheng chu ahung don doh laitah ahiye. 
Hiche lah achun suhlou chang leh butun chang vang ahoidoh nai, ajeh chu suhlou chang leh butun chang chu aduo aum loulai phat ana hiye. 
Chuin Mose jong Pharaoh angsung khopi dalhan achetai. Pakai lam asang'in ataovin ahile, vanthamjol kithin le gelchang khe chu atang jeng tan, leiset pumpia gojuh jeng jong atang hel tan ahi. 
Pharaoh in jong gojuh, van kithin, leh gelchang kheh akitang tai ti ahin hetdoh phat in ama le anungjui ho achonse kit tauvin, angaiya bangin Pharaoh lungthim atah kit tai. 
Pakaijin Mose heng'a ana seidoh sa bangin Pharaoh lungthim angaiya bangin atah kittan, Pharaoh in jong Israel mite asoldoh deh poi.
