Potdohbu.
10.
Chuin Pakaijin Mose heng'a aseijin, “Pharaoh heng'a chekit inlang, ga seikit tan. Keiman ama leh asohte ho lungthim ka suhtah ahin, melchihna hicheng jouse hi kakilah sah ding ahi,” ati. 
“Chule nangman jong na chapa te leh natupate jah a ichan geija kagim sah uva chule melchihna tampi kamu sah uve ti na jahsah theina diuva kasei ahiye. Nangho jengin jong keima hi Pakai kitipa chu kahi ti nahet doh jeng diu ahi,” ati. 
Mose leh Aaron jong Pharaoh heng'a ache lhon'in asei lhon tai, “Hebrew mite Pakai, Pathen'in hiti hin aseiye: Itih chan ka-angsung'a kun ding da nahi tam? Keima kin agabolna ding uvin, kamite soldoh tan. 
Nanop louva ahile, vetan! Jing nikho tengleh na gamsung'a khaokho te kahin lansah ding ahi. 
Gam pumpi alodim soh uva tol lham jong na muthei louding ahi. Gelchang'in akhehlih lou amoh chengse khaokho ten aneh gamhel diu, loujaova keh thingphung ho jouse jong anehchai kei ding'u ahiye. 
Nangma insung hihen nasohte insung hijongleh Egypt gam insung jouse ahin lodim sohhel jeng diu, hiche tobang thilsoh gimnei hi napu napateu khang'a pat na gamsung uva anaum khalou lai ahi, tin ahenga agasei lhon tai, chuin Mose jong Pharaoh angsung'a kon'in apotdoh tan ahi. 
Pharaoh sohte ho jong ahung uvin Pharaoh heng'a asei tauve. “Amapa hin itih chan lom lom eiho dinga hahsatna alhunsah ding hitam? Mipi ho hi che uva a Pathen kin abolna diuvin soltan! Egypt mite pihi jou le nal'a mangthah gam ding ahi tauve ti na hetdoh hih laiyem?” 
Chuin Mose leh Aaron chu Pharaoh heng'a dingin ahin puilut kit tauvin. Pharaoh in jong amani jah a aseijin, “cheuvin lang na Pakai leh na Pathen kin gabol tauvin. Ahile koipen mong hi nahiuvem ache ding?” tin amani chu adong tan ahi. 
Mose'n adonbut in, “Ateh akhang, ka chapa te hou ahin ka chanute hou jaona a khatcha kidalha louding kahiuve. Ka kelngoi jouseu leh gancha jouse aboncha kapui sohkei diu, chule keihon kut chu alouthei louva kagabol teitei ding'u ahi,” ati. 
Pharaoh in jong aseijin, “Keiman nangho nacha sen gei uva ka soldoh tha ngei ngeija ahile Pakai umpina chu chang un! Na lungthim sung lhonna lungthim phalou na keng lhon'e. 
Hiche vang chu hithei louhel ding ahi! Nangho lah a Pasal chengse cheuvin lang na Pakaiyu kin gabol tauvin, hichu natup tenten uva hilou ham?” Chuin Pharaoh in aleng in a konin ahin nodoh lhon tan ahi. 
Chuin Pakaijin Mose heng'a aseijin, “Egypt gamsung pumpia khaokhopite alanna dingin na khut jahdoh jeng tan. Egypt gamsung'a hamphung gelchang in akhehlih moh chengse aboncha aneh gamset diu ahi,” ati. 
Mose’n atenggol chu Egypt gamsung pumpi chungvum a ajahdoh jeng tan ahile, hiche nikho chun Pakaijin Egypt gamsung pumpi nilhum kei leh khovah hel'in huiphalou solama kon'in tanglouvin anun sah tai. Jingkah in solam akon huijin khaokho te ahon hon'in ahin polut tauvin ahi. 
Chule Egypt gam pumpi chu khaokho ten alosoh hel jeng tan, hitobang'a gamsunga khaokho te chu imatih channa ana um kha loulai ahiye. 
Khaokho ten gamsung pumpi alodimlha tauvin ahile mu athimlha jeng tai. Gelchang in akheh moh chengse gam leiset a thingphung jouse jong anemang hel tauvin, Egypt gamsung pumpi thingphung chule hamphung abonchan ado khat jeng cha jong um louvin khaokho ten anechai hel jeng tauve. 
Chuphat in Pharaoh in jong asohte ho asol loiyin, Mose leh Aaron aga kousah un, hiti hin aseiye. “Keiman na Pakai leh nangni chung'a chonset phat louna kabol khatai,” ati. 
“Ka chonsetna nei ngaidam lhon'in, Pakai heng'a nei taopeh teitei lhon tan, hiche thigamna hoise hi keima angsung'a kon'in tolmang jeng lhon tan,” ati. 
Mose jong Pharaoh angsung'a kon'in a kondoh in Pakai heng'a aga tao tai, 
Pakaijin nisolam a kon'in hui hattah ahin nunsah in ahile, hiche huipi chun khaokho te jouse twikhanglen dung laiya alhoh lut sohhel jeng tai. Egypt gamsung pumpia khaokho te chu khat jeng cha jong aum tahih hel jengin ahi. 
Ahinlah Pakaijin Pharaoh lungthim asutah jing nalaijin, Pharaoh in jong mipi ho chu asol doh hih laiye. 
Chuin Pakaijin Mose heng'a aseijin, “Nakhut jah inlang vanlam sang tem in, Egypt gamsung pumpi muthim'in alo sohhel jeng ding ahi,” ati. 
Mose'n jong Pakaijin athupeh bangin vanlam asan jeng'a ahile, Egypt gamsung pumpi nithum sung mu athim lha jeng tan, ahi. 
Hiche phat sung chun khat le khat akimu thei tapouvin, koimacha jeng jong pot le theilou ahitai. Ahinlah Israel chate chenna mun vang chu khovah in aumpi jing uve. 
Ajonan, Pharaoh in jong Mose akoukit tai. “Cheuvin lang Pakai kin gabol tauvin,” ati. “Na chate'u kipui jun lang, ahinlah na kelngoi hon teu vang na dalhah ding'u ahi,” ati. 
Mose'n adonbut in, “Hichu hithei him him ponte. Ka gancha te'u chu nei puisah tei teiju angaije, ajeh chu ka Pakai uleh ka Pathen angsung'a kilhaina a ka manchah ding'u ahi, ati.” 
“Ka gancha teu aboncha, ka pui teitei diu ahiye; khat jeng cha jong ka dalhah thei louhel diu ahi. Ajeh chu ka ganchate'u lah a konna hi keihon Pakai angsung'a kilhaina'a katdoh dinga ka lhendoh cheh cheh diu ahi. Chule amun ka lhun kahse uva itilam dol'a Pakai kin kabol diu ham ti kahe chen nai pouve,” ati. 
Ahinlah Pakaijin Pharaoh lungthim chu khatvei asutah kit nalaijin, Pharaoh in jong mipi ho chu asoldoh hih laiye. 
Chuin Pharaoh jong apeng jah jeng tan, Mose henga aseijin, “Ka angsunga kon'in potdoh jengin!” ati. “Keiman nangma ka gih nahiye. Avel'a nei hung kinung mupi kitthei louhel ding ahi! Nei kimupi kit ngai ngaiya ahile hiche nikhoa chu nathi jeng ding ahi,” ati. 
Mose'n adonbut in, “Apha jenge,” “Avel'a ka kimaitopi tah louding nahiye,” ati.
