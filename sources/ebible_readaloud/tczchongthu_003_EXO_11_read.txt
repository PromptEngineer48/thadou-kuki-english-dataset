Potdohbu.
11.
Pakaijin Mose heng'a aseijin, “Keiman Egypt gamsung leh Pharaoh chung'a ka hin lhunsah ding hoise khat akivat nalaije. Hiche hi aso chai chai tengleh, Pharaoh in agamsung dalha dinga nahin no jeng ding'u ahitai,” ati. 
“Israel mite heng'a seipeh inlang, ama ama cheh in aheng akom Egypt mite sana dangka akijepna ding uvin kithum cheh tauhen,” ati. 
(Tun Pakaijin Israel chate chu Egypt mite mitmun lunglhaina achansah tai. Chule Mose chu Egypt gamsung pumpia amin akithang in, Pharaoh mitmu chang leh mipite mitmun amin che lheh jeng tan ahi.) 
Chuin Mose'n Pharaoh heng'a aseijin, “Hiche hi Pakaijin aseiye: “Keiman jankim tah le Egypt mite lungthim kahin suhthou ding ahi. 
Egypt gamsung'a chapang peng masa jouse, Pharaoh chapa peng masapen ahin, alaltouna a peng masa penpa ahin, a sumbuh laiya changbong goija pang asohnu chapa chan geija abonchauva thigam kei ding, gancha peng masa jouse jong aboncha thisoh hel ding ahiye, ati. 
Chuteng Egypt gamsung pumpia ka le mao kithong lha ding, ajeh chu hitobang thilsoh gimnei hi imatih channa anaum khalou laiya ahin, avel'a hung kilah kit talou ding chu ahi. 
Ahinlah Israel mite vang chu lungmon nan aumpi jing diu, uicha jeng jong hampoh louding ahi. Chuteng Pakaijin Egypt mite leh Israel mite kikah a ijakai datkhenna asemdoh tai, hichu nang hon na hetdoh cheh ding'u ahi, ati. 
Chuteng Egypt gam'a na nungjui ho leh nasohte abonchauva keima lam'a hung kipelut ding'u hitia hi asei diu ahi, khaiye! 'Nangma leh nanung juiho abonchauvin hikom'a kon hin potdoh tauvin!' tin aseije atipeh tai. Chuin Mose jong lungphamo tah in Pharaoh angsung'a kon'in achedoh tan ahi. 
Tun Pakaijin Mose heng'a aseijin, “Pharaoh in nangma thusei ahin ngailou ding, ahinlah keiman Egypt gamsung'a thil kidang melchihna ka bolbe nahlai ding ahi,” ati. 
Mose leh Aaron'in jong Egypt gam Pharaoh angsung'a thil kidang melchihna tampi aki lahsah lhon'in, ahinlah Pakaijin Pharaoh lungthim chu kidang tah in asutah jing nalaijin ahile, Pharaoh in Israel mite chu Egypt gamsung a kon'in ana soldoh hih laiye.
