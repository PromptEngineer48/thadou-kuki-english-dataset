Potdohbu.
12.
Israel mite Egypt gam'a aum jing laijun, Pakaijin Mose leh Aaron heng'a hicheng hi thupeh aneiye: 
“Tu apat hiche lha hi nangho dinga lha kipatna ahin, kumkhat adia lha kipatna a kisim ding ahitai. 
Israel te kikhop khomna munpi jouse ah ga seipeh lhon tan. Hiche lhasung nisom alhinni tengleh ama ama cheh in apu apateu insung kai lhah apat, insung khat cheh akon kelngoi khat cheh katdoh dinga ahin kai diu ahiye. 
Kelngoinou khat kitha dinga insung khat a mijat chu alhom khah a ahile, inneipu le aheng akom'a pa kinopto ding kelngoi nou adol ding chabep khat chu aga kimat lhon ding, amichan cheh in aneh khopset ding kelngoinou khat ding mijat chu phatecha nasim toh lhon ding ahi. 
“Kelngoinou chu nolna beihel hi ding, kumkhat lhingsa hitei ding, hiche kelngoinou chu kelngoi honlah a konna hihen kelcha honnlah konna hijongleh athah beh mong mong'a na kimat ding'u ahi.” 
“Hichea manchah ding kelngoinou chu hiche lha nisom leh nili lhinni geija nahen cheh diug'u hichu nilhum lam tengleh Israel kikhom pumpi chun ama ama cheh adia akitha ding'u ahiye.” 
Gancha kithat thisan chu phabep asodoh diu, hichu kelngoinou thisan a chu in kotpi phung le inkam'a aloinat cheh cheh diu ahi. 
Hicheni jan teng chule kelngoinou sa chu meiya akihul min uva aneh cheh diu, hichu cholsolou changlhah leh anchekha toh ametha cheh cheh diu ahiye. 
Hiche sa chu asel puma hihen twija kihonminsa hijongleh koima chan naneh thei louhel diu ahi. Sa pumpi aluchang le akeng chule asung jouse jaona meiya na kihulmin tho uva naneh cheh diu ahi. 
Sa amoh neocha jong jingkah channa na khovah sah louhel diu ahi. Akinelou taphot chu jingkah masanga meiya na govam jeng ding'u ahiye.” 
“Hicheng hi sa naneh teng ule najui cheh ding'u ahiye: nangma cheh na kivon lhingsel uva, nakeng chot ho'u na kisi cheh diu, chule nakhut uva tenggol na kiphu sohkei ding'u ahi. Chuteng naneh khom sohkei diu hichu Pakaiya dia Kalchuh Kut ahiye. 
Hiche jan teng chule keiman Egypt gamsung kahin jotpa a mihem leh gancha aboncha apeng lha masa jouse kasuh gamhel ding ahi. Chule Egypt gamsung'a milim doi kisemthu jouse chung'a jong thu katan ding ahiye, ajeh chu keima Pakai kahi!. 
Ahinlah nangho jouse dinga hiche thisan kiso chu melchihna hung kilah tading, keiman Egypt gam pumpi kahin suhmang ding, hiche thisan chu kahin mudoh jiteng keiman nangho jouse chung'a natna hoise hung lang thei jouse a konna kaven doh ding nahiuve. 
Hiche nikho hi nangho dinga hetjingna nikhoa pang jeng ding ahitai. Hichu kumsih chule akhang akhang'a Pakaiya dia kut nikho ahi ti na geldoh jing ding'u ahiye. Na khang lhumkei uva chonna dan hitia nanit jing diu ahitai. 
Nisagi sung'a cholsolou changlhah jeng naneh ding'u ahi. Nisagi alhinni tengleh nangma insung cheh a chol um jouse pamlamma na paidoh sohkei ding'u ahi. Nalah uva koi tobang khat chun hiche cholsolou changlhah, nisagi lhinnia pat naneh khah ngeingei uva ahile, ama chu Israel mite lah a konna kisumang jeng ding ahi. 
Kut kipat nikho masa penni teng khompi len khat nanei uva, nisagi alhinni teng jongleh khompilen theng khatma nanei diu ahi. Hiche nikho teni hi atumbeh a nanit cheh diu, amichan'in aneh khop ding nabol peh teitei ding'u ahi. 
“Hiche hi Cholsolou changlhah kut a naman diu ahiye, ajeh chu hiche nikhoa hi keiman nangho Egypt gamsung'a konna kahin puidoh nau na geldoh jing diu, nakhang lhumkei uva nangho jousen hiche nikho chu chonna dan ahi tia nanit jom jing ding'u ahi,” ati. 
Lhamasa nisom le nili lhin nikho nilhah lam tengleh na boncha uva hiche cholsolou changlhah chu naneh pan diu ahi 
Nisagi sungse'a vang chu nangma nangma insung cheh a, chol sosa changlhah khat jeng cha jong kilah louding ahiye. Kholjin mi ahin agamsung cheng den miho hijongleu chun cholsosa changlhah nekha tapou chu, Israel khompi a konna kisumang jeng ding ahi. 
Hiche phatsung hin amitakip in cholsosa changlhah kiti phot naneh louhel ding'u ahiye. Ajeh chu cholsolou changlhah jeng naneh diu ahi.” 
Chuin Mose'n jong Israel sung'a upaho jouse akou khom in aheng uvah aseipeh tai, “Cheuvin, nangma cheh insung ding kelngoinou gaki kaiyun lang, chule Kalchuh Kut a dingin kithauvin.” 
“Khutsilna kong'a gan thisan chu koiyun. Chule hichu hyssop nado phabep lauvin lang dekot uvin. Hyssop chu lauvin lang thisan achun kotpi khom teni ahin kotpi chunglah inkam a chun loinat sohkei uvin. Jing khovah kahsea koima chan hichu akallhah thei louding ahi. 
Ajeh chu Pakaijin Egypt mite jouse sugam'a hung ding kahi, chuteng Pakaijin kotpi chung lah a thisan kiloinat ho chu amu ding, hitia chu asuhgam ding hojal a chu hiche insung chu apeldoh ding ahiye.” 
“Geldoh jing'un, thupeh hichengse hi nangho jouse ding leh nachateu khang geija chonna dan ahi tia najui jing ding'u ahi. 
Pakaijin nangho peh dinga akitepna sa gamsung nalhun teng ujongleh hiche chonna dan hi najui jing diu ahi. 
Chule hiche chonna hi na chate uvin 'Ipi dinga kibol ham?' ti nahin doh ding'u ahi. 
Nang hon jong na donbut uva, “Hiche chonna dan hi Pakaiyaa dia gantha kilhaina ahin chule Pakaijin Egypt mite ana thagam lai chun Aman Israel mite in ho chu ana kalchuhji e,'” tia na seipeh ding'u ahi. Chuin Mose'n thupeh hichengse chu achai phat in mipi jouse Pakai angsung'a akunlha sohkei uvin chibai aboh tauve. 
Pakaijin Mose leh Aaron athupeh bangin Israel mite'n jong abol tauvin ahi. 
Chule hiche Jan khangkim chun Pakaijin Egypt gamsung'a naocha peng masa jouse, Pharaoh chapa apeng masapen alaltouna toupa a kipat, akimvella cheng asohpa chapa peng masapen ahin, chule gancha peng masa jouse athat gamhel tan ahi. 
Jankim laijin Pharaoh, asohte ho chule Egypt mite abonchauvin athou doh tauvin, Egypt gamsung pumpia ka le mao awgin akithong sohhel tai. Ajeh chu intin cheng'a mi thina lou umlouva ahitai. 
Jankim laijin Pharaoh in Mose le Aaron aga kousah tauvin, “Ka mite dalhan che tauvin chule Israel mite kipuijin che tauvin! Nei tepna bangun cheuvin lang Pakai kin chu gabol jeng tauvin. 
Na seima bangin na kelngoi jouseu leh na gancha jouse'u abonchan kipui unlang, che tauvin, nei dalhah ding tah jeh un keima jong phatthei neichan lhon ding ahiye,” ati. 
Egypt miten abonchauvin Israel te dingin akisaboi lheh jeng tauvin, Egypt gamsung'a konna Israel mite soldoh loiding lunggel akeng tauvin, ama hon “Keiho ka boncha uva thigam ding kahi tauve!” tin asei uve. 
Israel mite'n jong changbong neldi anei jouse'u aki hamkhom tauvin. Changbong ameh nau lhengkam jouse jao nan poh dingin akigot cheh tauvin ahi. 
Mose'n athupeh bang bang uvin, Israel mite'n jong abol tauvin ahi; Egypt mite a kon'in sana le dangka kijepna ding jouse akithum doh gam tauvin ahi. 
Pakaijin Israel mite chu Egypt mite mitmun alunglhai sah sohkeiyun ahile, Egypt miten jong anei chasun uchu itcha louhel lin ana pesohkei uve. Egypt mite haona jouse toh akon tauvin ahi! 
Hiche jan chun Israel mite Rameses khopia kon'in Succoth lam'a dingin akipat doh tauvin ahi. Numei chapang simtha louvin keng lamma che pasal jat mong chu sang jagup alhing uve. 
Amaho ahiuvin chule miphuh miphah, gancha ho, kelngoi hon tampi le gancha hon tampi jaonan aki tol tauve. 
Egypt gamsung'a aum lai uva kisem changbong chu alhon lhon'in akiken jiuvin ahi. Egypt gam'a kino tah a kisoldoh ahijeh uvin ama hon changlhah chu chol aso man tah louvu, chule ama cheh neh ding jeng jong ana kigon man tapouve. 
Israel mite chu Egypt gamsung'a kum jali le kum somthum ana cheng uve. 
Chuin, Kum jali le kum somthum lhinin Pakai thaneijin Israel mite chu Egypt gam'a kon'in ahin puidoh tauvin ahi. 
Hiche jan chun Pakaijin Israel mite Egypt gam'a konna ahin puidoh din kitepna aneijin ahi. Hiche jan hi Pakaiya mong ahin, hijeh a chu Israel mite'n akhang-akhang uva anit jing diu ahiye. 
Chuin Pakaijin Mose le Aaron heng'a aseijin, “Hichengse hi Kalchuh Kut kibol ding dan ahi. Kalchuh Kut ankong hi kholjin min anehthei louhel ding ahi. 
Amavang hiche mihem chu sum'a kichodohsa le cheptan sa ahile aneh jeng thei ahinai. 
Kholjin mai-mai le tha a kila a kigoi mihon aneh louding ahi. 
Insung cheh a Kalchuh Kut an chu kine ding ahi. Inpolam'a naneh theilou diu chule agu jong khatcha na suhboh lou diu ahi. 
Hiche hi Isreal khompi jousen phatecha anit cheh diu ahi. 
“Gamdang mikhat tou ahung kholjin khah a Pakai Kalchuh Kut a ajaonom'a ahile pasal kiti phot chun achep kitan cheh uhen, chuteng Kalchuh Kut chu anailut uva aneh thei ding chuteng amaho chu Israel mite bang'a lhathei ding ahiye. 
Hiche thupeh hi amitakip ading ahin, agammi Israel te hihen gamdang mi hijongleh dan khat a um cheh ding ahi,” ati. 
Pakaijin Mose le Aaron athupeh bangin Israel mite'n jong abol sohkei uve. 
Hiche nikho chun Pakaijin Israel mite Egypt gamsung a kon'in galsat sepai te bangi nahin puidoh tauvin ahi.
