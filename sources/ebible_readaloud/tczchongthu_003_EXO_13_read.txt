Potdohbu.
13.
Chuin Pakaijin Mose heng'a aseijin, 
“Israel mite lah a apeng lhamasa phot kaheng'a na todoh dingu ahi. Mihem hihen gancha hijongleh aboncha keija ahi,” ati. 
“Mose'n mipi ho heng'a aseijin, “Hiche nikho hi imatih channa geldoh jing uvin Egypt gam'a nasoh chan nauva konna nahung kitoldoh nikhou chu ahiye. Tunin Pakaijin aban thahat panin nahin puidoh tauve. (Geldoh jingun, cholsolou changlhah jeng naneh ding'u ahi),” ati. 
Hiche nikho hi phalbi matah hichu Abid* nikhoa hi na boncha uva chamlhat tah a na potdoh sohkei ding'u ahiye. 
Pakaijin nangho chan dia napu napa teu heng'a akitepna Canaan gam mite, Hit mite, Amor mite, Hivi mite chule Jebus mite chenna (khoiju le bongnoi lonna gamsung'a nahin puilut tengu leh hiche lhasung'a chu nangman jong hiche chonna dan chu nabol ding ahi), ati. 
Nisagi sung'a cholsolou changlhah jeng naneh diu ahi. Nisagi lhinni tengleh vang Pakai heng'a kut khat nabol diu ahi. 
Nisagi sung'a cholsolou changlhah jeng bou naneh thei diu ahi. Tahbeh in hiche nikho sagi sungsea hi na gamsung kimvel'u aboncha koiman cholsosa changlhah kiti toh naum khom louhel diu ahi.” 
“Nisagi lhinni tengleh na chate hou heng'a nasei uva, ‘Egypt gamsung'a keima kaum laiya Pakaijin hiche melchihna hi eibol peh ahiye,’ nati ding ahi. 
“Hiche golvahna hi melchihna khat a pang jing ding, chule namit jeng a jong hetjingna a pang ding, hiche kihilna hi na kamsung'a navop jing ding ahi: Pakaijin nangma aban thahat pan'a Egypt gamsung'a konna nahin puidoh ahi,” ati. 
Hijeh chun hiche golvahna hi kumseh angai ngaija bang'a nabol jing dingu ahi. 
“Hiche chonna dan hi Pakaijin khangluija pat a kitepna anei bang'a hichu aguilhun kahsea nabol jom jing diu, nanghon Canaan mite chenna nalhun teng uleh nabol diu, 
hijeh a chu Israel sung'a konna pasal pengdoh masapen mihem hihen gancha hijongleh Pakaiya dia napeh doh jing diu ahi, hichu Pakaiya ahi. 
Hiti achu sangan nou penglha masa chan chu kelngoi nouva nalhat diu, nalhat tah lou ujong le sangan nou ho chu angong chang na hekelep diu ahi. Chule na chapa te lah a apeng masa chan nale lhatdoh cheh cheh diu ahi.” 
“Chule khonung teng, na chapan thil umchan dol nahin doh ding, 'Hiche ho jouse hi ipi jeh pentah a kibol ham?' Nangman hitia hi na donbut ding, 'Egypt gam'a soh ichan lai uva Pakaijin aban thahat panna mipi jouse leh gancha aboncha eihin puidoh u ahiye, nati diu ahi, ati. 
Pharaoh ana lungtah lheh jengin ahile, Egypt gam'a konna ipotdoh diu chu ana phal tapon, ajonan Pakaijin Egypt gamsung'a pasal peng masa jouse leh gancha jouse abonchan athat chaikeiye. Hijeh a chu tua hi keiman Pakaiya dia kilhaina kasem ahin hibang'a chu keiman ka chapa te lah a apeng masa pen chan chu lhatdohna kaneiji ahi.' tia na seipeh ding ahi, ati. 
Hiche chondan hi nakhut teni ahin, namit teni chule na nahlhong geija melchihna thil'a navop jing ding ahiye. Hichu Pakaijin aban thahat panna Egypt gam'a konna nahin puidoh hetjingna ahi.” 
Ajonan Pharaoh in Israel mite ahin soldoh tan ahile, gamtepna lam lhunna naicha ahi vang chun, Pathen'in Philistine galbol ho jeh chun lampia ahinpui pouvin ahi. Pathen'in aseiye, “Kamite hon gal tohkha ta le alung heikhao vintin Egypt lam'a kile ding ahiuve,” ati. 
Pathen'in amaho chu gamthip lah a ahinpui leuvin, Twipi San* lam mun chu ahin jon tauvin ahi. Israel mite chu galsat mi bangin ahung kitol tauvin ahi. 
Mose'n jong Joseph gu le chang ahin pon, ajeh chu Joseph in hiche thulhahna hi ana neijin, hiti hin aseiye, “Pathen nangho kithopia hung jing ding ahi. Aman nangho nahin panpi teng ule, hiche kagu kachang hi nahin poh ding'u ahi,” ati. 
Israel miten Succoth mun ahin dalha uvin gamthip lah Etham kiti mun ahunglhung tauvin hiche mun a chun ngahmun akisem tauve. 
Pakaijin amite chu malam'a alhonpi jingin, Sunteng meibol khompi bangin alhonpi uvin, jan teng meivah khompi bangin alhonpi jiuve. 
Pakaijin amite dalha louvin meilhang meivah in aumpi jing uve.
