Potdohbu.
14.
Pakaijin Mose heng'a thupeh hicheng hi aneijin: 
“Israel mite heng'a seiyin nung lam'a akihei uva Pi-hahiroth le Migdol kikah san-dung chule Baalzephon gal lamma ngahmun asem nai diuvin aheng uvah seipeh un. 
Chutileh Pharaoh in hitia hi angaito ding, 'Israel mite alungdong tauvin, suhna ding helouvin gamthip noija akitol le tauve!' ati ding ahi. 
“Hitia chu keiman avel'a Pharaoh lungthim ka suhtah ding, aman nangho nung ahin del ding ahi.* Ajeh chu keiman ka gontupsa ahitai, Pharaoh le agalsat mite chung'a ka loupina kakilah sah ding ahi. Chuteng Egypt mite'n keima Pakai kahi ahin hetdoh diu ahitai!” Aheng uva kiseipeh bangin Israel mite'n hiche munna chun ngahmun akisem tauvin ahi. 
Pharaoh lengpa le asohte ho heng'a Israel mite ajam tauve tithu alhun phat in, Pharaoh leh asohte lungthim akikhel kit tan, “Ipi khat ibol uva Israel mite asoh chan nauva konna jam gam'u hitam?” tin asei uve. 
Pharaoh in jong agalsat miho le sakol kangtalai ho chu agontup sah loijin ahi. 
Pharaoh in sakol-kangtalai hatcheh jagup le Egypt gamsung'a sakol kangtalai dang ho jouse abonchan vaihom mitampi chu atou tauvin ahi. 
Pakaijin Pharaoh kiti Egypt lengpa lungthim asutah nalaijin ahile, aman jong Egypt gamsung dalha a jam Israel mite nung chu adel tan ahi. 
Egypt mite'n jong Israel te chu Pharaoh in galsat miho thaneina lhingset apen anung adel tauvin a sakol jouseu leh sakol-kangtalai jouse, sakol touthem jouse le agalsat mijouse abonchauvin adimlha tauvin ahi. 
Pharaoh in jong Israel mite umna lam anailut jep phat in ama hon Pharaoh lam chu avetou uvin, hichun ama hon anunglam uva Egypt mite ahung kitol chu amu tauve. Abonchauvin Pakai heng'a atao uvin, 
Mose heng'a aseiyun, “Ipi dinga gamthip noija hi thigam dinga neihin puidoh u hitam? Egypt a chu keiho dinga lhanmun lhingset uma hilou ham? Ka chung uva hi ipi nabola nahi hitam? Ipi dinga Egypt nei dalhah sah uham? 
Egypt gamsung kaum lai uva chu hitobang hi soh tei ding ahi tia ka seiju hilou ham? 'Nei dalha jeng tauvin! Egypt mite khut noija soh in umkit tau vinge. Hiche lai munna ka thigam sang uvin Egypt mite khut noija soh a kaum'u apha joi!'” tin asei tauve. 
Ahinlah Mose'n mipi heng'a aseijin, “Kicha hihhel un, dingdet jingun veuvin, tunia hi Pakaijin na huhdoh diu ahiye. Chule tunia kipat avel'a Egypt mite namukit tah lou diu ahi. 
Pakai ama tah in nangho gal asat ding ahi. Nangho umthim beh un,” ati. 
Chuin Pakaijin Mose heng'a aseijin, “ Ipi dinga kaheng’a kap a nahim? Mipiho malamma akitolna diuvin seijin! 
Na tenggol jan doh tem in twikhanglen chung vum achun domin. Twipi chu sukhen'in chutileh Israel mite twikhanglen lailung tolgo lah a chu kijot ding'u ahi. 
Chule keiman Egypt mite lungthim kasuh tah ding, ama hon Israel mite thagamna ding angaito diu ahi, chuteng asakol kangtalai jouseu, asakol tounau nangho that gam dinga hungkon ahiuvin, abonchauva keiman ka thaneina aheng uva kakilah sah ding ahi. 
Egypt mite ma-sang'a kaloupina kakimusah ding ahijoi, chuteng keima Pakai kahi ahin hetdoh diu ahi!” ati. 
Chuin Pakai vantil'in Israel mite ahin lhonpi'n, mipi masang lam'a meilom chu nunglam joh a ahung tan ahi. 
Chuin meilom chu Israel mite le Egypt mite kah a ahung umtai. Mu athimlha jeng tan, meilom jong meikong akisoh in kho ahung thim tan ahile, jan khovah keijin Israel mite chu Egypt miten ahin delpha tapouvin ahi. 
Chuin Mose'n atenggol chu Twipi San dung vumma ajah doh jeng'a ahile, chuin Pakaijin twikhanglen dung chu solama kon hui ahung nungin ahin semhoh tan ahi. Jan khovah keijin huijin twikhanglen chu asemin, twikhanglen jong aki khen'in tol lham golai ahung umdoh tan ahi. 
Chuphat in Israel mite abonchauvin tolgo laiya chun akitol'un alang alang'a bang bangin twi chu akiting tan ahi! 
Chuin Egypt mite Pharaoh galsat sakol jouse, sakol kangtalai jouse chule sakol touthem jouse jaona'n twikhanglen lailung'a kitol Israel mite chu adel tauvin ahi. 
Jingpi ahi phat in Pakaijin Egypt mite chu meikong leh meilom mangchan ahin vesuh tauvin ahile, chuin Egypt galsat miho chu abonchauvin suhna ding beijin anoh gam tauvin ahi. 
Pakaijin akangtalai jouseu abonchan atuhtang gam tan ahile, kangtalai ho chu abonchan akitang den gamtai. Egypt miten jong asei tauvin! “Israel mite angsung'a kon'in jamdoh loiju hite!” ajeh chu “Pakaijin Israel mite apan pin Egypt mite chung'a gal asat tai!” atiuve. 
Israel mite agalkai phat uvin, Pakaijin Mose heng'a aseijin, “Egypt mite twijin ale lonkhum na dingin na tenggol jahdoh kit in, ati.” 
Nisa ahung sodoh toh ki lhon'in, Pakaijin athupeh bangin Mose'n twipi chung vumma akhut ajah tan ahile, twipi jong angaija bangin ahung um doh kit tai. Egypt miten hoidoh agouvin, hinlah Pakaijin dillen lah a atollut tauve. 
Chuin Twi akitol mat kittan, Israel mite nung hindel a Egypt galsat miho le sakol touthem jouse, kangtalai jouse abonchauvin twijin achup gamhel tauvin khat jeng cha jong asohcha tapouvin ahi. 
Ahinlah Israel mite chu dillen lailung'a tolgo achun akitol uvin, dillen jeng jong ajet aveija bang bangin akisong den jengin ahi. 
Chu che nikho chun Pakaijin Israel mite chu Egypt mite khut a konna Israel te ahuhdoh dan chu ahi. Chule Israel mite'n Egypt mite Dillen in achup ho chu amu tauvin ahi. 
Egypt miten Isreal mite douna anei uva Pakai thahat le natoh kidang amuchen tauvin ahile, Israel mite'n Pakai chu aging tauve. Pakai le alhachapa Mose jong aging tauvin ahi.
