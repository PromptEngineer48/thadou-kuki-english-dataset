Potdohbu.
18.
Mose dinga anupa pa, Jethro Midian thempupa'n Mose le asopi Israel mite chung'a Pakai natoh thil kidang abol ho ajadoh tai. Amapan jong Pakaijin Israel mite Egypt gama konna ahin lhatdoh chu kidang asaval jengin ahi. 
Mose'n jong anupa pa, Jethro heng'a ajinu Zipporah le achapa teni chu asol'in, chuin ama hon insung'a apuilut uvin ahi. 
(Mose chapa apeng masa min chu Gershom ahin*, achapa apen lhah chun Mose'n hiti hin aseiye. “Mi gamsung'a khosa kahi,” tia ana minsah chu ahi. 
Ani channa achapa min chu Eliezer* asah in, Mose'n hiti hin aseije, “Keima khanglui laiya pat ka Pathen chun Pharaoh chemjam akon eihuhhing ahi,” tia asah ahi.) 
Mose nupa pa, Jethro chu Mose villin gammang lam'a ahung tan ahile aman ajinu le achapa teni chu ahin puijin, Mose le amiten gamthip lah a Pathen molsang kom'a ngahmun akisem nau mun ahinpha tauvin ahi. 
Jethro in jong Mose chu thu athot tan, “Keima Jethro nanupa pa kahin, keima nangma vil'a najinu le na chapa teni toh hung kahi,” ati. 
Mose jong pamlam'a aga chedoh in anupa pa chu aga kimupi tai. Ama jong akum lhan abeng achop peh tan ahi. Hiti chun khat le khat akilem touvin Mose chenna ponbuh sunga alut tauvin ahi. 
Mose'n jong anupa pa heng'a Pakaijin ichan geiya Egypt gam'a konna Israel mite chung'a na ahintoh ham ti thudol'a aseipeh in ahi. Chule lamkah jousea thohgim hahsa athoh nau le agentheina jouseu abonchan anupa pa henga aseipeh in ahi. 
Pakaijin Israel te ichan geiya Egypt mite khut a kon ahin panpi hitam tithu le ahin huhdohna thudol phatah a ahet chet phat in alungthim akipah lheh jengin ahi. 
Jethro'n hiti hin aseiye, “Pathen thangvah in umhen.” “Ajeh chu Aman Pharaoh le Egypt mite a konna nahin huhdoh jal in. Henge, Pakai bou chu ahiye Egypt mite thaneina a konna na huhdoh uva!” ati. 
“Tun keiman ka hechen sel taije, Pakai hi pakai jouse lah a thanei chungnungpen Pakai ahiye. Ajeh chu Aman aban thahat apanin Egypt mite akiletsah nauva konna Amite ahin huhdoh ahi,” ati. 
Chuin Jethro, Mose dinga anupa pan jong Pathen angsung'a pumgo thilto kilhaina asemin ahi. Aaron le Israel sung'a upaho ahung uvin Pathen angsung'a kilhaina ankong chu asoh khom uve. 
Ajing nikhon Mose'n jong atouna a konin mipi ho thutan dingin ahung doh tan ahile, mipi ho jong chu nilhum keijin ading tauve. 
Mose nupa pan, hitia mipi ho chung'a athilbol amudoh phat in aman Mose heng'a aseijin, “Hiche lai mun a hi ipi mong mong tongdoh inge nati ham? Ipi bol mong inge natia jingkah a kipat nilhah chan geiya mipi ho hi nahiti dinsah hitam?” ati. 
Mose’n adonbut in, “Ajeh chu mipi ho hi kaheng’a hung uva Pathen akon vaihopna hi akilah got u ahi. 
Amaho hi ahung kinah komu le kaheng ahin jonlut jiuvin, chuteng keiman thu katan peh jiuvin, Pathen akon nun leh khan dan toh kitoh a Pathen phatsah bang'a ka hilchen jiu ahiye,” ati. 
Chuin Mose nupa pan “Na thilbol hi adih poije!” ati. 
“Mipi ahin nangma nahin nabon uva na gimna diuva ahi bouve ajeh chu na thilbol hi abaipon ahi. Nangma changseh adin thil hahsa tah khat ahiye. 
Tun keiman kahil nahiye, ajeh chu keiman kultah a kahil nahi ngaijin lang Pathen'in naumpi jing hen. Mipiho jouse khella hi Pathen ansung'a ding jing nahin, akibaotam nau jouse Pathen angsung'a na selhah ding ahijoi. 
Mipiho hi Pathen akon chonna Dan ho le phatsahna ho hilchen inlang, hichu abolteina diuvin thumop in. Hinkho hi iti man ham ti vetsah in. 
Ahinlah mipi ho lah a konna thilbol thei mi mantam, Pathen ging, nehguh chah guh beija natong thei ding nalhen doh ding ahi. Amaho chun mi sangkhat lah hihen, jakhat lah, somnga lah, somkhat lah hijongleh aloi loija vaihom'a na pansah ding ahiye. 
Phat tinna mipi thutan a napan sah diu, thupi deuva thutanna ding aum vang le nangma heng'a hinpuiyun tin, thuneo ho jouse vang chu ama hon ama cham a atanlhah jeng theiyu ahi. Nangma dia jong agom lam hijo ding chule ama hon jong nangma nahin kithopi nau jong ahi. 
Hichengse hi najuiya ahile, nangma chunga Pathen'in toh ding nahin peh teng doujouna tha nanei joh ding chule mipi hon jong lungmoung sella ama ama in cheh akile jot thei diu ahi.” 
Mose'n jong anupa pa Jethro thusei chu angaijin, aman asei bang bangin aboltan ahi. 
Israel mite lah a kon'in thilbol thei mi mantam cheh alheng doh tai. Amaho chengse chu Israel mite lah a lamkaija pang ding hichu mi sangkhat, jakhat, somnga, somkhat chung'a vaihomin apansah sohkei uve. 
Amaho chun kiphunna le baotamna neiho chu Mose heng'a ahin lhut jiuvin ahi. Thuneo boina ho vang chu ama hon acham'un asulham jiuvin ahi. 
Hiche ho jouse nung hin, Mose'n anupa pa availhah in, ama jong agam langa akile tan ahi.
