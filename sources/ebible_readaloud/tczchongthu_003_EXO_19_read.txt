Potdohbu.
19.
Israel miten Egypt gamsung* ahin dalhah kalseu lhani lhin'in Sinai mun gamthip ahunglhung tauvin ahi. 
Rephidim ngahmun ahin dalhah nung uvin Sinai gamthip noi ahin jot un Sinai Mol kom'a chun abusa tauvin ahi. 
Mose molchunga akaltou tan Pakai toh aga kimuton ahile, Pakaijin molchung'a kon'in ahin kouvin aseije, “Thupeh hiche ho hi Jacob insung le Israel mite heng'a seipeh tauvin: 
'Egypt mite chung'a ipi ti lamdol'a na katoh nang hon namu sohkei uve. Keiman nangho muvanlai lhaving'a kahin pui uve ti namu chenset tauvin ahi. 
Chule tua hi kathu nangai uva kakitepna nakoi jing uva ahileh, nangho hi vannoi mite jouse lah a keima dia atumbeh a naum diu; ajeh chu vannoi leiset pumpi hi keima a ahi. 
Chule keima lenggam a chu nangho na leng chan diu, ka gamtheng nalo ding'u ahi. Hiche thu hi nangman Israel mite heng'a na seipeh tei ding ahi,” ati. 
Chuin Mose molchunga kon'in akumsuh in Israel sung'a upaho akou khom'in Pakaijin a thupeh chengse abonchan aseipeh sohkei uve. 
Chuin ama hon adonbut cheh uvin, “Ijakai Pakai thupeh bang'a keihon kabol diu ahi,” atiuve. 
Chuin Pakaijin Mose heng'a aseijin, “Keima naheng'a meilhanga kahung ding, Mose, nangma kahin houpi teng Israel miten ajah cheh diu, chuteng ama hon jong nangma nahin tahsan jing diu ahi,” ati. Mose'n jong Israel miten adonbutna chu Pakai heng'a aseijin ahi. 
Chuin Pakajin Mose heng'a asei in, “Kumsuh inlang keima kahungna dingin Israel mite gongtup tan, tuni le jing nikhoa din seipeh uvin chule avon jengu jong kisop theng cheh uhen. 
Nithum lhinnia dingin kigontup sah sohkei uvin, nithum lhin niteng amitmu tah uva Pakai chu Sinai Mol chung'a pat hung kumlha ding ahi tipeh un. 
Mol kimvel achun gamgi semin. Mipi ho chu gihgah in, 'Kichih theiyun! Koima molchung lam'a kaldoh hih hel'un chujongleh gamgi jeng jong koiman natham khah theilou diu ahi. Koi hileh mol thamkha chan chu thi jeng ding ahi. 
Mihem hihen ganhcha hijongleh gamgi kallhah taphot chu songa se jeng ding, ahilouleh thalpi a kap jengun. Amaho chu tha doh ding ahi. Ahinlah, sumkon ahung kimut ginga pat mipi jong molchung lam'a kal thei ding ahiuve,” ati. 
Chuin Mose'n jong mipi lam chu ahin jonsuh tai. Aman houkhomna a din mipi thensona aneijin, mipi hon jong ama ama von cheh akisop theng uvin ahi. 
Aman mipi heng'a aseijin, “Nithum lhin nia dingin ki gotup cheh uvin chule nu le pa kitimatna a jong naki kangse ding'u ahi,” ati. 
Nithum lhinni jingkah khovah lam'in, vah akithing'in kolphe avah in, molchung achun meilhang ahung kum lha jeng tan ahi. Sumkon hatah in ahung ging tan ahile, ngahmun sung lam'a um mipi abonchauvin akihotlhul lhajeng tauve. 
Mose'n jong mipi abonchauvin Pathen toh kimaito dingin ahin pui uvin, chuin mipi abonchauvin mol bul achun ading gam tauve. 
Sinai molchunga konin Pakai chu meikhu in ahung kum lhan ahile, lhum sung'a konna mei bangin ahung khudoh jeng tan, liing jong akihot lha jengin ahi. 
Sumkon gin chu ahat cheh cheh jeng tan ahile, Mose jong akipat in hiti hin aseije, van gin kithong lah a konin Pathen'in ahin houpin ahi. 
Pakai chu Sinai molchung vum'a kon'in ahung kumsuh tan chuin, Molchung vum'a kon'in Pakaijin Mose ahin kouvin ahi. Mose jong akipatdoh in molchung lam'a chun akal touvin ahi. 
Pakaijin Mose heng'a aseijin, “Kumsuh inlang mipi heng'a seipeh loijin, ajeh chu Pakai amunom uva gamgi khu ahin khal uva ahile thigam ding'u ahi. 
Chule thempu ho Pakai kom nai jing ho jengin jong ama cheh akithenso uva ahile bou Pakaijin asuhmang lou diu ahi,” ati. 
“Hinlah Pakai,” tin Mose'n aseijin, 'mipi abonchauva Sinai molchung'a hung kal theilou diu ahi. Ajeh chu nangman nei gihgah u ahitai. Nei seipeh bangin, 'Mol kimvella gamgi sem a athenga umsah dinga nei thupeh sa'u ahiye'” ati. 
Ahinlah Pakaijin aseijin, “Chesuh inlang Aaron gakipuiyin lang hin kilhonpi tan. Amavang thempu ho le mipi ho abonchauvin thupeh neijin lang koimacha gamgi khokhel a Pakai lam'a hung theilou ding'u ahiye,” ati. 
Chuti chun Pakajin athupeh bangin Mose achesuh in mipi ho heng'a aga seipeh tan ahi.
