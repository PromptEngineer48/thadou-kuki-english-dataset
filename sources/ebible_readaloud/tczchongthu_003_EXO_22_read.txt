Potdohbu.
22.
Mikhat touvin bongchal ham kelngoi ham aguhdoh a hichu akitha ham ahilouleh akijoh doh ham ahile bongchal khat chu bongchal nga a ale ding, chule kelngoi khat chu kelngoi li alepeh ding ahi. 
Gucha chun jankah a inbang aphetvang jeh a ama chu akimatna akivohlih khah tah a ahileh, hiche guchapa hinkho that pa chu tolthat a kisim louding ahi. 
Ahinlah hiche thilsoh hi sun khovah pet ahivang leh mithat pa chu tolthat a kisim ding ahi. “Ajeh chu gucha kiman chun, athil guhdoh jouse aboncha alesah kit ding ahin, alesah joulouva ahileh ama chu kijoh doh a athil guh ho sahna a kinei ding ahi.” 
Khat touvin bongchal hihen lang, Sangan hihen, Kelcha hijongleh aguhdohsa khat ama insung nei le gou lah a aga kimatdoh khah a ahileh ajat lenia asah kit ding ahi. 
Mihem koi hileh loulaija gancha alutsah a lengpi lei ham khat gasoh aneh khah a ahileh gancha chingtup louva loulaija alha thang jeh a hiche pa chun aloulaija gasoh phapen a chu alepeh teitei ding ahiye. 
Chule loulai meiyin ahinkah khah a ling le khao leilah ahin kah jeng'a, chang aki at-lai leh a ki-at loulai mun lah chule loulai chu akahvam lhah jeng tah a ahile, meihal pen pen chun akahvam chengse aboncha alesah cheh cheh ding ahiye. 
Tekahnan mi khat touvin aheng akom'a sum ham thil khat koi dinga angensea ahile, hiche thil chu akoipa insung'a konna aki guhmang khah tah a ahile hiche gucha pa chu akimat matna a athil guh chengse aboncha ajat nia alepeh cheh cheh ding ahiye. 
Amavang hiche guchapa chu akimatdoh khah tah louva ahile aheng akompa chun aguh ham ti hetchetna dinga Pathen angsung'a kipui lut ding ahiye. 
Tekah in, mini chun bongchal, sangan, kelcha, ahilouleh ponsil ham, thil dang amang khat chu ama nei danin asei cheh lhon tan ahi. Amani chu Pathen anga hung lhon ding, chule Pathenin athemmo chu aphondoh tengleh chuche pa chun khat pa chu ajatni a ale ding ahi. 
Tun tekahnan mi khat touvin aheng akom khat ngansena aneija sangan ahilou le bongchal ham kelngoi ham gancha khat pen pen apeh a ahile, hichu koima muphah nalou munkhat a akidel mang tai.” 
Hitia chu aum khah tah le anigel lhonna Pakai angsung'a kihahselna anei lhon ding ahi. Chuteng gancha neipan jong kihahselna chu anganse jeng ding hiche gancha chu asah louding ahitai. 
Ahinlah akoipa a konna kigumang anahi khah tah le, gancha koipan aneipa chu phatecha alepeh cheh cheh ding ahi. 
Gamsahang in atha khah ahitah le hiche hetchetna chu gancha koipan jong avetsah ding, chuteng gancha koipan asah tah louding ahi. 
Mi khat touvin asopi khat kom’a gancha manchah dinga alah a hiche gancha chu akisuh khah, ahilouleh athi khah tah a ahile, hiche vang chu alapan aman phatah a lepeh cheh cheh ding ahiye. 
Ahinlah aneipa umpet toh akito khah a ahile alapa chun asah tah louding, manpeh dinga alah ahi jongleh alahman toh akitosah cheh cheh ding ahitai.” 
Pasal toh kihepha loulai nungah khat chu pasal khat in ajol lhah a alupi khah a ahile, aman jong hiche nungah nu man chu apeh ding chuteng jia anei pai ding ahi. 
Ahinlah nungahnu pan achanu chu hiche pasalpa chu apeh nom hihbeh jeng'a ahileh, amapa chun nungah thengho man kikhum peh jat jat apeh ding ahiye. 
Nangman numei doithem kiti nahinghoi louding ahiye. 
Chule koi hijongleh gancha toh lumkhom kiti phot chu tha jeng ding ahiye. 
Koi hijongleu chun Pakai kom tailouva kilhaina dang nei mihem chu tha jeng ding ahi. 
Maljin kiti chu adih louva nabol louhel ding, na suhgenthei louhel ding ahi. Ajeh chu nangho jong Egypt gamsung'a maljin ana hisa nahiuve. 
Meithai le chaga jeng jong na ngailut ding ahi. 
Amaho na suhgim uva ahile amaho kaheng lama hung diu chuteng keiman amaho taona kasan ding ahi. 
Chuteng ka lungsatna nachung uva hunglhung ding naji teu kasat lhuh gam diu, nacha teu chaga kisoh gam ding ahiuve. 
Chule naheng nakom'a um kamite khat tou sum le pai nabat sah ahile, vaichapa chung'a chun sumkol vei tobangin umdan lang, sumtong jeng jong ama chung'a chu nadel khum louhel ding ahiye. 
Naheng nakom ponsil khattou tundoh dinga nalah a ahile, nilhum masang'a hiche ponsil chu aneipa nalepeh ding ahi. 
Ajeh chu aman hiche bou chu tahsa ponsilla anei ahin, pondang silla imutpi ding aneilou ahin ipi pon akisil ding ham? Keima heng'a hung tao henlang hile keiman ama chu kangai peh teitei ding ahi ajeh chu keima hi milainatpi jing kahi. 
Pathen doumah nasei thei louding chule namite lah a vaihompa jeng jong gaosapna nabol louding ahi. 
Na lhosohga le na lengpi gasoh chengse chu lung opkaina neilouhel'a nakatdoh jing ding, nachapa te lah a apeng masapen chu keima nei peh ding ahi. 
Na bongchal le na kelngoi jeng jong hitia chu nabol ding, hiche gancha nou chu nisagi sung'a api koma umden ding, ahinlah niget lhinni tengleh keima nei pehdoh ding ahiye. 
Nangho ka mitheng te nahi diu ahi. Chujeh a chu gamlah a sahang'in athasa kiti naneh louhel diu ahi. Hiche tobang ho chu uicha te neh dinga napeh diu ahiye.
