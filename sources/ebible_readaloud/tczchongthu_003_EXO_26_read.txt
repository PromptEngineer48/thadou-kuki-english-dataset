Potdohbu.
26.
Nangman nasah ding Houbuh chu tupat pondal som khat a nasah ding pon'a kikhong hi ding ahi. Pondum chule pon pol them naman khom ding, pondal chengse chu Cherub ho lim hoitah a kisem bang'a nakhai cheh cheh ding'u ahiye. Hichengse a chu Houbuh nasah ding ahi. 
Pondal som ho chu alet le aneo dan kibang cheh ding – asao dan Feet 42 hi ding chule avai feet 6 hi ding ahi. 
Pondal nga ho chu pondal sao khat naso ding, adang nga jong chu anina pondal sao naso ding ahi. 
Pondal masa chengse chu aninggol hoa akol kol'a tampi patdum'a nasem ding, hiche bang bang'a chu pondal golni loi lah a apam nungpen pondal gol lah a chu abil nasut hom ding akol kola tampi nasem doh ding ahi. 
Pondal kimkhat chu abil navu hom peh ding akol kol somnga nasem peh ding, chule pondal kolni lhinna gollah a jong abil navu ding akol jong somnga jen nasem peh ding ahi. Akol navu hom chengse chu ama cheh lhing'a toding ahi. 
Sana kol somnga nasem ding, hiche sana sumkol ho a chu pondal chengse chu na kaimat cheh cheh ding, chuteng Houbuh thil ho jouse kigom khoma Houbuh khat a nasah doh ding ahi. 
Houbuh chung khuna ding nangman kelcha mul'a kikhong pon phabep na manchah ding, agoma dinga pondal som le khat nasem ding ahiye. 
Pondal khat a feet 45 sao hi ding chule avai tong li cheh hi ding, hiche pondal som le khat se chu aboncha alet le aneo dan kibang chet ding ahi. 
Pondal nga ho vang chu na kilmat sohkei ding, adang pondal gup jong na kilmat sohkeiya, agup channa pondal vang chu ponbuh maiya chu na khu ding ahi. 
Chule pondal gol masa pen chu apam chin lah a akol somnga jen nasem ding, pondal golni channa jong chu aninggol ho a akol somnga mama navu hom ding ahi. 
Nangman sum-eng somnga jen nasem ding, sumkol hose chu aboncha pondal kol navu hom hoa chu nathil lut ding, ajeh chu ponbuh chu khat seh a akitundoh na dinga na kaimat sohkei ding ahiye. 
Chule ponbuh a pondal amoh chengse chu, Houbuh ponbuh nunglam a chu nakailhah sah cheh cheh ding ahi. 
Ponbuh kisahna a amoh chengse ho chu alang khat a hihen khat langa hijongleh Houbuh langto gella dinga inchi 18 cheh kikhai lhasuh ding ahi. 
Hiche ponbuh khuna dinga chu nangman jong kelgnoi chal vun le kelcha vun nemlai jeng na manchah ding ahiye. 
Houbuh a manchah thil ho chu nangman thinggi thing jol namsella nasem cheh cheh ding ahi. 
Kol khat a na manchah ding chu feet 15 hi ding, akol khat ding chu avai inchi 27 a sao hi ding ahi. 
Hiche thing kol khat cheh chu among lang'a abil navu hom peh cheh ding, Houbuh thing kol jouse chu aboncha abil nahoi peh cheh cheh ding ahi. 
Houbuh dinga thing kol nasem peh ding chule lhang lamma dingse chu aboncha thing kol som cheh hi ding ahi. 
Thing kol khat cheh le among lang suhmatna dinga chu, thingkol som inchung'a pansa dinga abil hom somin nadinga a dangka a kisem abiltom somli nasem ding, thingkol khat among lang'a chu asuhmatna thei ding abil tom ni cheh nasem peh ding ahi. 
A golni channa Houbuh chunglam kaiya pang ding chu thingkol somni a nasem ding ahi.” 
Dangka'a kitomjol abil somli hi ding, thing kol khat mong lang'a pang ding chu abil tomni hi ding chule thingkol lang khat a pansa ding jong chu abil tomni mama hi ding ahi. 
Lhumlam nga a Houbuh nunglama pang ding chu thing kol gup nasem peh ding ahi. 
Chule Houbuh nunglama'a pang dia abiltom'a dinga ni mama na sem peh ding ahiye. 
Hiche thing kol teni chu anoi lam douva pang lhon ding, chule alu lam'a vang chu chaokol masapen chutoh adang ho kaimatna a pang ding ahi. Hiche teni chu aning koltenia pang ding mong mong ahiye. 
Dangka a kisem abil tom ho jouse chu agomma thingkol get hi ding, abil tom aboncha som le get lhing ding ahi; thingkol khat a pang ding chu abilkol tom hi ding, anoija pang ding ma chu abiltom ni mama hi ding ahiye. 
Houbuh lang khatna thingkol chengsea vang chu thinggi kisui nam kotgol nga nasem ding ahi. 
Hichu Houbuh lang khat a thingkol hose avaiya pang dinga nga nasem ding, chule Houbuh nunglama lhum lama pansa dinga agol'a jam dinga ngama nasui ding ahi. 
Chule thing kol chengse chu akimlaija pang dinga alailha pen chu among langto gella dinga pang ding ahi.” 
Hiche thingkol chengse chu aboncha sana a natom jolsel ding chaokol ho jouse jong chu aboncha sana a na tomjol ding, agola kijam jouse jong chu sana mama a na tomjol cheh cheh ding ahi. 
Molsang chung'a Houbuh na kimu sah bang bang'a na tundoh teitei ding ahi. 
Chuteng Houbuh chu pondum, ponsandup chule ponsan pol thim na manchah ding ponbuh chu nakhu ding, tupat ponnem a kikhong na manchah tei teija; hiche pon ho chu aboncha khut a kikhong cheh cheh hi ding, Cherub lim bang'a kikhong ding ahi. 
Chule apon chu sana a kitomjol hi ding thinggi thing'a kisui khom li chung'a hi ding, abilkol a kol li ho a chu nakhai lhah cheh ding ahi. 
Alukhuh asumkol ho chu na khai lhah cheh cheh ding, hettohsahna thingkong jong chu pon lukhuh noija chu nahin koi ding ahiye; hichu pon lukhuna a konna chu muntheng chungnunga konna chu muntheng lai chu nasem khen diu ahi. 
Chuteng hettohsahna thing kong muntheng laiya chu hepi touna khat nado ding ahi. 
Pon lukhuna polama chu dokhang khat nasem ding Houbuh lhang lama dokhang toh kingato lhon ding akimlai tah a chu thaomeivah natun ding ahi; hiche dokhang chu sahlam sanga nado doh ding ahi. 
Ponbuh kot maiya ding chu pondum leh ponsandup chule ponsan pol thim. Tupat ponnem a heojang pahcha jem kijepna hoa bou nasem peh ding ahi. 
Pondah na dinga thinggi khom nga nasui ding, hichengse chu aboncha sana a natom jolsel ding, akikhai lhahna ding thihkol chengse jong sana twiya kisem jeng hi ding, abil tom nga jong chu sum-eng jenga nasem cheh cheh ding ahiye, ati.
