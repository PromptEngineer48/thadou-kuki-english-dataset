Potdohbu.
37.
Chuban'in Bezalel chun thinggi thing mangchan thingkong asem tan; adung tongni le akeh ahin, avai tongkhat le akeh chule atun tongkhat le akeh ma alhingin ahi. 
Aman jong asunglam le apolam ani gellin sana in atom jolsellin, akimvel jouse jong sana mama in atom'in ahi. 
Aman jong aning gol'a pansa ding chun sana kol-li asemin pang langkhat achun kol-ni asem'in ahi. 
Chuin aman moljol ni thinggi thing mangchan asuiyin, sana mama in atomna in ahi. 
Thingkhong putna dingin moljol teni chu thingkong geiya chaokol teni achun athillut in ahi. 
Chuin aman sana thengsellin thingkong khuna asemin, chule hepi touna jong asemin adunglam chu tongni le akeh ahin, availam chu tongkhat le akeh alhingin ahi. 
Chuin aman sana a kikheng Cherub lim bang'a um ni asemdoh in hepi touna among lam'a chun hiche teni chu apansah in ahi. 
Chule aman Cherub khat aning lam khat achun apansah in among langkhat ma achun Cherub khat ma apansah in hichengse chu khat in asemdoh in ahi. 
Cherub teni chun chunglang anga lhon'in chule alha ajap lhon'in hepi touna chu alhaving tenin avengbit lhon'in ahi. 
Chuin Bezalel in jong thinggi thing mangchan dokhang asemin ahi. Adunglam chu tongni in asemin, avai chu tong khat ahin chule atun lam chu tong khat le akeh alhing'in ahi. 
Aman akhom ho jouse chu sana in atomjol sohkeiyin ahi. 
Akimvelse chu khutphang tebep a saovin akhuna asemin chule ageikol jouse jong sana in atomjol sohkeiyin ahi. 
Chuin aman dokhang'a manchah dingin sana chaokol li asemin, dokhang kengli lama chung apansah in ahi. 
Chaokol chengse chu abonchan kinai chan aumsah sohkeiyin, chaokolho se chu dokhang domin akimang chan ahi. 
Aman hichengse chu thinggi thing jeng mangchan asuiyin, abonchan sana in atomjol sohkeiyin ahi. 
Chule dokhang'a manchah ding chun sana kong thengsel phabep asem'in lhengkong ahin, gimnamtui halna ding chule lengpitwi chapna ding jong abonchan sana in atomjol sohkeiyin ahi. 
Chuin Bezalel in thaomei khom asem'in hichu sana thengsel a asem ahi. Thaomei khom tunna ding ahin aputna ding, ani gellin phatah in sehcha'n akheng in ahi. Hichea pahcha kijem'a pang le alutom ijakai jouse chutoh thaomei khom jaonan khat in asemdoh tan ahi. 
Thaomei khom chu gup ahin, aning ho a kon'in abah thum cheh ahin jamdoh in ahi. 
Alang alanga abah gup ajamdoh ho akon chun thaomei vah khon bangin ajamdoh in alu ajumin apah jeng jong ahung um doh in ahi. 
Hiche thaomei khom chu almond pah bangin aning li aum'in ahi. 
Thaomei khom a konna hung jam doh abah gup chengse chutoh anoilam'a chengse jaonan khat in asemdoh tan ahi. 
Almond bah hung jamdoh chengse chutoh abonchauvin khat ahung sohdoh theiyin, hichengse chu sana thengsel a kikheng jeng seh ahi. 
Aman jong thaomei sagi asemin amei khao chetan na chengse jaonan sana thengsel in asemdoh in ahi. 
Thaomei khom chu ahin, amanchah jouse jaonan sana thengsel dangka somsagi le nga mangchan asemdoh in ahi. 
Bezalel chun gimnamtuiya dingin maicham khat thinggi thingin asem'in ahi. Hichu adung tong khat chule atun tong khat ahi. Chule hichea kimangcha saki chengse chutoh khat in ahung umdoh in ahi. 
Aman maicham chu sana thengsel in atom namselin, achung vumlam ahin chule ajet le avei jenga jong, chule a saki jouse jong abonchan sana in atom namselin ahi. Hiti chun aboncha'n akimvel jouse chun sanan ajemin ahi. 
Sana kijempah noiya akol a pang dingin aman sana chaokol ni asemdoh in, khat cheh chu aki ngatto sah in akhaiyin, maicham putlena manchah dingin asemdoh in ahi. 
Aman amoljol jouse jong chu thinggi thing jengin asemin sana in atomnam sohkeiyin ahi. 
Chuin aman kinu thei dingin thaotwi asemdoh in gimnamtui jeng jong chu halnam thei dingin asemin ahi. Hichengse chu abonchan thao sem them hon asemdoh bang bangin asem tan ahi.
