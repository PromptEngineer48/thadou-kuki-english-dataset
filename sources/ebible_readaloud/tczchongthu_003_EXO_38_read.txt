Potdohbu.
38.
Chuban in Bezalel in jong thinggi thing amangchan, pumgo thilto maicham asemin ahi. Hichu atun feet sagi le akeh ahin asaodan jong feet sagi le akehma alhingin chule asan dan tongli le akeh alhingin ahi. 
Aman aningli chengse achun saki-li asem'in hiti chun saki chengse le maicham chu khat in aumdoh theiye. Aman maicham chu sum-eng in atomin ahi. 
Chule aman maicham'a manchah dingin sum-eng amangchan - bel, khekeh, khangbel, kong le sumkhe asemin ahi. 
Chujouvin aman sum-eng jengin lhuhkhuh asemin ahi. 
Aman hiche dopna ding chun ningli asem'in ajol toh tho a pansahna ahin, hichengse chu sum-eng jeng mangcha a alhah ahi. 
Aman thinggi mangcha'n thingjol jouse chu sum-eng jengin atom'in ahi. 
Aman maicham putna dinga akol-li asem ho chun moljol chengse athillut in ahi. Chuin maicham chu aumna ding asemtoh in ahi. 
Bezalel chun ponbuh kotphung'a lhacha a pang numei ho kisilna kong ahin chule atunna ding jong chu sum-eng jeng'a asem'in ahi. 
Chuin Bezalel'in pal agenkim sohkeiyin, pal'a khai ding pon jouse chu tupat ponnem a kisem jeng ahi. Chule lhanglam'a khai ding pondal se chu feet jakhat to somnga alhing'in ahi. Hiche pal lhanglama khai ding pondal chengse jong chu tupat ponnem jenga kikhong ahin agom chun tong jakhat jen alhing e. 
Apal khom somni ahin, aboncha sum-eng jeng'a kisem ahi. Pal khom'a akol kisem chengse jong dangka jeng'a kisem ahi. 
Aman pal sahlam'a pansa dingin feet jakhat toh somnga chule akhom somni jaona hichengse chu sum-eng jeng'a asem ahi. Pal khom ahin, akhaina kol aboncha sana le dangka jeng ahi. 
Chule lhumlam'a pondal pal chengse chu feet somsagi le nga alhing'in akhom ahin, akol kisem jouse chu sana le dangka jeng ahi. 
Mailam'a pansa pondal chengse jong chu tong somsagi le nga sao ahi. 
Kelkot polam'a pondal khat chu tong somni le ni le akeh alhingin, chule atunna khom jong thum ahi. 
Chule kelkot langkhat a pansa jong chu kibangin asem'in tong somni le ni ahin, chule atunna khom jong thum ahi. 
Pal kigetna a pansa pondal jouse chu tupat ponnem jeng'a kisem ahi. 
Palkhom ahin atunna khom aboncha sum-eng jeng'a kisem ahi. Akhom'a kimang ho jouse jong chu sana le dangka jeng'a kisem ahi. 
Aman palmai kot phung'a ding chun pondal asem'in ahi. Hichu tong somthum in asaovin, asandan tong sagi le akeh ahi. Aboncha pal'a kigen chengse chutog kibang ahi. 
Akhom chengse chu li ahin, atunna jouse jong chu sum-eng jeng ahi. Chule atunna, akhaina kol jouse jong chu sana le dangka jeng ahi. 
Houbuh ahin chule akimvella pondal jouse jong sum-eng jeng ahi. 
Hichengse Houbuh ahin, chule hettohsahna Houbuh hichu Mose thupeh dungjuiya kisem, thempu Aaron chapa Ithamar ngaisahna dungjuiya Levi te natohna dinga kisem ahi. 
Chule Juda phung khaiya kon Hur chapa, Uri chapa Bezalel in jong Pakaijin Mose athupeh bang chu abol sohkeiyin ahi. 
Amapa chu Dan phunga kon Ahisamach chapa Oholiab chule khut themna neitah le ijakai thilbol them - patdum ahin, ponsandup chule tupat ponnem bol them'a jong pang ahi. 
Houbuh le muntheng sahdohna dingin sana Shekhel somni le ko leh jasagi leh somthum ahin choi khom uve. 
Israel pumpi'n dangka talent jakhat toh Shekel sangkhat toh jasagi leh somsagi leh nga jaonan apedoh uvin muntheng sahna a kimangcha shekel dungjuiya kisim ahi. 
Hiche dangka kidongkhom chengse hi min kisim dungjuiya hung kipelut chu ahi. (Hichu beka khat, shekel kehkhat toh kibang ahin, muntheng laiya manchah ding ahi). Pasal Kum somni apat chunglamsea konna kidong sangjagup le sangthum le janga le somnga alhing'in ahi. 
Muntheng chung khuna ding, chule atunna ho aboncha'n sana le dangka sangsagi le janga alhingin atunna khat cheh chu dangka somsagi le nga a kisem ahi. 
Amoh chengse dangka somli le nga chu akhom ahin, akhaina dinga kisem akol chengse tomnan amangin ahi. 
Chule mipi chun sum-eng a kisem dangka sangnga le jathum le somkhat ahin choijun ahi. 
Hichengse chu akhomho chule Houbuh semnan amang'in maicham achun sum-eng apansah in chule sum-eng chu thil ijakai semnan jong amang'in ahi. 
Sum-eng mangchan pal kikai jouse, kelkot chule Houbuh sung'a thil ijakai jouse semnan aneijin ahi.
