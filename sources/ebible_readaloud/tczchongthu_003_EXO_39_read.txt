Muntheng'a natong ding hon amanchah diuvin asandup chule asanpol a kikhong pon akhui uvin—Aaron'in muntheng'a na atoh teng akivonna ding chu Pakaijin Mose heng'a athupeh ahi. 
Bezalel chun sana mangcha'n konggah asem'in hichu asandup le asan-ao patnem jaonan asem'in ahi. 
Aman sana khat lim'in hoisel in khutthem tah in asem'in ahi. Hichu asandup, asan-ao patdum mangcha'n sehcha'n akheng tuoh'in asemdoh in ahi. 
Chule konggah akon chun akhaina ding anung le amalam'a aumto'n lengkou chan geijin akhailhahna aum'in among lam chu akijopmat in ahi. 
Hiche jong hi angaija bangin hoisel'in asem'in: phatah a kipheh, adum, asan-dup, asan-ao sana jeng'a kitom ponnem mangcha a kisem, hichu Pakaijin Mose heng'a athupeh dungjuiya kisem ahi. 
Chule Onyx song chu ni amangcha uvin chuin sana alhon'in agoltoh sohkeiyun ahi. Song ho chu Israel mite ama ama insung dungjui cheh in amin'u mohor kinam khum bangin agoltoh sohkei uve. 
Aman hiche song agol chule konggah lengkou vumma kikhailha a kon chun Israel mite geldoh jing na dingin asemtoh sohkeije. Hichengse jong hi Pakaijin Mose athupeh bangin abol sohkei uve. 
Bezalel chun khutthem tah in Ompho asem'in ahi. Aman konggah bang bang chun asemdoh in hichu sana ahin, adum chule asandup ponnem jaonan asem'in ahi. 
Aman ompho chu lheptup theiyin asemdoh in akilhep teng adung avai inchi ko ahiye. 
Ama hon songmantam chu golli in agolsang uve. Amasan Carnelian song-san apansah uvin, a engthim, chule emerald song ahi. 
Golni channa dingin blue lapis lazuli, chule songgil ahi. 
Golthum channa dingin aiha song, khipi chule umbel song ahi. 
Golli channa dingin song-eng, Onyx song chule songmantam-eng apansah uve. Hichengse hi abonchan sana twiyin akaimat sohkeiyun ahi. 
Song golkhat cheh chun Israel mite ama-ama kailhah dungjuijin amin jouse toh akijih tha'n songkhat cheh a chu Mohor kisem ahiye. 
Ompho le konggah kijopmatna ding chun sana thengsel a kisem khao bangin akhuiyun ahi. 
Chule sana patjang'a kisem le sana akolni jong asem uvin sana kol teni chu ompho ning lama chun akaimat uvin ahi. 
Ompho tenia kikhai sana kol teni chu akhitmat uvin ahi. 
Chule sana patjang teni le sana gol teni chu ajopmat uvin ahi. Konggah khaina lengkou tenia kon chun agen kikhailha teni chu ompho maiya chun abehpeh peh uvin ahi. 
Chuin ama hon sana kolni asemkit uvin, ompho geilam hichu konggah toh chun akaimat uvin ahi. 
Chule sana patjang kolni asemdoh kit uvin ani tah chun lengkou vumma konggah kikhaina achun akaimat uvin ahi. 
Chuin ompho chu patjang khat mangcha'n akolsung'a chun athilmat uvin konggah a beh akol chengse toh athilmat uvin ahile phatah a kipheh bangin aumdoh tan ahi. Hitia abol phat uchun konggah le ompho chu achah kheh jeng tan ahi. Hichengse chu Pakaijin Mose athupeh bangin abol sohkei uvin ahi. 
Bezalel chun adum'a kikhong kivonna dingin sangkhol asem'in, 
hichu Aaron'in aki-ah ding chule hiche sangkhol chol chu a-ehlouna dingin agei gol akhui cheh cheh in ahi. 
Ama hon sangkhol-chol chu kolbuthei jem'in asem uvin patdum, asan-ao chule ponnem mangcha'n asemdoh uvin ahi. 
Chuin ama hon sana thengsel'in chaojang asem uvin hichengse chu abonchan kolbuthei jem le sangkhol golla chun akoiyun ahi. 
Chaojang khat khaina achun kolbuthei jem khat in akiba cheh-cheh in ahi. Hiche sangkhol-chol thempu khat chun na atoh teng hoilai mun hijongleh Pakaijin Mose athupeh bang'a kivon ding ahiye. 
Ama hon Aaron le achapa te ho jouse dingin sangkhol chol len tupat jengin asempeh uvin ahi. 
Delkop chule lukhuh ding jong jaonan tokachol jong pon phatah in asem uvin ahi. 
Kigah ding jouse chu ponnem asandup, asan-ao phatah a heojang'a kisem jeng, hichu Pakaijin Mose athupeh bang'a kibol ahi. 
Achaina'n lal lukhuh sana thengsel in asem uvin ahi. Mohor kinam den bangin aum khum uvin: Pakai athenge, ti thucheng akoi un ahi. 
Ama hon lukhuh chu adum khat Aaron delkop dingin asem uvin, hichu Pakaijin Mose athupeh bang'a abol ahi. 
Hiti chun ajona lamin kikhop khomna Houbuh* chu sahchaiyin aumtai. Pakaijin Mose athupeh bangin Israel mite'n jong atong chai sohkeiyun ahi. 
Chuin ama hon Mose heng'a Houbuh chule thil-le-lo ijakai abonchan ahin choijun: akhaina-kol ho, agol ho, akhomho, akhom noi nung'a ho hiti chun abonchan ahin choi sohkeiyun 
ponbuh khuna ding chun kelngoi chule kelchal vun; hichengsea chu khu ding; 
hettohsahna thingkong; thingkong putna dinga moljol teni; chule hepi touna mun; 
dokhang le thilkeo ho; chule changlhah thilto chengse jaona ahi; 
sana thengsel'a kisemdoh thaomei-khom chule thaomei-khon chengse jaona, amanchah chengse le athaotwi jaona jong ahi; 
sana a kisem maicham, chule kinu ding thaotwi; gimnamtui jaona le pondal jouse jong; 
sum-eng'a kisem maicham; chule aputna dinga sum-eng jol ho jaona; kisilna kong le atunna jouse jong; 
bangpheija kigol pondal ho; pal khom chengse ahin; chule Houbuh lutna lam; Houbuh a kimang thil le lo jouse jaona ahi; 
thempuho dinga hoitah a kikhui hichu muntheng laiya na atoh teng ule akivonna diu Aaron thempu chule achapa te hon thempu kin abol teng ule akivonna diu ahi. 
Pakaijin Mose heng'a athupeh bang chun Israel mite'n jong abonchan ajuikim sohkeiyun ahi. 
Chuin Mose'n anatoh jouseu abonchan avelhan ahi. Ama hon toh ding ijakai Pakai thupeh bangin atongkim sohkei uve ti amuchet phat chun phatthei aboh tauvin ahi.
