Thempu Dan.
18.
Pakaijin Mose henga hiti hin thu aseipeh in, 
Israel chate henga seipeh in. Keima hi nangho Pakai na Pathen chu kahi. 
Nanghon naboncha uva masanga Egypt gamsunga nachen laiyuva na chonna bangun Keiman kahin puilutna Canaan gam'a vang nachon louhel diu ahi. Amaho chonna dan jouse hi na juilou diu ahi. 
Nanghon naboncha uva keima chonna dan jeng nanit diu, nun le khan toh thoa keima chonna dan aboncha najui diu ahi. Ajeh chu keima nangho Pakai leh Pathen kahi. 
Hijeh chun na boncha uva keima chondan jouse le chonna dan aboncha, nun le khan toh thoa nanit jing diu ahi, hichu nit ding chua konna hinna ahiye, ajeh chu keima Pakai chu kahi. 
Nangho jouse leh pasal kiti phot in aki naipi ainko ham chu ajotlut a anih ahopeh louhel ding ahi. Pakai le Pathen keima kaum jing e. 
Nanu sagohkeo ham, napa delchen nasut peh thei louhel ding, nanu nihchen le sil jong naho peh thei louhel ding ahi; ajeh chu ama nahingnu ahin, nangman anih chu naho peh louhel ding ahi. 
Napa jinu nihchen jong naho louding, hichu nabol khah ngai ngai leh napa delchen nasut tobang ahi. 
Napa a konna chanu hihen, nanu a konna chanu hijongleh, hiche na sopi numei chu insung a konna peng hihen kholgama konna peng hijongleh, anih naho peh louhel ding ahi. 
Na chapa te chanu, na tunu te ham khat chu anih nahopeh louhel ding ahi; ajeh chu na tute nihchen nahodoh peh chu nangma le nangmaa kihodoh peh tobang nahi. 
Nangman napa jinu chanu ham, napa a konna hungpeng numei chu anih naho peh louhel ding ahi, hiche nu chu na sopinu ahi. 
Nangman napa sopi numei jeng jong anih nahodoh peh louhel ding ahi, hiche nu chu napa dinga sopi kinaicha ahi. 
Nanu sopi numei jong anih nahopeh thei louhel ding, nanu a konna asopi numei akinaipi cha ahi bouve. 
Napa sopipa delchen jong nalhap peh louding, ajinu jong na nailut thei louding ahi, ajeh chu nang dia nanungah ahi. 
Na mounu jeng jong anih nahodoh louding ahi, ajeh chu amanu hi nangma chapa dia ajinu ahin, anih nahodoh thei louhel ding ahi. 
Na sopipa jinu nih jong nahodoh peh thei louding, ajeh chu amanu chu na sopipa nabolna tobang bang hi ding ahi. 
Numei khat chu anih nahodoh peh tah jongleh, achanu chu anih nahopeh louhel ding ahi; chule hiche numei nu chapa le chanu hihen achanu hijongleh, anih hodoh dia na kipui thei louding, amaho cheng chu nang dia nakinaipi tikhat nahiuvin, hichu nabol khah tah leh gitlouna a chon chu nahi tai. 
Chule numei khat chu asopinu damlai sung sea, asopinu chu anih hodoh peh dia napui thei louhel ding ahi. 
Numei chu athinei pet a na nailut a anih nahopeh louhel ding ahiye. 
Nangman kiphal theng tah dana na inhengpa jinu chu luppi ding lung navop louhel ding, amanu chutoh chonset a na lhahlut a na kisuhboh thei louding ahi. 
Na chate khat a khatcha jong meiya govam ding chule Molech adia na todoh louhel ding, hiche hoa konna chu na Pakai le Pathen min na suhthang theilou ding ahi, ajeh chu keima Pathen kahi. 
Pasal hi numei tobang banga na luppi thei louhel ding, hichu thet umtah thilbol ahi. 
Kiphal theng tah dana gancha nanailut a na luppi louhel ding ahi, hitobang chu chonset ahi bouve, numei jeng jong kiphal thengtah a gancha angsunga konna kido louhel ding ahi, hichu conset lah a gitlouna lenpen khat ahi. 
Hichengsea kon hin nangho chonset a lhalut hihhel un, ajeh chu keima tah in nangho masang a namtin vaipi kadohna chu hitobanga ana chonset khah jeh u ahi. 
Hitia chu gam le leiset jong ana suhboh u ahitai; hichea konna chu themmo channa talent kamat sah ahin, hitobanga chu agam leiset a cheng mite jouse jong acham louva kitolmang ahi tauve. 
Nanghon na boncha uvin keima chonna dan ngai le ka chonna jouse hi nit sohkeiyun; agama cheng hihen ahilouleh nalah uva konna hung kholjin hijongleh, hitia thet umtah thil ho hi khat a khatcha jong nabol thei louhel diu ahi. 
Ajeh chu thet umtah thil hichengse hi, nangho masanga cheng miten nana bolu ahitan; hiti chun agam jeng jong ana suboh tauvin ahi. 
Tun nang hon agam hi suboh kit leu chun, nangho masanga ana cheng namtin vaipi te bang banga chu na chedoh jeng diu ahi. 
Koi hijongleh hitobang thil thet umtah a konna chonkha aum ngai ngai leh hitobang bolkha mihem chu nangho jouse lah a konna pampaiya um jeng ding ahi. 
Keima thupeh hi nanit jeng diu, na masang uva miten ana bolsau thet umtah chondan chengse hi nabol louhel diu ahi, hichea kon chun kisuboh hihhel un, Keima hi nangho Pakai le na Pathen u kahi, ati.
