Thempu Dan.
23.
Pakaijin Mose henga aseijin, 
Israel chate jouse jah a seipeh tan, Pakai dia phat tepsa kut naboncha uva atheng ahi tia na phondoh cheh diu, Keima dia hiche phat tepsa kut ho chu ahileh, 
Nigup sunga mihon na akitoh cheh ding, nisagi lhinni vang chu imacha bol louva kicholdo nikhoa nanei cheh diu, hichu thengsel'a kikhop nikhoa ding ahi. Hiche nikho kitia hi natoh nabol thei louhel diu ahi. Hoilai chan a cheng jong leu chun hiche nikho hi kicholdo nikhoa nanit jing diuva thupeh ahi. 
Hicheng hi Pakaiya dia phat ana kitepsa kikhop khomna theng chu ahin, hitobang nikho jousea hi aphat kitepsa dungjui cheh a nang hon mi nakou khom diu ahi. 
Lhamasa nisom le nili lhin nikho, hiche nilhah lam vang chu Pakai dia Kalchuh Kut hi ding ahi. 
Chule hiche lhasung nisom le ninga lhinni chu Pakai dia cholsolou Changlhah Kut nikho ding ahin, nisagi sung vang chu cholsolou changlhah tang louva naneh diu ahi. 
Nikho masapen nileh nabonchauva khoppi theng naman diu, lalna thei na natoh lou diu ahi. 
Nisagi sungsea chu Pakai dia govam thil tang louva na todoh diu, nisagi lhin nikho vang leh khoppi theng nikho ahitan, hiche nikho teng chuleh naboncha uva na kichol diu lalna thei na natoh lou diu ahi. 
Pakaijin Mose henga thu aseipeh in, 
Israel chate henga ajah uva hiti hin seiyin, keiman kapeh nau gamsung nahin phah uva gamga soh nahinlo tengu leh na changphal gau masapen chu thempupa lama nachoi diu ahi. 
Hichea konna chu thempu pan nangho santhei dol nahi theina diuva nathenso diu, ajing nikho cholngahni tengleh thempu pan thensona anei ding ahi. 
Hiche thensona kibol nikhoa changpal natodoh tengu leh kelngoinou achal khat jong Pakaiya dia nolna beihel chu nakatdoh tha diu ahi. 
Hiche nikhoa lhosohga todoh ding chu suhlou changbong neldisel Ephah khat dim, hopsoma hopni kiso ding; hichu thaotwi toh kihal, Pakaiya dia gimnamtuisel a govam ding, don ding lengpithei jong chu khon dimkhat hoplia konna hopkhat chu hichea ding chu ahi. 
Chule hiche nikho masang chun, na Pathen pa diuva thilto ding nahin pohlut kahseuvin changlhah jong nehda uvinlang, chang kikang poh ahin changlhah kigou tho jong nehdauvin, hoilai chan a cheng jong leu chun hicheng hi nakhang nakhang uva chonna danin jui jingun. 
Cholngah nikho aki chai apat changpal nahin choi uva thensona thilto nabol nikho uva pat nikho nasim cheh cheh diu, hapta sagi chan nasim diu ahi. 
Cholngah nikho vel sagi chan geiya nasim uva chule ajing nikho nisomnga lhin chan geiya nasim tha diu, chujou tengleh Pakai heng lama lhosohga thilto natodoh diu ahi. 
Na inson cheh uva pat Ephah dim khat cheh hopsom lah a hopnia kikhen thenso thilto dinga changlhah lhon sao ni jaona nahin choi diu ahi, hiche teni chu cholsosa ahin, Pakaiya dia lhohsoga masapen thiltoa pang ding ahi. 
Chule hiche changlhah jaona chu kumkhat lhingsa nolna beihel kelngoi sagi, bongchal golpai khat, kelngoi chal ni jaona a nakatdoh uva, hiche gancha ho chu Pakaiya dia pumgo thiltoa kimang ding gimnam tuisel a meiya govam ding ahi. 
Chonset thilto dingin kelchal khat mang unlang, chamna thilto le gantha kilhaina dinga kum khat a tah kelchal ni nahin kat diu ahi. 
Chule hicheng ho chu thempu pan Pakai angsunga thensona anei ding, lhosohga masa thilto changlhah jaonaa, kelngoi chal teni jong chu athenso tha ding ahi, hiche hose hi thempu dia Pakai angsunga thil thengsel'a um ahi. 
Hiche nikhoa hi khoppi khat nanei diu, hichu khoppi thengsel'a naman uva, lalna thei natoh na bollou diu, nakhang lhumkei uva hoichan a chejong leu chun hiche hi chonna dan dungjuiya najui jing diu ahi. 
Naboncha uva na gamsung uva konna chang na-at tengu leh, na loujao uchu apam chin jousea na-at chailou diu, nachang atna nung uva changkhai ho jong nachom thengsel lou diu ahi; vaichate leh kholjin ho dia na koipeh diu ahi, ajeh chu keima nangho Pakai, Pathen kaum jing e, ati. 
Pakaijin Mose henga aseijin, 
Israel chate jouse jah a hiti hin seipeh tan, lhasagi lhin teng, hiche lhasung nimasani leh naboncha uva naki chol uva khoppi theng nanei diu, sumkon mutgin toh thoa naphon jalji diu ahi. 
Thabeina thei na tong hih un, Pakai henga pumgo thilto nahaimil lou diu ahi. 
Pakaijin Mose henga thu aseipeh in, hiti hin bolin, ati. 
Lha sagi lhin nikho nisom alhin nikho hi ki thoidamna ahin, hiche nikhoa hi thon louva khoppi theng khat nabol diu, anngol toh thoa Pakai dia pumgo thilto nato doh diu ahi. 
Hiche nikhoa hi koima chan thabeina thei natoh na bollou diu, ajeh chu kithoidamna nikho loupi khat ahin, na Pakaiyu leh na Pathen'u henga thoidamna nanei diu ahi. 
Koi hijongleh hiche nikhoa anngol lou aum leh, amite lah a konna pampaiya um ding ahi. 
Hitia chu koi hijong leu chun hiche nikhoa hi thabeina thei natong aum khah leh ama chu amite lah a konna ka suhmang ding ahi. 
Naboncha uva natoh thilbol akon nakikangse cheh diu, hoilai mun'a hijongleh na chenna jouseuva chonna dan ahi tia najui cheh diu ahi. 
Hiche nikho hi na boncha diuva kicholdo nikhoa naman jing diu, hitia chu anngol toh thoa hiche lhasung nikho ahung lhinni apat ajing nilhah lam chan a choldona a naman cheh diu ahi, ati. 
Pakiyin Mose henga aseijin, 
Israel chate jouse henga seipeh tan, lhasagi lhinna chule nisom le ninga lhin nikhoa pat nisagi sungse hi Pakaiya dia Lhambuh kut a kimang ding ahi. 
Kut kipat nikho chu khoppi theng nikho ahin, hijeh chun thabeina thei natoh ima bol-hih un, ati. 
Tanglouhel a nisagi sunga Pakaiya dia thilto nabol jom jing diu, niget alhin nikho tengleh khompi theng khat nabol diu, Pakai angsunga pumgo thilto nakatdoh diu chu ahin, hichu khoppi theng laitah ahijeh in thabeina thei natoh akon nakikangse cheh diu ahi. 
Achunga kimin phah chengse hi Pakaiya dia phat tepsa kut ho chu ahin, hiche na ding chun Israel chate koukhom sohkeiyun lang, khoppi theng toh thoa thilto ding ho ahin, pumgo thiltona a naman diu anikho mama a abol ding dan jouse govam ding hihen, donthei lengpitwi hijongleh nabol diu ahi. 
Hiche ho jouse hi naboncha uva Pakaiya dia na thiltou hichu deilhen choma na thilto u ahitan, chule athenso ho jouse jaonan nathil pehdoh jouseu leh Pakaiya dia cholngah nikho le adang dang nanit kimsel hou kisimtha lou ahi. 
Lhasagi chan nisom leh ninga lhin nikho leh naboncha uva gamsung lhosohga nachop kimsoh niuva pat nasim uva Pakaiya dia nisagi sungsea kut tanglouva nabol jom jing diu, hiti chun kut akipatni chu choldo nikho theng hi ding, niget lhinni chan chu choldo nikhoa naman diu ahi. 
Kut kipat nikho teng thingga ahoilai nalo lhah diu, lusuna tampi jaona, thingboh phatah tah nahe lhah diu, vadung pang lah a tomdo he toh thoa, hicheng chu na kichoi uva, na Pakaiyu leh na Pathen'u angsunga tang louva nakipa jing diu ahi. 
Kumkhat seh le Pakai heng lama nisagi tang louva kut khat nabol jing diu, hichu nakhang lhumkei uva chonna dan ngaiya na manchah jing diu, lhasagi lhinna lhasunga chu naman diu ahi, ati. 
Nisagi sunga naboncha uva lhambuh a nachen diu, Israel sungkon agammi cheng aboncha lhambuh sunga cheng ding ahiuve. 
Hichea kon chun nachilhah teuvin keima tah in Egypt gam'a konna Israel chate jouse kahin puidoh chun lhambuh sunga bou kachen sahji e ti ahin hetdoh thei diu ahi, ajeh chu Keima hi na Pakaiyu leh na Pathen'u kahi, ati. 
Mose’n Pakai dinga phat tepsa kut ho jouse chu Israel chate jouse lah a aphondoh peh ahi.
