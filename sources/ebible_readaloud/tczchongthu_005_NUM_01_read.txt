Minbu.
1.
Israel ten Egypt gamsung ahin dalhah kal u kum khat ahung lhin chun Pathen'in Sinai gamthip a Houbuh a chun Mose ahoulimpi tan ahi. Hichu alhani channa nikho masapen ni ahin, Pakaijin hitin aseipeh e, 
“Israel namsung pumpi a kon'in aphung cheh uva kon a gal hangsanho leh a insungmi hou min jouse aumden dingin jihlut cheh in. Pasal ho jouse chu jihlut soh hel in 
kum somni apat chunglam gal boltheiho jouse chu nang le Aaron'in amin u na jihlut teitei lhon ding, 
chuleh hiche phungho jouse lah a kon a chu lamkai khat cheh in nangma na kithopi ding ahi. 
“Hiche phungho a kon a lamkai ho hi na kithopi ding ho chu ahiuve. Phung Lamkai Reuben Shedeur chapa Elizur 
Simeon Zurishaddai chapa Shelumiel 
Judah Amminadab chapa Nahshon 
Issachar Zuar chapa Nethanel 
Zebulun Helon chapa Eliab 
Ephraim son of Joseph Ammihud chapa Elishama Manasseh son of Joseph Pedahzur chapa Gamaliel 
Benjamin Gideoni chapa Abidan 
Dan Ammishaddai chapa Ahiezer 
Asher Ocran chapa Pagiel 
Gad Deuel chapa Eliasaph 
Naphtali Enan chapa Ahira 
Amaho hi japi lah akon a kilhengdoh lamkai cheh, apu apa phung kailhah a pat lamkaiho chuleh Israel te phung ho a konna mi upa cheh ahiuve.” 
Hichun lamkai kilheng doh chengse chu Mose le Aaron in akou khom lhon'in, 
hiche nikho ma ma chun Israel te jalhang pumpi jong akhom tup lhon'in ahi. Mipiho jouse chu ama ama phung leh insung cheh kailhah a kon'in amin akijihlut cheh in ahi. Pathen'in Mose athupeh bangin, Israel pasal kum somni apat chunglamse chu khat khatnin akijihlut in ahi. 
Hiti chun Mose in amaho min jouse Sinai gamthip lai mun a chun ana sutup sohkeiyin ahi. 
Ama ama phung dungjui cheh a amin u kijihlut banga pasal kum somni apat achunglam galsat a konthei amijat ho chu hicheng hi ahi. Phung Mijat Reuben (Jacob chapa tahpen) 46,500 
Simeon 59,300 
Gad 45,650 
Judah 74,600 
Issachar 54,400 
Zebulun 57,400 
Ephraim (Joseph chapa) 40,500 
Manasseh (Joseph chapa) 32,200 
Benjamin 35,400 
Dan 62,700 
Asher 41,500 
Napthali 53,400 
Israel phung som le ni lamkaiho toh Mose le Aaron in akhumlut u pasal ho cheng hi abonchauva ama ama phung chilhah dungjui cheh a kikhumlut cheh ahiuve. 
Amaho chu ama ama insung duijui cheh a kikhumlut ahiuve - Israel te pasal kiti pou kum somni apat chunglam gal bolthei jouse hi abonchauva a insung dungjuiya kijihlut cheh ahiuve. 
Amaho chu agomma 603,550 ahiuve. 
Ahinlah hiche kisim khomna a hin Levi te vang apang pon ahi. 
Ajeh chu, Pathen'in Mose kom'ah hiti hin ana seiyin ahi, 
Min najihlut teng Levi te hi najihlut loubeh ding, Israel mite lah a nasim tha loubeh ding ahi. 
Kikhop khomna ponbuh le aki mangcha thil ho aboncha a Levi te mopohna napeh ding, nachena chan uva Ponbuh le amanchah chengse aboncha ama hon apoh uva, akimvella ngahmun sem toh thoa alhacha a pang ding ahiuve. 
Kikhop khomna ngahmun chon phat alhin teng, Levi chapa te chengin aphet lhah diu, hitia chu ngahmun nalhun tengu leh ama hon athah sondoh kit diu, amavang koi hileh phalna beiya kikhop khomna ponbuh ganailut behseh chan chu thi ding ahi. 
Phung jousen ama ama insung ponlap cheh utoh ngahmun akisem diu ahi. 
Israel pumpi chunga Pakai lunghan louna dinga Levi chapa ten vang kitepna ponbuh kimvella ngahmun akisem cheh uva avetsui diu ahi. Hitia chu hiche ponbuh ventupna chu Levi te mopohna hi ding ahi. 
Hiti chun Pathen'in Mose henga athupeh bang tah chun Israel chaten imajouse abol tauvin ahi.
