Minbu.
2.
Hichun Pakaijin Mose le Aaron chu thu hicheng ahin hilchahna aneitan ahi: 
“Israel ten ngahmun asem tengu leh, ama ama phung kai cheh a um ding ahiuve. Aphung phung leh ama ama insung micheng chu ama ama ponbuh noija khosa cheh ding ahiuve. Aphung aphunga kihomtohho chun angahmun cheh uchu kikhop khomna Houbuh pangli kiemvel a akisem dingu ahi, hinlah hichu kikahjep hi ding ahi. 
Kikhop khomna ponbuh niso lama ngahmun sem ding phungho chu Judah, Issachar leh Zebulun hidiu ahi. Anoia ho hi amin kikhum galsat ding jat, aphungmin uleh alamkai hou chu: Phungmin Lamkai Amijat Judah Amminadab chapa Nahshon 74,600 
Issachar Zuar chapa Nethanel 54,400 
Zebulun Helon chapa Eliab 57,400 
Judah phung lamkaina noiya galsat mi ahung kikhom jat chu mihem 186,400 ahi. Israel ten ngahmun thah ajot a asemji tengu leh hiche phung thumho leh alamkai hou hi mapui a pang dingu ahi. 
Reuben, Simeon leh Gad chu kikhop khomna ponbuh lhanglam a ngahmun sem a pang ding phungho chu ahiuvin, aphungjem u noi cheh a um cheh ding ahiuve. Anoiya ho hi galsat dinga amin kikhum jat, aphungmin uleh alamkai hou chu: Phungmin Lamkai Amijat Reuben Shedeur chapa Elizur 46,500 
Simeon Zurishaddai chapa Shelumiel 59,300 
Gad Deuel chapa Eliasaph 45,650 
Reuben phung lamkaina noija galsat thei aloi loiya kikhomdoh jat chu agom a mihem 151,450 ahi. Israel te ahon a achenna jouseuva hiche phung thum ho hi anina a pang dingu ahi. 
Chuleh houponbuh Levi ten aput uchu ngahmun lailung apat a hung kipudoh ding ahi. Phung jouse ngahmun ana kison bang cheh u sukeh louhel a ama ama phung jem noicheh a kitol diu ahi. 
Kikhop khomna ponbuh lhumlam a ngahmun sem ding phungho chu Ephraim, Manasseh leh Benjamin hidiu ahin, aphungjem u noi cheh a um cheh ding ahiuve. Anoiya ho hi amin kikhum galsat ding jat, aphungmin uleh alamkai hou chu: Phungmin Lamkai Amijat Ephraim Ammihud chapa Elishama 40,500 
Manasseh Pedahzur chapa Gamaliel 32,200 
Benjamin Gideoni chapa Abidan 35,400 
Ephriam phung lamkaina noija galsat thei aloi loiya kikhomdoh jat chu agom a mihem 108,100 ahi. Israel te ahon a achenna jouseuva hiche phung thum ho hi athumna a pang ding u ahi. 
Kikhop khomna ponbuh sahlama ngahmun semding phungho chu Dan, Asher leh Naphtali hidiu ahin, aphungjem u noi cheh a um cheh ding ahiuve. Anoiya ho hi amin kikhum galsat ding jat, aphungmin uleh alamkai hou chu: Phungmin Lamkai Amijat Dan Ammishaddai chapa Ahiezer 62,700 
Asher Ocran chapa Pagiel 41,500 
Naphtali Enan chapa Ahira 53,400 
Dan phung lamkaina noija galsat thei aloi loiya kikhomdoh jat chu agom a mihem 157,600 ahi. Israel te ahon a achenna jouseuva aphungjem noi cheh uva hiche phung thum ho hi anukhahpen a pang dingu ahi. 
Agomlam tah in, aloi loiya um Israel te, a insung cheh uva kon kijihlut ho chu abonchauva gomsoh in mihem 603,550 alhing uve. 
Pakai thupeh bang chun Levi te vang chu amin u akisim tha tapon ahi. 
Hiti chun Pakaijin Mose ana thupeh bang bang chun Israel ten ima jouse abolsohkei tauvin ahi. Pakai thupeh jui in ama ama insung leh phung jem noi cheh a chun akijotji un chuleh ngahmun jong asemji tauve.
