Minbu.
3.
Hiche hi Sinai molchunga Pakaijin Mose ahoupi lai a ana kijihlut Aaron le Mose insung khanggui thu chu ahi. 
Aaron chapa te ho min chu Nadab (atah pen), Abihu, Elezear chule Ithamar ahiuve. 
Hiche Aaron chapa te ho chengse hin thempu kintheng atoh theina ding uva thao anu uva chule atumbeh a kilhandoh ahiuve. 
Ahinlah Nadab le Abihu chu Pathen umpet a Sinai gamthip a maicham asemlai uva Pathen thupeh toh kikal a meikong dihlou ahal lhon jeh chun, ani lhon'in hiche mun'a chun athilhon tan ahi. Chapa dang anei tahlou jeh in kintheng natong ding chu adalhah lhon Eleazer le Ithamar chutoh apa lhon Aaron chubou ahi tauve. 
Hichun Pakaijin Mose henga thu aseijin, 
Levi phung le changho chu koukhom inlang, Aaron kintheng natohga kithopi dingin gapuiyin. 
Amaho chu Aaron leh mipi jenlea pang dingu, Houbuh leh akimvel a kintheng natonga pang ding ahiuve. 
Levi te jousen hiche ponbuh theng chu avettup dingu Israel mipite thalhenga ponbuh maicham a chu kintheng na atoh diu ahi. 
Levi chilhahho jouse chu Aaron leh achapa te vaihomna noiya umsah in. Amaho chu Israel mipi ho jouse lah a konna Aaron le achapa te jenle ding leh kithopi dinga kipedoh ah ahitauvin ahi. 
Aaron le achapa te ho jouse chun thempu kintheng na atoh ding uvin seipeh in. Koi hijongleh nganse chang louva muntheng ganailut ho chu thitei tei diu ahi. 
Chuin Pakaijin Mose kom'a aseijin, 
“Ven, Levi te hi Israel japi lah a konna Israel chapa te apengmasa jouse thalhenga natong dinga kalhendoh ahitai. Levi te ho jouse keiya ahi. 
Ajeh chu chapa tahpen jouse keima a ahi. Egypt chapa te lah a apeng masa jouse kana suhgam nikho apatna keima Israel chapa tahpen jouse hi kei dia atumbeh a ka lhendohsa ahin, mihem a hihen gancha a hijongleh amaho jouse chu keiya ahiuve; Keima Pakai chu kahi.” 
Pakaijin Sinai molchunga chun Mose hengah thu aseikit in, hiti hin ati, 
“Levi te chu ama ama insung leh phungkhai lhah in amin u jihlut cheh in. Pasal jouse lhakhat a pat chunglam se jihlut in.” 
Hichun Mose chun Pakai thupeh bang chun minho chu ajihlut tan ahi. 
Levi in chapa thum aneijin, amaho chu Gershon, Kohath chule Merari ahiuve. 
Gershon a konna phungguiho chu ama chilhah te lah a kon a mini min, Libni leh Shimei a kiminsah ahiuve. 
Kohath akon a phungguiho chu ama chilhah a kon miliho- Amran, Izhar, Hebron chule Uzzeil a kiminsah ahiuve. 
Merari a konna phungguiho chu ama chilhah te lah a konna mini min, Mahli le Mushi a kiminsah ahiuve. 
Gershon chilhah te chu Libni le Shimei phunggui akon ahiuvin ahi. 
Gershon te phunggui a kon a Pasal kum khat a pat chunglam chu abonchauvin mi 7,500 aum uve. 
Amaho chu Houbuh akon a lhumlam lang chu angahmun kisahna ding uva kipedoh ahiuve. 
Gershonte phungho chunga lamkaiya ana pang chu Lael chapa Eliasaph ahi. 
Hiche phung teni chunga mopohna a um chu ahileh Houbuh avetsui diu, ponbuhtheng leh achunga kidal akhuna chengse ahin, maicham ponbuh lutna phunga kikhai pondal ahin, 
maicham ponbuh leh maicham phung kimvel a ki-dah pondal chengse ahin, akhaoho ahin, chuleh manchah a kimangcha ding jouse jaona ahiye. 
Kohath chilhah te chu Amram, Izhar, Hebron chule Uzziel phung a kon ahiuve. 
Gershonte phunggui a konna pasal kum khat apat chunglam chu abonchauvin mi 8,600 aum uvin, amaho chu muntheng ventup leh vettupna mopoh kipe ahiuvin, 
chuleh angahmun sahna ding uva Houbuh a konna lhanglam kipe ahiuve. 
Kohath phungho chunga lamkai a ana pang chu Uzzeil chapa Elizaphan ahi. 
Hiche phung li ho hin Pathen Thingkhong, dokhang, thaomei khom, maicham phungho, Houbuh sunga kimang thil le lo chom chomho, asung dan pondal, chuleh amaho manchah dinga angaicha jouse chunga mopohna anei dingu ahi. 
Thempupa Aaron chapa Eleazer chu, Levi te ho jouse chunga vaihom pipu anahin, Houbuh sunga nakitong jouse vetup dinga deichom a mopohna kipe ahi 
Merari chilhah te chu Mushi le Mahli phunga kon ahiuve. 
Merari phunggui a konna pasal kum khat apat chunglam chu abonchauvin mi 6,200 aum uve. 
Amaho chu Houbuh akon a sahlam lang chu ngahmun akisahna ding uva kipedoh ahiuve. Merari phungho chunga lamkaiya ana pang chu Abihail chapa Zuriel ahi. 
Hiche phung teni hin Houbuh thingkol ho, ingolvaiho, akhumho, akhomdouho leh amaho man ding manchah ho jouse avettup diu ahi. 
Chuleh Houbuh kimvel a palkhomho leh athehho ahin, akikhainaho le khaoho jouse jong avettup dingu ahi. 
Maicham ponbuh mai lhang, solam niso lamkot lang sese chu Israel mipi ho thalhenga Houbuh munthenga natohna dinga mopohna nunungpen kipe, Mose le Aaron chapa te ponbuh kisahna dinga ana kikoitup a ahi. Koi hijeng jongleh Thempu le Levi te tilou Houbuh muntheng maicham lang hinnai lailut tapou chu tha jeng ding ahi. 
Mose le Aaron chun Pakai thupeh banga Levi te phunghoa kon'in pasal lhakhatna pat chunglam se chu asim lhon'in ahileh mi 22,000 alhing uvin ahi. 
Hichun Pakaijin Mose jah a aseijin, “Tun nangman Israel te chapa te apeng masa lhakhat lhingtasa apat chunglam jouse amin u jihlut in. 
Keima Pakai kahin, keima dinga Levi te chu Israel chapa tahpenho thalheng dinga kikoitup teitei ding ahi. Chuleh Israel te pumpi a gancha apengdoh masa ho khel dinga Levi te gancha ho chu kikoitup teitei ding ahi. 
Hiti chun Mose’n Pathen thupeh bangtah in Israel chapa te lah a atahpen ho chu asimtan ahi. 
Lhakhat a lhingsa leh achunglam chapa tahpenho chu 22,273 anahiuve. 
Hichun Pakaijin Mose jah a aseijin, 
“Levi te chu Israel chapa te lah a atahpen ho thalheng chun la in. Chuleh Israel te gancha apeng masa ho khel dinga Levi te gancha ho chu nalah ding ahi. Keima Pakai kahi, Levi te chu keiya ahi bouve. 
Levi te sang'in Israel chapa te apeng masa mi 273 in atamjo uvin ahi. Amaho lhatdoh a aum theina dingun, 
mikhat cheh a dinga Sumgoi a Shekel nga nalahkhom ding, hiche shekel chu muntheng laiya shekel ho toh agihdan nakibahsah ding, gerah somnia shekel khat dungjuiya nate ding ahi. 
Chapa pengmasa avahdoh ho lhatdohna dingin hiche sum chu Aaron le achapa te petan.” 
Hiti chun Levi te jat sanga hung tamjo Israel chapa tahpen ho lhatdoh a aumna ding uvin Mose’n in sum ho chu achom khom in ahi. 
Aman Israel chapa te lah a apengmasa ho thalheng chun sum pum 1,365 achomdoh in ahi. Hiche ho khat cheh hi muntheng a Shekel khat cheh toh agihdan kibang cheh ahiuve. 
Chuin Pathen'in thu apeh bang bang chun, akilhatdoh theina diuvin Mose in hiche sumho chu Aaron le achapa te apetan ahi.
