Hichun Pakaijin Mose le Aaron henga thu aseijin, 
Levi phung sunga konna Kohath insung le chilhah te chu amin hou na jihlut lhon ding ahi. 
Amaho lah a Houbuh sunglam maicham phunga natong thei dinga lhinna nei, kum somthum apat kum-somnga sungse ho chu abonchan jihlut lhon'in. 
“Kohath hon Houbuh sunga anatoh dingu chu manchah thil athenga umho jouse vettupna chung chang chu ahi. 
Ama hon ngahmun achon ding teng ule Houbuh maicham sungnung pondal chu Aaron le achaten alahlhah diu Kitepna Thingkhong khuna a anei dingu ahi. 
Chuteng hiche pondal chu kelcha vun a kisuhtah a atom dingu, hiche ho jouse chu pondum khat seh a apha chansoh dingu ahi. Che ho jouse kichai teng Thingkhong chu aputna thinglhon toh asuhto dingu ahi. 
“Abankit a changlhah umpi jing ahiu melchihna a, hiche changlhah kikoina dokhang chunga pondum khat aphajal dingu ahi. Chuleh hiche pon kipha chunga chu lhengkongho, gimnamtui halna lhengho, khonho, twi lhitthengna, chuleh deisahtah a um jing jeng changlhah ho kikoi ding ahi. 
Ama hon hiche ho jouse chu ponsan a akhu diu, chutengleh kelchavun thengsel'a hiche ponsan chu kikhuson kit diu ahi. Chujou teng dokhang chu aputna ding thinglhon toh asuhto dingu ahi. 
Hiche banjom a chu ama hon pondum-eng a chu thaomei khum chu ahin khu a, thaomei semtohna, achoina kong, chuleh Olive thao kikoina khon jong chu akhu khum dingu ahi. 
Chule ama hon pondum a chu thaomei khom leh athil manchah ho jouse chu kelchavun thengsel'a atom khum uva chuleh hiche alom chu akiputna kolchahna chunga chu akoi dingu ahi. 
“Chuban leh pondum khat a sana gimnamtui maicham kilhaina chunga aphajal ding u, kelchavun thengsel'a hiche pon chu akhu diu ahi. Chuteng aputna ding thinglhon toh maicham chu asuhto dingu ahi. 
Maicham kin a dia manchah akigong tuplou laise chu pondum khat a tom ding, kelchavun thengsel khat a hiche pon chu kitomson ding, chuteng aputna kolchah a chu kikoi ding ahi. 
“Ama hon kilhaina dinga maicham phunga konna vutvam ho chu alodoh diu, chule ponsandup a maicham phung chu atom diu ahi. 
Maicham thilkeu ho jouse - meihalna kong, thihkhe, leilodohna longkhol, kisilna kongho, chuleh thilkoina ho jouse - pon chunga chu kikoi a, chuleh kelchavun thengsel chu hiche ho chunga chu kiphajal a kikhu ding ahi. Chuteng aputna thingjol chutoh asuhto dingu ahi. 
Aaron le achapa ten munthengho ahin, athenga um thil le lo ho ahin, ahintom jousoh teng ule ngahmun a konna kitol thei diu ahitai. Kohath chapa ten hiche hi lhunna ding mun chan ahin put diu ahi. Ahivanga thil theng ho chu asuhkhah louhel dingu ahi, achuti louleh thilo ding ahiuve. Hichengse hi Kohath chapa ten Houbuh a konna aput diu ho chu ahi. 
Aaron chapa Eleazer thempupa mopohna chu thaomei khom a thaotwi ho, gimnamtui ho, niseh a kitoh lhosoh thilto ho, kinu ding thaotwi ho avettup jing ding ahi. Houbuh pumpi leh asunga thil ijakai, muntheng le munthenga kimangcha jouse chu Eleazar vettupna noi a um ding ahi. 
Pakaijin Mose le Aaron henga thu aseijin, 
“Levi te phunga kon'in Kohath chilhah ho mahthah sah hih in. 
Thil thengpen ho ahin nailut teng athi louna diuva nangin hiche hi nabolpeh ding ahi. Aaron le achapa ten aki lhonlutpi jing diu chule amichang cheh a ato ding abol dingu chu napeh cheh ding ahi. 
Kohath chilhah te chu phat chomcha hijongleh muntheng laiya thil umho vedinga lutlouhel dingu ahiuve, achuti louleh thi tei tei diu ahi.” 
Chuin Pakaijin Mose henga ahin aseijin, 
Levi phung sunga konna Kohath insung le chilhah te chu aminhou na jihlut ding ahi. 
Amaho lah a Houbuh sunglam maicham phunga natong thei dinga lhinna nei, kum somthum apat kum-somnga sungse ho chu abonchan jihlut in. 
Gershon chilhah te chu japi natohna a leh thilgih dom ding ho chunga mopo ding ahiuve. 
Ama hon Houbuh pondal ho jouse apoh diu, Houbuh jong aki tomna pon puma, kelcha vuntheng sel a akitomna pum a, chuleh Houbuh lutna kotphunga pondal toh ahin pohtha diu ahi. 
Houbuh polama ahin amalam ahin Houbuh leh maicham phung vel'a pondal ho jouse jong, khaoho ahin, chuleh amaho natoh toh kisaiya amanchah hou jouse ahi. Hiche ho jouse chunga hi Gershon chilhah ten avettup diuva mopohna chang ahiuve 
Aaron le achapa te mopohna Gershon ten amopohna hou jouse, thil dop le ding hihen, natohna dang dang tohna ding hijongleh Aaron leh achapa ten akomu peh jing diu ahi. Gershon chilhah ten thilgih putna dinga jong ngansena apeh dingu ahi. 
Hichengse hi Gershon chilhah ten Houbuh sunga atoh diu kipe chu ahin, Ama ho hi Aaron thempupa chapa Ithamar vetsuina noiya um diu ahi. 
Levi phung sunga konna Kohath insung le chilhah te chu amin hou na jihlut ding ahi. 
Amaho chapa te lah a Houbuh sunglam maicham phunga natong thei dinga lhinna nei, kum somthum apat kum-somnga sungse ho chu abonchan jihlut in. 
Amaho kin chu Houbuh thil ho dop le ding ho ahi. Houbuh kolchah thingho, agol kamho, khom ho, chuleh ato theh ho adop diu ahi. 
chukit jongleh houbuh kimvel a pal khomho leh ato theh ho, akikhainaho le khaoho chuleh amaho natoh toh kisaiya amanchah ho jouseu jong avettup dingu ahi. 
Hichengse hi Merari chilhah ten Houbuh sunga atoh diu kipe chu ahin, amaho hi Aaron thempupa chapa Ithamar vetsuina noiya um diu ahi. 
Hichun Mose, Aaron chule phung lamkai hon Kohath insung le chilhah te chu amin hou ana simun ana jihlut tauvin ahi. 
Hiche min kijihna chun Houbuh sunglam maicham phunga natong thei dinga lhinna nei, kum somthum apat kum-somnga chan ho chu abonchan jihlut cheh ahiuvin, 
chule amaho chu agom a mi 2,750 ahiuve. 
Hicheng hi Kohath chilhah ho lah a Houbuh a natong thei cheh dinga lhinna nei ho ahiuve. Pathen'in Mose athupeh bangin Mose le Aaron in jong ajihdoh lhon'in ahi. 
Gershon chilhah te jong chu hitobang ma chun aphung achang dungjuijin aki sundoh cheh uvin ahi. 
Hiche min kijihna chun Houbuh sunglam maicham phunga natong thei dinga lhinna nei, kum somthum apat kum-somnga chan ho chu abonchan jihlut cheh ahiuvin, 
chule amaho agom a mi 2,630 ahiuve.

chule amaho agom a mi 3,200 ahiuve. 
Hicheng hi Merari chilhah ho lah a Houbuh a natong thei cheh dinga lhinna neiho ahiuve. Pathen'in Mose athupeh bangin Mose le Aaron in jong ajihdoh lhon'in ahi. 
Hichun Mose le Aaron chule Israel phung lamkai hon Levi te chu a insung le ama phung dungjui cheh in ana jihlut tauvin ahi. 
Kum somthum apat somnga chan Houbuh a natong thei leh thil ho po le theija pang thei ding ho chu 
agom a mi 8,580 ahi. 
Pathen'in Mose thu apeh dungjuiya aminhou jouse jihlut a aum phat chun, pasal khat cheh chu atoh ding dol cheh uleh apoh ding dol cheh u akipe cheh tauvin ahi. Hiti chun Pathen'in Mose thu apeh bangtah in min kijihlutna natoh chu akichai tai.
