Minbu.
6.
Chuin Pakaijin Mose henga hiti hin aseyin, 
“Israel mite chu hiche hilchahna ho hi pen. Mipiho lah a koi hijongleh numei hihen pasal hijongleh atumbeh a Nazir te kitepna a kitem aum a, Pakaiya dia atumbeh a ama leh ama kipedoh aum leh, 
amaho chun khamnathei juho leh lengpithei twiho don angah dingu ahi. Hitobang miho chun lengpi theitwi ahilouleh adang dang khamnathei a kisem juthuh kilhahna ho chu amanlou dingu ahi. Lengpiga apat a twi kilhi thah jeng jong adonlou ding, lengpi theiga ahin lengpi theiga gopsaho jeng jong aneh uva adonlou dingu ahi. 
Amaho chu Nazir te kitepna kol sunga aum laise uva lengpigui a konna kisemdoh ho chu aneh diu le adondiu phal ahipoi - Lengpi thei ahoh le amu jeng jong anehlou dingu ahi. 
“Nazir te tia kitepna kol sunga aum sungse uva chu asam u jong ache louhel diu ahi. Akitep nau suh bulhit a aum phat sungse a chu asam u akihoisao dingu ahi. 
Chule hiche kitepna kol sunga aum sung sese uva hi thilong umna avel lah a che louding ahiuve. 
Hiche mithi long chu apenna mong mong pa hihen, anu hihen, asopipa ahilouleh asopinu hijongleh amaho chu kisuboh louding ahiuve, ajeh chu alu uva asam uchu Pathen a dinga kitum lhandoh ahinau lim a um ahi. 
Pakai kintheng natong dinga lhenchom a aum chan uva hiche kigahna ho hi ajui jing diu ahi. 
Itiham khat a ama angsunga mikhat chu alhuh a athi jeng tah a ahileh asam kithenso chu kisuboh ahitai. Nikho sagi kho ngai dingu, asam u akivotol dingu ahi. Chuteng amaho chu akisuhbohna uva konna athenga umkit a ahi tauve. 
Niget lhinni teng vakhu kopkhat ahilouleh in vapalnou ni Houbuh maicham lutna dontah a thempu kom'a ahin choi dingu ahi. 
Thempu chun vacha teni lah a khat chu chonset kilhaina a atodoh ding, khatpen chu pumgo maicham adia atodoh ding ahi. Hiti dol a chu thempu pan mithi tahsa suhkhah jeh a themmo channa chu asuhtheng ding ahitai. Ama hon akitep nau chu aphudet kit uva, asam u chu akihoisao kit diu ahitai. 
Amaho aboh a aum masang uva kitepna nikho ajo cheng uchu kisimlou a ahitai. Amaho chu athahbeh a Pakai angsung a Nazir ahinau chu alhumkei a dinga kilhandoh kit dingu chuleh ama cheh in themmo kilhaina thilto dinga kumkhat lhingsa kelngoi achal khat ahin kai ding ahi. 
“Hiche hi Nazir te chonna dan chu ahi. Nazir adia akilhe khen nau phat nunung lam teng, Houbuh kotphunga chu che diu 
chule Pakai anga athilto hou alhantheng diu ahi. Hichu kumkhat lhingsa nolna beihel kelngoichal khat pumgo thilto a ding, kumkhat lhingsa nolna beihel kelngoi anu khat chonset kilhaina ding chuleh nolnabei kelngoi achal lhingset khat hichu chamna maicham ding, 
cholsolou changlhah pocha khat - hichu akilhengdoh changbong Olive thao toh kimehhel chule changlhah neocha cha a paset a kisem a olive thao kichapkhum- chuleh peh dinga umsa lhosoh thilto leh atwi a kilhaina thilto ho ahiuve. 
Hiche thilto ho hi Thempu in Pakai angsunga pehdohna anei ding ahi. Amasa penna chonset kilhaina thilto leh pumgo maicham thilto ahin, 
aban'a chamna maicham dinga kelngoichal lhingsel chu ahin, chuleh cholsolou changlhah pocha khat ahi. Peh dinga umsa lhosoh thilto leh atwi a kilhaina thilto ho jong chu Thempu in Pakai kom'a pehdohna anei ding ahi. 
“Chuteng Nazir te ho chun Houbuh lutna kotphunga chu asam u akivolhah diu ahi. Chamna maicham kilhaina noiya mei lah a chu ana kikatdohsa asam u chu akoilut diu ahi. 
Nazir hon asam u akivotol jouteng uleh, thempun amaho cheh dinga akihulminsa kelngoi chal dah chu alah ding, Pocha sunga um cholsolou changlhah leh changlhah neocha cha a paset a kisem chu alah ding ahi. Hichengse chu aman Nazirpa khut tenia akoi ding ahi. 
Thempun hiche ho chu adopsanga kilhanna thilpeh tumbeh Pakai anga abol ding ahi. Thempupa dinga atheng hung kitohdoh ho chu ahileh, atumbeh a hung kitoh a-omlhang leh thengtah a Pakai anga akitohdoh leh kidomsang amal jaona cheng chu hi ding ahi. Hiche kintheng hi ahung kichai teng Nazirpa chun lengpitwi adon thei ding ahitai. 
Hicheng hi kilhaina Pathen kom hinlhut dinga kitepna nei Nazir te chonna dan chu ahi. Hiche chungchon a kilhaina apehjou chan uva jong apeh thei u ahi. Chuleh amaho chu Nazir tia lhendoh chom a aum tengu leh khopho tah a hitia akitepna jouseu chu abol dingu ahi. 
Chuin Pakaijin Mose henga aseijin, 
“Aaron le achaten Israel te mipi jouse chu atumbeh a phatthei aboh ding uvin seipeh in: 
Pathen'in phatthei naboh uhenlang naveng jing uhen. 
Pathen'in lhaisel in nave jing uhenlang nachunga lung lhaisel jing tahen. 
Pathen mitvet in lunglhaina changin chule chamna nape hen. 
Aaron le achapa ten keimin a Israel te phatthei aboh tengu leh keiman jong amaho chu phatthei kaboh ding ahi.
