Minbu.
17.
Chuin Pakaijin Mose henga hiti hin aseijin, 
“Israel mipi henga seijin, ama hon tenggol som le ni hin kisem uhen, insung leh aphungkhai cheh uvin khat cheh kisem uhenlang, aphung lamkai min cheh hin kisut sohkei uhen. 
Phung lamkai khat min cheh chu kijihlut ding ahijeh in Aaron min chu Levi te phung lamkai ding chun sunin. 
Hiche tenggol ho hi Houbuh sunga Kitepna songpheng ho kikoina Thingkhong masanga ikimutona mun a chun koijin. 
Keiman kalhen mipa Tenggol chunga chu ajihdon hung um ding ahi. Chuteng na chunga mipi kiphinna le phunchelna chu achaina kahisah ding ahitai. 
Hichun Mose’n Israel mipi chu hilchahna aneijin chule Israel phungsom le ni lamkai ho jouse jong chu Aaron tenggol jong chu ahin choiyun ahi. 
Pakai umpina Houbuh sunga Kitepna achun Mosen tenggol ho chu akoitai. 
Mose chu ajingin Houbuh sung’a chun agalut a ahileh Levi te insung thakhel dia Aaron tenggol chu ana dondoh in, amum aum in, apahlha in, Jonmot ga minsa ana um tai! 
Mose’n tenggol jouse chu Pakai masanga ahin choidoh cheh in, aman hiche ho jouse chu mipi ho chu avetsah sohkeiye. Mijousen ama ama phungkhai tenggol chu akilah cheh uvin ahi. 
Chule Pakaijin Mose jah ah: Aaron tenggol chu Kitepna Thingkhong chung’a chu a-itih a natun jing ding ahi, Hiche chu miphalou ho hilna a pang’a, Pakai douna a akiphin’u ajom ngam tahlouna diu leh a thilhamji nauva kon ahoidohna diuva hettohsah a pang jing ding ahi, ati. 
Chuti chun Mose’n jong Pakaijin ahil bang bang chun aboltan ahi. 
Israel mipi ten Mose kom'a hitin asei uvin: “Ven, keiho pihi amang thah ding bep a um kahi tauve. Keiho athisa kahi tauve, keiho akichai gam ding kahi tauve! 
Koihileh Pakai maicham nailut chan athi, athi chai ding bep kahiu hitam? ati tauve.
