Minbu.
24.
Balaam Israel mite phatthei aboh leh Pathen alunglhai chu ahet phat chun phat dang laiya bang’in doithu ho mangcha din akigong tapoi. Chusangin akiheijin gamthip lam agalvet len, 
hitia Balaam a dadoh a avetpet chun Israel mite ama phung cheh a angahmun u amun ahi. Chutah chun Pathen Lhagao chu achungah ahung chu tan, 
chule hiche hi aseidoh chu ahi: Beor chapa Balaam in asei doh thu chu, kicheh tah a mit a mupa sei ahin, 
Pathen thu kicheh tah a japa'n asei, Hatchungnung a konna gaova mu a, mit koulep a dilsu pa chu: 
Vo Jacob, na ponbuh ho iti hoiya hitam! O Israel, na in ho iti lungset um hitam! 
Vadung panga honlei kibol'a, ka masanga tum thing ho jaodeh bangin akimui. Pakaijin aphudoh thingphung sang ho toh abangin, twipang’a keh Cedar phungho toh abang jeng’e. 
Twibel akon atwiyu hung let lhan tin; achilhah uvin angaichat jouseu anei diu ahi. A lengpau jong Agag sang'in thupi jo intin; alenggam’u jong choisanga um ding ahi. 
Pathen in Eygpt a kon ahin puidoh un; amaho din Ama chu gam bongchal hattah abang'e. Amaho douvah nam jouse Aman avallhum sohtan, agu achang jouseu asuhchip peh in, athalpi hon akap e. 
Sakei bahkai bangin Israel chu akitaulen alumji e; Sakai banga kon ama aga thodoh ngam ding hitam? Vo Israel, nangma phatthei naboh ho chu ahampha uvin, chule nangma na gaosap ho chu gaosap chang ahiuve,” ati. 
Balak lengpa chu Balaam chunga hatah in alunghang tai. Aman lunghang tah in akhut teni abengin chule apengin , “Keiman kamelma te gaosap dinga kakou nahi! Chusangin, nangman thumvei phatthei naboh jotai. 
Tun hiche a kon hin potdoh tan! Na in'ah kile tan! Keiman kipaman tampi peh dia kitem kahin, hinlah Pakaijin na kipaman san ding chu akipel sah ahitai,” ati. 
Balaam in Balak jah ah, “Thupole nahin sol ho jah a kasei chu na geldoh lou hitam? 
Sana le dangka aleng insung dimset eikipe ding hijongleh, keiman Pakai thupeh kalval seina ding tha kaneipoi, Pakaijin eihetsah bou kasei ding ahi tia kaseisa ahi!. 
Tua keima ka mite koma kile ding kahi tai. Ahinlah khonunga Israel ten na mite ipi ahin bolpeh diu chu naseipeh masat inge,” ati. 
Hiche thu hi Balaam in aseije: “Hiche hi Beor chapa Balaam thusei ahin, amit tenia muchenset pa thusei chu, 
a thu hi Pathen thu ho japa a kon, Chungnungpen a kon hetna neipa, mit koulep a dilsu pa chun: 
Ama chu kamui, hinlah hilai hi ahipon chule tuhi ahipoi. Ken kahei, ahivangin ahunglhung ding phat a ahi. Jacob a konin valpa hung soh intin; Israel apat leng tenggol hung peng ding ahi. Hichun Moab mite luchang asuhchip ding, Sheth mipi lugu avohkeh ding ahi. 
Edom toupha a um ding, chule Seir, adoupa chu, jo in um intin, amavang Israel chu galjou in akitol e. 
Jacob a vaipo hung pengdoh intin ama chun Ir hinsah ho asuhmang ding ahi.” 
Chuin Balaam in Amalek mipi lam chu aven hiche thu hi aseijin ahi: Amalek hi nam jouse lah a athupi penin, ahinlah achaina chu manthahna ahi!” 
Chuin aman Ken mite lam aven hiche thu hi aseijin ahi: Na in chu abit nai; na buh chu songpi ho lah a kisa ahi. 
Hinlah Assyria in gal hingin nakai intin Ken mite chu kisumang ding ahi.” 
Balaam in athusei khumkhana in: “Kaden, Pathen lungtina louva koi hingjou ding ham? 
Cyprus twipang ah kong-innei ho hungun tin; Assyria abolgim uva chule Eber chu asuh genthei diu, ahin, ama ni chu, akhonna kheh a kisumang lhon ding ahi,” ati. 
Chuin Balaam in adalhan a inlam'a akile tan, chuleh Balak jong alampia akile tan ahi.
