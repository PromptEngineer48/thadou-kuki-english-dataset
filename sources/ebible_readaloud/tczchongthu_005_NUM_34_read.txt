Minbu.
34.
Chuin Pakaijin Mose heng’a thu aseijin, 
“Hiche hilchahna ho hi Israel te pen: Keiman nagoulo diuva kapeh nau Canaan gam chu nalhun tengu leh, agamgi ho chu hiche ho hi hi ding ahi. 
Lhanglam a gamgi ding chu Edom lhaang chin'a Zin gamthip a hung kilhung pan ding ahi. Lhanglam gamgi chu Twipi Thi solam hi ding ahi. 
Chu a pat lhanglam Aipi Paltan a kilhungsuh Zin langkai hi ding ahi. Alhanglha pen mun chu Kadesh-barnea hi ding, chu apat a kilhung a Hazar-addar, chule Azmon chan'a kilhung ding ahi. 
Azmon apat gamgi chu Egypt vadung langa kihei ding chule akingahna chu Mediterranean Twipi hi ding ahi.” 
“Lhumlam na gamgi diu chu Mediterranean Twipi pang chin hi ding ahi.” 
“Sahlam gamgi ding chu Mediterranean Twipi kipan ding chule Hor Molsang solam chan kilhung ding, 
chu a konna Lebo-Hamath, chule Zedad 
le Ziphron apat Hazar-enan chan kilhung ding ahi. Hiche hi sahlam na gamgi diu chu ahi.” 
“Solam gamgi chu Hazar-enan a kipan ding chule Shepham langa kilhungsuh ding, 
hitia chu Ain solam gamkaiya um Riblah chan geija kilhung lut ding ahi. Chu a konna agamgi chu solam Galilee Dillen pang geiya gamgi kilhung ding, 
Chuban leh gamgi chu Jordan vadung apat kilhungsuh peh ding Twipi Thi chan geiya kilhung lha ding ahi. Hiche ho hi na gamgi diu chu ahi.” 
Chuin Mose’n Israel chate henga hiti hin aseijin, “Hiche gam hi nangho le nangho kah a vang nasan uva nakihop diu nagam uchu ahi. Pakaijin thupeh chu phungko leh phung kehkhat ho kihom diu ahi. 
Reuben phung, Gad phung, chule Manasseh phung kehkhat hon gam kiphalpeh chu anei sohkeiyun 
Jordan Vadung solam lang, Jericho gal apat nisona lam chu ahi.” 
Pakaijin Mose henga hiti hin aseijin, 
Thempu Eleazar leh Nun chapa Joshua chu mipi ho dia gam homtoh ding teni ahi lhon'e. 
Aphung cheh a lamkai khat cheh le atoh ding semtoh lhon'in. 
Hiche ho hi phung ho le lamkai ho min ahi: Phung Lamkai Judah Jephunneh chapa Caleb 
Simeon Ammihud chapa Shemuel 
Benjamin Kislon chapa Elidad 
Dan Jogli chapa Bukki 
Manasseh son of Joseph Ephod chapa Hanniel 
Ephraim son of Joseph Shiphtan chapa Kemuel 
Zebulun Parnach chapa Elizaphan 
Issachar Azzan chapa Paltiel 
Asher Shelomi chapa Ahihud 
Naphtali Ammihud chapa Pedahel 
Amaho hi Canaan gamsung a Israel chaten alo diu gam homtoh ding’a Pakaijin angense ho chu ahiuve,” ati.
