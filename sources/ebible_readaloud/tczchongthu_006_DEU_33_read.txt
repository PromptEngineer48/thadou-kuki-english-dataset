Danbu Nina.
33.
Hiche hi Pathen mipa Mose chun athi masanga Israel mite phatthei abohna ahi. 
“Pakai chu Sinai Mol'a konin ahungin chule Seir Mol'a konin ichung uva kho ahin vahsah e; chule Meribah-kadesh a konin ahungin akhut jetlam'a meikong achoijin ahi. 
Tahbeh in, aman amite angailun; amitheng jouse akhut a aum'e. Amahon akhonung ajuiyun chule athuhilna asang uvin ahi. 
Mose’n Pakai gahna chu eipeuvin, Israel mite dia goulupen ahi. 
Japi lamkai ho ahung kikhop khom uva, Israel phung ho khat a akigop phat uchun Pakai chu Israel leng ahung hitai.” 
Reuben phung chungchang'a Mose thusei: “Reuben phung chu lhomcha hijongleu, hingjing uhenlang thi hih uhen. 
Judah phung chungchang'a Mose thusei: “O Pakai, Judah kana khu ngaijin chule mipi toh thakhat ahina diuvin hinpuijin. Aboinauva akivenjou theina diuvin hatna pen; agal mite a konin vengin!” 
Levi phung chungchang'a Mose thusei: “O Pakai, nasoh natong kitah tah Levi te chu nangman na thiltheng—Thummim le Urim chu napen ahi. Nangman Massah achun amaho na patep un chule Meribah twiya chun amaho toh na kidouvin ahi. 
Levi ten nathu anit uvin chule na kitepna jong avengun ahi. Amahon anu le apateu sangin nang'a din akitahjo uvin chule achate jengu jong ahe tapouvin ahi. 
Amahon na dan ho Jacob ahil'un; na gahna ho Israel apeuvin ahi. Amahon na angsung'ah gimnamtui atohdoh uvin chule maicham'a pumgo thilto atohdoh uvin ahi. 
O Pakai, Levi te natoh hi phatthei boh in chule akhut uva atoh jouseu sanpeh teijin. Agalmi teu atam theipenin deng inlang; agelmi teu jeplhu'n chutileh amaho thoukit louhel diu ahitai.” 
Benjamin phung chungchang'a Mose thusei: “Benjamin mite chu Pakaijin angailun chule ama pong'a bittah in akhosa uve. Aman amaho chu aumkiim jingin chule hahsatna jousea aveng jingin ahi.” 
Joseph phung chungchang'a Mose thusei: “Agamsung hi Pakaijin phatthei boh hen, van ho a kon thilpeh mantam daitwi chule leinoiya kon twijin; 
nisa noiya gathei tamtah keh hen, lhasim leh gasoh tampi sep hen; 
gollui laiya molsang ho a kon muchi phapen pen ho, chule a itih a molnem ho a kon ninglhin nan; 
leiset le aga sepna in aphapen asepdoh a, chule hamboh kong a kilah a chu khohsah tum hi hen. Hiche phattheina ho hi Joseph luchung'a kinga hen, asopite lah a leng chapa lallukhuh kikhuh hen. 
Joseph hin bongchalgol lutna aneijin; aman gam'a bongchal ki apu e. Aman nam dang ho apih ding, leiset kolmong geiya apih ding ahi. Hiche hi Ephriam mipi leh Manasseh mi asang sangho dinga phatthei kabohna ahi. 
Zebulun le Issachar phung chungchang'a Mose thusei: “Zebulun mipi ho chu alamlhahna uva phatthei chang uhen. Issachar mipi ho chu aponbuh sunga phatthei chang uhen. 
Amahon mol lang'a thilto phatecha toh dingin mipiho akou uve. Amahon dillen haosatna chule nelgam'a gou kisel chu aphatchompi uvin ahi.” 
Gad phung chungchang'a Mose thusei: “Koi hileh Gad dinga gam kehpeh a chu nunnom hen! Gad hi khut le luchang khottel dingin sakei bangin apansan ahi. 
Gad mipi hon gampha laitah amaho dingun akilah tauve; lamkai chan ding chu akipe tauvin ahi. Japi lamkai ho akikhop tengu leh, amahon Pakai thutah chu apodoh un chule Israel a dingin achonna ho chu ajabol'un ahi.” 
Dan phung chungchang'a Mose thusei: Dan hi Keipi bahkai nou ahin, Bashan a kon'in ahung kichomdoh jin ahi.”. 
Naptali phung chungchang'a Mose thusei: “Vo Naphtali, nangma deisah tum'a nahaovin chule Pakai phatthei bohna na dimset e; lhumlam le lhanglam hin chenpha'n.” 
Asher phung chungchang'a Mose thusei: “Chapa dangho sangin Asher phatthei boh chang hen; asopi hon ama hin ngaisang uhen; olive thao in akengphang teni hin chup hen. 
Na kelkot kamna chu thih le sum-eng hihen; na nikho jouse chu houina hihen.” 
“Israel Pathen tobang adang aumpoi. Nangma panpi dingin vanho a akitol len, vanthamjol a lalna le loupi nan akitollei. 
Tonsot Pathen hi nakiselna ahin, chule tonsot adia abanjang teni chu nanoiya aum'e. Aman nagal mite na masanga anomangin; 'Sumangun!' tin apengjah e. 
Hijeh a chu bitna a Israel khosa ding, Jacob phattheina chu bitna hiding, ju thah le chang le mim gam'a chu, van daitwi lha ding ahi. 
Vo Israel, iti hamphat a nahi hitam! Pakaijin ahuhdoh mite, nangho bang koi dang um'em? Ama chu nabit nau pho ahin chule na galjo nau chemjam ahi! Nagal miteu chu na masang uva hung kineosah diu, chule nanghon aphahsam'u na lhopha diu ahi!”
