Joshua Lekhabu.
1.
Pakai lhacha Mose athi phatnin, Nun chapa Mose lhacha a pang Joshua kom'a chun Pakaijin thu ahin seijin, 
“Ka lhacha Mose athitai. Hijeh chun nangma kipat inlang hiche Jordan hi galkaijin, nang le hiche mipi ho hin Keiman Israel chate kapehna gam hi jonlut tauvin. 
Mose kom'a kana teppehna bang-banga chu hoilai mun hijongleh na kengphang’un achot phahna muntin chu nangho kapeh ding nahiuve. 
Gamthip gam le Lebanon gam'a vadunglen chan gei, Eupharates vadung, Hit mite gam pumpi chuleh nisa lhumna lang twikhanglen chan gei nagam’u hi ding ahi. 
Na hinkho lhumkei uva na masang uva koimacha ding joulou ding, Mose kana dinpi banga nangho jong kadinpi jing diu, ka dalhah louhel diu chuleh kanungsun louhel ding nahiuve. 
Hat inlang hangin, hiche mite hi peh dinga apu apateu kana teppeh gam chu agoulo diuva na hoppeh ding ahi. 
Hat inlang hang pou-pouvin, Ka lhacha Mose kana peh danthu jouse nanit jouna dingin, hichea kon hin jetlang hihen veilang hijongleh kiheimang hihbeh in, chutileh na chena channa na khantou thei ding ahi. 
Hiche danthubu hi nakam'a kon mangthah louhel ding, amavang asun ajanna nagel joh ding, akijih dungjuiya hi nachon jing diu ahi. Chitile bou na khantou ding chuleh na lolhin tei ding'u ahi. 
Keiman thupeh kahinpeh louva nabol ham? Hat inlang hangin, kicha hihbeh in, tijatna jong neihih in ajeh chu Pakai na Pathen'in na chena chan uva na lhonpi jing dingu ahi,” ati. 
Chujou chun, Joshua’n mipi lamkaiho chu thu apen hitin aseije, 
“Ponbuh sunga khun cheuvin lang mipi ho khu aneh diu akigot toh dingun gaseipeh un, ajeh chu nithum sunga hi Jordan luipi khu na galkai uva Pakai na Pathen'in napeh u gam khu galo tauvin.” 
Chuleh Reuben phung leh Gad phung chuleh Mannasseh phung akehkhat ho kom'a chun Joshua’n hitin aseije, 
“Pakai lhacha Mose’n nakom uva asei chu sumil hih un, 'Pakai na Pathen uvin na kicholdo sah tauvin, chuleh hiche gam hi napeh u ahitai.' 
Najiteu le na chateu, chuleh na gancha teu chu Mose’n napeh nau hiche Jordan solam gamkaiyah hin umsah un, ahinlah nalah uva gal-hat ho leh mihangsan chengse chun gal manchah akipat uva asopihou masanga hi gal lamkaija pang diu, 
Pakaijin nangho gam napeh bang uva amaho jong alodiu gam apeh kahseuva nakithopi diu ahi. Chujou tengleh nahung kileuva Pakai lhacha Mose’n napehnau Jordan solam nagam uva nachen diu ahi. 
Hiti chun ama hon Joshua chu adonbut un, “Ipi hileh nangin nei thupeh jouseu chu kabol diu, chuleh nei sol-sol nauva chu kache jeng diu ahi atiuve”. 
“Keihon Mose thu kana ngai bang- bang uva chu imalam jousea nangma thu jong kangai diu ahi. Pakai na Pathen chun Mose ana umpi bang bangin nang jong naumpi jinghen,” atiuve. 
“Koi hileh nangma thupeh jouse chu nit louva aum'a, nahin dou uva aum pou-pou leh thadoh jeng ding ahi. Hat inlang hangpou pouvin!”
