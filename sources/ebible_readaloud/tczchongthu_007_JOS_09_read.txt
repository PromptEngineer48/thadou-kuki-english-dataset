Joshua.
9.
Tun Jordan vadung lhumlam gam'a leng hon thilsoh ho hi aja tauvin ahi. Hite ho chu Hit mite, Amor mite, Canaan mite, Periz mite, Jebus mite lengho, lhumlam molbul'a chengho leh twikhanglen pang Lebanon mol ho chan geija chengho ahiuve. 
Hiche lengho hin lungkhat tah a athau jekhom'a Joshua leh Israel mite hi kisatpi dingin akiloikhom'un ahi. 
Ahinlah Gibeon miten Joshua’n Jericho leh Ai abolna hi ahet phatnun, 
amaho kihuhdohna dingun lungthim chomkhat agong tauvin ahi. Ama hon Joshua kom'a palai asol uvin, sakhao lui le asesa, jupeng lui le akibehthu ho, sangan chunga aheng’un, 
ama hon von sesa akivonun, ponsesa akisilun, kengchot kibehthu akichotnun chuleh neh dinga akipoh u changlhah chu agopsa le amonsa ahi. 
Amaho Gilgal a Israel te ngahmuna ahunglhun phatnun Joshua kom'a chun, “Keiho nangma toh kichamna thulhuh sem dinga kholgamla tah a konna hung kahiuve” atiuve. 
Israel ten, hiche Hivi mite ho kom'a chun adonbutnun, “Nangho hi anai vella chengmi nahi nahilouvu keihon iti kahet diu ham? Anai vella cheng nahi khah uleh keihon nangho toh kichamna thulhuh kanei theilou diu ahi” atiuve. 
Ama hon, “Keiho hi nasohte kahiuve” tin adonbutnun ahi. Ahin Joshua’n, “Nangho koi nahiuva, hoiya konna hung nahiu ham?”ati. 
Ama hon adonbutnun, “Nasohte hi ka chennau mun gamla tah a konna hung kahiuve. Keihon na Pakai na Pathen'u thanei dan Egypt gam'a anatoh ho kana jauvin ahi. 
Keihon Jordan solam'a Amor leng teni leh Heshbon lengpa Sihon le Ashtaroth a um Bashan lengpa Og chunga anatoh ho kajauvin ahi. 
Hijeh chun ka upa hou le ka mipite jousen eihin thulhah un, 'Khol gamla tah jotna dingin neh le chah kipoh un, Israel mipite chu gakimupi uvin lang hiti hin seijun, “keiho nasohte kahiuve keiho toh kitepna nei neipi uvin, tin gaseijun'” atiuve. 
“Hiche ka changlhah hou hi ka in uva konna kahung kipatdoh uva chu tapkonga konna sapeh a kahinpoh u ahin tuhin agobetnin a-ap tan ahi. 
Hiche jupengho jong hi kahin thal dim uva chu thahleh a ahin, tuhin aluijin a-ehgam tai. Chuleh kavon uleh ka kengkoh u jong hi ka lamsao jotna uva konna luigam'a ahitai” atiuve. 
Hiti chun Israel ten aneh achah u chu ana kholchil’un, ahinlah Pakai dohna ananei pouve. 
Hiche jou chun Joshua in amaho chutoh kinoptona ana neipi in amaho chu ana hinghoi tan ahi, khopi sunga alamkai houvin jong hiche kinoptona chu kihahselnan ana sudetnun ahi. 
Hiche kitepna aneiyu nithum jouvin hiche mite ho hi akomcha uva chenga ahibouvui ti ahedoh tauvin ahi. 
Israel te chu akholtoh dingin acheuvin nithum sungin akhopi houva chun agalhung tauve. Hiche khopiho chu Gibeon, Kephirah, Beeroth, chule Kiriath-Jearim ahiuve. 
Ahinlah Israel ten khopiho chu anokhum tapouvin ahi, ajeh chu Israel lamkai ten, Pakai Israel Pathen min panna kitepna ana neiju ahitai. Israel mipite chu, alamkai houvin chutobang kitepna chu anabol jeh un achung uvah ana phunun ahi. 
Ahinlah alamkai houvin ana donbutnun “Eihon Pakai Israel Pathen minna kihahselna inanei nau ahitah jeh a amaho chu itohkhah theilou diu ahitai. 
Eihon amaho chu ihinghoi diu ahitai, ajeh chu eihon ikitepnau isuhkeh uleh Pathen lunghanna ichung uva chuding ahi. 
Amaho chu ihinghoi diu ahitai” atiuve. Hiti chun ama hon Israel lamkai ten athupeh bangun akhopi sung uva twikhai ding leh thingpo dingin amangcha tauvin ahi. 
Joshua’n Gibeon miho chu akou khomin hitin aseije, “Nanghon ipi dinga nei joulhep uham? Nangho hila kalah uva chenga nahi bouvun, ipi dinga gamla tah a cheng kahiuve natiu ham? 
Nangho hi sapset chang nahi tauvin, hijeh a chu tua pat a nangho hi ka Pathen'u in a dinga thingchom le twikhaija napan jeng diu ahitai,” atipeh in ahi. 
Ama hon adonbutnun, “Pakai na Pathen uvin Mose ana thupeh dungjuiya hiche gam hi nangho napeh uva chule agamsung mite jouse hi nathagam sohkei diu ahi ti nasohte keihon kana jauvin ahi. Hijeh chun keihon nangho hi kana kicha lheh jengun ahileh hitia hi kana bol'u ahi,” atiuve. 
“Tun keiho hi nangho khutna um kahi tauve, hijeh chun nadei dan danin neibol tauvin,” atiuve. 
Hiti chun, Israel ten amaho atha diu chu Joshua’n ana janom tapon ahi. 
Ahin hiche nikho chun aman Gibeon mite chu Israel mipite le Pathen'in adeina mun-mun a maicham semna ding twikhai le thingchom'in ana pansah tauvin ahi. Hiche hi tuni geija ahintoh jingu ahi.
