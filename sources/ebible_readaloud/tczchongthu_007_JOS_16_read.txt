Joshua.
16.
Joseph chilhah ho chan ding kipeho chu Jericho kom'a Jordan vadunga kon'in ahung kilhungin, Jericho twinah solam, gamthip gam hoipen Bethel thinglhanga alutnin ahi. 
Bethel a kon'in Ataroth apal galkaijin Arki mite gam'a alutnin ahi. 
Hichea kon chun lhumlam'a akum suh in Japhlet mite gam'a alutnin Beth-horon chan geijin alutnin, chea kon chun Gezer lah alutnin Mediterranean twikhanglen'a alut galkaijin ahi. 
Hiche hi Joseph chate Manasseh leh Ephraim insung mite gamlo dinga kipe ahi. 
Noija ho hi Ephraim phung abahjam ho jouse achan diuva kipe ahi. Amaho gamlo ding solam gamgi chu Ataroth-addar a kipan ahi. Hichea kon chun Beth-horon sah langa alutnin, 
chujouvin Mediterranian twikhanglen'ah alutnin ahi. Micmethath sahlanga kon'in, gamgi chu ahung kon'in Taanath-shiloh ahopan solam Janoah ajonin ahi. 
Janoah a kon'in lhanglang ahin jonin Ataroth le Naarah a alutnin Jericho hopan Jordan vadunga atangin ahi. 
Tappuah a patnin gamgi chu lhumlam a akilhungin Kanah vacha a kon'in Mediterranian twikhanglen na alutnin ahi. Hiche hi Ephraim phung bahkai jouse gamlo ding chu ahi. 
Hiche banna chun khopi phabep le avel'a khoho Manasseh phung kehkhat chan dinga ana kipe jong chu Ephraim phung dingin akikoipeh tan ahi. 
Ama hon Gezer a kon'in Canaan mite chu ana nodoh jou pouvin ahinlah Gezer mite chu tuni chan geijin Ephraim mite soh in apang tauve.
