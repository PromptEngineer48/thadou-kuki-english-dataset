Joshua.
18.
Tunvang gamsung chu Israel te khut noiya uma ahitai, Israel mite chu abonchauvin Shiloh a akikhomun hichea chun kikhopna ponbuhkhat atungdohn tauve. 
Ahinlah hichea chun phung sagi ho chu alodiu chennagam kipeloulai aum nalaijin ahi. 
Hichun Joshua’n amaho chu adongin, “Napu napa teu Pakai napu napa te Pathen'in nalo diuva napeh u gam nachan diu chu itih chan gei nanga diu ham?” ati. 
“Phungkhat a mithum cheh hinlhengdoh unlang keiman amaho chu gam vetoh dingin gahsol intin, amaho cheuvin tin amaho cheh in alo diu gam chu hinvetoh untin keikom'a thu ahin lhut kit diu ahi. 
Judah ten achannu lhanglam gamleh Joseph in achennao sahlang gam panglouva ama hon agamvet uchu hop sagi’a ahopdiu ahi. 
Hitia chu ama hon agamvet u ajihlutnao chu keikom'a ahin choilutdiu, aphungdungjuiya achondiu akipehtheina dinga keiman Pakai I-Pathen'u angsung’a vang theng kasanding ahi. 
“Levi te chun gamchan aneiloudiu ahi. Amaho chan ding chu Pathen thempu kinbol chu hi ding ahi. Chuleh Gad, Reuben leh Manasseh phung kehkhat ho chu gam chanbeloudiu ahitai. Ajeh chu Pakai lhacha Mose’n amaho chu Jordan vadung solam gamkaija anapehsa ahitan ama hon gamchan chu akimusao ahitai,” ati. 
Amaho gam gakholtoh ding leh gajihdoh dinga akipatdoh uchun Joshua’n hitin thupeh ananeijin ahi, “Cheuvinlang agamchu gakholtoh unlang kichihtah in hinjihdoh un. Chujou teng kakom'a hungun, huting keiman Shiloh munna Pakai angsung’a vangsan na kanei ding chuteng aphung dungjui a agam chu kahoppehdiu nahiuve” ati. 
Miho chun gampumpi chu agakholtoh dungjuijin ahin jihdoh un khopi ding leh khoneoding agongtoh un hopsagi in ahom'un lekhabua ajihkit un Shiloh ngahmunna Joshua kom'a ahinpo lutnun ahi. 
Chuleh Shiloh achun Joshua'n Pakai angsung’a vangsan’na aneijin hoilai munchu koi phungpen chang dingham ti avetan ahi. 
Vang kivetna-a chan masa chu Benjamin phunga alhungin ahi. Hiche chan hi Judah le Joseph gamchan kikah’a um gam chu ahi. 
Benjamin channa sahlang gamgi chu Jordan vadunga hung kipan ahin, Jericho sahlang juilang ajonnin, hiti chun lhumlama thinglhang gamleh Beth-aven ahin pal galkaijin ahi. 
Hiche apatin gamhi chu Luz (Bethel tina) lhanglama achepeh in hijou chun achesuh in noinung Beth-horon lhanglam thinglhang chunga um Ataroth-addar munna alutnin ahi. 
Gamgi chu ahung kileheijin lhanglang ajonsuh in Beth-horon maingat thinglhang lhumlam gei hina alutnin Kiriath-baal (Keriath-jearim tina) khokhatna atangin ahi. Hiche hi Judah phung khopi khat ahi. Hiche hi lhumlam gamgi chu ahi. 
Lhanglam gamgi chu Kiriath-jearim khopamma kon'in ahung kipanne. Hiche lhumlam gamchinna konhin ahung kilhungin Naphtoah twinah put namun ahinjotpan, 
Ben-Hinnom phaicham pangmol bullah alutnin ahi. Hiche hi Rephaim phaicham sahlang kichaina mun ahi. Hichea konhin Hinnom phaicham'a achesuh peh-in Jebus mite chenna lhanglam suh apaltannin En-Rogel chan gei in achonlut peh in ahi. 
En-Rogel gamgia kon'in sahlang jotna lammah alutnin En-Shemesh ahinjonin Geliloth (Adummin langa kaltouna toh kigalsai ahitai) chunga alut jellin ahi. Hichea konhin Bohan (Reuben chapa min) songphung geijin achesuh in ahi. 
Hichea konhin Jordan phaicham hin galdotna sahlang gamkai langa achepeh in ahi. Hiti chun gamgi chu phaicham lamma achesuh in ahi. 
Beth-hoglah sahlang lhang apal galkaijin Chitwi-len sahlang pangah alongin ahi. Hiche hi Jordan vadung lhang langa achaina ahitai. Hiche lhanglang gamgi chu ahitai. 
Solam gamgi chu Jordan vadung ahitai. Hiche hi Benjamin phung bahkai ho jouse gamlo dinga kipe gamgi chu ahi. 
Hiche ho hi Benjamin phung sunga abahkai ho kipe khopi chu ahi. Beth-hoglah, Emek-keziz, 
Beth-arabah, Zemaraim, Bethel, 
Avvim, Parah, Ophrah, 
Kephar-ammoni, Ophni, and Geba— khopi som le ni chule  vella khoho ahiuve 
Chujongleh Gibeon, Ramah, Beeroth, 
Mizpeh, Kephirah, Mozah, 
Rekem, Irpeel, Taralah, 
Zela, Haeleph, Jebus (hichu Jerusalem), Gibeah, and Kiriath-jearim — khopi somleli chule  avel'a khoho ahiuve. Hicheng hi Benjamin phungmite le abahkai ho lodia kipe gam ho chu ahi.
