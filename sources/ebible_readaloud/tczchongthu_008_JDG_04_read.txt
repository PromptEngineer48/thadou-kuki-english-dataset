Thutan Vaihom Ho.
4.
Ehud thi nungin, Israel ten Pakai mitmun thilse jeng abolkit tauvin ahi. 
Hijeh chun Pakaijin Canaan mi Hajor lengpa Jabin khut ah amaho chu apelut in, ama sepai lamkai chu Harosheth-haggoyim mi Sisera ahi. 
Siserah in thih sakol kangtalai 900 aneijin, kum somni sungin Israel te ana sugenthei lheh jengin, hijeh chun Israel te Pakai kom'a panpi ngaiyin ana kap tauvin ahi. 
Hiche phatlai sunga chu Lappidoth jinu Deborah hi themgao ahin chule Israel te chunga thu anatan a vai ana hom ahi. 
Themgaonu chu Ephraim thinglhang gam Ramah le Bethel kikah a tumphung lim noiya ana touji ahin, hiche mun'a chun Israel te ana che jiuvin, amanun achungthu u ana tanpeh jiu ahi. 
Nikhat Deborah chun, Naphtali gama Kedesh khopia cheng Abinoam chapa Barak aga kousah in, ama kom'a chun hitin ana seijin, “Hiche hi Israel Pathen'in na thupeh ahi, nangman Naphtali leh Zebulun phunga kon'in galsat mi 10,000 Tabor Mol chunga hin koukhom in. 
Chutengleh keiman Jabin kiti lamkaipa le athih sakol kangtalai ho, chule agal mite chengse khu Kishon vadunga ka koudoh ding; chuteng amaho chunga galjona nangma kapeh ding ahi,” ati. 
Barak in jong adonbut in “Nangman nei kilhonpi ding leh che nange,” ati. 
Hichun Deborah in adonbut in, “Aphalheh jenge keiman kalhonpi ding nahi, ahinlah tuchung kalsonna a hi nangin minphatna namu louding ahi, ajeh chu Sisera chunga Pakaijin galjona eipeh diu hi numei khutna konna bou hi ding ahi”, ati. Hiti chun Deborah in Barak chu Kadesh geiyin ahin kilhon lutpi tai. 
Kadesh a chun, Barak in Zebulun leh Naphtali phunga kon'in galsat mi sangsom akou khom in ama hon ahin juiyun, Deborah injong akilhonpin ahi. 
Hikom'a chun Mose nupapa Hobab chilhah holah a Ken mi Heber chun ama phung lah a kon'in apotdoh in, achombeh in Kedesh kom Zaanannim gangpi phung kom'a chun aponbuh ana songin ahi. 
Sisera jah a chu Abinoam chapa Barak chu Tabor Mol chunga akaltoutai, tia aseiphat uvin, 
aman thih sakol kangtalai 900 leh a sepai jouse akoudoh in Harosheth-haggoyim ana kon'in Kishon vadung geiya ahung tou un ahi. 
Hichun Deborah in Barak kom'a aseijin, “Kigosadem’in um'in tunia hi Pakaijin Sisera chunga galjona napeh ding ahi, ajeh chu Pakai hi na masanga akalsong tan ahi,” ati. Hiti chun Barak in galsat ding mi 10,000 chu alamkaijin Tabor Mol a kon chun akumsuh tauvin galmun chu ajonlut tauve. 
Barak in ahin nokhum phat chun Pakaijin Sisera leh asakol kangtalai jouse leh agalsat ding jouse chu asuh nohphah pehtan, Sisera chu asakol kangtalai a kon'in akichomlhan akengin ajamdoh tan ahi. 
Hichun Barak in sakol kangtalai ho leh agal miho chu Harosheth-haggoyim chan gei chun adellut in, Sisera galmi jouse chu athat gamsoh hel'in khatcha ahing hoi tapon ahi. 
Hiche kahlah chun Sisera chu Ken mi Heber jinu Jael ponbuh a ana jamlut in ahi, ajeh chu Heber insung mite leh Hazor lengpa Jabin chu jolle gol hina a ana kihou thei ahiuve. 
Jael chu apotdoh in Sisera chu akimupin akom'a hitin ana seiye, “Kaponbuh sunga hunglut in, kapu kicha louhel'in hunglut in,” ati. Hiti chun aponbuh sunga ana lut in aman ponsil khat in ana khu khum’in ahi. 
Hichun Sisera in Jael henga chun, “Twi don ding themkhat neihin pen kadang achah behseh e,” ati. Hiti chun amanun savun peng sunga bongnoi themkhat chu anapen pon chu akhu khum kit in ahi. 
Hichun Sisera’n amanu kom'a chun, “Ponbuh kot phung kom'a chun dingin lang koitobang hamkhat ahunga na insung’a mi khattou um am tia ahung holleh aumpoi tin,” ati. 
Ahinlah Sisera chu athachol behseh jeh a a-imut mil hel phat chun Jael chun, thih hemkhat akhutna achoijin ama kom'a chun guhthim chan alut in hichun ponbuh thih hem chu angeichang a akhenlut in toltoh akhen mat jengin ahileh ama athitan ahi. 
Barak chu Sisera hol'a ahung chun Jael chu apotdoh in akimupin hitin aseiye, “Hungin na mihol chu keiman na vetsah inge” ati. Hiti chun aman ponbuh sunga chun ajuilut in ahileh, Sisera chu achalpanga ponbuh thih kikhenlutna athisa a alup chu agamu in ahi. 
Hiche nikho chun Canaan lengpa Jabin chu Pakaijin ajopeh tauve ti Israel ten amuchen tauvin ahi. 
Hiche nikhoa pat chun Israel te chu Jabin lengpa dounan ahung hatdoh un ajonan asumang hel tauvin ahi.
