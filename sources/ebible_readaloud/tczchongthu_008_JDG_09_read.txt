Thutan Vaihom Ho.
9.
Nikhat hi Gideon chapa Abimelech chu Shechem ache in, apupa hichu anu sopipa chu gavil dingin achen ahi. Aman amaho leh anu insungmi adang ho jah a chun hitin aseije, 
“Shechem khomite lah a lamkai ho kom'ah Gideon chate som sagi hon nachung uva vai ahom ding nadei uvam ahilouleh mikhat in vai ahom ding nadei joh uvam tin dongun ana tin ahi. Chuleh keima hi na thisan uleh naphe navou u kahi ti geldoh un” atin ahi. 
Hiti chun Abimelech pute hon ama thakhellin Shechem mite ho chu ana dongun ahi. Hiche thu hi Shechem mite hon giltah a agel jouvun Abimelech chu akinaipiu ahijeh in achung uva vaihom dingin alheng tauvin ahi. 
Hijeh chun ama hon Baal-berith Hou-In a kon'in dangka somsagi anape uvin, hichu mangcha in aman miduhdahlou mi phabep anungjui dingin akisemin ahi. 
Chujouvin Abimelech chu Ophrah khopia apa insunga ache in, songkhat mangchan asopite Gideon chapa somsagi chu agathat gamtan ahi. Ahin uchaho lah a aneopen Jotham chu jamdoh in ana kiseldoh tan ahi. 
Hiche jou chun Shechem leh Beth-millo khomite lah a lamkai ho chu Shechem khompi kom gangpi phung noijah akikhom'un Abimelech chu leng achansah tauve. 
Jotham’in hiche hi ajahdoh phat in Gerizim molchung vum'ah akaldoh in kho ahin samtan ahi, “Shechem khocheng mite ho ka thusei hi ngaijuvin! Pathen'in na thuseiju angai ding nadei uleh ka thusei hi nei ngaipeh un! 
Phat khatnin thingphung hon alengpau ding alheng gouvin amasapen in Olive thingphung kom'ah aseijun, ka lengpau dingin nei panpeh un atiuve! 
Ahinlah Olive thingphung anompon, hitin aseijin ahi, 'Keiman Olive thao kaputdoh sah a Pathen le mihem te kakipasahji hi kangah a, iti thingphung dang ho chunga vai kagahom ding ham? ati. 
Chujouvin theichang phung kom'a agaseijun, nangma ka lengpa uvin hung pang tan agati uve!. 
Ahinlah theichang phung chu ana nompon hitin ahin seije, ‘Keiman theichang lhumkei kagadoh sah hi ka dalhah a hiche thingphung holah a hi iti kaga vaihom ding ham?’ ati. 
Hiche jou chun lengpithei phung kom'a agaseijun ka lengpa uvin hung pang tan agatiuve, 
Ahinlah lengpithei phung chu ana nompon hiti hin ahin seije, ‘Keiman Pathen leh mihemte kakipasahna hiche twilhum ka putdoh sah hi kangah a iti hiche thingphungho chunga hi kaga vaihom ding ham ana tin ahi. 
Ajona in thingphung jouse chu acheuvin lingphung kom'ah hungin lang ka lengpa uvin hung pang tan agatiuve. 
Hiche lingphung chun thingho kom'ah hitin ahin seije, “Nanghon keima hi na leng uva neitun nom uleh, hungun lang kalimnoija hin hung kisel tauvin, achuti louleh keija kon'in mei hung kongdoh intin Lebanon a um Cedar thingphung jouse akahvam ding ahi,” ati. 
Jotham'in aseiben, “Tun nang hon Abimelech chu na lengpau dinga natun uhi lungtheng tah leh bolding lomtah a nabol'u hinam chuleh Gideon leh achilhah te chunga hi them nachan diu hinam? Aman atohdoh ho chunga ahi ding bangtah a jana le hetpehna achan ding doltah nachan sah u hinam? 
Ajeh chu aman ahinkho phal ngam’a nangho Midian te a konna na huhdoh u ahi. 
Ahinlah tunin nang hon kapa leh achilhah ho chungah khan gitlouna nasatah nabol'un achate somsagi jen chu songkhat mangchan nathat tauvin ahi. Hiti chun asoh numeinu chapa Abimelech chu nasopiu ahijeh in na lengpa uvin natung tauvin ahi. 
Tunia na natoh uhi Gideon chung le achilhah ho chunga lungtheng tah le jana neitah a natoh u ahileh Abimelech chungah nang hon kipana mu uvin chuleh aman jong nangho chungah kipana muhen. 
Amavang nang hon lungtheng tah a nabol'u ahilouleh vang Abimelech a kon'in mei hung kongdoh henlang Shechem leh Beth-millo a chengho lah a lamkaiho jouse vallhum hen, chuleh Shechem leh Beth-millo a chenghoa kon'in mei hung koudoh henlang Abimelech vallhum hen,” tin anasam in ahi. 
Hiche jou chun Jotham ajamdoh in, asopipa Abimelech akichat jeh in Beer ah agacheng tai. 
Abimelech hin Israel te chung’a kum thum lhingset vai anapo in ahi. 
Pathen’in lhagao khat ahinsol'in Abimelech leh Shechem khomite lamkai ho kikah a kiboina asodoh sah tan, hichun amaho akiphin tauve. 
Abimelech chu Gideon chapa sagi atha jeh chuleh Shechem a cheng hon Abimelech in asopiho atha chu ana tosot jeh un Pathen’in amaho chu gotna apetan ahi. 
Hichun Shechem khomi ten Abimelech chu molchunga achaanglhi uvin hilanga cheho jouse chu ana chom’un ahi. Ahin hitobang atohgot uchu mikhat in Abimelech henga aga seipeh tan ahi. 
Nikhat hi Ebed chapa Gaal kitipa chu Shechem a asopi hotoh achaolut uvin ahileh, akhomite lamkai hon ama chu atahsan jotauvin ahi. 
Shechem’a kumlhun neh le chah kikhol khom phatna kimang golvah chu akhomun'a cheng ten semthu pathen ahounau hou-in achun amangun ahi. Lengpithei twi chu nengtah alon jing pettah chun mijousen Abimelech chu asamse-asamse jeng tauvin ahi. 
“Abimelech hi koi hija ham?” tin Gaal kitipa chun asamtan ahi. “Ama chu Shechem cha tahbeh ahipoi” ahileh ama lhacha kin ibola itoh diu ham? Ama chu Gideon cha bou ahin chuleh Zebul hi ama noidoupa mai-mai ahi bouve, Shechem phutdohpa Hamor chapa tahbeh ho kinbou ibol diu ahi, ipi dinga Abimelech lhacha na itoh diu ham? 
Keima hi hilai mun'a vaipo hileng Abimelech hi ka suhmang ding ahi. Keiman ama kom'ah sepai phabep hin kipuijin chuleh hungin lang kisatto hite kati ding ahi,” tin Gaal in ana samtan ahi. 
Ahin Zebul hiche khopia lamkaipa chun Gaal thusei chu ajahdoh phatnin alunghang lheh jengin ahi. 
Hichun aman Arumah a um Abimelech kom'a chun thupole ho khat asol'in hiti hin aseije, “Ebed chapa Gaal leh asopite chu Shechem’a cheng dingin ahungin, tun ama hon khopi mitehi nangma dou dingin atildoh tan ahi. 
Na sepai te toh janin hungin lang loujao dunga khun hung kisel inlang, 
jingkah kho ahungvah vah leh khopi hi hin bulu jengin, Gaal leh aloiho nangma dou dia kisaho se-se chunga chu nangman nadei deija nabol thei ahitai” ati. 
Hijeh chun Abimelech leh amite jouse janin acheuvin loili in akihom khen'un Shechem chu aum tauvin ahi. 
Abimelech le asepai te chu akisel nauva konna ahung potdoh phat chun Gaal chu khopi kelkot achun ana dingin ahi. 
Gaal chun amaho chu amu phatnin Zebul kom'ah ahin seije, “Ven molvum'a kon'in mihonpi phabep ahungsuh uve” ati. Zebul chun ahin donbutnin “Hichu thinglhanga thil le ho pou chu mihem lim'a namu khattou hinte” ati. 
Ahinlah Gaal chun aseikit in, “Ahipoi mol'a kon khun mipi phabep ahungsuh in ahi” atikit in ahi, chuleh loi chomkhat jong doi kisanna gangpi phung galkaina lampia kon jong khun ahung suh uve,” atin ahi. 
Hichun Zebul chun ama lam ahin nga’n ahin donbutnin ahi. “Tua hi nakam'a na nop-nop nasei chu hoilang geija hitam? Abimelech koi ham, eihon ama kin ipi dinga ibol dinguh am nati hilou ham? Nangin na houset ho chu tun khopi pam'a aum uve! Potdoh inlang gakisatpi tan,” atipeh tan ahi. 
Hijeh chun Gaal in Shechem khomite lamkai ho chu Abimelech toh kisat dingin apuitan ahi. 
Ahinlah Abimelech chun Gaal ahin dellin, chuleh Shechem mite tamtah chu akisukha un, chule khopi kelkot langa hunglejam ho chu alhukaam tauvin ahi. 
Abimelech chu Arumah langah akile tan, Zebul chun Gaal leh asopi ho chu anodoh tan ahi. 
Ajing chun Shechem mipite chu galsat dingin ahung kondoh kit tauvin ahi. Abimelech chun hichu ajahdoh phatnin, 
amite chu loithum’in ahom khennin loujaovah amaho chu achaanglhi tauvin ahi. Abimelech in Shechem mipi te ho chu akhopi uva konna ahung kondoh amudoh phat in, amaho jong akisel nauva kon'in ahung potdoh un akisatpi tauvin ahi. 
Abimelech loihon khopi kelkot chu anokhum un Shechem mite chu ahunglelut kit theilouna diuvin akhahtan tauvin ahi. Langkhat ah Abimelech mite loini kihomkhen chun phaileijah amaho chu anasat chap jengun ahi. 
Nilhum keijin akisatnun ajona in Abimelech chun khopi chu alatan ahi. Aman mipi ho chu athat gamin khopi chu asuchimin toljang pumpi chu chi asokhum tan ahi. 
Khosung chengho lah a lamkaiho Shechem inting vumma chengho chun thilsoh ho chu ajah phat un ajam’un Baal doi houna in a chun alut un akisel tauvin ahi. 
Mikhatnin Shechem khomi lamkai ho chu asemthu pathen houna insunga akisel uve ti Abimelech chu agaseipeh phat nin, 
asepai te chu Zalmon Mol langa apuitou tan ahi, aman heicha khat alan thingboh ho chu atulhan alengkouva akiput in ahi. Chuin aman amite jah ah “Gangtah in keibol tobang hin bollun,” ati. 
Hiti chun abonchauvin thingbah chu akisatnun Abimelech bol tobang chun abolsoh tauvin ahi. Ama hon thingbah ho chu a hou-in bang chunga chun asekhom'un meiyin ahal khum tauvin ahi. Hichun Shechem inting vumma um mipi jouse chu athigam tan ahi. Amaho chu numei pasal agomin mi sangkhat ahiuve. 
Hiche ban chun Abimelech in Thebez khopi chu anokhum in alatan ahi. 
Ahinlah khopi sunga chun intingvum lhoutah khat aumin, khopi sunga mipite jouse chu numei pasal abonchauvin hiche mun achun ajamlut tauvin ahi. Amaho leh amaho kot akikam khum'un intingvum chung vum'a akaldoh tauvin ahi. 
Abimelech in amaho chu ajuilut in intingvum a chun nokhum agon ahi. Ahinlah aman alutnau mun chu gahhal ding agot laitah in, 
numei khatnin inchung dan a konna sumhei songkhat ahin seplhan ahileh Abimelech luchanga chun achun alugu chu asepkeh tan ahi. 
Aman agal manchah poa gollhangpa kom'a chun gangtah in “Na chemjam satdoh loijin lang neihin thatnin, achuti louleh min numei khatnin Abimelech athattai tiget inte,” ahi. Hichun gollhangpa chun Abimelech chu ana sunlih tai. 
Abimelech miten ama chu athitai ti amudoh phat un akikhen tellun a incheh uvah akile tauvin ahi. 
Hiti hin Abimelech in apa douna a asopite somsagi ana tha jeh chun Pathen’in achunga phu ana latan ahi. 
Shechem mipite jong chu thilse abol jeh un Pathen’in achung uva phu alatan ahi. Hiti chun Gideon chapa Jotham in agaosapna chu aguilhung tan ahi.
