Thutan Vaihom Ho.
19.
Hiche phatlai hi Israel ten leng ananei masangu chu ahi. Ephraim thinglhang gamkun’ah Levi mikhat ana chengin ahi. Nikhat hi aman Judah gam Bethlehem a kon'in numei khat athaikem dingin ahin kipuijin ahi. 
Ahinlah amanu hitoh akina-in ahileh Bethlehem'a apa in'ah ana kile kittan ahi. Lha li alhin jouvin, 
ajipa chu Bethlehem jon dingin akipat doh in amanu chu gajol lungdam’a hin kile puikit agot ahi. Aman sangan kopkhat toh alhachapa chu ana kipuijin ahi. Ama ajinu pa in agalhun chun ajinu pa chun ngailutna neitah in ana sangin ahi. 
Anupa pa chun nichomkhat beh um dingin ana ngeh in ahi. Hijeh chun, ama chu nithum anichongin, aneuvin, adonun chuleh ajan geh un ahi. 
Nili channi chun amapa chu matah in athouvin cheding akigo tan, ahinla anupa pa chun amapa kom'a chun “Nache kah un bu themkhat beh gahne kitnun,” atin ahi. 
Hijeh chun amani jong atoukit lhon'in themkhat anelhon tan ahi. Hijou chun numeinu pa chun hiti hin ahin seikit in, “Lungset tah in jankhat beh gehkit inlang kicholdo kit un ati.” 
Levi pa chu akipatdoh in achego tan ahileh anupa pan aum kitna diuvin angeh teitei jengin ahileh ajona in anom’in jankhat ageh kittai. 
Ni nga channin ama chu matah in athouvin che dingin akigo tai, ahin numeinu pa chun, “Nehthei themkhat beh nenlang nilhah lamleh chenauvin nate” tin ahin seikit in ahi. Hichun amaho chun nehkhomna aneikit un ahi. 
Hiche jou chun amapa le athaikemnu chuleh alhachapa chu che dingin akigo tauvin ahi, anupa pan aseijin, “Ven tuhi nilhah lang hi ding ahitai, tujan gehkit inlang nomtah in kicholdon lang jing tengleh matah in thouvin natin na inlam'u jon tauvin nate,” ati. 
Ahinlah hiche chung vang hin chetei agotan ahi. Hijeh chun asangan teni leh athaikemnu chu akipuijin Jebus (Hichu Jerusalem) lang ahin jon tauvin ahi. 
Jebus anai phat in agei thim lheh tan alhachapa chun akom’a chun “Jebus khopia hin kinga pa uhitin, jan geh tau hite” ati. 
Apakaipan “Hithei ponte Israel mi umnalou gam chom mite khopia ige theilou diu ahi, hiche sang chun Gibeah lam ana jonsuh taohite. 
Hungin Gibeah ahilouleh Ramah lhuntei go uhitin hiche khopiho khat pen-pen ahin gehnau hite” ati. 
Hiti chun ache tauve. Gibeah agalhun uchun nisa lhum ding kon ahitai, Gibeah chu Benjamin gamsunga khopi khat ahi. 
Hilai mun achun akinga uvin jan-geh dingin akigo tauvin ahi. Koiman a inna lhunsah dinga akoulou jeh uchun hiche khopi lamlen khatna chun akicholnga tauvin ahi. 
Hiche jan chun tehse alouva anatohna a kon a-inlam hinjon khat aumin ahi. Ama chu Ephraim thinglhang a konna Gibeah a hung kichaolut ahin, hiche khopia chengho hi Benjamin phungmi ahiuve. 
Amaho chu khopi lamsom’a anatou chu amu phatnin “Hoiya hungkon hoilang jon ding nahiuvam?” tin ana dongin ahi. 
Amapan adonbutnin, “Keiho hi Judah gam Bethlehem’a konna Ephraim thinglhang gamgil'a um ka inmun u jon ding kahiuve. Ahinla koiman a-in na lhung dingin eikou pouvin ahi. 
Keihon ka ngaichat diu jouse kanei nauve, sangan neh ding changpol kaneijun, keiho neh ding changlhah le theitwi lhingset in kanei naovin ahi” atiuve. 
Tehsepu chun, “Kei kom'ah geh tauvin, keiman na ngaichat diu jouse nape nauvinge, amavang hiche lamsom’a vang hin jan-geh hihbeh un,” ati. 
Hiti chun a-in na apuijin asangan teni jong chu avahtan ahi. Ama hon akeng akisil uvin an anekhom'un chule adon khom'un ahi. 
Nomsatah a agah umpet laitah un, khopi sunga kon chun miphalou honkhat in in chu ahin umkhum'un, kot chu ahin chum’un tehsepu chu ahinsap thouvin, “Na in'a lhungpa chu hin puidoh in keihon numei bol'a kabol diu ahi” atiuvin ahi. 
Tehsepu chu amaho to kihou dingin apotdoh in aga kihoupin “Ahipoi sopi teho, hitobang thilse chu bolpou hite, ajeh chu amapa hi ka-in na lhung kamaljin ahi bouve, chutobang thil melse tah chu bol poute. 
Veuvin, kachanu nungah thengtah khat leh hiche mipa thaikemnu hi hin pedoh inge nalo lo lotauvin, ahinla hiche mipa chunga vang hin chutobang thil melse chu bol hihbeh un” ati. 
Ahinlah ama hon angaideh pouvin ahi. Hijeh chun Levi pa chun athaikemnu chu kotpam'a asondoh in ahileh, khopi mite chun amanu chu khovah lhah tokah in kichepnan anei tauvin ahi. Khovah then-then chun amanu chu alhakang bep un ahi. 
Khovah lhah chun amanu chu ajipa lhunna a chun ahung kilen kotbul aphah chun alhulhop tan khovah lhah geijin ana kijam tan ahi. 
Ajipan gahpot dinga kotchu ahin hon leh athaikemnu chu kotbulla ana kijam jengin akhut teni chu tolla ana kiphatho jengin ahi. 
Aman “Thouvin! chetau hite!” atileh adonbut tapon ahi. Hiti chun atahsa chu sangan chungah ajamin ain geijin apoluttai. 
Ain alhun phatnin, chem khat alan athaikemnu chu boh som le bohni ason bohkhat cheh chu Israel gam pumpia phungkhat cheh chu athot in ahi. 
Hiche mujouse chun, “Israel ten Egypt ahin dalhah uva pat tuni chan geijin hitobang thil melse hi sohkha hih laijin ahi chule mujong akimu kha hih laiye, hichepi hi gel temun, tua hi ipi ibol uva i-ilo diu hitam? Koiham aseithei ding’ah?” akiti tauvin ahi.
