1 Samuel.
10.
Chuin Samuel in olive thao thei khat alan, Saul luchung’ah asunglhan achop in ajah ah, “Keiman hiche kabolna ajeh chu Pakaijin nangma Israel chunga lamkai dinga, panmun sanga dinga na lhendoh ahitai. 
Tunia hi nangma keija konna na potdoh tengleh, Benjamin gamgi Zelzah a Rachel lhan kom'a mini namu ding, sangan ho chu akimutai ti chu amanin aseilhon ding ahi. Napa sangan ho jeh a na lungkhamna chu akichai tan, tua hi nangni dinga lungkham ah ahi. Aman miho jah ah ka chapa namu uvem tin adong le-le in ahi,” ati. 
“Chule chua kon chun chebe lhon lechun, Tabor phaicham lhungin natin chua chun Bethel’a Pathen henga chetouva mithum nakimutopi ding, khat pan kelcha nou thum, khatpan changlhah thum chule khatpan lengpitwi haikhat achoi namu ding ahi. 
Chule amaho chun chibai naboh diu, changlhah ni napeh dingu, hichu akhut uva konna nakisan ding ahi. 
Chutengleh Phlistine sepaiho umna Pathen lhaang’a chu nahung ding, chule hiche khopia chu nahung tengleh tumging, khongcha, kuli, selangdah, amasang uva hintho a munsang’a konna hung kumlha themgao honkhat nakitopi ding, amaho chun gaothu asei dingu ahi. 
Hiche phatteng chule Pakai Lhagao thahat tah a nachung uva hung chu ding, amaho toh gaothu nasei khom ding, chule michombeh khat nakiso ding ahi,” ati. 
“Chule hiche melchihna ho hi nachung’a ahunglhun tengleh, Pathen'in naumpi jeh chun Pathen'in napuina lam-lam chu bol jengin. 
Chutengleh nangma kei sanga Gilgal’a na chesuh masat ding, chule hikom’a chu ikimua govam kilhaina le chamna thilto gan kilhaina bol ding’a ikisuhto ding ahi. Nangman hikom'a chu nisagi chan nei nga ding chule maban a na thilbol ding ka seipeh ding nahi,” ati. 
Hiti chun Saul chu, Samuel’a konna kinungle dinga ache got chun, Pathen'in ama lung chom khat apen, hiche ni chun melchihna jouse chu aguilhung sohtai. 
Saul le asohpa Gilgal’a ahunglhun lhon phat in, ama hon themgao thah loi khat amaho langa ahung amu phat un, Pathen Lhagao thahat tah in Saul chung’ah ahung chun, ama tah jong chun gaothu aseijin ahi. 
Chuin Saul anahe ho chun, “Kish chapa, Saul jeng jong themgao hi ngap hitam?” atiuve. 
Chule chukom'a ding miho lah a khat in, “Apateu itobang hijongleu, mijouse themgao hitheiya ham?” ati. Hichea pat a chu, “Saul jeng jong Themgao soh ngap hitam?” tia hung kiseipan ahi. 
Chuin Saul in gaothu aseijou phat in, Pathen houna mun’ah ahung kaltouvin ahi. 
Chuin Saul pangah pan ama le asohpa jah a, “Hoiya na gache ham?” tia adoh leh “Sangan hol'a kache lhon ahin, ahinlah kamu lhonpon, Samuel dong dinga kache lhon ahi,” tin Saul in ana donbut e. 
Apangah pan adong kit in, “O Samuel in ipi naseipeh lhon em?” tin adongkit in ahi. 
Saul in adonbut in, “Sangan chu akimutai tin eina seipeh lhon'in,” ahi. Ahinlah Saul in apangah kom'a Samuel in lenggam thu asei chu aseidoh poi. 
Chujouvin Samuel in Pakai masang’ah Israel mipite jouse Mizpah mun'a kimuto din akouvin ahi. 
Ajah uva, “Hiche hi Pakai Israel te Pathen phondoh ahi: Keiman Egypt a kon'in kahin puidoh un, chule Egypt mite a kon'in kahin huhdoh un, chule namdang mite jouse bol gentheina a kon'in kahin hoidoh un ahi. 
Ahin na genthei nauva konna ka huhdoh vangun, na Pathen’u tunin napai tauve chule hitin naseiyun, idang sangin leng khat kadei uve natiuve. Hijeh chun tun, naphung nachang uvin, na nam pumpi uvin Pakai ang’a dingin kigong un,” ati. 
Chuin Samuel in Israel phung jouse Pakai masang’ah ahin puikhom’in chule vang asangin Benjamin phung chu alhengtai. 
Benjamin phung insung jouse chu akoukhom kit in, chule amaho lah a chun Matri insung alhengdoh kit tan ahi. Achainan Kish cahpa Saul chu amaho insung lah a kon chun ana lhengdoh un ahi. Ahinlah ama chu ahol’u le aholmo tauve. 
Chuin ama hon Pakai adoh uleh, “Thilkeo lah a akisel'e,” tin Pakaijin ahin donbut e. 
Hiti chun ama hon jong Saul chu agamudoh uvin ahin puidoh uva mipi lah a adin leh, alengkouva pat chunglamse chu mijouse sangin asangjon ahi. 
Chuin Samuel in mijouse jah ah, “Hiche mipa hi ahi Pakijin na lengpa ding uva alhen chu ahi, Israel te lah a ama tobang adang aumpoi,” ati. Chuin mipi ten, “Lengpa damsot hen,” tin asam’un ahi. 
Chuin Samuel in mipite jah a lengpan atoh atham ding thudol ahil'in, chule lekhajol khat a asunlut in, Pakai angsung'ah akoitai. Chuin Samuel in mipi ho chu ama-ama incheh ah asoltai. 
Chuin Saul jong Gibeah kho a-inlam ah achetan, chule Pathen'in alungsung atohkhah mi honkhat in alhon piuvin ahi. 
Ahinlah panna bei mi phabep chun, “Hiche pan iti eihuhdoh diu ham?” atiuve. Chuin ama hon ama nahsahmon abol’un, thilpeh imacha ahin choipeh pouvin ahi. Ahinlah ama athipbeh in ana nahsah pon ahi.
