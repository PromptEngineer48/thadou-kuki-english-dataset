1 Samuel.
20.
David chu Ramah gam'a Naioth a kon'in ajamdoh’in, Jonathan kom ajonin, “Ipi kabol’a, ipi kabol khel hitam? Itobang gitlouna ipi um’a napan tha dinga eihol le-le ham?” ati. 
Jonathan in, “Hichu dihlou ahi, nangma thipon nate, abol ding jouse thilneo hihen lang thil len hijongleh kei hetlouvin ima abolji poi, eihetsah jinai. Kapan hitobang thil ho hi kakom'a aselguh louhel ding ahi,” tin ana nelkal in ahi. 
Chuin David chu Jonathan masang'ah akihahsel'in, “Napan iki golngai lhon hi kichentah a ahet sohkei ahi, hijeh chun ama le ama akihouvin, Jonathan hetsah da tange, ipi bolla alung kasuhnat ding ham? Ahinlah hingjing Pakai le nangma min pan a kasei ahi keima le thina kikah hi kal khat seh bou uma ahi!” ati. 
Jonathan in, “Ipi ti kakithopi thei ding ham nei seipeh in,” ati. 
David in adonbut in, “Jing nikho hi lhathah kut ahin, lengpa to ankong umkhom dingdol kahi, ahin keima jing nikho tengleh loulaiya kakisel'a hichea chu nithum channa nilhah tokah a kaum den ding ahi,” ati. 
“Napan hoiya che ham tia nahin doh a ahileh, Bethlehem ain’ah kumseh kilhaina bol dingin kakom'ah phalna athum’in ache e tin seipeh in,” ati. 
“Aman ‘Aphai!’ atia ahileh, thu anom'e ti nahet ding ahi. Ahinlah alung ahanna alung alhaso khah a ahile, eithanom ahi ti nahet ding ahi. 
Lungset tah in hiti chun nei bolpeh in, thengsel’a kaheng'a Pakai ang’a nakitepna nit in. amavang napa douna a themmo kahile nangma tah in nei that jengin. Ahinlah nei joulhep hihbeh in,” ati. 
Jonathan in, “Hitobang chu agel in geldan, kapan chutobang’a nangma thana ding lunggel neocha hijongleh anei mong’a ahile, nang kahin mahetsah tei ding ahi,” ati. 
Chuin David in, “Napa alunghang’e ahilou le alunghang poi tichu ken iti kahet thei ding ham?” tin adongin ahi. 
Jonathan in adonbut in, “Hungin loulai langa khun gapotdoh hite,” ati. Chule polam’ah akijotdoh khom lhon'in ahi. 
Chuti chun Jonathan in David kom'ah, “Pakai Israel Pathen chu a hetoh hijeng hen! Keiman jing tuphat don ahilouleh nithum jou tengleh, kapa donging katin, na chunga alungdam nah le thu kahin thot ding ahi. 
Ahinlah ijem tia kapa alunghan’a nangma tha tei ding nagot a, nangma na hindoh tei-teina dinga keiman nahenga ka seipeh masat a kasolmang lou pou-pouva ahileh Pakaijin Jonathan kiti keima hi eibolsejo hen ahilou jongleh eithat jeng hen. Pakaijin kapa ana umpi bangin nang jong naumpi hen,” ati. 
“Nei ngailut monga ahileh, ka hinkho aumlai sungsen Pakai ngailutna sangtah in nei ngailun. Ahinlah kathia ahileh, 
ka insung mite din tahsan umtah ngailutna sangtah in hin ngailun chule Pakaijin nagal mite jouse jong leiset a kon'in sumang gamkei hen,” ati. 
Hijeh chun Jonathan in David toh kitepna khat asemin, “Pakaijin nagal mite jouse sugamkei hen!” ati. 
Chule Jonathan in David chu ama aki ngailut bang banga angailut jeh chun, David in jong angailut din akitepsah kittan ahi. 
Chuin Jonathan in, “Jing nikho le lhathah kut kimang ding ahin, natouna dokhang a-on jeh a nakiholmo ding ahi. 
Athaini nilhah lam leh, tumasanga nakiselji na mun songpi pang ma-ma achun che inlang gakisel tan. 
Chule keima kahungdoh a doikhat nei banga thalchang thum hiche panga chu kahin kap ding ahi. 
Chule chapang khat thal chang ho hol ding’a kahinsol ding ahi. Chule chapangpa jah a, ‘Ven, thalchang ho chu nasihlam panga aum'e,’ tia kasei najah a ahile, hingjing Pakai min'a kasei ahi, abonchan apha sohkeiye, imacha hahsatna aumpoi. 
Amavang keiman ama jah a, 'Gamla jepmah chen, thal chang chu nakhel lamah aum'e, katia ahile, nalampia gangtah in che jengin, ajeh chu Pakaijin na solmang ahi, ti hetna ahi,' ati. 
Chule Pakai ahettohsah ahijeh in, ikitepna vang chu Pakaijin tonsot geiya einit sah tei-tei lhon hen,” ati. 
Hiti chun David chu loulaiyah akisellin, chule lhathah kut kimanna ahunglhun phat in, Lengpa chu, anne dingin atoulha tan ahi. 
Chule lengpa chu nidanga bangin bang kom'a touna chunga chun atouvin ahi. Jonathan toh akimai ngatton, chule Abner asih-lang ah atouve. Ahin David touna chu a ongtai. 
Ahinlah Saul in hiche ni chun imacha aseipoi, ajeh chu ama chunga thil thenglou khat touvin David asuhboh hinte, tia lung agel ahi. 
Ahinlah, ajinga jong David touna a-on kit phat in, Saul in Jonathan jah a, “Ipi jeh a Jesse chapa janhia jong tunia jong hunglou hitam?” tin adongtai. 
Jonathan in, “David in Bethlehem a che dingin hatah in kakom'a phalna athumin ahi. 
Aman, ‘Lungset tah in, nei chesah in, ajeh chu ka insung miten kumseh a khosunga gan kilhaina kanei jiu ahin, chule kasopi te ho jong kakimupi nom’e, tin ataovin ahi.' Hijeh a chu lengpa dokhang’a atouna ong’ah ahi,” atin ahi. 
Chuin Jonathan chunga Saul alunghang behseh jengin, “Numei chaveinu chapa!” tin lunghang tah in ahousen ahi. “Nalunggel ho hi ken kahet louva nagel ham? Na khella lenga natun got ham? Nangma le nanu jumso pa,” ati. 
“Jesse chapa David ahinlai sungsen, nang leng’in pang thei pon nate. Tun chenlang gaholdoh in katha ding ahi,” ati. 
Jonathan in, “Ipi jeh a kithat ding ham? Aman ipi abolset ham?” tin apa adongin ahi. 
Chuin Saul in achapa Jonathan chu tha dingin tengchan akho jengin ahi. Hichea kon chun Jonathan in jong apan David tha ding’a agot mong ahi tichu ahedoh tai. 
Hichun Jonathan chu lunghang tah in dokhang a kon'in akipatdoh in, apan ajachat sah behseh jeh chun chuche kut ni-ni lhinni chun an ane tapoi, David chu alungkhampi lheh tan ahi. 
Ajing jingkah in David toh akitepna bangin, Jonathan chu chapang khat toh apotdoh in, loujaova chun ache lhontai. 
Chuin aman chapangpa jah a chun “ka kapgot thalchang ho hi gaholdoh in,” ati. Chuin chapangpa chu alhaijel in ahile, Jonathan in akal langpeh a thalchang chu akap’in ahi. 
Chuin chapangpa chu Jonathan in akapdoh thalchang kom aphah phat ding kon'in, Jonathan in chapangpa jah ah, “Thalchang chu akal langpeh ah achubouve,” atin ahi. 
Jonathan'in chapangpa chu asamin, “Hikom'a chun dingden hih in, agang gangin hinchoi loiyin,” ati. Chuin chapangpa chun thalchang ho chu achom khomin, apu henga ahungtai. 
Ahinlah chapangpa chun imacha ahepon, Jonathan le David in bou thil kibol ho chu ahet lhon ahi. 
Chuin Jonathan in amanchah ho chu chapangpa apen, chenlang khopi sungah ana polut in tin asoltai. 
Chuin chapangpa apotdoh doh in, songlhuh pang’a akiselna a kon'in David chu ahung potdoh in, thumveijen tollah amai asulut in Jonathan maiyah akunin, ani lhon'in amitlhi pum'in akikolto lhon'in ana kivailhah lhon tai. Hichu Jonathan sangin David in apona dehset in ahi. 
Jonathan in David kom'ah, “Lungmong’in chetan, Pakai min'a thengsel’a ikihahsel lhon nang le kei, na chilhah te le keiya din, ikitepna chu tonsot geiyin Pakaijin eikoi jing uhen,” ati. Hichun David in adalhan, chule Jonathan jong khopi lam’ah akile kittai.
