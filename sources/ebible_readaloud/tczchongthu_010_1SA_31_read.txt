1 Samuel.
31.
Philistine miten Israel mite chu anokhum tauvin ahile, Israel mite chu Philistine te masanga ajam cheh uvin, Gilboa molsanga aken laiya chun Philistine ten athatgam tauve. 
Philistine miten Saul le achapa teho jong adelphapa uvin, achapa thum Jonathan, Abinadab, chule Malkishua jong athat tauve. 
Saul panmun kimvel’a jong gal akihasat lheh jengun, akhonna in thalpi kapthem hon akapkha un, melsetah in amavo uvin ahi. 
Chuin Saul in agalvon popa jah ah, “Na chemjam satdoh inlang nei that in hiche Philistine Pathen helou ho ahung uva eimoh sutlih sangun,” ati. Ahinla agalvon popa akichan athat ngampon ahi. Chuin Saul in jong ama chemjam asatdoh in, achemjam chu abokhum in ama le ama akithat tai. 
Chuin agalvon popan Saul athitai ti ahetdoh phat in achemjam asatdoh in alhuh khum in alengpa kom'ah ama jong athi tan ahi. 
Hiti chun Saul, achapa thumho, chule agalvon popa, abonchauvin nikhat sungin athi sohhel tauvin ahi. 
Jezreel phaicham agal langkhat, Jordan vadung niso lam’a Israel ten, Israel sepai teho agaljam gam tauve tithu ajahdoh uva, Saul le achapa te ho jong athat tauve ti ajah phat un, akhopi hou adalha uvin ahile Philistine mite akhopi sung uva alut un akhopiu chu alo tauvin ahi. 
Ajing nikho in Philistine miten mithilong ho hetup dinga ahung uleh Gilboa Mol chunga Saul le achapa te thumho le agalvon popa, alongu ahung mudoh un ahi. 
Chuin Saul alu atan uvin agalvon chengtoh apotauve. Saul thi jeh in Philistine gamsung jouse le a pathen hounau hou-in’ah kipa thuphon aneiyun ahi. 
Saul galvon chu amilim doi ahounau Ashteroth kiti hou-in sung'ah akoijun chule atahsa chu Beth-shan kiti khopi sunga bang ah akhaiyun ahi. 
Ahinlah Jabesh-gilead miten jong Philistine miten Saul abolnau ajah phat un, 
Gal-hang pasal jouse akipatdoh un, ahung kitol’un, jan khovah hellin Beth-shan a kon'in Saul le achapa teho long chu banga kon'in ala lhauvin, Jabesh khopi ah ahin pulut un hikom'a chun ahalvam un ahi. 
Chujouvin agu ho chu alauvin Jabesh khoa tamarisk thingphung noiyah avui uvin, chule nisagi jen apuldou nan an angol uvin ahi.
