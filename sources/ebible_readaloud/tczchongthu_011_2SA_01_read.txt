1.
Saul thi jouvin ahitin ahi, David chun Amalek mite chunga galjona anei jouvin ahung kinunglen Ziklag munnah nini sung aumden in ahi. 
Anithum nin mikhat Saul ngahmun a kon'in von tel dehduh le aluchunga leiset nat pum in lunghem vetsahnan ahungin, David angsung'ah jana neitah in abohkhup tan ahi. 
Chuin David in adongin, “Hoiya kon hung nahim”? atileh amapan adonbut in, “Israel te ngahmun a kon hung jamdoh kahi” ati. 
Chuin David in adongin, “Ipiti hitam?” “Gal umchan thu nei seipeh in” ati. Chuin aman adonbut in, “ Sepai jouse galmun akon ajam gam uvin, mitampin thina atoh un, chule Saul le achapa Jonathan jong athi lhon tai,” ati. 
Chuin David in gollhangpa ja ah, “Saul le Jonathan athi tai ti iti nahet ham?” ati. 
Chuin mipan adonbut in, “Keima Gilboa Mol chungah kaga umkhan ahileh, hi ah chun Saul in a tengcha chu anaop phan chuleh agal mite sakol kangtalai ho leh sakol them hon ahin delphah kon chau chu ahitai. 
Chuin ama ahung kiheiyin keima eimu phat in ama kom adin eikoutai. Chuin keiman kadongin, iti kaki thopi ding ham kati e. 
Hichun aman eidonbut in, 'Nang koi nahim eiti?' Chuin keiman kaseipih in, 'Keima Amalek mi kahi kahi,' kati. 
Hichun ama 'Kaheng'ah ataovin, hungin lang neihung that tan, ajeh chu keiman kanatna hi kathoh jou tapon hijeh chun kathi nomtai,' ati. 
“Hijeh chun “Keiman ama chu kathat tai,” tin Amalek mipa chun David kom'ah aseitai. “Ajeh chu keiman kahet ahitai ama ahinjou tah louding chu. Hiti chun a Lallukhuh leh abanbul tomna ho kahin lan nakom'ah kahin lhut e, ka pakai,” ati. 
Hitobang thusoh chu David leh amiten ajah doh phat un apon u abot eh un alung genthei lheh tauvin ahi. 
Hiti chun ama hon sun nilhum in Saul chule achapa Jonathan ding, Pakai sepai ho leh Israel nam mite adin an angol'un, akap un, pul adou un alunghem un ahi, ajeh chu hiche nikho a hi amaho chemjam a thisoh ahiuve. 
Hichun David in thu hinpo gollhang pa kom'ah chun aseitan, “Hoiya kon hung nahim?” ati. Chuin aman adonbut in, “Keima gamdang, jatdang Amalek mi, nagam uva khosa kahi,” ati. 
Chuin David in adongin, “Ipi tidan a nangman Pakai thaonu mikhat chu tha ding kichatna nanei louhel ham?” ati. 
Chuin David in amiho khat pen kom'ah chun asei in, “That in!” ati. Hichun mipan Amalek mipa chu achemjam in adot in athat tan ahi. 
Chujouvin David in aseijin, “Nang le nang themmo kichan nahi, ajeh chu nangman nakam tah a Pakai thaonu pa chu kathat tai,” nati. 
Hiti chun David in Saul le Jonathan din kala asemin, 
chuleh hiche hi Judah te jong hil ding ahi tin thupeh aneijin ahi. Hiche la hi thalpi la tin akihen chuleh hiche hi Jashar Lekhabu.a kijih lut ahi. 
Vo Israel, naloupi nau le nakipa nau chu molsang chungah athi tai! Oh, i-atileh gal-hang mihat ho chu lhugam uhitam! 
Gath mun'a thusoh hi seidoh hih un, Ashkelon lamlen dungah jong phongdoh hih un, achuti louva ahileh Philistine chanute kipah untin chule cheptanlou ho jong galjou banga nui diu ahi. 
O Gilboa molsang ho, na chungah daitwi lhah dahen ahilouleh go jong juhda hen, chule loujaova lou hon jong amuchiga hin sepdoh dauhen. Ajeh chu mihatpa lumdal chu suhboh in aum tan; Saul lumdal chu thaotwi nulouva um ahitai. 
Jonathan thalpi chu nasatah ahin, chule Saul chemjam chun na loupitah atongdoh in ahi. Ama hon agal miteu thisan aso uvin chule gal-hat mi hangsan ho tahsa asunin ahi. 
Saul le Jonathan chu iti ngailut le lungset um hitam! Ahinlai sung lhon leh athigei lhon'in aumkhom jing lhon'in ahi. Amani hi Muvanlai sanga gangjo leh keipi bahkai sanga jong hatjo ahi lhon'in ahi. 
Vo Israel chanu te, Saul a dingin kap un, ajeh chu ama hi ponsanthip deium man tamtah a nachun pau le sana a najem pau chu ahi. 
Oh, iti gal-hat miho galmun'a lhugam'u hitam! Munsang chungah Jonathan jong athisan akijam tai. 
Jonathan kasopipa, nangjeh in ka kap-kap jeng e! Oh, ichan a kangailut nahi hitam! Chule nangman nei ngailutna hi athuh lheh in, numei ngailutna sangin athuh jon ahi! 
Oh, iti mihat ho lhugam a! Gal manchah ho mangsoh a, amaho athia kijam chap jeng hitam?
