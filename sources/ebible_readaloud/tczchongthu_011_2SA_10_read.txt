2 Samuel.
10.
Hiche jou phat chomkhat jouvin, Ammon mite Lengpa Nahash chu athitan, hijeh chun achapa Hanun kitipan Ammon mite chungah lengvai ahinpo tan ahi. 
Hichun David in jong aseijin, “Nahash chapa Hanun hi apan keima jana neitah a eina bol bang-banga ken jong amahi jana neitah a kabol ding ahi,” tin anaseiye. Hijeh chun David in jong sottolle ho asol’in Hanun chu apa thi jeh a lhemmong din asolpeh e. Ahin David sottolle ho chu Ammon mite gamsunga aga lhun phat un, 
Ammon mite lamkai hon apupau Hanun kom'a chun aseiyuvin, ‘Nangman hiche miho hi napa jabol dinga hiche mun a hi hunga na gelthei mong hinam? Ahipoi! David in khopi hi hunga suhgam ding agot jeh a sottolle ho hi khopi sung kholtoh leh velhi le dinga ahinsol ahi bouve, tin aseipeh tauve. 
Hijeh chun Hanun hin David sottolle ho chu abonchauvin aman in melse tah in akhamul akeh langkhat avo chaipeh sohkeiyin, chule aponsil ule akivon nau akimlai tah akongbuh dontah achetan peh sohkeiyun jachat umtah in anungsol tauvin ahi. 
Hiti chun David sohte chunga thusoh ho David in ajahdoh phat in, amite ho thu gaseipeh din mi asoltan ahi “Nakhamul’u akikhan lhit kit masang sen Jericho khopia hung umden unlang akikhan lhit tengleh hung kile kit tauvin,” tin thu ahiltai. Ajeh chu amaho hin alimsou chu ajachatpi mong-mong u ahi. 
Chujouvin Ammon miten ichan geija David chu asuh lunghang u akihet doh phat uvin, Ammon mite akihoutoh uvin Bethrehob a cheng Aramea mite leh Zobah a cheng Aramea mite tol'a panga galsat sepai 20,000 thalah ding leh, Maacah lengpa le amite 1,000 thalah ding chule Tob mite 12,000 thalah dingin mi asol tauve. 
Hiche thusoh David lengpan ajah doh phat in lamkai Joab le asepai te mihat chengse chu abonchauvin gal boldin asoltai. 
Chuin Ammon mite chu ahung kitoldoh uvin kelkot phunga chun galsat din akigol tauve; chule Aramea mite Zobah le Rehob khoa kon leh Tob le Maacah mite chu mun onglen laiyah akigol tauvin ahi. 
Joab chun amasanglam le anunglam'a gal miten atingtanto tai, ti amudoh phat in, Israel lah a mi son um lhentum cheh phabep alhengdoh in, Aramea mite dou din mun onglai ah apansah tai. 
Chule mi amoh chengse chu asopipa Abishai lamkai peh din akoiyin, Abishaiyin asopite chengse chu Ammon mite toh kisatto din agoltoh tai. 
Chuin Joab in aseitai, “Aramea mite hi Keima dinga ahat behseh jeng uva ahileh nangman neihung kithopin lang, chule Ammon mite chu nang dinga ahatval behseh jeng uva ahi jongleh keima hung ing ting nahung kithopi nange, ati. 
Hijeh chun hangtah in hung pang uvin lang, imiteu ding leh I-Pathen’u khopi jouse din pasal jouse chu hatah in pangu hitin; chule Pakai lungdei chu guilhung tahen,”ati. 
Hiti hin Joab le ama toh pang khom sepaiho chun Aramea mite chu asat panun ahileh; Aramea mite chu ajamcheh gam tauvin ahi. 
Aramea mite jam chu Ammon miten jong amudoh phat uvin amaho jong chutobangma chun Abishai masanga ajam uvin akhopi sunga alhailut tauvin ahi. Hichun Ammon mite toh akisat nauva kon'in Joab jong Jerusalem'a ahung kile kit tan ahi. 
Hichun Aramea miten Israel chate toh kidinte joulou ding ahiu chu ahin kihetdoh phat un ahung kithah gopkhom kit uvin, 
Hadadezer kitipan asol in Euphrates Vadung gal a cheng Aramea mite aga kousah in ahileh, amaho jong Hadadezer sepai lamkaipa Shobach lamkai nan Helam geiyin ahung kitol’uvin Aramea mite ahinjop kit un ahi. 
Chuin David in hitobang thil umchan chu ajah phat in, aman jong Israel chate pumpi akhom-khom sohkeijin chule Jordan ahung galkaijin Helam channah a sepai te ahin puilhung tan ahi. Hichun Aramea mite jong David lengpa dou din achamtah un anapang uvin akisatpi tauve. 
Ahinlah Aramea mite chu Israel te masanga ajam kit tauvin ahi. Hichea hin David sepai ten Aramea sepai sakol touthem ho mi 700 le tol'a pang sepai 40,000 chule agal lamkaipau Shobach kitipa jaonan athat tauvin ahi. 
Chuin Lengte jousen Hadadezer toh akitho vang uva Israel te ajolou u amudoh phat un, Israel mite khut ah akipelut un amaho noijah aum tauvin ahi. Hiche jouva pat chun Aramea mite chun Ammon mite kithopi ding chu akicha tauvin ahi.
