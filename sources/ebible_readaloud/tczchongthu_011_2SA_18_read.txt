2 Samuel.
18.
Hichun lengpa David chun apanpi sepai te chu asimdoh sohkeiyin, achung uva mi asang-sang chunga lamkai ding, aja-ja chunga lamkai ding agoltoh sohkei tan ahi. 
David chun sepai hop thum'a hop khat chu Joab alamkai sah in, hop thum'a hop khatma chu Joab sopi pa Zeruiah chapa Abishai chu alamkai sah kit in, chutengleh hop thum'a hop khat kit ma chu Gath mi Ittai chu alamkai sah in; hichun miho kom'a chun hitin aseiye, “Keima tah jong nangho toh gal ikon khom diu ahi,” ati. 
Ahivangin mipin aseijin, “Nangma gal na kondoh louhel ding ahi bouve. Ijeh-inem itileh keiho hi hung galjam ujong leng, ama hon iman igel khoh pouvinte, keiho akeh khat hel thi jeng tau jong leng ama hon ima agel deh lou diu ahi. Nangma vang keiho mi 10,000 toh kibang nahi; chujongleh nangman khopi sunga konna kei ho panpi mi nahin sol tho joh ding hi thil phajo ahi,” atiuve. 
Hichun lengpan jong hitin aseiye, “Ipi hita jongleh nangho dinga aphapen tah hi ding chu keiman kabol jeng ding ahi,” atitai. Hijeh chun lengpa chu kelkot phunga ading denin, sepai te vang chu aja-jan, asang sang'in akitol doh tauve. 
Chuin lengpan Joab, Abishai chule Ittai chu ahil dem demin, “Keima janan gollhangpa Absalom chu lungneng tah in hin bol uvin,” ati. Absalom chung changa lengpan sepai lamkai mithum athulhahna chu mipin abonchan aja sohkei uve. 
Hiti hin Ephraim gammang lah a gal akipan tan, 
David sepai ten Israel mite chu ajou tauve, hiche nikho a mi atha u chu atam lheh jengin, mihem 20,000 alhinge. 
Gamsung pumpia gal a kithe chan sohkei tan, chule chemjam a kisatlih sang'in gammang lah a ohlih mihem atamjon ahi. 
Absalom jong chun David sepai te atohkha khel-khel’in ahi. Absalom chu asakol pol chunga hung tou ahin, hichun sakol pol chu gangpi thing lentah khat abahkai nelnul lah khat a chun a lhailut in abahlah ahopan ahileh, Absalom chu aluchang gangpi bah lah a a-ohden tai. Chule sakol pol chun adalhan anoiya achepai tan ahi. 
David sepai khat chun Absalom chu amudoh phat in Joab kom'a aseipeh in; “Ven, keiman Absalom chu gangpi phung bah lah a akikhai den kamui,” ati. 
Joab jong chun akom'a thuseipa chu adong paijin, “Ipi? Ama namu a ibola natha lou ham? Chutin hinbol le chun keiman dangka shekel som khat beh le kong gah khat nape dinga chu!” ati. 
Ahivangin mipa chun Joab kom'a asei kit e, “Keiman dangka shekel sang khat kamu ding hijongleh lengpa chapa thana dingin kakhut lam hihhel inge; ijeh-inem itileh keiho jah tah in jong lengpan nangma le Abishai chule Ittai thu nape cheh uvin, ‘Keima janan gollhangpa Absalom chu ahingin hin hoi tei-tei un,’ tin asei bouve. 
Lang khatma keiman kathi le kathi, tin jou le nal’in hin bol jeng jong leng, lengpa mudin imacha akisel theiya aum deh poi, chutengleh nangman nang ding bou nagel a neidon louhel ding ahi,” ati. 
Hichun Joab in asei paiyin, “Keiman hitobang hin nangma toh phat sumang mang ponge,” atin, gangpi phung bah lah a Absalom ahinga aum nah laiya ahileh tin Joab chun tengcha achom thum alan agah pei a ahileh Absalom lungchang don tah a akiphut tai. 
Chuin Joab galvon poa pang gollhang sepai som khat in Absalom aumkim uvin ajep un athat tauve. 
Chuin Joab chun saki pengkul amut in ahileh sepai ho chu Israel te nung adel nauva kon'in ahung kinungle sohkei tauve. 
Chuin ama hon Absalom long chu adom lhauvin gammang lah a kokhuh lentah khat sunga aleh lut uvin, achunga song tum tampi asep khum uvin ahi. Israel chate vang chu ama-ama inlam cheh a akijamlut soh tauvin ahi. 
Chule Absalom hin ahinlaiyin lengpa phaicham a chun khom khat ana phut doh in ahi; ijeh-inem itileh aman ana seiye, “ Keiman kamin aman louna dingin chapa la kanei pon,” atin, akhom phudoh chu ama min aputsah in, hijeh chun hiche hi tuni geijin Absalom kihet jingna tin akiseiye. 
Chujouvin Zadok chapa Ahimaaz kitipa hin “Keima lhai jing ting, Pakaijin amelma te khut a kon'in ama ahuhdoh tai, tin lengpa David kom'a ga seipeh tang e,” ati. 
Ahinla Joab chun ajah a aseiye, “Tuni nangma thupole a napan thei louding ahi; nidang dang leh thupon pang nan nate, tunia vang thupo a napan thei louhel ding ahi; ijeh-inem itileh lengpa chapa athi tai,” ati. 
Chuin Joab chun mipa kom'a, “Nangma chen lang nathil mubang bang chun, lengpa gaseipeh tan,” ati. Chuin mipa jong chu salam bolnan akun in, chujouvin alhaijel tai. 
Ahin Ahimaaz chu Joab kom'a aseikit in, “Ipi soh jongleh, lungset tah in kei jong mipa nunga khun lhai tang e,’ atikit leuvin ahi. Hichun Joab chun aphoh in, “Na thupoh jeh a kipaman bon mulou dinga, ipi pen deichat na a nangma nalhai jeng ding ham, chapa?” ati. 
Ahivangin aman, “Heng'e, hijongleh kei jong lhai jeng ing e,” atia aseikit phat in, Joab jong chun ajah a aseipeh in, “Aphai, lhaijin,” ati tai. Chutah chun Ahimaaz chu phaicham lang Mahanaim lampi a chun alhai in ahileh Ethiopia mipa chu akhel tai. 
David chu kelkot teni kikah a toujing jeng ahin, kelkot ngahpa chu kelkot chung, inchung achun akaldoh in palvum a chun aum'in ahileh mikhat achanga amaho langa hunglhai khat amu tan ahi. 
Chuphat in kelkot ngah pan lengpa ahin kouvin aseipeh tai. Lengpan jong ale seipeh kit in, “Achang ahileh thusoh ahinpoh ding ahi,” ati. “Ahung lhailut pai-pai in, ahin nailut tan, 
chuin kelkot ngah pan michom khat ma amukit in, ama jong chu ahung lhai-lhai jeng e; chuphat in kelkot ngah pan kelkot phung lama chun ahin kouvin, “Ven, ven, mi khat jong achang seh in ahung lhai kit e!” ati. Lengpan jong asei kit in, “Ama jong khun thupha ahin poh hiya khu,” ati. 
Kelkot ngah pa chun, “Ahung lhai masajo pa khu Zadok chapa Ahimaaz lhaitoh kakilom sah lheh jenge,” atileh; lengpan jong, “Ama chu mipha ahin thupha ahin poh hiteiyin te,” ati. 
Chutah chun Ahimaaz hin lengpa ahin kouvin, “Ijakai apha sohkeiye,” ati. Chujongleh ama chu lengpa angsung a abohkhup in tol'a amai asulut in aseiye, “Pakai na Pathen, kapu lengpa douna akhut lam jouse eipedoh sohkei uvah ama chu nunnom hen,” ati. 
Hichun lengpan jong adong pai in, “Gollhangpa Absalom ima tilou hinam?” atileh, Ahimaaz chun adonbut in, “Nasohpa hi Joab in eihin sol pet chun mihonpi kinong lul-lul kamun, ipi iti ham ti kahepoi,” ati tai. 
Chuphat in lengpan jong, “ kikhin doh inlang hikom'a khun ga ding tan,” atileh; ama jong akikhin doh pai jengin, ading thipbeh jeng tai. 
Vetan, hichun Ethiopia mipa chu ahunglhung pai tan ahi; hichun Ethiopia mipan hitin asei e, “Kapu lengpa dingin thupha tah kahinpoi! Ijeh-inem itileh Pakaijin tunia nangma dou dia kipan mite khut a kon'in na huhdoh tan ahi,” ati. 
Chuin lengpan jong Ethiopia mipa chu adong paiyin, “Gollhangpa melma te jouse leh nangma douna a thilse bol dinga kipan jouse hiche gollhangpa chutoh bang sohkei uhen,!”ati tai. 
Chuphat in lengpa chu adipdam behseh jengin, hichun indan chungnunga chun akaldoh in, akaptai; achepum pumin, “O kachapa Absalom! Kachapa; kachapa Absalom! Nang khel a kei kathi leh iti ham! Vo Absalom, kachapa, kachapa!” atin ahi.
