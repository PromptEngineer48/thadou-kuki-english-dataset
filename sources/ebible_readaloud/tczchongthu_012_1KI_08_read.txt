1 Lengte.
8.
Solomon Lengpan Israel te phung bahkai ho leh phung jouse lamkai ho leh upaho jouse Jerusalem'a hunga David khopi Zion a konna Pakai Kitepna Thingkhong chu hinpua Hou-In'a thahlut dingin akou khom tan ahi. 
Hiti chun Israel te chu lha sagi channa Ethanim lhasung in hoibitna golvah umkhom dingin ahung kikhom tauvin ahi. 
Israel lamkaiho jouse ahung kikhop khom phat un thempu hon apu uvin akipat doh tauve. 
Levi te le thempu hon Pakai umpina ponbuh chuleh manchah thengho jouse chutoh Hou-In'ah apolut un ahi. 
Solomon Lengpa leh Israel mipi ho jouse chu Pakai Kitepna Thingkhong masanga chun akikhom un kelngoi tamtah leh bongchal tamtah athat un kilhaina pumgo thilto abol'un agancha tha hou chu atam behseh jeh in simjou ahipon ahi! 
Hichun thempu hon Pakai Kitepna Thingkhong chu Hou-In sunga apolut un cherub teni lhaving noi Muntheng Chungnung pen achun akoi tauvin ahi. 
Hiti chun thingkong chuleh aputna moljol teni jong chu cherub teni lhalim noija chun aum tan ahi. 
Hiche aputna mol teni hi Mun Theng Pen masang’a chu kikoi ahin Mun Theng apat a chu idin leh jangpet in akimu theijin, mun choma konin vang akimu thei pon ahi. Hiche moljol teni hi tuni geijin aum nalaije. 
Hiche Kitepna Thingkhong sung’a hin thil dang aum pon Israel te Egypt a konna ahung potdoh uva Pathen'in Sinai Mol’a Mose ana peh Israel te toh akitepna songpheng teni bou chu aum'in ahi. 
Thempuho chu Mun Theng Pen a konna ahung potdoh tengu leh meilom sa-tah chun Hou-In chu akhu khum jeng jin ahi 
Meilom chun Hou-In ahin khu tengleh Pathen loupina chun Hou-In alodimji in Thempu hon na atong jom theiji tapouvin ahi. 
Hiteng chuleh Solomon in taona amang jin, “O Pakai, meilom a khun kaumji na-na ti, 
Pakai tuhin tonsotna na chenna thei dingin hiche Hou-In thupitah hi ka sahdoh peh tan ahi!” ati. 
Hijou teng chuleh lengpa chu ahung kiheijin avel’a ding mihonpi hiti hin phatthei aboh in ahi. 
“Pakai, Israel te Pathen chu thangvah in umhen! Aman kapa David kom’a ana kitepna chu amolso tan ahi, ajeh chu Aman kapa kom'ah, 
‘Kami Israel te chu Egypt a konna kahin puidoh apat chun, keima ei kihouna ding Hou-In akisahna dingin Israel te chenna gam khopi khatcha jong kana lhengpon ahinlah David nangma hi kami Israel te chunga vaihom dingin kana lhengin ahi!’” anati. 
Chuin Solomon chun aseiben, “Kapa, David, chun Pakai Israel te Pathen min loupi dopsangna dingin Hou-In ana sagon ahi. 
Pakaijin Keima min loupina ding’a Hou-In sah nagot nalungtup chu ahoinai, 
hinlah hiche Hou-In sa ding chu nang nahipoi, keima min loupina Hou-In sa ding chu na chate lah a khatpen in asah ding ahi bouve!” ati. 
“Tun Pakai nangin na kitepna chu na lolhin sah tan kapa khel'in keima leng kahung chang tan chuleh Pakai nangma nakitepna bangin Israel laltouna a chun keima katou tan ahi. Tun Israel Pakai nangma min loupina dingin Hou-In chu kahin tungdoh tan ahi. 
Chuleh Pakai nangin kapu kapa te’u Egypt a konna nahin puidoh hotoh na-na Kitepna Thingkhong jong chu akoina ding kahin gong tan ahi,” ati. 
Hijou chun, Solomon chu Israel mipite jouse masang Pakai maicham noija chun adingin, vanlam’ah akhut adom sang'in, 
hitin atao tai, “Vo Pakai Israel Pathen, hiche vannoi leiset ahin nang tobang Pathen dang aumpoi. Nangma mitmua alungthim pumpia kalsongho chungah na kitepna dungjuijin na ngailutna lhasam louhel'in na musah jingin ahi. 
Nangin kapa David kom'a nakitepna chu na lhakang pon ahi. Nangman nakam’a na phondoh nakitepna bangin na khut tah in tunin na lolhin sah tan ahi,” ati. 
“Chule tun, O Pakai, Israel Pathen, nangin kapa David kom'a na-na kitepna aban jong chu subulhit tan. Ajeh chu nangman ama kom’a na-na sei chun, 'Na chilhah ten lamdih ahin chepi uva, nang chouna banga kitahna neitah a ajui uva ahileh na chate lah a khat pen-pen chu Israel laltouna a hung toupeh ding ahi bouve nati chu.' 
Tun, O Israel Pathen, nasohpa kapa David kom’a hitia na-na kitepna chu tuhin subulhit tan. 
Ahinlah Pathen chu hiche leiset a hi iti chen ding ham? Ijeh inem itileh, van sangpen in jong nadol ponte chuti ahileh hiche keiman ka tundoh Hou-In hin iti nadol ding ham? 
Hijeng jongleh, O Pakai ka Pathen, ka taona le kathumna hi nei ngaipeh in, nasohpa na kom’a ka kana le ka taona hi nei ngaipeh teijin. 
Nangin 'Kamin um jing ding ahi,' na-na tina hiche Hou-In hi asun ajanin hin velhi jingin lang, hiche lang ngatna katao chan’in nangin kataona nei ngaipeh in. 
Nami Israel te lah ahin keima kahin hiche lang ngatna kisunemtah le ngaichatna nei tah a kahung tao tengu leh neihin ngaipeh teijun, van na chenna mun a konna nahin jah teng jongleh nei ngaipeh un, najah tengleh nei ngaidam’un. 
Khat touvin mikhat chunga bolkhel aneiya, hiche Hou-In maicham maiya hi kamopoi tia kihahsel a auma ahileh, 
chuteng nangin vanna kon'in hin ngaijin lang mihehpa le akihehpa kikah a thu hintan’in lang athemmo chu achan dingdol chansah jengin, chuleh themmona nei loupa chu them chansah jengin. 
Nami Israel ten nangma douna a achonset jeh uva agalmi hon ahinjo uva chuteng ama hon athemmonau ahin kihet uva na kom ahinbel uva hiche Hou-In a hi ahung tao uva ahileh, 
hiteng chule vanna kon'in ataonau hin ngaijin lang nami Israel te hi achonset nau hin ngaidam inlang, apu apateu na-na pehna hiche gam'ah hinlepui kit teijin. 
Namite hi nangma douna a achonset jeh uva go na juhsahlou tengleh amaho achonset nau ahung kisih uva namin ahin pat uva hiche Hou-In lang ngatna ahung tao teng uleh, 
nangman van a kon'in ataonau hin ngaijin lang nami Israel te hi achonset nau hin ngaidam in. Ama ho hin lampi dih achepi theina diuvin hin hil’in chuleh amaho ding tumbeh a napeh gam leiset chunga hin go hin juhsah kit jitan. 
Ijem tia gamsung’a hi kellhah ham ahilouleh natna hivei lang’a ham ahilou jongleh anche louhing ho khaokho te ham, lungkang te ham ahung lan jeng ham ahilouleh akhopi hou hi min ahin delkhum jeng ham ahi louva natset ham in ahin lonvuh jengu ham, 
nami Israel te hin hiche Hou-In lang hi ahin ngatna aban ahin jah uva ahung tao uva ahahsat nau hi ahin lhut teng uleh, 
hiteng chule na chenna van gam a kon'in hin ngaidam in. Na mite hin achonnau dungjuiya achan diuva lom chu pejengin, ajeh chu nangin mi lungthim jouse jong nahet sohkei sa ahi. 
Hitia chu nabol teng apu apateu na-na pehna hiche gam ahi achen laise uvin nangma na ging jing tauvinte. 
Khonung tengleh nami Israel te hilou gamchom miho jong hin, nangma hina hin hedoh untin, namin jal’a gamla tah a kon a hi hung diu ahi. 
Ajeh chu amaho hin namin loupi kithan hi ahin hetdoh diu, naban thahatna ahin bol diu chutengleh amahon jong Hou-In lang hi ahin ngat uva hung tao diu, 
hiteng chule na chenna van a kon'in hin ngaipeh inlang chule ataonau chu hin sanpeh in. Hitia chu nami Israel ten nahet bang uva hi leiset chung’a mipi ho jouse hin nahina ahin het diu nahin gin diu ahi. Amaho jong hin namin loupina dinga hiche Hou-In kahin tundoh hi ahin het diu ahi. 
Namite hi agalteu toh kisat dinga akon uva hahsatna ato jeh uva keiman namin loupina dinga kahin tundoh hiche Hou-In ahin ngat uva ahung tao tengu leh, 
chuteng van mun a kon'in ataonau hin ngaijin lang angaichat u hin suh bulhit peh in. 
Amaho nangma douna a ahung chonset jeng uva ahileh koi hi achonse khalou um am? Nangma jeng jong nalunghanga agal miteu na josah thei ahilouleh agal miteuvin agam uva gal hing’a na kaimang sah thei jong ahi. 
Ahinlah hiche a asoh channau migam’a chu alung’u ahin hei uva nangma kom ahinbel uva, 'Keiho ka chonse uvin thilse tah tah bol kahi tauve,' atiuva ahung tao uleh, 
hitia chu alungthim pumpi uva nangma kom ahinbel uva, hiche asohchan nau gamchom mun a konna chu nangin apu apateu na-na pehna gam lang ahin ngat’u ahilouleh nangin napeh khopi lang ahin ngat u ahilouleh namin loupina dinga kahin tundoh hiche Hou-In lang hi ahin ngat uva ahung tao uleh, 
hiteng chuleh na chenna van a kon'in atao nau chu hin ngai inlang ahahsat nau hin vetpeh teijin. 
Chujongleh na mite hi nangma douna a achonset tengu leh nangma doumah abolnau achonset nau hin ngaidam teijin. Gal hing’a mangho agal miteu khotona hin musah teijin, 
ajeh chu amaho namite Egypt meibang’a sa thohgimna a konna nahin puidoh na deilhen nam mite ahiuve. 
Keima thil thum leh nami Israel te thil thum neihin vetpeh unlang amaho hin itih phat hijongleh na kom’a akanau ngaipeh tei-teijin. 
Ajeh chu, O Chungnungpen Pakai, nangin nasoh Mose kom’a chun Israel te hi leiset chunga namdang ho lah a kon’a nanga dinga nami deilhen ahiuve na-na tin ahi,” ati. 
Solomon in hiche Pakai kom’a atao sunga chu akhut teni van chung langa adop sang’a ana dilsua ahin; taona ho leh athil thum ho hi achai phat in Pakai maicham maija konin ading doh tan ahi. 
Israel mipi ho jouse chu ogingtah in phatthei aboh in: 
“Pathen chu thangvah in umhen, ajeh chu ami Israel tehi aman ana kitepna jouse hi eina teppeh bang bangun, kicholdona phat chu eimu sah tauve. Aman alhachapa Mose a kon’a ana phondohsa ana kitepna thil loupitah ho chu khatcha athon um louvin ahin tongdoh sohkei tan ahi. 
Pakai I-Pathen uvin ipu ipate’u ana umpi bangin eiho jong eiumpi jing tauhen, aman ei dalhah da uhenlang pai jong eipaidoh hihbeh uhen. 
Imalam jousea ama deilam hinkho eimansah uhen lang, chuleh ama thupeh leh Dan semho einitsah uhenlang chuleh alung lhaina lampia ipu ipateu ana pui bangin eiho jong eipui uhen. 
Chuleh Pakai anga tunia ka taona thucheng ho hi asun ajanin a angsung’a ging jing hen, hitia chu ami Israel te hi nitin ingaichat dungjui uva thudih a eipui theina diuvin. 
Vannoi leiset a cheng mihem jousen Pakai bou hi Pathen ahin adang aumpoi ti ahin het diu ahitai. 
Hiteng chuleh nang hon jong Pakai I-Pathen kitahna lhingsetna najui diu, chuleh a chondan ho leh a thupeh ho jouse hi tuni chan geija nahin jui ahileh najui lhungkei thei diu ahi,” ati. 
Hijou chun lengpa leh Israel mipi ten kilhaina gantha abol tauvin ahi. 
Solomon in chamna thilto in abongchal 22,000 atoh in kelngoite le kelcha 12,000 atoh in ahi. Hiti hin lengpa le Israel mipi ten Pakai Hou-In achun todohna anei un ahi. 
Hiche nikho ma-ma hin lengpan Pakai Hou-In maiya hongcha chu athenso in ahi. Aman hikom'a chun pumgo thilto atoh in, lhosoh thilto jong atoh doh in chamna thil to sathao jong atoh in asuhtheng ahi, ajeh chu Pakai angsung’a kido sum-eng maicham chu pumgo thiltona, lhosoh thiltona, chamna thilto, sathaoho todohna dinga kitung chu aneo behseh tan ahi. 
Hijou chun Solomon leh Israel pumpin Pakai I-Pathen'u angsunga hoibitna golvah kut abol'un ahi. Sahlanga Lebo-hamath apat lhanglam Egypt twinah putna chan geijin mipi tamtah akikhom doh un ahi. Hiche golvah hi agomin nisom le nili alut in nisagi hi maicham todohna ahin, nisagi hi hoibitna golvah kut ahi. 
Hiche golvah hi akichai soh phat in, Solomon in miho chu a incheh a asol tan ahi. Ama hon lengpa chu phatthei aboh un kipah tah le thanom tah in ain uva akinung le tauvin ahi. Ajeh chu Pakai hi alhacha David ding leh Israel mipite dingin apha lheh jengin ahi.
