1 Lengte.
9.
Tun Solomon in Pakai Hou-In leh ama leng inpi asa chai tan ahi. Aman toh dinga agontoh dungjuijin ijakai atongchai sohhel tan ahi. 
Hichun Pakai chu tu masang’a Gibeon a ana kilah bang bangin, tun anivei channa in Solomon kom'ah ahung kilah kit tan ahi. 
Pakaijin aheng'ah aseijin, “Keiman na taona le na ngaichat nahin lhut ho chu kahe tai. Keiman hiche Hou-In hi ka thenso tai. Hiche hi a itih channa keima min kija jing na dinga na sahdoh ahitai. Hiche hi ka lungthim gil’a ka ngainat khat ahijeh in keiman hiche hi vesui jing nang’e. 
Chuleh nangin napa David banga kitah tah leh lungtheng tah a nei juiya, kathupeh leh ka Dan ho nanit jing’a ka chondan najui jing’a ahileh, 
hiteng chuleh Israel lenggam hi atonsotna katundoh jing ding ahi. Ajeh chu napa David kom’a kana kitepna banga: 'Israel laltouna’a hi nachilhah khat katousah jing ding ahi.' 
“Ahinla nangma ahilouleh na chilhah ten nangma ka seipeh, ka Dan sem ho leh kathupeh ho hi na paidoh a, na juida a pathen dang nahou uva na jon uleh, 
chuteng keiman hiche gam kapeh nauva Israel hi kakaidoh ding kamin loupina ding’a hiche Hou-In ka thensosa jong hi kapaidoh ding chule Israel jong hi namdang te nuisat leh tot thanghoiya naum ding’u ahi. 
Chujongleh tuhin hiche Hou-In hi thupi lheh jeng jongleh hiteng chule ahin hopa jousen alu u apei uva adatmo diu ahi. Amaho kidongto uvin tin, “Iti dan a Pakaijin hiche gam le hiche Hou-In hi manlou aso hitam?” ati diu ahi. 
Chule amaho chu hitia hi kidonbutto diu ahi, “Ajeh chu hiche mite hin Egypt a kon’a apu apa teu hin puidoh a, Pakai a Pathen’u chu adonda uva pathen dang ahou uva, achibai jeh u ahi, hijeh a chu Pakaijin achung uva hitobang hamsetna hi alhunsah ahitai,” ati diu ahi. 
Solomon in Pakai Hou-In asahna le ama leng inpi sahna a kum somni asuhlut nung phat kichai lam in, 
aman Tyre Lengpa Hiram chu Galilee gam’a khopi somni chu ana pen ahi. (Hiram hin, Solomon in ana thum dungjuiya, Cedar le Cypress thing chuleh sana ana peh ahi.) 
Ahin Tyre a kon'in Hiram hi ahungin Solomon in apeh khopi ho chu ahung ven ahileh akipah behseh pon ahi. 
Hijeh chun Hiram in Solomon jah a, “Sopi, hiche khopiho pikhu ipi ding ham?” atin ahi. Hijeh chun Hiram in hiche gam chu Cabul (hichu “panna bei”) tin amin asah e. Tunigei hin hiti chun amin akivo den in ahi. 
Ahiyeng vang'in, Hiram in Solomon chu sana talent jakhat le somni ana pen ahi. 
Solomon lengpan soh natong mi ana goiya ana sahdoh ho chu; Pakai Hou-In, Leng inpi le avel ho, Jerusalem pal kikaikhum, Hazor, Megiddo leh Gezer khopi ho chu ahi. 
Egypt lengpa Pharaoh in Gezer chu gal anokhum’a analah a, Canaan mite ana thagam’a meiya ahal lhah jou uva hiche khopi chu Solomon toh kicheng achanu akanpeh ahi. 
Solomon in hiche Gezer khopi chu asapha kit in, aman Beth-horon noi lang khopi ho jong chu asapha kit in ahi. 
Hiche gamsung’a gammang lah a um Baalath le Tamar jong chu ahi. 
Aman thil kholna ding leh sakol ho leh sakol kangtalai ho koina dingin jong khopi ho ana sadoh in ahi. Aman Jerusalem le Lebanon a chuleh agamsung pumpi’a asah doh nom jouse ana sahdoh sohkei ahi. 
Agamsung’a chun Israel mi hilou phabep jong ana chengun ahi. Ama ho chu Amon mite, Hit mite, Periz mite, Hiv mite leh Jebus mite ahiuve. 
Ama ho hi Israel miten ana thamoh nam mite son le chilhah ho ahiuve. Hijeh chun Solomon in amaho chu soh natong in ana pansah in hiti hin ama hon tuni geijin soh natong in ana pang jingun ahi. 
Solomon in Israel te vang namna a natong lah a ana pansah pon ahi. Hiti hin Israel mite chu galsat sepai leh gal lamkai jat chom-chom in ana pansah in sakol kangtalai tol'in jong ana pansah in ahi. 
Solomon in gamsung’a kivaihomna a natoh jat chom-choma alamkai ding leh avesui dingin mi 550 ana pansah in ahi. 
Solomon in Pharaoh chanu akichenpi ajinu chu David khopi’a kon'in Leng inpi thah ama ding’a ana sahpeh na mun achun achonlut in ahi. Hiche achun Solomon'in hiche inpi to kimat in dang jong asan ahi. 
Solomon in Pakaiya maicham semna dinga ana sah mun ahin pumgo thilto le chamna thilto kumkhat sungin thumvei ana boljin ahi. Aman Pakaiya gimnamtui jong ana halnam jin ahi. Hiti hin Hou-In sahdohna a natohna jouse chu ana chai sohkei jin ahi. 
Solomon Lengpan Twikhanglen pang, Edom gam'ah Elath kongkai mun kom'ah kong-innei hon khat ana sem’e. 
Hiram in kongtol themho Solomon mite toh kongtol khom dingin ahin solpeh in ahi. 
Ama hon Ophir mun a atol'un chule Solomon dingin sana ton som le gup ahin pohpeh un ahi.
