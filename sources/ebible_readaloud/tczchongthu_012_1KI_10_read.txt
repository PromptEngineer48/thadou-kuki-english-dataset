1 Lengte.
10.
Sheba lengnu in Solomon minthanna hi ichan geija, Pakai min loupina adopsang ho thu ajah phat in thudoh hah tah-tah ho doh a patep dingin ahung kon tan ahi. 
Ama chu anungjui tamtah toh, gimnamtui tamtah tah, sana le songmantam tamtah ahinpo uvin sangongsao honkhat ah ahung touvun Jerusalem ahunglhung tauvin ahi. Amanu'n Solomon toh akimuto phat in akihoulimpi in thudoh ahung kigotna jouse tamtah chu adong tan ahi. 
Solomon in athudoh jouse chu bailam tah in adonbut sohkeijin, khatcha jong lengpa ding chun ahahsa aumpon ahi. 
Sheba lengnu chun Solomon chu ichan geija ching ham tichu amu chet jeh chuleh aleng inpi kisah dan chu amudoh phat in, 
alungthim’ah datmona in adim lheh jing tan ahi. Amanu’n aneh achah u dokhang chung’a umho chu amun, avaihomna noija kinpi natohna von ho, acha donnau khon domho chuleh Solomon'in Pakai Hou-In a namtui alhut nam ho chu amun ahi. 
Amanu in asaptho jengin lengpa jah ah, “Na chihna le na tohdoh ho kagam sung’a kaum’a kajah ho jouse chu adih sohkeije!” ati. 
Ka thujah ho chu keima tah kahunga kamit tah a kahung muchet tokahsen kana tahsan pon ahi. Ahin tahbeh in seileng ken kanajah ho chu akehkhat jeng jong alhing joupoi. Na chihna ho leh na haona ho hi kanajah ho chu gam chengin akho khal in ahi. 
Na mipite hin ichan geijin nom asa tadiuvem! Na vaihomna noiya natong na masang’a ding jing’a na chihna thungai jing ho hi ichan geija hamphatna chang hitauvem! 
Pakai na Pathen chu thangvah in umhen, aman lung lhaisel’a Israel laltouna hi na losah ahitai. Na Pakaijin Israel te tang thei louva angailut jeh a kitahna le dihna a vaipo dinga nang hi aleng uva na tundoh ahitai,” ati. 
Hijou chun amanu’n lengpa chu sana pounds 900, gimnamtui leh songmantam tamtah jong apen ahi. Sheba lengnun Solomon lengpa gimnamtui apeh tobang hi koiman ape khapon ahi. 
(Hiche ban a jong hin Hiram kongtol hon Ophir a kon'in sana toh thingnamtui akihetna dimset toh chuleh songmantam hotoh ahinpo uvin ahi. 
Solomon lengpan hiche thingnamtui ho chu Pakai Hou-In douna le lengpa in douna in amangin, lathem ho dingin semjang saithei leh selangdah din jong asempeh in ahi. Tumasang’a ahin tunung’a ahin hitobang thing namtui ahung kipolut aum kha tapon ahi.) 
Solomon lengpa chun Sheba lengnu chu athum jouse ape jengin chuleh angaichat chan chu hongphal tah in anape jengin ahi. Hijou chun Sheba lengnu chu asohte cheng chutoh agam lang’ah ana kile kittan ahi. 
Kumseh a Solomon lengpa henga sana hunglut hi tons 25 alhingin ahi. 
Hiche hi akivei gunset ho leh kahlah a thil joh leho a konna kai kila ho chuleh Arab lang’a gamvaipo ho a konna kimu ho pang nailou ahi. 
Solomon lengpan sana a kikhong Ompho lentah tah 200 asemin khat cheh hi pound som le nga in agih in ahi. 
Aman sana a kikhong Ompho aneoloi 300 jong asemin khat cheh chu pound li lhing deh agih in ahi. Lengpan hiche Ompho ho hi Lebanon Gammang kiti a Leng Inpi a chun akoijin ahi. 
Solomon lengpan laltouna lentah khat jong saiha in asemin ahi. Hiche laltouna a kisem chu sana mangchan hoitah in atomjolsel in ahi. 
Laltouna chu akaltouna ding kalbi gup kido touva ahin, laltouna achungnung lam chu akikol'in ahi. Hiche laltouna ajet aveija chun khut ngapna aum cheh in chule alangto a chun Keipi bahkai ading limkhat khat toh asemin ahi. 
Hiche kalbi gup chu khat cheh pang achun Keipi bahkai khat cheh adingin ahileh abon'in Keipi bahkai lim som le ni alhing in ahi. Vannoi leiset a laltouna dang ho hiche toh tekahpi thei ding aumpon ahi. 
Solomon lengpa twidonna khon jouse chu sana thenga kisem ngen ahin, chuleh Lebanon Gammang kiti Leng Inpi sung’a manchah jouse jong chu sana a kisem ngen ahin, dangka a kisem khatcha jong aum pon ahi, ajeh chu Solomon khanglai achun dangka chun aman aneipon ahi. 
Lengpan kiveina a manchah ding kong innei khat jong aneijin, hiche ho chu Hiram kong innei hotoh aki tolkhom jin ahi. Hiche kong innei ho hi kum thumseh leh inlang'ah ahung kile jiuvin, sana, dangka, saiha, guldu leh vahong kong dimset set in ahinpo jiuvin ahi. 
Hiti hin Solomon lengpa hi leiset chunga leng dang ho sang'in ahao jon chule aching jong aching jon ahi. 
Hijeh chun Pathen'in apeh, chihna thungai ding leh thudoh nei dingin muntin a kon'in mipi ahung hung jingun ahi. 
Kumseh in ama vil’a hung jousen kipa thilpeh in sana, dangka, ponsil, gal manchah, gimnamtui, sakol ho leh sakolpol ho ahin poh peh jingun ahi. 
Solomon sakol ho leh sakol kangtalai ho jong thaneitah in ana tungdoh in ahi. Aman sakol 12,000 aneijin, sakol kangtalai 1,400 aneijin ahi. Aman phabep chu sakol kangtalai koina khopi ho a akoiyin phabep chu Jerusalem sung ama kom naina ah akoijin ahi. 
Lengpan dangka aneiho vang chu songtoh ana bahsah jengin ahi. Chuleh Cedar thing mantam tah ho jong chu Judah gam'a keh theipi thing toh abahsah jengin ahi. 
Solomon in sakol ho hi Egypt leh Cilicia a konna achohji ahin, lengpa ding’a thilcho ho hin Cilicia mun a hi kimantah a achoh jiu ahi. 
Hiche phat lai chun Egypt a kon'in sakol kangtalai khat chu dangka 600 a kicho ahin sakol khat chu dangka150 a kichoji ahi. Hijouteng chuleh Hit leh Aram lengho kom'a ajoh son kitjiu ahi.
