1 Lengte.
16.
Hiche hi Pakaiya konna Hanani chapa Jehu Themgao mangcha a Baasha Lengpa kom'a thupeh ahi. 
“Keiman nangma hi vutvai lah a konna katundoh a kami Israel te chunga vaihom’a ka pansah nahi, ahinlah nangin Jeroboam setna lampi najuijin nangin jong Israel te hi na chonset sah in kalung na hansah tan ahi. 
Hijeh chun keiman Nebat chapa Jeroboam chilhah te ka suhmang bang’a nang le na insung mite jong ka suhmang ding ahi. 
Baasha insung mite khopi sunga thiden ho chu, uicha ten aneh diu, gamlah a thiho chu muthong ten aneh diu ahi,” ati. 
Baasha'n vai ahom sunga thilsoh ho chuleh aman athaneina a ana tohdoh ho Israel Lengte Thusimbu a aum'in ahi. 
Baasha athi phat chun, Tirzah mun'ah avui tauvin ahi. Hijou chun achapa Elah chu ama khel in leng ahung changtan ahi. 
Hanani chapa themgao Jehu mangchan Pakaijin Baasha leh a insung mite dounan thu ana seijin ahi. Baasha in Jeroboam leh a insung miten atohbang bang’a Pakai doumah ana boljeh a agihna thu hi ana peh ahin, chuleh hiche Baasha hin Jeroboam insung mite aboncha ana thagam ahi. Pakai lunghanna chu Baasha chunga ana deojah jengin ahi. 
Asa lengpan Judah mite lah a vai apoh kum somni le gup channan, Baasha chapa Elah hin Israel chunga vai ahin hompan tan ahi. Aman Tirzah khopia kumni vai ana hom in ahi. 
Hijou chun lengte galsatna sakol kangtalai ho akehkhat chung’a lamkai Zimri chun ama chu tha ding ana gongin ahi. Tirzah khopia nikhat hi Elah chu khopi sung vaipopa Arza insung a ju ana kham in, 
Zimri chu avahlut in asat lhun athat tai. Hiche hi Asa lengpan Judah mite lah a aleng chan kum somni le sagi alhinna thilsoh ahi. Hichun Zimri chu leng ahung chang tan ahi. 
Zimri hin Baasha insung mite jouse gangtah in ana that jou keijin pasal chapang neokhat cha jeng jong ana hinghoi tapon ahi. Aman a insung lamdol a akinaipi behseh jong hilou chuleh agol apaiho kisan ana that in ahi. 
Hiti hin Pakaijin Jehu themgao mangcha a ana phondohsa bangin, Zimri hin Baasha chilhah ho jouse jong ana sumang soh tan ahi. 
Hiche thilsoh hi Baasha leh achapa Elah chonset jeh leh Israel achonset sah lhon jeh ahi. Hitia hi manlou semthu doilim ho jeh a Pakai Israel Pathen chu ana suh lunghang’u ahi. 
Elah vaihom sunga thilsoh ho leh ana tohdoh ho jouse chu Israel Lengte Thusimbua akijihlut sohkeijin ahi. 
Judah gam'a Asa in lengvai apohna kum somni le sagi lhin kum hin, Israel pumpia Zimri in vai ahin hom in ahinlah Tirzah khopia nisagi bou vai ana homin ahi. Israel sepai ten Philistine khopi Gibbethon chu ana nokhum'un ahi. 
Ama hon Zimri in kitahlou tah in lengpa athat tai tithu ajah phat un, hiche nikho ma-ma chun sepai lamkai Omri chu Israel te Leng dingin ana lhengdoh tauvin ahi. 
Hiti chun Omri in Israel galmi pumpi chu ahin lamkaijin Gibbethon a kon'in Tirzah ahin nokhum tauvin ahi. 
Zimri chun khopi chu alatai ti ahetphat un, hiche khopia chun achen ama le ama meikong akihal khumin athi tan ahi. 
Ajeh chu ama jong hin, Pakai mitmu’n thilse jeng anabol’in ahi. Aman jong Jeroboam in Israel te ana chonset sah tobang chun ana chepin ahi. 
Zimri vaihom sunga thilsoh ho leh aman dan dihlou tah a na anatohna ho jouse Israel Lengte Thusimbua akijihlut sohkeijin ahi. 
Ahinlah tuhin Israel te kehni akiso tauvin ahi. Mipi akeh khat in Ginath chapa Tibni chu achung uva lenga pang din adeijun ahinlah akeh khat in Omri joh chu adeijun ahi. 
Ahin Omri deiho chun Tibni deiho chu ajou tauvin ahi. Hichun Tibni chu athat tauvin Omri joh chun lengmun ahinlo tan ahi. 
Judah gam'a Asa leng chan kum somthum le khat alhin chun, Omri in Israel te lah a vai ahin hompan tan ahi. Aman kum som le ni vai ana hom in kum gup chu Tirzah a ana pansan ahi. 
Hijou chun Omri in tua Samaria kiti thinglhang gam chu aneipa Shemer a kon'in dangka pounds 150 ape in ana kichoh doh in ahi. Aman hichu khopi khat in ana semin aneipa Shemer a kon'in Samaria ana sah ahi. 
Ahinlah Omri in ama masanga lengvaipo ho jouse sangin Pakai mitmun thilse ngen chang anabol in ahi. 
Aman jong Nebat chapa Jeroboam chonsetna jousea Israel te ana chonset sah bangin ama jong hiti hin ana chonin ahi. Mipi ten pannabei milim ana hou uvin Israel Pathen chu ana lunghan sah tauvin ahi. 
Omri vaihom sunga thilsoh dang ho chuleh athaneina chuleh ana tohdoh ho jouse chu Israel Lengte Thusimbu achun akijihlut sohkeijin ahi. 
Omri chu athi phat in, Samaria munna ana vuijun ahi. Ama banin achapa Ahab chun lengmun ahinlo tan ahi. 
Judah gam'a Asa leng chan kum somthum le get ahung lhin chun, Omri chu Israel te lengmun ahinlo pantan ahi. Aman Samaria mun’ah kum somni le ni sungin vai ahin hom’in ahi. 
Omri chapa Ahab hin Pakai mitmun thilse jeng ahin bol’in, ama masang’a lengho jouse sangin thilsejo ahin boldoh in ahi. 
Aman Jeroboam thilse bol chu anung ajui keu hilouvin, Sidon Lengpa Ethbaal chanu Jezebel chu ana kichenpin Baal doi chu ana houtan ahi. 
Amasan Ahab in Samaria mun ah Baal doi phung leh hou-in ana tungdoh in ahi. 
Hijou chun aman Asherah doiphung ana tungdoh kit in, hiti hin aman ama masanga leng dang ho sangin Pakai Israel Pathen chu ana phin lunghang penin ahi. 
Ahab leng vaipoh laitah in, Bethel a kon'in Hiel kitipa in Jericho ahin sempha kittan ahi. Aman inbul khom aphudoh nin achapa tahpen Abiram kitipa chun ahinkho alha tan ahi. Aman asahchaija akehkhat ahin tundoh ahin achapa lhumpenpa chun ahinkho alha tan ahi. Hiche hi Nun chapa Joshua in Pakaiya kon’a Jericho chung chang thu ana phondoh chu guilhung’a ahitai.
