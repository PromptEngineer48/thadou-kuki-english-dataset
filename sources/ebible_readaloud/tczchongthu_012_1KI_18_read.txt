1 Lengte.
18.
Phat chomkhat jou, gojuh louna kum thum alhin kum in, Pakajin Elijah kom’ah, “Chenlang Ahab lengpa kom’ah, Pakaijin go ajuhsah vah ding ahitai gatin,” ati. 
Hichun Elijah jong Ahab kom’ah achen ahi. Alangkhat na Samaria khopia chun kel ana khoh lheh jengin ahi. 
Hijeh chun Ahab chun khopi vesuija pang Obadiah chu ana kouvin ahi. (Ama chu Pakai nungjui dihtah ahi. 
Jezebel’in Pakai themgao jouse tha gam ding ana got lai chun, hiche Obadiah kitipa hin songko mun nia themgao jakhat ana huhdoh in ahi. Aman songko khatna mi 100 ana sellin amaho chu an le twi ana pen ahi.) 
Ahab in Obadiah kom’ah, “Chen lang gamsung jouse gakhol sohkeijin twinah jouse gaven chuleh phaicham jouse jong gachon sohkeijin, ijeh inem itileh hamhing pouna munkhat beh imudoh uva ka sakol ho leh sakolpol phabep beh ihinso thei khat diuham,” ati. 
Hiti chun amani chu gamsung khol ding chun akihom khen lhon'in, Ahab jong ama tah akipan in gamkai lang khat cheh jonin akihomkhen lhon tan ahi. 
Hitia chu Obadiah avale leh, photlot louvin Elijah chun ama komlang ahin jonin ahi. Obadiah chun ama chu ahepai jengin, a angsung’ah abohkhup in ahi. Aman ajah a, “Kapu Elijah na hilou haimo?” ati. 
Elijah in, “Kahinai, tun, chen lang napupa kom’ah, ‘Elijah hikom’ah aum’e’ gatin,” ati. 
Ahivangin Obadiah chun, “Ipi pen tah a keiman chonsetna kaneiya, nangman na sohpa keima hi Ahab khutna nei pehdoh a ama thasah ding neiti ham?” ati. 
“Hingjing Pakai na Pathen min’a kasei ahi, kapu Ahab in nangma holnan leiset chunga lenggam jouse le nam jouse hi kolkimvel'a ahol soh ahitai. Elijah hikom'ah aumpoi tihi ama kom alhut teng ule hiche lenggam mite chu akihahsel sahji ahi. 
Ahinla tun nangman napupa jah a, ‘Elijah hikom'ah aum’e’ gatin neiti. 
Ahin nangma ka dalhah lhah teng lhagaovin nangma kahet khah louna mun khat ah, nalhoh mangkit intin, Ahab in nangma na mudoh louding ahi. Hiteng chuleh na sohpa keima hi Pathen gingtah mi kahi vang'in Ahab chun eithalo tei ding ahi. 
Jezebel lengnun Pakai Themgao te thagam dia agot laiya, thilsoh chu koiman najah a aseilou ham? Keiman themgao ho 100 chu songko nia kana seldoh’a an le twi kana peh ahi. 
Ahin tun nangin chenlang napupa jah a, ‘Elijah hikom'ah aum’e,’ gatin nati. Hepu, hichu kasei leh Ahab in eitha tei-tei ding ahi,” ati. 
Ahinlah Elijah in, “Keima a angsung’a kadin jing, Pakai thaneipen minna kihahsel’a kasei ahi, tuni tah a hi Ahab chu keiman kakimupi tei ding ahi,” ati. 
Hijeh chun Obadiah in Ahab kom’ah, “Elijah ahung’e,” aga tin ahileh, Ahab chu Elijah toh kimuto dingin achen ahi. 
Ahab in amu phat in ahin samin, “Israel suboi jingpa nangma nahung hinam?” atin ahi. 
Elijah in adonbut in, “Keiman Israel kasuboi poi, nangma le na insung mite in na suhboi ahi bouve, ajeh chu nangin Pakai thupeh nit louvin, Baal semthu pathen joh nahou uvin ahi. 
Tun Israel te jouse Carmel mol’ah khom in, Baal themgao 450 toh Asherah themgao jali Jazebel in avah ho chu kouvin,” ati. 
Hiti chun Ahab in Israel mipi ho leh themgao ho chu Carmel mol ah akou khom in ahi. 
Hichun Elijah chu amaho masang ah ading doh in, “Phat i-chanpi hi lunggel khenna nei louva lungni pua naum jom jing nahlai diu ham? Pakai hi Pathen ahi natiu leh jui jeng’un, ahinla Baal hi pathen ahi natiu leh jui jeng’un,” atin ahi. Ahinlah mipi chu athipchet jengun ahi. 
Hijou chun Elijah in amaho kom’ah aseitai, “Pakai themgao akihing hoiya keima changbou kahitai, Baal doi themgao ho vang 450 alhing uve. 
Tun bongchal ni hin kailut uvin lang, ama hon bongchal khat kilhen masat uvin tin, that uhenlang, asemtel jou tengu leh thinglom chung’a khun koi uhen, meivang ati theilou diu ahi. Kenjong bongchal khat joh chu that ing ting thinglom chunga koi jing tin amavang mei kati louding ahi. 
Hijou teng chuleh na pathen’u min kou un, chule keiman ka Pakai min kakou ding ahi. Hiteng chuleh mei konga hin donbut pen-pen chu, Pathen dih ahi ti hetna ahi jenge,” ati. Hichu mipi hon anop peh tauvin ahi. 
Hichun Elijah in Baal themgao ho kom'ah, “Nangho atamjo nahiuvin, bol masauvin. Bongchal khat joh chu kilhen unlang kisem un, na pathen’u chu kouvun, amavang thinglom chung’a chun mei tih hih un,” ati. 
Hiti chun ama hon bongchal khat chu alheng un, athat un, asemtoh un, maicham chunga chun akoiyun, jingkah apat sun geijin, “O Baal neihouvun” tin apeng-peng jeng tauvin ahi. Hijou chun amaho alam’un, maicham vella chun asuh chop atou chop jingun ahi. 
Sunkim ahung hi phat in, Elijah chun amaho chu atot in, “Na hasap jep u ngai inte ajeh chu ama pathen ahin, asun imut phat ahiloule, akicholdo phat ahilouleh akhol jin pet ahilou le, a imut pet hija suhkhah ngai himai thei ahi,” ati peh’e. 
Hijeh chun ama hon, ahasap cheh, aha sap cheh jengun chuleh achon nau dungjuijin chemcha le chamjam in athiu ahung kikhodoh masang sen aki at-chen chen jengun ahi. 
Hiti chun nilhah lam kilhai phatdon geiyin akap un apengun, ahivangin, ogin ima la aja pouvin imacha donbutna amu deh pouvin ahi. 
Chutah chun Elijah jong chun mipi jouse jah a, “Nei hin nailut uvin,” atileh mipi abonchauvin ahenga ahung kikhinlut soh tauve. Hichun Elijah chun Pakai maicham akivo chimsa chu asem phan ahi. 
Hichun aman Israel phungkhat cheh thalheng dingin songtum somle ni ana lhoh khom’in ahi. 
Chuleh song tumho chunga chun Pakai min'in maicham ana thah semin ahi. 
Elijah chun thinglom jong chu agol golin ajamin chuin bongchal chu asemtel tel’in, thinglom chunga chun ajamtan ahi. Hichun Elijah in aseijin, “Twi dimset set bel li gadom uvin lang, pumgo thilto chung le thinglom chunga chun chap kot sohkeijun,” ati. 
Hichu aboljou un, Elijah in, “Bol kit uvin,” atin ahileh ama hon avellin abolkit tauve. Aman asol kit in, “Thumvei lhinnan gabol kit uvin,” atia ahileh mihon jong athumvei lhinnan abolkit tauve. 
Hiti chun maicham kimvel a chun twi along dimset tan, twilam jong chu twi adimset tan ahi. 
Nilhah lang pumgo thilto phat donin, Elijah themgao pa chu maicham mun'a akal touvin atao tan ahi. “Vo Pakai Abraham Pathen, Isaac Pathen, Jacob Pathen tuni nikhoa hin nangma hi Israel Pathen nahina leh keima hi nasohpa kahina hin phongdoh in, hiche ho jouse hi nangma thupeh a kana bol ahi jong hi hin phongdoh in. 
“Vo Pakai, neihin donbut in! Hiche mite hin nangma hi Pakai, Pathen nahin chule nangin alungthim’u hi nanga dinga na le heipeh u ahitai tihi ahet doh diu ahi,” ati. 
Chutah chun Pakaiya kon'in meikong ahung lenglhan, pumgo thilto bongchal sa chu akavam jengin, thinglom jong akavam jengin, leivui jong aleogo jengin, twilam’a twivei jeng jong chu ahul gohel jengin ahi. 
Mipin hiche thilsoh hi amudoh phat uvin, amai u tol’ah asulut cheh uvin hitin asei tauve, “Pakai—ama hi Pathen ahi, ahiye, Pakai hi Pathen ahi,” atiuve. 
Hijou chun Elijah in mipi ho chu thu apen, “Baal themgao ho chu man’un, khatcha jong lhaso hih un,” ati. Hichun mipi hon amaho chu aman’un, Elijah in Kishon Phaicham a apuisuh in abonchauvin athat gamtan ahi. 
Chuin Elijah chun Ahab kom'ah, “Chenlang an ganen lang twi gadon tan ajeh chu keiman gojuhna dinga van kithin husa kajatai,” ati. 
Hiti chun Ahab jong achen an anen twi adon in ahi. Ahinla Elijah chu Carmel Molchunga akaldoh in, tol achun akun chah kheh in, akhup teni kikah a amai asulut in taona aneijin ahi. 
Chuin asohpa jah a, “Chen lang twikhanglen lam khu gave tan,” ati. Hichun asohpa jong achen agaven, Elijah kom'a ahung kile kit in “Ima kamupoi” ati. Sagivei jen Elijah chun achea aga vet dingin anasol'in ahi. 
Asagi vei channa chun asoh pan hitin aseiye, “Vetan, meilhang neo cha mihem khut phang tebep khat hi twikhanglen a kon'in ahung kitung doh e,” ati. Chuphat in Elijah chun asohpa jah a, “Ahab kom achun cheloijin lang hitin hin gaseipeh in, ‘Na sakol kangtalai ah chun kaldoh inlang na inlanga cheloijin achuti louva ahile geisang le chun gojuh in nalap ding, nache theilou ding ahi, gatipeh in!” ati. 
Hichejou jou chun, van ahung thim cheh-cheh jengin, meilom ahung kikhom khom jeng tan hui ahung nungpan tai, chuin go hajuh in ahung jun ahi. Ahab chun asakol kangtalai akitol in Jezreel henglam’ah gangtah in achetan ahi. 
Hichun Pakaijin Elijah chu thahatna jat chombeh apen, aman jong atai akigah in, Ahab masang’ah ana kheltan in Jezreel kot phunga ana lamtin kittan ahi.
