Lengte Nina.
1.
Ahab lengpa thi jouvin, Moab ten Israel dounan gal ahin sat uvin ahi. 
Nikhat Israel te leng thah, Ahaziah chu Samaria khopi inchung dan kalbia kon'in ahung kitang-lhan akiha suhkhah lheh jengin ahi. Hijeh chun aman Ekron gam mite semthu pathen Baalzebub hou-in’ah mi asol'in, kadam doh jou ding hinam tin agadoh sah in ahi. 
Ahin Pakai vantil khat in Tishbe a konna hung Elijah kom’ah – “Chen Samaria lengpa thupole ho khu gakimaito pin lang hitin gaseipeh in, “Israel lah a hi Pathen umlouva hitam? Nangma ipi dinga Ekron semthu pathen Baalzebub kom’a leng damdoh ding leh damdoh louding donga nache ham?” tin gaseipeh tan ati. 
Tun, hijehchun, Pakaijin hitin aseije, “Nangin nalupna najalkhun na dalhah louding nathiden tei-tei ding ahitai,” ati. Hiti chun Elijah achen hichu aga seipeh tan ahi. 
Hichea thupole ho chu lengpa kom agalhun phat un “I-atileh gang tah a nahung kilekit u hitam?” tin adongin ahi. 
Ama hon adonbut un, “Mikhat kakom uvah ahungin nangho kilekit uvin lang hiche thu hi lengpa kom’a seipeh un eitiuvin ahi,” chuleh Pakaijin hitin aseije – “Israel te lah a Pathen umlou hija Ekron gam’a semthu pathen Baalzebub kom’a na damdoh ding le na damdoh louding donga nache uham?” atipeh uvin ahi. 
Lengpan adongin “Ama chu itobang mihem ham? Chule alim chu itobang ham?” tin ana ledoh kit in ahi. 
Ama hon adonbut un, “Ama chu asam asaovin chuleh akongah savun konggah akigah e,” atiuve. Hichun lengpan jong, “Tishbe a konna Elijah hichula em?” ati. 
Hijou chun aman sepai lamkai khat chu asepai somnga toh, Elijah man ding chun asol tan ahi. Ama hon molchung khat a atoupet agamu uvin ahi. Sepai lamkaipan ama kom’a chun, “Pathen mipa lengpa thupeh dungjuijin hung kumlhan kilhon uhite,” ati. 
Ahin Elijah in sepai lamkaipa kom’a, “Keima Pathen mi kahileh vanna kon'in meikong hung lenglhan tin nang leh namite somnga chu sumang hen,” ati. Hichun vanna kon'in meikong ahung lenglhan amaho chu athat gamtan ahi. 
Hijeh chun lengpan mi somnga toh sepai lamkai khat ma asol kit in, sepai lamkaipa chun “Pathen mipa lengpan hung kumlha loihen ati,” aga tin ahi. 
Hichun Elijah in adonbut in, “Keima Pathen mi kahi leh vanna kon'in meikong hunglha henlang nang le namite som nga chu sumang hen,” ati. Chuti chun vanna kon'in meikong ahung lenglhan amaho chu athat gam kittan ahi. 
Avellin lengpan jong athum channa in sepai lamkai khat mi somnga toh asol kit in ahi. Ahin tuvang'in sepai lamkaipa chu molchung'ah akal touvin Elijah kengbul’ah akhup boh in achun akom’a chun ataovin, “Vo Pathen mipa, kei leh nasohte mi somnga ho hi nei hinghoi teiteijun. 
Ven vanna konna meikong hung lenglha chun mi loini chu athat gamtan ahi. Tunvang lungset tah in nei hinghoi teijun!” atin ahi. 
Hichun Pakai vantil khat in Elijah kom’ah hitin aseije, “Ama hitoh kumlha khom lhon'in, ama hi kicha hih in” ati. Hiti chun Elijah akipatdoh in ama chutoh lengpa kom’ah chun achelhon tan ahi. 
Elijah in lengpa kom’a chun, “Pakaijin hitin aseije nangin ipi bolla Ekron mite semthu pathen Baalzebub kom’a na damdoh ding le na damdoh louding thu donga nasol ham? Israel te lah a Pathen umlou hitam? Hitia hi nachon jeh in tua nalupna hi na dalhah louhel ding ahitai; chuleh nathi tei-tei ding ahi.” atipeh tan ahi. 
Hiti chun Pakaijin Elijah mang chan aseidohsa athutep bang-bang chun Ahaziah chu athi tan ahi. Ahaziah chun ama goulo ding chapa aneilou jeh chun asopipa Joram chu ama banin leng ahung chang tan ahi. Hiche hi Judah leng Jehoshaphat chapa Jehoram leng vaihom kumni channa thilsoh ahi. 
Ahaziah vaihom sunga thilsoh dang ho chu Israel Lengte Thusimbua chun akisunlut in ahi.
