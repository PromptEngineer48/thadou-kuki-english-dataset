Nikhat chu themgao ho lah a khatpen ji ana hia meithai khat hi Elisha kom’ah ahungin ahung pengjah jengin, hitin aseiye, “Nangma noija na natong kajipa chu athitai, aman Pakai agin dan chu nangin nahet ahi. Ahinla tuhin sum kabatpa ahungin kacha teni hi soh a kai ding eigo tauvin ahi,” ati. 
Hichun Elisha'n amanu kom’a chun, “Ipi kabolpeh thei um am? Nei seipeh in, na insung’a hi ipi naneijem?” ati. Amanun jong adonbut in, “Olive thao thei khat tilou imacha adang kaneipoi,” ati. 
Elisha’n jong achutia ahileh “Na-insah na-inlhang hokom hihen, nagol napai hokom hijongleh belkeu namu thei chanin batnin gala tan. 
Chujou teng chule nacha teni chutoh insungah lut unlang, kot kikha khum’un. Chutengleh Olive thao chu atheija kon chun bel keoho a chun sunglhauvin lang adim phatleh kaidohji jiuvin,” ati. 
Hichun amanun jong aseipeh bang-bang chun abol tan ahi. Amanun bel chu adim phat-phat leh acha tenin ahin pelutji lhon'in, hiti chun bel jouse chu adipsoh tauvin ahi. 
Gangtah in Olive thaotwi koina ho jouse chu adim letset tan ahi! Amanun acha teni khat kom’a chun, “Bel khat neihin pehben,” atileh, aman, “aum tapoi!” ati. Hichun Olive thao jong chu abeitan ahi. 
Amanun Pathen mipa kom’a chun thilsoh chu aseijin ahileh, aman amanu kom’a chun, “Tun Olive thao chu joh unlang nabat u chu sadoh tauvin chuleh nang le nacha teni avalla chu nakivah thei diu ahi,” ati. 
Nikhat hi Shunem khopi sunga chun Elisha agachen ahileh numei haotah khat ana chengin, amanu chun a inna anne dingin akouvin ahi. Hiche jou chun hilang chu ahopa chanin hilai mun achun anne dingin akinga jin ahi. 
Amanun ajipa kom’a chun, “Ven, hilanga ahung chan le hung kingaji pa hi Pathen mitheng khat hitei din katahsan’e. 
Hijeh chun ama ding hin inchunga hin indan khat sem hitin lupna khat, dokhang khat, touna khat le thaomei khat koipeh hite. Chutileh ahung kom leh mun anei thei ding ahi” ati. 
Nikhat hi Elisha chu Shunem inna chun ahung kile kit in, a inchung danna chun akal touvin akicholdo tan ahi. 
Aman asohpa Gehazi kom’a chun, “Hiche Shunem numeinu kom’a khun thukhat kasei nom’e gatin,” ati. Ama ahung phat in, 
Elisha’n Gehazi kom’ah “Amanu chu seipeh in hichan geija eikhohsa hi kakipah lheh jenge ipi kabolpeh ding ham? Lengpa kom’ah ahilouleh asepai lamkaipa kom’ah ipi seipeh leng adeijem?” ati. Hichun amanun adonbut in, “Ima ka ngaichapoi ka insung min eivetup nai,” ati. 
Khonungin Elisha’n Gehazi chu adongin, “Amanu hi ipi ibolpeh thei ding ham?” ati. Gehazi'n adonbut in, “Amanu hin chapa aneipoi, ajipa jong atehse tai,” ati. 
“Ganung kou kit in” ati. Numeinu chu ahung kinungle in kotphunga adingin ahi. 
“Khovei tuphat leh nangin na anga chapa khat nakipom ding ahi,” atipeh e. Amanun “Ahipoi, kapu! Pathen mipa nei joulhep hih in chuleh nei kimohnep sah hih in,” ati. 
Ahinlah hiche numeinu chun nao ahin voptan ahi. Hiti chun akum kit in Elisha seibang bang chun cha pasal khat anei tan ahi. 
Hiche chapang chu ahung len jeptan ahileh apa chang-atna a chun akithopi dingin agachen ahi. 
Pohphungin ahung peng jah jengin, “Kaluchang anai! Kaluchang anai,” ahin tin ahi. Apa chun asohte khat kom’a, “Inlang’a anu kom’a potauvin,” ati. 
Hichun asohpan jong in'ah apolut in ahileh anun jong aphei chung’ah apom’in ahi. Ahinla sun ahung hi phat in athitan ahi. 
Amanun apo touvin Pathen mipa lupna chunga chun alupsah in kot akhah in, ahin dalha tan ahi. 
Aman ajipa kom’ah thu athotnin, “Isohte hou khat pen-pen chu sangan khat toh hinsol’in hitia chu keima Pathen mipa kom’a chu kaga chea kahung kilekit loina dingin” atin ahi. 
Amapan, “Tuni tah a hi ipi che ngaija ham? Tuni hi lhathah nikho golvah la ahipon, chuleh cholngahni lah ahipon,” ati. Ahinla amanun, “Akhohpoi, phanante” atin ahi. 
Amanun jong sangan chungah chun apho akoidoh in, asohpa kom’a hitin aseijin, “Ken thu kapeh ngallou leh gangin noloijin” ati. 
Amanun Carmel Molla um Pathen mipa chu aphah kon'in Elisha chun galla kon'in amanu chu ahin mun Gehazi kom’a chun, “Ven, Shunem a kon'in numeinu ahung e. 
Lhaijal inlang gadongin,” “Naji na cha toh naphat phat uhinam?” ati. Amanun Gehazi kom’ah, “Aphanai ima jouse aphanai.” 
Ahinlah amanu molchunga Pathen mipa kom ahung lhun phat in amu masanga tolla abohkhup in akeng chu atuh tan ahi. Gehazi chun amanu chu kaidoh agot leh Pathen mipan, “Suloh hih in, amanu hi lunggim'a ahin, Pakaijin keima ima eiseipeh hih laiye,” ati. 
Hichun amanun aseijin, “Hepu, nakom’a chapa dia kathum khah em?” Ken kasei louham, “Neiduha bol hih in chuleh nei kimoh nepsah hih in kati hilou ham?” ati. 
Chuin Elisha chun Gehazi kom’ah, “Gangtah in kigon lang kamol hi choi jinlang che loijin, lampia koimacha kom’a houlimpa hih in, cheloijin lang chapangpa mai’ah chun kamol hi gakoi loijin,” ati. 
Ahin chapangpa nu chun aseijin, “Hingjing Pakai leh nangma hinlaisen nangto ikilhon louding leh keima ka inna cheponge,” ati. Hichun Elijah toh ahung kilhon lhon tan ahi. 
Gehazi chu gangtah in ana che masan mol chu chapangpa chunga chun aga koiyin ahileh ima atipon ahi. Ahindoh dehlou jeh chun Elisha kom’ah ahung kilen, “Chapang chu ahingdoh dehpoi,” ahung ti in ahi. 
Elisha chu ahung lhun phat in, chapang chu athisa tahbeh in, themgaopa lupna a chun akijam nalaijin ahi. 
Ama alutnin achangin kot akikha khum’in Pakai kom’ah ataovin ahi. 
Hichun chapangpa chunga chun akijam in, amuh leh chapangpa muh asuto in, amit leh amit asuto in, akhut chu chapangpa khut chunga chun akoiyin ahi. Hiti chun chapangpa chunga chun akijam in ahileh chapangpa tahsa chu ahung lumdoh tan ahi! 
Hiche phat chun, Elisha chu akithoudoh in indan sung’a chun avahle in, hijou chun chapang chunga chun alum kit in, hiche phat chun chapangpa chu sagi vei ahung chi in, chuin amit ahung hah tan ahi! 
Hijou chun Elisha’n Gehazi chu akouvin, “Chapangpa nu chu gakouvin!” ati. Amanu ahung lut phat in, na chapa hikom’a aum’e kilah tan ati. 
Amanu chu akipah behseh jengin akengkhopi muh atuh in chibai aboh jeng tan ahi. Hijeh chun anao chu a-anga akipom’in akumsuh tan ahi. 
Elisha chu Gilgal langa ahung kile in ahileh gamsunga chun kel ana lhan ahi. Nikhat hi themgao phabep amasanga ana touvun ahileh aman jong asohpa Gehazi kom’ah aseijin, “Mei chunga chun bel lentah khat songin lang anchim lhah in ibonchauva ineh diuvin hin hon in,” ati. 
Gollhang ho lah a khat pen chu apotdoh in gamlah a hampa hollin ache in ahileh, gam umpong dimset in ahinpon ahung kile tan ahi. Aman ahal hallin athem lhan, nehthei lou thina thei ahilam helouvin bel sunga chun alhah tan ahi. 
Anchim themkhat chu ahin hom’un khatvei nivei agah tepmun ahileh ahung peng jah jengun Pathen mipa anchim'a hin thina thei gu aum'e atiuvin anethei tapouvin ahi. 
Hichun Elisha’n jong “Changbong thamkhat hinchoijun,” ati. Ahin choi phat un aman bel sunga chun agah lhah in, “Tun aphatai, netauvin,” ati. Hichun imacha aphamo aum tapon ahi. 
Nikhat hi Baal-shalishah a kon'in mikhat ahungin Pathen mipa chu changhum bora khat leh changlhah pheng somni ahin pohpeh in ahi. Elisha’n, “Mipi ho chu peuvin nenau vinte,” ati. 
Asohpan jong an ahinsem in, “Hicheng seh a hi mihem jakhat jen iti kivah ding ham?” atin ahinlah Elisha’n ahin donbut in, “Mipi hon aneh diuvin pedoh un, ajeh chu Pakaijin aseije: Mijousen neuvin tin phabep val nalai ding ahi!” ati. 
Hiti chun mipi ho chu apeuvin ahileh mijouse ding chun alhingsetnin Pakai thusei dungjuijin phabep jong ane val nalaijun ahi.
