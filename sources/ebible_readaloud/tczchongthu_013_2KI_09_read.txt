2 Lengte.
9.
Alangkhat ah Elisha themgaovin thempu kiloikhom ho lah a mikhat chu akouvin ama kom’a chun, “Kholjin din kigon chuleh Olive thao bom khat kichoijin lang Ramoth-gilead langa chen, 
hichea chun Nimshi chapa Jehoshaphat chapa Jehu chu gaholdoh inlang aloiho lah a kon'in indan sungnung khatna koulut in, 
chule aluchungah thao nunlang, hiti hin seipeh in, ‘Pakaijin hiti hin aseije: Nang hi Israel te chunga leng dinga thao kanu nahitai.’ Chujou tengleh kot hong inlang na hinkho huhnan jamdoh loi jengin, tin aseije!” ati. 
Hiti chun themgao gollhangpa chun akiseipeh bang bangin Ramoth Gilead lam’ah achetan ahi. 
Ama hiche mun agalhun chun, Jehu chu sepai lamkai dang hotoh ana toukhompet un agamudoh in ahi. Hichun aman jong, “Keiman nang dingin thupeh khat kahinpoi,” ati. “Keiho koipen dingin em?” tin Jehu chun ahin dongin ahi. “Lamkaipu, nang ding ahi,” tin adonbut in ahi. 
Hichun Jehu’n adang ho chu ahin dalhan insunga chun aluttan ahi. Hichun themgao gollhangpa chun Jehu luchunga chun thao asunglhan, hiti hin aseipeh tai, “Hiche hi Pakai Israel Pathen'in asei ahi: Keiman Pakai mite, Israel chunga leng ding’a thao kanu ahi. 
Nangin napupa insung mite na suhmang ding hitia chu keiman Jezebel in kathemgao ho leh Pakai sohte athagamna phu chu kalah ding ahi. 
Ahab insung mite jouse natha gam sohhel ding ahi. Keiman achilhah pasal jouse, asohte le asoh hilou pasal jouse Israel te chenna muntinna ka suhmang ding ahi. 
Keiman Nebat chapa Jeroboam insung leh Ahijah chapa Baasha insung ka suhmang banga Ahab insung jong ka suhmang ding ahi. 
Jezreel munna gambeh khatna Ahab jinu Jezebel chu uicha in aneh ding koiman amanu chu avuilou ding’u ahi,” ati. Chuin khangdong themgao pan kot chu ahongin ajamtan ahi. 
Jehu chu asepai lamkaiho kom’a ahung kinungle kit in ahile amaho lah a mikhat chun adongin, “Hiche mingolpa chun ipi adei ham? Aphamo umlouva hinam?” ati. Jehu’n adonbut in “Hitobang miho thusei ding chu nahetsao hichula,” ati. 
Amahon, “Nangin thil khat na im'e, nei seipeh un,” atiuvin ahi. Hichun Jehu’n amaho chu aseipeh un, “Aman kakom’ah Pakaijin hitin aseije: 'Keiman nangma hi Israel te chunga leng dinga thao kanu nahitai,'” ati. 
Hichun ama hon akengtonoi uvah pon aphauvin kelchal ki amut un, “Jehu leng ahitai!” atiuve. 
Nimshi chapa Jehoshaphat chapa Jehu chun Joram lengpa dounan gal aboltan ahi. (Joram hi Ramoth-gilead munna Israel te huhna dia Aram lengpa Hazael sepai te toh kidouva uma ahi. 
Ahinlah Joram lengpa chu gal kisatna a ana kisukha in, akisuhkhahna ho kijen dam dingin Jezreel a kinunglea ahi.) Hichun Jehu in akinaipi ho kom’a chun, “Nanghon leng dinga neidei uleh keiman thilsoh ho hi Jezreel a miho agahet sah louna diuvin hiche khopia kon'in koima potsah tahih un,” atin ahi. 
Hijou chun Jehu chu asakol kangtalai achun atouvin Jezreel munna damlouva kijen a um Joram kimutopi dingin achetan ahi. Judah lengpa Ahaziah jong hiche munna chu Joram vedinga ana che ahi. 
Jezreel kulpi chunga khosatna a kon'in khongahpan Jehu le aloiho ahungu chu agalmun, Joram kom’a chun ahin sam'in, “Keiman sepai honkhat ahung kamun ahi!” ati. Hichun Joram lengpan thu apen, “Sakol chung toukhat soldoh inlang chamna thudol'a hung hinauvem gadohsah in,” ati. 
Hichun sakol toukhat alhaidoh in Jehu kom'a chun aga dongin “Lengpan chamna thudol'a nahung hinam ti ahenom’e,” agati. Jehu’n adonbut in, “Chamna chu ipi ding nati ham? Neihin juitan!” ati. Hichun khongah pan Joram lengpa kom’a chun, “Sottol pa chun amaho akimupin ahung kile tapoi,” tin aseipeh tai. 
Hichun lengpan sakol chunga tou ani channa asoldoh kit in, ama chun akom uva, “Lengpan chamna thudol'a nahung u hinam ahenom e,” tin aga dong'e. Jehu’n adonbut in, “Chamna ipi ding nati ham? Neihin juitan,” ati. 
Hichun khongahpa chun ahin sam’in, “Sottolpa chun amaho akimupin ahinla ama ahung kile tapoi! Ahunga khu Nimshi chapa Jehu toh abange, asakol tildan khu aham vevu jenge,” ati. 
Hichun Joram lengpan thu apen “Gang in! Ka sakol kangtalai chu gotsan koiyin!” ati. Hiche jou chun Israel lengpa Joram leh Judah lengpa Ahaziah chu Jehu toh kimuto dingin asakol kangtalai chungah ahung kijot doh lhon'in ahi. Amanin Jezreel munna Neboth gam munna chun agakimupi lhon tan ahi. 
Joram lengpa in adongin, “Jehu, chamna thudol'a nahung hinam?” ati. Jehu’n, “Nanu Jezebel semthu pathen houna ho leh doithu sapna in hon eium kimvel laiseuva hoilang chamna iti um thei ding ham?” tin ana donbut in ahi. 
Hichun Joram lengpan sakol ale heijin ajam'in, Ahaziah lengpa kom’ah “Leng mun chu ahi, Ahaziah!” atin ahi. 
Ahin Jehu chun, athalpi a agah kap a ahileh, athalpi chang chu Joram lengkou teni kikah a alut in, hiche thalchang chun alungchangtah akhan asakol kangtalai chung’a chun alhu lhop in, athiden tan ahi. 
Chuin Jehu chun anoija pipu pa, Bidkar kom’a chun, “Atahsa chu Jezreel a Naboth gamkai sunga chun lehlut tan. Apa Ahab nunga Sakol a ina lhailai chu nagel doh em? Pakaijin ama douna a hitia hi ana phondohsa ahitai: 
'Janhia Naboth le achate atha kamitchang tah a kamu ahi, hiche gambeh chunga hi keiman aphu kalah ding ahi tin Pakai akihahsel in ahi.' Hijeh chun Pakaijin aseibang bangin Naboth gamsung a chun lehlut tan,” ati. 
Chuin Judah lengpa Ahaziah in thilsoh amu phat in, ama jong Beth-haggan lamlen langa ajamtan ahi. Ahin Jehu’n ama chu adellin, “Ama jong kaplih un!” ati. Hiti chun ama hon jong Ahaziah chu Ibleam kom'a um Gur lang kaltouna laitah a chun ana kaplhu un, ahin Megiddo geijin ana jam phei peh in, hikom'a chun ana thiden tan ahi. 
Asohten athilong chu asakol kangtalai in apouvin, Jerusalem alhut un David khopia apu apate kivuina a avui tauvin ahi. 
Ahab chapa Joram leng vaipoh kal kum som le khat lhin kum chun Ahaziah chu Judah chunga leng ana chang in ahi. 
Chuin Lengpa nu Jezebel chun Jehu chu Jezreel ah ahunge tithu ajah phat chun amit teni ajem hoisel in, asam akipojolsel in, bangkot achun atouvin ahi. 
Jehu chu khopi kelkot ahunglut jou chun, amanun jong hasap in ahinsam in, “Tolthatpa chamna thudol'a hung nahi hinai mo? Apupa Zimri thatpa tobang nahi,” ati. 
Jehu chun chunglang agahvet leh bangkot a chun amanu agah mun, hitin asam in ahi, “Keilam a panga koi-koi nahiuvem?” tia agah sap le, nukiso mini le thum'in ahin veuvin ahi. 
Hichun Jehu’n jong, “Amanu chu hinsep lhauvin,” atin ahileh amanu chu bangkot a kon'in ahin sonlhauvin, athisan chu bang leh sakol chungah chun akithe tho jengin ahi. Jehu’n atouna sakol kengto ah achil'in ahi. 
Chuin Jehu chu khopi sunga alut’in, ane'n adon’in ahi. Hiche jou chun, “Khattou cheuvin lang sapset chang hiche numei nu hi gavui tauvin, ajeh chu ama hi leng chanu ahi,” ati. 
Ahin amaho chu avui ding’a achedoh uleh, ama hon, alugu, akeng chuleh akhut teni tilou adang amu tapouvin ahi. 
Chuin amaho ahung kile uvin Jehu kom’a aseijun ahileh aman, “Pakaijin Tishbe khoa alhachapa Elijah kom’a anasei chu guilhung ahitai, 'Jezreel khosung’a Jezebel tahsa hi uichan aneh ding ahi. 
Chujongleh Jezebel tahsa chu Jezreel khosung’a chu jun le eh banga kithe cheh ding, koima chan hiche hi Jezebel kitinu chu ahi tia ahetdoh lou ding’u ahi,'” ati.
