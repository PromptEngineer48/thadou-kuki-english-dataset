2 Lengte.
12.
Israel a leng Jehu vaihomna kum sagi lhin kum'in Judah a Joash in vai ahom panin ahi. Ama hin Jerusalem’ah kum somli lengvai anapo in ahi. Anu chu Zibiah ahin, Beersheba khomi ahi. 
Joash in ahinkho lhumkeijin Pakai mitmu’n thilpha jeng ana bollin ahi, ajeh chu ama chu Jehoiada thempun ana khoukhah jing jeh ahi. 
Ahijeng vang'in aman pathen dang kithoina doikhom ho ana sumangpon ahi, hiti chun mipi hon kithoina gantha ho leh halnam ho anabol jingun ahi. 
Nikhat hi Joash lengpan thempuho kom’ah, “Pakai Hou-In'a hung kitohdoh sum jouse ahin, mihem in amichangval a ahin todoh sum jouse ahin, mihem ama-ama lungthim sung cheh a thanopna aneiya Pakai Hou-In sunga ahin todoh sum jouse ahin abonchan khol tup un. 
Hiche sumho lah a kon hin phabep hi thempu hon la uhenlang, angaichat dungjuiya Hou-In semphatna in mang uhen,” ati. 
Ahinlah Joash vaipohna kum somni le thum channa geijin jong thempu hon Hou-In semphatna natoh ima anapan polaiyun ahi. 
Hijeh chun Joash lengpan Jehoiada leh thempu dang ho akoukhom in, “Ipi dinga Hou-In na semphat loulai uham? Sum ho hi nangho phatchomna dingin moh manchatvai tahih un, tu apat vang'in hiche sum ho hi Hou-In semphatna dinga aboncha naman diu ahi” ati. 
Hiti chun thempu hon mipi ho a konna sum lah tahlou dingin akilol un, chuleh Hou-In semphatna dinga mopohna jong hi midang nganse dingin anom tauvin ahi. 
Hichun Jehoiada thempu pan thih thingkong lentah khat asem’in, asin avitvangin hichu Pakai Hou-In hunglutna maicham jetlang sanga chun akoiyin ahi. Hichun thempu kotngah a pangho chun, mipi sumto ho chu alauvin thingkong sunga chun ahenglut jiuvin ahi. 
Hiche thingkong chu adimphat leh, leng insunga kimangcha lekhajihpa leh thempulen pa chun Pakai Hou-In’a hung kitohdoh dangka ho chu asimtohji lhon'in, chujou teng chule khaodip khatna akhumji lhon'in ahi. 
Chujou tengleh sum chu Pakai Hou-In semphatna a vaipo ho khutna apedohji lhon'in, ama hon Pakai Hou-In semphatna a natong ho apekit jiuvin ahi. Amaho chu, thingthem bol ho leh insaho, 
thing vaibol ho leh songkheng ho, apeuvin ahi. Ama hon sum chu Pakai Hou-In semphatna dia thing le song kikheng chaisa ho chohnan jong amangun, chujongleh Hou-In kisah phatna dia manna ding jousen jong ana mangcha uvin ahi. 
Hiche sum, Hou-In a hunglut ho chu, Hou-In sunga manchah ding kisilna dangka kong ahin, thaomeija manchah ding chaiche ahin, konglenho le khon ho ahin, sumkon chuleh sana le dangka a kisem thil adang-dang ho kisemnan khatcha akimang pon ahi. 
Sum chu mopo ho apeuvin, Hou-In semphatna a bou akimangin ahi. 
Hiche natohna a kimangcha ho vesuija pangho hi milungtheng leh tahsan theitah ngen ahijeh un, Jehu in sum kimanchahna ho chu dihnam tia kholtoh ding ngaikhoh asapon ahi. 
Ahinlah suhkhelna ho leh chosetna ho thoidamna dinga hung kitohdoh ho vang Pakai Hou-In’ah ahunglut jipon, hiche ho chu thempuho apeh jiuva ama hon adeidan danna akimanchah jiu ahitai. 
Hiche phat laitah hin, Aram lengpa Hazael chu Gath sat dingin akon'in ajouvin alenggam sung'ah akilahlut tan ahi. Hiche jou chun ahung kinunglen Jerusalem nokhum ding agotan ahi. 
Hichun Joash lengpan ama masanga Judah lengho, Jehoshaphat in Pathen'a ana todoh ho, Jehoram, Ahaziah chuleh ama tah in ana todoh thengho jouse chu achom khomin, Pakai Hou-In leh leng inpia sumkholna mun'a sana ho jouse toh abonchan Hazael chu athot tan ahi. Hichun Hazael in Jerusalem anokhum got chu anokhum tapon ahi. 
Joash vaihom sunga thilsoh ho jouse leh anatoh, athilbol ho jouse chu Judah Lengte Thusimbu a aum sohkeijin ahi. 
Joash chu anoija natong pipui hon ama dounan tohgon ana neijun, Silla jotna Beth-millo lamlen'ah ana that tauvin ahi. 
Ama ana that ho chu Shimeath chapa Jozacar leh Shomer chapa Jehozabad ahi lhon'e. Amani hi ani lhona hi lengpa dinga tahsan umtah lengpa thumop’a pang anahi lhon'in ahi. Joash chu David khopi ah apu apate kivuina a ana kivui tan ahi.
