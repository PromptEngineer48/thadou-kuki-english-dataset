3.
Hebron khopia achen laiya David in ahin achapa te chu hicheng hi ahiuve: Achapa apeng masapen chu Ammon ahin, apenna nu chu Ahinoam akitin Jezreel kho mi ahi. Achapa ni lhinna pa chu Daniel ahin, Carmel kho numeinu Abigail toh ahin lhon ahi. 
Athum lhinna pa chu Absalom ahin, anu chu Maacah akitin, Geshur lengpa Talmai chanu ahi. Li lhinna pa chu Adonijah ahin, anu chu Haggith ahi. 
Nga lhinna pa chu Shephatiah akitin, anu chu Abital ahi. Gup lhinna pa chu Ithream ahin, David in ajinu Eglah toh ahin lhon ahiye. 
Hebron khopia kum sagi le akeh khat sunga lengvaipoa anapan sungin David chun chapa gup hi ana hingin ahi. Chujouvin David chun kum somthum le thum sungin Jerusalem'a lengvai ana pon ahi. 
Jerusalem khopia achen laiya David in ana hin achapa te chu Shammua, Shobad, Nathan, chule Solomon ahiuve. Amaho penna nu chu Ammiel chanu Bathsheba ahi. 
Chujouvin David in chapa ko ahinbe’in amaho chu: Ibhar, Elishua, Elpelet, 
Nogah, Nepheg, Japhia, 
Elishama, Eliada chule Eliphelet ahiuve. 
Athaikemho toh ahindoh ho tailouvin hichengse hi David chapa te jeng ahiuve. Ama hon sopi numei Tamar kitinu bouseh aneijin ahi. 
Solomon son achilhah ho chu Rehoboam, Abijah, Asa, Jehoshaphat, 
Jehoram, Ahaziah, Joash, 
Amaziah, Uzziah, Jotham, 
Ahaz, Hezekiah, Manasseh, 
Amon, chule Josiah ahiuvin ahi. 
Josiah chapa te chu Johanan (apeng masapen pa) ahin, Jehoiakim (ni lhinna pa) ahin, Zedekiah (thum lhinna pa) ahin, Jehoahaz (li lhinna pa) ahi. 
Jehoiakim son achilhah ho chu achapa Jehoiachin leh asopipa Zedekiah ahi lhon'e. 
Babylon miten sohchanga ana kaimangu Jehoiachin chapa te chu Shealtiel, 
Malkiram, Pedaiah, Shenazzar, Jekamiah, Hoshama, chule Nedabiah ahiuvin ahi. 
Pedaiah chapa teni chu Zerubbabel le Shimei ahi lhon'e. Zerubbabel chapa teni chu Meshullam le Hananiah ahi lhon'in, (Amani sopinu chu Shelomith akiti.) 
Aman chapa adang nga jong ahingin, amaho chu Hashubah, Ohel, Berekiah, Hasadiah, chule Jushab-hesed ahiuve. 
Hananiah chapa teni chu Pelatiah le Jeshaiah ahi lhon'e. Jeshaiah chapa chu Rephaiah ahin, Rephaiah chapa Arnan ahi. Arnan chapa Obadiah ahin, Obadiah chapa Shecaniah ahi. 
Shecaniah chilhah chu Shemaiah ahin chule achapa te chu Hattush ahin, Igal ahin, Bariah ahin, Neariah ahin, chule Shaphat pumin mi gup alhing uve. 
Neariah chapa te chu Elioenai ahin, Hizkiah ahin, chule Azrikam pumin mi thum ahiuve. 
Elioenai chapa te chu Hodaviah ahin, Eliashib ahin, Pelaiah ahin, Akkub ahin, Johanan ahin, Delaiah ahin, chule Anani ahin, aboncha uvin mi sagi alhing uve.
