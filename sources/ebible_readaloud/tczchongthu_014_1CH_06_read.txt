1 Thusimbu.
6.
Levi chapa te chu Gershon, Kohath, chule Merari ahiuve. 
Kohath chilhah ho chu Amram, Izhar, Hebron, chule Uzziel ahiuve. 
Amram chate chu Aaron, Moses, chule Miriam ahiuve. Aaron chapa te chu Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, chule Ithamar ahiuve. 
Eleazer hin Phinehas ana hing'e. Phinehas hin Abishua ana hing'e. 
Abishua hin Bukki ana hing'e. Bukki hin Uzzi ahing'e. 
Uzzi hin Zerahiah ahing'e. Zerahiah in Meraioth ahing'e. 
Meraioth hin Amariah ahing'e. Amariah hin Ahitub ahing'e. 
Ahitub hin Zadok ahing'e. Zadok hin Ahimaaz ahing'e. 
Johanan hin Azariah chu ahing'e. Azariah chun Johanan ahing'e. 
Johanan hin Azariah chu ahingin, ama hi Jerusalem khopia Solomon Hou-In sahdoh sunga Thempu Chungnunga hung pang ahi. 
Azariah hin Amariah ahing'e. Amariah chun Ahitub ahing'e. 
Ahitub hin Zadok ahing'e. Zadok chun Shallum ahing'e. 
Shallum hin Hilkiah ahing'e. Hilkiah chun Azariah ahing'e. 
Azariah hin Seraiah ahing'e. Seraiah chun Jehozadak ahingin, 
Pakaijin Judah mite leh Jerusalem mite sohchanga asol manga Nebuchadnezzar khut a atolmang lah ah ajaovin ahi. 
Levi chapa te chu Gershon, Kohath, chule Merari ahiuve. 
Gershom chilhah lah a Libni leh Shimei akisimtha lhon'e. 
Kohath chilhah ho lah a Amram, Izhar, Hebron, chule Uzziel akisimtha uve. 
Merari chilhah lah a Mahli le Mushi akisimtha lhon'e. Anoiya mi hichengse hi apu apau kailhah cheh ama-ama insung dol cheh a Levi miho insung jeng ahiuve: 
Gershon insung ahileh, Libni, Jahath, Zimmah, 
Joah, Iddo, Zerah, chule Jeatherai ahi. 
Kohath son achilhah ho chu Amminadab, Korah, Assir, 
Elkanah, Abiasaph, Assir, 
Tahath, Uriel, Uzziah, chule Shaul ahi. 
Elkanah son chilhah ho ahileh, Amasai, Ahimoth, 
Elkanah, Zophai, Nahath, 
Eliab, Jeroham, Elkanah, le Samuelahi. 
Samuel chapa teni chu apeng masa Joel ahin, chule ni lhinna pa chu Abijah ahi. 
Merari son achilhah ho chu Mahli, Libni, Shimei, Uzzah, 
Shimea, Haggiah, le Asaiah. 
Pakai Hou-In sunga Pathen Thingkhong ahung kitollut nunga David in Hou-In sunga la lamkaiya pang dia anganse miho chu hicheng hi ahiuve. 
Jerusalem khopia Solomon'in Pakai Hou-In asah doh masangsen la lamkaiya pang ho hi mipi kikhopna ponbuh a la lamkaiyin ana pang uvin lhacha na atong jing uve. Hitobang hin ama hon atoh ding dol kinse chu hoikei chan atong jing jeng uve. 
Hichea la lamkaiya pang ho hi achapa teu toh tongkhom ho ahiuve: Heman tumging thempa chu Kohath chilhah miho lah a Joel chapa ahin, Joel chu Samuel chapa ahin, 
Samuel chu Elkanah chapa ahin, Elkanah chu Jeroham chapa ahin, Jeroham chu Eliel chapa ahin, Eliel chu Toah chapa ahin, 
Toah chu Zuph chapa ahin, Zuph chu Elkanah chapa ahin, Elkanah chu Mahath chapa ahin, Mahath chu Amasai chapa ahi. 
Amasai chu Elkanah chapa ahin, Elkanah chu Joel chapa ahi, Joel chu Azariah chapa ahin, Azariah chu Zephaniah chapa ahi. 
Zephaniah chu Tahath chapa ahin, Tahath chu Assir chapa ahin, Assir chu Abiasaph chapa ahin, Abiasaph chu Korah chapa ahi. 
Korah chu Izhar chapa ahin, Izhar chu Kohath chapa ahin, Kohath chu Levi chapa ahin, chule Levi chu Israel chapa ahi. 
Heman’in manchah phapenna anei masatpa Gershon chilhah miho lah a Asaph kitipa chu Berekiah chapa ahin, Berekiah chu Shimea chapa ahin, 
Shimea hi Michael chapa ahin, Michael chu Baaseiah chapa ahin, Baaseiah chu Malkijah chapa ahin, 
Malkijah chu Ethni chapa ahi; Ethni chu Zerah chapa ahin, Zerah chu Adaiah chapa ahin, 
Adaiah chu Ethan chapa ahin, Ethan chu Zimmah chapa ahin, Zimmah chu Shimei chapa ahin, 
Shimei chu Jahath chapa ahin; Jahath chu Gershon chapa ahin, Gershon chu Levi chapa ahi. 
Heman in manchah phapenna anei ani channa pa Merari phung'a Ethan chu ahi. Ethan chu Kishi chapa ahin, Kishi chu Abdi chapa ahin, Abdi chu Malluch chapa ahin, 
Malluch chu Hashabiah chapa ahin, Hashabiah chu Amaziah chapa ahin, Amaziah chu Hilkiah chapa ahin, 
Hilkiah chu Amzi chapa ahin, Amzi chu Bani chapa ahin, Bani chu Shemer chapa ahin, 
Shemer chu Mahli chapa ahin, Mahli chu Mushi chapa ahin, Mushi chu Merari chapa ahin, chule Merari chu Levi chapa ahiye. 
Amaho sopite Levi phung sung mite jouse chu abonchauvin Pathen Hou-In Houbuh sunga natong kinbol din akipansah sohkei uvin ahi. 
Aaron le chilhah ho bouseh chu thempu kin bol'in apang uvin ahi. Ama hon pumgo thilto kigona maicham chung le gimnaitui kihalvamna maicham chunga thilto ding jouse tohdoh in apang uvin, Mun Theng Pen a natohna ding thil toh din apang uve. Amahon Pathen lhachapa Mose thupeh dungyuiyin Israel te kithoina kinbol’in apang jiuve. 
Aaron chilhah ho chu Eleazar, Phinehas, Abishua, 
Bukki, Uzzi, Zerahiah, 
Meraioth, Amariah, Ahitub, 
Zadok, le Ahimaaz ahiuve. 
Hichengse hi amaho gamgi hop sunga achenna mun chengseu chu ahiye: Aaron chapa te Kohath phung sung mite dinga phun kisan chu aphat jeh in, 
Mihon amaho chu Judah gamsunga Hebron khopi le akimvel gam se-se toh chule avel’a hamhing gam gancha vahna ding jouse chutoh apeh tha tauve, 
amavang akhopi loujao lah chengse leh akhoneo dung jouse chu Caleb kitipa Jephunneh chapa chu apetauve. 
Aaron chapa dinga khopi anapeh u chule ahamhing gamsunga cheh toh anakipeh tha ho chu: Hebron khopi (kiselna khopi), Libnah khopi, Jattir khopi, Eshtemoa khopi, 
Holon khopi, Debir khopi, 
Ain khopi, Juttah khopi, chule Beth-shemesh khopi toh hichengse hi ahi. 
Chuleh Benjamin phunga konna Gibeon, Geba, Alemeth, chule Anathoth ahin akhopi le ahamhing gamsungse toh kipehtha cheh ahiuve. Aaron chapa te chan jat khopi chu abonchan khopi som le thum ahi. 
Kohath chapa te amoh chengse dinga phum aphatna dungjuijin, Manasseh phung keh khat a kon chun khopi som khat jen akipen ahi. 
Gershon chapa te chu phun kisan aphat dungjuijin, Issachar, Asher, Naphtali, chule Jordan solam’a um Bashan gam'a Manasseh phung, Jordan solam'a konna kidong khom khopi agomin khopi som le thum achang uvin ahi. 
Merari chapa te jong phun kisan aphat dungjuijin, Reuben, Gad, chule Zebulun gam'a kon kidong khom khopi som le ni achang uvin ahiye. 
Hitobang hin Israel chaten Levi te chu khopi chujat chutoh ahamhing gam se-se toh ana peuvin ahiye. 
Hitobang hin phun phatna dungjuijin, Judah, Simeon, chule Benjamin phunga kon'in achunga kisei khopi ho hi ana peuvin ahi. 
Kohath chapa te insung mi phabep jong Ephraim phunga kon'in agamsung uva khopi phabep le ahamhing gam cheh achang uvin, 
hiche khopi ho chu: Shechem (kiselna khopi Ephraim thinglhang gam'a um) ahin, Gezer, 
Jokmeam, Beth-horon, 
Aijalon, chule Gath-rimmon khopi chengse hi ahi. 
Kohath chapa te amoh chengse kipe khopi ho chu Manasseh phung keh khat a kon'in jong Aner le Bileam khopi cheh leh ahamhing gam cheh toh ahi. 
Gershon insung mite kipe khopi ho chu Golan (Bashan gamsunga) khopi le ahamhing gamsung se-se, chule Manasseh phung keh khat a konna Ashtaroth khopi le ahamhing gamsung se-se ahiye. 
Isssachar phunga konna kipedoh khopi ho chu Kedesh, Deberath, 
Ramoth, le Anem khopi le ahamhing gam se-se ahi. 
Asher phunga konna kipedoh khopi ho chu Mashal, Abdon, 
Hukok, chule Rehob khopi le ahamhing gamsung se-se ahi. 
Naphtali phung sunga konna kipedoh khopi ho chu, Galilee gamsunga Kedesh, Hammon, chule Kiriathaim khopi le ahamhing gamsung se-se ahi. 
Merari chate amoh chengse chu Zebulun phunga kon'in Jokneam, Kartah, Rimmon, chule Tabor khopi le ahamhing gamsung cheh akipen ahi. 
Reuben phung sunga konna kipe khopi ho chu Jordan vadung gal lang Jericho khopi dunga, Jordan kal niso lam’a Bezer khopi (neldi gam khopi), Jahaz, 
Kedermoth khopi le Mephaath khopi le ahamhing gamsung se-se toh akipen ahi. 
Chutengleh Gad phunga konna kipedoh khopi ho chu Gilead gamsunga Ramoth, 
Heshbon, chule Jazer khopi le ahamhing gam cheh toh ahi.
