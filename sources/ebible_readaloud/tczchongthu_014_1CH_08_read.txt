1 Thusimbu.
8.
Benjamin chapa apeng masapen chu Bela ahin, ni lhinna pa chu Ashbel ahin, thum lhinna pa chu Aharah ahin, 
li lhinna pa chu Nohah ahin, chule a nga lhinna pa chu Rapha ahi. 
Bela chapa te chu Addar, Gera, Abihud, 
Abishua, Naaman, Ahoah, 
Gera, Shephuphan, chule Huram ahiuve. 
Ehud chapa te, Geba a cheng mite dinga a insung cheh uva lamkai ho chu, abonchauvin Manahath gamsunga sohchang din akaimang tauve. 
Ehud chapa te chu Naaman, Ahijah, chule Gera toh ahiuve. Gera hi sohchanga apuimang’u ahin, ama hin Uzza le Abihud ahingin ahi. 
Shaharaim hin aji teni Hushim le Baara ada nungin jong Moab gamsunga cha tampi anei nalaiyin ahi. 
Ajinu Hodesh kiti nu chutoh chapa Jobab, Zibia, Mesha, Malcam, 
Jeuz, Sakia, le Mirmah ahing lhon'in ahi. Hichengse hi amapa chapa te ahiuvin, a insung cheh uva lamkai cheh ahiuve. 
Aman ajinu Hushim toh jong chapa ni Abitub leh Elpaal ahing lhon'in ahi. 
Elpaal chapa te chu Eber, Misham, Shemed (Shemed hin Ono khopi le Lod khopi toh chule avel'a kho ho ana sa'n), 
Beriah, chule Shema ahiuve. Amaho hi Aijalon khopia cheng insung phung upa cheh ahiuvin, chule Gath a cheng ho toh kisat a nomanga pang ho chu ahiuve. 
Ahio, Shashak, Jeremoth, 
Zebadiah, Arad, Eder, 
Michael, Ishpah, leh Joha amaho hi Beriah chapa te jeng ahiuve. 
Zebadiah, Meshullam, Hizki, Heber, 
Ishmerai, Izliah, leh Jobab, amaho chengse hi Elpaal chapa te jeng ahiuvin ahi. 
Jakim, Zicri, Zabdi, 
Elienai, Zillethai, Eliel, 
Adaiah, Beraiah, leh Shimrath amaho hi Shimei chapa te ahiuve. 
Ishpan, Eber, Eliel, 
Abdon, Zicri, Hanan, 
Hananiah, Elam, Anthothijah, 
Iphdeiah, chule Penuel hichengse hi Shashak chapa te jeng ahiuve. 
Shamsherai, Shehariah, Athaliah, 
Jaareshiah, Elijah, chule Zicri hichengse hi Jeroham chapa te ahiuvin ahi. 
Mihem hijat hi akhangguiyu lekhabua kisut dungjuijin ama-ama insunga phung upa cheh ahiuvin, chule amaho hi Jerusalem khopia ana cheng uvin ahiye. 
Gibeon pa Jeiel chu Gibeon khopia ana chengin ahi. Ajinu min chu Maacah ahin, 
chule achapa apeng masapen chu Abdon akitin ahi. Jeiel chapa te adang ho chu Zur, Kish, Baal, Ner, Nadab, 
Gedor, Ahio, Zechariah, 
chule Shimeam hingpa Mikloth ahiuve. Amaho chengse hi a inkoteu toh kigalsai cheh in Jerusalem khopia chun ana cheng uvin ahi. 
Ner chun Kish ahingin, Kish chun Saul ahingin, Saul’in Jonathan, Malkishua, Abinadab, chule Esh-baal ahinge. 
Jonathan’in Merib-baal ahingin, Merib-baal in Micah ahinge. 
Micah chapa te chu Pithon, Melech, Tahrea, chule Ahaz ahiuve. 
Ahaz chun Jadah ahingin, Jadah chun Alemeth, Azmaveth, chule Zimri ahinge. Zimri hin Moza ahinge. 
Moza chu Binea pa ahin, Binea chu Rephaiah pa ahin, Raphaiah chu Eleasah pa ahin, chule Eleasah chu Azel pa ahi. 
Azel hin chapa gup aneijin, amaho chu: Azrikam, Bokeru, Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah, chule Hanan ahiuve. Amaho chengse hi Azel chapa te jeng ahiuvin ahi. 
Azel sopipa Eshek chun chapa thum ahingin ahi: Apeng masapen chu Ulam ahin, ani lhinna pa chu Jeush ahin, chule athum lhinna pa chu Eliphelet ahi. 
Ulam chapa te chu mihat gal-hang jeng, thalpi kap them jeng ahiuvin ahi. Ama hon chapa tampipi aneiyuvin chule tute jong tamtah aneiyun, abonchauvin mi 150 jen alhing uve. Mi hichengse hi Benjamin son chilhah jeng ahiuvin ahi.
