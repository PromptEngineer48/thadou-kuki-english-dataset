1 Thusimbu.
14.
Tyre gam a leng Hiram in asottole a pang thingthem bol le songkhen them ho chu Cedar thing ho a David inpi sahpeh dingin ana sol'in ahi. 
Chule Pakaijin Israel pumpi a lengdia atun a amite Israel te dinga alenggam phatthei aboh chu David in jong ana geldoh tan ahi. 
Chujouvin David in Jerusalem a chun ji akipuibe in chanu le chapa jong tampi aneikit in ahi. 
Amaho hi David in Jerusalem’a anahinsa achapa te chu ahiuve: Shammua, Shobab, Nathan, Solomon, 
Ibhar, Elishua, Elpelet, 
Nogah, Nepheg, Japhia, 
Elishama, Eliada,le Eliphelet ahiuve. 
David Israel pumpia aleng chan ding chu Philistine miten ajah phat uvin Philistine sepai ho chu David man dingin ahung kon uvin ahi. David in jong hiche thu ajah phat chun amaho kimaitopi din akondoh pai kit in ahi. 
Hiti chun Philistine sepai ho chun gang tah in Raphiam phaicham chu ahin nokhum pai jengun ahi. 
Hichun David in Pathen adongin, “Hiche Philistine te khu gasat leng achung uva galjona neichan sah ding ham?” ati. Chuin Pakaijin adonbut in, “Chenlang gasat in achung uva galjona nachan ding ahi,” ati. 
Hichun David le asepai te chu Baal-perazim lam jonin akitol uvin Philistine te chu agasat uvin ana jou tauve. Chuin David in hitin asei e, “Pathen'in ka melmate chunga twisuh long banga ka lonkhumna din eina mang chai,” ati. Hijeh chun hichelai mun hi Baal-perazim ana kisah in ahi. 
Chuin Philistine te anajam uvin milim doi pathen semthu ana dalhah hou chu David in ana halvam sah in ahi. 
Ahivangin Philistine te chun hetmanlou kah in phaicham chu ahin nokhum paikit uvin ahi. 
Hijeh chun David injong ipi bol ding hitam tin Pathen adong kit in ahile Pathen'in, “Amaho nungjui kit louvin amasang lam uva konin hinle del khumun balsom thingphung a hin nokhum un,” tin asei e. 
Nangman thingleh vum langa pat mitampi kijot kengto gin najah teng pot inlang galsat pan jengin ajeh chu Pathen'in Philistine sepai te satgamna dia nangma nahin panpi ding chu avetsah ahi. 
Hiti chun David in jong Pathen thupeh bangin abollin ahile Philistine sepai te chu Gibeon a kipat in Gezer chan in ana deljam suh in ahi. 
Chuin muntin jousea David ahung minthan phat in Pakaijin David chu nam jouse te gin le ja in aumsah tan ahi.
