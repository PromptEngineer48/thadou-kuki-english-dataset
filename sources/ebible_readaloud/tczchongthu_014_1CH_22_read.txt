1 Thusimbu.
22.
Chuin David in aseitai, “Hilai mun hi Pathen Hou-In mun chu hi ding ahitai, chule kilhaina, pumgo thiltona mun jong hi ding ahi!” ati. 
Hijeh chun David in gamsung a kholjin mijouse akoutup in, aboltheina cheh un Hou-In sahna ding song khengjol dingin apansah tai. 
David in kelkot a man ding thingkil semna din thih tampi leh sum-eng tekhopset apen ahi. 
Tyre le Sidon miten leng David ana peh Cedar thingtum ho jong chu tampi ana peh thai. 
David in aseijin, “Kachapa Solomon hi achapang nalaiyin, jilna le hetna jong neibeh nailou hijongleh, Pathen'in anganse a Hou-In sahdoh ding hi loupi ding, minthang ding chuleh leiset pumpia dia jong nasatah hi ding ahin, hijeh a chu tua hi kagonpeh pan ding ahitai,” ati. Hijeh a hi David in athi masanga Hou-In sahna dia thil le lo tampi akoitup ahitai. 
Hichun, David in achapa Solomon jah a Israel te Pathen chenna ding Hou-In theng asahdoh teina din athumop tai. 
“Kachapa, keiman ka Pathen jabolna din a Hou-In theng khat kasadoh nom’e,” tin David in aseipeh e. 
“Ahivangin Pathen'in kajah a aseiye, nangman na galbolna munna mi tampi nathat tai. Chuleh, keima mitmua thisan tampi naso tah jeh a, nangman keima jabolna dia ka Hou-In theng nasah theilou ding ahitai. 
Ahivang’a, chamna sem chapa khat nahin ding ahi, aman lengvai apoh sungsea Israel gam leh akimvel a agal mite kisan geiya chamdel a kalhat sah ding ahi. Amin Solomon kiti ding chuleh ama leng vaipoh sung’a hi Israel cham kalensah ding ahi. 
Ama hi ahi keima jabolna dia Hou-In theng sadoh ding chu. Kachapa deitah hintin, Keima apa kahi ding ahi. Chuleh keiman a itih a alenggam sung’a chamna le lungmon konna kapeh ding ahi.” 
“Kacha tuhin, Pakaijin nakithopi intin aman nangma chung chang thudol’a asei tobangin, nangman jong Pakai, Pathen Hou-In nahin sahdoh tei ding ahi. 
Pakajin thil hetkhen themna le datkhenje dan napeh ding, Israel chunga vaihom a natun ding, ngansena nahin peh teng jongleh Pakai, na Pathen danthu nanit jingna dingin nabol peh tahen. 
Israel te dinga Pakaijin Mose athupeh chonna dan ahin, chondan jouse chu nanit jing ding chule ching thei tah a na khantoupi thei ding ahi. Hijeh chun hat inlang, hang in, kicha hih hel in chule lungthoina jong nanei louding ahi!” 
“Genthei hesohna tothon keiman Pakai Hou-In sahdohna dingin, sana tons 4,000 kakhol khom in ahi. Chule dangka tons 40,000 jong kakhol khom in, sum-eng le thih jong ajat a tekham valset kakhol khom e, ijeh-inem itileh atam in, in thil le lo jong kakhol khom sohkeijin, hiche ho jouse hi nangman nabolbe ding ahina laiye. 
Nangman songkheng them leh thingthembol chule khutthepna chom-chom nei mi tamtah naneije. 
Sana lha-them, dangka kheng them, sum-eng lha them, thih kheng them chule adang mitampi nakoi ding ahi. Tun kipat in na tong tan, Pakaijin napanpi jing tahen!” ati. 
Chuin David in Israel mite sunga lamkai chengse thupeh aneijin, achapa Solomon akithopi diuvin thu aseijin ahi. 
“Pakai, na Pathen uvin nangho napanpi jingu hilou ham? Ama tah in najet le navei uva hungkon nagal miteu asuhthip a chamna naneiju hilou ham? Ajeh chu Pakai, ama tah in agamsunga cheng mite chu keima khut a apedoh in, hijeh chun agamsung jouse hi Pakai angsung leh amite masanga suhnem’a uma, mite noija kikunsah ahitai. 
Tun nang hon na lungthim pumpiu Pakai, na Pathen'u hol tauvin. Kipat un Pakai, Pathen muntheng Hou-In satauvin; chutileh Pakai Kitepna Thingkong le Pathen Hou-In a manchah um le bel chule thilkeo atheng jouse hiche Pakai dinga Hou-In kisadoh sunga chu pohlut a um ding ahitai,” ati.
