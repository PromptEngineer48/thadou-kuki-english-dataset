Thusimbu Nina.
1.
David chapa Solomon chun alenggam dettah in atungdohtai, ijeh inem itileh Pakai a Pathen chun aumpia chuleh sangtah a athanei sah ahitai. 
Solomon chun Israel lamkaiho galsat sepai asang aja chunga luboh ho thutan vaihom ho, gamsung lamkai hojouse chule phung lamkai ho akoukhomin ahi. 
Chuin Solomon chun houbung mipi chu Gibeon khoa chun Pathen houkhom dingin alamkaijin ache tauve, ijeh inem itileh hiche khoa Pathen Houbuh chu umma ahi (Hiche lai mun hi Pakai Lhacha Mose’n gamthip laiya Pathen Houbuh asemna mun chu ahi.) 
David chun Kiriath-Jearim a konna Jerusalem'a Pathen Thingkhong chu Houbuh a ana pohlutsa ahitai. 
Ahinlah Uri chapa, Hur tupa Bezalel kitipan asem sum-eng maicham chu Gibeon mun'a Pakai Houbuh maiya chun aum nalaijin ahi. Chuti chun Solomon le mipi chu Houbuh masanga chun akikhom un Pakai chu ahou tauvin ahi. 
Chukom'a Houbuh masanga chun Solomon chu sum-eng maicham umna achun akal touvin Pakai angsung'ah pumgo thilto 1,000 jen aga lhandoh tan ahi. 
Chuche jan chun Solomon henga Pathen ahung kilah in hitin ati, “Ipi pentah nadeijem? Thum'in, chule Keiman nangma kapeh ding nahi!” ati. 
Solomon in Pathen chu adonbutnin, “Nangman kapa David chu lungsetna longlouhel nachan'in, tun keima ama khelin lengin neipan sahtai. 
O Pakai Pathen, lungset tah in kapa David toh nakitepna koi jingin, ajeh chu nangin mihem atam'a tam leiset a vutdi jat lah a lengin nei pansah tan ahi! 
Mipi hijat chunga kituptah avaihom thei ding ham? Hijeh chun namite chunga vai kahom them theina dingin chihna le hetna neipen,” ati. 
Pathen chun Solomon jah a hitin aseijin, “Nei le gou, haona , loupina ahiloule nagal mite thina ding ahiloule na hinkho sotna joh thum louva na ngaichat sangpen na mite kithopi theina ding leh achung uva vaihom them theina nathum tah jeh hin, 
Keiman nathum pen chihna le hetna chu kapeh ding nahi. Chujongleh nei le gou , haosatna le minthanna jong kapehtha ding, hitobang thil hi na masang leh khonunga leng hungchang ding hon jong aneilou diu ahitai!” ati. 
Hiti chun Solomon chu Gibeon khoa Pathen Houbuh a kon chun Jerusalem lam'a ahung kinunglen Israel te chunga lengvai ana potan ahi. 
Solomon chun kangtalai le sakol touthem ho chu akoukhom in ahileh kangtalai sangkhat le jali 1,400 leh sakol touthem 12,000 aneijin ahi. Aman hichengse chu kangtalai khopiho a phabep akoijin chule phabep chu ama kom nai Jerusalem a jong akoijin ahi. 
Lengpa chun sana le dangka jong Jerusalem sunga chun atam dan songlheu lah abahsah jengin chuleh Cedar thingtum jeng jong Judah phaicham a theiba thing kehho abang jengin ahi. 
Solomon sakol ho chu ama dinga kivei hon Egypt gam le Celicia gam'a konna man tamtah a ahin tollut u ahi. 
Chuche phatlai chun Egypt gam'a kangtalai ho chu, dangka 600, chule sakol ho chu dangka 150 a ana kicho jouvin ahi. Hiti chun thil ho chu Hit lengte le Aram lengte kom'a ana kitoldoh jin ahi.
