Aman adungavai tong somni chule tong somma sang, sum-eng maicham phungkhat asemmin ahi. 
Chujouvin twitunna kongtong lentah khat asemin, agei langkhatna pat langkhat chan chu tong som ahin, hichu twipi tin aki minsah in; hiche kongtong bellen chu tong nga’n athuh in, tong somthum in akollin ahi. 
Hiche kongtong agei noitah’a chun bongchal lim tobanga kisem golnin akim vellin a kongtong kol lhungkeichun bongchal lim kengkhatna gup gupmin akigolhung keijin chule hiche ho chu kongtong kisema chu kisemtha ahitai. 
Twipi kisah kongtong lentah chu sum-eng bongchal som le ni chunga chun akikoi yin, abonchauvin apamlam angaove. Bongchal thum chun solam angai, chule amaho chunga chun kongtong lentah twipi kisah chu akingam in ahi. 
Twipi kisah kongtong chu khut phang toh kibanginasa’in , ageichin chu khon bangin apaltou in, twikoh pahcha pah tobang ahi. Hiche kongton chun twi tena sangsom sagi le li toh Jani le somnga adollin ahi. 
Chujongleh aman pumgo thiltonaa kimang umle bel ho sopna dingin jong kongtong som asemin, kongtong nga chu lhanglama akoiyin nga ma chu sahlama akoiyin ahi. Ahinlah thempuho chun kongtong twipi kiti a chun akisop jiuvin ahi. 
Chule aman sana thaomei khom som akiseipeh bang chun, asemin abonchan Hou-In sunga chun atungtai. Thaomei khom nga chu Hou-In bang jet langa akoi in, nga chu veilanga chun atungin ahi. 
Chujongleh aman dokhang som asemin, hichelah a chun dokhang nga chu bang jetlam'a adon, nga ma chu availam achun adotan ahi, chule sana kong jakhat jong asemin ahi. 
Ama chun thempu ho veilama thutana ding hongcha, chule indan inchu hongcha asahpeh in ahi. Hongcha hoa lutle potle nadingin akotjong atopeh in chule sum-eng in atom khumpeh in ahi. 
Sum-eng kongtong lentah twipi kitipen chu, Hou-In lhunna solam anunga chun akoiyin ahi. 
Chujongleh Hiram-abi chun Pathen houna dinga angaicha khoh thil ho sopna kongtong, vutlo chule chakong jong asemin ahi. Hiti chun Huram-abi chun Lengpa Solomon'in Pathen Hou-In a dinga ngaicha thil ho jouse anganse bangchun asem jouse asemjou sohkei tan ahi. 
Khompi teni luvuma kijopson; konglim kisem alukhuh teni, khon lukhuh teni jep hoina dinga kisem lencha pheh banga kipheh ho, 
khom lukhuh chunga kito lencha chunga kikhai kolbuthei 400 ho (Kolbu thei golni cheh chu khompi chunga kitoson khom kiphut jep hoina ding chu asemjou tan ahi); 
kongtong to kitomat kangtalai ho, 
twipi kiti kongtong le anoija bongchal ho; 
vut koina kong ho, vutlo ho, satul ho, chule akisamkaipi thil dang ho toh ahi. Haram-abi chun chuche thil ho jouse chu sum-eng jenga asem ahi. Pakai Hou-In a dinga Lengpa Solomon in athupeh banga, 
lengpa chun thil ho jouse chu Jordan phaicham Succoth le Zarethan kikah a chu alhahjol sohkei ahi. 
Solomon chun chutobang tah a mantam sum-eng chu amanchah ahin, agih dan jong seijou hoi ahipoi. 
Chujongleh Solomon chun Pathen Hou-In a dinga manchah jouse asemin: sana maicham, changlhah agol golla koina ding dokhang asemin; 
akisemdoh akisei banga muntheng chungnunga vah ding, sana thengsel'a kisem sana thaomei le thaomei khom ho; 
pahcha kijemhoi ho, thaomei chule chaiche ho chu aboncha sana thengsel'a kisem cheh ahiuve; 
Thaomei thamitna ho, kongtong ho bukong ho chule thil namtui halkhutna ho jong aboncha sana thengsel'a kisem ahi; Hou-In a Mun Theng Pen lutle potlena kotcha ho leh, kotpi ho jong chu sana thengsella kito ahisohkeijin ahi.
