2 Thusimbu.
8.
Solomon chun Pakai Hou-In leh ama leng inpi sahna in kum somni asulut in ahi. Hiche phat ho ahung kichai lang chun, 
Solomon chun Hiram lengpan anapeh khopi ho chu thah sah kit dingin lunglutna ahin neikitnin chule hiche khopi hoa chun Israel te chu achensah in ahi. 
Chujongleh Solomon chun Hamath-zobah khopi chu gallin akisatpin, akhopi chu alatan ahi. 
Gamthip gamsunga um Tadmor chule Hamath gamkaija um khopi ho chu athah sah kitnin, thil kholna mun in amangin ahi. 
Chunglam Beth-horon le noilam Beth-horon khopi ho chu kulpi akai in, abang ho athah sah kitnin chule thihkot aton ahi. 
Chujongleh Baalath le thil kholna munpi adang-dang ho asapha kitnin chule sakol kangtalai ho leh sakol ho koina thei ding khopi ho jong asadoh in ahi. Jerusalem le Lebanon chule alenggam pumpia aman angaichat jouse chu asadoh tan ahi. 
Gamsunga chun Israel mi hilou Hit mite, Amor mite, Periz mite, Hiv mite le Jebus mite jaonan mi phabep khat aching jing nalai un ahi. 
Amaho hi chidang namdang ho chilhah Israel miten ana suhmang lou ho ahiuve. Hiti chun Solomon chun amaho chu ada acham louva thaguma natongin apansah in, chule tuni geijin amaho chu thaguma natong in apang jing nalai uve. 
Ahinlah Solomon chun Israel mite chu koimacha ada acham louvin thagum natongin apansah poi. Hiche khel chun amaho chu galsat sepai ding, sepai sunga lamkai ding, sakol kangtalai ho lamkai ding chule sakol kangtalai ho tol dingin anganse tan ahi. 
Leng Solomon chun amaho lah a mi 250 chu mipi ho natoh vetup dingin apansah un ahi. 
Solomon chun ajinu Pharaoh chanu chu David khopia kon chun amanu dinga athah a asah leng inpi achun aumsah tan ahi. Chuin Solomon in aseijin, “Kajinu hi Lengpa David inpia hi um thei mong louding ahi, ajeh chu Pakai Thingkhong chu khuche lai mun'a ana uma ahin, chule hiche lai mun hi muntheng ahi,” ati. 
Chuin Solomon chun Hou-In lutna indan maiya aman ana semsa maicham chunga chun, Pakaiya din pumgo thilto ho chu alhandoh tan ahi. 
Hiti hin Solomon chun Mose in athupeh bang chun cholngah niseh, lhathah kut ho chule akum lhah a kimang kut thupi thumho—Kalchuh Kut, Lholhun Kut chule Lhambuh Kut a dingin maicham thilto ho chu alhandoh tan ahi. 
Thempu ho toh ding ngansena kipehna lama hin Solomon chun apa David dan semho chu ajuijin ahi. Chuleh Solomon chun Levi te chu Pathen vahchoina lama mipi ho alamkai diu chule thempu hon niseh a atoh diu natohna chu akithopi diuvin angansen ahi. Chule Pathen mipa David thupeh ho dungjuiya kot ngahho chun ama-ama kot anga tup diuvin anganse tan ahi. 
Solomon chun thempu ho leh Levi te chule gou thil ho chung chang thua hin, David dan thupeh ho chu neocha jong alam louvin achepi pon ahi. 
Hitobang hin Solomon chun anatoh ding jouse, Pakai Hou-In kisah tilla pat akisah chai nikho chan gei chu atong chai tan ahi. 
Chujou chun Edom twikhanglen panga Ezion–geber le Elath munho a agache in ahi. 
Chuin Hiram chun ama kongtol lamkaiho leh thepna sangtah nei kongtol ho chu asolpeh in ahi. Hiche kong innei ho chu Solomon miho tochun Ophir chan atol uvin, chuleh ahinpoh uu sana tons som le sage lhing dehchet chu Lengpa Solomon ape tauvin ahi.
