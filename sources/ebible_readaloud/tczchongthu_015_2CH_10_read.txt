2 Thusimbu.
10.
Rehoboam chu Shachem mun'a achetan ahi, ajeh chu hichelai munna chu Israel mipi chu hung kikhom uva ama chu Lengpa’a pansah ding atiu ahi. 
Hiche thu chu Nebat chapa Jeroboam in ajadoh phat chun, Lengpa Solomon a konna sochatna ding deina jal'a Egypt gam'a ana jammang na munna (Egypt mun'a), kon chun ahung kile kit tan ahi. 
Israel lamkai ho chun ama chu akou uvin, chuin Jeroboam le Israel mipi chu Rehoboam kihoupi ding chun acheuvin ama kom'a chun hitin aseijiuvin ahi. 
“Napan kachung uva akoi namkol jon hi agih valle, hiche natoh hahsa le kachung uva kikoi namkol jon gihtah hi nei suh jang peh uvin,” atiuvin ahi. 
Rehoboam in adonbut in, “Nithum joule kaheng'a hung kile kitnun,” ati. Hiti chun mipi ho chu achemang tauvin ahi. 
Chuin Rehoboam lengpa chun apa Solomon damlai’a alhachaa anapang upaho ahoulim pin, hiche miho hi iti donbut leng natiu vem? tin aman adong tan ahi. 
ama hon adonbut’un hiche miho chu nakhotoa ahile amaho chu lunglhai sah in, lang angaichat bang bang u chun donbut tan; chutileh amaho chu nalhachan pangjing tauvinte , atiuvin ahi. 
Ahinlah Rehoboam chun upahon thumop apeh uchu anoppeh tapon, chuin ama toh thakhatna hungseilen le ama jenle khangthah ho chu ahoulim pin ahi. 
Aman amaho chu adongin, “Nangho thumop ipi hi ding ham? Itilama hiche mihon kakom'a aseiju napan kachung uva akoi namkol jon hi neisuh jang peh un, tia asei uhi iti lama adonbutna ipeh diu ham?” 
Ama toh hungseilen khom khangthah ho chun adonbut nun, mipi hon nakom'a aseiyu, “Napan kachung uva eikoipeh’u namkol jon hi agih valle, hijongleh nangman neisuh jang peh un atiuchu kakhut jung mecha hi kapa kong sanga lenjo ahi,” tin seipeh tan atiuve. 
“Kapan namkol jon gih chu keiman agih jep'a kabol ding ahi,” tin seipehtan atiuve. “Kapan savun khaohola najep’u ahin, keiman tenggol pah mun’a kajep ding nahiuve,” tin seipeh un atiuve. 
Lengpan, “Nithum sungin kakom'a hungkit nun,” atibang bang chun Jeroboam le mipi ho jouse chu nithum jouvin Rehoboam kom'a ahungkit tauvin ahi. 
Lengpan upaho thumop chu anotthap min, engse tah in amaho chu ana donbut in. 
“Khangthah ho thumop ajuijin, kapan na namkol’jon gihtah in asemin , keiman agihjo cheh’a kasempeh ding nahive , kapan savun khao holla najep u ahin, keiman sakol jepna a kajep ding nahiuve,” atin ahi. 
Hiti chun Lengpa chun mipi ho thu chu angaipeh tapou vin ahi. Ajeh chu hiche thilsoh ho jouse hi Pathen a konna Sheloh mipa Ahijah in Nebat chapa Jeroboam kom'a, Pakaijin anaseidohsa thu chu guilhung ahi. 
Israel mipi ho chun lengpan athu sei angaipeh louphat uchun lengpa chu adonbut nun, “Jesse chapa David kom'a keihon ipi chan, ipi mun kanei diu ham? Israel! Naponbuh achun David in na insung bou vesuitan,” atiuvin ahi, hiti chun Israel mipi jouse chu ain lam uvah ache tauvin ahi. 
Ahinlah Judah khopi hoa cheng jing nalai Israel mite ding chun, Rehoboam in vai ahom nalaijin ahi. 
Lengpa Rehoboam chun Adoniram kitipa ada achamlouva nakitohsah ho chunga mopopa chu asoldoh in, ahinlah ama chu Israel mipi hon song a anaseplih tauvin ahi. Hijeng jongleh Lengpa Rehoboam chu a sakol kangtalai achun anakaldoh man in, Jerusalem lama ana jamdoh in ahi. 
Hiti chun Israel mite chun tuni chanin, David insung chu adou jing nalai un ahi.
