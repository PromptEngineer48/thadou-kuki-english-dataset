2 Thusimbu.
13.
Israel gamsunga Rehoboam leng chan kal kum somle get lhin kumin, Abijah chun Judah gamsunga vai ahin hom panin ahi. 
Aman Jerusalem a kumthum lengvai ahinpon, anu chu Gebiah mi Uriel chanu Maacah ahi. Chuin Abijah le Jeroboam kah’a gal asohdoh tan ahi. 
Judah te lengpa Abijah chun gal-hang mi sang jali alhengin, akhom tupmin alamkaiyin; alangkhatna Jeroboam chun Israel gamsunga kon'in sepai sang jaget akhom doh kit in ahi. 
Judah sepai ho chu Ephraim thinglhang gam ahung lhun phat uchun, Abijah chu Zemorin molchunga addingin Jeroboam le Israel te chu asam in, “Neihin ngaipeh temun, 
Pakai Israel te Pathen'in David toh kitepna asema ama le achilhah te Israel laltouna atonsotna dinga apeh chu nagel doh lou’u hitam?” 
Ahivangin David chapa Solomon lhachaa pang mai mai Nabet chapa Jeroboam chun, apupa adouvin ahi. 
Chujongleh migilou pumpi chun ama ajopmun, Solomon chapa Rehoboam chapangcha le hetna thepna neiloulai chu athu ngailouvin; dingdoh theilou khopmin ana lhauvin ahi. 
Chule tun David chihlhah te khutna um Pakai lenggam dou dingin tohgon nasem kit uvin, nang hon sepai tampi leh Jeroboam in na Pathen diuva asem sana Bongnou lim chu nanei maithei’u ahinai. 
Hijongleh nang hon Pakai thempu nakilhen’u hilouham? Koi hijongleh bongnou khat le kelngoi sagi toh, hiche alimsemthu na pathen hou kom'a hung kipedoh tapou chu thempua napansah jiu ahi. 
Keiho dinga vang Pakai chu ka Pathen’u ahi, chule keihon ama chu ka dalha pouve; Pakai lhacha’a pang thempu ho chu Aaron chapa te bou ahiuvin, Levi ten akithopi jiu ahi. 
Amaho seh jingkah nilhah seh in Pakai angsunga pumgo thilto le gimnamtui hal aboljiuvin ahi. Nilhah sehle dokhang theng chunga chun changlhah agoldoh jiuvin, chule sana thaomei chunga chun thaomei ho avah jiuvin ahi. Keihon Pakai ka Pathen thuhil kajui jingun, ahinlah nang hon ama chu nalha tauve. 
Chuti chun veuvin Pathen chun eiumpiuve. Ama hi kalamkai pao ahi, Ama thempu hon asumkon’u amut uva galmun'a nang douna eilamkaiju ahi. “Vo Israel mite, Pakai napu napa teu dounan galsat hih un; ajeh chu na lolhin lou diu ahi.” 
Hiche kahla achun Jeroboam chun guhthim chan asepai te phabap khat chu, Judah te nung lama konna umkim vella changlhi din asel’in ahi. 
Judah te chun anung ama konna ahung kino khumu chu ahetdoh phat uchun, akithopi diuvin Pakai kom'a ataouvin, chuin thempu hon sumkon amut un, 
chule Judah mite chun kho asam pan tauvin ahi. Agalsat khosap ogin husan chun Pathen'in Jeroboam chu ajopeh un chule Israel te Abijah le Judah te sepaiho masang chun adelmang tan ahi. 
Israel sepai ho chu Judah te masanga kon'in ajamang un, chuin Pathen'in Judah te chu Israel mite khut nakon ahuhdohtan ahi. 
Abijah le sepai te chun amaho chu nasatah in atong khaovin, hiche ni chun Israel te sepai athemcheh mi sang janga athi in ahi. 
Hiti chun hiche thilsoh chunga hin Judah ten Israel te ajou un ahi . 
Abijah le asepai te chun Jeroboam sepai ho chu aga nung del peh-un, khopi phabep Bethel, Jeshanah le Ephron jaonan chule akimvella kho neo ho chu aga la tauvin ahi. 
Chuti chun Abijah dam sungin Jeroboam chu athaneina akinung le doh kit tapon, chule achaina lama Pakaijin ajeplhun athi tan ahi. 
Ahinlah Abijah thaneina vang chu akhangtou jingin, Abijah chun ji som le li aneijin; chule chapa somnile ni le chanu somle gup aneijin ahi. 
Abijah Lengvaipoh sunga thilsoh dang ho leh, athusei alahsah ho leh anatoh athibol ho jouse chu; Iddo themgao Lekhabua chun akijih lut sohkeiyin ahi.
