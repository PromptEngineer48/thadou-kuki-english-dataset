2 Thusimbu.
18.
Jehoshaphat hi haona le jabolna'a bulhing setnin ananeitan ahi. Ama hin numei kilah tona lamma Ahab to kisam kaina ananei lhon'in ahi. 
Kum phabep jouhin Samaria Ahab villin aga che suhin ahileh, Ahab in bongchal leh kelngoi athat nin ama leh ahin kilhon piho neh ding chun nengtah in anagon peh in; chuleh Ramoth Gilead langa chetou dingin ana tem in ahi. 
Ahab lengpa chun Jehoshaphat jah a chun Ramoth Gilead toh kidouna dinga hi neihung kitho pi ding ham anatin ahi. Jehoshaphat chun adonbut in, “Keile nang thakhat ihin, keimite jong nang mite ahin hiche galla chun nahung kithopi nange,” ati. 
Chujongleh Jehoshaphat chun Israel lengpa kom'a chun aseijin, “Tunin Pathen'in epi asei jem veohite ati” 
Hichun Israel lengpan themgao jali akou khommin amaho chu adongin, “Keiho hi Ramoth – Gilead satna galkon na kachediu ham ahilouleh kacheda diuham?” ati. Hichun amaho chun, “Chetou jengun amaho chu Pathen'in lengpa nangkhut a ahin pehdoh ding ahi,” atiuve. 
Ahinlah Jehoshaphat in, “Tua hi eihon idoh theiju themgao adang umlou hitam?” ati. 
Israel lengpa chun Jehoshaphat kom’a, “Eihon idoh theiju Pakai themgao khatvang aumme, ahinlah keiman ama hi kavetnom poi; ajeh chu ama hin keidinga thilpha aseingai jipon, thil phalou ngen eisei peh ji ahi. Ama chu Imla chapa Micaiah kitipa chu ahi. Hichun Jehoshaphat in,“That’s not the way a king should talk! Let’s hear what he has to say.” “Lengpa hiti chun thu seihih in,” ati. 
Hiche phat chun Israel lengpan a sepai lamkai khat chu akouvin, Imla chapa Micaiah chu gangtah in gapuijin ati. 
Israel lengpaleh Judah lengpa Jehoshaphat chu aleng von lhon cheh a akivon lhon'in, Samaria khopi lutna kelkot lutna tollhang achun atou lhon'in amasang lhonna themgao ho chun gaothu aseijun ahi 
Zedekiah chun thih alan saki limin asemmin, Pakaijin hitin aseije, “Nangman hiche saki hi namanna Aram mite chu na suhmang ding ahi,” ati. 
Hiti chun themgao ho jouse chun gaothu aseisohkeiyun, Ramoth Gilead jonin chetouvun nalolhin diu ahi, ajeh chu Pakaijin amaho chu lengpa khutna ahin pehlut ding ahi atiuve 
Micaiah kouva kisol pa chun akom’a hitin aseijin, “Ven, themgao ho jouse chun lengpan gal ajo dingin aseisohkeiyun ahi, nangman jong hitimachun seile chun phading ahi,” atipeh e. 
Hichu Micaiah in adonbut in, “Hingjing Pakai minna kasei ahi, ka Pathen'in asei seibou kaseiding ahi,” ati. 
Micaiah chu lengpa kom'ahung lhun phat in, lengpan, “Micaiah Ramoth – Gilead na hi galsatna kache diuham ahilouleh kache dauva pha ham? tin adongin ahileh aman, “Cheovin galolhing tauvin amaho chun nangma khut-a pehdoh in umnante,” atipeh in ahi. 
Hichun lengpan hitin aseije, “Micaiah nangkom mahi ijatvei Pakai minnin thudih seijin tia kangeh ding nahim?” atin ahi. 
Hichun Micaiah in aseijin, “Keiman Israel tehi achingbei kelngoi banga molchung dunga akithe cheh-u kamui,” chuleh Pakaijin hitin aseijin “Amaho hi lungmongin a incheh uva kileu hen,” ati. 
Israel lengpa chun Jehoshaphat kom'a hitin aseije, “Ama hin kakom'a thilpha asei khapon, thilse jeng aseije kati hilouham?” atin ahi. 
Hichun Micaiah in aseijin Pakaijin hitin aseije, “Ngaijun Pakai chu alal touna – a atouvin van mite chu ajet aveijah adingun ahi.” 
“Pakai chun hitin aseijin, “Koipenin Ahab kom'a nacheova Ramoth – Gilead nakhu aga lhuhkam nadinga naga seipeh diu ham? Hichun vantil khatchun hitin aseijin achom khat nin thilchom aseijin ahi. (check) 
Hichun lhagao khat nin Pakai angsung'ahin nailut nin keima chengtin galhem lhange ati, Pakaijin i-nagalo ding ham? ati. 
“Hichun aman keima chengting Ahab themgao jouse chu jou gaseisah ingkate ati, Pakaijin chenlang hiti chun gabollin lang hung lolhing tan,” ati. 
Hijeh chun veovin! “Pakaijin a themgao jouse chu jou aseisah ahin, Pakaijin naman thah nadinga asei sah ahi bouve,” ati. 
Hichun Chenaanah chapa Zedekiah chun anailut nin angeiphe abeh pih in, itih a chu Pakai lhagaovin keima eidalhah a nangkom'a thu asei ham?” ati. 
Micaiah chun adonbut in “Tomlam a nalhailut’a naga kisel teng hetannate!” atipehin ahi. 
Hichun Israel lengpan a sepai lamkai kom'a chun, man inlang khopi vaihompa leh leng chapa Joash kom'ah ga pelut tan ati. 
Amani kom'a chun songkulla akoilhon ding keima damsella kahung kilekit kahsen aneh ding changlhah themkhat toh twi themkhat anapeo vin ati. 
Micaiah in asamjah jengin, nangma nahung kile poupouleh Pathen'in keikom'a thu aseilou hi ding ahi! ati. “Chuleh aman Avel'a umho kom'a chun hiche kasei hi jasohkeiyun,” ati. 
Hiche jou chun Israel lengpan Ahab leh Judah lengpa Jehoshaphat chu, Ramoth – Gilead khopi chu abulu dingin akondoh lhontai. 
Ahab chun Jehoshaphat kom'ah hitin aseijin, “Galmunna ilut tengleh nangin lengvon kivon nin lang, ken vonchom kivon inge,” ati. Hichun Israel lengpa chun vonchom aki vonnin gal munnah aluttan ahi. 
Aram lengpa chun sakol kangchunga tou gallam kai ho chu Israel lengpa bou atup diuvin thu apen ahi. 
Hijeh chun Jehoshaphat lengpa chu amu phatnun Israel lengpa ahisah sohkeiyun, anokhum tauvin ahi. Ahinlah Jehoshaphat chu apeng jah in ahileh, Pakaijin ahuhdoh in ama nokhumho chu adalhah sah tan ahi. 
Sakol kangchunga tou lamkai ho chun ama chu Israel lengpa ahilou ahedoh tauvin adalha tauve. 
Ahinlah Syria sepai khatnin thalchang khatchu agah donkapmin ahileh, Israel lengpa galvon kikah – a chun akaplut khatan ahi, hichun asakol kangtalai tolpa kom'a chun kakitong khatai galmunna kon'in neitol doh tan atin ahi. 
Hiche ni chun galkisat chu akhoh cheh jengin, Israel lengpa vangchu sakol kangtalai chunga aoplhatan, nilhah lang geijin Aram te lang chu angan nilhum kon'in athitai.
