2 Thusimbu.
21.
Jehoshaphat chu apu apate toh akicholdo khom tauvin David khopiah ana kivuitaan ahi, hijou chun achapa Jehoram min vai ahin hom tan ahi 
Aman Jehoshaphat chate sopi phabep ananeijin ahi: Azariah, Jehiel, Zachariah, Azariahu, Michael, chuleh Shephatiah ahiovin amaho jousechu Judah lengpa Jehoshaphat chate ahiove 
Amaho chu apaovin Sana dangka chuleh thilman tamtah tah hi anapeuvin chuleh kul kikai khum Judah khopi ho khat chehtoh anape chehin ahinla lenggam vangchu Jehoram anapei ajeh chu ama hi acha masapen ahi 
Jehoram chu alenggam akisuhdet phatnin athahat becheh nadingin asopiho jouseleh Israel leng chaho jong athat gamsohhellin ahi 
Jehoram leng ahung chanhin akum 32 alhingtaan ahi, chuleh Jerusalem mahin kum 8 bou aleng changin ahi 
Ama hi Israel lengte chonnin achonnin Ahab tobangin atongin ahi, ajeh chu aman Ahab chanu akichenpin Pakai deilou lamjengin akimangchan ahi 
Ahivangin David toh kitepna aneidungjuijin Davip chilhah ho hi asumang nompon ahi, ajeh chu David lengmun alojing diuva Pakaijin kitepna ananeisa ahi 
Jehoram vaihom sungin Edommin gal ahin bollin lenggam atummin ahin kitundoh tauvin ahi 
Ahin Jehoram leh agal lamkaiho chu Sakolkang talai chungah atouvun Edom ana nokhum mun ahi, ahinlah Edom sepai ten akol khummin ahin amaho jannin ajamdoh un ahi 
Hichea patnin Edom ten Judaha kon'in chamlhat na ahinnei tauvin ahi, Hichesunga hin Libnah khopi injong kiphin na ahinneitaan ahi, ajeh chu Jehoram hin apu apa te Pathen apaidohtaan ahi 
Ama hin doihouna munho anatung doh in Judah leh Jerusalem mipi ho hi Pathen dounan ana chonset sahgamtaan ahi 
Elijah themgaovin Jehoram chu lekha ahin thotnin, “Pakai napu napa David Pathen chun nangmahi napaidohtaan ahi, ajeh chu nangin napu Asa lengpa napa Jehoshaphat chonbangin nachon tapon, 
Amavang Israel lengho chonin nahung chonnin Judah le Jerusalem mipite hi Pathen doumah nabolsahin Ahab leh Israel lengho bangin na chontan chuleh nangsanga phajo nasopiho jong nahinthat gamhellin ahi 
Hiche jeh a hi Pakaijin na mipite leh nacha nanao teleh najite asuhgenthei ding chuleh naneinagou ho jong asuhmang ding ahi 
Nangmatah jeng jong nagil sunga thoh lel umtah hivei nakhumpeh a niseh a khoh cheh cheh ding ahi 
Hiche chung chon hin Pakaijin Philistine teleh Ethopia kom'a cheng Arabiate Jehoram doudingin ahin choudoh tan ahi 
Amaho hi Judah gam'a ahunglutnun Judah te akisatpiovin leng inpia neilegou um jouse achom'un hiche tilou vinjong aji achate se akaimangun, achate lah a aneopen Jehoahaz bou akhenpehin ahi 
Hiche jouhin Pakaijin lengpa gilsunga chun thohlel umtah hivei anatdohsah taan ahi 
Kumni sungin lengpa natna chu athohlel cheh chehin ajona in gentheitah in athipi taan ahi. Amipi ten jong apu apateu athiteng alung hempinao vetsahna a mei atijio chu atih tapouvin ahi 
Jehoram min kum 32 (Somthum le ni) alhinna lengmun alo pat ahin kum 8 sungin bou vai ahommin ahi. Athichun koimacha alhase alunghem aumpon ahi. Ama chu lengte kivuina –a avui pouvin David khopi sunga vang anavui naovin ahi
