2 Thusimbu.
28.
Ahaz hi leng ahungchan chun kum 20 alhingtaan ahi, ama hin kum 16 sungin Jerusalemma vai anahomin ahi. Ama hin apa Davidnin ana bolbangin Pakai mitmun thildih anabol pon ahi 
Ama hin Israel lengte chondaan nin anachonin Baal doi lim ho anasemdoh in ahi 
Ama hin hinnom chapa phaigam'a chun gimnamtui meiya analhut nammin, achate meijah anagouvin doi houna in pumgo thiltonan anamangin, Israel te hung kijotna gam'a konna Pakaiin ana nodoh nam mite chonna kidah umtah chonnin ana chonnin ahi 
Aman munsang dungleh, molchung dungah chuleh thingphung noi tinnah pumgo thilto ana bollin gimnamtui analhut nammin ahi 
Hitobanga achonphat ahin Pakai a Pathen'in Aram lengpa khutnah anapeh dohtaan ahi. Ama hon ajophatnin mi tamtah gal hingin ahin kaijun Damascas lang alhut tauve hiche jou chun ama chu Israel lengpa khutna pehlut nin aummin Ahaz in gal alaltaan mi tamtah jong athiovin ahi 
Ramaliah chapa Pekah chun Judah te chu nikhat sungin mihangsan cheh 120,000 athat nin ahi, ajeh chu ama hon apu apateu Pakai Pathen chu anasuh mil jeh u ahi 
Mi mihattah Zichri kitipa chun lengpa chapa Maaseiah chu athatnin, khopi sunga vaihomma pang Azrikam leh lengpa dinga sepai lamkai anichanna Elkanah jong athatnin ahi 
Israel tedinga Judah te chu asopi teu ahivangin Israel sepai ten numei chapang 200,000 galhingn amannun achomdoh u thil keo tamtah toh Samaria gam'ah galhingin akaijun ahi 
Ahin Oded kiti Pakai themgao pa chu Samaria sepaiho kom'a chun achen hitin agaseije: “Veovin napu napateu Pa Pathen chu Judah te chunga alunghan jeh a amaho chu nangho khutna ahin pehlut ahi, ahinlah nang hon lungkhen louhellin nathatnun apen ginnu kakom'ahunglhungpeh in ahi 
Tunjong nang hon Judah leh Jerusalem mite chu pasal ahin numei ahiovin nasohun napansah gotauvin ahinlah nangho jong hi Pakai na Pathen'u maija chonse nahilou uham? 
“Tun kaseihi ngaijun nasopi te ho chu galhinga namat houchu nungsolkit tauvin ajeh chu nachung uvah Pakai alunghang taan ahi. 
Hichun Ephraim chate lah a miupa phabep ho chu adingdoh un, Jehohanan chapa Azariah, Meshillemoth chapa Berekiah, Shallum chapa Jehizkiah, Hadlai chapa Amasa chu adingdoh un galsat hungkile ho chu akimai to piovin hitin aseijun ahi 
Nanghon galhinga mi nahin kailutlou diu ahi: “Nanghon ichonset nau nabelapmun galhingin mi nahin kaitauvin ahi, eihon nasatah in Pathen Israel chungah Ilunghan sah jeng tauvin ahi” atiove 
Hichun thalchoi sepai ho chun amimat houleh athil chomdoh ho chu khopi lamkaiho masanga chun ahin koitauvin ahi 
Amincheh a kipanna kinganse ho chu ahung ding dohun thilkeo kichomdoh ho leh galhinga kiman ho chu ahin puidoh un akeova umho chu von avonpehun sandel asepehun anneh twidon apeuvin thaojong anupehun ahi. Alhasam ho chu sangan chungah atousah un hiti chun jubiphung khopi Jericho – a asopihou kom geichu ahungthah un ahi. Hichea konchun Samaria a akile tauve 
Hichephat lai chun Ahaz lengpan Assyriah lengpa chu akitho pidingin atemmin ahileh anompon ahi 
Ajeh chu Edom mite ahungun Judah chu ahinbulu uvin galhingin amannun akaitauvin ahi 
Philistine tejong ahungun Judah phaicham leh lhanglam ahin bulu uvin, Beth Shemesh, Ajalon, Gederoth, Shocho thinglhang khoho chuleh Gimzo leh thinglhang kho ho chu alonvuh un achenkhum tauve 
Ajeh chu Judah lengpa Ahaz in a mite chan asuhboipeh a Pakai douna na atohjeh a Pakaijin Judah tehi a engbol ahi 
Assyria lengpan Ahaz hi kithopi tahsangin adouvin apangjon asugim jon ahi 
Hijehcun Ahaz hin Hou-In na kon leh, khopia kon'in chuleh mipi lamkai ho kon'in Sana ana chomkhom min Assyria lengpa chu anapen, ahivangin aman ana kithopi dehpoi 
Hahsatna akhon geija atotah vang'in Ahaz hin Pakai doumah abolbe jingin ahi 
Ama hin achunga galhin bolla ahinjou chuleh ahin sugenthei Assyria mite semthu pathen ho kom'ah pumgo thilto abollin kilhaina asemmin ahi, aman aseijin “Pathen hon Aram lengho akithopin ahi, hijeh chun kenjong hiche pathen ho kom'a hin kilhaina semleng keijong eikithopi diu ahi” ati. Ahinlah hiche hin ama le amite chungah hahsatna alhunbe jon ahi 
Hiche bannahjong hin aman Pathen'in na thilkeo ho jouse achu alaan asugoi sohkeijin chuin Hou-In kotchu akhah in Jerusalem khopi muntin'ah doiphung atungdoh in ahi 
Judah gam'a khopi leh khopi neo ho munjousea semthu pathen houna atungdoh in gimnamtui ho ahalnammin gamdanga pathen ho chu ahou taan ahi, hiti chun aman apu apate Pakai Pathen lunghanna chu akiloilut khum taan ahi 
Hiti chun avaihom sunga anatoh athilbol jouse chu apatna patna ajona gei Judah leh Israel lengho thusimbua akijih lutsohkeijin ahi 
Hitia Ahaz lengpahi athitaan ahileh lengte lhanna vuilouvin Jerusalem khopi sunga anavuitauve. Achapa Hezekiah chu apa khellin leng achangtaan ahi
