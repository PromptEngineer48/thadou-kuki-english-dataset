2 Thusimbu.
29.
Hezekiah hi kum 25 alhinkum chun leng ahung changin kum 29 suingin Jerusalem mah vai anahomme, anu hi Zachariah chanu Abijah anahi 
Ama hin apa David nin ana bolbang bangin Pathen deilam ngen anabollin ahi 
Avaihom kum amasapen leh lha masapennin aman Pakaijinchu akot ho jouse ahongin abonin asemphan ahi 
Abannin aman thempu ho leh Levi ho jouse chu Hou-In solang hongcha – a chun akhommin 
Amaho kom'a chun hitin aseije: “kasei hi ngaijun Levi te tun nangho le nangho kisuthengun chujouleh napu napateu Pakai Pathen Hou-In suthengun, chujouleh, thil thenglou jouse chu Hou-Inna kon'in paidoh un. 
Ajeh chu Ipu ipateuvin Pakai I-Pathen'u mitmun thilse jeng anabollun achonkhel tauvin ahi, Pathen anungsunnun Pathen chenna a kon'in amai aheimang tauvin ahi 
ama hon Hou-In kot akhah un thaomeiho amutlih un chuleh Israel Pathen Hou-In nah pumgo thilto abol tapouvin gimnamtui jong ahal tapouvin ahi 
Hitia hi anachon tahjehun Pakai hi Judah nam leh Jerusalem chungah analunghangin achung uva ichan geiya na anatoh hitam, hiche hin mijouse ana lungdon sah in akichat sahtaan ahi. Hiche hi nang hon nahe sohkeijuve 
Ipateu galla anathat nun ijiteu leh ichateu gal hingin ana kaimangun ahi 
Tuhin keiman Israel Pathen ipakaiyu kom'a hin kitep nakhat kasem nomme chutileh ama eiho chungah lunghang taponte 
“Kachate ho heche hi nahsah mon koihih un, ajeh chu Pakaijin nangho hi a anga ding jinga ama lungdeilam bolding leh akin tong dinga chuleh gimnamtui hal namdinga nalhen u ahi” ati 
Hichun Levi te ahung kipatdoh tauve: Amasai chapa Mahath chuleh Azariah chapa Joel, Kohathites chate ho Merari chate ho, Abdi chapa Kish chuleh Jehallelel chapa Azariah, Gershon chate ho Zimmah chapa Joash chuleh Joash chapa Eden 
Elizaphan chate chu Shimri leh Jeiel ahin, Asaph chate chu Zechariah leh Mattaniah ahin, 
Heman chate chu Jehiel leh Shimei ahin, Jeduthun chate chu Shemaiah leh Uzziel ahi 
Hiti chun amaho insung chu akikhomun amaho leh amaho akisuthengun lengpa thupeh leh Pakai dei dungjuijin achonnun Pakai Hou-In chu asuthengun ahi 
Hichun thempu ho chu Pakai Hou-In sunglanga chun alut un asuthengun chuleh Pakai Hou-In Pakai hongcha chan gei jin ahin pou – vin chuleh Levi ten apodoh un Kidron phaicham'ah aga pailhaovin ahi 
Natoh chu lhamasa – a nikho maspenni chu anapatnu ahi niget channin ana tongchai sohkeiyun ahi, hichea hin Hou-Inna lutna dansung jong achai tauvin hichejou chun niget abol beovin hiche lha sung ni 16 chan geijin abollun Hou-In chu houkhom na thei aso tauvin ahi 
Levi te chun lengpa Hezekiah kom'ah hitin thu alhut nun ahi: “Keihon hou dan dungjuiya thenna kinchu Hou-In pumpi ahin pumgo thilto na maicham phung geijin chuleh changlhah kisemna table leh amanchahho pummin kahin sutheng tauve”atiovin ahi 
Hiche chung chonchun Ahaz lengpa Pathen doumah abolsunga anapaidoh thilkeo ho jong chu kahin chomkhom'un kasuthengun Pakai maicham masangah kakoi tauve” 
Jingpi tah in lengpa Hezekiah in khopi sunga lamkaiho jouse akoukhom min Hou-In langa ache tauve 
Lengte insung leh Judah mipite chonsetna suhtheng nading leh Hou-In suhtheng na dingin ama hon bongchal 7, kelngoi 7 chuleh kelchal 7 hicheng hi Aaron chilhah te thempuhon maicham a pumgo thilto a atodoh diuvin aseipeh in ahi 
Thempuhon bongchal chu athat masaovin abannin kelngoi chal ho abannin kelngoi ho athat nun akitodoh gancha thisan ho chu maicham chunga atheovin ahi 
Achaina in kelchal chu lengpa kom leh taona mangho kom'a chun akailutnun ama hon gancha ho chunga chun akhut angam mun ahi 
Hijou chun thempuhon kelchal ho chu athatnun athisan chun mipite chonsetna apohmang na dinga kitodoh hi dingin maicham chunga chun atheovin ahi 
Nathan themgao leh lengpa themgao Gad nin Pathenna konna leng David thu ana pohchu Hezekiah lengpa hin phatah in ana juijin ahi. Aman Levi te chu selangdah leh tumging anakichoi sah in Hou-Innah pan analah sah in ahi 
Hiche atumgingu manchah chu leng David nin ana choi tobang bang chu ahi, thempu ho jong sumkon kichoi pummin adingun ahi 
Hezekiah in pumgo thilto chu patdinga thu ahinpeh phatnin thilpeh chu ahung kipan tan chuleh mipi chun Pakai thangvah na la asaovin, tumging saithem ho chun sumkon mut toh agingthei jouse ahin sugingun ahi 
Mipi aum jouse chu Pathen houna – a chun apangun ahi; chuleh pumgo thilto ho chu akahlhah jou kahsen tumging leh la kisa ho chu achelhah jingin ahi 
Hijou teng chuleh Hezekiah lengpa leh mipi jouse chun adilsuo vin Pathen chibai abohun ahi 
Lengpa Hezekiah leh nampi lamkaihon themgao Asaph leh David nin anasem thangvah na lachu Levi ten asahdiuvin thu anapeuvin ahi, amaho adilsuo vin Pathen chibai abohun thangvah lachu kipah tah in asaovin ahi 
Hezekiah chun mipi kom'ah aseijin “Tua hi nangho dan dungjuiya thenga nahitauvin, hijeh chun Pakai thangvah na in pumgo thilto dinghi tohdoh tauvin atin ahi. Ama hon sei angaijun mi phabep chun acham chammin pumgo thilto dingin aganchao ahin kaijun ahi 
ama hon bongchal 70,000 (Sang som sagi) kelngoichal 100 leh kelngoi 200 Pakaiya pumgo thilto a dingin ahin tohdoh un ahi 
ama hon Bongchal 600 leh kelngoichal 3000 (Sangthum) mipi ho nehdigin atodoh un ahi 
Hiche gancha ho hi that dingin thempuho alhahsam jeh in natoh chu akichai tokah in Levi ten ana kitho piovin ahi. Hijou chun thempuho chu ana kithenso beovin ahi. Levi ho chu thempuho sangin kisuhtheng nalam ma chun anagun chujo uvin ahi 
Pumgo thilto hijat banna jonghin chamna thilto sathao jong ahinalai jin, chuleh donthei thilto ho pumgo thilto a manchah ding jong ahinalai jin ahi. Hiti hin Pakai Hou-In sunga akin bol ding jouse chu akisutoh sohkeitan ahi 
Hiti chun Hezekiah lengpa leh amite chu akipah lheh jengun ahi, ajeh chu Pathen kithopina jallin toh ding jouse chu gangtah in achai tauvin ahi
