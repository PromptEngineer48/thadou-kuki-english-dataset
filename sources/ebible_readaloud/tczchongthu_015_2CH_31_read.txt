2 Thusimbu.
31.
Hiche kut hi akichai jouvin Israel mipite chu Judah khopi jousea acheuvin song khomho, Asherah semthu pathen limho akhen se uvin chuleh namdang Pathen houna maicham ho jouse asusesohkeiyun ahi. Ama hon Judah gamsung jouseleh, Benjamin, Ephraim chuleh Manesseh gamsung jousea hiti chun abolsohkeiyun ahi 
Hezekiah lengpan thempu ho leh Levi te kimanchah ding daan athahsem kitnin hichea hi apanmunu dungjui cheh a atohdiu toh umtha cheh ahi. Thempu ho leh Levi hon pumgo thilto atodiu, chamna thilto a tohdiu, Hou-Inna Pathen houkhom na – a pan alahdiu dan leh, kipana le thangvah chuleh Hou-In'a jatchom chomma atoh ding daanhou aboncha ahopsoh ahi 
Lengpan ama neile ama thil ho a konna kipehna, pumgo thilto ding a apehdoh ding ho, jingkah nilhah a pumgo thiltoding ahin, cholngah nikho ho a pumgo thilto ato ding ahin, lhathah nikho a toding ahin, aphat tepsa kut dinga thilto – a toding ahin, hitia hi Pakai daan thu lekhabu kisun banga chu toding jouse chu atodoh jeng ahi 
Hiche ban nahin lengpan Jerusalem ma chengho kom'ah thempu ho leh Levi hon daan dungjuiya Pakai kin toh na – a akipumpeh theina diuva panhu na thilpeh apehdiuvin thupeh aneijin ahi 
Hiche thupeh hi akiphondoh jouvin Israel chaten chaang gamasa lengpithei ju leh khoiju chuleh alousoh jouseu thiljousea konna hopsom toh bulhingset nin ahin choijun ahi 
Israel teleh Judah te Judah khopiho – a cheng hon bongchal ho leh kelngoichal ho hopsom chuleh Pakai a Pathen diuva thil thenga atodoh hou atamma tam ahin choijun ahi 
Kipehna a thilpeh ho lhathum channa – a patnin ahung lutpan nin akit lha li channa geijin ahung lutbe jing jenge tamtah akiset tho jengin ahi 
Hezekiah lengpa leh anoija natong lamkaihon ijatgei hunglut ham ti amuchen nun ahileh Pakai athangvah un chuleh amipi teu jong apahchaovin ahi 
Lengpan thempu ho leh Levi te kom'a chun hiche kiphal dohna thilpeh ho thu hi anaseijin ahi 
Chuleh Thempulen Azariah leh Zadok chilhah ho chun ama kom'ah anaseijun “Mipi hon hitobang a kiphal dohna thilpeh ho Hou-Inna ahinpeh lutjeh un nehle chah anengin avalleovin ahi. Hiti ho hi Pakaijin amipite phatthei aboh jeh u ahi” atiove 
Lengpa thupeh dungjuijin ama hon Hou-Innah thil koina anagong toh un ahi 
Hiti chun ama hon kipeh na thil ho leh hopsom ho chu abitkeina ding munnah anakoitup mun ahi. Ama hon Levi mi Cononiah chu hiche vesui dingin ana nganseovin asopipa hi ama kithopidin apansah un ahi 
Levi te mi 10 (Som) amani noija natong dingin apansah un ahi, Jehiel, Azaziah, Nahath, Asahel, Jerimoth, Eliel, Ismachiah, Mahath leh Benaiah ahiove. Hitobang tohgon ho hi lengpa Hezekiah leh thempulen Azariah thaneina noija kitungdoh ahi 
Levi mi Imnah chapa Kore chu Hou-In kelkot solang panga vengtup pipuipen ahin, ama hin kipehna thipeh Pakaiya kipeh ho chu alatup leh ahomdoh a pang ahi 
Thempuho chenna khopi dung ho a chun, ama chu Levi mi hicheng hin ana kithopiovin ahi; Eden, Miniamin, Jeshua, Shemiah, Amariah leh Shecaniah ahiove. Amaho hin asopio Levi ho chu anatoh u dungjui cheh in kibang cheh in anahom doh jiovin ahi 
Phung le chang dungjuiya hilouvin, ama hon pasal ho chu kum 30 lhingle achunga ho chu Hou-Inna amoppoh nao apanmunnu dungjuijin achandiu ahoppeh un ahi 
Thempu ho chun aphung dungjuijin toh ding apeuvin chuleh Levi ho chu kum 20 lhingleh atahjo ho chu anatoh dungjuijin nganse na aneiyun ahi 
Amaho chu ajiteu leh achateu chuleh aincheng houdungjuijin aminnu akijihlut nin ahi – ajeh chu amaho hin itihphat hijongleh a kinthengu tohna akimanchah theina diuva tohgon hija ahi 
Khopi a cheng thempu ho lah a kinganse Aaron chilhah ho ahoilouleh hiche khopia chengho thinglhang gam'a umho ahin hiche holah a mopohna neiho hin thempu insunga pasal ho leh Levi phungmi amin kijihlut ho jouse kom'ah nehle chah hommin apangun ahi 
Hezekiah lengpan Judah ho jouse kom'ah Pakai a Pathen mitmua adih jeng abollin Pathen alung lhaisah in ahi 
Ama hi atohna jouse alolhingin ahi. Ajeh chu aman Hou-In chung changa atoh ahin chuleh mipin daan ajuija na diuva atoh ahin Pathenna kitah nanei tah a atoh jeh ahi
