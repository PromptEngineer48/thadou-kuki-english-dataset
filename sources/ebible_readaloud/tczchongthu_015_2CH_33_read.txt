2 Thusimbu.
33.
Manasseh leng ahung chanchun kum 12 bep anahin aman Jerusalem mah kum 55 geijin vai ana hommin ahi 
Ama hin Pakaijin Israel chate masanga anano doh namdang ho chonna kidah umtah ho chonna chu anache pi in Pakai mitmun thilse ngen abollin ahi 
Ama hin apa Hezekiah in ana vohlhuh munsang ho chu anasapha kitnin, Baal doihouna anatung doh kitnin pathen limsemthu Asherah doi ho chu ana tungdoh kitnin ahi 
Aman Pakai Hou-Inmai leh amiten ama ahin houna ding mun atiova chun semthu pathen houna maicham atungdoh kitnin ahi 
Pakai Hou-In na hongcha teni a chun ahsi ho houna maicham anatung doh in ahi 
Hinnom phaicham mah achate pumgo thilto in anatodoh in ahi. Themka pou anadongin, ailhimjong jong, phunsan jong anachepin, thillha gaochang mijong ana tahsannin mitphel doithem ho jong anamangin. Hiti hin Pakai mitmun thet um tahtah anabol doh in Pathen ana phin lunghangin ahi 
Hiti in Manasseh hin milim doihou najong Pathen Hou-In sungah anatungdoh in ahi hiche Hou-In chung changa Pathen'in David le achapa Solomon jah a anasei, “Hiche Hou-In sung le keiman Israel phung hijat lah a kalhen dohsa Jerusalem khopi sunga hi a itihchan na jong keima eiki houjing nading munna kalhen doh ahi 
“Chuleh Israel mipi ten kathupeh hi ajuijuva chuleh kalhacha pa Mose’n anapeh u daanchu anit uva ahileh apu apateu kanapeh gam ma konna koima kakaidoh sah louhel ding ahi” 
Manasseh in Judah mipi te achonset sah hi keiman kamite ahung kitolluva kana nodoh peh namdang ho chonset sangin agimnei jon ahi. 
Pakaijin Manasseh leh amipi te anagih vang'in ama hon ana nahsah pouvin ahi 
Hijeh chun Pakaijin Assyria sepai te atildoh in Judah te chungah gal abolsah in, ama hon Manasseh chu aman nun thihkol toh abutauvin thihkhaovin akan nun Babylon nah akailut nun ahi 
Ahahsat phatnin ahung kineosah in a Pakai a Pathen nah ahung kiheilut nin kithopi athummin ahi 
Pathen nin Manasseh taona asangin Jerusalemma ale sollin avaihom sahkitnin ahi. Hiche phat chun Manasseh in Pakai chu Pathen tahbeh ahi ahechen taan ahi 
Hiche jouhin Manasseh chun David khopi solang panga chun polang pal chu asesangin, nga kelkot sahleng Gihon twiphul kom phaicham ma kon'in Ophel kiti khopi kom geijin pal chu asesangin ahi. Kul kiget khum Judah khopi ho khat cheh a chun sepai aloi loijin lamkai khatnoija akoiyin ahi 
Hou-In na konchun gamdang pathen ho leh milimsemthu aman anakoi ho leh, namdang maicham thinglhanga atundoh nao aHou-In houleh Jerusalem mundang danga umho chu aladoh sohkeijin aga paimang jengin ahi 
Aman Pakai ahounao maicham jongchu asemphan ahi, hiche chunga chun chamna thilto leh thangvah na thilto abollin ahi, aman Judah mipi te chu Israel Pathen Pakai chu nahou diu ahitin thu apen ahi 
Ahinlah mipi ho chun munsang dunga pumgo thilto chu achepi jing nalaijun ahi, ahivangin Pakai a Pathen'u kom'a bou abol tauvin ahi 
Manasseh in anabol jouse, Pathen henga ataona, themgao hon Pathen minna akom ma thu aseihou abonchan Israel lengte thusimbua abonchan akijhlut sohkei jin ahi 
Lengpa taona leh Pathen'in adonbutna alung ahin hei masanga achonsetna ho jouse le thilse abol ho, namdnag pathen houna le adoihou munchom chomma atundoh ho jouse chu themgao ho thusimbua akijih lut nin ahi 
Manasseh athiphat nin khopi sungah avuijun achapa Amon nin lengmun alotan ahi 
Amon chu Juda gam'a leng ahung chanchun kum 22 alhingtan ahi, ama hin Jerusalem'a kum ni aleng chang ngin ahi 
Ama apa Manasseh bangin Pakai dounan achonse tan ahi aman apan anahou doiho chu ana houvin ahi 
Ahinlah apa banhgin Pakai anga kisuhnem na anabol pon apa sangin chonset ana ha boljon ahi 
Amon noija natong lamkai ho chu ana kihoutoh un leng inpi sunga chun ana that tauvin ahi 
Judah mipi ten Amon that ho chu ana thatdoh un achapa Josiah chu leng anachan sah un ahi
