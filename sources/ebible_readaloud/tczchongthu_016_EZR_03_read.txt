Ezra.
3.
Lhasagi ahunglhin toh kilhon'in Israel te chu ama ama khopia ahung chenglut tauvin ahi. Hichun Israel mipite jouse chu lungkhat tah in Jerusalem’ah ahung kikhom tauvin ahi. 
Hichun Jehozadak chapa Jeshua chu athempu chan khompiho Shealtiel chapa Zerubabel leh a insung mite toh ahung kitho hom uvin, Israel Pathen maicham chu ahin sapha tauvin ahi. Pathen mipa Mose thuhil banga pumgo thilto chu Pakai maichama asem got u ahi. 
Israel te chun amun a anacheng saho chu ginna anei vangun maicham chu asempha uvin ahi. Hijou chun ama hon jingkah nilhah in Pakai maicham achun pumgo thilto anabol jengun ahi. 
ama hon Lhambuh Kut abollun, hichu Mose daan dungjuiya niseh le anikho a ngaichat dolla pumgo thiltoa kilhaina ding jongchu atoh jengun ahi. 
Pakaijin agonpeh u dungjuijin, angaija pumgo thilto dinga apediu ahin lhathah jousea kibol thilto ding ahin chuleh kumlhum seh a kut ho dingin, ahin tohdoh jiuvin ahi. 
Lhambuh Kut kipat masang ni somle nga chan nileh thempu hon Pakai kom’ah pumgo thilto a dingin kilhaina anei masa jiuvin ahi. Hiche hi Pakai Hou-In kisah masang’a govam kilhaina chu ana pantauvin ahi. 
Hijou chun mipi ten thihkheng them ho leh thingthem bol ho athalah un chuleh Tyre leh Sidon a kon'in thing kitan bongho ahinpo uvin, aman dingin nehle chah lengpitwi le Olive thao anapeuvin ahi. Thingtum ho chu Lebanon molla kon'in ahinpo uvin Mediterannean Twikhanglen pang juijah Joppa geijin akithol lhah sah un ahi. Hiche hi Cyrus lengpa phatsah na noiya abol'u ahi. 
Tun Jerusalem ahunglhun jou’u kumni le lhani channa in Pathen Hou-In sah akipan tauvin ahi. Natongho chu sohchanna a konna hung potdoh jouse le Shealtiel chapa Zerubababel chuleh Jehozadak chapa Jeshua leh athempu chanpi hotoh chuleh Levi te kum somni lhing ho leh aval ho chu Pakai Hou-In sahphatna in alamkaijin ana pangun ahi. 
Pathen Hou-In sa ho chu Jeshua le achate chule asopi te ho Kadmiel le asopiho chujongleh Hodaviah chilhah ho jouse avesui jin ana pang un ahi. Hiche anatohnaova hin Henadad insung leh evite nana kithopiu vin ahi. 
Hiche insaho hin Pakai Hou-In chu aleibul ahin tundoh phat uvin thempu hon avon’u ahin kivon’un sumkon mut dingin amun cheh u ahi kilo uvin ahi. Hiteng chuleh David lengpan anasemtoh bangin Asaph chilhah ten Pakai thangvahna in atumging thei ho ahin suging uvin ahi. 
Thangvah pumin Pakai vahchoila ahinsaovin ahi: “Ama hi aphabehseh jenge! Aman Israel te alungset nahi tonsot nading ahi!” atiuve. Hijou chun mipi chun Pakai Hou-In leibul chu ahung kitundoh jeh chun Pakai thangvahna in hasap in asam un ahi. 
Ahinlah thempuhon atehse ho leh Levi te chuleh phung upa dang ho Pakai Hou-In masa ana mukha jouse chu ha kan akap un ahi. Mipi adang ho vang chu kipah tah in hasap’in asam un ahi. 
Akipanao husa leh mipi phabep kana ogin chutoh kigom in hatah in ana thongin ahileh gamla tah a umhon jong ajaphauvin ahi.
