Ezra.
8.
Artaxerxes lengpa vaihom sungin Babylon na konna kahin kilhondohpi ho chu insung lamkai ho leh aphunggui’u dungjuijin hiti hi ahi: 
Phinehas insunga kon’in Gershom ahin, Ithamar insunga kon’in Daniel ahin, David insunga kon’in Hattush ahi, 
Ama hi Shecaniah chilhah ahi. Parosh insunga kon’in Zechariah leh adang ja le som nga akijihlut in, 
Pahath Moab a kon’in Serahiah chapa Eliehoenai leh midang jani ahiuve, 
Zattu insunga kon’in Jahaziel chapa Shecaniah leh midang jathum ahiuve, 
Adin insunga kon’in Jonathan chapa Ebed leh midang somnga ahiuve, 
Elam insunga kon’in Athaliah chapa Jeshaiah chuleh adang mi som sagi ahiuve, 
Shephatiah insnga kon’in Michael chapa Zebadiah le midang som-get. 
Joab insunga kon’in Jehiel chapa Obadiah leh midang jani toh somle get ahiuve. 
Bani insunga kon’in Josiphiah chapa Shelomith leh midang jakhat le somgup ahiuve. 
Bebai insunga kon’in Bebai chapa Zechariah leh midang somni le get ahiuve. 
Azgad insunga kon’in Hakkatan chapa Johanan leh midang jakhat le som khat ahiuve, 
Adonikam insunga kon’in khonunga hung Eliphelet, Jeuel, Shemiah leh midang somgup ahiuve, 
Bigvai insunga kon’in Uthai, Zaccur leh midang som sagi ahiuve. 
Keiman sohchanga konna hungdoh ho chu Ahava canal munnah kakhomin hichea chun nithum kageh un, hichea chun keiman mipi ho toh thempu hichelai mun hunglhung ho chu kakhol in ahileh Levi te khatcha jong ahung pangpoi ti keiman kamudoh in ahi. 
Hijeh chun keiman alamkai hou Eliezer, Ariel, Shemiah, Elnathan, Jarib, Nathan, Elnathan, Zechariah leh Meshullam kaga koush in, chuleh Joiarib leh Elnathan jong kakousah in ahi, ajeh chu amani hi thumop them ahi lhon'e. 
Chuleh keiman Casiphia mun aum Levi te lamkai Iddo kom'a chun mi kasolin amite a insung mite leh Hou-In lhachaho kom'a chu Jerusalem ma Pathen Hou-In lhacha sunga lhacha a pang ding mi ahinsol diuvin, ngehna nei dingin kasoltai. 
Ka Pathen u khotona khut chun eipuihoi jeh un, ama hon Sherebiah kitipa achate le asopite somle get eihin solpeh un ahi. Ama chu Israel chapa Levi chilhah ho lah a Mahli chilhah minasatah khat ahi. 
ama hon Merari chilhah holah a kon’in Hashabiah chule Jeshaiah toh ahinsol thauvin chuleh achate le asopite somni jong ahin sollun chuleh 
Hou-In lhacha jani le somni jong ahin sol thauvin ahi. 
Hichun hichelai Ahava twilam mun’ah hin ka Pathen’u angsung’ah eiho leh keiho kisunem tah a kabon chauva an-ngolla taodingin thupeh kaneijin ahi. Hiche kataonao chu ahileh, kakholjin naova hi keiho leh kachateu chuleh kathilkeo hotoh eipuihoi nadiuvin kataovun ahi. 
Ajeh chu keiman lampi dung’a melmate a konna eihongbit diuva lengpa kom'a sepai leh sakol chungtou sepai thum ding chu jachat um kasan ahi. Ajeh chu keiman lengpa kom'a chun, “Ka Pathen’u hin amahoute chu abanthahat’in ahongbit nin, amavang ama donlouva koite vang alunghanna hoise tah chun asugim jin ahi,” tia kana seidoh tan ahi. 
Hijeh chun keihon ka Pathen’un kataonao sana eihoi bitna diuvin an-ngol pummin lunggil tah in kataovun ahileh aman kataonao eisanpeh un ahi. 
Keiman thempu lah a kon’in lamkai som le ni Sherebiah leh Hoshabiah chuleh thempu dang som chu kalhengdoh in mopohna panmun kanapen ahi. 
Ama ho chun sana, dangka, sana khon ho leh thilkeo dang ho toh lengpa le a athumop them a vaihom hon Pathen Hou-In na koiding’a ahin pehdoh uchu polut ding kapedoh tan ahi. 
Keiman hiche thillu ho chu kapeh dohtoh kilhon’in, ka tetoh in ahileh hijat hi alhing in ahi: Dangka ton somni le li, Dangka a kisem thilkeo ho pound sangsagi le ja nga, sana pound sangsagi le ja nga, 
sana a kisem khon somni hichu Sana sum pum sangkhat toh aman kibang chuleh sana mantamtah toh kibang sum-eng kilha ngimsella kisem thil ni hicheng hi ahi. 
Keiman hiche thempuho kom'a chun: “Nangho leh hiche thilmanlu ho hi Pakaiya dinga athenga kipedoh a nahi tauve, hiche dangka le sana ho hi ipu ipateu Pakai Pathen a dinga hung kitodoh ahitai. 
Hiche thillu ho hi thempu ho leh Leviho chuleh Israel lamkaiho khut a napehlut tokah un, phatah in chingtup un, ama hon Jerusalem’a Pakai Hou-In'a akoina munna akoi tengu leh phatah a atetoh diu ahi,” ati peh in ahi. 
Hiti chun thempuho leh Levi te chun Jerusalem Hou-In'a Pakai thilkoina munna hiche thillu sana le dangka ho chu pohlut nadinga mopohna chu ladingin anom tauvin ahi. 
Lha masapen, nisom le ni ni lhin nin, keihon Ahava twilam munchu kadalha tauvin ahi. Chuleh Pathen mikhotona khut chun lamlhon lhungin melma ho leh michomho khutna kon’in eihoidoh sah un ahi. 
Hiti chun Jerusalem chu damsel le bit kei chan kaga lhungun hichea chun nithum kakicholdo uvin ahi. 
Kagalhun jou’u ani li channa in sana le dangka chuleh adang thillu ho jouse ka Pathen’u Hou-In nah hoitah in katetoh un, Uriah thempupa chapa Meremoth leh Phinehas chapa Eleazer toh Levi mi Jeshua chapa Jozabad chule Binnui chapa Noadiah kom'ah pehlut na le nganse na akinei tan ahi. 
Thiljouse chu ajatle agih jatchu aki tetoh sohkeijin hicheng chu agihna jat pumpin kivaipohna dungjuijin hoitah in akijihlut in ahi. 
Hijou chun sohchanna a konna hungpotdoh ho cheng chun Israel Pathen’ah pumgo thilto akibolin ahi. Hiti chun ama hon Israel Pathen’ah Israel pumpi dingin bongchal somle ni, kelngoi chal somko le gup leh kelngoi ja sagi le som sagi atodoh un ahi. Ama hon chonset thoidamna dingin kelchal somle ni jong atodoh un ahi. Hiche ho jousehi Pakai kom'a pumgo thilto kin bolnan aneiyun ahi. 
Lengpa thupeh chu Euphrates vadung lhumlam gamkaija gamvaipo pa leh vaihom lenpen ho jouse kom'ah akithot lut sohkeijin, hijeh amaho chun akithopi sohkeiyun ahi.
