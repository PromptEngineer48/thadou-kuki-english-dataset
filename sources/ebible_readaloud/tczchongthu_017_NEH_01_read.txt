Nehemiah Lekhabu.
1.
Hacaliah chapa Nehemiah tohdoh chengse ho chu ahi, Artaxerxes in lengvai apoh kal kum somni lhin kum Kislev lhasungin, keima Nehemiah hi gamsung’a khopi lenpen Susa khopi sunga kana um'in ahi. 
Kasopi ho lah a mikhat Hanani kitipa chun mi phabep khat toh Judah gam'a kon'in eihung vil’un ahi. Keiman amaho chu Juda te ho Babylon a asohchan nauva a konna ana kile kit ho chu itobang nauvem chule Jerusalem chu itobang nam tithu kana dongin ahi. 
ama hon hiti hin eidonbut uve, “Sohchan naova konna hung kile Juda te chu, hinkho hahsa tah le Avel'a cheng namdan ten anoise lheh uve, Chule Jerusalem kulpi bang chu akisuchim nia pat, kelkot ho jouse jong meiya ana kihal lhah gam jou apat semphat louvin aum'e,” ti eiseipeh un ahi. 
Hiche thu hi kajah phat in katou lha jengin kakap jah tai. Chuti chun nikho phabep sung chun lunghem tah in an ka-ngol’in chule van Pathen kom'a kataovin ahi. 
“O Pakai, van Pathen,nathupeh nit ho leh nangma ngailu jingho chunga na milungset na le nakitepna chepi jinga oupe tah le tijat umtah Pathen nahi! 
Nami Israel te dinga sun le jana kataona hi venlang chule kataona hi neihin ngaipeh in! Ajeh chu keiho hi nangma douna a chonse kahi tauve, keima insung le keima tah jeng jong kachonse tauve tia ka phondoh ahi. 
Chule nalhachapa Mose a konna neipeh’u nathupeh ho le chonna dan chule neithu gah na ho kanit pouvin melse tah in kachonse tauve. 
Chule nangman nalhachapa Mose kom'a nanaseisa, “Nangho keima kom'a nakitah lou’ule keiman chitin namtin lah a kathe cheh ding nahiuve. 
Amavang nangho kaheng’a nahung kile hei’uva kathu peh nanit’uva hiche dungjuiya chu nachon’uva ahileh leiset kol mong geiya ana kithe chehsa hijongleu chun kamin dopsang na dinga keiman kagon pehnau gam ma chu kahin nung puilut kit diu ahi nati,” chu hingel doh kit in. 
Hiche nam mite hi nalhacha te ahiuve, naban gol thahat na le natha neina’a nahuhdohsa ahiuve. 
O Pakai kataona hi lungset tah in neingai peh in! Nangma ja bolna a thanomte taona ngaipeh in, tuni hin lengpa khohsah na neimu sah inlang neilolhin sah in; lengpa lungsunga khoto na neimu sah in.” kati. Hiche phatlai chun, lengpa juhaidom in kana pange.
