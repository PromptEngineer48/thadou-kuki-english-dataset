Nehemiah.
2.
Artexerxes vaihom na kum somni lhin Nisan lhasungin, lengpa kom'a a adon ding ju kadoplut peh in ahi. Keiman akom'a khat vei cha lungkham mel kana put khahlouhel anahi. 
Hijeh chun lengpan eina dongin, “Ipi dinga hibangtah a hi lungkham a nahim? Adammo dan la nahi pon, lungkhamna khohtah khat vang nanei hinte eiti; hichun keima kati ajalheh jengin ahi. 
Ahinlah keiman kadonbut in, “Lengpa hingsot’in! Keima iti kalungkham da ding ham? Kapu kapate ana kivuina khopi chu amangthah tan, chuleh akulpi kelkot ho jong meiyin akalha gamtai,” kati. 
Hichun lengpan eidongin’ “Aphai; chuti chu ahileh ipi lampen a kaki thopi thei ding ham?” eiti; hichun keima van Pathen kom'a kataovin; 
Hitin kadonbut tai, “Lengpa nasohpa keima chunga khoto na naneiya nalunglhaina ahi ding leh Judah gam'a neisol’inlang kapu kapate kivuina khopi chu gasa phange” kati. 
Hiche pet’a chu lengnu toh toukhom lhon ahin, lengpan phat itih chan naga sulut dem? itih donleh nahung kile thei dem?” eiti, Keiman phat itih chan kaga suh lut ding ham ti kahetsah jou chun aman eiphat sah peh tai. 
Lengpa kom'a chun keiman kaseibe kitin, “Lengpa nalung alhai dinga ahile, Eupharates vadung gal lhanglam gamkaiya gam vaipo ho kom'a lekha eijih peh inlang Judah gam kajot na a agamsungu boilouhel a kajot galkai theina dingin thupeh neithot peh in,” kati. 
“Chule lengpa thing gampo pa Asaph kom'a lekha neijih peh inlang, Hou-In kulpi khopi kulbang sah na ding leh keima chen na dingin sahna a Kamanchah ding thing eipeh theina dingin thupeh neithot peh in,” kati. Hichun Pathen'in kachunga khotona anei jal'in lengpan kathum jouse eiphal pehin ahi. 
Eupharates vadung lhumlam gamkaiya gamvaipo ho kom kalhun phat in lengpa lekha jih ho chu kape lutin ahi. Gahseibe ding chun lengpan sakol sepai ho leh vaipo ho keima eiveng dingin eihin solpeh in ahi. 
Ahin Horon mi Samballat leh Ammon mi Tobiah leng lhacha a pang teni hin kavai lhun ahetdoh lhon phatin Israel chate panpi ding mikhat ahungtai tin alung nom mo lheh jeng lhon'in ahi. 
Hiti hin nithum jouvin Jerusalem kaga lhung tai. 
Jankhangin aguh in kathoudoh in mi phabep toh kahin, Pathen'in Jerusalem a eitoh sah nom ho chu koimacha ka seipeh pon ahi. Katouna sa-ngan tailou gancha dang khat cha kapui pouvin ahi. 
Hiti chun jan khang kim in kapotdoh’un phaicham kelkot langa kacheuvin, Sial twikul chu kaho pauvin, ehbohna kelkot geiyin kacheuvin akivo chimsa pal ho leh akihal lha kakhol toh sohkeiyun ahi. 
Chujouvin, twisam putna kelkot le lengpa twikul geiyin kacheuvin ahileh katounau sa-ngan chu songchip ho lah a chun athol tapoi. 
Hiti chun jan khanglai chun Kidron phaicham geiyin kachen, palchu kahin kholpeh in, chujouvin kahung kilen phaicham kelkot langa kahung lut tai. 
Hiti chun khopi vaihom hon itih a kapotdoh a ipi kaga bol ham ti ahepouve; ajeh chu keiman katoh gon chu koimacha kana hetsah pon ahi. Judah lamkai ho leh thempu ho, chule mithupi ho leh vaihom ho ahilouleh vaihom na mun'a umho khat cha kana hetsahpon ahi. 
Tun vang keiman amaho chu kahet sah tan ahi, “Itobang hahsatna itou hitam Jerusalem hi khopigem ahitan, akelkot ho jousela maiyin akavam soh tai tihi nang hon nahe sohkei’uve; tun eihon Jerusalem kulpi pal hi sapha kitnu hitin ijum nao hi subei tao hite,” katin ahi. 
Hiche jou chun keiman itibang Pathen kaihoi kachan ham chuleh lengpa toh iti kahung kihou lim lhon ham ti ka seipeh tan ahi. 
Ahinlah Samballat, Tobiah le Arab mi Geshem te hon katoh gon’u chu ahet doh phat’un noise tah in eina houvun ahi. “Ipi nabol’u ham? Lengpa douna a na natoh u ham?” tin eidongun ahi. 
Hichun keiman kadonbut in, “Van Pathen'in eipan hu’uva eitoh chai sah diu ahi. chule keiho ama lhachaten khopi pal hi kasah phat diu ahi; amavang nang hon hiche ahin chan anei pouve, thaneina jong nanei pouve, Jerusalem khopi ahin nachan diu goujong nanei pouvin ahi,” kati.
