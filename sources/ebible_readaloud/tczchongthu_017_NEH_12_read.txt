Nehemiah.
12.
Hiche ho hi Shealtiel chapa Zerubbabel le thempulen Jeshua toh hung kinungle thempu ho leh Levi te chu ahiuve: Seraiah, Jeremiah, Ezra; 
Amariah, Malluch, Hattush; 
Shecaniah, Harim, Meremoth; 
Iddo, Ginnethon, Abijah; 
Miniamin, Moadiah, Bilgah; 
Shemaiah, Joiarib, Jedaiah; 
Sallu, Amok, Hilkiah chule Jedaiah, amaho hi Jeshua khanga thempu lamkai ho le atohkhom pi hou chu ahiuve. 
Levi te amaho toh hung kinungle ho chu Jeshua, Binnui, Kidmiel, Sherebiah, Judah, chuleh Mattaniah ama hi atoh khompi hotoh thangvah na lasah na lamkai ahi. 
Atohkhom pi hou Bakbukiah le Unni hin Hou-In sunga kin kibol teng amaho chu toh kimai ngat ton ana ding jiuvin ahi. 
Thempulen Jeshua chu Joiakim pa ahi, Joiakim chu Eliashib pa ahi. Eliashib chu Joiada pa ahi. 
Joiada chu Johanan pa ahi. Johanan chu Jaddua pa ahi. 
Tun Joiakim thempulen ahiphat in insung lamkai ho chu noiya ho hi ahiuve: Meraiah chu Seraiah insung mite lamkai ahin, Hananiah chu Jeremiah insung lamkai ahi. 
Meshullam chu Ezra insung lamkai ahi. Jehohanan chu Amariah insung lamkai ahi. 
Jonathan chu Malluch insung lamkai ahi. Joseph chu Shecaniah insung lamkai ahi. 
Adna chu Harim insung lamkai ahi. Helkai chu Meremoth insung lamkai ahi. 
Zechariah chu Iddo insung lamkai ahi. Meshullam chu Ginnethon insung lamkai ahi. 
Zichri chu Abijah insung lamkai ahi. Miniamin'insung lamkai jong ana um'in ahi. Piltai chu Moadiah insung lamkai anahi. 
Shammua chu Bilgah insung lamkai anahi. Jahonathan chu Shemaiah insung lamkai anahi. 
Mettenai chu Joiarib insung lamkai anahi. Uzzi chu Jedaiah insung lamkai anahi. 
Kallai chu Sallu insung lamkai anahi. Eber chu Amok insung lamkai anahi. 
Hashabiah chu Hilkiah insung lamkai anahi. Nathanel chu Jedaiah insung lamkai anahi. 
Eliashib, Joiada, Jonathan, chule Jaddua chu thempulen anahi nao kumsung chun Levi te insung minbu chu phatah in ana kikoiyin ahi. 
Levi te insung lamkai ho minbu chu khanggui thusimbu a ana kikoijin hichu Eliashib tupa Johanan khang geiya umsuh peh a ahi. 
Hicheng hi Levi te lamkai ho insung mite ahiuve: Hashabiah, Sherebiah, Jeshua, Binnui, Kadmiel, chule akiloi khompi ho ahiuvin, amaho hi thangvah na le kipa thusei na kin kibol teng kingatto jia ahiuvin Pathen mipa David in ana thupeh banga loikhat chu loikhat toh kidonbut toji ahiuve. 
Hichea chun Mattaniah Bakbukiah chuleh Obadiah jong ana panguve. Meshullam, Talmon, chule Akkub chu kelkot phunga thilkeo koina indan chu angahtup ma panga ahiuve. 
Amaho hin Jehozadok chapa Jeshua chapa Joiakim chule gamvaipo Nehemiah leh thempulen danthu them Ezra khanglaiya ana toh’u ahi. 
Jerusalem kulpi bang athah chu thensona kin na hi ahung kithopina diuvin gamsung muntin na um Levi te chu akoukhom uvin ahi. Amaho chun atumgingu thih pheng kisai gin, selangdah ginleh semjang kisaiging puma hiche kin loupia hi thangvah na lasah puma thanomtah a pan alah u ahi. 
Jerusalem vel'a Netophath mite khohoa konna lasa ho akhom sohkeiyun ahi. 
Beth-gilgal leh thinglhang gam Geba leh Azmaveth kom veldunga kon'in jong ahungun ahi. Ajeh chu lasa miho hin Jerusalem veldunga chu chenmun ana kisem u ahi. 
Thempu ho leh Levi te chu amasan amaho leh amaho akisutheng un chujouvin mipi ho leh kelkot ho chule kulpi bang chu asuthengun ahi. 
Keiman Juda lamkai ho chu kulpi bang chunga chun kapuijin thangvah na pe dingin lathem hon ni kaga gong toh'in ahi. Lathem honkhat chu Eh-Kelkot lang ajonun, lathem honkhat chun lhanglam ajonun Eh-Kelkot lang kulpi bang chung langa chun aheuvin ahi. 
Hoshiah leh Judah lamkai akehkhat in ajuijun ahi. 
Azariah, Ezra, Meshullam; 
Judah, Benjamin, Shemaiah, chuleh Jeremiah jong ahiuve. 
Hicheng jou chun sumkon mut thempu ho phabep ahungun Jonathan chapa Zachariah, Shemaiah chapa, Mettaniah chapa, Mecaiah chapa, Zaccur chapa, Asaph chilhah ho jonghi apang uve. 
Chuleh Zechariah tohkhompi ho chu Shemaiah, Azarel, Milalai, Gilalai, Maai, Nethanel, Judah chuleh Hanani ahiuve. Chule amaho hin Pathen mipa David in anasuh tohsa tumging thei ho chu ana mang hauvin ahi. Ezra in mipi kijot ho chu ana lamkaiyin ahi. 
Twikhuh kelkot na kon chun khopi kulpi banga kal touna kalbia chun jangpet in acheuvin David khopi lang ajonun ahi. Ama hon David inchu aho paovin chuin solang Twi Kelkot lang chu ajon’un ahi. 
Lathem hon ni na ho chun thangvah pumin sahlang ajonun lang khat lam a chun avel’un amahotoh kimuto dingin acheuvin ahi. Kenjong amaho nung kajuijin mipi akehkhat chutoh kalhonne, kulpi bang vumma kacheuvin meikhulong hung kitundoh chungvum langa kacheuvin, kulpi bang ningkoi lang geiyin kacheuve. 
Chuin Ephraim kelkot chunglam a kacheuvin, kelkot luilang Nga Kelkot kom Hananel insung lam, Kelngoi hong Kelkot phung geiyin kacheuvin ahi, chujouvin miho chu kelkot ngah ho din munna chun ahung ding uvin ahi. 
Lathem hon ni ho chun thangvah na la sa pumin Pathen Hou-In lang chu ajon’un hilaiya chun apansa tauve. Keileh kaki lhonpi lamkai ho jong hilaiya chun kaum tauve. 
Keiho chu sumkon mut thempu ho Eliakim, Maaseiah, Miniamin, Micaiah, Elioenai, Zechariah chuleh Hananiah; 
Chule lasaho- Maaseiah, Shemaiah, Eleazer, Uzzi, Jehohanan, Malkijah, Elam chuleh Ezer, mi hicheng hitoh kaki lhonun ahi. Ama hon lathem lamkaipa Jezrahiah mapuina in tum ging toh thon hasah in asauvin ahi. 
Hiche kipa thanop ni chun pumgo thilto tamtah anatoh doh’un ahi, ajeh chu Pathen'in mipi chu kipana lentah anapen ahi. Hiche golnop nikhoa hin numei le chapang ho jong ana pangun chule Jerusalem mipite kipana ogin chu gamla tah a kon'in jong akijan ahi. 
Hiche nikho chun maicham ma hung kitoh doh ho, lhosohga masa hung kitoh ho leh hopsom hung kitohdoh ho kholna indan ho vesui dingin mi phabep akipansah in ahi. Danthun angeh dungjuiya thempu ho leh Levi te chan ding dol khopi pamlanga chengho kom'a hindong dingin amaho chun mopohna aneiyun ahi. Ajeh chu Judah mipite hin thempu ho leh Levi te natoh na a hin alunglhai lheh jengun ahi. 
ama hon David leh achapa Solomon ana thupeh bangin Pathen kin leh kisuh thengna kin hi atongun ahi, chuleh lasa a pang ho leh kelkot ngah hon jong ajongun ahi. 
Vahchoi la le Pathen thangvah na lasah na a lathem ho lamkai ding tohgon chu David le Asaph nikho lai peh a patna hung che jom peh ahi. 
Hiti chun Zerubbabel le Nehemiah khang hin Israel mipi ten lasa ho leh kelkot ngah ho chule Levi te neh le chah ding niseh in ahin pohpeh jiuvin ahi. Levi te chun amahoa thilmua kon chun Aaron chilhah thempuho ding phabep ape jiuvin ahi.
