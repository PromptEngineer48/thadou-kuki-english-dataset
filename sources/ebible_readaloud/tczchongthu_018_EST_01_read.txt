Esther Lekhabu.
1.
Leng Xerxes vaipoh laiya thilsoh ho ahi, ama hin India a pat a Ethiopia gam geija gam jakhat le somni le sagi chunga vai anahop ahi. 
Hiche phatlai chun Xerxes lengpa chu Susa khopia alaltouna a anatouvin lengvai anapo in ahi. 
Avaihom na kumthum channa a chun amilen le amilal ho jouse ding leh vaihom ho jouse dingin golvah loupitah khat ana bollin ahi. Hichea pang ding hin Persia le Media gamsunga leng ho leh sepai lamkai ho jouse jong akouvin ahi. 
Golvah loupitah chu ni jakhat le somget geijin achelhan ahi. Hiche sungchun, agamsung loupina le thaneina, ahaona le aloupina atah langin ahi. 
Hicheng chu akichaisoh phat in, lengpan Susa khopi sunga um mi alenpen apat aneopen geidingin golvah khat abolkit in ahi. Hiche chu lengpa khopi honsung tol lhanga chun nisagi achelha kit in ahi. 
Hichea hin len inpi leitol chu pondal pon, ponkang jong, pondum jong tupat ponnem patjang chutoh akijop matsohkeijin, ponsandup chuto jong akijop in, dangka sumkol ho chutoh akijopmat sohkeijin, songval khompia kisemma kiphut hoa chun akihenin ahi. Phatah a kijempol kalbi leijong chu sana le dangka kiphan songmantam ton akihallin songval akigollin, twikoh chang le songmantam dang dang tampi toh akihallin ahi. 
Leng in na juchu neng chatna jatchom choma kijem sana khon ho a chu kidon ahin, hichu lengpa ninglhinna le ahongphalna atah langin ahi. 
Akhopi sunga vaihom ho chun min ama ama ngaichat dungjui cheh a agotchan na adon ding uva ahomdoh diuvin lengpan thupeh aneijin ahi. 
Hichetoh kilhon hin lengnu Vashti in jong leng Xerxes khopi sunga numeiho dingin golvah ana bollin ahi. 
Hiche golvah nisagi channa niachun lengpa Xerxes chu ju akhamlheh jengtan ahileh ama jenle’a pang nukiso mi sagiho, Mehuman, Biztha, Harbona, Bigtha, Abagtha, Zethar chule Carcas chu, 
Lengnu Vashti in alallukhuh kikhuh puma lengpa angsung’a ahung dingin gapuijun tin asoltan ahi. Amanu chu numei hoitah ahijeh chun amilen amilalten amel-hoina chu ana vetsohkeidiu chu adei ahi. 
Ahinlah ama hon lengpa thupeh chu lengnu Vashti kom’a aga hetsah phat’un amanu chu ahung nom tapon ahi. Hiche chun lengpa chu alung ahansah lheh jengtan, alunghan akoudoh leuvin ahi. 
Aman Persia mite danle chena lhongpiho hethem ama thumop apang phattinna ana dohjing saho chu gangtah in adongtan ahi. 
Amaho minchu Carshena, Shethar, Admatha, Tarshish, Meres, Marsena chule Memucan - Media leh Persia mithupi sagi ho chu ahiuve. Amaho hi lenggamsunga panmun sangpen loiho chu ahiuvin, lenga toh phat tinna kimuto jing jiuva ahi. 
“Lengnu Vashti hi i-loding ham? Lengpa keiman nukisoho kaga kousah a lengpa thupeh palkeh a hung nomlou lengnu chu dan dungjuiya itobang gotna peh dinga lom am?” tin lengpan adongtan ahi. 
Memucan chun lengpa leh amithupi ho chu hiti hin adonbut’e, “Vashti lengnu hin lengpa keo hilouvin na lenggam pumpi sunga milen milal ho jouse leh gamsunga cheng mipi ho jouse hi anoise ahi. 
Lengnu Vashti hin lengpan akom’a hunghen tia akou in anompoi tihi muntinna numeihon ajah tengu leh ajiteu hi anoise diu ahitai. 
Tunilhum masang ngeihin Persia le Media gamsunga milen milal ho jouse jiten lengnu umchan hi hin jadoh untin ajipa teu hi ahin nahsah loudiu ahitai. Hitia chu lunghan na le thangtom na a hung dimset ding ahi. 
Hijeh chun lengpan na phatsah dinga ahileh Persia le Media gamsunga khelthei louhel ding dan khat jihtho inlang phongdoh lechun kadeije. Lengnu Vashti hin lengpa Xerxes hi amai amukit tah louhel dingin a-imatih channa dingin kaidoh in um henlang lengpa dingin nakiloppi tah lengnu chomkhat kilheng doh hen katin,” ahi. 
“Hiche dan hi lenggam pumpia hi ahung kiphondoh tengleh muntinna mipate hin itobang panmun nei hijongleu aji teuva konna achan diuva lom jana akimudiu ahi,” atipeh tai. 
Lengpa le amilen amilal hojousen Memucan’s thumop hi pha asasohkei tauvin ahi. 
Hiti chun lengpan agamsung pumpi gamkai sung jousea ama ama pao le lekhajem a athotdoh in, mipa jousehi ama ama insunga thanei vaihom hi ding chule athu thua hi ding ahi tin aphongdoh tan ahi.
