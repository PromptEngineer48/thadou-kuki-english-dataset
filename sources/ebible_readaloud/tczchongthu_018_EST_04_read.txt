Esther.
4.
Mordecai jin hitobanga thilsoh hi ahetdoh phat in, apon abot’eh in khaodip pon aki ah in vut akinun, chuin khopi achun alhailut in awging tah le lungna tah in apengjah jeng tan ahi. 
Hiti chun ama khopi kelkot geijin ache in ahi, ajeh chu hitobanga lung hesohna von pum in koima khopi kelkotna lutthei lou ahi. 
Chuleh hiche lengpa dan aga kiphondoh na gamkai jousea chun Juda te ho lah a lunghem na nasatah alhungtai. Ama hon an angol’un, akap un, aping un, chuleh mi tamtah in vut akinu uvin khaodip pon akisil un ahi. 
Lengnu Esther jenle nungah ho leh nukisohon Mordecai umchan chu aseipeh phat un, ama jong alungkham lheh jengtan ahi. Amanun khaodip pon akisil khella akisil dingin pon agah thotnin ahinla Mordecai chu anompon ahi. 
Hiche jou chun Esther in lengpa dinga nukiso holah a ama lhacha dinga kipansah pa Hathach chu akouvin, Mordecai chun ipi hahsatna atoh ham chule ibola lunggim tah a uma ham ti gakholdoh dingin asol tan ahi. 
Hijeh chun Hathach chu khopi kelkot mai lang leitol a um Mordecai kom’a chun agachen ahi. 
Mordecai in thusoh ho jouse leh Juda te suhmang’a aumna dinga Haman in sum ijat lengte sumkhol na a dinga atodoh got ham tichu hoitah in ana seipeh sohkeijin ahi. 
Juda te jouse kisuh mangna dinga Susa khopi sunga kisodoh dan lekha pehkhat chu Hathach anapen ahi. Aman hiche lekha chu Esther anavetsah ding chuleh thil umdan chu hoitah a ahilchet dingin Hathach chu agah ngan sen ahi. Aman Hathach jah a chun Esther chu sollin lang lengpa kom’a amite inging a taohen ati. 
Hiti chun Hathach chu Mordecai jin angan sena pumchun Esther kom’a chun ahung kile tai. 
Hiche jou chun Esther in Mordecai kom’a, athu lepoh dingin Hathach chu asolkit in ahi. 
“Lengpa vaipo ho leh gamkai hoa cheng mipi ho jouse jengin jong, koi hileh lengpan akou hilouva lengpa kom’a lut aum a ahileh lengpan a sana tenggol ahin lhunlut louleh thitei tei ding ahi ti hi mijousen ahet khatchu ahi. Tuhin lengpan eikou louna ni somthum alhingtai” ati. 
Hathach chun Esther thusei chu Mordecai kom alhut kittai. 
Mordecai in Esther chu adonbutna alethot in; “Khopi sunga naum man in Juda te jouse akitha gamteng kihoidoh nante tin kigel hih in. 
Hitobang phat nahi thipbeh a naum a ahileh, hoilang ham khatna kon ahi Juda te tedinga hoidoh na le olna hungsohdoh ding, amavang nang le na insung mite thi ding ahi. Kon ija ahetn em hitobang phatna ding jeh a hi lengnu mun nalo hithei tah ahi!” 
Hichejou chun Esther in Mordecai chu hiti hin thu athot kitne; 
“Susa khopia um Juda te jouse gakoukhom inlang keidingin an-ngollin hungtao vun. Nithum sungin anjong nelou twijong donlouvin asun ajan in um un. Kanungah ho leh kenjong hitima chu kaboldiu ahi. Hiche jouteng chuleh dan in aphallou hijongleh, kathile kathi, kahinle kahin, lengpa kom’a kagalut ding ahi,” atipeh tai. 
Hiti chun Mordecai apotdoh in Esther in ahin thupeh dungjui chet chun aga boltai.
