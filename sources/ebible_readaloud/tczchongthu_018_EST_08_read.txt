Esther.
8.
Hicheni mama chun Xerxes lengpan Juda te galmi Haman thil leh lo ho jouse chu lengnu Esther apedoh tai. Hichejou chun Esther in Mordecai chu insungmi ahi lengpa kom’ah aseidoh tan ahileh Mordecai chu lengpa kom’a ahin puilut tauvin ahi. 
Lengpan Haman’a konna ana nung lahdoh asoi namna khutjem chu aladoh in Mordecai chu apetai. Hiti chun Esther chun Mordecai chu Haman thil le lo chunga vaihom in ana pansah tan ahi. 
Hijou chun Esther chu lengpa kom’a ache kit in akeng khopi muh a abohkhup in Agag mi Haman in Juda te te douna thil giloutah ana gon chu suh-tangna dingin mitlhi pumin anatauvin ahi. 
Avellin lengpan Esther dingin asana tengol chu ahin lhungdoh in hiti chun ama athoudoh in a-angsung’ah adingtai. 
Esther in ajah a, “Lengpa nalunglhai dinga chuleh khotona kamu theiding ahija chujongleh nalung lhaina kachanthei dinga ahile Agag mipa Haman thupeh ho hi suhbei na dingin dan khat semdoh le chun kati, ajeh chu aman lengpa gamkai jousea cheng Juda te ho jouse suh gamna dinga thupeh ananei ahi,” ati. 
Ajeh chu kamipite le ka insung mite akitha gam'a akisuhmang ding ken iti kathoh dingham?” 
Hichun Xerxes lengpan lengnu Esther le Juda te mi Mordecai kom’ah, “Keima Haman thilkeo jouse Esther kape tan chuleh aman Juda te te suhmang ding agot jeh in thinglhon chunga amajoh akikhailih tan, 
Tun lengpa min’in Juda te te chu nadei dandan’in thuthot tan chuleh lengpa khutjem chun mohor namkhum tan. Chule lengpa minna khatvei mohor kinamsa chu kinung lahdoh kit thei talou ding ahi, ti melchih lhon'in, atipeh tai. 
Hiti chun lhathum lhinna, Sivan lha, nisom ni le nithum nichun, Mordecai in lengpa lekha jeh a pangho chu akikou khomin chuleh Mordecai jin jih in ati dungjui chun ajih tauvin ahi. Hiche chu Juda te ho komlah vaihom ho jouse kom, gamvaipo ho kom, gamkai jakhat le somni le sagi, India a patna Ehopia chan geijin milen milal ho jouse kom’ah akithot dohtan ahi. Hiche dan chu lenggamsunga mipite Juda te op um'in ama ama paole ama lekhajem cheh in akisunin ahi. 
Hiche dan chu Xerxes lengpa minna kisun ahin, akhut jemma mohor kinamkhum ah ahi. Mordecai in hiche thu hi gangtah a homdoh dingin lengpa kin’a kimang sakol lhaihat cheh chu asoldoh in ahi. 
Hiche leng thupeh hin khopi tinna um Juda te ho chu kipun khomna kihuhdohna anei thei diu ahi. Koi nammi hihenlang hoilai gamkai a konna hijohgleh aji achateu leh amaho hindel buluho jouse chu athaova, asuhmang diu chuleh asuhgamhel theidiu chuleh agalmi hou thilkeo akichom theidiu ahi. 
Xerxes lenggam jousea hitobang tohna dinga nikho kilhengdoh chu Lhakao nisagi ni kumkit hi ding ahi. 
Hiche dan kisodoh hi gamkai jousea jui ding dan hi ding mipiah jousea phondoh ding, hitia chu Juda te ten hiche nikho ahi agalmihou chunga phu alah theina diu ahi. 
Hiche hin leng thupeh chun akinotah ahijeh in, thu ho mjal leho chun lengpa kin’a manchah dinga kikoi Sakol hatcheh aman uva gangtah a ahopdoh diu ahi. Hiche dan Susa khopi sunga jong akiphong doh tan ahi. 
Hiche jou chun Mordecai chun lengpon oupe aduple akang akisil’in, sana lallukhuh akikhuh in, tupat ponnem le pondup akisil’in lengpa a kon'in ahung potdoh'in ahileh khopi sunga mipi chu akipah in othong je-jun asam sam jengun ahi. 
Juda te te chu kipana le thanopnan adimun chene chan uva miho jana aching un ahi. 
Gambih tinle khopia, lengpa dan lhunna channah Juda te te akipah un alopna aneiyun anikho nitding leh mipin golvah neikhom dingin aphongdoh un ahi. Gamsunga mipi lah achun Juda te chu ahung pungjal jengun ahi ajeh chu Juda te ho chun eibolse get unte tia akichat’u ahitai.
