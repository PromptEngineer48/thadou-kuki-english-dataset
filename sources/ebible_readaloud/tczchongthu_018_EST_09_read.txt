Esther.
9.
Hiti chun alha Lhakao nisagi nichun lengpa dan thusoh teni chu ahung chelha paitan ahi. Hiche nikho chun, Juda te kidoupiho chun amaho chu matchom jengding anagot u ahin, ahinla akaleh lang tah in asohtan ahi. Juda te ho johchun agalmi houchu ajolo tauvin ahi. 
Juda te te chu amaho khopi ho leh gamkai ho jousea pumkhat in dding khom un ahin nokhum hou chu asat chap jeng’un ahi. Koimachan amaho chu akimaito ngam tapouvin ahi, ajeh chu mijousen amaho chu aging tauvin ahi. 
Gambih a milen milal ho ahin, vaihom lenpenho ahiuvin, gamvaipoh ahiuvin chuleh lengpa vaihom ho ahiuvin Mordecai akichat jeh un abonun Juda te te akithopi gamtauve. 
Ajeh chu lengpa khopia chun Mordecai chu sangtah a atunlet ahitan, Gambih jousea jong aminthang cheh cheh jengin chuleh athahat cheh cheh jeng tan ahi. 
Hijeh chun Juda te chu anikho kitep nikhon agal miteu chu chemjam in asat chap jengun ahi. Ama hon agal miteu athat’un asumang gamji tauvin amaho hin vetdaho jouse chu anop nop in aboljeng jiuvin ahi. 
Susa khopi sung seh seh a jong Juda te ten mi ja-nga athat un ahi. 
ama hon Parshandatha, Dalphon leh Aspatha athatnun, 
Chuleh Poratha, Adalia le Aridatha athatnun, 
Parmashta toh Arisai, Aridai le Vaizatha athatnun, 
Juda te galmi, Hammedatha chapa Haman chate som jong chu athatdoh tauvin ahi. Ahinla athil alo hou achom pou’ve. 
Hiche nikho tah chun Susa khopia athajat nuchu lengpa kom’a ahetsah tauvin, 
Hiche jouhin lengpan lengnu Esther akouvin ajah a, “Juda te ten hiche Susa khopi seh seh a jong mi 500 athat un chule Haman chate som jong athat tauve. Ama hon hilaitah a bon chujat atha uleh gamkai dang ho a ijat athat tadiuvem? Tua hi ipi nadeibe nahlai ham? Nakibolpeh ding ahi, seijin ipi thumbe ding nanei nah lai’em? Nathum chan chu kibolpeh nalai ding ahi,” ati. 
Esther in adonbut in, “Lengpa nalung lhaina ahi ding leh Juda te te chu Susa khopia tunia abol tobang un jing jongleh bolsah kit inlang chuleh Haman chate 10 chu mol lhon chunga kikhai sanghen,” ati. 
Hiti chun lengpan anoppeh tan Susa khopi sunga hiche dan chu akiphong doh in chuleh Haman chate thilong o mho chu mol-lhon chunga chun akhaisang tauve. 
Hichun Susa khopi a Juda te ho chu pumkhat’in dding khom’un alha Lhakao ni get nin mi jathum athat be kitnun athillu vang achom pouve. 
Hiti chun lengpa gamkai jousea Juda te chu amaho kihuhna dingin dding khom un ahi. Avetda’uva pang mihem sang som sagi le sang nga athat gam’un, agalmi teuva kon'in a-ongthol tauve. Ahinla ama hon athil le lo imacha akichom pouve. 
Hiche thilsoh ho chu Adar lha nisomle nithum nia thilsoh ahi, ajing nikho nimsom le nili nin akicholdo uvin agaljo nao chu kipahtah in golvah abollun alopna aneiyun ahi. 
Susa a Juda te hon agal mite nisom le thum, nisom le nili geiya mi athauva chule nisom le ni nga ni akicholdo un kipana golvah anabollun ahi. 
Hijeh chun, tuni chan gei’in thinglhanga um Juda te kho gamla a umhon hiche nikho leh, phalbi kichai kon lamleh kumlhun golvah abollun akipah khomun nehle chah thilpeh ho akithot to jiuvin ahi. 
Mordecai in hiche thilsoh ho hi ajih lut’in Xerxes gamsung jousea anai ahin agamla a um ahin Juda te ho hi lekha anathot jin ahi. 
Hiche nini sung hi kumseh a golvah abolji diuvin ana sei peh tan ahi. 
Ama hiche nikho sunga hi kipana golvah bolding leh nehle chah kipehto a, gentheite manbeiya pehdingin ana seipeh in ahi. Hiche hin Juda te agalmi teuva konna kipana amun'ao, alunggim naova konna thanopna amun'ao ageldoh jing nadiu ahi. 
Hiti chun Juda ten Mordecai thilgon ho hi anop peh un, hiche hi kumseh in achepi tauvin ahi. 
Juda te galmi Agagi mi Hammedatha chapa Haman chun Juda te tehi suhmang helna dinga nikhokhat lhendohna dinga vangvetna ananei ahi. (Hiche avangvetna chu Purim akiti) 
Ahinlah lengnu Estherhi lengpa anga ahunglut phat in, aman dan chomkhat ahin sem’in hichun Haman tohgon phaloutah chu ama chunga achuhlo tan ahi. Haman le achate chu mol lhon hemjet chunga akhailih tauve. 
Hijeh a chu hiche golnop nahi Purim kiti ahi, ajeh chu hiche hi gollui paova vangvetna ktina ahi. Amaho tah in anathoh khahsao ahijeh leh Mordecai in anajihlut ahijeh in ahin chepi tauve. 
Gamjousea Juda te ten hiche chon nahi chepi dingin anom’un chuleh achilhah te jousen jong ajui diuvin ana kilol’un ahi. Ama hon hiche nikho teni hi kumseh leh akitepna nikho ahi alopna neijing dingin ana phongdoh un ahi. 
Hiche nikho ho chu lenggamsunga khopi ho leh gamkai ho jousea insung jousen akhang akhanga ageldoh jing uva anit jing diu ahi. Hiche Purim golvah ankong hi Juda te lah a itih hijongleh mangthah louva anit jing ding chuleh ipi thil anasoh ham tihi akhang akhanga mansah louhel ding ahi. 
Abihail chanu lengnu Esther le Juda te mi Mordecai in lekha khat anajih lhon'in, masangjep a Mordecai in ana sutsa Purim kiman di thudol se se chu, lengnun thaneitah namdetna anei tan ahi. 
Hiche lekha chu Juda te jouse anathot un chule Xerxes lenggam a gamkai jakhat le somni le sagi sungah ana thot soh hel un ahi. Lekhathot a chu Juda te lah a chamna le lungmon na leng hen akitin ahi. 
Juda te mi Mordecai leh lengnu Esther in dan a anatundoh lhon Purim golvah hi anikho kipeh dungjuiya kumseh a nit dinga ana kiphutdoh ahi. (Mipite hin amaho ding leh achilhah ho diuva anngol na leh lunghemna anathoh khom bang uva hiche Purim golvah jonghi ana tundoh u ahi). 
Hitia hi Purim chon nahi Esther thupeh in namdetna anei hetjing dingin lekhajol khat a kijih lut tai.
