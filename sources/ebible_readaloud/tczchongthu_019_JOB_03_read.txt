Job.
3.
Thilsoh hijat nung hin Job akamkan apen nikho agaosap tai. 
Job in aseijin, 
“Kapen nikho le keima mihem a kahung kijil na jan chu chulmangin umhen, 
Hiche nikho chu muthim soh jeng hen, Chung Pathen a ding jengin jong mang helhen, chule khovah in salvah hih helhen. 
Hiche nikho chu muthim khojinin kitom mang jeng hen, meivomin khu jeng hen chule muthim chun kichat tijat sah hen. 
Hiche jan chu nikho kisimna a kon'in kichop mang jeng hen, kum sunga nikho kisimna a avellin kisimtha kit tahih hel hen, lha kisimna holah a jong avellin hung kilang lut hihhen. 
Hiche jan chu nao kijil theilouna jan hihen. 
Gaosap them Leviathan suthou thei khop a gaosap them hon hiche ni chu gaosap uhen. 
Hiche nikho chule jingvalpa jong thim den hen, vah ding kinem hen lang ahinlah panna bei hihen lang jingkah khovah jong muhihbeh hen. 
Kanu naobu khah louva anakoi nikho chu gaosap in umhen, leiset gim gentheina jouse mu dinga eina pen sah jeh chun. 
Ipi jeh a athisa a kanapen louham? Kanu naobu a konna kahung doh a chu kana thilou ham? 
Ibola kanu phei chunga chu eina kilup sah ham? Ipi bolla kanun anoija eina vah ham? 
Kahung pen chun ana thiden leng kei tua hi lungmong tah a kauma kaimutna kicholdo tadinga. 
Leiset leng le prime minister oupe u oupe tah tah mangthahsa hotoh kicholdo khoma kium dinga. 
Sana haotah tah le ain sungu dangka dimset ho chutoh kichodo khom tading. 
Athisa a peng chapang ho leh naosen khovah mukhalou ho banga eina kivui den lou ham? 
Mithisa ho lah a chun miphalou in hahsatna asosah tah lou jeh chun thachol jouse aki choldo tai. 
Thikhol khulla chun sohchang kihen ho jong angah a pangpa sapsetna ajatapouve. 
Mihao leh migenthei aum khomun chule soh jong apupa a kon'in a ongthol tai. 
Ipi dinga migentheipa hi khovah kimusah a migentheipa hi hinkho kipea ham? 
Thi ding angaicha lheh uvin ahinlah thina chu ahunglhung pon, gou kiselguh sangin ngahlel tah in ahol un ahi. 
Gentheina jouse athoh chai uva athi tengu leh kipana adimset jiuvin lhankhuh amu tengu leh akipah jiuve. 
Akhonung ding neilou Pathen'in hahsatna dimsetna aumkhum ho chu hinkho kipeuva ham? 
Khoisatna ding kanei thei pon , kathoh hahsat ho hi twi bangin akisung lhai. 
Keiman kaki chat pen ipi ham khat kachunga asoh in, hatah a kakichat leh kalung gimna pen ahunglhung tai. 
Lungmon konna kanei poi, thipbeh cha umna kanei poi, choldona kanei pon, boina le hahsatna bou ahung ji'e.”
