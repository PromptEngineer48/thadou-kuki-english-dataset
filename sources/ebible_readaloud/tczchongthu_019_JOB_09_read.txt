Job.
9.
Hichun job in asieikit in : 
Henge, keiman alhangpin hiche hi adih e ti kahei. Ahinlah Pathen mitmua mihem khat chu themmona neilou ahitai tia kiphong doh thei ding ham? 
Mikhat touvin Pathen chu kiheh pi ding nom taleh ama chu khatveija sang khat vei kidonbut jou thei ding hinam? 
Ijeh inem itile Pathen chu ha chih a chinga, chule thahat tah ahi. Koipen in ama chu aphin doh a anel jou ding ham? 
Aman hetsahna masat beijin molsang ho achon mang jin, alunghan tengleh aleh khup jin ahi. 
Aman aumna munna kon'in leiset ahot ling jin chule abul akithing ji'e. 
Aman thu apeh a ahile, nisa soh tapontin chule lha jong vah taponte. 
Vanho jong aman achanga apha jal'a chule twikhanglen kinong jong gamgi asem peh ji ahi. 
Ahsi somleng, Bombiel leh juhei suhtum, vantham jol lhanglang kaija ahsi ho jouse abon'a aman asem ahi. 
Aman hetphah hoi hilou thil oupe tah tah ho asemin, sim senglou thil kidang aboldoh e. 
Ahivangin, ahung naiji teng, kamu thei pon , ache teng jongleh ache kamu deh poi. 
Mikhat chu ahinna alahpeh a ahileh kon a suhtang thei ding ham? Ipi bolla nahim tin adong ngam dem? 
Chutia chu Pathen'in a lunghanna chu atuhtang lou hileh twikhanglen'a ganhing tamtah tah ho jong akeng tonoija achilngim ding ahi. 
Hijeh a chu kei koi kahija, Pathen chu donbut dia kagot ding ham? Ahilouleh kaki nelpi jeng ding ham? 
Keima ana dih kha jeng jonglehng kihkonna ding kanei louding ahi. Eihepi nadinga bou katao thei ding ahi. 
Chule keiman ama chu kouving lang, chule aman eihou nama jongleh aman ka thusei angai ding kaging chapoi. 
Ajeh chu aman huipi gopi a eino khuma chule ajeh beija tang louhella eisuh nat ding ahi. 
Aman ei haijom sah thei pon ahinlah gentheina khaveng vungin eisun dim khume. 
Thahat sanna no ding hijeng jongleh, ama chu thahat tah ahin thudih a tanding kiti jongleh koipen in ama chu thutanna munna dinga akou ngam ding ham? 
Keima hijeng jong leng, keima kamtah in themmo eichansah ding ahi bouve, themmona neilou hijongleng chonse a eiki sim nalai ding ahi. 
Keima nolnabei kahi, ahinla hichun keija dingin kikhelna eibolpeh deh pon, kahinna jong kadei tapoi. 
Nolna bei mi hihen michonse hijongleng Pathen dingin abonchan akibang cheh in hijeh a chu themmona neilou le michonse ania asuhmang cheh ahi, kati. 
Vangsetnan ahin lhun khuma nolnabei mi thina chu aman anuisat bepme. 
Leiset pumpi hi migiloute khutna um ahin, chule Pathen'in thutanho mit asuh chotji ahi. Ama bol ahilou le koibol ba hi ding ham? 
Milhai hat pa sangin kahinkho achegang jon kipana mukhalouhel lin aleng mang jitai. 
Pumpenga kisem kong bangin akitol mang jitai, muvanlai aneh ding kimat dinga gangtah a hung lenglha abange. 
Keiman kakiphin naho sumil ingting, kalung gimna maiso pailhang ting, chule thanom tah in um inge tia kaki gelji vang'in, 
Kanat thoh gentheinaho hi nasatah a kichatna leh lunggimna kaneije, ajeh chu O Pathen, nolna beija neimu louding kahei. 
Ipi iti henlang hijongleh themmo hange eikimu ding ahileh ipi phachom dinga ei kibol gim gim ham? 
Kei le kei sabon in kisil ngim jeng jong leng chang-al in kakhut sop theng jong leng, 
Nangin bon lhoh umna kotong sunga nei sonlut in natin chule keima vonnen hon jong eideimo diu ahi. 
Pathen chu kei banga thibai hilou ahin, hijeh chun ama to kakinel theipoi, ahilou jongleh thutanna munna kapuilut theipoi. 
Keini eisucham theilhon khat anaum hihen lang, mikhat touvin thakhatna eipui khom thei lhon hihen, 
Misuchampa chun Pathen chun eijep a asuhtang thei hileh ama bolgenthei kichatna chu kanei louding ahi. 
Chutileh kichatna beihel a ama kom'a thu kasei thei ding ahin, ahinlah keiman keima thahat in hichu kabol theipoi.
