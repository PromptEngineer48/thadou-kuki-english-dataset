Job.
10.
Kahinkho hi kahechen tai, lhangphong tah in kiphin inge, kahin gentheina jeh a hi kiphin ding ahi. 
Pathen kom'a ima hilouvin nei themmo sah hih in kati ding ahi. Keidouna mona eihin lhut khum peh ding chu nei seipeh in. 
Kei nei suhgentheina chu ipi phatchomna naneijem? Nakhut monga nasemsa keihi ibola nei paidoh ham? Migilou thilgon chunga meheuva naumpet nahi. 
Namit teni hi mihemte mit tobang hija, mihem hon amuthei hou bou hi namu thei ham? 
Nahinkho hi mihem hinkho chanbep bou saova ham? Nahinkho hi chomlheh jenga ham? 
Hijeh chun kathemmonaho kintah in neikhol chil peh inlang chule kachonsetna nahol hol jeng ding ham? 
Themmona bei kahi nahet jeng vang'in nakhut teni a kon'in koiman eihuhdoh jou ponte. 
Nakhut tenia neigkonna neisem ahijeng vang'in tun nei sumang hel tai. 
Leivuija konna neisem ahi, neigeldoh inlang, leivui nei nungso sah loi kit ding ham? 
Nangin kathilgon hoa neipui hoijin, chule naobu a neigong in ahi. 
Katahsa leh kavunin neitomin, kagu le kachang ho neijop mat peh in, 
Nangin hinkho neipen chule na ngailutna thonlou neimu sah in; ame hoidamna noija kahinkho neihoitup peh e. 
Ahijeng vang'in nachaloh dan adihtah, nathilgon dihtah ho, 
Nangin neivet lhih jinga chule kachonsetna ahileh kachonsetna chu nei ngaidam pon nate. 
Kachonset khah a ahileh keija dinga phamo lheh jeng ahin, chule nolnabei hijeng jong leng kalu kadomsang theipoi. Ajeh chu jachatna leh gentheinan eikidip letset e. 
Ijem tin kalu domsang taleng, nangin keipi bahkai sadel bangin neidellin natin, keidouna a kichat tijat naumna na thaneina chu nei musah bep ding ahi. 
Kei douna a nanatoh chu avel vellin nahettohsah jin, na lunghanna khang toujing chun kachunga neisun khumin chule kei dou dingin sepai hon thah thah nahin puilut ji'e. 
Chuti chu ahileh ipi dinga kanu naobua konna nei lahdoh ham? Kapenna chu ipi dinga nei thiden sah lou ham? 
Chutile ahung peng khalouhel kabah a naobua konna jangkeija lhahkhuh anache tadinga, 
Keiman nikho lhomcha bou kanei ahitan hijeh chun kachangin nei dalha tan phat chomcha khat beh nopsahna phat kanei nadingin, 
Kile kit lou dinga kadalhah a khojin le muthim lhangkhal kiheh nagam kajot masangin, 
Jan khangkim lai banga khojin nagam muthim leh nohphah nagam, khovah jeng jong jan khang kim lai khojin toh abah nagam chu!
