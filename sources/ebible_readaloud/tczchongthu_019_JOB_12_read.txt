Job.
12.
Chujou chun Job in aseikit in, 
Nangho mihem te hin thil ijakai nahet sohkeiju hilou ham? Chule nathi tengu leh chihna jong nangma toh thitha ding ahi. 
Aphai, kei matah in thil themkhat hetna kaneije, chule kei sangin nang naphajo deh poi; hitobang thil ho hethei lou nangin thu nasei sei jeng ding ham? 
Kagol kapai ten einuisat un, eidonbut ding kinema Pathen kakou jeh chun, keima hi midih nolnabei kahin; ahijeng vang'in einuisat uve. 
Anop nopma um mihon, hahsatna toh ho chu ija selou tah in anuisat jiuvin, amaho chun akipal ho chu ason opden jiuve. 
Ahinlah michom ho chu mong tah in aki dalhan, chule Pathen sulunghang ho bitsel in achengui. 
Ganhing ho chudong jong le chun ama hon nahil dingu ahi; van lai jolla vachate chu dong le chun ama hon naseipeh unte. 
Leiset chu kihoupi le chun abol ding dan nahil ding ahi, twikhanglen'a nga hon nakom'a thu seijuhen. 
Kamanthah nachu Pakai khutna hung kon ahi ti ahet sohkeiju ahi. 
Ajeh chu thil ahing thei jouse hinkho leh mihem jouse haina hi ama khut a um ahi bouve. 
Kam in anneh ho atwi a-al ahetkhen banga nakol in jong ajah ho hi apatep ji ahi. 
Chihna hi upaho a ahin, chule thil hetkhenna hi tehseho a ahi. 
Ahinlah chihna tahbeh leh thahatna chu Pathen kom'a bou kimu ahin, thumop themna le thil hetkhen thei nahi ama a ahi. 
Ipi hijongleh aman asuhmangsa chu kisapha kit thei louhel ahi; aman mi khat chu songkul'a akhum teng jamdoh nading um thei lou ahi. 
Aman go chu atuhtang tenglehiset chu nelgam asohji tai, twi chu ahin lhadoh tenglehiset chung ahin chup soh jitai. 
Henge, thahatna leh chihna chu ama a ahin, milhem lhaho leh akilhem lhaho chu ania athaneina um lhon ahi. 
Thumopma pangho ho apui mangin, athutan phatnao alah mang peh jin, thutan chingtah ho ahung ngol ji tauve. 
Aman lengpa sangkhol chol asutlhah peh in, akong buh uva khao akiveijin puimangin aumji tauve. 
Aman thempu ho apui mangjin, adinmun u alahmang peh in, kum tamtah thahatna pang ho chu aleh khup jin ahi. 
Tahsan umtah thumopma pang ho asuthip in, upaho thil hetkhen theina alahmang peh jitai. 
Leng chapa te chunga minsetna asunlhah khumin, mithahat manchah beijin akoije. 
Muthim lah a kisel thil kidang ho ahohdoh jin khojin mang keina mun ahin salvah jin ahi. 
Namtin vaipi atungdoh jin chule aman amaho chu asu mang jin, namtin vaipi ho chu akikehjal sah jin chule don louvin akoi ji'e. 
Leng ho thil hetkhenna alahmang peh jin, lampi umlouna gam phalou lah a vahle din adalha jitai. 
Khovah beijin muthim lah a akimai leleuvin, jukhamma londang dang ho bangin aseme.
