Job.
20.
Hichun Naamath mi Zophar chun adonbut in: 
Kadonbut tei ding ahi, ijeh inem itile keima nasatah in eiki sunoh phah e. 
Nami phosal nahi kahin thoh joulou ahitan, ahinlah ka lhagaovin donbut nomna eihin neisah ahitai. 
Phat kipat tillai leiset chunga mihem te ana um masat pen laiju chu geldoh in, 
Migilou te kichoisang nahi phat chomcha bou ahin, chule Pathen neiloute kipana jong chomkhat a ding bou ahi. 
Pathen neiloute kiletsah nan van pha hen lang, chuleh alu uvin meibol ga tongkha jongleh, 
Amaho chu tonsot a mangthah ding ahiuvin, a eh u kipaimang banga amaho jong kipai mang ding ahiuve. Koi hijongleh ahepha jousen dong uvin te. 
Amaho hi janmang bangin mangun tin chule kinung mukit lou diu ahin, amaho hi jan a thil kimu aman bangin mangthah tauvinte. 
Amaho chu amukha sa hon anungmu kit lou diu ahitan, a inkoten anungmu kit louhel diu ahi. 
Achate houvin mivaichate kom'a akhut do uvinte, ijeh inem itile ama hon aguh u haosat naho chu anung peh kit diu ahi. 
Khangdong hijongleu agubuh u leivui lah a kijam ding ahi. 
ama hon agitlou nau chu kipana ana neiju ahin, alei noi uva hung junlha ding ahi. 
ama hon twi asauvin akamsung uva sottah akoi uve. 
Ahinlah hetman louhel in a oisung uva a anneh u chu athuh jitai, a oisung uva thina thei gu aum jeh chun, 
ama hon aval lhum sau anei agou hou chu alodoh un ahin Pathen'in akoi lhahsah lou diu ahi. 
ama hon gulsoh gu hin chop untin gulsen amaho ahintha dingu ahi. 
ama hon olive thao twidung banga long ahilou le khoiju leh bongnoi vadung banga long amukhah kit louhel diu ahi. 
ama hon atohman uva amu thil jouse anungpeh uva anei agou jouseuvin nopsahna hin lhut peh pouvin te. 
Ijeh inem itile ama hon vaichaho asu genthei un chule genthei lengvai jin adalhau vin ama hon asah u hilouhel inho akilah uve. 
Amaho akilose uvin chuleh lung nachimna anei pouve, aval beihella thil jouse nei cham kim ding bou agel gel uve. 
Imacha khen neilouhel lin amaho changseh in anei jou hel uvin hijeh a chu ahaosat nau jong umsot louhel ding ahi. 
Neng tah a aumlai tah ule hahsat nan ahin lhunkhum in hahsat gentheina achung uva hunglhung ding ahi. 
Pathen'in amaho chu hahsat gentheina a a oisung dimset uvin peuhen Pathen'in alunghanna achung uva hin juhlhah sah hen. 
Thih hemma konna jamdoh ding go jongleh sum-eng a kisem thalchang in adot pai ding ahi. 
Thalchang chun atungtun langa konna ki botdoh ding chule athalchang muh chu thisan nat dehduh a thina kichat umtah chu achung uva chu ding ahi. 
Agou jouse muthim lhangkal kiheh lah a kisep lut ding, athil keu jouseu akidalha beihel a gamlah meiyin akahvam gam hel ding ahi. 
Vanho jousen athemmo nau ahin phon jal ding chule leiset in jong ama douna ahin phot chet ding ahi. 
Ain jouseu twisoh in alhoh manga Pathen lunghanna chu twi hattah long banga hung longlha ding ahi. 
Pathen'in migilou apeh ding tohman hiche hi ahin, Pathen'in achan diuva ateppeh sau chu ahi.
