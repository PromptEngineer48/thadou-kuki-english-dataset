Job.
21.
Chuphat in Job in asei kit in: 
Kathusei ho hi ipi ham ti naichan hin ngai uvin, hichu kilhamkonna neipeh thei sun u chu ahi. 
Neithoh piuvin chule ken thu sei inge, keiman thu kasei jou tengleh nang hon nei nuisat pan kit nauvin nate. 
Pathen kom'a kiphin kahin, mihem ho kom'a ahipoi, kanga sot joulou nau ajeh hoitah kaneije. 
Nei veuvin chule thipchet in um un, nakhut uvin nakam kisip cheh uvin, 
Ken ipi kasei ding ham tia kagel teng, kaki thing tuntun in katahsa akihot hot e. 
Ipi dinga migiloute hi khang tou touva ateh gei uva khang uva chule thahat hiuvem? 
Chule amaho hi achateu khantou leh a inchen uve dinga hing ahiuvin chule atuteu chunga akipah uve. 
A in hou jong kichat tijat na a kon'in a ongdoh un chule Pathen'in agimbol poi. 
Abongchal hou jong nou neilou vin aum pouvin, abongpi houvin nou aneiyun athollha jong aumpoi. 
Kelngoite bangin achateu akichop le sah uvin, achapang hou chu akichom un chule alam lam uve. 
ama hon semjang saigin leh selang dah toh lhon'in la a asauvin, theile kimut gin in kipa golnop abol'uve. 
ama hon ahin nikho hou nopsah nan amangun, chuti chun lungmong tah in lhankhuh ajon ji tauve. 
ama hon chemangin nathil imacha kadei pouvin, nalam lhah jong kadei pouve tin PAthen kom'a aseijuve. 
Hatchungnung kitipa chu koi hija chule ipi dinga athu kangai diu ham? 
ama hon ahausat najouseu hi amaho thahat a kilam doh danin agel un, ahinlah hitobang lunggel na a chu keiman imacha bol ding kanei louding ahi. 
Ahijeng vang'in migilou thaomei jong akimumit peh khapoi. Amaho hin hahsatna anei khah uvem? Pathen'in lunghang tah a lung gentheinahi amaho ana hoppeh khah em? 
Changpol banga amaho huijin amasang amutmang khah hitam? Huipi hat in changvai alhohmang banga alhoh mang khah hitam? 
Aphai Pathen'in achateu beh agimbol ding ahi tin nasei juvin ahinlah chonset bol pen pen vang chu agim bol ding ahi tin kaseije. Chutile ama thutanna chu ahet thei diu ahi. 
Amanthah nau ama hon amit tah uvin veu hen hatchungnung pa lunghanna thuhtah chu twidon in don uhen. 
Ijeh inem itile ama athi nung tengleh a inkoteu iti dingu ham ti angaito pouve. 
Ahinlah koipen in Pathen chu ahil thei ding ham? Mithahat chung thu jeng jong atan ji ahi. 
Mikhat chu hausa tah le lungmong tah hin boina beihel in athi jin ahi. 
Mithahat misalhing ho leh tahsa damthei ho lim chu ahi. 
Mikhat kit chu nopsahna hepha louhel in vaichatna khonteh in athi jin ahi. 
Ahinlah ani hkonna chu leivui lah a kivui cheh ahi lhon'in, ani lhonna than hon aneh cheh lhon ding ahi. 
Ven nangin ipi nagel kahen nangin kei douna a nathilgon kahen ahi. 
Achonset jeh uva ain u mang hella migilou leh mihausatah thu neihin seipeh ding ahi. 
Ahinlah akimvella um ho hi dong le chun ama hon adihtah chu naseipeh dingu ahi. 
Vangset phat laileh amaho chu aki hing hoijin chule manthah na a konna ajamdoh diu jong akiphal peh e. 
Koimachan amaho hi lhangphong tah in aseise ngam pouvin ahilou le anatoh athilbol hou jeh in jong lethuhna akipe pouve. 
Lhankhuh jkonna amaho akiput tengu le alhan u chu mihon nidan kah khat bep bou anga peh jiuve. 
Lhanmol a chun mithi kivuina kin akimang in atahsa choldo dinga akilupsah namun a chun mihon ajanau apeuve, chule leiset in kicholdo na nomtah in apei. 
Nanghon keihi iti dan a thu hom keuva nei lhamon diu ham? Nahil chet najouseu jong jou ahi.
