Job.
27.
Job in athusei ajom kit in: 
Kathaneina eilah mang peh pa Pathen a kihahselna kaneijin kalhagao eisuhnatpeh Hatchungnung pa chu, 
Pathen a konna haina hu kaneija kahai laisung sea, 
Kakama thuse kasei louding chuleh kalei jin jong thujou sei ponge. 
Keiman nadihna kapom peh louhel ding ahi, kathi chan geija lung thengsel'a kaum na hi kahuh jing ding ahi. 
Keiman nolnabei kahina hi lhaso louhela katuhchah jing ding kahin lai sung sea ka se le pha hetna hi thengsel ah ahi. 
Kamelma pa chu migiloupa bangin kigim bolhen, kamelma te jong miphalou ho tobang hiuhen. 
Pathen'in asatlhah tengule Pathen neilou ten ipi kinepna anei nahlai diu ham? Chuleh ahinkhou akilah mang tengleh, 
Hahsatna achung uva ahunglhun tengleh Pathen'in akanau angai peh dem? 
Hatchungnung pa kom'a kipana akimu diu hinam? Itih phat hijongleh Pathen ahou thei diu ham? 
Pathen thahatna chu keiman kahil ding nahi. Keiman Hatchungnung pa chung chang thu imacha im kanei louding ahi. 
Hicheng ho jouse hi namusoh ahitan ahivangin panna bei thu jeng kakom'ah nasei nalaije. 
Migilouvin Pathen kom'a konna amu ding chu hiche hi ahi, Hatchungnung pa a konna agou chan diu chu hiche hi ahi. 
Chate tamtah nei maithei unte, ahinlah achateu chu gal kidouna ahilou le anngolla thidiu ahi. 
Ahingdoh nalai ho chu gamna hise a thidiu, chule ameithai hou jengin jong apul u adou lou diu ahi. 
Miphalou ten sum alom lomin nei maithei untin chule ponsil lhemlhung bangin sevum maithei unte. 
Ahinlah hiche ponsil ho chu michonpha hon akivon diu chule nolnabei miho hiche sum chu kihom diu ahi. 
Migilou hon mompan banga se baijin in kisah untin, thingbah kanga kisa bangin paset in kisah unte. 
Migiloupa chu alupna ah hausa tah in chen tin ahin ahung khah doh teng anei jouse ana beisoh amu ding ahi. 
Kichat nan twisoh bangin vuh khum untin chuleh amaho chu janna khopi hui nung chun amut mang tauve. 
Solam hui chun amaho apomang un chule amaho chu achemang a kitheh anga hi tauve. 
Dipdapna neilouvin apei lhu uvin, hiche thaneina a konna jamdohna ding akiha bol uve. 
Ahinlah mijousen anuisat u chu iman agel pouve.
