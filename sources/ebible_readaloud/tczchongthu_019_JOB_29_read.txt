Job.
29.
Job in athusei ajom kit in: 
Pathen'in eina chin hoi jing lai kum chemangsa ho kagel doh kit e. 
Kamasang lampi eivah peh a muthim lah a bitsel a kana vale lai. 
Pathen toh golcha a ka in a apansah lai, aluboh pen a kapan lai chu hileh. 
Hatchungnung pa chu kakom'a aum jing lai, chuleh kachate kakimvel a anaum laijin, 
Kabongpi ten bongnoi ninglhing set a asosah thei laiju chuleh kahon na keh hon olive thao alondoh sah laiju chu hileh. 
Khopi kelkot phung kajot a ja um tahtah le lamkai len leh lalte ho toh katou khomle lai nikho ho chu hileh, 
Khangdong hon eimu teng ule eipel doh jiuva upa tehseho jengin jong akom'a kahung tengleh jana neitah a adindoh ji laiju. 
Leng chapa te jong thipbeh a aum uva chule akam uva akhut u akoi uva. 
Khopi sung vaipo lenpen pen ho thipchet a adin uva jabolna a adin uva aleiju atuh chahkheh uva. 
Kathu japha ho jousen eipahcha uvin, eimu kha jousen phate chan eihoupi jiuve. 
Ijeh inem itile mivaicha ten angaichat nau leh chagate kithopina ngaicha ho chu kakithopi jin ahi. 
Keiman kinepna beimi ho chu kaki thopin ahile ama hon phatthei eiboh jiuve, chule meithai ho lungthim kakipa doh sah jin, chuteng ama hon la asajiuve. 
Kabol jouse chu lungtheng sellin kabol in chonphat nan sangkhol chol bangin eikhu khume, chule thutan dihna chu lukop bangin kakikop e. 
Mitcho ho din mitchang tenin kapang in, chule elbai ho din akeng tenin kapange. 
Chaga pabeite din mipan kapang in chuleh het khahlouhel miho jong kithopi angaichat nau vah kapanpi ji e. 
Keiman Pathen neilou mi engse ho ha kasuhbal peh jin, chuleh gimbol a um chu aha a kon'in kasalhah peh ji'e. 
Hinkho pha sautah kahin man nung hin tahbeh mongin kathi tengleh kainko ten eikimvel unte tin kana gel’e. 
Ijeh inem itile, thingphung ajung hon twi lah aphah a abah ho daitwi in achap nou jing tobang kahi. 
Jabolna thah thah kachunga kilong louhel in eikipe jing jin, chuleh kathahatna jong tang louhel in akibelap jinge. 
Amichang cheh in kathumopna angaijun ka thusei ding hi ama hon thipchet in angah uve. 
Chuleh kasei chai tengleh ama hon belap ding anei ji pouve, Kathumop chun amaho alung lhai sah jeh u chun. 
Mihem ten gojuh ding kinem tah a anga jing bangun ama hon ka thusei ding kinem tah in angah jiuvin, ka thusei ho chu chavang gotwi bangin adon uve. 
Amaho alunglhah tengu leh ken amaho chu nuiseuvin kave jin ahileh mailhai sel a kavet chu amaho a din manlutah ahiji. 
Haosapu bangin ama hon atoh diu ka seipeh in sepai hon lah a chun lengpa bangin kachengin chuleh lengvai ho kalhamon jin ahi.
