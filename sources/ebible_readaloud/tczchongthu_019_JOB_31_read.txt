Job.
31.
Keiman kamit teni toh kigahna kasemin khangdong numei chu jon lunga vet loudingin, 
Ijeh inem itile chunga Pathen chun eiho ipi dinga eilhen u ham? Chungsang a um hatchungnung pa a konna goulo ding chu ipi ham? 
Migiloute dinga vangsetna hilou hija chule thil phalou bolte dinga vangset umtah dinmun chu hilou ham? 
Pathen'in kabol katoh jouse amulou hija chuleh kakalse jat jong ahet hilou ham? 
Mikhat tou kom'a jou kasei khah a chuleh mikhat tou kalhep khah em? 
Thudih kitena chun Pathen'in eitetoh hen, ijeh inem itile aman kalung thenna hi ahet ahi. 
Alamlhah a konna ahilouva ijem tia kamu ji ho a kalungthim jon lung ngaichatna kanei khah a ahilou le chonsetna dang khat a themmona kanei khah a ahile, 
Hiti chun mikhat touvin hange mim kaphu na ana nejeng hen, kaphu doh chengse abonchan kibotdoh soh jeng hen. 
Ijem tia kalungthim in numei khat tou alheplhah a ahilou le ka inheng pa jinu jon lunga kavet khah a ahile, 
Chuti chun kajinu jong pasal dang ji hijeng hen, pasal dang khat in luppi jeng hen. 
Ijeh inem itile jon lung put hi jachat umtah chonset ahi, hiche dandih louva chon ho chu gimbol dinga lha ahi. 
Hichu damun lampi lhung keija meiyin akah tobang ahin, kanei jouse kamang hel ding ahi. 
Ijemtia kasohpa ham kasoh numei adih louva kabolla ahileh ama hon akiphin nau kakom'a ahin lhut tengu leh, 
Pathen chu iti kakimaito pi ding hitam? Aman thu eihin doh teng ipi kasei tadem? 
Ajeh chu Pathen'in kei leh kasoh te kaniuva eisem kop u ahi. 
Vaichate panpi ding kada khah a ahilou le meithai ho kinepna kasuh keh peh khah em? 
Ka anneh chunga kachip khah a chaga pabeite toh nehkhom ding kada khah em? 
Ahipoi, mipa khat bangin, kaneo laiya pat in chagate kahin hoitup in chule kahinkho lhumin meithaite hoitup nan kahin mange. 
Itih lai hijongleh chenna ding neilou ponbeija umho chule von ding neilou gentheiho kamu tengleh 
Akisillum diuva samul pon kapeh ji jeh a eipachat jiu hilou ham? 
Thutanpa kalanga apan nadinga chaga khat douna a kakhut kadop khah a ahile, 
Hiti chun kalengkou aumna a kon'in kisatlha jeng hen, kaban jang jong akisuhto na a kon'in kisatlha jeng hen. 
Hichu Pathen thutanna kimaitopi sangin phajonte, ijemtia oupe Pathen chun einan chahkheh a ahile kinepna ipi kanei ding ham? 
Keiman katahsanna summa kakoi khah a ahile, ahilou le kasana a hi kakison khah a ahile, 
Kanei kagou jouse leh kakhol khom jouse jeh a kipah a kahile, 
Vantham jol ho a vahpel a vah nisa leh lha alampia chesuh khu kavet a, 
Chuleh aguh a kalungthim kalheplhah a amaho houna kaga galchop khah a ahile, 
Achuti tah a ahile thutan vaihom hon eigim bol uhen, chuti chu ahi tah leh van Pathen thusei kangaipeh lou hi ding tina ahi. 
Kamelma te chu manthah nan ahin phah tenga ahilou le alampiuva hahsatna ahung tengleh, hetnom ten tena aum jeh uva kipah kahile, 
Ahipoi, keiman mikhat tou kaspset a, ahilou le phulah ding kasei kha pon, hitobang chonsetna hi kabol khapoi. 
Kasoh pan jong adang ho chu gilkel in kachesah tai tin asei khapoi. 
Hetkhah lou mi jong kakihei mangsan kha pon, ahinlah mijouse din kot kahonpeh ji bouve. 
Midang ho banga ken kachonsetna selmang ding kagot a, kasuhkhel kalungthima ka imden khah ham? 
Mipiho kakichatna ahilou le kikhop khom naho kadeimo a hiche ho jeh a chu thipbeh a insunga kaum den khah ham? 
Mikhat tou beh in ka thusei eingai peh hen lang hileh ven kakihkonna dinga kamin soi kakai ding ahi. Hatchungnungpa chun eidonbut hen lang eiheh pan kei douna thu sundoh hen. 
Keiman themmo eichan sah nau chu kiloupi sah tah a kaki maitopi ding lallukhuh banga kakikhuh ding ahi. 
Ijeh inem itile keiman kathilbol adihtah chu ka seipeh ding ahin, ama masanga chu leng chapa te banga kahung ding ahi. 
Kagam in themmo eichansah khah tah a tucha maha jouse kitoh tah a ahung ka sohkeijuva, 
Ahilouva achang leh mim kana guh khah a ahilou le anei ho kana tha khah tah a ahile, 
Chuti chun gehu khellin ling leh khau agama chun keh henlang, chang khellin hampa keh hen. Job thusei ho akichaitai.
