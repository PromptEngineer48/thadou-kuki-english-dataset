Job.
34.
Hiti chun Elihu in aseijin: 
Miching hon ka thusei hi ngai uvin, chihna themna neihon phate chan hin ngai uvin. 
Ngaijuvin kam’in antwi leh twilou ahet khen bangin bilkol in jong thucheng ajah jouse hi apatep jin ahi, tin Job in aseije. 
Ipihi adih ham ti eima cheh a din hekhen u hitin, ipi hi thilpha ham ti kihilkhom sohkei uhite. 
Hijeh chun Job in jong aseijin keima hi nolnabei kahin ahinlah Pathen'in ka thusei eilah mang peh tai. 
Keima nolnabei kahin ahinlah ama hon mijou eitiuve. Kachonset lou vang'in kathoh gimna hi jendam thei ahipoi. 
Dang goleuva daichat vaina leh nuisatna thoh jing Job tobang mi aum kha dem? Neiseipeh tem uvin. 
Amapan kalhat khompi dingin thilse bolte alhengin aphat ho jouse migiloute toh amang khom e. 
Ipi dinga Pathen lunghol na a phat jouse namoh manchai ding ham ti chanin amapan aseije. 
Thilhet khen themna neihon ngaijuvin ka thusei hi, Pathen'in chonset abolpoi ti mijousen ahet ahin, hatchungnungpa chun thil dihlou aboltheipoi. 
Aman mihem hi atoh dungjui cheh a lethuhna apeh ji ahin, aman mihemte hi achonna dungjui a alethuh ji ahi. 
Tahbeh mongin Pathen'in thil dihlou abol louhel ding ahi. Hatchungnung pa chun thudoh chu aheimang louding ahi. 
Mihem khat touvin leiset pumpi hi athu noija akoi khah em? Leiset pumpi hi kitup chetna koipen in akoi ham? 
Pathen chun alhagao ana kinunglah khat tou hihen chuleh ahaina hu chu akinung lah hitaleh, 
Hinkho jouse kichai ding ahitan, chule mihingte jouse hi leivui soh kit ding ahitai. 
Tun miching hon ka thusei hi ngai uvin, ipi kasei lunglut tah in ngai uvin. 
Thudih deilouva Pathen'in vai ahom ding ham? Hatchungnungpa thutan hum o nachan ding ham? 
Ijeh inem itile aman leng ho jah a nang hi migilou nahin chuleh milen milal pa kom'ah nang hi langnei nahi tin aseije. 
Aman mihem chu itobangin oupe jongleh iman agelpon chuleh mivaichapa sangin mihausa pa chu akhohsah chompoi, aboncha ama khutsem ahi cheh e. 
Mitphet kah louvin athi theijin, jankim lai teng jongleh amangthah jiuvin, mithat hatpa jong chu mihem khat pang louvin akisumang jitai. 
Ijeh inem itile Pathen'in mihem tehi iti hin ham ti ave jingin, athilbolu amusohkeije. 
Migiloupa kiselna ding chun muthim jong ama mitmun kiselna din alhing joupoi. 
Pathen thutanna'a itih le idin diu ham ti eihon, gonsan ikoi pouve. 
Aman mihat pa chu koimacha dong louhel in asumang jin, chule ama munna chu adang khat atung doh ji'e. 
Aman ipi abol'u ahet ahin, chuleh jan tengleh alehkhup in chuleh asumang jitai. 
Aman agitlou jeh uvin ajeplhu jin mijouse mu thei ding leh ima jousen amu theidin abol ji'e. 
Ijeh inem itile, ama nung ajui nauva kon'in akihei mangun alampi jeng jong donlouvin akoi tauve. 
ama hon mivaichate chu aka sah un, akanau chu Pathen kom'a alhunge. Pathen'in mi gentheite kana ho ajahpeh jin ahi. 
Ahinlah ama chun thipbeh a um ding chu lheng jo tahen lang hileh kon ama chu themmo achan thei ding ham? Aman amai aselmang teng mikhat ham ahilou le namkhat hijeng jongleh koiman aholdoh joupoi. 
Mihemte thanga a o louna diuvin aman Pathen neiloute vaihomin apansah jipoi. 
Kachonse tai, ahin chonset bolkit taponge tia mihem ten ipi dinga Pathen kom'a aseikit jilou u ham? 
Ahiloule ipi thilse kabol kahe khapoi neisei peh in thilse kabol aum khah leh tutah hin ngai jenginge. 
Nathum najeh a chu Pathen'in athudih nachu asemphat ding ham? Ijeh inem itile nangin ama chu napaidoh sa ahin, akilhen ding chu nang nahin, kei kahipoi, sei jomin lang nachihna chu keiho kom'a phong doh in. 
Hicheng jou nungin milungval hon eiseipeh untin chuleh miching hon ka thusei ngai uvinte. 
Job in hetoh louva asei ahin, athucheng ho chu agilpoi. 
Job migilou thuseija nathusei jeh a akhonna leh nachung thu kitan ding nakikhol khom ahi. 
Ijeh inem itile nangin na chonset na chunga doumah nabol ben, nangin Pathen dounan lunghanna thu nasei lelen, jabolna nanei tapoi.
