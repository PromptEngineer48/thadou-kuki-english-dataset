La Bu.
2.
Ipi dinga chitin namtin kitohmoa, thilphachomlou ngaito na a aphat'u amoh man chatvai u hitam? 
Yahweh Pakaijin lengdinga athaonupa dounan leiset lengte akikhom un, chule vaihom thunei hon jong thulhuh asem un, hitin asei ui. 
“Ei-kihennauva kon ahin kilhamlhao hitin, Pathen Pathen thaneina'a kon'in kilha ong-u hite” atiuve. 
Hinlah Yahweh Pakaijin avan laltouna'a kon'in amaho chu ahin nuisat in, Yahweh Pakai a din nuijat umtah ahi tauve. 
Chuin Yahweh Pakaijin amaho chu lunghang tah in aphoh in, alunghan jah jeng chun amaho atijatsah tai. 
Chutah in “Keiman kalhendoh lengchu kalhang theng Zion molvum a katousah'e,” ati. 
Keiman thugahna chu phongdoh inge; Yahweh Pakaijin kaheng'ah hitin asei e, “Nangma hi kachapa nahi, Keiman tunia nangma hi kahin nahitai.” 
Kaheng'a nathum pouleh chitin namtin hi nagoulo dinga kapeh ding, vannoi leiset pumpi geija nagou kahisahding ahi. 
Nangman amaho hi thihjolla najep boh'a; belkhengpa bel kivoh chip banga ahal hal a nakoi ding ahi. 
Hijeh chun Vo leiset lengte kichih theiyun; Chule leiset thutan ho nang hon thuhil ngaiphaovin. 
Yahweh Pakai chu ging tah in akin bol un, chule kithing pum in Ama chu houvun. 
Yahweh Pakai lungsatna nachung uva achuh a namanthah masangun akengphang kolchah in chop un, ajeh chu Yahweh Pakai lungsat na neocha jong eihodia deojah ahi. Atahsan nau Yahweh Pakai a koi jing ho chu anunnom ahiuve.
