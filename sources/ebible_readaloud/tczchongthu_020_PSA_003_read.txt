La Bu.
3.
O Yahweh Pakai, idia eisulam vaiding mi pung jing hitam! eidou din mi tamtah akipandoh tauve. 
Ama kihuhdoh na hi Pathen Pathen a aumpoi tin kalungthim in mi tamtah in aseijui. Selah. 
Ahinlah Yahweh Pakai, nang hi eiveng tup, kalum'a kisoung nahi; Ka loupina le khodah ngam-a, gal jona eipeh jing nahi. 
Keima O sangtah in Yahweh Pakai henga kataovin, Aman alhang thenga kon'in eihin donbut tai. Selah. 
Keima kalum'in ka-imu jin; Yahweh Pakaijin eichin tup'a eihoidam jeh'in, kajing-thou kit tai. 
Keima doudin mihem sangsom sangjan ei umkimvel jongleh kichakit taponge. 
Hung thoudoh in O Yahweh Pakai; neihuhdoh'in, Pathen ka Pathen: Eidou hojouse khagu eivohchipeh in; nangma migilou ho hahemtah jong vopul soh ji nahi. 
Huh hingna hi Yahweh Pakai a kon bou ahi: Na phattheina jong namite chunga aum'e.
