La Bu.
4.
Kakou teng neidon but'in, them eichan sah a O Yahweh Pakai; ka lhahsam nikho teng jong le nangman lunglet neipe ji; neikhoton lang kataona neingai peh tei in. 
Vo mihem chapa te, nangho hin itihchanpi neisuh jum diu hitam? Itihchan ajehbei a nei thepmosah uva, itihchan pihi jouthu nachepi diu hitam? 
Ahin nang hon heuvin, Yahweh Pakaijin ama gingho Ama dingin atum'in alhengdoh'e. Yahweh Pakai kakou tengleh Aman ei-donbut teijin ahi. 
Ki sonlelnan kithing pumin umun, ahin chonset boldaovin; hichu nalupnao mun'a jong nalungthimu toh gelkhom jing uvin lang umdet jingun. 
Lungthengsel a kilhai naho boljingun chule na tahsan nao Yahweh Pakai a koi jingun. 
Hiho sanga phajo eimusah thei diu koi ham? tin mi tamtah in seiunte, Yahweh Pakai namaivah achun keiho neihinsu emsel un. 
Nangman lengpitwi leh changle mim nengchet a kilamdoh ho kipani sangin, kalungthim nakipasah tai. 
Keima Nanga dia lungmonga kalup a ka-ihmut ding ahi, Yahweh Pakai nei hoidoh inlang neivengtup in.
