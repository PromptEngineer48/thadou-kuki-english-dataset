La Bu.
6.
O Yahweh Pakai, nei lung han san loujen in! Lungsat namoljol a chun neikhoukhah hih helin. 
Neikhoto teijin lhasam le hatlou kahi Yahweh Pakai, neidam sah in kagu kachang jousen eichip ui. 
Kalhagao alengvai lheh jeng tai, O Yahweh Pakai itihchan pi hitia hi kaum ding hitam? 
Hungkilen O Yahweh Pakai, kalhagao huhdoh in. Nami ngailutna longlou jeh chun neihin huhdoh tei in. 
Ajeh chu thina ahin nangma geldoh na aumpoi: lhankhuh a konna jong koiham nahin thangvah ding? 
Ka ka kamao nan kachao lhatai; janseh a kakanan kajalkhun achin in, kaka na mitlhi hon kalupna jalkhun akotsah sohtai. 
Genthei a kaka nan kamit athimtan; kagal miten eisuh genthei jeh uvin apomhel jengtai. 
Gitlouna bol ho jouse kakom'a kon'in potdoh tauvin; Ajeh chu Yahweh Pakaijin kakana eijahpeh tai. 
Yahweh Pakaijin kangaichat a kathum na ho eihetpeh tan; kataona hi eidonbut ding ahitai. 
Kagal mite jouse kijumsou henlang kithing pumin um uhen: Jum le ja pumin gangtah in kinungle taohen.
