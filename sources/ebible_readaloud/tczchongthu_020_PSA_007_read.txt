La Bu.
7.
O Yahweh Pakai, Pathen ka Pathen eihoidam din nangma kahin belle. Eipedoh a pang hoa kon'in neihoidohsah'in, neihuhdoh'in. 
Achuti loule panpi ding bei a kaum a, keipi bahkai-jin mi abolset banga eiboset uva, ahal halla eibotel diu ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai, k a Pathen keiman thil suhkhel kaneija, thil dihlou kana tohdoh khah a, 
Lungmonga kachenkhom pi khat chonphatlouna kalethuh a, ajeh beija kagalmi kanchom khah a ahileh, 
Kagal mite khut-ah neipedoh jengin lang kahin na lajengu hen, ama hon tolla eichotpha jeng uhen chule kaloupi najouse bon lah a kaithang jeng-u hen. Selah 
O Yahweh Pakai, lunghan in hungthoudoh'in, Doumit melpu kagal mite jeh in nang hung kichoisang'in; Chule hung kipatdoh'in Pathen ka Pathen nathutan dihtah chu hinpolut in. 
Namasangah chitin namtin kikhom soh hen: Ama ho chunga namun dihtah chu hin kimusah kit in. 
Yahweh Pakai chu chitin namtin ho thu atan ahi: Kachondih naho nei phondoh peh tan, O Yahweh Pakai, ajeh chu keiman them mo na kanei poi O hatchungnung. 
Mi phalouho thil dihlou bolna kichai sah tan, chule michonpha ho chu domsangin. Ajeh chu nangman mihem lungthim gilsung ve Pathen dihtah chu nahi. 
Pathen ka Pathen hi kalum'a pang ahin, alungthim dih le akitah ho chu ahuhhing jin ahi. 
Pathen ka Pathen hi thutan dihtah ahin, niseh'in miphalou chunga alunghangjingin ahi. 
Mihemkhat chu akisih louva ahileh, Pathen ka Pathen'in achemjam kinol hem intin, athalpi kaosa akoijing ahi. 
Aman thina manchah gimneitah chu kigot intin athalpi meikou chu akapding ahi. 
Miphalou hon setna avop un, umhelna le thil phalou va agaiun, jouthu ho ahinpen doh sah jiu ve. 
ama hon mi lhahlutna dingin kotong thuhtah alaijiuvin, ahinlah amaho joh alhalut jiuvin ahi. 
Midia set na abol hou chu amaho angsung joh in achuhjin ahi. Michunga chudinga thilse agon houchu amaho luchunga joh achujin ahi. 
Keiman Yahweh Pakai chu akitahna jeh'in thangvah ing-ting, keiman Yahweh Pakai hatchungnung min chu ka thangvah ding ahi.
