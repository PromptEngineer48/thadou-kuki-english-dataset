La Bu.
10.
O Yahweh Pakai, idia gam lhatah a ding nahim? Genthei kathoh jiteng ibol'a kisel jia nahim? 
Migilouten noise tah in migentheite adel mangjiu vin, mi-o nadinga asem'u thang a chun amahojoh hin-o sah'in. 
Ajeh chu amhon thilselam a alung ngaichat hou akiletsah piuvin, mikilose ho apah chauvin, Yahweh Pakai chu ataitom-un ahi. 
Migilou te vang Pathen Pathen holdin akiletsah val un, alungthim pumpiu vin jong Pathen Pathen aumpoi atiuve. 
Ahinlah amaho hi abol-lam lam uvah alolhing'un ahi. Achung uva hunglhung ding eng-bolna ho jonghi amupha jipouve. Ama hon agal-mihou jouse chu ataitom-un ahi. 
ama hon, kachung uva thilse kiti chulouding ahi tin atahsan un; boina hoa konna a-itih a ongthol ding kahitauve atiuve. 
Akam-uvah sapsetna, joule nal, chule gihsalna adimset ui. Alei muh uva jong thilse le thil phalou jeng aum'e. 
ama hon khopam lam gamthip lah hoa mi achanglhi jiuvin, them-mona neilouho thading agojiuve. Amit un alhasam te umna aching lhi jingun ahi. 
Keipi bahkaiho bangin akisel'un lhasam le hatloute mat jeng dingin angahun ahi. Sachangho bangin tah-lelte chu len akhu khum un amandoh jitauve. 
Lhasam a bolsetna toh te chu asugep jiuvin; miphalouho thahatna noi a chun alhalut jitauve. 
Migilou ten Pathen Pathen'in eimupha pouve atiuve! Aman amit sing intin, “Eihon ibol uhi mu hihbeh inte” atiuve! 
Hung kipat tan O Yahweh Pakai, migiloute hi gotna petan, O Pathen Pathen! Hatlou lhasam te hin sumil hihbeh in. 
Migiloute Pathen Pathen sam se pum puma iti ongthol diu ham? ama hon Pathen Pathen'in eithemmosah pouvinte tin angai touve. 
Ahinlah Yahweh Pakai nangin athilse bol-houhi namusohkeije. Nangin hiche ho hi melchih inlang amaho hi gotna pen. Hatlou lhasam ten atahsan nao nanga akoi un, nulou palou te jong nangin vengbit in. 
Hiche migilou mi-engseho hi abanjang-u heh bongin! abon-uva na suhmang kahsen anung u deljingin. 
Yahweh Pakaihi atonsot tonsot leng ahi! Pathen Pathen neilou nam mite chu gamsung akon a mangthah ding ahiuve. 
Yahweh Pakai nangin tah-lelte kinepna hi nahetsa ahi, nangin thon louva akana-u najahpeh a nahin lhamon ding ahi. 
Nangin nulou palou te leh bolgentheija umho chungthu adih a natanpeh ding, chuteng athi thei mihem in amaho hi asuh lengvai theilou diu ahitai.
