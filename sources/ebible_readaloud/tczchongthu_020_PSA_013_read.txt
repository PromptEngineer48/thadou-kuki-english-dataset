La Bu.
13.
O Yahweh Pakai, phat itihchan geihi neisuhmil ding hitam? Itih chan keija kon'a nakiselmang ding nahim? 
Itihchan gei hiche hahsatna hi kathoh ding ham? Itih chan asun ajan'a lungkhamna dimset'a kaum ding ham? Itih chan kagal miten hanla eisap khum diu hitam? 
O Yahweh Pakai, Pathen ka Pathen hung kiheiyin, neihin donbut tan! Katha kile dohsah'in, neithisah hih'in. 
“Ama hi kajou tai!” tin kamelma te hi ka lhuh nin kipasah hihbeh in. 
Amavang nami ngailutna longlouvachu kingai jing kahi. Neihuhdoh jeh a keima kipa jing ding kahi. 
Keiman Yahweh Pakai vahchoi lan sang e ajeh chu Ama keidin thilpha tah eibol peh ati.
