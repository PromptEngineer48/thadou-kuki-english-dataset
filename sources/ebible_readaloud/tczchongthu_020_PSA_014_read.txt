La Bu.
14.
Mingol'in bou alungthim in “Pathen aumpoi” ati. Amaho hi lepche ngen ahiuvin, chule athilbol ujong adihlou jeng ahin, khatcha jong thilpha bol aum pouve. 
Yahweh Pakaijin vanna kon'in lei mihemte jouse ahinven, mikhattou aching tahbeh um-em, chule Pathen Pathen ngaicha'a hol-a um-em atin ahi. 
Hinlah aumpoi, abon'un aki-heimang gam tauve, abonchauva lepche ngen ahiuvin, koimacha apha aumpoi khatcha jong aumpoi. 
Setna bolten hetna anei lou u hintem? Amaho hin kamite changlhah bangin aneuvin, Yahweh Pakai komin la taoding agelpouve. 
Amaho hi tijatnan alodim diuahi, ajeh chu Yahweh Pakaijin athua nung ho aumpin ahi. 
Migilou ten bolgentheija umho tohgon asuhsetpeh jiuvin, ahin Yahweh Pakaijin amite ahuhdoh jingin ahi. 
Zion molsang a kon a Israel huhdoh dinga koi hung ding ham? Yahweh Pakaijin amite aki-ledohsah kit teng, Jacob in kipana asap phon a, Israel in Pathen Pathen athangvah ding ahi.
