La Bu.
16.
Neihoibitnin O Pathen Pathen: ajeh chu nangahi kinem jing kahi. 
O Kalhagao, nangman Yahweh Pakai kom'a Nanghi ka Pakai nahi nati, thilpha kaneijouse hi nanga kon bou ahi; 
Leiset a Pathen Pathen lhentumte hi pachat umtah ahiuvin, amaho hi keidinga kagal lamkai le kakipa thanop napen ahiuve. 
Pathen dang ho nung jui a delle ho chu alung khamnao kibejing ding ahi. Amaho thisan a kilhaina ganthat a pang ka hilou ding, a pathen nu jong kahoupeh loudiu ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai hi ka chan ding gou le ka phatthei channa khon chu ahi. Yahweh Pakaijin ka ijakai eichintup peh e. 
Nangin neipeh gam jonghi gamphapen ahi, itobanga goupha kidang neipeh hitam! 
Ijakai eihilla ka Yahweh Pakai chu keiman thangvah inge; janteng jong le avel in eigeldoh sah jin eihil jie. 
Yahweh Pakaijin eiumpi danhi keiman hetna kanei jinge. Ama hi kakom'a naichakhatna um ahi ti kahen hijeh chun keima lungthomhao ponge. 
Hijeh hin keima kalung amongsellin chuleh kipanan kadimmin Pathen Pathen ka thangvah e. 
Ajeh chu nangin thina mun a neidalha pon natin namitheng te jong lhankhuh a lhahlutsah pon nate. 
Nangin hinnalampi chu neimusah'in natin, neiumpi jal a kipana le nei umpina tonsot nopna chu nei pen nate.
