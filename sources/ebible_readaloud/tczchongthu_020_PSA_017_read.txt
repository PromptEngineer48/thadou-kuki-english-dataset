La Bu.
17.
O Yahweh Pakai adih thumna a kaka ogin hi nei jahpeh in, katao nahi nei ngaipeh in, lungthengsel le dihtah katao ahi. 
Kathem chan na neiphondoh peh'in, ajeh chu nangin thudih a natong namuchet ahi. 
Nangin kalunggel ho napatep jin, janteng jongleh ka lungsung navelhaji. Nangin kapum changin neivechillin kathepmo na namupoi; ka kamsung jenga jong set na potlou ding ahitai. 
Keiman nathupeh Dan dungjuiin, migilou ho le mi-engseho ho khonung kajui ji poi. 
Kakalson dan hin nalampi kajui jingin nangma lampia kon'in kapotdoh pon ahi. 
Keima nangma kom'a taova kahi ajeh chu O Pathen Pathen nangin neisanpeh tei ding kahei. Kataona hi lunglut tah in neingaipeh in. 
Nami ngailutna longlou chu kidangtah in neimusah'in, agal mitea konna hoidohna ngaichaho chu nathahatna gimneitah chun nahuhdoh ji e. 
Nangman namit teni na chintup jing bangin keima jong nei vesuijin. Nalhaving limnoija chun neisellin. 
Kavel a eidoujing migilouho a kon'in eivengtup in, eithatgo kamelma hoa kon'in nei huhdoh in. 
Amaho hi mi khotona bei ahiuvin akiletsah nao thucheng chu ngaitan! 
ama hon eihin juilutnun eiumkimvel uvin tola selhuh dingin agomkom'ahol un ahi. 
Aneh ding changlhih le keipi bahkai hangtah bang le keipinou vin gamthip lai matding avetlhih le tobang ahiuve. 
Hung kipat tan O Yahweh Pakai kidintepin lang seplhu jengin! Na chemjammin migilouhoa kon'in neihuhdoh in. 
O Yahweh Pakai naban thathatnin vannoia ama phatchomna ngaito ho hi sumangin. Amavang nangma goulu ho hi nangman vah va jingin. Achateu neijong suhnengpeh inlang ason apahteu goulo ding geija adalhah peh theina diuvin gontoh peh'in. 
Monabeija hinkho Kaman jeh a namel kamu tei ding ahi. Kathodoh tengleh nangma toh kimaitoa ikimuto ding chule kalungnachim tante.
