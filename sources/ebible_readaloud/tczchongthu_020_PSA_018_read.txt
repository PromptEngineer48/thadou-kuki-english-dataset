La Bu.
18.
Keiman nangma kangailui Yahweh Pakai; Nangma hi kathahatna nahi. 
Yahweh Pakai hi kasongpi, kakulpi, chuleh eihuhhingpu ahin, Pathen ka Pathen hi kasongpi ahin eiveng tup jing ahi. Ama hi kalumdal, eihuhhinga thahat chu ahin, chuleh kahoidohna munpi chu ahi. 
Keiman thangvah dinga lomtah Yahweh Pakai chu kakouvin, aman kamelma tea kon'in eihuhdoh'in ahi. 
Thina khoisatna chun eiumkimvel tan, manthahna twipi chun eichup tan ahi. 
Damun lungkhamna chun eiumkimvelin, kalampi a thinan thang akam e. 
Ka lungkham val phatnin Yahweh Pakai chu kakouvin, Pathen ka Pathen kom'a chun panpi ngaichan kataovin Aman aHou-In thenga kon'in kataona asangin ahi. 
Hi jou chun leiset akilingin akihot e. Molbul ho jeng jong kihot e ajeh chu Pathen Pathen lunghang a ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai nah hom a meikhu ahung potdoh in, meikou gimneitah akam a kon'in ahungkoudoh'in chuleh Mei-am al lenglung Ama-a kon'in ahung koudoh'in ahi. 
Aman vanho chu ahongdoh'in meilom thimletlut chu ahin chotphan ahung kumsuh in, 
Yahweh Pakai chu vantil pipui khat chunga atouvin, huipi chu alhavingin amangin alengin ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai chu muthim in ahung kitom in, meilom twi jingletlut pumin ahunglhungin ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai vah'in ahintom chu meilom satah in ahinkhu khum in gelchang le mei-am chu gojuh'in ahung julhan ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai chu vanna kon'in ahung kisen in hatchungnung ogin chu gelchang leh mei-am koulah achun akithongin ahi. 
Aman athalpi ahin kapmin agalmiho chu athe cheh in, avah em lahlah chun amaho chu asuboi gamtan ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai nangin nagalmi ho napho a lunghangtah a nakisen chun twikhanglen ato geijin akikangin, leiset bulpi jouse jong akeuvin aumjie. 
Aman vanna kon'in akhut ahin lhangin eihin kaidoh'in, twipi thuh lah a kon'in jong eikaidoh'in ahi. 
Aman kagalmi hattah tah ho a kon'in eihuhdoh in, kasang a hatjo ka dougal hoa kon'in eihuhdoh'in ahi. 
Hahsatna kato pettah in ama hon eihin delkhum un ahinlah Yahweh Pakaijin eipanpin ahi. 
Aman kahoidohna mun nah eipuilut in eihuhdoh tai ajeh chu ama kipana kahi. 
Yahweh Pakaijin thildih kaboljeh in kipaman eipen mona kaneilou jeh'in eikiledohsah tai. 
Keiman Yahweh Pakai lampi kajui in, thilse lam jon'in Pathen Pathen'a kon'in kakiheimang poi. 
Keiman Yahweh Pakai chonphatna lampi kajui jingin, a Dan thupeh ho kapaidoh khapoi. 
Pathen Pathen masangah nolnabei kahin, chonsetna kon'in kaki kangse jing e. 
Yahweh Pakaijin thildih kaboljeh in Aman kipaman eipejin ahi. Aman themmonabei kahi amui. 
Mikitah kom'ah nangmatah jong nakitah in, monabei ho kom'ah monabei nahi. 
Milungtheng ho kom'ah nalungtheng in ahinlah miphalouho kom'ah nakhohjin ahi. 
Nangin mikineosah ho nahuhdohjin ahinla mikiletsah ho nasujumjin ahi. 
Nangin thaomei neidetpeh'in, Yahweh Pakai, Pathen ka Pathen'in kamuthim jong neisalvah peh e. 
Nangma hatna chun kagalmi hi neisuhchip peh'in Pathen ka Pathen toh kapankhom poupou leh itobang bang sang hijongleh kakal ngapchan ahi. 
Pathen Pathen lampi chu nolnabei ahi! Yahweh Pakai kitepna jouse alolhing jinge. Ama hi ahuhdoh ding ngaicha jouse dinga lum le pho ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai tailou pathen dang um'am? Pathen Pathen tailou songpi det dang um'am? 
Hiche Pathen Pathen hin eihatdoh sah'in kalampi jong hoibitnapen ahisah ahi. 
Aman molsang hahsa chung dunga kadindet theina din kakeng jong sakhi keng bangin atah sah e. 
Aman kakhut teni jong galsatna a manthei dingin agongin, kabanjang jong sum-eng thalpi loithei dingin ahatsah'in ahi. 
Nangman nagaljona lum chu neipetan nabanjetnin neipanhun ahi. Neikithopina hin eithupi sah'in ahi. 
Nangman kakeng phang tenin alhonal louna dingin kalampi neikehlet peh'in ahi. 
Keiman kagal mite kadel'in kaman'in ahi; amaho hi kajo kahsen katangpon ahi. 
Amaho hi ahung ki thodoh jou louna diuvin kasat lhun kakeng noijah akijam'un ahi. 
Nangin kagalsatna dingin thahatnan neivon in; Nangman kagal mite chu kakengphang noi a neikoipeh e. 
Nangin angongchang'u neichotpha sah'in eivetda ho jouse chu kasumang sohtan ahi. 
ama hon panpi athum'un koiman ahung huhdoh pouve. Ama hon Yahweh Pakai akouvun ahinla Aman adonbut pon ahi. 
Keiman amaho chu huilah a vutvai kithethang bangin tol ah kagoibong sel'in, bonlhoh lah a kasunat gamatai. 
Nangin eihouse jing ho chungah galjona neipetan ahi. Nangin namtin vaipi chungah vaihom'in neipansah tan ahi. Kanahet phahlou miho jeng jong kalhachan ahungpang tauvin ahi. 
Kamin ajah jah'un kanoijah alut un gamchommi hojeng jong ka-anga akisunem gamtauvin ahi. 
Hansanna aneijou tapouvin kithing pumpum'in ka-ang ahinbel tauvin ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai ahing jinge, kasongpia apanjeh'in ka thangvah e! Ka sochatna Pathen ka Pathen chu loupi jing tahen! 
Ama hi eibolse ho lethuh jinga Pathen ka Pathen chu ahin, aman namtin vaipi hi eisuhnem peh'in kanoijah eikoipeh tai. 
Chuleh kagal mitea kon'in eihuhdoh'in nangman kagal miten aphah joulouna munnah neikoijin, eidouva mi-engse hoa kon'in neihoidohsah tan ahi. 
Hiche jeh hin O Yahweh Pakai namtin vaipi lah a nathangvah ingkate, na min vahchoinan la sang nge. 
Nangin leng a natun pa din goljona loupitah chu napetai; Chuleh nathaonupa David leh achilhah te chunga nalungset longlou namusah e.
