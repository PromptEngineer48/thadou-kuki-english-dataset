La Bu.
19.
Vanho'n Pathen Pathen loupina aphongdoh un ahi. Vanthamjol hon jong akhut thepna atahlangui. 
Anitin in aphongdoh jing jengun; jansim janseh le ama chu aseiphong jingui. 
Ocheng soh lou vin thu aseiyun; a ocheng'u hi akijakha ngaipoi. 
Ahinlah athu chun vainoi alodim in, thucheng jong chun vannoi leiset athechansoh in ahi. Vanthamjol hoa Pathen Pathen'in nisa chenna ding asem peh e. 
Hihchu kichen jouva moulang emsel a ahung potdoh bangin avahchan jengin ahi. Kisongtah a lhaitet golseh a pango banginhaltah le kipah tah in aum'e. 
Vanmong langkhatna kon'in nisa ahung sohdoh jin ajotna ding vanmong lang khat gei in ajui peh jie. Hiche asat a kon chun imacha ahoidoh ji poi. 
Yahweh Pakai thuhil ho hi achamkim in, lhagaova asuhhal jingin ahi. Yahweh Pakai Dan thu ho hi tahsanthei tah ahin milham jong chihsah jengthei ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai thupeh ho hi adih'in, lungthim kipasah ji e. Yahweh Pakai thupeh ho hi akicheh in lungsunga hetthem theina eipeuvin ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai ginle jana neihi athengin ahi, hiche hi tonsotna ding ahitai. Yahweh Pakai thutan hi adih'e; a etih a dih jing jeng ahi. 
Hiche ho chu sana sanga deiumjo ahin, sana thengpen sanga jong deiumjo ahi. Khoiju sangin jong alhumjon, akhoiphang dunga kilhoh lhatil sangin jong alhumjoi. 
Hiche ho hi nasohpa dinga gihsal na ahin, nathu nung hoa dia kipana chanvou lentah ahi. 
Kalungsunga chonset kisel guh jouse hi itikahetdoh jouding ham? Hitobanga kiselgua setna hoa konhin neisilthengin. 
Nasoh hi henasasa chonset bolna a kon'in neihuhdoh'in! Hiche ho hi kachunga vaihomsah hihbeh'in, chuteng keima chonsetna a konna kahoidoh ding ahi. 
Kakam a kon'a hung potdoh thucheng ho leh ka lungsunga kalunggel ho nalunglhaina hihen, O Yahweh Pakai kasongpi leh eihuhhingpu.
