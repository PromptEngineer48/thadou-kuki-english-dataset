La Bu.
20.
Na hahsat nikhon Yahweh Pakaijin nakana taona nadonbut hen. Jacob Pathen Pathen min chun thilse jousea kon'in nahoidoh hen. 
Aman aHou-Intheng a kon'in kithopina nahin pejing henlang Jerusalem a kon'in nahin hatsah jinghen. 
Pathen Pathen'in namaicham thilto ho hingeldoh jinghen lang chuleh napumgo thiltoho jong khotona in nahin vetpeh jinghen. 
Pathen Pathen'in nalunga nangaichat nape henlang nathilgon jouse nalolhinsah sohkeihen. 
Nagaljona thu kajah tengu leh kipah'a kasapdiu ahi, chuleh Pathen ka Pathen u minna galjona ponlop kadopsangdiu ahi. Yahweh Pakaijin natao na jouse donbut jing hen. 
Tun keiman athaonu lengpa chu Yahweh Pakaijin ahuhdohji ti ka hetai. Aman avan mun thenga kon a ahin donbut ding, chuleh athahatna nasatah a chu ahuhdoh ding ahi. 
Namtin vaipi abankhat chun a sakol u leh sakol kangtalai hou akiletsah piuvin, ahin eihonvang Yahweh Pakai, eihon Pathen Pathen u minbou ikiletsahpi diu ahi. 
Namtin vaipi ho hi hunglhu'u vintin chip lhadiu ahi, ahinla eihovang idindoh diu chule dehtah a idin diu ahi. 
Kalengpau chu galjona pe'n, O Yahweh Pakai! Panpi ngai a kakana neidonbut tei in.
