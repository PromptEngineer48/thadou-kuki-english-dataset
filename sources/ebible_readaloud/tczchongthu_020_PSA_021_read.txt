La Bu.
21.
Nathahatna ahi lengpa iti kipah hitam O Yahweh Pakai! Galjona napehjeh'in aman kipah'in kho asam e. 
Ajeh chu nangin alungthim'a angaichan napen aman athumho khatcha jong nadapeh poi. 
Nangin lolhin na leh houtheinan nalamto jin, aluchungah sana hoipen lallukhuh nakhuhsah'in ahi. 
Aman ahinkho nahoitup peh din athumjin, chule nangin ataona nasanpeh jie. Ahin nikho jong tonsot a din nasuh saopeh ji e. 
Nagaljona chun jabolna lentah achansah in, thupina le loupinan najemhoijin ahi. 
Nangin tonsot malsomnan naphungvuh in chuleh nang toh umkhom a nopna kipana achun naphung vuh e. 
Ajeh chu lengpa'n Yahweh Pakai atahsan e. hatchungnung ngailutna longlou chun alhuhna dinga kon'in na hoidoh sah jingin ahi. 
Nangin nagalmiho chu namattup sohkei ding, nangma nadouho jouse chu thahat tah na banjetna chu na mattup ding ahi. 
Nangma nahung teng amaho chu meikong lah a naselut ding ahi. Yahweh Pakaijin alunghanna a asuhgam ding meikonga ahalgam ding ahi. 
Achateu leiset chunga kon a nathaimang ding; ama hon sonlepah aneikitlou diu ahi. 
ama hon nangdounan thil gong jonglehu, athilse gonhou chu lolhing louding ahi. 
Ajeh chu ama hon nathalchang amaho kapna dinga nadoi chu amutengu leh kiheimang untin jammang diu ahitai. 
O Yahweh Pakai na thahat na nasatah a chun hungthou doh tan. Tumgin le lasah to thoa nahat na natoh chu kakipa pi diu ahi.
