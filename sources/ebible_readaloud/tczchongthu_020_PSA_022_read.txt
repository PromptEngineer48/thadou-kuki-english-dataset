La Bu.
22.
Ka Pathen Pathen, ka Pathen idia neidalhah hitam? Panpi ngai a kamao teng idia gamla tah a um ji nahim? 
Niseh in nangma kakoujie, Pathen ka Pathen, hinlah nangin nei donbut poi. Janseh jongleh kakou ogin naja jin, hinlah lungmon na kanei jipoi. 
Israel'in athangvah jing laltouna-a tou nangmahi Atheng nahi. 
Kapu kapa teuvin nangma na tahsan un, nangman amaho nahuh hing tai. 
Naheng a akap un huhhing in a umtauve. Ama hon nangma na tahsan un nangman amaho nasujum poi. 
Hinlah keima hi mihem a dia lhing joulou lungtol bang bep kahi. Mijouse dia thet umle musit umchu kahitai! 
Eimu jousen eihouseovin, aleiju adosah un, alu apeijun asei uve, 
“Ama pa hi Pathen Pathen a ki ngai pa chu hilou ham? Achuti le a Pathen Pathen chu kihuhdoh sah hen! Yahweh Pakaijin ama hi angailut a ahileh aYahweh Pakaijin huhdoh hen!” 
Nangin kanu naobusunga kon'in neina hoidamin nei pui galkai in, kanu a-anglum laija patna nangma tahsanje neina hil ahitai. 
Kahung pen a patna nangma ban thahatna a eikipelut a ahitai. Kahung penlhah a patna ka Pathen chu nangma nahi jing e. 
Keija kon'in gamla loujen in ajeh chu boina ahungnai in, koima dang eikithopi thei aumpoi. 
Bongchal ho'n aum kimvel bangin kagal miten eihin um kimvel tauvin, Bashan bongchal khohlai hon eium kimvel tauve! 
Hang tah le kisong tah Keipi bahkai in aneh ding aboltel bangin keima val lhum dingin akamkau ve. 
Kahinkho hi twi banga kisunglha ahitai, kagu kachang ho jong akilhep gamtan, kalungchang jong khoilu bangin ajunlha gamtai. 
Nisan asah gep leilhang bangin katha akanghel jengtai. Kaleijong kadang chungah akikamat tai. Nangin vutvai lah a neijam in athidin nei dalhatan ahi. 
Kagal miten uichahon bangin eium kimvel tauvin. Migilou honkhat in eium kimvel tauvin ahi. 
Kagu kachang ho jong simthei ahisoh tan. Kagal miten eiveovin eivesalteh jengun ahi. 
Kavon ho jong vang asan un akihom gamtauvin ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai keija kon'in umgamla hihbehin! Nangma kathahatna nahi neihung kithopi loijin. 
Na chemjam chun neihuhdoh in, kahinkho manlutah hi hiche uicha hoa kon'in neihuhdoh peh in. 
Keipi bahkai kam a kon'in neiloidoh peh in lang hiche gambongchal hang kiteni ki a kon'in neihuhdoh in. 
Kasopi pasal ho leh numeiho kom'a naminhi phongjal leng katin, Houbung kikhom holah a nangma ka thangvah ding ahi. 
Ama gingho jousen Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah hen! Jacob chilhah te jousen ama chu ging uvin! Israel chate jousen ama chu jana peohen! 
Ajeh chu aman migentheite thohgimna jouse chu anungsun pon, panpi ngaichatna a taona neiho angaipeh in ahi. 
Keiman Houbung kikhopna lah a nangma kathang vah ding ahi. Nangma chibai boh ho angsunga kakitepna kasuh bulhit ding ahi. 
Mivaichaten neovintin vaset diu ahi. Yahweh Pakai holte jousen ama chu athangvah diu ahi. Alung thim u tonsotna kipah jingding ahiuve. 
Vannoi leiset miten Yahweh Pakai chu ahin hetdoh diu chuleh akom'a hung kilediu ahi. Chutengleh namtin vaipi insung jousen a anga hungkun cheh diu ahi. 
Ajeh chu leng thaneina chu Yahweh Pakai ama a bou ahi. Aman namtin vaipi chunga vai ahom ahi. 
Leiset chunga mihaohon ankong sem untin chibai aboh diu ahi. Ahinkho kichai a leivui soh ding athi thei mihem jouse a anga kun cheh hen. 
Ichate jouseuvin jong Ama kin ahinbol diu ahi. Ahunglhung ding khang miten jong Yahweh Pakai kidanje chu ahin jahpeh diu ahi. 
Tua penglou laiho kom'a jong thudih a anatohnaho jouse hung kiseipeh ding ahi. Ama hon athilbol natoh naho jouse ahin jahdiu ahi.
