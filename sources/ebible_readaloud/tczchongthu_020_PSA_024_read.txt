Ajeh chu Aman leiset khombul hi twikhanglen chunga atun ahin, twipi thuhlah a aphudet ahi. 
Koiham Yahweh Pakai moul chunga kaldoh jouding? Koiham amunthenga dingthei ding? 
Akhut le Alungthim thengho bou, chuleh milimsemthu houlou le jou seilouho bou hidiu ahi. 
ama hon bou Yahweh Pakai malsomna amudiu ahin, chuleh ahuh hingpao Pathen Pathen toh ki guijop na dihtah anei theidiu ahi. 
Hitobang miho hinbou nangma naholthei diu chuleh Jacob Pathen Pathen nangma nachibai theidiu ahi. 
Khanglui kelkot ho kihonglen un! Khanglui kotpiho kihong len un lang leng loupi chu lutsah un. 
Koiham lengloupi chu? Yahweh Pakai hattah leh thupi tah, Yahweh Pakai koiman ajojou lou galhattah chu ahi.

Koiham lengloupi chu? Van Pathen Pathen sepai, Ama hi leng loupi chu ahi.
