La Bu.
26.
O Yahweh Pakai themmona bei kahihi phongdoh tan, ajeh chu keiman kitah na neitah in hinkho kamangin nungchonlouhel in Yahweh Pakai a tahsan na dettah kaneije. 
Yahweh Pakai neipatep inlang neikhol chil in, kachondan leh kalungthim hi vechen mongin! 
Ajeh chu nei ngailutna longlouhi keiman kahejing e, chuleh keiman nathudih dungjuijin hinkho kamange. 
Keima mijouho tojong kakivop khompon, miphalhem ho deilam jong kachepipon ahi. 
Keiman migiloute kikhop na kathet in thil phalou ho jong kanuse ji e. 
Themmona kaneilou vetsahna in kakhut kasil in namaicham kahin jon e O Yahweh Pakai, 
Thangvah la sapum in nakidan na ho kaphong doh e. 
Na Hou-In theng kangailui, Yahweh Pakai, naum pi na loipi tah kilan doh na mun chu. 
Michonse ho thoh ding neithoh sah hih in, tolthat hoto kibanginneithep mo sah hih in. 
Set bolnom nan akhutnu asuboh un, chuleh ama hon phatseh in nehguh akilah un ahi. 
Keiman hitobang hinkho chu kamangpon, kitahna hinkhoa kading jinge. Hijeh chun neilhat doh inlang nami hepina chun neipuijin. 
Tun keima tolgo lai a dettah in kadinge chuleh keiman lhangphong tah a Yahweh Pakai kathang vah ding ahi.
