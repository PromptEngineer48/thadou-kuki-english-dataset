La Bu.
27.
Yahweh Pakaihi keidin vah leh huhhingna ahi. Chuti ahileh idia kakichat ding ham? Yahweh Pakaihi kakulpi, hahsatna a eihuhdoh pa ahi. Chuti ahileh ken idia kakithin ding ham? 
Migilou ten suhgamdia eihindel khum uva, kagal mite leh eidou teuvin eihin nokhumtengu leh kipal lhuvin tin chuleh lhugam diu ahi. 
Galmi tamtah in eium kimvel jeng jongleh kalung linglao ponte, ijemtin thagam dingin eihin delkhum jongleho Pathen ka Pathen a lung mongtah a kaum ding ahi. 
Hiche thil khat Yahweh Pakai henga kathum kadei pipen chu kahinkho lhumkeija Yahweh Pakaijin na um ding, Yahweh Pakai lolhinna a chu lunglhaisel a um a aHou-In na aphatnaho geljinga hinkho man ding hi ahi. 
Ajeh chu aman hahsatna a konna eihoidoh sahding a Hou-In thenga chu eihoibit ding Aman mi maiphah louva songpi chunga eiselding ahi. 
Ajeh chu boina hon eihin lonkhum jong le Aman eihoi dam ding ahi. Ama Hou-Intheng sunga kipah le thanom tah a pumgo thilto kabolding semjang saipum a Yahweh Pakai thangvah la kasah ding ahi. 
Kahung taove neingai peh in O Yahweh Pakai. Namikhoto na pum in neidonbut in. 
“Hungin lang neihoulim pin” nati chu kalungthim in ajan hitin adonbutne, “Yahweh Pakai kahunge” 
Yahweh Pakai neinung ngat hihbeh in. Na lunghan nan nasoh hi paidoh hihbeh in. Nangmahi eikithopi phapen chu nahijenge. Tun neipaidoh hih in; neidalha hih in, O Pathen Pathen ka huhing pu. 
Kanu le kapan eipaidoh jeng jongleh Yahweh Pakaijin eikolchah jing ding ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai kahinkho man ding Dan neihil in lamdih jenga neipuiin, ajeh chu kagal miten eichang lhijingun ahi. 
Amaho khut a neilhah lutsah hih in, ajeh chu ama hon kabol louhel thil a themo eichan un, phatin in kachungah thilse abolgo jingun ahi. 
Ahijeng vangin, hiche mihing gam a kaum laisen Yahweh Pakai phatna kamu jing jeng ding kakinem e. 
Yahweh Pakai chu lungneng tah in ngah jingin, Hanginlang hatnin, Henge, Yahweh Pakai chu lungneng tah in ngah jingin.
