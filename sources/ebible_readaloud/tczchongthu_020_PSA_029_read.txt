La Bu.
29.
Nangho vanmi hon Yahweh Pakaichu jana peuvin; Aloupi na le athahatna jeh in Yahweh Pakaichu jana pecheh un. 
Amin loupinajeh in Yahweh Pakai chu jabolcheh un. Athenna thupitah jehchun Yahweh Pakaichu chibai bohun. 
Twipi chungah Yahweh Pakai ogin athong doh e. Vanthamjol ginbanginPathen Pathen loupina akithonge. Yahweh Pakaihi twikhanglen chungah akithonge. 
Yahweh Pakai ogin hi thaneitah ahi, Yahweh Pakai ogin hi thupitah ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai Ogin hin Cedar thing thupitah ho jong aban tumboh gam e; Yahweh Pakai thupinan Lebanon Cedar thingho jong asubong soh gamtai. 
Aman Lebanon moljeng jong bongnou bangin akichop sah e, Aman Hermon moljeng jong gambong bangin akichop sah e. 
Yahweh Pakai oginhi kolphe meivah gimneitah banga ginga ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai Ogin chun gamthip gamgolai jeng jong akilinsah e; Kadesh gamgolai jong Pathen Pathen'in akilin sah e. 
Yahweh Pakai Ogin hin gangpi phung phatah ho jong ahot ling in, gammang jong asuchai hel e. AHou-In theng sungah mijousen “Aloupi” tin asam uve. 
Yahweh Pakai chu twipi chunga chun atouvin vai ahom e. 
Yahweh Pakaijin amite thahat apen, Yahweh Pakaijin amite chamna apen phatthei aboh'e.
