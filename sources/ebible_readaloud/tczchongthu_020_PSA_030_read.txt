La Bu.
30.
Yahweh Pakai nangin neihuhdoh tahjeh in kahin thangvah e. Nangin kagal mite kachunga nakithang-at sahpon ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai, Pathen ka Pathen panpi ngaichan kahin kouvin nangman ka tahsa nakiledoh sah tai. 
O Yahweh Pakai nangin lhankhuh a kon'in neihin puidoh in, thina kokhuh a ka lhahlut na dinga kon'in jong neihoidoh sah e. 
Nangho Pathen Pathen mite jousen Yahweh Pakai thangvahna lasaovin! Amintheng chu choi an'un. 
Ajeh chu alunghan hi phat chomcha bou ahijin, ami hepina vang hinkhoa ding ahi. Ka na nikho ho jong kipanin ajui pai jitai. 
Keiman ka phatthei nikhon, “Imachan eisutang jouponte!” kanati. 
O Yahweh Pakai nami hepinahin muol bangin eisudettai. Keija kon a nakiheimang jitengleh keima kading jou jipoi. 
O Yahweh Pakai nahenga kahung tao ahi. Nami hepina chu kathum in hitin kaseije, 
“Keima kathi alhankhuh a ka lhalut teng nangdinga ipi phachom dingham? Katahsa leivui in nang navah choi thei dingham? Nakitah na thu chu hiche hin aphondoh thei ding ham? 
Neihin ngaipeh in Yahweh Pakai neihin khoto in. O Yahweh Pakai neihin kithopin” 
Nangin kalung khamnahi kipah tah a lam nan neiheidoh pehtai. Nangin kalung khamna silpon neilahdoh pehin kipana silpon joh neisilpehtan ahi. 
Hitia chu thipsan louva thangvahna la kasah jing theina dingin. O Pakai ka Pathen, keiman nangma tonsot in thangvah jing tange.
