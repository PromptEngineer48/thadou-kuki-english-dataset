La Bu.
31.
O Yahweh Pakai neihuhdoh ding ngaichan nakom kahin bel e; Jumna neimusah hihbehin. Neihuhdohin ajeh chu nangma chondihtah Pathen Pathen nahi. 
Kataona najah nadin neingai peh tei in; neihung huhdoh loi in. Kakiselna songpin pang jinginlang, kahoidohna kulpi hijingin. 
Nangmahi kasongpile kakulpi nahi namin loupina dingin kahahsatna a kon'in neihuhdoh in. 
Kadougal hon kalhuhna dinga thang eikampeh naova kon'in neihuhdoh in, ajeh chu kahoidoh nahi nangma a bou aum'e. 
Kalhagao nangma khut a ka pedoh tai. Neihuhdoh in Yahweh Pakai ajeh chu nangmahi kitah jing Pathen Pathen nahi. 
Phatchom nabei semthu pathen hou hokhu keiman kathet ahi. Kenvang Yahweh Pakai katahsan jing e. 
Nami ngailutna longlou jeh in keima kipah thanom jinginge, ajeh chu nangin kaboina ho neihet peh jingin chuleh kalung gimna jouse nangin neihet thempeh jingin ahi. 
Nangin kadougal ho khutnah neipelutpon, lungmon na munjoh a neikoi jin ahi. 
Neikhoton Yahweh Pakai ajeh chu keima hahsatna a um kahin kamitlhin kamit akhutan ahi. Kahinna le katahsa abeipaiding ahitai. 
Lungkham a thipaiding kahitai, gentheinan kahinkho asuhchom tai. Chonset in kajaitha anem sah in asung langa beipai ding kahitai. 
Kamelma ten eisamseovin, kaheng kakom ten eitheuvin ahi. Kagol kapaite jeng jongin eihin naingam tapouvin ahi. Lampi dunga eimu jongleh ajammang gamji tauve. 
Keima mithisa bang leh bel kehsa bangin notthap in kaumtai. 
Keima chung changhi thujoungen eikisei khum in tijatnan eium kimvel tan, kadougal ten keidounan thilse agong un kahinkho hi suhmang ding agouve. 
Ahinlah Yahweh Pakai keima nanga kingai jing kahin, “Nangma Pathen ka Pathen nahi!” kati. 
Khonung kakhankho hi nangkhut a um ahin hitia tanglouva eidelle jinghoa kon'in neihuhdoh in. 
Nami khotona chun nasohpa hi hinsalvah in, nami ngailutna longlou chun neihin huhdoh tan. 
O Yahweh Pakai neijumso hihbeh in, ajeh chu panpi ngaichan nangma kakou jinge, miphalou ho chu jumsah jon, amaho chu alhankhuh uvah thipsan sahtan. 
Jouthu pot na alei u suthip in, ami noise nao le Pathen Pathen mite a houset nao leichu suthip jengin. 
Vo Yahweh Pakai nangma gingte dingin nangin ijakai nasuh nengpeh hi iti phat apha hitam. Panpi ngaicha a nakom'a hungte nangman naphat thei in, vannoi mite mitmu changtah in nangman phatthei nabohjin ahi. 
Nangin amaho chu suhsetding gohoa kon'in na angsunga nahoi bitjin, chuleh ahousetnao leile danga kon'in jong na angsung'ah nahoibit jinge. 
Yahweh Pakai chu thangvahin umhen, ajeh chu Aman angailutna longlou kidangtah chu eimusah jingin, kachen na khopi gal akinokhum a kon'in jong eihoidoh sahtan ahi. 
Lunglinglao pum in keima kapengjah in, “Yahweh Pakai toh eiki sukhentai!” kati. Hinla Yahweh Pakai nangin nei kho to dia ka ka na neijahpeh in panpi ngaicha a kakouna neidonbut tai. 
Nangho Pathen Pathen mite ho Yahweh Pakai chu ngailu vin! Ajeh chu Yahweh Pakaijin ama a kingai jingho chu ahuhdoh jingin ahi. Ahinlah mi noiseho chu engbolna nasatah apejin ahi. 
Hijeh chun Yahweh Pakaiya kinepna neiho jouse hatnunlang hangun!
