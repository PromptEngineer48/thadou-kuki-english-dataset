La Bu.
32.
Oh, asei ngailou nahou ki angaidam a achon set nahou kisuhmang peh ho iti thanop um hitam. 
Yahweh Pakaijin ajihlutsa achonset nao athaimang peh a ahinkhou jongkitah tah a mangho chu iti kipa um a hitam! 
Keiman kachonset kaphon nomlou chun katahsa chu amangthah anahin, niseh in kapeng in kamao jinge. 
Sun le jan in kipui dih na nakhujol chun eidou ve. Nipilai a nichangsat in asuhkang twihu bangin katha asukang hel jengin ahi. 
Akhon nan kachon setna nahenga kaphong in, kamo najouse selguh ding golou vin kaseitai “Kalouchal kasetna jouse Yahweh Pakai kom'a phongdoh tang e” kati. Hiti chun Yahweh Pakai nangin neingaidam e! Kachonsetna jouse abeisoh tai. 
Hijeh chun, phatpha umlai hin Pathen Pathen mite jouse naheng a taocheh uhen, hiche thutanna twilen lah a alhahlut lou nadiuvin. 
Ajeh chu nangma hi kakiselna kulpi nahin; nangin boinana a kon'in neihuhdoh e. Galjona lan neiumkimvel tai. 
Yahweh Pakaijin aseije, “Nahinkho a lampi phapen achu kapui le ding ahi. Keiman kathu mop a kavetsui jing ding nahi. 
Sakol le Sangan bangin hetna beiyin um hih in, ajo leh kamdal le thihkol bupeh a thunoiya ki koi nabah loding ahi.” 
Miphalou ho hi lungkhamna tamtah in abomjin, ahinla Yahweh Pakai tahsan hovang angailutna longlouvin aum kimvel jingin ahi. 
Hijeh chun ama lungdei a chon ho Yahweh Pakai a kipah thanom jingun! Milung thengho jouse thanom tah in khosam un!
