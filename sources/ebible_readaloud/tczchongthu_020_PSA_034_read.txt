La Bu.
34.
Keiman Yahweh Pakai chu phat tin in thang vah in kate. Yahweh Pakai thagvah nahi kakam sunga gingdoh jingding ahi. 
Keima Yahweh Pakaiya bou kithang-at jing ding kahin; Panpi beite jouse lunglut cheh uhen. 
Hungun Yahweh Pakai loupi nahi seiphong khom u hitin amin hi choi-an khom u hite. 
Keima Yahweh Pakai kom'a kataovin aman kataona eisanpeh tai. Aman kakichatna jousea jong eilhat doh tai. 
Ama panpi ding ngaicha jouse vah bangin hungvah doh untin, amai uva jachatna muthim jinglouhel ding ahi. 
Kahahsat behseh phat in kataovin Yahweh Pakaijin eingai peh e; aman kaboina jouse eisuh lhap peh tai. 
Yahweh Pakai vantil chu hoidamna ahin; ama ging te aman aumkimvel in ahuhdoh jinge. 
Yahweh Pakai phatje chu patepmun lang veovin, Yahweh Pakaiya kisel jing mite chu iti kipa hitauvem! 
Nangho Pathen Pathen mite ho Yahweh Pakai chu ging jingun ajeh chu ama ging jingten angai chat jouse amudiu ahi. 
Keipi bahkai gol hat laitah jong agilkel jin, ahin Yahweh Pakai tahsandet jing hovang thilpha alhasam ngaipouve. 
Kachate ho hung uvin kaseihi kingai phaovin, chuleh keiman Pathen Pathen ginje kahilding nahiu ve. 
Hinkho sottah leh phattheina lhingset a hingnom umkha em? 
Thuse seinan na lei tuhtangin lang chuleh nakam jong jouseinan mang hih in! 
Thilse a kon'in kihai mangin lang thilpha bol in, chamna hol inlang hichu chepin. 
Yahweh Pakai mitin thilpha boljingho avesui jingin; panpi ngai a koute din akol tenin jong akihong jing e. 
Hinla thilse bolte chu dou mit nin aven, aman amin u leiset akon athaimang ding ahi. 
Yahweh Pakaijin panpi ngaichaho kouna angaipeh in. Aboi naova kon'in ahuhdoh jin ahi. 
Lungthim kisuchipte kom'a Yahweh Pakai chu anai in; Alhagao kisu gimte ahuhdoh jin ahi. 
Michonpha hon boina tampi atohjiuvin, hinla Yahweh Pakaijin phat tinin ahung huhdoh jin ahi. 
Ajeh chu Yahweh Pakaijin michonpha ho gule chang ahoibit pehjin; khatchajong abohsah pon ahi! 
Hamset nan migiloute hinkho alah tei ding, michonpha ho hotbol tejong got na chang tei diu ahi. 
Hinla Yahweh Pakaijin ama kin bol ho vang alhatdoh ding, ama a kisel jing honjong themmo changlou diu ahi.
