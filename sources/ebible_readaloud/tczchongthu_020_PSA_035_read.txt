La Bu.
35.
Yahweh Pakai, eidou ho hi nangin kidoupin, ei kisatpi ho jong nangin kisatpin. 
Nalum leh naumpho hin kipat in. Galmun a din kigon lang neihung kithopin. 
Eihin delkhum ho dounan natengcha leh nateng muh chu hin lam tan. “Keiman gal kajopeh ding nahi!” ti neijah sah tan. 
Eithat goho chunga hin jumna le jachatna lhun sah in, eigimbol a eisumang go ho hi jumson lang nungno jeng tan. 
Huilah a changvai alenmang bangin lenmang sah in, Yahweh Pakaiya kon a vantil banga hung chu semmang sah in. 
A lam lhah nao thimsah inlang lhonalsah in, Yahweh Pakai vantil chun nungdel peh u hen. 
Keiman amaho kabol sepoi, ahin ama hon kei matna dingin kotong eilai khum un ahi! Ken amaho kabol sepoi ahin ama hon keimat nadin kotong alai uve. 
Hijeh chun amaho chunga hetman louvin mathahna hunglhung hen! Kei ona dia asem u kotong chu amaho manthahna hijo tahen. 
Chuteng keima Yahweh Pakai akakipa ding ahi. Eilhatdoh jeh a keima kipa thanom ding kahi. 
Katahsa chunga kagu kachang jouse pum a Yahweh Pakai ka thangvah ding ahi: “Yahweh Pakai koiham nang toh kitekah theiding? Koidang ba ham mihatpa thaneina a konna panpi beipa huhdoh a chu?” 
Engbolna dih louvin eithemmo sah un. Kahetphahlou jouthu a mkonna eikichan e. 
ama hon kathilpha bol jeng jong thilsen eile thuh un lung lhahnan kangui heltai. 
Ahijeng vangin ama hon anat tengu leh keiman amaho kalainatpi jin khaodip pon kakisil in anngol in amaho kataopeh jin ahi. Hinla kataona donbut louvin ahung kinung le jitai. 
Ka golpha le insung mi monga kagel jeh in kalungkham pin, eihinga kanu kalainatpi bang bangin kalainat pi jin ahi. 
Hinlah ken tunia boina katophat in amaho akipah un keidounan thanom tah in akiloi khomun, hiti chun kahetkhahlou miho kisan in tujengin jong eino khum un; phat tin in eihouse jing jengun ahi. 
ama hon einuisat un eitam seovin noise tah in eihagel khum un ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai itih chan gei pi neivet a donlouva neikoi ding hitam? 
Chutengleh mipi tamtah ki khom te lah a kipathu kahin seiding ahi. Mipi ho jouse masanga nangma kahin thangvah ding ahi. 
Kajolel nachung a hin eidou te kipa thanop sah hihbeh in. Kamuiset nachungin jong ajeh bei a eivetda ho kitai sah hihbeh in. 
ama hon chamna thu aseipouvin, ama hon thepmona neilou atoh dingdol bep tongho dounan thilse agong jingun ahi. 
“Aha! Aha! ipi bol kigo nahi kamit tah un kamudoh tauve!” tin asam uve. 
O Yahweh Pakai nangin hicheghi namusohkei in nahesoh e, donlouvin koihihbeh in O Yahweh Pakai. neidalha hihbeh in O Yahweh Pakai. 
Hungthoudoh in! eihuhdoh dingin hung dingdoh jengtan! Yahweh ka Pakai le Pathen ka Pathen kachung thu hi neitan peh in. 
O Yahweh Pakai ka Pathen Pathen, kamo louna neiphondoh peh tan, ajeh chu nangma thudih a thutan nahi. Kaboina chung chon a kadougal te nuisat in neium sah hihbeh in. 
“Veovin itup uchu imutauve! Tua hi ahingsan a inehchai diu ahitai!” tisah hihbeb in. 
Kahahsatna nikhoa kipah ho sujum inlang jachat sah in. Kachunga hanla samho hi jumna leh minsetnan lhuh khumin. 
Ahin koi hileh keima ei huhdia hung ho jouse chu kipana lentah pen. “Yahweh Pakai aloupi e aman asoh hi lunglhai tah in phatthei aboh in lungmon na apei!” tin tanglouvin samjingu hen. 
Chuteng keiman nathutan adihji samphong ingting niseh a nangma kavahchoi ding ahi.
