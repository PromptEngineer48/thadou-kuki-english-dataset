La Bu.
36.
Chonsetnin miphalou ho lungthim gilsungah thu aseijin ahi. Ama hon Pathen Pathen ginna him him anei pouve. 
Akihisah naovin amaho le amaho akijou lhepmun ichan geija gilouleh phamo hiuvam ti akihet pouve. 
Athusei anatohna hou jousehi adihlou leh kijou lhepna ngen ahi. Chingthei tah a natoh leh thilpha bol dinghi adatthei pouve. 
Jankhosot in ahah un thil dihlou tohgon tamtah agong jiuvin ahi. Atohdoh uhi apha khatcha aumpoi. Thilsea konna kiheidoh ding angaito mong mong pouve. 
O Yahweh Pakai nami ngailutna longlouhi van bangin aki hohjale, nakitahna hin meilom kaina mun ho hi akho khel e. 
Nadih na jong muolsang vum tobang ahi. Nathutan dihdan jong twikhanglen thuhtah abang e. Nangin mihemleh ganhing ho jong kibanga nangailut ahi. 
O Pathen Pathen nami ngailutna longlou hi eti mamtam hitam! Mihem jousen nami ngailutna lhaving limnoija chun choldo na anei ui. 
Na insunga nehkemlou ann a chun navah van, kanglou nalui a twi chun nadon sah e. 
Ajeh chu nangma mihem hung kondohna twinah nahin, keihon kamu u vahjonghi nanga kon ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai nangma ngailuho chung a nami ngailutna longlou chu londoh sah in, milung thengho chunga thu adih in tanpeh in. 
Mi kiletsah ho neichotpha sah hih in, ahilouleh miphalouho sot tol le neihisah hih in. 
Ven! Thil dihlou bolte ho alhugam tauve! Amaho chu kithoudoh kit loudinga sot lhuh ahitauve.
