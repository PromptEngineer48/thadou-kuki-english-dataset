La Bu.
37.
Mikitahlou te chung a lungkham dan, thil dihlou bolte jong thangse hih in. 
Ajeh chu amaho khu hamhing banga molloi diu, nipilai pahchaho banga goplha loidiu ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai chu tahsa inlang thilpha bollin. Chutileh gamsunga lungmongin chengin natin nakhan touding ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai a kipah jingin, chutileh aman na lungsunga nangai chatchan napehding ahi. 
Nabol natoh jouse Yahweh Pakai a pelut jengin, ama chu tahsanin chutileh aman nakithopi ding ahi. 
Aman nathem na chu khovah banga muthimlah a avahdoh sahding ahi. Chuleh nachanchin jong sunlai nisa banga avahdoh sahding ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai masangah lungneng chan ngahhat in Aman ahintoh ding chu ngah hat in. Mi giloute ahaotheiukhu lungkhampi hih un, athilse gon ujong lunghimo peh hih in. 
Lungchom lunghan bol-hih in! Lung sat a kon'in jong kihei mangin. Nalung lhaso hih in hichun thilse lamma bou napui lut ding ahi. 
Ajeh chu migiloute kisumang ding ahin, amavang Yahweh Pakai tahsan a dingdet ho chun gamsung alodiu ahi. 
Migilou ho hi gangtah a mangthah diu ahin, amaho chu hol le jonglehchun mangthai ding ahitauve. 
Kisunem tah a umhon gamsung alodiu ahin, lungmong tah leh ninglhing tah a inchengdiu ahi. 
Mi gilouten Pathen Pathen ngaisah miho douna ahinbol diu; ama hon engset sanna ahin nelpehdiu ahi. 
Hinlah Yahweh Pakaijin amaho chu ahin nuisat in, ajeh chu aman achung thu kitanna ding nikhou ahunglhun chu amui. 
Mi-engsen hon mivaicha ho leh suhgam theija umte that dingin achemjam u hin asatdoh un, athalpiu hin loisal uvin tin, thilpha bolte chu ahin suhmang gotdiu ahi. 
Hinlah achemjam un amaho lungchang asutpai johding chuleh athalpiu chu bong ding ahi. 
Ase bola thil tampi neisangin Pathen Pathen mite hia lhomcha nei aphachomjoi. 
Ajeh chu migiloute hatna chu kisumang ding, Pahen mite vang Yahweh Pakaijin avenbit jing ding ahi. 
Ani ania Yahweh Pakaijin themmona neilou ho chu avetsui ding, chule ama hon tonsotna mang louding gou chu alodiu ahi. 
Amaho chu hahsat nikho jongleh kijumso loudiu kellhah nikho jongleh angaichat ho anei teidiu ahi. 
Migiloute mangthai diu ahi, Yahweh Pakai dougal miho jong loulaija pahcha tobang ahiuve-mei banga hung mang loi diu ahi. 
Migilouten asumpa lah u anungsah ngaipouvin, Pathen Pathen mite vang hongphal tah a peh jeng ahiuve. 
Yahweh Pakai phatthei changhon gamsung alodiu ahin, aman asapset ho vang mangthah diu ahi. 
Pathen Pathen mite, Yahweh Pakaijin akalsondan avetpeh in, ahinkho mandan jouse aman akipapin ahi. 
Amaho chu kipal jonglehu lhupai loudiu, ajeh chu Yahweh Pakaijin akhut in atuh jing ui. 
Phat khat lai chun khangdong kahin tun kateh tai, ahin Pathen Pathen mite paidoh a aumlam kahepon, achateu jong khutdon apangkhapouve. 
Pathen Pathen miten khotona neitah in sumpa ahomjiuvin, chuleh achateujong phatthei achang jiuve. 
Thilse a kon'in kiheidoh in thilpha bol in, chutileh tonsot a gamsunga nachen ding ahi. 
Ajeh chu Yahweh Pakaijin thudih bou adei in, Pathen Pathen'in acha teu jong donlou a akoi louhel ding ahi. Aman ahoibit jing ding, Gitlou bol chate vang mangthah diu ahi. 
Pathen Pathen chaten gamsung alodiu ahin, tonsotna cheng jingdiu ahi. 
Pathen Pathen mite kamsunga thudih apotjin adihle adihlou ahilthem jiuve. 
Pathen Pathen Dan chu amaho changval din akisan un, hijeh a chu Ama lampi a konna vah lonle loudiu ahi. 
Migilou ten Pathen Pathen mite achanglhi leuvin, suhmang nading lampi ahol jiuve. 
Hinlah Yahweh Pakaijin migiloute alolhin sah louhel ding Pathen Pathen'in mikitah te patepna a aum tengu le themmo achan louhel ding ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai ah kinem jingin. Alamlhong achun ching thei tah in lamjot in. Aman nadopsang a agam leiset nalosah ding ahi. Migilou te manthahvang namu ding ahi. 
Keiman migilou le khotonabei te Lebanon gama Ceder thingphung phatah banga akhantouvu kamui. 
Ahin kanungvet kitle, ana beisoh tauve! Ken kahol le vang in kamudoh joutapoi! 
Milungtheng ho leh miphaho khu veovin, chamna adei ho chu amakhouva thilphatah in angah jing ui. 
Hinla thilse bolte suhmanga umdiu ahi; ama hon khonung hinkho anei pouve. 
Yahweh Pakaijin mikitah te chu ahuhdoh jin; boina nikho hoa jong Ama hi akulpiu ahi. 
Aman amaho chu akithopin, migiloutea kon'in ahuhdohjin ahi. Aman amaho chu ahuhdoh in, ama hon ama a lungmon na anei un ahi.
