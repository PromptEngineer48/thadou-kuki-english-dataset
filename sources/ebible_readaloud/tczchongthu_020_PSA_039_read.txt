La Bu.
39.
“Katohna chunga chingthei tah in thil bol ing kating, ka thusei doh chenga chonseponge. Kavel a Pathen Pathen ginglou ho aumteng kalei kakituh tang ding ahi” tin kei le kei kakihou ve. 
Hinla hitia thipbeh a kaum a thupha jeng jong changkhat cha seilouva kaum chun ka lungsunga lung gimna akhoh secheh in ahi. 
Kalunga kagelbe chan in kalung gimna akhoh cheh in, hiti chun seilou in kaum thei pon ahi: 
“Yahweh Pakai leiset a kahinkho hi ichan a chom hitam neihin hetsah jingin, kahin nikho hi akisim jinghi neigeldoh sah in, kahin kho iti chom hitam? 
Nangin kahinkhov hi ichan geija chom a nasem hitam kakhut phang sangin jong asaojo poi. Nang dingin kahinkho lhum kei jong hi haikhat bou ahi soh tan, Mihem hinkho khat cheh jong hi haikhat cheh bou ahi.” 
Mihem hinkho hi milom kitol bang bep bou ahin, kisah lunggim naho jong hetman lou kah in akichai loi ji tai. Gou ikhol khom un, kon ahin man ding ham jong ihe doh ji pouve. 
Hijeh chun Yahweh Pakai hoi a kinep na kakoi ding ham? Nanga bou kakinep na aum'e. 
Kaset bolnom na a kon'in neihuhdoh in, mingol ho neinoise sah hih in. 
Keima na ansung a thipchet in kaum'e; imacha jong kasei louding ahi. 
Ahinla Yahweh Pakai nei engbol hih in! Nakhut gu dounan kachao lhagam tai. 
Kachonset u suhdih na dia neijep tengu le, kasah lut u chonsetna chu nget in aneh banginajun lhaji tan ahi. Tahbeh in mihem khat cheh hi haikhat bou ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai kataona ngai inlang panpi ngaija kataona neingai pih in! kamit lhi lon nah sah mon koi hih in, ajeh chu keima hi namaljin bou kahin, kema masang a kapu kapa teu bang a kholjin vahle kahi bouve. 
Hiche leiset hinkho hi kadalhah a kahin kho abei masang kakipa a kanui kit na din nalung han na, nagim bol na ho toh neichom khen in.
