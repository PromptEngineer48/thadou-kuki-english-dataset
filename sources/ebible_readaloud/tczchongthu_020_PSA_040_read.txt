La Bu.
40.
Panpi ding ngai in Yahweh Pakai chu lungneng tah in kangah jing e, aman kahenglam ahin ngan kataona eisanpih tai. 
Aman lunglhah nathei kotong a kon'in eikai doh in, buol sung a buon ngan lah a kon'in eikai doh in tolgo chung a eidin sah in kakal skonna jong eisuh dih pih e. 
Kasah ding lathah khat eipen, I-Pathen'u Pathen vahchoi na ding la khat eipen ahi. Mi tamtah in ana toh hin mu un tin adatmo ding u ahi. Hitia chu ama hon Yahweh Pakai chu atah san diu ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai tahsan na a ding det ho iti akipah tadiuvem, ama hon mikiletsah ho chung a tahsan na anei pouvin ahilouleh semthu pathen limhou ho jong atahsan pouve. 
O Yahweh Pakai, Pathen ka Pathen, nangin keiho dingin thil kidangtah tah neibolpeh tauve. Neigon peh u thil ho hi simjou sutjou hoi ahipoi. Nangto kibang ding koimacha aumpoi. Na kidang natoh doh ho hi sim ding kagon, achaina gei kalhun louding kahei. 
Nangman pumgo thilto ho a hin kipana nanei poi. Nei ngaisah thu ho chu tun kahechen tai, nangin chonset thoidam na thilto leh pumgo thilto jong nangeh poi. 
Hichun keiman kaseijin, “Ven keima kahung e. Pathen Pathen Lekhabu a kisun bangin: 
Nangma lungdei lam bolding hi katha anop na ahi, O Pathen Pathen, ajeh chu ka lungsung a nangma Danthu ho hi akisun lut tai.” 
Namite jouse lah a nathutan dih je hi kaphongdoh tai. O het themna nei Yahweh Pakai, nangma banginkichaloubeh in kasei doh tai. 
Nadih jedan Kipana Thupha hi keiman kachang a din kalungthim a selguh in kakoi poi; Nakitah na le nami huhhing na thu kasei e. Nahoubung mitheng te lah a namingai lut na long lou le nakitah na chu tanglou in kasei phong jinge. 
Yahweh Pakai nangin tanglou vin neingailu jing jengin. Nangailutna le nakitahna chu neihoi bit jing sah in. 
Boinan eibom in sim a simjou hoi ahipoi! Kachonsetna akikhol sang'in kalampi jong kamu thei tapoi. Kalujang a kasam sangin atamjo tan hansan na him him kanei jou tapoi. 
Yahweh Pakai lungsettah in neihuhdoh in! hung vah in Yahweh Pakai neipanpin. 
Eisumang goho hi jumson lang kisuhboi sah in, kahahsat nikho a kipah ho lungdong in nungjam sah in. 
“Aha tun akimaikhah tai” ti a asei jeh un, jum nan kichao hen. 
Nangkom hinbel jouse kipa na le thanop nan dimset uhen. Nami huhhing na kipapi ho chun “Yahweh Pakai chu aloupi e” tin sam jing u hen. 
Keidin vang gentheile vaicha kahi jeh in, Yahweh Pakaijin alung sung a eihoi jing hen. Yahweh Pakai nang chu eikithopi le eihuhhingpu nahi. O ka Pathen Pathen khongai sot hih in.
