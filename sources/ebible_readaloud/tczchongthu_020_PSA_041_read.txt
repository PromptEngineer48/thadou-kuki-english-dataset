La Bu.
41.
Mivaichate khoto ho chu anunnom ahi! Amaho chu Yahweh Pakaijin boina ato nao a kon a ahuhdoh diu ahi. 
Yahweh Pakaijin amaho chu ahoibit in ahinghoi jiu ve. Aman amaho chu gamsung ah phatthei aboh in galmiho akon na ahuhdoh ji e. 
Yahweh Pakaijin adam mo naova panpin tin adamdoh sahding ahi. 
“O Yahweh Pakai”, katao ahi “eilungset in, neidamdoh sah in ajeh chu nangma dounan kachonsetai.” 
Ahinla kadougal ten keima dounan thilse jeng aseiyun ahi. “Itih tah le athin suhmil in aum tadem?” atiu ve. 
Kagol kapai bangineihung vil jiuvin, hinla chomloukah in kachung chang guhthim thu asei khom leuvin, eidalhah tengu le muntin in aphonjal ji tauve. 
Eihot bolten keichung chang a thil phalou ngan aseijun, kachung a thilse ahung lhun dingbou adei un ahi. 
“Ama hi thina thei hiveija na ahi” atiuve. “Ama hi damdoh joulou ding ahitai!” atiuve. 
Katahsan pen kagol phapen, an kaneh khom pipa jeng jong keima eidouvin apang tai. 
Yahweh Pakai neilungset in. Neidamdoh sah kitin amaho hi kalethuh jou nadin! 
Yahweh Pakai nangma kachunga nalung lhaije ti kahei ajeh chu nangin kamelma te kachungah gal na lel sahtai. 
Nangin kahinkho neihing hoipeh tai, ajeh chu keiman lhamkonna kaneipoi, chuleh nangin atonsot a din na-angsung a neipui luttai. 
Israel Pathen Pathen Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah in umhen, Tonsot tonsot a hingjing pa chu. Amen chule Amen!
