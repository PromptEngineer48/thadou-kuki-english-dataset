La Bu.
42.
Sakhi sitwi chimset ngaichat dangchah bangin, kahin ngaicha e O Pathen Pathen. 
Hingjing Pathen Pathen chu dangchah in kalha ngai e. Itih tah le kache a a-anga kachibai theiding ham? 
Sunle jan in lonlhi bou ann in kanei tai, kagal miten phat tin in ei enghouvun “Na Pathen Pathen hoija umham atiuve?” 
Kalungthim analheh ji e thil umdan ho chu kageldoh ji teng: Pathen Pathen Hou-In a mihonpi chutoh kilhon a, tanggol a Pathen Pathen'inlam jon a kitol a thangvah la kisa a mihonpin golvah kibolji ho chu! 
Ibol a kei lunglha dah kahim? Ibol a kalung hibanga hesoh a ham? Keiman Pathen Pathen a kinepna giltah kaneijin! Ken ama kathang vah kit ding ahi ka huhhing pu Pathen ka Pathen. 
Tun keima kalung alhadah lheh jengtai, hinlah Jordan hung kondohna, Hermon molsang lang hijongleh kahin gel doh jing ding nahi. 
Nangman twipibang nakinon a naki ho twikhanglen gin chu kajai, nangma twipi kidilleh twipi kitol chun eihin lonvuh in ahi. 
Ahinlah niseh in Yahweh Pakaijin angailutna longlou chun eiphung vuh jingin, chuleh janseh leh ama vahchoila kasajin hinkho eipe a Yahweh Pakai kom'a chun kataojin ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai kasongpi keima kakapme, ipi dinga neisuhmil hitam? Ipidinga kadougal te bol hesoh jeh a lunggima kavale jingding ham? 
Eihou setnao hin kagep lhatan ahi, “Na Pathen hoija umham,” tin eidong jingun ahi. 
Ipi dinga keima hiti lungkham kahin, epidinga hiti lungboi kahim? Keiman Pathen Pathen nah kakinepna koijing ting chuleh eihuhhing paleh ka Pathen chu avellin thangvah kit inge.
