La Bu.
43.
O Pathen Pathen themmona bei kahi neiphongdoh in! Pathen Pathen ginglou hoa kon'in nei vengdoh in, chutengleh mikitah lou ho leh joupan hoa kon'in neihuhdoh in. 
Ajeh chu nang hi kakiselna Pathen Pathen nahi, ibol a neipaidoh hitam? Ibol a keima kadougal ten eibolset jeh a lunggim a kava lele dingham? 
Nadih na le navah na chu neihinsol peh in, hichun eilamhil hen. Hiho chun nangma chenna muol theng chu eilhut hen. 
Hikom'a chun keima Pathen Pathen maicham achu kasemjang puma kathang vah ding ahi, O Pathen Pathen. 
Idia lungkham kahim? Ipidinga kalung lhadah ham? Keiman kakinepna Pathen Pathen a koi inge! Eihuhhingpa le Pathen ka Pathen keiman nangma thangvah kit inge!
