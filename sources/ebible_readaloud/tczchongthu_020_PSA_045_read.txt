La Bu.
45.
Kalungthim in thu hoi tah ahung kilang e. Keiman lengpa chung chang hiche jaila ngaitah hi lungchin in sei inge, ajeh chu kalei hi jaila themtah pa mol jap tobang ahi. 
Nanghi mihem jouselah a ahoipen nahi, na lei a thungei tamtah ahung potdoh e. Pathen Pathen ama tah in tonsotna din phatthei naboh tan ahi. 
O galhatpa! nachemjam chu hin kipoh in! nangmahi naloupi in nathupi e! 
Nathupina a chun hung kivon inlang galjou dingin hung kondoh tem in, thutah leh thudih chuleh kisuhnemna ho hi chejom sahdingin kondoh inlang datmo umtah tah thil ho chu hung tongdoh tan! 
Nathal muh ho chu ahem in nagal mite lungsung asunvang jeng tai. Namtin vaipi nakengto noija aumtaive. 
Vo Pathen Pathen nalal touna hi tonsot tonsot a chejom jing ding ahi. Naleng tengol a chu thudih tah a vai nahomding ahi. 
Nangin thudih tah a thutan nadeijin thilse nathet ahi. Hijeh chun Pathen Pathen, nangma Pathen Pathen chun thao nanu ahitai, lengdang sanga nang chungahi kipale thanopna thaotwi chu nasunlhah khum ahitai. 
Myrrh, aloes, chuleh cassia hon navon anamtui sel e. Saiha a kisem khopia chu tumging ngaitah a nakilamto ding ahi. 
Leng Chanute chu na numei nunnem te lah a aum un. Najet langa ophir sana hoipen ho a kijem lengnu ading e. 
O leng chanu te ka thusei ngai un; amapa hi jabol un ajeh chu ama hi na Yahweh Pakai pau ahi. 
Ajeh chu sahlut tah naji hi namel-hoinan alunglhai sel e; jabol in, ajeh chu amapa hi na Pakai pa ahi. 
Tyre lengpa chanuten jong kipa thilpeh tampi nahung pehdiu ahi. Mihaoten nalung lhaina ding ahin holdiu ahi. 
Mounu, leng chanu, chu kivon na sana a kisem sangkholchol a chu iti vet loupia hitam. 
Hiche avon loupi tah kivon in lengpa kom lam a ahin pui un, anunjui nungah ho chutoh apuilut un ahi. 
Kipah tah le thanom tah a leng inpi sunga ahung akijot lut uchu iti lou a loupi hitam! 
Nachate jong apa tobang a leng hung hicheh ding ahiuve. Nangin amaho chu gam tamtah a lenga napan sah ding ahi. 
Khang jousea na min thanna kahin pohlut pehding ahi. Hijeh achu namtin vaipin tonsot a nahin thangvah ding ahi.
