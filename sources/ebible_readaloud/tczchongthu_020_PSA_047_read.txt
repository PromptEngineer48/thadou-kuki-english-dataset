La Bu.
47.
Mijouse hungun! Nakhut benguvin! Pathen Pathen kom'a kipah tah in thangvahna sam un! 
Ajeh chu Yahweh Pakai Hatchungnung chu gimneitah ahi. Ama hi leiset chunga leng thupitah ahi. 
Aman imasang uvah namtin vaipi hi asonkun in eidougalteu jong ikengto noi uvah eikoipeh tauve. 
Gamtep gam chu, angailut Jacob chilhahate, kithang-at tah a kijot eiho lodinga alhen ahi. 
Pathen Pathen chu gimnei tah in akal tou in, Yahweh Pakai chu sumkon gin kithong pum'in akal tou in ahi. 
Pathen Pathen thangvah la sauvin, thangvah la sauvin; ilengpau thangvah la sauvin, thangvah la sauvin! 
Ajeh chu Pathen Pathen hi leiset chung pumpi chunga leng ahi lasah pum'in thangvah un. 
Pathen Pathen alaltouna theng chunga atou in namtin vaipi lah a vai ahom'e. 
Vannoi leiset vaihomte Abraham Pathen Pathen mitetoh atoukhom'un ahi, ajeh chu leiset chunga leng jouse hi Pathen Pathen'a ahi. Ama hi vannoi leiset chung muntin'a jana achang'in ahi.
