La Bu.
48.
Yahweh Pakai hi iti loupia hitam, ichan gei a pachat dinga lom hitam, i Pathen'u Pathen chu akhopia acheng'in alhangtheng'a atou in ahi! 
Hiche hi asang'in ahoilamdang'e. Leiset pumpi'n mu ding atup un athanom cheh ui. Lhangtheng Zion molhoi hi leng thupipa chenna khopi ahi! 
Pathen Pathen ama tah chu Jerusalem indan sung'a chun acheng'in ahi Ama tah hi avengbit'a pang ahi akilang doh e. 
Vannoi mite lengho akigom'un hiche khopi dounan ahin nokhum'un ahi. 
Ahinla ama hon amuchet phat'un atangchot jeng'un atijauvin ajammang gam tauve. 
Amaho chu tijatnan numei naonei hon hahsatna atobangin akithingleu jeng'un ahi. 
Ahin nang'in Tarshishte kong thupitah ho solam huijin asuhset bangin nasuse tan ahi. 
Keihon hiche khopi loupina chu kanajaovin tunvang Pathen Pathen hatchungnung khopi chu keiho tah in kamuchen tauvin ahi. Hiche hi I-Pathen'u Pathen khopi ahi; Aman tonsot a ahoi doh ding ahi. 
O Pathen Pathen, na Hou-In theng sunga nangma kavahchoi lai un nami ngailutna longlou chu kagel jing ui. 
O Pathen Pathen namin ja dia kilom tah ahibanginleiset kolmong geija vahchoiya naum ding ahi. Najet lam ban thahat chu galjona a dimset ahi. 
Zion mol'a mipi ho kipah uhen. Judah khopiho nathutan dihjeh hin kipah uhen. 
Vo Pathen Pathen mite cheuvin Zion khopi gakholchen'un akimvel'a kijot uvin ainsangho jong gasim'un. 
Akhopi jouse jong gachon uvin lang akulpi bang nasa tah ho jong melchih un, chule nang hon nason nachilhah teu naseipeh thei diu ahi. 
Ajeh chu Ama hi hitobang mong Pathen ahi. Ama hi tonsot tonsot geija dinga I-Pathen'u Pathen ahin Aman ithi tokah uva eipui hoi diu ahi.
