La Bu.
49.
Nangho mipite'n hiche hi ngaijuvin! Nangho vannoi mite jouse'n ngaicheh uvin! 
Milen le mineo, ahao avai in ngaisohkeiyun! 
Ajeh chu ka thusei ho hi chihna thu ahin, chuleh kalung'a kagel jong hi hetgilna dimset ahi. 
Keiman thuchih kaseiho chu giltah in kagel'in hiti chun semjang sai a kitilkhou in thulem kaseiho suhlhap na kanei jie. 
Kagal mite'n eihin umkimvel'uva boinan eihin lhunkhum teng idia kakichat ding ham? 
ama hon anei agouhou atahsan un ahaotheinau a akiletsah ui. 
Ahijeng vang'in athina diuva kon'in amaho le amaho akihuhdoh joupouvin Pathen Pathen henga sum in ahinkhou akichoh thei pouve. 
Lhatdamna kiti hi bailam tah a um ahipoi, ajeh chu koiman alhingset'in apejou poi; 
tonsot a hingjing a lhanmun'a chelouhel nading chu. 
Michingho chu nikhat thitei diu ahi, hiche mingol le lungel neilou ho banga anei agouhou chu adalhah cheh di'u ahi. 
Lhanmol chu atonsot inn'u ahitan achen jingna di'u mun chu ahitai. Anei agouho'u chu amaho min putsah jong leu. 
Aminthan nao chu sotlou ding ganhing ho bang'a thithou thou diu ahi. 
Amaho hi min miching dan'in geljong leu mingol bang'a thina ato thou thou di'u ahi. 
Kelngoiho bang'a lhanmun lhung thou thou di'u thina chu akelngoi chingpa'u hi ding, anei agouhou a kon'a gamla tah a lhanmun khat a monlha thou thou diu ahi. 
Ahin keivang Pathen Pathen'in kahinkho eilhatdoh peh'in tin, Aman lhanmun'a kon'in eihuhdoh nante. 
Hijeh chun migilou ho ahung hao uva, a in chen ujong ahung hoibe cheh tengu le lunglhadah hih in. 
Ajeh chu amaho athitengu leh imacha akipoh di'u aumpoi anei agou'un lhan sung'ah juilut thei pouvinte. 
Hiche lei hinkho'a hin alolhin jeh un vangpha le ochat um'in kigel jong leu, 
Chemasa ho bang bang'a thidiu khovah amukit louhel di'u ahitai. 
Anei agou'u kiletsahpi ho chun amaho jong ganhing bang'a phat khat leh thithou thou diu ahilam akihetdoh lou'u ahi.
