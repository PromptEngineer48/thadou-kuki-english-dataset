La Bu.
51.
O Pathen Pathen, na mi ngailutna longlou jeh'in na mi khotona chu neimusah in. Na mi hepina kidangtah jeh chun ka chonset nenbeh jouse neisuhbei peh in. 
Kathemmona ho jouse'a kon'in neisutheng inlang neisoptheng in, ka chonsetna a kon'in neisuthengsel in. 
Ajeh chu ka phatlouna ka kihet'in sun le jan'in eijui jing'in ahi. 
Nangma douna mong'a chonse ka hi, Nangma mitmun thilse chu ka nabol tai. Na thusei in nadihna aphondoh ding ahi, chuleh ka chung'a nathutan jong adih'in ahi. 
Ajeh chu ka nu oisung'a ka ki jil'a pat a chonset pum'a kijil ka hin chonsesa hung peng a ka hi. 
Hinla nang'in ka nu oisung'a pat'in jong atheng a kaum ding nana ngaichat ahin hiche mun jeng'a jong chu chihna lungthim neina hilpat ahitai. 
Ka chonsetna a kon'in neisutheng lechun ka then ding ahitai, neisoptheng lechun buhbang sang'in theng tang'e. 
Oh! Ka kipana neipekit in, nang'in nei hesoh sah ahin tun neikipasah kit tan. 
Ka chonsetna ho neivet peh jing hih in, ka setna nenbeh ho sumang in. 
Lungthimthah neisem peh in O Yahweh Pakai. Kasunga um lhagao hi nei thah sempeh in. 
Na angsung'a kon'in neipaidoh hih'in, chuleh keija kon'in na Lhagao Theng chu lamang hih'in. 
Nei huhhing nikho'a ka kipana chu avel'in neipe kit inlang chuleh nathu a nung jing mi neihisah in, 
Chuteng na thua nunglou ho jong nangma deilam'a ahung chonna di'uva ka hil ding chutengleh amaho nangma kom lang'a hung kile kit ding ahi'uve. 
O Pathen Pathen mi huhhingpu, keiman thisan kaso tahjeh in neingaidam'in chutileh neingaidamna chu kipah tah a vahchoila a ka sah ding ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai lungnachim'a nangma ka vahchoi theina dingin ka lei neilhap peh'in. 
Nangman kilhaina thilpeh na ngaicha pon, pumgo thilto ding khat jong na ngaicha pon ahi. 
Nangin na ngaichat kilhaina chu-Lhagaova kisuh gep ahin, nang'in lung kisuchip pum'a kisihna neiho napaidoh poi o Yahweh Pakai. 
Zion hi lungsetna mitvet in venlang hin kithopin, Jerusalem pal hinsem phan. 
Chutengleh kilhaina lungtheng sel a kipe pumgo thilto hon lunglhai in natin chule pumgo thil to pum bongchal ho avel'in hung kitodoh tante.
