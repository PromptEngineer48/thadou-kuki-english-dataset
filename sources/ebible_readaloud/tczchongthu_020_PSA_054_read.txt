La Bu.
54.
O Pathen Pathen thaneitah in hung'in lang neihuhdoh in! Na thahatnan nei veng'in. 
O Pathen Pathen ka taona ngaijin, ka thumna hi neihin gelkhoh peh'in, 
Ajeh chu gamdang mite'n eihin delkhum uvin, mi engse ten tha dingin eihol'un ahi, ama hon Pathen Pathen imachan agel pouve. 
Ahinlah kei dingin Pathen Pathen hi eipanpi ahin, Yahweh Pakai hin eihinghoi ahi. 
Ka galmi ten thilse agon jouse'u hi amaho chunga le chuh hen! Na dei dan tah in achung uva na tong'in lang amaho khu subei in. 
Keiman naheng a kiphal tah in thilpeh hinchoi ing e; Keiman namin hinthang vah ing kate Yahweh Pakai, ajeh chu hichu apha ahi. 
Ajeh chu Yahweh Pakai nang'in ka hesohna a kon'in neihuhdoh tan, ka gal mite chung'a jong galjona neimusah tan ahi.
