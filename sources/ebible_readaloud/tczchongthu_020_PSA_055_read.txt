La Bu.
55.
Vo Pathen Pathen ka taona neingaipeh in, panpi ngehna ka taona neigelkhoh peh'in! 
Lungset tah in ka taona neingaipeh inlang neihin donbut teijin, ajeh chu kagimnan eihin lonvuh tan ahi. 
Ka gal mite'n eisamseu vin, eigihna'u hi akithong le'u jengin ahi. Aman boi na eilhunkhum sah un lunghang tah in eihin delkhum tauve. 
Ka lung aphul lap jengin thina gimneitah hin eihin nokhum tan ahi. 
Tijatna le lunggimna'n eilonvuh tan kithing louvin kaum theipoi. 
Oh, vakhu bang'a lhaving nei hitave leng o keima lengdoh ingting ga kicholdo tang'e! 
Keima gamla tah in lengdoh ingting gamthip noi olnamun gabel tang'e. 
Hitobang kivetdatona huipi kinunglah a konhin gangtah in gamla tah a jammang tang'e. 
Yahweh Pakai amaho hi kisuhmosah inlang athilse gon'u jong hi suhmopeh jeng tan, ajeh chu athilgon'u hin khopi sung'a thilse tamtah asodoh sah ding ahi. 
Akulpi bang'u jong galmi'a kon'a hoidohna dinga asun ajan a anga'u ahi, ahinla akhohpentah chu khopi sung'a chonsetna ho hi ahi. 
Thil jouse hi asegam'a ahitai, kailhang jeng'a jong kigihna le ki joubol na adimlha tan ahi. 
Ka dougal'in eitai tomna jong ahipoi hichu ka doujou thouve, kiletsah tah a eijumso jing'a ka kidoupipa jong ahipoi, amaho a kon chun ka ki seldoh theinan ahi. 
Amavang hiche ho chu hilouvin, ka kibahpi, ka kivoppi, ka golngai nangma chu na hi. 
Phat khatna chu ichan geija ina kingailut hitam Pathen Pathen Hou-In jeng'a jong ina kijotlutji ahie! 
Ka dougalte hi thina'n lhunkhum hen, lhankhuh in ahing'in vallhum jeng'u hen, ajeh chu setna'n alungsung'u chen nan aneitan ahi. 
Ahinlah keiman Pathen ka Pathen ka kou ding chuleh Yahweh ka Pakaijin eihuhdoh ding ahi. 
Jingkah, sun, nilhah in ka lung hesoh jeh'in ka kap'in ahileh Yahweh Pakaijin ka-o eijahpeh tai. 
Mi tamtah in eidou jing hen lang, kei douna'n gal kisat chohjeju jeng jongleh Yahweh Pakaijin eilhatdoh'in ol-heuvin eiumsah tai. 
Tonsot a vaihom jing Pathen Pathen chun ka taona sang'intin ka dougalte asuhnem ding ahi. Ajeh chu ama hon achondan'u akhelnom pouvin Pathen Pathen jong aging pouve; 
Keima kiloipipan vang, aloihi alhem lhan akitepna asukeh'in ahi. 
Athu cheng chu thaotwi bangin analjet in hinlah asung uvin gal lungthim apu'uve. Athu cheng'u thaotwi nang abangin hinlah asunglang uvah chemjam ngen aum'e. 
Na pohgih chu Yahweh Pakai chung'a seplha tan Aman na ngai jangpeh nante. Aman mi chonpha ho chu akipalsah louding lhuh jong alhuhsah louding ahi. 
Nangvang O Pathen Pathen mi gilou ho chu lhuh na kokhuh a nasotlhah ding ahi. Tolthat ho le jonpan ho jong khangdong cha na thisah ding ahi, hinla keimanvang eihuhing din nang jeng katahsan e.
