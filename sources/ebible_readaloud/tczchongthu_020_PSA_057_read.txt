La Bu.
57.
Neilungset in O Pathen Pathen, nei khoton! Neivenbit jingna dingin nang malam ka hin sang'e. Hahsatna ho abeito kahsen na lhaving lim noi a ka kisel jing ding ahi. 
Ka chung'a athilgon eisuhbulhing pehna dingin Pathen Pathen hatchungnung chu ka kouve. 
Aman eihuhdoh ding chuleh eidel lele ho jumsahna dinga van'a kon'a panpina eihinpeh ding ahi. Pathen ka Pathen'in angailutna longlou leh akitahna a chu eihung vil ding ahi. 
Keipi bahkai hangtah hon mihem te a anchah dia achanlhih le banin gal mite'n eiumkimvel'uvin, aha uhin tengcha leh thalchang bangin asunvangthei in, alei'u jong chemjam hemset tobanginasat tan thei uve. 
O Pathen Pathen nangma hi vanmun jouse chungvum sang'in hung kichoisang'in! Na loupina hin leiset chung mun jouse hi salvah hen! 
Ka gal mite'n thang eikampeh uvin, keima lung gim leh lunglengvai in kaum'e. Ama hon ka lampi'a kotong thuhtah alai un ahinlah amaho ma alhalut jiuvin ahi. 
O Pathen Pathen nang a lungmon na kanei e; nang a kalung among e. Nangma thangvah la ka sahthei hi thil kidang ahipoi. 
Thou in kalung! Thou in, o Semjang le tumging! Ka vahchoila'n jingninou hi thoudoh ing e. 
O Yahweh Pakai mipi ho jouse lah a na thangvah ing'e. Namtin vaipi jouse lah a nangma na thangvah ing kate. 
Ajeh chu na mi ngailutna longlou hi vanho bangin asang e. Na kitahna in meilomlah apha'n ahi. 
Hung kichoi sang'in O Pathen Pathen, van mun sangpen ho sang'in na min hi dopsang'in umjo hen. Na loupina in leiset chung pumpi hi salvah hen.
