La Bu.
58.
Vaihom ho nang hon thutah kiti hi ipi kisei na ahi nahet u hinam? Nanghon thu adih a natan'u hinam? 
Ahipoi! Nangho'n na lungsung un thudihlou na gong'un gamsung a hunam a natohna ngen na chelheh sah un ahi. 
Miphalou ho hi chonsesa peng'a ahiuvin, ahung pen'uva pat'in jou apan'un ama deidan cheh in achon'un ahi. 
Ama hon gul bangin gu hat tah alhao vin; gulsoh banginthuhil angainom pouve. 
Gul lam sahhon atheile'u ichan geija amut ngeisel vang un akihetmosah un ahi. 
O Pathen Pathen a-ha uhi suhbong jengin, O Yahweh Pakai hiche keipi bahkai ho hi a-ha'u suhgoi peh jengin. 
Tolgo'a twi isunlhah a agotloiji bangin mangthah loi jeng'u hen. Akhut uva achoi u agal manchah'u chu manmo sosah jengin. 
Dahdeng amacham'a ajunlhah ji bangin junlhahsah'in, naosen athisa hung peng nisa mumanlou bah sah in. 
Hampa kisatlha banginateh akhang Pathen Pathen'in lhamlha jeng uhen, leibel meikou lah a ling le khao kigouvam sang a gang jo jeng u hen. 
Thildih lou ho akilethuh teng mi chonpha ho kipah di'u ahi. 
Chuteng achaina le mijousen asei diu ahi, “Pathen Pathen a dia hing ho dihtheina chu kipa thilpeh ahin; leiset a jong thudih a thutan Pathen Pathen aum mong e.”
