La Bu.
60.
O Pathen Pathen nang'in keiho hi neipaidoh tauvin ka kiselna'u jouse jong neisuh chimpeh tauve, nangma keiho chung'a analunghang jongleh chun na lungsetna neimusah tauvin. 
Ka gamsung'u na hotling in chuleh na pokehsah ji'n ahi, tun vang akehlai mehbit tan, ajeh chu gamleiset akithing'in ahi. 
Keiho hasatnatah nahin pen, jukham a lon lele ho nei sodoh jeng u ve. 
Ahinla nang'in nangma ginna neiho dingin ponlap khat neina dopsang peh'un, hichu min eihin nokhum teng'uleh eikipunkhom sah'in anapang'in ahi. 
Tun na ngailutna mite hi huhdoh tan, ka taona ngai in lang na ban thahat'in neihuhdoh tauvin. 
Aloupi na in Pathen Pathen'in, “keima kichoisangna a hiche kitep nahi ahin sem tai. Shechem hi kipa na a kakehso ding ahi. Sukkoth phaicham hi ka mite ka hoppeh ding ahi. 
Gilead hi keija ahin, chuleh Manasseh jong ahi. Ephraim ka thih lukhuh hin galhat miho ahinsep doh ding, chue Juda ka leng tenggol hin leng tamtah hinsepdoh ding ahi. 
Ahin Moab vang hi ka kisilna kong ahin ka sohna ahin toh ding ahi chuleh Edom a hi ka kengto kathehngim ding chutengleh Philistia chung a ka galjona ka hin sapdoh ding ahi.” 
Koi pen'in kul kigetkhum khoipi'a hi eipuilut ding ham? Koi pen in Edom chung'a hi galjona eihin pohlut peh ding ham? 
O Pathen Pathen nang'in neipaidoh'u hitam? Nang'in ka sepai te toh galsatna a neihung lhonpi louding hitam? 
O lungset tah in ka gal miteuva kon'in neipanpin, ajeh chu mihem panpina hi aphachom aumpoi. 
Pathen Pathen panpina jal'a hi thil loupi tah tah tohdoh a um ding ahi, ajeh chu Aman imelmate'u achotpha ding ahi.
