La Bu.
64.
O Pathen Pathen, ka kiphinna hi neingaipeh tei in. Ka melmaten eigihnauva kon hin neihuhdoh in. 
Hiche engset lungput a tohgon nei mipi ho a kon'in neiselmang in, hiche thilse bol hon hoa kon'in. 
ama hon chemjam kinolhem bangin alei'u anolhem'un thalchang kikap doh bangin thucheng ngaimo tah tah in mi adoi leleu ve. 
Themmona neilou miho chu akiselnauva kon'in ahin kap un kichatna neilouvin gangtah in anokhum jiuvin ahi. 
Thilse bol dingin amaho le amaho akitilkhou un chujongleh aguh a iti thang kam ding ham, tichu agong'un ahi. “Hiche hi kon amudoh ding ham?” atiuve. 
Athilse gonpat'u chun hitin aseijun, “Hiche hi thilgon hoipen ahitai!” atiuve. Henge mihem lunggel le lungthim vang hi aphalou mong ahi. 
Ahinla Pathen Pathen ama tah in athalchang ahin kap ding gangtah a akaplhuh diu ahi. 
Amaho tah kampao in ahin thagam diu ahi, koi hileh amaho muchan chun alunghan khum uva alu ahihdiu ahi. 
Chujou teng mijouse kicha diu, Pathen Pathen thil boltheina kidangtah chu aphondoh diu, chuleh athilbol datmo umtahho chu ahin hetchen diu ahi. 
Mi chonphaho Yahweh Pakai a kipah thanom diu chuleh Ama chu kiselna ahin neidiu ahi. Koi hileh thilpha bol ho jousen Pathen Pathen ahin vahchoidiu ahi.
