La Bu.
68.
O Pathen Pathen hung kipat inlang namelmate hi thethang jengin, Pathen Pathen doumah bolte jouse akihuhdohna diuvin jam uhen. 
Amaho khu meikhu banginmut mang in, meisalah a khoilu ajollhah banginjollhahsah in, Pathen Pathen ansung a kon'in migiloute mangthah jeng uhen. 
Hinla miphaho chu kipah u hen. Amaho chu Pathen Pathen angsung'ah kipah u hen. Kipa thanopnan dimset u hen. 
Pathen Pathen le amin thang vah la saovin! Meibanglom puile pa thangvahla chohthet in sauvin. Amin chu Yahweh Pakai kiti ahi–a angsung a kipah un. 
Pa neilou te dia Pa nahin, meithaiho hoidam jing nahi-hiche hi Pathen Pathen ahin aumna chu muntheng ahi. 
Pathen Pathen'in changkhatpu hinkho chu insungmi lhingtah in akoi in; Songkul a tangho chamlhat na apen akipa sah tai. Ahinlah thuanunglou ho chu nisan akahgepna neldigama achensah e. 
O Yahweh Pakai na mite Egypt gama kon a na hin puidoh a chun, gamgo lah a na hin puigalkai chun, 
Israel Pathen Pathen, Sinai Pathen Pathen nangma masang'ah leiset akithing in vanhon go ahin juhlhahsah in ahi. 
O Pathen Pathen nangman leigo chapnousah din go nengchet in neihin peuvin ahi. 
Akhon nan hichea chun na mite na chensah in, neh le chah nengchetnin na pen, na mite ngaichat jouse jong na pe tai. 
Yahweh Pakaijin thu chengkhat agahseileh mi tamtah in hiche kipana thu chu apoleuvin ahi. 
Melmate lengho le asepai te ajamgam tauvin Israel numeiten athilkeo u achomgam peh tauve. 
Kelngoi hong a chengho jengin jong nei le gou tamtah-alhaving teni dangka le amul sana a kisem vapal akimu uvin. 
Hatchungnung chun Zalmon molchung'a buhbang kithejal bangin melma lengho chu athejal gamtan ahi. 
Bashan a molho hi athupi thei lheh jeng uve, mol monghem tamtah in vanthamjol a kilhungdoh jenge. 
O molsang gumtahho ibolba thangthip tah a navet uham, Zion molsang Pathen Pathen'in alhen na mun, tonsot a achen nading chu? 
Sakol-kangtalai simjoulou hon akolkhum Sinai mol a kon a hung'a amuntheng a lut ding ahi. 
Nangma achung sang a na kaltou tengleh galhing a kiman tamtah na puitou ding, mipi ho'a kon'a kipaman tamtah na san ding, nangma na douho a kon'a jong na san ding ahi. Hiche'a chu Yahweh Pakai eiho lah a cheng ding ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah in umhen; eihuh hingpau Pathen Pathen chu thangvah in umhen! Niseh a aman abanjang a eikai u ahi. 
Eiho Pathen hi mihuhhing Pathen Pathen ahi. Yahweh Pakai lengchungnung hin thina a kon a eihuhdoh u ahi. 
Pathen Pathen'in amelmate luchangho asuhchip ding ahi, thilse jeng bolna a kipeho chu alugu u asuhchip ding ahi. 
Yahweh Pakaijin asei e, “Bashan a kon'a ka melmate ka hinpui lhah ding ahi; twikhanglen lailung a kon'a ka hin puidoh ding ahi. 
Ka mite, nang hon athisan uva chu na keng u na kisoptheng diu, chuleh na uichahou jengin jong chan aneidiu ahi.” 
O Pathen Pathen gal nahin jaova na hung kijot u chu muthei in ahung kilangdoh tai, Pathen ka Pathen le ka lengpa muntheng a na kijotlut u chu. 
La saho amasang a aum un, tumging saiho a nung a aum ui; alai lunga chun nungah hon vohging theiho avouve. 
Israel mipite Pathen Pathen thangvah un; Israel hinkho hungkondohnaho Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah un. 
Ven phung neocha Benjamin'in ahin lamkai e. aban'ah Judah a kon a vaihomho, chule Zebulun le Naphtali a kon'in vaihom tamtah ahung uve. 
O Pathen Pathen, na ban thahat chu jedoh in. Achesa nikho ho bang chun na ban thahat chu nahat na hung kilangdoh hen. 
Jerusalem a na Hou-Intheng ah leiset lenghon na hunglutna u thil ho ahinchoijuve. 
Na melma namtin vaipiho, pang lah a kisel gamsaho, ahatmo bongnouho lah a bongchalho hi phohsal in. Kisunem tah a ahunglutnau thil in sumlhonho hinchoisah in. Galbol'a thanom namtin vaipiho thethang in. 
Egypt a kon'in kipa thilpeh dingin thil mantamho hinchoi u hen, Ethiopia mite Pathen Pathen a akipehlutna uvin bohkhup uhen! 
Leiset gam lengho'n Yahweh Pakai, Pathen Pathen kom'ah vahchoila sauhen. 
Vanlaijol'a kon'a ogin hat tah hung kithongdoh a, masanglai vanho lah a ki tollepa vahchoila'n la sauvin. 
Pathen Pathen thuneina hi mijouse hetsah un, aloupina chun Israel te ahinsalvah in athahatna hi vanho chung'ah akilang jing'e. 
Pathen Pathen hi amuntheng'ah gimneitah in aum jing'e. Israel Pathen Pathen'in amite chu thuneina le thahatna ape'n ahi. Pathen Pathen chu thangvah in umhen!
