La Bu.
69.
Eihuhdoh in, O Pathen Pathen, twisoh hin ka ngong gei achuptai. 
Bonlhoh lah a hin ka lhalut cheh cheh tai; pansatna ding ka mujou tapoi. Keima twithuh lah a ka lhalut tan twisoh hin eichup tai. 
Panpi ngai a kaka na katha achollha tai; ka lolhomsung jong agolha tai. Ka ka kanan ka mit jong apomtan, Pathen Pathen'in eipan pi ding kangah e. 
Ajehbei hel a eivetdaho hi ka lujang a ka samjang sang'in atamjouve. Ka melmate hin jou thu jeng a suhmang ding eigouvin, hiti hin ka guh louhel thil chu kanung peh din athum uve. 
O Pathen Pathen ichan gei a ngol kahi nahenai; Ka chonsetna ho hi nang'a kon'in akiselmang theipoi. 
Keijeh in, nangma tahsan ho chu jumsah hihbeh in, O van a lengchungnung Yahweh Pakai, kei jeh in nangma houmi tehi kisuh jum na nei hisah hihbeh in, O Israel Pathen Pathen. 
Ajeh chu nangma jal a housetna ka thoh ahi; jumna le jachatna kamai a adimset tai. 
Ka sopiho tah jengin jong eikihetmosah tauvin gamchommi bangin eibol tauvin ahi. 
Na Hou-In a ding a ka tompan jeh in eichaideh tan ahi, nangman na housetnau jouse chu ka chunga achu tan ahi. 
Kappum a an kangol a chun, noisetah in eihou un ahi. 
Ka genthei phondohna a khaodippon ka kisil'a ahileh einuisat un ahi. 
Keima hi khopi sung a jalhang a kihoulim nopna bep ka hitan jukhamhon kei chung chang thu totnopnan anei tauve. 
Hinla Yahweh Pakai naheng a katao jingin, tuchung beh neihin khoto ding kakinem e. O Yahweh Pakai na mingailut longlou chun katao nahi huhing na tahbeh a chun nei donbut in. 
Bonlhoh lah a kon hin neihuhdoh in; athuh jep a neilhahlutsah hihbeh in, eivetdaho lah a kon'in neihuhdoh inlang hiche twithuh lah a kon hin neikaidoh in. 
Twisohhat lai neichupsah hih in, ahilouleh twithuh gillai jong nei vallhum sah hih in, ahilouleh thina lhan kotong jong nei nehmang sah hih in. 
O Yahweh Pakai ka taona neingaipeh in, ajeh chu na mi ngailutna longlou hi kidangtah ahin, neikaihoi jingin, ajeh chu na mikhotona hi lolhing tah ahi. 
Na sohpa a kon hin kisel mang hih in; gangtah in neidonbut in ajeh chu keiman boina nasatah ka toh tai. 
Hung'in lang neihung lhatdoh tan; ka melmat a kon'in neilha-ong tan. 
Nang'in eiki houset na eikijum bolna le ei kijachat sah naho jouse na hesohkei in ahi. Ka melmaten abol jouseu na hesohkei e. 
Ami taitomnauvin ka lungthim asukeh in, kadailheh jeng tai. Mikhat seh hijongleh lungset na eimusah um hen lang; Khattou hung kiheihen lang eihin lungmonsah le kanom e. 
Ahinla chutimalah in ama hon ka nehdin gu eipeuvin ama hon ka don dingin juthuh eipeuve. 
Amasang uva neh-le-chah dimset dokhang chu akipal nao soh henlang, ahaonau chu amaho dingin thangkol hisah jeng tan. 
Kho amutheilouna diuvin amit u chojeng henlang atahsao kithing thing in umsah jengin. 
Na lunghanna chu achung uvah sunglha jengin; na lunghanna meikong achun amaho chu kavam jengin. 
A-in'u chu ahomkeo soh doh jeng henlang aponbuh u jong kihoh doh jeng hen. 
Gotna napeh mi chun, akisuhkhah na chung uva jumbol na akoibe uve; akisukha ho chu natna abelap peh uve. 
Achonsetnau setvum peh unlang, khatcha ongthol'in lha hihbeh in. 
Amin u chu hinna lekhabu a kon'in thaimangpeh in; michonpha ho lah a kisimtha sah hihbeh un. 
Keima natna thohgim in kaum e, O Pathen Pathen, na mihuhhingna thahat chun neihuhdoh in. 
Hiteng chuleh keiman na min pachatna la'n sangting chule thangvahna pum'a jana ka peh ding ahi. 
Ajeh chu hichun kilhaina bongchal ki todoh sang'a Yahweh Pakai chu alunglhaisah ding ahin, bongchal thaotah le akeng tin hoitah thilpeh kipe sang'in jong aphajoi. 
Mi kineosah tena Pathen Pathen'u natoh muo vintin kipahdiu ahi. Apanpi dia Pathen Pathen holte jousen kitilkhouna neicheh uhen. 
Ajeh chu Paka in ngaichageova umte taona angai in; Aman sohchang'a umho donlouvin akoi jipoi. 
O van-le-lei in Pathen Pathen vahchoi u henlang twikhanglen le asung a cheng jousen jong vahchoi u hen. 
Ajeh chu Pathen Pathen'in Jerusalem ahuhdoh ding Judah khopiho asemphat ding ahi. Hiche'a chun amite cheng untin gamsung chu alodiu ahi. 
Ama thu nitjing chaten gamsung chu louvin tin, chule ama ngailuho jouse hiche mun a chu lungmong a cheng diu ahi.
