La Bu.
71.
O Yahweh Pakai eihuhdoh ding ngaichan na kom kahinbel e. 
Neihuhhing in lang neihuhdoh in, ajeh chu nangma adih na hi, na kol hinsung'in lang ka sei neingaipeh in, chule neichamlhatsah in. 
Ka kisel jing theina din kahoidam na songpi hijingin. Keima huhhing ka chan theina dingin thu peh chu pen, nangma ka songpi le ka kiselna na hi. 
Ka Pathen Pathen, miphalouho thaneina a kon'in neihuhdoh in, eibolse a mi engbolseho'a kon'in neihuhdoh in. 
O Yahweh Pakai nangbou ka kinepna na hi, O Yahweh Pakai nangma hi ka chapan laiya pat a ka hin tahsan na hi tai. 
Henge, ka pen'a pat nang'in neina umpi jingin, ka nu naobu sung'a ka kijil'a pat a neina chintuppat ahitai, hijeh a nangma ka thangvah jing hi datmo ding ahipoi. 
Ka hinkho hi mi tamtah din kihilna thei ahi, ajeh chu nangma hi ka thahatna le ka kiselna na hi. 
Hijeh a chu nangma ka thangvah chim theilou na hi; Nilhum kei kei a hi na loupina ka phondoh jing ahi. 
Tua, ka tehphat hin neipaidpoh hih in. Ka tha'n ajolou teng neinungsun hih in. 
Ka melmate hi keima douna di'n akihoutoh tauve. Amaho hin thadoh ding eigo tauvin ahi. 
ama hon, “Pathen Pathen'in anungsun tai che'u hitin gaman'u hite ajeh chu tua hi koiman apanpi louding ahi” atiuve. 
O Pathen Pathen, munchom a potdoh hih in, Pathen ka Pathen neihung panpi loi in. 
Keima eihehseho hi sunem'in lang sumang jengin, keima eibolsenomho hi sujum'in lang jachatsah in. 
Keima vang neikithopi dinga kinem jing ding ka hi; Keiman nangma ka thangvahbe jing ding na hi. 
Keiman nangma chonphatnaho hi mijouse ka seipeh ding ahi. Keima thusei them hi hih jong leng na mihuhhingna thahatna hi nilhumkei kei a ka phondoh jing ding ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai thaneichungnung, keiman na thilbol nasatah tahho jeh a ka thangvah ding na hi. Thudih choija nangma bou na hi hi keiman mijouse ka seipeh ding ahi. 
O Pathen Pathen nang'in ka chapanlai a pat a neinahil ahitai. Ken jong na thilbol kidang ho hi midang ho ka seipeh jing ding ahi. 
Tunvang keima ka tehtan ka sam akang tai neinungsun hih in. Tu khangthahte lah a hin na thaneina hi samphong lengnge. Kei nung'a hung ding miho lah a jong na thilbol datmo umtahho jong phongdoh ing kate. 
O Pathen Pathen na chonphatna hi van sangpenho lah jong aphapeh e. O Pathen Pathen na thilbol kidangtahho hi koitoh na kitekahpi thei ding ham? 
Hahsatna nasatah tah neithohsah in ahinlah nang'in ka hinkho na kiledohsah ding ahi'n chule lhan lah a kon'in jong neikaidoh in. 
Nang'in lolhinna sangjo cheh a jong neikiledohsah ding avel'a neihin lhamon ding ahi. 
Hiteng chuleh keiman semjangsaito tho'a nangma ka thangvah ding ahi, ajeh chu O Pathen Pathen nangma na kitepna a na thon ngaipoi. 
Keiman nangma thangvahna la hi kipah tah a thongleuva ka sah ding ahi, ajeh chu nangin neihunglhatdoh tan ahi. 
Keiman nangma thilpha bol ho hi sun nilhum a ka seiphongle ding ahi, ajeh chu keima eibolsego chun jachatna le jumna atoh tauvin ahi.
