La Bu.
73.
Yahweh Pakai chu Isreal te din aphai, hitobanga lungthimtheng ho dinga chun. 
Keima din vang, kalamjotna beikoncha bou ahitai. Kalamlhong anal in lhulham pai ding kahitai. 
Ajeh chu keiman mikiletsahho gitlou bol pum hijongleu akhangtou u kamuteng ka otchalheh jin ahi. 
Amaho hi atahsa'u aphatheiyin, adamthei un, ahattheiyun lungkhamna imacha neilou dan'in aum jiuve. 
Amaho hin midang bangin boina imacha aneipouvin midang ho'n aneiyu lunggimna ama hon imacha anei pouve. 
ama hon kiletsahna songmantam a kisem khivui bangin aki ouvin, gitlouna chu pon bangin akisil'un ahi. 
Hiche miboltheiho hin alungsung uva angaichat chan'u anei un ahi. 
ama hon mi jahda dingngen aseijun kiletsah tah in midang chu suhmang ding ago uvin ahi. 
ama hon van douna'n thu asei uvin a-o cheng'un leiset pumpi asamsesoh jeng'un ahi. 
Hiti chun mipi ho'n athusei u hi ajah uleh akicha uvin akisulungdong gam tauvin ahi. 
Pathen Pathen'in ipi ahet am? “Hatchungnung in thilsohho hi ahena dem?” atiuve. 
Hiche migilouho hi veuvin hinkhonom amang un ahao tultul jeng'un ahi. 
Imacha phachomlou dinga lungthim theng ka put ham? Themmona beihel'a hinkho ka man hi mohseh ham? 
Nilhumkei in imacha phatchomna ka mupon hahsatna ngen ka mu jo'n ahi, jingkah seh in lunggimna ngen in eilonvuh in ahi. 
Keiman hitia hi ka seikhah a ahileh na mi ka hilou hijenginte. 
Hijeh chun keiman miphalouho hi i-atileh khangtou cheh cheh u ham ti hetchet dingin ka khol'in ahi. Ahinla iti hahsat hitam? 
Hichun O Pathen Pathen, nangma muntheng'a ka lut'in ahileh ajona in migiloute akhankho diu chu kahin hedoh tan ahi. 
Tahbeh in nang'in amaho hi lhuh nathei munjeng a nakoi ahin alhuh gam nadiu a kiling gih geh songpi chung a nakoi joh ahi. 
Chomkah lou in amaho amahthahgam tauvin ahi, tijatna'n alhohmang gam tauve. 
O Yahweh Pakai nangma na hung kipatdoh tengleh nang'in amaho ngolna chu na nuisat ding ahi, mikhat in jingkah lang'a amang anuisat tobang bou ahi. 
Hijou chun ka lungsung analheh jengin, hiti chun ka sunggil lang achiplha jengin ahi. 
Keima vang mingol le thilhethemlou kana hi-Nang a ding chun khohephalou gamsa tobang bep ka na hi bouve. 
Henge keima nang'a ka hi jing'e, nangman ka jetlang na tuh jingnai. 
Na chihna'n eikaihoi jingin, munloupi tah chu neilhut ding ahi. 
Van'a nang tailou koidangba ka nei ding ham? Leiset chung'a thil dang jouse sang'in jong kalung in na ngaicha joi. 
Ka tahsa bei henlang ka lhagao lhadai jongleh, Pathen Pathen chu ka lungthim'a ka hatna ahijing'in Ama hi tonsot a kei a ahijing'e. 
Ama dalhaho jouse chu mahthah ding ahiuve, ajeh chu nangma dalhaho jouse chu nangman na sumang jin ahi. 
Ahinla keima dingin Pathen Pathen kom nai hi iti phat a hitam! Keiman vang Yahweh Pakai Lengchungnung chu ka kiselna ahijing e, chule keiman na thilbol kidang ho hi mijouse ka seipeh ding ahi.
