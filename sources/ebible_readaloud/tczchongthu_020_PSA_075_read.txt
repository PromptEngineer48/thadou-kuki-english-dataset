La Bu.
75.
O Pathen Pathen nangma kahin thangvah e! Yahweh Pakai nangma nailama naum jeh in kahin thangvah uve. Muntin a um mipi ten nathilbol kidangtah ho aphongdoh un ahi. 
Pathen Pathen'in asei e, keiman thutanna phat khat kagong e, keiman dihtah a thu katan ding ahi. 
Leiset hi kihot linghenlang mihemte tija jonglehu leiset bulpi tuhchah jingding chu kahi. 
“Keiman mikiletsah ho kagih-in ‘kiletsah datan!’ Migiloulou ho kom'achun, ‘nakhut tumjel hihin!’ 
Vanho dounan nakhut tum jel hihin, ahilouleh nahsah loutah in thu seihih in” kati. 
Ajeh chu, leiset chunga koimacha solam a kon hihen lhumlam-a kon hijongleh chuleh gamthip noi a kon hijongleh kihisah tah a akhuttum ajel louding aphai. 
Ajeh chu thutan vaihom-a hi Pathen Pathen Ama bou ahin, Ama hin koipen ding doh ding ham ahilouleh koipen lhuding ham gonga Ama bou ahi. 
Ajeh chu Yahweh Pakaijin juphon gimnamtuitoh kihal chu akhutna khonkhat in achoiye. Aman hiche ju chu thutanna mun a sunglut in, hichu migilou chengin adon teitei diu adontheng heldiu ahi. 
Hinla keima vangin, Pathen Pathen thil bol ho hi ka phondoh jengding; Jacob Pathen Pathen henga vahchoila kasha jingding ahi. 
Ajeh chu Pathen Pathen'in asei e “Keiman miphalouho thahatna lai chu kasuhchip ding ahinla michonphaho thahatna kahatsah beding ahi” ati.
