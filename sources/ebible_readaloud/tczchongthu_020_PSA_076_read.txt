La Bu.
76.
Judah a Pathen Pathen hi akijabol in, Israel a Amin hi aloupin ahi. 
Jerusalem hi achenna ahin; Zion molchu a inn ahi. 
Hicheachun agal mite meikong tobang thalchang chu asubong in, a umphou le achemjamhou chuleh agalmachah houjouse jong asuhchip pehin ahi. 
Nangma hi tonsot a um jing mol ho sang'in jong naloupi in nalamdang deh e. 
Eidou u galmi hangpenho jouse jong akisugep gam tauve. A imumil banginkamasang uvah akijamdel tauve. Galmi koiman kamasang uvah akhutnu alamp ngapchat lou diu ahitai. 
O Jacob Pathen Pathen nakam sunga nahaina hu chu ahung gindoh chun asakol jouse leh akangtalai jouseu akolchah sah soh jengin ahi. 
Nangma hitobang tah-a ginle ja naumhi datmo ding ahipoi. Ajeh chu nalunghan ahung deujah leh koiham na-anga ding ngam dingah? 
Van'a kon'in nagal mite chunga thu nahin tan in, leiset hi akithingin na-angsung'ah adingin ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai nangma nadingdoh-in mi engsete thu natan in leiset abol genthei a umte nahuhdohjin ahi. 
Mihemte hi nangma doumah a achonteng hichu naloupina kilandohna ahijon ahi, ajeh chu hiche chu nangma manchah-a pangbep ji ahi. 
Na Pakai Yahweh, na Pathen Pathen heng a kitepna neiyin lang hichu nit'in, mijousen kichat umtah Pathen Pathen kom'a pehdingdol hinpecheh hen. 
Ajeh chu Aman leng chate kiletsahna avochip in, lengten jong Ama ginle jana aneiyun ahi.
