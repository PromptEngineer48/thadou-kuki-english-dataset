La Bu.
77.
Keiman Yahweh Pakai kakouvin; henge, kasaptho jengin ahi. Oh, Pathen Pathen'in eingaipeh tei inte! 
Keiman hahsatna kato teng Yahweh Pakai chu kaholjin ahi. Jankhovah in vanlang sangin kakhut katungin kataojin ahi. Ahinla kalhagao in olna amujoupoi. 
Keiman Pathen Pathen kagelin apanpina ngaichatnan eilonvuh-in kamaojin ahi. 
Nangman jankhovah in nei imutsahpon ahi, ajeh chu keima kalung ahesoh behsehin kataotheipoi! 
Keiman phat sottah chetasa hinkholui nopsahna chengchu kageldohjin, 
kipana dimsetna vahchoina la ana kisahna janho chu kalhagao lungthim a kakholchil tengleh tuhin masangtoh akibah louna kageldohjin ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai hin a-itih a dia eipaidoh mong hitantem? Ama hin khotona eimulou mong mong ding hitam? 
Angailutna longlouchu chemang monga hitam? Akitepna ho chu lolhing lou mong ding hitam? 
Pathen Pathen'in eihepina chu asuhmil mong hitam? Eihepina kotchu akha mong hitam? 
Hiti hin keiman kaseijin, “hiche hi kachan ding mongchu ahi, chungnungpen'in ei engbol ahi,” kati. 
Ahinla O Yahweh Pakai nanatoh ho hi kahin geldohin, tuma sang peh-a nanatoh kidangtah ho chu kageldohin ahi. 
Hiche ho chu ka lungsunga amil thei pon ahi. Nanatoh loupitah ho chu gellouvin kaum thei jipon ahi. 
O Pathen Pathen nalampi hi atheng e. Nangma tobang patheng dang um mong nantem? 
Nanghi datmo kidangtah Pathen Pathen chu nahi ahi! Nangin chidang namdang te lah-a nabol theina gimneitah chu nakilandohsah ahitai. 
Naban thahatna a nami Jacob leh Joseph chilhah te chu nahuhdoh ahitai. 
O Yahweh Pakai twikhanglensan chun namu phat in twichun nahinven akithingtan ahi! Twikhanglen chu asunglang ho ngeiyin akithingin ahi. 
Meilhang in go ahin juhlhahsah in, van gin kithong chun vanthamjol chu agin akithonsah in ahi. Nakolphe thalchang chun ahin salvah in ahi. 
Chimpei lah-a kon chun van gin ahung kithongin kolphe chun vannoi leiset ahin salvah in ahi! Leiset chu akithingin akihot-e. 
Nalampi chu twikhanglen'ah akilhunglutnin ahi, Nakeng nung twilah ajong akigollut e, ahinlah nakhonung koima chan amudoh joupoi! 
Nangin kelngoi chingpa hina in Mose le Aaron mangcha in namite hi kelngoi honbangin napuilen ahi.
