La Bu.
78.
O kamite ho, ka thuhil hi ngaiyun. Kathusei a hin nakol'u hinsungun, 
ajeh chu keiman thulem a nahenga thu kaseiding ahi, keiman achesa thilsoh ho-a kon a kahin hilding nahi, 
eihon thusim ijahsa houleh ihetsahou, thusim ipu ipateuva kon a hung kiseilhapeh ho ahi. 
Eihon hiche thutah ho hi icha inaoteu i-impeh loudiu, Yahweh Pakai natoh loupi tahtah ho hi, athaneina le akidanna ho hi ikhangthah houhi iseipehdiu ahi. 
Ajeh chu Aman Jocob kom'a Dan anapeh-a, Israel kom'a thuhil ho anapeh ahi. Aman ipu ipateu atu achateu ahil diuva thupeh anapeh ahin, 
hitiachu ahunglhung ding khangthah ten ahet nadiuleh ahung pengloulai chapang ho dinga, chuleh aman amaho chapangte ahilskonna diuva ahi. 
Hitiachu khangthah ahunglhun channa Pathen Pathen a kinepna thah aneisah nadiu, Yahweh Pakai natoh kidang thil loupi ho chu asuhmillou nadiu chuleh athupehho anitjing nadiuva ahiljing diu ahi. 
Hiteng chuleh ipi iputeu tobang talou a lungtahna, kiphinna, kitah louna Pathen Pathen a alungthim'u pehnom louna anei lou diu ahitai. 
Ephraim galsat hon thalchang akipoh vangun akinung hei un galmun a kon'in ajam tauve. 
ama hon Pathen Pathen toh akitepnau ajuitapouvin ama thuhil dinpi ding anom tapouvin ahi. 
Pathen Pathen'in ipi abolpeh uham thil kidangtah tah anabol ho chu, 
napu napa teu dinga thil kidang anatohdoh ho, Egypt gamsungle Zoan phaicham chunga, atohdohho asumil tauvin ahi. 
Ajeh chu Aman twikhanglen ana homkhen in twichu bang bangin aki khen in amaho anapui galkaiyun ahi. 
Sunleh meilhang in anapuijin, janleh meikong in anapuijin ahi. 
Aman gamthip gamm a songpia kon'in adondiu twi anaputdoh sah-in ahi. 
Aman songpia konna twichu vadung bangin analondohsah in ahi. 
Ahijeng vangin neldi gam achun ama hon chonset anabol'un hatchungnung dounan ana kiphin'un ahi. 
ama hon alungsung uchu lungtahna neipumin Pathen Pathen ana patep un neh ding anathumun ahi. 
ama hon Pathen Pathen Ama tah dounan thu anaseiyun “Pathen Pathen'in gamthip gam'ah an eipejou pouve” atiuve. 
“Henge, songchu ajep'un twichu aputdoh nai, ahinla amite changlhah le sa me eipejou pouve” atiuve. 
Pathen Pathen'in hiche hi ajahdoh phatnin alunghang lheh jengin, Jacob dounan alunghan na mei akoudoh jengin ahi! Hiti hin Israel douna a lunghanna ahung koudoh jengin ahi, 
ajeh chu ama hon Pathen Pathen atahsan pouvin chuleh amaho akhoukhah diuvin jong ana tahsan pouvin ahi. 
Pathen Pathen'in chungleng vanthamjol thu apen vankot jouse jong ahongdoh in, 
Aman aneh dingun van a kon'in Mana gojuh in ahin juhlhah sah in van changlhah chu ahinpen ahi. 
ama hon vantil buchu aneuvin, Pathen Pathen'in alungna chimsetnun apen ahi. 
Aman solam huichu ahin nunsah in, Athahatnan lhanglang huijong anunsah in ahi. 
Aman aneh dingun sachu vutdi jat ahin juhlhah sahin, vachaho chu twikhanglen panga neldi jat ahin, 
Aman vachaho chu aponbuh sungu leh avel uvah ahin lenlhah sah in ahi. 
ama hon oivasetin aneuvin ahi. Angaichat dungjuitah in apen ahi. 
Hinla ama hon angaichat dungjuiya anehva masangu, akam sung uva sa aumpet jeng jong chun, 
Pathen Pathen lunghana achung uvah ahunglhungtan ahileh Aman alah uva ahatpen pen ho chu athat tan ahi. Israel khangthah anasapen ho chu athat gamtan ahi. 
Ahijeng vangin mipi ho chun chonset aboljom jingun ahi, thilkidang ijat abol vangin atahsan dehpouvin ahi. 
Hiti chun ahinkhou chu lhuhgam na ahitan akum khouchu kichatnan adimtai. 
Pathen Pathen'in amaho ahin thapat phatnun ama hon ajona langin Ama chu ahin holtauvin ahi. Kisihna ahin neitauvin Pathen Pathen ahin holtauvin ahi. 
Hiti chun Pathen Pathen chu asongpiu ahi chuleh Pathen Pathen chu hatchungnung le ahuhhing pao ahi ahin geldoh tauvin ahi. 
Ahijeng vangin hichu kamnal a anaseijiu ahibouvin alei u mangchan jou anaseiyun ahi. 
Alung sungu chu Pathen Pathen a ana petheng pouvin Pathen Pathen toh anakitep nauva jong ana dingdet pouvin ahi. 
Hinla lungsetna dim Pathen Pathen chun achonset nau anangai damun amaho chu ana sumang deh pouvin ahi. Phat atamjon alunghan na anatuh tangin hiche bangchun ima ana tongdoh pon ahi. 
Tahsa mihembep ahiuve ti ahejingin, hui anun leh heikhat kahbou ahin ahung kiletapoi ti ageldoh in ahi. 
Gamthip sunga ijatvei Ama douna ana kiphin jengu chuleh alungsung anatsah vangin, 
Avel vel in Pathen Pathen thohhat nachu apatep jiuvin Israel Pathen Pathen chu asulunghang jiuvin ahi. 
ama hon athahatna in agalmi houva kon a anahuhdoh u hi ageldoh joutapouve. 
Egypt gamsunga thil kidang ijat abolu hitam chuleh Zoan phaicham a athil kidang bol ho chu ageldoh tapouvin ahi. 
Vadungho thisan asosah a don mohella alha, vadung twi donmo hella alha jengchu. 
Pathen Pathen'in amaho lah a thousi vaibong alansah a, uphohte alansah a agam ujong a suh set peh chu ahepha tapouve. 
Aman achang jouseu khongbai aneh gamsah in alousoh jouseu jong khaokhopi anehgam sah e. 
Alengpithei jouseu gel avohlhah sah sohkei in, atheipiu phung jouseu jong daibang athagamsah in ahi. 
Agancha jouseu gel lah a dalhah in aumsah in, akelngoi houjong keh alhah lihsah sohtai. 
Achung uva nasatah in alunghangin, alung dammona a lunghanna, mi sumang vantil hon tamtah alhahkhum tan ahi. 
Aman alunghanna achung uvah achuh sahtan, Egypt mite hinkho anahing hoipon, amangthah gamtan ahi, 
Egypt insunga achapa tahpen cheh athat in Egypt gamsung pumpia khangdongte pahcha amolsahtai. 
Hinla Aman amite chu kelngoi honbangin apuiyin gamthip noija bitkei in apui galkai tan ahi. 
Aman mongtah in apuilen hijeh chun ama hon kichatna anei pouve, chujongleh twikhanglen in agalteu alhoh mangpeh gamtai. 
Amaho chu Pathen Pathen'in agamtheng abanjetna atundoh, molsang vum achun ahin puilut in, 
Amaho masangah namtin vaipi adelmang peh sohkeiyin, abonchauvin achan dingu gam ahoppeh chehin, Israel phung namtin abonchauvin ama ama ponbuh cheh a achensah tai. 
Ahijeng vangin ama hon hatchungnung Pathen Pathen dounan thil abol jingun athupeh ho khatcha anitpeh pouvin ahi. 
Apu apateu bangin avah mang-uvin jou jengin na atonguvin ahi. Amaho chu thalpi janglou bangin akitah pouvin ahi. 
ama hon pathen dang ho doiphung aphutdoh un Pathen Pathen alunghan sah uvin, chuleh semthu pathen ahoujeh un Pathen Pathen athangthip sahtauvin ahi. 
Pathen Pathen'in ajahdoh phat in hatah in alunghangin Israel chu pumpai in apaimangtan ahi. 
Pathen Pathen'in Shiloh achenna mun adalhan mihemte lah a aumna ponbuh chu adalhatai. 
Aman aloupina le athaneina hetna Kitepna Thingkhong chu galmiho khutna apehdohtan ahi. 
Ama amite chunga alunghan jeh-in agal miteu athasah gamtan ahi. 
Gollhangho galah athigam tauvin, nungah ho akichenpidiu aumtapon ahi. 
Thempute athat gamtauvin ameithai houvin alhasetpi thei tapouvin ahi. 
Ajonan Yahweh Pakai chu a-ihmua ahung khangdoh bangin ahung thoudoh in mihatpa hung kiltildoh bangin ahung kitildohin, 
Galmi ho chu anungno in a-itih a ding jumna atosah tan ahi. 
Ahinla Joseph chilhah ho chu apaidohin, chuleh Ephraim phungchu alheng tapon ahi. 
Aman akhellin Judah leh angailut Zion molchu alhengin, 
hichea chun vanho san banga sang, leiset banga chah a-itih a um jing dingin Hou-In khat atungdoh tan ahi. 
Aman asohpa David chu akelngoi chinna a kon'in anakoudoh'in analheng tan ahi. 
Aman kelngoipi le anou achin laijin akoudoh'in amite Israel Jacob chilhah ho chu kelngoi chinna ching dingin anapansah tan ahi. 
David in amaho chu lungtheng tah leh chingtheitah in analamkaiyin ahi.
