La Bu.
79.
O Pathen Pathen chidang namdang ten nanga dinga nanakilhendoh nagamchu ahinlo tauvin, na Hou-In theng asuboh'un chuleh Jerusalem chu khogemsa abahsah tauvin ahi. 
ama hon nasohte tahsa chu vantham jolla lengle vachate adalhahpeh tauvin, pathen mi thengte tahsaphe chu gamlah a ganhingho an le chahin apangtan ahi. 
Jerusalem kimvellah thisan hi twilon in along jengin mithi vuiding koimacha aumpoi. 
Kaheng kakomho noise ding leh kajet kavei a umho dingin noise le taitomin kaumtauve. 
O Yahweh Pakai phat ichan geija kachung uva nalunghan ding hitam? Tonsot geijin em? Phat ichan geija nathangthipna chu meikong banga aljeng dingham? 
Yahweh Pakai nalunghanna chu nang na donnom loute chidang namdang ho leh naminpan nomloute lenggam ho chungah bunglhatan. 
Ajeh chu ama hon namite Israel te hi avallhum gamtauvin chuleh gamsung hi gamgem asodohsah tauvin ahi. 
Kapu kapateu chonset jehin keiho neithep mosah hihbeh un! Nami khotonan gangtah in kangaichatnu neisuh bulhitpeh un, achutilouleh kakinepnau beiding ahitai. 
O Pathen Pathen kasochatnau, neipanhu loiyun! namin loupina dingin neipanhuvin, kachonset nau ngaidam inlang neihuhdoh'un namin loupina dingin. 
Ipi dinga chidang namdang te hin eihouset uva “A Pathen u Pathen hoilai a umham?” natisah nahlai dingham? Hiche chidang namdang ho chunga naphulahna chu neimusahih in, ajeh chu amaho hin nasohte thisan aso u ahi. 
Sohchangho lhasetna hi ngaipehin, thi dinga athu kitansa ho hi huhdoh inlang na thaneina chu neivet sah'un. 
O Yahweh Pakai kaheng kakom hon noise tah a nahousetnao hi ajat sagin lethuh tei in. 
Hiteng chuleh keiho namite na ngailutna ankonga kivah nakelngoiten akhang khangin nang vahchoi ungting, vahchoijing taovinge.
