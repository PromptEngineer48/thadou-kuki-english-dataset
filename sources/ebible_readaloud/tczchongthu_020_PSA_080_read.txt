La Bu.
80.
O Israel neingaipehun, nangma Joseph chilhah te gancha hon ho banga puihoija kelngoi chin a chingpa. O Pathen Pathen Cherub chunga laltouna a toupan naloupinavah hoi chu hin kimudoh sah in. 
Ephraim, Benjamin leh Mannaseh chungah hintah langin, nathahatna gimneitah chu neimusah unlang neihung huhdoh tauvin. 
O Pathen Pathen nangma kom'ah neihoilutnun, namaivah chun neihin salvah un, chuteng boule huhhing kachandiu ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai, Pathen ka Pathen, phat itihchan gei a kataona sanlou a nakoi dingham? 
Nangin lungkhamna a ichan gei a neivah uva chuleh khondimset a mitlhi neilonsah diuham? 
Nangin hengle kom chidang namdang te taitomna neichansah tauvin kagalmi teuvin totnan einei tauve. 
O Pathen Pathen hatchungnung nangma kom neingatsah un, namaivah chun neisalvah'un chutenglehbou huhhing kachandiu ahi. 
Nangin lengpiga twi bangin Egyptma kon'in neihin puidohun, nangin chidang namdang ho nadel mangna na gamsunga neipuilutnu ahi. 
Nangman keiho ding amun neisuhtheng pehun chuleh hichea chun keiho kajung khouvin gamsung chu kadimset tauve. 
keiho len molsangho atomjouvin, kabah houvin Cedar thingphung lentah tahho atomjouvin, 
keihon kabah hou lhumlam Mediterranean twikhanglen gei in kathejalun chuleh kajungun Euphrate vadung gei ka thejallun ahi. 
Hinla tunvang, kabangu ipi dinga neivoh lhahpeh uva, hilang lampia hinhopa jousen kagao akiguh u hitam? 
Gammanga kon'in ngalchang hangin avallhum in, gamlah ganchahon kivahnan anei un ahi. 
Hungkile kit in O Pathen Pathen hatchungnung, kahung tao un ahi. Van'a kon'in neihin veuvin kahahsat nau neihin vetpeh un, hiche lengpi gui hi hin khoukhah'in. 
Nangma tah in nanatu ahi. Hiche chapa hi nangma tah in nangma dinga nahin khoukhah ahi. 
Ajeh chu keiho hi thing banga eikitan bong bonga kagal miteuvin eihal lhah'u ahitai. Nangman navetsah chu amutengu leh manthahnan juiju hen. 
Mihem na ngailut nacha nadeilhen chu hatsah-in, 
Chutengleh keihon itihchan injong nanungsun pouvinge. Nei suhal kit un chutileh namin avel'a kahin koukit theidiu ahi. 
Nangma komlang nei ngatsah kitnun, O Yahweh Pakai, Pathen ka Pathen hatchungnung namaivah chun neihin salvah'un, chutenglehbou huhhing kachandiu ahi.
