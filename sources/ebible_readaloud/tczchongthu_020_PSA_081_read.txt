La Bu.
81.
Ei thahatsah u Pathen Pathen thangvahnan la sauvin, Jacob Pathen Pathen vahchoinan la sauvin. 
Sauvin! tumging tothon avahchoila sauvin voh gingthei vouvin, semjang kisai ginleh selangdah gin to thouvin, 
Lhathah janle sumkon mut-uvin lang, lhalih janle lhalih nikhoa golvah bolteng sumkon mut uvin. 
Ajeh chu hiche hi Israel dinga chondan thupeh ahin Jacob Pathen Pathen thugahsa ahi. 
Egypt gam a kon a ahungdoh uchun Aman Joseph kom'a hettohsah khat anaphutdoh tai. Keiman kahetchetlou thukhat kajan, 
Keiman apohgih kangai jangin akhut a jong thilgih kangai jangin akhut a jong thilgih hahsa kathamsah tapoi. 
Nagimna a kon'in neihin kouvin keiman kahuhdohtai. Meivom lhangkhat kiheh chetchut lah a kahin houjin, Meribah a twi umloupet in natahsah kapatepjin ahi. 
Ngai uvin O kamite, nangho gihna nasatah kapeh pet-hin, Vo Israel kaseihi nangai poupou uleh, 
Nanghon imatihchan ajong gamchom mit pathen koipouvin nate, pathen lhem masanga kun pouvin nate. 
Ajeh chu Egypt-a kon a nahin puidoh uva Yahweh Pakai na Pathen'u Pathen keima kahi. Nakam hakatnin ka-in keiman thilpha tamtah adimsetna kahin sunlut pehding ahi. 
Ahinla ka miten angainom pouvin, Israel'in Keima eingaicha pouve. 
Hijeh chun Keiman alouchallu dungjuijin amaho deilam lam a chondin kalha kangtai. 
O ka miten kaseihi ngaipou pouleu, O Kamite Israel te hin eijuiju henlang kalampia hin chenom poupouleu! 
Ichan geija gang in agal miteu kajopeh tadiuvem! Ichan geija gangtah in akidoupiteu chunga kakhut kalha tadem! 
Koi hileh Yahweh Pakai nahsah loute chu a-angsunga kisuchipdiu atonsotna mahthahdiu ahi. 
Ahinla keiman nehthei aphapen a kavah ding nahin, keiman songpia kon a kimu khoijua kavahdiu nahi.
