La Bu.
82.
Van'a thutanna chunga Pathen Pathen'in vai ahomin Van mite chunga athutanna aphondoh e. 
Nangman itihchan hi miphalouho napanpia thudihlou ho nachelhah sah dingham? 
“Chaga leh vaichate chungah thudih chepin lang; tahlel tele engbolla umte chungah thudih phongdoh in. 
Panpi bei tele engbolla umte huhdoh in, miphalouho khut a kon'in huhdoh-in. 
Hinla mibolse ten imacha ahepouve; amahokhu hetna lhasam ahiuve. Muthim noi a avah leuvin, leiset sungil lamgei in ahotling tai. 
Keiman kasei in, ‘Nangho Pathen nahiuve; nangho hatchungnung chilhah te nahiu ve. 
Hinlah nangho mihem tobang bep a nathi diu, thunei vaihom dang ho banga nalhuh tei diu ahi.’” 
Hung kipatin, O Pathen Pathen vannoi leiset hi athu tannin, ajeh chu chidang namdang ho jouse jonghi aboncha nanga ahi.
