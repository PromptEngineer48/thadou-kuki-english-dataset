La Bu.
84.
O Yahweh Pakai hatchungnung naHou-In hi iti kangailut hitam? 
Hiche a chu iti kaum nom hitam! Yahweh Pakai Hou-Inn chu kangaicha lheh jenge, kahina apumpin hingjing Pathen Pathen ah kakipana lan sange. 
Inva jengin jong abuh akisah in, chuleh pengpalep injong intum akison e. Vo Yahweh Pakai hatchungnung, kalengpa le ka Pathen Pathen! Vachan jong namaicham kom'a anou kicholdo nading abuh akisem ahi. 
NaHou-Inn'a chenghon ichan gei a hinkho nopna anei'u hitam phat tin in nangma vahchoila asauve. 
Athahatna Yahweh Pakai a kon a kimuho hinkho iti nop a hitam, alungsungun Jerusalem'ah akholjing jengun ahi. 
Beca phaicham ajot tengu jongleh, twisam putna twinah tampi umna bangin abol'un, gotwi hunglha masapen in jong twikul tampi ahinsemdoh pehjiuve. 
Amaho akalson chan uva ahetbe cheh chehjingdiu ahin, ama hon Jerusalem a Yahweh Pakai chu amudiu ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai, Ka Pathen Pathen hatchungnung kataona neingaipeh'in, O Jacob Pathen Pathen. 
O Pathen Pathen kalengpauhi phatthei boh'in, ama hi kaluum u ahi, nathaonusa nalhen pahi khotona musah jingin. 
Munchom a ni sangkhat umsangin na Hou-In a nikhat um aphajoi. Miphalou inna chen sangin ka Pathen Pathen'inn'a kotngah a pan aphajoi. 
Ajeh chu Yahweh Pakai, Pathen Pathen hi eiho dia nisa le umpho ahi. Aman khoto na le loupina eipeuvin ahi. Yahweh Pakaijin thildih jeng bol mihoa kon'in thilpha alamang pon ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai hatchungnung nangma tahsan miho hi iti kipa um a hitam?
