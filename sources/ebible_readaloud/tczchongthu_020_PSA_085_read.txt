La Bu.
85.
Yahweh Pakai nangin na gamsung'ah phatthei bohna nasunglhan ahi! Hiti chun nangin Israel phatthei naboh'in ahi. 
Nangman namite chonsetna nangaidam in hichun achonset nau jouse na lekhu peh e. 
Nangin na lunghan na natuh tang'in, na lunghanna deujah jengchu naki kham jie. 
Ei suhal kit uvin, eihuhhingpau O Pathen Pathen! kachung uva nalunghan na tolmang kittan. 
Kachung uva nalunghan jing jeng dingham? Na lunghanna khang jouse chung naumsah jing jengding hitam? 
Nei suhhal kit loudiu hitam? Namite kipa le thanom a aum jing theina ding a. 
Vo Pathen Pathen nami ngailutna longlou chu neimu sah'in nami huhhing najong neipe uvin. 
Pathen na Pathen chun ipi asei am, chingthei tah in ngai in ajeh chu Aman mi kitah ho kom'ah chamna thu asei e. Ahinla amaho angolna lampi uvah kilekit dahen. 
Tahbeh in Ama gingte dingin huhing na naicha um a ahi. Chuti leh igamsung u jong aloupina dimding ahi. 
Ngailutna longlou leh thutah akimuto'in chonphat na le chamna akichopto lhon e. 
Kitah nahi leiset a kon a hungdoh ahi. Chuleh chonphatna chu van'a kon a mai lhaisel'a hung kumlha ahi. 
Adih'e, Yahweh Pakaijin phatthei boh hi ahin lonlhahsah ahin gam leiset a hi ninglhing tah a ahin gadoh sah a ikisan ji u ahi. 
Chonphat nahin Yahweh Pakai hung nading semtoh nading nikho asap masat ji ahi.
