La Bu.
86.
O Yahweh Pakai hung kisunem'in lang kataona neingai peh'in, neidonbut in ajeh chu napanpi ngaicha jing kahi. 
Yahweh Pakai nanga dinga kiphaldoh jing kahi neihuhdoh'in ajeh chu nangma hi ka Pathen Pathen nahin, keima hi nakinbol kahin chuleh nangma hi katansan jingin ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai nami khotona neimusah jingin ajeh chu keiman Nangmahi ka koujing nahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai kipana neimusah jingin ajeh chu keimahi Nangkom'a kiphaldoh jing kahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai nangma hi naphatna jalla eingaidam jing nahi, nakom'a panpi ngaichaho dinga longlou ngailutna dimsah nahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai kataonahi khotonan neingaipeh'in gangtah a kap kahi neingaipehin. 
Kahahsat channa nangma kakou ding ahin nangin neidonbut jingding ahi. 
O Pathen Pathen! Semthu pathen ho nang tobang khatcha aumpoi, nabolthei ho bolthei jong aumpoi. 
Naemsa chitin namtin ho jong na ang a hungdiu chibai naboh cheh diu ahi Yahweh Pakai; ama hon na mintheng avahchoi diu ahi. 
Ajeh chu nangma bou Pathen Pathen nahin naloupin, nangin bou thil loupiho naboldoh jin ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai, na lampiho neihil in, nathudih dung jui a hinkho kaman theina dingin, nangma loupina kadopsang jingna dingin lungtheng neipen. 
O Yahweh ka Pakai, Pathen ka Pathen! kalungthim pumpin nangma nathangvah ingkate, phattin'in naloupina domsang jing tange. 
Ajeh chu nei ngailutna hi akidange. Nangman thina kulkhuh a kon'in nei huhdoh tai 
O Yahweh Pakai mijumso hatho keima dounan ahung ding doh un; misunoh phah a pang hon thading eigouve. Ama hon nangma hi imachan nagel pouve. 
Ahinlah Yahweh Pakai Nangma hi khoto na leh ngailutna nei Pathen Pathen nahin lunghang vahlou chuleh kitahna leh tang theilou lungsetna dim a nahi. 
Nami lungsetna chun noilang hinven lang nasoh hi tha neipen, nasohpa chapa kahi neihuhdoh'in. 
Nei hepina melchihna chu hin tahlang'in chutileh eivetda ho hin jumna amudiu ahi. O Yahweh Pakai neipanpin nei lungmonsah jingin.
