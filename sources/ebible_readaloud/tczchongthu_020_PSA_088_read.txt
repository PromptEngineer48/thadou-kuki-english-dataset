La Bu.
88.
O Yahweh Pakai, eihuhhing'a ka Pathen Pathen, sun leh nangma kakou in, jan leh nakom kahin nailut ji e. 
Tun kakouna neijah peh'in lang katona neisan peh'in. 
Ajeh chu kahin kho hi boina adim in, thinan eikainai chehcheh e. 
Athisa bang bep kahitai, mithahat tah khat athahat na bei nungsang bep kahitai. 
ama hon mithi lah a eidalha uvin lhan ah mithi lup'in kalum e. Keima hi suhmil in kaumtai, nami kaihoina a kon'in eiki lamang tai. 
Nangin kotong noinung pen athim pen ah neison lha e. 
Nalung han na gihtah chun eidelphan, hichu twi kinong bangin akinong'in eichup tai. 
Keima hi doumah in neisem in kagol kapai ho neidelmang peh gamtai. Keima hi thang'ah ka-oh in kitoldoh theilouvin kaum'e. 
Kamit lhin kamit achupmin kho muthei louvin kaum'e. O Yahweh Pakai niseh'in na panpina kathum in nami lungsetna ngaichan kakhut teni kahin sange. 
Nathil kidang bol ho hi mithi dinga phachom ding hinam? Mithi hungthodoh a nathangvah theiding hinam? 
Lhankhuh a kon'in ama hon nami ngailutna longlou hi ahin phongdoh theina diuvem? Suhmangna mun'a umhon nakitah nahi aphongdoh theina diuvem? 
Muthim'in nathilbol kidang ho hi aseiphong nadiuvem? Thil suhmil delna gama umhon na chonphat na ho chu aseiphong theina diuvem? 
O Yahweh Pakai nangma kakouve, niseh-a nakom'a katao jingding ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai, ibolla kei nei paidoh hitam? Ibol a kei a kon a naki heimang hitam? 
Kakhang don lai a pat a, kanatna hin thina komcha eina lhutsa ahitan, nagim neina masanga hin thabei leh lungkham tah in kading e. 
Nalunghan na tijat umtah chun eilonvuh'in, tijat naumna chun eithabei sah e. 
Hiche ho chun sun nilhum in twisoh bangin kavel a eilon khum un ahi. Muthimhi kakinai pipen kagol in apang un ahi. 
Nangman ka mite le ka ngailut cheng neilahpeh tai, Thimjing hi kaki naipi pen ka loi chu ahitai.
