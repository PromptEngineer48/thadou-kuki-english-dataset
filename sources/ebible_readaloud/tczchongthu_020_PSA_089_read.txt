La Bu.
89.
Keiman Yahweh Pakai mi ngailutna longlou thu hi vahchoilan sajing tange, khangdong ho leh tehse hon nakitahna hi ajauvin ahi. 
Nami ngailutna longlou hi itih hijongleh um jing ding ahi. Nakitah nahi vanho bangin aum jinge. 
Yahweh Pakaijin hitin asei e “kalhendoh kasohpa David toh kitepna kanabol ahi. Hiche kitepna a hi kana kihahsel ahi. 
Keiman na chilhah te chu lenga katun jingding ahi. Amaho nalaltouna a chu tu apat tonsot gei a toujingdiu ahi.” 
Yahweh Pakai van mite jousen nakidan naho hi navahchoi diu ahi. Nakitahna ho jeh a hi simjoulou vantil ten navahchoi diu ahi. 
Ajeh chu van gam a umho jouse koipen tah hi Yahweh Pakai toh tekah thei umbeh am? Vantil thupipen ho jeng jong Yahweh Pakai toh tekah pithei um'am? 
Vantil thaneipen ho jong Pathen Pathen ang'a kithing thinga ahi bouve. Ama hi alaltouna vel a ding ho jousen jong agimnei na aphapouve. 
O Yahweh Pakai hatchungnung sepai ho! Hoilai mun a nang tobanga thanei a umbeh am Yahweh Pakai? Nanghi kitahna lhingset nahi. 
Twikhanglen jong na thaneina noia umma ahi. Nangin huipi le twipi kithoa twi kinong jong nasutang'in, 
twikhanglen'a um sa lenpen ho jong na sugep in, Naban thahat in nagal mite jong nahocheh jitai. 
Vanho jouse jong nanga ahin, leiset jong nanga ahi, vannoi a um thiljouse jong aboncha nanga ahin nangin nasem ahi. 
Nangin sahlang lhanglang jong nasem ahin, Tabor Mol leh Hermon mol'in jong naming athangvah ahi. 
Nabantha hi iti hat hitam! Nakhut hi iti hat hitam! Najetlang jonghi nathahat loupitah in adomsange. 
Chonphat na le thudih chu nalaltouna khombul ahi. Longlou ngailutna le kitahna chu nalhachate bangin na angsung'ah avahlen ahi. 
Pathen Pathen houna dinga kipa umtah kouna jaho chu anunnom ahiuve, ajeh chu Yahweh Pakai amaho hi na umpina vah a chu lhalediu ahi. 
Naminthan na kidangtah ho hi ama hon nilhum kei keija akipapi diu ahi. Ama hon nachon phatna hi akithang-atpiu vin ahi. 
Nangma hi amaho dinga thahatna loupitah chu nahi. Keiho thahat sah dinghi nangma kipana ahi. 
Ahi, keiho hoidohna hi Yahweh Pakai a kon ahi. Chuleh Israel Pathen Pathen hin lengjong eipeh u ahi. 
Masang lai in kitah tah a nangma jui namipite lah-a gaothu nana seijin ahi. Nangin nasei in, Keiman galsat ding kanalhengdoh'in keiman milhamho lah a konna lengding jong kanalhendoh ahi. 
Keiman kasoh David kana mudoh'in chuleh kathaotheng kana nun ahi. 
Keiman kakhut a kana dindoh sah a, kaban thahatna kahatdoh sahding ahi. 
Agal miten ajoloudiu ahilouleh miphalou honjong athajou loudiu ahi. 
Keiman akidoupi ho hi amasang tah a kasuhlhuh ding ahin chuleh ama douho jouse jong ka suhmang ding ahi. 
Kakitahna leh kangailutna longlou hin aumpi jingding kathuneina jalla ama hi atha kahatsah jingding ahi. 
Keiman twikhanglen chunga jong athaneina kalhunlut ding vadungho chung jenga jong vai ahomding ahi. 
Chutengleh aman eihin kou intin, “Nangma kapa nahin ka Pathen Pathen, kasongpi le kasochatna nahi” atiding ahi. 
Keiman Ama hi kachapa masapen, leiset chunga leng thaneipen kahisah ding, 
Kangailut ding chuleh tonsot gei a kakhoto ding, ama toh kaki tepna hi angahtih umlouding ahi. 
Keiman ama dinga ason apah ho kahoibit pehding, van gam'a nikhon beitih aneilou banga alaltounan jong beitih aneilouding ahi. 
Ahinlah achilhah ten ka thuhil ho hi apaidoh uva, kachedan hi ajuilou uva, 
ka Dan semho hi anit lou uva chuleh kathupeh hi angailou uleh, 
keiman achonset nau chunga gambol na mol kalap ding, chuleh athungailou naojeh a kavoh diu ahi. 
Hijeng jongleh kangailutna chu beideh louding kakitepna jong achunga kasuhmil deh louding ahi. 
Ahipoi, kakitepna chu kasuhkeh louding ahi; keiman kana seidoh saho chu changkhat jong kanung lahdoh louding ahi. 
Keiman David kom'ah kihahselna khat kana nei in chule atheng kahi jeh in jou seiponge. 
Ama chilhah te itihchan hijongleh chelha jingding alenggam jong nisa banga chelhum ding ahi. 
Vantham jolla ka hettohsah nachu lhavah banga kikheltih aneitah louding ahi. 
Ahinlah nangin tuhin ama chu nakahdoh tan chuleh napaidoh tai. Tuhin Nangma nathaonu lengpa chungah nalunghang tan, 
nangin Ama toh nakitepna napaidohtan, nangin alal lukhuh jong vutvai lah-a nasep lhatai. 
Nangin akhopiu bangho navohlhah pehtan chuleh akulpiu jong khogem nasodohsah ahitai. 
Koi hileh ahin hopa jousen athilkeu u akichomgam hellun aheng akomho jousen anuisat'un ahi. 
Nangin agal miteu galjona namusahin, abonchauvin nakipasah'in ahi. 
Nangin agal manchah u manmo naso'in chuleh galmunnah gal na lelsah'in ahi. 
Nangin aleng tenggol nalahpehin chuleh alaltouna tolsenan naneipeh in ahi. 
Nangin aphat achang louvin natehset sah'in chuleh jumthet in nalhan ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai nangma hi hitia hi kisel jing jingding nahi hitam? Ichan gei nalunghanna meikong jing jeng dingham? 
Kahinkho hi itihchom a hitam geldoh'in, keiho hi thitei dinga neisem'u ahi ti geldoh'in! 
Mihing koihi athi theilou ding um'am? Mihem koipen ham lhan a kon a ahoidohthei dingah? 
Yahweh Pakai tumasanga nei ngailut nau chu ahet theina mudohthei ipi umam? David kom'a nakitepna ho hi hoilang geija hitam? 
Nasohpa keimahi ichan gei a jumna kato hitam? Hiche nangma hedohlou mitehin eisapsetnau ichan gei a kathoh hitam? 
O Yahweh Pakai nadeilhen na lengpa hi nangma dou miten nahsah loutah in abollun ahi! Achena chan a asujum un ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah in um jing jenghen! Amen, Amen!
