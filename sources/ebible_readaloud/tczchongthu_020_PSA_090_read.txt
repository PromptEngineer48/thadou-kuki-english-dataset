La Bu.
90.
Yahweh Pakai, akhang khanga nangmahi kachennao inn nahijenge! 
Moul le lhang ho pen masang, vannoi le leiset hi natundoh masangin, thiljoue kipat a pat ajo na chen in jong, nangma Pathen Pathen nahijing e. 
Nangin mihemhi anahina masa-a kile kitdingin nasol'in leivui na lesosah kitjin ahi. 
Nangdin Kum sangkhat jeng jonghi achejing tunikho bangbep ahin, jan phat chomcha tobang bou ahi. 
Nangin twisoh bangin neilhohmang jiuvin mang sangin asot jodeh poi. Keiho hi hampa jingkah langa hung pahdoh tobang bou kahiuve. 
Hichu akhangdoh loijin ahinla nilhah langle agop lhajin athijitai. 
Keiho hi nangma lunghan nan eisumang jitauvin chuleh nalung satnan eikichatsah'un ahi. 
Nangin kachonset nau namasangah natahlang jin ahi, aguh a kachonset nauhi nakilandoh sahjin ahi. 
Nalungsat na noi a higjing kahinkhou ahin, kakumkhou jong lungsat nan abeiloi jitai. 
Kei ho hi kum 70 hingding bep kahiuvin, kahat tah'uleh kum 80 bep hingding kahiuve, ahinla hiche sunga chun natna le boina bou ahin; amang loiji tauvin, chule keiho kaleng mang tauve. 
Nalunghanna thahat koiham ahe thei dingah? Naging a nalungsat khohje gelkhoh a koi ham? 
Kachih theina diuvin kahinkhou chomdan hi neihetsah tauvin. 
O Yahweh Pakai, kaheng lam uva hung kile kit tan! Itihchan neikho ngai diu hitam? 
Jingkah seh le nalungset longlou chun kalungthim kipasah in, chutile kahinkhou lhumkei a navah choi la kasha jing diu ahi. 
Hesoh genthei kana thoh hou chuga kon'in lunglhai na themkhat beh nei peu vin! Kakhoisat nao kumho jong kipana neisoh doh peh un. 
Nasohte hi nathilbol kidangtah ho chu neimusah'un; kachilhah teuvin na thaneina loupitah chu mu uhen. 
Chuteng Yahweh Pakai ka Pathen Pathen un namalsom nauchu eimu sah uhen lang katohna jouseuvah lolhinna eipetao hen. Henge, katoh gao neiso peh un.
