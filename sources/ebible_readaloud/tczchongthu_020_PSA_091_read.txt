La Bu.
91.
Chungnungpen cholngah na noi a um chan chun hatchungnung lhalim noi a choldo na amu ding ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai chung chang a hiche hi kaphong e: Ama bou eivengbit ahin, kahoidam na mun ahi; Ama chu ka Pathen Pathen ahin, keiman ama katah san e. 
Ajeh chu aman thangkol jouse a kon'in nahuhdoh intin thinathei hivei hoa kon a nahuhdohding ahi. 
Aman alhavinga na le khu ding ahi. A lhalim noi a nahoidoh ding ahi. Kitah tah a akihahsel naho chu nading a galvon le ompho ahi. 
Jan a misu lung linglao ho kichat dan, sun lai a thalchang leng le ho jong kichat dan. 
Jan noi a donthah nantna hivei gimnei tah ho kichat dan, sun lai a nadelkhum hesoh na jong kicha hih in. 
Nasih langa sangkhat lhu henlang, navel a sangsom thijongleh, hiche boina hon nangma natoh khah louding ahi. 
Namit hah in lang vejengin, itilom a migilou te chung a got na lhung hitam? 
Nangin Yahweh Pakai chu napanpi ding a nakisem le, Yahweh Pakai chu nahuhdoh ding a nakigon ahi le, 
Setna imachan najo louding ahi; natna hivei khatchan jong na inn ahinphah louding ahi. 
Chutengleh Pathen Pathen'in navale nachan a navengbit din avantil te thu pen tai. 
Nakeng chu songa nakisuhkhah louna dinga vantil ten akhut uva nadopdiu ahi. 
Nangin keipi bahkai ho nasuh goi a; gul hang ho chuleh gunei gul ho nalhonat gamding ahi. 
Yahweh Pakaijin hitin ati, “keima eingailu ho kahuh hing ding ahi. Kamin a tahsan na nei ho chu kahoi dam ding ahi. 
ama hon eihin koutengu leh keiman kadonbut ding chuleh hahsatna ato tengu leh keiman kaumpi ding ahi, keiman amaho chu kahuhdoh a jabolna kachan sah ding ahi. 
Keiman hinkho sotna kapehding chuleh kavenbit jingdiu ahi” ati.
