La Bu.
92.
Thangvah na Yahweh Pakai peh a, chungnungpen vah choi ding hi thilpha ahi. 
Jingkah le nami ngailutna longlou sap phong a, nilhah le nakitah na sap phong hi thilpha ahi. 
Tumging jangsom to tho a lyre gin ngai tah to tho dia pha tah ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai nei bol peh thil loupitah ho chun kalung lai atongkha in, natohdoh ho jehchun keiman kipa la kasan ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai nanatohdoh ho hi iti loupi hitam! nalunggel ngeito ho jong iti gil a gil jeng hitam. 
Mihem mai mai het jou hoi ahipon, chih na a lhasam hon jong hedoh jou pouvin te. 
Migilou ho chu hamhing bangin khangtou'un tin, thilse bol ho chu pungthei jong leu, tonsot a suhmanga umdiu ahi. 
Hinla Yahweh Pakai, nangma vang, tonsot a dopsang a naum jing ding ahi. 
Namel ma te jouse mangthah tei diu ahi; chule gitlou nabol jouse suh chat vai a um diu ahi. 
Nangin keimahi bongchal bangin neihatdoh sahtan, chuleh nangin kipanan neimalsom tan ahi. 
Kamit hin kagal mite lhuh amun; kana kol in jong eidou a migiloute gallel na thu ajai. 
Michonphate chu chahthing bangin khangtou untin, Lebanon a Cedar thing banga hat tah khangtoudiu ahi. 
Amaho hi Yahweh Pakaijinn a kehdoh thingphung likeh ahiuvin, I-Pathen'u Pathen angsung a khangtouva ahiuve, 
Teh nung jeng a jong ga aso nalai diu; chuleh phattin'a noujing leh hatjing diu ahi. 
ama hon aphondoh uva hitia hi asei diu ahi, “Yahweh Pakai chu adih jingin! Ama chu kasong pi ahi! Ama a chun set na aumpoi!”
