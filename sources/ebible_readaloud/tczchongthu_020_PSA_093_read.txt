La Bu.
93.
Yahweh Pakai hi lengpa ahi! Ama loupi na in a kivon e. Hiti chun Yahweh Pakai chu loupi na le hat nan akivon e. Leiset hi ading det in suh ling hoi ahipoi. 
O Yahweh Pakai nalaltouna chu phat kisim masang a pat a umsa ahitai. Nangma jeng jong phat kipat masanga aum jingsa nahitai. 
O Yahweh Pakai twikhanglen akikhan tou e. Twisoh hi van gin abang tai; twisoh in gih tah in akinon sah e. 
Hinlah twikhanglen kinong ging dem dum sang a nasa jo le, twipi pang a twinong kivoging sang'in jong Yahweh Pakai thilbol theina anasa joi. 
Yahweh Pakai na Dan thu sem ho hin kikhel tih anei poi. Navai hop na jong Yahweh Pakai, tonsot a theng jing jeng ding ahi.
