La Bu.
95.
Hungun, Yahweh Pakai vahchoiyu te! Eihuhdoh uva Pathen Pathen ah kipah tah in vahchoila sau te! 
Thangvah pum in a angsung jon u hite. Avahchoina la sau te. 
Ajeh chu Yahweh Pakai chu loupitah Pathen Pathen ahin, pathen dang chung a leng loupi ahi. 
Mollen penle leiset laigil athuhlai abon'a Aman akhut a atuhchah ahi. 
Twikhanglen hi Ama a ahi, ajeh chu Ama sem ahi. Ama khut in leigo jonghi asem ahi. 
Hungun, eisempau Yahweh Pakai angsung'ah dilsu-u hitin amahou u te, 
ajeh chu Ama I-Pathen'u ahin, eiho hi aman achinjing amite ihiuvin akaihoijing akelngoihon ihiuve. 
Yahweh Pakaijin asei e, “Israel ten gamthipnoi masah a abol bangu leh Meribah a alungtah bang bangun nalung tahsah hih un. 
Ajeh chu hilai munna chun napu napa teu vin kathilbol ho chengse hi amu uvin chule eipatep'un ahi. 
Kum somli sunga hin keima achung uva kana lunghang'in chuleh Keiman kasei in ahi, ‘amaho hi keija kon a kinung heidoh mipi chu ahiuvin, keiman kasei dungjui a chon nomlou ahiuve.’ 
Hijeh chun kalunghan jehin kana kihahsel'in: ‘amaho hi kachol ngahna munna hunglut thei louhel diu ahi!’”
