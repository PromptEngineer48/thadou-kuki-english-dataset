La Bu.
96.
Yahweh Pakai kom'ah lathah sauvin! Leiset pumpin Yahweh Pakai vahchoila sauvin! 
Yahweh Pakai vahchoila sauvin amin thangvah un. Ami huhhingna kipa umtah chu niseh'in samphongin. 
Chitin namtin holah a athilbol kidangtah ho hi samphong'in, Aman thil loupi tahtah abol ho hi mijouse seipeh'un. 
Yahweh Pakai hi aloupi e! Ama hi vahchoi dinga lompen ahi! Ama hi pathen jouse sanga kichat umpentah ahi. 
Chidang namdang ho pathen ho hi semthu pathen ngen ahiuve hinla Yahweh Pakai hin vanho asem ahi! 
Gin na le jana-in aumkimvel ahi, thahatna le mel-hoina hin muntheng alosoh ahi. 
O chitin namtin vannoi mite ho, Yahweh Pakai hethem un Yahweh Pakai ahat na le aloupina ho hi hethem un. 
Yahweh Pakai chu achan dinga lomtah loupi na le jana chan sah'un! Nathilpeh ding kichoiyin lang a-angsunga lut in. 
Yahweh Pakai chu athenna loupina vah chutoh kitohin chibai boh'un. Vannoi leiset pumpi hi a-angsung'ah kithing jenghen. 
Chitin namtin hi seipeh un, “Yahweh Pakaijin vai ahom e!” Vannoi hi adingdetna ahitan holingbut ahitapon ahi. 
Vanho kipah hen chuleh vannoi leiset kipah-u hen! Twikhanglen leh asunga um jousen thangvah'un. 
Loujao ho leh asunga kehho jouse jong kipahtah in hung gadoh sohkei uhen, gammangsunga thingphungho jousen jong 
Yahweh Pakai angsung'ah thangvah la sauhen, ajeh chu Ama ahungtai! Leiset chungthu tan dingin ahunge. Aman vannoi mite le chitin namtinhi kitahna-a achungthu atan ding ahi.
