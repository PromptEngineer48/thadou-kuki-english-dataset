La Bu.
99.
Yahweh Pakai hi leng ahi! Chitin namtin kithing thingjeng hen! Ama Cherub teni kikah a laltouna a atouvin ahi. Vannoi leiset pumpi kithing hen. 
Yahweh Pakai chu Jerusalem ah loupitah in atouvin ahi. Chitin namtin ho jouse chung vum a dopsang'in aum'e. 
ama hon namin gimneitah chu thangvah uhen, naminhi athenge! 
Lengloupi thudih ngailua, nangin thildih hi nadomsang'in, thudih in kal nasong'in Israel lah-a thudih nachelhah sah'in ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai I-Pathen'u Pathen hi dopsangin umhen! A-angsung'ah bohkhup un, ajeh chu Ama hi atheng ahi. 
Mose le Aaron jongchu thempu ahilhon e, Samuel injong Amin anakouve. Ama hon Yahweh Pakai kom'ah panpi ngaichan anakouvun ahi, chuleh Aman anahouvin ahi. 
Aman meilom lah a kon'in Israel ho lah-a thu anasei in, ama hon Yahweh Pakaijin apeh-u Dan le thupeh ho chu anajui un ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai ka Pathen'u Pathen! nangin amaho nana donbut in ahi. Nangma hi amaho ding a mi ngaidam jing Pathen Pathen nahi, ahinla amaho achekhel tengu leh nagim boljin ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai, I-Pathen'u Pathen hi domsang'un chuleh Jerusalem a moltheng achun chibai boh'un ajeh chu Yahweh Pakai, I-Pathen'u Pathen hi atheng ahi.
