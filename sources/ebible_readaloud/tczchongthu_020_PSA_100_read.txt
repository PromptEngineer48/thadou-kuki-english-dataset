kipah tah in Yahweh Pakai chu chibai boh'un vahchoila sapum pumin a-angah hungun 
Yahweh Pakai, Pathen Pathen ahi tin hettohna phongdohun, Aman eisem uva eiho Ama a ihiu ahi. Eiho Ama mite ihiu vin a-an a kivah akelngoite ihiuve. 
Thangvah pum in akulpi sungah lut un, vahchoi pum in a insungah lut in, Akom'a kipa thu sei un, amin thangvah'un. 
Yahweh Pakai phatna jehchun, ami ngailutna longlou hi tonsot in aum jinge, chuleh akitah nahi akhang khangin aum jinge
