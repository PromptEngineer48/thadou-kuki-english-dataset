La Bu.
101.
Yahweh Pakai keiman nami ngailutna le nathudihna lan sange. Keiman nangma thangvah nahi lan sange. 
Keiman monabei hinkho kaman theina dingin chingthei tah in um inge, itihleh eipanpi dinga nahung ding hitam? Keiman ka insungah kitahna hinkho kaman jingding ahi. 
Keiman ipi hijongleh athenglou chuleh vet hoilou ho veponge. Thil adihlouva kitongho jouse kathet ahi, hichengse hitoh kakitimat louhel ding ahi. 
Keiman dei umlou thil ho jouse hi kapaidoh sohkei ding thilgilou hojousea kon a kakidalse ding ahi. 
Aheng akom aseiseho jouse kadeplouhel ding ahi. Keiman akiphatsei leh akiletsah ho kadeplou ding ahi. 
Keiman tahsan um miho kaholdoh ding amaho chu kakivoppi ding ahi. Koi hileh oimothei hilou miho bouchu kakivoppi ding ahi. 
Keiman milepche ho ka insunga kamanchah louding ahi. 
Aniseh a katoh ding chu mi jonchih ho deldohding chuleh khopi hi hitiho tobanga konna hoidoh sahding hi kakin ahi.
