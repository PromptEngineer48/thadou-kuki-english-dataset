La Bu.
102.
Yahweh Pakai kataona ngai in! Ka ngeh nahi neisanpeh in. 
Kei a kon'in kiheimang hih'in, kahahsat phatleh khohsah tah in neingaipeh in, kangaichat phatleh neihin gelkhoh in, 
Ajeh chu kahinkho hi meikhu bangin abeipai jitai, kagu kachang hi asadohji tai. 
Kalungsung hi anadohjin hamhing bangin agolha jitai, chutengleh chun kagilkel jong abeipaiji tai. 
Ajeh chu lunggim a kakhoisat sat jeh'in kavun le kagu hobou kahitai. 
Keima neldi gam a sumbupi toh kabangtai, gamla tah gamthip gam a sumbupi noutoh kabangtai. 
Keima kahah khovahjin, inting vumma vacha changkhat seh lunghel tah-a um kabange. 
Kagal miten sun nilhum in eitaitom jingun, ama hon eisamseuvin eitaitom jingun ahi. 
Keima buneh jong vutvai jeng ahitai. Kadon na sunga jong kamitlhi alonglut tai. 
Nalunghan na le nagimbol nachun eidom sang'in eipaidoh tai. 
Kahinkho hi nilhah lang nilep bangin achemang loijitai. Keima hampa bangin kagop lhaloi jitai. 
Ahinla Yahweh Pakai nangvang nalaltouna a natoujing ding ahi. Naminthan na akhang khanga chelhajing ding ahi. 
Nangma hung kipat in natin Jerusalem hi nahin khoto ding ahi. Tua hi nangnam na khohsah phat ahin, tuhi na kitepna dungjui a mi kithopi phat ahi. 
Ajeh chu namiten bang'a song jouse angailu uvin chuleh lamlenna vutvai hojeng jong angaisang'un ahi. 
Chujou tengleh chitin namtin ten Yahweh Pakai ang-a kithing thingding ahi, 
Ajeh chu Yahweh Pakaijin Jerusalem ahin thahsem kitding chutengleh Ama aloupina-a hung kilang ding, 
Aman tahlelte taona ahin sanding Aman angaichat hou asanpehding ahi. 
Khonunga khangthahho dinga kijihlut pehding hitiachu tua pengloulai hon Yahweh Pakai chu ahin thangvah dingu ahi. 
Yahweh Pakaijin van muntheng a kon a ahin vetsuh ding, Aman van a kon a leiset hi ahin vetsuh ding 
songkul tangte khoisatna ajahding thi dinga themmo kichanho alha doh dinga, 
hiteng chuleh Yahweh Pakai minthanna chu Zion a lopna um ding, chule Jerusalem a kithangvah ding ahi. 
Mihonpi tamtah chu ahung ki khopkhom tengleh lenggam hojouse chu Yahweh Pakai chibai boh a hungdiu ahi. 
Aman kaban thahat akim laitah avolhun kahinkho asuchomtai. 
Hinla keiman Ama kakouvin, Oh Yahweh Pakai tonsot a hingjing kakhandon laitah ahin kahinkho lahih'in. 
Kum tamtah achetai, nangin hiche leiset leibul nana lhungdoh'in, vanho hi nakhut tah in nanasemdoh in, 
hihojouse hung mangthah ding ahinlah nang tonsotna nahinjing ding ahi, amaho ponlui bangin hungvui lhantin, Nangin amaho chu von banga nakhel ding chujou teng napai mangdiu ahi. 
Hinla Yahweh Pakai nangvang abang chu nahijingin ahi. 
Nangma mite chate jouse lungmong a umdiu ahi. Achate chate ho chu na-anga hung khanglen diu ahi.
