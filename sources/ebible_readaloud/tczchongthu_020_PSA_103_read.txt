103.
Kahinaho jouse pum'in Yahweh Pakai chu thagvah ingkate. Kalungthim pumpin amintheng choian inge. 
Kahinaho jouse pum'in Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah ingkate. Keidinga thilpha eibolpeh ho itihchan hijongleh sumil ponge. 
Aman kachonsetna jouse angaidam tai, chuleh kadam mona hojouse le kahivei hojouse eiboldam pehtai. 
Aman kathina dinga kon'in eihuhdohtai chuleh ngailutna leh khotona dimset in eiphungvuh tai. 
Aman kahinkho hi thilpha dimset in eiphungvuh tai, Muvanlai bangin kakhandon hinkho akiledohsah tai! 
Yahweh Pakaijin chonphatna leh dihna chu suhgenthei a umte chu amusah ding ahi. 
Aman ahina tahbeh chu Mose kom'ah anatahlang'in chuleh anatoh ho chu Israel mipite lah-a ana phongdoh 'in ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai hi mikhoto them tah leh mihepi themtah lunghang vahlou chuleh longlou ngailutna a dimset ahi. 
Ama hin phat tinnin eithepmosah pouvin tin ahilouleh lunghang jingjeng ponte. 
Aman ichonset nauva jong ei engbol pouvin tin chuleh Aman eiho chan ding dol hotbolna jong einei pouvinte. 
Ama gingte chunga angailutna tanglou hi leiset chunga van asan banga hi sanga ahi. 
Aman eiho chonset nahi, solam le lhumlam akigamlat bangin gamla tah in atolmang tai. 
Yahweh Pakai hi chapangte dinga apauva panga ahin, ama gingte dinga khoto them tah le ngailutna dimset ahijing e. 
Ajeh chu Aman ihatmonau eihet thempeh'un chuleh eiho hi vutvai tobang bou ihiu ageldoh jingin ahi. 
Leiset chunga ihinkho hou jong hampa bangbep ahin, gam pahcha tobangin ipahdoh'un athi loikitji tai. 
Hui ahung nung jin a-itih a aumkhlou banginbep in eimut mang ji tauve. 
Hinla Yahweh Pakai gingte din vang ami ngailutna nitin in aum jinge. Ami huhhing nanjong achate chate ho gei in, 
hitobanga aDan thua kitahna nei ho le, athupeh jui jing ho din aumpeh jing e. 
Yahweh Pakaijin vanmun ho hi alaltouna in asemin; hichea kon'in muntin chunga vai ahom e. 
Nangho vantil te ho, nangho mithupitahte ho, ana tongdoh a pangho Yahweh Pakai hi thangvah un. 
Ahi, nangho vantil sepai te ho alungdeilam boljinga Ama janlea pangjing ho Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah'un. 
Asemsa ho jousen, chuleh alengam a um jouse n Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah'un. Keima kahina jouse pum in Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah jinging kate.
