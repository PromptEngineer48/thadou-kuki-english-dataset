La Bu.
104.
Kahina jouse pum in Yahweh Pakai thangvah inge. O Yahweh Pakai, ka Pathen Pathen, iti lom a nathupi hitam! Nangma, naloupina le nathupina toh nakivon e. 
Nangman khovah chu ponsil bangin nakisil in, Nangin vanho ahin ahsi ho hi pondal bangin nadahdoh in; 
twimei chunga na iin natungdoh in, meilom chu sakol kangtalai in namangin, huihi nalhavingin namangin na lenglen ahi. 
Huihi sottol len namangin, meile meikong jong nasoh'in namangin ahi. 
Nangin, itihchan hijongleh kil ling louhel dingin nangman vannoi leiset bul naphutdoh tai. 
Nangin twipi veihi leiset dingin ponsil in nasilpeh'in molsangho jong nachupsah soh hel in ahi. 
Nangin thu napehleh twipi chu alengmang jingin, na ogin ajahleh ajammang hel jenge. 
Mol ho adingdoh in phaicham atonglha jengin nangin nathupeh bang bangin akijam jengin ahi. 
Chujouvin nangin twikhanglen ho chu aphah nadiu chanbep naphahsah'in, hitiachu leiset hi avel'a achupkit louna diuvin nasemtoh tan ahi. 
Nangin twisamho naphuldoh sah'in kotong dunga nalonlutsah'in, vachaho chu molle lhanga kon'in ahung lengsuh'in, 
ganhingho jouse dingin twi na suhmang peh'in ahi, chuleh sangan honjong dangnou nan anei un ahi. 
Vacha hon va pang dunga buh akisem un thingbah lah a la asajiuvin ahi. 
Nangin navan inmun a kon'in molle lhang chunga go nahinjuhsah in chuteng nakhutsoh gachun leiset nasudimjin ahi. 
Nangin hampa ho nakhandohsah in ganchaho, thingphungho le mihemho manchah pha nasodoh sah'in ahi. Chujongleh nangman leiset chunga amaho dingin nehpha nasodohsah'in ahi. 
Akipana diuvin adondiu lengpitwi nagonpeh in atahsauva akinu diuvin Olive thao nasosah'in, chule athaneina diuva neh ding changlhah napen ahi. 
Yahweh Pakaijin athingphung ho hi phatah in akhoukhah in, Lebanon ah Cedar thingphung ho akedohsah'in, 
hichea chun vachaten abu-u akison un chuleh vakang ten Cypress thing chunga inn akise un ahi. 
Molsang chungvum langa gamlah kelho achengun chuleh songpi ho chu kollah saho dingin na phatah in apangin ahi. 
Nangin lha nasemmin kum kiheichin aphongdoh e, chule nisan alhum ding phatjong akihetne. 
Nangin janjong nasemin, jan ahunglhung jin ahi, gammang ganchaho anunkho jiuvin ahi. 
Keipi nouhon amatdiu ho chu angihjiuvin, Pathen Pathen'in apeh'u amaho anchah ho chu matdingin agemjiuvin ahi. 
Jingpitah leh abusung uva kicholdo dingin anungche jiuvin ahi. 
Hichejou tengleh chun mipi ho chu ama ama natohna dingcheh ajon un hicheachun nilhah gei in na atong ji tao ve. 
O Yahweh Pakai nangin thil ho hi jatchom chom in nasem jinge! Nachihna a hiche ho hi nasemdoh ahije. Leiset hi nathilsemsa ho adimset e. 
Hiti chun twikhanglen lentah leh kiphahjal tah nasemin, asunga ganhing achatthei alen aneo nasemin ahi. 
Ven, kong inneiho akitol un, Leviathan jong twikhanglen'a kichem din nasem e. 
Amaho jousen angaichat'u an amun'adiu hi ngang chunga kingaicheh ahiuve. 
Anehdiu nangin napehtengu leh ama hon achomkhom jiuvin nangin nakhut a nalhangdoh in namop jin hiti chun amaho lungna chimset'in navah jin ahi. 
Ahinla Nang, amahoa konna nakinunghei tengleh amaho kichadiu ahi. Nangin ahinna hu'u nalahdoh tengleh amaho thi ding vutvai soh ding ahi tauve. 
Nangin nahu napeh tengleh hinna nasemdohji a, hiteng chuleh leiset maihi akithahsem kitjin ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai loupina hi tonsotnin um jing jenghen. Yahweh Pakaijin athilsem jousea hin kipana aneijin ahi. 
Leiset hi avetleh akithing jengin molho jonghi akhoi khahleh meikhu alengdoh jengjin ahi. 
Hinkho kanei laisen Yahweh Pakai vahchoi la sange, kahai hu bei chan geijin jong ka Pathen Pathen thangvah ingkate. 
Kalunggel ngaito jouse jonghi Ama lunglhaina hihen, ajeh chu Yahweh Pakai hi kakipana ahi. 
Hiche leisetna konhin michonsete jouse mangthah gam jengu hen, miphalou ho jouse mangthah jengu hen. Kahina ho jouse pummin Yahweh Pakai thangvah ingkate, Yahweh Pakai loupina ding hitahen!
