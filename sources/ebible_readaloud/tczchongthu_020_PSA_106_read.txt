La Bu.
106.
Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah'in umhen! Pathen Pathen chu aphatna jeh'in thangvahna peuvin. 
Yahweh Pakaijin thil loupi kidangtah atohdoh ho hi kon asim jou ding ham? Kon Ama hi ahina bangtah in athangvah joudem? 
Mi ahina bangtah a pachatna pethei ho leh phat tin a adih jeng bol hon kipana anei thei jengun ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai nangin namite khotona namusah tengleh neihin geldoh'in lang neihin nai in neihuhdoh'in. 
Nalhendoh namite phatthei naboh nahoa keijong chan neineisah'in. Namite kipana a keijong neikipa sah'in. Nagou chang toh nangma thangvah khom kanom e. 
Kapu kapateu bangin keiho jong kachonse tauve, keihon kabolkhel tauve, keiho phaloutah in kanachon tauve, 
Egypt gam a ana um kapu kapateu chu Yahweh Pakaijin thil kidang aboldoh ho chun atilkhou pouve. Ama hon ading uva Yahweh Pakai phatna ho chu ana sumil loiji tauve. Ama hon Twipi San mun nachun Yahweh Pakai dounan ahung phunchel tauvin ahi. 
Achuti jeng vangun, Amin loupi dopsangna ding leh athaneina thupitah chu akilandohna dingin amaho chu ana huhdoh thouthou jin ahi. 
Aman Twipi Sanchu akan-na dingin thu apen, Aman twikhanglen chu neldigam abahsah'in Israel te chu ahinpui galkai in ahi. 
Hiti chun agal miteuva kon'in anahuhdoh jin, chuleh akidoupihoa kon'in ana hoidohsah'in ahi. 
Twichu akinung ledoh sah'in agal miteu akhukhum jin khatcha jong anahingdoh pouvin ahi. 
Hitengleh chun amiten akitepna chu atahsan jiuvin Ama vahchoina la chu asajiuvin ahi. 
Ahinla Yahweh Pakaijin abolpeh houchu gangtah in asumilji tauvin, Ama thuhil angahji pouvin ahi. 
Gamthip noi achun amaho tahsa ngaichat thilngen anathum'un hiche gamchai lah a chun Pathen Pathen chu anapatep'un, 
hijeh chun Aman angaichat'u chu anapen ahinlah nat na toh apehthan ahi. 
Camp sunga umho chu Mose chungah athangthipmun chuleh Yahweh Pakai thempu theng Aaron a-ot'un ahi. 
Hiche jeh chun leiset chu ahung kamkan, Dathan, Abiram leh akiphinho jouse chu anavallhum'in avuimangtan ahi. 
Anungjui ho chungah meikong achun miphalou ho jouse chu meikong'in ahalgam tan ahi. 
Sinai mol'a sana a kisem bongnou chu chibai aboh'un, 
Loupitah Pathen Pathen chu hampane bonglim toh analheh tauve. 
ama hon ahuhingpau, Egypt gam'a konna ahinpuidoh pau, 
Ham gamsunga thil kidang tamtah ahin boldohpeh pau, Twipi San na gimneitah thil ana tongdohpa a Pathen'u Pathen chu anasumil tauvin ahi. 
Hijeh chun Aman ka suhmang ding ahi tin ana phongdoh tan ahi. Ahinlah Mose, lhendoh mipa chu Yahweh Pakai leh amipite kikah a anadingtan, aman Yahweh Pakai kom'a chun alunghanna'a konna kile heija amipite chu asuhmang louna dingin ataotai. 
Mipite chu gam nom lutding chu ana nom tapouvin ahi, ajeh chu Pathen Pathen'in ana kitepna banga apuihoi dingchu ana nom tapouvin ahi. 
Ajeh chu akaleh langin achon un, aponbuh sung uvah ana phunchel gam un, Yahweh Pakai thu anangainom pouvin, 
hijeh chun Yahweh Pakai chu lunghemtah in ana kihahsel'in amaho hi hiche gamthip noi a kathagam ding ahiuve anatitai. 
Chutengleh ason apahteu chu chitin namtin lah a kathejal soh ding, chuleh gamla tah tah a kaimang'a umdiu ahi ati. 
Hiti chun, ipu apateu chu Baal leh Peor chibai bohna a anapang'un, mithi dinga kitodoh sa jeng jong ananeuvin ahi. 
Hitiho jehhin ama hon Yahweh Pakai chu ana sulang hangun hijeh chun alah uva natset analangtan ahi. 
Ahinla Phenehas kitipa chun hangsantah in nat se chu ana tingtan'in ahileh natset chu atangtai. 
Hijeh chun hichea patchun michonpha tin jana anapetauvin ahi. 
Meribah-a jong Yahweh Pakai chu ana lunghansah'un hichun Mose chu anasuboi lheh'in ahi. 
ama hon Mose chu analunghan sah'un ahileh amanngolkaitah in thu anaseilo tan ahi 
Israel ten chidang namdang ho chu Yahweh Pakaijin thu anapeh dungjuijin anasohjou pouvin, 
hichu hilouvin agamsung mite lah a chun ana chenghal un achonna o phaloutah ho chu ana joptauvin, 
amilim doi houvu chu anahou un hichun alhuhnao asodohsahtan ahi. 
ama hon achateu kisan chu anatodoh'un chuleh achanuteu jeng jong thillha hounan anapedoh'un, 
ama hon themmona neilou thisan anasouvin, achapa teu leh achanuteu thisan chu Canaan mite doi hounan anamang'un tolthanan gamsung chu anasuboh'un, 
amaho leh amaho jong thilgilou bolnan anakisuboh'un adoihou thanopnau chu Yahweh Pakai mitmun jonthanhoi ana hidohtan ahi. 
Hiche jeh chun Yahweh Pakai lunghanna chu amite chungah anakoudoh jengin ahileh Yahweh Pakaijin ama-a tum ho chu ana kidah lotan ahi. 
Hijeh chun Yahweh Pakaijin amaho chu agamsung nammite lah a chun ana pedoh'in chuleh athet nammiteu chun amaho chungah vai anahomji tauvin ahi. 
Agalmi houchun asugep jiuvin chuleh abolgimnao khut thahatna noi a anakoiji tauvin ahi. 
Avel vellin Aman ahuhdohjin, ahinlah ama hon Ama dounan akipedohji tauvin ahi, chutengleh amaho chu achonset jeh un suhmangin aumji tauvin ahi. 
Ahijeng vangin ahahsat tengu leh Aman akhotokit jiuvin akanao angaipeh kitjitan ahi. 
Aman amaho toh akitepnao ahin geldoh kitji in angailutna chultheilou jeh in alung ahinhei kitji in, 
Aman ganhing a amanteu khutnah khotona amusah kitji nalaijin ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai, ka Pathen'u Pathen neihuhdoh'un! Chitin namtin lah a konhin neichomkhom kitnun hitiachu namintheng ka thangvah theina diuvin chuleh kipah a nangma ka thangvah theina diuvin. 
Israel Pathen Pathen, Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah'un, Ama hi tonsot a pat tonsot geija hingjing Pathen Pathen ahi! Mi jousen “Amen” tiuhen. Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah'in umhen!
