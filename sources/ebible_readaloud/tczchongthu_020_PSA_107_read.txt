La Bu.
107.
Yahweh Pakai hi aphatjeh'in thangvahna peuvin, Ami ngailutna dihtah hi tonsot a dettou jing ahi. 
Yahweh Pakaijin nalhatdoh hitam? Achutileh phongdoh'in! Aman namelma holah a kon a nahoidohsahhi miho seipeh'in. 
Ajeh chu Aman gam tamtah a kon'in solamle lhumlam'a kon'in, sahlam le lhanglam a kon'in sohchangho ahin puikhom'in ahi. 
Miloi khat hi gamthip gam mah avah mang'un, in le lou beijin avah mang'un, 
Gilkil leh dangchah geovin a thi ding konchan aum mun, 
“Yahweh Pakai neipanpin” tin ahahsat nauva Pakai Yahweh akouvun, chuteng Aman agenthei nauva kon'in ahuhdoh jin ahi. 
Aman amaho chu bitkei chan apuijin achenna theiju khopi khatna akoiyin ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai chu angailutna thupi tah jehleh thil kidangtahtah abolpeh jeh un thangvahna peuhen. 
Ajeh chu Aman adangchah ho adangnou sah'in, agilkil'ho thilpha tamtah tothon a-oiva sahjin ahi. 
Miloikhat muthim lah athimlah pen a atouvin, lung gimna thihkhao vin ahen oh'in ahi. 
Amaho Pathen Pathen thu dounan akiphin un, Pathen Pathen thumop chu anelpeh'un ahi. 
Hijeh chun Aman amaho chu akhut hamtah in avo lhun, amaho alhuh phatnun koima akithopidiu aumpon ahi. 
Ahahsat tengu leh Yahweh Pakai akoujiuvin “Yahweh Pakai neipanpin” atiuve. Hitengleh chun Aman athohgim nauva kon'in ahuhdohjin ahi. 
Aman amaho chu muthimleh ahahsat nau thimjin banga thimlah a kon'in apui galkaijin akihennao asutlhappeh'in ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai chu ami ngailutna thupitah jehleh thil kidangtah tah abolpeh jeh'in thangvah uhen, 
ajeh chu Aman sum-eng songkul kotpi avohlhahpeh'in chuleh akihennao thihkhao avohtan pehtan ahi. 
Mingol themkhat chun boina asemuvin achonset jeh un thohgimna atoh'un ahi. 
An le twi ngaichatnan athohlel lheh'un hiti chun amaho chu athi ding kon ahiuve. 
“Yahweh Pakai neikithopin” tin ahahsat tengu leh Yahweh Pakai akoujiuvin Aman athohgim nauva kon'in ahuhdohjin ahi. 
Aman thu changkhat agahseijin aboldamjin, thina kotbul a kon'in aloidohtan ahi. 
Ami lungsetna thupi tah jehleh amaho dinga thil kidang tamtah anabolpeh jeh'in Yahweh Pakai chu thangvahna peuhen. 
Kilhaina gantha pummin thangvahna peuhen, chuleh athilbol kidangtah ho jeh'in thangvahna peuhen. 
Mi phabep chu kong innei mangchan twikhanglen'ah vannoi leiset kiveina lampi ajot un akitollun ahi. 
Amaho jonghin twikhanglen athuhlang tah a Yahweh Pakai thuneitah a anatohna leh thil thupi tahtah abolna ho amu'vin ahi. 
Aman thu aseijin hui ahungnung doh'in twikhanglen ahin kinongsah'in ahi. 
Kong innei ho chu vanlanga anuh touvin chule twi thuhlah a ahung kilehlut kitjin kongtol ho chu kichatnan akileh leuvin ahi. 
Amaho jukham ho bangin a-onle leuvin kichat tijat nan adim'un ahi. 
“Yahweh Pakai neihuh'un” tin ahahsat phatnun Yahweh Pakai akouvun, Amanjong agimnauva kon'in ahuhdohjin ahi. 
A-o neochakhat'in hui le guo kitho chu athipsah'in chuleh twi kinong jong asutang'in ahi. 
Hiche athip theina chu ichan gei a pha a phatthei bohna hitam? Kongchu kong mun'ah bitkeichan apuilut in ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai chu angailutna thupitah jehleh thilkidang tamtah abolpeh jeh un thangvahna peuhen. 
Nam lamkaiho masangleh, mipi kikhomho masangah japi hetthei doltah in thangvahna peuhen. 
Aman vadung jong neldigam aso sahjin, twisam putna munho jong gamgo leh adang chahna aso sahjin ahi. 
Aman ga phatah sona gam phatah jong ima sodoh louna chitwi-al gamgo asosah thei in ahi, agamsunga cheng mipite phatmo jeh a hitiachu abolji ahi. 
Ahinlah Aman neldigam chu twikul asosah'in, chuleh gamgo jong twi putna mun aso sahjin ahi. 
Aman gilkel dangchah te chu khopi semdoh dinga hiche mun a chu achensah ding ahi. 
ama hon aloulai uva muchi kitheuvin tin lengpi gui tu uvin tin ga lhingsetna akilodiu ahi. 
Aman ichan gei a phatthei aboh hitam? ama hon insungmi tamtah atungdoh'un chuleh aganchahou ninglhingset ding ahi. 
Ijemtia bolgentheina jalle, boina le hahsatna jalla umho mihem a akemsuh uva avaichat phatnuleh, 
Yahweh Pakaijin alengteu hinpaidoh intin amaho gamgo laija panna bei in umsah leleu vintin, 
hinla Yahweh Pakaijin mivaichaho ahahsat nauva kon'in kelngoihon phatah bangin a insunga hinpunsah intin, 
Miphahon hiche thilsoh ho hi hinveu vintin kipahdiu ahinla miphalou ho chu suhthipma umdiu ahi. 
Michingho jousen hitia thilsoh ho hi lunggiltah in hinheu vintin, ama hon hitobang ikhankhouva kon a hi Yahweh Pakai mi ngailutna dihtah chu ahin mudohdiu ahi.
