La Bu.
108.
O Pathen Pathen kalungthim hi nang ah akinem jinge, ahijeh chun kalungthim pumpia nangma thangvah la kasha theihi datmo ding ahipoi. 
Hungthouvin, katheile leh kasemjang, jingpi ihmuhi kavahchoilan sukhang'ing kate. 
O Yahweh Pakai mitinlah a hin nangma hin thangvah ingkate. Keiman chitin namtin lah a hin nangma vahchoila sange. 
Nami ngailutna longlouhi vanho sangin asangjon ahi. Nakitahna hin meilomlah ho aphan ahi. 
O Pathen Pathen vanho lah a asangpen sangin namin hi dopsangin umjo hen. Naloupina leiset pumpi chung vumma hin vahdoh hen. 
Tuhin name ngailut tehi huhdoh tan. Naban thahatnan neidonbutnun lang neihuhdoh tauvin, 
Pathen Pathen'in athenna a chu hiche hi akitepna ahi. “Keiman kipahtah a Sechemhi kabokhen ding, Keiman Succoth phaicham hi katetoh ding ahi. 
Gilead hi kei a ahin, Manasseh jong kei a ahi, Ephraim kathih lukhuh ahin, ama a kon a hi kagalsat ding ho hungkondoh diu ahi, chuleh Judah hi kaleng tenggol ahin ama a kon a hi kalengte hung kondoh diu ahi. 
Hinla Moab hi kakeng silna kong ahin ama a kon a hi kasohte hungkondoh diu ahi, chuleh Keiman Edom chunga hi kakeng kasilding ahi. Philistia chunga galjona kasapkhum ding ahi.” 
Koipenham kulpi kikaikhum khopi sunga eipuilut ding ah? Koiham Edom chunga galjona a eipuilut ding ah? 
O Pathen Pathen nangin neipaidoh hitam? Nangin kasepai te toh galsat'a nakonkhom louding hitam? 
O Yahweh Pakai kagal mite toh kidouna a hi neipanhu louding hitam, ajeh chu mihem kithopina hi aphachom aumpoi. 
Pathen Pathen toh pankhom hi thil loupi tahtah tohdoh theina ahi, ajeh chu Aman eikidoupi ho chu akengtoa alhopha ding ahi.
