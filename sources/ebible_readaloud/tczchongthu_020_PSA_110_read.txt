La Bu.
110.
Yahweh Pakaijin ka leng pakai kom'ah, “nagal mite kasuhnem a, nakengtoa kachotpha sah tokahsen kakhut jetlam naloupina mun tou jengin” ati. 
Yahweh Pakaijin na lenggam thaneitah chu Jerusalem a kon a ahin lhundoh ding, nangin nagal mite chunga vai na homding ahi. 
Galsat'a nacheteng namiten thanomtah a najuidiu ahi, nangma vonthenga nakivon ding chutengleh jingkah daitwi banga nisehleh natha kidodoh jingding ahi. 
Yahweh Pakaijin kihahselna anei ahin akitepna chu asuhkeh louding ahi. “Nangma Melchizedek hina banga tonsot thempu nahi ding ahi” ati. 
Yahweh Pakai chu najet langa dinga napuihoi jing ahi. Ama alunghanna ahung koudoh leh lengle lalho tamtah asuhlhuh ding ahi. 
Aman chitin namtin ajepding agamsunga thilong dimset ding, aman leiset pumpia lengle lalte aban vohlhuh ding ahi. 
Ahinlah amavang lampi pang dunga long twi adon a hatdoh jingding ahi. Hitiachu ama hi galjoupa hijengding ahi.
