La Bu.
112.
Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah un! Yahweh Pakai ginna neija athupeh ho lunglhai tah a juiho dinga iti kipa um a hitam. 
Achaten muntin'a lolhinna anei ding ahi. Khangkhat lhingset a mipha ngen hina phatthei changdiu ahi. 
Amaho hung haodoh ding ahiuve chuleh athilpha bol u um jing jingding ahi. 
Michonpha ho dingin jan jeng jong avah'in ahi. Amaho hi ahongphal un, khotona aneiyun chuleh thudih achepiuvin ahi. 
Hongphal tah a sumpa hom ho leh thengtah a akiveina chepiho phatthei changdiu ahi. 
Hitobang miho chu thilsen asuhmo joulou ding ahi. Mikitah ho hi min asumil thei pon ahi. 
Amaho hin thuse hung kithangho jeng jong akichapon ama hon Yahweh Pakaijin ahoibit diuvin tahsan na dettah aneiyun ahi. 
Amaho hin tahsanna dettah aneiyun kichatna aneipouvin ahi, chuleh akidoupi hojeng jong jotei ding tahsan nan akimai topiuvin ahi. 
Hitobang ho hin panpi ngaichaho hongphal tah in apeuvin kiphaltah in mitoh chan aching khomji nom un ahi. Atohphat nahou geldoh'in aum jingin ahi. Amaho hin mi thujona leh jana achang'un ahi. 
Milungdihlou hon hiche hi mu uvin tin alung hanpi diu ahi. Ama hon lunghang tah in aha gel unte, chuleh akinepnau thonjeh'in jumthet in kilapdoh tauvin te.
