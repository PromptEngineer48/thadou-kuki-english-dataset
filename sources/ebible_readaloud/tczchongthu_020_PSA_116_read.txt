La Bu.
116.
Yahweh Pakaijin ka o eingai peh eikhoto dia katao na eisan peh jeh in keiman Yahweh Pakai kangailui. 
Aman eihin ang ngat a kataona eingaipeh'in kahaithei laisea keima katao jing jingding ahi. 
Thinan apon in eihin tomkhum e; lhankhuh gimneitah chun eihin lonvuh e. Keiman lungkhamna leh lunggimna ngen kamu e. 
hiteng chuleh keiman Yahweh Pakai min kakoujin, “Yahweh Pakai lungset'in neihuhdoh'in!” katijin ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai hi itobang tah a milungsetthem hitam! Chuleh itobang tah a pha a hitam! I-Pathen'u Pathen hi ichan gei a mikhoto them hitam! 
Yahweh Pakaijin chapang lungthengtah atahsanna neijachu ahahsatna'a ahoidoh jin ahi. Keima thina toh kimaitoa kaum laitah in aman eihuhdoh'in ahi. 
Kalhagao, nacholdo namun hinjon kittan ajeh chu Yahweh Pakai hi keidingin aphalheh jenge. 
Aman kathina dinga kon'in eihuhdoh'in kamitna mitlhi lonlouna ding leh kakeng hi kakiselhuh louna dingin eiveng jenge. 
Hiche leisetna kahin laisen Yahweh Pakai masanga kavahle jengin ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai keima kagenthei behseh jengtai katiteng jongleh nangma katahsan jing nalai e. 
Kahahsat teng jongleh nangma kahin koujin ahi, ajeh chu hiche mitehi tahsan thei ahi pouve. 
Thilpha simjoulou eibolpeh jeh a hi Yahweh Pakai chu ipi kapehding ham? 
Keiman huhhingna khon hi domsang'ing ting eihuhhing jeh a amin ka thangvah ding ahi. 
Keiman amite ho jouse masangah Yahweh Pakai ah kakitepna nitjinging kate. 
Yahweh Pakaijin angailut mite athitengleh alainatpi lheh jengjin ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai keima nasoh kahi, nangin kasoh channa'a kon'in neihuhdoh tai. 
Keiman thangvahna hi kilhaina gantha napengting Yahweh Pakai min'in taovinge. 
Keiman Yahweh Pakai a kakitepna chu amite jouse masangah pengtin, 
Yahweh Pakai Hou-In ah Jerusalem lailungah subulhit'ing kate. Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah'in umhen!
