La Bu.
119.
Yahweh Pakai thupeh dungjui a chonho leh kitahna neiho chu kipah uhen. 
Koi hileh a Dan thu nit a alungthim pumpia Ama holjing ho chu kipah'u hen. 
Amaho chun thil phalou achepi pouvin Yahweh Pakai deilampi joh ajui un ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai nangin nathupeh ho chu phatah a kanit diuvin nei seipeh'un ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai kanitin hinkho Mandan ahin nangman neideipeh hinkho hi kilangdoh jengle kati e. 
Chuleh nathupeh leh ka chondan kavetkah teng kajum louding ahi. 
Keiman nei thuhilna dihtah ho chu kahet dungjui a kakiman ding dol a kakimanchah theijeh a nangma chu ka thangvah ding ahi. 
Neidalha hihbeh'in, keiman na Dan chu kanitjing ding ahi. 
Khangthah khatchu itileh ahinkho atheng theidem? Nangma thu angaileh hitheiding ahi. 
Keiman nangma mudoh tei ding kagelkhoh'in ahi, nangma thupeh a kon'in neilam vai sahhih'in. 
Nangma douna kachonset louna dingin ka lungsung ah nathu chu kachensah jingin ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai nangma ka thangvah'e nathupeh nitji dan neihil in. 
Ka chonding dan nangin neipeh ho chu keiman kasimphongjin ahi. 
Nei le gou va housangin na Dan thu chu jui ding katha anomjoi. 
Neithupeh na Dan ho chu keiman simjingingting nei deipeh lampia chejing dingin kingaito jing nange. 
Na Dan thu ho hi kakipapin, jui dinga neithupeh ho jong sumil ponge. 
Nasohpa keima hi thilpha neibolpen kahinkhoa hi nathupeh kanit jingna dingin. 
Nathupeh hoa thudih kidangtah ho hi kamuchet theina dingin kamit hi hadohsah'in. 
Keima hiche leisetna hi kholjin bep kahi bouve nathupeh ho chu keima nei impeh hihbeh'in. 
Phatin in keiman nachondan jui ding ngaichatna hin eilonvuhtan ahi. 
Nangin mikiletsah ho chu nahousejin, nathupeh a kon a pampet ho chu nasamsejin ahi. 
Na Dan sem ho hi kajui jingnai amaho hi neinoise sahhihbeh'in. 
Leng chapa te ho toukhom u henlang keima dounan thu seijonglehu keivangin na Dan semho hi jui ding geljing nange. 
Na Dan semho hi kakipapi lheh jenge ajeh chu hiche ho hin chihna thu eihil jingin ahi. 
Keima leivui lah a kijama kahitai nathu chun neisuhal kittan. 
Ka chondan, kaumchan nakom'ah kaphongdoh tai, tun nadan semchu neisei pehtan. 
Na Dan thupeh chu neithem sah'in, chutengleh keiman natohdoh datmo umtah ho chu kalungin geljing nange. 
Lungkhamnan eibom in kakaptai, nakam changing neihaldoh sahkit tan. 
Mijou milepche hotoh neichom khen in, nathuhil kahetthem theina ding thuguh chu neipen. 
Kitah tah a hinkho man ding hi kakilhen ahitai, nathuhil dungjui a hinkho man dinghi kadinpi ahitai. 
Na Dan semhi katuhchah ahitai, O Yahweh Pakai neijumso hihbeh'in! 
Nathupeh ho hi kajui jingding ahi, ajeh chu nangin hetthem theina neipeh jingding jehin. 
O Yahweh Pakai nathupeh chu neihillin, keiman achaina gei in juijinging kate. 
Nathuhil kajuijing theina dingin hetthem theina neipen. Kalungthim pumpin hichu natohman podoh ingkate. 
Nathupeh dungjuijin neikalson sah'in hichu kakipanapen ahi. 
Sum ngaichatna sangin na Dan thu hetthemding ngaichat theina neipejon. 
Thil phachomlou hoa kon'in kamit heimangin lang nathuchengin neihindoh sah'in. 
Nangma ginle jana neiho napeh kitepna chu keima kom'ah neisuhdetpeh'in. 
Kajumna hinkho hi neipailhah sah'in ajeh chu na Dan semho hi aphalheh jengin ahi. 
Nathupeh ho hi kajuinomlheh jenge naphatna'in kahinkho hi neithah sempeh kittan. 
Yahweh Pakai nami ngailutna longlou tonsot hinna neiteppeh chu neipetan, 
Chutileh eitaitom ho donbutje kahetding ahi ajeh chu keiman nathutep chu katahsan in ahi. 
Phat tin in thutah jeng neisei sah'in ajeh chu nangma chondan thupeh a bouhin kinepna kaneijin ahi. 
Keiman nathupeh ho chu itihchan hijongleh kanit jingding ahi. 
Keiman chamlhatna tahbeh chu kaneitai, ajeh chu nangma thuhil jui dinga kigo kahitai. 
Keiman nathupehho hi jahchalouhel la lengte masanga jong ka phondoh ding ahi. 
Keiman nang thupeh jui dinghi kakipanapen ahitai ajeh chu hiche ho hi keiman kadeilheh jengin ahi. 
Keiman na thupeh ho hi kadeilheh jengin chuleh jana jong kaneijin ahi, na Dan thu ho hi giltah in kagellin ahi. 
Kakom'a na kitepna chu geldoh'in hiche bouchu ahi kakinepna. 
Hiche nakitepna hin kahinkho akiledohsah kit in kalungthim boina hoa kon'in jong lungmon na eipen ahi. 
Mikiletsah hon noise tah in eibol jonglehu keivang nangma thuhilhoa kon'in kapotdoh pon ahi. 
Masanglai peh a na Dan sem nachepi ho chu kangaito leh O Yahweh Pakai hichechun eilhamon sahlheh jenge. 
Keima miphalou ho chunga kalung ahang'in ahi, ajeh chu ama hon nathuhil apompouve. 
Hiche kakholjinna munhi hoilai hijongleh na Dan thu semho hi kala ngeipen ahijinge. 
O Yahweh Pakai jantengleh nangma nahina hi ka lungsung ah akilangjin, hijeh chun nathuhil ho hi kajui jingin ahi. 
Nathupeh juijinghi kahinkho mandan hitoh kilhon khoma ahitai. 
O Yahweh Pakai, kachan ding gouchu nangma nahi! Nathupeh jui dingin kakiphaldoh jinge! 
Kalungthim pumpi hi namalsomna ngaichajing kahi, Nakitepna bangin neihepi jingtan. 
Kahinkho ka lam lhahdan hi kanung gel jin chutengleh na Dan thu juina dingahin ka lutkit ji tai. 
Nathupeh juina dingahin gangtah in kipelut jing nange. 
Miphalou hon chonsetna a lhohlut tei ding eigo jiuvin ahinla keima vang na Dan thuhilna a hin dettah in kading jingin ahi. 
Na Dan thupeh ho hi adih jeh'in jankhang jongleh kathoudoh'in nangma ka thangvah jin ahi. 
Nangma thupeh juijing ho leh nangma ging jingho dingin agolphao kahijinge. 
O Yahweh Pakai nami ngailutna tanglou hin leiset chunghi adipmin, na Dan thu hi neihil jingin. 
Yahweh Pakai nangin nakitepna dungjuijin thilpha tamtah neibolpeh tai. 
Nathupeh hoachun kading jinge, hetna thepna leh thu adih a tantheina thuguh chu neihil in, 
Nangin neihilchah masang chun kana lamvaijin, tunvang nathu thun kanungtai. 
Nangman naphatna'in thilpha bou nabol jie, na Dan thu chu neihil in. 
Miphalou ho jouthu eisei khum jiuvin, ahinla kenvang kalungthim pumpin nathupeh ho dihtah in kajuijing nai. 
Alungthim u ngol hoitah ahin; keivang nangma thupehho juijing hi kakipana ahijinge. 
Ka thohgimna chu keidinga thilphajo anahi, ajeh chu hiche hin nathupeh juina ding langa lunglutna eipetheijin ahi. 
Nathuhil ho jui ding hi sana le dangka simsenlou sangin aman atamjon ahi. 
Nangin neina gon a neinasemdoh ahin, tunvang nathupeh juina dinga hin hetthem theina neipen. 
Nangma ging ho jousen kei a konna hin kipa na mu u heuhen, ajeh chu keiman nathupeh ahin kinepna dihtah kaneijin ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai keiman na Dan semho adihdan hi kahethem tai, neisuhgim ji ding hi keidia thilphachom ahi. 
Tuhin nasohpa neiteppeh bangin nami ngailutna longlou chun eilungmonsah jin ahi. 
Kahintheina dingin namihepina chun neiumkimvel jingin ajeh chu neithuhilna ho hi keidinga kipana leh lunglhaina ahi. 
Keichung changa thujou seiho miphalou ho chu jumle jachatna penlang, keivangin nangma thupehho chu kangai jingding ahinai. 
Nangma Dan he themho leh nangma ging jingho chutoh nei ki vopkhom sah jingin. 
Na Dan thu kajuina ahin bolkhel kaneilouna dingin neivengin, chutileh jumna leh jachatna kaneilou ding ahi. 
Nangma huhdohna ding kangana lamma hin abeipaiding kahitai, ahinlah nathupeh ahin kakinepna adetjinge. 
Nakitepna ahung gulhunna ding vetnan kamit achahleutai, itih tah leh neilhamon dingham? 
Lengpitwipai savun ahun ahulgot bangin kagolhatai ahinlah na Dan thu jui ding kasumil dehpon ahi. 
Itihchanpi hi kanga jingding hitam? Keima eibolse ho hi itihleh aphu nalah dingham? 
Nangma thuhil nahsahlou mi kiletsah ho hin keima kipalna ding ko thuhtah alaijun ahi. 
Nathupeh jouse hi tahsan dinga lomtah cheh ahi. Ajeh bei a eidel lele hoa konhin neihuhdoh'in. 
Amaho hin eichaidohpai got u ahitai, ahinla keiman nathupeh ho hi kajuida dehpoi. 
Na mi ngailutna longlou chun kahinkho hi huhdoh'in, chutileh na Dan thu kajui theiding ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai tonsot a dinga nakitepna chu van a dettah in aum jinge. 
Nangin nanatundoh hiche leiset hi aum jing bangin nakitahna jong akhang khanga um jing ahi. 
Nathilgon jouse hi nathupeh dungjuijin tuni chan geihin aumsohkei un ahi, ajeh chu amaho hi aboncha nalhacha ahisohkei un ahi. 
Na Dan thupeh hin kipana hinkho'ah eihin khoukhah hihleh kagenthei lengvaina a kana mol lem ding ahitai. 
Na thupeh Dan ho hi itihchan hijongleh ka suhmillouhel ding ahi, ajeh chu hiche ho hin eihinghoi ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai nanga kahi; neihuhdoh'in! Ajeh chu nadan thu nitna a hin ka kihabol lheh jenge. 
Mi phalouhon suhmang dingin lampi dungah eichanglhi le jonguleh keiman vang na Dan thu hi ka lungsungah thipbeh chan geljing nange. 
Thiljouse hin akhonna aneisohkeije ahinlah na Dan thupeh hin akhonna aneipoi. 
Oh, na Dan thupeh hi iti kahin dei hitam! Ni lhumkei kei in hiche ho hi kageljin in ahi. 
Na Dan thupeh ho hin kagal mite sangin eichihsah'e, ajeh chu hiche ho hin phattin in eiveng jinge. 
Na Dan thu hi phattin a ka geljing jeh'in eihilho sangin jong hetgilna ka neijotai. 
Na thupeh Dan ho kanitjing jeh'in kachunga upa tehse ho sangin jong kachingjo tai. 
Nathupeh dettah a kajuijing theina dingin thilse bolna a cheding kanompon ahi. 
Nangin phate keicha neihil ahitah jeh'in na Dan thu juina a kon'in ka ki heimang nompon ahi. 
Na thupeh hi kapatep leh alhum lheh jenge, khoiju sangin alhumjon ahi. 
Na Dan thupeh ho hin hetthemna eipen; hiti chun hinkho adihlou lam a cheding hi kathet lheh ji e. 
Nathupeh hi kakalskonna a eipuiding thaomei jin apangin, kalampi salvah ding vah'in apangin ahi. 
Na Dan thupeh dihtah ho hi kajuijing ding ahin, hiche dinga hi kitepna kananei ahitan avel a kakitep kitding ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai nathutep dungjuijin ka hinkho hoidoh sah'in ajeh chu kathohlel behseh jenge. 
O Yahweh Pakai ka thangvah nahi kilhaina thilto bangin neisanpeh'in lang chuleh na Dan thupeh ho neihil in. 
Keima ka hinkho hi kilep ditdet in aum'e, ahinlah na thuhil hi ka juidadeh pong e. 
Mi phalou hon ka kipalna dingin thang akam jingun ahi, ahi vangin nathupeh a kon a ka ki heimang doh louding ahi. 
Na Dan thu hi keidinga gou ahi, hiche ho hin ka lungthim akipasah'in ahi. 
Ka thini chan gei a na Dan thu nitding hi ka dinpi ahitai. 
Kitahna neiloute hi kathet ahin keivang na thuhil ho jui dinghi katha nopna ahi. 
Nangma hi kakiselna leh kalumdal nahi, nathupeh hi kakinepna bulpi ahijinge. 
Milung gilouho keija kon'in potdoh'un, ajeh chu keiman ka Yahweh Pakai thupeh ho hi kajuinom e. 
Yahweh Pakai, nakitepna bangin kahinjouna dingin tha neipen kakinepna hi neijumso hihbeh'in, 
Nathahatnan neituh'in chutileh kahoidohding ahi, chutengleh nathupeh hi kachepi jingding ahi. 
Nathupeh a kon a potdoh ho hi nangin napaidoh gamheltan ahi. Amaho hi amaho leh amaho kijoulhep ahi bouve. 
Nangin mi-engse ho chu leiset chunga kon'in bohse bangin napaidoh jitan ahi, hijeh chun kei vangin na Dan thu ho hi kajui nom e. 
Nangma ginnan ka kithinge, na thutan dihtah ho jeh'in ki chatnan kadim e. 
Keiman thudih tah leh kitahna neitah in na katonge, kagal mite khut a neidalhahih'in. 
Phatthei neibohding kinepna neipen, mi kiletsahho neibolsetsah hihbeh'in. 
Nathutep dihtah chu asuh bulhit nadinga neihuhdohna dingchu kavet vetnan kamit achah leutan ahi. 
Nami ngailutna longlou chun nasoh hi neibolpha jingin, chuleh na Dan thu ho chu neihil in. 
Na Dan thu ho hi kahettheina dingin nasohhi hetthem theina neipen. 
Yahweh Pakai tu hi nakipatna na tohphat ahitai, ajeh chu miphalouhon na Dan thu apal kehgam tauve. 
Tahbeh in nathupeh ho hi sana mantampen sangin kadeijon ahi. 
Na thupeh ho hi adihsohkei in, hijeh a chu lamdihlou ho hi kathet ahi. 
Na Dan semho hi akidang theilheh jengin hijeh a chu kalungthim pumpia kajui ahi! 
Na Dan thuhil'a hin vah akilangin hijeh chun milham minjong ahetheijin ahi. 
Nathupeh ho kangaichat behseh jeh'in kaki gon chule kakam kah in kangah jinge. 
Nangma ngailuho nabolna bangin hung kihei in lang nami khotona neimusah'in. 
Nakitepna dungjuijin kalhuh louna dingin neituh'in, thilse eijosah hihbeh'in. 
Eisugenthei hoa kon'in neihuhdoh'in, hitiachu nathupehho hoitah a kajuijing theina dingin. 
Nasohpa kei ahin namaivah chu hin kilan dohsah'in chutengleh na Dan thu chu neihil in. 
Mi hemten na thuhil ajuitah lou jeh un kamit na hin mitlhi twi vadung bangin along'e. 
O Yahweh Pakai nangma thudihtah nahi, na Dan semho jong adih'e. 
Na Dan semho hi nolnabei ahin chuleh tahsanthei mong mong ahi. 
Ka gal miten na thupeh hi anahsah loujeh un ka lunghan hi aki khonhel jengin ahi. 
Na thutep ho hi kapatep sohkei ahitan adih lheh jengin keiman ka deilheh jenge. 
Keima hi hetkhamlou leh notthap ka hivang in nathupeh ho hi ka sumil dehpoi. 
Yahweh Pakai nanga hin tonsotna thudih na aum'e, chuleh na thuhil dihtah ho jonghi nolnabei ahi. 
Hahsatna le lung khamna in eilonvuh jeng jongleh nathupeh ho hi keidinga kipana tahbeh ahijinge. 
Na Dan semho jouse jonghi adihjing jenge, hinna ka neitheina dingin neihet them sah'in. 
Kalungthim pumpin kataove O Yahweh Pakai neihouvin, keiman na Dan thu ho chu kanit jingding ahi. 
Nangma kahin kouve Yahweh Pakai neihuhdoh'in, na Dan thu ho chu ka juijing theina din. 
Khovah masangin matah in kathouvin, nathutep na chun kakinem in panpi kathum e. 
Jan khovah'in kahah'in nathutep chu kagel jinge. 
Nami ngailutna dihtah a konchun O Yahweh Pakai kataona ngai in lang na Dan thu juina ahin neichoh hing kittan. 
Dan neilou miten eihung delkhum un ahi, amaho hi nangma thuhilla kon'in agamla lheh'un ahi. 
Ahinlah O Yahweh Pakai nangma naichan naum'in ahi, chuleh nangma thupehho jouse adih'in ahi. 
Na Dan semho hi tonsot a ding ahije tihi kaneolai a patna kanahetsa ahitai. 
Kathoh gimna hi venlang neihuhdoh'in, ajeh chu keiman na thuhil ho chu ka sumil khapoi. 
Keima langa pangin lang ka chung chang thu nei seipeh'in nakitepna bangin neihuhdoh'in. 
Mi phalou hovang ki huhdoh louding ahiuve. Ajeh chu ama hon na Dan thu hi anahsah pouve. 
O Yahweh Pakai namihe pina hi anasalheh jenge, na Dan semho hi jui ing kating ka hinkho hi neikile dohsah tan. 
Mi tamtah in asugenthei jin eisuboijin ahi, ahinlah na Dan thu juina a kon'in kakiheimang dehpoi.

Yahweh Pakai nangin neihuhdoh dinghi ka ngaicha lheh jengin ahi, hijeh a chu nathupeh ho hi ka juijing ahi.
