La Bu.
122.
Yahweh Pakai Hou-Inn ah cheuhite tia kakom'a asei u chun keima kakipahlheh jengin ahi. 
Vo Jerusalem tuhin keiho nakelkot sung langah kadingtao ve. 
Jerusalem hi dettah a kisa khopi ahin apal jonghi detchetna kigenbitna ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai mite Israel phungho jousen hicheahi kintheng bolla ahungnao ahi. Israel te chonna dan dungjui a Yahweh Pakai kom'a thangvahna pedinga ahungnao ahi. 
Hichea hin lengte thutanna laltouna ho chu aum'inDavid chilhah ho laltouna ahi. 
Jerusalem a cham lenna din taovun, hiche khopi ngailu jouse khangtouhen. 
Vo Jerusalem, nakulpi sunga chamleng hen chuleh nakhopi sung khangtou hen. 
Ka insung mite leh kagol kapaite khohsahna in keiman Jerusalem ah “Nanga chamna umhen” kati. 
Yahweh Pakai, I-Pathen'u insung khohsah na'in, O Jerusalem nang dinga aphapen chu kahol ahi.
