La Bu.
123.
Van'a alaltouna a tou O ka Pathen Pathen nangma munomna in kadah in kahinvei. 
Sohten amit uva apupao avetjing bangun Yahweh Pakai, I-Pathen'u Pathen khotona ngaichan keihon kahin vejing ui. Hiti hin soh nungah honjong api'u chaloh akivetgeu jingun ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai ka chung u vah khotona neipeuvin ajeh chu keiho hi vetda changsa ka hiuve. 
Ajeh chu keiho hi mi kiletsahho housetna leh mikhoh ho tai tomnan eichupmu ahitai.
