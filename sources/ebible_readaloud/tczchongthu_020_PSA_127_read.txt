La Bu.
127.
Insa-a hi Yahweh Pakai apan louleh, asahon mohseh a asah'u ahi. Khopi ngah'a hi Yahweh Pakai ajao louleh, khongah ho panna bei a hahkhovah ahiuve. 
Jingkah matah a nakipat uva jankhang gei a neh ding an holna a na natoh'u pan nabei ahi, ajeh chu Yahweh Pakaijin angailut achate hi a-ihmut sung uva hi ijakai agontohpeh ahi bouve. 
Veuvin, chatehi Yahweh Pakaiya kon a gou ahin, naobu sunga konna aga kimu jonghi kipa thilpeh ahi. 
Khang don laija cha kineiho hi galsatpan akhutna thal changho akichoi tobang ahi. 
Thal chang abom dimsetna kipohpa anunnom ahi. Ama chu agal mite toh thutanna munna akimuto teng gal lel hilouhel ding ahi.
