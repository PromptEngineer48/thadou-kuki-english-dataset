Veuvin, insungkhat hi kilungkhat tah a achenkhom teng iti phat a pha lunglhai um a hitam? 
Hiche hi Aaron chunga thao mantam kisungah aluchanga kon a akhamul lah a longlha a avon mong chan a longlha suhpeh tobang ahi. 
Kitohdel a Hermon mol chunga kon a longlha daitwi theng tobang a Zion mol a hung longlha chu ahi. Hichea kon a chu Yahweh Pakaijin malsomna chu ahin phondoh a hichu tonsot hinkhoa ding ahitai.
