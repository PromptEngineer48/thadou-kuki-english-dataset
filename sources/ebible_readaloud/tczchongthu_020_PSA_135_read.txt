La Bu.
135.
Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah un! Yahweh Pakai min chu thangvah un! Yahweh Pakai lhacha ten Ama chu thang vah un, 
Nangho Yahweh Pakai lhacha na tong, Pathen Pathen, Hou-In sung a natong ten. 
Yahweh Pakai chu aphatna jeh'in thangvah'un; amin ngaitah chu vahchoilan domsangun. 
Ajeh chu Yahweh Pakaijin Jacob chu Ama a dingmong monga alhen ahin, Israel chu atumbeh a amagou dinga agon ahi. 
Keiman Yahweh Pakai aletna leh aloupina chu kahen, iPakai u Yahweh hi pathen dang sanga lenjo le loupijo ahi. 
Yahweh Pakaijin van le leiset chunga hin, twikanglen thuh sunga hin Aman along lhaina a adeidei abol ahi. 
Aman meiho jonghi leiset chunga kon a alentousah ahin, Aman gojuh pet in koljong aphetsah'in, huijong hi athil kholna a kon'in ahin lhadohjin ahi. 
Aman Egypt te insunga chun ainson in mihemle ganhing jong apeng masa jouse ana sugam in ahi. 
Aman Egypt a chun Pharaoh le amipi jouse douna in datmo umtah thil kidang ho ana tongdoh'in ahi. 
Aman namlentah tah ho anavolhun leng thupi tahtah ho jong ana satchap e. 
Amor lengpa Sihon, Bashan lengpa Og chuleh Canaan lengho jouse anathat gam in ahi. 
Aman agam u chu Ama goulo Israel te gamtum dingin anapen ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai namin hi tonsot a um jing ding ahi. 
Ajeh chu Yahweh Pakaijin amite chunga thutah a vaihom ding asohte chunga khotona anei ding ahi. 
Chitin namtinte doilim ho hi sana le dangka maimai mihem in asemthubou ahi. 
ama hon kam anei un ima aseithei pouvin, mit anei un ima amuthei pouvin, 
kol anei un ima ajathei pouvin nahkui anei un ima anahthei pouvin ahi. 
Doilimsemthu ho jouse le hiche tahsanho jouse chu hitobang ngen ahiuve. 
Vo Israel Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah'un! O thempu Aaron chilhahho, Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah'un, 
O Levi te Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah'un! Nangho Yahweh Pakai ginna neijouse Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah'un! 
Zion'a kon'in Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah'un ajeh chu Ama Jerusalem'a hin achengin ahi. Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah'un!
