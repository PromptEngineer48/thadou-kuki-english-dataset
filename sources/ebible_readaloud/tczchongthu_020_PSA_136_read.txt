La Bu.
136.
Yahweh Pakai chu aphat na ho jouse jeh in thangvah'un! Ajeh chu ami ngailutna dihtah hi tonsot gei a longlou ahi 
Semthu pathen ho, Pathen Pathen chu thangvahna peuvin. Ami ngailutna dihtah chu tonsot gei a longlou ahi. 
Pakai ho Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah na peuvin. Ajeh chu Ami ngailutna dihtah chu tonsot geija longlou ahi. 
Ama bou hin thil loupi ki dangtah chu abolji ahi. Ami ngailutna dihtah chu tonsot gei a longlou ahi. 
Thepna giltah a vanho hinsemdoh a Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah na peuvin. Ami ngailutna dihtah chu tonsot geija longlou ahi. 
Twilah a leiset umsah pa chu thangvahna peuvin. Ami ngailutna dihtah chu tonsot gei a longlou ahi. 
Van'a kon a vah semdohpa chu thangvah'un. Ami ngailutna dihtah chu tonsotna longlou ahi. 
Nisan sun a vai ahom in ahi, Ami ngailutna dihtah chu tonsot a longlou ahi. 
Chuleh lha leh ahsi hon jan ah vai ahom un ahi. 
Egypt gam a apeng masapen ho anathagam jeh'in Ama chu thangvahna peuvin. Ama mi ngailutna dihtah chu tonsot na longlou ahi. 
Aman Egypt ma kon'in Israel ana puidoh'e, Ami ngailutna chu tonsot a longlou ahi. 
Aman akhut thahatna leh aban thaneina pum in na atongin ahi. Ami ngailutna dihtah chu tonsotna longlou ahi. 
Twipi San sukhen a chu Ama ahi, thangvah na peuvin. Ami ngailutna dihtah chu tonsotna longlou ahi. 
Aman Israel chu anapui galkai e, Ami ngailutna dihtah chu tonsotna longlou ahi. 
Aman Pharaoh leh asepai te Twipi Sanlah a ana lehlut in ahi. Ami ngailutna dihtah chu tonsot a longlou ahi. 
Gamthip noi a amite apuigalkai jeh'in thangvah na peuvin, Am ingailutna dihtah chu tonsot a longlou ahi. 
Leng thupitah tahho anajeplhuh jeh'in thangvahna peuvin, Ami ngailutna dihtah chu tonsot a longlou ahi. 
Aman leng thahat tah tah ho anathat gam in ahi. Ami ngailutna dihtah chu tonsot a longlou ahi. 
Sihon Amor lengpa; Ami ngailutna dihtah chu tonsot a longlou ahi. 
Og Bashaan lengpa, Ami ngailutna dihtah chu tonsot a longlou ahi. 
Pathen Pathen'in hiche lengho gamchu agoulo din amite anapen ahi. Ami ngailutna chu tonsot a longlou ahi. 
Asohte Israel ten gam tumbeh a alodiuva anapeh ahi. Ami ngailutna dihtah chu tonsot a longlou ahi. 
Aman ihatmo nauvah eihin geldohjiu vin ahi. Amibngailutna dihtah chu tonsot a longlou ahi. 
Aman igalmihou va kon'in eihuhdoh jiuvin ahi. Ami ngailutna dihtah chu tonsot a longlou ahi. 
Aman hinkho neiho jouse dingin aneh achahdiu apejingin ahi. Ami ngailutna dihtah chu tonsot a longlou ahi. 
Van Pathen Pathen chu thangvahna peuvin, Ami ngailutna dihtah chu tonsot a longlou ahi.
