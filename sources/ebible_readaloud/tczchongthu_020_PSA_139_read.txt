La Bu.
139.
O Yahweh Pakai nangin kalungthim nakholchil in neihechen sohkeitan ahi. 
Nangin katouthimpet hihen, kadindoh pet hijongleh nahei. Gamla tah a kaum jeng vangin nangin kalung gel ngaito jouse nahei. 
Kakholjinpet hihen, inna katouthimpet hijongleh nangin neimun ahi, chuleh kabol jouse nahesohkei e. 
Yahweh Pakai ka thusei got jonghi kasei masangin nahejitai. 
Kamasanga nachen, kanunga jong neijuijin ahi, naphatthei bohna khutchu kaluchungah nangam'in ahi. 
Hitobang hetna hi keidingin aki dang val in, chuleh hitobang hetchet dinghi keidingin ahahsaval e. 
Nalhagaova kon a iti kajam mang thei dingham? Nanga kon a keima iti kajam mang theiding ham? 
Keima van a kaltou jongleng nangma naum in, mithigam ah lutjong leng nangma naum'e. 
Keima jingkah lhaving in lengdoh ingting twikhanglen gallang peh a gacheng jong leng, 
hichemun jenga jong chun nakhut in neikai hoi in natin, nathahat na in nei vengbit in nate. 
Keiman muthim jah a neisel in tijongleng chuleh kavel a vahjeng jong muthim sosah jongleng, 
muthimlah a kisel jing jongleng nanga kon a kakisel mangthei louding ahi. Ajeh chu nang dingin ajing jongchu sun banga vah ding ahi. Muthimleh vah jong nangdingin thakhat ahi bouve. 
Nangin ka oisung a thil umjuse jong najopmat mat a kanu naobu sunga neigontoh ahi. 
Hitobanga thil kidangtah a neina gontoh jeh'in ka thangvah'e! Na natoh nathil bol danhi datmo umtah ahi ti kalungthim apumpin kahe them e. 
Keima jatchombeh a kanu naobu muthimlah a eina kigontoh jeng jongchun nangman neinavesui jingin ahi. 
Kapen lhah masang a nangman nei namusa ahitai, nitin ka hinkhoa ka ki manchah na ding dan, phatche dungjui a ana kigongtoh chu nikhatcha jeng jong achelhah masanga na lekhabua ana kigoltohsa ahitai. 
O Yahweh Pakai kachung changa nalung gel nathilgon tohsaho chu iti mantama hitam simjou jai ahipoi! 
Asim jeng jong keijo chi ahipoi, neldi halsiho sang'in jong atamjoi. Kahung khahdoh gei injong nangin neina umpi jing nalai e! 
O ka Pathen Pathen miphalouho hi na suhmang hitavele ong! Tolthat ho kahinkhoa kon'in potdoh'un. 
Amaho hin na suminseu ve, nagalmi ho hin namin hi asamseu ve. 
O Yahweh Pakai nangma nahotbolte ho hi keiman jong kahot louding ham? Nangma nadouho hi kenjong kathet louding ham? 
Ahinai, nangman nathetho hi keiman pumthetna kathet ding ahi, ajeh chu nangma galmi ho chu keima galmi ho ahiuve. 
O Yahweh Pakai neikhol chil in kalung neihet peh in; neipatep inlang kahina hi kilang doh sah in. 
Kasunga hi nangma douva thilse umnalai am nei nodoh peh inlang tonsot lampi a neipui tan.
