La Bu.
140.
O Yahweh Pakai, miphalou hoa kon'in neihuhdoh in, pumhat-a natong hoa kon'in neihoidohsah'in, 
ajeh chu alung sung'uva thilse jeng agong un, beitih um louvin gal abol jiuvin ahi. 
Alei gui-u gul in mi achuh banginamang chauvin, athuseidoh jouseu jong gul-gu tobang ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai, miphalou ho khutna kon hin neihoidoh in, pumhatna natong hoa kon'in neihuhdoh in; ajeh chu kakipal lhuhna din thisle jeng agong un ahi. 
Mi kiletsah hon kei matna din thang akam un, khao ajan-doh'un lampi dunga thang ei-kampeh'un ahi. 
Keiman Yahweh Pakai kom'a, “Nangma hi ka Pathen Pathen nahi!” O Yahweh Pakai, khotona dinga kataona nei sanpeh tan. 
O Yahweh Pakai hatchungnung, athahat pan-a eihuhdoh pa; nangman gal kisat nikhoa nei huhdoh ahi. 
Yahweh Pakai, migilouho adei bangin chesah hih'in, athilse gonhou chu lolhin sahhih'in; achuti louva ahileh kiletsahna dim diu ahi. 
Kagal miten kachunga thil phalou tohgon anei jal un, manthah nan lhung uhen. 
Meiam kong chu alu chunguvah chuhsah'in, meilah a seplut in; ahilouleh ajamdoh louna diu twiveina kotong'ah lhalut jeng uhen. 
Ka gamsung uva hin ajou jouva mihehse ho khantousah hihbeh'in, pumhat'a natong ho achung uva thilse chuhsah'in lang sumang in. 
Hinlah suhgentheija umte Yahweh Pakaijin asumil poi, ti kahen; Aman vaichate thu adih a atanpeh ding ahi, ti kahei. 
Tahbeh in mi kitah hon namin athangvah diu, chule michonpha ho na kom'a cheng diu ahi.
