La Bu.
141.
O Yahweh Pakai, nangma kakouve, Panpi ngehna katao hi nei ngaipeh loijin! 
Kataona hi nahenga gimnamtui kilhut bangin chule kakhut teni kadopsang hi nilhah lang kilhaina gantha bangin neisanpeh tan. 
O Yahweh Pakai, kakam'a hin kamdal neikoipeh'in. Kakam sung nei vetpeh jingin. 
Thilse bolna din kalungthim aselam-a kon'in neihoidah sah in, thil dihlou bolna'a neipansah hih'in. Thil dihlou bol hon aphat chompiu thangset umtah thil hoa chan nei neisah hih'in. 
Miphan eiphoh hen hichu ngailutna joh ahi! ama hon eisuhdihna chu damna ahipoi, Miphalou hon thilse abol dounan katao jinge. 
Alamkaiteu kol'a akisot lhah tengleh, miphalou hon ka thusei angaidiu chuteng dih ahinsah diu ahi. 
Songpi akikhet keh teng tol'a akitakeh bangin, miphalou ho gule chang ho jong vui louva kithe jal ding ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai hatchungnung, panpi ngaijin na henga kataove. Nangma kakiselna kulpi nahi; amaho khut-a thina neitosah hih in. 
Kei ona dinga thang akam-uva kon'in neihuhdoh in, thilpha lou jenga chon ho thangkol'a nei o-sah hih in. 
Miphalou ho chu amaho thang kam-a chun oh uhen, kijam chap tauhen; keivang nei hoidoh sah'in.
