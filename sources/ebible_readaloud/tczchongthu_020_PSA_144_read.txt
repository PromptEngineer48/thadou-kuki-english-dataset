La Bu.
144.
Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah'un Ama chu kasongpi ahi. Aman kakhut teni hi galsatje dan eihil in, chule kakhut jung ho hi galsatna'a manchahje dan eihil ahi. 
Ama hi eipanpi phapen leh kakulpi ahin, kahoidohna pal leh eihuhdohpa ahi. Chitin namtin anoijah akunsahji ahi. 
O Yahweh Pakai, mihem hi ipi hija hibanga nagel-lut a, mihem chapa jeng jong ipi hija hibanga nakhohsah hitam? 
Ajeh chu mihem hi hai khat seh a hing ahibouvin, ahin-nikho jong nisa-lep tobanga mangpai thei ahi bouve. 
Yahweh Pakai vanho hinhong in hung kum lhan, molsang ho hi tham'in lang meikhun len mangsah in. 
Kehchang len-dohsah inlang gal mite thejalsah in! Nathalchang kap inlang jamsah soh hel in. 
Van'a kon'in nakhut hin lhang in, twithuh lah a konleh kagal mite thaneina'a kon'in nei huhdoh in. 
Akam'un jou jeng aseijun, thudih kaseije atiuvin akihahsel un, hinlah jou jeng aseijun ahi. 
O ka Pathen Pathen, na henga lathah khat sange; selangdah jangsom kibut chu mangchan na vahchoila sange. 
Ajeh chu nangman lengho galjona napen! Nalhacha David jong athi nading chemjam'a kon'in nahuhdoh jin ahi. 
Neihuhdoh'in! Kagal mite thaneina'a kon'in neihuhdoh'in, akam un jou jeng aseijun; thutah kaseiding ahi tin akihahsel-un, hinlah jou jengseh aseijun ahi. 
Kachapa teu akhandon laijun thingphung phatah banginkhanlhit sah in, ka chanuteu jong leng inpi jemhoi dinga kisem khompi hoitah ho bang uhen. 
Ka changpang jouseu jong lousoh jouse pum'in dimsoh hen, kaloujaova kagan chateu asang sang'in, sangsom som chan geijin pung hen. 
Kagancha teu gai pha hen, veiset le apengsol um hih hel hen! Ka khopi sung jeng uva jong lung gim khoisat beihel hen. 
Hitobang thilpha achunga lhung ho kipah uhen! chule a Pathen'u Pathen, Yahweh Pakai hikha nam mite chu kipah cheh'u hen!
