La Bu.
148.
Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah'un! Vanhoa kon'in Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah'un! Vanthamjol-a kon'in Ama chu thangvah'un! 
Avantil te jousen thangvah'un! Van sepai te jousen thangvah'un! 
Nisa le lha-in ama chu thangvah'un! Aphelhet lhet ahsi hon Ama chu thangvah'un! 
Achungsang lam'a um vantham jol'in thangvah'un! Meilom chung sangpeh a um meitwi hon Ama chu thangvah'un! 
Thilsemho jousen Yahweh Pakai chu thangvahna peuvin, ajeh chu Aman thu apen ahileh amaho chu ahung kisemdoh jengun ahi. 
Aman tonsot geija dinga anagontohsoh ahitai. Ama Dan semchu itih hijongleh ki khokhel louding ahi. 
Leisetna kon'in thangvah'un, twikhanglen lailunga thilsemhon thangvah'un, 
meileh gel hon, buhbang leh meilom hon thangvah'un, huileh adap asatnin Ama thu chu ngaijun. 
Mol leh lhangho, aga nei thingphung ho le Cedar thinghon, 
gamsa ho le ganhingho jousen, ganhing neochacha ho le vachahon, 
leiset chunga lengho le mipite jousen, leiset chunga vaihom ho leh thutanho jousen, 
pasal khangthah ho le numei khangthah hon, tehse ho leh chapanghon, 
abonchauvin Yahweh Pakai min thangvah'u hen! Ajeh chu Aminhi aloupi lheh jenge, hiche Amin loupina hi leiset le vanho jouse sanga sangjo ahi. 
Aman amite chu ki khat atundohpeh tan ahi, ama dinga kitahna neijouse Aman angailut Israel mipite chu jana apetan ahi. Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah'in umhen!
