La Bu.
149.
Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah'un! Yahweh Pakai vahchoina lathah sauvin. Tahsan chate kikhopna ah avahchoila sauvin. 
O Israel nasempa chunga kipah'in, O Jerusalem mipite, na lengpau chungah kipah'un. 
Khongpi gintoh semjang saito thon lampum pum'in Aminchu vahchoijun, 
Ajeh chu amite chungah akipah'in, mi nun nem te chu jana lallukhuh akhupeh tai. 
Pathen Pathen chate chu hitobang jana achanjeh un, jankhovah'in avahchoila sauhen, 
Akhut'in chemjam hemtah kichoiyu henlang, akamsunguuvah kipah tah in Pathen Pathen thangvah la sauhen. 
Chitin namtin ho chunga aphulah theina diuleh, namtin chunga engbolna apeh theina diuvin, 
alengteu thihkhao kola akihen'uva, chuleh alamkaiteu thihkhaova akikan theina diuvin, 
achung uva thu kitanna dungjuiya akitohdohna dingin. Hiche hi Pathen Pathen chate chan loupitah achannao chu ahi. Yahweh Pakai chu thangvah'in umhen!
