Thuchihbu.
3.
Kachapa kahilna ho sumil hihbeh in, Ka thupeh ho na lungsunga chen sah jingin, 
Hichengsea kon hin, nakum ajop sau thei ding, chule phatheina bukim nachan theina ding ahi. 
Itih chan hijongleh kitah na le khotona nalha-kang lou ding, hiche teni hi na ngonga khivui oa naki o-ding, hichengse hi na lungthim sunga lekhajem banga naki-jih lut ding ahi. 
Chuteng Pathen Pathen le mipi lungdei lam nahung hi thei ding, minphatna naki-lam doh thei ding ahi. 
Nalungthim pumpin Yahweh Pakai bou tahsan in, nangma hetna jenga kisong hih in. 
Nalam lhah natin chenga Yahweh Pakai jeng na phondoh ding, chuteng Aman na lampi nakomu peh ding ahi. 
Nangma mitvet jenga kichang chihsah boldan, Yahweh Pakai ging pum-in thilse dalhan. 
Chutileh natahsa dinga damna hi ding, chule nagu na chang dinga hatdohna hi ding ahi. 
Nanei nagou chule ga masa jouse Yahweh Pakai jabol pum'in tohdoh in, 
Chutileh Aman nachangpang nasuh dimpeh a, na lengpitwi kholna jong aletset'a nasun dim peh ding ahi. 
Kachapa, Yahweh Pakaijin nasuhgim nachu nahsah mon koi dan, chule Aman nasuh dihna chu lungnop mo peh hih in. 
Ajeh chu Yahweh Pakaijin asuhdih ho hi angailut jeh ahin, mipan acha dihtah asuhdihji banga aphosalji ahi. 
Chihna le hetthem theina kimu chu lungmonga kipah ding ahi. 
Ajeh chu chihna kiti hi dangka sanga phachomjo ahin, chule aga chu sana sanga jong phachomjo ahi. 
Chihna hi songmantam sanga mantamjo ahin, nangaichat thil ho jeng to jong tekah thei ahipoi. 
Hinkho sausah thei jong chihna hin akhut jet lam'a atuh chah ahin, haosatna le jabolna jeng jong akhutvei lam'a akituh chah ahi. 
Chihna hin kipa thanop na lampia napui ding, alampi jouse chu lung chamna jeng ahi bouve. 
Chihna kisanlut chan dinga hinna thingphung ahin, chihna tuh chah jing ho chu anunnom ahiuve. 
Chihna mangchan Yahweh Pakaijin vannoi leiset ana tungdoh in, thil hetthemna jal'a vanmun ho asem doh ahitai. 
Ama thil hetkhenna jal'a komtong ho kisemdoh a, meilhang pumpi jong gotwi a julhaji ahi. 
Kachapa, chihna tothon, thil datkhenji dan them in, namit vet in hiche ho jouse hi panna bei sosah hih in. 
Ajeh chu hiche ho hin nalhagao asuhnou jing a, nangong chang jenga jong khivui banga kijep thei ahi. 
Chuteng lungmonga nalam lhah le ding, nakeng jong kipal talou ding ahi. 
Kichatna beihel'a jalkhun najot ding, na imut teng jongleh lungmong tah a na-imut ding ahi. 
Kiging man louva thi ding kicha hihbeh in, migilou hoa kon manthahna hunglhung ding jong nagin louhel ding ahi. 
Ajeh chu Yahweh Pakai bou na kulpi ahin, Aman nakeng jeng jong thanga ao-sah louhel ding ahi. 
Nathahat laisen na kithopi thei chan dingin, thilpha nabol peh ding ahi. 
Naheng nakom jenga jong chen lang hung kilen, jing le na le peh nanga tin, hinlah neinasan bol hihbeh in. 
Naheng nakom dounan thil gong hihbeh in, ajeh chu ama hon nakison piuva nakom'a cheng ahiuve. 
Nachunga thilse bol-lou chu ajehbei in kinahpi dan, 
Chule pumthoa natong mihem chu thangsetpi hih in, alam lhahna tinchenga kon chu nakiven joh ding ahi. 
Hitobang milung dihlou chu Yahweh Pakaijin athet ahin, milungtheng vang chu Yahweh Pakaiya kingai jing ahi. 
Yahweh Pakaijin asapsetna chu miphalou ho inchunga alhung jin, mikitah ho chenna in chu Yahweh Pakaijin phatthei aboh ding ahi. 
Yahweh Pakaijin minoise ho chunga noisena alhunsah jin, akineosahte chunga lunglhaina aneijin ahi. 
Miching chun mite a kon jana akimu ding, mingol ho vang muda chang diu ahitai.
