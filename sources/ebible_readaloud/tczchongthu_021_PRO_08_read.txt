Thuchihbu.
8.
Chihnan kho ahin sap teng jatei ding gon, chule thil hetkhen nan lhang asap teng ngaijin. 
Lampi pam le lhangvum dung a, chihna chu ading le'in ahi. 
Khopi lutna kelkot phung dung a, kho len tinchang maija, chule kotjum lutna thei jousea lhang asam in ahi. 
“Mijouse jah ah kakou ahin, mihem chapa jouse kakou ahi. 
Milham ho nahiu vin, lunggel them hina din kihil un, mingol ho jengin jong lunglut in ngaijun. 
Ngaitem un, keiman thilphachom tah nahil uvinge, kakam sunga kon thudih jengbou pot ding ahi. 
Ajeh chu kakam in thudih bou aseiji ding, adihlou sei kiti hi kakam in ajahda ahi. 
Kakam akon thu kisei jouse adih ahin, akikal le ache khel ding ima um lou ahi. 
Kathu hil hekhen theipa chun adih akisan ding, hetthemna neipa dinga chu adih bou ahi. 
Sumlepai sang'in ka thuhil kilhen un, Sana thengsel sanga jong hetna naki lhen joh diu ahi. 
Ajeh chu sana kijem pah sanga chihna hi thupi ahin, na lungdei chan toh kitekah thei ahipoi. 
Chihna hi keima kahin chingthei tah a um kahi, hetna le thil hetkhen themna nei kahi. 
Yahweh Pakai ginna chun thilse ahotbol in, hijeh chun keiman kiletsahna le hoithona, thil aphalou, ahilou louva thu kisei chan chu kathet ahi. 
Keima'a kon hin, chihna giltah le phachom tah a kihilna aum in, asung thua hetna le thahat chu keija bou um ahi. 
Keijal'a lenghon vai-apoh ahin, keijal'a bou vaihom ten adih a thutanna anei ahi. 
Keijal'a alen alal ten vai ahop jiu, chule keijal'a bou alal ten vai apoh theiyu ahi. 
Keima eingailu ho kangailun, giltah a eihol ten eimu tei jiuvin ahi. 
Keima haona le jabolna jousen eijui in, phattheina le haona chun eidalha jipoi. 
Ka thilpeh ho hi sana sanga phachom ahin, sana thengsel sanga jong hoijoa, chule kanatoh jong sum le pai sanga phajo ahi. 
Keima thudih tah a lamlha jing kahin, thutanna adih lam jeng chu kaho-ji ahi. 
Keima eingailu hon gou alo diu kasem peh in, agou kholnau jouse kasuh dimpeh-jin ahi. 
Yahweh Pakaijin atil abulla athilsem apat ei-umsah ahin, khanglui apat athilsem masapen kahitai. 
Atil-a leiset kisem masang, kum asang sang masanga pat keima tundohsa chu kahitai. 
Kapen chun anem, atong lai jong ana um pon, twi putna jouse jong khatcha ana um poi. 
Mol le lhang kigol toh masang, chule thinglhang jouse kisemtoh masanga pat ana um kahitai. 
Pathen Pathen'in leiset le tollhang asem masang, vannoija leigum kisemdoh masanga pat um kahi. 
Pathen Pathen'in vanho atundoh masanga um kahin, twikhanglen kuol khum vanthamjol alhundoh masanga jong um kahitai. 
Pathen Pathen'in chunglam'a vanthamjol ho atundoh ahin, leinoija pat twi aput dohsah masanga jong um kahin, 
Twikhanglen geilah jong alet lhahna lou dinga asem toh, twipi jengin jong athu angaina dinga asem toh, leiset khompi jouse jong atundoh masanga pat um kahi. 
Keima tah jong akom-cha'a khut them bang um kahin, nitin le phat tin in akom-a kapansan kipatah in kaum jingin ahi. 
Pathen Pathen'in mihemte chenna dia leiset atundoh ahin, keima jong kipah tah-in kaum in, mihemte lah a thanopnan kadim e. 
Hijeh chun kachate ho kasei ngaijun, kathu ngaija ka lampia che jing ho kipah ding'u ahi. 
Kathuhil ho ngaijun lang ching theijun, kahilchah-nau hi tuh chah jingun. 
Ka thusei ngaija mihem chu anunnom hi ding, ama chun nitin'a kakot phunga anga jing ding, ka kotpi phunga tatlouvin adingin ahi 
Ajeh chu keima eimudoh chan chun hinna akimu tan, chule Yahweh Pakaiya kon'in hepina achang'un ahi. 
Amavang keima eidonlou chan chun, ama le aman gimna akimu jin, keima ei hot chan chun, thina jeng angai chat ahi bouve.”
