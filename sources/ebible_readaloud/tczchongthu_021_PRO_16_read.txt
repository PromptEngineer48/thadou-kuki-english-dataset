Thuchihbu.
16.
Mihem in alungsunga tohgon aneijin, kam kilhang sel'a thu kisei jong Yahweh Pakaijin adonbut teijin ahi. 
Mihem in angatna lampi chu amit-tenin aven adih jengin agel in, hinlah Yahweh Pakaijin alunggel ngaito jouse akhol chil jin ahi. 
Nabol natoh abonchan Yahweh Pakaiya pedoh jengin, chutileh nathilgon aboncha lolhing ding ahi. 
Yahweh Pakaijin thil jouse akimanchahna ding dol cheh a aumsah ahin, migilou jeng jong hahsat nikhoa dinga aumsah ahi. 
Yahweh Pakaijin mikiletsah kiti athet in, hijeh chun hethem un, amaho chu gotna chang tei diu ahi. 
Tahsan theija chonna le kitahna hin, themmona jeng jong adihsah in, Yahweh Pakai ginna jeh'in mihem in thil phalou apeldoh theijin ahi. 
Mihem chu achena lam jouse Yahweh Pakai lungdei ahitengleh, Yahweh Pakaijin ama chu amelmate toh jong akichamsah jitai. 
Thil dihlou pum'a kai-tampi kidon khom sang'in, thildih'a lhomcha nei ding aphajoi. 
Mihem in alung in chena ding lampi akisem in, Yahweh Pakaijin achena ding lam ako-peh jin ahi. 
Lengpa kamsunga kon'in Pathen Pathen thusei ahung gingdoh jin, hijeh chun lengpan thu a tankhel jipon ahi. 
Yahweh Pakaijin thil adih in atetoh jin, hijeh chun thil ijakai Yahweh Pakaijin atetohsa jeng ahi. 
Leng ho din thil adihlouva bol hi thet umtah ahin, ajeh chu Ama vaihomna hi thudih a kingam ahi. 
Leiguia kon thudih kiseidoh hi lengpa din lunglhai aum in, ajeh chu lengpan thu adih a sei chu angailun ahi. 
Lengpa lunghan nahi gihsalna ahin, hinlah miching chun lengpa lunghanna adamsah jin ahi. 
Lengpa mailhai sel'a aum teng, damna akibe jin, lengpa lunglhaina jong hi, go hung phin lhaji meilhang tobang ahi. 
Sana sang'in chihna kimu dei aum jon, dangka sang'a jong thil hetthemna kisan ding ahi. 
Michondih chu thilse lam'a kon'in akiheidoh jin, achena tinchenga limgehpa chu kihinso tei ding ahi. 
Manthah ding kon le kiletsahna ahunglhung loi jin, kipal lhuh ding konleh, lhagaova hoithana ahung um loi jitai. 
Mikiletsah hotoh thil kichomdoh kihop sang'in, kineosah a vaichate toh lhat khom anom e. 
Min ahilna ngaija chu khangtou jing ding, chule Yahweh Pakaiya kisong chan chu anunnom ahi. 
Mi-lungchingpa chu thil hethemmi kiti ding, chule lunglhaina'a thuseipan mi athujo tei ding ahi. 
Chihna nei mi chu hinna twinah tobang ahin, mingol ho ngolna hi talen matna bep ahi. 
Miching lungsunga kon chihna thucheng hungpot chan chu, Dan toh akikal jipon, akamcheng soh jouse chu mi thununna ahi bouve. 
Ngailutna'a thu kisei hi khoiju tobang ahin, lhagao le tahsa dingin antwitah damna ahi. 
Mikhat dingin lampi khat chu lamdih jengin akimun, hinlah alampi chu ajona thina ahi. 
Natong gimpa hin, aneh ding gelna'a atoh ahin, ajeh chu aneh nomna alol chun achama atohsah ahitai. 
Mihem panna bei chun thil phalou jeng agel in, akamcheng soh jong melkah gin tobang ahi. 
Thil phalou bolpan, baotamna aso-sah jin, aguh a thuphong lepan golcha kingaitah jong asukhen in ahi. 
Pumthoa natong hon aheng akom jeng jong atilseu-vin, chuting aheng akompa jong lamdih-lou alhung jitai. 
Koi hileh mit teni-singa phetsah jichun, thilpha lou jeng angaiton, muh phunsah sah pan jong thil phalou abolsoh ahita bouve. 
Sam-kang liuva hinna hi, loupina lallukhuh ahin, adih jenga hinkho akiman teng, hitobanga hi hinkhosot thei ahi bouve. 
Ki hatsah sang'in thohhat tah a lungneng apha jon, alungput athua nunsah joupa chu, khopi khat nokhum'a kilah sang'in aphachom joi. 
Khutphangsunga vang akive jin, hinlah vang phatna leh phatlouna chu Yahweh Pakaiya hung kon bou ahi.
