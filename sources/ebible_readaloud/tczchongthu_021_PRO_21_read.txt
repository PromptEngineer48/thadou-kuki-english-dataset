Thuchihbu.
21.
Pathen Pathen'in vadung twi alon sahna nom nom'a alonsah banga, lengpa lungthim jong ahei lele thei ahi. 
Mihem in achonna jouse chu amit mun dih asa-jin, hinlah Yahweh Pakaijin mihem lungthim akhol chil jin ahi. 
Kilhaina gantha sang'in, thildih bol'a thu adih a tan ding hi, Yahweh Pakai lunglhaina ahijoi. 
Kiletsahna lungthim pua mitha chule miphalou ho natoh aboncha chonset jeng ahi. 
Miching hon gunchutah a tohgon anei jouseu abulhing theijin, amavang amin mei meija natong ho alhasam teijin ahi. 
Jou le nal thoa nei le gou kichom khom jouse amangthah ding bep ahin, thina thang kisong tobang ahi bouve. 
Thu adih a tan ding mihon anahsah lou jeh un, miphalou pumthoa natong hon panna bei jin asumang jitauve. 
Themmona neipan lamdih ajot jipon, lungthim thengpa lam lhahna adih jingin ahi. 
Numei hanse khat toh in nom khat a chenkhom sang'in, chungden in-vum'a chen ding aphajoi. 
Miphalou lhagao'in thilse jeng bou angai chan, aheng akom in jong ama'a kon'in khotona amu jipoi. 
Panna beija mi tot chaveipa gotna akipeh teng, milham in chih-nan anei theijin chule miching ho akihil chan in hetna akibelep jie. 
Mikitah chu miphalou in amelchih jingin, miphalou vang chu achunga hamsetna lhung tei ding ahi. 
Koi hileh migenthei akateng kijahmosah chu, ama kana ahung lhun teng koiman ajah phah peh louding ahi. 
Aguh'a kipa thilpeh hin alunghang jong adamsah theijin, koima hetlouva nehguh kipe hin lungsat jong adaisah jitai. 
Thu adih a kitan hin mikitah akipasah in, hinlah miphalou ho dingin tijat umtah ahi. 
Mi koi hileh hetkhen themna'a kon lamvaija vahmang chan chu, mithi hotoh kivop khomna ding mun ajot ahitai. 
Tahsa nopsahna lamjeng lhangai ho chun vaichatna akimudiu ahin, koi hijongleh jule noplenna-a kipe ho chu itihchan hijongleh haodoh loudiu ahi. 
Koi hileh min akhoto ding jeng ngaicha a chu, vaicha pum'a um jing ding, chule ju-a lop jong haodoh thei louding ahi. 
Numei lungkhoh le lungsa jing, jia neija chen khom sang'in, het phahlou neldi gam'a chen aphajoi. 
Miching chenna in-ah nei le gou lutah tah aum in, hinlah mingol in abonchan ane-mang jitai. 
Koi hileh chonphatna lam khohsah a, chule mi khotona neipa chun jabolna amu tei ding ahi. 
Miching mihatpa chenna khopi jong akholchil sohkeijin, chule akisonpi pen-u kulpi jong alekhup'a asuhchim hel ding ahi. 
Koi hileh thipkhen cha um'a lei kituh tang chu, gim le hesohna'a jong kivengdoh ding ahi. 
Mi noise le kiletsahna jong hi, panna beija tot chaveipa kiseina ahi bouve. 
Mi thase ho chu aduchat uvin acham lou'a thina alhut ahi. Ajeh chu ama hon akhut-u mangcha'a natoh ding anom pouve. 
Miphalou hon nitin in anei asit jingun, miching in anei ahom in, akile lah ngai poi. 
Miphalou ho kilhaina gantha chu thet umtah ahina chungchon in, phatlouna lungpun hin phaldoh taleu, ichan geija thet um cheh ding hitam! 
Jou le nal seija hettohsah a pang ho suhbeija um ding, hinlah adih a hettohsah a pangpa thusei min angaipeh jing ding ahi. 
Miphalou chu adihlou'in aki ngamsah jin, hinlah mikitah chun atoh masang'in ageltoh masa jin ahi. 
Mihem chihna le hetkhen themna chule aguh a kithumop themna chengsea kon hin, Yahweh Pakai deh-jou ding aumpoi. 
Galsat nikhoa manchah din sakol gonsan aum in, hinlah gal-jona hi Yahweh Pakaiya kon bou ahi.
