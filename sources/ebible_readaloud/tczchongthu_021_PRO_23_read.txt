Thuchihbu.
23.
Thunei vaihom khat toh ankong sohkhom dinga naum tengleh, na ang-sunga kiloi chengse chu melchih in. 
Milop nahileh nalol chutoh chemcha kanbeh jengin. 
Vaihompa neh ding anho chu ducha hih in, ajeh chu nangma lhep lhahna ding an jeng ahi. 
Haodoh ding ngaito na boujeh in na-tong hih in, chule jou le phatlou a kon'in haodoh ding jong ngaito hih in. 
Hitia haodohna dia namu chan chu miphetkah louva mang ding ahi. Ajeh chu chomlou kah'in muvanlai banginvanlam angan aleng mang jitai. 
Muda sasa'a navepa toh an nekhom hih in, aneh chu twitah hijongleh ducha hih in. 
Amapa chu alungthim in aphun in, nangma jah din, “Haneh in lang, hadon in don in” tia asei vang chun alungthim in nathet bouve. 
Nangman themcha naneh jong chu naloh lhah ding, chule lunglhai nathu nasei jouse jong panna bei soh ding ahi. 
Mingol ho jah dingin houlim boldan, ajeh chu chihna thu nasei jouse imalouvin gel khaget inte. 
Gol lui laija pat umsa gamgi song chu nabodoh louhel ding, chule nu le pa neilou chagate lou-laia jong chelut hih in. 
Ajeh chu amaho lhatdohpa chu mihat tah ahin, alhatdohpa tah uvin amaho athumop a apanhu ding ahi. 
Nalungthim in nakihilna ngaisang'in lang, hetthemna thu kisei chengse jong ngai phan. 
Na chate chu gahna beijin koihih in, nangman mol-lam jong le chun thilou diu; 
Chule nangman achunga mol nalap-a ahileh, thina lampia kon nahuhdoh ding joh ahi bouve. 
Kachapa, nangman chihna lungthim nanei poule, keima jong kipah ding kahi. 
Thudih chu nakam sunga kon nasei teng, keima lhagao jong kipah lheh ding ahi. 
Nalungthim in mi chonsete thang thip-pi dan, amavang nitin a Yahweh Pakai nagin ding ahi. 
Adihtah in nikho hunglhung dinga kinepna khat aum jing e, nangma kinepna jong mangthah louhel ding ahi. 
Kachapa ngai tem in, chule chingthei tah in um in, nalungthim in lamdih jenga lam lhah ding ngaiton. 
Ju-kham holah a lha-hihbeh in, sane holah jenga jong jao ding geldan. 
Ajeh chu ju le sa'a lop chan chu vaichat nan alhun khum diu, chule emut so nan atom u va ponse tobang diu ahi. 
Nahinga napa thumop ngai jingin, nanu jong ateh teng thetbol hih hel in. 
Thutah chu kisan lut in lang pedoh hih in, chihna le kigah chahna chule hetthemna jong nakisan lut ding ahi. 
Chate adih teng apa athanom in akipah jin, chapa chingtah hingpa jong achapa chunga kipah lheh ding ahi. 
Hijeh chun nanu le napa kipah thanom in umsah jingin, chule nahing nu nanu jong kipah tah in umsah jingin. 
O Kachapa, nalungthim neipen lang, kanun le khan abonchan namit tenin vepha tan. 
Numei kijoh ho hi tijat um kokhuh tobang ahin, numei hija jonva le jong twikul tobang ahi. 
Amanu chun gucha michom tobanginmi achang lhih le jin, chule pasal tamtah akipal sah in asuchavei jitai. 
Koiham avangse a pang? Koiham genthei thoh a pang? Koiham mitoh kiphun? Chule Koiham aki-engsah? 
Hitobang miho chu ju-phung dalha louva tou chule jua lop ho ahiuve. 
Ju chu asan keikui teng vehihbeh in, chule khon na vetnom umtah hihen lang; bailam tah a gah don thei di hijongleh, vetding jong go-hih hel in. 
Ajeh chu achaina teng gul gunei in mi achuh banga nachuh lo ding ahi. 
Namit tenin thil kidangtahtah amu ding, nalunggel jeng jong kikalah lah ding ahi. 
Chule twikhanglen lailunga lum den banga nalup den ding, konga kitungdoh khom chunga umpa banga naum'a nalup den ding ahi. 
Chutengleh “amaho hin eivo tauve,” tia nasei ding, hinlah “eivoh nau maha ima kamupoi,” ama hon eivo nauvin, ahinlah kathoh lal poi. Kakhahdoh teng aban kahol nah lai ding ahi, tia nasei ding ahi.
