Thuchihbu.
27.
Jing nikhoa dingin kiletsah tothon thu seihih in, ajeh chu itobang nikho ahung lhun ding nahepoi. 
Nakamsunga nakipachat sangin, midang kipachat sah jon, chule nang le nang nakam sunga naki vahchoi sangin, hetkhahlou kholgam mi kivahchoi sah in. 
Songhi agih lheh in, neldi jong gihtah ahi. Hinlah mingol lunghanna hi neldi sanga jong gihjo nalai ahi. 
Lunghan hi giloutah ahin, lungsat in jong mi achup jin; hinlah thangthipna hi akhoh jon ahi. 
Aguh a kingailut sangin, lhang phonga kiphosal aphajoi. 
Gol phatah a kon kimavona chu kitah jal ahin, melman eibeng chop vang kilhep lhahna ahi bouve. 
Mihem aoi-vasa dingin khoiju jeng jong lohso nabep ahin, agilkilpa din vang an twimopen jong alhum tobang ahi. 
Ain'a kon vahmangpa chu, abusunga kon lengmang vahcha tobang ahi. 
Thao le gimnamtui in mihem lungthim akipahsah jin, gim hesohna vang hin mihem lhagao athohlal sah jin ahi. 
Nagol hihen napagol hijongleh nung sun hih in, hahsat nan nahin phah nikhon jong nasopipa in-a sunglha hih in; ajeh chu gam latah a um penpi sopi sang'in, naicha um hengle kom aphachom joi. 
Kachapa, chingtheijin kalungthim kipa sah jingin, chutileh eidoupa jeng jong kadonbut thei ding ahi. 
Milung ching in thil phalou hunglhung ding ahethem masan, ama le ama ahoidoh theije, hinlah milham vang chu kho hephalou tah in aum in, gim hesoh atoh jin ahi. 
Hetkhah lou mikhat batsah nadinga kitung pa chu, aponsil kichom peh jenga! Hetphalou mi dinga, hitobang mihem chu akipehdoh jeng thei nadem! 
Jingkah matah-a thouva othong jejua, phatthei osam chun aheng akom agaosap joh ahi bouve. 
Tanglouva nilhum keija gojuh hi, numei phunse tohbang bep ahi. 
Hitobang numei phohtang theija chun, khohui geija aphotang jou ding ahin; akhutphang jet lam'a jong, thaotwi akihamdoh thei ding ahi. 
Thihchun thih mama anol hemthei bangin, mihem jong khat in khat akikhelsah thei ahi. 
Koi hileh theiphung phut chan in aga aneh bangin, koi hileh apupa ngaichat jouse hethem chu, mi jabolna achang teijin ahi. 
Twichung ivet teng mihem eima mai akilahdoh ji bangin, mihem kiti jong hi alungthim in aphondohji ahi. 
Thina le thilse in bei tih aneilou bang chun mihem mit teni jeng jong thil vet achim joupoi. 
Khangbel hi dangka lhahtheng na ding ahin, thuh khuh jong hi sana sonjolna'a kimang ahi; hijeh achu mihem jeng jong pachatna dinmun'a aum laitah a bou patepna changji ahi. 
Mingol chu lhengkong sunga suhdun changbong toh goitha jongleh chun, angolna laitah chu ama tahsa'a kon potdoh dehlou ding ahi. 
Nakelngoi honte umchan hethem jingin, chule nabong honte jeng jong phaten vephan; 
Ajeh chu haona hi tonsotna ding ahipon, lallukhuh jong hi akhang khang ding um hinam? 
Hampa apul chaisoh tengleh ana ahung noudoh kit jin, chule molchunga hampa louhing jouse jong kichom khomji ahi. 
Nakelngoi teuvin ponsil ding natopeh diu, chule nakelchateu vin jong loumun khat man natopeh diu ahi. 
Nadon khopset kelcha noitwi tatlouva um jing ding, chule na in-sung vahna ding leh nalhacha nungah ho vahna dia jong lhingset ding ahi.
