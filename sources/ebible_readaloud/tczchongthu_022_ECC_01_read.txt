Thuhilpa.
1.
Hiche hi Thuhilpa, Jerusalem’a vaipoa pang, Leng David chapa thuseidoh ho ahi. 
Thuhilpa chun, “Ijakai ajehbei tobang ngen ahi, ajeh bei tobang ngensen ahi sohkeije,” ati. 
Nipi noija hi mihon atoh gim jeh’uva hi ipi aki mudoh uvem? 
Khang achemang in, khang ahunglhung kit in, hinlah leiset hin kikhelna aneikha hih laiye. 
Nisa ahung sohdoh in chule alhum kit jin, chule kinotah’a kipandoh kit dingin akigopai jin ahi. 
Hui chu lhang lama anungin, chule sah lama ahung kihei jin ahi. Avel kol avelkol in, ahui nun chun akollin ahi. 
Vadung ho twikhanglenna alonglut’uvin, hinlah twikhanglen adim thei deh poi. Chuti chun twi ho chu vadunga ahung kile kit jiuvin chule ahung longdoh un twikhanglenna chun alut kit jiuve. 
Thil ijakai hi seithei louhel lin thachol chol a um'in ahi. Ijat imit uva imu vang un ilungnau achim poi. Ijat ina khang uva ijah jeng vang un ilunglhai jou pouve. 
Achesa thusim kivelso kitna ahi bouve. Masang peh’a ana kibol chaisa ngensen ahiuve. Nisa noija imacha thil athah tahbeh aumpoi. 
Khatvei veileh mihon aseijun, “Imacha athah kiti aumpoi,” atiuve. Hinlah tahbeh mong in alui ahi bouvin, imacha athah kiti hi aumpoi. 
Achesa phatna ipi anasoh em ti igeldoh pouve, chule khang ahunglhung ding hoa jong tua i-thilbol houhi koiman ahetdoh louding ahitai. 
Keima, Thuhilpa hi Israel te leng kahin, chule Jerusalem'a chenga kahi. 
Hetkhen themna holdoh theina dingin keima ka kipum pehdoh jengin, chule nisa noija na kitongdoh ho chengse hi chihna’a kholdoh dingin ka gel’e. Keiman kahin mudoh chu Pathen'in mihemte henga aumna akiphondoh hi ahi. 
Keiman nisa noija thil ache jomho ijakai hi kavetna ahileh atahbeh in, abonchan ajeh bei aphachom louding tobangbep ahije. 
Adih lou chu aki sudih tahbeh joupon, chule amangsa jong chu akinung mukit tapoi. 
Keima ka lungthima ka kihoulim in, ka seijin, “Ven, kamasanga Jerusalem'a leng changho vaihomlai sangin jong ka ching joi. Keima amaho khatpen sangin jong chih john a le hetjohna kaneije ,” kati. 
Hiti chun keima ijakai chihna’a konna ngolna chule kingol sahna geija hetdohna dingin ka kalsong doh in ahi. Hinlah keima tah in kahetdoh chu hiche ho jouse hi panna bei thil tobangbep ahije. 
Ka chihna akibe cheh le ka gimna akibe cheh in, hetna akalbe cheh le lunggim na akibe cheh bepmin ahi.
