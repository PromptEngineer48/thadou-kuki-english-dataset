Ijakai dingin aphat aumcheh in, vannoija hin ijakai achat loh thei dingin phat aum cheh’e. 
Penna ding phat aum'inchule thiphat aum’e. Aphu phat aum'inchule a‘at phat aum’e. 
Atha’phat aum'inchule asuhdam phat aum’e. Aphet lhah phat aum'inchule asahdoh phat aum’e. 
Ka phat aumin chule nui phat aum'e. Lunghem phat aum'inchule lam phat aum'e. 
Song sethang phat aum'inchule song khopkhom phat aum’e. Ki-angkol phat aum'inchule kihei mang phat aum’e. 
Thil holphat aum'inchule thil hol ngah phat aum’e. Akholkhom phat aumin chule athe cheh phat aum’e. 
Abo-eh phat aumnin chule akhui bit phat aum’e. Thipbeh’a umphat aumin chule thusei phat aum'e. 
Kingailut phat aumin chule kivetda phat aum'e. Kinah phat aum'inchule kicham phat aum’e. 
Ana tohhat man uva miho hin ipi akimu mong mong uvem? 
Pathen’in i-bonchau chunga pohgih eikoipeh cheh’u kamu doh tai. 
Ahijeng vang'in, Pathen’in ijakai amachang cheh dingin hoisel in asemin ahi. Aman mihemte lungsunga tonsot akhum lut peh in, hinlah chuti ahijeng vang'in, mihem in Pathen tohgon apatna pat achaina geijin amu phapoi. 
Hijeh a hi keiman, mihem chu akipa’a chule ijochan geijuva thanopna inei dingu sanga phajo dang aumpoi, tia ka gel lhah ahitai. 
Chule miho hin aneh’uva chule adon’uva chule atohgim gahou, athanoppi diu hi Pathen’in manbeiya athil peh-u chu ahi. 
Chule ipi hijongleh Pathen'in athilbol chan chu achaina ahi jenge. Imacha gah koibe ding ahilouleh gah lahdoh ding aumpoi. Pathen'in alung gon chu mihem ten Ama agin ding’u hi ahi. 
Tua hung sohdoh ho hi tumasanga ana sohdohsa ahitai, chule khonunga hung soh ding ho chu tuma’a ana sohdoh sa ahitai, ajeh chu Pathen'in hiho chu avel vella ahin um dohsah kitji ahije. 
Nisa noija keiman ka mudoh kit chu, thutanna dan aphalou um ka hedoh’e. Tahbeh in , Dan Thutanna mun chu thudih aumpoi. 
Keima tah in ka lungthima, “Aphatcha Pathen'in apha hileh ase hileh amichang cheh chu anatoh hou jouse jal'a achungthu’u atan ding ahi,” kati. 
Keiman mihemte dinmun thua kagel lin-Pathen’in mihemte henga amaho jong gancha ho leh gamsaho tobangbep ahiuve, ti aphochen in ahi. 
Ajeh chu gamsaho le mihemte chan kibang ahinau aum'e-haitheina anei gel uvin chule thi thei gel ahiuve. Hitia hi mihemin gamsa ho sanga phatchom john a tahbeh anei deh pouve. Ajeh bei iti hi’a hitam! 
Ania munkhatna chekhoma ahi-vutna konna hung kondoh’uva ahin, aniuva vut kisohkit diu ahi. 
Ajeh chu koiham mihem lhagao akaltouvin chule gamsaho lhagao leisetna akumsuh’e ti, koi jin aphotchet thei jem? 
Hitia hi keiman mihem dinga anatoh chunga akipa diu sanga phajo kamupoi. Eiho hi koiman ithi jou tengu leh nopsahna dinga thina’a konna eihin lepui ding’u ahi tapoi.
