Thuhilpa.
6.
Keiman nipi noija thil chombeh aphamo tah khat kamudoh aum in, hichu mihemte lah a aum jingin ahi. 
Pathen’in mi phabep chu haona bulhingset le jabolna chule angai chatbe chan diu geijin apen, hinlah hiche ho chu nopsah pina neithei manlouva koi-mi aumin ahi. Amaho chu athiuvin, chule midang hetphah loubeh mi khatnin anei le gou, thil lamdoh chu anopsahpi lheh jin ahi! Hiche hi hamsetna nat pithei leu panna bei ahi bouve. 
Mi khat in cha jakhat jen nei theijinte chule atehset geijin hingkhat nante. Hinlah ama chun hinkhoa lungkimna neihih henlang, chule kivuina pha neita hihleh, ama dinga chu ahungpena athisa ana-hitaleh phajo ding ahi. 
Mihem a apen chu ajehbei tobang hi ding, chule muthim lhang khal kiheh lah a ahinkho kichai tobangbep hi ding ahije. Ama chun min kiti jong nei louva, chule nisa jong mu talouva chule nisa-umlam jong hepha lou hi ding ahitai. Hijongleh ama chun kipah loutah’a ahung seilet sanga lung olna anei joh ding ahi. 
Ama chu kum sangkhat val, nivei geijin hing ding hijongleh lungkimna aneitah louding ahi. Chule ama chun midang khat tobanga thina ato ding, hichun ipi aphat chomsah ding ham? 
Mijousen ahinkhou apum pia anholna’n kimai jun tin, hinlah anei lhingset diu ginchat aumpoi. 
Hitia hi miching ho hi mingolho sanga phachom jouva hilou ham? Mivaicha hon achih jeh’uva ipi phatchomna akimuva chule midang masanga iti chon ding akihet’uvem? 
Na nei lou na ngaichat sang'in, nanei chunga lunglhaijon. Thilpha thil hoi nei nomna lunggel kiti hi aphat chomna bei kidel tobang ahi. 
Thil ijakai ho hi kigong tupsa ngen ahiuve. Mi khat cheh hi ipi ahi ding’u tona kigong sapeh ahi tauve. Hijeh chun natonsot khankho ding chung chang thua jong Pathen nanel kallou ding ahi. 
Thu naha seitam chan-a ima phachomlou ahi. Ahileh hiche ho chu ipi phachom em? 
I-hinlonau panna bei nikho phabep sunga hi, i-hin nikhou hi iti kimang hoi thei pen ding ham ti koiyin ahet nem? I-hinkhou hi lelim tobangbep ahi. I-che nung tengu leh hiche leisetna hi ipi ahunglhung ding koiyin aseithei jem?
