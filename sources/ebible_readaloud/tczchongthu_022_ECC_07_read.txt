Thuhilpa.
7.
Minphatna kimu hi gimnamtuipen sang'in jong amanlu joi. Chule napen nikho sang'in jong nathi nikho phajoa ahi. 
Thina kin’a kimanchah hi golnop bolna mun’a gache sangin aphajoi. Ahimongin, mitin kithi ding ahijeh in, mihingin hiche hi alunga agel ding doltah ahi. 
Lungkham hi muilhah sangin aphajoi, ajeh chu lungkhamna hin i-chunga kisuhthengna ahin sodohji ahi. 
Miching khat chun thina chung chang hi aha gel’in ahi, hinlah mingol vang chun phatpha mu ding bou ageljin ahi. 
Mi ngol khat in apachat hisang'in, miching khat oimo chan apha joi. 
Mi ngol nuijatna chu lingphung meiyin akah gin kija tobang ahin, imacha kah louvin aki chai jitai. 
Miching chu aki-engbol teng angol lojin, chule guhthim thilpeh hin lungthim asuhmo bepji ahi. 
Achaina hi apatna sang'in apha jon chule kiletsah sang'in, thohhat aphachom joi. 
Lunghan kitim them in, ajeh chu lunghan hin angoltoh kibang bep naso ding ahi. 
“Achesa nikho phaho chu ngaicha gunset hih in, hichu mi chinga ahi poi.” 
Sum nanei tengleh chihna hi phachom cheh ahi. Hinkhoa kho-sahna dinga ania phachom tah ahi. 
Chihna le sum hin ipi hijongleh nanei sah jeng thei ahinai. Hinlah chihna kiti bouhin na hinkho ahuhhing jou ding ahije. 
Pathen thilbol ho chu janom jengin, ajeh chu aman ahe konsa chu koiyin asuh-jang jou ding ham? 
Ning lhinga naum laisen kipah’in, hinlah hahsat nan nahin phah teng jongleh, ania Pathenna hungkon ahiti tahsan in. Hiche lei hinkhoa hin ima hiti mong ding ahiti, akisei theipoi kiti hi geldoh jingin. 
Keiman hinlona ajeh umlou tobangbep leiset hinkhoa hin, khangdong phatah tah ho athia chule migilou ho hinkho asot’uhi kamu doh in ahi. 
Hijeh chun mohphat behset hih in, ahilouva ahileh chingval behset hih in! Idia nangmatah kisumang ding nahi ham? 
Chutima chun gilou behset jong bol hih in. Ngol hihbeh in! Ipi dinga naphat lhin masanga thi ding nahim? 
Hiche kihil naho hi lunggut in gel in, ajeh chu koi hitaleh Pathen ging mi chun; hitobang che valna hi ania hintoh ponte. 
Khopi sunga lamkai mi som sangin, miching khat chu gamchengin ahat joi. 
Leiset chunga hin mihem khatcha aphajeng bol chonse kha louhel aumpoi. 
Mi thusei jouse het ding go hih in, nasoh jengin jong na gaosap get in nate. 
Ajeh chu nangma tah jengin jong midang na gaosap ji chu na henai. 
Keiman kajo channa chihna in kalunggel le kachonna eilamkai jing na dingin ka ngai ton ahi. Keima tah in kaseijin, “Keima chingthei dingin ka kigel lha tai.” Hinlah ka chuti theijou hihbeh in ahi. 
Chihna hi gamla tah’a aum jingin chule holdoh jong ahah sai. 
Keiman muntin'a chun, chihna le hetkhenna chu iti hung umhija ham tin ajeh kahol in ahi. Keima tah in kholchilna neija kon'in gitlouna hi ngol jeh ahin, chule ngol kiti hi lung chavai kiseina ahije, tiphot chenna kanei ahitai. 
Keiman kavet chil’a ahileh, duha-thuhtah numei chu thi theina thangkol sanga khohse joa ahi chu ka kholdoh ahitai. Ami hebol jong kipalna thangkol bep ahi, chule akhut nemtah jong chu kihenna thihkol ahi bouve. Pathen lunglhaina’a umho chu amanua kon’a hoidoh ding, hinlah chonse ho chu amanu thanga ga-oh ding ahiuve. 
Thuhilpa chun, “Hiche hi keiman hoima lamjouse ahitheina ding kavet toh lea konna achaina kasem chu hiche hi ahi. 
Keiman kavel hol hol jeng vang'in, ka hol lona pentah chu kamu joupoi. Mi sangkhat lah a khatbou gunchena nei pasal khat ahijouvin, hinlah numei khat jong akimu joupoi. 
Chule keiman hiche hi kamui: Pathen’in miho hi gun chena neija asem ahin, hinlah ama hon achang cheh-uva aphalou akilam doh-u ahi bouve.
