Thuhilpa.
9.
Hiche jonghi keiman chingthei tah in ka khol in: Pathen ngaisah ho leh miching ho jong Pathen khutna uma ahiuvin, amaho hi Pathen’in ahepi ding hinauvem ti koima ahetoh’a aumpoi. 
Mi tinin atonsot mun diu ngah jinga uma ahiuve, michonpha hihen, migilou hijongleh, mipha hihen, miphalou hijongleh, athenga kigel ho hiuhen, athenglou hijongleu, hou ngaisah hihen, hou ngaisahlou hijongleh, mipha hon jong michonse ho bangma’a Pathen’a kitepna neiho jong kitepna neilouho toh kithakhatma ahije. 
Amitakip nipi noija umho jouse chan kibanga athiltou hi lungdon umtah ahi. Hitobang ho hin thilpha bolbe nadinga ching thei lou’uva ahi. Chusang'in ama hon angol nau joh chu aki deilhen uve, ajeh chu amaho kinepna neilou ahiuve. Amasang uva imacha dang umlou thina bou chu uma ahi. 
Mihing dingin kinepna bou aum’e. Ama hon asei uvin, “Uicha hing chu keipi thisa sangin aphachom joi.” 
Mihngho chun athi diu beh akihet’uvin, hinlah athisa hon imacha ahe tapouve. 
ama hon ahinkhouva ipi hijongleh abholchanu chu ki-ngailut, ki-thet, ki-mitthip abon'a achemangsa ahitai. Ama hon hiche leiset chunga hin bolbe ding anei tapouve. 
Hijeh chun na-an chu kipah thanomtah in nen, chule na-lengpitwi chu lung kipah pum in don in, ajeh chu Pathen’in hichu aphatsah ahi. 
Von hoipen chun kivon in, chule lou namtui chu kinu-in. 
Numei nangailutnu chuto nipi noija Pathen’in napeh nahinkho umpet chun kipahtah in hing khom in. Najia nanei chuto phaten hingkhom lhon'in. 
Nabol chan chu hoitah in bol tan. Ajeh chu nangma lhankhuh’a na chetengleh natoh ding um talou ding, tohgon jong um talou ding, hetna jeng jong umta pontin, chihna geija bei ding ahitai. 
Keiman nipi noija adang chom khat jong ka mudoh’e. Lhaija hat penpan jong kipaman amu jing ahipoi, chule galsatna hatpenpa jeng jong chu galjona chang jing ahipoi. Miching jong chu kelthoh’a avale jipet aum in, chule khut them hon jong lungkimna amudeh pouve. Chule lekhathem tahho jong chu lolhinna hinkho mang jinga ahi pouve. Hiche ho jouse hi vang'in apohji ahin, hitia chu aphatcha amun'a gatoh kha kiti ahi. 
Itobang phat hahsa hunglhung ding ahi koima asei theija aumpoi. Ngalen sung’a um vacha athangkol sunga um tobangin miho chu phulou helouvin hamset nan apha jiuve. 
Hichea hin chihna themkhat keima vannoi khosah dan hin eilung lut sah aum'e. 
Khopi neocha khat asunga cheng mijong themkhat bou ahiuve, chule leng lentah khat chu asepai hotoh ahungun, khopi chu aum kimvel tauve. 
Mivaicha, michingtah khat chun hiche khopi chu iti huhdoh ding ahi ahen, chule hiti chun huhdoh in aumtai. Hinlah hiche jou chun koima chan akom'a kipa thu aseipouve. 
Hiti chun, chihna hi thahat sanga aphatjoh vang'in, miching chu avaichatleh mi nahsahmo achang e. Athuseidoh ho chu kipapi sotna koiman anei poi. 
Leng ngolpa kisen aw jahsang'in michingpan olcha thu asei jah nom aum joi. 
Galman chah hoa kivon kichei sang'in chih thujah apha joi. Hinlah michonse khat chun apha tampi asuhmang thei joh ahi.
