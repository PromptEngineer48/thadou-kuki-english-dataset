Thuhilpa.
11.
Nangman na muchi chu twipi gallam geijin thotdoh in, chule aphatcha a-pungdoh chu nahenga hung le lhun kit ding ahi. 
Hinlah nangman na sumkhol ho chu mun tampia homjal in, ajeh chu ipi hahsatna in ahinphah ding nangin nahepoi. 
Meibol kitol atam tengleh, go ahung julha jin ahi. Thingphung chu sahlam hihen lhanglam hijongleh alhuhna’a chun aum den jeng’e. 
Loubol mi chun gophat pet anga leh chang phutdoh ponte. Meibol kitol avetsui jing uleh , itih in jong chang at pouvinte. 
Nangman huinun na chu alampi na hetdoh theilou tobangin, ahilouleh naocha anu oisunga kijil chu iti hungkhan let ham ti na het thei lou tobangin, nangman hiche ho jouse hinbol’a Pathen chaloh dan nahet thei louding ahi. 
Jingkah tengleh na muchi kitun lang, ni lhangkai jongleh gunchun ponpan in, ijeh inem itileh hoipenna chu apung ding pen chu hung sohdoh ding ham ti nangman nahe poi. 
Vah hi anom’e! Khovah thah hunglhun vet hi iti lunglhai um hitam? 
Mi chu ateh geija ahinna ahileh, anitin hinkhoa kipah hen. Chujongleh hinkhoa gimna nikho jong tampi hunglhung ding ahiti jong geldoh uhen. Thil ahunglhungbe ding ho hi ajeh bei tobangbep hung hi theija ahi. 
Khangdong ho, khandonlai hi iti kidan hitam! Kipana tothon nalunggil in kihilna nei jingin, nabolnom chan bol lin; nalunggot nalam jousea chelen, chule namit tenin avetthei chan vehen. Ahinlah nathilbol chan chu Pathen henga namopohna hi ding ahi, ti geldoh in. 
Hijeh chun lungkhamna kiti bol hih in, chule natahsa damtheina din kichin tup in; chule hiche hi geldoh jingin, khandonlai na masanga na hinkho umsun hi ajehbei tobangbep ahi bouve.
