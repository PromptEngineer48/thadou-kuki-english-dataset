Thuhilpa.
12.
Nakhangdonlai thanopna chun na Sempa suhmilna sodoh hih hen. Natehset masang nakhangdon laihin, Ama chu jabollin chule “Hinkho hi anomtapoi,” tin seijin. 
Nateh’a namit tenin nisa lha le ahsiho vah avetna athim masang'in, chule meibol’a konna go hung ju lha ding van chu athim masangin Ama chu geldoh in. 
Na in ngah a pang nakengho chu ahung kithin masang'in, na mihat ho alhahsuh uva chule na lengkou teni ahung bon masang'in, na Sempa geldoh in. Nasoh um amoh chengse le naha hon ima alhai theilou masang'in; chule namit tenin amutheina bangkot, numei hon avetna chu ahung thim masang'in, Ama chu geldoh in. 
Hinkhoa phat kijen naneipat ahung kichai masang'in chule natohgim phat ahung kichai masang'in, Ama chu geldoh in. Husat ginho ahung thipma chule vacha hamgin najah masat tengleh nangma nahung kithodoh ding ahi. 
Nangma lhuh ding nahung kichat tijatna, chule lamlen jotna’a jong setto ding nalung khama, vaopah banga nasam ho ahung kana, chule khaokhopi tobanga nakhupboh’a lam nahin jot masang'in, chule jitoh kingaina imacha nei talouva nahung um masang'in, Ama chu geldoh in. Nangman na tonsot mun na jot masanga, lhankhuh nanai tul tul’a, na kivuina mun'a lunghem ho aka phat’u hung lhun masang'in, Ama chu geldoh in. 
Nangma nakhandon pat hin dangka khaogui akibotan masang, sana khon aki vohkeh masang, twinah phunga twibel aki tachip masang, twibel phunga kangtalai aki vohbal masang'in, na Sempa geldoh in. 
Ijeh inem itileh hiche phat chu ahung lhunteng, vut leiset kisoh kit intin; chule lhagao chu apepa Pathen henga kile kit ding ahi. 
Thuhilpa chun, “Ijakai ajehbei tobangbep ahi, ajeh bei ahisohkeije,” ati. 
Hiche hi nalung in ching jingin: Thuhilpa chu miching ahin, chule ama chun ahetchan chu miho ahilji ahi. Ama chun thuchih tampi chingthetah’a angaitoa, agel-a chule ahop khen tohle ahi. 
Thuhilpa chun, thudihtah chu mudoh dinga ahol-a thutah chu kichehtah’a aphondoh nom ahi. 
Miching thusei ho chutengcha hemtah tobanga ahin, najongleh phachoma ahi. Athuseiju kilakhom ho hi thingkil kikil matchet tobang leuva kelngoi chingpan akelngoi ho huikhomna’a amanchah ji tobang ahi. 
Hinlah kacha, Keiman thuhilna naneibe kit inge: Chingtheijin, ajeh chu lekhabu ho jihdoh kitia kichai tih aumpoi, chule lekha hasim hin na cholsah ahije. 
Hiche hi aboncha thu kaigom ahi. Tun keiman thukhumkhana kanei hi ngai phan: Pathen ginna neijin chule athupeh ho jui jin, ajeh chu hiche hi amitakip toh ding kin ahi. 
Ijakai chung changa, guhthim thudol- apha hihen ase hijongleh, Pathen'in eiho thilbol dungjui cheh’a thutanna anei ding ahi.
