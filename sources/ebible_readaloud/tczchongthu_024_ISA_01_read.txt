Isaiah Themgao Lekhabu.
1.
Amoz chapa Isaiah-in Juda lengho Uziah, Jotham, Ahaz leh Hezekiah phat laiya Judah le Jerusalem chung chang gaova thil amuho: 
Vanho ngaiyun! leisetnin phaten ngaiyun! Pakaijin asei chu hiche hi ahi: “Kahin vahkhang leh kahin seiletsah chaten kachungah nachonseuvin neidou tauve. 
Bongchal jengin jong apupa akihetnin, chule Sanganin jong apu’n apehna kong akihetnin, ahinlah ka miten vang amaho kahin vahkhang dan ahephapouvin, Israelin apupa akihettapoi. 
Ohe themmona dimsette chonse nam mite, gitlouna thilbol chilhahho; themmona dimset mite, hiche mite hin a-Pakaiyu adalhatauvin, Israel Mithengpa chu donselouvin akoiyun ajamsan tauve. 
Ipi dinga gimbolna ding nakikou bebeu hitam? Itih chan gei neidou nahlai diu ham? naluchang apumin anatan, nalungchang jong ana’n ahi. 
Nakengtoa pat nalujang chan adam lai mong mong aumtapon, akita-dupna jeng, apat lai jeng chule athi lonna maha jeng ahi’n, anaikolin, tom jong akitompon, thao jong akinupoi. 
Nagamsungu jong ahomkeovin alumtan, nakhopiu jong akavamtai. Namitmu tah'un gamdang miten nagam’u alosohtan, thil jouse amitmuva amu jouseu asusetauve. 
Jerusalem khopi hoitah chu lengpi leiya buh kisong tobang leh changmai leiya buh kisong bangin, akidalhan avengbit ding umlou khopi abangtai. 
Pakai van-khopi sepaihon eiho themkhat jeng jong eivenbitlouvu hileh Sodom le Gomorrah tobanga thehngima ium diu ahi. 
Sodom khoa vaihom lamkaihon Pakai thusei ngaiyun lang, Gomorrah miten Pathen dan thupeh ngaiyun. 
Pakaijin aseiye: “Nakilhainau jouseu hi ka-ipi phatchomna um dinga nagelluham? Keiman kelngoichal pumgo thilto le gancha kivah thaotahtah jong kaneiye. Keiman bongchal tampi thisan leh kelngoi thisan chule kelcha thisan hijongleh kakipapipoi. 
Keima eihou dinga nahung tengu leh koiham nakithoinau puma ka-inpi chonthang dinga nakouvu? 
Panna bei thiltoho chu hinpolut tahih-un, nagimnamtuiyu jong keima dinga thet-umtah ahi! Lhathah nikho, Sabbath nikho chuleh achombeha na-anngol nikho manhou chonset bulom leh adihlou ahi. Nakikhopkhomnau jong kadeitapoi. 
Nalhathah kut bol hou, kumlhun golnop nabol hou jong keidinga pohgih bep ahin, abonchan kapochimtan, kakidah ahitai! 
Vanlam sanga nakhut-u natundohuva natao tengu jongleh kahungvetnuva nataonau kangailhahpehlou ding ahi. Ajeh chu khohelouho thisan nakhutnuvah adimsetne. 
Nangho kisilngimu uvin lang chule kisuthengun! Nachonset nau chu kamitmun paimangun, na chonset nau lampi chu dalhauvin, 
Thilpha bol dan kithemchuh-un, thudih chu holun. Bolgentheina thoh ho chu panpiuvin, naochagaho chu thu adih in tanpeh-uvin lang, vengphauvin. Meithaiho chu thildih jeng seipeh-un.” 
Pakaijin hiti hin aseiye, “Hungun kihoutoh-u hite. Nachonset nau chu thisan bangin san jongleh buhbang banga kabansah ding, sandup jeju jongleh kelngoi mul jolsel tobanga kajolsah ding ahi. 
Ka thupeh nanit-uleh nang hon nehlechah ninglhingset’na nanei diu ahi. 
Ahinlah kathupeh nanitlou-uva nakiheimangsanule namelmateu chemjama lousoh helding nahiuve, tia na Pakaiyu keiman ka seipeh nahi tauve!” 
I-atileh Jerusalem khopi kitahtah chu notia pang jenga hitam? Thu adih-a kitanna khopia chun tuhin tolthana jengin aumtai. 
Nadangka thih chu twieh asohgamtan, na lengpitwi chihtah chu twi toh akichohhal gamtai. 
Namilen milalteu jong doumahmah bolin apangun, gucha michomho jol le golin apang tauve. Nehguh chahguh adelleleuvin, nulou palouho thu le meithaiho thu jong adih in atanpeh nom tapouve. 
Hijeh chun Pakai, vantil galsat te Pakai Hatchungnung, Israel Pakai chun aseiye, “Keiman kamelma te chunga phu kalah ding ahi. 
Nachunga kakhut kalha ding, nathih-eh thengsella kathehngima nasum kilhajol chengse kaloikhensohkei ding ahi. 
Chutengleh thutan vaihomthemho masang banga kahinpeh kit ding, Jerusalem khopi chu Thudih-a Kivaihomna Khopi chule Khopi Kitah tia kisei kit ding ahitai,” ati. 
Zion chu thudih-a akitan teng kiledoh kit ding chule akisihho chu chonphatnaa kiledoh kit diu ahitai. 
Michonseho vang suhmanga um ding ahiuve, ijeh-inem itileh amaho Pakai hengah akile nom pouve. 
Namilim hounau leh aguh-a gangpithing noi lah a na chonna u leh milim houna'a nakipehdohnau jeh a jumleja nathoh diu ahi. 
Anagopsa gangpi phung nabah diu, chule twilutlouna honlei toh nabah diu ahi. 
Nalahuva ahatpenpa jong chu changpol tobanga mangthah ding; athilbol dihlouhou chu meisipah mei chohkaiya pang ding, amaho le athil dihlou bol hou chu meikong lah-a kachaiyintin, chuleh meikong chu aphelmit thei ding koima umponte.
