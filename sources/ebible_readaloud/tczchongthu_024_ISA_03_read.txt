Isaiah.
3.
Pakai, van galsat te Pakai chun, Jerusalem le Judah ten akingainau athil jouseu, achanglhah lhon jouseu le atwi jouseu alahpeh ding, 
Apasal hat jouseu le agal-hang jouseu chule athutanhou le athemgaohou, athempuhou le upaho panna-a, 
Galsat lamkaiho le avaihom lamkailenho, thumop-ho chule khutthemho le ahsi lekhathemho jouse kitolmang diu ahi. 
Keiman chapang khangdongho chu alamkaiyuva kapansah ding, chapang chachan achung uva vai ahop diu ahi. 
Chuteng khatnin khat abol genthei ding, mihem khat chun mihem khat abol gentheiya chule amitinin aheng-akom abol genthei ding ahi. Khangdonghon jong upaho ahin noise diu, milham maimai ho’n jong jaumtah miho chu anuisatnuva anoise diu ahi. 
Hiche nikho lam chuleh pasal khat chun asopipa henga, “Nangin sangkhol chol naneijin, kalamkaiyuva napan’a nangma vaihom nanoiya ikilamkai diu ahi,” ati ding ahi. 
Ahinlah amapa chun ahindonbut ding, “Kanompoi! Kei pangthei ponge. Ajeh chu keiman anneh ding le ponsil ding kahaopoi. Hijeh chun mopohna lathei ponge!” ati ding ahi. 
Ijeh-inem itileh Jerusalem hung kipallhuding, Judah jong hung lhuding ahi. Ijeh-inem itileh ama hon Pakai dounan thu aseiyun, Pakai thupeh ajuinom pouve. Chule Pakai maichangtah a taitomna aneikhumun ahi. 
Amaisou chun ahinau aphongdohun, Sodom mite bangin lhangphongtah in achonseuvin ahi. Achonsetnau selmangin aumtapon, Ohe! ada ahilhontai. Amanthahna diu ama hon achamuva akiboldoh-u ahi bouve. 
Midihho vang chu seipeh-un, amaho dinga ijakai phasoh ding ahi. Atohphat gau akisan diu ahi! 
Ohe migilouho vang mangthah diu ahi. Ajeh chu agitlounau ga chu ahibangtah a akimu diu ahi. 
Chapang tobang lamkaiho chun kamite asugentheiyun, numeihon jong avaihopkhumui. O kamite, nalamkaihovin nahin pui lamvai tauvin, lamdihlouva nahin pui tauve. 
Pakaijin thutanna mun alotan, amite thutanpeh dingin atoutai. 
Pakai chu athutanna phongdoh dingin ahung naiyin, mipi vaihom ho le upaho thu atanin, “Nanghon kalengpilei, Israel nachom tauve. Na insung uvah migentheiho thil nachomhou akisedimset tai. 
Ipi nakingapchatnauva kamite nachotphauva, mivaichate maichang chu leivui toh nagoithau ham?” tiin Pakai, van galsat te Pakai chun aseiye. 
Pakaijin hiti’n aseijin, “Vetlomtah Zion hi akihoithosah-in, angong avaiputnin, amitnin mi alhemngolin, kihoithosahtah in akal asepmin, akeng-ngonga chaocha ging lhinglheng pumin lam ajotne. 
Hijeha chu Pakaijin Zion Chanute chu alujangu pat dehduh-a ahin koi ding, alu-u tol gamding ahi. 
Thupi tan nikho chuleh Pakaijin akicheihoinau jouse anoimangpeh diu, alu-uva akop-uleh akhi-ou kisan asutlhahpeh ding, 
Abilbahou, achaochau, amaikhuhou, 
Apon lutom le bantom, kengngonga aki-ou chaochaho, konga kivei diel jouse asutlhahpeh ding chule lunglathei kicheihoinaho le thaonamtuiho; 
Khutjem, khi-o, 
Golvahni vonho, sangkholcholho, sangkhol-bantong le sumbomho, 
Phatvet, tupat ponnem, lujemhoiho chule ponsilho aboncha kilahdohpeh ding ahi. 
Gimnamtui nam talouva aui-athang joh nam diu, diel khella khaojang akigah diu, asam jolsellu jong pullhasoh ding ahi. Chule pon-ah hoitah khella khaodip-pon hamthethu akiah diu, jumnan amel-hoina jouseu alekhu ding ahi. 
Khopia pasal ho chu chemjama kithatgam ding chule agal-hat-hou jong kidouna pholla thiden diu ahitai. 
Zion kelkot phunga chu kana le puldouna um ding, akhopi chu numei kisuse-kisuthanghoi tolla kikhelpha-den tobang hi ding ahi.
