Isaiah.
8.
Avelin Pakai chun kaheng'ah, “Thuhetsah tahlangna lentah khat semin lang achunga chun: ‘Maher-shalal-hash-baz’ tiin sun in,” ati. 
Keiman kathilsut chu naosen vop’ma aum masang ahinai ti photchenna dingin thempu Uriah le Jeberekiah chapa Zechariah, mithutah gella kiheteni chu thuhetoh-a pang dingin kaseiye. 
Chuin keiman kajinu kaumpin, amanu ahung gaiyin, chapa ahin neitai. Hichun Pakaijin aseijin, “Amin Maher-shalal-hash-baz tiin kouvin. 
Aminin aphondoh chu kum phabep jou, hiche naosenin ‘hepa’ chule ‘henu,’ tia akoudohthei masanga Assyria lengpan Damascus le Samaria ahinbulua nei le gouho akichommang ding ti ahi,” ati. 
Hichun Pakaijin kakom'a aseikitnin, 
“Judah mite kagelkhoh dan hi Shiloah-a twi nemheova long dildel tobang ahi, hinla ama hon einungsun tauve. Ama hon Leng Rezin le Leng Pekah chunga thil hunglhung ding bou athanoppiuve. 
Hijeha chu Pakaijin amaho chu Euphrates vadung twilet hattah—Assyria lengpa le agalsat sepaiho alonvuhsah ding ahi. Hiche twi hattah chun twilam jouse ahinjuiya longlet gam diu, 
Chuleh Judah gamsunga ngong chan geiya hung kinonglut ding ahi. Alhaving kijaldoh ding, na gamsung among langkhat’na pat alangkhat geiya achupsoh ding ahi, O Immanuel. 
‘Hungkhomun, nangho nammite, kichat tijatnin dimun. Ngaiyun, gamla tah a umhon jong. Kisatto din kigongun, ahivanga jeplhuh-a um ding nahiuve! Henge, gal sat din kigongun, jeplhuh-a um ding nahiuve! 
Nagalsat lamkaihou kikoukhomun, ahivanga panna bei hidiu ahi. Galla panlah ding dan kigonphatnuvin, ahivanga lolhinglou ding nahiuve. Ijeh-inem iti leh Pathen chu keiho lamah apange,’ tia Assyria ten asap diu ahi,” ati. 
Pakaijin mijousen thil agel banga thil gellou dingin gahtah in eithukhamin, 
“Kot kikhakhuma aguh-agalla thil kigongho chu nakichaina ding ahitai tiin kichahih-in. 
Hatchungnung Pakai tailou imadang gin neihih-in. Ama bou chu Athengpa chu ahi. Ama nagin pouleh imadang nakichat angaipoi. 
Ama’n bitsella nakoi ding ahi. Hinla Israel le Judah dinga miho kipalna songchang hintin, miho kilelhahna songpi hi ding ahi. Chuleh Jerusalemte dinga thang le pella hung pangding ahi. 
Mi tamtah kithoudoh kit talou dinga kipallhua kitanglha ding ahi. Amaho chu thanga ohdenuva mat’na um ding ahiuve,” ati. 
Pathen thuhil ho hi chingphauvin. Athupehho hi keima nunga seijui ho henga jong chinsahsonun. 
Keiman eipanpi diuva PAKAI chu kanga ding ahi, Ama’n Jacob chilhah te eikinungheisan ta jonglehu. Kakinepna pumpi Ama chungah kangap ding ahi. 
Keima le kacha, Pakaijin eipeh hi Zion Molsang chunga Hou-In thenga chengpa Van galsat te Pakai thilgon le thukhamho Israel te henga hetsah din kapange. 
Loikhat in, “Doithemho le athisaho toh kihoutheiho dongutin, phuisap bolsah leu hen eiseipehthei nauvinte,” ati nom diu ahi. Ahi’n Pathen'in eilamhilna diuva itao diu hijolouham? Ahing nalaihon athisaho iti kikipuihoisah ding ham? 
Pathen thupeh ho le thuhil ho veuvin! Kohileh Ama thupeh ho nelkal chu muthimlah-a um imahelouhel ahiuve. 
Hitobang miho chu mun chom choma thachol le gilkelin cheleuvinte. Agilkel jeh un, lunghanguntin alengpau le a Pathen’u jeng jong asapset diu ahi. Vanlam angat diu, 
Chuleh leilam angat kit diu, ahivanga ahoilang avetnujongleh, boina le lungkhamna chule lungjinna jeng muvinte. Amaho chu thimpi jing lah-a kilehlut diu ahi.
