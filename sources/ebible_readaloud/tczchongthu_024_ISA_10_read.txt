Isaiah.
10.
Ohe itobang lunghemnan hiche thudihlouva thutanho le dan dihlouva vaihom ho chu alamto tadem! 
ama hon migentheiho chungah kithutandihna alhunsah pouvin, kamite lah-a alhasamhon angaichat achandiu dol chanvou jong aphalpeh pouve. Meithaiho agem’un, naochagaho lhahsamna chungah phatchomna akiholuve. 
Gamlatah-a kon hahsatna kahin lhunsah-a gotna kahin chansah tengule ipi nabol diu ham? Koi kom nangatnuva panpina nathum diu hitam? 
Galmun’a nathilouvujongleh soh-a dinga mi’n nakaimang diu ahitai. Hiche geiya jong Pakai lungdam dehlou ding, talen matsahbe dia khut alap jing nahlai ding ahi. 
“Ohe! Itobang vangsetnan ahinlamto tadem—Keiman kalung hanna mite jepna tenggol banga kamanji Assyria chu. 
Keiman Assyria hi eilunghansah mite kadelkhumsah-in, anei agou jouseu kachomgamsahin, tolbol lah a amite kachilthangsah-e. 
Ahinlah Assyria Lengpan ama chu kamanchah, kakhetbuh ahilam akihetpon; alungin angaito phapoi. Ama lunglam chu bailamtah a nam khat a pat nam khat suhmang jeng ding ti bou chu ahi. 
Ama’n seiyinte: ‘Kagal lamkai jouse jong hi lenga hungpang loi diu ahi. 
Calno kasumangui, Carchemish kabol bangun. Hamath jong Arpad bangin kamasanguva abohkhup tauve. Chuleh Samaria jong Damascus bangin kasumang tauve. 
Henge, lenggam tamtah, apathen’u Jerusalem le Samariatea sanga loupijoho jeng jong abonuvin kajousoh tauve. 
Chuti ahijeh a chu Jerusalem le apathen’u chu ka suhmang diu ahitai, Samaria le apathen ka suhmangtha bang uva,” tiin. 
Pakaijin atohgon Zion molsang le Jerusalem’a asuh bulhitna dinga Assyria lengpa amanchah banga, Ama’n Assyria lengpa chung ngeiya jong gotna alhunsah ding ahi—ijeh-inem itileh ama chu akiletsah-in ahamhoitho ta’n ahi. 
Ama chun, “Keima banthahat vanga bou hichan hi kaboldoh ahitai. Keima chihna le themna dungjuiya thilgon kabol ahi. Chitin namtin galkul kaphutvangin anei-agouvu kachomngim tai. Alengteu jong bongchal kikailhu bangin kasulhugamtai. 
Loubolpa’n ahtwi alokhom bangin agoubom’u le alenggam jouseu kalohkhom sohhel tai. Koiman keima douna-a alha ajap ngam tahlou ding chuleh keima douna kamcheng khat jeng jong aphondoh ngamlou ding ahitai,” tiin aseiye. 
Ahileh, heicha’n athingtanpa sanga thupijoa kiseithei mong ding hinam? Thing-at chu athing-atpa sanga thupijoam? Thing tenggolin achanga lam ajotthei ding hinam? 
Hijeh a chu, Pakai, Van galsat te Pakai chun Assyria te galsat hamhoithoho lah a chu hise alhunsah ding, akiloupisahnau chu meikongin ahallhum ding ahi. 
Pakai, Israel te salvah chu meikongin pangintin, Athengpa chu ameikou hi ding ahi. Ama’n ling le khao jouse le melmate chu jan khat kah-a meikonga ahallhum ding ahi. 
Pakaijin Assyria loupina chu theiga phatna gam'a gammang meikah banga meiya aleochaisoh ding; hichu nat-hise la’n jeh a hiveiho amahthahgam banga manthaina um ding ahi. 
Hiche gammang thupitah sunga thingphung lhomcha sohcha ding—alhom dan chu chapang jengin jong ajat asimthei khambep hi ding ahi. 
Chuniteng chuleh Israel gam'a akidalha ahingdoh chengse chun Van galsat te Pakai, Israel te Athengpa chu kitahtah-a atahsan diu ahitai. 
Hiche akidalha ahingdohho, Jacob chilhah te chu Pathen Hatchungnung hengah kiletauvinte. 
Israel te chu twipi panga neldi jat phajonglehu, alahuva ahingdohho bou chu Pakai kom'a kile ding ahiuve. Pakai chun amite chu asuhmang dinga thulhuhna dihtah a abol ahitai. 
Henge, Pakai, Van gatsatte Pakai chun agamsung pumpi chu suhmang dinga anagotsa ahitai. 
Hijeh chun Pakai, Van galsat te Pakai chun hitin aseiye: “O Zion’a um kamite, Masanglaiya Egypt gam'a suhgentheiya naum lai bang uva Assyria ten mollhon le hem’a nahin sat tengule kichahih un. 
Chomkhat joule nachung uva kalung hanna hung beiyintin, chuteng kalung hanna chu namelmateu suhchimitna dingin kileheitante. 
Keiman Oreb songpi chunga Midian mite kakhaoguiya kajep’a Gideon galjona kapeh-a, chuleh kamoljol akidopsanga Egypt galsat te twipin alhohmanggam banga Assyria te jong ka suhmanggam ding ahi. 
Chuniteng chuleh Pakaijin amite sohchanna phat akichaisah ding, alengkou-uva kon’a soh namkol asutlhahpeh ding ahi. 
Veuvin, tuhin Assyria te Aiath ah aumtauve. Migron ajotpauvin amanchah thilkeohou Micmash mun'ah akoikhomuve. 
Agallangkhat ahung galkaiyun, Geba mun'ah ajan-geh tauve. Ramah khopi kichatnan akithingtai. Saul khopi, Gibeah mite jong ajam gam tauve. 
Tijatnan kholhang akisam’e: Vo Gallim mite! Laishah mite ngaiyun! Khoto naum ngeiyui, O Anathoth mite! 
Madmenah mite jong ajamcheh gam tauve. Gebim khomiten kiselna ding mun aholtauve. 
Tu’n jong Nob khomun'ah nilhumin melmate akicholdovin, Jerusalem lhanga Zion Molsang hoitah chu agaldotnun akhuttum ajelkhumui. 
Hinla veuvin! Pakai, Van galsat te Pakaijin Assyria te thingphung lentahpa chu thahat tah a aphuhlhuh ding ahi! Akiloupisahna chu asatlhuh ding ahi. Hiche thingphung sangtahpa chu kiloilhu ding ahi. 
Gammang thingphungho banga heicha-a kitulhugam diu ahi. Lebanon chu Hatchungnungpa masanga lhuding ahi.
