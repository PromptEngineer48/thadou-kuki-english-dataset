Isaiah.
13.
Amoz chapa Isaiah in Babylon kisuh mang nading chunga thuhil khat anamun: 
“Kihetsahna ponlap thinglhang vum'a khaisangin Babylon kimai topi din galsat ding ho kou khomin thahat tah leh thaneitah leng inpi ajottengu leh amaho tilkhounan nakhut lhangin. 
Pakai, keiman, hiche natoh tonglha dinga galsat ho kapehdoh ahi. Ahin, ka lunghanna phondoh nadinga gal hang ho kakouva, choisanga kaum teng amaho jong kipah thanom diu ahi.” 
Mol le lhanga husa khu ngaijun! Sepai tamtah kijot husa khu ngaijun! Namtin husa len pen ahi. Van Janel Pakaijin hiche galsat sepai ho hi akou khom ahi. 
Amaho hi gam latah a konna hunguva, kolning khel lama konna jong hung ahiuve. Pakai lungsatna podoh dinga amaho hi Pakai manchah ahiuve. Pakaijin amahotoh gam abon'a asuhmang ding ahi. 
Kichat tijat pumin a eo vun ajeh chu Pakai nikho chu ahunglhungtai- Hatchungnung in asuhmang ding phat chu. 
Ban jouse ki chat pumin chatthei louvin aumin, lung jouse ajunlha gamin, 
Chuleh mipi suh tijat in a ume. Lung genthei nan amanchah kheh un, numei naosonat thoh abangu ve. Kipanpi leh kihuto thei lou vin khat le khat akive to un, tijatnan amai u atom jenguve. 
Ajeh chu ven, Pakai nikho chu ahung lhunge a lungsat a lunghan tijat umtah nikho. Agam jong mi chen khahlou na gam asoding, chuleh michonse ho jong suhmanga um tha dingu ahi. 
Vanlaijol ho jong hung vom lha intin, ahsi hon jong vah ahinpeh loudiu ahi. Nisa aso teng thimpi jingding, chuleh lhathah in vah apeh tah louding ahi. 
Pakai, keiman, vannoi phatlou jeh leh migiloute chonset jeh a gotna kapeh ding ahi. Mi kiletsah ho ahoithonao kasuhchip ding, a thahat nao kiletsah piho kasuh nem ding ahi. 
Keiman mihemte hi sana sanga manlujo a kasemding. Ophir sana theng sanga lujodiu ahi. 
Ajeh chu keiman vanho kaloling ding ahi. Pakai lungsatna Pakai van sepai hon ahin vetsah tengu leh leiset jong a umna a konna kichondoh ding ahi. 
Gamleng ho a konna sakhineo ajammang banga Babylon a cheng mipi jong jam mang diu, kelngoi aching umlou bang diu ahi. Ami u’ akihol diu chule agam languva jam diu ahi. 
Koi hileh akiman tapou chu satan’a um ding-chemjam hemset na lhailut bang ding ahi. 
Amitmu tah uva achateu cheng talih a umdiu ahi. A innu kibulu ding,chuleh ajiteu suhsetna umdiu ahi. 
“Ven, keiman Medes te kachoudoh a Babylon te kaki maito sahding ahi. Dangka in jong lhem lha jou pouvin tin, sana a jong a jollhah joulou diu ahi. 
Galsat sepai hon khangthah ho thalpi a akaplhuh diu ahi. Panpibei naosen ho chunga hepina anei lou diu, chuleh chapang ho chunga jong khotona anei lou diu ahi.” 
Babylon lenggam adia loupipen, Chaldean ten Pahcha banga akithang-at piu hi Pathen'in Sodom leh Gomorrah asuhmang banga asuhgam ding ahi. 
Babylon hi koiman a lo kittah louding ahi. Akhang akhanga akeoseh a um ding ahitai. Umdetna nei louva chao lele jat hon ngahmun dinga jong adeilou diu ahi, chuleh kelngoi chinghon akelngoi u agepi nomlou diu ahi. 
Neldi gam’a gancha ho jong mangthai khopi sunga kichonlut un tin, chuleh Si al ham banga ham gancha hon insung jouse alodim diu ahi. Sumbu changlit hon umnan neijun tin, chuleh gamkelcha ho hilai munna chu galha diu ahi. 
Akulpi sunga ngeiho peng le le diu, chuleh leng inpi nomtah sunga si al hon ko akisem diu ahi. Babylon kem suh nading jat nikho akisim pan tai: aman thah nading phat hunglhung ding ahitai.
