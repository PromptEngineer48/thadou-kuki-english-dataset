Isaiah.
16.
Gamsung vaipo kai pehdin kelngoinou ho chu hin sol’un sela a kon'in.Nelgam akon zion molsang gei adin hin solun. 
Moab numei ho chu Arnon vadung palgal kaina adong lai achun vacha buh neilou bangin dalhah in aumun ahi. 
“Nei panpiu vin,” tin apengun ahi.” Neihuhdoh un kagal mi teu a kon'in. Lungsetna beija gal eisat hoa kon'in nei vengbit un.Kapel doh tah jeh un tun nei lhem tahih un. 
Ka mite galjam ho chu nalah uva umuhen kichat tijat beikah in galmi ho a kon'in anasel tup un.” Kibolgimna leh manthahna ahung kichai teng, galmi mibulu ho a manthah tengu leh, 
Pathen'in David khanggui a konna leng khat ahin tun doh ding ahi. Ama chun hepina leh thutah a vai ahom ding ahi. Adih chu ipiham aman abol jing ding, chuleh thilpha bolding alungvil ten ding ahi. 
Moab kiletsah dan chu kana jaove- akiletsah nao, ahoithonao leh angolnao. Ahin akiskonnao jouse amang gam tan ahi. 
Moab gamsung mijouse akap un ahi. Ahin, Moab a cheng mijousen a puldou uve, Kir-hareseth akon lengpithei agop tan ahi.Tuhin a umgam tapouve. 
Heshbon a louho dalhah in a umtan; Sibmah a lengpilei ho jong dalhah in a umtai chitin namtin chunga vaihom hon Moab asukeh tauve-hoitah tah lengpithei gui ho chu.Akhaogui sah langa patnin Jazer khosung gei jin akiphan, solam gamthip gamgei jin a kikhai lha in ahi. Ajihdon lhumlam gei aphan, twikhanglen gal a ajam gal kai tao ve. 
Hijeh chun tun keiman Jazer leh Sibmah lengpilei kaka pitai; ka mitlhi twi Heshbon leh Eleahleh a din along'e.Nipi laiya honga louga khoptup jeh a kipana lhangsap jong aum tapoi. 
Kipana chu tun a umtapoi, neh khoptup jeh a kipana aum tapoi. Lengpilei sunga lasah jong umtapon tin, kipana lhangsap jong um taponte, lengpitwi’a akipanao jouse kasuh hai peh ahitai. 
Selangdah tuma puldou va akimao banga Moab adinga ka lungsung kap ahi.Kir-hareseth adin lunggenthei in kadimin ahi. 
Moab miten a Hou-In uva milim hin houvun tin, ahin imacha phachom louding ahi. A pathen kom'a kap un tin, ahin koima chan amaho ahuhdoh louding ahi. 
Hiche thudol ho Pakaijin phat masa peh a anasei doh ahitai. 
Ahin tun Pakaijin aseije, “Kumthum sunga nisim a simin, Moab loupina chu bei ding ahi. Michujat a konna mi lhomcha bou ahinga kidalha diu ahi.
