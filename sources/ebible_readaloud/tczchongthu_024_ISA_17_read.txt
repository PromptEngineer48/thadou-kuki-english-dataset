Isaiah.
17.
Damascus chung chang thu’a hiti hin thuhil ahunge: ven, Damascus khopi hi hungmang thah ding ahi! Mangthai thil kise lom hung hi ding ahi. 
Aroer khopiho dalhah in hung um mintin, kelngoi hon lhat lena a aneidiu chuleh asuboi diu umlouva kol diu ahi Anodoh ding mijong umlouding ahi. 
Israel kulpi sunga um khopi ho jong suhmangin hung um intin, Damascus leng thaneina jong kisubei ding ahitai. Syria'a amoh chengsen Israel chate loupina kinung lahpeh dinmun hung chang diu ahi,” tin van janel Pakai chun aphongdoh tai. 
Hiche nikho teng chuleh Israel loupina hung thim cheh cheh intin; a tahsa hoi na jong moh man jeng ding ahi. 
Chang le min aki khoptup joutengleh agamsung chu chang le mim lou hung bang ding ahi. Mi chen louna gamthip hintin, Rephaim phaichama chang le mim ki khoptup jouva lou ho hung bang ding ahi. 
Amite hon lhomcha bou dalhah a umdiu, theichang tamtah kilo nunga lamvai tah a lhang themkhat kikhen bang diu ahi.Ni ham thum ham abah sangpenna um muntin, li le nga chu abah noilanga ana kithethang inte,” tin Pakai, Israel Pathen'in aseije. 
Akhon nileh min asempa ahin vetdiu, chuleh amit nun Israel Mitheng Pa lang ahin ngat diu ahi. 
Milimsemthu hoa konna panpina avet tah loudiu, akhut tah uva asemu milim ho jong ahoutah loudiu ahi. Asherah chunga jong bohkhup talouhel diu, milim houna ding asah u Hou-In sunga jong ahou tah louhel diu ahi. 
Akho piu lentah ho gammang koima umna lou bangding, Israel te phat masa peh a ahunglut jeh uva Hivites teleh Amor miten adalhah u gam bang ding ahi. Mi chenlouna gammang nasatah hi ding ahi. 
Ipi jeh enem itileh nahuhhing thei Pathen a kon'in na kihei mange. Nasel thei songpi chu na sumil tai. Man neitah muchi ho nahin poh lut theija, lengpi thei phatah na tu theina ding ahi. 
Na phu nikho leh ahung kedoh pai theina ding; Ahi, na tuni jingkah mama leh ahung pah lhah theina ding, ahin lengpithei ga khatcha loding umlou ding ahi. Khop tup ding nanei chasun chu lung gentheina dimset leh thoh joulou natna hi ding ahitai. 
Ngaijin! Chitin namtin galsat sepai ho twi khanglen gin kithong leng lung banga agin lullul u khu. Ngaijin thahat tah a hung kithong jenjun ho khu twi khanglen kinon gin abangu ve. 
Ahin twi khanglen pang sukeh gin kithong bang jonglehu, Pathen'in asuhthip diu, chuleh amaho chu lhaimang diu ahi.Hui jin changvai amutcheh bang diu, ching peijin leivui apeimang tobang ding ahi. 
Nilhah teng Israel in tijah tah a angajing diu,ahin kho hung vahteng agalmi ho u ana thiji tauve.Hiche hi eichom hou dihtah a achandiu dol changu va ahin, eisumang hou chan dinga lom chang uva ahi.
