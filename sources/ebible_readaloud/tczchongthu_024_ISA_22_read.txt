Isaiah.
22.
Jerusalem chung changa thuhil hitin ahunge-Gaova kimu phaicham: Ipi thu um hitam? Inchung sao langa chu ibola mijouse lhai lou vaham ? 
Khopi sung jouse tijan aboi tauve. Ipi kamu hitam kipana a anadim khopi a hi? Thilong ho muntinna akijam in, gala thijong ahipouvin, natna leh kella thi ahibou vui. 
Na lamkai hou ajammang gam tauve kithuh a pangsa louvin akipedoh jeng tauve. Mipi jong a jammang gutnaova mat tup in aum tauve. 
Hijeh a chu kasei ahi, “Kapdin neidalha uvin; lhepbi ding neigo hih un. Kamite kisuhmang kavet toh kilhon'in amaho din kap inge.” 
Aw, iti kisuchipdeh gallel nikho hia tam! Lungboina leh tijatna nikho hia hitam, van janel Pakaijin ahin lhunsah hi, gaova thilmun'a mun phaicham ahi. Jerusalem bang jouse akisuchip tan, molsang jouse a kon'in mithiho kana husa a kithong jeng jung jengin ahi. 
Elamite ten thalpi a kipoh un, sakol kangtalai ho leh a tolthem ho toh ahiuvin ahi. Kir miten galvon ompho ho a tuhdoh tauvin ahi. 
Sakol kangtalai jin na phaicham mun hoi jouse hin lon dimin tin, atolhon na kotpi ahin tum hong diu ahi. 
Juda te kivenna chu sutlhah peh in a um tan,galvon kikoina a gal manchah ladinga nalhai ding ahi. 
Jerusalem bang kisuchim chu naga khol chilin, dilneo hoa twi nakholtup in ahi. 
In ho na ga tetoh in, themkhat na phetlhan, bang ho alhou nadin songho nagol challin ahi. 
Khopi sung bangho kikah a twikholna khat na sadoh in, dilneo lui a kon'in twi nakhom tup e. Ahinla, abolpa kom'a panpina nathum naisaipoi. Phat sot a patna hin gong jing Pa nageldoh khapoije. 
Hiche phat chun Pakai,van janel Pakai chun na kouvin, puldou ding leh kapdin nasole. Na chonset naho jal'in lung lengvai tah in nalu kivomuijin atin, chuleh lung kisih na vetsahnan khaodip pon kivon din aseije. 
Ahin alamchom beh in nalam in, na kichemin ahi; na gancha ho leh na kelngoi nathat in ahi. Ju leh sa in golnop nabolin ahi. Hiti jong hin na seije, “ Neu hitin don uhite, ajeh chu jing leh ithi diu ahi ! “ 
Van janel Pakai chun kei kom'a ahin seidoh tai: “ Nathi nikho a hunglhun masang sen hiche chonset jeh ahi ngaidam nachan louhel ding ahi. “Hiche hi Pakai, van janel Pakai thutan ahi. 
Hiche hi ahi Pakai,van janel Pakaijin kaheng'a asei chu: “Shebna, leng inpi vaihom chu kimaitopin chuleh hiche thuhil hi pen: 
“Koihija naki gel a, chuleh hilai jahi ipi nabol a, nakivuina ding khuh hoitah nakisah a, songpi chung sanga hetjing nading na kitun ham ? 
Ajeh chu Pakaijin na lemang vah ding ahitai, mihatpa. Aman nahung matchah ding ahi, 
Kanglum akisea akichon banga naki chon sah ding, chuleh napei a, gam latah a napei mang ding ahi. Hilai achu nahung thi den ding, naloupina kangtalai ho kisusen tin, man thei hilou ding ahitai. Na lamkaipu molphouna nahung hi ding ahi ! 
“ahi, natoh namun a konna katoldoh ding nahi,” tin Pakaijin aseije. “ Nadin mun sangtah a konna kakai lhah ding nahi. 
Hiteng chuleh kasohpa Hilkiah chapa Elaikim, nakhel dinga kakou ding ahi. 
Ama chu na lengvon kakivon sahding chuleh na minvo leh nathuneina kipeding ahitai. Jerusalem leh Judah tedinga mipa hung hi ding ahi. 
David chenna in kanglong hkonna kapeh ding-leng thutanna mun dia dinmun sangpen ahi. Aman kot ho ahon tengleh koiman akhathei louding, akha tengleh koiman ahon theilou ding ahi. 
A insung ahin choisang ding ahi. Ajeh chu keiman ama chu dettah a kakoi ding, Thingkil banga kikhen lut bang ding ahi. 
Mopohna lentah peuvin tin, a insungmi aneopen geija choisanga um ding ahi.” 
Ahin,van janel Pakaijin hitijong hin aseije: “ Phat hunglhung ding ahi, thingkil detchet na kikhena angaitou chu, keiman kabo doh ding ahi. Hung doh intin, tol'a lhading ahi. Asuhdet ho puma hunglha mang diu ahi. Keima Pakaijin kasei ahi!”
