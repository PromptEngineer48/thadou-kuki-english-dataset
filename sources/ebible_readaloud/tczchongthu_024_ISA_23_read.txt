Isaiah.
23.
Tyre chung chang thua hiti hin thuhil ahunge kap un, Aw Tarshish a um konglen cheng, ajeh chu kong kaimun leh Tyre injouse aum gam tapoi! Cyprus akon'a thu kithang najah u chu adih sohkei ahi. 
Thipchet nin puldou un, twikhanglen pama umho leh sidon a sumkolvei ho. Na sumkolvei miho chun twipi agal kai tao vin. 
Twi thuh tah chunga akitol un ahi. Amaho chun chang le mim Egypt gam a kon'in nahin poh peh un chule Nile joi ah kon'in nahin poh peh uve. Vannoi in kailhang kiveina mun'a nanei u ahi. 
ahin tun jachat thoh in nakikoi tai, Sidon khopi, ajeh chu Tyre, twikhanglen kulpi chun aseije, “ Tun chaneilou kahitai; Chanu leh chapa ho jong kanei poi.” 
Tyre chung chang thusoh ho, Egypten ahin jah tengleh lunghem na nasatah hung um ding ahi. 
Tarshish a tun thuthot nin! Kap loi loi un,gamla tah a cheng mipi ho! 
Na khopi kipana leh thanopna mun tia ana kihe chu, tua thipchetna mangthai a bou kihe a hitam ? Malai na umchan thusim hi asao mong monge! Kho a cheng mi gamla tah a nasoldoh chu ngaiton. 
Vangsetna Tyre chunga hinlhun sah a, lenggam ho hin phutdoh mihattah chu koi ham? Amite akivei ho jong leng chapa te ngen ana hiuvin, a sumkolvei miho jong alen alal ngen anahiuve. 
Van janel Pakai chun na kiletsahna suh mang nadinga anabol ahin, leiset chunga dinmun sangtah a umho asuhnem ding ahi. 
Hungun, nangho Tarshish mite, Nile a twilen bangin agamsung chu theh doh in, ajeh chu Tyre in kivenna ding aneitapoi. 
Pakaijin twipi chunga akhut ahin jah doh in, leiset lenggam jouse ahin hotlingin ahi, Phoenicia douna thu ahin seijin, akulpiu suhmang din thu ahinpei. 
Hitin aseije, “Nakipa thanop kit tah louhel ding, O Sidon chanu, na kisugam a nahitai. Cyprus gam geijin jam jong lechun, kicholna ding namu louding ahi.” 
Babylon gam khu ven-hiche a cheng gam mite chu a umtapoi je! Assyrian ten Babylon chu nelgam a um gamsa ho apedoh tauvin ahi. Bangho a um kimun, leng inpi aphelhao vin, songhal song bong ho aselom tauvin ahi. 
Ka pun,O Tarshish kong len ho, ajeh chu na kongkaimun uchu akisumang tai ! 
Lengpa hinkho chu kum som sagi a saoding, Tyre chu suhmil a um ding ahitai. Ahin noti chunga la kisa banga khopi chu hung uldoh kit ding ahi: 
Selangdah kichoi jin lang, lamlen a vahlen, suhmil a um, notinu. Na kigel doh kit din, awngei tah in nala ho chu sa in. 
Ahi, kum som sagi jouleh Pakaijin Tyre asuh thou kit ding ahi. Ahin amasa a sangin lamdang dehlou ding ahi. Vannoi lenggam jouse adinga notinu hikit ding ahi. 
Ahin achaina leh atong jouse Pakai kipe ding ahi. A gou ho kikhol khom louding, amavang Pakai thempuho adin anpha leh vonpha channa a anei diu ahi.
