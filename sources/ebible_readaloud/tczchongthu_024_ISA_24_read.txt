Isaiah.
24.
Ven! Pakaijin leiset asuhmang ding kon ahitai,chuleh gam ong lentah asoding ahi. Leiset chung asumang in, amite athethang soh tai. 
Thempu ho leh milham ho, sohho leh vaihom ho, sohnu ho leh inneinu ho, sum hom pa leh sum laho, acho ho leh ajoh ho, sum bat sah ho leh sum pa laho, khat cha soh cha louding ahiuve. 
Leiset chung kihin sutheng sel ding chuleh kichom ngimsel ding ahi tin Pakaijin aseitai. 
Leiset in pul adou in, a golha gamtai, chuleh lousoh ho a kisumangin, a nang gamtai. Leiset chunga milen tah hojeng jong amah thah gam tai. 
Amite chonset jeh in, leiset in a thoh gimin ahi. Ajeh chu Pathen thuhil hoa pei chavai un, a danthu ho a sukeh un, tonsot a dinga akitepna asuseh tauvin ahi. 
Hijeh chun, gaosap nan leiset asumang in ahi. Amiten amite chonset jeh a genthei hahsatna ato diu ahi. Meiya akisumang un, mi themkhat bou ahingin akidalhan ahi. 
Lengpi gui ho jong mohseh in akisumang in, ju athah jong a kimu jou tapoi. Kipa thanop bol jouse jong akhoisa un, alung hem uve. 
Kipa thanop na khong gin athip heltai; golnop a kipa aw thong a kija tapoi. Selangdah a awngaitah a la kisa athip heltai. 
Ju leh la a kipa thanop na jouse achemang tan; judu ho judon ho jong akam uva akha tan ahi. 
Khopi kinchuh chuh in acheh chao in; insung jouse moh ponghung lut ho jeh in akikam soh in ahi. 
Mipi lamlen ho a akikhomun, ju asam sam jenguve. Kipana chu lungjin asoh tan ahi. Kipana chu hiche gam'a kon chun nomang in aumtai. 
Khopi chu mang thai in aki dalhan, a kot ho jong akivo lha gam tan ahi. 
Leiset chung jouse a hiche thusim hi akibange- amoh bou akidalhan, theiphung chunga theichang khat pampet a kidalha tobang, lengpi thei themkhat akikhop tup jouva agui a kidalha tobang hi ding ahi. 
Ahin dalhah a um jouse n kipana jeh in a eu vun, la asaove. Lhumlam a umhon Pakai loupina chu a vahchoi uve 
Solam gamkaija umhon Pakai loupina apeuve. Twikhanglen gal langpeh a umhon Israel Pathen, Pakai minchu avahchoi un ahi. 
Leiset kolning lang peh a kon'in vahchoila kisa kajaovin, chonpha Pa loupina kipe la ahi! Ahin ka lungsung hi lung genthei nan adim e. Nei kapiu vin, ajeh chu agolhapai ding kahitai. Jouva kibolna ache nalaije, chuleh leptholna hi muntin'a a um nalaije. 
Leiset aum miten, kichat tijatna, kokhuh chuleh thang tamtah nakimai topi diu ahi. 
Tijat na a jamhon kokhuh sunga lhalut untin, kokhuh sunga konna sohcha hon, thang nga hung oh diu ahi. Manthah na hi vanna konna go julha banga hung julha ding ahi; leiset bulpi ho hung kihot ding ahije. 
Leiset hi akisu keh tai. Nasatah a chimlha ahitai; Nasatah in akihot in ahi. 
Leiset hi jukham bangin alon dong dongin ahi. Hui lego in ponbuh asem bangin leiset akithingin, lhu intin thoudoh louding ahitai. Ajeh chu dougal asatnao gitlouna chu sangval a ahitai. 
Hiche nikho teng chuleh Pakaijin van munhoa um van miho gotna apeh ding chuleh leiset namtin chunga kiletsah tah a vaihom ho gotna apeh ding ahi. 
Amaho chu ki umkhum a, songkul'a kikoitup diu ahi. Songkul'a kikam tup chet diu, chule achaina leh gotna sangtah kipe diu ahi. 
Chuteng lha loupina chu hung mang suh cheh intin, nisa vah jong hung nol lha ding ahi, ajeh chu vanmi janel Pakai chun Zion lhanga vai ahin hom ding ahi.
