Isaiah.
26.
Hiche nikho tengleh Judah gamsung cheng mijousen hiche la hi asah diu ahi: Ka khopiu hi dettah ahi! Pathen mi huhhingna banghon eium kim velun ahi. 
Michonpha hodin kotpi hon peh un; tahsan umtah ho chu lut sah un. 
Nangma tahsan ho jouse, lungmong sel'a nakoiding ahin, alunggel jouse na chunga koidet ho chu. 
Pakai chu tahsan jing jengun, ajeh chu Pakai Pathen chu tonsot songpi ahi. 
Mi kiletsah ho chu asunem jin, khopi kiletsah chu asu lhujin, leivui asojin ahi. 
Mivaicha leh suh genthei a umhon akengtou vin achot phaovin, genthei vaichaho achunga avahleu vin ahi. 
Ahin, michonpha hodin lampi chu aken pon, ajot hahpoije. Thildih jengbol Pathen chu nahin, amalam u lampi nam pet in nalhai. 
Pakai, na danthu ho kanit uhin katahsan nao aphongdoh in, namin loupi ding hi kalung ngaichat pennu ahi. 
Jan khovah in nangma kaholjin; jingkah teng gunchu tah in Pathen kahol ji. Ajeh chu leiset thutanna nahung tenglehbou, miten adih chu ahet diu ahi. 
Migiloute nangai lut nan thilpha aboldoh deh pouve. Midangin thilpha abol vang'in, migilouten thildih lou abol jingu vin, Pakai loupina chu hetphah in anei pouve. 
Aw Pakai na khuttum dopsang eman agel pou ve. Na mite na huhdoh nomdan chu musah in, chuteng amaho chu jachatna dimdiu ahitai. Na meikong chun nagalmi ho sumang jeng hen 
Pakai chamna neipeh diu ahi; kabol ka suh bulhit jouseu hi nang jal'a bou ahi. 
Aw Pakai ka Pathen u, midang hon kachung uva vai ahin hom uve, ahin nangma bou nahi keima hon ka hou uchu. 
Kana jen leuho chu athiuvin, aum gam tapouve. A lhagao dalhah hou hung kile talouhel ding ahi! Nangman nasat nin, na sumang gamtai. Amaho chu sottahpi gelphah louvin aumtauve. 
Aw Pakai, kanam u nei semlet peh tauve; lentah nei so tao vin ahi. Ka gamgi hou jong nei kehlet peh un, loupina nangma kahin peuvin ahi ! 
Pakai, lung genthei in nangma kana hol ui. Na thunun pohgihna nanoija kahungtao uve. 
Numei naovop in nao anei na'a na asah a, apenchoi choi banga, Pakai, na umna'a kaum tengu leh kachuti jiu ve. 
Keiho jong lungna in kijel chot chot in ka umun, ahin kagenthei thoh ujong mohseh ahi. Leiset adin huhhingna kape thei pouve,hinna jong vannoi a kapolut thei pouvin ahi. 
Ahin Pakai a thiho chu hung hing diu, atahsau hung thoudoh kit ding ahi ! Leiset a imu ho chu hungthoudoh diu chuleh kipa jal'a la asah diu ahi. Ajeh chu na hinna-vah kipa chu jingkah daitwi alhah banga namite thi umna munna hunglha ding ahi. 
In na cheuvin, kamite, na kot hou kam tup un! Chomkhat adin kisel mangun, Pakai lunghanna akichai masang sen. 
Ven! Leiset mite a chonset jeh un, gotna pedin Pakai chu van'a kon'in ahungin ahi. Leiset in akithat ho asel jou tah louding, mijouse mu dinga hung kipuidoh diu ahi.
