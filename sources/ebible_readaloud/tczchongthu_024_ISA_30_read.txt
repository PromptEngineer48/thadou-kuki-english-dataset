Isaiah.
30.
“Ipi lung genthei nan na ngao ham, thu a nunglou kachate ho,” tin Pakaijin aseije. “Keija toh akikalin thilgon nanei un ahi. Ka lhagao in adeilou hotoh kigop khomna nanei jun, hichun na chonset nao na sesang toupeh tauvin ahi. 
Ajeh chu keima donglou vin, nache suh un Egypt panpina naga thum uve. Pharaoh huhbitna chu na tahsan un ahi. Ama lima chun nakisel gu uvin ahi. 
Ahin Pharaoh na tahsan jeh uva suh molphou'a naumdiu, ama chunga nakingap jeh uva jachatna a naum diu ahi. 
Ahin thaneina Zoan chan gei a keh beh in um jeng jongleh a noidou ho Hanes gei hunglhung ta jong leu, 
ama tahsan jouse chun jumna amudiu ahi. Aman nakithopiu malah a na suhmol phoudiu ahi.” 
Negev a gancha ho chung changa thuhil kaheng'a hitin ahung lhunge; Kholjin hon tijat umtah in nelgam ahin jot nun, Egypt gei adin olin akitolui- ahaonao jouse sangan ho chunga ahin pouvin, agou luhou sangongsao chunga ahin pouve-Egypt ten aventup nadiuva abon'a pehding atiu ahi. Gamthip gam ahin jot un, keipi bahkai achal le anu umna mun, gulse ho leh gunei gul ho umna mun ho ahin jotpao vin ahi. Hiche jouse hi ahin,Egypt in imacha nahin pehle louding ahi. 
Egypt kihahselna ho chu panna bei ahi bouve! Hijeh chun, Rahab tin kakouve-setna neilou Dragon. 
Tun chenlang hiche thucheng ho hi sun lhan. Lekhabu a sun lut in. Phat kichai kah adinga hettohsah a pang diu ahitai. 
Hichu, hiche mite hi milouchal midouhat ho ahiuvin, Pakai thuhil lunglutna ngai ding nomlou ahiu ve. 
Mitvah neiho jah a hitin asei uve, “Themgao ho jah a, Thudih nei seipeh hih uvin. Ngainom um chacha neisei peh uvin. Jouthu leh semthu ho neisei peh un. 
Lung jin nahi sumil soh in. Nalampi jim a kon'in kichondoh in Israel Mitheng Pa chungthu neiseh peh u, ngadoh tan.” 
Hiche hi Israel Mitheng Pan adonbutna ahi: “ Kathu seipeh ho namusit jeh leh nabolgim ho, jou le nal na tahsan joh jeh in, 
phulou helouva vangsetna na chunga hung chu ding ahi-bangho pohdoh a, khikeh a lhuh tobang ding ahi. Apet pet in hung chim lhan tin, bong hel hulin hung lha tan te. 
Bel keh banga naki suchip ding ahi-achip dan dan chu halneo khat a jong mei am kiloh theilou, twikul a kon'a jong twi themkhat chabeh jong kithal thei lou ahi. 
Thaneipen Pakai,Israel Mitheng Pan, hitin aseije: “ Kaheng'a hung kinung le hobou, keija kicholdo hobou huhdoh a na umdiu ahi. Lungneng sel leh tahsan nachu na thahat nao ahi. Ahin khatcha nachan lou diu ahi. 
Nangman naseijin, ' Ahipoi Egypt akon panpina kamu diu ahin, galsat nadinga sakol hattah ho eipeh diu ahi,' nati ahin, gangtah leh hattah namu diu chu ahileh nagal mi teuvin hattah leh gangtah a nahin del diu chu ahi bouve! 
Alah uva khatnin ahindel ding chu nangho sangkhat hi ding ahi. Alah uva nga in nabonu va najamsah soh diu ahi. Thinglhang chunga ponlah khom khat bou kidalha banga na kidalha ding nahin, molvum'a ponlap kikhai pheleng leng toh nabah ding ahi. Hijeh a chu Pakaijin ama kom'a nahung ding anga tei ding, hitia chu aman nangailut dan leh nakhoto dan chu nahetsah nom ahi. Ajeh chu Pakai hi tahsan umtah Pathen ahi. Apan pina ngahjing ho chu a nunnom ahiuve. 
Aw Jerusalem'a cheng, Zion mite, nangho naka kit loudiu ahitai. Ahenga panpina nathum uleh Ama chu mi lungset them ahi. Na kanau awgin chu Aman adonbut teitei ding ahi. 
Pakaijin an adin vangsetna, chuleh don din thoh natna nahin pe jong lechun, nahil din nakom'a um jing nalai di ahi. Na hil'a chu namit tah a namu ding ahi. 
Na nakol tah a najah ding ahi. Na nunglang tah a kon thu hung kisei ding,”Hiche lampi hi ahi nachena got chu,” Jetlang hihen,veilang hijongleh. 
Chuteng na dangka a kisem milim kihou, chule nagou lutah sana a kisem lim ho chu, na suhmang ding ahi. Ponse nen paimang banga na paimang ding, “Phasel to!” tia nasei khum ding ahi. 
Chuteng Pakaijin gojuh a phatthei naboh ding ahi, thil tuphat ahiteng. Kidang tah a agaho naki khop khom ding, chuleh na ganchate neh ding hamhing ninglhing setna um ding ahi. 
Loukhoi bongchal ho leh sangan hon anpha anehdiu, avai vang huijin alhoh mang ding ahi. 
Hiche nikho tengleh na galmi hou akitha a,insang ho ahung lhuh teng, vadung twi ho mol le lhang jouse hung longlha ding ahi. 
Lha chu nisa vah bangin vah intin, chuleh nisa vah chu jat sagi'a vah cheh ding ahi- nisagi sunga vah chu nikhat a vah tobang ahi! Hiti chu hi ding ahi Pakaijin amite sudam dinga akipatna, akisuh khah nao maha hou damna ahin peh teng. 
Ven! Pakai chu mun gamla tah a kon'in ahunge, lungsa pum leh meikhu nasatah in atomkhum in ahi. Amuh hoa lunghan na adimmin; athu ho , meiyin asuhmang toh abange. 
Ahaina hu sat chu twisoh a galmi ho ngong chan aphah bangin akisunglha abange. Chitin namtin kiletsah ho chu suhmang dinga asepchil ding ahi. Nahkhao akhi peh a, mangthai dinga ahoidoh ding ahi. 
Ahin Pathen miten golnop phatah kibolna'a la kisa ho banga kipa la asah diu ahi. Theile mutpan mun thenga Pathen houva cheho honkhat Jerusalem, Pakai lhangchung-Israel te Songpi'a apui banga, kipana' adimset dingu ahi. 
Chuleh Pakaijin aloupina aw ajahsah ding ahi. Abanthahat thaneina avetsah ding ahi. Meikong in aval lhum bangin hung kum lhan tin, van gin ho leh gelchang lentah tah hung lhabanga hung kum lhading ahi. 
Pakai thupeh dungjui a Assyrian te ahal hal 'a kehdiu ahi. A leng tenggol'a ajeplhuh gamdiu ahi. 
Hitia Pakaijin amol'a ajep uva, gotna apeh tengu leh, amiten selangdah atum uva, khong akhet uva golnop abol diu ahi. Abanthahat ahin doptouva, Assyria te ahin dou ding ahi. 
Topheth-meikou na mun-Assyria lengpa dinga phatsotna gontohsa kikoi ahitai; akigo nading thingho sangtah in akiselom tai. Pakai haina hu chun, meikong hung kikhodoh banga ahin halkou ding ahi.
