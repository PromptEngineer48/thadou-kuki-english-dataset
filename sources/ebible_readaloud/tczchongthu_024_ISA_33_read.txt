Isaiah.
33.
Ipi lunghemnan nangao ham nangho Assyria te, mi sumang ho, ahinla nangho leh nangho a itih in nakisumang kha hih helu vin ahi. Midang najou lhep jiuvin, ahinla nangho lheplha in na um kha hih uve. Mi suhmang nangah tengu leh nangho nakisumang diu ahi. Mi lheplhah nangah tengu leh nangho nakilhem lhadiu nahiuve. 
Ahin Pakai, nei hepiu vin, ajeh chu nangma ngah a um kahiuve.Nisim in kaban thahat nun hungpangin lang, hahsatna'a kaum tengu leh eihuhdoh diuvin hung pangin. 
Na awgin ajah jeh un melmate a jam uve. Nahung dindoh tengleh chitin namtin ajam jitao ve! 
Lungmul, peng palep leh khaokho hon louho leh lengpi gui ho akeova alha bang uva Assyria galsat sepai ho jong lhu uva, ima hilou diu ahitai ! 
Pakai chu alet a len hihen. Van mun'a cheng hijongleh aman Jerusalem chu thudih leh chonphatna in na asemdoh ding ahi. 
Hiche nikho tengleh ama chu na kingap detchet na abul hintin, huhhing'a ninglhing setna kikoi khol, hetna leh chihna napeh ding ahi. Pakai gin chu nadinga gou hi ding ahi. 
Ahin tun vang nagal hang hou lhang phongin akap uvin ahi. Chamna semho nommo tah in lunglha in akap uve. 
Na lampiu ho jong akamdel tai; Koi macha alamlha tapouve. Assyrian ten chamna dinga kinopto nachu asukeh tauvin, ahetoh a pangho masanga kitepna ananei uchu anahsah tapou vin ahi. Koimacha gin le ja anei pouve. 
Israel gam chu puldou nan achup lhatai. Lebanon jong jachat nin ananglha tai. Sharon phaicham chu tun gamthip gam ahitai. Bashan leh Carmel chom in ana umtai. 
Ahin Pakaijin aseije: “Tun keima kadindoh ding ahi. Ka thahat na leh kahatna tua hi kavetsah ding ahi. 
Nangho Assyria miten hampa gop ahilouleh kolbu kung tailou ima nasodoh pouve. Nahai nau hu chu mei hung kisoh intin, nahin vallhum dingu ahi. 
Ling hamboh a kisatlhah a, meiya akilelut bangin na mite jong kachai keidiu ahi. 
Ipi kabol doh ham phaten ngaijun, nangho chitin namtin gam la tah a umho! Chuleh naicha'a umhon kathahat hi hetpeh in nei neijun !” 
Jerusalem'a um michonse ho tija in akithing thing uve. Pathen houlou ho chu tijatna nasatah in aman tauve. “Hitobang mi vallhum jengthei meikong hi kon athoh jou mong ding ham?” tin akap uvin ahi. “Hitobang meikong nasatah akon'a koiham asohcha ding ?” 
Mi kitah leh mitheng ho, joule nal'a phatchomna ding nomlou ho, nehguh deilou jeh a munchom gam latah a umdoh ho tolthat dinga guhthim'a thilgon ho thusei ngaipeh lou'a da chah khet jeng ho, thilpha lou bol dinga lhepna jouse mangcha guho akon'a amit u sing jeng ho- 
mun sang'a cheng ding ho chu amaho hi ahiuve. Mol le lhang 'a um songpi ho chu akulpiu va pangding ahi. Neh le chah hin kithah peh diu, chule twi jong ning lhing setna aneidiu ahi. 
Lengpa aloupina jouse toh namit tah uva namu diu, chuleh gamla tah a sao gam leiset chu namit'a namu diu ahi. 
Hiche tijat umtah nikho hi nahin nunggel doh diu, chuteng nahin doh diu chu, “Insangho sim toh'a pang Assyrian milun ho chu hoiya um uva hitam? I khopi u alhuh nunga thil le lo ki chomdoh ching tup'a pang lekhabu koitup ho chu hoilai ja um uva hitam ?” 
Migilou ho leh mikhanse hon hetkhah lou leh kidangtah paochom athohou hi navel mukit tah louding ahi. 
Zion chu golnop phatah bolna mun'a nahin lemu joh dingu ahi. Jerusalem chu chamna leh bitkeija umna khopi'a nahin mu dingu ahi. Khoilupon in, akhao kiloi detchet leh akhom ho chah thet na kitung tobang ding ahi. 
Eiho dinga Pakai chu Hatchungnung Pen hung hi ding ahi. Ama chu vadung vailentah in bitkei'a akoi, galmi hon ahin palkai joulou u bangdi ahi. Galmi ho kong jong kitolna thei hilou ding ahi. 
Ajeh chu Pakai chu eithutan pao ahin, dan eipeuva leh i lengpau ahije. Aman eikhohsah untin, eihuhdoh diu ahi. 
Galmite kongtol napon akilhamin, kongleh lang achi pin, manchah ho jong panna bei ahitai. Anei agou houjong Pathen mite kihom diu ahitai. Elbai ho jengin jong achan vou u akilah diu ahi! 
“Kadam pouvin chuleh panpi bei kahiu ve,” tia Israel miten asei tahlou diu ahi. Ajeh chu Pakaijin achonset nao angaidam ding ahitai.
