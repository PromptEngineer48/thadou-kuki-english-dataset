Isaiah.
41.
Twikhanglen gallam sanga um gamho, kam'a sanga thipbeh in umun, kingai phat cheh uvin. Nalung lhailou nau pipen chu hin polut’un.Tuhin hung uvin lang chule seijun.Nachung thu’u kitanna ding mun gontup ahitai. 
Solam gam’a konna hiche Lengpa hi, Pathen kinbol thei dinga koi hin tildoh hija ham? Hiche pa hin chitin- namtin galla ajo ding chule alenghou jong akeng toa noigep dinga phalna pea chu koi hin tem? Achem jam’in agalmi ho chu huilah a vutvai aso jenge. Huijin changvai athemang bangin ama’n athalpia amaho chu athe chehtai. 
Achena khahlou tol chu ahojeng vang'in, amaho chu ano mang sohkeijin chule bitkei jin achepeh’e. 
Phat akipat til’a patna khang hunglhung nalai ding ho chunga hat tah’a tonga kou khom’a chu koi ahidem?Hiche hi keima Pakai, apatna le chaina, Keima kiti bou hi Kahi. 
Twikhanglen gal lama gam ho chun tijatah in aveuve. Leiset mong lama umho jong atija’uvin chule galbol din akitil’uve. 
Milim semho jong akitil toh’uvin, khat le khat akihou uvin, “Lungam’un!” akitiuve. 
Khut them bolpan sana khengpa atilkhouvin, chule sehcha khenga pangpa chun thih khengpa atilkhouvin, Aphatai, ahung hoilheh jengtai, tin aseijuve. Chingthei tah in ama hon asutouvin, akilonga le adet loulai umlouna dingin ama hon akil detchetnuve. 
Hinla kagolpa Abraham’a konna hung kikhai lha Jacob kalhen doh jal'a hi, Israel nangma kalhacha nahije. 
Leiset kolkim vella konna nangma kaheng'a kalekou nahi, Nangma kalhacha nahijeh a keiman nangma kalhen doh nahin, kapai doh louhel ding nahitai,tia kasei ahi. 
Kicha hih hellin, ajeh chu keiman nangma kaumpinai. Lunglha hih hellin, ajeh chu keima na Pathen kahi. Keiman natha hatsah ing ting chule kapanpi ding nahitai. Keiman kajona khut jet lama nangma kadop touding nahi. 
Vetan, namelma te ho jouse khu chea lumho khu, lung nohphah’a chule kijum soa ahi tauve. Nangma dou chan chu thitei ding chule imacha hung hilou ding ahije. 
Nangma jotei dinga kigoho chu navet jongleh mohseh’a vebep nahi ding ahitai. Nangma nadel khum ho chu imacha hilou dingu ahiuve. 
Ajeh chu Keima, na Pakai, na Pathen keima tah in nangma khut jetlam katuhnai,chule najah a kasei ahi, Kicha hih in, keima nangma panpi dinga hikom'a uma kahi. 
Nangma akineosah lungtol tobangbep, vo Jacob, Kicha hih uvin, Israel mite ho, ajeh chu Keiman kapanpi ding nahiuve. Keima na Pakai, na Huhhingpa. Keima Israel Mitheng kitipa chu kahi. 
Nangma chang chilna manchah thah, ha’hemtah tampi neija kangkui tobang nahi ding ahi. Nangman na melmate chu nabotel’a naloi khen ding, lhangsang tah tah hoa koldeh kisoh ding ahitai. 
Amaho chu huilah a napei le le ding, chule hui chun aboncha kei’uva asem mang hel dingu ahi. Chimpei jin amaho chu athe cheh dingu ahitai. Chutengleh nangma Pakaiya nakipa ding, Israel tedinga Atheng Khat kitipa’a kithang-at ding nahitai. 
Mivaicha le ngaichat neihon twi aholleuva amujou lou uva, chule dangchah’a adangu agotlhah tengleh, Keima, Pakaijin amaho chu kahin hou ding, Keima, Israel te Pathen’in, itih channa jong kanungsun louhel dingu ahitai. 
Keiman amaho dinga chu munjang lai lai hoa vadung kalonsah ding, keiman amaho dinga phaicham dung’a twinah ho kahin putdohsah ding chuie nelphaiho jong chu twi kiveinaho kasosah ding ahi. Vadung twi hung long chun tolgotna laiho jouse chu apalphei ding ahi. 
Keiman gaso theilou nelphaja thingphung ho -ceder thing, thing gi, pahcha thing, Olive thing, thao thing,theichang phungle chah thing ho kaphu ding ahi. 
Hiche kabol hin, hiche thilkidang kibol mu ho jousen ipi kiseina ahi ahetkhen thei diu, hiche kibol hi, Pakai Israel mite dinga Atheng Khat kitipa, asempa’a kon ahi. 
Na milim hou jeh un nachung thu kiseijun, tin Pakaijin aseije. Ama (milimsemthu) ho chun ipi abolthei’uvem hin vetsah’u hen, tin Israel te Lengpa chun aseije. 
Masang peh a chu ipi anasoh a ham? ti eima hon aphotchet igeltoh theina ding uvin amaho asei dingin kigo uhen. Ahilouva ahileh, hitia chu eihon ipi thil hungsoh thei ja ahi, amaho hin ikhonung dingu thu eihil diu angaije. 
Ahije, nikho hunglhung ding hoa ipi hungsoh ding nei seipeh’uvin. Chutah leh pathenho nahiu hetauvinge. Ahimongin, apha hihen, ase hijongleh nabolnom nom’u bol’uvin. Kalunguva kidangsahna chule kichatsahna lunggel sosahna dingin thil ipiham hinbollin. 
Hinla nomlou. Nangho imacha nahi hihbeh’uvin, imacha jong naboltheipouvin ahi. Nadeilhen hou jong amahotah chu akisuboh’uvin ahi. 
Hijongleh Sahlam gam'a kon'in Keiman lamkai khat kahin tildoh in ahi. Solam gam'a kon'in ama chu aminin kakouvin ahi. Keiman lengho le leng chapa te ho chunga gal kajosah ding ahi.Aman amaho hi bel dengpan leingan abolna banga amaho chu achilgoi ding ahi. 
Tkonna patna hiche hunglhung ding ahi tihi koiyin naseipeh ham? Hiche hi koiyin gaova aphondoh hiya, nangman athusei chu adih ahi tia na tahsan jong ham? Koiman thucheng khat jong sei aumpoi. 
Keiman Zion jah a chun,’Ven! Panpina pe dinga chu ahung naije! Tia aseipeh masapen kahi. Keiman Jerusalem chu Kipana Thupha pole ding kasolpeh ding ahi. 
Na milim ho lah a khatpenin jong nahilchah pouve. Keiman thudoh kabollin ahileh, thu kadoh phatnin khatchan jong donbutna ahinpepouve. 
Ve’uvin, amaho hi abonchauvin angol ngensen ahiuvin, aphatchomna bei ahiuve. Na limsemthu hou jong hui tobanga agohkeo ahiuve.
