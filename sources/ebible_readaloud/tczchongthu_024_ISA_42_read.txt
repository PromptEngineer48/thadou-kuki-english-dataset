Isaiah.
42.
Kalhachapa, keiman thahatna kapeh pa chu veuvin. Ama kadeilhenpa eilung lhaipen’a chu ahi. Keiman Kalhagao asunga kakhumpeh tai. Aman chitin-namtin lah a thu adih’a atan ding ahi. 
Aman kho sampon tin, mipi lah a jong aw sanga thu aseilou ding ahi. 
Aman pumpeng chip jong ahebohpai louding chule khoilu thaomeipat akhulai jong asuhmit pai jeng louding ahi. Aman alam louva kibol ho jong abonchauva chunga thu adih’a atan ding ahi. 
Leiset pumpia thu adih’a vaihomna aum masang sea, Ama lhasam pontin, lung jong lunglha louding ahi. gamla tah’a gamho twikhanglen gal lama hon jong ama thupeh anga diu ahi. 
Pakai Pathen’in vanho asem’in chule apha jal lin ahi. Aman leiset le asunga um jouse asem’in ahi. Ama’n mijouse haitheina hu ape’in, leiset chunga vahthei jouse hinna apeh ahi. 
Pakai kiti Keima, kachonphatna vetsah dinga kakou nahi. Keiman nangma kakhutna kapuija chule kahonbit nahin, chule kamite Israel te henga kapeh nahi hi keima toh amaho kakitepna vetsahna khat ahitai. Chule nangma chitin-namtin puihoi lea napan ding ahije. 
Nangman mitcho ho kho namusah ding, sohchang ho songkulla konna nacham lhatsah ding chule songkul thim lah a touho chamlhat sah ding nahi. 
Keima Pakai kitipa chu Kamin ahi! Ka loupina chu koima dang kapehdoh louding, chule eivahchoi nau semthu milim ho chuto chan kaneitha louding ahi. 
Gao thuseina kanei chasun aguilhung cheh in, chule tun keiman gao thu kasei kit ding ahi. Ahung guilhun masanga khonung thudol ka seipeh ding nahi. 
Pakaiya lathah sauvin! Ama vahchoina ho leiset kolning lia chenghon sauvin! Twikhanglen hoa kongtol ho le nangho twikol gam mun gam latah’a chengho geijin jong na boncha uvin la sauvin. 
Gamthipma um khopiho lachom kisahna’a jao’uvin; Kedar khoho jong kipah uhen.Sela mite hon kipah in la sauhen; lhangchung vum hoa kon'in vahchoina ho sap thouvin! 
Vannoi pumpin Pakai chu loupina peuhen; Avahchoina sauhen. 
Mihat chungnung tah tobangin PAKAIchu kipandoh intin, galsatpa tobanga lunghang tah’a kondoh ding ahi. Aman galsatna dinga kholhang asapma, amelmate jouse achilgoi ding ahi. 
Ama’n, Keima sottahpi kathip beh anahita’n, ahinai, keima hi kana kikangse anahi. Hinla tua hi naosonat thoh minu khat tobanga kapenna chule kakhoisatna,chule ka-eo’jah jeng ding ahitai. 
Keiman lhangsang ho le mol ho chu kasuhcham ding chule a’eng jin jen ho ka suhmangpeh ding ahi. Keiman vadung ho chu tolgolai kaso ding chule twi kiveina mun jouse chu kangsoh ding ahiuve. 
Keiman khomulou Israel chu lampi thah’a kapuile ding, ache khahlou nauva kapui le ding ahi. Keiman amasangu lampi thim jong kavahsah ding, chule amasangu lampi kanamsah ding ahi. Hitia hi keiman hichengse hi kabol ding, amaho kanungsun loudingu ahi. 
Hinla semthu milim tahsan ho, nangho ka pathen'u nahi tiho chu, jacha’a kihei mang ding ahiuve. 
Na’ngong hon ngaijun,mitcho nang hon veuvin, muchen’un. 
Kamite, tobanga mitchoa kasoh, koi um’em? Ka sottol tobanga na ngonga koi umem?Kalhendoh mite, Pakai sohpa, tobanga mitcho koi um’em? 
Nangman adih chu ipi ham? ti namun chule nahedoh jengin, hinla hiche chu nabolnom hih in ahi. Na nabeng’a najanai, hinla jahchet ding nagopoi. 
Pakai chu chondih tah ahijeh in, Athu loupitah chu achoi’an tai. 
Hinla Amite chu aneiyu achom’un chule asuhmang peh’un, asohchang un, songkul'a’a ahenun, chule thang kolla a’oh gam tauve. 
Hiche thu hi koiyin thusim’a konna angaija, ahetdoh thei’ uva chule khonunga thil hung kisumang ding ho nanga diu ham? 
Israel chu chomgam ding leh suhkhah dinga phatsah koi ham? Ama douna’a ichonset jeh’uva Pakai chun aphal jeh ahi. Ajeh chu miho chu Alamlhah a che nom hih’uva, A Danthu jong ajui nom lou jeh’u ahi. 
Hijeh a hi Alunghanna chu achung uva abuhlhah’a, chule gal’a atha’u ahi. Amaho chu meilhum sunga akilelut jeng vanguvin, hetdoh ding agopouve. Amaho meikouva akihal jeng vang’un, chihnan aneideh pouve.
