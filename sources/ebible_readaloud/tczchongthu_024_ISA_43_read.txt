Isaiah.
43.
Hijongleh tun , Vo Jacob nasempa PAKAI sei ngaijin. Vo Israel, nagong dohpa chun aseije, “ Kicha hih in, ajeh chu Keima’n nangma kachohdohsa nahitai. Keima’n namin’a kakou nahin; nangma keija nahi. 
Twithuh lah najot tengleh, Keiman kaumpi ding nahi. Hahsatna vadung najot tengleh, twija nalhahlut louding ahi. Kibolsetna meilah napal teng jongleh, meiyin nahalkhah louding; meiyin nakah louhel ding ahi. 
Ajeh iham itileh Keima Pakai, na Patehn kahi, Israel te mithengpa, na Huhhingpu chu kahi. Keiman Aigupta chu nacham lhatna dinga nakilhat dohna ding’a kapeh nahin; Nangma mun'a Ethiopia le Seba chu kapehdoh ahitai. 
Nangma keima dinga sahlut tah nahi jeh in, nangho khella midang chu kaphal doh a. nangma dinga ahin nau chu kapehdoh ahitai. Keima’n Kangailutna hi jeh a nangma jabolna changa nahije. 
Kichahih in, ajeh chu Keiman kaum pinai. Keiman nangma le nachilhah ho chu solam le lhum lama konna kachop khom ding nahiuve. 
Keiman sahlam le lhanglam jah a , kachapa te le kachanute chu leiset kolkim vella kon'in Israel’a hin puilutnin kati ding ahi. 
Keima a Pathenna einei jouse chu hin puijin, ajeh chu keiman amaho chu kaloupina dinga kasem ahi. Keima amaho sempa chu kahi. 
Mit neija ahia kho mulou, bilkol neija khojalou ho chu mipi lah a kon'in hinpuidoh un. 
Chitin-namtin chu hinpui khomun! Vannoi mite chu hinkhom tup’uvin! Milimsemthu anei’u chun hitobang hunglhung ding hi aseimasat khah em?Jinga ipi ahungso ding aseithei pen aumna diu vem? Hitobang thuseidoh hettohsah hoiya um uvem? Amaho chun thutah asei’u ahi tia aphotchetna pengam aumna dem? 
Hinla nangma keima hettohsah a panga nahiuve, Vo Israel! Tin Pakaijin aseije. Nangma kalhacha nahi.Nangma hi keima eihechen dinga, eitahsan dinga, chule Keima bouhi Pathen kahi ti hetkhen themna neija nahi. Pathen dang aumpon-Itih’a jong ana um khalou, chule itih’a jong umkhalou ding ahi. 
Kei, Keima tah hi, Pakai chu Kahi, chule Huhhingpu dang aumpoi. 
Amasatah in huhdoh a naum diu thudol kaphon peh’uvin, chuphatnin kahuhdoh’un chule vannoiya kaseiphongin ahi.Koimacha gamdang pathenin hiche hi abol khapoi.Keima bou Pathen kahi hi nangho a hettohsah a panga nahiuve, tin Pakaijin aseije. 
Tonsotna pat tonsot geija Keima Pathen kahi.Koiman kakhutna konna koima alahdoh theilou dingu ahi.Katohdohsa chunga koima’n imalou sothei pon te. 
Hiche hi Na Huhhingpu, Israel Mitheng Khat kitipa Pakai chun asei ahije: “Nangho jal'a keiman Babylon douna’a galmi honkhat kasoldoh ding, Banylon miten” akithang-atpiu akong innei houva chu jamdoh dingu ahi. 
Keima Pakai, nangho Athengpa Khat, Israel sempa le Lengpa chu kahi. 
Keima Pakai twi lah a lampi hin hongdohpa,twikhanglen’a tolgo hin umdohsahpa chu kahi. 
Keiman Aigupta galhat ho chu asakol ho’u le asakol kangtalaiho’u pumin kakoudoh in, keiman amaho chu twi kinong sunga kanolutnin,ahileh alha lhum tauve. Chule ahinkhou chu khoilu thaomei pat kihal kisumit jouva jemkai to abanguve. 
Hijongleh hiho jouse chu sumil jengun- Keiman kahinbol ding hitoh imacha dang atepithei ding aumpoi. 
Ajeh chu keiman thilthah khat kabol ding ahi.Veuvin, kapantai!Muloulai nahiuvem? Gamthip laiya kenglampi kalhundoh ding ahi! Gamgo gam’a chu keiman vadung kahin lonsah ding ahi. 
Loujao hoa gamsa ho, si’al ho le sumbupi jeng hon jong gamthip gam'a twi kapeh jeh’uva eichoi’at dingu ahi.Hitia chu keiman gam gotna leimun'a jong chu vadung ho kalonsah ding, hichun kalhendoh mite hung kidodoh tauvinte. 
Keiman Israel chu keima’a dingin kakisemtai, chule amaho chun nikhat le vannoi pumpi masanga jabolna eipeh dingu ahi. 
Hinla Jacob insung mi deitahte, nang hon kapanpi dingin nanom hih laijuve. Vo Israel, nang hon keima neichim chah lheh jenguve! 
Nanghon kelngoi le kelcha hoa kilhaina pumgo thilto dingin neihin choipeh pouvin ahi. Lousoh ga thilto hoa chule bego-gimnamtui thilto hoa kilhaina thiltoa ‘ neija bol tapouvin, keiman nangho ahahsa pohgih pohsah louva chule nagimna diu ima ngeh louhel kahi. 
Nanghon bego- gimnamtui le kilhaina thilto thao ho neihin choipeh pon ahi. Chusangin na chonset poh gihho neipoh sah in chule nathemmona houva neitha cholsah tauvin ahi. 
Kei, Keima bouvin, keima tah kahi jal'a bou na chonsetna ho kachul mangin chule hicho chu gelkit talou ding kahi. 
Athil umdol velvet khom kitnu hitin, chule nathemo louna thudol nakisei thei ding ahi. 
Atil laipeh’a kipatna napu napate ho’uvin keidouna’a anachonseuvin, nalamkai ho jouseuvin kadan thu apalkeh tauve. 
Hijeh a chu na thempu hou toh kakinopmo’uva chule Jacob dinga amahthahna hel kathu lhuh’a chule Israel dinga jachatna soh’a ahitai.
