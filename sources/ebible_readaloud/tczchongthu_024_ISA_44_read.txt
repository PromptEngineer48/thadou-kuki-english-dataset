Isaiah.
44.
Hijongleh tuhin kalhachapa Jacob, kalhendohpa Israel kasei ngaijin. 
Pakai nasempa le napanpi chun aseije:Kicha hih in, Vo Jacob, kasohpa. Kalungset vo Israel, kalhendoh penpa. 
Ajeh chu keiman nadangchah dangnou sahna dingin twi kahinsun lhah ding chule naloulai ju aleiset goho chu boltheina dinga twi kahin nensah ding ahi. Chule keiman ka lhagao nason-nachilhah ho’u chunga ka Lhagao kabuhlhah ding ahi. 
Amaho hi twi kichapnou ham hing tobang ding, vadung pama huisum thing phung tobang diu ahi. 
Abangho chun kiletsah tah’a, “Keima Pakaiya kahi,”atidingu ahi. Adang ho chun, “Keima Jacob chilhah kahi,”atidiu ahi. Mi abang khatloi chun Pakai min chu akhut chunguva ajih dingu chule Israel min chu amahoa akisan dingu ahitai. 
Hiche hi Pakaijin aseichu- Israel te Lengpa le Huhhingpu chu, Van'a Pakai galmi ahiuve, ati dingu: 
Keitobang koi umham? Ama chun malama kal hin song hen chule athuneina photchenna hinpe hen. Keiman phat masa laiya kana phudetna chule akhonung ding kana phudohsa peh tobangin ama jong chun boldoh hen. 
Tijat bol hih un, kicha hihbeh un. Keima masang peh’a nangho dinga Pathen dang koi umem? Kalung gon ka phondohsa hita lou ham? Nangho hi Keima hettohsah’a panga nahiuvin- Pathen adang um mong em? Songpi dang aumpoi- khat jeng jong aumpoi. 
Milim semtu sema pangho itobang tah’a ngol hiu vem?Asahlut tah hou hi aphatchomna bei ahi. Milimsemthu houhon hiche hi ahepouvin, hijeh a hi abonchauva kisum souva ahi. 
Koiham angol’a ahilouleh pathen akisem thu thei ding ham?Milim kisemthu khatnin themcha’a jong akithopi theilou hija ahi. 
Milimsemthu hou ho, adeh’a asema pang ho hi jachat umtah ahiuvin- mihem mai mai bep ahiuvin-pathen kasem thei diu ahi tin akithang-at’uvin ahi. Amaho pang khom theinau vinte, hinla amaho hi tijatna le jachatna mun’a hung ding diu ahiuve. 
Chuphatnin, thih khengpa chu ameisa a kon'in manchah hemtah sem dingin adingdoh in, athaneina pumpi panin thih chu akhengin chule adei bangin asemin ahi. Anatoh jallin ama agil kellin chule alhachomin, chule hichun adangchah sah in chule alhadah jengtai. 
Chuphatnin, thingthem bolpa chun thing bong ho chu ate tohna alan, chule agol kaina’a chun gol akaijin, atetoh in chule agongso chu aumdoh sahtai. Aman chah hemtah chu apanin chule atoltoh in, hiti chun mihem limin ahin semdoh in, mihem’hoina limpun asem’in, chuin a pathen houna in na neo sunga chun akoitai. 
Ama’n Cedar thingho chu aphuh ‘in, kheng thing le khongnang thing phung chu alhengdoh in, chah thing chu gam mang lah a aphutnin chule go hung ju lha chun achap nou jin ahi. 
Chujouvin aman thing go phabep chu meiya atih’in, hiche chu aman aki’oijin chule aching lhah jong akisem’in ahi. Chutah in amoh chengse chu pathen’na houthei dingin akisemin, hiche alimsemthu masanga chun akuntai. 
Ama’n thing phabep chu atih kouvin, sa chu akigon, chule oi jong aki’oijin, hiche meisa hi anom’e, tin aseitai. 
Chujouvin aman thing amoh chengse chu ala’n, semthu pathen’lim akisuitai. Chuche masanga chun adilsun, ahouvin chule ahenga chun ataove: “Nei huhdoh in, nangma ka-pathen nahi” tin aseije. 
Hibang lom’a ngol chonna le hetna neilou hi! Amitnu asin jeh un, amuthei tapouve. Alungu akihonlou jeh in, agelthei tapouve. 
Alimsemthu sema panga jong chun, “hiche hi thinggo kisem thua ahi bouve!” Mei luma tidingin akeh khat kahaltai, chule kachanglhah kanna ding le sagomina dingin kamangtai.Iti danna amoh chengse chu pathen kisohthei ding hija? Keiman thing amoh um chu Pathen houva kahou ding hija ham? tia lunga dattohna jong aneipoi! 
Mi vaicha chu ahilouhel atahsan sah un, mi ngol vutvaija kivah ahi. Apanpi thei louhel ding khat chu atahsan ahitai. Hinla, “Hiche lim kisem thu kakhutna katuh hi jou thubuh hilou ham?”tia amachang kidohna anei thei dehpon ahi. 
Vo Jacob, lung gillin ngaiton, ajeh chu nangma kalhacha nahi, vo Israel. Keima Pakai, nasempa kahin, chule Keiman kasuhmil louhel ding nahi. 
Keiman mei bang kitol to banga na chonsetna ho katol mangtai. Keiman nasuhkhel ho chu jingkah daitwi tobangin kanoimang gam ahitai. Oh kaheng'a hungkile kitnin, ajeh chu nangma chamlhatsah dinga keiman aman kapehchai ahitai. 
Vo Vanho, vahchoina la sauvin, ajeh chu Pakaijin thil kidangtah aboltai.Kipah thanomin samuvin, leiset noi lama hon jong! Vo lhangsangho le gammang ho chule thingphung tinin, La hinsa doh uvin! Ajeh chu Pakaijin Jacob ahuhdoh in chule Israel lah a choi’atnin aumtai. 
Hiche hi Pakai na Huhhingpa le na Sempa chun, “Keima hi Pakai thil jouse sempa chu kahi.Keiman van ho hi kapha jal ahi. Leiset kasempetna kakom'a koi umem? Tia asei ahi. 
Keiman themgao lhem ho chu mijou ahiuve tia kasosal’a chule thil hunglhung dia gaova thusei ho mingol ho kati ahi. Keiman miching ho chu thumopna phalou kapeh’a, hitia chu amaho chu angolnau kaphot chet ahitai. 
Hinla ka themgao ho thuphon chu kasuh bulhit peh in ahi! Amaho chu Kamanchah’a Jerusalem jah a, “ Miho ho chu hiche mun'a hi chengkit ding ahiuvin chule Judah khopi hoa, “Nangho kisadoh kit ding nahiuvin, nanei amangho kahin kiledohsah kit ding nahiuve,” tia ka seipeh ahi. 
Keiman vadung ho jah a, “Kang tauvin,” kati teng, kang jeng diu ahitai. 
Cyrus jah a chu, “Ama ka Kelngoichingpa ahi, tia kasei teng, kasei tobanga aman aboltei ding hija ahi. Aman thu apeh’a, “Jerusalem sapha kitnun, atiding: ‘Hou’In sempha kit’uvin,’tia aseiding ahije.
