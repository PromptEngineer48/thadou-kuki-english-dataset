Isaiah.
54.
Vo cha neilou nu, cha hing khalouhel nu nangman, lan san! Vo Jerusalem, aw ging le kipah thanom tah in la san. Naoso nat thoh khalouhel numei, dalhah’a um numei nu chun, ajipato chengkhom nu sangin, cha atam hin johtai, tin Pakaijin aseije. 
Na in keh lenin, chule abanin khat sahben. Na in sah chu kijalsah in, chule sumchena ding itchah hih hellin. 
Ajeh iham itileh bailam tah’a nangma na kidop sangna chun nakipa sah ding ahi. Nason nachi lhah ho chun namdang ho gam alo ding chule khopi mangthah hoa chu gacheng kit diu ahi. 
Jachatna hinkho naman kit louding ahitah jeh in, kicha hih in. Nangma not thapna chang louding nahi tah jeh in kicha hihbeh in. Nangman na khangdonlai jachatna ho le meithai lunghemna jong na geldoh kittah louhel ding ahitai. 
Ajeh iham itileh na- Sempa chu najipa hung hi ding; Amin chu Van Galmite Pakai akiti. Ama chu Vannoi pumpi Pathen, Israel te Atheng Penpa, na Chodohpa chu ahi. 
Ajeh iham itileh Pakaijin - nangma khangdong cha jinei, ajipan adalhah tobanga, nangma nalung hemna’a konna hin koukitna ahitai, tin Pakaijin aseije. 
Phat chomcha kadon lou nahin, hijongleh khoto them tah’a kale lah kit ding nahi. 
Lunghan koujah jeh a chomcha khat kamai kahei mang anahi. Hinla tonsot ngailutna’a keiman nangma kakhoto ding nahi, tin na Pakai, na -Chodohpan aseije. 
Keiman Noah phat laiya kana kihahsella, Keiman twi-sangletna leiset pumpi kachupsah kit tahlouhel ding ahi tia kanasei banga, tua hi kakihahsel’a, Keima kalunghangkit talou ding chule kagimbol louding nahitai. 
Ajeh iham itileh molsang ho kichon hen lang chule mol’ho kichon mang ding hijongleh, ka ngailutna kitahna nangma chunga kaumsah den ding, ka kitepna phatthei bohna chu itih’a kisukeh louhel ding ahitai, tin ahepina na chunga koipa Pakaijin aseije. 
Vo khopi huijin asuh ling jing, boija chule kinepna um talou khopi! Keiman nangma songmantam’a kasah phatna chule na’inbul ho chu saphir songa kaphudet ding ahi. 
Keiman na in sang ho chu songmantam vah lap lap’a kasem’a, na kelkot ho chu valleh leh’a kajepma chule na- in bang ho jong chu songmantam’a kijep ding ahije. 
Keiman nachate ho jouse kahil ding, chule ama hon lungmon konna bulhingset’ nomsa ding ahiuve. 
Thu adih le ahoia kivaipohna khat umna’a chu nangma ki huhbit ding nahi. Na melmate nangma’a konna gamla tah’a kichon doh dingu, nangma lungmonga nachen ding, tijatnan nahin nailou ding ahitai. 
Nangma douna a nam khat galla nasat dinga ahunga ahileh, keima hinsol ahipoi. Koi hijongleh nano khum ho chu gallel’a kumsuh ding ahiuve. 
Keiman thih khengpa kasem ahin, ama chun pum meiya meihol ajapma chule gal manchah kisuh gamna ding akhendoh ahi. Keiman asumang ding sepai ho semsa kakoi ahitai. 
Hijongleh ahunglhung ding nikho’a chu nangma douna’a gal manchah pan ho chu lolhing pouvinte.Nangma oimo dinga aw sanga thusei ho chu nangman napho thip ding ahi. Hiche phatchomna ho hi Pakai lhachate chang ding ahiuve. Hiche kisei adihna photchetna hi keija kon hunga ahi, Keima Pakaijin kasei ahitai.
