Isaiah.
55.
Adangchah khattou umem? Sum nanei lou jongleh-Hung in, hungdon nin! Hungin, nadeipen pen lengpitwi hihen, bongnoi hijongleh- abon'a manbei ahi. 
Ipi dinga tha na petheilou an chohna’a nasum’u mang cha a nahiuvem? Nangho dinga aphachom louva idia sum sumanga nahiuvem? Kasei ngaijun, chule apha chu neuvin. An phapen chu lung kipah tah’a naneh dingu ahi. 
Ka kom’a hung uvin, na kollu phatah in hin do’uvin. Ngaiyun, chutileh hinna namu dingu ahi. Keiman tonsot kitepna nangho toh kasem ding ahi. Keiman David kom'a kakitepna, lhadai ngailou lungsetna chu aboncha kapeh ding nahiuve. 
Keiman ama chu kathaneina miho lah a vetsah dinga, iti kamanchah ahi, heuvin. Keiman namtin lah a lamkai khatna kasem ahitai. 
Nangman nahet khahlou nam ho chu thu napeh ding, chule nahet phahlou mite chu nakom'a hunglhai lut’uva, nathusei angai dingu ahi, ajeh chu keima Pakai na Pathen, Israel te Mitheng Pa hin, loupia kasem nahi. 
Pakai chu namu thei pet hin hol’ in. Anai pet hin Ama chu kouvin. 
Agilou chun alampiho khel hen, chule thildih lou abolna lung gel dalha hen. Pakaijin ahepi theina dingin. Ama heng lama hung kihei’u hen. Ahije, na- Pathen henga hung kihei’uhen, ijeh inem itileh Ama’n lungset hepia angaidamna achan tei ding ahi. 
Nangho lung gel chu Keima dinga imacha hilou ahi, tin Pakaijin aseije. Chule ka lampiho hi nangho gah gelphah jou hoi ahipoi. 
Ajeh iham itileh vanho chu leiset sanga asanjoh tobanga, ka-lampi ho jong nangho lampi sanga sang jo’a ahi chule kagel ho hi nangho gel sanga sang joa ahi. 
Go twi le gel twi hi van hoa konna hung julha’ a hichun leiset chapnou dingin, leisetna aumden’in ahi. Amaho hin muchi- chivo ho chu akhan letsah in, loubol miho dingin ga asodoh in chule agilkel dingin an ahi. 
Ka Thujong chutima ahi. Keiman kasoldoh’a chule hichun ga asodoh jingin ahi. Keiman kalunggot banga asuh bulhit ding chule kasolna channa abulhit molso tei hi ding ahi. 
Nangho kipah le lungmonga nachen dingu ahi. Lhangsang ho le mol ho chun la ahinsah doh jeng dingu, chule loujaova thingphung hon akhutnu abeh dingu ahi. 
Ling phungho akenao munho’a chu thing sang tah tah ho hungkeh ding chule khao-guiho umna hoa chu len mui phung –eng jinjen chu hungkehdoh ding ahi. Hiche thilsoh ho hin Pakai min jana lentah ahin lhut ding, hiche ho jouse hi atonsotna athaneina le angailutna melchihna hung hi ta ding ahi.
