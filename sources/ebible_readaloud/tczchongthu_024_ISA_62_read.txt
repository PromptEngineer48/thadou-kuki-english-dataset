Isaiah.
62.
Keiman Zion kangailut jeh a, keima thip beh’a ka’umlouhel ding , kalungin Jerusalen alungvil jing jeh a kathip beh louhel ding ahi. Keiman chonphatna chu kho hungvah doh tobanga ahung vahlhah masangsea, amanu(Jerusalem) dinga hi tang louva katao jing ding ahi. 
Chitin-namtinin nachonphatna mu’uvin tin, vannoi vaipipo ho jong chu na loupina avet ngam lou dingu ahi. Chule nangma hi Pakai kamsung tah in minthah napeh ding ahi. 
Pakaijin nangma khutnin natuh intin, mijousen amutheina dinga -Pathen khutna loupina lallukhuh na khuhpeh ding ahi. 
‘Dalhah Khopi’ahilouleh ‘Gamdaigam’ tia nakikou kit talouhel ding ahi. Minthah naput ding chu: “Pathen Lunglhaina Tah” chule “Pathen Mounu” kiti ding ahitai, ajeh iham itileh Pakai chu na chunga along lhaijin, chule a mounu tia naseipeh ding ahi. 
Vo Jerusalem, khangthah chun aji adeilhennu kom'a akipehlut tobanga chu- nachateu amaho mong mong nangma heng’a hung kipelut dingu ahi. Chutengleh, moulangin mounu chunga akipa thanop tobanga chu, Pakai chu na chunga hungkipah thanom ding ahi. 
Vo Jerusalem, Keiman khongah ho chu nakulpi banghoa kapan satsah in ahi; amaho hi sun le jana taojom jing ding ahiuve. 
Jerusalem chu leiset kithang-atpi thei ahunghi kahsen, Pakaijin anatoh abulhit molso kahsen, Pakai chu kicholdo sah hih hel’uvin. 
Pakai chu Athahatna Jerusalem henga hung kihah sella: “Keiman nangho melmate khutna avel'a kapehdoh kit tah louhel ding nahiuve, ati. Gamdang mi na gamga hou le na lengpitwi hou lamang dinga gal nabol khum ding uva hungkon kit talouhel ding ahiuve. 
Louva tohga na kimu dingu, nang hon hichu naneh’uva Pathen na vahchoi dingu ahi.Hou’In mai lama nangma hotah in naheh lhah’u lengpi-twi chu nadon dingu ahitai. 
Kelkot pam lama gapotdoh’uvin! Kamite ahungkile kitna ding uvin lamlhongpi gongtoh’uvin! Lamlen chu sunam’uvin, songpi ho loidoh un, chitin-namtin nin ahinvet ding uvin ponlap khai sangun. 
Pakaijin hiche thu hi gamtin'a asoldohtai: Israel nam mite seipeh’uvin, Veuvin, na Huhhingpu chu ahunge. Ven, tohman kichoi tothon Ama ahunge. 
Amaho chu, “Athenga Mite”le “Thisanna Pakaijin Achohdohsa Mite”kiti ding ahiuve. Chule Jerusalem hi “Dei umtah Mun” le “Khopi Kinungsun kit talou ding” kiti ding ahitai.
