Isaiah.
63.
Edoma konna hunga, Bozrah khopia konna hung'ah, aponsil thisan beh hi koi hija ham? Alalna sangkhol chol puma, athahatna’a hungpa chu koi hija ham? Hiche hi Keima, Pakai huhhing'a naumna ding phongdohpa chu kahi!Hiche hi Keima Pakai, nangho huhdoh thei thaneipa chu kahi! 
Nangman lengpi-ga nachil khat tou hiya, ipi itia navon hi tia hi san ahidem? 
Keiman kachangseh’a lengpi-ga chu kachil ahin, eipanpi ding koima aumpoi.Ka lunghan jeh a ka melmate chu lengpiga kachil tobanga kaban chil jeng ahi tauve. Athisanu chu kavon ho chunga behden’a ahije. 
Ajeh iham itileh ka mite abol hesoh nauva konna lhatdohna’a, phu kalahna ding phat hunglhunga ahitai. 
Keiman amaho agimbol pa’uva konna pan hu dinga koimacha akikum lou hi kadatmo lheh in ahi. Hijeh a hi, keima tah mong kakikuma kabanjang thahatna amaho kahuhdoh kigot ahin, ka lunghanin eichoh thoudoh jenge. 
Keiman chitin-namtin ho hi ka lunghanna’ a ka chilgoi ahin, chule amaho lon le leuva tolla lhu lhop’uva, athisannu leisetna ahin vatlhah sah ding’u ahi. 
Keiman Pakai ngailutna lhadai ngailou chu kasei ding, athibol ho jouse jal'a hi Pakai chu ka vahchoi ding, akhotona le angailutna jal'a lungsetna anei chu, Israel dinga aphat behset jeh a hi kipah thanom ding kahi. 
Aman aseijin, “Ama ho hi Ka mite mong mong ahiuve. Ama ho hin keima eipedoh kit talou diu ahi.” Chule Ama ahuhhingpu’u ahung hitai. 
Gim hesoh athoh nauva Aman jong agim pin, Ama tah amaho huhdoh in apange. Angailutna le akhotona jallin, amaho alhatdamin, Ama’n amaho hi kum sunghoa ahin domkhang’in chule apole’in ahi. 
Hinla amaho hi Ama douna’a akisauvin, a Lhagao Theng alung phatmo sah’uvin, hijeh chun Ama amaho dingin melma akisoh in, amaho ahin doutai. 
Chuphatnin ama hon Mose in amite apuidoh lai phat’u, nikho chesaho chu ageldoh tauve. Chujeh in ama hon asam uvin: Twipi-sanna Israel te hin pui galkaipa chu hoiya uma ham? Mose achingpau chu anahi lou ham? Lhagao Theng hinsolpa Amite umpipa chu hoiya uma ham? 
Mose in a khut ajah a chu koiham athahatna hin vetsah’a chu? Amasang tah’uva twipi-san hop nia so’a, Ama tah minthanna atonsotna hin semdoh pa chu hoiya umham? 
Twi khanglen thuh tah noinung lampeh’a konna amite ho hin pui galkai’pa chu hoi lamba hitam? Ama ho hi Sakolchal til kilalou hoitah ho tobangin gamthip gam'a ahung kilhaitet’uva jong kipal louhel ahiuve. 
Gancha hotoh phaicham lhunga lung monga kilhonuva, Pakai Lhagaovin amaho chu akicholdo sah ahiiai. Pakai nangman namite chu napui jin, chua chun minthanna loupitah chu 
Pakai, vanna kon'in neihin vesuh’uvin: na chenna In thengtah le loupitah’a kon'in hin ven. Kachung uva neihin gelkhoh nau le nahatna neivetsah jiu chu hoilamba hitam? 
Thonlou hella nangma ka Pa’u nahi jinge! Abraham le Jacob’in einol diu hijongleh, Pakai, nangma ka Pa’u nahi jing nahlai ding ahi.Nangma akhang akhana achesa phat hoa ei-Lhatdampu’u chu nahi. 
Pakai, iti danna na lampia konna kihei mang dinga neiphal jengu hitam? Ipi dinga Nangma kagin louna ding uva louchalna lungthim neipeh’u hitam? Hung kilehei kitnin lang chule nei pan piuvin, ajeh iham itileh keiho na lhachate kahiuvin, nangman na deilhen phunga mite kahiuve. 
Chomcha kahsung bou ahi nalaije na mun theng sung namiten kalo nau phatsung hi, hinla tun ka melma teuvin eisugam hel tauve. 
Khatvei kom le keiho hi nangma’a hikhalouhel to kabang jitauvin, keiho hi nangma mite tia kihekhalouhel kabanguve.
