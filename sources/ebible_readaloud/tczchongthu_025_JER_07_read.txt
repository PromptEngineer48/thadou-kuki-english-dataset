Jeremiah.
7.
Hiche thu hi, Pakaijin Jeremiah henga aseijin, hitin ati. 
Kipat in, Pakai Hou-In lutna kotbul’a gadingin, mipite henga hiche thu hi gaphongjaltan. Vo Juda mite, Pakaiya hungkon thu hi ngaiyun. Pakai chibai boh dinga hilai mun’a hunglut jousen, nabon’un ngaiyuvin. 
Pakai Van Pathen, Israel Pathen chun hitin aseiye. Tuyeng injong, nahindolu leh nathilbolu aphalou jouse dalhauvin. Chutileh nangho na gamsung uva hi kachendensah ding nahiuve. 
Ahinlah, hilaimun’a Pakai Hou-In aumnai, nangho nahoidoh teidiu ahi, tia jou leh nal’a thusei ho kilheplhah sah hih un. Ama hon muntin’a, Pakai Hou-In hilaiya aum'e, Pakai Hou-In hilaiya aum'e, tin asei lele uvin ahi. 
Amavang, nang hon nalunggel’u leh nathilbolu aphalou jouse na dalhahuva, nangho kah’a khat leh khat thu adih’a nakitanpihuva ahileh, keiman nangho kahepiuva kakhoto ding nahiuve. 
Nanghon gamdang kholjin miho, chagate leh meithai ho nabolgenthei dauva, chuleh toltha thisan so nabol dauva chuleh semthu pathen ho nahou dauleh, 
Keiman napu napa teu kanapehsa hiche gamsunga hi nangho a-itih’a kachensah ding nahiuve. 
Nanghon, Pakai Hou-In hilaiya aum'e, eiho huhdoh’a um ihitauve, tia jouthu seile ho’a chun kingai hih un, panna bei ahi bouve. 
Hitia chu, nang hon naguh chatuva, tol nathauva, mitoh najonuva, Baal pathen houna gimnamtui nahluva chuleh pathen dang ho nung najuijing uva, 
Chuteng hilai mun’a keima angsunga nahung dinuva, kamin pan’a ‘Eiho huhdohihitauve’ natiuva, chuleh nang hon thet um tahtah ho nahiti boljing diu ham? 
Nangho mitvet’a hi, hiche kamin’a kiminvosa Hou-In hi, gucha michom ho kokhuh ahi, nati ngapu hitam? Tahbeh in hilai mun’a thilse kibol jouse keimat ah in abonchan kamusoh hel e, tin Pakaijin aseiye. 
Shiloh mun’a tun cheuvin, keima neihouna diuva kamin’a kalhendohsa mun chu ahi. Kami Israel te chonset jeh a achung uva ipi kabol hitam, gavetauvin ati. 
Nanghon chonset hijatpi nabolun, keiman tanglouhel ’a kaseipih vangun ngaisah in nanei pouve. Chuleh keiman nangho kakouvin neihoudeh pouve, tin Pakaijin aseiye. 
Hijeh a chu, keiman Shiloh ka suhmang banga, tua hi kamin’a nakisonpiu hiche Hou-In hi ka suhmang ding, chuleh napu napa teu kapehsa hiche mun hi ka suhmang ding ahi. 
Chuleh keiman na inkote Israel te chunga kabol banga, nangho kamitmua kon’a sohchang dinga kadelmang ding nahiuve. 
Vo Jeremiah, hiche mite hi taopih datan. Amaho din taodan lang chuleh kap jong kap hih in. Chuleh amaho din kaheng'a hung tao hih in, ajeh chu keiman kangaipih louding nahi. 
ama hon, Judah gam khopi ho leh Jerusalem khopi lailunga, anop nop abolu khu, nangman namu lou ham? 
Keima kalung analheh e! Vetan, chapang hon thing achom un, mipaten thil tona ding mei atih un, numeihon van lengnu asah u pathennu houna dingin changbong ameh un, changlhah asemuve. Chuleh keima lung natsah nadin ama hon pathen dang ho henga donthei thilto atohuvin, eipohnat sah uve. 
ama hon alung natsahuva alunghansah uva chu keima kahilou ham? tin Pakaijin aseiye. 
Hijeh chun Hatchungnung Pakaijin aseiye, Keiman hilaiya hi, mihem hihen, sahem hihen, thingphung ho leh loujaova lousoh gaho hijongleh, kalungsatna meikou chu kabuh lhah ding, chuleh meikou phalmit mohel banga akah ding ahi, ati. 
Pakai Van Pathen leh Israel Pathen chun hitin aseiye, pumgo thilto nabol uva chuleh kilhaina thilto chom chom nabol hou chu, kilah uvin lang nang hon nachaisoh uvin. 
Keiman napu napa teu Egypt gam’a kon’a kahin puidoh chun, amaho kom’a pumgo thilto leh kilhaina thilto kangehpoi. 
Keiman kangeh chu,’ kathu ngaiyun, chutileh keima nangho Pathen kahia, nangho kamite nahidiu ahi. Chuleh naphat theina diuvin, ijakai kathupeh bangin bol uvin, kati. 
Ahivangin ka miten ka thusei ngai nahsah in anei pouve. Ama hon lungthim setah leh louchaltah in, adei deiyin abol uvin, mache louvin anungche jouvin ahi. 
Napu napa teu vin Egypt gam ahin dalhah niuva pat tuni geiyin, keiman kalhacha themgaoho tanglouvin niseh in ahenguva kasol jingin ahi. 
Ahivangin ka miten ka thusei angaipouve, chuleh anakhanguva jah ding jong agelpouve. Louchal tah in aum un, apu apateu sangin thilse jo aboltauve. 
Hijat hi amaho seipehin, amavang na sei ngaideh pouvinte. Nangman gihsalnan amaho kou jong lechun, ama hon nahin houydeh pouvinte. 
Chuteng nangin amaho seipeh in, Hiche nam mite hi Pakai a Pathen'u thusei ngailoute ahiuvin, chuleh engbolna jong hilna-a kisanthei loute ahiuve. Alahuva thutah amangtan chuleh akamsungva jong thutah akijatapoi. 
Nalu kihettol inlang puldouvin, chuteng molsang laiya nachangin ga kaptan. Ajeh chu Pakaijin alunghan jeh a hiche nam mite hi apaidoh a adalhah ahitai. 
Ajeh chu, Judah miten kamitmu changin chonset aboltauve. Ama hon kamin a kiminvo Hou-In sunga thet umtah doilim akoiyun, muntheng asubohtauve, tin Pakaijin aseiye. 
ama hon milim houna doi-phung tampi, Ben-Hinnom phaicham munsanglai Topheth mun’a chun, asemdoh uvin, hichea chun ama hon achanuteu leh a chapa teu jong govam din atohdoh jengun ahiuve. Ahin hitobang thil hoise bolna din keiman thupeh kabolpon, chuleh kalung gel’a jong kagelkhapoi. 
Hijeh chun heuvin, anikho chu ahung lhunge, tin Pakaijin aseiye. Hiche munsanglai ho chu Topheth kititalou ding, chuleh Ben-Hinnom phaicham tiajong kihetalou ding, amavang kithagamna phaicham kitiding ahi. Ama hon Topheth mun’a chu mithi avui avuidiu ahi, kivuina ding mun onglouhel ’a mithi um ding ahi. 
Chuteng kamite athiho tahsa chu vachate leh gamsate neh leh chah hi ding, amaho gahoh jamding koimacha umlou ding ahitai. 
Chutia chu, keiman Jerusalem khopi lailunga kipa golnop kithong gin kasuh tangding ahi. Chuleh Judah khopiho’a jong kicheng dinga um mou leh moulang ho kipa thanop agin jouse kijatalou ding ahi. Ijeh inem itileh, agamsung chu ahom keuva kijam ding ahitai.
