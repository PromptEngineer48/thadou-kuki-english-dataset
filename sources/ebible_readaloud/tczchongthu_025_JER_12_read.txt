Jeremiah.
12.
Vo Pakai, adih’a thutan nangbou nahi. Hijeh chun, nakom’a kakiphinna thu sei-inge. Ipi dinga migiloute khangtou tou’uva, Chule joule nal a thilbol ho phatou touvu ham? 
Nangin amaho naphut det in; Hijeh chun ama hon ajung akho det un, chule akhang theiuve. Akam seh in namin apha jingun; Ahivangin, alungthim u nang’a kon'in agamla lheh e. 
Hijongleh, Vo Pakai nangman kei lungthim nahet ahi. Nangman keima neimuchen in, ka lunggel jong nei patep e. Amaho vang thadinga kikai kelngoi bangin, abonchauvin kaimang soh in, thadin gotsa koidoh jengin. 
Itihchan gamsung hi lunghem’a um ding ham? Gamsung mite gitlou jeh in, gamsa ho leh vachate jong gamsunga kon'in akithehmang sohtauve. Ama hon hiti jong hin aseiuve, “Eihon ipi boljong leuhen, Pakaijin bon muphalou” atiuve. 
Nangma mitoh nakenga nakilheitet’a bon nachol leh, nangman sakol iti na lheitet jou ding ham? Chule nangma chu, gamnom lai le a-ong laiya nakiselhuh theileh, Jordan vadung panga, gamnoiya iti nadin jou ding ham? 
Ajeh chu nasopi te leh nanginsung mite jong nangma douva pang ahiuve. Ama hon nang dounan kiphinna thu aseiyun, thading jenginjong agel uve. Hijeh chun, ama hon thu ngaitah tah seijonglehu, tahsan hihbeh in. 
Keiman ka goulo ding kamite ka dalhah’a, amaho chu donlouva kakoi ahitai. Keiman ka-ittah kamite chu, amelmateu khut a kapehdoh ahitai. 
Kalhentum ka miten eidouvun, gammang noiya keipi kisen bangin eikisenkhum’un: Hijeh chun, amaho hi keiman kahot lheh jeng e. 
Ka lhentum ka miten, muthong ho umchan athouve. Ahinla muthonghon amaho joh aumkimvel’u ahitai. Amaho negam ding leh nengim dingin gamsa ho gakoukhomun. 
Gamdang vaipohon ka lengpilei asuseuvin, kaloulai jouse jong akeng in achilthang tauve. Ka-ittah ka gamsung abon'in gamkeo leh nelgam asosahtauvin ahi. 
ama hon gamsung pumpi ahomkeo leh panna beiyin akoiyun, ahomkeova aumphat un, akanau ogin kajan, ahinla koiman gelkhoh in aneipoi. 
Molchung achailah ho jousea, amaho sumang ding galmi ho ahung kitollut tauve. Pakai chemjam chun, leiset ninglang khat a pat a langkhat geiya, namtin vaipi chu a bahgamtai. Koimacha asohcha umlou ding ahi. 
Ka miten suhlou chang atu-uvin, ahinlah ling le khao jeng a-atkhomui. Ama hon choltah in na atongun, ahivangin imacha aphatchompi pouve. Ama hon alho-lhun u ajumpi dingu, ajeh chu, Pakai lungsatna chu achung uva chu ahitai. 
Pakaijin hitin aseiye, “Keiman kami Israel te kapehsa gamsung sumanga, Israel heng le kom, nam gilou ho chu, gamsung’a kon’a thingphung ajung geiuva kibotdoh bang’a kabodoh ding ahi. Chuleh alah uva kon’a keiman Judah chu kaloidoh ding ahi. 
Chutah le keiman amaho chunga lainatna kaneikit ding, Amitakip chu ama ama goulo mun leh ama ama gamsung cheh’a kahin le puilut kitding ahi. 
Chule hitiding ahi, hiche mite hin, kamite jal’a kihilna thah aneiyuva, keima minpan’a ‘Hingjing Pakaijin’ tia akihahsel theiuva, keima eihouvu leh, ama hon kamite lah a mun anei thei dingu chule khangtou diu ahi. 
Amavang koi hileh, nam khat touvin ka thusei angailouleh, amaho chu thingphung kibodoh banga kabodoh a, ka suhmang ding ahi, kei Pakaijin kasei ahi,” ati.
