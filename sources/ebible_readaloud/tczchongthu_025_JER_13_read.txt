Jeremiah.
13.
Pakaijin kaheng’a hitin aseiye, “Chen lang taigah pon, tupat ponnem khat gachon lang, kigah in. Chule hichu twiya nasoplou ding ahi,” ati. 
Hijeh chun, Pakaijin athusah dungjuijin, kenjong taigah pon kagachon kakigahtai. 
Chujouvin Pakaijin kaheng'a thu ahinseikit in, 
Nataigah pon chu kichoiyin lang, Ephrates vadung a chen. Chukom’a songpi khikah’a chun nathil chu gaselmang tan ati. 
Hichun keiman, Pakai thupeh dungjuijin Ephrates vadunga kachen, kagasel mangtai. 
Chujou, nikho tamtah jouvin, Pakaijin kaheng'a thu ahinseikit in, “Ephrates vadunga chekit inlang na taigah pon kaselmang sah chu, gakilah kittan,” ati. 
Chuin keima Ephrates vadunga kachen, taigah pon kasel chu kagakhot doh kittai. Amavang, hiche taigah pon chu, amon in anase gam heltai. Taigah pon chu, imacha-a apanna ding aum tapoi. 
Chuin Pakaiya kon'in, kaheng'a thu ahunglhungin, 
“Hiche hi Pakai kamsoh chu ahi, keiman Judah leh Jerusalem mite kithang-at nachu, hitobanga hi kasuhset pihding ahi.” 
Hiche migiloute hin, ka thusei angaipouvin, louchal tah in achon un, ama ama cheh lung ngaichat abol'un, chule semthu pathen ahouvun ahi. Hijeh a chu, amaho hi taigah pon banga chu, phatchomna beihel ahi tauve. 
Ijeh inem itileh, taigah pon mihem taibuh a akiveiden banga, Judah leh Israel mite chu, keima chunga kiveiden jeng dinga kasem ahi, tin Pakaijin aseiye. Hitia chu, amaho kamite hiuva, keidinga loupina leh eikivahchoina thei hidiu ahin; Hinlah ama hon ka thusei angaidehji pouvin ahi. 
Hiche thu hi nangman amaho hetsah in, Pakai Israel Pathen'in hitin aseiye, “Hai jousea lengpitwi nathaldim sohkeidiu ahi,” ati. Chutengleh ama hon najah a, “Hai jousea lengpitwi thaldim sohkei ding chu, keihon kahetlouvu ham? “atidiu ahi. 
Chutengleh nangin amaho jah a, Pakaijin hitin aseiye, tin. “Ven hiche gamsung mite jouse: David laltouna-a tou lengho a pat thempuho, chule themgao ho, chule Jerusalem a chengho jouse, abon uva khamnathei gu-twi ka donkhamsah soh hel dingu ahi. 
Keiman amaho, khat le khat katato ding, mipate le chapa te jong katato ding ahi, tin Pakaijin aseiye. Hitia chu, amaho suhmang louna dinga, achung uva hepina le lainatsah pihna, kaneilouhel ding ahi, ati. 
Thu kasei ngaiyun lang, nanakhang’u hindo cheh’un. Kiletsah hoitho bol hih un. Ajeh chu Pakaijin thu aseitai. 
Phat ageisang masang'in, Pakai na Pathen chu loupi sah un. Aman muthim ahin lhunsah masang, molsang laiya nakipal lhuh masang. Muthim lhangkhal gimneitah akiheh sah’a, khovah ngaicha geova naum masangun. 
Nanghon hichea jong, Pakai thusei nangailou uleh, nakiletsah hoitho jal uva, keima kachanga kaka ding ahi. Keima mitlhi long chut chut a kaka ding; Ajeh chu Pakai mite abonchauva sohchanga kikaimang diu ahitai. 
Lengpa leh lengnu kom’a seipih un. “Na laltouna’a kon'in hung kumlha lhon'in, tollhanga toulhon'in. Ajeh chu, na loupina lallukhuh chu nalua kon’a kilamang ding ahitai,” ati. 
Negev gam’a khopi jouse kot akikhah sohtan, koima ahongdoh ding aumpoi. Judah mite jouse sohchanga kikaimang ta, Chuleh gamsung mijouse sohchanga kikaimang ahi tauve.

Nangin gol le pai dia nakisemsa ho chu, Pakaijin nangma chunga vaihom’a apansah tengleh, nangin ipi nasei ding ham? Numei naoso nat banga, nang dinga natna nasatah hi ding ahi. 
Nangman na lungsunga, ipi dia thil hicheng pihi kachunga hunglhung ham, tia nakidoh nomleh: Hiche hi, na chonset alet behseh jeh ahi. Hijeh a chu na ponsil jouse kibottel’a, hesoh genthei nasatah nathoh ahiye. 
Ven, Ethiopia min avun avouso akhel thei am? Chule lutnun achunga ajem-pol ho akheldoh thei ham? Achuti theile, thilse bolgunset nang hon jong thilpha nabol theidiu ahi. 
“Keiman nangho na boncha uva, nelgam huipin changvai amut mang banga kalecheh ding nahiuve. 
Hiche hi nangma chandol ahin, keiman nang dinga tedohsa-a kakoi ahi, tin Pakaijin aseiye. Ajeh chu, nangman keima neisumiltan, jouva kisemdoh pathen ho chu na tahsan tai. 
Hijeh a chu, keima mong mong in na ponsil kahodoh peh’a, najamo geiya kimu ding ahiye. 
Keiman, nathilbol thet umtah ho, mito najonthanhoina, kidah umtah na chonna ho, Chule loujao tinleh thinglhang’a doilimsemthu nahouna, kamusohkeiyin ahi. Ohe ada nahi, Vo Jerusalem! Na kisuhthengna dinga phat itihchan lutding ham? “ ati.
