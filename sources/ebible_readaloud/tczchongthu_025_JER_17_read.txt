Jeremiah.
17.
Pakaijin aseiye, “Judah te chonset hi, thih-mol hemtah khat a kijih’a, chule songmantam ning-hem khat a kijih’a, song bang’a tah alungsung uva chu kijihlut peh’a, chule amaicham phung ningho’a kigeilult peh ahi tauve’’ ati. 
Molvum tin leh thingphung tin’a, asemdoh u milim doi pathen maicham leh Ahsherah pathen'u melchihna kitunna min jouse, achateu kisen acheuvin chibai agaboh jiuve. 
Hijeh a chu, na gamsung uva nahaona jouseu leh nakholdoh jouseu, chuleh na maicham phung jouseu chu, na chonset jeh uva, namelmateu khut a kapehdoh diu nahi tauve. 
Chule nagoulo dinga kapehdohsa jong chu nangma khut a kon’a kitoldoh’a nalhaso ding ahi. Keiman namelmate khut a kapehdoh ding, nangman nahetkhah louhel gam’a sohchang a naum ding ahi. Ajeh chu, kalung hanna meikou bangin akou jengtan, a-itih’a kou jing ding ahitai. 
Pakaijin aseiye, “Pakaiya kon’a alungthim kiheimanga, mihem’a kisonga chuleh mihem thahat kisonpi ho chu gaosap changhen, ati. 
Hitobang mi chu, nelgam’a hamphung keh tobangbep ahiuvin, kinepna ding ima anei pouve. Amaho chu gamthip leigo lah a cheng hidiu, mihem chenna theilou chilei lah a cheng hidiu ahi. 
Amavang, koi hileh, Pakaiya kingaiya, chule akinepna leh akiskonna Pakai hijingpa chu anunnom ahi. 
Amaho chu luipanga thingphung keh tobangm twi lah a thuhtah’a ajung kholut’a hingjing tobang ahiuve. Hitobang thingphung chun, nipilai nisat ding leh gotwi lhahlou ding agingpoi. Aphatseh in ana ahingjingin chule aphatchan agadoh jingin ahi, ati. 
Mihem lungsung hi, adang jouse sanga phatlouna leh lheplhahna dimset a, sea ahitai. Mihem lungsung hekhen them ding koiham? 
Amavang, kei Pakaijin miho lunggil jouse kakhol toh jin, achonna u dungjuijin kape cheh jin ahi. 
Adihlouva sum mkua haodohpa chu, athah louhel midang twi oppeh tobang ahi. Ama chu ahin hoilai tah’a, ahaona ho hung'ah mang ding; Achaina le, achunga vaichatna leh ngolna hung chu ding ahi. 
Amavang, na laltouna tonsot’a um jing chu, sangtah le loupitah ahin, keihon nangma chibai kaboh jing uve. 
Vo Pakai, Israel mite kinepna! Nanga kon’a kiheimanga nadalha ho abon'uva kijumso diu ahi. Ajeh chu, ama hon Pakai anungsun’uva, hinna twinah jong adalhah tah jeh’un, amaho leivui lah a kivui diu ahitai. 
Vo Pakai, keima neidam sah in, chutile damtheng tange, neihuhhing in chutileh hinna tahbeh kachan ding ahi. Chuteng keiman nang jeng bou kavahchoi ding nahi. 
Vetan, mihon ei-enghouvun, kajah a “Pakaiya kon na phondoh ho chu iti hitam? Ibol’a nathusei ho guilhung lou ham?’’ atiuve. 
Pakai, namite chingtup’a neipansahna katong jing e. Namite chunga thohgimna alhun ding kadeipoi. Kakamsung sohsa ka thusei ho abon’a nahetsoh ahi. 
Pakai, kichat tijat umtah in neibol hih in! Ajeh chu nikhophalou hunglhun nia, kakiselna leh kakinepna nangbou nahi. 
Keima bolsea pangho chunga jum le ja lhunsah in, Amavang keiman jum le ja kathoh ding ho’a kon'in neihuhdoh in. Amaho chung joh’a tijat na leh manthahna, alenia nasajon lhunsah in. 
Pakaijin kajah a hitin aseiye, “Chenlang Jerusalem khopi kelkot phung jousea gadingin. Amasan Judah lengte lut leh potna kelkot’a gadingin; chujoule kelkot dang ho jong gakhol suh in.’’ 
Miho jouse henga seiyin, “Judah lengte leh Jerusalem a cheng Judah mite jouse, hiche hi, Pakai thusei ahi, ngaiyun.’’ 
Pakaijin hitin aseiye, “Nahinkho diuvin kigingphauvin, kasei hi ngaiyun. Sabbath nikhon Jerusalem kelkot ho’a, na sumkol veinau thil ho pohlut leh pohdoh bol hih un. 
Sabbath nikho’n nangma insung cheh’a jong, natong hih un. Amavang, napu napa teu henga thupeh kapehsa bangin, Sabbath nikho chu atheng in nit jingun.’’ 
Ahinlah, ama hon ka thusei hi angaipouve. Louchal tah in aum uvin, kathupeh jouse donlouvin akoi uve. 
Pakaijin hitin aseiye, “Amavang, nang hon kathupeh chengse najui uva, Sabbath nikho chu theng tah’a nanit dingu ahi. Hiche nikho leh kelkot dunga thil napoh le lou diu, chule na natohlou diu ahi. 
Chutengleh, lengho leh achapa teu kelkot hoa chu lutle potle jing diu, chule leng David laltouna chu alah uva um jing ding ahi. Lengho leh achapa teu chu kangtalai leh sakol chunga tou’uva, Judah mipite toh kilhonlut le jing diu ahitai. 
Chutah le, Jerusalem kimvel’a kon, Judah gamsung leh Benjamin gamsung khopi jousea kon, thinglhang gam’a kon, chule Negep gam’a kon'in, mipi tamtah hung kikhom uvin tin, ama hon govam thilto, kilhaina gamtha, chang le bego thilto chule thangvahna thilto tampi, Pakai Hou-In sung a ahinpohlut diu ahi. 
Amavang, nang hon ka thusei na ngaidauva, chule Sabbath nikho chu atheng’a nanit lou’uleh; Chule Sabbath nikho’a jong, Jerusalem kelkot dunga nikho dang banga, nathil hou napohlut leuleh; Veuvin, keiman kelkot phung dunga mei kahal ding, hichua leng inpiho geiya akahlhah ding, koiman aphelmit joulouhel ding ahi,’’ati.
