Jeremiah.
18.
Pakaijin Jeremiah heng’a hitin aseiye, 
“Beldengpa in-ah khun gachesuh in, chukom’a chu keiman najah a thu kaseiding ahi,’’ ati. 
Chuin, Pakaijin asei bang chun, keima beldengpan kang apeija pontho tah’a bel asempet katoh in ahi. 
Aman abel denchu, adeibang tah’a asodoh louteng, bellei chun asugoiyin, thilchom in asemdoh jitai. 
Pakaijin kaheng'a hitin aseiye, 
Vo Israel, beldengpan leingan chu adei deiya asemdohji banga, keiman nangho hi kabolmo nahiuvem? Bellei chu beldengpa thu thua asembang a, nangho jong kakhut a um nahiuve. 
Keiman namkhat ham ahilou le lenggam khat ham chu, ajung bodoh’a suhmang ding kati teng, 
Hiche nam mite chun, agitlounauva kon’a lung ahin heikituva, ahung kilehei kit ule; Keiman achung uva bol dinga kagelhuhsa thilsea kon’a, kalung kakhel kitding ahi. 
Alangkhat a, namkhat ham ahilou le lenggam khat ham chu, kasemphat a kaphudet ding ahi tia, ka phondohsa ahitai. 
Ahivanga, hiche mite chun, kamitmua thilse leh thil phalou abol uva, ka thusei angailou ule, kalung kakhelkit jing ding, phatthei kabohlouhel diu ahi, ati. 
Hitijeh chun, tun chenlang, Judah te leh Jerusalem mite gaseipeh in: “Pakaijin aseiye, Nangho douna kathilgon, nachung uva talen kamatsah ding koncha ahitai. Hijeh chun nangho ami takip chu, nagitlounauva kon'in hung kilehei kit uvin chuleh kisemphauvin,’’ati. 
Ahinlah ama hon adonbut uvin, “Hichu panna bei naphat mansah ahi bouve. Keiho kalungtah nom ule lungtah ungting, thilse kadei ule kadei dei boltah tangu, ipi khoh am?’’ atiuve. 
Hijeh chun Pakaijin hitin aseiye, Namtin vaipi lah a jong gadong leuvin. Hitobang thu hi ahekha koi um am? Kachanu deitah Israel in thil kidah um tahtah aboltai. 
Lebanon molsang vum’a buhbang beikha a, chule gamla tah a hiche molsang ho’a kon’a hung longdoh twidapjen vadung ho kang kha am? 
Ahinlah, ka miten kitahna anei pouve. Ama hon keima einungsun’un, panna bei semthu doilim hounan gimnamtui ahaltauve. Achena diu lamlen chu kiselhuhnan aneiyun, lampi dihlouva alamlha gam tauve. 
Hijeh a chu, agamsung u akeova kijam ding ahi. Ajeh chu, ama hon thil kidah umtah tampi aboltauve. Agamsungu jotpa tapou tija lihdiu, chule alu-u athinkhum diu ahi. 
Nisolam khohuiyin leivui vanpil thena anei banga, keiman kamite chu, amelmateu masanga, kathecheh gamdiu ahi. Chule hesoh genthei, hamsetna ato niuleh, keiman kanung kangatding, kapanhu louhel diu ahi,’’ati. 
Hichun ama hon aseiyun, “hungun, Jeremiah hi alonaje ding khat gongu hite. Eiho eihil uva eiphoh diu thempu ho, miching ho chule themgao ho tampi ineinauve. Eihon hiche pa hi ingaichapouve, athusei ho ngapih pou hitin, ama douna thujoh haseijin seiyu hite,’’ atiuve. 
Vo Pakai, neikhoh sah inlang nei panpin, Kamelma ten kachunga eidounau thu asei chengseu hi, neihet peh in, katao ahi. 
Thilpha chu thilsea kilethuh angai mong ham? ama hon kathina ding kokhuh alaiyun, kenla amaho dingin na-angsunga taona kaneiyun; Achunguva nalunghan achuh lou nadin kappum in kataopeh in ahi. Nangin hingeldoh tei teiyin. 
Hijeh chun, achateu kel thohsah inlang, Amaho abonchauvin chemjam in lousah in. Ajinuteu cha neilouvin abonchauvin meithai soh uhen, Tehse hou natna in thiu hen, chule gollhang ho galmun’a chemjam in satgam uhen. 
Hetman louvin, amaho chu gal miten lhunkhum uhen, A-injouseuva ka le mao ogin kijasohkei hen. Ajeh chu ama hon keima thina ding kokhuh alaiyun, chule kalam lhahna-a thang akam leleuve. 
Vo Pakai, kei thana dinga athilgon’u abonchan nangman nahei. Athilse bol’u chulnampih hih in, chule achonset nau jong ngaidam hihbeh in. Nalunghan nachun amaho hi bolgim inlang, na-angsunga thisah jengin, kati ahi.
