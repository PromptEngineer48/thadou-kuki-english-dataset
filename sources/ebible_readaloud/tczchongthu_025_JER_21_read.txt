Jeremiah.
21.
Malkijah chapa Pachhur leh Maaseih chapa thempupa Zephaniah chu, Zedekiah lengpan, Jeremiah themgaopa toh kimuto dinga ahinsol pet chun, Pakaijin Jeremiah henga thu ahin seitai. 
ama hon, Jeremiah jah a, “Nangin keiho din, Pakai kom’a neitaopeh uvin; Babylon lengpa Nebuchadnezzar in kachung uva gal eibol khumtauve. Pakaijin alungset longlou chun keiho eilungset uhin, chule bolmo kidang nasatah bol intin, Aman Babylon lengpa Nebuchadnezzar chu bailam tah’a akinungtol sah ding ahi,’’ atiuve. 
Hichun Jeremiah in adonbut in, “Zedekiah lengpa kom’a kilekit’uvin lang, gaseipeh un.’’ 
Pakai, Israel Pathen chun, hitin aseiye, Vetan, Babylon lengpa leh cheldia mite douna dinga, nakhut uva nachoiyu gal manchah ho abon’a panna bei leh thaneilou kasosah ding: Keiman namelmateu chu khopi lailung tah’a kahinpuilut diu ahi. 
Chule keima mong mong in lunghang tah le thahat tah’a, kabantha kalhan ding, nangho douna gal kabolkhum diu nahiuve. Ajeh chu nang hon keima nasatah a neiphin doh u ahitai. 
Keiman hiche khopi hi, gamna hise kalha khum ding, mihem hihen chule gancha hijongleh kasuhgam hel ding ahi. 
Chujou tengleh, keiman Zedekiah lengpa, alhachaho leh asohte, chule khopi sunga chengho gamna hise leh chemjam’a chule kel’a hingdoh chengse chu Babylon lengpa Nebuchadnezzar khut leh melmate khut a kapehdoh ding ahiuve. Aman dip damna leh khotona beihel’a amaho se chu chemjam’a asatgam diu ahi,’’ ati. 
Mipite henga naseiding, Pakaijin hitin aseiye, “Keiman nakilhen diuvin namasang uva, thilni – Hinna lampi leh thina lampi kakoipeh gel e. 
Koi hileh, khopi dalha louva, umden ho vang chu, chemjam ham, natna hise ham, chule kel jeh ham’a thi ding ahiuve. Amavang, khopi musea, Babylon mite henga kipelut chu hingdoh diu ahi. 
Ijeh inem itile, keiman hiche khopi hi, achung a apha hilouva hamsetna lhunsah dinga kakoi ahitai. Hiche khopi hi Babylon lengpa kipedoh ding, aman vut asosah ding ahi, tin Pakaijin aseiye. 
Judah lengpa insung jah a, Pakaijin hitin aseiye, ngaiyuvin, gatipeh in. 
Pakaijin, David insung jah a, hitin aseiye, jingkah seh in, thudih chu adih in tanpih uvin, chule amaho chu mibolgentheipa khut a kon'in huhdoh uvin. achutiloule, nagitlounau jal'a chu, kalung hanna meikong banga hung kongdoh ding, koiman aphelmit joulou ding ahi. 
Chule keima tah in, Jerusalem douna gal kabol khum ding ahi. Ama hon, koiham eiho eihung tongkha diuva, chule koiham hiche kulpi hin sujal dinga, atiuvin angong avaiput leuvin ahi, ati. 
Hijeh a chu, keima mong mongin, amaho chonna dungjuiya talen kamatsah ding ahi, tin Pakaijin aseiye. Chule keiman gammang lah mei kahal ding, hichun akimvel a umho abon’a akahvam ding ahi,” ati.
