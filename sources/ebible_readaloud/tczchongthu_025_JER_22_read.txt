Jeremiah.
22.
Pakaijin hitin aseiye, “Nangma Judah lengpa in-ah chesuh inlang, hiche thu hi gaseipeh tan,’’ 
Juda lengpa, David laltouna toupa, ngaiyin, chule namite leh nasohten jong ngaicheh’uhen. 
Pakaijin hitin aseiye, Thu adih in tanpih uvin chule thil adih in bol uvin. Athil kichompih ho chu panhu’uvin lang, amaho chu akibol ganthei nauva kon'in huhdoh uvin. Chule nagitlounau chu dalhauvin. Gamchom mileh kholjin miho bolse hih un, chule chagate leh meithai ho chu pumhat in boldauvin. Chule themmona bei mite chunga thisan sodauvin. 
Nanghon kathu nangaiyuleh, hiche mun’a hi, David laltouna-a, David son le pah ho toujing ding ahi. Chutia chu, lengho leh alhachac ho chule asohte, kangtalai leh sakol a tou uva, leng inpi kelkot a chu, lutle potle abol diu ahi. 
Amavang, nang hon ka thusei hi donlouva nakoi vang ule, keima tah kamin’a kakitepna kasem ahi; Hiche mun hi ahomkeo leh keohel’a kalha ding ahi, tin Pakaijin aseiye. 
Pakaijin, Judah leng inpi thua hitin aseiye, “Keidingin nangma hi, Gilead lhang leh Lebanon gam hoitah banga chule mihem chennalou gam kasosah ding nahi. 
Keiman nangma sumang ding mi kakou ding, ama hon ama cheh manchah ahin kichoi uva, nasuh gamdiu ahi. Ama hon, Cedar thing hoilai ho aphuh uva, meiya ahalvam diu ahi. 
Gam chom chom’a kon hung kholjin namtin vaipin, hiche khopi hinjotpa jousen, ‘Ibol’a Pakaijin hiche khopi lentah hi ahiti suhmang jeng hitam? tia amaho leh amaho kihoudiu ahi. 
Chutengleh, “ama hon Pakai a Pathen’u toh akitepnau asuhkeh jeh uva, kisumang ahiuve,’ ti ahetdoh diu ahi. 
Lengpa din kadauvin, lunghempi dauvin, ama chu athi ahitai. Amavang, sohchanga kikaimangpa chu kapiuvin lang, apul douvun. Ajeh chu, aman agamsung ahung mukit louhel ding ahitai. 
Hiche thu hi, apa Josiah khel’a leng chang Jehoahaz chungthu asei ahi. ama chu gal-hinga kikaimang ahin, agam a hung kilekit louding ahi. 
Ama chu migam’a thiden ding, agamsung anungmu louhel ding ahi. 
Pakaijin hitin aseiye, ‘Ohe ada ahi, lamdih lou tah’a in kisahpa! Thudih louva inpi kehlen’a, aheng akom tohman pelouva goipa, chule pumhat a kulpi kisempa ! 
Hiche pa hin, ‘Keiman inlentah khat kisah inge, indan tampi sem ing katin chule bangkot tampi Cedar thing in sem inge. Hichu rong sen in hoitah in loi ing kate,’’ ati. 
amavang, Cedar thinga leng inpi nomtah leh loupitah kisahdoh jeh a, leng vaipoa kipang ahipoi. Napa Josiah chu neh le don aning lhing set in; ama chu thudihtah ahin, ijakai adih in abol’e. Hijeh chun, Pathen phattheiboh achange. 
Aman vaicha gentheite apanpin, dihtah in vai ahom e. chule ama din ima jouse aphatouvin akhangtouve. Hiche hi hilouham, keima toh kivopjing, kitichu,’’ tin Pakaijin aseiye. 
Nanginvang, namit teni leh nalungthim hi, nangma changseh ding le alam louva phatchomna mun'a ding bouvin naming in; monabei nathat in, vaicha gentheite nabol gentheijin, chule hepina umlouvin vai nahom e. 
Hijeh chun, Josaih chapa Jehoiakim chung thu hi, Pakaijin hitin aseiye. “Amapa hi, mihon athini le along a-oplou diu, vo sopipa vo golpa tiajong koima kaplou ding ahi. Amite jenginjong, vo Lengpa, kapu tia akapi umlou ding ahiuve. 
Athi long chu, sangan thisa kivui banga, Jerusalem khopia kon’a kikaidoh’a, kelkot polam’a gakivui ding ahi. 
Lebanon’a naloi ho din kapin, chule amaho ding chu Bashan mun’a othong leuvin gasamin. Ab’arim mun’a amaho chu gaholtan, chule amaho din kapin, amaho chu abon'un akisugam tauve. 
Nangma nakhantoupet in kana hil in, Ahinlah, nangman ngaiseh in naneipoi. Hitia hi, naneolaiya pat a ka thusei ngailouva hung umpeh chu nahi. 
Tua hi, khopi huiyin napankhompi jouse asumang ding chule naloi nagol ho abon’a sohchanga kikaimang ding ahiuve. Chutah le nagitlouna ho jal’a, nangma najah chading chule nakisuh lungdong ding ahi. 
Lebanon gam Cedar thinga kisa leng inpi hoitah sung’a busah chu anom e. Hijongleh, numei naosonat tobanga na thohgim mna ding hesoh gentheina na chunga hunglhung ding ahi. 
Pakaijin aseiye, ‘Keima kahing jinge. Juda lengpa Jehoiakim chapa Jehoiachin, keiman nagma kadalhah ding nahi. Keidinga nangma hi kajitlam’a kibut khutjem nahivangin, kasutlhah ding nahi. 
Nanghi, thadinga nahol namelmate khut a, chule nakichat mama jeng Nebuchadnezzar leh asepai te khut a kapehdoh nahitai. 
Nang le nanu jong hiche gamsung’a kon’a kanodoh ding, chule nangma hiche gamsunga nathi louding, migam danga nathiden ding ahi. 
Chule nagam’a nahung kilekit tah louding ahi. 
Ibol’a Jehoiachin kitipa hi, belkehsa panna neilou banga um hitam? Ibol’a ama leh achate jouse mi gamdanga soh’a kinomang u hitam? 
Vo leiset, vo gam leiset, Pakai thusei hi ngaiyun! 
Pakaijin hitin aseiye, Jehoiachin kitipa hi, son leh pah neilou, ahinkho sunga ima boldohlou mipa ahi, tin sunlut un. Ajeh chu achate atute khatcha beh jong, David laltouna touva, Judah gamsung a vaihom ding umlouding ahi.
