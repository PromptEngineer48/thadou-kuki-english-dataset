Jeremiah.
23.
Ohe ada nahiuve, kamite chunga vaihomte; ama hon kakelngoite athecheh un chule asumangun, agelkhoh pouve. 
Pakai Israel Pathen chun hitin aseiye, ‘Nanghon kamite nachingtup pouve, nathe cheh’un chule nadelmang gam tauve. Nathilse bol jeh uva, keiman nachung uva talen kamatsah diu nahiuve, tin Pakaijin aseiye. 
Amavang, kamite ahingdoh amoh chengse chu, kathe chehna gamsung hoa kon’a kamite kachop khomding, amaho chu agamsung uva keiman kahin lepuikit diu ahi. Chule amaho phatthei chang uva, pung jal dingu ahiuve. 
Chutengleh, amaho kelngoi chinbanga chingtup ding mikhat kalhenpih ding ahi. Hita chu ka miten kichat tijat atokit loudiu ahi, kei Pakaijin kasei ahitai. 
Pakaijin aseiye, ‘David guiya kon’a chilhah theng khat katun doh nading nikho chu ahunglhung e. Ama chun chingthei tah’a lengvai ahin pohding, chule gamsung pumpia dihtah’a thil abol ding ahi. 
Ama chu, ‘Pakai eiho themchanna pa kiti ding ahi. Hiche nikho chule, Judah te huhdoh’a um ding, chule Israel te lungmonga khosa ding ahiuve. 
‘Phat ahung lhunge, mihon akihahsel teng ule, ‘Pakai hingjing, Israel te Egypt a kon’a hinpui dohpa ‘tia asei tahlou diu ahi,’ tin Pakaijin aseiye. 
Miho chun, ‘Pakai hingjing, Israel te athechehna gamho’a kon’a, chule sahlam gam’a kon’a. hinlepuikit pa,’tia kamin aseijoh diu ahi. Hitia chu amaho gamsunga cheng diu ahi,’ ati. 
Thujou jeng sei themgao lhemho jallin, kalung ana behseh in, kagu kachang geiyin akithinge. Pakaijin amaho douna asei athutheng ho kajan, hichun keima jukham bangin kalon le le jenge. 
Ijeh inem itile, gamsung hi huh le jon adim in, Pakai gaosap achutai. Gam leiset alunghem tan, gamlah a hamhing jouse agoplha gamtai. Ajeh chu ama hon athaneinau chu thilse bol nopnan aneitauve. 
Thempu ho leh themgao hon Pathen hephata louvin, gitlouna adimset tauve. Aphatlounau hi, keima insung jeng’a jong kamudoh tai, tin Pakaijin aseiye. 
Hijeh chun, amaho lamlhahna alampiu chunalding, chule kho hungthim ding ahi. Ijeh inem itile, keiman achung uva hahsatna kalhun sah’a, talen kamatsah ding phat chu ahung lhunge, tin Pakaijin aseiye. 
Keiman Samaria themgao ho lah a chonset khohtah kamui. Ama hon, Ba-al min'in gaothu aseiyun, kamite apuimang gam tauve. 
Thilse bolna lam’a alamkaiyun ahiuve. Ven, amitakip gitlouna dalha-a, chule setna nungsun aum pouve. Amaho hi, Sodom leh Gommorah setna tobang ahi tauve. 
Hichun, thaneipen Pakaijin hitin aseiye. “Keiman amaho hi namtul jungkha-a kavah ding chule thina gutwi kadonsah ding ahiuve. Ajeh chu amaho’a kon'in gamsung’a Pathen nahsah louna adimsohtai,’’ ati. 
Thaneipen Pakaijin hitin aseiye, “Themgao ho gaothu sei chu ngai hih un, ama hon panna beiya nakinep sah’u ahi bouve. Amaho lunggel gel aseiyun, Pakaiya kon’a aseidoh’u aumpoi. 
ama hon Pakai thusei nahsah louva koiho kom’a chun, “Olmo hih un, nangho ima tipouvin nate” atiuvin ahi. Ama hon louchal tah in abolnom nom abol uvin, miho jah a chun, “Na chunguva thilse lhungponte’’ atiuvin ahi. 
Hiche themgao ho hi, Pakaijin aumpi jing’a, chule Pakai thusei ajah mongu hinam? Amaho lah a koipen khu, Pakai thusei ngaikhoh um’uvem ? 
Ven, Pakai lunghanna chu khopi hui le go kitho’a, chule chimpei kipeiya, michonsete luchang asemmang tobang hi ding ahi. 
Pakaijin alungsatna chu, agel banga aboldoh kahsea, alunghan dailha deh louhel ding ahi. Hichu ninunung tengleh nang hon kicheh tah’a nahetdiu ahi. 
Hiche themgao ho hi, keiman la kasolpon, ahivangin, amaho alhei lhei jeng uve. Chule akom’uva thu jong kaseipon, hijongleh ama hon gaothu asei sei jeng uvin ahi. 
Amaho chu, ka-angsunga um jing hileu, ka thusei angaiyuva hichu miho kom’a aphonjal diu ahi. Chutile ama hon kamite chu, achekhelnau lampi’a konle athilse bolnauva kon’a ahin lepuikit diu ahi. 
Keima nailam cha-a um jing Pathen kahi lou ham? Keima munjousea um jing Pathen kahi, ti Pakaijin aseiye. 
Koiham keiya kon’a kisel mang thei dinga? Keima van leh leiset munjousea um jing kahi,’’ tin Pakaijin aseiye. 
Hiche themgao hon, asemthu-u gaothu aseijingu hi, keiman kahetai. Ama hon miho jah a, “Pakaijin kamangin thu eiseipeh e, ngaiyuvin,’’ atiuvin, kamin'in jouthu aseileuvin ahi. 
Itih chan chu, ama hon asemthu-u gaothu mangcha-a kamite ajoulhep uva, apuimang diu ham ? 
Apu apateuvin keima eidalhah uva Baal ahou bangun, ama hon semthu mang aseikhumun, kamite eisuhmil sah ding agel uve. 
Themgao lhem hon amang semthu-u chu sei sei uhen. Ahinlah, keima kamcheng ja-a keima themgao hon, kitah tah in kathu chu seiphong’uhen. Vetan, changpol leh chang ga akibahna aumpoi, ati. 
Kathu chu meikong hattah tobang hilou ham? Chule songpi ahal hel a vogoi thei sehpi tobang hilou ham? tin Pakaijin aseiye. 
Hijeh chun Pakaijin aseiye, “Keiman hichu jou le nal’a gaothu seiho, chule Pakaiya kon’a thuguh mu a kiseiho, kadou ahi,’’ ati. 
Jouthu seiya lei kilehpel ho, Pakaiya kon thu kaseiyu ahi tia, keima min pom’a thusei ho, keiman kadou ahi. 
Hiche themgao lhem ho hi, keiman hatah in kadouve. Ama hon mang asemthu uvin, gaothu aseiyun, kamite hi chonsetna a apuilut sohtauvin ahi. Ahivangin, keiman amaho hi kasolpoi. Chule amaho heng a thu jong kasei khapoi, tin Pakaijin aseiye. 
Hiche mite khattou hon, themgao khattou ham, chule thempu khattou ham chun, nahin doh’uva, “Pakaijin apohgih thu ipi asei am?’’ atiule, nangin amaho jah a, “Pakai pohgih chu nangho nahiuve. Hijeh a chu Pakaijin nangho hi napaidoh diu ahitai, tin Pakaijin aseiye,’’ tia naseipeh ding ahi. 
Chule themgao khat ham, thempu khat ham, chule mite lah a khatpen ham in, “Pakaijin kaheng'a thu aseiye, ti mi aum leh, ama leh a insung mite abon’a keiman talen kamatsah ding ahi,’’ ati. 
Hisanga chu, nangho khat le khat nakidoh touva, Pakaijin ipi aseiyam? Chule Pakaijin iti adonbut uvem? nati joh dingu ahi. 
Amavang, nang hon ‘Pakaiya kon Pakai pohgih’ ti thucheng hi naman louhel diu ahi. Ajeh chu mihon, amaho dei dei bol nopnan amangun, Pathen thu chu aleheisoh gamtauvin ahi. 
Nangman themgao ho chu hiti hin dongin, “Pakaijin iti nadonbut uvem? Chule Pakaijin ipi aseiyam?’’ natiding ahi. 
Ijemtia ama hon “Kaseiyu hi Pakaiya kon ahi” atiuleh; Nangin seipeh jengin, “Pakaijin aseiye, keiman neiminphah lou diuva kagih vangun, nang hon,” hiche hi Pakaiya kon ahi, nati tei teiyun; 
Hijeh a chu, keiman nangho kageldoh kit louhel diu, napu napa teu kapehsa hiche khopi jong ka suhmang ding, chule nangho jong kachop kom’a kadelmang diu nahiuve, ati. 
Chule keiman nachung uva, tonsot kumkhoa jum le ja kathoh sah a, a-itih’a vetda nachannau beilou ding ahi, tin Pakaijin aseiye, ati.
