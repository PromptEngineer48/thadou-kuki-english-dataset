Jeremiah.
28.
Judah lengpa Zedekiah leng chan kalkumli lhin kum, alha-nga lhin chun, nikhat Gibeon khomi Azzur chapa Hananiah chun, Pakai Hou-In ah, thempu ho leh mipi angsung’a, keima jahtah in, hitin aseiye. 
Hatchungnung Pakai, Israel Pathen chun hitin aseiye. Keiman, angonguva nakihennau Babylon lengpa namkol chu kaheboh’a kalah doh ding ahitai. 
Keiman kumni sung’a, Nebuchadnezzar lengpan Babylon gam’a anapoh mang, Hou-In sung gouthil ho jouse, kahin le pohlut kitding ahi. 
Chule Babylon gam’a, sohchang’a kikaimang Judah lengpa Jehoiakim chapa Jehoiachin leh midang ho jouse jong, keiman kahin le puilut kitding ahi.Keiman Babylon lengpa namkol nangonguva kihen chu kaheboh ding ahi, tin Pakaijin aseiye,’ati. 
Hichun Jeremiah themgao pan jong, Thempu ho leh mipi ho angsung’a chun, Hananiah chu adonbut in. 
Hitin aseiye, “Amen, nasei bang chun guilhung hen! Pakaijin nagaothu seiho chu abonchan, na sei bang banginboltei hen! Babylon gam’a sohchang a kikaimang ho leh Hou-In sunga kon’a kipomang goulu ho, abonchan hiche mun hinnung lhut teitei hen! 
Amavang nangma jah ding leh mipite jah dinga, tunia keiman hiche thu kasei hi, phaten ngaiyin. 
Nang le kei masanga themgao hon, khanglui laiya pat, namtin vaipi douna gaothu anaseiyuvin, gal um ding, kellhah ding, gamna hise chom chom in miho gihna ananeiyuvin ahi. 
Chuti ahijeh chun, Themgao khat in chamna leh mkonna gaothu asei chu, mutheiya ahung guilhun tengleh bou, amapa chu Pakaiya kon ahi mong e, iti eihon ihetbep dingu ahi, ati. 
Hichun Hananiah themgao pan Jeremiah ngong’a kihen namkol chu, aladoh in aheh bongtai. 
Chuin Hannaniah jong chun, mipite jah dingin, avelin thu aseijin, ‘Pakaijin hitin aseiye: Keiman namtin vaipi sohchanna angonguva kihen, Babylon lengpa Nebuchadnezzar namkol chu, kumni sunga kalahdoh a, kaheboh ding ahi, ati. Hichun Jeremiah themgao chun Hou-In adalhan achetai. 
Hananiah themgaopa toh akidinte jou chun, Pakaijin Jeremiah henga thu ahinsei kittai. 
Chenlang Hananiah chu gaseipehtan, “Pakaijin hitin aseiye, nangman thing nam-kol in nakheltai. 
Ijeh inem itile, Hatchungnung Pakai Israel Pathen'in hitin aseiye: Keiman namtin vaipi ngong’a thih namkol kakoipih’a, amaho chu Babylon lengpa Nebuchadnezzar soh’a pangdin ahi. Chule keiman ijakai ama thunoiya kakoi ding,gamsa ho geiya ama kapeh doh ahi, tin seipeh in, ati. 
Chuin Jeremiah themgao chun, Hananiah jah a hitin aseiye. “Phaten ngaiyin, Hananiah! Pakaijin nangma nasol ahipoi. Ahivangin, tun mipi ten jouva gaothu nasei hi, atahsan uve. 
Hijeh Chun Pakaijin hitin aseiye. Nangma nathi tei ding ahi. Nangman Pakai doumah nabol jeh a, tukum sung’a nathi tei ding ahi, ati. 
Hichun, lhani jouvin Hananiah themgaopa chu athitai.
