Jeremiah.
44.
Egypt sahlam gamsunga Migdol, Tahpanhes, Memphis khopi ho leh lhanglam gam’a cheng Judah miho thudol’a, Pakaijin Jeremiah henga thu ahinseiye. 
“Thaneipen Pakai Israel Pathen'in hitin aseiye,” Jerusalem leh Judah gamsung khopi jouse chunga manthahna kalhunsah chu, nang hon namittah uva namu-u ahitai. Tugeiyin ahomkeovin aum un, koima achengpoi. 
ama hon thilse tintang abol uvin, keima eiphin lunghang uve. Gimnamtui ahal uvin, ama hon ahetkhahlou chule apu apateu jong anahetkhah lou, pathen chom chom anga kilhaina abol'uve. 
Keiman avel vel in kalhachate themgao ho kasol in, ajah uva, hitobang thilse kahot tah hi bol hih un, tin ka seipeh e. 
Ahivangin, ama hon ka thusei angaipouvin, chule athilse bol’u jong adalha deh pouve. Ama hon pathen chom chom houna gimnamtui ahaljing uvin ahi. 
Hijeh a chu, keiman kalungsatna meikou kisunglha banga Judah gamsung khopi jouse chunga, kasunlhah khum ahiuve. Tuni geiyin, hiche khopi ho chu ahomkeovin, mi chenna louvin akijam nalaiye. 
Tun thaneipen Pakai Israel Pathen'in hitin aseiye. Ibol a nangho leh nangho thilse kibolkhum jeng nahiuvem? Nangho asohcha naumlouhel diu, pasal jong, numei jong, chapang jong, Judah gam’a kon’a hilaiya hung chengho jouse, na boncha uva chunga manthahna kilhut khum’a, khatbeh jong ahingdoh umlou ding nahiuve. 
Ibol a nang hon Egypt pathen chom chom doilim ho nakisem thu-uva, a-anga gimnamtui nahal uva, keima neiphin lunghang’u ham? Nangho le nangho kisumang thah a chule leiset namtin vaipi lah a nuisat leh gaosap change naum dingu ahi. 
Nanghon Jerusalem leh Judah gamsunga, napu napa teu phatlouna ho, Judah lengte gitlouna ho, chule nangho leh najiteu setna ho, ibol a nasuhmil u ham? 
Tuni geiyin, nang hon lung kisih a kisemphat ding nagopouve. Nangho le napu napa teu kathupehsa ho, nang hon jui ding nagel pouve. 
Hijeh chun thaneipen Pakai Israel Pathen'in hitin aseiye, keiman nangho na boncha uva kasuhgam ding nahiuve. 
Keiman Judah mite akidalha amoh chengse, Egypt gamsunga hunglut ho, abonchauva kasuhgam heldiu ahi. Amaho chu aneopen’a pat alenpen geiya: gal a thiloi, kelthoh a thiloi, chule natna hise a thiloi umdiu, abonchauva thigam heldiu ahi. Amaho chu gaosap chang leh kidah um chule mi nuisat a umdiu, ahiuve. 
Keiman Jerusalem talen kamatsah banga, Egypt gamsung’a jong amaho chunga, gal kisat, kellhah chule natna hise a, talen kamatsah ding ahiuve. 
Dalhah a um amoh cheng, Egypt gam’a hung juamlut ho, khatvei Judah gam’a kilekit ding ngah ho, koimacha asohcha-a kilekit umlouhel diu ahi. Ama hon agam uva kilekal ngahlel ujongleh, ahingdoh themchakhat bou tilou, koimacha kilekit umlou ding ahiuve,” ati. 
Hichun ajiteuvin milimdoi houna gimnamtui hal uve, ti hesa pasal ho jouse leh, Egypt gamsunga cheng Judah mite khoppi bol mi tamtah lah a, gapang numeiho jouse chun, Jeremiah jah a aseiyun, 
Nangman Pakai min’a nathusei ho chu, keihon ngai pouvinge, atiuve. 
Keiho kanop nop’u bol dinga um kahi tauve. Keiho le kapu kapateuvin, chule kalengteu leh avaihom teuvin Judah gamsung le Jerusalem khopi sunga anabol bang uva chu, keihon jong van lengnu houna-a gimnamtui kahal uva chule juning kalhit khum diu ahi. Ajeh chu, hitia kabol laiya chun, keiho din neh le chah aneng jeng jungin, kaphatou lheh un, chule thilse ima jong katoh pouve. 
Ahinlah keihon van lengnu houna gimnamtui kahaldauva, chule juning kalhitda phat un, keihon gentheina nasatah kathoh un, gal in eikithat uvin chule kalthoh in kathigam tauve, atiuvin ahi. 
Chule numeiho chun aseikit un, “Keihon van lengnu houna-a kahal uva chule juning kalhit khum uva, chule amanu lim’a changlhah kasem uhi, kajipateu hetlouva, amaho phalna louva kasem beh u ham? Ahipoi! atiuvin ahi. 
Hichun Jeremiah in, numeiho leh pasal ho chule mipi ho jah a chun, 
“Nangho leh napu napa teu vin, chule na lengteu leh gamvaipo ten, Judah gamsung le Jerusalem khopi sunga milimdoi houna gimnamtui nahal u chu, Pakaijin ageldoh lou mong dinga nagel u ham? 
Nanghon kidah umtah thilse nabol hou chu, Pakaijin athoh nomtah louphat a, aman na gamsungu le nakhopi hou abon’a ahomkeova ajam’a, mi acheng ding beikeiya gaosap chang ahitai,” ati. 
“Thilse hichengpi nachung uva alhun hi, nang hon milim doi houna gimnamtui nahal uva chule Pakai douna na chonset jeh u ahi. Chule nang hon ama thusei nangai dauva chule athupeh najuilou jeh uva, nachung uva thu atankhum ahitai,” ati. 
Chuin Jeremiah in mipi ho jouse leh numeiho jah a chun, “Egypt gamsunga cheng Judah miho na boncha uvin hiche Pakai thusei hi nagiyuvin, 
Thaneipen Pakai Israel Pathen'in hitin aseiye, Nangho leh najiteu numeihon naseiyun, keihon kakitepnau banga, van lengnu houna gimnamtui kahaldiu chule juning kalhit khum diu ahi, natiuvin; Nasei bangun nakhut tah uvin namoldo tauve. Hijeh chun naseisa bangun cheuvin gabol tauvin,” ati. 
Amavang Judah mite Egypt gamsunga chenghon Pakai thusei hi ngaiyun. Pakaijin hitin aseiye, keiman kamin’a huneitah a kakihahsel ahitai. Nanghon kamin naphahtah louhel diu; Nalah uva koima chan jong kamin pan’a nakihahsel tahlouhel diu ahi, tin thaneipen hingjing Pakaijin aseiye. 
Ajeh chu keiman nangho kavet lhih a, nachung uva thilse kalhunsah ding, apha nato louhel diu ahi. Judah mite na boncha uva nathigam hel kah uva, gal a nathi dingu chule kelthoh a nathi diu ahi. 
Mi themcha khat athimoh chengse chu Egypt gam’a kon’a hung jamdohuva Judah gam’a hung kile diu ahiuve. Chuteng tah le ahingdoh chengsen koi thusei dihjo ham, ti ahetchen diu ahi. 
Chule Pakaijin aseiye, Hiche hi nangho dinga thudih melchihna-a kapeh chu ahi. Keiman nangho kagihsa banga nachung uva lhung ding, chule hilaimun’a hi keiman nangho talen kamatsah ding nahiuve. 
Pakaijin hitin aseiye, Keiman Judah lengpa Zedekiah chu akidoupi Babylon lengpa Nebuchadnezzar khut a kapehdoh banga, Egypt lengpa Pharaoh Hophra chu, ama thatnom’a hol ho khut a kapeh doh ding ahi,” ati.
