Jeremiah.
49.
Ammon mite chung changa thuhil khat ahung kipen ahi. Hichi hi Pakai sei ahi: “Gad gam lodinga Israel son le pah umlou hitam? Molech hou ho nang hon epidinga khopi sunga na chensah uham? 
Nikho ho ahung lhun tengleh.” tin Pakaijin aseijin, “Na khopiu Rabbah douna a gal ka kou ding ahi. Lahlou thil mangthah kiselom banga akhopiu chu gam hel ding, chuleh akimvela kho ho jong kihal lhasohkeiding ahi. Chuteng Israel te gam analahpeh uchu aki nung lah diu ahi,” tin Pakaijin aseije. 
“Aw Heshbon, kap doh in, ajeh chu Ai khopi chu akisumang heltai. Kap un, Aw Rabbah mite! Lunghemna von chu kivonun, kap unlang peng uvin, Pal dunga chun kiselun, ajeh chu na Pathen un Molech, chule athempu ho leh vaihom lamkai ho ada adoi uva gamla tah mun'a akai mang diu ahi. 
Na phaicham leiphat lai na kiletsahpi uvin ahi, hichi ho jong chu hung mangtha ding ahi. Tahsanlhom nu, nanei nagou a na kisongin koiman ima eilothei ponte tin na ngaiton ahi. 
Ahin ven! Tijat umtah na chunga kahin lhunsah ding, tin Pakai thaneitah chun aseije; naheng na kimvel a chenghon nagam a kon'in nahin deldoh diu, chule nakino mang naa konna koiman nahuh louding, amaho jeng jong jamcheh diu ahi. 
Ahin keiman Ammon mite haosatna ka le tundoh peh kitding ahi, “Keima Pakaijin kasei ahi.” 
Edom chung changa hichi thuhil hi ahung ki pen ahi, Thaneitah Pakaijin hitin aseije: “Teman mi a chihna umlou va ham? Miching mihilchah ding khat jong umlouhel hitam?” 
Kihei unlang jammang un! Dedan mite leiko thuh hoa kisel uvin, ajeh chu vangset na nasatah Edom chunga kahin lhut tengleh nang jong gotna ka peh tha ding ahi! 
Lengpi thei ga kholtup hon, vaicha te din themkhat adalha jiuvin ahi. Janteng guchahon abonin agudoh jipouve. 
Ahin Edom gam chu ngemsela kalha ding ahi. Chule agamsunga kiselna ding munneo khat jeng cha jong umlou ding ahi. Achateu asopiu pasal ho, chule aheng akom hou jouse suhmanga um ding ahin, Edom jeng jong umlou ding ahitai. 
“Ahin keiman nalah uva umnalai naochaga ho chu kavenbit ding ahi. Na meithai hou jong kachunga kithopi dia kingai thei ahiuve.” 
Hiche hi Pakaijin asei chu ahi, “Mona neilou in athohgim mong ding ahileh, nangin ajat ja na thohgim dading ham! Gotna chang louva naum louding ahi! Thutanna khon a nadon tei ding ahi!” ati. 
Ajeh chu Keima min panna kakihahsel ahi, tin Pakaijin aseije, “Bozrah, tijat umna mun leh a mangthai di thil ho khoptup na mun hung soh ding: Sapset le nuisatna hung um ding ahi. A khopi sung uleh akholai hoa itih a dinga mangthah diu ahitai.” 
Pakai kom'a thuhil khat kajan, hichu: chi le nam ho henga palai ahung kisolin, “Edom dounan kipum khom unlang galsat din kigosa demin umun!” 
Pakaijin Edom henga aseijin, “Chitin namtin lah a neocha kahin soding, mijouse thet a nahung um ding ahi,” ati. 
Midang kichat naa na thumop chun nang le nang naki lheplhah sah tan, chule na ki letsah nan lheplhah na umtam ahi. Molsang sang tah ho lah a songpi kulsunga na chengin ahi. Ahin Muvanlai bangin lhang leh a buh sajong lechun, ka tahlhah ding nahi,” tin Pakaijin aseije. 
Edom chu tijat umtah khat hung hi ding ahi. A lamlha jouse kicha uvin tin, akisuh mang muho chu tijatnaa dimdiu ahi. 
“Sodom leh Gomorrah chule akimvel’a um khoho kisumanggam tobang ding ahi.” Tin Pakaijin aseije, “Koima chenglou ding, koima chan alo louding ahi.” 
Keipi bahkai Jordan gammang a konna kelngoi abahkhum banga ka hung ding ahi. Edom gamsunga konna kadel doh ding, chule keima deidan a lamkai khat kapoh sah ding ahi. Ajeh chu kei to koi kibanga, chule koiham eichou dinga? Kalungdei a koivaihom naa adoudal ding umham?. 
Ngaijin, Edom leh Teman mite dounaa Pakai thilgon ho hi. Chapang neocha hogeija kelngol banga kikai thangding, chuleh a chennau in ho jong kisumang soh hel ding ahi. 
Edom lhuh gin achu leiset chung kithing intin, hesoh gentheija ka-na chu Twipi Sangdung geija kijapha ding ahi. 
Ven! Melma chu, Muvanlai in alha ajal a ahung lenlhah banga, Bozrah chu man dinga gangtah a hung leng suh ding ahi. Galhang hattah ho jeng jong numei naoso nat tobanga tasa gimna anei diu ahi. 
Damascus chung changa hitin thuhil ahung kipen ahi. Hiti hin Pakaijin aseije: Hamath le Arpad khopi ho kichat tijatnan adim in, ajeh chu suhmanga aum thusoh chu ajaovin ahi. Twikhanglen'a hui le go hattah nun bangin alungsung’u jong aki chol tapouvin ahi. 
Damascus atha ahung lhasam tan, amite jouse jam din aum tauvin, numei naosonat kituh chah bangin, atuhchah tan ahi. 
“Hiche khopi minthang, kipana khopi chu nungsun a um ding ahitai! 
A khangdong hou jong lamlen na lhu untin thidiu ahi. Asepai jouseu kithat gamhel diu ahi,” tin thaneitah Pakai chun aseije. 
“Chule Damascus khopi bangho meiya ka hal ding, Ben-Hadad leng inpi ho kahal lhah ding ahi.” 
Kedar le Hazor lenggam teni, Babylon lengpa Nebuchadnezzar in asat chung changa thuhil khat ahung kipen ahi. Hiche hi Pakaijin asei ahi! “Kedar chu nailut un! Solam a kon'in agollhang hou chu sumang un! 
A gancha houle a khoilu pon jouseu hung kilahpeh untin, anei agou le asangongsao hou hung kilahpeh diu ahi. Muntin a kichat na thubou kithanga kija ding, aki heina jou seuva kichat naa dim diu ahi.” 
Naki hinsona diuvin jam uvin, tin Pakaijin aseije, “Leiko thuh a kiseluvin nangho Hazor mite, ajeh chu Babylon leng Nebuchadnezzar in nangho douna le nangho suhmang dingin kigosan aumtai.” 
“Chetou unlang hiche nomsate khu ga sat un,” tin Pakaijin aseije, “A miteu achang seh un kot le bang jong umlou nelgam a achengun ahi. 
A sangongsao houleh agancha hou abon'a nanghoa hi ding ahi. Keiman amaho hi huilah a kathe mangding, mun gamla tah tah a cheng ho hi muntin’a kon’a vangsetna achung uva kalhun sah ding ahi,” tin Pakaijin aseije. 
“Hazor chu Sial hon chennan hin neijuntin, a-itih a mihem koima cheng talou ding ahi. Hilai mun a chu koima cheng talou ding, koiman alo louding ahi.” 
Elam chung chang thuhil khat themgao Jeremiah henga Judah Lengpa Zedekiah vaihom lai in, Pakaiya kon'in ahungin ahi. 
Hiche hi thaneitah Pakaijin asei ahi: “Keiman Elam te thalkap themho ka suhmang ding – agal sat-hou lah a ahangsan pen, 
melmaho muntin’a kon a kahin puilut ding, Elam mite chu vanthamjol ning li a konna hui a kathe mang ding ahi. Vannoi gamtin'a konna kaidoh a umdiu ahi. 
Keima tah jong Elam melma hotoh kache khom a asukeh lah a kajao ding ahi. Ka lung satna konna manthahna lentah Elam mite chunga kahin lhut ding ahi,” tin Pakaijin aseije. “Kasuhmang hel masanga amelmateu vin chemjama adeldiu ahi. 
Elam a ka Laltouna kadoding,” tin Pakaijin aseije. “Chule Keiman alengpau le avaihom hou ka suhmang ding ahi.” 
Ahin phat ho ahung lhun tengleh, Elam haosatna ka tundoh kitding ahi, Keima Pakaijin kasei ahi.
