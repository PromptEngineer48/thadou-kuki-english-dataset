Jeremiah.
51.
Hiche hi Pakaijin aseiye: “Keiman Babylon mite douna mi sumangpa katil doh ding ahi.” 
Gamchom mite hunguva huisin changpol alhoh manjel bang a, gimna nikhoa amanu doudia jet le veiya konna ahindel khum diu ahi. 
Galvon kivon manlouvin lhauvin, ahilou le thalpi kap thempa jong chun athalpi kamda henlang; galsat mi phalai ho hing hoi dauvin! Sepai jouse sugam hel’ un* 
Babylon mite gamsung le lamlen ho-a akithat ho lhuchap ding ahiuve. 
Van Sepai Pakaijin Israel leh Judah adalha poi. Ama chu a Pathen'u ahijingin, agamsung-u chonsetna le Israel mithengpa douna adim jeng vang uvin. 
Babylon na kon'in jam un! Kihinsona din lhaiyun! Amanu talen kipehnaa chun kisat lhah thasah hih-uvin! Pakai phulah phat ahin lethuhna lhingset ding ahi. 
Babylon hi Pakai khut a sana khon tobang chu ahin, vannoi pumpi kidon khamsah ahi, namtin vaipin Babylon lengpitwiya kham ahin, chule hichea chu ngolgam a ahi tauve. 
Hetman louvin Babylon alhun Amanu din kap’uvin. Amanu ding damdoi peuvin. Ijemtia adam kitthei a ahi le. 
Kithopi ding kanom in, ahinlah tun huhdoh thei ahi tapoi. Amanu dalhauvin, chetau hite igamcheh uva. Ajeh chu athu kitan lhah na-in van asun, anasa behseh in akitepi abeihel e. 
Pakaijin idihna aphongdoh tai. Hungun, Pakai I-Pathen’u thilbol Zion a seichoi juhite. 
Thalchang ho notval un! lumho domsang un, Pakaijin Medes lengte lung asuthoutai. Ajeh chu atup chu Babylon a suhmang ding chu ahi, ajeh chu hiche Pakai phulah, a-Hou-In phulahna ahi. 
Babylon kulbang vum'a gal ponlap tungdoh-un, khongah hatsah-un, khongah ho tungdoh-un, ajeh chu Babylon mite douna’a Pakaijin asei chu agongtoh in, atong tai. 
Hiche gamsung’a hin vadung tampi alongin. Kiveina manlutahtah aum'e, ahivangin aphat akichaina ahunglhun tai. 
Pakai Sepai chu ama tah akihahsel e, ama min'in, “Nakhopi sung’u nagal mite hungdim din, khaokho ho tobang hela mihem kahindip teitei ding nahi, ama hon nangma douna’a hung ao-doh diu ahi.” 
Pakaijin leiset hi Amathahatna asem ahin, asetlouna dinga achihnaa akoi, ahetchetnaa konna jong Aman van aphajal ahitai. 
Ama a-aw asodoh teng, van hoa twipi tamtah ahung umjin van monga kon'in meilhang asemkhom e. Gojuh teng kol aphetsah-in, akholmun'a kon khohui anunsah e. 
Hiche amuteng ule mitin a ngolden jiuve chule hetthemna anei pove! Mikhut thiem takip semthu milim a kijumso ahiuve. Ajeh chu amilimsemthu uchu joubulom ahin, amaho – a haitheina hu le thilbol theina aumpoi. 
Hiche milimsemthu ho chu mohseh, athu aki kholtoh phatteng ule mangthah cheh diu ahi. 
Ahinlah Israel Pathen vang chu milimsemthu ahipoi! Thil ijakai Sempa le ahung konna ahin, ama amite ama goulo ahi. Van Pakai sepai janel chu Ama min ahi. 
Pakaijin aseijin, nangma kagal heicha le chemjam nahin, “Namtin vaipi le lenggam jouse suhmangna kaman nahi.” 
Chule nangma’a sakolte le achunga toupa kasuh chip ding, kangtalaiho le atolte jong mangthah ding ahi. 
Nangma’a pasal le numei jong kasuchip ding, tehse le chate jong kasuchip ding ahi; Chule nangma’a nungah le gollhang jong kasuchip ding ahi. 
Nangma’a kelngoichingpa le akelngoihon jong kasuchip ding, nangma’a loubolpa le abongchal ho, chule agal lamkaiho le vaihom ho jong kasuchip ding ahi. 
Pakaijin aseijin, “Keima in Jerusalem a kamite chun thilse abol chengseu Babylon mite kale thuhding ahi,” ati. 
Pakayin aseijin “Ven, thahat mollentah leiset pumpi sumang! Keima nakidoupi kahi.” Nachung a kakhut kalhan doh a, munsang’a konna kasuh lhah’a, nangma ima hilou akavamsa mol kaso ding nahi. 
Nangma tonsot’a dalpeia umdin nanga konna in – ning song ding le inbul song dia alah lou diu ahitan, nangma hi pampei hel’a um ding nahitai, tin Pakaijin aseije. 
Namtin din ponlap khat tahdoh unlang, namtin vaipi lah a sumkon gintoh kigotsah un kisat ding! Ararat sepai te hinpuidoh un, Minni, chule Ashkenaz gal lamkai semun lang, khao khoho banginsakol ho hungtousah – un. 
Amanu douding namtin vaipi hinpui un, lamkai ding Medes lengte chule a vaihom ho jouse toh, 
agam chu akithing thing jengin anasalheh jeng e. Pakaijin Babylon dounaa thil agon jouse kikhel louding ahi. Babylon gamsung ahomkeova umsah ding aching beihel’a koi ding ahi. 
Amanu galhat ho, galsat ahaiyun. Akul sunga aumden tauve ahatnau adailhan numei toh abang tauve. Achen nau ahallha uvin chule khopi kelkot jouse atumkeh – un ahi. 
Thusep khat chu achomto kisuto ding, thangkou khat chu achom khattoh kisuto – a, akhopi chu among khatna kon'in akilatai ti hil dingin. 
Jam na diu lampi ho akikhah in pumpeng aneihou meiya akihal lhan, sepai te akicha gam tauve. 
Ijeh-inem itileh thaneipen Pakai, Israel Pathen'in hitin aseiye, “Babylon chanu chu kengto chotpha akinei tengleh changpol tobang ahin, Ahivangin phat chomkhat nung leh achang –at phat hunglhung ding ahitai,’’ ati. 
“Babylon lengpa Nebuchadnezzar in keima eival lhum in, aman eisuchip in; kathahat jouse asugoitan ahi. Hing ne bangin keima eivel lhum tai, ka haosatna oiva set in eine peh tan. Aman kagamsunga kon'in eipei doh tai. 
Keima chungle kaphunggol mite chunga pum hat jinga thil kibol chu Babylon khopi chunga chujeng hen,’’ ati. Zion cheng mite chun seiju hen, “Keima thisan man chu Babylon mite gam'a chengte chung a chu jeng hen,’’ tin Jerusalem in jong seihen. 
Jerusalem mite kom'a Pakaijin aseijin, “Nakhan khou gel’ing ting, namelma teuvin nabolnau chu kale ditsah ding ahi. Babylon twikul kansah ingting chule avadung jouse kangsoh ding ahi.” 
Chuteng Babylon chu ingem jeng kisechop ding, gamsa chenna soh ding; chule koima chenna hitalouva, gamsa jengseh chenna hi ding ahi. 
Amaho abonchauvin keipi bahkai hon banga kitum tum diu, sakei noute banga ngih ngih jeng diu ahi. 
Amaho nei-yin ahul sat pet tah’a keiman golvah kagonpeh ding, ju kadon sah a; a-itih chan geiya ihmu mu jeng diu, kithoukit louva thidiu ahi, tin Pakainyin aseiye. 
Keiman amaho thu hi kelngoi nou akitha na ding le kel ngoichal ho kelcha ho banga kilhainaa ki that bang hi ding ahiuve. 
Itidan a Babylon chu lhujeng hi tam* vannoi pumpi in apachat khopi chu tua hi, amanu namtin a dingin kichat umta thil ahidohtai. 
Babylon chu twikhanglen in achup tan, amanu achunga twipi aki deldel e. 
Akhopi ho chu akam hel in, gamgo, gamthip, mikhat cha chennalou gam, mihem chapa khatchan ajotpa louhel asoh tai. 
Keiman Babylon Pathen bel chu talent kapeh ding, aval lhumsa chu akam sunga konna kalah doh peh ding ahi. Namtin vaipi ho Amahou dinga aheng a hung kitol tol louhel ding ahi. Ajeh chu Babylon khopi pal chu chim ahitai. 
Hung potdoh'un, kamite, Babylon khopi a kon'in jam doh loiyun nangma kihuhhing cheh un! Pakai lunghan na kon'in jam un. 
Nalungthim lhahsam sah hih-uvin, Gamsunga thu kithang jeh hin kicha hih uvin; kum khat sunga thu thang khat hunglhung ji ahin, khonung teng akum kit’a chu thu thang khatma hunglhung jia ahiye; Gamsunga pumhat jeng aum tan,Vaihom khat chu vaihom khat dou umcheh ahitai. 
Hijeh chun vetan, keiman Babylon khopia pathen limsemthu ho talent kapeh na ding nikho ho chu ahung lhunge. Agam sung pumpi hi kijum so ding, alam len dunga amite ki that jouse ki jam dan ding ahiuve. 
Chujou tengleh van le leiset chule asung a um jouse n, Babylon khopi chunga kipa la asah diu ahi. Ijeh-inam itileh amaho dou dinga Misumang ho chu Sah lam gamkaiya konna hung kondoh ding ahi tauve, tin Pakaijin seiye. 
Babylon in Israel mite athagam banga, chuleh leiset pumpi kitha gam jeh a amanu lhu a ahitai. 
Pohdoh un, nangho chemjam’a konna houidoh ho! ding den hih un chule ve hih un – jam thei ahi pat in! Pakai chu geldoh jingun kholgam lhatah a naum vang un, chule na gam lang u Jerusalem gel doh jingun. 
Jumsona kachang un ahi, ijeh-inem itileh houset kachan u-ahitai. Kamai jengu jong jabolna akilah tapoi, ajeh chu Pakai munthenga gamdang miho ahunglut tauve, tia nasei diu ahi. 
Hijeh chun ven, hiche nikho ho chu ahunglhung tai, tin Pakaijin aseije; milimsemthu ho chunga thu katan ding, agam pumpi lung leng ding chule maha pua peng ding ahi. 
Babylon chu vanlam sanga kaltou ding hihen, akulpi jeng jong sangtah a aget ding hijongleh, kamite ama sumang dinga achunga hunglhung ding ahi. 
Babylon awgin kithong leh Chaldea mite gam'a manthahna awgin akithong e. 
Ijeh-inem itileh Pakaijin Babylon hi akeosah a aumsah ahitan, husa gin beihel a alhaa; acheh chaunau gin jong twi kinong tobangin aging in, a awgin husan ujong agimnei cheh cheh in ahi. 
Misumangpa Babylon chunga achutan, agal hang sepai ho jong akhomtup soh tai. Athalpi jouse jong abang abaijin aum gam in, ajeh chu Pakai chu milethuh Pakai ahin, thon louhel’a lethuhna apehji ahi. 
Aleng chapa te ahin, ami ching ho jouse jaona ju ka-khamsah soh hel ding, chule agam vaipo ahin asepai lah a thunei vaihom ho, agal hang ho jouse jong khamsoh hel diu ahi. Thabeiya imu lhu diu, kithou talou diu ahitai, tin thaneipen Pakaijin aseiye. 
Hatchungnung Pakaijin hiti hin aseiye, Babylon in pal nasatah akai chu tollham a 
Judah lengpa Zedekiah leng ahikal kumli alhing in, Jeremiah themgaovin Babylon mun’a ana kilhonp’n Mahseiah chapa, Neriah chapa Seraiah thu anapen hiti hin anaseijin ahi. Saraiah kitipa hi thil ijakai chingtup dinga ngensena ana chang ahi. 
Hiti chun Jeremiah chun Babylon mun’a lhung ding thil abonchan anajih lut’in, Babylon thudol in thu hijatpi chu ana kijihlut tan ahi. 
Chuin Jeremiah in Saraiah henga hiti hin aseijin, “Babylon mun nalhung teng thu hichengse hi nasim doh tei ding ahi, phatah in vephan,” atin, 
hiti hin aseijin, ‘Pakai nangman hiche mun chung chang thudol’a hin seiyin, hiche mun hi kisu mang ding, asung hi chenna hita louding; mihem ahin chule gamsa jeng jong a-itih channa chenna hilou ding ahom keu jeng hi ding ahi.’ 
Hiche lekha hi nasim chai teng songkhat toh nakan beh ding, hichu vadung lailunga nase lut ding ahi. 
Chuteng keiman hiche mun’a kahin lhut ding thilse jal’a hi Babylon jong kidodoh kit talou ding, hitia chu mangthah hel ding ahitai, tin seiyin,” ati. Hichengse hi Jeremiah thusei dohho ahi.
