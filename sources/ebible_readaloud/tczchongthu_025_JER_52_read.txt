Jeremiah.
52.
Zedekiah chu leng ahung channin kum somni le khat alhingtai, Jerusalem ‘a kum som le khat aleng chang e, anu min chu Hamutal ahin, Libnah kho – a Jeremiah chanu ahi. 
Ahivangin Zedekiah jong Pakai mitmun thilsi abol in, Jehoiachin lengpa banginaum'in ahi. 
Hiche thil hi anasoh in ahi. Pakai lunghanna kon'in Jerusalem le Judah gamsung chu amit mua kon'in abonchauvin atolmang tai. Zedekiah chu Babylon lengpa dounan gal abol’ in ahi. 
Hichun Tuolbuol som le nga nikhon, a aleng chan kumko lhinkum, Babylon lengpa Nubuchadnezzer chu aseipaite jouse toh Jerusalem satdin ahungun, amaidon na chun ponbuh asongun, akhopi kimvela galkul akaikhum tauve. 
Hiti chun Zedekiah lengpa leng chankal kum som le khat lhin geijin Jerusalem chu aumchah tauve. 
Lhamul som le get, kum som le khat lhinna Zedekiah leng vaipoh chun kel akhohtan, gamsung mite din neh ding abeihel tai. 
Chujouvin kulpi pal atumkeh tauve, Babylon miten khopi aumchah jeng vangun, jankah-in sepai jouse ajamdoh tauve, kulbang nikah-a lengpa hon lampi kotpi-a chun ajamdoh – un, Jordan phaicham lampi chu ajon tauve. 
Ahinlah Babylon Sepai ten Zedekiah lengpa chu adal uhvin. Jericho phaicham a aman tauve, Ama Sepai te jouse ama – a kon'in akithe cheh tauve. 
Hichun ama chu amanun , Hamath gam'a Riblah a Babylon lengpa henga apui tauve. Hia chun achung athu akitan tai. 
Ama mitmu changtah- in achapa te le gam vaipote athapeh tai. 
Chujouvin Zedekiah mitchang teni chu akaldoh pehuvin, chun thihkhaovin akanin, Babylon lama akaijin, athi nikho geijin songkul’ a aumtai. 
Lhajing som le sagi nikho hiche kum, Nubuchadnezzar lengpa leng vaipoh kal kum som le ko lhinna ahin, Babylon lengpa kinbol, Sepai gal lamkai Nebuzaradan chu Jersusalem;a ahunglhun tai. 
Ama Jerusalem a cheng milen milalte inho le leng inpi chule Pakai Hou-In ahallhai. 
Chuin Aman Sepai jouse chu Jerusalem kimvela kulbang jouse avochim soh hel ding athulhah tai. 
Chujouvin Sepai gal lamkai Nubuzaradan’ in miho lah – a migenthei phabep le khopi sung a umnalai amohho, Babylon lengpa henga kipelut ho, chule khutthem umden loi ho sohchangin akaimang tai. 
Mivaicha behseh, thil le lo bei chengse vang chu Judah gam'a adalhan, lengpilei le louva natong din akoiye. 
Babylon miten Pakai Hou-In mai a sum-eng khomho le sum – eng velkol, chule sum-eng konglen chutoh avobongsohkeiyun, abonchan sum-eng jouse Babylon lama atol tauve. 
ama hon sum-eng chaiche le, maicham suhtheng teng vutlona, thaomei limbolna manchah chengse, kilhaina gantha teng thisan dolna khonho, gimnamtui lhutnamna khon chengse, adang dang Hou-In kinbolna kimang sum-eng manchah chengse chutoh apomang tauve. 
Nubuzaradan, Sepai lamkaipa chun khon neoho le vutloho, kongtongho, khangbel lenho, meivah khomho, thihkhe ho, chule khon ho, sana- a kisem chu sana in, dangka- a kisem chu dangka-in akilah- in ahi. 
Hou-In manchah dinga Solomon lengpa semsa sum-eng jouse, khom teni, velkol ho, atunna bongchal som le ni lim, agihval’ in tethei jong ahipoi. 
Khompi khat chu asandan feet somni le sgai, akimvel feet somle get akol khumin, hichu bel li sa-a ahin, asung hom ahi.

Kolbuthei lim somko le gup cheh apang khatna aumin, themneltah a kijem chung achu agoma jakhat in akimvel ahi. 
Nebuzaradan, Sepai lamkaipa in Thempu Chungnung Seraiah le Thempu ni channa Zephaniah chule kotngah thum ho chu sohchang ding akaiyin ahi. 
Khopi sunga kon'in sohchang din, Sepai gal lamkaipa, khopi sunga umnalai lengpa thumop’a pang mi sagi toh, gal lamkaipu thalhenpa, ama hi sepai thusim sun a pang ahin, chule milen cheh somgup toh akaithai. 
Nabuzaradan, Sepai lamkai pan, abonchauvin Babylon lengpa umna Riblah alhut uvin ahi. 
Chuin Babylon lengpan Hamath gam a Riblah mun’a athat gamtai. Hitobang chun Judah chu ama gam'a kon'in sohchangin aki kaimang tan ahi. 
Nubuchadnezzarin aleng chan kal kum sagi in soh chang a akaimang miho sangthum le somni le thum ahi. 
Chujouvin Nebuchadnezzar leng chan kum som le getna in Jerusalem’a mi jaget le somthum le ni akai mangbe in ahi. 
Nebuchadnezzar leng chan kum som ni le thum kumin sepai lamkai Nebuzaradan asol in Judah te jasagi le somli lang ahinkai in, abonun mi sang li le jagup ahiuve. 
Kum somthum le sagi lhinna Judah lengpa Jehoiachin soh chan kal Lhakao somthum le khat hiche kum chun Evil Merodach Babylon lengpa dinmun chu alotai. Judah lengpa Jehoiachin chu khoto manin songkul’ a kon'in alhadoh tai 
Jehoiachin hepitah –in, sohchang khom leng dang ho sang'in jong jana len cheh achansah in ahi. 
Asohchanna vonho akhelpeh-in, ahinkho lhum keiyin lengpa dokhang ankong anejom jingpeh tan ahi. 
Chule aneh le chah dingin Babylon lengpa athini geijin tanglouvin ape jing tan ahi.
