Phatkhat mi ana dimsetna Jerusalem khopi chu tunvang kho gem a sodoh tai. Chitin namtin lah a ana minthang khopi chu tunvang meithai a sodoh tan ahi. Vannoi leiset lengnu dinmun ana lo chu tunvang soh dinmun alo jotai. 
Jan khovah vah'in mitlhi long chutchut in akap in ana ngailu cheng holah a jong alhamon ding aum tapouvin aloi agol chengse la aheodah leh adou gal in apang gam tauvin ahi. 
Galhingin Judah akai tauvin soh channa leh engbolna athoh tan ahi. Gam dang nammite lah a achengun kicholdona mun amujou pouvin ahi. Agal mite uvin a umkimvel’un jamdoh thei louvin akoichah tauve. 
Jerusalem lampiho akhul lhatan koimacha Pathen houna kin bol dingin ahung doh tapouvin, khopi kotpihola donlouvin aumgamtan athempu hou akhoisa gam tauvin anungah khangthah teu akhankho diu gelnan akap gam tauve. 
A sugenthei houhi a Pakaiyun apang un ajalou teuchu akhantou tul tul jengun ahi. Pakaijin achonset jih uva Jerusalem chu aengbol uva achateu galhinga kiman a gamla tah’a kikaimang gamu ahi tauve. 
Jerusalem khopi, ahoina le aloupina chu amang thah ahitai. Alamkaiteu jong neh ding ham hing mu joulou sakhigol toh abang gam tauvin, agal miteu masanga jam doh nading tha jong anei jou tapouve. 
Lung lengvaija Jerusalem avale laitah in, tumasanga aphatlai nikho agel doh’e. Tunla agal mite khutnoija aum tan koimacha apanhu ding aum tapoi. Agal miteuvin ajep lhu’uvin alhu ho chu aveuvin anuisat’un ahi. 
Achonset behseh jeh'in Jerusalem ponse bangin paimangin aum tan. Tumasanga anagel thupi hon, tuhin anoisetauve, ajeh chu aponu kiho-lhah peha jumle jachat thoha um ahitauve. Hijeh chun hahdoh ngamlouhel la khoisa jing jenga um ahitai. 
Amaho hin a khonung diu kigel phah louhelin jonthanhoina in akisuhboh uve. Amaho a kemsuh jing tauvin, a lhamon diu aphong ding diu koima aum tapoi. Pakai ka hahsetnao nei vetpeh un tin akap tauve. 
Galmiten ajokhophelun anei jouseu akichom tauve. Pakaijin a Hou-In sunga lut dia anaphallou namdang te chun aHou-In sungu asuh boh pehu chu amudoh tauvin ahi. 
Amiteu neh ding hollin apeng jen jun tauvin, a hinjouna diuvin anei agouvu ajohdohun, vo Pakai neihin veuvin e-chan geija thet le kidah’a kaum’u hitam tin akap tauve. 
Vo chele vahle, hilang hin hopa ho hiche thilsoh hi ija na gellou u-ham? Pathen'in ka chunguva alunghan abuhlhah na le eibol gimnau hi vetem uvin. 
Aman vanna kon'in meikong ahin sep lhan kagu kachang’u ahalgo tai. Aman ka lampi uva thang a kamin eile hoitauve. Aman eichom thengselun nilhum lhumin hahsatna eithoh sahun ahi. 
Ka chonset nau khaovin eikanun sohchanna nam kolin eihenun ahi. Pakaijin katha’u eisuh ngoi pehun kagal miteu kom'ah eipedohun akhutuva chatmon kaum tauve. 
Pakaijin kalah'uva um kami hatteu thaneina asulhan kakhang thahteu suhmangna dingin gal mite amangchan ahi, Pakaijin lengpithei chilna akisuhchip bangin akhopi hoitah chu a suchip tan ahi. 
Hiche ho jouse jeh'in kakapin kamitlhi kamaija alonglha jenjung jingin ahinlah koima eilhamon ding le eitilhembi ding aum pon ahi. Galmiten eijou khup del tauvin, kakhang thahte’u khonung ding jong abing tan ahi. 
Jerusalemin panpi ngaiyin akhut alhaang dohin ahinlah apanpi ding amu joupoi. Pakaijin Ami Isrealte chunga, aheng akom ho chu agalmi asohdoh pehhan! Ama ho hi ponse banga paimanga um ahitai. 
Jerusalemin aseijin Pakai hi adih’e, ajeh chu keiman ama dounan na kana tong’e, chitin namtinin ka thusei hi neingai pehun, ka thoh gimna hi neihet pehun, ka chanute’u leh ka chapa te’u khol gamla tah a galhingin eikaimang peh tauve. 
Keiman kaloi kagolte kom'a kithopina kathumin ama honla eingai peh pouve. Ka themputeu leh ka lamkaiteu khopi sung’a kelin athi gam tauve, ahinkhou kihuhdohna ding aholun amu jou pove. 
Pakai kalung lengvaina neivetpehin, ka chonset jeh'in ka lungthim ahesoh’e, kholai dung’a kitha tona aumin'insung lang’a thilong aki jam’e. 
Ka lung gim ka khoisatna mi tamtah in eijah pehin ahinlah koima chan eihung lhamon pon ahi. Ka gal miten kathoh gimna ajah phatun Pakai nangin neibolnao hi akipa piuvin ahi. Oh Pakai keiho thoh tobang banga amaho nathohsah dinga naseipihnao nikho chu lhunsah loiyin. 
Pakai ka chonset nau jeh a nei engbol bangu chun ama hon eibolsetnao hi venlang amaho jong engbolin. Ka lengvainao hi atamvalin ka lungthim sung’u asuna lheh jeng’e.
