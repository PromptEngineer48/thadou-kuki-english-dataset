Ka La.

Sana jong adeite khuta among joutai! Sana hoi pen jong amoljeng tai. Muntheng sunga um songmantam ho jeng, jong lam lhongp dunga athangsoh tai. 
Veuvin Jerusalem chate, Mipha chomtah tahsan hoitah tah sanga mantam jo ho chu, Tun leibelho tobangin abol tauvin ahi. 
Sialte jengin jong anoute avah jiuvin, ahinla kami Isrealten avah pouvin ahi. Neldi gam'a vacha len “Vagonsang” hon achasenteu anahsah lou bangun ama hon jong a nahsah pouvin ahi. 
Dangchahin achateu lhei jouse adanga abehden jouve. Achateu gilkelin an aholun, Ahin koiman pehding aneipoi. 
An twitah tah ana neji ho chun tun lampi dunga khut don apang tauvin ahi. Phatkhatna vonhoi tahtah anavon ho chun tun thilnen kikholna munhoa neh ding aholun ahi. 
Kamite chonsetna hi Sodom mite chonsetna sangin alenjon ahi. Hiche munna chun hetman louvin suhmangna nasatah anachun koimacha akithopi thei ana umpoiye. 
Ka lengteu jong phat khat chun amel-hoidanu chu bongnoi leh buhbang sangin ana bangjon, amai’u jong songmantam bangin ana vah'in ahi. 
Tunvang amelu jong meihol sangin avomjoi. Lampi dunga jong koiman ahedoh thei tapouve. Avunu jong agu uva abehdan gamin, thing go bangin, agon atah jenge. 
Chamjamma kithat ho chu gelkella thiho sangin avangphajo ahitauve. Ajeh chu lou laiya neh aput tahlou jeh'in keltho hin amangthah jitauve. 
Masanga mi lainatpi theitah tah nute hon achateu chu neh dingin akihon gam tauve. Ama hon gim hesoha konna hoi dohna dingin achateu chu ana nejengun ahi. 
Tunvang alunghanna a kon chun Pakai alungna chim tai. Alunghanna hoise tah chu abuhlhah ahitai. Jerusalem'a lunghanna mei ana koudohin hichun khopi chu aleibul kiphuna gei chun ana hallha jeng’e. 
Leist lengte ahin - Vannoi mite koiman - Jerusalem kelkot sunga galmi alutthei ding chu ana tahsan pouve. 
Ahinlah a themgao ho chonsetna leh a thempu ho chonsetna jeh chun hiti hin anasohdoh tan ahi. Amaho chonset jeh a themmona bei miho thisan kisoa, khopi kisuboh ahi. 
Amaho hi lamlhongpi dunga ana vahle uvin mi thisana akisubohun koiman amaho atongkha ngam pouvin ahi. 
Mipiten chemangun tin asamun aboh nahi tauve, neitong khahih un atiuve. Amaho gamla tah in ajamun gamchom mite lah a avahle uvin ahinlah koiman achensah nom pouvin ahi. 
Pakai ama tah in amaho hi apanpi nom tapouvin muchom chomma athethang gam’u ahitai. Mipihon jong thempuho hi anahsah tapouvin, alamkai hou jong jana anei tapouvin ahi. 
Eihon i-pankhompi houvin eihung huhdoh diuvin ingaichauvin, eikithopi theiloudiu nam mite kom jenga jong panpi ingaichauvin a phachom pon ahi. 
Thi ding kichat jeh'in lampi dunga jong kavahle ngam pouvin, amahthah ding nikhou jong ahung naitan, amah thah ding ihitauve. 
Igal miteu hi mulaopi sangin jong aleng hat jon, Molsang chung dunga jammang go jong leuhen eimudohdiu ahin, gamthip langa kisel jong leuhen hiche munna jong chu eina chan nahlai dingu ahiye. 
Eihon leiset nam pumpi jouse hoa konna eihuhdoh diuva ina monpiu Pakai thaonu i-engpau jeng jong, akiselna munna kon'in a mandoh tauvin ahi. 
Vo! Edom mite Uz gamsunga hi nakipa theiju hinam? Ahin nangin jong Pakai lunghanna khkonna konna hi na don tei ding ahi. Nang jeng jong na jukhamna munna sagoh keova naki koi ding ahi. 
Vo khopi hoitah Jerusalem, naki engbolna nihko kichai ding ahitai, Naki engbolna gam'a konna nahung kile vah ding ahitai. Ahinlah Edom naki engbolna hi kipan bepseu ahinalaiye; Na chonsetna ho jouse hung kiphongdoh vah ding ahitai.
