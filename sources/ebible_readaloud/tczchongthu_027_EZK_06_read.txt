Ezekiel.
6.
Pakai thusei kaheng'a ahunglhung kit in, 
“Mihem chapa, kiheiyin lang Israel molsang lang khu namai ngat in lang amaho dounan gao thu seijin. 
Israel molsang ho douna a Thaneipen Pakaiya kon thu kisei hi lhangsapin. Hiche hi thaneipen Pakai thusei ahi. Molsang ho leh molho chuleh komtong laileh phaicham ho keiman Pathen houlou ho muntheng ho a ahal halla suchip dinga na chunga gal kahin pohlut ding tin aseije. 
Namaicham jouseu kisusea chule na Pathen hounau mun jouse kisumang ding ahi, namilim doi hou masanga namiho katha katha ding ahi. 
Nami thilong hou chu namilim doi masang a kajam ding chule nagubuh hou chu na maicham kimvella kathethang ding ahi. 
Hoilaimun hijongleh nachenna mun jouse chu mi chen louna mun hintin, chule keiman na Pathen houna muntheng jong ka suhmang peh ding nahi. Namaicham ho jouseu kisusea namillim doiho kitat chip a namilim houna mun jouse kiloi chim ding, chule houlam thua tup le doi nasem jouseu jong kisumang ding ahi. 
Hiche mun chu thilhong ho hung thangdeh duh ding chutah le nangman keima changseh hi Pakai kiti chu kahi ti nahin hetdoh ding ahi. 
Ahinvang keiman kamite phabep khat hiche suhgamna a konna hi kasochat sah ding chuleh amaho vannoija namtin vaipi ho lah a kithethang diu ahi. 
Chutia chu amaho namtin vaipi ho lah a hi akidelmang teng ule ama hon eihin geldoh diu ahi. Alungthim u tahsan aum lounau le milim doi angaichat nau mitvet jeh a hahsat genthei athoh u ahi ahetdoh dingu ahi. Hitia chu achaina leh kidah umtah a achonset nau jeh a amaho leh amaho hung kideimo diu ahi. 
ama hon keima changseh hi Pakai kahi ti ahin het uva chuleh keiman hitia hi vangsetna kahin pohlut khum ding ahi tia tomngai tah a kasei thu hin aphetdoh diu ahi. 
Hiche hi thaneipen Pakai thusei ahi. Kichat chat um'in nakhut beng inlang nakeng kho tat in, Israel mipite kidah um chonset ana boldoh hou jouseu jeh in kap jengin, tua hi amaho galla kel lha a chuleh natna a thi ding ahiuve. 
Natna hise chun gamla tah a kidelmang ho chu ahin lhun khuma thudiu, anaija umho chu galla thidiu ahi. Chule koitobang khat a hing galkai ana um jongleh kel in atha ding ahi, hijeh a chu achung uva kahin manchai ding ahi. 
Molsang ho leh molho chule thingna, thingphungna engnoi tin leh thingphung lim jousea amilim doi hou leh amai houva milim doihou kilhai thilto atonau munho a mikithatsa ahung kithethang tengleh keima hi Pakai kahi ti ahin hetdoh diu ahi. 
Lhanglang gammang a patna sahlang Riblah chan geija konna amaho hi kasuh keh a chu khopi jong ahom keuva kalha ding ahi. Hitia chu ama hon keima hi Pakai kahi ti ahin hetdoh dingu ahi.
