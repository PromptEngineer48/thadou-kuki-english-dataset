Ezekiel.
8.
Jehoiachin lengpa sohchan kal kumgup lhin kum, lhalam som le sagi nikhon Juda lamkai ho kain a ana um pet un thaneitah Pakai chun eina tuhchah e. 
Mihem put tobang pu gongso khat kamui, kathilmu pa kongbuh noilam chu akilangin, amapa chu meikong toh abange. Akongbuh a pat achunglang chu khisong valla val meikou ol a kimu jap jap bangin akimui. 
Amapa chunk hut toh kilou khat ahin lhang doh in kasamin eihin manne. Hichun lhagaovin vanthamjol lang gei eidom tou tan, chuleh Pathen a konna thilmu nei dingin Jerusalem ah ei thah tan ahi. Hichun keima Hou-In leitol sahlang kelkot sunga chun eipuije, hiche mun chu nasatah a Pakai alungnat sah milim loi lentah umna mun chu ahi. 
Phulou helouvin phat masa laiya phaichama kana musa Israel Pathen loupina ana um'in ahi. 
Hichun Pakaijin kajah a aseije, “Mihem chapa sahlang sang khu ven” atin hijeh chun keiman kaven ahileh sahlang sang kelkot gah lutna pama maicham kom'a chun Pakai nasatah ana lunghan sah milim doi kitung chu ana dinge. 
“Mihem chapa,” ahin tin, “Ipi abulu namu tah em? Ka Hou-In a konna kei nodohna dinga kidah umtah chonsetna abol'u khu namu tah em? Ahin kahungin nangin hiche ho sanga kidah umjo chonsetna tamtah namube ding ahi.” 
Hichun aman banghoma konna asunglang kimu theina Hou-In leitol kotphunga chun eipuilut e. 
Aman kakom'a aseijin “Tun mihem chapa, bang khu lai vang'in,” eiti. Hijeh chun keiman bang chu kalai vang'in ahileh lampi kiselguh chu kamutai. 
“Lut in,” ati. “Chuleh gitlouna leh chonsetna abol'u khu ven,” eiti. 
Hijeh chun kalut tan ahileh, bang dunga ganhing kitholtho ho lim leh kidah dah um thimsem ho lim khet thi a kisem ho chu kamutai. Israel mipi ten ahouvu milim doi jatchom chom ho jong chu keiman kamudoh tai. 
Israel lamkai som sagi ho chutoh alai juva Shaphan chapa Jaajaniah adin jong kamudoh tai, amaho khat cheh chun gimnamtui hal bom chu akichoijun, hichea kon chun gimnamtui kihalna meikhu meilom tobang chu aluchung uva ahung kitom jenjun jenge. 
Hichun Pakaijin kaheng'a hin ahinseije, “Mihem chapa pindan thim sung khu Israel lamkai hon ipi abol'u ahi namu khah tah em? Pakaijin igam u adalhah sa ahitah jeh in eimu pouvinte,” tin ama hon asei uve. 
Hichun Pakaijin aseiben “Hungin chule keiman hiche ho sanga tamjo kidah dah umjo ho chu navetsah inge.” 
Aman Pakai Hou-In sahlang kelkot a chun eipuijin, hiche a chun numei themkhat ana tou uvin, a pathen u Tammuz chu ana kauve. 
“Hiche hi namu hitam” tin aman eidonge. “Ahinlah hiche ho sanga tamjo kidah dah um chonsetna ho navetsah inge” ati. 
Hichun aman Pakai Hou-In leitol sungnung a chun eipuilut e. pindan sung nung lutna leh sum-eng maicham kikah a muntheng phung lutna a chun upa somni le nga tobang Pakai muntheng lam nung ngat in ading uve. Amaho chun solam angauvin, nisa hou nan tol ah abohkhup uve. 
“Mihem chapa, hiche ho hi namu khah tah em? tin aman eidonge. Hitobanga kidah umtah chonsetna abol uhi Juda mite dinga imalou thema angaito u ham? Hiche hin anam pumpiuva thaguma kino tona a apui uva, nahko hom leng lunga eivet uva chuleh eiphin lunghang bebe jeng ding ham? 
Hiti ahijeh a chu ka lunghanna kadonbut diu, khatcha jong kahing hoi louding, chuleh keiman kalungthim a jong, kalainat pilouhel ding ahi. Chuleh kahepina diuvin hung kap jong leu keiman kangaipeh louhel diu ahi.”
