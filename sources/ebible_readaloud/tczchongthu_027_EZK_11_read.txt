Ezekiel.
11.
Hichun lhagao chun eidom sangin chuleh Pakai Hou-In solam kelkot phung khopi sunga milal cheh mi somnile nga ho kanamu namun a chun eipuilut e. Amaho lah a chun Azzur chapa Jaazaniah le Benaiah chapa Pelatiah, mipi ho lamkai teni jong chu ana um lhone. 
Lhagao chun kakom'ah “Mihem chapa, hiche mihem ho hi khopi sunga thil dihlou gonga, chuleh thuhil dihlou jeng hilho chu ahiuve. 
Amaho chu mipi jah a chun insah phat hilou ham? Hiche khopi hi thihbel tobang ahin, eiho hi belsunga umsa bang kihong bit ihiuve tin amaho chun aseipeh uve tin kajah a aseije. 
Hijeh chun mihem chapa amaho dounan ging tah leh kicheh tah in gau thu seijin. 
Hichun Pakai lhagao kachungah ahung um'in kajah a hiche thu hi seijin eiti. Hiche Israel mipite kom'a Pakai thusei ahi, keiman nathusei u chu kahenai, ijeh inem itile na lungsung uva hung lut a lunggel jouse chu keiman kahet ahi. 
Nangman khopi sunga mi tamtah nathat in lamlen ho chu mithi nadip set e. 
Hijeh a chu thaneitah Pakai chun hitia hi asei ahi, aphai hiche khopi hi thihbel chu ahi. Ahinlah asahal ho chu nang hon thudih louva natha hou sa chu ahi. Ahin nanga dingin vang keiman hiche bel sunga konna khu kaloidoh ding nahi. 
Keiman na chunga kichat umtah chemjama kidouna na chunga kahin pohlut khum ding nahi tin thaneitah Pakai chun aseije. 
Jerusalem'a konna keiman kadeldoh ding, nadouna a kathutanna mangcha a thu hin tanding, gamdang miho khut a kapeh doh ding nahi. 
Israel gamgi dung, nalampi dunga chu nakisat chap diu hitia chu keiman na chunga thutanna kahin bolla chutah le nangin keima hi Pakai kahi tihi nahin hetdoh ding ahi. 
Ahipoi hiche khopi hi nang ding thihbel chu hilou ding, chule sable sunga umsa tobang jong chu nehilou ding ahi. Keiman Israel gamgi chin geija nathu kahin tanding ahi. 
Chutia chu nangin keima hi Pakai kiti chu nahin hetdoh ding ahi ati. Ijeh inem itile nangin kadan thupeh ho leh kachondan ho jui ding nanom pon, nakim vella nam mite chondan joh najui jin ahi.” 
Hitia gaothu kasei jing laihin Benaiah chapa Pelatiah chu athitai. Hichun keiman tolla chun kamai kasulut in kakap doh tai. “O thaneipen Pakai, nangin Israel sunga mijouse natha gam ding hitam?” 
Hichun Pakaiya thusei chu kaheng'a ahung lhunge. 
“Mihem chapa, Jerusalem kidalha nalai chengsen, nangma leh Israel mi akidal mangsa miho thu jeng bou asei jiuve”. Ama hon mipi ho chu “Pakaiya konna agamlat tah jeh uva tua hi agam u chu eiho eiki peuvah ahitai” tin asei uve. 
Hijeh chun soh chang ho chu seipeh un, hiche hi thaneitah Pakai thusei chu ahi. Keiman nangho vannoi gam jousea sohchanga kathemang jeng vangun, keima hi nasoh chan phat sunga jong namun theng u kahi jinge. 
Keima thaneitah Pakaijin kathethang nau namtin vaipi ho lah a konna kahin le khop tup kit uva, chuleh Israel gamsung chu khatvei kalepeh kit ding nahiuve. 
“Mipiho chu achennau gamsunga ahung kile kit teng ule, ama hon kidah um milim ho leh, kidah dah um milim doi ho melchih jouse alahdoh diu ahitai. 
Chule keiman amaho chu alungchang uva lung ngaitona khat seh kapeh uva, chuleh asung uva lhagao thah khat kakhum peh diu ahi. Keiman alung tah nau song tobang chu kalah mang peh uva, chuleh lungnem nachu alungchang uva kakhum peh dingu ahi. 
Hitia ama hon kathupeh leh kachondan ho ajui diu ahi. Amaho chu kamite dihtah hiuvin tin, keima a Pathen u kahi ding ahi.” 
Ahinlah kidah um milim leh kidah dah um milim doi ngaicha jing nalai ho chu achonset nau jeh a bulhingsetna lethuhna kapeh dingu ahi tin keima thaneitah Pakaijin kasei ahi. 
Hiti chun Cherub ho chun alhaving u ajah un, apangpeh uva akang ho chutoh huilah a chun aleng tou tauve. 
Hichun Pakai loupina chu khopi chunglanga alengtou vin, solam mol chunga akingan ahi. 
Hicheng jou nung chun Pathen Lhagao chun Babylon gam'a sohchang ho kom'a chun eilepui kit tai. Chuleh chuti chun lhagao thilmu na chu akichai tai. 
Chule ken sohchang ho chu Pakaijin keima eimusah ho jouse chu ka seipeh tai.
