Ezekiel.
18.
Hichun kaheng'a Pakai thusei achom dang khat ahung lhunge. 
“Ipi dinga hiche thuchih hi Israel gamsung chung thudol nasei kit kit uva, “Minu mipate lengpithei thuh aneuvin ahinlah achateu athei twina jeh chun akamsungu atol lhatai,” tia nasei jiu ham? 
Tahbeh mongin keima kahing jinge tin thaneitah Pakaijin aseije. Israel lah a hiche hi nanungsei kit louhel diu ahitai. 
Ijeh inem itile mijouse hi keiman athu katan cheh ding ahin, mipate hihen achateu hijongleu agoma kibang cheh ahiuve. Hiche hi kachonna dan ahi, ‘Koi hileh chonset bolla chu thitei ding ahi.’ 
Tekah nan, mipa khat chu michonpha ahi, adih chetna atoh a chuleh adih a ahile, 
Amapa chun molsang chunga Israel milim manga an aneh khah hih hella ahilou le ahouva apna louva ahileh, amapa chun jonthanhoi abolda a, ahilouleh numei khat chu athinei pet a aluppi da a, 
Amapa chu sumbat hom mikhoto them ahia, mivaichan bat mana athiltun u akoiden louva ahileh, mivaicha ho nei le gou achom peh hih leh, hiche sanga chu agilkel ho ann aneh sah a, saguoh keuva umho ponsil apeh a, 
Sum akan lalouva sumbat ahomma, thudih louva thu kitanna konna akikangse a, lungthengsel ahia, chule midang thutanna agamlat a, 
Chuleh tahsan umtah kathupeh le kachondan ho ajuija ahileh, mikhat touvin hiche thil ho hi adih a abolla ahile ama chu hing teitei ding ahi” tin thaneitah Pakaijin aseije. 
Ahin hitobang mipa khat chun chapa khat nei hen lang, amapa chu michom tolthat hihen lang, chule thilpha bol ding chu nom tahih henlang hileh, 
Chule hiche chapa chun apan abol khah louhel thil phalou jouse bol tahen lang, molsang chung dunga milim doiho chu gahou hen jonthanhoi bol taleh, 
Mivaicha leh panpi ding neilou ho chu a engse sea abol'a, asumbat neipa a konna gucha jia, athilbat manna kitungho kilelhat sah ding nom louva, milim doiho hou kidah dah um chonset bolna a kipumpeh, 
Chule sum homma aval vala akan kilah, hitobanga chonsepa chu hing thing ding hinam? Ahipoi ama chu thitei tei ding chule themmona dimsetna akilah ding ahi. 
Ahinlah hitobang chonsetna dim mipa chun chapa khat nei henlang hiche achapa chun apa gitlouna chengse chu amudoh tengleh hitobang hinkho chu mang talou dingin kigeltha taleh, 
Hiche chapa chu molsang chung dunga milim doi ho chu hou ding nom tahih hen lang chuleh jonthanhoi jin kipeh lut da jeng taleh, 
Mivaicha ho adihlouvin bolda henlang, ahinlah hiche ho sang chun mi sumbat a lahna a kitah hen lang amaho chu chommang da hen. Aman agilkel ho ann nehsah hen lang chule angaiho ponsil peleh, 
Mivachaho kithopi hen, akan dingin sum hom dahen. Chuleh kachondan ho leh kathupeh ho jui bukim soh hen, hitobang mi chu apa chonset jeh a thilouhel ding, amavang hing teitei ding ahi. 
Ahinlah apa vang chu achonsetna tamtah ho jeh a, akhanset jeh a, miho achom jeh a, chule mitmu a kicheh tah a thil dihlou abol ho jeh a chu thi ding ahi. 
Iti danna apa chonset man achapan apeh lou ham ipi jeh ham natiuve. Ahipoi chapang chun thilpha abolla adih a achonna chuleh kathupehho ajuija ahile hing teitei ding ahi. 
Ahinkho a chonsepa vang chu thi ding ahi. Apa chonset jeh a achapa ki engbol louding, chule achapa chonset jeh a apa ki engbol louding ahi. Michonpha ho chu achonphatna jal'a tohphatman asan uva, chuleh migilou te chu agitlou nau jal'a, ki engbol dingu ahi. 
Ahinlah ijem tin migilou te chun achonset nau jousea kon'in hung kihei mang uhenlang chuleh kathupeh ho hin jui pan uhen, chule thilpha leh adih chu hin bol uhenlang hileh hing teitei dingu ahin, thilouhel dingu ahi. 
Achesa phat a achonset nau jouse kisuhmil peh a chuleh chonphat abol hou jouse jeh a hing diu ahi. 
Migilouho thi chu kamu nomma nangaitou ham tin thaneitah Pakai chun aseije. Migilou chu agitlouna a konna akihei manga ahin ding chu kadei louva nabolu ham? 
Iti hen lang hijongleh michonpha khat tou chu achonphatna lampia konna akihei manga chonset abolpat tah a, midang chonset banga achonset tah a ahileh, amaho chu ahin diu kiphal peh ding ham. Ahipoi hithei louhel ding ahi bouve. Achonphat nahou jouse chu kisuhmil peh a chule achonset nauva thiden diu ahi. 
Ahijeng vang'in Pakaijin thilpha chu abollou ham tin nasei jiuve. O Israel mipite! Thilpha bollou chu kei kahi ham? Ahiloule nangho nahiu ham? 
Michonpha ho chu achonphat nauva konna akihei mang uva chonset thil abolpat tah uva ahile hiche a chu thi diu ahi. Henge achonset nau jeh a chu thidiu ahi. 
Chuleh migilou ho chun agitlou nauva konna ahung kihei doh uva, dan ajui uva, chuleh thilpha leh adih abol uva ahileh, amaho chun ahinkhou chu akihuhdoh diu ahi. 
Achonset nauva konna kiheidoh dinga akigel lhah nau alunggel ho jeh a amaho chu hing diu ahi. Hitobang mi chu thilou ding ahi. 
Ahijeng vang'in Israel mipi ten hitin aseije uve. Pakaijin thilpha abolpoi, “O Israel mipite, thilpha bollou chu nangho nahiuvin kei kahipoi.” 
Hiche jeh a chu O Israel mipite, nachon nau dungjui cheh uva khat cheh a nachung thu katan ding ahi tin thaneitah Pakaijin aseije. Nalung heijun lang chule na chonset nauva kon'in keihei mangun kisuhmang sah hih un. 
Doumah mah nabol chengseu chu nanung languvah koijun, lungthah sem uvin lang lhagao thah neijun. Ipi jeh a nathi jeng diu ham O Israel mite. 
Kinung heijun lang hingun, nathi diu kadeipoi tin thaneitah Pakaijin aseije.
