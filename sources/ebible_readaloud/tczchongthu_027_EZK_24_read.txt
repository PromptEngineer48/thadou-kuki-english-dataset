Ezekiel.
24.
Leng Jehoiachin songkul'a akihen jou kal kum ko khinkum Tolbol 15 nihin Pakaiya kon'in hiche thu hi akhenga ahung lhunge. 
“Mihem chapa, tuni nikho melchihna hi sun in, ajeh chu hiche nikho ahi Babylon lengpan Jerusalem ahin bulu pat nikho ahi. 
Hiti chun doumah bolte chu thaneitah Pakaiya kon hiche thusei hin hilchetna pen. Meichunga chun belkhat songdoh in, chuleh twi themkhat sungin; 
Deilhen samalsal lhah lut in, amalphe ho leh atophe ho chule nemdih deh a kichun sa ho chu lhah in, 
Kelngoi hon lah a kelngoi phapen pen chu mangin, bel noija thuhkhuh a chun thinglom chu tih in. bel chu hon phul let lut in chule aguho chu asatoh chun hon than. 
Tun hiche hi thaneitah Pakai thusei ahi. Hitobang tah a gentheina hin Jerusalem anga jing ahi, tolthana khopi! Amanu hi anhkonna bel ahi, dan kalla anatohnao siltheng jou hilou te chu, asaho chum oh lahdoh doh jengin, ajeh chu halkhat chu halkhat sanga phachomjo deh lou ahi. 
Ajeh chu amanu atolthana thisan chu song chunga, chun jon ahi. Tollhanga jong chu kithe thanga hilou ham? 
Hiche jeh a chu keiman amanu thisan chu mijouse mu ding song chunga kajonsah ding ahi. Hichu kalunghan vetsahna le amnu douna a phulahna hi ding ahi. 
Hiche hi thaneitah Pakai thusei ahi. Hitobang tah a gentheina hin Jerusalem anga jing ahi, tolthat hat ho khopi! Keima tah in amanu noija chu thing kachumlut peh ding ahi. 
Henge, thing ho chu setlom peh in bel phulna ding chun meichu deu jeju jeng hen. Sa chu hal tamtah in lhon'in, chule hiche jou teng chule aguho chu hal vamin; 
Tun belkeu chu meihol chunga chun song in lang bel chu ampeh in koiyin, anenho dankalla a chonna o chu kamang hen. 
Ahinlah dan kalla achonna o akisop theng thei lou jeh chun panna bei ahitai. Hijeh chu meilah a chun seplut tan. 
Nangma nathen theilouna chu najon lungput chu ahin chule namilim doi houna chu dan kalla na chonna chu ahi. Keiman soptheng kagon ahivangin nang nanompoi. Hijeh a chu tua ahi nangma douna a kalung han kabulhit kahsea nathen louna a chu naum jing ding ahitai. 
Kei, Pakaijin kasei ahi. Phat chu hunglhunga ahita in katuh tang tahlouhel ding ahi. Keiman kalunggel kakhel louding chule keiman na chunga lainatna jong kanei kit louhel ding ahi. Na chonna gilou tah dungjuiya nangma chungthu kitan ding ahi tin thaneitah Pakaijin aseije. 
Hiti chun Pakaiya kon hiche thu hi kaheng'a ahung lhunge. 
Mihem chapa, nangailut tah nagou chu khatvei seh seh kajepma kalah mang ding ahi. Ahenga amanu thina a chu lunghemna imacha namusah louding ahi. Kap hih in, nalhi jong potsah hih in. 
Guhthim in kap inlang chule amanu khuh chunga chu kaloi loina umlou ding ahi. Naluchang chu hohlha hih in, ahilou le na sandal jong ladoh hih in. Puldou nikho a angaina a houlam dolla chonna ho jong bol hih in, ahilouva nagolpai ho nangma lhem dinga hung hon ahin choiju anneh jong chu nehih in. 
Hijeh chun keiman ajing jing chun mipi ho masanga kasam phongtai, chule nilhah langin kajinu athitai. Ajingkah chun bol dinga eikiseipeh jong chu kabol tai. 
Hichun, mipi ho eidongun, ‘hiche hi ipi tina ham? Ipi nei seipeh nom u ham?’ 
Hijeh chun keiman amaho chu ka seipeh in, Pakaiya kon thukhat kaheng'a ahung lhunge. 
Chule hiche thu hi Israel mite pedinga keima eikiseipeh e. Hiche hi thaneitah Pakai thusei ahi. Nahoidoh na le nakiloupi sahna, nalungthim kipana hung kondohna mun ka Hou-In kasuhboh ding ahi. Nachapa teu leh nachanu teu Juda gam'a nehin dalhah hou chu chemjama kisat lih peh diu ahi. 
Hiteng chule Ezekiel in abol bang banga chu nabol dingu ahi. Mipi lah a nangin pul nadou louding ahilou jongleh nagol napai ten nahin choipeh an jong naneh louding ahi. 
Nalu a pon nakikhuh a chu nakeng chot jong nalah doh louding ahi. Nangin pul nadou ding ahilou jongleh naka louding ahinlah na chonset nau jeh a namoh lung lhahsam diu ahi. Nathilse bolna ho jouse jeh a chu nachangseh a pul nadou ding ahi. 
Ezekiel chu nangho dinga vetsahna ahin, aman anabol bang banga chu nangin jong nahin bol ding ahi. Chule hiche phat chu ahung lhun teng nangin keima thaneitah Pakai kahi nahet doh ding ahi. 
Hichun Pakaijin kaheng'a aseije. “Mihem chapa, akulpiu keiman kalahpeh nikho chun-akipa nau leh aloiupi nau, alungthimu uvin angaichat pen angailut pen tah u ahou hou, keiman achapa teu leh achanu teu jong kalahpeh thading ahi. 
Chule hiche nikho teng chule Jerusalem'a konna hingdoh chu Babylonna nahenga hung uva chule ipi thilsoh em ti naseipeh dingu ahi. 
Chule amapa ahung lhun phat chun phulou helouvin na o hung ging doh in, chutia chu amapa toh nakihou thei lhon ding chule nangma amaho dinga vetsahna a napan ding ahi. Hiteng chuleh ama hon keima hi Pakai kahihi nahet doh diu ahi.
