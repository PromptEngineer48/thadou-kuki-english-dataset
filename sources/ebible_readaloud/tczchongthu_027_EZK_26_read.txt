Ezekiel.
26.
Leng Jehoiachin sohchan kal kum som le ni channa Bulte nithum lhinni hin Pakaiya kon'in hichethu hi kaheng'a ahung lhunge. 
Mihem chapa, Jerusalem lhuhna chunga kipana aneijin, amanu solam jotna haosatah aluina lampi chu akitum hong tai, aha tin aseije. Chule keima agou ding kahitai. Amanu ahom keuva akikoi tah jeh a chu keiman kahao sah ding ahitai. 
Ajeh chu keima thaneitah Pakai thusei ahi. Keima namelmapa kahin, O Tyre, twikhanglen'a twi kinongin vapam atah a tah banga nam tamtah nangma douding kahin puidoh ding ahi. 
ama hon Tyre kulbang ho asuhmang uva chule insang ho chu akoi chim diu ahi. Aleiset ho jouse kakhodoh a song lheo keoseh kahi sah ding ahi. 
Keimahi twikhanglen'a songpi a kapan ding ngaman hon alen asejal nao kahi ding ahi tin keima thaneitah Pakaijin kaseisa ahitai. Amaho namtin vaipi anchah a pang diu ahi. 
Chule agam lailung penna kho jong chu chemjama ka suhmang ding ahi. Hiteng chule ama hon keima Pakai kahi ahet diu ahi. 
Hiche hi Pakai thusei ahi: Keiman Tyre douna leng Nebuchadnezzar chu sahlanga konna kahin puiding ahi. Ama chu leng ho leng ahin chule asakolho, sakol kangtalai ho, kangtalai tolho chule sepai tamtah ahin puiding ahi. 
Amapan nagam lailunga kho ho asuhbei ding ahi. Hitengleh aman leisang asema kulpi nakai khuma chule nangma douna ding lum aset thoma nahin bulu ding ahi. 
ama hon nakul bang ho chu thingtum lentah tah a akhet chim uva chule nainsang ho chu seh a akhet chim diu ahi. 
Sakol tin kika ho chun na khopi chu achil thanga vutvai asoding chule sakol kangtalai le sakol kang ho chun nakul bang ho chu aholing ding, huipi gopi kitho banga kelkot kitum honga chuteng lut diu ahi. 
Amapa sakol tou miho chun nakhopi lamlen jouse achot chin soh diu ahi. Ama hon namite asattel gamuva chule akhompi detlai ho jouse aholing sohdiu ahi. 
ama hon nahaosat najouse uva chu kiveina thil jouse akichom mang uva chule nabang jouse avohchim soh diu ahi. Ama hon nadei tah nain hou asuhmang uva, nasong hou le nain thing chule na lei phalai jouse twikhanglen'a aselut diu ahi. 
Keiman nalasah nau tumging thei jouse kasuh thip ding ahi. Naselang dah gin jouse jong namite lah a kinung jah kit louhel ding ahi. 
Keiman natwikol gam songlheo keuseh kasosah a ngamanho dinga lense namun kahisah ding ahi. Nangin avel'a nanung sah doh kit louhel ding ahi, hijeh a chu kei Pakaijin kasei ahi, henge keima thaneitah Pakaijin kasei ahi. 
Hiche hi thaneitah Pakaijin Tyre henga asei ahi. Nangma lhuh ginna chu twikhanglen pam jouse kihot ling ding ahi. Hichu kithana mun a amachang ho pen pen gin tobang hi ding ahi. 
Kong kai munna vaihom ho jousen alal tounauva konna hung kum lhauva chuleh aleng ponsil chol jong akisutlhah uva chule aponsil hoi lai jouse jong asutlhah diu ahi. Amaho chu nangma kisuhmang najeh a chu tijatna kithing pum puma tolla tou diu ahi. 
Hiteng chuleh nang dinga kap loi loijuva hiche thi lhahna la hi asah diu ahi. O minthang tah a twikol gam khatvei twikhanglen vaihompa iti danna naki sumang jeng hitam? Namite kong sepai thahat nanei ho khatvei vannoi leiset a kichat uma kithang 
Tun twikhanglen pam jouse nangma lhuh jeh in akithonge. Nakitol mang chun twikol gamho alung thoi tai. 
Hiche hi thaneitah Pakai thusei ahi. Keiman Tyre chu midang tamtah ho banga ka suhmanga ahom keva kajam ding ahi. Tijat umtah twikhanglen kinong tobang melmate buluna noija kavui ding nahi. twikhanglen thuhtah chun naval lhum ding ahi. 
Masang laiya anakum suh sa lah a lha dinga kokhuh sunga kasol lhah ding nahi. Na khopi chu ahom keuva kajama leinoiya kavui ding ahitai. Mihing gam'a hi nangma jabolna nachan louhel ding ahitai. 
Keiman nangma kichat tijatna munna kahin puilutna chuleh avel'a naum kit tah louding ahitai. Henge nakihol in ahinlah nangma nakinung mukit louding ahi tia thaneitah Pakaijin kaseidoh sa ahi.
