Ezekiel.
35.
Avellin Pakaiya kon thusei khat ahunglhung kitne. 
Mihem chapa, kiheiyin, chuleh Seir molsang lang khu maingat inlnag chule amite dounan gaothu seikit in. 
Thaneitah Pakaiya kon hiche thu hi seipeh in, keima namelma kahi O Seir molsang! Chuleh keiman nangma douna a kakhut tum kalap a ka suhmang hel ding ahi. 
Keiman nakhopi ho kasuhsetna chule ahomkeuva kajam ding ahi. Hiteng chule nangman keima Pakai kahi ti nahet doh ding ahi. 
Nangin Israel mite kicha-itih nei louva nahot bol jeng chun panpi beija aum laitah u, achonset nau jouse jeh a ka engbolla panpi beija aum laitah un nasatlih sah game. 
Keima kahing jinge tin thaneitah Pakaijin aseije. Nangin thisan naneh nom lou navet sah a panna chu keiman nangma thisan mama nakisil nadinga kapeh ding ahi, naphat hunglhungah ahitai. 
Keiman Seir molsang kam hella kajama, amite jamdoh dinga kigo jouse leh ahung kinung le jouse katha gam ding ahi. 
Keiman na molsang jong mithi kadip ding ahi. Nalhang sangho naphaicham ho chuleh nagam kotong ho jouse chemjama mihem kithatsa kadipset ding ahi. 
Keiman nangma atonsot a kam hel dinga kasem ding nahi. Nakhopi ho avel'a kisapha kit louhel ding ahi. Hiteng chuleh nangin keima Pakai kahi ti neihet doh ding ahi. 
Ijeh inem itile nangin Israel leh Juda gam chu keiho a ahi nati, keihon amaho chu kalo khum diu ahi natiuve. Hichun munna chun Pakai aum'e tia kagel chom diu ham? 
Hijeh a chu tahbeh mong in keiman kahing jinge, tin thaneitah Pakaijin aseije. Keiman na lunghanna nanatoh ho kalethuh ding nahi. Keiman na lunghanna toh nathangsetna chuleh namihotna ho jouse jeh a ka engbol ding nahi. Nangma chunga kathilbol a konna keima tah Israel te lah a ka phondoh ding ahi. 
Hiteng chuleh nangin keima Pakaijin Israel molsang douna a musitna thucheng nasei jouse hi ajadoh taiti nahet doh ding ahi. Ajeh chu nangin amaho hi dalhahsa ahiuvin neh dinga eiho eiki peuvah ahi tin naseije. 
Hiti chun naseijin kei dounan nasatah in naki loupi sah in, chuleh hiche ho jouse chu kajan ahi. 
Hiche hi thuneitah Pakai thusei ahi. Vannoi leiset pumpi hi keiman nangma ahom keuva kakoi tengleh kipah diu ahi. 
Israel gam ahom keuva akijam petna nangin nakipapi ahi. Tua keima nangma chung changa kipah ding kahi. Nangma nahin nangho Seir molsang mite chule Edoma cheng jouse nakithe ngim hel diu ahi. Hiteng chuleh nang hon keima hi Pakai kahi ti nahetdoh diu ahi.
