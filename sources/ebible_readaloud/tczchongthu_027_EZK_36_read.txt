Ezekiel.
36.
Mihem chapa, Israel molsang ho chung changah gaothu seijin. Hiche thu hi amaho khu seipeh in. O Israel molsang ho Pathen thusei hi ngai temun. 
Hiche hi thaneitah Pakai thusei ahi. Namelma teu chun totnan naneijun, hitin aseujuve. Aha! Tun masang laiya munsang lai jouse eiho ichang tauve. 
Hiche jeh chun mihem chapa, Israel molsang ho chu thaneitah Pakaiya kon hiche thusei hi seipeh in. Muntinna kon'in namelma ten nahin bullu tauve. Namtin vaipi nei le gou ding leh midang ho totnop na leh suh minset dingin nasem doh tauve. 
Hijeh chun O Israel molsang ho, thaneitah Pakai thusei ho hi ngai temin. Aman lhang sang ho leh molsang ho gam kokhuh ho leh phaicham ho chule ahomkeu leh alheng lholla kijamsa khopi kisumang saho chuleh akim vella namtin vaipi nuisat ding bepma um nahi tauve tin aseije. 
Hiche hi thaneitah Pakai thusei ahi. Thangse tenna ka lunghanna meikou chu hiche nam mite ho ahideh set in Edom dounan hung koudoh tai. Ajeh chu kagam chu kipah tah in amadingin achom gam un nasatah a deimo aseiju eivetsah un ahi. 
Hiche jeh chun Israel lhang sang ho leh molsang ho kokhuh ho leh phaicham ho dounan gaothu seijin, hiche hi thaneitah Pakai thusei ahi. Nakim vella cheng namtin vaipi ho masanga jachatna nathoh thoh jengu chu keima nasatah in kalung ahang behseh jenge. 
Hiche jeh a chu hiche hi thaneitah Pakai thusei ahi, keiman dettah a kitepna kanei ahitai. Hiche namtin vaipi te chun jachatna athoh diu ahi. 
Ahivangin Israel molsang ho chun kamite dinga theiga tamtah tah asodoh peh ding ahi. Ijeh inem itile amaho agamuva hung kile loi diu ahitai. 
Ven keiman nangma kahoi tupma chule keiman nangma lunggil tah a kaumpi ding nahi, chule na leiset chu kikal bot intin chule namuchi ho chu kitu ding ahi. 
Keiman Israel mijat hapun puna kapunsah ding ahi. Chule khopi ahomkeuva kijamsa ho chu kisa pha kitna chule mihem in alo kit ding ahi. 
Keiman mihem ho keuseh kapunsah louding, chuleh na gancha ho jong kapunsah ding ahi. O Israel molsang ho na chunga hung cheng dinga mihemte khatvei kahin puilut peh ding nahi, keiman nangma masanga nahaosat nasanga kahaosat sah be nahlai ding nahi. Hiteng chule nangman keima hi Pakai kahi ti nahet doh ding ahi. 
Keiman kamite na chunga khatvei kahung lamlen sah kit ding ahi. Chuleh nangma chu agamu nahi ding ahi. Nangman avel'a achateu nachom gam kit louding ahitai. 
Hiche hi thaneitah Pakai thusei chu ahi. Namtin vaipi dang hon totnan naneijun, hitin aseijuve. Israel tehi amaho mite vallhuma chuleh ahinsao achateu kichom mang peh te ahiuve, atiuve. 
Ahinlah nangin namite navallhum kit tah louding ahilouleh nachateu jong na chom gam kit louhel dingu ahitai, tin thaneitah Pakaijin aseije. 
Keiman namdang ten najumso nau thu kajah sah kit loudingu ahi, amaho tot thang hoiya naum kittah louhel diu ahitai. Nangma anam mite lhahsah gam chu nahi louding ahi tin thaneitah Pakaijin aseije. 
Hiti chun Pakaiya kon'in hiche thu hi kaheng'a ahung lhun be kitne. 
Mihem chapa, Israel mite agamsungu mong monguva ana chen laiju chun athilse bol uchin agamsung ana suboh uve. Keija aum chanu chu numei anihsop neijouva apon thisan dimset bangin anen uve. 
ama hon agam sung chu toltha na leh milim doi hounan anasu nen uve, hiche jeh chun kalung han achung uva kana bung lhai. 
Thilse jeng abol jeh u chun amaho engbol nan gamtin'ah kana thethang in ahi. 
Ahinlah amaho namtin vaipi lah a akithe thang u chun kamin theng chungah jumna ahin polut tauve. Ijeh inem itile namtin vaipi ten aseijuvin, amaho hi Pakai mite ahiuvin, ahinlah ama agamsungah bit sellin akoi theipoi atiuve. 
Hichun keiman kamintheng chung changa namtin vaipi masanga jumna asosah u chu kahin he doh tai. 
Hiche jeh chun thaneitah Pakaiya kon hichethu hi Israel mipite chu seipeh in. Keiman kahin nungpui kitnu hi nathil bol phat jeh u ahipoi, keiman kamintheng huhdohna dinga kabol ahi bouve. Namtin vaipi lah a naki thethang laijuva kamintheng tah jachat umtah a nasokhah jeh u ahi bouve. 
Keiman kamintheng chu ichana loupi ham ti kavetsah diu nahi – namtin vaipite lah a jachat umtah a nabollu chu. Chuleh keiman nangho a konna kathenna kahin phondoh tengleh ama hon amit cheh uva amu diu ahi tin thaneitah Pakaijin aseije. 
Keiman nangho namtin vaipi a konna kakhop khomuva chule na gamsung mong monguva nain u kahin lepui kit ding nahiuve. 
Chutengleh keiman nachung uva twitheng kachap khumuva chuel nangho nathen dingu ahi. Nanet nau jouse kisop thenga chule chutia chu nang hon milim doi nahou kit tahlou diu ahitai. 
Chule keiman nangho lungthim thah khat kapeh uva chule lhagao thah khat kakhum peh ding nahiuve. Keiman nalung chang song tobanga louchal tah chu kalah doh peh uva chule keiman nunnem tah, gangtah adonbut jeng thei lungchang kapeh ding nahi. 
Chule keiman kalhagao nasunga kakhum peh a nangman kathupeh phatecha najui theija chule kadan thu ho ching thei tah a najui thei ding ahitai. 
Chule keiman masang laipeh a napu napate kana pehsa Israel gamsunga nachen ding ahi. Nangho kmaite nahiuva chule keima na Pathen'u kahi ding ahi. 
Keiman nachon nau thenglou jousea konna kasil theng uva, keiman lhosoh ga phatah tah kapeh diu, chule keiman agamsunga chu kel kalhah sah kit louding ahitai. 
Keiman nathei phungho nalou ho a konna ga tamtah tah kasosah a chule nakim vella cheng namtin vaipi hon na gamsunga kellhah jeh a nanuisat kitlouhel diu ahitai. 
Hiteng chule nangin nachon setna masa ho chu nageldoh kitna chule kidah dah um thil nabolsa chengse jeh a nang le nang imacha nakigel louding ahi. 
Ahivangin kihet themin tin thaneitah Pakaijin aseije. Keiman hiche hi natoh dungjuiya kabol ahipoi, O kami Israel te, nathilse bolsa hou jeh a jachatna nadimset diu ahi. 
Hiche hi thaneitah Pakai thusei ahi. Keiman na chonset nauva konna kasil theng tengu leh, nakhopi ho jong avel'a mihem kachen sah kit ding, chule khogemsa jong kisathah kit ding ahi. 
Loumun lholouva umsa ho leh dalhahsa phaicham ho miten amujing ho chu louva kithah sem kit ding ahi. 
Chule keiman nangho kahin lepui kit tengu leh mihon hitin seijunte. Ahomkeuva kijamsa gam chu tun Eden hon tobang ahitai tiuvinte. Dalhah sa leh suhchim sa khopi ho chun tun bang lhou tah tah aneijin chule asunga mihemte acheng kittai. 
Namtin vaipi akimvella cheng hon akiledoh kit ahetdoh tengu leh keima Pakaijin khogemsa chu athahsah kit in chule ahom keuva kijamsa munna thingphung akehdoh kittai, ti ahetdoh diu ahi. Ijeh inem itile keima Pakaijin kaseidohsa ahin chule kaseidohsa chu kabol teitei ding ahi ati. 
Hiche hi thaneitah Pakai thusei ahi. Keima Israel taona ngai dingin gotsan kaum jinge, chule kelngoi banga ajatnu punsah dingin. 
Amaho chu kut lai tengleh Jerusalem khopi lamlen a ahon hon'a kilhai gantha dinga atam a tam ho banga hung kijot le diu ahi. Khopi ahom keuva kijamsa ho chu mipi hung dimset kit diu ahin chule mijousen keima hi Pakai kahi ahin hetdoh diu ahi.
