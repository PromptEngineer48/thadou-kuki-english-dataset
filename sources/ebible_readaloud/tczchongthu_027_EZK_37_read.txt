Ezekiel.
37.
Pakaijin akhut in eituh chah kheh in chule Pakai lhagaovin ei pomang in gule chang dimsetna phaichama chun eipuilut e. 
Aman tollhanga gule chang dimsetna phaichama chun eipuilen ahi. Tollhanga chun adimset in gule chang akithang jengin, chule abonchan agosa ahisohkeije. 
Hichun aman eidongin mihem chapa hiche gu ho hi mihing hung soh kit thei diu ham? O thaneipen Pakai tin keiman kadonbut e. Nang changseh in hiche khu adonbutna nahet ahi. 
Hichun aman kajah ah aseijin hiche guho jah a hin gaovin lang thu seijin, chule gu gosa ho Pakai thusei hi ngaijun tin seipeh in. 
Hiche hi thaneitah Pakai thusei ahi, ven keiman nasunga changlhah kakoi peh ding nahi. Chule kahin sah kit ding nahi. 
Keiman tahsa kakoi peh chule, thajung jam ho chule vunna katom ding nahi. Kahin hu nasunga kakhum peh a chule nahung hindoh kit ding ahi. Hiteng chule nangin keima hi Pakai kahi ti nahet doh ding ahi. 
Hijeh chun hiche thusei hi eikiseipeh bang bangin kaseitai. Phulou helouvin ka thusei laitah in phaicham lhungkei jin aphatna phatah gin khat ahung ginge. Tahsa gule chang ho chu ahung kigom khomun chule mihem khat dinga lhingset gu keuseh chun akimat soh tai. 
Keiman hitia kavet jing laijin guho chunga chun thajung ho leh phe ahung kisem doh tai. Hiti chun tahsa chu vun in atom tan ahi. Ahinlah hichan gei jong chun asung uvah haina hu anei hih laijuve. 
Hichun aman kaheng'a ahin seijin, gauvin lang huiho lah a khun thu seijin, mihem chapa gaothu seijin chule hiche hi thuneitah Pakai thusei ahi tin seijin. O haina hu! Chule hiche kolning li a kon hui nungin, hiche tahsa thilong ho hintheina dingin hin haikhumin. 
Hijeh chun aman eithupeh bang bangin keiman kaseitai. Chule haina hu chu atahsauva aluttai. Amaho abonchauvin ahing tauve chule sepai tamtah in akengun ahung dingdoh tauve. 
Hichun aman kaheng'a aseijin, mihem chapa hiche guho hin israelte thalhenga um ahiuve. Ama hon aseijuvin eiho gu gosa isoh gam tauve – kinepna jouse abeitai, inam uhi akichaitai. 
Hijeh chun gauvin lang amaho chung chang thu seijin, hiche hi thaneitah Pakai thusei ahi. O kamite! Keiman kasol mang nau lhankhuh ho kahondoh a chule kathodoh kit ding nahiuve. Hitia chu keiman nangho Israel gam'a chu kahin lepui kitding nahiuve. 
Thil hicheng ahung so teng, O kamite nang hon keima Pakai kahi nahet doh diu ahi. 
Keiman kalhagao nasunga kakhumpeh uva chule nagam uva nain chuh uva nakile kit diu ahi. Hiteng chule nang hon keim Pakaijin kasei ahi ti nahetdoh uva chule kaseisa chu kabol ahi. Henge, Pakaijin asei ahi. 
Pakaiya kon'in kaheng'a thukhat ahunglhung kitne. 
Mihem chapa thing halkhat lan lang chule hiche thucheng ho hi jihdoh in. Hiche hin Juda leh apanpi phung mite thakhel ahiuve. Hiti chun thinghal chom dang khat jong lan lang chule hiche thu ho hi jih doh in. Hiche ho hin Ephraim leh sahlang gam cheng Israel phungho thakhel hidiu ahi. 
Tun hiche thinghal ho chu khat seh bangin nakhut in tuh chah kheh temin. 
Namiten hiche nathilbol chu ipi vetsahna ham tia nadoh tengu leh 
Amaho jah a chun hiche hi thaneitah Pakaijin asei ahi, keiman Ephraim leh sahlang gamkai phung miho chu kapui khoma chule Juda te toh kagop khomm, amaho chu kakhutna thinghal khat seh banga kameh khom ding ahi. 
Hiti chun thinghal thucheng najihna ho chu ama hon amu theina ding uvin dom sangin. 
Chule thaneitah Pakaiya kon hiche thu hi amaho chu seipeh uvin, keiman Israel te chu namtin vaipi a konna kakhop khom dingu ahi tin keiman amaho chu akithecheh naumun jousea kon amaho gamsung mong monga ain cheh uva kapuilut diu ahi. 
Israel molsang chunga chu keiman kagop khomuva nam khat kasem ding ahi. Amaho chu abonchauva leng khat in achung uva vai ahom ding ahilouleh lenggam nia jong kikhen kit talou diu ahi. 
Amaho amilim doi semthuva kon chule kidah um mihem chule doumah bol keipi limho a avel'a kisuboh kit talou diu ahi. Ajeh chu amaho chonsetna akinung hei kit nauva kon kahuhdoh uva, kakisop theng diu ahi. Amaho chu kamite tah tah chu hidiu chule keima a Pathen'u kahi ding ahi. 
Kasohpa David chu amaho lengpa hi ding chule mahon kelngoi ching khat bou anei diu ahi. Ama hon kachondan ho ajuijuva chule kathupeh ho chingthei tah a anit diu ahitai. 
Keiman Jacob kana peh nagam apu apa teu ana chenna gam'a chu cheng diu ahi. Hiche munna amaho hin chule achateu le atuson geijuva amaho nung teng atonsotna akhang khang hung cheng diu ahitai. Chule kasohpa David chu atonsot a alengpau chapa hijing ding ahi. 
Chutengleh keiman amaho to chamna kinoptona khat kasema hiche tonsot kinoptona hi ding ahitai. Keiman agamu kale peh kit a chule amijat kapunsah ding, chule alaijuva atonsotna dinga kaHou-In katun doh ding ahi. 
Keiman amaho lah a chu chenna kaki sema, keima a Pathen'u kahi ding chule amaho kamite hidiu ahi. 
Chule atonsotna dinga kaHou-In alaijuva aum teng, anam mite chun Israel sopthengpa Pakai kahi ahetdoh dingu ahi.
