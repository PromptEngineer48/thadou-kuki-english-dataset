Ezekiel.
38.
Hiche hi Pakaiya konna kaheng'a thudang khat hunglhung chu ahi. 
Mihem chapa kihei jinlang Megog gam'a Gog, Meshech le Tubal nammite chunga vaihom leng chapa lama namai ngat inlang chule amaho dounan gaothu seijin. 
Amapa chu thaneitah Pakaiya kon hiche thu hi seipeh in, Gog, keima hi namelmapa kahi ati. 
Keiman nangma kapei kim vella chule nakhalhanga ngakoi kabupeh a nangma leh nasepai jouse –nasakol jouse galvon lhingsetna kivonna sakol kangtalai touho jouse chukitle namite chemjam le lum kitho na boncha uva kapui mang diu ahi. 
Persia, Ethopia chuleh Libya ho jong agalvon pum piu puma nahin jop diu ahi. 
Gomer leh asepai te jousen Beth-Togamah, sahlang gamkai leh adang tamtah gam latah tah konna sepaihon jong nahin jop diu ahi. 
Gotsan um'in, semtup san um'in, nasepai te chu galsat dingin kigosan koiyin chule ama thupeh pen. 
Phat sottah jou nung tua hi nangma galdou dinga nakikou ding ahitai. Khonung phat sottah jou leh nangin Israel gamsung nahin bokhum suh ding ahi. Gal kichai nunga gam tamtah a kon hung kile kit Israel molsang chunga lungmong tah le kipah tah chengho chunga gal nahin sat ding ahi. 
Nangma le napenpi ho – kichat tijat um leh atama tam sepaiho – huipi gopi banga nahung kitol uva chule agam chu meilom bang natomjol dingu ahi. 
Hiche hi thaneitah Pakai thusei ahi. Hiche phatteng chule na lungsunga lung gilou lut intin chule nangin thil gilou tah nahin gon ding ahi. 
Nangin seijin natin Israel hi ngatuplou gam abang umlou phaichama aum ahi. Keiman amanu douna a kajot suh a lungmong tah a um miho chu ka suhmang ding ahi, tin nate. 
Namtin vaipi a kon delmangsa hung kile kitna, phat masa langa ahom keuva kijamsa khopi hija tua mihem hung chenglut ho kom'a kache ding keiman nei le gou tamtah kichom doh ho kachom khom ding ajeh chu amiho chun gancha ahao theijun chule nei le gou dang tampi jong tun anei tauvin ahi. Ama hon vannoi leiset pumpi hi amaho kimvella kipei danin agellun ahi. 
Ahinlah Sheba leh Dedan chule Tarshish a kivei mihon hing dong uvin tin Sepai nakhop khom hou hin dangka leh sana chom doh jou nauvin te tia nagel uham? Na sepai hou chun agancha hou delmang jouvin tin chule athil hou kilahpeh in tin chule thil kichom doh chengse kipoh mang inge tia nagellu ham? 
Hiti ahijeh chun, Mihem Chapa! Gog dounan gaothu seijin. Thaneitah Pakaiya kon hiche thu hi seipeh in. kamite agamsung uva lungmonga achen tengu leh nang leh nang nahung kitho doh ding ahi. 
Nangma nachenna gam sahlang gamkai mun gam latah a kon'in nasakol chung tou galsat miho leh nasepai tamtah hi tih nahung ding ahi. 
Chule nangin kami Israel te hin bulu in natin, agamsung chu meiyin atom bangin hin tom in nate. Khonung phat sottah jou tengleh mijouse vetdinga kamite douna nangma kahin puija nahung niteng chule thilsoh chun kathenna aphondoh ding ahi. Vo Gog, hitia chu namtin vaipin keima hi Pakai kahi ti ahin hetdoh dingu ahi. 
Hiche hi thaneitah Pakaijin athudoh chu ahi. Nangma hi phat sottah masanga kana houlimpi pa chu nahim, Israel themgao ho a konna khonung thu ding kiphondoh nachu. Keiman nangma chu kamite douna a kahin puiding nahi tia kasei khah em? 
Ahivangin hiche thaneitah Pakai thusei ahi. Gog in Israel te ahin delkhum tengleh kalung satna chu twi souva sou ding ahi. 
Kathangset na le ka lunghanna deu jap jap a chu, hiche niteng chule Israel gamsung nasa tah a kaholing ding ahi tin kakihahsel tai. 
Thil hingthei jouse – twikhanglen'a nga ho hihen, van tham jolla leng le vacha ho hihen, tolhanga lhale ganhing ho hihen ganhing neucha cha kalphi chetna lhaiho hihen, chule leiset chunga mihem jouse – ka angsunga kihot ling soh diu ahi. Molsang jouse kihot nem soh a, kol tah ho jong kihot chip soh ding, bang ho jong leisetna lhu gam diu ahi. 
Keiman Israel lhang sang ho chunga chu nangma doudinga chemjam kikoi ding ahi tin thaneitah Pakaijin aseije. Namiten achemjam u amaho le amaho khat leh khat douna alap dingu ahi. 
Keiman nangma leh nasepai ho natna hise le thisan lonna ka engbol ding; keiman gotwi long hoh hoh banga kajuh sah a, gel kalhah sah a, mei le kath meika hicheng hi kahin solding ahi. 
Hitia hi keiman kaloupina le kathenna ho kei kei ken vannoi leiset chitin namtin te jouse hettheija kakiphon ding ahi. Hitia chu ama hon keihi Pakai kahi ahet diu ahi.
