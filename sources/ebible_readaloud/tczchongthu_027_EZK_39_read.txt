Ezekiel.
39.
Mihem chapa! Gog dounan gaothu seijin. Thaneitah Pakaiya kon hiche thu hi amapa chu seipeh in. keima namelmapa kahi O Gog, Meshech le Tubal nam mite chunga vaihompa. 
Keiman nangma kahei kollea chule Israel molsang langa kanodoh ding nahi, hiche dinga chu sahlang gam kai mun gam latah a konna kahin pui nahi. 
Keiman nakhu vei langa nathal choi chule nathal changho nakhut jetlam'a nachoi ho chu kabeh lhah peh a chule nakithopi ding beija kadalhah ding nahi. 
Nangma nahin chule nasepai te ahiuvin chule napan khompi ho jouse molsang chunga chu na thiden gam diu ahi. Keiman nangho muthong ho leh gamsa ho vahna kanei dingu nahi. 
Nangho tollhanga chu nalhuh gam diu ahi, ajeh chu keima thaneitah Pakaijin kaseisa ahi. 
Chule keiman Megog le apanpi ho twikhanglen panga lung mong tah chengho jouse chunga meikou gojuh a kajuh lhah sah ding ahi. Hiteng chule nang hon keima Pakai kahi nahet doh dingu ahi. 
Hiche nikho a hi keiman kamite Israel te lah a kamin theng hetsah na kanei ding ahi. Keiman koimacha jachatna hiche chunga kapohlut sah louding ahi. Chule namtin vaipin jong keima Pakai hi Israel mithengpa kahi ahetdoh diu ahi. 
Hiche nikho chuleh thutan hung um ding ahi tin thaneitah Pakaijin aseije. Ima jouse kasei dohsa bang banga hung soh ding ahi. 
Hiteng chule Israel khopi chunga miho chu chedoh untin chule nalum ho, thalho, chule thalchang ho, tengcha neuho chule tengcha alen aneuva aga chomkhom uva chule meithinga aman dingu ahi. Ama hon kum sagi sunga nengchetna akiti diu ahi. 
ama hon loujau lah leh gammang lah a ti ding thing agasat u ngai talou ding ahi, ajeh chu hiche galamanchah ho chun angaichat chan uva apeh diu ahitai. Amaho chom dinga kigo chu ama hon achom uva chule athil keuvu ana lahpeh te chu athil keuvu alahpeh diu ahi tin thaneitah Pakaijin aseije. 
Chule keiman Gog leh amite dinga Twithi Solam sang, kholjin miho phaicham munna chu lhanmol lentah kasem peh diu ahi. Hichun hiche mun jotpa ho lampi chu akha ding chule ama hon hichelai mun chu amin akhelluva Gog leh amite phaicham kiti ding ahitai. 
Agamsung suhtheng na ding leh thilong ho vuidohna ding chu mihem hon lha sagi sung alah diu ahi. 
Mijouse kithokhom diu ahi, ijeh inem itile Israel tedinga jong niloupini hi ding ahin hiche ni chu kaloupi kahilchet nikho hi ding ahi, tin thaneitah Pakaijin aseije. 
Lha sagi jou teng chuleh pasal phabep khat alhendoh uva amaho chun mihem aholdoh uva avui diu dingu hitia chu gamsung asuh theng kit dingu ahi. 
Hoilai mun hijongleh gu akimudoh a ahile amelchihna asem uva, hijeh a chu gule chang vuidinga kinganse hon Gog le amite phaichama chu avui diu ahi. 
(Hiche mun chu khopi khat amin Hamonah kiti khat umintin, hichu Gog mite tina ahi) chule chutia chu achaina leh gamsung kisutheng ding ahi. 
Chule tun, mihem chapa hiche hi thaneitah Pakai thusei ahi, vacha ho leh gam'a gamsa ho chu kouvin, hungun hung kikhom uvin lang gamla tah tah a kon chule anaija kon'in Israel molsang chunga hungunlang keiman kilhaina gantha golvah loupi tah kabolpeh uva hiche sa naneh uva chule thisan nadon dingu ahi. 
Pasal thahat ho sa neuvin chule leng chapa te thisan don uvin – amaho chu Bushan gamsunga kelngoi chal ho, kelngoi ho, kelchal ho chule bongchal ho – gancha thao ho jousen hung neuvin tin amaho chu seipeh in. 
Nachim set kahsen nengchet in nalung nachim set in nen lang nang le nang kivahva in chule nakhamset kahsen thisan jong chu don don jengin. Hiche hi nangho dinga kagotpeh kilhaina gantha golvah chu ahi. 
Kagolvahna ankong dokhang chunga chu sakolho sa, chule sakol chunga tou mithahat ho sa chule galsatna hangsan ho jouse sa chu hung neuvin tia thahat tah Pakai chun aseije. 
Hitia hi keiman namtin vaipi ho henga kaloupi kahil chet ding ahi. Keiman amaho ka engbolla kasuh natna mitinin avet uva chule kakhut tuma kakhet teng kathahat dan chu ahetdoh dingu ahi. 
Chule hiche akipatna chu Israel ten keima a Pakai a Pathen'u kahi ahetdoh diu ahi. 
Namtin vaipin ipi jeh a Israel te hi kasol mang hiu ham ti ahetdoh uva – hichu a Pathen uva dinga akitah lou nau chonsetna jeh a engbolna chu ahi. Hijeh a chu keiman kaki heimang sanna chule melmate suhmang dinga kana peh soh ahitai. 
Keiman kamai kahei manga akisuhboh jeh u leh achonset jeh uva engbolna ahi. 
Hijeh a chu tua hi thaneitah Pakai thusei ahi kamite sohchanna a kasuh tang ding ahi. Keiman Israel te jouse chunga hepina kanei ding, ajeh inem itile keiman kamintheng dinga katom panna kachin jing ding ahi. 
Amaho ho kicha ding beihella agamsung uva lung mong tah a ahung chen tengu leh masanga ajachatna hou leh tahsan aum louna hou jouse jeh a chu amopohna hou jouse akisan thei diu ahitai. 
Keiman amaho amelma teuva konna ain lamuva kahin lepui tengu leh namtin vaipi mitmu anga amaho lah a kathenna hi kavetsah ding ahi. 
Hiteng chule ka miten keima hi a Pakai a Pathen u kahi ti ahetdoh diu, ijeh inem itile keiman amaho chu sohchanga kasol mang a chule ain languva kahin puilut kitnu ahi. Keiman kamite khatcha jong kanunga kadalhah louhel diu ahi. 
Chule keiman amaho a konna kamai kahei mang kit tah louding ahi, ijeh inem itile keiman kamite Israel te chunga ka lhagao kasun lhah ding ahi tin thaneitah Pakaijin aseije.
