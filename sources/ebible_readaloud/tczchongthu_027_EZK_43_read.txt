Ezekiel.
43.
Hicheng jou nung chun mipa chun solam kotpi vella chun eilepui lutne. 
Phulou helouvin Pathen loupina chu solam langa kon chun ahung kilah e. Ama hung kitol gin chu twi tampi kitol gin toh abangin, chule leiset pumpi chu aloupinan asalvah e. 
Hiche gao thilmu jong hi adanga kamu ho toh abangin, amasan Kebar vadung panga chule Jerusalem sumang dinga ahungna hichun keiman kamai tolla kasulut jenge. 
Chule Pakai loupina chu solam kotpi a kon'in Hou-In sungah ahung lutne. 
Hichun lhagao chun eihung pui touvin, thutanna sungnung langa chun eipuilutne. Chule Pakai loupina chun Hou-In adipset jenge. 
Chule keiman Hou-In sunga konna kaheng'a thusei khat kajai. Hichun Hou-In te a pang pa chu kakom'a ana dinge. 
Pakaijin kaheng'a aseijin mihem chapa hiche hi kalal touna ahin, chuleh kakeng kangapna ding mun chu ahi. Keima atonsotna Israel tetoh kachen khom ding ahi. Amaho aleng teuvin kamin asuh boh kit talou diu ahi. Jonthanhoi dimsetna Pathen dang ahou nauva kon ahilouleh aleng teu thisa ho ajabol nauva konna 
ama hon milim doi maicham chu keija ban tah a akoijun hichu bangin keija leh amaho chu akhen mai mai bou ahi. Ama hon kidah dah uma chonset nauva chu kamintheng asuhboh jiu ahin, hijeh a chu keiman ka lunghanna a chu kabah gamu ahi. 
Tun ama hon pathen dang hou nga tauhen, chuleh aleng teu thisa ho ajabol nau. Chule keima atonsotna amaho lah a kachen ding ahi. 
Mihem chapa keiman kavetsah Hou-In chu Israel mite kom'a kicheh tah in seipeh in. Chutileh achonset nau hou jouse a kijapi diu ahi. Athilgon hou kihil uhen. 
Chuleh athilbol sa houva chu jahcha diu ahi. Hou-In kisem ding dan kicheh tah kavetsah chu kicheh tah in hilchen in alutna ding leh apotdohna ding ho pumin chuleh aboncha keijin thupeh ho leh chondan ho seipeh in. Hiche ho chung chang chu kicheh tah in sutdoh peh in. Chu thupeh avet uva hitia chu ageldoh kitnuva chule ajui theina diuvin. 
Chule hiche Hou-In athenna den jouse kipatna ahin, Hou-In kisahna molsang pumpi hi thenga ahi. Henge, hiche Hou-In chonna dan bulpi ahi. 
Hiche ho hi maicham tena chu ahi. Maicham kimvella chun twikai aumin, hichu inche somni le khat a thuh ahin, chule avai inche somni le khat ma ahi. Atuhna aning chan geija inche ko ahi. Hiche hi maicham asan dan ahi. 
Maicham chu atuhna a patna feet thumle akeh’a kikhang sang ahin, hichun maicham chu akol lhung kei ahi. Chuleh hichu athuh lam inche somi le khat ahin, chuleh availam inche somni le khat ahi. Anoinung jum laiya pat maicham chu feet sagia sang ahin, availam inche somni le khat a achung lang ajum chan geija kikhang doh ahi. 
Maicham chun tapkong chu feet sagia sanga kikhang sang ahin, aninglia kipatna saki hung kitung doh ahi. 
Maicham chung chu aningli ahin, hichu inche somni le khat a konna inche somni le khat ma ahi. 
Achunglang ajum lai jong chu aningli ma asem ahin, hichu ateng dungjuiya feet somni le khat le akeh chule somni le khat ma ahin, hichu twikai inche somni le khat chule atuhna inche som le akeh agei lhung keija toh hitha ahi. Hiche a chu maicham solam lang sanga chun kalbi dang eat achetouvin ahi. 
Hichun aman kaheng'a aseijin mihem chapa hiche thaneitah Pakai thusei ahi. Hiche ho hi maicham pumgo thilto kibol ding dan ho chu ahi. Chule maicham akisah tengleh thisan thekhum dan ho ahi. 
Hiche phatteng chule Zadok insung mi Levi te thempu ho ka angsunga kakin bolla pangho chu chonset thilto dinga bongchal gol khat kipe ding ahi tin thaneitah Pakaijin aseije. 
Nang thisan them khat naham doh a maichama saki li ho chunga chu naloi ding ahi. Achunglang aningli laiya chu nanu ding ahi. Chuleh agei kimvel lhung keija atunna a chu nanu tha ding ahi. Hiche chun maicham asuhtheng na leh chonset thoidamna hi ding ahi. 
Chuteng chonset thilto bongchal lagol chu alah a chule Hou-In kimvella lhendohsa kikoi laiya chu agovam ding ahi. 
Anini channi leh chonset thilto kilhaina dinga chu nolnabei kelchal gol khat nato ding ahi. Chutengleh bongchal khat na chonset suhthengna leh thoidamna nabol bang banga chu maicham suhthengna leh thoidamna nabol ding ahi. 
Hiche suhthenga kin chu nabol jou tengleh nolnabei bongchal dang khat leh nolnabei khat ma a kon lah a ahkonna nalah doh a nato ding ahi. 
Nangin hiche ho chu Pakaiya natodoh a chule thempuhon hiche ho chunga chu chi athekhum uva chule Pakaiya dinga pumgo thilto a ato dingu ahi. 
Hitia chu nisagi sunga kelchal khat, bongchal gol khat, chuleh kelngoi khat ahonlah a konna nalah doh a chonset thilto kilhaina dinga nato ding ahi. Hiche gancha ho chu nolnabei seisetna bei cheh hidiu ahi. 
Hitia hi nisagi sunga niseh leh maicham dinga suhthengna leh lhatdohna nabol peh peh ding ahi. Athenga manchah dinga asekhen diu ahi. 
Nisagi lhingset chu aboljou tengu leh aniget channi leh maicham chunga chu thempu hon govam thilto kilhaina abol diu chule mipi ho dinga chamna thilto ato doh diu ahi. Chutengleh keiman nang kakisan lut diu nahi, keima thaneitah Pakai kitipan kaseidoh sa ahi.
