Hosea.
2.
Hicheni teng chuleh, nasopipa chu Ami- ‘Kamite’ chule nasopinu chu-Rahamah- ‘Kangailut khat’ nati ding ahi. 
Nahingnu chu dondauvin, Dondauvin hitobang numei hi kaji ahipoi, keijong amadin aji kahipoi, ajon maiput hi kheldohhen, anoiteni kaha ajon na thangnam jong chu sutheng hen. 
Achuti louva ahile keiman avon jouse kahodoh’a akeoseh’a kaum sah ding ahi. 
Achate jouse jong kangailut louhel ding ahi, ajeh chu ahung kigil’uva kon’a notia hung kigil ahi tauve 
Ahingnu-u jong jachatna nei louva notia kijoh ahin, anaovop’a jong chu jumle ja helouva chon ahi. 
Amanun aseijin, eingailu midang ho kom'a lhaitange, kaneh ding an, kadonding twi eipeho chule kasilding samul le tupat ponnem eipeho kom'a chetange ati. 
Angailu ho kom’a lhai jongleh, anungu aphah jou louding ahitai, amaho chu holle le jongleh mujong amujou louding ahitai. Chule amanun jong asei ding, tun chengting kaji masapa henga kilekit tange, ajeh chu tulai kaumchan sangin chulaija kaumchan anomjoi. 
Hiche numeinu hin kana thilpeh ho anei jouse, sana, dangka, lengpiwi thah, olive thao, kathilpeh ho jouse Baal doi apegam tan ahi. 
Ahinlah kachang jong a atphat le kakilelah kit ding, lengpitwi thah jouse jong, kakilelah ding ahi. Asagoh keu hupna dinga kanapeh samul ponsil le tupat ponnem jong kanung lah kit ding ahi. 
Tua hi akideipi ho mitmu changtah a, ajamo kahodohpeh ding ahi, ahin kakhut a konna koiman amanu hi eichulhah jou louding ahi. 
Agolnop kut hihenlang, a lhathah kut hihenlang, akumlhun kut hijongleh kasuhbei ding ahi. 
Alengpi gui jouse kavohsetna, chule atheichang phung jouse jong kavoh heo ding ahi. 
Baal doi masanga gimnamtui ahalna ho kasuhset peh’a chule a sana jempah chengsea akivonna, adongma akideipi hotoh kei suhmilna dinga akijepna jeh a talen kapeh ding ahi, tin Pakaijin aseiye. 
Ahin keiman hichenu hi kanung jollhah kitna, gamthip noiya kapui mang ding, chule awnem heuva kaki houlimpi ding ahi. 
Alengpi leiya kahin lepui kitna, Achor phaicham jong kinepna kotpi phung kasosah ding, hikom'a chu amanun jong anungah lai banga adonbutna, Egypt gam'a ahung potdoh til banga eidonbut ding ahi. 
Hiche nikho chu ahunglhun nile Pakaijin aseiye, Ka Pakai tilouva “Kajipa tia neikou ding ahi”. 
O Israel, namuha konna Baal min tamtah ka suhmanga chule naminphah kittahlou ding ahi chule 
Hiche nikho chule gam'a gamsa jouse le chunga leng vacha jouse toh kitepna khat kasema, thalpi kasuh beija chemjam kalensah louding, chuteng nakichat tahlou dingu, cham le lungmonga naimut dingu ahi. 
Keiman atonsot’a kajinua kasem ding nahitai, chutengleh Chondihna le thutah kavetsah ding nahi. 
Hitia chu ken kitahna neitah-a jia kanei ding nahin, nangin jong Pakai chu nahetdoh ding ahiye. 
Hicheni chule, keiman nadonbut inge, Tin Pakaijin aseiye. “Hiche nikho tengleh keiman van mun ho jouse kahouva thu kasei ding, ama hon jong, van mun jousen jong leiset jaha gojuh puma thu asei diu ahi. 
Chutenglehiset in jong awgin puma penga asapma adonbutna, chang, lengpitwi, chule olive thaovin jong adonbut ding, Jezeel jaha thu aseidiu ahi. 
Hiche phatteng chule keiman Israel dinga muchi katu ding, keima dinga kakhansah ding. Keiman ‘kangailupoi’ katiho kom'a ngailutna kavetsah ding, chule kami nahitapouve tia kaseiho kom'a ‘tun nangho kamite nahi tauve’ tia kasei ding ahi. Chule ama hon eidonbutnuva, ‘Nangma ka Pathen'u nahi’ eitidiu ahi.
