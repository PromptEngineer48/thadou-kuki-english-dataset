Hosea.
4.
Pakai thusei ngaiyuvin, O Israel mite! Ajeh chu Pakaijin gamsunga chengte toh kinelna khat aneiye. “Kitahna aumpoi, kikhotona jong aumpoi, na gamsung uva Pathen hetna jong aumpoi. 
Kihahselna jeng alengin, jouthu jeng akiseiyin, na kithatun chule naguchauvin, jonthanhoi nabollun, muntin jousea nakisatun, khat le khat nakithat touvin ahi. 
Hijeh chun na gamsungu hi mihem lunghem bangin alunghemtan, gamsunga cheng miho jong alunggimsoh tauve. Chule mijousen phat namoh mansahuvin, gamlah a gamsaho jeng jong alunghem sohtauvin, chunga leng vachate jeng jong alunghem gam tauve. Twilah a nga ho jeng jong, aum gam tapouve. 
Koimacha nakhutjungin koh hihin, chule akiheh tojong koima umda jenghen! Ajeh chu keitoh kinelkal, nangjoh nahi, vo thempu. 
Hijeha chu sunlai changa jong nakipal lhuh ding, jan tengleh themgao pa jong nakipal lhuh tha ding ahi. 
Kamite chu hetna alhahsam jeh un amang gam tauve. 
Thempu atam chenin, chonse kei douding atame. 
Miten chonset thilto ahin pohlut chanin, thempuho athaouve. 
Chule thempun bolkhat aneile, mipi ten jong abolluve. 
ama hon neuvin tin, ahijeng vanga gilkel ding ahiuve. Notin panguvin tin hinlah pungthei louding ahiuve. Ajeh chu Pakai dalhan notia pang dingin, donlouvin akoitauve. 
Ju jeng, juthah jeng adonuvin, jun kamite aguhmang ahitai. 
ama hon thing khat pou adongun, molkang khat touvin akhonung thu-u aseipeh theiding atahsanun ahi. Milim doihou angaichat chun, angolsah-u ahitai. Ama hon noti hi akichepnauvin, pathen dang ajenun ahi. 
ama hon molsangvumah, kilhainan gan athatuve. Amaho lhangvumah akaltouvun, gimnamtui ahaluve. 
Ahinlah ibola kagimbol dingham natia apanu leh ajonthanhoi jeh a? O hetna lhasam mingol ho! thil hetkhanna nanotthap tauve. Namiten jong amaho bol bang bang abolluvin ahi. 
Israel, nang notin pangjeng jongleh chun, Juda vang themmo chansah loujenin. Gilgal khopia hunglut dan, Beth-aven a jong hung lutdan. 
Bongla gol louchal bangin Israel hi alou challe. Pakaijin hamhing gam lentah a kelngoi avah banga, Israel te hi avah jing jeng ding hinam ong? 
Israel hi achangin dalhan, ajeh chu ama hi jonthanhoi toh kicheng ahitai. 
Jukham hon khat chu noti ho toh alumkhomtauve. Aloupi diu sangin ajachat diu adeijouve. 
Khoppi huijin alhaving tenin achun chah kheh in Maicham hijat asemthu jouse jeh a hi kijumso diu ahi.
