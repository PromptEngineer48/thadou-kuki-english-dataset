Hosea.
7.
Keiman Israel kasuhdam nome, ahinlah achonset nao asang behsehe. Samaria hi jou le nal jeng seinaa dimset jeng ahiuve, Gucha insunga alut tan, michom ho polama ading jingui! 
Ken kamu sohlam koiman ahedeh pouve. Achonset nauvin alonvuh dimu, kamusohkeiye. 
Gitlouna thil abolluvin, lengpa geiyin akipasahuvin, Lengpa chate jengin jong ajou thilbolu chu akipapiuve. 
Abonchauva hi mitoh kisubohsa jeng ahiuvin, Meilhum sunga kihulsa tobang jeng ahiuvin, Amaho hi tapkonga meilhum asat kingah tobang chol kiso changbong akichankah kingah tobang ahiuve. 
Cholngah nin leng chapten ju adonun, tot chavai hotoh totnan mi anuisat khom. 
Alungsungu jong lhummei salai abangin, jan khovahin lungdam mon aum’un. Jingkah leh mei salah lah bongin adiu jeju jiuve. 
Lhumphung salai abangun, alamkai hou jong avallhum sohun, Alenghou jong aban banin athat-un, ahinlah koima chan kakom'a panpina athum pouve. 
Israel te namtin vaipi pathen helou hotoh akihal sohtan, Changlhah kikang min sohlou tobangbep ahi tauve. 
Gamchom mite pathen ahou-uvin, atha alhalhop sahin, nahinlah ama hon akihet pouve. Aluchunga asamjeng jong kangsoh ding ahitan, ahinlah akihet polaiye. 
Israel kiletsahna chu amaho hehin apangin ahinla amaho Pakai henga ahung kiledeh pouvin, holding jong agong pouve. 
Israel mite hi vakhu banga lungthim bei le nuijat umtah ahiuve. Egypt mitepou akouvin, Assyria gamsung lam pouva akitoluve. 
Chule achung uva len kikhu khum ding, chunga leng vacha banga kamat ding ahiuve, athilse bol chengseu jeh a kabolgim dingu ahi. 
Ohe ada ahiuve, ajeh chu kakom'a kon'in akihei manguvin, eidouvin apang uve. 
Lung thengsellin kakom'a akappouve, hiche sang chun, ajalkhun chung dunguva atouvun, angahun ahi. Amaho leh amaho akisun chan chanun semthu milim pathen kom'a henga ju akithum un, kakom'a imacha athum nompuve. 
Keiman kahiluva kahatsah nom vanguvin, kei dounan thilse agong gonguve. 
ama hon Chungnungpen velouvin, muntin avele leuvin ahi. Amaho panabei thalpi chang lengkoi lebep ahiuve, agalmi te hon alamkaihou athading. Chuteng Egypt miten anuisat diu ahi.
