Hosea.
11.
Israel chu aneolaiyin kangailui, chule kachapa chu Egypt gam'a kon'in kakoudoh’e. Kaha kouva ahile, gamla cheh cheh in eidalhauvin. Baal doiho ahouvin, kilhaina gantha abolluvin ahi. Milim doi kisemthu ho pou chu ahouvun Gimnamtui alhutun ahi. 
Keima tah in Israel alamjot ding dan kahil ahi, kakhutah-a katua kana lamkaiyu ahi. Ahinla aman ima ahepon, donin jong eiseppoi. Hitiho chu keima kanahi lam jong ahepouve. 
Keiman ngailutna khaoguile voujang natna khaovin kana lamkaiyin, angonguva namkol kibut lahdohpeha pangpa tobang kanahin chule anehdiu kana luipehe. 
Ahinlah hichan geijin kamite kahenglama ahung kile pouvin ahi. hijeh chun Egypt gamah kinungle uvin tin, hunam in Assyria jennauvinte. 
Akhopi hou chunga gal kisat um ding, kelkot phunga kotgol jouse ataboh sohkeija kulpi phungdunga chemjam alapdiu ahi. 
Kamite keiya konna kinungtol kit diu, Chunung Penpa tia eikoudiu, Ahinlah jana tahbeh eipenom hih unte. 
O Israel keiman nangho iti kadop sang thei diu ham? Keiman nangho hi iti kasoldoh thei dingu ham? Keiman nangho hi Admah banga iti ka suhmang thei dingu ham? Ahiloule Keiman nangma hi iti Zeboiim tobanga kakoi ding ham? Kalungchangsunglamah akibotkehin chule kami khotona aletlhatai. 
Kalungnat tijat chu boldating, Israel suhmang kitdin gong taponge. Ajeh chu keima Pathen kahin, mihem kahipoi. Keima Atheng, nalahuva um chu kahin, nangho hung suhmang ponge. 
Itihham nikho leh mipi ten kanung ahinjui dingu ahi. Kei, Pakai, Keipi banga kitum ding, Kakitumteng, kamite kitihot puma lhumlam-a konna hung kile diu ahi. 
Vacha honte banga amaho Egype gam'a konna hung diu ahi. Vapalte banga, ti-kiling dingu, amaho Assyria gam'a konna hung kinung lediu ahi. Chule keiman inpia kapui lutkit dingu ahi, tin Pakaijin aseiye. 
Israel in jou le nalin ei umkimvelin Ahinlah Judah in Pathen thusei angai nalaiyuvin chule amaho Athengpa dinga tahsan umtah ahiuve.
