Pakaijin hiche thu hi Pethuel chapa Joel henga anasei ahi. 
Hiche thu hi ngaijun, nangho mipite chunga lamkaihon ngaijun, gamsung’a cheng jousen, Na thusimuva hitobang hi sohkha mong nam? 
Hitobang thu hunglhung ding hi nachateu henga seipeh’un, chule nachateuvin jong achateu heng’a aseipeh son dingu ahi. Thusim hi akhang akhanga kiseipeh son peh ding ahi. 
Khaokhopin anche louhing aneh jouvin, amoh cheng Khaokhopi chom dangin anekit in, abankit in jong khaokho dang hon anen akichainan jong khaokhopi chom khat in anekit in ahi. 
Khangun, nangho jukham ho, chuleh kapun, na kamsung uva ju lhumkei akisuh mong jeh'in, judon hat jouse kap sohkeiyun. 
Ka gal mite simsen lou gal sat dingin kagamsunga ahung kitollut tan ahi, hattah ahiuvin, atam dan ajat jong simsenlou ahiuvin, aha-ujong ahemdan keipi bahkai ha abangin ahi. 
Ka lengpi lei le ka theichang phung jouse jong eisuh gampeh tauvin ahi, abahgeijin amalngimun agolhasoh heltan tai. 
Avoma kijem nungah jinei joutil kabangin, 
Pakai Hou-In sunga lhosoh leh Lengpiga thilto ho jong abeisohtan, hijeh chun Pakai Thempu kin bolla pang lhacha ho jong alunghem gamtauvin ahi, chuleh Pakai Thempuho jong akaptauve. 
Loulai jouse jong aki sugamsohin, gamsung jong abeitai, gasoh jouse jong aki sumong sohtan, lengpithei jong aga tapon chuleh olive thao jong akichai gam tai. 
Vo loulhouho kinepna beijin lunglhauvin, Vo lengpilei lhouho, puldouvun Kapun, ajeh chu loulaija gasoh suhlou chang leh sakol chang abeitah jeh'in, 
Lengpigui agop tan chuleh theichang phung agop lhatan, kolbuthei phung, tum phung chuleh theithup phung, chule thingga phung jouse agop lhasoh tan, mijouse kipana jong agop lhasohtan ahi. 
Nangho thempuho jousen khaodip pon sillin kivonun lang, Pakai kinbol Jousen jan teng maicham phunga hungun, kap’un, ajeh chu na Pathen Hou-In a na lhosoh thilto ding leh lengpitwi thilto ding abeisohtan ahi. 
An ngolding phongdoh un, Pakai Hou-In sung leh gamsung a mipite lultah in kou khomun, gamsunga lamkaiho jouse koukhomun, chuleh hichea chun kap jahuvin.
