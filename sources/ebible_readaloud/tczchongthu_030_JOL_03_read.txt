Joel.
3.
Hiche thilsoh laitah ding chu Pakaijin aseije, “Chuteng chuleh keiman Juda gamsung leh Jerusalem khopi hamphatna kasemphat teng, 
Leiset pumpia galmijouse Jehosaphet phaichama kakhop khom ding, Hichea chu thu katan ding ahi. Ajeh chu ama hon Israel nam hi namtin vaipi lah a alhoh thangsohkeiuvin, ka gamsung geijin akihomtauve”. 
Chuleh kamite chu soh dingin vang asanuvin, pasal chapang ho jong numei noti ho toh akilhehjiuvin, nungah khat geijin jong lengpitwi man din akijohjiuve, chule lengpitwi chu akham sasan adon jiuve. 
Kei douna a ipibol nahiuvem, Tur le Sidon gam chule Philistia? Kei dinga nangho hi ipia kibol nahiuvem? Kei chunga phula dinga kigong nahiuvem? Nanghon kachunga phulah ding nagotuva ahileh, keiman jong natohnau chu hetman loukaha nachung uva ka sunlhahpeh diu ahi. 
Ajeh chu nang hon keima dangka leh sana nalauvin, kakhol tupsa thil manlutah tah ho jeng jong na pomanguvin gamchom mite indoi houna a na koitauve. 
Nanghon Juda mite le Jerusalem khomite chu Greek mite henga na joh monguvin, gamgi kal lang peha nathah mong sohtauve. 
Ahinlah keiman na johmong namun uva konna ka nung lah kit ding ahi. 
Keiman nachateu numeiho le pasal ho jong Juda mite kom'a ka joh duha, ama hon Jong hetphah lou Seba gam mite henga ajoh mong kit diu ahi. Ajeh chu Pakai keiman kana seisa ahitai. 
Gaphong jal uvin hiche namtin vaipi lah a . “Galsat dingin kigontauvin, gollhang thahat jouse kikoudohun, mithahat gollhang jouse kitilkhou sohkei’uvin. 
Na sehcha’u chemjamin kikhenun, na kangkuiju chutengchan kikhen unlang, milhasam tah jong chun “Galhat kahije” tin seijuhen. 
Kintah in kikhom loiyun, akimvella um natin vaipi jouse phaichama hung kihom loiyun. Chuleh tun, Aw Pakai nagal mite koudoh in. 
Namtin vaipi amaho leh amaho hungkon uhen, Jehosaphet phaicham a Kijot uhen, Keima, Pakaijin hichea chu katouva Thu katan ding ahi. 
Koite kigotin chang-at ding aminin akuitai. Hung inlang lengpiga hung chilin, lengpiga chilna adimsoh tai. Bel le kong jouse jong aletlha soh tai. Ajeh chu hiche mite agit louna’u anasa behseh jengin ahi. 
Thutanna phaicham a asang asangin angah tai, Pakai nikho chu hunglhungvah ding ahitai. 
Nisa le lhathah hung thim ding, chule ahsi ho jong vahta louding ahi. 
Pakai Awgin Ziona kithong ding chuleh Jerusalem'a konna keh lhading chuleh Van le leiset kiling ding, ahin Pakai chu amite kiselna hi ding, Israel mite dinga kiselna kulpi hi ding ahi. 
Hiteng chuleh keima na Pakai na Pathen'u, Zion lhang thenga cheng kahi neihetdoh dingu ahi. Jerusalem a imatih chana theng jing jeng ding, chuleh gamchoma gal miten imatih chana ajo louding ahi. 
Hiche nikho teng chuleh mol chunga konna Lengpitwi lhum hung longa, lhangdung jouse jong bongnoi hung long ding. Juda gamsung’a vacha hom jouse twi dim soha, Jerusalem Pakai Hou-In Thenga konna, Acacias phaicham geija hung long lha ding ahi. 
Ahinlah Egyp gamsung ahom keova hung lum ding, Edom jong ahom keova Gamthip hung soh ding, ajeh chu ama hon Juda mite chu pumhat jengin anabol uvin, agamsung uva themmona bei mite thisan anasouvin ahi. 
“Ahinlah Juda gamhi a imatih chana amite dimset ding, chuleh Jerusalem Jong akhang khanga amiten kichenpi ding ahi. 
Keiman kamite asuhkhel nau ka ngaidam ding chuleh keima Pakaijin Jerusalem a, ka mite jouse toh ka in ka sah ding ahi.”
