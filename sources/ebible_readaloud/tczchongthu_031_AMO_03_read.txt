Amos.
3.
Pakaijin nangho douna thu asei hi ngai jun, Vo Israel leh Judah mite, keiman Egypt gam’a kon’a kahin puidoh na insung pumpi douna-a kisei ahi. 
“Leiset chung mihem insung jouse lah a, keiman nangho bou kahet nahiuve. Hijeh a chu na themmonau jouse chung’a, tei tu ding nahiuve” 
Ajotna ding mun kihoutoh masa louva, mini lampi khat a kijot khom thei mong hinam? 
Neh ding kimat masah louva, gammang noija sakei bahkai hutum ngai am? Chuleh aneh ding ima kimat louva, sakei bahkai nou jong peng mong hinam? 
Anchah beija kikam thang’a, vacha oh thei mong ding ham? Achonpil umlouva tol’a thang kikam chu kipil thei ding ham? 
Gal ginna pengkul kimut teng, khopi mite tija louva um thei ding ham? Pakaijin abol louva ahileh khopi chung’a thilse moh lhun thei ding ham? 
Dihtah mong in, Hatchungnung Pakaijin, alhacha themgao ho henga phongdoh masa louvin, imacha aguh in abol hih hel e. 
Sakei bahkai akitumtai, hijeh chun, koiham akicha louding? Hatchungnung Pakaijin thu aseitai, hijeh chun, koiham Pakai thuseiphong jal louding? 
Hiche thu hi, Ashdod mipite leh lamkaite heng’a phongjal un, chuleh Egypt gamsung a milen milal ho heng’a sei phong leuvin. Tun, nangho Samaria leh akimvel’a molchung dung a hung kikhom uvin lang, Israel mite kibol gentheina leh acheh chao nau hi hungve uvin” ati. 
Pakaijin aseiye, “Ka miten thudihtah’a thil bolje ahaimiltauve. Akulpi hou jong, pumhat leh guchat’a kichom khom nei leh gou dimset in akholkhom tauvin ahi. 
Hijeh chun Hatchungnung Pakaijin aseiye, “Melma na kidoupi khat ahung kon’e. Aman gamsung pumpi aumchah kheh ding, kivenna pal ho jong aloichim ding, chuleh akulpiu jouse jong asuh chim ding ahi.” 
Pakaijin hitin aseiye, “Kelngoi chingpan sakei bahkai kam sunga kon’a kalngoi huhdoh agot’a, akeng teni leh abilkol khat seh bou achulhah ham ham ji bang a, Samaria khopi a nomsa tah’a cheng Israel chate leh Damascus khomite chu, alupnau jalkhun ningchong kah’a abong abaiya kiloidoh ding ahiuve. 
“Ngai uvin, chuleh Israel dounan gamsung pumpia thu gasei jun,” tin , Pakai, van Pathen chun aseiye. 
Hche nikho chuleh keiman achonset jal’a Israel chu talen ka matsah ding, Bethel kho’a milimdoi maicham’a saki ka satlhah’a, chuleh maicham phung jong ka suhmang ding ahi. 
Chuleh mihaosa hon phalbi-in ding’a asah’u innom tah ho leh nipilai-in dinga asah’u inho jong, chuleh saiha-a kisa leng inpiho jong abon’a ka suhmang ding ahi.
