Amos.
5.
Vo Israel mite, na chung uva thi-op la kasah khum’u hi ngai cheh tem’un! 
Nungah tobang Israel chu akiseplhu tai, avel’a dingdoh kitlou dingin tollhanga akijam den tai. 
Hatchungnung Pakaijin hitin aseiye, “Galsat mi sangkhat avaikon teng, mi jakhat bou hung kile jia, mi jakhat avaikon teng, mi somkhat bou hingdoh’a hung kile ji ahiuve’’ ati. 
Tun, Pakaijin Israel mite jah a hitin aseiye,’ “Nahin na diuvin, kaheng'a hung kilekit tauvin, 
Bethel kho’a doi-pathen houdin amaicham phunga cheda uvin, Gilgal leh Beersheba mun’a jong doi houdin lut dauvin. Ajeh chu, Gilgal khomite jong sohchanga kikaimang diu, chuleh Bethel khomite jing imalou soh uva mangthah diu ahi,’’ati. 
Nahin na diuvin Pakai kom’a hung kilekit uvin, achuti louleh, Pakai chu Israel chunga meikou kisunkhum banga hung deujah ding, na boncha uva nakah vam diu, Bethel kho’a na-doi-pathen uvin jong hichu aphelmit thei louding ahi. 
Nanghon thudih chu na heikon un, hesoh gentheite din changkha kine nabah sah’un, Nanghon athudih laitah chu kidah um nasosah’un, tolsenan nanei tauve. 
Pakaijin ahsi ho asemin, ahsi lah a Bombel leh Ngambom lungthu jong asem e. Aman muthim lhangkhal jong jingkah khovah asosah in, sunlai nisa jong jan muthim asosah theiye. Aman twikhanglen’a twipi ho achopdoh in, leiset chunga aphilhah sah e. Ama chu amin Pakai kiti ahi. 
Aman athahat apan in, ahat leh apaote asumang in, akulpi jouseu jong asuhchim peh tai. 
Nanghon thu adih’a tanpa nahot bol’un, thutah seidoh ho jong nadal bol uvin na kidah thouve. 
Nanghon vaicha gentheite chotphan nanei jun, dihlou tah in kai don nopnan nanei jun, achang jouseu nalahpeh jiuve. Hijeh chun, nang hon song-in hoitah tah nakisah vangun, asunga nachen louhel diu ahi. Chuleh lengpilei na deitah’a nabol uva tamtah naphu vang un, hiche ho’a kon’a chu nang hon lengpitwi nadon louhel diu ahi bouve. 
Ajeh chu, keiman nasuhkhel u ijat hija, na chonset nau ichan geija nasa ham, ti kahesohkeije. Nangho hi nehguh kilah a, midih bolgenthei ja pang nahiuve, chuleh thutanna mun’a vaicha tahlelte dihna selmang’a pang nahiuve. 
Hijeh chun, hitobang phat phatlou laitah’a, akam kihup’a umthim beh chu, milung chingho ahiuve. 
Apha chu ipi ham, hol uvin lang, thilse chu jamsan’un, chutileh nang hon na sei jiu banga, Pakai van Pathen chun na umpi jing thei diu ahi. 
Thilse hotbol unlang, apha chu ngailuvin; chuleh thutanna munho chu thudih lenna hisah’un, chutileh Pakai van Pathen chun, amite ahingdoh amoh chengse chunga alungsetna akoi ding ahi. 
Hijeh chun Pakai van Pathen chun hitin aseije, khosung leh vengning koilah jousea thi-op ho ka leh mao o-gin dimlha ding, chuleh akap gunset dinga ka-je them ho jong akondiu, ahi. 
Lengpilei lai jousea mi kap thum jeju ding, ajeh chu Keiman amaho talen kamatsah’a kasuhgam hel soh ding ahi, tin Pakaijin aseiye. 
Ohe, ada nahiuve! Pakai nikho chu hunglhung taleh tiho. Nanghon Pakai nikho chu ipi tobang ham nahepouve Hiche nikho chun khovah ahinpohlut louhel ding, muthim lhangkhal ahin lhut ding ahi bouve. 
Hiche nikho chuleh nangho, Pasal khattou sakei bahkai kicha-a ajamleh vompin ana lamtin’a, vompia kon’a ajamdoh kit’a a-insung alhun’a, a-inbanga akingaileh akhut gul’in ahinchuh tobang nahi diu ahi. 
Chutia chu, Pakai nikho chu muthim leh lungjin nikho hi ding, lung navah bei leh kipana beikei nikho hi ding ahi. 
Keiman kut nabol jouseu kahon kadei poi, khoppi theng nabol’u jong ka kipah dehpoi. Ajeh chu abon’a atah hilou na bolthu ngen’u ahi bouve. 
Nanghon pumgo thilto nadeitah’uva nabol’u jong kamit a ka vetpih louhel ding nahiuve. 
O thong jejua vahchoila nasah’u jong thip doh un, selangdah tampi ngaitah’a nasai ju jong ka ngainom poi. 
Chusang'in, thudih chu twilong hattah bangin nei musah un, chuleh kanglou twidung bangin chondihna chu lonsah’un. 
Vo, Israel mite, gamthip noija kum somli sunga chu, kilhaina gantha chuleh thilto chom chom ho chu kaheng'a nahinchoi jingu hinam? 
Ahipoi, nang hon na-doi-pathen’u na lengpau Sakkuth leh ahsi pathen na kisem thu-u bou chu najen’un nahou-ve. 
Hijeh a chu keiman nangho hi Damascus kalpeh’a sohchanga ka kaimang diu nahi tin, Pakaijin aseijin, ama min chu Pakai van Pathen ahi.
