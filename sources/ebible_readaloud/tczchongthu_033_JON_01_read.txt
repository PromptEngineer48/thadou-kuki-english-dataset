Jonah Themgao Lekhabu.
1.
Amittai chapa Jonah kom'ah Pathen thupeh ahunglhungin, 
Kipat dohin lang Ninevah kiti khopi lentah a khun gihna thu gaphongdoh tan ajeh chu amite’u gitlouna hi kamu chen tan ahi. 
Ahinlah Jonah akipat dohin Pakaiya kon'in akihei mangin Joppa kongkai munlanga achen kong sunga alut in atou man apen Tarshish lam jonin akitoltan ahi. 
Ahin Pakaijin twikhanglen chunga chun huipi hattah in ahin nun sahin kong suhkeh theileu chun hui le go nasatah in ahin nuhtan ahi. 
Kong sepai ho chu thina khop hellin akichatauvin, lung lengvai pumin ama ama Pathen ngehnan ataovun, chuleh kong jankhaina ding chun kong sunga kon chun thil gehsa ho chu aseplha tauvin ahi. Ahinlah Jonah chu kong indan noinung penna akum lhan ana lummin kho helou khopin ana imu lhatan ahi. 
Hichun kong sepai lamkai pa chun Jonah chu aga mudohin ajah a “A imupa ipi nangaito hitam thoudoh in na Pathen henga tauvin chutileh aman ithigam nadiuva konna hi eihuhdoh theidiu ahi” ati. 
Hiti chun amaho akihouvun, khaije hiche vangsetna hi eiholah a koipen jeh hi ding ham vang veuhite akitiuvin, vang avetauvin ahileh Jonah chunga chun achu tan ahi. 
Hichun ama hon Jonah kom'a ipi jeh a hitobang vangsetna hi ichung uva chu hitam? Nang koi nahim? Ipi na tong na him? Hoiya konna na hung ham? Koigam mi nahim? tin adong tauvin ahi. 
Jonah in adonbutin, keima Hebrew mi kahin, Leiset leh Twikhanglen semma van Pathen ging jinga um mi kahi ati. 
Jonah chun Pathen na konna hung jamduha ahi chu a seidoh phat in amaho aha kichat behsehun, epi na bolkhel hitam atiuvin ahi. 
Twikhanglen chu aha khoh cheh cheh phat chun ama hon nang hi i-nalo uleng hiche Twikhanglen hi thip thei ding ham atiuvin ahi. 
Hichun aman amaho kom'a chun keima hi nei dom sangun lang Twikhanglen'a hin neiseplut leu chun hichi twipi kinong hi tang tante “Ajeh chu hiche twipi kinong hi keijeh ahi” ati. 
Ahijeng vang'in ama hon ajo kham chanun kong chu leigo langa toldoh tei ding agongun ahinlah Twikhanglen kinong chu akhoh cheh jotan, 
Hijeh chun amaho Jonah Pathen kom'a chun ataovun “Vo Pakai nei lungset unlang hichi pa chonset jeh'in nei sumang hih un chuleh heche pan thina ato jeh'in keiho mo neipehihbeh un, O Pakai nangin hichi mipa jeh a hi hitobang thilse hi nasohsah ah bouve ti kahe tauve” atiuvin ahi. 
Hichun kongtol hon Jonah chu aman’un aselhahuva ahileh Twikhanglen kinong hoise tah chu athipchet jeng tan ahi. 
Kongtol ho chun Pakai thanei dan chu amudoh phatun akichalheh jingun pumgo thilto abolun kitepna anei tauvin ahi. 
Hichun Pakaijin Jonah val lut dingin nga lentah khat ana gongin hiti chun Jonah chu hiche ngapi sunga chun nithum le janthum atham tan ahi.
