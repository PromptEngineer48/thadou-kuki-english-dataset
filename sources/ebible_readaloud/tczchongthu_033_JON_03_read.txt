Jonah.
3.
Anivei channa in Jonah kom’a Pathen thu ahunglhung kit tan, 
“Kipatdohin lang khopi lentah Nineveh a chen lang akhopi mite kom’a keiman nang kom’a ka seipeh sa thu hi gasam phongtan atin, ahi.” 
Hiti chun Jonah akipat dohin Pakai thupeh dungjuijin Nineveh langa achetan ahi. Nineveh hi khopi lentah ahin nithum lam kijot ahi. 
Jonah chu akhopi sunga alut in nikhat lhingset ajot pan tan ahi. Hichea chun Jonah in, “Ni somli jou tengleh Neniveh khopi hi akup athalla kileh ding ahi!” tin asam phong tan ahi. 
Hichun Nineveh mipi ten Pathen thupeh chu atahsanun aneo pen’a pat alen pen geijin an angolun khaodip pon akisilun alungkhamna’u chu aphong doh tauvin ahi. 
Nineveh lengpan Jonah thusei chu ajahdoh phat in alaltouna a kon'in ahung kum lhan alengpon loupi chu ahohlhan khaodip pon chu akisilin ahi. 
Hichun lengpa leh amilen amilal ten thupeh khat khopi sung pumpia aphongdoh un mihem hihen lang gancha ho hijongleh anneh twidon abollouhel ding ahi. 
Mihem hihen gancha hijongleh lunggim lunghem pum’a, Pathen a lunggil tah a taona iman diu pumhatna natoh leh ithilse bol nauva konna ikihei mang ding’u ahi, tin a phongdoh tan ahi. 
Ijeh innem itileh, Pathen jengin jong alung hin heijin tin eisuhgimna diuva kon’a eihin dohsah jing theiju ahi, atiuvin ahi. 
Hitobang thil abol’u chu Pathen'in amudoh a itobang tah a thilse bolna a konna kihei manga kingai tom’a hiuvem ti amudoh phatin alung aheitan achung uva lhunsah dinga aseisa thilse chu abol datan ahi.
