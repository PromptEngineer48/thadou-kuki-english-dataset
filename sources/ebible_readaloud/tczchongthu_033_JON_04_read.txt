Jonah.
4.
Pathen'in atohgon akhel chun Jonah alungnopmo sahlheh jengin hatah in alunghangin ahi. 
Hijeh chun aman Pathen henga alungnop mona aphongin Pakaijin a ka kipat doh masanga kana sei hilou ham? Hiche jeh a hi Tarshish lamma kache hilou ham, ijeh-inem itileh Pathen nangma hi lungset them, hepi them, lunghangvah lou, longlouva lungsetna nei jing Pathen thilse bol louva lunghei kit ji Pathen na hiti kahet jing sa nahi, ati. 
Hijeh chun Pakai ka hinkho hi keija kon'in lamang tan, kei dingin hin sangin thi aphajo tai. 
Chujou chun Pakaijin “Hitia na lunghang jengpi hi dih na kisah hinam atin ahi”. 
Hijeh chun Jonah chu khopi a kon'in solam’a apotdohin lhambuh akisonin hiche limnoija chun atou in khopi chu epi hungsoh mong am tin atouvin ave tan ahi. 
Hichun Pakai Pathen'in umphung khat akedoh sahin Jonah umna chu akhukhum sah in lim in ason peh tai, hitia hi bolphatna alung hesoh chu lungmonsah ding agon ahi. Hichun Jonah jong umphung khat in ajam khum chu akipah behseh jengin ahi. 
Ahinlah ajing nikhoa kho ahungvah phat in Pathen'in lung alhalut in umgui chu aneuvin ahileh um-phung chu anechaijun umphung jong chu agot lhahsah tai. 
Nisa ahung so doh phat in Pathen'in nisa aveven asat sah jingin, nisa chun Jonah lujang asal a ahileh ama jong alhalhop jengin athinom leo jengin ahi. “Kei dingin hitia hin sangin thima apha joi” ati. 
Hichun Pathenn in Jonah jah a, um-gui athia na lunghan jeng chu dih nakisah hinam? ati leh Jonah in adonbut nin “Heng’e thina chan gei ja lunghang ka hitai” ati. 
Hichun Pakaijin, “Nangin natu louhel hiche um-gui hi khan jong na khansah theilou jankah khatna mang thah kit ahitai. 
Achutileh hiche Ninevah khopi lentah, akhosunga mihem sang jakhat le sang somni jen lhagao, muthim noija umnalai ho leh ganhing tamtah um ho hi ka lainatpi louding ham?” ati.
