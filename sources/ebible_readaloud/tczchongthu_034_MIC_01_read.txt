Micah Themgao Lekhabu.
1.
Pakaijin hiche thu hi Jotham phatlaija Micah henga anasei ahi. Ahaz le Hezekiah chu Juda te leng ana hilhon'in, Samaria le Jerusalem chung chang thua thil anamu sahin ahi. 
Kigosan umun! Vannoija um chitin namtin jousen, ngaijuvin nangho vannoija um mitin vaipi jousen, Vo leisetle asunga um jouse n ngaisohkeiyun, Pakai thaneipenin Hou-In thenga kon'in nangho dounan hettohsah in apang jinge. 
Ven! Pakai ahung’e! A van mun alaltouna a hin dalhaha, leiset munsang lai jouse ahin chotpha ding ahi. 
Molsang jouse akengto noija jolsoh ding, phaichama longluta, khoilu meiyin akah jolla, asuhlanga twi kisunglha bang ding ahi. 
Ipi-jeh a hitobang thil sohham! Ajeh chu Israel galbol jeh le Juda insung pumpi chonset jeh ahi – a nampumpi chonset jeh ahi;. Israel galbol hi koi pentah themmo kichansah ding ham? Samaria akhopiu! A milim doi hounau khopi lailung hoilai ham? Akhopiu Jerusalem ahi. 
Hijeh chun, kei Pakaijin Samaria kho hi tollhanga alomchanga kasetna min lengpilei bolna bepma kaumsaha, lhang chunga songpal jouse jong phaichama katollhaha, khombul jouse jong kakal doh ding ahi. 
Khopi sunga milim doi kisem thu ho jouse jong kavoh chip ding, alodoh agou jouse jong meiya kahalvamding, ajeh chu hiti ho jouse chu aboncha notiho mana kidong khoma kimu ahi. 
Hijeh chun keiman lunghemin pul dou’inge. Kengchot se lou in keng keovin vahle ing’e. Sial bangin ham- ingting, chule sumbupi bangin lunghem- inge. 
Ajeh chu Pakaijin kamite abolsetna ma ha hi amelsebeh seh jeng tan, adamdohna ding ahahsabeh seh jenge, kamite Jerusalem kelkot phung ahung geitai. 
Igalmi Gath jah a seipoh hihhel un, Beth Lephrah a jong kap hih hellun kinepna vetsahnan natahsao leivui lah a kilihle leovin. 
Nangho Shaphir- a um mite, sohchang kikai mong bangin, sagohkeole jahchan kile kit tauvin. Nangho Zaanan mite kulpia kon'in potdohdatauvin. Nangho Beth-ezel mite lunghem un, nakhopiu khombul jouse akilon soh tah jeh in. 
Nangho Maroth a um miten vang panpina hung ding nangah lallheh jengui, ahinlah Pakaiya konna khaveng vung thutanna ahung lenglhan, Jerusalem kotpi phung ahung geitai. 
Lachish khoa chengte, nasakol kangtalai a kaina ding bupeh unlang lhauvin, nangho Juda mite, khopi masapen nahiuve, nang hon Jeusalem chonsetna a napuilut’u ahitai. 
Moresheth-gat jong chu kilhahna ding thilpeh tilou hoidoh nading umlou ahitai. Aczib khopi mite, Israel lengpa dinga lheplhaha hung pang ding nahi tauve. 
O Mareshah mite nakhopi jou ding galmi nahenga kahinpuilut ding ahi, chule Israel lamkaiho, Israel loupina chu Adulam henga che ding ahitai. 
Oh Juda mite ho, lunghem vetsahnan, nalujang sam kivotolunlang, muthong bangin lutol deldullin um uvin, ajeh chu nachate nahenga konna migama sohchanga kikai mong ding ahi tauve.
