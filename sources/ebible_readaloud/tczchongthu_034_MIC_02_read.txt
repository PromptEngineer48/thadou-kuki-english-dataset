Micah.
2.
Ajalkhun chunga alupna mun'a, ajan jan a thilse bolding ngaitoa gel gelho ada ahiuve, ijeh-inem itileh athodoh teng abol nathei tha aneiyu ahi. 
Gam phabep na deichat jong chu nakilah jengin ahi, mi koitobang ham insah na deichat jong chu nakilah jengin ahi. 
Hiche hi Pakaijin asei ahi, “Thilse naboljeha thilse kapeh ding; nanah homma konna na lolchang na bodoh jou louding, Chuleh kiletsah tah in lam jot hih'in, phat khohtah hung ding ahi. 
Hiche nikho teng chule nagal miten totnop na-a na nei ding, nang douna-a kiletsahna la asah ding, keiho amongthah kahi tauve, Pakaijin kamite chan ding akheltan, kakom'a kon'in kachan ding akilah dohtan ahi. 
Pakai miten nagamgi iti kisekhen ham tia aei sangun, michom dangin nagamgi asekhen ding ahitai. 
Hitobang chu seitahih in, mipi ten adonbutuve. Hitobng chun gaothu seidan. 
O Israel insung mite, hitobanga hi thu kiseimong mong ding hinam? Pakai lhagao chu thohhat tah a um mong hinam? Adih chu nabol nom leh, kilhamkonna thu keija konna najah ding ahiye. 
Tutah in kamite eidoudin akipanun, gal mite bangin, mi lungmonga umjong anung lamah asangkhol na holhah pehun, kiphatlam tah a umho jong gal nabol khumuve. 
Kamite numeiho, nomtah a achennaova kon'in anodoh un, achateu asenhoa kon'in jong Pathen loupina alamang tauve. 
Kipatdoh un! Chetauvin! Hikom mun hi na gamule na in-u ahipoi, nangho dinga chonsetna dimset mun ahi, chule themmona jeh a kichai deh jeng nahiuve. 
Avetkah nan Themgao tamtah lah a jousei themgao khat in, na kom'ah, “Keiman tunia ju leh khamna theija Kipana Thupha kasei ding ahi!” ati. 
“Nikhat leh O Israel keiman kahin khop ding nahiuve, keiman akidalha amoh chengse kahin khop ding nahiuve, kelngoihonte banga hamhing lah a kahin lonkhom kit ding nahiuve. Heng’e, na gamuva mi hung tam kit ding ahi. 
Na lamkaiteuvin nakikai mong naova konna nasuhtup kit diu ahi, chule nagal miteu kelkot phunga konna na hong sunga nahung kile kit dingu ahi, na Lengpauvin na lamkai untin na Pakaiyu mong mongin na ventup jing dingu ahi”.
