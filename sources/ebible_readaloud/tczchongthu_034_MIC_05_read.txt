Micah.
5.
Tun galsat dingin sepai jalamkai ho kigong tauvin, Jeruslem khopi, ikimvelluvah gal miten eiumkimvel tauve, galsatdin sepai mihonpi hung kikhom loijun. Amaho Israel lamkaipa satdin ahung kon tauve. 
Ahinlah nangma, O Bethlehem Ephrahthah, Judah phunga aneopen nahi, hijongleh nanga konna hi kei dinga Israel sunga vaihom ding khat hung peng doh ding, Ama chu akipatna ahung konna gollui laipeha ahitan, ni masalai, lha masalaija um jing sapeh ahitai. 
Pakaijin amite naosonat thohnu naonei kahsea chu donlouva akoija ahaisending ahitai, chutengleh amoh ahing nalai chengse chu amite henga hung kile kitding ahi. 
Pakai thahat vanga ama chu dinga akelngoi hon chu avah ding ahiye. Hitia chu amite chu koiman asuhlong louding ahitai chule leiset kolmong geija atha’u ajeding ahitai chule amaho jong lungmonga chengdiu ahitai. 
Chuleh ama chamkonna hung hi ding, Assyria miten gamsunga gal ahin boltinguleh, kelngoi chinga mi sagi le mipi lah a milen milal miget pang diu ahi. 
ama hon Assyria gam'a chemjama vai ahopuva Nimrod gamsung jong vai ahop diu Assyria mite a konna hi ama hon eihuhdoh diu ahi 
Chuteng Israel amoh chengse mitin vaipi lah a um uva, Pakaiya konna hungkumlha daitwi tobang ahilouleh hampa chunga go hung julha tobang hidiu ahitai. 
Israel amoh chengse chu namtin vaipi lah a um uva, kithe thang soh diu, gammang lah a keipi bahkai um banga, chule kelngoi hon lah a keipi bahkai nou banga umdiu, kelngoi chu ahung potdoh tih tengule abotel teldiu koima chan ahuhdoh theilou tobang hi ding ahi. 
Nagalmi, nakidoupite chunga Israel in nadinpi ding, chule nagal mite kithat gamding. 
Hiche nikho chuleh na sakolteu katha gamding, nakangtalai jouse jong ka suhset ding. 
Nakhutna na tohdoh phunsan jouse jong kasuh mongsoh helding 
Nakulpi jouse jong kasuhchim soh hel ding. 
Milim doihouna nasem jouse jong kasuh mong soh ding. 
Nakom'a konna asrins doi nahou jong ka suhmong ding chuleh nakhopi jouseu jong kasuh mongsoh helding ahi. 
Lunghang tah a namtin vaipi thungai louho chunga ka phulahna ka sunlhah ding ahi.
