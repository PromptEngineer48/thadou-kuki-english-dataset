Micah.
6.
Pakaijin ipi aseijem ngaijuvin, kipat dohin lang na chung chang thu hi molsang jouse maijah ahetoh dingin phongdoh temin. 
Chule tun, O molsang jouse Pakai minel na thu kiseidoh hi ngaijuvin, aman amite chungah kinelna thu aseiye. Ami Israel te chunga gahna apeh ding ahi. 
O kamite, nachung uva ipi kabolset em? Nachung uva ipi kabol gentheina umem? Nei donbut un. 
Chujongleh keiman Egypt gam'a kon'in kahin puidohuvin chuleh nabulput na henlhah nauva kon'in kana lhatdoh tauvin ahi. Aaron, Mose chule Miriam nakithopi ding uvin kahin sollin ahi. 
Kamite, nageldoh lou-u mong ham? Moab lengpa Baalak chun itobanga thilse anagon hija, Beor chapa Balaam chun itobanga ana donbut ham, ka miten geldohuvin. Chule Acace mun'a kipana Gilgal chengeija nakholjinnao chu geldohuvin. Keima na Pakaiyuvin kakitahna a thil ijakai kabolpeh nahiuve. 
Ipi thilpeh choija Pakai angsunga kalut dingham? Itobang maicham thil kapeh ding ham? Bongnou jeng pumgo thiltoa kachoilutna a angsunga bohkhupma kapoh lut ding ham? 
Kelngoi chaljeng asang asang chule vadung sangsom jen dimset Olive thaotwi kapehleh lunglhai mong ding hinam? Ka chonset ngaidamna dinga kacha apeng masapen kapeh a lunglhai ding hinam? 
Vo mihem, Pakaijin thilpha chu navetsahsa ahitai, Pakaiy in nanga konna adei chu ipiham? Thu adiha natanpeha, mi khoto ding nakhohsaha, chule na Pathen nunga kinemsahtah a lam najot ding hi ahi bouve. 
Nachiha ahileh Pakai ging in! Pakai ogin in Jerusalem a mijouse akouve. Sugenthei dingin Pakaijin galmi ahin sol’e. 
Migilou insunga kikhol gitlouna, joulhepna chu ken ipi kasei ding ham? Gaosap na chunga thil adihlou kite chu kasuh mil thei ding hinam? 
Thil tena dihlouva kitea, hitobanga lhing louva kiveiho chu, keiman iti kathoh ding ham? 
Nalahuva mihaothei hon jong pumhat jeng athouvin, nagam mite hon jong jou thu jeng aseijuve. 
Hijeh a chu keiman nang kadenpan nahitai! Na chonset jeh a ahomkeova kajam nahitai. 
Nejong lechun nava theilou ding, na oisunga nagil keljing jeng ding, sum kikholdoh nan natin, ema na koidoh thei louding, nangin na kholdohsa chu keiman najou ding ho kapeh ding ahi. 
Muchi natu jing jeng ding, ahinlah aga nalo louhel ding ahi, Olive thao naheh heh ding, ahinla nangman thaotwi nanei louding ahi. 
Omri kitipa dan semsa najuijin, Ahab leng giloutah nung na juijin, hijeh a chu mipi namu jousen ija nahsah louva nanuisat ding ahi.
