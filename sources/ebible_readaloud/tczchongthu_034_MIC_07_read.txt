Micah.
7.
Ada kahitai! Ajeh chu thingga akilona nunga koiman neh ding kimu talou bangbeple lengpi theiga leh theichang kichomna nung tobanginkaumdoh tai. 
Leiset chunga Pathen gingte la amangthah gamtan, mihemte lah a lungdih pua khatcha Jong aumpon, thisan soa tolthat jeng ahiuve. Amijousen asopi kipalna dinga thang kamjeng ahiuve. 
Akhutuvin thilse jeng a bol uvin lung nachang mongin thilse abol uve. Thutanna pang milen milalhon jong nehguh ding akithum thum'un ahi. 
Alahuva aphapena kisei jong chu ling tobang ahin, lungdih pentah a kiseipa jong chu ponlah abang’e, min akailuht nadiu nikho leh talen amat nadiu nikho chu hunglhung ding ahitai. 
Koimacha tahsan hihin, nagol ngaitah jeng jong ahilouleh na inneipi jeng jong tahsan hih in. 
Chapan jong apa, chanun jong anu, mounu in jong atehpi chunga, namelma chu na insung tah a kon hi ding ahi. 
Keiman Paki jeng bou eipanpi din vejingingting, eihuhhing ka Pathen kanga jing ding ahi, Chutileh ka Pathen'in kataona angai tei ding ahi. 
Kagal miten duchatah in neive hih un, lhujong leng kathodoh kit ding ahi. Muthim lah a toujong leng Pakai chu kameivah hi ding ahi. 
Pakaiy in eigimbol jongleh ka thohhat ding, ajeh chu ama dounaa kachonset jeh in. Ahinlah aman kachung thu hinkhol nante. Kei dinga thu atan masang sea kangai ngai ding ahi. Pakaijin khovah a eipuidoh intin, chuteng chon dihna kamu ding ahi. 
Chuteng kamelma ten Pakai kei langa apan ahetdoh diu, na Pakai hoiya umem tia aseiju chu kijumsoa jahchabeh seh diu ahi. Hichenu chu kamit in noise tah a avet ding ahi. Amanu chu lamlen kholai dunga min achotpha a leivui chil banga kichil ding ahi. 
Hiche niteng chule Israel, nakhopi kisapha kit ding, chule nagamgi ho jong kikehlen ding. 
Gam tamtah a konna mipite hunga Assyria a kipana gam latah Egypt, Egypt a kipana Euphrate luipi chan gei, twipi leh mol ho kallanga, molsang jousea konna lhang sang jouse geija hung diu ahi. 
Leiset vang hi agoh keova hunglum ding, ajeh chu masanga ana cheng masaho chonset jeh ahi. 
O Pakai nakelngoi china tenggollin namite neichingun, nakelngoi honte neilam kaijun, gammang lah a hin achangsehin cheng jong leo, amaho hi Bashan gamle Gilead gam'a kelngoi lhat bangin lhauhen, gollui laiya bangin umuhen. 
Henge, tin Pakaijin aseije. “Keiman thil kidang nasatah tah kabol peh diu Aigupta a konna nahung potdohuva kanabol banga kabol 
Leiset pumpia namtin vaipin kidang asah sahdiu, Pakaijin nang hi dinga thil kidang abol tengleh jachatna aneidiu, anakoluva jong kho ajah lou diu ahitai. 
Gul banga levui aleija aledio, leisetna a-opma kithol ganhing banga umdiu ahi. Akulpiuva konna kihot hotna hung potdoh diu, akichat behseh jeh uva Pakai ahin beldiu ahi. Nangma jeh a amaho hi abonchauva kichasohkei ding ahiuve. 
Nang banga agoulo amite ahungdoh amoh chengse dinga themmona, ngaidamna, suhkhelna, subei soh thei Pathen koipen um ding ham? Pathen chu alunghang jing jengpoi, ajeh chu Pakai hi alungset longlou anei hi kipah jo ahi bouve. 
Neihin lungset kit’un natin; ka chonsetnau chengse jong nakengto noija koi in natin; twikhanglen'a na lelutding ahi. 
Jacob jong kitahna neitah a nabolla, long louva Abraham nalungset ding, masanglai a kapu kapateu kom'a na kihahsel banga nabol tei ding ahi.
