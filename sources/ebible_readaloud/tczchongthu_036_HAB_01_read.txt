Habakkuk Themgao Lekhabu.
1.
Hiche hi Habakkuk themgaovin thilmu aneina thudol ahi. 
Vo PAKAI, itihchan geija panpi ngaichaa kakou kou di hitam? Hinla nangin nei ngaipeh poi! “Muntin'a nosopma kibolna aumtai!” Ka kap in, hinla eipanhu dingin na hungpoi. 
Hiche gitlou kibol ho hi kavet vet jing ding hitam? Hiche gim gentheina ho jouse hi ibol’a kavet vet jeng ding ham? Keiman kavet chanin mahthahna le nosopma kibolna hobou ka mudoh’e. Mi nelkal hat ho leh kinahpia hat mihonpi chun eihin umkimvel tauve. 
Dan hi donlou tobangbep chu ahitai, chule thutanna munho’a jong thudih umtalou ahi. Migilou ho chun midih ho sanga gamchenga atamjoh jeh uvin, thudih chu heikoija um bepma ahitai. 
Pakai chun adonbut in, “Chitin namtin ho khu gahven chuleh kidangsa temin! Ajeh chu keiman nangma nikho laiya hi thil khat kahin boldoh ding, mi koiham khat touvin seijongleh na tahsan louhel ding chu kahinbol ding ahije. 
Keiman Babylon mite ho, giloutah le nosopma mibol ho chu ka hin tundoh ding ahi. Ama hon vannoi muntin apaltan’uva kijotle ding’u chule gam dang tampi ahinjo dingu ahi. 
Amaho hi gitlou abol nauva engsetah’a chonna ahiuvin, chutia abolnom chengseu chu abol’u ahi. 
Sakol aneihou jong lutnu sangin alhai hatjouvin, khomu chipchep a ngeihon lhakhom sangin jong ahoise jouve. Sakol kangtalaija touho chun gamla tah a kon'in a sakol ho’u chu ahin thunun jeng uvin; Muvanlai lengle hon a anchah’u abokhumuva avallhum jiu kisei tobang ahiuve. 
Oh, ahung tauve, abonchauvin nosopma boldin akunsuh tauve. A gancha hon hou jong chu nelphaija hui bangin ahung kitol’un, amasanguva asohchang ho’u ahin masot hou chu neldi tamin atam jeng uvin ahi . 
ama hon leng ho le leng chapa te ho chu isahlou tah in abolun, chule akulpi ho jouse chu isah loutah in abol uve. 
Amaho chu hui nung bangin akitolun chule achemang tauve. Hinla amaho sungil’a themmona nei ahiuve, athahat uchu pathena nei ahiuve.” 
Vo Pakai ka Pathen, nangma tonsotna um, ka Mithengpa Nangman nei theh manghel jeng hih nauvin nate na? Vo Pakai, ka Songpiu, nangman hiche Babylon mite hi eisudih dinguleh ka chonsetna ho’u atam jeh a ei-gimbol ding uva na hinsol ahi. 
Nangma hila athenga nahin chule agilou chu n maikoh a um theilou ahi. Chuti ahileh amaho do’ha joulhepna namitsat ding hijaham? Mi engsete chun asanguva chonphajo avallhumu jongleh thipbeh a naum ding hitam? 
Keiho hi ahinga kimandoh dinga le akithat dinga kiman ho tobangbep kahiuvem? Kei ho hi twikhanglen na um ganhing ho lamkai bei tobangbep ka hiuvem? 
Amaho kipanaa le golnop bolna ding uva, ngakoija eiki khaidoh dinga chuleh lensunga ohden bep ding bou kahi dingu hitam? 
Chutengleh ama hon lenthang anei hou chu Pathen tobangin houvun tin, chuleh amasanguva chun gimnamtui halna nei uvinte. Amaho chun, “Hiche lenthang pathen ho hin keiho eihaodoh sah’u ahi” ati ding’u ahi. 
Nangman amaho hi hiti dinmun'a hi nasolmangpai pai ding ham? Amaho hi phottohna beihella agaljouva hi i-tiha maipah jing dingu hitam?
