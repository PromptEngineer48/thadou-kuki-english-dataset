Habakkuk.
3.
Hiche taona hi themgao Habakkuk in lakaija ahin sahdoh ahi. 
Pakai, nangma chung chang thu hi abon'a kana jahsa ahi. Na thilbol ho hi kadatmo lheh jengin ahi. Achesa kum ho’a nana bolsa ahi bangin, ka ngaichat dehset petlaitahu hin nei panpi kituvin. Chuleh na lunghan laitah a jong mikhoto them nahina chu geldoh in. 
Keiman Pathen chun Edom gal lama patna gamthip ahinjot galkai kamu’n, Paran molchunga konna Mitheng Pa chu ahung kamui. Avah emsengsung chun vanthamjol alodimin, chuleh leiset chung hi avahchoinan alodim jengin ahi. 
Ahungna chu nisa hung sohdoh bangin akilang jenge. Akhutna kon'in vah ahung kikhodohin, athaneina kiselna mun'a konna hungdoh a ahi. 
Amasanga amangthah ding ho akijotnun, chule anunglama pulin ahinjuipaijin ahi. 
Ama adinleh leiset akithingin, avetna ahileh namtin vaipi akihot jenguve. Tonsotna ana um mol ho chu athechehin, chuleh a i-tiha thinglhang anaum ho chu asuchamin ahi. Ama Tonsotna Umpa chu ahi. 
Keiman Kushan miho chu lunglhatah a a’umu kamun, chule Median namte jong chu kicha a tijatah a a’umu kamui. 
Pakai, nangman lunghanga twi chu najepma, twipi chu kikhena hija ham? Amaho chunga lunghimoa nahim? Ahipoi, nangin huhhingna sakol kangtalai chu nahin sol ahijo me! 
Nangin nathal le na thalchang koina na livathei chu napeije. Nangman leiset hi vadung twi ho lonna dingin na hongdohin ahi. 
Mol ho akivetle akithingin, twi kinong chun amasanglama ahin nuhin ahi. Twi thuh tah chun ahin samdohin, akhut chun Pakai lam ahin sang’e. 
Nisa le lha chu nathalchang ho jangkeija alenna chule natengcha vallihleh chu akipeipet in vanlaijolla ading dentai. 
Nangman gamsung chu lunghangin napaltanin, chule chitin namtin chu lunghangin nachotpha tai. 
Na lhenchomte huhdoh dingin, nathaonute huhdohna dingin nakipat dohtai. Nangin migilou luchang chu na noigepin chule alua pat akengtou geijin najep pehtai. 
Huipi hungnung banga hung kitola, bailamtah a Israel bahgam dinga go chu, nangin ama galvon maman nasumang’e. 
Nangman twipi chu nasakolla na paljengin, chule twi thahat tah chu sangtah in kiting denin na umsah jeng’e. 
Hiche thu kajah in kasungil akithing jengin, ka muh jong kichatnan aphusepsupmin, kakengteni jong akihot hotnin, chule kichat tijatnan kakithing jenge. Keiman ka chunguva gal hungbol mipi ho chu vangsetna nasatah in ahinphah dingu nikho chu thipkhencha kanga ahitai. 
Theichang phungho jong chu pahdoh hih uhenlang, lengpi guija lengpiga ho umhih hen; Olive phunga thao putdoh tahih jong leh, chuleh loujao hoa ima pou hih henlang chuleh ga sodoh tahih jong uleh; chuleh loujao hoa gancha ho thi’u henlang, ganhing ho jeng jong chu agoh in umu henlang hita jongleh; 
Keima Pakai a kipah nalai ding kahi! Ei huhhingpa Pathenna chu kipahna lai ding kahi! 
Leng vaipo thaneipen Pakai chu ka thahatna ahi! Sa san bangin keng thaneina pen tin, munsang lai laiya jong eichopsah ding ahi. (Zai pipu chun: Hiche taona hi semjang khao tampi tothoa atumginga alamkai ding ahi.)
