Zephaniah.
2.
Kikhou khom’un nabon’un, kikhomsohkeiyun, nangho jum leh ja helou nam mite. 
Pakai thutanna nangchunguva alhum masangin, kikhom’un, Hui a changvai kisep mang banga na kisep mang kah in, kisih’un, Pakai lunghanna meikong banga aki buhlhah masang, chuleh Pakai lungset gimnei tah ahunglhun masangin, kisihun. 
Gamsunga mi noinungten Pakai jeng hol’un, chuleh athupeh nit’un. Adih jeng’a thil bolding gouvin, chuleh nunem tah in hinkho mangun. Chutileh Pakaijin, alunghan nikho’a na seldoh’uva chuleh sumang louva na huhdoh theiju ahi. 
Ajeh chu Gaza leh Ashkelon khopi jong ahomkeuva kidalha ding, chule Ashdod leh Ekron khopi jong kisuchim’a kidel jamdiu ahi. 
Ada nahiuve! Nangho twikhanglen panga cheng Philistine mite, chuleh Canan gamsunga cheng ho jouse jong, hiche thutanna hi nangho chunga chuding ahi. Ajeh chu Pakaijin nangho nabeikei kah’uva na suhgam dingu ahi. 
Philistine gam, twikhanglen pang ho, hamhing gamlah naso ding, chuleh kelngoi chingho pansatna leh ganchate kikholkhomna mun hi ding ahi. 
Judah mi ahingdoh amoh chengsen, hiche mun hi, kineh holna a aneidiu. Janteng Ashkelon a ingem ho’a jan choldo diu ahi. Chutah leh, Pakai a Pathen’u chun, khotona neitah a amite ahinvil ding chuleh asemphat kit’a hamphatna apeh ding ahi. 
“Keiman Moab mite ham hoithona kajan, chuleh Ammon mite ajumso nau kajai. Ama hon kamite noise tah’a anuisat’uva chuleh isahlou tah’a gal abolden’u kamu tai. 
“Tun, keima ka hingjing ’e,” tin Israel Pathen, thaneitah Pakai chun aseiye. “Moab leh Ammon mite kisugam ding, Sodom leh Gomorah banga kisumang soh hel ding ahi. Agamsungu jong ling le khao dimset, chikhuh jeng hi ding, chuleh a-itih’a ahomkeova kijam ding ahi.” Chuleh kamite ahingdoh chengsen, amaho adelmang’uva agam sungu achenden diu ahi,” ati. 
ama hon akiletsah ga akisan diu ahi. Ajeh chu ama hon Pathen mite chu noise tah’a atot thang’uva, thaneipen Pathen douna abol’u ahi. 
Pakaijin agamsung uva leiset Pathen umho jouse asuhmang ding, chutah leh amaho tija tah’a alha ding ahi. Chuteng namtin vaipin ama cheh gamsung a, Pakai chu bohkhup a ahou diu ahi. 
“Vo, Ethiopia mite nangho jong ka chemjam’a na lousoh hel diu ahi” tin Pakaijin aseiye. 
Chuleh Pakaijin sahlam gamho douna-a akhut tum alapding Assyria gam asuhmang peh ding ahi. Nineveh khopi jong ahomkeuva aumsah ding, panna bei nelphai gammgo banga akoi ding ahi. 
Akiletsah piu khopi chu, bongte le kelngoite kivahna leh kolna hi ding, chuleh gamsa jouse jong kol diu ahi. In-gemsa ho lah a chu, sumbupi ho, saphute, chuleh muthong ho hung cheng diu, aham gin’u kholai sung kithong ding ahi. Cedar thing a kisa in jouse jong ki suchim gam ding, chuleh in kot ho jong thilse ho kinot dim gam ding ahi. 
Hiche khopi chu ahi, kiletsah tah’a “Keima bou alenpen kahi” tia ama kichoi sang’a, kulpi detpen’a ana um chu. Ahinlah, tunvang ahomkeuva kijam ahitan, gamlah gamsate chenna mun ahitai. Avahle ajotpa jousen, akhut tum ajeldenun, nuijat nopnan aneitauvin ahi.
