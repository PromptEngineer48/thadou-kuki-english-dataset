Zechariah.
2.
Keiman kavet kitleh, pasal khat in thiltena molkhat akichoi kamui 
Hiti chun keiman hoilanga che ding nahim tin ka dongin ahileh aman, “Keima hin Jerusalem hi adung avai itiham tetoh dinga hunga kahi” ati. 
Hiche jou chun keiman ka kom'a um vantil chu ama kom'a hung vantil chu kimupi dingin achen ahi. 
Vantil khatpa chun aseiye, hungloijin lang hiche gollhangpa kom'a chun seijin, Jerusalem hi phat khat leh mipi leh ganchaho hung dimset diu ahindol loudiu ahi, hitia chu mipi tamtah khopi polanga hung cheng diu ahi. 
Chuteng keima tah in Jerusalem kimvel chu meikong palla ka kolkhum ding ahi tin Pakaijin aseiye. Chuleh keiman khopi sung chu kaloupina kahi sah ding ahi,” ati. 
Pakijin aseiye,” Babylon sahlanga kon'in hung jamdoh un, nangho hi leiset kol ning lia konna thecheh nahiuve. 
Zion mipite hungpotdoh uvin, nangho Babylon gam'a ana jam lut ho!” 
Loupi Pathen hatchungnung Pakai chun nangho nachom gam'a namite chu doudinga eihinsol ahi, nangho nabolse ho chun kei eibolset a kalah ahi. Keiman kakhut hinlam ingting amaho chu kasuhchip ding chuteng asoh houvin amaho achomdiu ahi. Chutengleh hatchungnung Pakaijin eihin sol ahi ahet diu ahi tin, 
Pakaijin aseiye, Vo khopi hoitah Jerusalem kipah tah in kho asam un, ajeh chu nangho lah a cheng dinga kahung ding ahi. 
Hiche nikho teng chuleh namtamtah in Pakai keima eihin jop diu chuleh amaho chu kamite hidiu ahi. Keima nangho lah a kachen ding chutengleh hatchungnung Pakaijin Keima nangho kom'a eihinsol hi nahetdingu ahi. 
Gamthenga chu Judah gamchu Pakaijin Ama gam tumbeh a dinga alahkut ding chuleh Jerusalem chu ama tah in akhopi dinga alhen kit ding ahi. 
Pakai angsunga mihem jouse thipbeh chan umhen ajeh chu Ama chu amunthenga konna hung kipat doh ding ahitai.
