Zechariah.
7.
Darius lengpa Vaipoh kumli channa lhako lhinna Chislea lha kiti nili lhinnin Pakaijin Zechariah henga thu ahin seije: 
Hichun Bethel khomiten Saherzer le Regem-molech toh amiteu chu Pakai lunglhaina holdin asol tauve, 
Chujongleh thaneipan Pakai Hou-In na thempu leh themgao ho chu thu aga dong’e, “Keiman hitih chan khanga kahin bolngai banga chu lhanga lhinseh leh lunghem kabolla an kangol ding ham, kum hijat sunga kabol bang banga?” tin sei uvin tin asol uve. 
Hiche ahin thanei pen Pakaijin kajah in thu ahin seijin hitin ati. 
Gamsunga mite jahle thempuho jah a khun hiti hin seijin, nang hon lhanga lhin leh lha sagi tengleh lunghem anbollu khu, hiche kum som-sagi sunga hi keima dinga anngolla nahiuvem? 
Chuleh nang hon an naneh uva twi na donteng u-jongleh khun nangho le nangho dia na kineh uva chule nangho le nangho dia na kidon jiu hilou ham? 
Jerusalem khopia mi achen lai, nei le gou thalhin lai akimvel dunga jong khopi tampi aumna lhanglam gamla gammon lai dung jong min achenpha laiya chu hicheng mahi Pakaijin themgao masaho kom'a aphondoh sa hilou ham?” tin ati. 
Pakai thupeh chu Zechariah kom'a ahunglhungin hitin ati. 
Thaneipen Pakaijin hitin aseije, min asopi kom'ah ngailutna leh khotona nei tah in thudih in thu tanhen, 
Meithai hihen nuneilou pa neilou hijongleh kholjin hihen mivaicha hijongleh bol genthei hihen chuleh na lungthim in na sopi chunga thilse gel hihen, 
ama hon nathuseiju ngaida uhenlang, nanung ngat’u henlang na thuseiju ajah lou na diuvin akollu sip jong leu, 
A lungthi un nanahsah dau henlang danthu jong kihet mosah jonglehu themgao masaho kom'a hatchungnung pen Pakaijin ana phondoh ija tihih jong leu, hijeh achu hatchungnung Pakaiya konna hung deojah jenga ahi. 
Keiman amaho kakouva eingaipeh lou jeh un ama hon eihin kouteng ujongleh keiman jong kangai peh louding,ahi tin thaneipen Pakaijin aseijin ahi. 
Ama ho hi ahet khah louvu chitin namtin lah a chimpei banga ka thethang gam ding ahi. Hitia chu agamsung uchu akeo hella kijam ding koima chan ahung vilkhah louding ahitan, agam nomtah uchu manlou asodoh sah u ahitai.
