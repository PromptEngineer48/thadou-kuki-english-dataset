Zechariah.
9.
Hiche hi Pakaiya konna Ram gamleh Damascus khopi demna in Pakai thupeh ahunglhungin ahi. Ajeh chu Israel phungho leh mitin vaipi mitnin Pakai lambou ave tauvin ahi. 
Damascus kom'a um Hanath leh Tyre le Sidon khopiho achih lheh vangun mahthah teidiu ahi. 
Tyre hin galdouna phatah nei jongleh sana le dangka lamlenna vutvai bahsah jeng jonglehu, 
Tun Pakaijin Tyre neilegou jouse achomkhom in twikhanglen'ah asep lhatan chuleh meiyin akahlhah ding ahi. 
Ashkelon khopi hin Tyre a suhlhuh ding chuleh lungkhamna dimset ding ahi. Gaza tijatnan lodim intin, Ekron kinepna hung beiding Gaza lengpa hung kithat intin, Askelon a koima hung cheng louding ahi. 
Ashdod khopi gam chom in ahin lodim ding keiman Palistine kiletsahna kasuh ding ahi. 
Keiman akamsunga konna thisan dim atahsa phechu kaloidoh pehding chuleh ahakah uva konna kidah um pumgo thiltosa aneh ho chu akamma konna ka loidoh peh ding ahi. Chujongleh Philistine ahing doh ho chuna Pathen'u hinhou untin Judah phungkhat toh hung bang diu ahi. Ekron Philistine ten kamite hinjop untin Jubusite khatvei anabol tobanga chu abol diu ahi. 
Keiman ka Hou-In kaven tup jing ding chuleh agal miteu a konna kahuhdoh ding, chuleh gamdang miten agam sungu ahin lonvuh louna diuva kavetsui ding ahi. 
Kipah-un O Zion mite, vo Jerusalem mipite galjona samphong un, Ven na lengpa chu ahunge, Ama hi thudih le galjoupa ahi, ahinla ama hi sangan nou chunga hungtou minunnemtah ahi. 
Keiman Israel a konna galsatna sakol kangchu kalah doh ding chuleh Jerusalem'a konna galsatna sakol ho chu kalah doh ding ahi. Keiman galmun na konna gal manchah ho chu ka suhmang ding chuleh na lengpau chun chitin namtin lah a chamna ahinpohlut ding alenggam in twikhanglen langkhat a konna twikhanglen langkhat Eupharates vadunga konna leiset kolmang geija kijesal ding ahi. 
Nangho toh thisanna kimankhum kakitepna joh achu twi gotna kotong a konna na sohchang teu chu kalha doh ding ahi. 
Na hoidoh nading munnah hung kilekit tan, kinepna neinalai sohchang ho na thohgim nau khat cheh chunga chu phatthei bohna ajatnia kale thuhding nahiuve. 
Judah hi kathalpi, Israel hi kathalchang chuleh Jerusalem hi ka chemjam nahiuve, galhatpa banga keiman Greek te douna kamanchah ding nahiuve. 
Pakai chu namite chunga hung kilangding athalpi chu kolphe banga hung lengding, lalchungnung Pakai chun sumkon ahin mutna lhanglam neldi gam'a chimpei banga gal ahinsat ding ahi. 
Hatchungnung Pakai chun amite ahin huhdoh ding, ama hon song lentahtah a ahinseuva gal ajo diu amaho chun jukham banga asapthodiu gal ajodiu ahi. Maicham chunga kithe khum thisan alon banga agal miteu thisan alondoh sah diu ahi. 
Hicheni chuleh Pakaijin kelngoi chingpan amite ahuhdoh banga amite ahuhdoh ding amaho chu lallukhuh kijapna song vahhoi banga gamsunga chu vahdoh diu ahi. 
Amaho chu iti kidanna iti hoiya hoi hitauvem? Gollhang ho chu chaang nengchatna kivahthao diu, nungah ho chu lengpithei thuha kivahthao diu ahi.
