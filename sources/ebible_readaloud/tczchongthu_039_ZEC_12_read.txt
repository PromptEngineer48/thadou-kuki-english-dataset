Zechariah.
12.
Israel te khankho ding chung chang a thuthot Pakaiya konna hung chu hiche hi ahi. Hiche hi vanho hingoltoh a, leiset bulpi hinphutdoh, chuleh mihem sunga Lhangao hin khumpeh a Pakaiya konna thuthot chu ahi. 
Veuvin Keiman Jerusalem kahin semding chu, avel'a hon Judah le Jerusalem ahin delkhum tengu leh jukham banga thabeija lamlele diu ahi. 
Hicheni lechun ken Jerusalem hi songpi banga suhling hoi hilou kahisah ding, avel'a chitin namtin hon ahung kichop khumuva ahin delkhum tengu leh amaho le amaho kisuhkhahna hung hilo bep ding ahi 
Hicheni chuleh Pakaijin ahi tin aseije: “Keiman asakollu kakichat sah ding chuleh achunga touho tijat na a ka hotling gam diu ahi. Keiman Juda mipite ka hin ang-ngat ding chuleh agal miteu sakol mit kahin chotsah ding ahi. 
Chujongleh Judah phung mite amaho leh amaho kihou lim untin Jerusalem mipi tena Pathen u hatchungnung Pakai thahatna change ahi tauve ati dingu ahi. 
Hicheni chuleh keiman Judah phung ho chu thingkho koudohsah mei, ahilouleh chang phellhah a kolphe thaomei kivah kabahsah ding, ama hon ajet avei uva cheng nammite ahallhah ding Jerusalem cheng mipi vang chu lungmonga cheng diu ahi. 
Pakaijin Judah phung amoh chengse chu galjona apeh masatding ajeh chu Jerusalem mipite leh David sonlepahte hin Judah phung amoh ho sanga hi loupina amudeh louding ahi. 
Hicheni lechun Pakaijin Jerusalem mipite ahuhdoh ding, alah uva alhasam pen jeng jong leng David banga hatdiu chuleh David sonho chu Pathen toh bangding, amasang sang uva che Pakai vantil to bangdiu ahi. 
Ajeh chu hiche nile hin Jerusalem doudinga hung chitin namtin jouse chu ka suh mang ding ahi. 
Hiche jouteng chuleh taona le ngailuitna lhagao chu David insungah Jerusalem mipite chunga kabuhlhah ding ahi. Hiteng chuleh asutpau keima chu eimu diu, min achapa khat seh chunga alunghom bang uva alunghom pidiu. Min achapa masapen athia alungnatpi banga alungnatpi diu ahi, 
Hichenia Jerusalem'a lungkhamna le lung hemna chu Maggido phaichama Haddad-rimmon lungkhamna nasatah chu toh bang ding ahi. 
Israel pumpi lungkhamna chu ama phung changcheh lungkhamna hi ding chuleh jipa chengin ajinu chengutoh akikhenniu tobang hidiu ahi. David phungin achangin lungkhampin tin, Nathan phungin jong alungkham piding, 
Hitia chu Levi phung jong chule Shimi phungjong chute ding ahi, 
Judah phung sunga ahing doh hoa konna hi achu atumma lungkham diu, chuleh ajipateu ajinu cheh-u toh akikhen niu lungkhamna hi ding ahi.
