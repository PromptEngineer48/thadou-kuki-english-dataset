Zechariah.
14.
Chingthei jun, ajeh chu Pakaigam hunglhung ding ahi, hiche nikho lechun na-angsung tah uva min nathil nalo’u achom ding ahi. 
Keiman Jerusalem satdinga chitin namtin kakhop ding, khopi hi alah diu, ima jouse hi achom diu, numeiho asuhset diu, mipi akehkhat galhinga kiman ding, chuleh amoh chengse chu khopi sunga kidalha ding ahiuve. 
Hiteng chuleh hiche chitin namtin ho chu tumasanga banga chu kisatpi dinga hung kon doh ding ahiuve. 
Hiche nikho chuleh akeng in Jerusalem solang Olive molchunga chu hung ding ding ahi. Hiteng chuleh Olivet molchu kehni hung kisontin solamle lhumlam hung kikhenna phaicham lentah khat hung kijedohding ahi. Mol akehkhat chun sahlang hin jon intin chuleh akehkhat chun lhang lang ajotding ahi. 
Nangho phaicham lam jkonna nachediu Azal chan gei naphahdiu ahi. Judah lengpa Uzzah nikhoa lingkiho jeh a najammu tobang chu hi ding ahi. Chujou teng Pakai ka Pathen chu hungintin amitheng te jousen ahin kilhonpi diu ahi. 
Hicheni lechun nisavah umlou ding, 
Ahinla sunchu hijengding ahi! Hiche hi iti hithei ding ham tichu Pakaijin bou ahet ahi. Angaija anaum sun le jan chu um talou ding ajeh chu nilhah lang jongleh vahvah jeng ding ahi. 
Hicheni lechun Jerusalem'a konna hintwi hung longdoh ding, akehkhat Dead Sea langa chentin akehkhat Mediterranean langa chen tin chuhleh khalla longlong jing ding ahi. 
Chutenglehiset pumpia Pakai hung leng chang intin, Pakai chu khatbou hi ding ama minbou chu min ahin houding ahi. 
Gamsung pumpi Judah sahlang Geba-apat Jerusalem lhanglam Rimmon geihi phaicham lentah hung hi ding, amavang Jerusalem vang hi ama mun mama-a kikhang sang ding chuleh in ningkoi kelkot gei, chujou teng hannanol inting Vamampat lengpa lengpitwi chillhana nungei mi hungcheng dimsoh ding ahi. 
Jerusalem hi mi hung dimding sapset chang talouhel ding, hung kisuse talou ding achaina keija hoibitna khopi hung hi ding ahi. 
Chuleh Pakaijin Jerusalem khopi satna nabol chitin namtin ho chunga suhgamna hoise ahin lhadingchu hitiding ahi. Akeng uva adinpet jeng uva jong chu atahsau monlha jeng ding, amit kosung uva jong amitchang’u monlha jeng ding chuleh akamsung uva jong aleiju monlha jeng ding ahi. 
Pakai jepna achung uva che ding amaho tijatna dim ding, amitakip atophah phah chunga akhutna alap jeng diu ahi. 
Juda ten jong Jerusalem huhna-a gal asat diu avel uva um namdang ho gou, sana dangka chuleh nihchen ponsil tamtah akichom dohdiu ahi. 
Hiche gamna hisehi sakolte le sakolpolte, sangong sauté leh sangante chunga jong chuleh gal mite ngahmun'a um ganchadang ho jouse chunga jong chusohkei ding ahi. 
Achaina teng, Jerusalem hinsatna galmiho hiche gam'a hisea konna hoi doh ho chu kumseh leh Jerusalemma chetou diu hatchungnungpen Pakai kiti leng chu aga hou diu chuleh Lhambuh Kut mang dinga che jeng diu ahi. 
Vannoi leiset a koinam hamkhat hatchungnung pen Pakai, kiti lengpa chu hou dinga che lou aum khah leh agam sung uva go ju louding ahi. 
Egypt mite hiche kut a hi apan nomlou uleh Pakaijin amaho chu namdang ho tobanga che nomlou ho chunga achuhsah gamna hise tobang bangchu achung uva achuh sah ding ahi. 
Egypt leh namdang jouse hiche Lhambuh Kut mang dinga che nom lou jouse chunga chu hiche engbolna chu chu ding ahi. 
Hiche nikho hileh sakolte kikanna savunkhao chung dunga jong, “Pakai ja atheng,” tia kijih sohkei ding ahi. Pakai Hou-In sunga bel jouse jong, maicham maija khon ho tobanga athenga um ding ahi. 
Atahbeh in, Jerusalem leh Judah gamsunga kho ho jouse jong hatchungnung pen Pakaiya dinga atheng hi ding ahi. Pakai hou dinga hung jouse chun hiche bel ho hi pumgo thilto ho hkonnadinga adeidei amantheidiu ahi. Chuleh hiche nikho lechun hatchungnung Pakai Hou-In ahi kivei mi ho um thei louding ahi.
