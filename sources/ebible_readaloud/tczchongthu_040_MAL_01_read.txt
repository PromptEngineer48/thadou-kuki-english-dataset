Malachi Themgao Lekhabu.
1.
Pakaijin Malachi mangchan Israel te kom'a thu athot in ahi. 
Pakaijin hitin a sei’e, “Keiman nangho ka hingailu jing uve” ati. Ahinlah nang hon nei donbutnuvin “Iti nei ngailutnu ham” natiuve. Pakaijin Esau chu Jacob sopi hilou ham? Ahinlah keiman Jacob kangailun ahi. 
Esau chu ka thet in chuleh keiman a thinglhang gam chu ka suhchai peh helin, agoulona ding gam chu Sialte lhatna neldi gam kaso sahin ahi. 
Esau chilhah Edom in hiti hin hinsei jintin, keiho nei suchip deluvin ahi. Ahinlah keihon kaga phah nauva konna kahung kile kituva ka kisem phat kit diu ahi, atiuve. hatchungnung Pakai chun hiti hin aseije. “ama hon sapha jong leu keiman ka phetlhah peh diu ahi. Amaho chenna chu mipha lou gam kiti ding chuleh amite’u chu Pakai lunghan jing na mite” kiti ding ahiuve. 
Nanghon a kisuhmang na chu na mit tah uva namu tengule nangin, “Tahbeh in, Pakai loupina hi Israel te gamgi kalpeh a mihon jong amu uve” tin seijin nate. 
Hatchungnung Pakai chun thempu ho kom'ah hitin aseije. “Chan apa ajabol le, soh in a pakai pa agin ding ahi. Keihi napau le na Pakai pau kahile hoi chi ham nei jabolnau, hoiche ham nei jen nau? Nanghon ka min hi nahsah louvin nabolun ahi! Ahinlah nang hon na seijun kei hon namin nahsah louva kakoi khah um natiu’ve. 
Nanghon ka maichama an thenglou na toh’un ahinlah nang hon iti danna nangma ka suhboh diu ham? natiuve. Nanghon Pakai maicham jana peh angaipoi natiu chu na suhbohu ahi. 
Nanghon ganhing mitcho pumgo thilto a nahin to uchu dih mong’a hinam? Chutengleh nang hon a elbai le a damlou ganhing nato uchu dih mong hinam? Nanghon hitobang ho chu nagam vaipo ho kipa thilpehin gape leu chun a kipah nadiuvem? tin hatchungnung Pakai chun a seijin ahi. 
Tun kipat un Pakaijin nakhotona diuvin tao’un , ahinla hitobang thilpeh chu maichama napeh jing le Pakai chun iti khotona na musah ding ham? tin hatchungnung pen Pakai chun aseije. 
Hiche Hou-In kot hi nangho lah a khat pen penin khah chahleu chun kati lheh jeng’e, chuti leh hiche thil thonlou hung kitodoh ho hi umlou ding ahi. Ajeh chu keima na chunguvah kalung lhaipoi chuleh na thiltou jong ka sanpeh lou diu ahi, tin van sepai te Pakaijin aseije. 
Ahinlah namdanga kon mipi ten jingkah nilhahin vanoi leiset pumpia hin gimnamtui leh atheng ho hi kamin loupina dingin a todoh jingun kamin hi aloupi sahun chitin namtin lah a hin kamin hi choisang'in aum'in ahi, tin hatchungnung pen Pakaijin aseijin ahi. 
Ahinlah nangho natohdan hin ka min na sumin seuvin ahi. Ajeh chu nang hon Pakai dokhang hi kisuhboh jongleh akhoh poi natiuvin thilthenglou pou na hintoh’un ahi. 
Nanghon, kon Pakai alunglhaisah jou ding ham natiuvin ka thupeh na taitomun ahi, tin hatchungnung pen Pakaijin aseije “Gel uvin adamlou a elbai chuleh a ki gu doh gancha pou na hin todoh jiuve, hichu ken iti ka sanpeh ding nahiuvem? tin Pakaijin aseije” 
Koi hijongleh Pakai kom’a toh dinga kitepna neija aki lomlou joh hintoh chan chu sapsetna chang ding ahi. Chutengleh chitin namtin lah a kamin hi mi gin le ja kahithei ding ahi, tin hatchungnung pen Pakai chun a seije.
