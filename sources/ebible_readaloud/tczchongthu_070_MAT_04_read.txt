Matthew.
4.
Chutah in Jesu chu lhagao puiyin gamthip a diabol lhep patep dingin aumtai. 
Ni somli le jan somli jen, anngol ahijeh chun agil akel behseh jengin ahi. 
Hiche phat sung chun Diabol chu ahungin ajah a, “Nangma Pathen Chapa nahileh, hiche songchang ho hi changlhah akiso theina dingin thu pen,” ati. 
Hinlah Jesun ajah a, “Ahipoi! Pathen Thubun aseiye, Mihem changlhah bouva hing thei ahipoi, Pathen kam sunga kon hung potdoh thua bou chu hing ding ahi,” ati. 
Chuin Diabol chun khopi theng, Jerusalem Hou-In ting vuma chun adinsah kit in, 
ajah a, “Nangma Pathen Chapa nahi leh, kichomlhan!” Ajeh chu Pathen Thubun aseiye, “Aman avantil te nangma hongbit dinga thu apeh ding, chule ama hon nangma akhut uva nadop doh dingu chutia chu nangman nakeng phang jeng jong songa nakita khah louding ahi,” ati. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Pathen Thubun hitin jong aseiye, nangman Pakai na Pathen chu lhem patep hih in,” ati. 
Chujouvin Diabol in molsang chungvum a chun apuiyin chule vannoi lenggam jouse le aloupina ho avetsah in ahi. 
Aman ajah a, “Nangma naboh khup a chule keima nei houva ahileh, keiman nangma aboncha hi kapeh ding nahi,” atikit in ahileh, 
Jesun ajah a, “Satan, hichea kon hin potdoh in. Ajeh chu Pathen Thubun aseiye, 'Nangman Pakai na Pathen chu nahouva chule Ama bou najen ding ahi,'” ati. 
Chutah in Diabol chu ache mang in, chuin vantil te ahungun, Jesu chu ahung jen le tauve. 
Jesu chun John aman tauve tithu ajah phat in, Judea gam adalhan Galilee gama ahung kile kittai. 
Ama amasatah in Nazareth achen, chuin chumun chu adalhan, Zebulun le Naphtali gamkai sung Galilee twipi panga, Capernaum khoa aga chen ahi. 
Hichu Isaiah Themgao kam a Pathen thusei: 
“Zebulun le Naphtali gam, twipi pam, Jordan vadung gal'a Galilee gam, chidang namdang tampi umna mun, 
muthim lah a touho chun vah loupi tah amu tauve, chule thina gam le alim a touho chu achung uva kho avah tai,” ati chu agui lhunna ahi. 
Hichea pat chun Jesun thu asei phongin, “Na chonset nauva kon'in lung heiyun chule Pathen lama hung kiheiyun, ijeh inem itileh Van'a Lenggam anai tai,” ati. 
Nikhat hi Jesu Galilee twipi pam'a achea avale pet in, aman uchani—Simon, Peter jong kitipa le Andrew in twi lah a len asepet lhon agatoh e, ajeh chu amani ngaman a kivah lhon ahi. 
Jesun amani chu akouvin, “Hung lhon'in, kanung hinjui lhon'in chule keiman miho iti mat ding ahi navetsah lhon'inge!” ati. 
Chuin amanin apet tah chun alen lhon adalha lhon'in chule Ama nung ajui lhon tai. 
Chule Ama chukom'a kon chun achepaijin ahileh, ucha dang mini Jacob le anaopa John kong sunga apau Zebedee toh atou khom uva len akhui phat pet'u aga mun, chuin Aman amani jong chu akouvin ahi. 
Amanin chupet tah chun kong le apa adalha lhon'in, anung ajui pai jeng lhon tai. 
Chule Jesu chu Galilee gamkai sea akhol jin in, Judah te kikhopna intin ah thu ahil in, Lenggam Kipana Thupha aseiphong len ahi. Chule Ama chun miho natna jouse le adamlou jouse chu aboldam in ahi. 
Ama chung chang thu chu Syria geiyin akithang doh in, hiti chun miho chun ana damlou jouse chu akom'a ahin puiyuve. Chule itobang natna le hivei neiya nat thohlel ho, thilhavop ho le alung chavai ho hihen, chule langthi ho hijongleh Ama kom'a ahin puiyun, aman amaho chu abonchauvin aboldam sohkeiyin ahi. 
Ama chena chan a chun Galilee, Khopi som (Decapolis), Jerusalem chule Judea gama kon le Jordan gal lama kon'in, mipi tamtah in anung ajui tauvin ahi.
