Matthew.
6.
“Chingtheiyun! Midang pachat na channa diuvin, mipi mu dingin thilpha bol hih un; chutia nabol leu van'a na Pauva konna kipaman mulou ding nahiuve. 
Ngaichat nei mi chu thilpeh napeh tengu leh, mi phalhem ho bangin bol hih un; ama hon kikhopna in hoa le lamlhong hoa sumkon amut uvin mi apanpina hou chu miho ahetsah jiuvin ahi. Tahbeh a ka seipeh nahiuve, amaho hin kipaman amu diu abona akimu'u ahitai. 
Hinlah nangman koi ham khat ngaichat thil napeh tengleh, nakhut jetlam in abol chu nakhut veilam hetsah hih in. 
Na thilpeh ding ho chu guhthim in pen, ijakai aguh a mua na Pa chun nangma kipaman napeh ding ahi.” 
“Nangma natao tengleh, mi phalhem ho banga lamlhong pam leh kikhopna inhoa mitin in amu diu deina a tao nom hotoh bang hih in. Tahbeh a ka seipeh nahiuve, hichu bou ama hon kipaman asan diu chu ahi jenge. 
Hinlah nangma tao dinga nache tengleh nangma changseh chen lang, na nunglam kot khah in chule guhthima mu na Pa henga taovin. Chuteng na Pa, ijakai mupa chun, nangma kipaman napeh ding ahi.” 
“Nangma natao tengleh, houdang ho bangin naseisa chu avel vel in seikit hih in. Amaho chun taona thucheng aman jiu chu avel vel'a sei jal a ataona chu sanpeh hi dinga aginchat u ahi. 
Nangho amaho toh bang hih uvin, ijeh inem itileh van'a um na Pauvin na ngaichat u chu nathum masang uva ahetsa ahitai! 
Hijeh chun hiti hin taovun: Van'a um ka-Pau, namin jatah in umhen. 
Na Lenggam hunglhung loihen, Van'a nalung lhaina aum tobanginlei chunga jong na lung lhaina umhen. 
Tunia ka ngaichat'u anneh nei peuvin, 
Keiho douna a chonse ho ka ngaidam bangun, keiho chonsetna jong ngaidam in. 
Chule lhepna thua nei nunsah hih un, ahin agiloupa a kon'in nei huhdoh uvin. 
“Nangho douna a chonseho na ngaidam uva ahileh, van'a na Pauvin nangho na ngaidam diu ahi. 
Hinlah midang na ngaidam nom lou uva ahileh, na Pauvin nangho chonsetna angaidam louding ahi.” 
“Chule nang hon an na ngol tengu leh, mi phalhem ho bangin ki nguisah hih un, ijeh inem itile ama hon an angolu min ahetna din amaisou alamdan sah jiuve. Tahbeh a ka seipeh nahiuve, ama hon kipaman hichea chu akimuu ahitai. 
Hinlah nangman an nangol tengleh, nasam kipon chule namai kiphet in. 
Chutileh na anngol chu van'a na Pa tailou koiman hepon tin, guhthim a na thilbol mua na Pa chun kipaman napeh ding ahi.” 
“Nget in aneh heng thei le ap in asuhmang theina, chule gucha hon asuhset uva akiguh thei nau, hiche leiset chunga hin gou khol hih un. 
Amavang nget le ap in asuhmang thei louna chule gucha hon jong asuhset uva aki guhdoh theilou nau van'a nagouvu kikhol un. 
Ijeh inem itileh nagou umna a chu, nalung ngaichat'u jong um ding ahi.” 
“Namit hi natahsa dinga vahpea pang ahi. Namit aphat pet a natahsa pumpi hi vahsoh keiya ahi. 
Hinlah namit hi aphatlou tengleh natahsa pumpi hi thimsoh jia ahitai. Chule vah neiya na kigel chu, thima hi tahbeh le hiche athim chu iti natsat ding hitam!” 
“Mihem koiman pakai ni aneikop theipoi. Ajeh chu aman khat chu thet bol intin chule khat joh chu ngailun te; nangma khat a naki pehlut ding chule khat joh chu na nahsahmo ding ahi. Nangma Pathen le sum ani gel a lhacha a na pankop thei ahipoi.” 
“Hiti ahijeh chun nitin nehkhop ding an ahilouleh, twi ahilouleh ponsil thua nalung kham louna diu ka seipeh nahiuve. Hinkho hi an sanga thupi joa chule na tahsa chu ponsil sanga thupi joa hilou ham? 
Chunga leng vacha ho khu ven. Ajeh chu amaho khun tula atu pouvin, at la a at pouvin ahilouleh changpang geiya aneilou vangun, van'a um na Pauvin amaho khu avah nai. Chule nangho amaho sanga khu gamchenga na manlut joh u hilou ham? 
Na lungkhamna hou chun, na hinkhou them khat beh ajop saobe theiyem?” 
“Chule sil le chen dinga ibol a lung khama nahiuvem? Lou jaova molhoipah ho khu iti akhan ngaito uvin. Amaho khun natoh la atong pouvin, aponsil diu jong akibol pouve. 
Ahivangin Solomon chu aloupina jouse pum'in jong hiche ho bangin ahoi joupoi. 
Chule Pathen'in gamlah a pahcha jeng jong tunia uma chule jing le meisunga kilehlut ding bon hibanga hi ajep hoiya ahileh, nangma ding jong thon louva agel khoh ding ahi. Itia hiti loma hi tahsan neo nanei uham?” 
“Hijeh chun, ipi kaneh diu? Ipi kadon diu? Ipi kachen diu? tin hiche ho thua hin lungkham hih un. 
Hiche lunggel ho hin tahsan lou ho lunggel athunun ahi, hinlah van'a um na Pau chun hiti ho jouse na ngaichat uchu ahet ahi. 
Hicheng jouse chungvum'a chun Pathen Lenggam hol'un, chule chonpha tah in hing uvin, Aman na ngaichat chan'u chu napeh diu ahi.” 
“Hiti ahijeh chun jinga dingin lungkham hih un, ajeh chu jinga ding hung um nante. Tuni gimna chu tunikhoa dingin aphaset tai.”
