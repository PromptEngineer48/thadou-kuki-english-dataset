Matthew.
7.
“Mi koima oimo dan, chutile koiman na oimo louding ahi. 
Ajeh chu midang na oimona banga chu, Pathen'in na oimo ding ahi. Nangman mi nabolna bang banga chu na chunga kibol ding ahi.” 
“Chule nangma mit a bohse hal um chu boipi louva, ibol a nasopi mit a bohse hal um chu kisah boipia nahim? 
Ahilouleh nangma mit a bohse hal um muthei louva, nasopi jah a keiman namit a bohse hal um chu na lahdoh peh inge, iti nati thei ham? 
Miphalhem! Nangma mit a bohse hal um chu amasan kilah doh le chun, nagol mit a bohse hal um chu kichen tah in muthei tan nate.” 
“Uicha ho khu thil theng pe hih un, chule vohcha ho angsunga khun twikoh chang jong lehlha hih un, chuti bol leu chun hichu akeng uvin chil'un tin chule hung kilehei uva nangho jong nahin bah diu ahi.” 
“Na thum uchu thum jomun, chutileh na thum uchu nakipe diu ahi; nahol'u chu holjom un chutileh nahol'u chu namu diu ahi. Nakiu uchu kiu jom un chutileh nakiu uchu kot kihonpeh ding nahiuve. 
Ajeh chu athum chan akipen, ahol chan achang uve. Chule akiu chan kot aki honpeh in ahi.” 
“Nangho minu mipa te, na chateuvin changlhah pheng athum leh hiche khel a chu song napeh uvem? 
Ahilouleh ama hon nga thum leu, amaho chu gul nape diuvem? Napeh lou diu ahi! 
Chutia nangho chonset bol hon bon na chateu thilpha man beiya pehje nahet uva ahileh, hisanga chu gam chenga van'a na Pau chun akom'a thumte chu thilpha manbeiya apeh louding ham.” 
“Hijeh chun imalam jousea midang in iti nabol leu nadei uvem, hitobang chun nang hon jong bol un. Hiche hi Dan bua kisun leh Themgao ho thuhil chu ahi.” 
“Nangho Pathen Lenggam'a chu kotjim'a konna bou nalut thei diu ahi. Ajeh chu manthahna lam lhong chu lenpi tah ahin, chule akot jong jaotah ahin, chua lut ding kilhen'a chu mi tamtah ahiuve. 
Hinlah hinna kot chu akichi lheh in, chule alampi jong ahah in chule mi lhomcha bouvin akimu diu ahi.” 
“Naheng uva hung jia themgao lhemho, kelngoi vona kivona ahia asunglam ngei hangtah ho chu kichih theiyun. 
Nanghon amaho chu agauva, tichu achon nauva konna na hetdoh thei diu ahi: Mihon lingnang lah a konna lengpiga alo jiuva, ahilouleh ling phunga konna theichang ga alo ngai jiuvem? 
Thingphung pha chun gapha asodoh in, chule thingphung phalou chun ga phalou asodohji ahi. 
Thingphung pha chun ga phalou asodoh thei pon , chule thingphung phalouvin jong gapha asodoh theipoi. 
Hitia chu thingphung gapha sodoh louse chu kiphuh lhua chule mei sunga kileh lutji ahi. 
Hijeh chun thingphung chu aga a konna hetdoh theina nanei bangun, miho chu achon nauva konna nang hon ahinau na hetdoh thei diu ahi.” 
“Keima, Pakai! Pakai! tia eikou tapou chu Van gam lut ding ahipoi. Van'a kapa lunglhaina bol ho bou chu lut ding ahi. 
Thutan nikho leh mitampi chun kajah a, Pakai! Pakai! Nangma min'in gaothu kaseiyun, chule nangma min'in thilha ho ka nodoh uvin, chule nangma min'in thilbol theina kidang kaboldoh uve ati diu ahi. 
Hinlah keiman, ‘Nangho itih in jong kahekha pouve. Pathen dan thu palkeh a nangho, ka kom'a kon'in potdoh un,’ tia ka donbut diu ahi.'” 
“Ka thuhil ngaiya chule jui chan chu koi hijongleh, miching songpi chunga in kisahpa tobang ahi. 
Go hungju henlang, twi hungsoh hen chule hui hungnung henlang, hiche in chu hinsu jongleh, songpi chunga kisa ahijeh a chu lhulou ding ahi. 
Hinlah ka thuhil ngaiya ahia la juilou chan chu mingol, nel lei chunga insa tobang ahi. 
Go ahung juh a twi chu ahung soa chule hui chun hiche in chu asuh a ahileh gimnei tah in alhupai jeng tai,” ati. 
Jesun hicheng thu asei jouvin, mipi chun athuhil kidang asa lheh jeng uve. 
Ajeh chu aman hou lamkai ho thuhil tobanga hillouvin, thunei tah in thu ahil'in ahi.
