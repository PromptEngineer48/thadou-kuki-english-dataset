Matthew.
8.
Chujouvin Jesu mol chunga konna ahung kuma ahileh, mipi atama tam'in anung ajuiyuvin ahi. 
Photlot in, “Miphah khat in Ama akimu pin chule a anga abohkhup in, ajah a, Pakai nalung ahileh nei damsah a chule nei thensah thei ahi,” ahung titai. 
Chuin Jesun akhut alhaangin chule athamin, “Kalung ahi, dam jengin,” atin ahileh apettah in aphah chu atheng tan ahi.” 
Chuin Jesun ajah a, “Hiche thudol hi koima kom'a seihih in. Chusangin, nangma thempu kom'a gachen lang chule aman nave chil hen. Mose Danbua miphah ho athen teng thupehsa thilto ding chu gatoh tan. Hiche hi nathengtai ti ahetna, mipi lah a na phondohna hi ding ahi,” ati. 
Chule Jesu Capernaum ahung kile phat in, Rome Sepai jalamkai khat ahenga ahungin chule ama chu atem in, 
“Pakai, kasohpa alangthia ahin, jalkhun'a alum'e chule anatna chu athohlel lheh in ahi,” ati. 
Jesun ajah a, “Keima hung ingting chule kahung damsah ding ahi,” ati. 
Hinlah sepai jalamkaipa chun, “Pakai, nangman ka-in neihung chotlut peh dinga lhingjou chu kahipoi. Na umna mun'ah thu chu hinsei lechun, kasohpa hi dam tante.” 
Hiche hi kahei, ajeh chu keima ka chunga thunei lamkai lenho aum'inchule keiman sepai te chunga thu kaneiye. Keiman, “Cheuvin katileh acheuvin, ahilouleh hungun, katileh amaho ahung uve. Chule keiman kasohte jah a, ‘Hiche hi bol un katileh, ama hon abol uve,’” ati. 
Jesun hichu ajah phat in adatmo lheh jenge. Aman anungjuiho jah a aseijin, “Thudih tahbeh ka seipeh nahiuve, Israel ho jouse lah ah hitobang tahsan hi kamu khapoi! 
Chule hiche hi ka seipeh nahiuve, chidang namdang ho mi tamtah vannoi muntin'a konna hung diu solama kon le lhum lama konna Abraham, Isaac chule Jacob toh Van gam'a golvah an nea tou ding ahiuve. 
Hinlah Israel mi tamtah ho adiuva lenggam kigonpeh ho chu polam muthim lah a ale lut dingu, hichea chu ka le hagel um ding ahi,” ati. 
Chutah in Jesun Rome sepai jalamkaipa jah a aseijin, “Inlam'a kile tan. Ijeh inem itileh nangman na tahsan chu aguilhung tai,” ati. Chule soh khangdongpa chu apettah chun adamtai. 
Jesu Peter in ahung lhun phat in, Peter jinu nu chu alupna a khosih natna neiya leltah a uma ahi. 
Hinlah Jesun ama chu akhut athampeh in ahileh, akhosih chun adalhatai. Chuin ama athoudoh in Ama ding chun anneh ding agong in ahi. 
Nilhah ahiphat in lhagao boh vop mi tamtah Jesu henga ahin pui tauvin. Aman lhagao gilou ho chu thu apen adamlou jouse chu aboldam tai. 
Hiche hin Pakai thu Isaiah themgao in asei, “Aman inatnau aladoh in chule ihiveiyu alamang tai,” ati chu aguilhun sah e. 
Jesun mihonpi akimvel'a aum amun, aman aseijui te chu dil langkhat a galkai dingin thu apen ahi. 
Chuphat in Hou dan thuhil ho khat in ajah a, “Houhil, na chena chan'ah najui inge,” ati. 
Hinlah Jesun adonbut in, “Sial hon chenna ding ko aneiyun, chule vacha hon gena ding bu aneiyui, hinlah Mihem Chapan alu ngapna ding mun jeng jong aneipoi,” ati. 
Aseijui dang khat in ajah a, “Pakai, in a gakile masa'ng ting kapa gavui inge” ati. 
Hinlah Jesun aseipeh in ajah a, “Tun neijui in. Lhagaova thi hon ami thiu kivui nauvinte,” ati. 
Chuin Jesu kong sunga alut in, chule aseijui te chutoh dil langkhat panga chun agalkai pan tauve. 
Photlot changin, twipi kinong in dil chu ahin nuh in, chule twi kinong ho chun kong chu ahin nuh a ahileh kong sunga aluttai; hinlah Ama a'imun ahi. 
Seijuiho chu aga cheuvin, chule asukhang un, akou un, “Pakai nei huhdoh un, twiyin eichup diu ahitai,” atiuve. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Ipi dinga kicha a nahiuvem? Tahsanna neocha nanei uve!” ati. Chuphat in Ama aki patdoh in chule huipi le twipi kinong chu aphoh in, chuin phulou helouvin athip paitai. 
Seijui ho chun adatmo lheh uvin, “Hiche mipa hi koi ham? Huipi le twipi kinong Ama thun anunge,” atiuve. 
Jesu chu dil langkhat pang agei tan, Gadarene gamkai alhungin ahileh lhagao boh vop minin akimupi lhon e. Amani chu lhan mun'a chengden lhona chule gilou huham behseh ahijeh lhon a, koiman hiche lam chu ahopa ngam lou ahi. 
Amani chun ahinsam lhon'in, ajah a, “Pathen Chapa, koi nahi hiya nei hung suhboiyu ham? Nangma Pathen phat tep masanga hikom mun'a hi eisugim diuva hunga nahim?” ati lhon e. 
Mun gamla tah a vohcha hon tampi loujaova lha a um uva ahi. 
Hiti chun Lhagaoboh vop teni chu atao lhon'in, “Nei nodoh diuva ahileh vohcha honsunga khun neisol un,” atiuve. 
Jesun amaho chu thu apen, “Aphai, cheuvin!” ati. Hiti chun lhagao boh ho chu mi tenia kon'in ahung potdoh un, chuin vohcha ho sunga chun alut tauvin, chule vohcha jouse chu abon'un mol pang dillen sunga chun alhai lha uvin, twi lah a chun alhalut gam tauve. 
Gancha chinga pang ho chu akom'a khopia chun alhai lut un, lhagao boh vop teni chunga thilsoh thudol chu amitakip aseipeh tauve. 
Chuphat in khopia miho jouse chu ahung potdoh un, Jesu ahung kimu piuvin, ama hon Jesu chu ache mang'a chule amaho adalhahna diuvin atem un ahi.
