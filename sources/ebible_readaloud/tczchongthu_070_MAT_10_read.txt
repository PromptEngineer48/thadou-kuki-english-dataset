Matthew.
10.
Jesun aseijui som le ni ho chu akou khomin chule amaho chu lhagao gilou ho nodoh theina le hivei namkim le natna ho damsah theina thuneina apen ahi. 
Seijui som le ni ho min: Amasapen, Simon (Peter jong akitin), aban Andrew (Peter sopipa), James (Zebedee chapa), John (James sopipa), 
Philip, Bartholomew, Thomas, Matthew (kaidongpa), James (Alpheus chapa), Thaddaeus, 
Simon (Zealot mi) chule Iscariot a kon Judas (ama matsah a pang), hi chengse hi ahiuve. 
Jesun seijui som le ni ho hiti hin ahilchah in: “Nangho chidang namdang ho ahilouleh Samaria ho kom'a na chelou diu, 
Pathen kelngoi mangsa Israel te kom'a bou nache diu ahi. 
Chule nache nauva Van'a Lenggam chu ahung naitai ti phongdoh un. 
Ana sudam un, mithi kaithou un, phahvei ho ngimsah un, chule lhagao boh ho nodoh un. Man beiya kilah a nahi bangun, phaldoh jengun!” 
“Na sumdip uva sum, na konggah uvah sana dangka ahilouleh sumgoi jeng jong kipoh hih-un. 
Kholjin na a sakhao jeng jong akop in pohih un, kengchot ho le tenggol jeng jong kiken hih un chule min najen phat uleh kiphalam hih un, ijeh inem itileh natonga chun aneh ding dol ahi.” 
“Khopi sung hihen, khoneo sung hijongleh nalut nauva chun lhunna theija kilom mi chu holdoh un chule khosung ho na dalhah kahse uvin hiche pa in a chun umun. 
In nalut tengu leh phattheina chu peuvin. 
Achang dinga kilom in ahiuleh na phatthei nau chu umden hen, kilom lou in ahiuleh phattheina chu nunglah doh un. 
Koi insung hihen, ahilouleh khopi sung hijongleh na lamdot lou uva, ahilouleh na thupoh u angainom lou uleh, na dalhah ding tengu leh nakeng uva vutvai geiyin thing lhauvin. 
Thutahbeh ka seipeh nahiuve, thutan nikho teng chuleh chutobang khopin athoh ding chu, gitlouna dim khopi teni: Sodom le Gomorrah thoh gamchenga oljo ding ahi.” 
“Veuvin, keiman ngeihon lah a kelngoi tobanga kasol nahiuve. Hiti chun gul ho tobangin chingthei jun chule vapal ho tobanginsuhset khoiset neihih un. 
Hinlah ching theiyun! Ijeh inem itileh thutan ho khut a nahin pehdoh diu chule kikhopna in hoa sakol jepna a nahin jep diu ahi. 
Nangho kanungjui nahi jeh uva gam vaipoho leh lengho angsunga na chung thu'u kitan ding ahi. Ahin hichu vaipoa pangho le atahsan lou ho henga, keima thu nasei theina diu phat kijen tah hung hi ding ahi. 
ama hon nahin mat tengu leh, iti ka donbut ding ham tin lungkham hih un; nasei diu apettah a Pathen'in napeh diu ahi. 
Ijeh nem itileh thuseija chu nangho hita louva na Pau Lhagao chun nanghoa konna thu ahin seidoh ding ahi.” 
“Sopi khat chun asopi chu thi dinga apeh doh ding, apa chun acha tahbeh jong chu apehdoh ding chule achaten anu le pa adou uva chule athasah diu ahi. 
Chule nang hon keima nung najui jeh uva nam jousen nahin hotbol diu ahi. Hijongleh achaina geiya thoh lhung jou chengse bou chu, huhhing a um ding ahiuve. 
Nanghon khopi khat abol hesohna nato tengu leh, aban khoa jamlut un. Thutahbeh ka seipeh nahiuve, Israel kho jouse naphah masang uva, Mihem Chapa hi hung ding ahi.” 
“Ahil uva pangpa sangin akihil ho alenjo ngaipoi chule soh ho jong apu sangin alenjo pouve! 
Akihil ho chu ahilpau to abah uva chule soh ho jong apu uto abah diu chu phaset ahi. Chule keima insung pumpi neipa bon lhagao bohho lengpa eitiuva ahileh, ka insung mite chu hisanga phamo joa akou diu ahi!” 
“Hijongleh nagih uva pang ho chu kicha hihbeh un. Ajeh chu akisel guh jouse ki phondoh phat hung lhug ding ahi. Chule aguh a kisel jouse jong mijouse het a um ding ahi. 
Tua keiman muthim lah a kasei hi, kho ahungvah tengleh lhang phonga kiphongdoh ding ahi. Keiman nakol uva thol a kasei hi mijouse jah dinga, in chunga kisam doh ding ahi!” 
“Na tahsau that go ho kicha hih un, ama hon na lhagao atongkha thei pouve. Pathen bou ging un, Ama chun na lhagao le na tahsau asuhmang thei chule damun a akoi thei ahi. 
Inva nin ijat amat nahet em? Paisa khat man bou ahi. Hinlah inva khat jeng jong na Pau het louva leiset a lhalou ahi. 
Chule nalu uva nasam jang'u geiya kisim ahi. 
Hijeh chun kicha hih un, inva ho aboncha uva sanga manlu joa nahiuve.” 
“Hiche leiset chunga Keima mipi ho lah a eiphongdoh a chu, Keiman jong van'a Kapa angsunga Ka phondoh ding ahi. 
Hinlah hiche leiset chunga hi koi hijongleh, ei seilep chan chu Van'a um Kapa angsunga kaseilep ding ahi.” 
“Keima leiset a hi chamna hinpo lut dingin nei gel hih un! Keima chamna poa hung hijo louvin, chemjam joh kahin poh ahi. 
‘Mikhat chun apa adou ding chule achanu chun anu adou ding, chule mounu tah chun atehpi adou dinga hunga kahi, 
na melma teu chu na insung miho lah a konna hi ding ahi.’” 
“Nangman napa ahilouleh nanu chu keima sanga na ngailut joh a ahileh, nangma keiya hi dinga kilom nahipoi; ahilou jongleh na chapa ahilouleh na chanu chu keima sanga na ngailut joh a ahileh, nangma keiya hi dinga kilom nahipoi. 
Thingpel chu nakilah a chule kiput ding nanop louva ahileh, keiya hi dinga kilom nahipoi. 
Na hinna na kikol chah a ahileh na mansah ding, hinlah nangman keima dinga na hinna chu napeh doh jenga ahileh, na kimu ding ahi.” 
“Nangma na sanga chun keima eisan ahi chule keima eisanga chun, eisol Pa chu asan ahi. 
Nangman themgao khat chu Pathen a dia thusei phong ahi chu nasan a ahileh, themgao khat hina a kipa man chu nasan ding ahi. Chule nangman mihongphal ho chu achonphat jeh uva nasan a ahileh, nangman jong amahoa tobang kipaman namu ding ahi. 
Chule nangman twi dap khon khat ka nungjuiho lah a aneopen jeng jong napeh a ahileh, nangman kipaman namu tei ding ahi.” ati.
