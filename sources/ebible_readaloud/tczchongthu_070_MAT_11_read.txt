Matthew.
11.
Jesun hichenga seijui som le ni ho thu ahil jouvin, gamkai pumpia khopi hoa thuhil le lhangsam dingin aki patdoh tai. 
Songkul a um John Baptist chun Messiah thil bol jouse thudol ajatan ahi. Hiti hin aseijui te ho chu asol in Jesu aga dohsah in, 
“Keihon kanga jingu Messiah chu nahi hitam, ahilouleh midang kanga be nahlai dingu ham?” atiuve. 
Jesun amaho chu aseipeh in, “John henga kile kit un chule nathujah hou le namusa hou hi gaseipeh un, 
mitchon kho amun, elbaiyin lam ajot in, miphah ho angim un, na ngong in kho ajan, athisa kai thouvin aum'inchule mi vaicha Kipana Thupha seipeh ahi. 
Chule ajah a ‘Keima jeh a kihei mang lou mi chu Pathen'in phattheina apei’ tiuvin,” ati. 
John seijui hon adalhah phat un, Jesun achung chang thu mihonpi chu ahoulim pin, “Koi itobang mi gave dinga gamthip a chu gachea nahiu ham? Ama pumpeng detlou hui anun chan a kilon lele tobang bep chu ham? 
Ahilouleh pon manlutah a kivon a hintem? Ahipoi, pon manlutah a kivon ho khu leng inpia aum uve. 
Themgao kimupi noma nahiuvem? Henge, chule ama themgao sanga chungnung joa ahi. 
John chung chang thu hi Pathen Thubun asei, 'Ven keiman na masanga palai khat ka solin chule ama chun na masanga na lampi ding nagon peh ding ahi.'” 
“Thutah ka seipeh nahiuve, mihing jouse lah a Twilutsah John sanga chungnungjo ding aumpoi. 
Twilutsah John in thu asei phonga pat tuchan geiyin, Van gam chu gum hachang in amachal in, chule migilou hon hichu anokhum jing uve. 
Ajeh chu John hung masanga kipat, themgao ho jouse le Mose Dan chun tulai phat hi agaldot uve. 
Chule keiman kasei hi nakisan theiyuva ahileh, ama hi Themgao hon aseiyu ahung ding Elijah chu ahi. 
Koi hileh thu ngaina ding nakol neiho chun ngaiyu hen chule hekhen uhen!” 
“Tulai khang mite hi ipi toh ka tekah ding ham? Kailhang mun a chapang kichemho toh tekah thei ahiuve. Ama hon agolte kom'a nopmo sahna aphongdoh uvin, 
‘Keihon golvahna la kasah peh uvin, hinlah nangho nalam pouve, chule thi opna la kasah peh uvin, hinlah nangho nalung hem pouve.’ 
Ajeh chu John in neh le don in phat anei pon, chuin nang hon 'Ama chu lhagao boh vop ahi,' natiuve. 
Alang khat in, Mihem Chapan aneh a chule adona ahileh, 'Ama hi milop le julop ahi natiuve, chule kaidong ho le mi chonse ho jol le gol ahi bouve!' natiuve. Hinlah chihna hi aga hon adihna avetsah joh ahi,” ati. 
Chuti chun Jesun melchihna kidang abolna khopi ho chu phongtah in themmo achan in ahi, ajeh chu amaho achonset nauva kon'in alung hei pouvin chule Pathen lama amai ahei pouvin ahi. 
“Korazin le Bethsaida! itobang lungkham nan nahin pha diuvem? Ajeh chu thilbol kidang naheng uva kibol ho hi Tyre le Sidon a kibol hita leh, masang peh chun achonset nauva kon'in lung heiyun tin, akisih nau chun khaodip pon kivon uvin tin chule aluchung uvah vutvai kinu tauvinte. 
Ka seipeh nahiuve, thupi kitan nikho tengleh Tyre le Sidon thoh ding chu athoh nomjo hi ding ahi.” 
“Chule nangho Capernaum mite, nangho van gam a kijabol dinga kigel nahiuvem? Chuti ponte, nangho mithi lah a kipuisuh ding nahiuve. Ajeh chu melchihna nalah uva kibol ho hi Sidon'a ana kibol hitaleh, tuni chan geiya hi um diu ahi. 
Ka seipeh nahiuve, Sodom jeng jong chu thupi kitan nikhoa nangho sanga phajo ding ahiuve,” ati. 
Hiche pet chun Jesu hitin ataove, “O Hepa, van le leiset Pakai, mi chingho le thil hetheiya kigel ho henga hicheng thil ho na selguh a chule chapang banga lunggel ho henga na hetsah jeh in ka thangvah e. 
Henge, Pa, nangman hiti lama bol ding chu na lunglhaina ahi!” 
“Ijakai ka-Pan einganse ahitai. Pa tailou koiman Chapa chu ahe tahbeh poi chule Chapan aki phondohna Chapa tailou koiman Pa chu ahe tahbeh pouve.” 
Chutah in Jesun, “Thachol ho le pohgih hahsa poho kaheng'a hung uvin, chutileh Keiman olsah uvinge. 
Ka namkol jon kilah uvin kibu uvin. Keiman kahil ding nahiuve, ajeh chu Keima nunnem tah le lungneng tah kahi chule nalung un lung olna amu ding ahi. 
Ajeh chu ka namkol jon poh hi abaiyin chule ka pohsah got hi ayang e,” ati.
