Matthew.
14.
Galilee vaipopa Herod Antipas in Jesu thu ajah phat in, 
Aman asemang ho kom'a chun, “Hiche hi Twilutsah John athia thoudoh kit a hitei inte! Hijeh a chu hitobang thil kidang ho hi aboldoh ahi,” ati. 
Ajeh chu Herod in John chu ajinu Herodias (Herod naopa Philip ji ana hi) lung holna a amat a songkul'a ahen ahi. 
John chun Herod jah a “Amanu na kichenpi hi Pathen Danthu toh kikal ahi,” atipeh jing anahi. 
Herod chun John chu atha nom ahin, hinlah mipin adou diu chu kicha a ahi, ajeh chu mijousen John chu Themgao khat ahi tia atahsan'u ahi. 
Hinlah Herod in apen nikhoa golnop abol nauva, Herodias chanu alam chun ama alunglhai lheh jengin. 
Hijeh chun amanu hin adei chan ipi hijongleh pe dinga Herod in kitepna anei ahi. 
Anu ngeh dungjuijin, “Nungahnu chun, keiman Twilutsah John luchang chu lhengkonga kadeiye!” atitai. 
Chuphat in lengpan thu asei chu akisih in, hinlah jin-gunho masanga akitepna ahijeh chun, aman aki ngehna bang chun bol dingin asoltai. 
Hiti chun Twilutsah John chu songkul'a alu atantai. 
Chuin alu chu konga ahung kidom in chule nungahnu chu apen, ama chun anu apetai. 
Khonungin, John seijui ho chu ahungun atahsa chu ahung lauvin chule avui tauve. Chutah in amaho aga cheuvin, chuin Jesu jah a ipi soh a ham ahung hil tauve. 
Jesun thusoh chu ajah jah in, Ama munchom beh a konga aga chen, achanga aga umtai. Hinlah mipi chun hoilama chea ahi aheuvin, chuin khopi tin'a kon'in Ama chu akeng in ajui tauve. 
Jesu chu konga konna ahung kumlhah a ahileh mihonpi chu amun, chule Aman amaho chu akhoton chule anat nau adamsah tai. 
Hiche nilhah chun seijui ho ahungun chule aseiyun, “Hiche lai mun hi mun ningkoi ahin chule phat hi geisanga ahitai. Nangman mihonpi hi soldoh tan, amaho ache uva khotin'a aneh diu akichoh theina dingun,” atiuvin ahi. 
Hinlah Jesun aseijin, “Hichu hithei ponte, nang hon neh ding peuvin,” ati. 
ama hon adonbut uvin, “Keihon changlhah pheng nga leh nga ni bou kaneiyuve,” atiuvin ahi. 
Jesun aseijin “Hichu hin choiyun,” ati. 
Chutah in aman miho chu phoilah a tou dingin aseipeh tai. Jesun changlhah pheng nga le nga ni chu alan vanlam aven, chule phatthei aboh in, chuin aman changlhah chu alan, ahehbal bal in, aseijui ho apen, amaho chun mihonpi chu apeuve. 
ama hon angaichat chanun ane uvin, aval chu seijui hon paipeh som le ni alohdoh uve. 
Chuche nikho chun pasal cheh mi sangnga chun ane vaset uvin, numei leh chapang ho jong ajao un ahi. 
Hiche jou chun Aman miho chu ain uva asol laiyin, aseijui te chu konga lut a chule Dil gal langkhatna ana galkai dingin asol in ahi. 
Amaho ain uva asoldoh jouvin, ama mol chunga tao dingin akaldoh in ahi. Jan ahung hitan, Ama achangseh in mol a aumtai. 
Hiche pet chun, seijui ho chun hahsatna atoh un, tolgo laiya konna gamla tah geiyuva ahi tauve, hui hattah ahung nungin, chule amaho chun twipi kinong hattah chu akimaito piuvin ahi. 
Jingkah pung thum don in Jesu amaho heng lama ahungin, twi chunga lam ajot in ahi. 
Seijui hon Aman twi chunga lam ahinjot chu amuvin ahileh akicha lheh jeng uve. Akichat jeh un amaho apeng uvin: “Lhadam ahi,” atiuve. 
Hinlah Jesun ahin hou paiyin, “Kicha hih un. Lungmong tah in umun. Hiche a kaum nai,” ati. 
Chuphat in Peter in Ama ahin houvin, “Pakai, Nangma nahi monga ahileh, nahenga twi chunga lam kahin jotna dingin neihin seipeh in,” ati. 
Jesun, “Henge, hungin” ati. Hiti chun Peter chu kong panga akum lhan chule Jesu lam jon in twi chunga lam ajot tai. 
Hinlah Peter in hui hattah nung leh twipi kinong amu phat in, akichan chule lhalhum dingin aumtai. Chuin aman asam in, “Pakai, nei huhhing in,” ati. 
Apettah chun Jesun akhut alhaang in chule ama chu aman in, “Na tahsan aneo behseh e, ipi dinga nei sonlel ham?” ati. 
Amani kong sunga alut lhon phat in, huipi athip heltai. 
Chuphat in seijui ho ahungun, Ama ahou tauve. Ama hon, “Nangma Pathen Chapa tahbeh nahi monge,” atiuve. 
Amaho Dillen chu agalkaiyun, Gennesaret alhung tauve. 
Miho chun Jesu ahi ahet phat un, agamkai pumpia chun ama hung lhun chu akisei thang sohtai, chule chomkah louvin miho chun ana damlou aneihou chu aboldam dingin abonchan ahin pui tauve. 
Amaho chun apon mongkai beh atham ding uvin angeh uvin, chule Ama thamkha jouse adam tauve.
