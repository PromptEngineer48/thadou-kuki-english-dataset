Matthew.
16.
Nikhat hi Pharisee ho leh Sadducee ho Jesu kom'a apatep dingin ahungun, athuneina thudol'a melchihna kidang khat ahung ngeh uvin ahi. 
Aman adonbut in, “Nanghon janteng van asan le jing leh kho pha ding ahi natiuvin; 
jingkah a van asan le nilhuma kho phalou ding ahi natiuva nahet u ahi. Nanghon kho phat ding thua van'a melchihna a konna nahet u ahi, hinlah phat umdol amelchihna na hedoh thei pouve. 
Khang gilou le jonthanhoi na khang miten melchihna kidang ahol uve, hinlah melchihna khat bou amaho kapeh dingu chu themgao Jonah melchihna bou hi ding ahi,” ati.Chuin Jesun amaho adalhan achemang tai. 
Chuche jouvin, Dillen langkhat lama agalkai phat in, seijui hon changlhah apoh lou uchu ahin geldoh tauve. 
Jesun amaho agihsal'in, “Pharisee ho le Sadducee ho chol'a kon'in kichih theiyun,” ati. 
ama hon changlhah apoh tahlou jeh u chun khat le khat akinel den tauve. 
Jesun ama hon ipi seija ahiu chu ahen, hiti chun, “Na tahsanu alhom tave! Changlhah neilou jeh a ipi dia kinela nahiuvem? 
Tugeiya hi nahetdoh lou uham? Mi sangnga changlhah pheng nga a kavahva a chu aval paipeh ijat nalodoh u hitam? 
Ahilou jongleh mi sangli changlhah pheng sagia, chule aval paipeh tamtah nalodoh u hitam? 
Ipi iti dana changlhah chung thua kasei ahipoi ti hedoh theilou nahiuvem? Hitia hi avel'a kasei ahi, 'Pharisee ho leh Sadducee ho chol lah achun kichih theiyun kati ahi,'” ati. 
Chuphat in anunung pena, Aman changlhah a chol thu asei hilouva, Pharisee ho le Sadducee ho lhep lhahna thuhil aneiyuva achihthei diuva ahil chu ahedoh tauve. 
Jesun Caesarea Philippi ahunggei phat in, aseijui ho jah a “Mihem Chapa hi mihon koi ahi atiuvem?” tin adong tai. 
ama hon adonbut un, “Mi phabep chun Twilutsah John ahi atiuvin, mi phabep chun Elijah ahi atiuvin, chule adang phabepma chun Jeremiah ahilouleh themgao ho lah a khatpen hinte,” atiuve. 
Chujouvin, amaho chu thu adongin, “Nanghon koi ahi neiti uvem?” ati. 
Simon Peter in adonbut in, “Nangma Messiah, Pathen hing Chapa nahi,” ati. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Nangma anunnom a nahi, John chapa Simon, ajeh chu nangman mihem koima a konna nahet ahipon, van'a ka-Pan na henga hiche hi ahin phondoh ahi. 
Na henga kasei ahi, nangma Peter ('Song') nahi, chule hiche Songpi (Christa) chunga hi Ka houbung Ka tundoh ding ahi, chule Damun thaneina in ajo joulou ding ahi. 
Chule Keiman Van gam kot heh ho kapeh ding nahi. Leiset chunga ipi hijongleh bollou dinga na thupeh chan chu van'a jong kibol louding, chule leiset chunga ipi hijongleh na noppeh chan chu van'a jong kinop peh ding ahi,” ati. 
Chujouvin Aman aseijui ho jah achun koima kom'a Messiah ahi chu, aphondoh louna ding uvin hatah in agihtai. 
Chuchea pat chun Jesun aseijui te kom'a kichen tah in, Jerusalem a aga che ding angai khohna aseijin, chule aman hou vaipo loi le thempu le houdan thuhil hoa konna thil tampi gimnei hahsa ho athoh ding chu ahiltai. Ama ahin tha diu, hinlah anithum lhinni leh thina a konna thoudoh ding ahi. 
Hinlah Peter chun Ama apuidoh in chule chutia thu asei chu haphon aphoh in, “Pakai Vanin jong oiponte, hiche hi na chunga lhung hihbeh jeng hen,” atitai. 
Jesu, Peter lama akiheiyin, ajah a, “Satan, kakom'a kon'in chemang in! Nang hi keima dinga tijat um thangkol nahi. Nangman Pathen a kon hilouvin mihem lunggel a konna navet jeh ahi,” ati. 
Chuphat in Jesun aseijui te kom'a, “Nalah uva koi hijongleh eijui noma chun, nacham chon nauva konna nahung kiheiyuva, na thingpel nakiput uva, chule neijui diu ahi. 
Na hinkhou naki kolchah jom uva ahileh, na mansah dingu ahi. Hinlah nang hon keima jal'a nahin nau naphal doh uva ahileh, naki huhhing diu ahi. 
Chule vannoi apumpi nanga hihen lang, na hinna chan tale chun ipi phachom ding ham? Na hinna sanga manlujo ipi um nalaiyem? 
Ajeh chu Mihem Chapa hi avantil te ho toh apa loupina a hung ding chule mijouse chu atoh dungjui cheh a athu u atan ding ahi. 
Chule Keiman thutah ka seipeh nahiuve, hikom'a tupetna ding ho lah a Mihem Chapa a Lenggama ahung masanga thilou ding aum uve,” ati.
