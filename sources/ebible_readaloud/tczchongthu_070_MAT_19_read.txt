Matthew.
19.
Jesun hicheng thu asei jouvin, Galilee adalhan chuin Jordan vadung solama Judea gamkaiya achesuh tai. 
Chuche mun achun mihon tamtah in anung ajui tauvin, chuin Aman anaho adamsah tai. 
Chuin Pharisee ho phabep ahungun hiche thudoh a hi kipalsah dingin apatep un: “Mikhat chun ajeh khat aum pouva ahileh aji ada thei hiya ham?” atiuve. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Pathen Thubua simdoh khalou nahiuvem? Hichea chun, atil abula pat 'Pathen'in amaho chu pasal le numeiya asem ahi.'” 
Chuin aman aseijin, “'Hiche hin ipi jeh a pasal khat in apa le anu adalhah a chule ajinu akolchah ding, chule ani lhona khat hilhon ding ahitai ti ahil chen e.' 
Amani chu mini hita louva khat ahilhon tah jeh in, Pathen'in agop khomsa chu koiman sukhen tahih hen,” ati. 
“Chuti ahileh ipi jeh a Mose chun Danbua pasal khat in ajinu chu, dana lekha jih henlang chule solmang hen ati hintem,” atiuve. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Na lungthim'u atah behseh jeh a kida thua phatsahna napeh u ahi bouve, hinlah hichu Pathen'in ana lunggon ahipoi. 
Chule hiche hi ka seipeh nahiuve, koi hileh aji da a chule achom kichenpia chun jon abol ahitai, ajinu chun kitahna neilou hinkho mang ahi ngal louleh,” ati. 
Jesu seijui te chun ajah a aseiyun, “Hiti chu ahia ahileh jinei lou aphajoi!” atiuve. 
Jesun aseijin, “Hiche thu kisei hi mijousen sang thei ponte. Pathen panpina changhoa ding bou ahi. 
Mi phabep chu apena cha hing theilou dia gonsa aum in, mi phabepma chu midangin chutia asempeh loi jong aum in, chule abang phabep kit chu Pathen Lenggam jal'a jineilou deilhen'a jong aum uve. Koi hileh hiche hi asang theiyin kisan hen,” ati. 
Nikhat hi chapang ho chu anu leh apa teuvin, Jesun achung uva akhut angap a ataopeh din ahin puijui. Ahin seijui te chun anu leh apa teu chu mi nasugim uve tin ana houseuvin ahi. 
Hinlah Jesun, “Chapang ho chu kakom'a hungsah un. Hungda sah hih un! Ajeh chu Van'a Lenggam chu chapang ho tobanga ding bou hiya ahi,” ati. 
Chuin Aman aluchung uva akhut teni angam in, chuin phatthei aboh jouvin adalhatai. 
Mikhat Jesu kom'a hiche thudoh hi dong dingin ahungin, “Houhil, tonsot hinna kanei theina dinga ipi natoh pha kabol ngai hintem?” ahung ti. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Ipi dinga apha chu ipi ham tia neidoh ham? Apha Khatseh bou ahi. Hinlah na thudoh donbutna dingin____ tonsot hinna chu nanei nom leh, thupeh ho juijin,” ati. 
Ama chun adongin, “Hoipen chu ham?” ati. Chuin Jesun adonbut in, “'Nangman tolthat hih in, nangman jon bol hih in, nangma gucha hih in, nangman thujou seiphong hih in. 
Napa le nanu jabol in. Nangma na kingailut bangin naheng nakom ngailun,'” ati. 
Khangdongpa chun adonbut in, “Hiche thupeh ho jouse hi kajuisa sohkei ahitai. Ipi adang kabol ding um nalaiyem?” ati. 
Jesun aseipeh in, “Nangma chamkim nahi nom leh, chenlang nanei jouse abon'in gajoh in chule asum chu mivaicha ho gapen, chutileh van'a gou khol ding nanei ding ahitai. Chujouleh hungin, neijuijin,” ati. 
Hinlah khangdong pan hichu ajahdoh phat in, gouthil tamtah nei ahijeh in, lung nommo tah in achemang tai. 
Chuphat in Jesun aseijui ho jah a, “Thutahbeh ka seipeh nahiuve, mihao khat dingin Van'a Lenggam lut ding chu ahahsa lheh jeng e. 
Keiman seikit inge—Sangong sang dinga heobil hom sunga alut ding chu, mihao khat Pathen Lenggam a alut sanga baijo ding ahi!” ati. 
Seijui hon adatmo lheh jengun, chutobang ahileh vannoiya hi Huhhinga koi umjou ding ham? atiuve. 
Jesun amaho chu ave chang mel'in chuin aseijin, “Mihem hina lamdol a sei dingin, hithei chu ahipoi. Hinlah Pathen toh pankhom na a ijakai ahithei sohkeiye,” ati. 
Chuphat in Peter in ajah a, Keihon ijakai ka dalha uvin nanung kahin juiyuve. Ipi kahin mu diu ham? ati. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Keiman kasei chetpeh nahiuve. Vannoi aki thahsem tengleh Mihem Chapa hi Laltouna Loupia atou tengleh kanung juiya nangho jong laltouna Som le ni chunga natou uva, Israel phungsom le ni chunga thu natan diu ahi.” 
“Chule koi koi hileh a inho ahilouleh asopinu le asopipate ahilouleh apa ahilouleh achate ahilouleh aneile agou jouse dalha jenga chu, chan jakhat geiya amu ding chule tonsot hinna chu alemu ding ahi. 
Hinlah mi tamtah tua choisanga umho suhnem hidiu, chule tua ima hilouva kikoi ho chuphat tengleh choisangna chang diu ahi,” ati.
