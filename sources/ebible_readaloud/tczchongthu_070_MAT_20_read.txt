Matthew.
20.
“Ajeh chu Van'a Lenggam chu louneipa jingkah matah a, lengpi leiya natong ding tha hola gapotdoh tobang ahi.” 
“Aman niseh a thaman jat apeh dingin akinopto uvin, chuin natong dingin amaho chu asoldoh tai.” 
“Jingkah nidan ko in kailhang ahopan ahileh, imacha bol louva moh um mi phabep chu amun. 
Hiti chun aman amaho chu agoiyin, nisa lhum teng keiman phante kati kham na penau vinge, ati. 
Chuin amaho natong dingin lengpi leiya ache tauve.” 
“Ni lhangkai nidan nga a ama khopi sunga aga umkit pet in, aman midang phabep amoh um amun ahi. Aman, ‘Ipi dinga tunia natong lou nahiuvem?’” tin adonge. 
“Ama hon, ‘Koima eigoiyuva aum loujeh ahi’ atiuve. “Lou neipa chun aseipeh un, 'Chuti ahile che-un chule ka lengpi leiya natongho lah a ga jaovun,'” ati. 
“Chuche nilhah chun aman natong ho lamkaipa chu aseipeh in, natong jouse koulut in chule natong nunungpen a atohman'u pe panin. 
Nidan nga sung kigoi ho chu sum apeh phat in, achang cheh un nikho thaman akimu cheh tauve. 
Akigoi masa ho chu asum'u ladinga ahung phat un, amaho chun tam mu cheh ding akinem uve. Hinlah amaho jong chu nikho thaman to kitoh akipeuvin ahi. 
ama hon atohman akimu phat un, amaho alou neipa chunga alungnom tapouve. 
Khuche hon pung khat bou na tong uva, keiho nilhum keiya nisa ahah laise thoh lhum keiho toh kibangin amaho na peuve atiuvin ahi.' 
“Aman amaho khat chu adonbut in, ‘Gol, keiman adihlou kabolpoi! Nangma nilhum keiya nikhotha man lo dinga nanop hilou ham? 
Nasum kilah in chule chen, hiche natong nunung pen hi nanga tobangin kape nom e, ati. 
Kasum chu kadeina banga kaman ding chu dantoh akikalna ipi uma ham? Keiman midang ka khoto jeh a nei mitthipna ding ipi umem?'” 
“Hiti chu ahin, tua anunungpen ho chu chuphat teng amasapen hi dingu, chule amasaho anunung hung hi ding ahiuve,” ati. 
Jesu chu Jerusalem lama achetou in ahileh, aman seijui som le ni ho chu guhthim in achoma akoudoh in chule achunga ipi hung soh ding thu aseipeh tai. 
Aman aseijin, “Ngaiyun, eiho Jerusalem jonin ichetou uve, chule thempu pipuiho le houdan thuhil ho khut a Mihem Chapa hi kipedoh ding ahi. Ama hon tha dinga apehdoh diu ahi. 
ama hon Rome sepai ho khut a apehdoh diu, sakol jepna a avoh diu chule thingpel a akhetbeh diu ahi. Hinlah ni thum nileh thinaa konna thoudoh kit ding ahi,” ati. 
Chua pat chun James le John Zebedee chapa teni nu chu Jesu kom'a acha teni toh ahung'un ahi. Aman jana neitah in adilsun lungsetna angeh e. 
Aman adongin, “Ipi nathum nom ham?” ati. Amanun adonbut in, “Na Lenggama kachapa teni hi jabolna mun'a khat najet lama chule khat navei lama koi gel in,” ahung tin ahi. 
Hinlah Jesun adonbut in ajah uva, “Ipi nathumu nang hon nakihet pouve! Keiman gim hesoh kathohna ding khon kadon ding chu nadon ngam hinam?” ati. Amanin, “Henge, kangam lhon'e” tin adonbut lhontai. 
Jesun ajah lhonna, “Nangnin tahbeh mongin kagimna khon hi nahin don lhon ding ahiye. Hinlah keiman koi kajet a ahilouleh kaveiya tou ding ham, ti thuneina kaneipoi. Ka Pan alhendoh a agonpeh ho ding mun ahiye,” tin aseipeh tai. 
James le John in ipi athum lhon ahi seijui adang som ho chun ajah doh phat un alunggil uva nommo asa lheh tauve. 
Hinlah Jesun amaho chu akou khomin, chuin ajah uva, “Nanghon vannoi vaipipo hon midang chunga kipiputsahna aneijiu naheuve chule lamkai lenho chun anoiyuva ho chunga thuneina aneiyu naheuve. 
Hinlah nangho lah a chutilou ding ahi. Nangho lah a alamkai pipu hinoma chu nasoh uva pang ding ahi. 
Chule nalah uva amasa hinoma chu nasoh u ahung hi angaiye. 
Ajeh chu Mihem Chapa jeng jong chu jen le dinga hilouva, ajen ding le mi tamtah hinna dinga ahinna pedinga hunga ahi,” ati. 
Chuin Jesu le aseijui hon Jericho khopi chu adalhah nung uvin, mihon tamtah in anung ahinjui uve. 
Mitcho mini chu lampi pama atou lhon'e. Amanin Jesu chun hiche lampi chu ahinho ding ahi ti ajah lhon phat in, amanin hasap in asam lhon'in, Pakai David Chapa, nei khoto lhon'in! atilhon tai. 
Mihonpi chun aphoh uvin, “Thip lhon'in” atiuve. Hinlah amanin aha-sap cheh lhon'in, “Pakai, David Chapa, nei khoto lhon'in,” ati lhon'e. 
Jesun a-aw lhon ajah phat in, adingin chule akouvin, “Ipi kabolpeh lhon ding nei deisah lhon'em?” atitai. 
Amani chun, Pakai, kho kamu nom lhon'e, ati lhon'e. 
Jesun amani chu akhohsah jal'in, amit lhon athampeh tai. Hiche pettah chun kho amuthei lhontai! Chujouvin amanin ajuipai lhontai.
