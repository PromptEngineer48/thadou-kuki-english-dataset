Matthew.
22.
Jesun amaho chu thulem dang jong aseipeh in ahi. Aman ajah uva, 
“Van'a Lenggam chu lengkhat in achapa jineina golvah loupi tah abol thusim toh tekah thei ahi. 
Neh le don ding gontupsoh ahiphat in, asohte asolin akikou ho chu agahetsah tauve. Ahin, amaho abon'un ahungnom tapouve!” 
“Hiti chun aman asoh dang ho chu amaho gaseipeh dingin asolin, golvah anneh ding gontupsa ahitai. Bongchal ho le gancha thaotah ho chu kithat a, ijakai kigong tupma ahitai. Ankong um dingin hungun agatisah e.” 
Hinlah akikou ho chun anahsah pouvin chule ama lampi cheh a ache tauve, khat alouva, achom dang khat akiveinaa, 
Adangsen asottolle ho chu aman'un chule jachat umtah in abol'un chule athat tauve.” 
“Lengpa chu alunghang in, chule asepai te asoldoh in tolthat ho chu asumang'in chule akhopiu jong ahal tauve. 
Chule aman asohte jah a, ‘Golvah an gonsan aumtai, chule jin’a kikouho jong jabolna bangtah in aum pouve. 
Tun lamlhong aningkoi tin'ah cheuvin chule namu mu hou chu gakou uvin,' ati. 
Hiti chun sohte chun amu chanu chu mipha le miphalou geiyin akou uvin ahileh, golvah in chu jin adimtai.” 
“Hinlah lengpa chu jinho kimupia ahunga ahileh, aman mikhat chu kichenna Golvah vona kivonlou amudoh tai. 
'Gol,' 'Golvah vona kivon louva iti dana hikom'a naum ham?' tia adoh le imachan adonbut tapoi. 
Chutah in lengpa chun alhachate ho jah a, 'Akhut le akeng ho kan'un lang chule polam muthima lehdoh tauvin, hichea chu kana le hagelna um ding ahi.'” 
“Ajeh chu mitampi kikouva ahi, hinlah mi themcha bou deilhen ahi,” ati. 
Chutah in Pharisee ho akihouvun Jesu chu athuseiya kipal sah a mat ding angaito tauve. 
ama hon aseijui teu phabep asol'un, chule Herod lama pangho jong jaotha ahiuve. Ama hon “Houhil,” mikitah nahi ti kaheuve. “Nangman Pathen lampi dihtah nahil'in koima datkhen naneipoi chule maipha nave poi,” atiuvin ahi. 
“Tun hiche thua hi ipi nagel em nei seipeh un: Caesar henga kai peh hi dih am ole dihlou ham?” atiuve. 
Hinlah Jesun alunggel'u phalou chu ahen, aseijin, “Nangho mi phalhem ho!” “Ipi dinga duha a neimat got uham? 
Kai kipehna sum chu neivetsah un,” ati. Ama hon Rome te sum avetsah tauve. 
Aman adongin, “Koilim le koipen min ham?” tin adong tai. 
Ama hon adonbut'un, “Caesar a,” atiuve. Aman aseipeh in, “Caesar a chu Caesar peuvin, chule Pathen a chu Pathen peuvin,” ati. 
Aman adonbutna chu adatmo uvin chuin achemang tauve. 
Hiche nikho mama chun Sadducee hon mithi ho thokitna aumpoi ti hou lamkai ho Jesu henga ahungun, hiche thudoh hi ahung bol tauve. 
“Houhil, Mose chun mi koi ham khat cha nei louva athi khah tah a ahileh anaopa chun meithainu chu akichenpia chule acha chun aupa min apoh ding ahi ati. 
Aphai, vetsahnan sopi pasal sagi aum'e. Atahpen pa chun ji aneijin cha neilouvin athitai, chuti chun asopipa chun meithainu chu akichenpi tai. 
Hinlah asopi anina pa jong chu athitan, chule asopi athum channa pan amanu chu akichenpi tai. Asagina chanin hiti chun abonchauvin aum tauve. 
Achainan, numeinu jong athitai. 
Hijeh chun nei seipeh un, thokitna a chu koipen ji hiding hitam? Ajeh chu asagi uva chu amanu akichenpiu ahi” tin adong tauve. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Pathen Thu le Pathen Thahatna nahet lou jeh uva na suhkhel jiu ahi. 
Ajeh chu mithiho chu thokitna a amaho kicheng talou, kichen jong kibolsah talou ahiuve. Amaho chu van'a vantil te tobang ahiuve.” 
“Hinlah tun, mithiho thokitna um ham tithua Pathen Thua na simdoh khahlou uham? Masangpeh a ana thisaho Abraham, Isaac chule Jacob ho chu athijoupeh uvin, Pathen'in aseijin, 
'Keima Abraham, Isaac chule Jacob Pathen kahi.' Hitia chu Ama ahing ho Pathen ahin, athiho Pathen ahipoi,” ati. 
Mihonpi chun athusei ajah phat un, athuhil chu adatmo lheh tauve. 
Ahin aman Sadducee ho paomo hel'a alha chu Pharisee hon ajahdoh phat un, amaho akikhom un, ama thudohna neikit dingin akinopto tauve. 
Amaho lah a mikhat Hou Dan thuthem khat chun hiche thudoh a hi kipalsah ding agon ahi. 
“Houhil, Mose dan dungjuiya hoipen hi thupeh lenpen ham?” ati. 
Jesun adonbut in, “'Nangman Pakai na Pathen chu nalung pumpia, na hinna pumpia chule na lungthim pumpia na ngailut ding ahi.' 
Hiche hi thupeh amasa le alenpen ahitai. 
Anina jong athupina kibang ahi. 'Nangma na kingailut banga naheng nakom na ngailut ding ahi.' 
Hiche thupeh tenia hi Danthu apumpi le themgao hon angeh jouseu chu kikhaiya ahi,” ati. 
Hipet chun Pharisee hon aum kimvel un, Jesun amaho thudoh khat adongin: 
“Messiah chung chang thua ipi nagel uvem? Ama koi cha ham?” ati. Ama hon adonbut un, Ama David Chapa ahi,” atiuve. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Chuti ahileh iti dan'a David chun Lhagao Thengin ahetsah a konna Messiah chu ‘Ka-Pakai’ ati hintem?” Ajeh chu David chun, 
'Pakai chun ka Pakai kom'a, Keiman na melmate kasuh nem'a nakengto noiya kakoi masang sen, kijabolna kakhut jet ah tou jingin,' ati. 
David in Messiah chu 'Ka Pakai,' tia akou thei leh, Messiah chu iti achapa hithei ding ham?” ati. 
Chuin koima chan ama chu adonbut thei tapouve. Chule hiche jouva pat chun, koiman thudoh abolngam tapouve.
