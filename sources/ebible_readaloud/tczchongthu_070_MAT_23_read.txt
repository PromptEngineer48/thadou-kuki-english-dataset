Matthew.
23.
Chutah in Jesun mihonpi le aseijui ho jah a chun, 
“Hou Dan thuhil ho leh Pharisee ho Mose Danbu ho sutoh dinga houhil lamkaiya lhendoh ahiuve.” 
Hijeh chun ama hon nahil chan'u chu bol un chule nahil nauva aseiyu ngaiyun, hinlah aum chan'u vang jui hih un. Ajeh chu ama hon ahilu chu abol pouve. 
Amaho hin mihon apohjou diu kalval chonna angeh un, hinlah pohgih ngaiyang na dingin akhut jung khat u jong chasah lou ahiuve. 
“Athilbol jouseu chu mimu dinga bou bol ahiuve. Abanjang uva taona bom akoiyun, asungah Pathen Lekhabua thucheng kijih aneiyun, chule ama hon sangkhol chol mong kailha agei kikhit ho akiah uve.” 
Chule amaho hi anneh khomna dokhang mun thupi laiya touva chule hou kikhopna in'a jabolna a tou anom uvin ahi. 
Ama hon kailhang mun'a min jaboltah a alem diu adeiyun, chule 'Houhil' ati diu adei uve.” 
“Koiman 'Houhil' nati diu janom hih un, ajeh chu nang hon mihil khat naneiyun, chule nangho na boncha uva sopite hina a kibang cheh nahiu ahi.” 
Chule hiche leiset chunga koima 'Pa' sah hih un, ajeh chu van'a um Pathen bou chu na Pau ahi. 
Chule koiman nangho 'Mihil' tin kikousah hih un, ajeh chu nang hon mihil khatseh, Messiah naneiyun ahi. 
Nalah uva alen pen hinoma chu soh ahi angaiye. 
Hinlah ama kichoisang ho chu kisu nem intin, chule akingai nem ho chu kichoi sang ding ahiuve.” 
“Itobang lunghem nan nahin phah diu hitam nangho houdan thuhil ho leh Pharisee ho. Miphalhem ho! Ajeh chu miho maikoh ah nang hon Van'a Lenggam kot chu nakhah un ahi. Nangho la nalut pouvin chule midang ho jong alut diu na janom deh pouve. 
Itobang lunghem nan nahin phah diu hitam nangho Houdan thuhil ho leh Pharisee ho Miphalhem ho! Nanghon meithai ho anei-agou thil chung'ah jachatna thei leuvin duhan nabol'un, chule hou ngaisah tah tobangin mipi lah a sotpi na taovun ahi. Hiche jeh a hi gimbolna nasatah kipe ding nahiuve.” 
“Itobang lunghem nan nahin phah diu hitam nangho Houdan thuhil ho leh Pharisee ho. Miphalhem ho! Ajeh chu nang hon mi khat na loilutna dingun, gamtin'a twikhanglen gal geiyin navah leuvin, chule khat touvin lung ahin heiya ahileh nangho ama chu nivei jen Damun cha in nasem un, nangho jong nahiu ahi!” 
“Mitcho lamhil ho! Itobang lunghemna in nahin pha diuvem! Ajeh chu nang hon 'Pathen Hou-In'a' kihahsel ima akhohpoi natiuve; hinlah 'Hou-In sunga sana a kihahsel chu khoh ah ahi,' natiuve. 
Mitcho mingol ho! Hou-In'a sana ahilouleh Hou-In sana suthenga chu hoijoh thupijo hintem? 
Chule nang hon 'maicham' mun'a kihahsel chu imacha akhohpoi, hinlah 'maicham chunga thil kipedoh a kihahsel chu khoh ahi,' natiuve. 
Itobang loma mitcho nahiu ham! Maicham chunga thilto kichoi chuleh thilto suthenga maicham joh chu hoiche thupijo ham? 
Nangho 'maicham pan'a' nakihahsel tengu leh maicham chunga umho geiya chu kihahsel a nahitai. 
Chule nangho 'Hou-In pan'a' na kihahsel tengu leh hiche maicham mun le maichama cheng Pathen'a kihahsel nahiu ahitai. 
Chule 'van min pan'a' na kihahsel teng Pathen laltouna le Pathen laltounaa toupa a chu kihahsel nahi ahitai.” 
“Itobang lunghem nan nahin phah diu hitam nangho Houdan thuhil ho le Pharisee ho Miphalhem ho! Ajeh chu nang hon nahonsoh uva konna muchi neopen geiyin som'a khat napeuve, hinlah danthupeh akhohjo, adih a thutan khotona chule tahsan nahsah in nagel pouve. 
Mitcho lamhil ho! Thousi vaibong naval louna ding uvin twi nalhiuvin, hinlah nang hon sangongsang napum val uve!” 
“Itobang lunghem nan nahin phah diu hitam nangho Houdan Thuhil ho leh Pharisee ho. Miphalhem ho! Ajeh chu nangho khon le kong chu apolam siltheng nathem un, hinlah na sunglam'u, chipsetna le kichang khohsah nan nadim uve. 
Mitcho Pharisee ho! Amasan khon le kong chu asung soptheng un, chutengleh apolam jong chu hungtheng tante.” 
“Itobang lunghem nan nahin phah diu hitam nangho Houdan Thuhil ho leh Pharisee ho. Miphalhem ho! Ajeh chu lhan kiloibang tobang bep nahiuve, apolam vet hoi, hinlah na sunglam'u mithi gu le nen dimset nahiuve. 
Polama vet dingin midihho to nakilou uvin, hinlah na sunggil lam'u phatlhemna le danbei nahi bou uve.” 
“Itobang lunghem nan nahin phah diu hitam nangho Houdan thuhil ho leh Pharisee ho. Miphalhem ho! Ajeh chu nang hon napu napa teuvin anathau themgao ho leh Pathen ngaisah ho lhan chu nasemhoiyun ahi. 
Chujongleh 'Nang hon kapu kapateu phatlai chun keiho anahing leuveng themgao ho athana uva kajaolou beh dingu ahi,'” natiuve. 
“Hinlah hitia nasei jeng vangun, nang hon themgao that ho son le chilhah nahiuve ti photchetna na kiphondoh u ahiye. 
Cheuvin lang napu napa teuvin ana-patsau chu nang hon gabulhit un. 
Gul ho! Gulse chilhah ho! Damun thutanna chu iti napel thei mong ding'u ham?” 
“Hijeh a chu, Keiman themgao ho le miching ho chule Houdan thuhil ho naheng uva kahin sol ahi. Hinlah nang hon abang chu thingpela na khetbeh dingu, chule adangse chu hou kikhopna in'a najep dingu, chule khopi khat a konna khopi choma nadel le dingu ahi. 
Hitia chu michonpha Abel kithaa pat Barachiah chapa Zechariah Hou-In maicham teni kikah a kithat ho jouse chunga themmona nahin kipoh dingu ahi. 
Thutahbeh ka seipeh nahiuve, hiche thutanna hi tukhang mite chunga hunglhung ding ahi.” 
“Vo Jerusalem, Jerusalem themgao ho that le Pathen thupo leho that a pangho! Ahpin anoute alhaving tenia a-opkhum tobanga na chateu itobang tah a kakhop tup kigot hitam! Hinlah nang hon neiphal peh pouve. 
Chule tun veuvin, na in'u chu dalhahsa le ahomkeo ahitai. 
Hijeh a chu hiche hi kasei ahi, ‘Pakai min’a hungpa chu anunnom’e’ tia naseilou seuva keima neimu kit lou diu ahitai!’”
