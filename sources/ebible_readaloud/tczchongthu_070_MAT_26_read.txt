Matthew.
26.
Hicheng ho jouse aseijouvin Jesun aseijui te jah a, 
“Ni ni sunga Kalchuh Kut kipan ding ahi ti naheuve, chuteng Mihem Chapa hi thingpel a khetbeh dinga pehdoh a um ding ahi,” ati. 
Chuche pet chun Thempu pipuiho leh upaho chu Thempu Chungnung Caiaphas in'a aki khomun, 
Jesu chu guhthim'a iti matdoh a chuteng tha thei hi ding ham ti agong tauvin ahi. 
“Hinlah hichu Kalchuh Kut kiman sung hi dahen, achuti louleh mipi venjou hipou vinte,” tin kinoptona aneiyuve. 
Hiche pet a chu Jesu Bethany khoa Simon, tu masang jep a ana phahpa, in a um ahi. 
Aman an aneh pettah in numei khat ahungin Alabaster haithei hoitah thaonamtui man tamtah chu ahinchoiyin, chuin alujanga chun asunglha soh jeng tai. 
Seijuiho chun hichu amu phat uvin alunghang un, “Iti kimoh suhmanga hitam?” 
“Hichu man tamtah a joh thei hiya, asum chu mivaicha ho peh thei ahi,” atiuve. 
Hinlah Jesun hichu ahedoh paijin, “Ipi dinga numei nun kachunga athilpha bolna thua oimoa nahiuvem? 
Mivaicha ho chu nalah uva um jing ding, hinlah keima nalah uva kaum jing louding ahi. 
Ama hin katahsa hi vui dinga agon'a thaonamtui hi kachunga asunlhah ahitai. 
Thutahbeh ka seipeh nahiuve, vannoi pumpia Kipana Thupha kihil lhaang na chan a, hiche numeinu thilbol hi kigeldoh a chule kihoulimna a kimang ding ahi,” ati. 
Chuphat in seijui som le ni lah a Judas Iscariot chu thempu pipui henga aga chen, 
chuin adongin, Jesu nangho kom'a pehdohna dinga sum ijat neipeh diu ham? agatin ahi. Chuin ama hon dangka nong somthum apeuvin ahi. 
Hichea pat chun Judas chun Jesu pehdohna dingin, phat kijenpet avetai. 
Cholsolou Changlhah Kut kipat in seijui ho Jesu henga ahungun, chuin adongun, “Kalchuh Kut anneh hoilaiya kagon diu na deijem?” atiuve. 
Aman aseipeh in, “Khosunga galut leu chun,” “Mi khat namu diu ahi, ama kom'a chun, 'Houhil chun aseiye: Ka phat ahung naitai, na in'a ka seijui te toh Kalchuh Kut kaneh diu ahi, tin seipeh un,'” ati. 
Hiti chun seijui ho chun Jesun asei bangin abol'un, chua chun Kalchuh Kut agong tauvin ahi. 
Nilhah ahung hi phat in, Jesu chu som le ni chutoh dokhanga atou khomun ahi. 
ama hon an aneh laitah uvin, aman aseijin, “Thudihtah ka seipeh nahiuve, nangho lah a khat in nei pehdoh diu ahi,” ati. 
Alunglha lheh jeng uvin, bannei chan adong jeng uvin, Keima kahi ham, Pakai? atiuve. 
Aman adonbut in, “Nalah uva tutah a lhenga kaso khompi hin eipeh doh ding ahi. 
Ajeh chu Mihem Chapa chu Pathen Thun anasei masahpeh ahi dungjuiya thitei ding ahi. Hijongleh Ama pedoh a pangpa ding chun itobang tijat hoise ahung tadem. Amapa ding chun apen'a penglou ana hijo leh phajo tadinga!” ati. 
Apedoh a pang dingpa Judas jong chun adongin, “Houhil, keima hichu kahim?” ati. Jesun aseipeh in, “Nakisei khah e” ati. 
An aneh laitah un Jesun changlhah phabep alan chuin phatthei aboh in, chujouvin neocha chan aheh balin chuin aseijui te apen, “Kilah un chule neuvin, ajeh chu hiche hi katahsa ahi,” ati. 
Chuin Aman khon a lengpitwi chu alan, chujouvin Pathen athangvah in, amaho chu apen chule aseijin: “Don cheh uvin. 
ajeh chu hiche hi ka thisan Pathen le amite kikah a kitepna photchetna mi tampi chonset thenna dinga ana longdoh chu ahi. 
Kasei hi melchih un, Keiman ka Pa Lenggam a nangho toh athah beh a kadon kit kahsea lengpitwi hi kadonkit louding ahitai,” ati. 
Chujouvin ama hon la khat asauvin, chule Olive mol a dingin apotdoh tauve. 
Lampia chun Jesun amaho aseipeh in, “Tujan a hi na boncha uva nei dalhah manghel diu ahi. Ajeh chu Pathen Thun aseiye, 'Pathen'in kelngoi Chingpa chu avoh lhuh ding, chule kelngoi hon chu cheh chau diu ahi.' 
Hinlah keima thina a kon kahung thodoh jou teng, keima na masang uva Galilee a kache ding chule chua chu keima neimu diu ahi,” ati. 
Peter in aphongdoh jengin, adang hon nahin dalhah cheh diu hijongleh, keiman vang na dalha ponge, ati. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Peter thudihtah ka seipeh nahi, tujan tah a hi, ahchal khon masanga thumvei jen henasa a kahepoi tia nei seilep ding ahi,” ati. 
Peter in asei teiyin, “Ahipoi, Keima Nangma toh thi-tha ding hijongleng na seilep ponge!” ati. Chuin seijui adang hon jong, hiti chun aseiyun ahi. 
Chujouvin Jesu amaho toh chun Olive thingphung ho lah a Gethsemane hon'a chun acheuvin chuin Aman aseiye, “Keima khukom mun'a khu kaga chea kagatao sungin, hilaiya hin touvun,” ati. 
Aman Peter le Zebedee chapa teni James le John chu akipuiyin, chuin Ama alunghem in chule alunglha lheh jengtai. 
Aman amaho aseipeh in, “Kalungthim hi thina chan geiya dingin ana behseh tai. Hikom mun'a hin um un chule keima neingah un,” ati. 
Ama agache chon in, chule amai tol'a asulut in, ataovin, “Hepa! Hithei ding hileh, hiche gimthohna khon hi keiya kon'in kichon mang hen. Hinlah keima thusah hijo louvin, nangma phatsah joh chu hijo tahen,” ati. 
Chuphat in aseijui te henga ahungin ahileh a-ihmut u ahungtoh tai. Aman Peter jah a, “Nidan khat jeng jong nei kigin peh joulou uham? 
Lhepna a nalhah louna dingun kigingun chule taovun. Ajeh chu Lhagaovin agonan, hinlah tahsa'n ajoupoi!” ati. 
Chuin Jesun amaho chu anivei nan adalhan chule ataovin, “Hepa! Hiche khon hi kadon louva kichondoh louding ahileh, nalung got hita hen,” ati. 
Chuin Ama amaho kom'a akile kit in, amaho chu a-ihmut u ahung mukit tan ahi, ajeh chu amaho a-ihmut u soh behseh jenga ahitai. 
Hiti chun Ama athumvei nan aga taokit in, asei ngaima chu agasei kit in ahi. 
Chujouvin aseijui te kom'a ahungin, hiti hin aseiye, “I-mu jengun. Ki choldo uvin. Hinlah veuvin phat ahunglhung e. Mihem Chapa hi mi chonse ho khut a pehdoh a uma ahitai. 
Kipat un cheu hite. Veuvin, eipedohpa hichea aum'e!” ati. 
Chuin Jesun hiche thu aseina laiyin, seijui som le ni ho lah a khatpen Judas chu mihon tampi toh chemjam le moltum choi pum in ahunglhung un ahi. Amaho chu miho lah a upaho le thempu pipuiho hinsol ahiuve. 
Amatsah a pang Judas chun amaho melchihna gontohsan akoiyin: “Nanghon koipen mat ding chu nahet theina ding uva, keiman kalem'a abeng kachop peh ding ahi,” ati. 
Hiti chun Judas chun Jesu ahinjon paijelin, “Chibai houhil,” atin abeng achop tai. 
Jesun ajah a, “Kajol, ipi boldia hung'ah nahim? Nabol ding chu boltan,” ati. Chujouvin ama hon Jesu chu aman tauve. 
Hinlah Jesu to umkhom ho lah a khat chun achemjam aladoh in, chuin Thempu Chungnung sohpa chu asat le abilkol asat lha jenge. 
Jesun aseipeh in, “Na chemjam chu koidoh in.” “Chemjam mangcha ho chu chemjam a thi ding ahiuve. 
Keiman Kapa jah a Vantil asang sang ho chu eihuhdoh diuva kathum mo ham? Chule Aman apettah a ahinsol paijeng thei ahi, nageldoh lou uham? 
Hinlah keiman chuti bol leng, Pathen Thubun asei tua guilhung ding ati hi, iti hithei ding hitam?” ati. 
Chujouvin Jesun mihonpi jah a chun, “Keima hi gamsung vaipohna khelgoa nei ngaitouva chemjam le moltum thoa eiman dinga hunga nahiuvem? Ipi dinga Hou-In a neimat lou uham? Chua chu niseh a thu kana hilji ahi. 
Hinlah hicheng jouse hi Pathen Thubua thu kisei, aguilhun na dinga hitia ahi,” ati. Chuphet tah chun, seijuiho jousen ajamsan tauvin ahi. 
Chujouvin Jesu man ho chun Caiaphas Thempu Chungnung in'a apui tauvin, hiche mun'a chu houdan thuhil ho leh upaho kikhoma ahiuve. 
Hiche phat kah sunga chu Peter in gal kanga Jesu chu ajui ahi. Ama agalut in chule avesuiya pang ho chutoh atoukhom in, chujongleh iti kichai uvem ti angah tai. 
Asunga thempu pipuiho leh upaho akikhom jouse chun Jesu chu jouthua hettohsahna neiya, hiti chun athemomna ding angai touvin ahi. 
Hinlah ama hon jouthua hettohsahna ding tamtah anei vangun, midang thua hettohsahje ahe tapouve. Ajonan mini masang lama ahungdoh lhon'in, 
amani chun, “Hiche pa hin keiman Pathen Hou-In ka suhmang'a chule nithum sunga katundoh kit thei ding ahi tin aseiye,” ati lhon'in ahi. 
Chuphat in Thempu Chungnung chu ading doh in, chuin Jesu jah a chun, “Nathemmo channau hi nadonbut louding ham? Nachung thua ipi nakisei nom um'em?” ati. 
Hinlah Jesu chu thipbeh in aum'in ahi. Chuin Thempu Chungnung chun ajah a, “Pathen hing min'a katem nahi, Nangma Pathen Chapa Messiah chu nahim, nei seipeh un,” ati. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Nasei khai. Chule khonung teng nang hon Mihem Chapa hi Pathen jetlam thaneina a atou namu diu ahi,” ati. 
Chutah chun Thempu Chungnung chun hoise val asah chu vetsahna in aponsil abotkeh in chule aseijin, Pathen ataitom e! Ipi hettohsah dang ingaichat beu ngaiyem? Nangho jousen Pathen ataitomna thucheng naja tauve. 
Ipi ham nangho thulhuh chu? Tia adoh le ama hon, “Athemmoa ahi! athi dinga pha ahi!” atiuve. 
Hiphat chun ama hon Jesu maiya chil aset un chule akhut tum uva achum tauve. Chule abang phabep in angeiphe abeng un, 
anuisat un, Messiah gaovin seiyin! Koipen in navoh ham? atiuve. 
Hiche pet a chu Peter chu thutanna mun pamlama touva ahi. Chuin soh numei khat ahung pheiyin “Nangma Galilee a Jesu nungjui ho lah a khat nahi,” ahung ti. 
Hinlah Peter chun miho masanga chun aseilep in, “Ipi thu nasei ahi keiman kahepoi,” ati. 
Chujouvin kelkot phung ageipet in, soh numei chom khat in ama chu ahen chule akimvel'a touho kom'a chun, “Hiche pa hi Nazareth Jesu toh um khom jia ahi,” ati. 
Peter in avelin aseilep in, kihahsel toh thon, “Hiche pa chu ahet a kahet lou ahi,” atitai. 
Chule chomkhat jouvin achom dang khat akimvela ding ho chu Peter kom'a ahungin, aseijin, “Nangma amaho lahna khat nahi, Galilee mi nahi, na pao aw chun ka hedoh jeng uve,” atiuve. 
Peter kihahsel to-thon, “Jou kasei le gaosap chang ing'e keiman hiche pa hi kahepoi,” ati. Chuin apettah chun ahchal akhongtai. 
Apettah chun Jesu thusei chu Peter lunggel a ahung kilangdoh in, “Ahkhon masanga thumvei nei seilep ding, Keima hi henasa a koi ahi kahe khapoi,” tia nasei ding ahi, tichu ageldoh in polama apot in lhase tah in akaptai.
