Matthew.
27.
Jingpi matah in thempu pipuiho leh miho lah a upaho atoukhom kit un, Jesu thana ding angaito tauve. 
Chujouvin amaho chun Ama akanun, apuimang un, chuin Rome gamvaipo Pilate henga apui tauve. 
Ama pedoh a pangpa Judas chun Jesu chu thichan geiya dinga themmo achan'u chu ahen, alunga akisih lheh jeng tai. Hiti chun aman dangka somthum chu alan, thempu pipuiho leh upaho kom'a achoiyin, 
aman aphongdoh in, “Ka chonse tai, ajeh chu keiman themmona neilou chu kapedoh tai” ati. Ama hon adonbut'un, “Keihon ija kagel beh uvem, hichu nangma boina hibouva chu,” atiuve. 
Chujouvin Judas chun dangka sumnong chu Hou-In sunga aleh lhan chule apotdoh in ama le ama aki khailih tai. 
Thempu pipuiho chun sumnong chu aloh khomun, aseiyun, “Hiche sum hi Hou-In a sumkoina a koikhom nom ponte, ajeh chu mithana dinga kipedohsa ahi,” atiuve. 
Aki houtoh jouvun, ajonan ama hon beldengpa honlei chohna dingin agellha tauve. Chule hichu gampam mi kivuina dingin asem tauve. 
Hijeh a chu leimun min chu Thisan Man kitia ahi. 
Hiche hi Jeremiah themgao thusei aguilhun nan “ama hon dangka somthum nong chu alauvin, Israel miten aman dinga agel uva aphasah nau man chu ahi. 
Chuin ama hon beldengpa honlei chu, Pakaijin ahilna bang tah in acho tauve.” 
Chupet a chu Rome gamvaipo Pilate anga Jesu ding ahin, gamvaipo chun “Nangma Judah te Lengpa nahim?” tin adonge. Jesun adonbut in, “Nasei khai” ati! 
Hinlah thempu pipuiho leh upahon ama douna thu aseiyun, Jesu athipbeh in ahi. 
“ama hon nangma douna thu hijatpi aseiyu hi na jahlou ham?” tin Pilate in adongin ahi. 
Chuin Jesun athemmosah naho'uva imacha adonbutlou jeh chun gamvaipo chun adatmo lheh e. 
Kalchuh Kut kumseh le kimanjina a chu mihonpin adeiyu khat gamvaipo chun sohchang khat alhadoh jiu ahi. 
Hiche kum'a hi sohchang giloutah khat Barabbas atiu ana um'ah ahi. 
Mihonpi chu Pilate in'a akikhop pet un, hiche jingkah chun, aman amaho chu adongin, “Nangho dinga koijoh ham kalha dohpeh diu? Barabbas ham ahilouleh Jesu kiti Messiah hi kalha dohpeh diu ham?” ati. 
(Ama hin houlamkai hon Jesu chu thangthip man'a amat u ahi, ti ahechen lheh in ahi). 
Pilate thutanna a atoupet tah in ajinu chun hiche thu hi athot in: “Hiche themmona beipa hi limsah hih in. Ama jeh hin tujan ka-ihmutna a chun ka thohgim lheh jenge,” ati. 
Hiche pet chun thempu pipuiho leh upaho chun mihonpi chu atem un, Barabbas chu lhadoh a auma chule Jesu chu thisah dingin atil tauvin ahi. 
Hiti chun gamvaipo pa chun adong kit in, “Koijoh hi lhadoh peh leuveng nalung uham?” ati. Mihonpi chun, Barabbas atiuve. 
Pilate in adonbut in, “Messiah kiti Jesu hila ikalo ding ham?” ati. Ama hon, “Thingpel'a khenbeh in!” atiuve. 
Pilate in “Ipi jeh inem? Ipi suhkhel len anei ham?” ati. Hinlah mihonpi chun aha sap cheh uvin, “Thingpel'a khenbeh in!” atiuve. 
Pilate chun imacha phatchomna aum louva chusanga boina aso cheh ding chu amutan ahi. Hijech chun, aman lhenga twi athum in chule mihonpi masanga chun akhut akisop in aseitai, “Hiche pa thisan kisonaa hin themmona kaneipoi: Nanghon kimopoh tauvin!” ati. 
Chuin miho jouse chun, “Ama thina chunga keiho le ka chateu geiyin mo kipoh naveh tangu!” tin ale donbut un ahi. 
Chuin aman Barabbas chu alhadoh peh in, Jesu vang chu sakol jepna thih lingneiya jep dingin Rome sepai ho thu apen chule ama hon thingpel'a akhetbeh diuvin apedoh tai. 
Gamvaipopa sepai ho chun Jesu chu atolpiu thutanna mun'a apuiyun chule sepai ho chu akoudoh un ahi. 
ama hon apon chu ahoh lhauvin, chule ponsandup asilpeh uvin ahi. 
ama hon lingbah ho chu lukhuh in asem un chule alu chunga akoiyun, chuin akhut jetlam'a peng tenggol akichoisah un leng-tenggol in aneisah uve. Chujouvin totnan a anga adilsu uvin chuin dainopnan, “Kajabol'ui! Judah te Lengpa!” atiuve. 
Chujongleh chil aset khum un chuin atenggol chu alahpeh un chule hichea chun aluchang avohpeh un ahi. 
Achaina keiya totnopnaa anei nung uvin, ama hon ponsan chu alahpeh uvin chuin ama pon asilpeh kit tauve. Chujouvin ama hon thingpel'a khetbeh dingin apui tauvin ahi. 
Lampia chun mikhat Simon, Cyrene a konna hung ahin, chuin sepai ho chun ada adoiyin Jesu thingpel chu aputsah tauve. 
Chuin amaho Golgotha atiu (Hichu lugumun kitina) mun chu alhung tauve. 
Sepai ho chun juthuh le gilkha kihal apeuvin, hinlah aman ate jouvin, adon nom tapoi. 
ama hon Thingpel chunga akhetbeh jouvun, sepaiho chun aponsil chu avangpha pha chang hen tin vang asang tauve. 
Chujouvin amaho akimvela atouvun chule ama kikhai pet in angah un ahi. 
Jesu luchunga chun melchihna khat atah un, ama douna a ahehset nau phondohnan, “Hiche hi Jesu Judah te Lengpa,” tin akijih in ahi. 
Gamsung kivaipoh kikhelsah goa mini chu khat ajet lama chule khat aveilama Ama toh akhetbeh thauvin ahi. 
Chule ahin hopa ho chun isahlou tah in akhasuh khum uvin, 
ama hon asam un, “Kiven tun, nangman Hou-In chu nasuh chima chule nithum sunga natundoh dingin naseiye. Chutia nangma Pathen Chapa nahileh nangma kihuhhing inlang chule thingpel'a kon'in hungkum lhan,” atiuve. 
Thempu pipuiho, houdan thuhil ho chule upaho jong chun totnop nan Jesu chu ahouvun, 
ataitom un, “Aman midang ahuhhing theiyin, hinlah ama akihuhhing theipoi” atiuve. Hitia hi ama Israel te lengpa hiya ham? Tutah hin ama thingpel a kon'in hung kumlha hen, chutileh ka tahsan diu ahi. 
Aman Pathen atahsan in, hijeh chun Pathen'in ama adeiya ahileh huhhing hen. Ajeh chu aman aseijin, “Keima Pathen Chapa kahi, ati” atiuve. 
Gamsung kivaipoh kikhelsah goteni jong chun hitima chun ahouse lhon'in ahi. 
Sunkim'a pat nilhangkai nidan thum geiyin gamsung pumpia mu athimin ahi. 
Nilhangkai nidan thum donin, Jesun aw sangtah in asamin, “Eli, Eli, lema sabach thani?” hichu “Ka Pathen, ka Pathen, ipi dinga neidon lou hitam?” ti kiseina ahi. 
Akimvela ding ho phabep chun ahekhel uvin, chuti chun themgao Elijah akouvin agel uve. 
Amaho lah a khat chu alhai jelin chuin twichop phon a juthuh chu aga de-lhop in, pumpeng leh a chun adon theina dingin atah sangin ahi. 
Hinlah midang ho chun, “Khongaiyin, Elijah ahuhhing dinga hung nam, veuhite,” atiuve. 
Chujouvin Jesun ahinsam kit in, chuin a Lhagao alhadoh tai. 
Hiche pet chun Hou-In munthenga pondal chu anah a pat ato geiyin kehni akiso tai. Leiset akithing in chule songpi ho jong akehlha jeng e. 
Chuin lhankot ho akihong in, Pathen ngaisah athisa ho tahsa chu kaithouvin aumkit un ahi. 
Jesu thokit jouvin ama hon lhankhuh chu adalha uvin, Jerusalem khopia alut un, chule mi tamtah henga aga kimusah uvin ahi. 
Rome sepai jalamkai pa le sepai dang ho akikhetbehna mun'a umho chun liingakihoa hiti ho thusoh chun atijatsah lheh uvin ahi. Ama hon aseiyun, “Hiche hi Pathen Chapa tahbeh ahiye,” atiuve. 
Chule numei tampi Jesu toh Galilee a kon'a ahin lhonpi ama gelkhoh ho chun gamla tah a kon'in aveuvin ahi. 
Amaho lah a Mary Magdalene, James le Joseph nu Mary, chule James le John (Zebedee chapa teni) ahiuve: 
Nilhah ahung hi phat in, Joseph Arimathea khoa mihaopa, ama hin Jesu nung anajui ahin, 
Pilate henga aga chen chule Jesu tahsa chu athum in ahi. Chule Pilate chun ama pehdoh dingin phalna apetai. 
Joseph hin atahsa chu alan tupat ponnem thengsel'in atom in ahi. 
Aman ama lhan thah, songpia kon'a kigeihom sunga chun akoiyin ahi. Chujouvin aman alutna mun'a chun songlentah khat atahlut jouvin achemang tai. 
Mary le Magdalene chule Mary chomnu lhan'a atouvun chule angah un ahi. 
Ajingin, Sabbath nikhon thempu pipuho le Pharisee ho Pilate kimupi din aga cheuvin ahi. 
ama hon ajah a, “Pu, hiche mi lhemlha pa chun ahinlaiya khatvei anasei chu kagel doh uve. Aman nithum joule thina a kon'a ka thodoh kit ding ahi. 
Hijeh a hi lhan chu nithum lhinkah a dingin mohor nam in. Hiche hin aseijui te ahung uva chule atahsa guhmangna chule mijouse kom'a ama athilah a kon'in athoudoh tai atina diu umta ponte. Hiti chu hileh amasa suhkhel sang'a phajo hi ding ahi,” ati. 
Pilate in adonbut in, “Hou-In ngah ho chu puiyun lang chule na honbit thei chanun hongbit un,” ati. 
Hiti chun ama hon lhan chu mohor anam'un chule avetup dingin angah akoi tauve.
