Mark.
4.
Avelin dil panga thu ahilpan kit tai. Mipi tamtah akom'a ahung kikhom pai jengun, Ama kong sunga aluttan ahi. Kong sunga chun atouvin, mipite chu vaokama atouvun ahi. 
Amaho chu thulem'in thu tamtah ahil'in, 
“Ngaiyun! Muchi thepa muchi then achen ahi. 
Athe athe leh abang lamsih ah achun, hiho chu vachaten ahungloh un anegam tauvin ahi. 
Chule abang songlheo chunga leipatna lah a achun, alei asalou jeh in akehdoh jengin, 
Ahivangin nisan ahin kah phat in asang gon, jung aneilou jeh in agoplha paitan ahi. 
Chule abang linglah a achun, ling ho chu ahung khangin chang phung dong nalaiho chu adeplih tan, ahinga aum tapoi. 
Adang ho chu leiphalaiya achun, ahung khangin aga tan, hiho chu ajat somthum, ajat somgup, ajat jakhat chengin apung tan ahi!” 
Chuin Aman aseijin, “Koi hileh khojahna ding nakhang neiyin ngaihen,” ati. 
Chomkhat jouvin aseijui som le ni ho toh akimvela midang ho toh aum khom pet in, ama hon athulem seiho chu ipi kiseina ham tin adong un. 
Aman adonbut in, “Pathen Lenggam thuguh chu nangho kipea nahi tauve. Hinlah thulema kaseina jeh chu polam miho jah ding ahi, 
Hichu Lekhabun aseisa agui lhunna ding: ‘Ka thilbol amu teng ule, ima jildoh hetdoh aneilou diu. Ka thusei ajah teng ule, ima hetdoh aneilou diu. Achutilou le keima lam'ah hung kiheiyun tin, ngaidam chang get unte,’” kiti chu ahi, ati. 
Chuin Jesun akom uva, “Nanghon hiche thulem hi nahetlou mong uham? Achutile thulem dang jouse iti nahe tadiu vem? 
Lou bolpan muchi athe chu Pathen thu midang kom'a kihil lhaang tobang ahi. 
Muchi kithe lamsih a chu hon avetsah chu ahileh, Pathen thu ja a ahivanga Satan in ahung kilhoh mang pai toh tekah ahi. 
Songlheo chung leipatna lah a chu hon avetsah chu, athu ajah jah a thanom tah a joppai jengho ahi. 
Ahivangin amaho chu jung neilou ahijeh un, apangsot jouji pouve. Pathen thu tahsan jeh a boina le suhgimna ato tengule, jung aneilou jeh uvin alhupaiji tauve. 
Ling lah a muchi kithe hon avetsah chu Pathen thu ja a, 
ahivanga leiset hinkho lungkhamna ten alodimsetna, haona lungthim chule thil dang dang ngaichat nan thu chu adeplih jeh a ga sodoh joulou tobang ahi. 
Chule leipha laiya chuhon avetsah chu athu ja a, hea jopte chu ahiuvin, abang ajat somthum, abang jat somgup chule abang ajatja a aga punsah beho ahiuve,” ati. 
Chuin Jesun ajah uva, “Koiyin meivah khu lo sung le jalkhun noiya atun ngaiyem? Koiman bolponte! Meivah chu avah hoiselna ding mun'a aki tungjin ahi. 
Ijeh-inem itileh thil kiselguh chengse chu achaina leh kiphongdoh ding, chuleh akiselguh jouse chu vahdoh ding ahi. 
Koi hileh khojahna ding nakhang neiyin ngaihenchule hethem hen,” ati. 
Chuin aman aseiben, “Najah chengseu chu lunglut tah in ngaiyun. Phatah a na ngai chan uva, hetgilna nakipe diu ahi—chule kipehbe ding bon nahiuve. 
Koi hileh ka thuhil ngaiho chu hetbena kipehbe ding, ahinlah koi hileh ka thuhil ngailou chu ahetsa themkhat jong amahoa kon'a kilahmang peh ding ahi,” ati. 
Jesun ajah uva, “Pathen Lenggam umdol chu loubolmi khat in leiya muchi athe tobang ahi. 
Asun ajan'a a-imut pet chuleh aha pet hijongleh, amuchi the chu ahung kehdoh in, akhanglen in, hinlah aman hitia chu thil iti hung umdoh ham ahepoi. 
Leiset in achamin ahin gadohsah jin, amasapen in avui ahung dondoh in, chuteng achaina leh ahung gadoh in aminji tai. 
Aga chu amin phat leh, a atphat ahitah jeh in loubolpa achen, koiten a atpaiji tai,” ati. 
Jesun aseiben, “Pathen Lenggam hi ipi toh kitekah ding hitam? Ipi thulema ka hilchet ding hitam? 
Ankam mu leiya kithe tobang ahi, hichu muchi lah a aneopen ahi. 
Ahivangin ahung kedoh tengleh hampa dang jouse sangin alenjon, abah lentah jong ahin sodoh jin, chuti chun chunga leng vachate abah lim noiya ahung geh jiuvin ahi,” ati. 
Chuin chutobang thulem tamtah mangchan ama hon ahetthei diu kham thu ahil e. 
Mihonpi jah a thulem tilouvin imacha asei khapoi. Ahinla aseijui te chengse vang chu guhthimin ijakai ahilchen jinai. 
Chuin nilhah in Jesun aseijui te kom'a, “Hungun, dil khu agal langa galkaiyu hite,” ati. 
Chuti chun mihonpi chu adalha uvin, Jesu chu konga akitolpi tauvin, kong dang phabep in vang ahinjuiyun ahi. 
Chuin khopi hui ahung nungin ahileh, twi kinong chu kong sunga ahung kinonglut matchet jeng tan, adimpai ding ahitan ahi. 
Apet chun Jesu chu kong mei lama lukhap kikhap in ana ihmun, aseijui ten asukhang'un ajah ah, “Houhil, kalhummang gam dingu hi imalouva nagel ham?” ati tauve. 
Jesu akhah phat in huipi chu aphoh in, twi-nong jah ah, “Thipmin! Thipchet in!” tia thu apeh leh, apettah in huipi chu athipchet jeng tan, kho aphasel tan ahi. 
Chuin Aman ajah uva, “Ibola nakichat uham? Tahsanna nanei nailou uham?” atitai. 
Seijui ho chu akithing jeng tauvin, “Hiche pa hi koi mongmong hiya, huipi le twi-nongin jong athu angai ahitadem!” tin aki dongto tauvin ahi.
