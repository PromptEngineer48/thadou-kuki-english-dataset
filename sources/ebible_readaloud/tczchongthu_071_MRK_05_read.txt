Mark.
5.
Chuti chun dil gal langkhat lama, Gerasene gam agalkai tauvin ahi. 
Konga pat Jesu ahung pot leh, lhan'a kon'in lhagao boh vop khat in ahung kimupin ahi. 
Ama chu lhanmol'a cheng ahin, koiman atuhtang joulou, thihkhaova jong ahen joulou'u ahi. 
Bul atuhsah uva thihkhaova jong ahenji vangun thihkhao chu asattan jin, bul chu atumkeh jin, koima chan abolngoi jou louhel ahitai. 
Sun le janin lhanmol a chun aumin chule molchunga a-aole a-aolejin, songhem in ama aki-at chen jin ahi. 
Gal a pat in Jesu ahin mun, ahung lhaijel in a angah abohkhup in ahi. 
“Pathen chungnungpen Chapa Jesu, ipi dinga neihung suhboi got ham? Pathen min'a nahenga katao ahi, nei bolgim hih in,” tin apeng jeng tai. 
Hichun Jesun ajah ah, “Mipa a kon chun hung potdoh in, nang lhagao boh,” tin ano tai. 
Chuin, “Namin ipi ham?” tia adoh leh, aman adonbut in, “Tamtah kahi jeh un kamin Janel ahi,” ati. 
Chuin chuche gamkai sunga kon'a nodoh ahilouna dingun Jesu henga hataovin atao tan ahi. 
Hiche mun lhang pang khat a chu voh hon tamtah kilha a ahi. 
Janelte chun, “Voh hon lah a kalut theina dingun neisol un,” tin athum tauve. 
Jesun jong aphalpeh tan, lhagao boh ho chu ahung potdoh un vohcha ho sunga chun alut tauvin, hichun vohcha sangni jen chu halhaiyin alhaiyun, kol ah alhai lhauvin dil ah alhailut un, twi lah a chun alhumgam tauvin ahi. 
Chuin ganching ho chu ajam un, khopi sung le loujaovah athu chu aseile tauvin ahi. Mipi chun ipi tidan hitam tin ave dingin ahung lhai pheiyun ahi. 
Chuin Jesu kom'ahung lhun un, thilha Janel voppa chun pon akisila chule lunglim'a ana tou chu amuvin akicha lheh jeng uve. 
Chuin thilhavoppa leh vohcha ho umchan achen achaiya mua hetoh hon thil umje chu aseipeh tauve. 
Hichun ama hon Jesu chu agamsung uva kon'a potdoh dingin ahenga atao uvin ahi. 
Chuin konga alut kon'in masanga thilhavoppa chun ama toh kilhon khom dingin angeh tai. 
Ahivangin Jesun ajadan ajah a, “Na in a chen lang na inkote henga Pakaijin itobang thil loupi nabolpeh a, iti nakhoto em abonchan gasei tan,” atipeh tai. 
Chuin amapa chu akipatdoh in Khopi som (Decapolis) jousea Jesun thil lentah abolpeh chu lhangphong tah in aseile pantai. Mijousen kidang asa lheh jengun ahi. 
Chuin Jesu konga akitol in agal langkhat alhungin, hiche achun mihonpi tamtah ahenga ahung kikhom un ahi. 
Hichun kikhopna in'a vaipoho lah a mi khat, amin Jairus chu ahungin, hiche achun amu phat in akengbul'ah abohkhup in, 
“Ka chanu khangdongcha thi ding dinmun'in aum tan, lungset in hungin lang achunga nakhut hung ngam inlang, ahinna dingin neijen dampeh in,” tin ahung taove. 
Chuin Ama chutoh akilhon pheiyin, mihonpin jong ahin juiyun, atom lellul jengun ahi. 
Hiche kahlah chun numei khat jong kumsom le kumni athi longa damlou aum'in ahi. 
Louthem tamtah akivetsahna a sum le pai anei jouse sumang jouva ahitan, imala phachom louvin khoh chehcheh a um ahi. 
Amanu chun Jesu thudol anajan, hijeh chun mihonpi lah achun ahungin Jesu pon mong chu ahung tham tai. 
Amanu chu aki ngaiton, “Apon jeng jong hi thamleng dam nange,” ti atahsan ahi. 
Chomloukah in athiput chu akangin atang jeng tan, anat thohlel a kon aol leh adamdoh dan chu atichunga akihet tan ahi. 
Chutah chun Jesun atichunga kon'a amidamsahna tha apotdoh chu akihet in, hichun mipi lah a chun akihei kimvelin, “Kapon thama chu koi ham?” tin adongtai. 
Aseijui ten ajah a, “Mipi hijat in naneh le jeng uva, iti dan'a, ‘Koiham eithama?’ tia dohna nabol ham?” atiuvin ahi. 
Hinlah Aman chuche bol a chu mudohna dingin avelen ahi. 
Amanu chun achunga thilsoh chu ahen, kicha sasan kihot pum in ahungin a angah abohkhup in, ama ahi akiseidoh tai. 
Chuin Jesun ajah a, “Chanu, na tahsan in nadamsah ahitai. Lungmong in chetan. Na thoh natna chu kichai ahitai,” ati. 
Amanu kom'a thu asei jing laiyin, thupole khat Jairus in'a kon'in ahungin, “Na chanu athitai. Houhil hi kisahboi sah ngai taponte,” tin Jairus kom'ah ahung seiye. 
Aki hounau chu Jesun ajatan, Jairus kom'a chun, “Kicha hih in, tahsan poupou tan,” ati. 
Chuin Peter, James le John (James naopa), hicheng tailou midang koiman ajui ding ajadan akijuisah poi. 
Kikhopna in vaihompa, Jairus in alhun'u chun, mihonpi chu ana poh cheichoi jengun, kap le maova aum'u chu Jesun amun ahi. 
Chuin ama alut in ajah uva, “Ibola hitia hi napo cheichoiyuva nakau ham? Chapangnu chu athi ahipoi, aihmu ahi bouve,” ati. 
Thi op mihonpi chun anuisat uve. Hichun abonchauvin asoldohsoh in, chapangnu nu le pa toh chule ahin kilhonpi seijui thum ho chengse toh chapangnu umna'a chun alut un ahi. 
Chuin chapangnu khut chu atuh in, “Talitha koum,” atin ahi. Hichu “Boinu, kithouvin!” tina ahi. 
Chupet tah chun chapangnu chu ahung thouvin, adingin lam ajotpai jeng tai. Amanu chu kum som le ni bou ahina laiye. Chutah chun amaho chun kidang asa lheh jengun. 
Hiche thu hi koima ahetsah louna diuvin Jesun akhamgah in, chule amanun aneh ding an apeh diuvin thu apen ahi.
