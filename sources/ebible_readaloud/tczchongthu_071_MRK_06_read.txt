Mark.
6.
Chuin chua kon chun Jesu achedoh in, achenna Nazareth lama aseijui te toh ache tan ahi. 
Cholngahni ahung hi phat in Judah ho kikhopna in'ah thu ahil pan in, chuin athuhil chu mi tamtah ajaho chun kidang asauvin, “Hiche pa hin hoiya kon'a chihna le thilbol theina hichengpi hi akilah ahi tadem?” atiuvin ahi. 
Chuin nuisat toh atho uvin, “Ama hi thingthem bol maimai, Mary chapa, chule James, Joseph (Joses), Juda le Simon sopipa ahi bouve. Chule asopi numei ho jong ilah uva acheng uve,” atiuvin ahi. Amaho chu alung-u adongin ahivangin atahsan nom pouvin ahi. 
Chuin Jesun adonbut in ajah uva, “Themgao chu achenna gamsung le a inkote lah chule ama insung tailouvin mun dang jousea jabolna achang jin ahi,” ati. 
Chukom'a miten atahsan lou jeh un ana damlou phabep khat chunga, akhut angapma adamsah tailou chun thil kidang ima abol thei tapoi. 
Atahsan lou dan'u chu adatmo lheh jeng e. Chuin kho anai dunga achelen thu ahil-le tai. 
Chuin aseijui som le ni ho chu akouvin, lhagao boh chunga thaneina apen, chujouvin mini-nin asoldoh tai. 
Chule ajin nauva tenggol tailou ima dang, changlhah jong, sakhao jong, dangka dip sunga sum them jong—aken louhel diuvin thu apei. 
Kengchot vang akise uva, sangkhol thoni vang aki ahlou diuvin aseipeh in ahi. 
“Hoikom hijongleh nachena chan uva, nalhun lhunnau in'a chun, khosung na potdoh kahse uvin umun. 
Ahinlah koi kho hileh nahin lhunsah lou uleh, nathu seiyu ngailou khomite na dalhah tengu leh, dalhah ahi tauve ti a hettohsah nan nakeng phang uva agam leivui chu thinglha uvin,” tin thu apei. 
Chuti chun akipatdoh un, mihem ten lunghei ding ahi ti chu ahil lhangle uvin, 
Thilha tamtah jong anodoh'un, ana damlou tamtah jong thao anu uvin aboldam un ahi. 
Jesu min gamtin'a akithan tah jeh chun, Leng Herod Antipas in hiti thu chu ajatan, mi phabep in, “Ama hi John Baptist athia kon'a thoudoh kit hinte. Hijeh a chu thil kidang boltheina anei ahi,” tin aseiji uve. 
Loikhat in, “Themgao Elijah ahi,” atiuvin, loikhat kit chun, “Malai themgao minthang ho banga themgao khat ahi,” atiuve. 
Herod in Jesu chung thu ajah phat in, “Alu katansa John chu athia kon'a hung thoukit hinante,” ati. 
Ijeh-inem itileh Herod in anaopa Philip jinu Herodias chu aki chenpi ahin, amanu jal'a chu ama tah in mi asol'a John chu amatsah a, akan'a songkula akhum ahi. 
John chun Herod jah a, “Nasopi ji jia naneiya naki chenpi hi dan ngai ahipoi,” anatin ahi. 
Hiche jeh a chu Herodias chun ama chu ha-hot in aho in, tha dingin atup in, ahivanginla Herod phalna louvin abol thei pon ahi. 
Ijeh-inem itileh Herod chun John chu michon phatah le mithengtah ahi ti chu ahet jeh in ajabolin, chuleh avesuiyin ahi. Herod in John ahoulimpi teng ahimoji lheh in, ahijeng vangin, athusei angainom kit jin ahi. 
Hinlah Herodias in phat gomkom anga jing chu Herod pen nikho chun ahunglhung tai. Chuche nikhoa chun gamvaipo natong alen alal ho, galsat-thalchoi lamkai ho chule Galilee gam'a milun ho chengse vahnan golvah abolin ahi. 
Hiche achun achanu chu ahung lut in, alam in ahileh leng Herod le atou khompi chengse chu alung lhaisah sohtan, chuphat in lengpan nungahnu jah achun, “Nagot chan thum'in, keiman peng kate,” 
“Nathum chan chu kapeh ding ahi, ka lenggam akeh khat geiya jong kapeh thei nahi,” atitai. 
Chuin amanu apotdoh in, anu kom'ah, “Ipi kathum ding ham?” tia agadoh leh, anun, “John Baptist luchang thum in,” ahin titai. 
Chuphat in amanu kintah in lengpa hengah ahung loiyin, “John Baptist luchang tutah in konglheng khat in neipe lechun kati penne,” tin athumtai. 
Chuin lengpa alunggim lheh jeng tan, ahivanginla mi masanga athutep le akihahselna chule a-ankong umpiho jal chun, amanu thil thum chu nahsahmon akoithei tapoi. 
Hijeh chun asolchah hemchoi ho khat chu John lu gala dingin asoltan, ama achen songkul sungah alu agatan dentai. 
Chuin alu chu kong khat in ahin domin nungahnu chu apen, aman anu aga petai. 
Chuin aseijui ten ajah phat un ahungun along chu ahung lauvin lhan'ah avui tauve. 
Chuin solchah ho chu anatohnauva kon'in Jesu henga ahung kile uvin, akimanchah dan'u le athuhil danho uchu aseiyun ahi. 
Chuin Jesun ajah uva, “Hungun, munthip a chomkhat gakichol ute,” ati. Ijeh-inem itileh ahungle achele mi atam behseh jengin, an jeng jong nemanlou ahi tauve. 
Hiti chun amaho munthip lam jonin kong in akitol phei tauvin ahi. 
Hinlah mi tamtah in hitia akitoldoh uchu anamu uvin, hichun khopi mun chomchom a kon'in mi tamtah chu twipang achun ana che pheiyun, agal langa chun ana lhung masa kit tauvin ahi. 
Kong sunga kon Jesun kal ahin sedoh leh hitia mihonpi anaum chu Jesun amun, achingbei kelngoihon toh abah jeh uchun alung asen, hichun amaho chu thil tampi ahil'in ahi. 
Nilhah lam'in aseijui te ahungun ahenga, “Hikom hi gamthip ahin, phat hila agei lheh tan, 
mihonpi hi ikimvel uva loujao le khosung hoa acheuva neh ding imathem aga kichoh diuvin soldoh tan,” atiuvin ahi. 
Hichun Jesun adonbut in, “Nanghon aneh diu peuvin,” ati leh, “Ipi-em kapeh diu? Mi hijatpi vahna ding vang chu sum tampi kakilona diuvin lha tampi lut inte!” tin ahin donbut uve. 
Chuin Jesun, “Changlhah pheng ijat naneiyum? Gave temun,” atin, hichun agave uvin, “Changlhah pheng nga toh nga ni aum'e,” atiuvin ahi. 
Chuin mihonpi chu abonchauvin hamhing eng lah a chu ahon hon'a atousah diuvin aseijui te athuhil tan. 
Chuin amaho agol golin—ja khat cheng, som nga chengin atou tauvin ahi. 
Jesun changlhah pheng nga toh nga ni chu alan, vanlam aven, phatthei aboh in changlhah chu ahehbal bal in, a anguva luipeh dingin aseijui te apen, nga ni jong chu ahoppeh sohkeiyin ahi. 
Hichu ama hon aneuvin, abonun aneva sohkei tauve. 
Akivahva jouvun changlhah le nga aval chengse paipeh som le ni aloh dimun ahi. 
Mihonpi kivahva chu numei le chapang ho sim louvin pasal cheh mi sang nga alhingin ahi. 
Chuin Jesun mihonpi chu alhadoh jinglai chun aseijui te chu, gal langkhat lama Bethsaida ana galkai masa dingin konga chun alutsah in asoltan ahi. 
Abon uva alhahdoh soh phat in, tao dingin mol chunga akaltou tai. 
Jansot lamin aseijui te chu dil lailunga kong chunga aumun, Ama achangin tolgo chunga aum den e. 
Chuin amaho dil chunga konga akitol uva, hui le twi nong in anuh uva alungdon uchu amun, khovah ding kon lamin akengin dil chunga lam ahinjot in, amaho chu khel ding agon ahi. 
Ahin ama hon dil chunga kenga lam ahin jot amu phat un thilha hinte atiuvin, a aosot jengun ahi. 
Aboncha uva Ama chu amu sohkeiyu ahin, atija lheh jengun, ahivangin Aman amaho chu ahin houpaiyin ajah uva, “Kicha hih un! Hangsan uvin! Keima hichea kaum nai,” ati. 
Chuin kong chunga chun akaldoh in, hui jong athip jengtai. Amaho chu alungdong lheh jengui. 
Ajeh chu changlhah kidang thudol jong chu hepha talou ahi tauve. Alungtah behseh nalam uva chuti ahi. 
Chuin agalkaiyun Gennesaret gamsung alhungun, kong chu twipam chin'ah anga tauve. 
Chuin konga kon'in apotdoh doh un, miho chun ana hepai tauvin ahi. 
Agamsung uva alhai kimvel leuvin, chuah aum'e ti ahetna chan uvin ana damlou ho chu alupna pheh pumin ahinpu uvin ahi. 
Achena chan khoneo, khopi chule loujao gamkochong alutna chan'ah adamlouho chu japi kititna kailhang mun'ah alupsah un, apon mongkai beh atham dingun ahenga ataovun ahi. Hichun athamkha chan chu adam jengun ahi.
