Mark.
7.
Nikhat hi Pharisee ho leh hou lamkai danthu sunho phabep Jerusalem a kipat in Jesu kimupi dingin ahungun ahi. 
Aseijui te mi phabep khat in dan nitlou leh, khut soplouva an aneh uchu ahung mudoh tauve. 
(Judah ho adehset'a, Pharisee hon akhang akhanga chonna dan dungjuiya khut phatea sopngim louva anehji lou u ahi. 
Hitobangma chun, ama hon kailhanga kicho hijongleh twiya khut adelut louleh ima ane ngai pouve. Hiche chu chonna ngai tampi—khomho, belho, sum-eng chule jalkhun ho sopthengna kin-gun—hitobang chondan tampi anei lah uva khat ahi.) 
Hiti chun, Pharisee ho leh hou dan thuhil hon adong un, “Ipi jeh a na seijui ten akhang akhanga ichonna ngaiyu banga khutsop louva an aneh uham?” atiuvin ahi. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Isaiah Themgaovin miphalhem nangho chung chang thua, ‘Hiche nam mite hin akamsung uvin eichoian un, alungthim ula keiya kon'in gamla tah ahi. 
ama hon eihou uhi moh ahi. Mihem semthu lungsuhto ho chu Pathen thupeh dan'a ahil u ahi,’ tin anaseiye. 
Nanghon Pathen dan thupeh ahibang tah chu nahsahmon nakoiyun nangho chon namlhah joh natuhdetjo tauve.” 
Chuin Aman, “Na chonna ngaiyu natuh det theina diuvin Pathen danthupeh tahbeh chu nahei lamdang uve. 
Atekahnan, Mose'n Pathen'a hungkon danthu napeuve: ‘Nanu le napa jabolun,’ chule ‘Koi hileh anu le apa housea chu thi jeng hen,’ ati. 
Hinlah mihon anu le apa kom'a, ‘Gelse hih un, kahin panhu joupoi, ajeh chu kahinpeh ding chu Pathen'a dia kapeh dingin kakitep tai,’ tia aseitheiyu jongleh aphatmona aumpoi natiuve. 
Hiti chun nule pate agel thupitah diu angaikhohpoi natiuve. 
Hitia hi nangho chon namlhah joh najui jing theina diuva Pathen thupeh ho chu juilouva nakahlhah jiu ahitai,” ati. 
Chuin mipite chu athuhil ngai din akouvin, “Nabon uvin ngaiyun, chule hetthem ding gouvin. 
Polama kon asunga lut chun ima asuboh pon; lungsunga kon'a hung potdoh joh chun asuhbohji ahi bouve.” 
Angaina ding nakhang neiyin ngaihen,” ati. 
Chuin Jesun mihonpi chu adalhan insung alut leh aseijui ten thulem asei chun ipi lamdol akoa avetsah ham tin adongui. 
“Nanghon jong nahet theilou laiyu ong?” tin adong'e. “Natahsa sunga lut an naneh ho chun nahin suhboh lou u ahi chu nahet theilou uham? 
An nanehlut uchu lungsunga lut louvin oisungah alut in posona-ah ahungdoh jitai.” (Hitia kisei hi, Pathen mitmun an jouse hi santhei dol ahisoh keiye ti aphondohna ahi). 
Chule Aman aseibe kit in, “Lungsunga kon'a hungpot chun mi asuhboh ahi. 
Ijeh inem iti leh mihem lungsunga kon'in apolamah—lunggitlou, jonthanhoi, guchat, toltha, 
midang toh jon, papset, gitlou, mi duhabol, huhna, kimitthipna, kitaitom, hoitho, ngolna kitiho hi ahung sohdoh ji'e. 
Hiche thil phalou ho hi lungsunga potdoh ahin, mihem subohji chu ahiuve,” ati. 
Chuin Jesun Galilee gam adalhan, sahlam'a Tyre gamsungah achetai. Hikom'a chun in khat a alhungin, aumna in chu koima hetsah anompoi, ahinlah akisel thei deh tapoi. 
Hiche achun Ama thudol ana jakha numei khat ahungin, akengbula ahung bohkhup in ahi. Amanu chu achanu lhagao boh vop ahin, 
Achanu a kon'a lhagao boh anodoh peh dingin ahung taovin ahi. Amanu chu Syrian Phoenicia tolkon, Gentile ho ahijeh chun, 
Jesun ajah a, “Amasan keima insung, Judah chate kavah masat ding ahi. Chate an lahpeh a uichate selhah peh angai ahipoi,” ati. 
Amanun jong, “Hichu ahinai, Pakai, hijongleh dokhang noiya um uichan jong chate lhengkonga konna an tolkhe anehkhah jiu khula em,” tin adonbut in ahi. 
Chuin Jesun, “Nadonbut hoi lheh e! Tuhin na in'ah chetan, nachanu chu thilhan adalhatai,” atipeh tai. 
Hichun Amanu jong a in'a ache leh achanu chu jalkhun'a analup amun, lhagao boh ana potdoh tan ahi. 
Galilee Dillen le Khopi som (Decapolis) lhaanga akile masangin Jesun Tyre adalhan Sidon lam’a akaltouve. 
Chuin anangong, pao jong paochen theilou khat hi ahenga ahin puiyun, achunga akhut angap dingin ataovun ahi. 
Hichun Aman mipi lah a kon'in apuidoh in, akhutjungin akol sung akhoiyin, 
Achil aset in, alei athamin, chuin vanlam aven akhoisan, “Ephphatha,” ati. Hichu “Kihongin!” tina ahi. 
Chupet tah chun ana avongin, aleigui jong akilham tan, kicheh tah in apaothei tai. 
Chuin Aman amaho chu koima jah a aseidoh lou diuvin akham tan ahi. Ahinla ama hon aha seiphong cheh un ahi. 
Kidang asa behseh jengun, avel avelin, “Aman ijakai abol jouse hi akidang thei lheh jenge. Nangongla ana avonsah in, paomola apaosah e,” atiuvin ahi.
