Mark.
9.
Jesun aseibe nalaiyin, “Keiman tahbeh a ka seipeh nahiuve, tua hichea ding ho kimkhat hi Pathen Lenggam thaneitah a ahung lhun amu kahseuva thina tepkhalou ding aum uve!” ati. 
Nigup jouvin Jesun Peter, James chule John atumbeh in molsang chungah apui touvin ahi. Chuah amaho angsung laitah a Jesu avou akikhel tan, 
Aponsil chu vah bangin aemleng jengin, leiya koiman pon hitobanga chu asop bang theilou khop in abangin ahi. 
Chuin Elijah le Mose ahung kilah lhon'in Jesu chu ahoulimpi lhon'in ahi. 
Chuin Peter in, “Houhil, hikom'a iumuhi eiho din anom ngeiye! Geldoh jingna din lhambuh thum song uhite—Nang ding khat, Mose ding khat chule Elijah ding khat,” atin ahi. 
Hiche asei hi akichat behseh jeh a seiding hetmoa asei ahi. 
Chuin meilhang khat in amaho chu ahin lekhun, meilah a kon'in aw ahung gingin, “Hiche hi ka Chapa deitah chu ahi, athu ngaiyun!” ati. 
Chuin akimvelu avet uleh, koimacha amu hih un, Mose le Elijah chu ana chemang tan, Jesu changseh le amaho changbou aum tauvin ahi. 
Molla kon'a ahung kumsuh jing laiyun, Jesun athilto'u chu Mihem Chapa athia kon'a athodoh kit kahsea koima jah a aseidoh lou diuvin akhamgah in ahi. 
Athusei chu alung uva aching jingun, athia kon'a athodoh kit chu ipi aseina ahidem, tin akidongto jiuvin ahi. 
Chuin ama hon “Ibola danthusun hon Messiah hung masanga Elijah hung kit masa ding ahi atiu ham?” tin adong uve. 
Aman adonbut in, “Elijah chu hung masa a ijakai ahung semphat ding mong ahi. Ahinla Pathen Thubun Mihem Chapan gim hesoh tamtah athoh ding chule mite nahsahmoa um ding ati ham? 
Ka seipeh nahiuve, Elijah chu hunga ahitai, chule thil hungsoh ding ana kisut bangtah in isahlou tah in anabol tauve,” atin ahi. 
Chuin seijui dang ho henglam'a ahung kileuva ahileh, amaho chu mipin ana umkimvel uchu amu'vin, hou lamkai phabep khattoh jong ana kinel'un ahi. 
Mihonpin Jesu amu phatnun, akipah thanomun ahengah ahung lhaiyun, ahung lamtouvin ahi. 
Hichun Jesun, “Amaho khutoh ipi thua kinel nahiuvem?” tin adongtai. 
Mipi lah a kon'in mi khat in ahin donbut in, “Pakai, ka chapa na damsah dingin nahenga kahin puiye. Ama hi lhagao boh in avop in, apao dohsah thei tapoi. 
Hoikom hijongleh lhagao boh hin amat matna'ah aheh lhujin, chule achilphon alhan, aha ageljin chule alhasamdel jitai. Hijeh a chu na seijui te kom'a einodoh peh diuva kangeh leh abolthei pouve,” ati. 
Hichun Jesun akom uvah, “Tahsanna neilou mite! Itih chan kaumpi ding nahiuvem? Kaheng'a chapangpa chu hinpui tangu,” atitai. 
Hichun chapangpa chu ahin puiyui. Ahin lhagao boh in Jesu amu phatnin, chapangpa chu nasatah in aveingoiyin ahileh tolla alhun, achilphon akai letlut in akitel lelen ahi. 
Chuin Jesun chapangpa apa jah a, “Ahitina hi itih a pat hitam?” tia adoh leh, apan, “Aneolaiya kipan ahitai. 
Tha dingin abang leh meiya, abang leh twiyah alehlut jin ahi. Hijongleh Nangman alonaje nahet leh kachung uva khotona neipumin nei kithopin,” tin aseitai. 
“'Alonaje nahet leh', nati chu ipi naseina ham?” tin Jesun adong'e. “Atahsanho dingin ijakai ahithei sohkeiye,” ati. 
Hichun hetman louvin chapangpa apa chu ahung kapjah jengin, “Pakai, katahsan'e, katahsan lelna abeina din nei kithopin,” tin ataotai. 
Chuin Jesun mi ahung kikhop tam chehcheh amu phatnin lhagao boh chu aphoh in, ajah a, “Ngaiyin, nang lhagao phalou, chapangpa paomosah le na ngonsahpa, thu kapeh nahi, chapangpa sunga kon'in hung Potdoh inlang lutkit tahih in!” ati. 
Hichun lhagao boh chu apengjah jengin, chapangpa chu hatah in aveingoiyin, chuin adalha tai. Chapangpa chu athi bangin aum tan, mihonpi chu akihou lahlah un, “Athi hitakhu!” atiuvin ahi. 
Ahin Jesun akhut in amanin, akaithouvin ahileh ahung thoudoh jeng tan ahi. 
Chujou chomkhat jouva Jesu le aseijui te insunga aum laiyun aseijui ten, “I-atileh keihon kanodoh lel'u ahidem?” tia adoh ule, 
Jesun adonbut in, “Hitobang hi taona thahatna a bou kinodoh thei ahi,” ati. 
Chumun chu adalha uvin, Galilee gam ajot un, min ahetdoh diu chu Jesun adeipoi. 
Ijeh-inem iti leh Aman aseijui te toh phat mankhoma thuhil ding phat angaichan ahi. Hichun Aman ajah uva, “Mihem Chapa chu melmate khut a pehdoh a um ding ahi. Ama hon atha dingu, ahivanga nithumni leh Ama thoudoh kit ding ahi,” ati. 
Athusei chu ipi asei ham ti ahethei pouvin Ama doh dingla akichauvin adong ngam pouve. 
Amaho Capernaum ahunglhungun, chuin insunga aumpet un ajah uva, “Lam dunga chu ipi na ngaitou ham?” tin aseijui te adongin ahi. 
Ahin, adonbut pouve, ajeh chu alah uva koi chu alenpen hintem tia kinel ahiuve. 
Hichun Jesu atouvin som le ni ho chu akouvin, “Koi hileh amasapen hinom chu nukhah pen ding ahi, chule mijouse jenlea pang hi ding ahi,” ati. 
Chuin chapang neocha khat alaiyuva adinsah in, chuin apomin ajah uva, 
“Koi hileh keima min'a hitobang chapang neokhat sanga chun keima eisan ahi, chule koi hileh keima eisang chan chun, keima bou eisan hilouvin eihinsolpa jong asan ahi,” ati. 
Hichun John'in Jesu kom'ah, “Houhil, amin'a thilha nodoh mi khat kamu uvin, hinla ama chu eiho lah a pang ahilou jeh in kakham tauve,” ati. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Kham hih un! Kamin'a thil kidang bol a keima eiseise loi thei koima aumpoi.” 
Koi hijongleh eidoulou pou chu eilama pang ahi. 
Koi hijongleh Messiah nungjui mi ahi tia twi khon khat don ding napechan uchu, tahbeh a ka seipeh nahiuve, itinama jong le ama chun kipaman asan ding ahi.” 
“Chule koi hijongleh keima eitahsan khangdong chaho jeng jong setna a kipallhuh sah chun angonga sumhei song lentah kikhai henlang twikhanglen'a kilehlut leh ama dingin aphajoi. 
Chule nakhut langkhat in na chonset sah a ahileh tanbong jengin. Khut ni neiya meikong mit theilou damun'a che sangin tibukimlou khut langkhat seh toh hinkemlouna a lut aphajoi. 
Chu mun'a chu thanho thi deh louding, mei jeng jong mit talou ding ahi. 
Nakeng langkhat in na chonset sah a ahileh tanbong jengin, keng ni neiya damun jot sangin keng langbeiya hinkem louna mun'a lut aphajoi. 
Chumun'a chu thanho thi deh louding, mei jeng jong mit talou ding ahi. 
Namit langkhat in nakipal lhuhsah a ahileh kaldoh jengin, mit ni neiya damun'a nakilelut sangin mit lang choa Pathen gam'a lut nangma dingin aphajoi. 
'Chu mun'a chu thanho thi dehlou ding, mei jeng jong mit talou ding ahi.'” 
“Ijeh-inem itileh mijouse meiya kipatep ding ahi. 
Chi hi apha ahivangin a-alna bei tahen lang tam taleh ipia ki-alsah kit ding hitam? Chi bangin gun neiyun lang khat le khat chamdelin um uvin,” ati.
