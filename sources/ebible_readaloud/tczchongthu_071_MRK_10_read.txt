Mark.
10.
Chuin Jesun Capernaum adalhan chua kon'in Judah gamkol sung leh, Jordan Lui solam gamkaiyah achesuh tan ahi. Mihonpi chu ahenga ahung kikhom kit tauvin, chuti chun Aman amaho chu thu ahilkit tan ahi. 
Chua chun Pharisee ho mi phabep ahenga ahungun mochan theina dinga kipalsahna dingin, “Pasal in aji ada hi danngai hinam?” tin thudoh ahung neiyui. 
Jesun athudoh'u chu adonbut in, “Mose'n nangho jida thua danthu itobang napeh uvem?” tia adoh leh, 
ama hon, “Da ding aphalnai. Mi khat in aji adana dia lekhasut apehlut a, chule amanu chu asoldoh thei ahi, tin aseiye,” atiuvin ahi. 
Chuin Jesun adonbutnan: “Nangho lungtah behseh jeh a hitia chu dan nahin ngaijang peh u ahi bouve. 
Ahivangin semtila pat a 'Pathen'in Pasal le numei anasem' ahitai. 
'Hichun ipi jeh a pasal in anu le apa adalhah a ajia beh ham ti avetsah in, 
chule amani chu pumni hita louva pumkhat,' tahsa khat hilhon ding ahitai. 
Hijeh chun Pathen gopsa chu mihemin sukhen hih hen,” ati. 
Phat chomkhat jouvin, aseijui te toh insunga amaho bou aumpet un, hiche thu hi ahin podoh kit'un, 
Aman aseipeh in, “Koi hileh aji da a jidang nei chun ajinu chu ajonsan ahi. 
Numeiyin jong apasal ada a midang toh akichen leh amanu jong chu ajong ahi,” ati. 
Nikhat hi minu mipate phabep khat in achateu chu Jesun atham'a phatthei aboh diuvin ahin puiyun ahileh, aseijui ten ana jadauvin ahi. 
Jesun hichu ajah phat in aseijui te chunga alung phamon, ajah uva aseijin, “Chapang neocha ho chu kaheng'a hung sah'un, jahda hih-un! Ijeh-inem iti leh Pathen Lenggam chu amaho tobang hoa bou ahi.” 
Tahbeh a ka seipeh nahiuve, koi hileh chapang neoho banga Pathen Lenggam sanglou chu itinama jongleh hichea chu lut louding ahi,” ati. 
Chuin chapangho chu apomin achung uva akhut angam'in phatthei abohtai. 
Chuin Ama Jerusalem lang jon'a ache ding kon'in mi khat ahung lhaijelin a angsung'ah adilsun, ajah-ah, “Houhil pha, tonsot hinkemlou kanei theina dinga ipi kabol ding ham?” ati. 
Jesun, “Ibola apha neiti ham?” tin adong'e. “Pathen khat seh tailou koima apha aumpoi, 
Ahin, na thudoh donbutna dingin Thupeh chengse: 'Tolthat hih-in, Jonbol hih-in, gucha hih-in, duhan mi bol hih-in, nanu napa jabolin,'” ati. 
Hichun khangdong pan, “Houhil, hichengse chu ka chapan laiya pat kahin nitpeh ahitai,” ati. 
Jesun ama chu aven, angailu lheh jenge. “Nabol loulai thilkhat aum nalaiye, chen lang nanei chengse gajoh inlang vaicha gentheiho gahoppeh tan, chutileh van gou nanei ding ahi. Chuleh hungin lang kanung juijin,” ati. 
Chucheng asei ajah phat in amai athim jeng tan, gou haotah ahijeh in lung nommo tah in achemang tan ahi. 
Chuin Jesun aseijui te avekimvelin, “Mihao theiho hi Pathen Lenggam'a alut diu iti hahsat hitam!” ati. 
Athusei chu aseijui ten adatmo lheh uve, ahinlah Jesun asei kit in, “Chate ho, Pathen Lenggam'a luttheina ding hi thil hahsa tah jong ahi, 
Tahbeh in, mihao ho Pathen Lenggam alut sangin sangongsang heo-bil hom'a alut ding abaijoi!” ati. 
Ama hon kidang asa lheh jengun, “Chuti ahileh koi huhhing'a umjou ding ham?” atiuvin ahi. 
Jesun amaho aven, “Mihem dingin hithei hih jongleh Pathen dingin ahimo aumpoi,” ati. 
Chuin Peter ahung paodoh in, “Keihon ijakai ka dalhauvin nanung kahin jui tauve,” ati. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Henge, ahinai. Chuleh tahbeh a ka seipeh nahiuve, keima jal le Kipana Thupha jal'a a-in, asopi pasal ho, asopi numei ho, anu, apa, achate chule anei-agou dalha hon, 
Ajatja a in le lou, sopi pasal, sopi numei, nu le pa, chate chuleh nei le gou—bolsetna thohpum puma akisan ding ahi. Chuleh khonung hinkhoa tonsot hinkemlou anei ding ahi. 
Amavang tua athupipen ho hung thupimo pen intin, tua thupimo penho hung thupipen tante,” ati. 
Hichun Jerusalem jonin achetou tauvin, Jesu chu amasang uva achen ahileh aseijui ten adatmo lheh jeng uvin, chuleh anung uva cheho chu kichatnan adim uvin ahi. Aseijui som le ni ho apanga achesah in, hichun khonunga Ama chunga hunglhung ding thu ho aseipeh kittan ahi. 
“Ngaiyun, Jerusalem jonin iche tauve, chukom'a chu Mihem Chapa hi thempu pipuiho le danthu sunho khut a pehdoh a um ding, chule amaho chun athina dinga themmo achansah uva namdang te khut a apehdoh dingu, 
Chuleh amaho chun a-el ela abol uva chil asetkhum dingu, avoh uva chuleh atha dingu, chuleh nithum jouleh ama thoudoh kit ding ahi,” ati. 
Chuin James le John, Zebedee chateni ahung lhon'in akihoulimpi lhon'in, “Houhil, kathum lhon neibol peh tei lhon'in,” ati lhon e. 
Jesun, “Nathum lhon chu ipi ham?” atileh, 
Amanin, “Naloupina'a natou tengleh khat najet lamah khat naveiyah nei tousah lhon'in,” atilhon tai. 
Hichun Jesun, “Nathum lhon nakihet lhon poi! Keiman chomkhat jou le kahin don ding thohgimna khon khachep'a chu nadon ngapchat lhon ding hinam? Thohgimna twilut a kachan ding chu nachan ngam lhon ding hinam?” tin adong tai. 
Amanin jong, “Henge, kangam lhonne!” tin aseilhon e. Chuin Jesun adonbut in, “Kadon ding khon'a nadon lhon ding ahin, chule thohgimna twilut ka chan ding jong changngam lhon nan nate. 
Ahivangin kajet le veiya tousah ding vang chu keima bolthei dol ahipoi. Pathen'in hichu alhendohsa ho dinga agotsa ahi tabouve,” ati. 
Seijui som ho chun James le John thilthum ajah u chun athimah-un alung phamouvin ahi. 
Jesun amaho chu akou khomin, “Nanghon naheuve, leiset mite lah a vaipo ho khu amite pakaijin apangun chuleh gamvaipo milun milal hon anoidou ho'u chunga athunei nau asouvin vai ahom uvin ahi. 
Nangho lah a vang chutilou ding ahi. Koi hileh nangho lah a alen hinoma chu na lhachauva pang ding ahi. 
Chuleh nalah uva apipuipen hinoma chu mijouse soh ahi ding angaiye. 
Ijeh inem iti leh Mihem Chapa tah jong chu jen dinga hung hilouvin mijenle ding le mitampi lhatdohna dinga ahinna tohdoh ding joh a hung ahi bouve,” ati. 
Chuin amaho Jericho khopi alhungun, Jesu le aseijui ten khopi sung adalhah ding kon'un mihonpi tamtah in anung ahin juiyui. Chuti lai chun mitcho khat, Timaeus chapa Bartimaeus chu lampam'ah ana touve. 
Nazareth Jesu chu nailam chan aumtai ti ahet phatnin, “Vo David chapa, nei lungset in!” tin ahin sam pantan ahi. 
Mi tamtah chun, “Thipmin!” tin ama chu aphoh un, ahivangin aman ging cheh in, “David chapa, nei lungset in!” tin ahin sam kit'e. 
Jesun ajah phatnin adingdenin, “Hilama hung din seipeh un,” atin, ama hon jong mitchopa chu aganai pheiyun, “Kipah thanomin! Hungtan, Aman nakouve,” atipeh un ahin pui tauve. 
Hichun Bartimaeus chun asangkhol-tho chu asut in akoilhan, chuin akichom touvin Jesu heng ahin jon tai. 
Jesun, “Ipi bolpeh leng nadeiyem?” tia adoh leh, mitcho pan, “Houhil, kho muleng kati,” ati. 
Chuin Jesun ajah a, “Chetan, na tahsan in na damsah tai,” ati leh, chupet tah chun kho amupai jengin, lamlhungin Jesu ahin juitai.
