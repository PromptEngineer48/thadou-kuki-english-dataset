Mark.
14.
Hichun Kalchuh Kut le Cholsolou Changlhah Kut kimanna ding nini aum tan ahi. Thempu pipuiho leh dan thusun hon Jesu chu guhthima mat'a tha theina ding lampi anahol jing nalaiyun ahi. 
Ahin, “Kalchuh Kut sung hida hen,” “achuti louleh mipi lah a thunoh umget inte,” tin akinopto uve. 
Chuin Jesu chu Bethany mun'a Simon phah anaveipa, in'ah alhunge. Chua an aneh laiyin numei khat in songval bomin thaonamtui mantamtah ahin choiyin, abom chu asukeh-in aluchungah abunglhan ahi. 
Ahin alah uva loikhat chu alung nommo uvin, “Ibol'a hitia hi thaonamtui mantam kimoh suhmang hitam? 
Ijeh-inem itileh hiche thaonamtui hi kijoh leh kum lhunga thalo jat toh kikot intin, hichu vaicha gentheiho pehthei hi ding!” atiuvin aphunkhum tauvin ahi. 
Ahinlah Jesun adonbut in, “Amanu achangin dalha uvin. Ka dinga thilpha abol jeh a idia naseiset uham? 
Ijeh inem iti leh vaicha le gentheiho nakom uva aum jing nauve, nagot chan uva nahu theiyu ahi. Kei vang nakom uva um jing ding kahipoi. 
Amanun abolthei chan abol ahitai. Ka thilong chu vuiya ahung um theina dinga thao einu masat ahitai. 
Keiman tahbeh a ka seipeh nahiuve, vannoi pumpia Kipana Thupha kilhangsapna chan'a hiche numeinu thilbol jong hi amanu hetjingna a kisei toupeh ding ahi,” ati. 
Chuin som le ni ho lah a mi khat Juda Iscariot chun Jesu chu matsah theina dingin thempu ho henga aga chen ahi. 
ama hon ahet phat un akipatdoh un, ama chu sum peh dingin atem tauvin ahi. Chuin aman Jesu chu pehdoh a matsah theina ding agomkom'ahol tan ahi. 
Cholsolou Changlhah Kut nimasa nia Kalchuh Kut kelngoi atha pet uvin aseijui ten ajah ah, “Kalchuh Kut Ankong hoikom'a kagon diu nadeiyem?” atiuvin ahi. 
Hichun aseijui mini khopi sunga gache lhon din asolin, “Khopi sung nalut lhon teng, twibel pua mi khat nato lhon ding ahi. Ama chu jui lhon'in. 
Ama alutna inneipa jah a chun, ‘Houhil'in: Ka seijui te toh Kalchuh Kut ankong ka sokhomna diu indan chu hoilai ham? ati,’ gati lhon'in. 
Chule ama tah in insong chunga indan lentah gontupsa a kikoi khat navetsah lhon ding ahi. Hikom chu ahi ineh khomna diuva na gonna diu mun chu,” ati. 
Chuin seijui teni chu apot lhon'in, khopi sungah alut lhon'in, akisei bang chun ima jouse chu agatoh lhon'in, Kalchuh Kut an ding chu aga gong lhon tai. 
Hichun nilhah in Jesu aseijui te toh chua chun agalhung tauve. 
Atou uva an aneh laitah un, Jesun aseijin, “Tahbeh a kasei ahi, tua kaneh khompi lah a khat hin eipeh doh ding ahi,” ati. 
Amaho alungkham pan tauvin, bannei chan khat khat in ahin dongui, “Kei kahim?” tin. 
Aman adonbut in, “Nangho som le ni lah a khat, tua lhengkong kasokhompi hi ahi. 
Mihem Chapa chu athi ding dol masanglai peh a pat Thubua ana kiseisa ahitai. Hinama jongleh, Mihem Chapa melmate khut a pedoh a johdohpa chu—Ohe, ada nading e! Ama chu apen'a ana penglou hile ama dinga phajo dinga!” ati. 
Chuin a anneh laitah un Jesun changlhah phabep alan, hichu phatthei aboh in, chuin ahehbal bal'in aseijui te apen, “Hiche hi katahsa ahi, neuvin,” ati. 
Chule khon'a lengpitwi alan, phatthei aboh in amaho chu apen, chuti chun abonchauvin adon sohkei tauvin ahi. 
Chuin Aman ajah uva aseijin, “Hiche hi ka thisan, Pathen le amite kah a kitepna thah um photchetna ahi. Hiche hi mi tamtah chonset ngaidamna dinga longdoh ahi. 
Tahbeh a ka seipeh nahiuve, keiman Pathen Lenggam'a athah a kadon kit ni ding tailou lengpitwi hi kadon louding ahitai,” ati. 
Hichun la asauvin, chujouvin apotdoh un Olive Lhang lamah ache tauvin ahi. 
Lampia achepet un Jesun ajah uva aseiye, “Nangho na boncha uva nalungthoi sohkei diu ahi. Ijeh-inem itileh, Pathen thubun: ‘Keiman kelngoi chingpa kavohlhuh ding ahi, chujou teng akelngoite chu chehchao diu ahi,’ ati. 
Ahinlah keima thina a kon kahung thodoh jouteng Galilee gam lama namasang uva kache ding chua chu neimu diu ahi,” ati. 
Chuin Peter in aseijin, “Aboncha uvin lungthoi jonglehu, kei vang lungthoi ponge,” ati. 
Hichun Jesun, “Keiman tahbeh a kasei ahi, Peter—nangman tujan'a ah nivei akhon masanga thumvei nei seilep ding ahi,” atipeh e. 
Peter in, “Kanompoi! Nangma toh thitha tei ding hijongleng, naseilep hihhel ing kate,” atichang melin ahi. Chule abonchauvin achuti seicheh un ahi. 
Amaho olive thinglei lah a Gethsemane atiu honsung achun ahungun, chuah Jesun aseijin, “Keima katao sungsen nangho hikom'a hin anatou jingun,” ati. 
Aman Peter, James chule John akipuiyin, alunghem lheh jeng tai. 
Chuin Jesun, “Thina khop helin kalung hesoh behseh jengtai. Hikom'a hin um unlang eina kiginpeh un,” ati. 
Chule amasang lamah neokhat ache chon'in tollah akhupboh in alhun, ahithei le hiche phat chu Ama a kon'a akitolmang theina dingin ataove. 
Akapjah in, “Abba, Hepa,” “Imajouse Nanga dinga hithei ahi. Ahitheile hiche gimthohna khon hi keiya kon'in kitolmang hen. Hinla Nangma lunggot bang joh hihen lang, keima lunggot bang hida hen,” tin atao tai. 
Chujouvin ahungdoh le khatte ho chu ana imutlhuh u ahung toh in, Peter jah a, “Simon, na imut hitam? Nidan khat jeng jong kiginga um joulou nahim? 
Lhepna a nalhah louna dingun, kigingun, taovun ijeh-inem iti leh lhagaovin agonan, tahsan ajolou ahi,” ati. 
Chuin amaho adalhan, ache kit in atao ngai bangin agatao kittai. 
Chuin ahung kile kit in ahile amaho chu ana imut'u ahungtoh kit in, aimutso behseh jeh un adonbutje ahepouve. 
Athumvei channa a ahung kile kit chun ajah uva, “Tun imu uvin lang kitha-ol tauvin. Aphaset tai, phat alhingtai. Hinla—veuvin, Mihem Chapa hi michonse te khut a kipedoh ahitai. 
Kipat un, chetau hite. Veuvin, eipedohpa hiche akhun aumtai!” ati. 
Jesun hichu asei jou jouvin, hetman louvin, Judas, aseijui ho lah a khat chu, mihonpi moltum le chemjam kichoi puma hungho toh ahung kilhon khom'in ahi. Amaho chu thempu pipuiho leh hou lamkai hon ahinsol'u ahi. 
Ajohdoh a pangpa, Judas Iscariot chun melchihna ahil masan, “Namat diu chu chibai kaboh a kachop penpen chu hi ding ahi. Ama chu puimang un,” ati. 
Ahung lhun lhun'un Juda chu Jesu hengah achen, “Houhil!” tin chibai bohnan achoptai. 
Chuin akhut alhauvin Jesu aman tauvin ahi. 
Jesu nungjuiho lah a mi khat in achemjam asatdoh in Thempu Chungnung sohpa asat leh abilkol langkhat asatlhan ahi. 
Jesun adongin, “Galguh bol ho man dinga kon banga moltum le chemjam thoa keiman dinga nahung uham? 
Hou-In sunga kaum laiya ibola neimat lou uham? Niseh leh nalah uva thu jong kanahil jinge. Ahinlah Thubua thu kiseisa aguilhun na dinga hitia hi thilsoh vang ahinai,” ati. 
Chuin aseijui te jouse akithecheh un, ajamgam tauve. 
Alah uva gollhang khat asangkhol tupat ponnem khat seh kiah aumin, ama chu ahungkon hon amat ule, 
Aman avon-ah chu akhut uva adalhan, asagohkeovin ajammangtai. 
ama hon Jesu chu Thempu Chungnung hengah akaiyun, chua chun thempu pipui chengse le vaipo upaho chule dan thusun ho ahung kikhomun ahi. 
Peter in galkangin ahin juipeh in chule jangpet'in Thempu Chungnung inpi leitol sungnung geiyin ajuilut peh in chua chun sottol leho lah a atouve. 
Asunglamah, thempu pipuiho leh hou lamkaiho chengsen Jesu tha theina dia hehsetna le themmo channa ding pansat theina ding ahol un ahivanginla amu pouvin ahi. 
Jouthu tamtah in ahehseuvin, ahinlah akitoh tapouvin ahi. 
Akhonna in, mi phabep akipatdoh un, 
“Amaa kon'a kajah u chu: khut a kisa hiche Hou-Intheng hi suse ingting, nithum jou leh khut a sahlou achom dang khat katundoh ding ahi,” ati e tin ahin hehse tauvin ahi. 
Ahivanginla hichea jong chun athuseiyu akitoh kit pouvin ahi. 
Chuin Thempu Chungnung chu mipi masanga adingdoh in, Jesu thudoh abolin, “Hite hon naheh nau thu aseiyuhi na donbut louding ham?” ati. 
Ahivangin Jesun donbut louvin thipbeh in aumin, chuin Thempu Chungnungin adongin, “Nangma Nunnom Chapa, Messiah chu nahim?” atitai. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Kahi,” atin, chuleh, “Nanghon Mihem Chapa hi thuneina mun'a Pathen jetlam'a atouva chule van meilah a ahung namu dingu ahi nalaiye,” ati. 
Chuin Thempu Chungnung chun alunghan vetsahnan aponsil abotkeh in, “Hettohsahna ipi iholbe nahlai dingu ham? 
Pathen ataitomna thu naja tauve. Nanghon iti nagel um?” atileh abonchauvin asam'un, “Athi dinga lom ahi!” tin aeo lahlah jengun ahi. 
Mi abangkhat in achil asetkhum'un chule amit akanpeh un, akhutnun achumun, “Gaothu nei seipeh un,” tin eltolin ahouvun, angeiphe abengun apuimang tauve. 
Hiche pet a chu Peter noilama leitol'a uma ahin, Thempu Chungnung ole numei khat ahungin ahile, 
Peter in mei aoi chu amun, amanu chun aven ajah a, “Nang jong Nazareth Jesu toh kiloi nahi,” ati. 
Ahinlah Peter in, “Nasei chu kahe hih hel'e,” tin adonbut in, leitol maiya apotdoh leh ahchal akhongtai. 
Chuin sohnu chun amun akom'a ding ho jah a, “Hiche pa hi amaho lah a khat ahi!” atikit e. 
Ahinlah Peter akilep kittai. Chomkhat jouvin, akom'a ding ho chun, “Amaho lah a khat mong mong nahi, ajeh chu Galilee mi nahi e,” atikit tauvin ahi. 
Ahivangin aman, “Kajou kasei leh gaosap chang inge—nasei pau hi kahekha hihbeh e!” atijeng tai. 
Chujou jouvin aniveina in ah ahung khong paitan ahi. Chutah chun, Jesu thusei: “Ah ni vei akhon masanga thumvei nei seilep ding ahi,” ati chu ageldoh tan, hakan akaptan ahi.
