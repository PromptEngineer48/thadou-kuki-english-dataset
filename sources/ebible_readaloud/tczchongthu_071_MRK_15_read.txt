Mark.
15.
Chuin jingpi tah in thempu pipuho, vaipo upaho, dan thusun ho, chule hou vaipoloi chengse akihou paiyun, Jesu chu akan'un, akaiyun Pilate khut ah apedoh tauve. 
Pilate in Jesu adongin, “Nangma Juda te Lengpa nahim?” ati leh, Jesun adonbut in, “Nasei chu adih e,” ati. 
Chuin thempu pipui hon thu tamtah in ahehse tauvin ahi. 
Pilate in adong kit in, “Amaho hi na donbut louding ham? Vetang thu hijat a nahin heh uhi?” Jesun adonbut loubeh phat in Pilate in adatmo lheh jenge. 
Jesun adonbut lou phat in, Pilate in adatmo lheh jengin ahi. 
Vaipopa chena ngai dol'a kumseh a Kut sungse leh, sohchang ho lah a amipin athum pen pen khat chu alhadohpeh ji ahi. 
Chulai chun songkul'ah Barabbas atiu gamsunga chohnoh bol le tolthat khat aumin ahi. 
Mihonpi chu Pilate henga acheuvin, angaina banga songkultang khat lhadoh din agaseiyui. 
Chuin Pilate in, “Juda te lengpa hi lhadohpeh leuveng nadeiyum?” tin aseitai. 
Ama chun thempu pipui hon thangset man'a apehdoh u ahi ti chu ahet ahi. 
Ahivangin thempu pipui hon Barabbas joh chu lhadohna dingin mipi chu atilse uvin ahi. 
Hichun Pilate in ajah uva, “Achutileh Judah te Lengpa nati pau hi i-kalo ding ba hitam?” ati. 
Chuin ama hon asamkit un, “Thingpela khenbeh in!” ati tauve. 
“Ijeh-inem? Ipi abolset em?” tia Pilate in asei vangin, mipi chun ahasap cheh un, “Thingpel'a khenbeh in!” ati tauve. 
Mipite lunglhaina dingin Barabbas alhadoh in, Jesu chu ajep jouvun thingpel'a khetbeh dingin apedoh tan ahi. 
Chuin hemchoi (Sepai) sottol hon Ama chu inpi sunga vaipo (Praetorium atiu) chun akailut un, sepai jouse akoukhom sohkeiyun ahi. 
Chuin Jesu chu ponsandup asilpeh un, leng lukhuh tobanga linga kiphan chu akhuhpeh ui. 
Chuin, “Judah te Lengpa, choianun!” tin, totnopnan chibai aboh un ahi. 
Chuin amaho chun peng tenggolin alu avouvin, chule achil asetkhum'un, totnopnan adilsu uvin chibai aboh ui. 
Achainan ael-ela totnopna'a abol jouvun aponsan chu ahoh lhauvin, ama pon ale silpeh un thingpela khetbeh dingin apuiphei tauvin ahi. 
Cyrene kiti gam kochong langa pat hungkon, Simon kitipa chutoh amaho chu lamkah a akitohpa uvin, hichun ama chu acham louvin Jesun ahinput thingpel chu aputsah un ahi. (Simon hi Alexander le Rufus penna pa ahi.) 
Chuin Jesu chu lugu-mun atiu Golgotha mun achun alhut tauvin ahi. 
Chuah myrrh toh kihal lengpitwi apeuvin ahile Ama anompoi. 
Chuin thingpel'a akhenbeh un, aponsil chu avangpha pha chang hen tin vang asang tauve. 
Jesu akhetbeh u chun jingvai nidan ko ahitan ahi. 
Achunga chun ahehnau hetnan, “Juda te Lengpa” tia kisun chu atah un ahi. 
Ama toh gamsunga chohnoh bol mini, khat aveilamah, khat ajetlamah akhenbeh un ahi. 
“Ama michonse chochibol ho toh akisimtha tai,” tia Lekhabua kisun chu aguilhung tan ahi. 
Chuin achele avahleho jousen akhasuh khumun, “Katei! Nangin Hou-In kasuhchima nithumni leh kasahdoh kit ding ahi natin, tuchun kivet in. 
Nangma kihuhhing inlang thingpel'a kon chun hung kumlha tangem!” atiuvin ael-elin ahouvun ahi. 
Chutobangma chun thempu pipuiho le dan thusun hon jong abol ui. “Aman midang ahuhhingin, Ama akihuhhing theipoi! 
Kamu uva kahet theina dingun Israel te lengpa chu tutah hin thingpel chunga kon'in hung kumlha hen!” atiuvin totnopnan abol'un ahi. Ama toh kikhetbeh tha teni jong chun hiti chun asei lhon e. 
Chuin sunkim vel hin gam jouseah nidan thum chan mu athim tan ahi. 
Hichun nidan thum dontah in Jesu hapen in apengin, “Eloi, Eloi, lema sabachthani?” atitai. Hichu “Ka Pathen, ka Pathen, ibola nei dalhah hitam?” atina ahi. 
Avel'a umho phabep chun Elijah akou hinte atiuve. 
Hichun mi khat alhaijelin, lengpitwi thuh a twichop phon ade lhop in, adon theina din peng tenggol leh chu adom in, amapa chun, “Khongaiyutin, Elijah in ahung huhdoh em veuhite!” ati. 
Chuin Jesu kithongtah in ahung peng kit in ahai atantai. 
Hichun Hou-In'a pondal len chu achunga pat anoilam geiyin a ehlha paiyin, kehni akiso tan ahi. 
Chule Rome sepai jalamkai khat in thilsoh ijakai amu jouvin, “Hiche pa hi Pathen Chapa tahbeh ahimonge!” tin aphongdoh jeng tai. 
Numei phabep chun thilsoh chu gal'a kon'in ahin galdot ui. Amaho lah a chun Mary Magdalene toh Mary (James le Joseph nu) chule Salome jong apangun ahi. 
Amaho cheng chu Jesu Galilee gam'a aumlaiya, Ama ajuile ajenlea ana pangho chu ahiuve. Chule numei tamtah Jerusalem'a Ama hin kilhon toupi ho jong chu ahiuve. 
Hichengse hi Cholngahni masang Kiginnia soh ahi. Chuin nilhah lam ahung hitan, 
Arimathea Joseph chun Jesu longdamsa chunga mopohna ladingin Pilate kom'a agathum tan ahi. (Arimathea Joseph chu jaumtah mi vaipoho lah a khat ahin, chule Pathen Lenggam hunglhun ding ngahjing mi khat ahi.) 
Pilate in Jesu chutia athiloi jeng ding chu atahsan pon, hijeh chun Rome sepai jalamkaipa chu akouvin Jesu athi tahbeh hitam tin adongin ahi. 
Chuin Rome sepai jalamkai pan athi chu aphotchen phatnin along chu Arimathea Joseph kom'a apedoh tai. 
Chuin Joseph chun along chu ala-lhan, tupat ponnem achon, hichun atomin, songpi kivut-hom lhan sungah chun alupsah tai. Chujouvin lhankot a chun songlen khat atanglut in, lhan chu asinnan ahi. 
Chule Mary Magdalene toh Joseph nu Mary chun Jesu longdamsa akoinau chu avelhi jing lhon'in ahi.
