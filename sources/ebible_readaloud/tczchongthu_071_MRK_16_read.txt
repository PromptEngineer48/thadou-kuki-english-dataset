Mark.
16.
Chuin Cholngahni nilhah ahung hia, cholngah nit akichai jouvin, Mary Magdalene, James nu Mary, chule Salome chu apotdoh un thaonamtui gachoh a Jesu tahsaa nu dingin akigong uve. 
Hapta nimasapen jingpi matah niso kon velin, amaho lhan lama chun aga cheuvin ahi. 
Lampiah amaho akihouvun, “Lhankot song chu kon eitahdoh peh tauvin tem?” akitiuvin ahi. 
Chuin ahung lhun phat un avet uleh song chu tahdohsa a anaum chu amu tauve. Asong chu lenpitah ahi. 
Hichun lhan sunga chu alut ule gollhang khat ponbangleh kisil'a jetlam'a tou amu uvin chuin amaho chu atija lheh jengun ahi. 
Ahivangin vantil chun amaho jah ah, “Nangho tija hih un, nang hon thingpel'a kithat Nazareth Jesu chu nahol'u ahi. Ama hikom'a aum tapoi! Ama thinaa kon'in athoudoh tai! Hungun, hung veuvin, hilai hi alonglupna chu ahi. 
Tun cheuvinlang Peter le aloiho kom'ah Aman athi masanga anasei bang chun Galilee gam'a na masang uvah ana che masatai, chua chu Ama namu diu ahi, ti gaseipeh tauvin,” ati. 
Numeiho chu atijauvin, kihot pumin lhan'a kon'in ahung potdoh un, akichat behseh jeh un ima seije jong ahepouve. Chuin ama hon agan'a gangin seijui ho—Peter le aloiho kom'ah aga seipeh tauvin ahi. 
Hapta nimasapen jingkah a Jesu athodoh kit jouva akimutopi masatpen chu Mary Magdalene, asunga thilha sagi anodoh peh nu chu ahi. 
Amanu chun seijui lunghem tah a anaum ho jah a chun thilsoh umdol ho chu aga seipeh tai. 
Ahivangin ama hon athusei chu atahsan pouvin ahi. 
Phat chomkhat jouvin lim chombeh in aseijui teni Jerusalem'a kipan'a thinglhang lama che teni kom'ah akilah in ahi. 
Amani ache lhon'in seijui dang ho jah a agasei lhon leh ama hon atahsan deh pouve. 
Chuin ankong aum laitah un seijui som le khat ho hengah Jesu akilah in athodoh kit nunga amuho thusei atahsan lou jeh ule alungtah jeh un asosalin ahi. 
Chuin Aman ajah uva, “Nangho vannoi leiset gam jouseah cheuvin lang thilsem mihem namtin jah a hiche Kipana Thupha hi gahil lhang leuvin. 
Koi hileh, atahsan'a baptize chang chu sohcha ding ahi. Atahsanlou vang chu themmo changa um ding ahi. 
Chule atahsante chu melchihna kidangin ajuipeh dingu ahi: Keima min'a thilha anodoh dingu, pao choma pao dingu, 
Gul akhut a adop dingu, thina thei ima don jongleu ima tilou ding ahiuve. Chule ana-damlou chunga khut ngamleu ana chu dam ding ahi,” ati. 
Chuin Jesun ajah uva chucheng asei jouvin van'ah lahtouvin aumin, Pathen jetlam'ah atoutai. 
Chuin amaho achedoh un muntin'ah ahillhang leuvin anatohna chan uvah Pakaijin akithopi uvin, chule melchihna in ajuisah in athuseiyu chu aphudet peh in ahi.
