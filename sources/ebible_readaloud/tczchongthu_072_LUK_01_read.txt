Luke Sut Kipana Thupha.
1.
Mi tampin eiho lah a ana guilhung thilsoh ho chung chang sutdoh agouvin ahi. 
Ama hon amittah a mu seijui masaho thulhut amangcha uvin ahi. 
Thil jouse abul'a pat phatah a kholchil ahitoh lhon'in, keiman jong chingthei tah a nangma dinga jihdoh ding kakigel lhah ahitai, jana sangpen chang Theophilus, 
chutia chu thutah nana kihil jouse chu ahimong nai ti nanopna ding ahi. 
Judea gam'a Herod leng ahi laiyin, Zechariah kiti Judah thempu khat ana um'in ahi. Ama chu Abijah thempu loi lah a khat ahin chule a'inneipi Elizabeth jong chu Aaron thempu khailhah a kon ahi. 
Zechariah le Elizabeth chu Pathen mitmun achonpha lhon'in, Pakai dan thupeh le chonna dan jouse chingthei tah a jui ahi lhon'e. 
Elizabeth in nao avop theilou jeh chun cha aneilhon pon, chule ani lhon'in hateh in ateh gel lhontai. 
Nikhat Zechariah chun Hou-In sungah Pathen kin abolin, ajeh chu chuche hapta sunga chu mopohna chang ahi. 
Thempuho chonna ngai banga, ama chu Hou-In muntheng sunga lut a chule thil namtui hal dinga lhendoh hi ahi. 
Thil namtui akihal jing laiyin, mipi tamtah pamlangah ading'un taona amang uve. 
Zechariah muntheng sunga aumpet in Pakai vantil khat ahengah ahung kilah in, maicham phung jetlam achun adingin ahi. 
Zechariah in hichu amu phat in akithing'in hatah in akicha tan ahi. 
Ahivangin vantil chun aseijin, “Kicha hih in Zechariah! Pathen'in nataona asangtai. Na inneipi Elizabeth in chapa khat nahinpeh ding, chule amin John nasah ding ahi. 
Nangman kipana lentah nanei ding, chule mi tampi ama pen a kipah ding ahi, 
ijeh inem itile ama chu Pakai mitmua hung len ding. Aman ju kiti le khamna thei thil kiti atohkhah thei louding. Ama chu apen masang jenga jong Lhagao Theng dimset ding ahi. 
Chule aman Israel mi tamtah Pakai a Pathen'u henga alehei ding ahi. 
Ama chun Elijah lhagao le thahat aken ding ahi. Aman mipi ho chu Pakai hung na dinga agontup ding ahi. Mipa te lung achate chunga aheiya chule doudal lungput neiho michonphaho chihna akisan sah ding ahi,” ati. 
Zechariah chun vantil jah a aseijin, “Hitobang hi aso ding ken iti kahet thei ding ham? Tun keima kateh tan, chule ka inneipi jong kum tamtah alhing tai,” tin adong tai. 
Chuin vantil chun aseijin, “Keima Gabriel kahi! Pathen angsung tah a ding kahi. Ama ahi keima nahenga hiche Kipana Thupha podinga eihin sol ah; 
Ahinlah tun, nangman kasei na tahsan pon, chujeh a nangma nathipbeh ding chule nao apen tokah a paothei louva naum ding ahitai. Ajeh chu ka thusei chengse hi aphat cha guilhung tei ding ahi,” ati. 
Achutilai chun mipi hon Zechariah chu ahung potdoh ding angah jingun, ipi bol'a hibanga hi sot den hitam tin alungdong gam tauvin ahi. 
Achainakeiya ahung potdoh phat chun ama apaodoh thei tapon ahi. Chuin ama hon amaiso le apaothei louva kon chun Hou-In muntheng sunga chu mu anei hinte ti ahedoh un ahi. 
Zechariah in Hou-In a athempu natoh hapta alhin phat in inlam'a akile tan ahi. 
Phat chomkhat jouvin ajinu Elizabeth chu ahung gai paiyin chule lha nga jen potdoh louvin aum thipbeh tan ahi. 
“Pakai iti phat hitam!” tin aphongdoh in “Aman ka chaneilou jeh a jumna eilah mang peh tai,” ati. 
Elizabeth naovop kal lhagup lhin'in, Pathen'in Gabriel vantil chu Galilee gam Nazareth kho ah, 
nungah theng Mary hengah asol'in ahi. Amanu chu Joseph kiti Leng David insung khailhah pasal khat toh kicheng dinga got a um ahitai. 
Gabriel chu amanu hengah akilah in aseitai, “Chibai, sahluttah nu! Pakaijin naumpi,” ati. 
Lungdong le lung kika lahlah pum in Mary in vantil thusei chu ipi tina ahi agel tai. 
“Kicha hih in, Mary,” tin vantil chun aseitan ahi, “Ajeh chu nangman Pathen lunglhaina nakimutai!” 
“Nangma gaiyin natin chapa khat nahin ding chule amin Jesu nasah ding ahi. 
Ama hung len ding chule Chungnung pen Chapa kiti ding ahi. Pakai Pathen'in apu David laltouna apeh ding ahi. 
Chule Israel chunga tonsot a vai apoh ding, alenggam in beitih aneilou ding ahi!” ati. 
Mary'n vantil chu adongin, “Hinlah, hichu iti hithei ding ham? Keima nungah theng kahi,” ati. 
Vantil chun adonbut in, “Lhagao Theng na chunga hung chu ding, chule chungnung pen thaneina in na lekhu ding ahi. Hijeh a chu naosen apeng ding chu atheng hi ding chule ama chu Pathen Chapa kiti ding ahi. 
Ipi aval um ding ham, nasopinu Elizabeth in jong ateh nungin nao avop tai! Mi hon aching'ah ahi atiuvin, hinlah tun chapa khat avop in lhagup alhingtai. 
Ajeh chu Pathen dingin ima ahimo aumpoi” ati. 
Mary'n adonbut in, “Keima Pakai sohnu kahi, kachunga lhung ding nasei jouse hung guilhung hen,” ati. Chuti chun vantil chun amanu chu adalhatai. 
Ni phabep jouvin Mary jong Judea gam thinglhang lang, 
Zechariah chenna khoa chun acheloi chel'in ahi. Amanu insung alut in Elizabeth chu chibai aboh e. 
Mary'n chibai aboh gin ajah chun Elizabeth naosen chu a-oisung a chun ahung kichom in, chule Elizabeth chu Lhagao Theng adimset tan ahi. 
Elizabeth in kipanan ka cheng ahinlon Mary jah a chun aseitai, “Pathen'in numei jouse chunga phatthei naboh ahitai, chule na cha jong anunnom'e. 
Ipi jeh a hibanga jana kachan a ka Pakai nu in eihung vil ham? 
Nei lemna awcheng kajah chun kasunga naocha kipah in ahung kichom'e. 
Anun nom nahi, ajeh chu Pakaijin asei chu abol ding ahi ti na tahsan e,” ati. 
Mary'n adonbut in “Ka lhagaovin Pakai achoi-an e. 
Kalhagao eihuhhing pu Pathen chunga iti kipa thanom hitam! 
Ajeh chu aman asoh numei nu dinmun ave jingin, chule tu apat a khang jousen nunnom eiti diu ahitai. 
Ajeh chu Hatchungnungpa chu atheng ahi, chule aman keima din thil loupi abol'e 
Aman ahepina akhang akhang'in ama gingte amusah jinge. 
Aban thahat chun kichat um tahtah thil abol'e! Aman mikiletsah le hoitho ho athecheh ji'e. 
Leng chapa te alaltou nauva kon'in aloilhan mi kineosah te adomsang ji'e. 
Aman agilkel te thilpha phan avah in chule ahao te khutgoh keovin asoldoh ji'e 
Aman asoh Israel akithopin khoto dingin ageldoh e. 
Ajeh chu aman hiche kitepna hi iputeu, Abraham, chule achate dinga asem ahi.” 
Mary chu Elizabeth kom'a lhathum tobang aum jouvin a'inlam ah akile tai. 
Elizabeth chu anaoso phat alhin phat in amanun chapa khat ahingin ahi. 
Chuleh aheng akom le asopi chengin Pakaijin akhotona ho ajahdoh phat un, mijouse amanu toh akipah khom cheh tauve. 
Naosen chu niget alhin in, amaho abon'un cheptansah dingin ahung'un ahi. Ama hon apa banga amin Zechariah sah ding agouvin, 
ahivangin Elizabeth in, “Ahipoi, amin John ahi,” atitai. 
“Ipi natim?” tin amaho ahung eojah jengun “Na insung uvah hitobang min chu aum khapoi,” atiuve. 
Hijeh chun naosen min chu apan ipi akisah nom am ti mit-heiyin adongun ahi. 
Aman lekhasutna pheng athum'in, mijouse lungdong in alhan “Amin John ahi” tin ahinjih e. 
Chutah chun Zechariah jong ahung paothei tan chule Pathen avahchoi pan tai. 
Akimvel'a cheng chengse din kichatna alhung tan chule Judea thinglhang gam sungse ah thilsoh umchan chu akithejal soh tai. 
Chuche thilsoh jajouse chun athilsoh ho chu alungin aching geh un, “Hiche chapang hi ipibep ahungsoh tadem?” atiuvin ahi. 
Chuin apa Zechariah chu Lhagao Theng adimset tan chule hiche gaothu hi aphongdoh in ahi: 
“Vahchoiyun Pakai, Israel Pathen chu; ajeh chu aman amite avil'in alhatdoh tai 
Aman huhhing hattah khat asoh David khailhah a kon'in eipeuve. 
Athemgao thengte henga malaiya akitepna banga 
Tua eiho igal mite uva kon le eihotbolte uva konna huhdoh a um ding ihitauve. 
Aman ipu ipateu khotonan akitepna thengho ageldoh jingin– 
Akitepna ipuluiyu Abraham kom'a ana kitepna bangtah in 
i-gal miteuva kon'in eihuhdoh uve, Pathen kichatna beihel'a ijen theina diuvin 
Then na le chondihna a ihin laiseuva dingin 
“Chule kachapa, nangma chungnung pen themgao kiti ding nahi; ajeh chu nangman Pakai lampi semtoh sa nakoi ding ahi. 
Nangman amite, achonset nau ngaidam na'a konna huhhingna akimudoh nadiu naseipeh ding ahi. 
Pathen mikhotona nemheo vanga jingkhovah chu van'a konna eiho chunga hung kisukeh go ahitai. 
Muthim'a touho le thina lim'a touho vah pedinga, chule chamna lampi komu peh dinga ahi,” ati. 
John chu ahung khanglen in chule lhagaovin ahung hatdoh in ahi. Chule Israel kom'a lhangphonga natoh apat kahsen ama gamthip lah dungah aumin ahi.
