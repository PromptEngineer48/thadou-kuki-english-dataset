Luke.
4.
Chuin Jesu, Lhagao Theng dimset pumin, Jordan vadunga kon'in akile tan ahi. Ama chu gamthip a din Lhagao puidoh in aumin, 
hichea chun diabol lhep patep in ni somli sung aum'e. Jesun hiche phat sungse chun ima anepon chule gilkel lheh jengah ahitai. 
Chuin diabol chun ahengah ahinseiyin, “Nangma Pathen chapa nahi le hiche song hi changlhah pheng akisona din seiyin,” ati. 
Ahin Jesun aseipeh in, “Ahipoi! Pathen Lekhabun mihem changlhah bouseh a hing louding ahi” ati. 
Chuin diabol chun apuitouvin, phat chomkhat kah sungin vannoiya lenggam ho avetsah sohkeiyin ahi. 
“Hiche lenggam ho loupina le achung uva thuneina nangma kapeh ding nahi,” tin diabol chun aseijin, “Ajeh chu aboncha keiya ahin kapeh nom nom kapeh thei ahi. 
Aboncha nangma kapeh ding nahi nangman keima nei hou leh,” tin aseitai. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Pathen Lekhabun hitin aseiye, ‘Nangman Pakai na Pathen bouseh nahou ding, ama kin jeng bou nabol ding ahi’” atipeh tai. 
Chuin diabol chun Jerusalem Hou-In tingvum ah apuiyin chule aseitai, “Pathen Chapa nahi le chomlhan! 
Ajeh chu Pathen Lekhabun hitin aseiye, Aman avantil te navengtup ding le nahoidam dingin thupeh hin neiyin te. 
Chule ama hon akhut uvin natuh intin, chutia chu nangman nakengpeh jeng jong song chunga naki suhkhah louding ahi,” ati. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Pathen Lekhabun hitin jong aseikit e, Nangman Pakai na Pathen na patep louding ahi” ati. 
Diabol chun Jesu alhep patep jou phat in, phat gomkom umkit kah a din adalha tai. 
Chuin Jesu Galilee langah Lhagao Theng thahatna dimset pumin akiletai. Ama chung chang thulhut ho agamkai pumpi ah gangtah in akithejal soh tai. 
Akikhopnau in tin'ah thu ahil jin mijousen apahcha lheh e. 
Ama Nazareth kho, ahung seiletna khoa ahung chun, abolngai jing bangin Sabbath nin akikhopnau in'ah alut in Pathen Lekhabu sim dingin adingdoh in ahi. 
Isaiah themgao lekhajol chu apeuvin ahi. Aman lekhajol chu alhamlhan hitia kijihna mun chu amudoh tai: 
“Pakai Lhagao kachungah aum'e, ajeh chu mivaicha ho dinga Kipana Thupha podingin thao einui, Aman sohchang ho akilhadoh diu thu phongjal din eisol'e—mitchon kho amu ding, suhnem a umho kijalen sah ding ahiuve. 
Chuleh Pakai khotona phat ahunglhung tai,” kiti lai chu asimdoh tai. 
Lekhajol chu anungjel in, lhachale pa khut ah anungpeh in, atoutai. Kikhopna in'a mijousen ama chu mitlha louvin aveuvin, 
chuin aman amaho jah a thu aseipan tai. “Pathen Lekhabua kisun tua najah u chu tuni tah hin aguilhung tai!” ati. 
Mijousen aseipha lheh jengun chule akamsunga konna aphondoh lungsetna thu ho chu kidang asauvin ahi. “Hiche hi iti ham?” tin ama hon adongun, “Hiche hi Joseph chapa hilou ham?” atiuve. 
Chuin aman aseijin, “Nanghon lung opkai loubeh a hiche thuchih hi nei seikhum tei diu ahi: 'Louthem, nangma kisudam in'–aledohna, ‘Capernaum a nabol tobang thil kidang ho chu hilai nakhomun'a jong bol'in’” tiuvin nate. 
Ahivanga thutah chu ka seipeh nahiuve, themgao koima ama gamsungah min apom ngaipoi.” 
“Tahbeh in Elijah phat laiyin Israel gamsungah ngaichat khoh tah nei meithai tamtah anaum'e, vankot kum thum le akeh akikha a gamsunga kel nasatah ana lhun chun. 
Ahivangin Elijah chu koima kom'ah ana kisol poi. Gamchom mi kom joh a sol anahi–Sidon gam'a Zarephath khoa meithai nu kom. 
Chule themgao Elisha phat laiyin jong Israel gamsungah miphah tampi ana um'in, ahivangin Syria mi Naaman bouseh suhdamin anaum'e,” ati. 
Hiche thu ajah phat un, kikhopna in'a umho chu alunghang tauvin ahi. 
Achomdoh un, aman'un akhochen nau kol mong lama chun apui tauve. Hiche kol'a chu sotlhah ding atiu ahi, 
ahivangin akikahlah uva akihehdoh in akiva mang tai. 
Chuin Jesu Capernaum kiti Galilee gam kho khat ah achen, chukom mun'a chun choldo nikho seh le akikhopnau in'a chun thu ahil ji'e. 
Hikom'a jong chun mihon athuhil chu kidang asalheh un ahi, ajeh chu aman thuneitah a asei jeh ahi. 
Khatvei akikhopnau in'a aumpet in, thilhavop khat–lhagao boh in avop khat–Jesu henga chun ahung pengjah jengin, 
“Che mangin! Ipi jeh a kalah uva hung kikumlut nahim, Nazereth Jesu? Keiho sumang dinga hung'ah nahim? Keiman nangma koi nahi kahenai–Pathen Mithengpa!” atin ahi. 
Jesun athusei alatan in, “Thip in! ?Hiche mipa a kon chun hung potdoh in,” tin thupeh aneitan ahi. Chutah chun thilha chun mipa chu lhonglaiyah ahin seplhun, mihonpin avetjing lai chun; chuin ama a kon chun ima bolsebe talouvin ahung potdoh tan ahi. 
Kidang san mipi ho chun aphongdoh un, “Ipi thuneina le thilbol theina hiche mipa hin anei hitam! Lhagaoboh jeng jong athun anungin, athupeh a kon'in ajam uve!” atiuvin ahi. 
Chuche gamkai pumpi a chun Jesu thu chu khotin'ah akithang tan ahi. 
Chuche nikhoa akikhopnau in adalhah jouvin, Jesu Simon in'ah achen, hikom'a chun Simon nupanu khosihpi jeh a nasatah a dammo agatoh khan ahi. “Lungset in sudam teiyin,” tin mijousen athum tauve. 
A-jalkhun kom'ah adingin, akhosih chu aphoh thip tan ahileh akhosih chun amanu adalhatai. Chuin amanu athoudoh pai ngallin amaho ding anneh sem in apangtai. 
Nisa alhum toh lhon'in, chuche nilhah chun, khosung pumpia a-insungmi damlou neiho jousen Jesu hengah ahin puiyun ahi. Itobang natna hijongleh akhut in atohkhah chan chu suhdamin aum tauve. 
Mi tamtah chu thilhavop ahiuvin; chule thilha ho chu athupeh a kon'in ahung potdoh un, apengjah un, “Nangma Pathen Chapa nahi,” atiuvin ahi. Ahivangin ama Messiah ahi ahetjeh u chun, aman amaho chu aphohthip in thu aseidoh sah tapoi. 
Ajing jingkah matah in Jesu munthip lah khat ah achedoh tan ahi. Mihonpi chun ama chu muntin aholle un, achaina kheh a amu phat uvin, aman amaho adalhah nalou diuvin atao tauve. 
Ahivangin aman adonbut in, “Keiman Pathen Lenggam Kipana Thupha hi khodang'a jong kaseiphong ding ahi, ajeh chu hiche dinga chu eikisol ahi,” ati. 
Chuti chun akholjin abanjop in, Judea gam pumpia akikhop nau in tin'ah thu ahillhang len ahi.
