Luke.
6.
Cholngahni khat Jesun bulei lai ajot lai hin, aseijui ten changvui asihtan un, akhut uvah anoiyun, chang chu aneuvin ahi. 
Ahivangin Pharisee ho phabep in, “Ipi jeh a nang hon dan na suhkeh uva Cholngah nia bolngailou changga nalo lhah uham?” atiuve. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Pathen Lekhabua kisun David le aloiten agilkel pettah uva ipi anabol'u nasimdoh khah lou u ham? 
Ama Pathen in'a alut in, dan asukeh in, thempu ho bouseh in anehthei changlhah theng chu anen ahi. Akiloipi ho jong phabep khat apen ahi.” ati. 
Chule Jesun aseiben, “Mihem Chapa hi Cholngahni chung jenga jong Pakai ahi” ati. 
Cholngah nikho dang khat a Jesun kikhopna in'a thu ahil laiyin mikhat akhut jetlang ngoi khat aumin ahi. 
Houdan thuhil ho le Pharisee hon Jesu chu avelhi keuvun ahi. Hiche pa chu asuhdam poupouleh Cholngah nin na atonge tia ngohna nei ding atiu ahi. 
Ahivangin Jesun alunggel'u ahen ahi. Akhut ngoipa jah a chun, “Hungin lang mijouse masangah hung ding tem'in,” tin akou tai. Hijeh chun mipa jong chu malangah ahungin ahi. 
Chuin Jesun anelkal ho jah a, “Ken nangho thudoh khat kaneiye. Danthun Cholngah nia thilpha bol aphal'am, ahilouleh thilse bolna ding nikho ham? Hiche nikho hi hinkho huhhingna ding nikho hai ahilou le suhsetna ding nikho ham?” ati. 
Aman amaho chu khat khat in ahin golvet vet in chule mipa jah a chun aseitai, “Nakhut lhaangdoh in” ati. Chuti chun mipa chun akhut alhaangdoh in ahile thahsem phat ahitai. 
Hiche a hin Jesu galmi ho chu alunghang un ama chu ilo ding ham ti akihou khom tauve. 
Chuin Jesun aseijui te henglam ngan aseitai, “Avaichate Pathen'in phatthei naboh uhen, Ijeh inem itile Pathen Lenggam chu nanghoa ahi. 
Tua gilkel te Pathen'in phatthei naboh uhen, ijeh inem itile lungnachima naum diu ahi. Tua kap te Pathen'in phatthei naboh uhen, ijeh inem itile phat khat leh nanui diu ahi” 
Phattheinan nangah uve, Mihem Chapa najui jeh uva mihon namuda uva napampai uva nataitom uva chule aphalouva nagaosap tengu leh. 
Hichu aso tengleh kipah un! Henge, kipah in kichom un! Ijeh inem itile tohphatman lentah in Van gam ah nangah uve. Chule geldoh jingun, apu apateu chun khanglui lai themgao ho jong chutobang chun anabol uve.” 
“Ipi lungkham nan nangau hitam nangho ahaote ho, ijeh inem itile nakipanau chu tua bou naneiyu ahitai. 
Ipi lungkham nan nangau hitam nangho tua thaochet a uma ninglhing te, ijeh inem itile gilkelna nasatah phat khat in na ngah jing uve. Ipi lungkham nan nangau hitam tua nui te, ijeh inem itile nanui nau chu kana le lunghem na kisoh ding ahi. 
Ipi lungkham nan nangau hitam nangho japin apachat ho, ijeh inem itile apu apateu jong chun themgao lhem ho ana pachat u anahi.” 
“Ahin, nangho thu ngainom ho, keiman kasei ahi, nagalmi teu ngailu'uvin! Namuda teu hengah thilpha bol'un.” 
Nasamse teu phattheiboh un. Nasukha teu dingin taovun. 
Mi khat touvin nabengphe langkhat abeh le, nabeng langkhat jong dopeh in. Khattouvin na ponlen alah got leh na sangkhol jong peh than. 
Athum chan chu pein; chule nathil nanga konna akilahdoh tengleh, nunglah ding gohih in. 
Min nang nabolna dinga nadei dan bang bangin midang kom'ah bol'in.” 
“Nangman na ngailuho bou na ngailut le, ipi jeh a naki thang-at ding ham? Michonse ten jong angailu teu angailut u ahi! 
Chuleh na chunga thilpha bol ho kom bouseh a thilpha nabol leh, ipi jeh a nakithang-at ding ham? Michonse ten jong hichan chu abol'u ahi! 
Chuleh nangman nahin sajou ding ho kom'a bou sum naphal leh, ipi jeh a nang kithang-at ding nahim? Michonse ten jong michonse te kom ma'a abon'a nungmu dinga aphal thei ahi.” 
“Na gal miteu ngailu uvin! Amaho kom'ah thilpha bol'un. Nungmu ding kinem louvin phal'un. Chuteng van'a kon natohphat man'u chu len ding ahi, chule Chungnung pen chate umchan tahtah natho diu ahi, ajeh chu ama hi kipaje helou ho leh migilou ho dinga pha ahi. 
Napau mikhoto them ahi banga nangho jong khoto them nahi diu ahi.” 
“Midang chungthu tan hih in chule nang chungthu jong kitan louding ahi. Midang demna thu seihih in, achutiloule na chunga hunglhung ding ahi. Midang ho ngaidam in nang jong naki ngaidam ding ahi. 
Pein, chutileh namu ding ahi. Nathilpeh chu aboncha a nangma kom hung kile ding ahi–akison chahkheh, lo chahkheh sa, mun a-konna dinga, letset a chule naphei chunga kisunglha ding ahi. Napehdoh jat chun namu ding jat aphotchen ding ahi,” ati. 
Chuin Jesun thutekah hicheng hi aseiye: “Mitcho khat chun midang kaihoi theiyin tem? Anilhon'a kokhuh a lhalut lhon talou ding ham?” 
Jillai ho ajilkungpau sangin alenjo poi. Amavang jillai khat bulhingset a kichuh chu ajilkungpa tobang hi ding ahi.” 
“Chuleh ibol'a naloi nagol mit a bohhal um chu na lunggimpi ham nangma mit a inkam uma chu?” 
Nangma mit a inkam kimu louva, 'Jol, namit a bohhal um chu nalahdoh peh inge,' tia seiding iti nagel theiyu ham? Miphalhem, amasan nangma mit a inkam chu kilahdoh masat in, chutileh nagol mit a bohhal um chu abol ding dan nahet ding ahi.” 
“Thingphung pha khat in ga phalou asodoh theipoi, chule thingphung phalouvin ga pha asodoh ngaipoi. 
Thingphung khat chu agan akihen ahi. Theichangga lingphung hoa kon'in akilou khapoi, chule lengpiga jong lingboh ah akilou poi. 
Miphan alungsung thil kholna pha a kon'in thilpha asodoh in, chule migilouvin alungsung thil kholna phalouva kon'in thilse asodoh e. Nasei chu ipi hileh na lungsunga hung kondoh ahi.” 
“Hijeh a chu ibol'a ‘Pakai, Pakai!’ tia neikou jing uham, kasei bon bollou uva? 
Keiman koiham khat kaheng'a hunga, ka thuhil ngaiya, chule eijui ho chu itobang hidiu ham navetsah unge. 
Ama chu mikhat inkhat sa a abulphu ding thuhtah a laiya akhombul song chunga phut tobang ahi. Twi ahungsoa in chu ahin nuh jongleh dettah a kisa ahijeh chun adingdet kheh in ahi. 
Amavang koi hileh athu jaa chule nunglou ho chu mikhat akhombul umlouva insa tobang ahi. Twipi ahungsoa hasuh a asuh le alhupai jitai.” ati.
