Luke.
7.
Jesun hiche ho jouse miho kom'a aseijou phat chun, Capernaum langah akile tai. 
Hiche lai chun Rome sepai jalamkai khat dinga asoh sahlut tah khat chu hatah in adammon thi ding kon cha ahitai. 
Sepai jalamkai pan Jesu chung chang ajahdoh phat in, aman ja umtah Juda upaho asolin, ahinkou uva asohpa ahung damsah din asol'e. 
Hijeh chun ama hon jong ngehkhoh tah in Jesu chun mipa akithopi din atao tauve. “Napanpina chang dinga lom khattou aum leh, ama hi ahi,” atiuvin ahi 
“Ajeh chu aman Juda miho eilungset un kikhopna in jong eisah peh uve,” atiuve. 
Hijeh chun Jesu jong amaho toh alhon'in achetai. Ahivangin in ahunglhun masangjep un, sepai jalamkai pan loi phabep asol'in hitin agasei sah e, “Pakai, ka-in'a hung dingin nang le nang kisugim hih in, ajeh chu keima chutobang loma jana chang dinga kilom kahipoi.” 
Keima hungdoh a nangma toh kimuto dinga jong lhingjou lou kahi. Naum na'a kon chun thu chu seijengin, kasohpa suhdam'a um ding ahi. 
Ken hiche hi kahenai, ajeh chu keima jong kachunga ho thunoiya um kahin, chule kenjong ka sepai ho chunga thuneina kanei ahi. Keiman “Chen” ti bouseh kasei le acheuvin, “Hungin” katileh ahung uve. Chule ka sohte kom'ah “Hiche bol'in kati leh abol jeng uve,” atiuve. 
Jesun hiche thu ajah doh phat in, atija lheh jenge. Mihonpi anungjui ho heng lam angan hitin aseiye, “Ka seipeh nahiuve, hitobang tahsanna hi Israel pumpi sungah kamukha poi!” ati. 
Chuin sepai jalamkaipa loiho chu a'inah akile tauvin ahileh sohpa damthengsel'a anaum chu amu uvin ahi. 
Chomkhat jouvin Jesu aseijui ho toh Nain kho langah acheuvin, mihonpi tamtah in ajui uve. 
Thivui dinga hung kijotdoh honkhat khopi kelkot phunga chun atohkha uvin ahi. Khangdong athipa chu meithainu chapa changkhat seh ahin, chule khosunga pat mihonpi tamtah in amanu aumpiu ahi. 
Pakaijin amanu amu chun, alungsung khotonan adimtan ahi. “Kap hih in!” tin aga lhamon e. 
Chuin thikong lama chun achen atongkhan ahile apoho adingden'un ahi. “Gollhang” atin “Ka seipeh nahi, thoudoh in” ati. 
Chuin athiah pasal chapang chu ahung toudoh in thu aseipan tai! Chule Jesun anu anungpeh tan ahi. 
Kichatna lentah in mihonpi chu alonvuh tai, chule ama hon Pathen avahchoiyun, “Thil nasatah bolthei themgao khat ilah uvah akisepdoh tai,” atiuvin chule “Pathen'in tunin amite aviltai,” atiuve. 
Chule Jesu chung chang thusoh Judea gam pumpi le akimvel thinglhang gamkai ho'ah thechansoh in aumtai. 
Twilutsah John seijui hon Jesu thilbol jouse chu John aseipeh tauve. Hijeh chun John in aseijui te lah a mini akouvin, 
chule adong dingin Pakai kom'ah asoltai. “Nangma Messiah kanga jingu chu nahim, ahilouleh achom dang khat kavet nahlai diu ham?” agatisah e. 
John seijui tenin Jesu amu lhon phat in aseilhon tai, “Twilutsah John in hiche dong dinga eihin sollhon ahi, Nangma Messiah, kanga jing pau chu nahim, ahilouleh achom dang khat kavet jing nahlai diu ham?” atilhon e. 
Hiche pettah a chu Jesun mi tampi anatnau, adammona hou asuhdam pet, lhagao boh vop ho aboldam pet, chule khomulou ho kho amusah pet anahie. 
Jesun John seijui teni chu aseipeh in, “John hengah kile lhon'in lang nathilmu lhon le nathujah lhon chengse seipeh lhon tan–mitchon kho amun, elbaiyin lam ajot in, miphah ho jendam ahin, nangong'in kho ajan, mithiho kaithouvin aumin, chule Kipana Thupha hi mivaichate hengah seiphong'in aumtai.” 
Chule seipeh lhon'in, keima jeh a kiheimang louho chu Pathen'in phatthei aboh'e,” ati. 
John seijui tenin adalhah jouvin Jesun ama thudol mihonpi jah a chun aseijin ahi. “Itobang mi vedinga gamthip a chu nache uham? Ama chu huimut ling le pengdon anem ham? 
Ahiloule ponsil man tamtah a kivon mi mu dinga naki ginchat um? Ahipoi, von hoitah kivon ho leh nomsa tah a hing ho chu leng inpia bou akimui. 
Themgao ve nahiu ham? Henge, chule themgao sanga chungnung jo ahi. 
John chu Pathen Lekhabu in hitia aseipa chu ahi, 'Ven, Keiman na masangah ka thu lhangsam kasol'e, chule aman nalampi namasanga asemtup ding ahi.' 
Ka seipeh nahiuve, anahing jouse lah a, koima John sanga lenjo aumpoi. Ahin Pathen Lenggam a aneopen jeng jong chu ama sangin alenjon ahi!” ati. 
Hiche thu ajah tah un, mipi jouse–kaidong ho jengin jong–Pathen lampi chu adih ahi ti apom'un ahi, ajeh chu amaho John khut a baptize ana chang ahiuve. 
Amavang Pharisee ho leh hou danthu themhon Pathen thilgon apampaiyun ahi, ijeh inem itile ama hon John twilutsah chu apomlouvu ahi. 
“Tukhang mite hi ipi toh katekah tadem?” tin Jesun adongin “Iti kahilchet ding ham?” ati. 
Amaho hi kailhang lah a chapang kichem tobang ahiuve. Amaho aloi agol ho kom'ah aphunchel un, 'Keihon moupui golvah la kasauvin, nalam pouve' hijeh chun thivuina la joh kasauvin chule nakap pouve. 
Ajeh chu Twilutsah John chun aphat ho changlhah neh ahilou le lengpitwi don kiti ho hin asumang pon, chule nang hon, 'Thilhavop ahi' natiuve. 
Mihem Chapan, alangkhat ah, anen adonin, chule naseiyun, 'Milop jukham chule kaidong ho le chonse dang ho jol le gol!' natiuve. 
Ahinla chihna chu adih ahi ti anungjui ho hinkho'ah tahlangin aum'e, ati. 
Pharisee ho lah a khat in Jesu toh nilhah an nehkhom din angeh in, hijeh chun Jesu jong ama in'a chun achen an nedin atoutai. 
Chukom mun'a an aneh ding chu numei jonthanhoi a chonse khat in ahetphat in, amanun songval bom khat ah thao namtui man tamtah kikhum ahinchoiye. 
Chuin amanu chu adilsun Amapa kengmuh achun adilsun akaptai. Amitlhi akengmuh lamah avalhan, hichu asam in athehgo in ahi. Chuin akengmuh chu namtui athe in achop chuh chuh tan ahi. 
Pharisee mipa anne a koupa chun hichu amu phat in ama le ama akihoulim in, “Hiche mipa hi themgao ahipou pouleh, itobang numei ham athamkha nu hi, amanu hi michonse ahi! ti ahet tei ding ahi,” atin ahi. 
Chutah chun Jesun alunggel chu ahin donbut in, “Simon” tin Pharisee pa kom'a chun, “Thilkhat nakom'ah kaseinom'e” ati. “Sei jengin, houhil” tin Simon in adonbut paiyin ahi. 
Chuin Jesun hiche thusim hi aseipeh tai: “Mikhat in mini sum abatsah in–khatpa dangka ja-nga chule khat dangka som-nga. 
Ahin anigel lhon'in asajou lhon tapon, hijeh chun hepi na neitah in angaidam in abat lhon akahpeh tai. Chujou chun koijoh chun ama chu ngailu jon tem tia nagel em?” tin adongtai. 
Simon in adonbut in, “Keigel in abat tamjopa chu,” ati. “Hichu adih e” tin Jesun aseitai. 
Chuin numeinu heng lam ngan akiheiyin Simon jah a aseitai, “Hiche numeinu hichea dilsu nu hi ven. Keima na'in a kahung lut chun, nangman twi kakenga vutkhu sillhahna ding neipepon, ahivangin hiche nu hin amitlhi twiyin asop lhan, chule asam in atheh ngim in ahi.” 
Nangman chibai neiboh pon, ahin kahung lut a pat hichenu hin kakeng muh achop chop jong anga hih laiye. 
Nangman kalua olive thao nuding na gelkhoh pon, amanu vang hin kakeng phang thao namtuiyin anui.” 
“Ka seipeh nahi, amanu chonsetna ho–tamtah ahiuvin, ngaidam ahitai, hijeh a chu amanun ngailutna lentah eimusah ahi. Amavang mi akingaidam lhom chu akipah lhom in ahi” ati. 
Chuin Jesun numeinu jah a, “Na chonsetna ho kingaidam ahitai” ati. 
Dokhanga tou miho chu amaho le amaho akihouvun, “Hiche mipa hi koi hiya michonse jong angaidam ham?” atiuvin ahi. 
Chule Jesun numeinu jah a aseitai, “Na tahsanin na huhhing ahitai, lungmongin chetan,” ati.
