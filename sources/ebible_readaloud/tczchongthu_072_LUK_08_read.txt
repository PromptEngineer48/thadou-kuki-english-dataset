Luke.
8.
Chujouvin Jesu anai vellah kho ho vil dingin akipandoh in, Pathen Lenggam chung chang Kipana Thupha seiphong leh hillhang le akop in ahi. Aseijui som le ni ho jong akipuiyin, 
lhagao boh vop a kon le anatna kisudam numei phabep khat jong ahiuve. Amaho lah a chun Mary Magdalene, thilha sagi anodoh peh nu; 
Chuza jinu Joanna, Herod sumkolveina vesuipa; Susanna; chule adang mi tamtah Jesu le aseijui te anei agouva panpina hin tohdoh jing ho jong ahiuve. 
Nikhat Jesun thusim khat thulem'in mihonpi tamtah, kho chom chom'a kon hung kisutoa athusei ngai dinga hung ho kom'ah hitin aseiye: 
“Loubol mikhat amuchi tu dingin achedoh tai. Aloulai dungah athe len ahileh, muchi abang chu lamsih a achun, chuva chun chotphan aumin, chule vachaten anedoh tauve. 
Muchi adang songlah a achui. Ahung khang doh in, ahin akehdoh chu agoplha loiyin asunou ding alhomjeh in athidoh tai. 
Muchi adang chu linglah a achun, ahung kehdoh in chule ling chun adeplih jitai. 
Muchi adang khat kit chu leipha laiyah achui. Hiche muchi chu akhang in, ga ahin son ana kitu sangin ajat jakhat in agan ahi!” ati. Hiche asei jouvin ahung pengjah jengin, “Koi hileh angaina ding nakhang nei chanin angaiya chule ahetchet ding ahi,” ati. 
Aseijui hon hiche thulem hi ipi kiseina ham adongun ahi. 
Aman adonbut in, “Nanghon Pathen Lenggam thuguh nahet theina diuva phalpehna chang nahiuve. Amavang midang ho hilna a thulem kaman hi Pathen Lekhabua kisun guilhunna dinga ahi: 'Ahin vet tengu leh, amu tahtah lou diu ahi. Ajah tengu leh, ahet theilou diu ahi.'” 
“Hiche hi thulem kisei avetsahna chu ahi: Muchi chu Pathen thu ahi. 
Lamsih a chu muchin avetsah chu, thu kisei jaa ahivanga agiloupa hunga atahsan theina louding le asochat louna dinga alungsunga kon alahmang peh miho aseina ahi. 
Muchi songlah a chu hon avetsah chu, thu kisei jaa chule kipah tah a kisanta, ahivanga jung akhothuh loujeh uva phat chomkhat kah a bou tahsan, lhepna atotengu leh lhuji ho aseina ahi. 
Muchi linglah a chu hon avetsah chu, athu ja a ahivanga athujah chu leiset gimna haosatna chule nopsahna hon atomchah a khangdoh thei talou ho aseina ahi. Hijeh chun apilhing thei tapouvin ahi. 
Chule muchi leipha laiya chu chun milungtheng, lungthim pha, Pathen thu jaa nung ho, chule lungneng tah a ga tamtah sodoh ho aseina ahi.” 
“Koiman meivah avah in chule lheng'in akhun ahilou le jalkhun noiyah asel ngaipoi. Meivah chu atunna ah akitungin, chuva chun avah chu insunga lut jousen amuthei bouve. 
Ijeh inem itile akiselguh jouse lhangphonga hung kipodoh ding, chule akiphongdoh lou jouse meivah noiya kipodoh ding mijouse kihetsah ding ahi.” 
“Hijeh chun iti najah uvem chingtheiyun. Kathuhil ngai jouse, hetthem theina kipeh be ding. Amavang thungai louho chu, ahetsa dan'a agel hou jeng jong amahoa konna kilamang ding ahi.” ati. 
Chuin Jesu anu le asopiho ama toh kimu din ahungun, ahin mihonpi jeh chun ama kom nailut thei louvin aum tauve. 
Koiham khat in Jesu aseipeh in, “Nanu le nasopiho polangah aum uve, chule naki mutopi nom uve,” atipeh tai. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Kanu le kasopi ho chu koi hileh Pathen thu jabol a athua nungho chu ahiuve” ati. 
Nikhat Jesun aseijui te jah a aseijin, “Dil gal langkhat ah galkaiyu hite” ati. Hijeh chun kong sungah alut un akipatdoh tauve. 
Konga akitol laiyun, Jesu chomkhat kichol din a-imu'e. Ahin sotlou laiyin, huipi hattah twipi chungah chun anungsuh e. Kong chu twi adim tan, amaho setoh tei ding dinmun'ah achu tauve. 
Aseijui te acheuvin aga kaithou un, “Pipu, Pipu, alhalut ding ihitauve!” tin apengjah jeng tauve. Jesu athodoh phat in, huipi le twi kinonglai chu aphohthip tai. Chuin apettah in huipi athip'in ima jouse anomsel tai. 
Chuin amaho chu adongin, “Natahsan'u hoiba ham?” ati. Amaho akihoulhi chilchel'un, “Thupeh anei teng huipi le twi kinong jengin jong athu angaiye!” akitiuve. 
Hichun amaho Galilee apat in dil gallang, Gerasene gamkai ahunglhung tauve. 
Jesu konga konna apotdoh leh, mikhat thilhavop khat ahungin ahung kimupin ahi. Phat sottah amapa chu in beiya chule sagoh keova khopi pam lhanmol lah a cheng ji ahi. 
Jesu amu mu chun apeng'in chule amasanga alhu jengin ahi. Chuin ahung peng in, “Ipi jeh a kakom'a hung kikumlut nahim, Jesu, Chungnung pen Pathen Chapa? Lungset tah in, katao ahi, nei gimbol hih in,” ati. 
Ijeh inem itile Jesun hiche lhagao boh chu amapa a konna ahungdohna dinga ana thupehsa ahitai. Hiche lhagao boh hin mipa hi athunun ji ahi. Akimattup a thihkhaova akibultuh jeng jongleh, aman imalouva atumtan jia gammang lah adelji, apumpia thilha thaneina noiya um ahitai. 
Jesun adongin, “Namin ipi ham?” ati. “Janel” tin ahin donbut e, ajeh chu hiche mipa chu thilha tampin abop ahi. 
Thilha ho chun Jesun atosin bei onghom'a asol louna diuvin angeh tauve. 
Hiche mun'a chu voh hon tampi mollanga lha ahiuvin, chule thilha ho chun hiche vohchaho sunga alutna diu joh in athum'un ahi. Hijeh chun Jesun phalna apen, 
chuin thilha ho chu mipa a kon'in ahung potdoh'un vohchaho sungah alut uvin, chule vohcha honpi chu molchung akensuh apat chun alhaisuh un dillen a chun alhai lhauvin alhum lut gam tauve. 
Achinghon thilsoh chu amu phat un, anai vel'a khoho le akimvel thinglhang gam ho ah alhailut un thusoh chu athejal le tauve. 
Thilsoh umchan vedingin mipi ho ahung lhaidoh uve. Mihonpi chomloukah in Jesu hengah ahung kikhom un, chule hiche mipa thilha ho a konna kilha ongpa chu amu uvin ahi. Amapa chu Jesu kengbul'ah atouvin, von akivon'in, chule alimgeh tan, chule amaho chu abonun akicha tauvin ahi. 
Chuin thilsoh umchan muho chun adang ho jah a thilhavop pa chu iti suhdam'a um ham ti aseipeh un ahi. 
Chuin Gerasene gamkaiya mipi ho jousen Jesu hengah agam'u adalhah a achemang nadin angeh tauve, ajeh chu kichatna nasatah huinun chun alodim u ahitai. Hijeh chun Jesu jong kong sungah alelut in adalhatai, dil gallang khat legalkai kit dingin. 
Mipa thilha kinodoh peh pa chun ama toh lhonkhom din athum'in, ahin Jesun inlang joh a asol'in, hitin aseipeh e, 
“Ahipoi, na insung mite kom joh a chen lang, chule Pathen'in nadinga nabolpeh jouse hi gaseipeh tan,” ati. Hijeh chun amapa chu khopi dung lah a achen, Jesun thil loupi abolpeh ho aseiphong len ahi. 
Dil gallang a chun mihonpin Jesu ana lem'un ahi, ajeh chu ama hon ana nga jingu ahi. 
Chuin Jairus kiti akikhopnau in'a lamkai khat, ahungin Jesu kengbul'a abohkhup in a'ina akilhonpi din ahung tem'e. 
Achanu changkhat seh, kum som le ni bep chu, thiah ahi. Jesu amapa chutoh alhon khom'in ahileh ama chu mihonpi chun atom chahkheh jengun ahi. 
Mihonpi lah a chun numei khat kum som le ni jen thilong'a damlou aumin, amanun damna akimu jou lou ahi. 
Jesu nung lang'a pat ahungin, amanun Jesu ponmang athamtai. Apettah chun athilon atang hel tai. 
“Koiham ei tongkha?” tin Jesun ahin dongtai. Mijousen ahilounau aseiyun chuin Peter in, “Pipu, mihonpi hin nasuhkhah ahi,” ati. 
Ahivangin Jesun aseijin, “Koiham khat in thamkhah ding lungput neimonga eitham ahi, ijeh inem itile keiya konna suhdamna thaneina apot kahei,” ati. 
Numei nu chun ki-immang thei kahi tapoi ti ahet phat in, kithing pumin akengbul'ah adilsu tan ahi. Mihonpi chun ipi jeh a atohkhah ham chule adampai jeng thu ahilchet ho chu ajauvin ahi. 
“Chanu” tin amanu jah a chun, “Natahsan in nadamsah ahitai, lungmongin chetan” ati. 
Amanu kom'a thu aseijing laiyin, kikhopna in'a lamkai pa, Jairus, in'a kon thupole khat ahung vailhungin ahi. Aman amapa jah a aseipeh in, “Nachanu athitai. Tua houhil nasuhboi chu phachomlou ding ahitai,” ati. 
Ahin Jesun thilsoh ajah chun, Jairus jah a hitin aseiye, “Kicha hih'in. Tahsan poupouvin, amanu suhdam'a um ding ahi,” atipeh e. 
Amaho in ahunglhun phat un, Jesun Peter, John, le James, chule numei chapangnu nu le pa tailou midang koima toh alut ding anompoi. 
In chu mi tamtah akap le amaovah dimset a um ahi, ahin aman aseijin, “Kap tahih un! Athi ahipoi, a-imu ahi bouve,” atipeh e. 
Ahivangin mihonpi chun anuisat un, ajeh chu athitai tichu hoitah a ahet u ahi. 
Chuin Jesun akhut in atuh in chule aw sangtah in, “Ka chanu, thoudoh in!” tin aseitai. 
Chuin hiche pet tah chun amanu hinna chu ahung kilen, kinloitah in ahung dingdoh e! Chuin Jesun neh ding khattou apeh diuvin aseipeh tai. 
Anu le apa chu akipah val behseh jengin, ahin Jesun thilsoh chu koima kom'a seidoh lou din ahilchah e.
