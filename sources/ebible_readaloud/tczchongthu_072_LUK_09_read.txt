Luke.
9.
Nikhat Jesun aseijui som le ni ho chu akoukhom in, thilha ho jouse le natna jouse suhdamna thahatna le thilbol theina apen ahi. 
Chuin aman amaho chu Pathen Lenggam thudol mijouse seipeh ding leh adamlou te damsah dingin asoldoh e. 
“Najin na diuvah imacha kipoh hih un,” tin ahilchah e. “Tenggol ham, sakhao ham, anneh ding ham, sum ham ahilouleh von khel jeng jong kipoh hih un. 
Na chena chan uvah khopi na dalhah tokah uvin nalhun nau in a chun um'un. 
Chule khopi khat chun nangho lem ding adauva ahileh, na kengphang uva vutkhu ho thinglhauvin, hiche miho chu aso so kiso tauhen tia lhahdohna in,” ati. 
Hiti chun ama hon khotin'a gamchot apan'un, Kipana Thupha lhangsap chule ana damlou damsah toh abol uve. 
Herod Antipas, Galilee gamvaipopan Jesu natoh thilbol jouse ajahdoh phat in alungboi dentai. Mi konkhat chun Twilutsah John athilah a kon thoudoh kit ahi atiuve. 
Adang hon Jesu chu Elijah ahilouleh themgao ho lah a khat athia kon thoukit ahi atiuve. 
“Keiman la John kathat tan,” tin Herod in aseijin, “Ahileh hiche mipa, athusim kajah pa hi koiham?” ati. Chule ama chu mutei ding atup jing tan ahi. 
Seijui ho ahung kileu chun, Jesu hengah athilbol jouseu aseipeh tauve. Chuin ama amaho chutoh guhthim chan Bethsaida kho langah ache tauvin ahi. 
Ahivangin mihonpi chun hoiya ache aholdoh un chule ajui tauve. Aman amaho chu alem in, Pathen Lenggam thudol ahil'in, chule ana damlou ho asudam in ahi. 
Nilhangkai vaigei thim lang hin seijui som le ni akom'a ahungun asei tauve, “Mihonpi hi naivellah khoho le loujao dungah soldoh tan, anneh le jan gena ding akimu theina diuvin. Hikom munthip a hin neh ding imacha aumpoi,” ahung tiuve. 
Ahivangin Jesun aseijin, “Nanghon amaho vah un” ati. “Ahin keihon changlhah pheng nga le nga ni bouseh kaneiyuve,” tin ahin donbut un, “Ahilou le kacheuva neh ding mihonpi hijat dinga hi kagachoh diuva na ngaitom?” atiuve. 
Ajeh chu chukom'a chu mi pasal sang nga jen umah ahi. Jesun adonbut in, “Mi somnga nga bep a aloi loiya tou dingin seipeh un,” ati. 
Hijeh chun mipi abon'un atou tauve. 
Jesun changlhah pheng nga le nga ni chu alan, vanlam aven, phatthei abohtai. Chuin changlhah ho chu ahal hal'a asuhkeh toh kilhon'in, changlhah le nga chu aseijui te kom'ah ahin pedoh doh jengin, ama hon mipi ho ahoppeh uve. 
Chuin abon'un aduchan chan aneuvin, chule chomkhat jouvin seijui hon nehmoh paipeh som le ni alohdoh uve. 
Nikhat achangseh a tao dingin Jesun mihonpi adalhan ahi. Aseijui te bouseh toh um ahin, aman hitin adong tai, “Mihon kei hi koi ahi eiti uvem?” atileh, 
ama hon adonbut un, “Abangin Twilutsah John nahi atiuvin, abangin Elijah, chule adang hon malai themgaoho lah a khat thoukit nahi atiuve,” atipeh uve. 
Chuin aman amaho chu adong'in, “Nanghon la koi ahi neiti uvem?” atileh Peter in adonbut in, “Nangma Messiah, Pathen a kon hung kisol chu nahi,” ati. 
Jesun aseijui te koi ahi koima kom'a aseidoh louna diuvin agihsal'in ahi. 
“Mihem Chapa hin gim genthei tampi athoh ding,” tin ahin seiyin, “Ama chu upaho, thempuho, chule houdan thuhil hon ahin pampai diu ahi. Ama chu tha a um ding, amavang anithum nileh athilah a konna kaithouva um ding ahi” ati. 
Chuin mihonpi jah a chun aseijin, “Nangho koi tobang kanungjui nom naum uleh, nangma chang kingailutna a kon'in kiheidoh un, na thingpel niseh in kiput un, chule neijuiyun. 
Na hinkho na huhhing nom le nachan ding, amavang keima jal'a nakatdoh le nahuhhing ding ahi. 
Chuleh ipi phatchomna mun natem, vannoi pumpi neiyin lang nangma tah kisusen kisumang le chun? 
Khattouvin keima le ka thusei eijachatpi leh, Mihem Chapan jong ama chu aloupina a chule Pa le vantil thengte loupina a ahung kile tengleh ajachatpi ding ahi. 
Thutah ka seipeh nahiuve, tutua hikom'a ding ho lah a mi phabep in Pathen Lenggam amu tokah uva thina atolou diu ahi,” ati. 
Niget hamkhat jouvin Jesun Peter, John, le James ho cheng hi molchung khat ah tao dingin apuitouve. 
Chuin atao laiyin, amaiso ahung kikhel'in, chule avon'ah ho kidangtah in abangsel jengin ahi. 
Phulou helouvin mini, Mose le Elijah ahung kilah lhon'in Jesu toh akihoulim pan tauve. 
Amaho chu vet dingin aloupi lheh jengui. Chule amaho chu leiset a pat a apotdoh ding thu, Jerusalem a guilhung vah ding thun akihoulim un ahi. 
Peter le adang teni chu ihmu lhugam ahi tauve. Akhahdoh uchun, Jesu loupina chu amu uvin chule mini ama toh adinkhom chu amu uve. 
Mose le Elijah in adalhah kigot pan laiyuvin, Peter in, aseijeng jong helou khop in ahung eodoh in, “Pipu, hilai mun'a ium uhi anom'e! Liim munthum hetjing nan song uhite–khat nang ding, khat Mose ding, chule khat Elijah ding,” ahinti e. 
Ahin, hitia asei jeng vang chun, meilhang khat in alekhu tauvin, meilhangin atomkhum toh lhon'in kichat nan amaho alodim tauve. 
Chuin meibol lah a kon chun awgin khat in hitin ahin seiye, “Hiche hi Kachapa ahi, Kalhendoh pa ahi. Athu ngaiyun,” ati. 
Awgin athip chun Jesu chu achangseh anahitai. Hiche athilmu hou chu chuche phat lai chun koima kom'ah aseidoh pouve. 
Ajing'a molchunga pat ahung suh jou uchun, mihonpi tamtah in Jesu akimupi uve. 
Mihonpi lah a mikhat chun ahin kouvin, “Houhil, lungset in kachapa neivet peh in tia katao ahi, ajeh chu ama bouseh hi kacha neisun ahi. 
Lhagao gilouvin aman jin, phulouvin apengjah jin, chule akam ah achilphon alhajin ahi. Ahehlhujin achangin amoh dalhah jipoi. 
Na seijui te hengah einodoh pehna diuvin kathum'in hinlah anodoh jou pouve,” ahung ti. 
Jesun aseitai, “Nangho tahsan bei le midihlou ho! Nangho itih chan kaumpiuva kakhongai jing ding nahiu ham?” ati. Chuin mipa jah a chun, “Nachapa hikom'ah hinpuiyin,” ati. 
Chapang pa ahung machon jep laiyin, thilha chun tol'ah ahin sulhun, nasatah in aveingoi jenge. Ahivangin Jesun lhagao gilou chu aphoh thip in chapangpa chu asudam tai. Chuin aman amapa chu apa alepeh kittai. 
Chutobang thilsoh Pathen thilbol theina kidangtah amu uchun mipi chu atiu ajalheh jeng tai. Athilbol ho jouse mijousen adatmah laiyin, Jesun aseijui te jah a hitin aseiye, 
“Ngaiyuvin chule ka thusei hi geldoh jingun. Mihem Chapa hi agal mite khut a pehdoh a um ding ahi,” ati. 
Ahin ama hon ipi aseina ham ahethei pouve. Athupina chu amahoa konna kiselguh ahi, hijeh chun ahethei pouve, chule doh ding jong kicha kit ahiuve. 
Chuin aseijui te koi alenpen ham ti akinel salah lah jeng tauvin ahi. 
Ahin, Jesun alunggel u ahen, hijeh chun chapang neocha khat akom'a dingin apuiye. 
Chuin aman amaho jah a aseiye, “Koi hileh hitobang chapang neocha keima thalheng'a sang'a chun keima eisan ahi, chule keima eisanga chun eisol'ah kapa jong asan ahi. Nalah uva aneopen chu alenpen ahi,” ati. 
John in Jesu jah a, “Pipu, koiham khat in namin'a thilha anodoh kamu uvin, ahivangin eiho loilah a ajao loujeh in angah dingin ka seipeh tauve,” ahin ti. 
Ahivangin Jesun adonbut in, “Kham hih un, koi hileh na doudallou phot chu nanga ding ahi” ati. 
Van'a lahtouva aumna ding phat ahung naitoh lhon'in, Jesu Jerusalem jon din dettah a kigellha pumin achen ahi. 
Aman thupo leho Samaria khokhat ah, availhun na dinga akigot theina diuvin asol masan ahi. 
Ahivangin hiche khoa mipi ho chun Jesu chu asang tapouve, ajeh chu ama Jerusalem jona achejing lai ahi. 
James le John in hiche amudoh lhon phat in, amanin Jesu jah a, “Pakai, van'a konna amaho gouvam jeng ding mei ikou lhah diu ham?” atilhon e. 
Ahin Jesu akiheiyin amani chu aphoh in ahi. 
Hijeh chun amaho khochom khat ah ache tauve. 
ama hon chutia lam ajot laiyun, koiham khat in Jesu jah a, “Nache na chan'ah najui inge,” ati. 
Ahin Jesun adonbut in, “Si-al hon chenna ding ko aneiyun, vacha ten gena ding bu aneiyun, ahivangin Mihem Chapan alu ngapna ding jeng jong aneipoi,” atipeh e. 
Aman midang khat jah a, “Hungin, neijuijin” ati. Mipa chu anom in hinlah hitin aseiye, “Pakai, amasan inlam kileng ting kapa gavui masa tadinge,” ati. 
Ahivangin Jesun aseipeh in, “Lhagaova thi hon amithi hou kivuiyuhen! Nangma kin chu chele a Pathen Lenggam thudol seiphong chu ahi,” ati. 
Achom khat in aseijin, “Henge Pakai, keiman najui inge, ahin amasan ka insung mite toh gakivailhah tadinge,” ati. 
Ahin Jesun aseipeh in, “Koi hileh akhut a langkon tuhta chule nunglam ve chu Pathen Lenggam a dingin alhing poi,” ati.
