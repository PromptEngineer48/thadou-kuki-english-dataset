Luke.
12.
Hiche pet chun, mihonpi chu asang ajan ahung khang in akisucheh chuh un chule khat le khat kichonkha ton aumun, Jesun hichun aseijui te henglam ahin ngan agih sal in, “Pharisee ho chol a kon'in kihong phauvin—aphatlhem nauva kon'in. 
Phat ahung lhunge, thil akikhukhum jouse kiphongdoh ding ahi, chule aguha um jouse jong mijouse kom'a kihetsah ding ahi. 
Muthim'a nasei jouseu chu khovah a kija ding ahi, chule kot kikhakhum na a olcha nakiseipeh toujong chu inchunga mijouse jah a kisamphong ding ahi! 
“Ngailut umtah jol le gol te, natahsa that thei ho chu kicha hih un.” Hiche jou le chun na chungah ima abolthei tapouve. 
Ahin kichat ding koi ham naseipeh unge. Pathen kichauvin, aman nangho thana thei le damun'a lelutna thei thilbol theina tha anei ahi. Henge, kichat ding ama ahi .” 
“Vasah nga man ijat ham–paisa ni man ham? Ahin Pathen'in khat jeng jong asumil pon ahi. 
Chuleh nalu'a na samjang ho jeng jong aboncha kisimtoh soh ahitai. Hijeh chun kicha hih un; nangma vasah honpi jouse sanga manlujo nahi.” 
“Ka seipeh nahiuve, koi hileh hiche leiset a lhangphonga keima eiseidoh chan chu, Mihem Chapan jong Pathen Vantil te jaona a aseidoh ding ahi. 
Amavang koi hileh hiche leiset a eiseilep chan chu Pathen Vantil te masanga kiseilep diu ahi. 
Koi hileh Mihem Chapa douna thusei chan chu kingaidam ding ahi, amavang koi hileh Lhagao Theng taitom'ah vang chu kingaidam louding ahi.” 
“Chuleh kikhopna in'a vaipo ho leh thuneiho anga athu tan dinga nahin kai tengu leh, iti nakiven ding ham ahilou le ipi nasei ding thu ah lunggim hih un. 
Ajeh chu Lhagao Theng chun apettah a seiding napeh diu ahi,” ati. 
Chuin koiham khat in mipi lah a kon chun ahin kouvin, “Houhil, kasopipa jah a kapa nei le gou eikihoppina dingin seiyin,” ati. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Jol, kon nangni kah a thutan'a eipansah a, hiti khati iti kagel lhah ding ham?” ati. 
Chuin aman aseiben, “Chingtheiyun! Neipap kiloset kiti jouseah kivengun. Hinkho hi nanei jat a kisim ahipoi,” ati. 
Chuin aman thusim khat aseipeh tai: “Mihao khat hin loumun khat, alei phatah, ga hoitah tah sodoh aneijin ahi. 
Amapa achangin akihoulim in, ‘Ipi kabol ding hitam? Ka lousoh ga jouse koina ding mun la kanei pon,’ ati. 
Chuin aman aseijin, 'Kahetai! Ka changsal jouse phelha'ng ting asanga lenjo cheh thahsah inge. Chutileh keiman ka chang le mim jouse kholkhomna ding dan ninglhingset in nei tange.' 
Chutengleh keima nomtah a katouva keima le keiman hitia hi kasei ding ahi, “Kajol, nangman kum sottah a ding ninglhing set a kikhol khom naneiye. Tun lung olmo hih in! nen lang, don'in lang, chule kipah in!” tinge ati. 
“Ahivangin Pathen'in amapa jah a chun, 'Mingol! Tujan ma ma'a nathi ding ahitai. Chutengleh nanatoh ga jouse hi koi chang tantem?' ati. 
“Henge, lei gouthil bouseh kholkhom'a Pathen toh haosatah a kiguijopna neilou mi chu angol ahi” ati. 
Chuin, aseijui te kom lang ngan Jesun aseitai, “Hiche jeh a hi keiman nitin hinkho a dingin lunggim hih un kati ahi—neh le chah ninglhing set a chule sil le chen lhingset a kanei ding hinam tin. 
Ajeh chu hinkho hi anneh ding sanga lujo, chule natahsa jong sil le chen sanga lujo ahi. 
Va-ah ho khu veuvin, ama hon tula atu pouvin, at la a-at pouvin ahilou le khol la akholkhom pouvin, ajeh chu Pathen'in avahnai. Chule nangho vacha ho sanga ama dinga loupijo nahiuve! 
Nalunggim nau jousen nahinkho phatbih khat ajopbe theiyem? 
Chule lunggim nan imacha chutobang thil neocha jong alolhin sah theilou leh, thil lentah chunga jong lunggim chu ipi phachom ding ham?” 
“Lily pah ho le akhan doh dan khu veuvin. Ama hon na jong atong pouvin chule avon hou jong akisem thei pouvin, ahin, Solomon jeng jong, aloupina jouse a akivon chun amaho tobang lom'in ahoijou poi. 
Chuleh Pathen'in pahcha ho tunia uma jinga meilhum sunga selut ding bon kidangtah a akhohsah a ahileh, aman nangma thon louva na khohsah teitei ding ahi. Ipi bol'a hibanga tahsanna neo hi naneiyu ham?” 
“Chuleh ipi kaneh ding ham ipi kadon ding ham tin lungboi hih un. Hiche ho thil'a chun lunggim hih un. 
Hiche thil ho hin vannoi pumpia atahsanlou ho lungsung alodim'in ahi, ahivangin Napauvin na ngaichat chengseu chu ahemasa jitai. 
Pathen Lenggam chu thil jouse sangin hol'un, chule aman na ngaichat chan'u napeh diu ahi.” 
“Hijeh chun kicha hih un, hon lhom ho. Ajeh chu nangho lenggam peh ding hi napau dinga kipana lentah ahi.” 
“Nanei nagou ho joh un lang angaicha ho peuvin. Hichun van'a gou nakhol peh dingu ahi! Chule van gam a sumdip hon chultih anei pouvin chule atovong aum khapoi. Nagou khol chu bitkeiya um ding ahi; guchan jong aguhmang theilou ding ahi chule nget in jong asuhset theilou ding ahi. 
Nanei nagou umna'a chu nalung ngaichat jong aum ding ahi.” 
“Natohna dingin kivontup san umun chule na thaomei vah jing'un; 
na Pakai pa moupui golvah na a kon ahung kile ding nanga bangin. Chutileh ahung lhun'a kot ahinsuh tengleh kot nahonpeh a nalutsah theina dia phasadema naum ding ahi. 
Soh ho kiging jinga umho le ahung kile ding ngah jing ho chu kipaman kipe diu ahi. Thutah kaseipeh nahiuve, ama tah in amaho chu atousah ding, atai akikhi ding, amaho chu atou uva aneh laiyuva chu ajen ding ahi! 
Ama jankim lah ahilou le khovah kon'a hung maithei ahi. Ahin, ahung pet a kiging jinga um sohte chu kipaman apeh ding ahiuve.” 
“Hiche hi hethem in, in-neipan itih phatchet a gucha hung ding ham ti heleh, aman a-in chu veihonga aumsah louding ahi. 
Nangjong kiging jinga naum jing ding ahi ijeh inem itile Mihem Chapa chu naginchat lou pettah uva hung ding ahi,” ati. 
Peter in adongin, “Pakai hiche thutekah chu keihoa ding bou hai mijousea ding ham?” ati. 
Chuin Pakaijin adonbut in, “Soh kitah le lung limgeh chu apakai pan insung pumpi le sohdang ho chunga jong mopohna apeh a avahsah ho chu ahi. 
Apakai pa ahung kile a asohpan na phatah a atoh ahung mu leh, kipaman um ding ahi. 
Thutah ka seipeh nahiuve, hitobang soh chu apakai pan anei jouse chunga mopohna apeh ding ahi. 
Ahinlah, iti tantem asohpa chun, 'Kapakai pa hi tem'in hung ponte' tin gel henlang, asoh dang ho vohen, neh nom nehen, ju kham kham jeng taleh? 
Apakai pa chu seiphong masalou leh ginchat umlou pettah in hungin tin, chuteng aman asohpa chu asat goi goi ding mikitahlou ho toh asolmang tha ding ahitai.” 
“Chule soh khat apakai pa dei he'a, ahivanga kigongtup lou chule akihilchahna ho tongdoh lou chu, nasatah a bolgimna ato ding ahi. 
Ahivanga ahelou khattou chun thilse abol leh, ama chu gihsa louva kigimbol ding ahi. Koiham khat chu tamtah akipeh le, ama a konna chu tamtah kile ngeh ding ahi; chuleh khattou chu mopohna tamtah akipeh le, asanga tamjo chu kingeh ding ahi.” 
“Keima vannoi hi meiya tih dinga kahung ahi, chule akoudoh hitaleh kati lheh jenge! 
Keiman thoh hahsa tah baptize chan ding kamakho'ah kaneiye chule hichu akimolso kah a kalung khamna iti san tam! 
Leiset a chamna hinpo dinga hunga nei gel'um? Ahipoi, keima miho kibung khensah dia hung'ah kahi! 
Tu apat a insung ho kikhentel diu ahitai, thum'in eijop uva, nin eidoudal ding–ahilou le nin eijop a thum'in eidou ding ahi. 
'Mipa chu chapa douna a chule chapa chu mipa douna a kibungkhen ding ahi; chanu douna minu chule minu douna a chanu; chuleh ateh in mou douna chule mouvin ateh douna a kikhenna um ding ahi'” ati. 
Chuin Jesun mihonpi lam aven aseiye, “Nanghon meibol lhumlam langa ahung umdoh phat le, nasei jiuvin, ‘Go hung juding ahi’ natiuve. Chuleh nangho nadih uve. 
Lhanglam hui ahung nun tengleh, nasei jiuvin, ‘Tuni chu salheh ding ahi’ natiuvin, chule achuti ji'e. 
Nangho mingol ho! Nanghon leiset chunga van umje nasuto theiyun, ahin nang hon tuphat umdan na ledoh thei pouve.” 
“Ipi jeh a nangho dinga adih chu naki gellhah theilou uham? 
Nangho thukitanna mun'a nahehse hou toh nache tengu leh, amun nalhun masangin kihoutohpi ding gon. Achuti louleh thutan vaihompa masanga nahehse pan nakai ding, aman vaipopa henga napehdoh a, aman songkul'a nakhum ding ahi. 
Chule hichu aso leh nangman achainapen sum geiya napeh doh masang sea itinama jongleh nakilhadoh louhel ding ahi,” ati.
