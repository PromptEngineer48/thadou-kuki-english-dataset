Luke.
17.
Nikhat Jesun aseijui te jah a aseiye, “Chonsetna dia lhepna hi aum jing ding mong ahi, hinlah itobang lungkham nan anga hitam alhem'a pang mi chu! 
Na-ngongchanga sumhei song naki-o a twikhanglen'a nakilelut chu hiche aneocha ho nakipal lhuhsah sanga phachomjo ding ahi. 
Hijeh chun kivephauvin!” “Tahsan cha adang khat achonset leh, ama chu sosal'in; chutia alung ahei le, ngaidam in. 
Hiche mipa chun ihamtia nikhat a sagi vei nachunga thil dihlou abol'a, abol phat jousea jong ngaidam athum leh ngaidam in” ati. 
Seijuiten Pakai jah a aseiyun, “Tahsan na kangaichat be uve, iti kanei diu ham nei seipeh un” atiuve. 
Pakaijin adonbut in, “Nanghon tahsanna ankammu te cha hijongleh naneiyuleh, hiche thing theimi phung kom'a hi, ‘Kibotdoh in lang twikhanglen'ah kilehlut in’ tijongleu chun nathu u angai ding ahi!” 
“Soh khat chu aloukaina ham ahilou le akelngoi chinna ham'a pat a ahunglut teng, apakai pan, ‘Hunglut in an nekhom hite’ tin tem? 
Tiponte! Hiti joh hin seiyinte, ‘Ka anneh ding nei gonpeh in, natai kigeh inlang, kaneh sungsen neijen in. chuteng ajoujep le nenan nate’ ati ding ahi. 
Chuleh apakai pan asohpa chu atoh dinga lom atoh jeh a apachat em? Apahcha dehpoi. 
Chutobang a chu nang hon kathua nanun tengu leh hitia hi nasei diu, ‘Keiho soh lhingjoulou kanatoh diuva lom bou tong kahiuve’ nati diu ahi,” ati. 
Jesu Jerusalem lam jon'a achejom jing laiyin, Galilee le Samaria kigamgitna mun alhung tai. 
Chutia khokhat agalut laiyin, chua chun miphah som gamla thimtah in adingun, 
ahung eodoh un, “Jesu, Pipu, nei khoto un!” atiuve. 
Aman amaho chu aven, “Cheuvin lang thempuho gakimusah tauvin,” atipeh tai. Chutia acheuleh, aphah natnau chu suhtheng in aumtai. 
Alah uva khat chun suhdam a aum chu akimu phat in, Jesu hengah ahung kilen, “Pathen Vahchoiyin umhen!” tin apengjah tai. 
Ama Jesu kengbul'ah abohkhup in, athil bolpehna chungah kipathu aseijin ahi. Hiche mipa chu Samaria mi ahi. 
Jesun adongin, “Mi som kasuhdam hilou ham? Adang mi ko ho la? 
Koiman Pathen loupina peh ding ageldoh lou hitam hiche gamdang mipa tailou?” atin ahi. 
Chule Jesun hiche pa kom'a chun aseijin, “Dingdoh inlang chetan. Na tahsan in nasuhdam ahitai,” ati. 
Nikhat Pharisee hon Jesu adongun, “Pathen Lenggam itih le hunglhung ding ham?” atiuve. Jesun adonbut in, “Pathen Lenggam chu muthei melchihna ah akihedoh theipoi. 
Nanghon, ‘Hikom'ah aum'e!’ ahilou le ‘hichea khun aum'e!’ tin seithei pouvin nate. Ajeh chu Pathen Lenggam chu nalah uva aumtai” ati. 
Chuin aseijui te hengah hitin aseiye, “Phat ahung lhunge Mihem Chapa ahung kilena ding nikho munom'a naum diu, ahivanga namu lou diu ahi. 
Mihon ahinsei diu, 'Veuvin, Mihem Chapa khukom'ah aum'e' ahilouleh 'hivah aum'e,' ati diu ahi, ahivangin potdoh hih un, chule jui hih un. 
Ijeh inem itile kolphe ahung phet a van mong langkhat apat mong langkhat gei asalvah soh banga Mihem Chapa hung ni jong chutobang hi ding ahi. 
Ahin amasa a Mihem Chapa hin thoh hahsa tampi athoh a hiche khang miten apampai diu ahi.” 
“Mihem Chapa ahung kile tengleh, Noah nikholai toh bang ding ahi. 
Chuche nikholai chun mihon golvah neh le don chule kichen abol'un, Noah kongsunga alut a chule twisang ahunglet a abonchauva asuhmang nikho geiyin nomsatah in hinkho amangun ahi.” 
“Chule vannoi leiset jong Lot nikho lai toh bang ding ahi. Miho nitin akimanchah nauvah akisaboi un–neh le don, choh le joh, loulho le insah, 
Lot in Sodom khopi adalhah ni jingkah chan in abol'un ahi. Chuin van'a kon'in mei le kath ahung julhan abonchauvin asumang soh tan ahi. 
Henge, 'angaima banga' kiseimona Mihem Chapa ahung kile kit ni geiya um ding ahi. 
Chuche nikho chuleh indan chunga um chun insunga athil kisemtup din lutkit dahen. Mi louva kondoh jong inlam a kile dahen. 
Lot inneipi chunga ipi anasoh em geldoh un! 
Nahinkho tuhchah jing nagot le namansah ding, chule nahinkho nachesah le, nahuhhing joh ding ahi. 
Chuche jan leh mini jalkhun khat a lumkhom ding; khat kila ding, khatpa kidalha ding. 
Nupi nin sumhei khat a chang agoikhom lhon ding, khat kila ding khat kidalha ding, 
lou mun'a um teni jong khat kida lhaa khat kila ding ahi, ati. 
“Pakai, hitiho hi itih leh soh ding ham?” tin aseijui ten adongun ahi. Jesun adonbut in, “Muthong ho kititna achun mithi long naivel ah aum'e ti aphotchet banga hiche melchihna ho jong chun akichaina anaitai ti avetsah ahi,” ati.
