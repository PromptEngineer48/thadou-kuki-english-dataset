Luke.
18.
Nikhat Jesun aseijui te atao jingna diu leh alhahdai louna diu vetsahnan thusim khat aseipeh e. 
“Khopi khat a hin thutan khat Pathen ginglou chule miho khohsahna jong neilou khat aumin ahi. 
Chuche khopia meithai khat chu kinong chel'in ahung jingin, ‘Ka galmiho toh kakiboi nau hi thu adih in nei tanpeh in,’ ahung tijin ahi. 
Thutan pan phat chomkhat amanu chu isahlou tah in ana koiyin, ahin achainakeiyin hitin akihoulim in, 'Keiman Pathen kaging pon ahilou le miho jong holeh tilou kahi, 
ahivangin hiche numei nu hin eisungol lhatai. Keiman thu adih a tanlhah peh ahina din vetpeh inge. Ajeh chu amanu hin athilthum jinga kon hin ei sulungvai lhatai,'” ati. 
Chuin Pakaijin aseijin, “Hiche chondihlou thutanpa a kon'in kihil un.” 
“Ama jengin jong achaina a thu adih a tan dia ahin gellhah ahi. Hijeh a chu Pathen'in alhenchom te, ama kom'a asun ajan'a kapjing te chu thu adih a atanpeh ding ahi ti nagel lou u ham? Amaho chu donlouvin koijingin te natim? 
Ka seipeh nahiuve, aman kinloitah a thu adih a atanpeh ding ahi! Ahinlah Mihem Chapa hung kit teng, leiset chunga hin mi ijat tahsan nei mudoh intem?” ati. 
Chuin Jesun hiche thusim hi mi konkhat ama chonphatna a kisonga midang musit ho dingin aseiye: 
“Mi ni Hou-In ah taona mangin ache lhon'e. Khat chu Pharisee mi ahin, chule adang khatpa chu musit umtah kaidong ahi. 
Pharisee mipa chu adingin hitin ataove: ‘Kakipah'e Pathen, adang ho banga chonse kahilou jeh in. Ajeh chu lepthol in kabol pon, kachonse pon, chule jonthanhoi jong kabolpoi. Keima atahbeh in khuche kaidongpa tobang kahipoi! 
Keiman hapta khat in nivei an kangol'in, kasum mu jat a kon'in som’a khat kapei,’” ati. 
“Ahivangin kaidong pa chu gamla tah a adingin chule amit jeng'a jong vanlam ve ngam louvin ataove. Chusang chun lungkham tah in aman a-op achum'in hitin aseiye, ‘O Pathen, neilungset in, ijeh inem itile chonse kahi bouve,' ati. 
Ka seipeh nahiuve, Pharisee pa hilou, hiche chonsepa chu Pathen ang-ah them achangin inlam akiletai. Ijeh inem itile ama kichoisang ho chu suhnem'a um ding, chule akineosah ho chu dopsang'a um diu ahi,” ati. 
Nikhat nu le pa phabep in achateu chapang neocha chaho chu Jesun atham'a phatthei aboh dingin ahin puiyun ahi. Ahivangin aseijui ten hichu amu phat un, anul e apateu kom'a chun ama nasuboi bep uve tin ahouse uvin ahi. 
Chuin Jesun chapang ho chu akouvin chule aseijui te hengah aseitai, “Chapang ho chu kaheng'ah hung uhen. Jahda hih un! Ajeh chu Pathen Lenggam chu hiche chapang neocha tobanghoa ding bou ahi. 
Thutah ka seipeh nahiuve, koi hileh chapang neocha banga Pathen Lenggam sanglou chu lutlouhel ding ahi,” ati. 
Khatvei hou lamkai khat hin hiche thudoh hi adonge: “Houhil pha, tonsot hinna kanei theina dinga ipi kabol ding ham?” ati. 
“Ipi bol'a apha tia nei kou ham?” tin aledoh in, “Pathen bouseh dihtah a pha ahi. 
Ahin na thudoh donbut nan, thupeh ho nahet chula: “Nangman jon bol hih in, tolthat hih in, gucha hih in, jouvin mi hehse hih in, nanu napa jabol in,” ati. 
Mipan adonbut in, “Hiche thupeh ho jouse hi kachapan laiya pat kajui sohkei ahitai,” ati. 
Jesun adonbutna thu ajah phat chun, hitin aseitai, “Nabol loulai thil khat aum'e. Nanei jouse joh in lang hiche sum chu mivaicha ho pen, chutileh van'a gou nanei ding ahi. Chuteng hungin, neijuijin,” ati. 
Ahivangin mipa chun hichu ajahdoh phat in alungkham lheh tan ahi, ijeh inem itile ama chu haosa tah ahi. 
Jesun hichu amu phat in, aseitai, “Mihaosa Pathen Lenggam alut ding hi iti hah hitam! 
“Atahbeh in, heobil hom'a sangongsao alut galkai ding hi mihaosa Pathen Lenggam a alut ding sangin abaijoi!” ati. 
Hiche akisei jajouse chun aseiyun, “Achutileh vannoi leiset a koi ham huhhingna chang jou ding?” atiuve. 
Aman adonbut in, “Mihem dinga hithei lou chu Pathen dinga hithei ahi,” ati. 
Peter in aseijin, “Keihon nangma jui dingin ka-in'u kadalha uve,” ati. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Henge” chuleh ka lhamon nahiuve, “Koi hileh Pathen Lenggam jal'a a'in ham, ajinu ham, asopiho ham, ahilou le anu apa ham achate ham pedoh chan chu, 
tamtah vei tu hinkhoa hi lepeh a um diu chule ahunglhung ding khang'a jong tonsot hinkemlou amu ding'u ahi,” ati. 
Seijui som le ni ho atumbeh'in apuidoh in, Jesun ajah uva aseiye, “Ngaiyun, eiho Jerusalem chetou ding ihiuve, Mihem Chapa chung chang thudol'a themgao thusei jouse guilhun na ding mun chu ahi. 
Ama chu Rome te khut a pehdoh a um ding ahi, chule ama chu tot thanghoia um ding, jachat umtah a kibol ding, chil kiset khum ding ahi. 
Ama hon avoh uva chule atha diu ahi, ahivanga anithum nileh thoukit ding ahi,” ati. 
Ahin, ama hon hitiho thu hi khatcha ahethei pouve. Athusei ho athupije chu amaho a konna ki immang ahi, hijeh a chu athusei chu amattoh theilou u ahitai. 
Jesun Jericho anaisuh lai hin, mitcho khutdo khat lamsih ah ana touve. 
Aman mihonpi kijot khuto gin ajah phat in, ipi thu hitam ti adongin ahi. 
Nazereth Jesun hikom chu ahopa ahi ti aseipeh tauve. 
Hijeh chun amapa ahung eojah tan, “Jesu, David chapa, neilungset in!” ahintin ahi. 
“Thip in” tin masang langa miho chun ahutot tauvin ahi. Ahivangin ama awgingjep in ahapen cheh tan, “David Chapa, neilungset in!” ati. 
Jesun ajahdoh phat in, adingin mipa chu ahin puilut diuvin athupeh tai. Mipa ahung nai toh lhon'in Jesun adongin, 
“Ipi nabolpeh leng natim?” tin adong tai. Aman, “Pakai, kho muleng kati!” ati. 
Chuin Jesun aseijin, “Aphai, kho mutan! Natahsan in na damsah ahitai,” ati. 
Apettah chun mipa chun kho amutai chuleh ama pan Jesu nung ajuijin Pathen avahchoi tai. Chuleh amu jousen jong Pathen avahchoi cheh tauve.
