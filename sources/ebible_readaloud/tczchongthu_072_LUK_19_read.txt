Luke.
19.
Jesu Jericho alut in, chule akhopi sung chu ajot in ahi. 
Mi khat amin Zacchaeus kiti chua chun aum'e. Ama chu hiche lhang'a dinga kaidong pipu ahin, ahaova haosa ahitai. 
Aman Jesu chu mutei ding agon, hinlah ama anem behseh jeh chun mihonpi lah a chun amu theipoi. 
Hijeh chun ana malhaijel'in lampi panga theiba phung khat ah anakal'e, ajeh chu Jesun hiche lampi chu aho ding ahi. 
Jesu ahung phat chun, Zacchaeus chu avetouvin chule amin'in akouvin, “Zacchaeus! Gang'in, hungkum'in! tunia na in'a maljin kahi ding ahi,” ahinti. 
Zacchaeus jong kinloitah in ahung kumlhan, Jesu chu a'in ah kipah le thanom tah in apuilut tai. 
Ahivangin miho chu alungphamo uvin, “Michonse chungnung in'ah agalhung taget e,” tin aphunchel tauve. 
Chuphat chun, Zacchaeus jong Pakai angsung'ah adingdoh in aseitai, “Pakai, kanei kagou akehkhat mivaicha ho peing'e, chule kai kadon na a miho lepthol'a kabol aum leh, amaho chu ajatli'n nungpeh inge!” ati. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Tunin hiche in'ah huhhingna alhung tai, ajeh chu hiche mipa hin Abraham chapa dihtah ahi akimusah tai. 
Ijeh inem itile Mihem Chapa chu amangsa holdoh le huhhing dinga hung'ah ahi,” ati. 
Mihonpi chun Jesu thusei jouse chu angailha sohkeiyun ahi. Chule ama Jerusalem anaitoh kilhon'in, aman thusim khat, Pathen Lenggam chu kipan pai dinga angaito'u suhdihna din aseiye. 
Aman hitin aseiye, “Leng chapa te khat mun gamla tah a leng gachanga hung kile kit dingin koudoh in aumin ahi. 
Achedoh masangin asohte lah a mi som akoukhom in dangka min'a som apen, ‘kachedoh sungin hiche hi nei veikan peh un’ ati. 
Ahivangin amiten avetnom pouvin, achedoh nungin palai asol'un, ‘Ama hi ka lengpau dingin kadei pouve’ tin agasei sah uve.” 
“Leng ahung chan jouvin, ahung kilen chule asohte sum apeh sese chu akoukhom tai. Aman aveikan jat u chu ahetnom ahi. 
Soh masapan thu ahin lhut in, ‘Pipu, keiman nasum kaveikan in, neipeh chu ajat som in apunge!’” ati. 
“Lengpan adonbut in, ‘Nabol pha e!’ ati. ‘Nangma sohpha nahi. Nangma themcha kapeh chunga kitah nahi, hijeh a chu natohphat kipaman'a khopi som chunga gamvaipo nahi ding ahi,’ atipeh e.” 
“Soh anina pan thu ahin lhut kit in, ‘Pipu, nasum kaveikan in neipeh chu ajat ngan kapunsah be e’ ati. 
“‘Nabol aphai!’ tin lengpan adonbut in, ‘Nangma khopi nga chunga vaipo nahi ding ahi’” ati. 
“Ahivangin soh athumna pan sum amu mama chu ahinchoiyin aseitai, ‘Pipu, keiman nasum chu kasel'in kana koitup peh e. 
Ka kichat nahi ajeh chu nangma mikikhel nahin, nanga hilou jong nakilah in, natu lou jong aga naki chomkhomji e.’” ati. 
“‘Nangma soh gilou!’ tin lengpa ahung eojah in, ‘Nathu'n themmo nachan ahi. Nangman mikikhel kahi nahet a, keiya hilou kakilah theiya, katulou jong ka-at thei leh, 
ipi dinga sumkoina in'a kasum chu nanakoi louham? Ken athoi themkhat beh kikimu tadinga,’” ati. 
“Chuin akom avel'a ding ho hengah akiheiyin, hitin thupeh aneitai, ‘Hiche sohpa a kon chun sum chu lahpeh unlang min'a som neipa chu petauvin’” ati. 
“‘Ahin, pipu, aman som aneitai!’ tin ahinsei yuve.” 
“Lengpan adonbut in, 'ahinai,' 'akipe mangthem ho chu, kipeh be ding. Amavang imacha bollou ho kom'a pat, anei sun'u themcha jong chu kilahmang peh ding ahiuve. 
Chuleh hiche kagalmi ho, lenga kapan ding deilou ho chu–hin puilut unlang ka-angsung tah a hin that doh tauvin,'” ati. 
Hiche thusim asei jouvin, Jesu Jerusalem lam jon'in, aseijui te sangin ana masan ahi. 
Ama Bethphage le Bethany khopiho Olive molchung lang ahung gei phat in, aseijui mini asol masan, 
“Hiche kho a khun galut lhon'in lang,” tin aseipeh in, “Nalut lhon tengleh, sangan nou kihen koiman atouna nailou namu lhon ding ahi. Hin sutlha lhon'in lang hilang'ah hinkai lhon'in. 
Khat touvin ahindoh a, ‘Ipi bol'a hiche sangan nou chu nasut lhah lhon ham?’ atileh, hitin seilhon'in, ‘Pakaijin aman nom'e’ tilhon'in” ati. 
Chuti chun amani jong ache lhon'in, Jesu sei bangin sangan nou chu agamu lhon tai. 
Chule atahbah in, asutlhon laiyin aneipan ahin adong lhon'in, “Ipi bol'a hiche sangan nou chu nasut lhon ham?” ahin ti. 
Chule seijui teni chun bailam tah in adonbut lhon'in, “Pakaijin aman nom'e” atilhon e. 
Hiti chun sangan nou chu Jesu hengah ahin kaiyun chule aponsil hou chu achungah atouna dingin aphapeh un ahi. 
Chutia ahung tou phei laiyin, mipi hon lampi ah aponsil hou ana phauvin ahi. 
Olive mol'a pat a lampi chesuh kipatna aphah chun, anungjui jousen kholhang asam un, ache toh lasah akop un, Pathen'in thil kidang abol ho amujal un avahchoiyuve. 
“Pakai min'a hung'ah lengpa chu anunnom e! Van'ah cham leng hen, chule van sangpena jong loupi hen!” atiuve. 
Ahivangin mihonpi lah a Pharisee ho phabep khat chun aseiyun, “Houhil, na nungjui hon hitobang thu ho aseiyu khu phohthip in!” atiuve. 
Aman adonbut in, “Amaho athipbeh uleh, lampam'a songho kipah a hung eodoh diu ahi!” ati. 
“Ama Jerusalem ahung naijep a khopi chu amu phat in akap pan tai. 
“Iti kadeisah nahi hitam tunikhon mijouse lah a nangman chamna lampi hi kihet tave lechun. Ahinlah, tun ageisang tan, chamna hi namit mua konna kisel ahitai. 
Sot louva nagalmi teuvin nakul pam'a pansatna akisem diu, amai lang jousea na umchinto diu ahi. 
ama hon tol'a nachil thang diu, chule nachateu jong chutia abol diu. Nagalmi teuvin song khatcha aumna a aumsah louding ahi, ajeh chu huhhingna dinga phat kilemchang nasan loujeh ahi,” ati. 
Chuin Jesu Hou-In alut in, kilhaina ding gancha joh ho ahin nodoh pan tai. 
Aman amaho jah a, “Pathen Lekhabun hitin aseiye, 'Ka in chu taona in kiti ding ahi,' ahin nang hon gucha michom ho kokhuh in nasem tauve” ati. 
Chujou chun, aman niseh leh Hou-In ah thu ahiljin, ahin thempuho, houdan thuhil ho chule adang mipi lamkai hon ama chu iti tha ding ham ti thilgon akisem pat un ahi. 
Ahivangin ima dang ageldoh chom pouve, ajeh chu mijousen athuhil apomchah jeh chun.
