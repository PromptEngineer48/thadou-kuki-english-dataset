Luke.
21.
Jesu Hou-In aumlaiyin, mihao hon sumdolna bom'a athilpeh hou chu aven ahi. 
Chuin meithai mivaicha khat jong ahungin sumsan pehni alehlut in ahi. 
Jesun aseitai, “Thutah ka seipeh nahiuve, hiche mivaicha meithainu hin adang jouse sangin atamto joi. 
Ajeh chu ama hon aneival hou lah a themkhat bou atou ahin, amavang amanu hin, avaichat to-toh a, anei chasun apeh ahitai,” ati. 
Aseijui te lah a phabep khat chun Hou-In kisah na a song kikheng ho chule bang'a melchihna kijempah ho chu anatsat dan aseipan tauvin ahi. Ahivangin Jesun aseitai, 
“Phat ahunglhung e, hiti ho jouse hi aboncha kisuchim ding ahi. Song khatcha khat chunga kisethom namulou diu ahi,” ati. 
ama hon adongun, “Houhil, hitiho hi itih le soh ding ham? Melchihna itobangin hitiho chu soh ding ahitai ti eivetsah diu ham?” atiuve. 
Aman adonbut in, “Koima kilheplhah sah hih un, ajeh chu mi tamtah kamin'a hung ding, ‘Keima Messiah kahi’ tia asei diu ‘Phat ahunglhung tai!’ ahinti diu ahi. Ahivangin tahsan hih un. 
Chuleh gal kisat le thunoh najah tengu leh tija hih un. Ahinai, hiti ho hi soh masa ding ahi, ahin achaina chu hijeng loulai ding ahi,” ati. 
Chuin aman aseiben, “Namkhat chu namkhat toh gal kisat a kon ding, chule lenggam khat jong lenggam dang khattoh kidou ding ahi. 
Lingpi nasatah tah kihot ding, kellha ding, chule muntin'a gamna hise lang ding, tijat um tahtah thilsoh chule van'a melchihna kidangtahtah namu diu ahi.” 
“Ahivanga hiche ho ahung so masanga, suhgenthei na phat nasatah um ding ahi. Nangho kikhopna intin le songkul hoa nakikai diu, chule kanungjui nahi jeh uva lengho le gamvaipo ho thutanna angsunga nadin diu ahi. 
Ahivanga hiche hi nangho dinga keima nei seiphongna diu phat kilemchang chu hi ding ahi. 
Hijeh chun nakihehset nau chu iti kadonbut dem tin lunggim masat dauvin, 
ajeh chu keiman nangho thu adihtah chule chihna, na doudal teuvin nahin donbut theilouna diu ahilou le nanelkal joulouna diuva kapeh ding nahiuve! 
Nakinaipi pen'u–nanu napa, nasopiho, na inkote, chule naloi le pai ho jengin jong nahin pehdoh nahlai diu ahi. Ama hon nangho abangkhat nahin tha nahlai diu ahi. 
Chule mijousen kanungjui nahi jeh uva nahin muda diu ahi. 
Amavang naluchung'a na samjang khat jeng jong mahthah louding ahi! 
Dettah a nadin uleh, na lhagao naki huhdoh diu ahi.” 
“Chuleh nang hon Jerusalem galmi sepai hon aum kimvel namu tengu leh, nang hon alhuhna ding phat hunglhung ahitai ti nahet diu ahi. 
Chuteng Judea gam'a cheng ho thinglhang langa najam doh diu ahi. Jerusalem'a umho napotdoh diu, chule thinglhang langa umdoh ho khopi langa nakile lou diu ahi. 
Ajeh chu hiche ho chu Pathen phulah nikholai hi ding, chule Pathen Lekhabua themgao thusei ho guilhung ding ahi. 
Chuche nikholai chu numei naovop ho leh naosen sin minute dinga iti da um ding hitam. Ajeh chu gamsunga hesoh genthei khohtah chule hiche miho chunga lunghanna nasatah chuding ahi. 
Amaho chemjam'a kithat diu ahilouleh sohchang'a vannoi nam jouse lah a solmanga um diu ahi. Chuleh chidang namdang te phatsung kichai kahsea Jerusalem hi chidang namdang ten achotpha ding ahi.” 
“Chule nisa, lha, chule ahsi hoa melchihna kidangtahtah hung um ding ahi. Chule leichung'a hi namtin boina a dim ding, twikhanglen kitum le twikinong gimnei chun asuh lungjing diu ahi. 
Miho chu leiset chunga thillhung ho amu uva konna kicha diu ahi, ajeh chu vanmun hoa thaneina cheng kihot ling gam ding ahitai. 
Chuteng mijousen Mihem Chapa meilom chunga thahat le loupi tah a ahung amu diu ahi. 
Hijeh a chu hitiho jouse hi ahung kipat tengleh, dingun lang chunglam veuvin, ajeh chu nasochat nau nai ahitai!” ati. 
Chuin aman hiche thutekah hi apen ahi: “Theichang phung khu veuvin, ahilou le thingphung dang hijongleh. 
Ado ahung pullhah teng, nang hon seipeh louvin jong naheuve, nipi anaitai. 
Chutobang chun, hitiho jouse aguilhun namu tengu leh, Pathen Lenggam chu anaitai ti nahet thei diu ahi. 
Thutah ka seipeh nahiuve, hiche khang hi chemang louding ahi, hitiho jouse aguilhun masang sea. 
Van le lei mang ding ahi, ahin kathu ho vang manglou beh ding ahi.” 
“Kihong phauvin! Nalungu, nopchonna le juneh motchonna jeh, chule tuhinkhoa lunggimna ho jeh in motsah hih un. Hiche nikho chu hetlou kah a thang banga kimatsah bol-hih un. 
Ajeh chu hiche nikho chu leiset chunga hing mijouse chunga lhung ding ahi. 
Phat jousen kiging jingin um'un. Chuleh hitobang kichat umtah hunglhung ding peldohna dia nahat jing theina diu le Mihem Chapa angsunga nadin jou theina diuvin taovun,” ati. 
Niseh le Jesu Hou-In'ah thuhil dingin achejin, chule nilhah seh le Olive mol'a jan phat mang dingin ahung kile jin ahi. 
Mihonpi chu Hou-In'ah jingkah seh le athuhil ngai dingin akikhom jiuvin ahi.
