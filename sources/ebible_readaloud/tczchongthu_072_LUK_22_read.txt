Luke.
22.
Cholsolou Changlhah Kut, Kalchuh Kut tia jong kihe chu ahung naitai. 
Thempu pipuiho leh houdan thuhil hon Jesu chu iti tha ding ham ti agong pan tauvin, ahin mipin ahin lethuh ding akichauvin ahi. 
Chuin Judas Iscariot, seijui som le ni holah a khat, sungah Satan alut tan, 
chule ama thempu pipuiho leh Hou-In ngahtup lamkaiho toh Jesu amaho henga pehdohna dinga lampi phapen ho kihoulim dingin achetai. 
Amaho akipah un, sum peh dingin akitem tauve. 
Chuti chun ama jong anom in chule Jesu pehdohna dinga phat kilemchang chu avelhi jingin, mihonpi umlou pet a amat theina diuvin avejinge. 
Tun Cholsolou Changlhah Kut chu ahunglhung tai, Kalchuh Kut a kelngoi akitha chun. 
Jesun Peter le John asol masan, “Chelhon'in lang Kalchuh Kut anneh na ding hin gongtup lhon tan, thakhat a ineh khom theina diuvin” ati. 
Amanin jong ahindong lhon'in, “Hoilai mun'ah gongtup lhon leng natim?” ati lhon'e. 
Aman adonbut in, “Jerusalem nalut lut lhon leh mikhat twibel a twi pu khat namu lhon ding ahi.” Ama chu juilhon'in. Alutna in a chun, 
inneipa jah a seilhon'in, 'Houhil'in adonge: jinlhun in, ka seijui te toh Kalchuh Kut an kanehna ding mun chu hoiba ham? ati tilhon'in.' 
Aman indan chung ka-lentah gotsa um khat a napuilut lhon ding ahi. Hiche lai mun'a chu nangnin i-anneh diu nagon lhon ding ahi,” ati. 
Amani khopi lam'a chun achelhon'in ahileh ima jouse Jesun asei bang bangin agatoh lhon'in chukom'a chun Kalchuh Kut anneh ahinsem lhon tai. 
Phat ahung lhun phat in Jesu le aseijui te dokhang ah atoukhom tauve. 
Jesun aseiye, “Ka thohgim kipat kah a hitobang Kalchuh Kut ankong nangho toh nehkhom ding hi kana ngalel jing ahi. 
Ajeh chu tua ka seipeh nahiuve, Pathen Lenggam'a aguilhunna aum tokah a hiche an hi kaneh kit louding ahitai,” ati. 
Chuin khon'a lengpitwi chu alan Pathen thangvahna apen ahi. Chuin aseitai, “Hiche hi kilah unlang nangho kihom un, 
ajeh chu Pathen Lenggam ahung lhun kah a lengpitwi kadon louding ahitai,” ati. 
Aman changlhah themkhat alan Pathen thangvahna apen ahi. Chuin ahehbal bal'in aseijui te apen, “Hiche hi katahsa, nangho dinga kipe chu ahi. Keima geldoh nan bol'un” ati. 
Jan anneh jouvin, lengpitwi khon dang khat alan hitin aseiye, “Hiche khon hi Pathen le amite kikah a kitepna thah–kinoptona ka thisan a kisudet, nangho dinga kilhaina a kisunglha chu ahi,” ati. 
“Ahivangin hiche dokhang a hin, ilah uva jol le gol hina a tou, mikhat eipedoh ding aum'e. 
Ajeh chu Mihem Chapa athi ding chu gotsa anahitai. Ahin, itobang lungkham nan anga hitam apedoh a pang pa chu,” ati. 
Seijuite khat le khat akidongto un, alah uva koipen chun chutobang chu abol mong ding ham akitiuve. 
Chuin amaho khat le khat alenpen koi ahidiu thu ah aki nelkal lah lah jeng tauve. 
Jesun aseipeh un, “Hiche vannoi leiset a hin lengho le milen milal ten amiho potgih apohsah un ahin ‘mipi ho jol le gol’ tin akiminvo uve. 
Ahin, nangho lah a vang achomdeu ngai ahi. Nalah uva alenpen chun panmun nem pen alo ngai ahi, chule alamkai chu soh tobang ahi ngai ahi. 
Koi thupijo ham, dokhanga toupa le ajenlea pangpa? Ahinai, dokhanga toupa athupijo nai. Amavang hikom'a chutilou ahi! ajeh chu keima nangho lah a ajenlea kapan ahi.” 
“Nanghon kathoh gentheina ah nei umpiuve. 
Chule kapan Lenggam eipeh banga, kenjong tua thaneina kapeh nahiuve, 
Chuche lenggam'a chu ka dokhanga naneh uva chule nadon theina diuvin. Chule nangho laltouna a natou diu Israel phungsom le phungni chunga thu natan diu ahi.” 
Simon, Simon, Satan in na boncha uvin chang kilhah chil banga lhah chil dingin nathum uve. 
Ahivangin taona ah kathumpeh uve, Simon chule na tahsan chu alosap lou ngai ahi. Hijeh a chu nalung naheiya keilam a nahung kile kit teng nasopi ho suthahat in,” ati. 
Peter in aseijin, “Pakai, nangma to songkullut dinga kigosa kahi, chule thina hijongleh thi ngapcha kahi,” ati. 
Ahivangin Jesun adonbut in, “Peter, thil khat naseipeh inge. Jing jingkah ahchal khon masanga, nangman thumvei nei seilep ding, kahe khapoi nati ding ahi,” ati. 
Chuin Jesun amaho chu adongin, “Keiman Kipana Thupha seiphonga kanasol uva, sum polou, sakhao kiken lou, ahilou le kengchot khel kiken louva naum uva chu ipi ngaichat nanei uvam?” atileh, ama hon adonbut un, “Imacha” atiuve. 
“Ahinlah tunvang nasum ule sakhao kipoh tauvin. Chule chemjam naneilou uleh naponsil joh unlang khat kichoh un! 
Ajeh chu phat ahung lhunge kei chung changa hiche themgao thusei guilhunna dingin: ‘Ama adoudal hotoh akisimtha tai’ tia kisun chu. Henge keima chung changa kisun themgao thusei jouse guilhung ding ahi,” ati. 
“Ven Pakai” tin ahin donbut un, “Chemjam ni eiho lah a aumnai,” atiuvin, “Hichu aphatai,” tin aseiye. 
Chuin, seijui te toh kivop in, Jesun indan chung adalhan chule abolngai ma bangin Olive mol langah achetai. 
Hiche a chun amaho aseipeh in, “Taovun, lhepna a nalhah louna diuvin,” ati. 
Themkhat, song sephahna thei chan bep ah, ache chon jep in, chule adilsun ataotai, 
“Hepa, nalung ahileh, lungsettah in thoh gentheina khon hi keiya kon'in ladoh in. Ahin, keima lunggot hilouvin, nangma lunggot joh akibol ding kadeiye,” ati. 
Chuin van'a kon'in vantil khat ahung kilah in chule atha asuhhat peh in ahi. 
Ama lungluttah in ataoben, chule alhagao thohgim natsat dan chu, a-ulsa chu thisan vat lentah tah bangin alenglhan ahi. 
Achainan adingdoh kit in aseijui te hengah ahung kilen ahileh ana ihmut u ahung mun, lungkhamna a kon joilha gamu ahitai. 
“Ipi bol'a na imut uham?” tin adongin, “Thoudoh unlang taovun, lhepna a nalhah lut louna dingun,” ati. 
Ahivangin Jesun chutia asei jeng vang chun, mihonpin ahin nai pheiyun, Judas, seijui som le ni lah a khat, in ahin lamkai ahi. Judas jong Jesu kom langah chibai boh a chop dingin achen ahi. 
Ahin Jesun aseijin, “Judas, nangman Mihem Chapa hi chop a pehdoh ding nagot ham?” ati. 
Seijui dang hon thilsoh ding chu amu phat un ahung eojah un, “Pakai, ikidelpi diu ham? Chemjam kahin pouve!” ahin tiuve. 
Chule alah uva khat chun Thempu Chungnung sohpa jetlang kol asatlhah peh in ahi. 
Ahin Jesun aseijin, “Kidoupi tahih un,” atin chule mipa kol chu atham in asuhdam peh e. 
Chuin Jesun ama man dinga hungho thempu pipuiho, Hou-In ngah lamkaiho, chule upaho kom'ah thu aseiye. Amaho chu adongin, “Keima tijat umtah doudal natong kahim, chemjam toh moltum thoa keima man dinga nahung u? 
Ipi jeh a Hou-In a neimat lou uham? Keima niseh a ana kium jinga. Ahivangin tuhi naphat u ahi, muthim thaneinan vai apoh pet ahi,” ati. 
Hijeh chun ama hon aman'un Thempu Chungnung pa in'ah apuiphei tauve. Chule Peter chun galkang in ahinjuiye. 
Angah ho chun leitol lailunga chu mei atiuva toubem u ahin, chule Peter jong agajaovin ahi. 
Soh numei chapang khat in mei kitivahna a kon chun ahin mun ama chu ave vejeng tai. Achainan amanun ahinseiyin, “Hiche mipa hi Jesu nungjui holah a khat ahi,” ahin ti. 
Ahin Peter in aseilep in, “Numei, ama khu koi ahi jong kahet lou ahi,” ati. 
Chomkhat jouvin koiham khat in ahinven aseitai, “Nang jong amaho lah a khat nahi tei ding ahi,” ati. Peter in adonbut in, “Ahipoiye, kahipoi,” ati. 
Nidan khat jou lang hin koiham khat in angoh kit in, “Hiche mipa jong hi amaho lah a khat hitei tei ding ahi, ajeh chu ama jong Galilee gammi ahi,” ati. 
Ahivangin Peter in aseijin, “Ipi nasei ham kahe hihbeh e,” atitai. Chuin chutia asei laitah chun ahchal ahung khong tai. 
Hiche pettah chun Pakai chu ahung kiheiyin Peter chu ahin ven ahi. Kinloitah in Peter lungsung'ah Pakai thusei ho chu ahung kilangin: “Ahkhon masanga, jing jingkah le, nangman nei seilep ding, kahe khapoi nati ding ahi” ati chu. 
Chuin Peter chun leitol adalhan, lainatah in agakap tai. 
Jesu ngah a pangho lah a mopopa chun totnopnan aneijin chule avopan tai. 
Amit atompeh un, “Gaovin lang seiyin, koiham nadeng'a chu?” atiuvin ahi. 
Chule ama suhjum nan bolthei jouse abol'un ahi. 
Jingkho avah in, upaho jouse, thempu pipuiho leh houdan thuhil ho pan nan akikhom un ahi. Jesu chu akikhom mipi angsung'ah ahin puiyun, 
chule ama hon adongun, “Nei seipeh un nangma Messiah chu nahim?” atiuve. Ahivangin aman adonbut in, “Naseipeh uleng, nei tahsan lou diu ahi. 
Chule thudoh naneiyu leng nei donbut lou diu ahi. 
Ahin, tu apat a Mihem Chapa hi thaneitah a Pathen khut jetlam a tou ding ahi,” ati. 
Amaho abonchauvin ahung eojah jeng'un, “Ahileh, Pathen Chapa kahie natina ham?” ahinti uve. Chuin aman adonbut in, “Naseiyu chu kahi” ati. 
Chuin ama hon, “Ipi dinga hettohsah dang ingaikhoh uham? Eiho tah in athusei ija tauve,” atiuve.
