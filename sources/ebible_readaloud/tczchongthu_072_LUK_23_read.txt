Luke.
23.
Chuin mipi kikhom chengse chun Jesu chu Rome Gamvaipo, Pilate hengah ahinpui tauve. 
ama hon apansat nau thu chu aseipan un: “Hiche mipa hin kamiteu apuileng'in, Rome solkal kom'a kai pehlou ding tin aseijin chule ama tah jong Messiah, Leng akihisah e,” atiuve. 
Hijeh chun Pilate in ama chu adongin, “Nangma Juda te Lengpa nahim?” ati. Jesun adonbut in, “Kahi chu nasei khai” ati. 
Pilate chu thempu pipuiho leh mihonpi hengah ahung kiheiyin chule aseitai, “Hiche mipa chunga hin ima adihlou kamupoi!” ati. 
Chuin ama hon asei teitei jengun, “Ahivanga, ama hin achena chan'a athuhil a konna boina asem jing jeng ahi–Judea pumpia, Galilee a pat Jerusalem geiya!” atiuve. 
“Oh, Galilee gammi ham?” tin Pilate in ahin dong'e. 
ama hon ahi atipeh phat un, Pilate in Herod Antipas kom'a dingin asol tai, ajeh chu Galilee chu Herod vaipohna noi anahi, chule Herod jong chu Jerusalem a aumpet anahi khan ahi. 
Herod in Jesu mu na dinga phat hoitah amujeh chun akitai lheh jengin ahi, ajeh chu ama chung chang thudol anajah a chule sot apat a thil kidang abol ho ana munom ten ahi. 
Aman Jesu chu thudoh tamtah adongin, ahivangin Jesun adonbut nom poi. 
Hiche lai chun thempu pipuiho leh houdan thuhil ho chua chun adingun ahehsetna thu u chu asam un ahi. 
Chuin Herod le asepai ten Jesu chu totnopnan aneiyun adai chavai pan tauve. Achainan, ama hon leng ponsil asilpeh un Pilate hengah ahin lesol tauve. 
(Herod le Pilate, masanga kimelmah teni chu, hiche ni chun jol le gol ahung hilhon'e). 
Chuin Pilate in thempu pipuiho leh hou lamkai adang ho, mipi ho jaonan, akoukhom in, 
chule athulhuh sem aphongdoh in ahi. “Nanghon hiche mipa hi kakom'a nahin puiyun, doudal natoh atonge tia ngohna naneiyu ahi. Keiman hiche thudol'a hin phatah in nangho mitmun kavelhan chule bolset neilou ahi kamudoh e. 
Herod in jong themmona amulou jeh a eiho kom'a ahin lesol ahi. Thina thei chan gotna pehna ding natoh hiche mipan abolpoi. 
Hijeh chun keiman gimbolna peing ting, hin lhadoh tange,” ahinti. 
Ajeh chu kut sunga chu ama hon mikhat alhadoh jing'u ahi. 
Chutah chun mihonpi lah a kon chun awgin sangtah ahung gingin, thakhat tah in ahin sam'un, “Ama hi thatdoh in, chule Barabbas chu nei lhadoh peh un!” atiuve. 
(Barabbas chu agam solkal douna natoh jeh le toltha jeh a songkul'a kikhum anahi.) 
Pilate in amaho anelkal'in ahi, ajeh chu aman Jesu alhadoh nom jeh ahi. 
Ahivangin ama hon asam sam jengun, “Thingpel'a khenbeh in! thingpel'a khenbeh in!” atiuve. 
Athumvei channa din aman adongin, “Ipi bol'in em? Ipi themmona aneiyem? Keiman thina chan'a gotna pehna ding thu ima kamupoi. Hijeh chun hin vogim ingting hin lhadoh tange,” ati. 
Ahivangin mihonpi chun awsang jep in ahasap cheh un, Jesu chu thingpel'a khetbeh ahina din athum'un, chule a-awgin u chun ajoujo tai. 
Hijeh chun Pilate in Jesu chu angeh dungjuiyun athi dingin atanlhah peh tai. 
ama hon athum dungjuiyun Barabbas, michohnoh le toltha jeh a songkul'a umpa, alhadoh peh in ahi. Ahin Jesu vang chu amaho khut ah anop nop alo diuvin apedoh tai. 
Chutia Jesu ahin puidoh uchun, Simon kiti Cyrene gam mi khat thinglhang langa pat a ahung pettah anahi. Sepai ho chun aman un thingpel chu achungah akoiyun Jesu nunga chun apohsah tauve. 
Mihonpi tamtah in anung ahinjuiyun, a-op chum'a kap nupiho jong ahiuve. 
Ahivangin Jesu amaho langah akiheiyin aseitai, “Jerusalem chanute, keiya dingin kap hih un, amavang nangho le nachateu dingin kap jouvin. 
Ijeh inem itile ama hon hitia aseina diu nikho hunglhung ding ahi. 'Cha neilou numeiho, naovop khalou naobu, chule nao noichepsah khalou ho anunnom uve,' kiti ding ahi. 
Mihon mol ho jah a ‘Neilhuh khum'un’ tia athum dingu, lhang jah a ‘neikhu khum'un’ atidiu ahi. 
Ajeh chu thingphung ahinlai'a bon hitia hi abol ule, iti da ding ham agot teng?” ati. 
Adang mini, anilhona miphalou, teni jong chu ama toh tha tha dingin ahin puidoh uve. 
Lugu mun atinau mun ahung lhun phat un, ama hon thingpel ah thingkil'in akilbeh un ahi. Chuleh miphalou teni jong chu akhetbeh tauve–khat ajet ah chule khat aveiyah. 
Jesun aseitai, “Hepa, ngaidam in, ajeh chu ipi abol'u ahepouve,” ati. Chule sepai hon aponsil chu vang asang'un achang ding akive lhauve. 
Mihonpi chun aveuvin chule alamkai hon ajontai'un. “Aman midang ho ahuhhingin” tin ama hon aseiyun, “Ama le ama kihuhhing tahta Pathen thaonu, Akilhengdoh pa chu ahi mong mong leh,” atiuvin ahi. 
Sepai hon jong adai chavaiyun, don ding juthuh apeuvin ahi. 
Ama ahin kou uvin, “Nangma Juda te lengpa nahileh, kihuhhing in!” atiuve. 
Achungah chun melchihna khat atah uvin, “Hiche hi Juda te lengpa ahi” tin asun uve. 
Miphalou akikhetbehpi teni khatpa chun ahin jontaiyin, “Nang, Messiah nahi hilou ham?” Nahi vetsahnan nang le nang kihuhhing in lang chule keini jong neihuhdoh lhon tang'em, hitia na thohgim laihin! ati. 
Ahivangin miphalou khatpa chun ahin phohsal in, “Pathen nagin lou ham athi dinga naum kisan'a? 
Eini hi athi dinga lom ihi ithil bolkhel jeh a, ahin hiche pa hin imacha bolkhel aneipoi,” ati. 
Chuin aman aseitai, “Jesu, nei geldoh in na lenggam'a nahung teng,” ati. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Tahbeh a ka seipeh nahi, tunia Paradise a iumkhom ding ahi,” ati. 
Hiche phat hi sunkim dontah ahin, chule nidan thum chan in gamsung pumpi muthim ahung jingin ahi. 
Nisa a kon vah achemang in, chule kinloitah in Hou-In muntheng'a pondal chu akimlai tah a akehlhasuh in ahi. 
Chutah chun Jesu ahung peng in, “Pa, nakhut ah kalhagao kapedoh e” ati. Chule chuche thucheng asei toh kilhon'in ahinna alhatai. 
Rome sepai jalamkaipa, kithana velhalea pang pan thilsoh umchan chu amun, Pathen chibai aboh in aseitai, “Hiche mipa vang michonpha ahi,” ati. 
Chuleh mihonpi akikhetbeh ve'a hung sesen jong thilsoh umchan chu amu uvin, amaho lungkham tah in inlam ache tauve. 
Ahivangin Jesu jol le golte, Galilee gam'a pat a hinjui numeiho chengtoh, amaho chu gamla thimtah in adingun ahin galvet uve. 
Tun Joseph kiti mipha le chondih tah khat aum'e. Ama chu Juda te vaipoloi lah a khat ahi. 
Ahin aman houlamkai dang ho thulhuh sem le natoh chu ana noppeh lou ahi. Ama chu Judea gam'a Arimathea khoa kon ahi, chule Pathen Lenggam hung ding chu anga jing ahi. 
Ama Pilate kom'a achen Jesu long chu athum'in ahi. 
Chuin aman atahsa chu thingpel a pat in alalhan, tupat ponnem ajolsao thimtah khat in atom in, chule song kilai lhanthah khat ah alupsah tai. 
Hiche chu kigotni nilhangkai don a vaigei thimtah a kibol ahi, ajeh chu Cholngahni kipat ding kon cha ahitai. 
Atahsa akilah mang phat chun, Galilee apat a hung numei ho chun ahin juiyun, atahsa kikoina lhan chu agamu uvin ahi. 
Chuin inlam acheuvin chule thil namtui le atahsa a nuding nuthei akisem uvin ahi. Ahin athilsem u ajo phat un Cholngahni anakipan tai, hijeh chun dan thun angeh dungjuijin akichol tauve.
