Ahin nimasani jingpi tah in, numeiho chu lhan lam'ah acheuvin, thil namtui asem u jong akichoiyun ahi. 
Lhan sung lutna a um song ana kitangdoh chu agamu uve. 
Hijeh chun alut un, ahivangin Jesu long chu amu tapouve. 
Chutia lungboi tah a adin laitah un, mini apettah in ahung kilah in, ponbang vallah lah akisil lhone. 
Numei ho chu atijauvin chule amaiyu tol'ah akunlhah sah un ahi. Chuin miteni chun ahin sei lhon'in, “Ipi bol'a athiho lah a ahingdoh khat nahol uham? 
Ama hikom'ah aum tapoi! Ama athilah a kon'in athoudoh tai! Galilee a aumlaiya nana seipeh ho'u chu geldoh un, 
Mihem Chapa michonse ho khut a pehdoh a um ding, thingpel'a akhetbeh diu, chule anithum ni le thoukit ding ahi, tia anasei chu,” atilhon e. 
Chuin ama hon hiche hi anasei mongnai ti ageldoh un ahi. 
Hijeh chun amaho lhan mun'a kon'in aseijui som le khat ho–chule adang ho jouse–thilsoh umchan seipeh dingin kintah in akile loi tauve. 
Solchah ho thilsoh umchan seipeh chu Mary Magdalene, Joanna, James nu Mary, chule numei adang ho jong ahiuve. 
Ahivangin miho ding chun athusim jah u chu mohthu homkeo danin agel uvin, hijeh chun atahsan tapouve. 
Ahin Peter vang chu achomdoh in lhan veding chun alhaijel'in ahi. Abung'in aven ahileh tompon akeova um bouseh chu amun, chuin inlam akile kit in, ipi iti hitam alungdong lheh jeng tai. 
Hiche ni mama chun Jesu nungjui mini, Emmaus kho jotna lampi, Jerusalem a pat mile sagia gamla achun ache lhone. 
Amanin lamjot toh thilsoh umchan jouse chung chang thudol kihoulim toh akop lhon ahi. 
Hitiho jouse thu akihoulim lhon laiyin, Jesu jong ama tah kinloi tah in ahungin amani toh alhon khom tai. 
Ahivangin Pathen'in ahetdoh lhon louna din aumsah e. 
Aman adongin, “Nalamjot pum lhon'a chu ipi thu lom lunglut tah a nakihoulim lhon hitaimo?” ati. Chomkhat akhongai lhon'in, amaiso lhon lungkhamna maiso apulhon'in. 
Chuin amani lah a khat pa, Cleopas chun adonbut in, “Tu niphabep masanga thilsoh ho jouse helou Jerusalem miho lah a nang bouseh hin nate,” ati lhon'e. 
Jesun adongin, “Ipi thilsoh ho am?” ati. “Nazareth a hung kon Jesu chunga thilsoh umchan ho,” tin aseipeh lhontai, “Ama thil kidang nasatah bolthei themgao khat anahin, chule Pathen le miho mitmu a thaneitah houhil ahi. 
Ahivangin kathempu pipuihou le adang hou lamkai hon thina chan gotna chang din themmo achan'un, chule ama hon thingpel'ah akhenbeh tauve. 
Keihon Israel huhdoh dinga hungpa Messiah chu ahi tia kakineppiu ahi. Hiche hi nithum masanga thilsoh ahitai. 
Chuti chun keiho lah a ama nungjui numei phabep khat tujingkah matah chun alhan'ah aum uvin, chule amaho ahung kileuvin kidangtah thulhut ahinpo uvin ahi. 
ama hon atahsa akiholmoi atiuvin, chule vantil amu uvin, aman Jesu ahingkit tai ati. 
Keiho lah a pasal phabep khat ave dingin alhaidoh un, atahbeh in, Jesu long chu numei ho seibang chun anaum tapoi,” ati lhon'e. 
Chuin Jesun amani jah a, “Vo lungthim bei miho! Themgao hon Pathen Lekhabua ajihlut ho tahsan ding hahsa nasah uve. 
Messiah chu aloupina a alut masanga hitiho jouse hi athoh galkai ding ahi ti kichen tah a kivetsah ana hilou ham?” ati. 
Chuin Jesun amani chu Mose le themgao lekhasut ho jouse le Pathen Lekhabu jousea kipat in ama chung chang hilchet abolpeh in ahi. 
Hiche pet a hi amaho Emmaus kho lhung koncha ahi tauve. Jesu chu achejom ding danin aum'in, 
ahin amanin alho lhon'in, “Tujan keini kom'ah gehtan, ajeh chu avaigei tai,” ati lhon'e. Hiti chun ama jong amani chutoh alhon tai. 
Anne dinga atoulhah uchun, aman changlhah chu alan phatthei aboh'e. Chuin ahehbal in amani chu apen ahi. 
Chupet tah chun amit lhon ahung hahdoh in, chule amanin koi ahi chu ahin hedoh lhon tai. Chuin hiche pettah chun Ama amangtai! 
Amani khat in khat jah a asei lhon'in, “Lampia ikihoulim uva Pathen Lekhabu hilchet abola chu ilungchang lhon kileojap bang lou ham?” ati lhon'e. 
Chule hiche nidan kah sung mama chun Jerusalem langah akile lhon tai. Chua chun seijui som le khat le adang amaho toh ana kikhom khom ho amu lhon'in, 
ama hon, “Pakai chu tahbeh in athoudoh tai! Peter henga akilah'e!” tin ahinsei uve. 
Chuin Emmaus khoa pat hung tenin jong amani thusim, Jesu aheng lhona lampia akijot laiyuva ahung kilah dan, chule changlhah ahin hebal phat a ahin hetdoh lhon dan, asei lhon'in ahi. 
Chule chutia asei laitah uvin, Jesu alah uva hetmanlou kah in ana dingin ahi. Aman ahin seijin, “Chamnan naumpiu hen,” ahinti. 
Ahivangin amaho abonchauvin atijauvin akichauvin, thilha mudan'in akigel uve! 
“Ipi jeh a kicha nahiu ham?” tin ahin dongin, “Ipi bol'a nalung sungu chu lungthoina a dim ham? 
Kakhut veuvin, kakeng veuvin, nang hon keima mong mong kahinai ti namu uve. Nei tham uvin lang thilha kahilou hi hechen un, ajeh chu thilhan tahsa mel tua neimu bangu hi aneipoi,” ati. 
Thu aseitoh thon akhut le akeng avetsah in ahi. 
Chuti chun amaho tahsan loutah in ading jing nalaiyun, kipana le lungdon nan adimset un ahi. Chuin aman ajah uva, “Neh ding nanei uvam?” ahinti. 
ama hon nga kikang khat apeuvin ahi, 
chule aman hichu anen ama hon aveuve. 
Chuin Aman aseijin, “Masanga nangho toh iumkhom laiyuva, kana seipeh soh nahi tauve, Kei chung chang thudol Mose danthua, themgao ho lekhabua, chule labua kisun jouse chu guilhung ding ahi,” ati. 
Chuin aman alungkot u Pathen Lekhabua thu ho ahettheina diuvin ahonpeh in ahi. 
Chuin aseitai, “Henge, masanglai a pat a chu Messiah hi thohgim ding chule thi ding chule anithum ni le athilah a konna thoudoh ding tia ana kisun ahi. 
Hitia jong hi ana kisun ahi, hiche thu hi namtin jouse henga amin thaneina a seiphonga um ding ahi, Jerusalem a kipan ding: ‘Chonset ngaidamna alunghei jousea dingin aum'e.’ 
Nangho hiche ho jouse hettohsah nahiuve.” 
“Chuleh tua Lhagao Theng kahinsol peh ding nahiuve, kapa ana kitepna banga. Ahin Lhagao Theng ahung'a van'a kon thilbol theina nadimset tokah uvin hiche khopi a hin um uvin,” ati. 
Chuin Jesun amaho chu Bethany langah apuiyin, vanlam'a akhut alhaangin amaho chu phatthei aboh'e. 
Amaho phatthei aboh laitah in, amaho chu adalhan chule van langah lahtouvin aumtai. 
Hijeh chun ama hon ama chu ahouvun chuin Jerusalem lamah kipana dimset pumin ahung kile tauve. 
Chuin aphat jouseu chu Hou-In ah Pathen vahchoinan amang tauve.
