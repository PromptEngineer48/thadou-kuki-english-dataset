John Sut Kipana Thupha.
1.
Thu chu atil abul'a ana umsa ahi. Thu chu Pathen toh umkhom ahin, chule Thu chu Pathen ahi. 
Ama chu atil abul'a Pathen toh umkhomsa ahi. 
Pathen'in amavang a thil jouse anasem ahin, chule Ama louva kisem doh khatcha jong aumpoi. 
Thu chun thil jouse hinna apeh ahin, chule Ama hinna chun amichang cheh vah apeh ahitai. 
Vah chun muthim lah ahinsal in, chule muthin'in vah chu asumit joupoi. 
Pathen'in mikhat, Twilutsah John ahin sol'in, 
aman vah thu a hettohsah chu ama thuseiya konna amichang cheh in atahsan theina dingu ahi. 
John ama tah chu vah ahipoi, vah thudol hettohsah a seiphong chu ahije. 
Vannoiya hunga, amichang cheh vah hung pea, Ama bou chu vah tahbeh ahi. 
Asemsa vannoiya ama ana hungin, hinla vannoiyin ama ahedoh pouve. 
Ama amite henga anahung chun, amiten ama ana sang tapouve. 
Ahivangin koi hileh ama tahsan le ama kisa'n jouse chu Pathen chate hitheina thuneina apetai. 
Amaho penthah chu tahsa pen ngai'a mihem lung lam'a konna tahsa lunggon'a penga ahipouvin, Pathen'a kon peng'a ahibou uve. 
Hiti hin Thu chu tahsapu mihem ahung kisoh in, chule ilah uva ahung chengtai. Ama lhadahlou ngailut le kitahna dimset ahin, keihon ama loupina, Pa Chapa changkhat seh ahina loupitah chu kamu tauve. 
John chun Jesu thudol'a hettohsah in, mipi ho henga asam'in “Hiche pa hi ahi, athudol kana hettohsah chu, Kanunga mi khat ahungin, ama chu kei sanga gamchenga lenjo ahi, ajeh chu ama keima masang peh a anaumsa ahitai, kati chu,” ati. 
Ama aning lhinsetna konna eiho jouse lungset vangbohna hi aban ban'a changa ihitauve. 
Ijeh ham itileh danthu chu Mose a kon ahin, hinla lhadahlou Pathen mi ngailutna le kitahna chu Jesu Messiah vanga hungah ahi bouve. 
Koima Pathen mukha ana umpoi. Ahin atepi umlou, ama tah Pathen Chapa changkhat seh, Pa lung to kinaicha um jing chun, eiho henga aphondoh ahitai. 
Hiche hi Juda te lamkai hon Jerusalem'a pat a thempu ho leh Hou-In a sottol leho, John henga “Nang koi nahim?” tia aga dohsah nauva, John in a hettohsah chu ahi. 
Ama ahung kiphong doh paijin, “Keima Messiah kahipoi” ati jenge. 
Chuphatnin ama hon adongun, “Achutile aphai, koi nahi ham? Nangma Elijah nahim?” atiuve. Aman adonbut in “Kahipoi” ati. “Ahile kaginchat u themgao chu nahim?” atiuve. Aman “Kahipoi” ati. 
“Achutile koi nahim? Eihinsol ho kom'a lhut ding donbutna kangai chauve. Nangman koi kahi nakiti em?” atiuve. 
John in adonbut in Keima gamthip a khosam awgin'in, “Pakai hungna ding lampi sutheng un,” tia Isaiah themgao thusei chu kahi, ati. 
Hichun Pharisee ho ahung kisol ho chun, 
Ama adong un, “Messiah nahi louva, Elijah nahi louva, chule themgao nahilou le, ipi thuneina naneiya Baptize nachan sah ham?” atiuvin ahi. 
Chuin John in amaho jah a, “Keiman twiya Baptize kachan sah ahin, ahinlah mipi lah a nang hon nahet dohlou u mikhat aum'e. 
Ama keima nunga hung ahivangin keima ama soh dinga jong lhing joulou chule akengchot khao sutlham dinga jong kilom kahipoi,” ati. 
Hicheng chu Jordan vadung gal solam Bethany mun'a John in Baptize achansahna a thilsoh ahi. 
Ajingin John in ama heng lam'a Jesu ahung amun, “Veuvin, vannoi chonset pomang Pathen kelngoinou khu,” 
Ama hi ahi keiman kasei, kanunga hunga kei sanga gamchenga lenjo, kati chu, ajeh chu ama kei um masang peh a ana umsa ahitai, ati. 
“Keiman ama Christa ahilam kana hepoi, ahivangin ama chu Israel lah a phondoh a aumna dinga keiman twiya Baptize kachan sah ahi,” ati. 
Chuin John in athu hettohsah nan, “Keiman van'a kon'a Lhagao Theng vakhu banga ahung lenlhah a achunga aumden kamui.” 
Keiman ama ahilam kana hepoi, ahivang'in Pathen'in twiya Baptize chan sah dinga eisol chun, aman kakom'a “Achunga Lhagao Theng achuh a aumden namupa chun, Lhagao Thenga baptize ahin chan sah ding ahi,” 
“Chule keiman Jesu chunga hicheng asopet hi kamun, hijeh a chu ama hi Pathen'in alhendoh achapa khat seh chu ahi tia ka hettohsah ahitai,” ati. 
Ajing kit in, John aseijui mini toh adinpet un, 
Jesun ahinjotpa chu amun, ama chu aven, “Veuvin Pathen kelngoinou khu,” ati. 
Chuin John seijui teni chun athusei chu ajalhon'in, Jesu nung ajuilhon tai. 
Chuin Jesu akihei leh amanin ajuilhon chu amun, “Ipi na ngaichat lhon ham?” atitai. Amanin adonbut lhon'in “Rabbi” (Hichu houhil tina ahi) hoiya na umji ham? atilhon e. 
Chuin aman, “Hung lhon'in hungve lhon'in,” ati. Amani Jesu toh akilhon khom lhon'in, aumna chu aga mulhon tai. Chule hiche nikho chun aumkhom tauve. Hichu nilhangkai nidan li don ahi. 
John thuseiya, Jesu nungjui teni lah a khat joh chu, Simon Peter naopa Andrew chu ahi. 
Andrew chun asopipa Simon aga holdoh in ajah a, “Keihon Messiah (Christa tina ahi) kamu tauve,” ati. 
Chuin Andrew in Simon chu Jesu henga ahinpuiyin ahi. Hichun Jesun Simon chu aven ajah a, “Nangma John chapa Simon nahi, hijongleh Cephas kisah ding nahitai. (Hichu Peter tina ahi).” 
Ajingin Jesu Galilee lam'a che dingin aum'e. Hichun aman Philip amun, ajah a “Hungin neijuijin,” ati. 
Philip chu Andrew leh Peter chenna khopi Bethsaida kho a kon ahi. 
Chuin Philip in Nathanael aholdoh in ajah a, “Mose le themgao hon athudol anasut u amapa tah chu kamu tauve. Ama chu Nazareth khomi, Joseph chapa, Jesu ahi,” ati. 
Hichun Nathanael in adatmo lheh in, “Nazareth khoa kon'a thilpha sohdoh thei hinam?” ati. Philip in adonbut in “Hungin nangma tah in hungven” ati. 
Akom lhon'a amani ahung lhon chu Jesun amun, ajah lhon'a, “Phatlhemna beihel mikitahpa, Israel chapa dihtah chu tun aumtai,” ati. 
Nathenael in adongin, “Iti neihet jeng ham?” ati. Jesun adonbut in, “Philip in naholdoh masanga theichang phung noiya na umpet a chu kamu nahitai,” ati. 
Chuin Nathanael tijatah in, “Rabbi nangma Pathen Chapa, Israel te lengpa nahi,” tin aphongtai. 
Chuin Jesun adongin, “Theichang phung noiya na umpet kamu ahitai, kati jeh a chu na tahsan ham? Hiche sanga chungnungjo namu ding ahi,” ati. 
Chujouvin ama jah a, “Atahbeh ka seipeh nahi, nangman van akihon'a chule Pathen vantil ho Mihem Chapa chunga akaltou le akumsuh namu ding ahi, ajeh chu ama tah chu van le lei kimatna kalbilei ahi,” ati.
