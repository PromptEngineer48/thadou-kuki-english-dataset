John.
2.
Ajingin Galilee gamsunga Cana khoa kichenna golvah kin khat aumin ahi. Chua chun Jesu nu jong aumin, 
Chule hiche kin a chu Jesu leh aseijui ho jong akouvu ahi. 
Golvahna aum petnin lengpitwi alhing tahih in, anu chun Jesu jah a “Lengpitwi anei tapouve,” ati. 
“Ngailut umtah numei, hichu eiho boipi ding ahipoi” tia Jesun adonbut ahi. “Keima phat lhing loulai hichu,” ati. 
Ahivangin anu chun lhacha ho jah a, “Aman najah uva asei ipi hijongleh bol un,” ati. 
Akom tah uva chun Juda te dan dungjuiya kisuhtheng na songbel lentah gup uma ahi. Bel khat chu twi-thei dim somni apat somthum dol cheh ahi. 
Jesun soh ho jah a “Songbel ho chu twi sung dimun,” ati. Ama hon songbel ho chu twi asun dim phatnun, 
Aman ajah uva, “Tun phabep thaldoh unlang golvah vaihompa henga choiyun,” ati. Hiti chun lhacha ho chun athupeh bang chun abol tauve. 
Golvah vaihompa chun, twisih lengpitwi kisosah chu ate khah phatnin, ahung kondohna hechen louvin (Lhacha hon vang aheuve), moulang chu akoutai. 
Aman ajah a, “Kinbol'a chun lengpitwi chihpen ahomdoh masa jin, mihon adon khop tengleh, adaojep ahomji bouve. Ahin nangman tugeiyin achih pen nahom nalaiye!” ati. 
Galilee gam'a Cana khoa hiche melchihna kidang abol hi ama loupi kiphondohna dinga anatoh masapen ahi. Chuin aseijui ten ama atahsan tauve. 
Kichenna golvah kichai jin, ama le anu toh, asopiho toh, chule aseijui ho toh Capernaum a nikho phabep din ache tauvin ahi. 
Chuin Judah te Kalchuh Kut anai phat in, Jesu Jerusalem'a achetai. 
Hou-In sunga chun aman kivei mihon kilhaina thilto ding bongho, kelngoiho chule vapal ho ajoh uva chuleh sum lhengho atouvu amudoh tai. 
Hichun Jesun khao phabep a jepna ding asemin, amaho jouse chu Hou-In a kon'in adeldoh in, chule aman bongho le kelngoiho anodoh in, sum lhengho sumnong chu athethang peh in chule adokhangu alekhup pehtai. 
Chuin, vapal joh ho kom'a achen ajah uva, “Hiche ho hi hilai mun'a kon'in podoh un. Kapa In chu nang hon kailhang mun sosah hih un!” ati. 
Hichun aseijui ten, Pathen Lekhabua themgao thusei, “Na-In dinga katompan hin katha jouse eine chaiye,” tichu ahin geldoh tauve. 
Ahivangin Judah vaipo hon ajah a “Ipi ham nabol? Hitobang bolna dinga thuneina Pathen'in napeh mong ahileh amelchihna kidang khat nei musah un,” atiuve. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Aphai, hiche Hou-In hi suchim un, chule Keiman nithum in tungdoh inge,” ati. 
Hichun amaho tijatah in, “Hiche Hou-In sahna a kum som li le kum gup jen lut a, nangin nithum'a iti nasah doh ding ong?” atiuve. 
Ahivangin, Jesun hiche Hou-In ati chu Ama tahsa aseina ahi bouve. 
Hiche hi Ama thina a konna kaithouva aum nung chun, aseijui ten ipi aseina ahi ahin geldoh un, Pathen Lekhabua thu ho leh Jesu thusei ho atahsan tauve. 
Kalchuh Kut laiya, Jerusalem'a aumpet a Jesun thil kidang melchihna abol ho jeh chun mi tamtah in ama atahsan tauve. 
Ahivangin amaho chu Jesun atahsan poi, ajeh chu mihem hina chu aman ahetsa ahi. 
Ama dingin mikhat thudol adang khat in ama henga hettohsah angaipoi, ajeh chu mijouse lungsung ahetsa ahi.
