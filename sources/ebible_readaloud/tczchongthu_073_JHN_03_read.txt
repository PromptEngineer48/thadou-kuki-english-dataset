John.
3.
Pharisee mi amin Nicodemus kiti Judah te hou lamkai khat aum'e. 
Amapa chu jankhat Jesu henga ahungin Jesu jah a, “Houhil, keiho dinga Pathen'in nahin sol ahi kaheuve; melchihna kidang hijat nabol ho hi Pathen'a kon nahi aphotchetna ahitai,” ati. 
Jesun adonbut in “Tahbeh a ka seipeh nahi, nangma napen kit louleh Pathen Lenggam namu louding ahi,” ati. 
Nicodemus chun datmo tah in, “Mi ateh nunga iti anu naobu a lelut kit a hung pengkit thei ding ham?” ati. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Thutah chu ahileh, twiya le lhagaova apenlou le koima Pathen Lenggam alut theipoi. 
Mihem tahsaphe a penga chu tahsa a hinna bou ahin, ahivangin Lhagao Thenga penga chu Lhagaova hinna chang ahi. 
Hijeh chun 'nangma napen kit ding ahi,' kati chu datmo hih in. 
Hui khu anopna lam lam'a anungin, nangin agin najan, ahivangin hoiya hungkon hoilam ajot nahepoi, chutobang chun Lhagaova pengho jong nangin nahe theipoi,” ati. 
Chuin Nicodemus chun ajah a “Hiche ho chu iti hithei ding ham?” ati. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Nangla Judah te houhil pipu khat nahin, hiche ho hi iti nahet theilou ham? 
Tahbeh a ka seipeh nahi, keihon kahet kaseiyu ahin, kamu ka hettohsah u ahi; ahivangin ka hettohsah uhi nang hon nasang pouve. 
Keiman leiset thil ho kaseiya na tahsan louleh, van'a thil ho thudol seileng iti na tahsan ding ham? 
Koima van'a akal'in, ahung kile khapoi; ahivangin Mihem Chapa vang chu van'a konna hung kumlha ahi. 
Chuleh Mose in gamthip a thing'a sum-eng gul adop sang banga chu, Mihem Chapa kidom sang ding, 
hijeh chun amitakip ama tahsan chan in tonsot hinna anei ding ahi.” 
“Ajeh chu Pathen'in hibanga hi vannoi angailut ahin: Koi hijongleh Ama tahsan chan chu mangthah louva tonsot hinna aneina dingin, aneisun achapa changkhat chu apetan ahi. 
Pathen'in achapa vannoiya ahinsol chu, vannoi huhhing'a aumna ding joh ahi bouve.” 
“Koi hileh ama tahsan chan chu, achunga thutanna umlou ahitai; ahivangin koi hijongleh atahsanlou vang chu, Pathen chapa changkhat chu atahsan loujeh a achunga thutanna umsa ahitai. 
Chule thutanna chu hiche hi ahi; Pathen vah chu vannoiya ahungin, ahivangin mihon vah sangin, achonna u aphatlou jeh in muthim joh adeijouvin ahi. 
Michonphalou jousen vah athet un, nai jong anailut ngam pouve, ajeh chu achonset nau hin phongdoh khante tin akichauve. 
Ahivangin thudih a chonphat bol ho vang chun Pathen lungdei abol'u chu mihon amutheina dingin vah a aum jing uve.” 
Chuin Jesu leh seijui ten Jerusalem adalhauvin, Judea gam thinglhang lam ajon tauve; chukom'a chun phat chomkhat aumkhom'un, miho baptize achansah jin ahi. 
Hiche pet chun Twilutsah John injong Salim kom'a Aenon mun'a twi ninglhing tah a aum jeh chun, miho baptize achansah jin, chule aphat seh in, miho jong baptize chang dingin ahung jingun ahi. 
Hichu John songkul'a akikhum masang ahi. 
Hichun kisuhthengna thudol'a John seijui ho leh Judah mi phabep kikah a kinelna anasoh in ahi. 
Hijeh chun John seijui ho ahenga ahung'un, ajah a “Rabbi (hichu houhil tina ahi), Jordan luigala nakimupi a ama Messiah ahi tia na hettohsah pa jong chun miho baptize achansah jin ahi. Chuleh mi tampi eiho henga hung talouvin ama henga ache tauve,” atiuvin ahi. 
Chuin John in adonbut in “Pathen'in van'a konna apehlou chu, koima ima achang theipoi. 
Keiman naheng uva kasei chu, nangho ahetoh ah nahiuve; keima Christa kahipoi, ama lampi semtoh dinga hung kahi bouve. 
Hiche hi ahi, mou kichenpia chu moulang ahin; chuleh moulang jolpa vang chu moulang kom'a adin'a moulang kitepna ajah chu akipana ahijenge. Hijeh chun ama lolhinna chun keima kipana dim'a eikoi ahi. 
Ama chu khangtou touding, chule keima kemsuh suh ding kahi. 
Ama chunga hungkon ahin, mijouse sanga chungnungjo ahi; Eiho leiset a kon ihiuvin, leiset thu iseiyu ahi, ahivangin ama chu chunga kon ahijeh in mijouse chunga achungnungpen ahi. 
Aman amu leh ajah chu a hettohsah ahi, ahivangin ama hettohsah chu koiman asangpoi. 
Hijeh chun koi hileh, athu hettohsah tahsan'a kisan chun Pathen dihtah chu apomdet ahitai. 
Ama chu Pathen hinsol ahi, Aman Pathen thutah aseiye, ajeh chu ama Pathen Lhagao dimset a kipe ahi. 
Pa chun Achapa angailun, chule ima jouse ama khut a apetai. 
Hijeh chun, koi hijongleh Pathen tahsan'a chun tonsot hinna anei ahitai. Pathen chapa thungailou vang chu tonsot hinna chang louva Pathen lunghanna thutan chu achunga chu ding ahi,” ati.
