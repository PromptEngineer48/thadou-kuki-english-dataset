John.
4.
Pakai chun John sangin seijui tamjo asem'in miho Baptize achansah jinge ti Pharisee hon aja tauve tichu Jesun ahetan ahi. 
Hichun Jesu ama tah in baptize achansah lou vang'in, aseijui ten abol'u ahi. 
Hijeh chun aman Judea gam adalhan Galilee gam'a akile tai. 
Aman alampi'a chun Samaria gamsung ajotpa in; 
Chuti chun Samaria mite khokhat Sychar mun chu ahunglhung tai; hiche mun chu Jacob in achapa Joseph ana peh loumun kom ahi. 
Chukom'a chu Jacob twikul um'ah ahi, Jesu chu lamsao ajotna'ah achol tan, twikul panga chun leltah in atouvin ahi; Hichu sunkim dontah ahi. 
Hichun Samaria numei khat twithal dingin ahungin, Jesun amanu jah a “Lungset tah in twi don ding neipen” ati. 
Hiche pet a chu Jesu achangseh a bou um ahi, ajeh chu aseijui te khosunga anneh ding choa achedoh kah u ahi. 
Numeinu chu atija lheh e, ajeh chu Judah hon Samaria miho chu athet uva imacha akikoppi ngailou u ahi; hijeh chun amanun Jesu jah a “Nangla Judah mi nahin, keila Samaria numei kahin, ipi dinga keikom'a don ding twi nathum ham?” ati. 
Jesun adonbut in amanu jah a “Pathen thilpeh nachan ding hi henlang, chule naki houpi hi koi ahi hele chun nangin kei kom'a nathum joh ding, chule keiman nangma hintwi pedinga,” ati. 
Chuin amanun ajah a, “Pu nangman athaldohna ding khao le bel jong nei louva, twikul jong athuh in, hintwi nati chu hoiya nalah ding ham? 
Chukit jongleh hiche twikul eina peuva kapului u Jacob sang'a nangma chungnungjo nahim? Ama tah in ana don'a achate leh agancha ho ana donsah a hiche twi sanga phajo hoiya nalah ding ham?” ati. 
Jesun adonbut in ajah a, “Koi hileh hiche twi don'a chu dangchah kit ding ahi. 
Ahivangin Keiman kapeh ding twi don jouse chu a itih a dangchah kit louding, amaho chu asung uva hintwi put a twinah theng soh ding, chule tonsot hinna chang ding ahiuve.” 
Chuin numeinun ajah a “Pu lungset in hiche twi chu kei neipen, chuteng avel'a ka dangchah kitlouna ding, chuleh hilai mun'a twithal'a kahungkit louna dingin,” ati. 
Chuin Jesun amanu jah a, “Chenlang najipa gakouvin,” ati. 
Amanun adonbut in, “Keiman ji kaneipoi,” ati, hichun Jesun ajah a, “Nasei dih e, nangman ji kicheh naneipoi____ 
nangman ji nga tah nanei tan chule tua naum khompi pa jong khu naji tah ahipoi, nasei dih lheh jenge!” ati. 
Chuin numeinun ajah a, “Pu,” “Nangma themgao khat nahi.” 
Ahile neisei peh in, ipi dinga keiho Samaria mihon, kapului teu bolbanga hiche Gerizim mol'a Pathen kahou uva, ahivanga nangho Judah mihon Pathen houna ding mun chu Jerusalem bou ahi, natiu ham?” ati. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Numei, nei tahsan in, nang hon Pathen nahouna dingu mun chu hiche mol'a jong hilou, chuleh Jerusalem'a jong hilou, phat chu ahunglhung e. 
Nangho Samaria mihon nahet chen lou u nahouvun, ahivangin keiho Judah mihon vang kahou u ama chu kahet chen'u ahi; ajeh chu huhhingna chu Judah te a konna hung ahi bouve. 
Ahivangin phat chu ahung lhunge, tutah hi ahi, ahou tahbeh hon Pa chu Lhagao leh thutah a ahou ding phat; ajeh chu Pa chun chutobanga hou ho bou chu amahou dinga ahol ahi. 
Ajeh chu “Pathen chu lhagao ahin, ahouten lhagao leh thutah a ahou ding mong ahi,” ati. 
Chuin numeinun ajah a, “Keiman Messiah hung ding chu kahenai, ama chu Christa ahi. Ama chu ahung tengleh aman ijakai eihil dingu ahi,” ati. 
Chuin Jesun amanu jah a, “Keima Messiah chu kahi!” ati. 
Hiche pettah chun, aseijui te ahung kile tauve, ama hon Jesu leh numeinu kihou chu amu uvin, atija lheh jengun ahi, ahivangin koiman ajah a, “Amanu henga ipi nathum ham?” ahilouleh, “Ipi dinga amanu naki houpi ham?” tia dong ngam aum pouve. 
Hichun numeinu chun a twibel chu twikul panga adalhan khosung lam'a ale lhai kit in miho jouse henga; 
“Hungun mikhat hung veuvin, aman kathilbol leh kachonna jouse aseidoh sohkeiye, Ama hi Christa hitei yinte,” ati. 
Chuin khosunga kon chun mipi tamtah ahung potdoh un ama chu ahung veuvin ahi. 
Hiche pet chun aseijui ten Jesu heng'ah, “Rabbi an themkhat beh nen,” atiuve. 
Ahivangin Jesun adonbut in, “Keiman nangho het phahlou anneh kaneiye,” ati. 
Hichun aseijui te amaho akidong touvin, “Eiho chedoh kah a mikhat touvin anneh ding ahin choipeh khat ham?” atiuve. 
Chuin Jesun ahilchen in ajah uva, “Eisolpa Pathen lunggot lam bol leh akin suh bulhit chu keima anneh ahi,” ati. 
“Nanghon changphu apat chang-at khomna ding lha li alut e' natiuvin ahi. Ahivangin keiman najah uva kasei ahi, khodah uvin loujao khu veuvin, chang amin'in a-atphat val ahitai. 
Chang-at chun athaman akisanji mong ahi, chule a-atkhom lousohga chu miho tonsot hinna a apuilut chu ahi; chutia chu aphut pa le a atpa anilhon'a kipahkhomji ahi! 
Akiseiji bang chun, ‘mikhat in aphut in adang khat in a-at khomji,’ kiti chu adihtah ahi. 
Hijeh chun keiman nangho, na phulou nauva a-atkhom dinga kasol nahiuve; midang in atoh gimnasa chu nang hon aga nakhop tup u ahi.” 
Numeinu chun, “Kathilbol leh kachonna jouse eiseidoh peh e,” tia a hettohsah a kon chun, chuche khoa Samaria mi tamtah in Jesu chu atahsan tauve. 
Chuin amaho Jesu henga ahung'un, akom uva umden dingin a ngeh un ahi, chuin Jesu chukom'a chun nikho ni achongden tan ahi; 
Chua aum sung chun mi tamtah in athusei ajah uva kon chun ama chu atahsan tauve. 
Chuin ama hon numeinu jah a, “Nangma thu hettohsah jengseh hilouvin, keiho tah in ama thusei kajauvin, tun katahsan tauve, chuleh ama hi vannoi huhhing dihtah ahi kahechen tauve,” atiuvin ahi. 
Chua ni ni achon jou chun, Jesun Galilee ajontai. 
Jesu ama tah in themgao chu achenna agamsung'a jabolna achangpoi, tia ana seidohsa ahi. 
Ahijeng vang chun Galilee miten ama chu ana sang uve, ajeh chu amaho chu Kalchuh Kut laiya Jerusalem'a ana um uva, chumun'a Jesu thilbol ho ana musoh keiyu ahi. 
Aman Galilee gamsung ajot in twisih lengpitwi asosah na Cana khoa chun ache kit in ahi, chuche kho kom'a Capernaum kho a chun vaipo milal khat aum'in, achapa chu ha dammo lheh jeng ahi. 
Chuin aman Jesu chu Judea gam'a kon'in Galilee gam'a ahunge ti ajah phat in, ama tah Jesu henga achen, Capernaum geiya ahunga achapa ahung damsah dingin ataotai, ajeh chu achapa chu athikon cha ahitai. 
Jesun ajah a, “Melchihna kidang hicheng ho hi mulou hile chun nei tahsan nadem?” ati. 
Hichun vaipopa chun, “Pakai lungset in kachapa athi masangin hungin,” tin ataotai. 
Jesun amapa jah a, “Na in'ah kile tan nachapa ahinge!” ati. Chuin mipa chun, Jesu thusei chu atahsan in, a inlam ajontai. 
Chuin mipa chu a in alhun masang'in, asohten lampia ahung lamtouvin, achapa damtheng thu chu ahung seipeh uvin ahi. 
Hichun aman asohte jah a, “Itih phat a chu damdoh ham?” tin adongin ahileh, ama hon, “Jan hi sun nidan khat dontah chun akhosih abeitai,” atiuvin ahi. 
Chuin mipa chun hiche phat laitah chu, Jesun ajah a 'na chapa ahinge' tia aseipet tah ahi chu ahedoh tan ahi; hiche jeh chun ama leh a Insung mite chengsen Jesu atahsan tauve. 
Hiche hi Judea gam'a kon'a Galilee gam'a ahungna a Jesun melchihna kidang abol aniveina ahitai.
