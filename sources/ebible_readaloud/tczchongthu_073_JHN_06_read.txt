John.
6.
Hiche jou hin, Jesu chun Galilee dillen, Tiberias dillen tia jong kihe agalkaiyin alangkhat achun achetan ahi. 
Chuin mihonpi tamtah in, melchihna kidang abol ho leh anaho asuhdam amu jeh u chun achena chan'a anung ajui tauve. 
Chuin Jesu chu mol chung khat a akal touvin, aseijui ho toh atoukhom uvin ahi. 
Hichu Judah ho Kalchuh Kut nikho koncha ahitai. 
Hichun mihonpi tamtah ama henglam jon'a ahung'u chu Jesun amun Philip jah a, “Mipi hijat neh ding changlhah hoiya ichoh dingu hitam?” ati. 
Hiche hi Philip patepna a asei ahi bouve, aman abol ding ageltohsa ahitai. 
Hichun Philip in adonbut in, “Eihon kum lhungin na tong jong leuhen mi hijat in themkhat khat a aneh khop u chohna ding kinga jou ponte,” ati. 
Chuin Simon Peter sopipa, Andrew chun ajah uva. 
“Hilaiya chapang khat in changlhah pheng nga leh nga ni aneiye, ahivangin mipi hijat lah a hicheng hi ipia pang kham ding ham,” ati. 
Hichun Jesun ajah uva, “Miho jouse chu tousah un,” ati; chuin amaho chu abon'un hamhing chung'a chun atou tauve. (Numei chapang simlouvin mipilhing sangnga jen ahiuve). 
Chuin Jesun changlhah chu alan, Pathen athangvah in miho hoppeh dingin apedohtai; chujouvin aman nga jong chutima chun abol in, chuleh mipi ho chun avaset cheh in ane tauve. 
Hiti chun amitakip ava soh phat un, Jesun aseijui ho jah a, “Tun neh moh chengse loh khom'un, moh suhmang hih un,” ati. 
Chuin ama hon changlhah pheng nga-a kon'a mipi nehmoh anong aval chengse aloh khom'un ahileh paipeh som le paipeh ni aloh dim'un ahi. 
Chuin ama hon, Jesun hiche melchihna kidang abol chu amu phat un, atija behseh un, “Eihon themgao ina nga jingu chu ama hi ahi mong monge,” atiuve. 
Chuin ama hon Jesu chu ada acham louva alengpau hisah ding agotauve, ti ahet jeh in, ama achangin thinglhang lam'a akilep mangtan ahi. 
Chuin nilhah ahiphat in aseijui te dillen pang lam'a acheuvin, Jesu angah tauve. 
Ahivangin khothim geiya Jesu ahung kile lou phat in, amaho kongsunga alut un, Capernaum lam jon'in akitol tauvin ahi. 
Chuin phulou helouvin hui hattah khat in amaho chu ahinno khum'in twi kinong chu asang jep asang jep in, akhohse lheh jengtai. 
Hiti chun amaho mile li vel akitol lhungun, phulou helouvin Jesun amaho lam jon'a twi chunga lam ahinjot chu amu uve. Akicha lheh jeng'un, 
ahivangin Jesun amaho akouvin, “Kicha hih un. Keima hichea kaum'e!” ati. 
Chuphat in aseijui ten, Jesu chu kong sunga lutsah loi ding athanom'un hetmanlou kah chun ajotnau mun alhung tauve. 
Ajingin dillen panga mihonpi umho chun, seijui hon kong khat umsun chu alah uva akitol'u chu amu uvin chuleh amaho lah a Jesu ajaopoi ti aheuvin ahi. 
Chuleh Tiberias a kon'a hunglhung kong dang tamtah, Jesun changlhah phatthei aboh a mipin anehnau mun kom'a chu uma ahi. 
Hijeh chun mihonpi chun, Jesu leh aseijui te chukom'a aum tapouve ti amu phat un amaho jong kong hoa chun alut un Jesu hol in Capernaum lang agalkai tauvin ahi. 
Chuin ama hon dil gal lang khat a Jesu amu phat un, “Rabbi nangma itih a hilai mun nalhun ham?” atiuve. 
Chuin Jesun amaho chu adonbut in, “Keiman nangho tahbeh a ka seipeh nahiuve, nang hon keima neihol'u hi neh ding changlhah kapeh jeh a bou ahin, melchihna kidang kabol ho nahet chen jeh uva ahipoi. 
Hijeh chun, amang thahji neh ding holnan hibang lom'in kisugim hih un, hisang chun mihem chapan napeh ding'u tonsot hinna joh chu hol'in kisugim jouvin; ajeh chu Pa Pathen'in keima hiche dinga chu mohor einamdet ahitai,” ati. 
Chuin ama hon adonbut un, “Keihon jong Pathen'a ding na katoh nom uve; ipi kabol dingu ham?” atiuve. 
Jesun ajah uva, “Hiche bou hi Pathen'in nanghoa kon'a adei natoh chu ahi, ahinsolpa hi tahsan un,” ati. 
Hichun ama hon adonbut un, “Nang katahsan theina ding'un ipi ham na bolthei melchihna kidang khat neimu sah un,” atiuve. 
“Chule kapu kapateu chun gamthip ajotlaiyun manna aneuvin ahi, hichu Pathen Lekhabun asei Mose in amaho chu van'a konna changlhah neh ding apeuve,” kiti chu ahi. 
Chuin Jesun ajah uva, “Keiman nangho atahbeh ka seipeh nahiuve, Mose in van'a kon changlhah apekhapoi, kapan apeh ahi, chuleh tun nangho dingin van'a kon changlhah tahbeh chu apetai. 
Pathen a kon'a changlhah tahbeh chu, van'a konna hung kumlha-a vannoiya hinna pea ama bou chu ahi.” 
Chuin ama hon ajah a, “Pu chuche changlhah chu keiho aniseh in nei peuvin,” atiuve. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Keima hinna changlhah kahi, koi hileh kaheng'a hung jouse gilkel kitlou ding, chuleh koi hileh keima eitahsan jouse a itih a dangchah kitlou ding ahi.” 
Ahivangin nang hon keima neimu vang'un nei tahsan deh pouve. 
Hijongleh, Paneipeh chengse ho chu kaheng'a hung ding ahiuve, chuleh keiman amaho chu kaledoh louhel ding ahi. 
Ajeh chu keima eihin solpa Pathen lunglam bol dinga van'a kon'a hung kumlha kahin keima lunggot bol dinga hung kahipoi. 
Chuleh Pathen lunglam chu hiche ahi, Aman keima eipeh chengse hi khatcha mansah louva, ninunung nileh amaho kakai thou ding ahi. 
Ajeh chu hiche hi kapa lunglam ahi, Chapa mua chuleh tahsan jousen tonsot hinna anei ahitai; keiman amaho chu ninunung nileh kakai thou ding ahi,” ati. 
Chuin Judah ho chu lunglhai lou tah in aphun gam tauve, ajeh chu Jesun, “Keima van'a kon'a hung kumlha hinna changlhah kahi,” ati. 
Hichun amaho akihouvun, “Hiche pa hi Joseph chapa Jesu hilou ham? Apa leh anu kihe'a iti aman keima van'a kon'a hung kumlha kahi atithei ham?” atiuve. 
Ahivangin Jesun adonbut in, “Kathusei ho jeh in phun hih un. 
Ajeh chu keima eihinsol'a Pa chun kaheng'a aloilut ho tilou chu koima ama cham in, kaheng'a ahung theipoi, chuleh keiman amaho chu ninunung nileh kakai thou ding ahi. 
Pathen Lekhabu'a 'Pathen'in amaho abon uva ahil ding ahi,' kiti bang chun, koi hileh Pa thu ngaiya chuleh ama a kon'a kihil ho bou chu kaheng'a hungji ahiuve. 
(Chule koiman Pathen amu khapoi; Pathen'a kon hung kisol Keima bouvin Pathen chu kamu ahi.”) 
Keiman nangho tahbeh a ka seipeh nahiuve, koi hileh ama tahsan chan chun tonsot hinna anei ahitai. 
Ahi, Keima hinna changlhah kahi. 
Napu napa teuvin gamthip a manna aneuvin, ahivangin amaho abon'un athi sohhel tauve. 
Koi hileh van'a kon'a changlhah neho vang chu a itih a thi talou ding ahi.” 
Keima van'a kon hung kumlha changlhah hing chu kahi, koi hileh hiche changlhah neho chu tonsot a hing ding ahi; chuleh vannoi hinna dinga kapeh ding changlhah chu katahsa hi ahi.” 
Chuin miho chu khat leh khat Jesu thusei doh chun akinel'un, “Iti hiche pan atahsa chu eiho neh dinga apeh thei ding ham?” atiuvin ahi. 
Hichun Jesun adonbut in, “Keiman nangho tahbeh a ka seipeh nahiuve, nang hon Mihem Chapa sa naneh lou uva chuleh athisan nadonlou uleh, nasung uva tonsot hinna nanei lou dingu ahi. 
Ahivangin koi hileh kasa ne'a, chuleh kathisan don'a vang chun tonsot hinna anei ahitai. Chuleh keiman ama chu ninunung nileh kakai thou ding ahi. 
Ajeh chu neh ding tahbeh chu kasa ahin, chule don ding tahbeh chu kathisan ahi. 
Hijeh chun koi hileh kasa ne'a chuleh kathisan don'a chu keima sunga ama aum'in chuleh ama sunga keima kaum'in ahi. 
Chule eihinsol'a hingjing Pa jal'a keima hing kahi; hiche bang chun keima'a kon'a kivah chu keima jal'a hing ding ahi. 
Ajeh chu keima van'a kon'a hung kumlha changlhah tahbeh chu kahi, hijeh chun koi hileh hiche changlhah ne-a chu, napu napa teuvin Manna ana neh uva athi sohhel'u bang hita louva, amaho chu tonsot a hingjing ding ahiuve.” 
Thu hicheng hi aman Capernaum kho a kikhopna in'a athuhil pet a asei ahi. 
Chuin aseijui ten ajah a, “Hiche thu hi hetchen ahahsa val'e koiman sangthei ponte,” atiuve. 
Hichun Jesun aseijui te alung himo thim'u chu ahen, ajah uva, “Hiche ka thusei hin nalungu asuh noh phah hitam? 
Achutileh nang hon Mihem Chapa van'a lahtouva aum mukit leu chun, ipi nati tadiuvem? 
Lhagao bouvin tonsot hinna apehji ahi, Mihem tahsa kihabol in ima abukim joupoi, chuleh keiman najah uva kasei doh thucheng ho hi, abon'a Lhagao leh hinna thu ahi. 
Ahivangin nalah uva keima eitahsan lou naum uve,” ati. (Ajeh chu Jesun atil'a pat a koi ham ama tahsan lou chuleh koiham ama pedoh ding ti ahetsa ahitai.) 
Chuin aman ajah uva, “Hiche jeh a chu Pan eipeh lou chu mi koima kaheng'a ahung theipoi, tia kasei ahi,” ati. 
Hiche apat chun, aseijui ho tamtah ache mangun, ama adalha tauvin ahi. 
Chuin Jesun som le ni ho jah a adongin, “Nanghon jong nei dalhah dingu ham?” ati. 
Hichun Simon Peter in adonbut in, “Pakai koi henga kache ding'u ham? Tonsot hinna pe-a thunei nangbou nahi. 
Keihon nangma Pathen theng khat seh nahi kaheuve, hichu katahsan uve,” ati. 
Chuin Jesun ajah uva, “Keiman nangho som le ni kalhendoh nahiuvin, ahivangin nalah uva satan khat aum'e!” ati. 
Hichu aman alhen doh som le ni ho lah a Simon Iscariot chapa Judas in khonunga ama apehdoh ding aseina ahi.
