John.
7.
Hiche jou chun, Jesun Galilee gamsung muntin achon'in avahlen ahi, ajeh chu Judah vaipo hon ama tha dinga agot jeh un, ama Judea gam'a chun aumnom tapon ahi. 
Ahivangin hiche pet chu Judah te Lhambuh Kut kon cha ahijeh chun, 
Asopi ten Jesu jah a, “Hilai mun dalhan lang Judea gam'a chen, chukom'a chu na seijui ten thil kidang nabol ho hi amuthei ding'u ahi. 
Hitia nakisel leh iti namin kithang ding ham? Hitobang thil kidang ho hi nabol ding leh vannoi kimu sah in,” atiuve. 
Ajeh chu asopi ho jengin jong ama chu atahsan lou ahi. 
Hichun Jesun adonbut in, “Tuhi kei chena dinga phatpha ahipoi, ahivangin nangho chena dingin phat jouse ahitheiye. 
Chuleh vannoiyin nangho nadou pouve, keima bou eidou'u ahi, ajeh chu ama hon setna aboljeh uva kaphoji ahi. 
Chule nangho cheuvin, keima chethei ponge, ajeh chu keima phat alhing jeppoi,” ati. 
Chule thil hicheng aseijou chun Jesu Galilee gam'a aumden in ahi. 
Ahivangin asopiho kut a achejou nung'un, mipi ho kimusah louvin, Jesu jong guhthim in achen ahi. 
Kut lai chun Judah vaipo hon Jesu chu ahol'un, mipi lah a khat khat henga chun Jesu namu em? tin adong leuvin ahi. 
Hichun mipi ho lah a, Jesu chung chang kinel baotamna asohdoh tan ahi, mi phabep chun, Jesu chu mipha ahi, atiuvin, ahivangin adang phabep chun Jesu chu imacha hilou mi lhem chavai bou ahi, atiuvin ahi. 
Ahivangin Judah vaipo hon abol genthei diu ginna jal chun, Jesu thudol panpina mipi maiya ngamtah a seiphong ding koima aum pouvin ahi. 
Chuin kut kibol kimlai tah chun, Jesu Hou-In'ah ache touvin chuleh thu ahilpan tan ahi. 
Athusei ajah u chun Judah ho chu alungdong lheh un, “Hiche pa hin kijilna neilouhel 'a iti thil hijat hi ahet ham?” atiuvin ahi. 
Hijeh chun Jesun ajah uva, “Ka thuhil hi keima cham'a hung kon ahipon, eihin sol'a Pathen'a kon ahi. 
Koi hileh Pathen lunglam hol'a bol ding go'a chun ka thuhil hi keima cham'a ham ahilouleh Pathen'a kon ham ti ahet ding ahi. 
Ajeh chu ama cham'a thuhil chun, ama chang kichoi-atna adeiyun, ahivangin ahinsolpa jabolna leh choi-atna holpa vang chun thutah asei ahi, ama a chun jou aumpoi. 
Mose in danthu napeh u ahin, ahivangin nangho khat in jong najui pouve, nang hon keima tha tahtah dinga neigot jingu ahi,” ati. 
Chuin mipi chun adonbut un, “Koiham nang tha dinga nagong'ah? Nang hi thilhavop nahi bouve,” atiuve. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Keiman Cholngah nikhoa thil kidang khat kabolin, nang hon nadatmo lheh uve. 
Ahivangin nang hon jong nabol uve, Mose dan thupeh palkeh lou bangtah in Cholngah nikhon mi chep natan peh jiuve. (Tahbeh in, cheptan hi Mose dan thupeh um masanga napului teuvin ana phudohsa ahitai.) 
Chuleh nang hon nachapa teu cheptan nikho chu Cholngahni atokhah jongleh Mose dan thupeh palkeh lou bangin hichu nabol jiuve, achutile ipi dinga keiman Cholngah nikho'a mi ka suhdam chu nang hon na lunghan piu ham? 
Kun’un lang veuvin, chutileh dihtah a thu natan thei ding’u ahi,” ati. 
Hichun Jerusalem miho chu amaho leh amaho akidongto uvin, “Vaipo hon tha dinga agot jing'u chu hiche pa hi hilou ham? 
Amapa la hilaiya aum in, phongtah in mipi lah a thu aseijin, chuleh vaipo hon jong ima alopouve. Achutileh ama hon jong hiche pa hi Messiah ahi ti apom ngap u ahi tadem? 
Ahivangin amapa hi hichu iti hithei ding ham? Eihon ama ahung konna kihe sohkeiya, Messiah vang chu ahung tengleh koiman ahung konna ahetlou ding, hung kilah jeng ding ahi bouve,” atiuvin ahi. 
Chuin Jesun Hou-In'a athuhil pet chun, ajah uva, “Adih e, nang hon keima neihe uve, chuleh kahung konna jong naheuve, ahivangin keima kacham'a hilaiya um kahipoi, keima eihinsolpa chu thudihtah ahi, chuleh nang hon ama chu nahe pouve. 
Ahivangin keiman ama kahe chen'e, ajeh chu keima ama a hung kon kahi, chuleh aman keima naheng uva eihinsol ahi,” ati. 
Chuin vaipo hon ama chu mat ding agouvin, ahivangin ama phat alhin loulai jeh in, koiman achunga khut alha pouve. 
Hichun Hou-In'a um mi tamtah chun Jesu atahsan tauve, chule amaho akihouvun, “Messiah ahung teng, hiche pan abol kalval'a nasajo melchihna kidang abolnah ding ham?” atiuvin ahi. 
Chuin mihonpi chu, Jesu thudol akihou lhih melmul'u chu Pharisee hon ajahdoh phat un, thempu pipuiho toh kijaton, Jesu man dingin Hou-In ngah ho chu asol tauvin ahi. 
Ahivangin Jesun ajah uva, “Keima nangho toh phat chomcha bou kaum khom ding ahi, chuleh keima eihinsolpa heng'a kakile kit ding ahi. 
Nanghon keima neihol'un natin, neimulou dingu ahi, chuleh keima chena a chu nangho nahung theilou dingu ahi,” ati. 
Chuin Judah vaipo ho chun, hiche Jesu thusei chu adatmo lheh un ahi, amaho akidong touvin, “Hiche pa hi hoilanga che ding kigo ham? Oleh, ama hi hiche gam dalha a mun danga um Judah ho henga che ding kigo ahidem? Ama hin Greek miho jeng jong agahil got hinte. 
Chuleh ipi aseina mong ahidem? Nanghon neihol'un natin, neimulou dingu chuleh keima chena'a chu nangho nahung theilou dingu atihi,” akitiuvin ahi. 
Chuin kut kichaini aniloupi pen nin, Jesu mihonpi lah a ading doh in, ajah uva, “Koi hileh adangchah a chu kaheng'a hunghen! 
Koi hileh keima eitahsan'a chu kaheng'a hunghen chuleh hungdon hen! Ajeh chu Pathen Lekhabun aphondoh banga 'ama lungsunga kon'a chu twi hing luiya longdoh ding ahi,'” tin asamtai. 
(Aman “twi hing,” ati chu ama tahsan jouse chunga Lhagao aum ding aseina ahi. Ahivangin Jesu chu aloupina a alut loulai jeh in hiche Lhagao chu kipe loulai ahiuve.) 
Chuin mihonpi chun, hiche asei chu ajauvin, alah uva phabep chun, “Hiche pa hi inga jingu themgao pa chu hitei jinte,” atiuvin ahi. 
Chuleh adang phabep chun, “Amapa hi Messiah ahi,” atiuvin ahi, ahivangin adang phabep chun, “Hiponte, iti Messiah chu Galilee gam'a hungsoh doh thei ding ham?” atiuvin ahi. 
Ajeh chu Pathen Lekhabu a kicheh tah a kiphongdohsa bangin, Messiah chu David chilhah hi ding, David khopi Bethlehem a kon hi ding ahi bouve, atiuvin ahi. 
Hijeh chun, Jesu chung chang thua, mihonpi chu akikhen tel gam tauvin ahi. 
Chuleh alah uva mi phabep chun, Jesu chu mat tei ding angaito uvin, ahivangin koiman achunga khut alhapouve. 
Chuin Hou-In ngah sottol le ho chu Jesu manlouva ahung kile phat'un, thempu pipuiho leh Pharisee ho chun ajah uva, “Ibol'a ama nahin puilut lou uham?” tin adongun ahi. 
Hou-In ngah sottol le hon adonbut'un, “Keihon hiche pa thusei tobang kaja khapouve,” atiuvin ahi. 
Hichun Pharisee hon anuisat un, “Nangho jong lhep lhah a na um'u hitam? 
Eiho lah a vaipo ho leh Pharisee ho khat beh injong amapa chu atahsan em? 
Khohelou mihonpi in dan thupeh ima ahetlou jeh uva ama nung ajuiyu ahi bouve, chuleh achung uva Pathen sapset chuding ahi,” atiuve. 
Hichun masanga Jesu toh gakimuto sa vaipo lah a khat amin Nicodemus chun, ajah uva; 
“Mikhat chu ama thu kija masa louva, achunga themmo chansah jeng chu dan ngaina ham?” ati. 
Chuin ama hon adonbut un, “Nang jong Galilee mi nahim? Pathen Lekhabua phet inlang sim'in Galilee gam'a kon'a themgao sohdoh khalouhel ahi,” atiuve. 
Hichun akikhop nau aki khen'un mijousen a in cheh akijot tauvin ahi.
