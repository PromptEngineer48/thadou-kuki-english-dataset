John.
9.
Chuin Jesu chu avahlen ahileh, apen'a mitcho'a peng mikhat amun ahi. 
Hichun aseijui ten ajah a, “Houhil, hiche pa hi ipi jeh a apen'a mitcho'a peng ham? Ama chonse ham? Oleh anu leh apa chonse a ham?” atiuve. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Hiche hi ama chonset jeh jong ahipon anu leh apa chonset jeh jong ahipoi.” “Hiche hi Pathen thahatna ama chunga akiphkonna dinga ahi bouve. 
Eihon kinloi tah a eisol pauvin einganse nau hi itoh dingu ahitai. Jan ahunglhung'e, chule koiman na atoh theilou ding ahitai. 
Ahivangin keima hiche vannoiya kaum laisea hi vannoi vah kahi,” ati. 
Chuin aman tol'ah achil aset in, achil a chun bonlhoh asemin chuleh hiche bonlhoh chu mitchopa mit a aloiyin ahi. 
Hichun aman ajah a, “Chen Siloam twikul'a nangma tah in gaki phetlhah in,” (Siloam chu “sol” tina ahi) ati. Hijeh chun mipa chu achen aga kiphetlhah tai, chuleh kho amun ahung kile tan ahi. 
Chuin aheng akom achenkhompi ho chuleh masanga amapa khutdoji musa ho chu akidong touvin, “Toumun'a khutdoji pa chu hiche pa hi hilou ham?” atiuvin ahi. 
Mi phabep chun “Amapa chu ahi” atiuvin, chuleh adang phabep chun, “Amapa chu ahipoi akilopi joh ahi bouve,” atiuvin ahi; hichun amapa chun, “Henge ama chu kahinai,” ati jeng tan ahi. 
Chuin ama hon adong'un, “koiham kho namu sah? ipi iti dan ham?” atiuvin ahi. 
Hichun amapan ajah uva, “Jesu atipau chun bonlhoh asemin, hichu kamit a eiloi peh in chuin kajah a ‘chen Siloam twikul'a nangma tah in gaki phetlhah tan eiti,’ hijeh chun keima kachen kaga phetlhah leh kho kahin mu ahitai,” ati. 
Chuin ama hon adongun, “Amapa chu tua hoiya um tam?” atiuvin ahi, aman adonbut in, “Kahepoi,” ati. 
Chuin ama hon masanga mitcho pa chu Pharisee ho henga ahinpui tauvin ahi. 
Ajeh chu Jesun bonlhoh asem'a amapa asuhdam nikho chu Cholngahni ahi. 
Chuin Pharisee hon amapa chu amit khomu dol abonchan adongun ahi, hijeh chun aman ajah uva, “Aman bonlhoh chu kamit a aloiyin, chuin keiman kaga phetlhah leh kho kamu jeng ahitai,” ati. 
Chuin Pharisee ho phabep chun, “Hiche mipa Jesu hi Pathen a kon ahipoi, ajeh chu aman Cholngah nikho apalkeh e atiuvin ahi.” Ahivangin adang phabep chun, “Iti michonse khat in hitobang melchihna kidang ho hi abolthei ding ham?” atiuvin, amaho lah a chun kibung khenna lentah aumdoh tan ahi. 
Chuin Pharisee hon, mitcho kho mupa chu adong kit un, “Namit kho namu sahpa chu nangman koi ahi natim?” atiuvin, aman adonbut in, “Themgao khat hiteiyinte,” ati. 
Hiche jeng jong chun, Judah vaipo hon hiche mipa chu, mitcho anahia tua kho amu ahitai, tichu atahsan pouve, hijeh chun ama hon anu leh apa akimu piuvin ahi. 
Chuleh ama hon anu leh apa chu adong'un, “Hiche pa hi apen'a mitchoa peng'a hinam? Ahileh tua iti kho amuthei ham?” atiuve. 
Hichun anu leh apan adonbut in, “Amahi apen'a mitchoa peng kachapa lhon ahi, kahe lhon e,” ati. 
“Ahivangin aman iti kho amu ham, koiyin amit adamsah ham, kahe lhonpoi; ama dong'un, ama tah achungthu kiseitheiya upa ahitai,” atilhon e. 
Hichu anu leh apan Judah vaipo ho kichatna jeh a achuti seilhon ahi, ajeh chu ama hon, “Koi hileh Jesu chu Messiah ahi tia sei aum leh kikhopna in'a kon'a nomang ding tia ana phondoh sau ahitai.” 
Hiche jeh a chu amanin ajah uva, “Ama kidoh un ama tah chu athu kisei theija len ahitai,” atilhon ahi. 
Chuin ama hon anivei nan mitcho khomupa chu akouvun, ajah a, “Hiche thua hi Pathen bou loupi sah in, ajeh chu Jesu kitipa chu michonse ahi, keihon kaheuve,” atiuvin ahi. 
Hichun aman adonbut in, “Ama michonse ahi ahilou kahepoi, ahivangin kahet chu hiche ahi, ‘keima mitcho kana hin tunvang kho kamutai,’” ati. 
Chuin ama hon adongkit tei teiyun, “Ama chun ipi abol'a iti namit chu asuhdam ham?” atiuve. 
Aman ajah uva, “Veuvin khatvei kaseisa ahitai, nang hon nangai pouve, ibol'a avel'a najah nom kit uham? Nangho jong ama seijui nahi nom'u ham?” ati. 
Chuin ama hon mipa chu agaosap un, ajah a, “Nangbou ama seijui nahin, keiho vang Mose seijui kahiuve. 
Pathen'in Mose heng'a thu asei chu kaheuve, ahinlah hiche pa hi hoiya hungkon koi ahi kahe pouve,” atiuvin ahi. 
Chuin amapan adonbut in, “Hichu datmo umtah ahi, aman kamit kho musahta a ahivanga nang hon ama hung konna nahetlou u chu; 
Pathen'in michonse ho thu angaipoi, ahivangin ama guttah a houva alunglam bol ho thu bou angaiye, ti iheuve. 
Chuleh vannoi leiset umtil apat koiham khat in, apen'a mitchoa peng kho amusah lam akija khapoi. 
Hiche pa hi Pathen'a kon ahilouleh aman hitobang hi abolthei louhel ding ahi,” ati. 
Hichun ama hon adonbut un, “Nang napum'a chonse sa a peng'a nangin keiho neihil got uham?” atiuvin, kikhopna in'a kon chun anodoh tauvin ahi. 
Jesun thilsoh umchan chu ajan, chuin mipa chu amun, ajah a, “Nangman Mihem Chapa chu na tahsan em?” ati. 
Amapan adonbut in, “Pu, ama chu koi ham? Keiman katahsan ding nati chu,” ati. 
Chuin Jesun adonbut in ajah a, “Nangman ama chu namutai, chuleh nahenga thuseiya hi ama chu ahi!” ati. 
Hiche pettah chun mipa chun, ajah a, “Henge Pakai katahsan e,” atin, Jesu chu chibai abohtai. 
Chuin Jesun amapa jah a, “Keima mitcho hon kho amu uva chuleh khomu a kigel ahivanga la mitcho, hon akigeldoh theina ding uva thutan dinga vannoiya hung kahi,” ati. 
Hichun avel'a um Pharisee hon ajah a, “Keiho hi mitcho nei hisah uham?” atiuve. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Mitcho hileu chun chonsetna naneilou dingu ahin, tunvang nang hon kho kamui nati jeh un nachung uva chonset um ahitai,” ati.
