John.
10.
“Keiman atahbeh ka seipeh nahiuve, koi hileh kelngoi hongkot a lut louva bang'a kal'a hunglut chu gucha michom ahi! 
Ahivangin kelngoi hongkot a hunglut amavang chu kelngoi chingpa ahi. 
Ama chu kot ngahpan kot ahonpeh jin, chuleh kelngoi hon ama aw aheuvin, aheng'a ahung jiuve, chule aman akelngoite chu amin cheh in akouvin, apuidoh jin ahi. 
Chule aman akelngoi ho ahoidoh soh tengleh, amaho chu alamkaiyin, chuleh akelngoi hon anung ajui jiuve, ajeh chu ama hon amapa aw chu aheuve. 
Chuleh kelngoi hon hetphahlou midang nungjui louhel ding ahiuve, ama a kon'a jammang ding ahiuve ajeh chu ama hon michom dang aw ahepouve.” 
Jesun hiche thulem asei jaho chun, aman ipi aseina ahi ahepouvin, 
hijeh chun Jesun ajah uva, asei chenin, “Keiman nangho atahbeh ka seipeh nahiuve, keima hi kelngoi hongkot chu kahi. 
Ka masanga hung ho jouse abon uva gucha leh michom ahiuve. Ahinlah amaho thu chu kelngoi hon angaipeh pouve. 
Henge, keima kelngoi hongkot chu kahi. Koi hileh keiya hunglut a chu huhhing'a um ding ahi. Amaho chu nom tah a lut le pot le dingu chuleh hamhing hoitah akimu dingu ahi. 
Guchapa hunglona chu agu mang ding leh asumang ding bou ahi, keima vang ama hon ninglhinna leh lunglhaina hinkho aneina ding uva hunga kahi.” 
“Keima kelngoi chingpha kahi, kelngoi chingpha chun akelngoite dingin ahinna apejin ahi. 
Ahin thalah kelngoi chingpa vang chun, ngei hung amu teng ama ajamjin chuleh akelngoite adalha jitai; ajeh chu ama kelngoi chingtah ahipon, kelngoite jong ama a ahipoi, chutengleh ngei chun kelngoite chu adelkhum in aman in chuleh asucheh jitan ahi. 
Ijeh inem itileh ama thalah kelngoi ching, sum mun'a ding bouva natong ahijeh chun, aman kelngoite ngailutna aneipoi.” 
“Keima kelngoi chingpha kahi, keiman ka kelngoite kahen, chuleh ama hon keima eiheuve. 
Kapan keima eihet tobang bangin keiman Pa chu kahei; hijeh chun, keiman ka kelngoite dingin ka hinna kapei. 
Chuleh keiman hiche hong sunga umlou kelngoi dang jong kaneiye, chule keiman amaho jong chu kaga puilut ding chuleh ama hon ka-aw ahet dingu chuteng kelngoi ho chu honkhat hiuva achingpa jong khat seh hi ding ahi.” 
“Pan keima eingailui, ajeh chu keiman ka hinna chu avel'a kilelah kit dinga kakatdoh tah jeh chun; 
Khattouvin, keiya kon'a ka hinna chu eilahpeh thei ahipoi, keiman kacham'a kakatdoh ahi bouve, ajeh chu keiman katdohna le avel'a kile lah kit theina chu kanei ahi. Hichu kapan keima eithupeh ahi,” ati. 
Aman thu hicheng asei jeh chun, Judah ho chu abung bungin akikhen kit tauve. 
Alah uva phabep chun, “Hiche pa hi thilhavop ahin alunggel kisuhlou ahi bouve, chule ibol'a ama thusei kingai ding ham?” atiuve. 
Chule adang phabep chun, “Athusei hi thilhavop um dan ahipoi, thilhan mitcho kho amusah ngaiyem?” atiuve. 
Hichu phalbilai ahin Hou-In katdohna kut abollaiyu ahin, Jesu Jerusalem'a aumpet ahi. 
Ama Hou-In'a aum in, Solomon hongcha kiti laiya chun ama avahlen ahi. 
Hichun Judah hon ama chu aumkimvel un, “Itihchan keiho lung hesoh a nei umsah ding'u ham? Nangma Messiah chu nahileh nei seipeh un,” atiuvin ahi. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Keiman nangho ka seipehsa ahitai, ahivangin nang hon nei tahsan pouve. Kapa min'a ka thilbol cheng hi Keima thudol hettohsah ahitai. 
Ahivangin nangho ka kelngoite nahilou jeh un, keima nei tahsan pouve. 
Keima kelngoi hon ka aw angaiyun, keiman amaho kahen, chuleh ama hon kanung eijui uve. 
Keiman amaho chu tonsot hinna kapen, chuleh amaho chu itih a jong mangthah louding ahiuve. Chuleh koiman keiya kon'a amaho chu eilahpeh louding ahi. 
Ajeh chu kapan amaho chu keima eipeh ahitai, chuleh kapa chu ima jouse'a thaneipen leh hatchungnung ahi. Koiman amaho chu kapa khut a kon'a alahpeh louding ahi. 
Chuleh Pa leh keima chu khat seh kahi lhon e,” ati. 
Chuin Judah hon ama selihna ding song akilo tauvin ahi. 
Jesun ajah uva, “Kapa thusah bangtah a keiman thilpha tamtah kabol ahitai. Hoiche pentah jeh a chu nang hon keima song'a neise dingu ham?” ati. 
Ama hon adonbut un, ajah a, “Keihon nang napha toh jeh a songa kase dingu nahipoi, Pathen na taitom jeh ahi bouve. Nangma mihem hiya Pathen naki hisah jeh ahi,” atiuve. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Hiche hi nangho Pathen Lekhabua kisun hilou ham? Pathen'in mihem ho jah a anaseisa, 'nangho hi pathen ho nahiuve kiti chu kasei ahi!'. 
Chuleh nang hon naheuve, Pathen Lekhabua kisun chu khel thei ahipoi. Hiche jeh chun, Pathen thu lhunna miho bon 'pathen ho' tia akikou thei leh; 
ibol'a ken, 'keima Pathen chapa kahi' tia kasei leh nang hon Pathen taitom nahi neitiu ham? Pan hiche dinga chu keima atumbeh a eikhen doh a eisuhtheng'a vannoiya eihinsol ahi. 
Eisol'a kapa natoh ho kabol louleh nei tahsan dauvin. 
Ahin, ama kin katoh ahivang leh, keima nei tahsan theilou jongleh, melchihna kidang kabol ho hi hettohsah nan neiyun lang, tahsan un. Chutileh nang hon Pa keiya aum'a chuleh keima Pa a kaum chu hetauvin nate,” ati. 
Chuin avel'in ama hon Jesu chu matding ago kit un, ahi. Ahivangin ama hiche mun'a kon chun apotdoh in amaho chu adalhatai. 
Chuin ama Jordan lui gallam'a amasa a John in baptize ana bolna lam'a chun achen aga umtai. 
Chua chun, mi tamtah in anung ajuiyun, amaho chu akihouvun, “John in melchihna kidang ho ana bolpon, ahivangin Jesu thudol aseiho abonchan atah in alhung sohkeiye,” atiuvin ahi. 
Chuin chukom'a um mi tamtah ho chun Jesu atahsan tauve.
