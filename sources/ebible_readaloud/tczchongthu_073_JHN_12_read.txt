John.
12.
Kalchuh Kut kipatna ding nigup masang'in, athi a konna athodoh Lazarus umna Bethany a chun Jesu ahungin, 
Hiti chun ama dingin jan an asemun, chuleh Marthan ajenlen Lazarus vang chu ankonga ama toh touho lah a chun apangin ahi. 
Chuin Mary in halpi bei Bego thao namtui man tamtah chu ahin choiyin Jesu kengphanga anun asamin atheh ngimin ahileh agimnam chun insung chu alosoh jengin ahi. 
Chuin seijuiho lah a khat Simon chapa Judas Iscariot, amatsah a pang dingpa chun, 
“Hiche thao namtui hi manlutah ahi. Man tamtah a joh a mivaicha gentheiho peh ding ahi,” ati. 
Aman hiche ati chu vaicha genthei khohsah a asei ahipoi, ama chu gucha ahin, chule sumdip avop ahin, asunga kikoi chu akilahji ahi. 
Hichun Jesun adonbut in, “Amanu chu achangin dalha uvin. Kakivui nia dinga kigontup theina a abol ahi. 
Nanghon genthei chaga aphat seh a nanei jing dingu ahi, ahin keima vang aphat seh a nangho lah a umjou louding kahi,” ati. 
Chuin mipi hon Jesu chukom'a ahunge ti ajah phat un, Judah ho mi tamtah ama chung chang jeng hilouvin, athilah a konna athodoh Lazarus jong chu venomin ahenga ahung tauve. 
Hichun thempu pipui hon Lazarus jeng jong thadoh dingin agongun ahi. 
Ajeh chu amapa jeh tah a jong chu miho lah a tamtah pin amaho anuse uva Jesu atahsan u ahitan ahi. 
Hiche ni ajingin kut a dinga hung mipi atama tamin, Jesu Jerusalem'a dingin ahunge, ti ajah phat un, 
Lusu na akidop un aga lamto uvin, “Hosana, Pakai min a hunga Israel te Lengpa chu anunnome,” tin asam asamun ahi. 
Chuin Jesun sangan chalgol khat amun, achunga atouvin ahi; 
“Vo Zion chanu, kicha hih in; Ven, na lengpa sangan nou chunga atouvin ahunge,” tia kisun bang chun. 
Hicheng thu chu Jesu seijui ten amasan ana hepouvin ahi, ahin Jesu choiat a aumdoh phat chun, hiche thu chu ama thudol'a kisunsa ahin, ama hon hitia chu abol'u ahi tichu ageldoh tauve. 
Hichun aman Lazarus akouva athi lah a konna athodoh pettah a aumpi mipi chengse chu athu hettohsah a pang ahiuve. 
Hiche melchihna abol ajah jeh tah uva chu mipi chun alamtou ahi. 
Hichun Pharisee ho khat le khat akihou lim un, “Veuvin eihon imacha ibol thei dingu aumpoi; ajeh chu mijousen ama nung ajui cheh tauve,” atiuvin ahi. 
Hiche Kalchuh Kut a chu Pathen hou dinga Greek mi phabep khat chetou ahiuvin; 
Hichun amaho Galilee gam'a Bethsaida khoa mi Philip henga ahungun ajah a, “Pu, Jesu chu kamu nomui,” ahung tiuve. 
Hichun Philip ahungin Andrew jah a ahung seiyin, chuin Andrew ahungin Philip toh Jesu jah a asei lhone. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Mihem Chapa choiat a aum ding phat ahunglhung e. 
Thutah kaseipeh nahiuve, chang malkhat chu tol'a tulut in aumjie. Hichu athilou le achangin aumden ji bouve; athi vang le tamtah agajin ahi. 
Koi hijongleh hiche vannoi leiset chunga ahinkho itcha ho chu mangthah ding ahi; ahin koi hijongleh hiche vannoi leiset chunga ahinkho pedoh ho chun tonsot hinkemlou anei dingu ahi. 
Koi hijongleh kalhacha a panga chun kanung eijuihen, keima umna a chu kalhacha jong um ding ahi; mi koi hile kalhacha a panga chu Pa chun ajabol ding ahi.” 
“Tun kalung adongtai; ipi kati ding ham? 'Pa, hiche nidan a kon hin nei huhdoh in.' Ahivangin hiche dinga hi hung kahi. 
“Pa, namin choian'in” ati. Hiche chun van'a kon'in Aw ahung gingin, “ka choian tai, chule choian kit nalai ding kahi,” ati. 
Hiche chun akimvel a ding mipi chun hichu ajauvin, “Van aginge,” atiuvin; adangsen, “Vantil in ajah a thu asei ahi,” atiuve. 
Jesun amaho jah a, “Hiche awgin najah u chu nangma ho phatchomna ding ahi bouve, keima a ding ahipoi. 
Hiche vannoi leiset chunga thutanna phat ahunglhung tai, Satan vannoiya vaihompa nodohna phat jong hunglhung ding ahitai.” 
Chule keima leiset a konna ka thodoh tengleh keiman mijouse kaheng'a dinga kahung pui ding nahiuve.” 
Hichu aman athi ding dan tahlangna a hitia chu asei ahi. 
Mihonpi chun adonbut in, “Keihon la Messiah chu tonsot a hingjing ding ahi, ti Pathen Lekhabua kon'in kaheuvin; Ipi jeh a Mihem Chapa thi ding ahi nati thei ham? Hiche Mihem Chapa chu amonga koi ham?” atiuve; 
Jesun adonbut in, “Kavah hi nalah uva chomkhat bou um nalai ding ahi; muthim in naphah louna dingun vah umpet hin vahle uvin; muthima vahlea chun achena ding ahepoi. 
Vah nanei pet chau hin vah chate nahi theina dingun vah chu tahsanun,” ati. Thu hicheng chu aseijin, apotdoh in amahoa kon chun akisel tai.” 
Isaiah themgao thusei: “Pakai ka thuseiyu kon atahsan em? Chule Pakai ban thahat koi henga kilang am?” 
Tia asei chu guilhun theina ding chun, a angsung uva melchihna kidang tamtah abolin, ahivangin atahsan pouve. 
Hiche thua hin Isaiah themgao chun avel in aseikit in: 
“Aman amit u asuchon, alungu asutah tai; achuti loule amit un mu uvin tin, chule alungun heuvin tin, chule hung kihei kit un tin keiman boldam get inge,” ati; hiche jeh a hi ama hon atahsan theilou u ahi. 
Isaiah chun aloupi chu amu jeh a hicheng chu aseiya; amaho thudol chu asei ahi. 
Achuti vang'in vaipo ho jeng jong tamtah in atahsan tauve; ahinla Pharisee ho jeh chun kikhopna a kon'in eiki kaidoh khante, tin aseidoh aum pouve. 
Ajeh chu ama hon Pathen choiat sangin mihem choiat adei jouvin ahi. 
Hichun Jesun aw sangtah in asam in, “Koi hijongleh keima eitahsan chun keima hilouva eisolpa joh atahsan ahi bouve; 
Chule keima eimua chun eisolpa amu ahi. 
Koi hijongleh keima eitahsan a chu muthim a aum louna dinga keima vah hina a vannoiya hung kahi. 
Mikhat touvin kathu jahen nit hih jong le athu katanpoi; ijeh inem itile keima vannoi thutan'a hung kahipoi, vannoi huhhing ding joh a hung kahi bouve. 
Koi hijongleh keima eipampaiya ka thuhil jong joplou chu, thupi kitan nile thutah kaseidoh hin achungthu atan ding ahi. 
Ijeh inem itile keiman kacham'a kasei ahipoi, eisol'a Pa chun kasei ding le kapao ding dan hi thu eipeh ahi bouve. 
Ama thupeh chu tonsot hinna ahi, ti kahei; hiche jeh a chu ka thusei hi Pan eihil bang banga kasei ahi,” ati.
