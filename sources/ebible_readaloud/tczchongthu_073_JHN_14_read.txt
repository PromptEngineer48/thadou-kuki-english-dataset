John.
14.
“Nalung'u khamsah hih un. Pathen tahsan'un, chule keima jong nei tahsan'un. 
Kapa in'ah chenna ding indan ninglhingset in aum'e; umlou hile najah uva kasei tah hih ding ham? Ajeh chu keima nangho dinga mun sema che ding kahi. 
Thil jouse gontup ahi tengleh, keima hung ingting, kaumna a chu naum jing theina diuva kahung pui ding nahiuve. 
Chule nang hon kachena ding mun alampi nahe nauve,” ati. 
Thomas in adonbut in, “Pakai keihon na chena ding bon kihelou a alampi iti kahet dingu ham?” ati. 
Jesun amapa chu adonbut in, “Keima hi lampi le thutah chule hinna chu kahi; keima henga pat tailouvin koima Pa kom ahung theipoi. 
Chule nang hon keima nei heleu chun Kapa jong chu nahet dingu ahitai; tu apat a vang nang hon amapa chu naheuvin chule namu tauve!” ati. 
Hichun Philip in adonbut in, “Pakai, Pa chu neimu sah un, hichu keiho dinga phaset ahi,” ati. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Philip, keima hitih chan nangho toh kaum khom a jong chu neihet loulai ham? Chule keima eimua chun Ka-Pa amu ahitai; achutile Pa chu neimu sah-un iti nati ham? 
Chule keima Pa a kaum a chule Pa jong keiya um ahi, hiche hi na tahsan lou ham? Chule keiman nangho jah a thu kasei chengse hi keima cham'a kasei ahipoi; Pa chu keiya uma na atoh ahi bouve. 
Keima Pa a kauma chule Pa jong keiya aum'e ti chu nei tahsan jengun; ahilou jong le nakitong chengse jeh tah hin tahsanun.” 
“Atahbeh a, keima eitahsan'a chun ka thilbol chengse hi aman jong abol ding ahi; asanga lenjo jo abol ding ahi, chule keima Pa henga kinung lekit ding kahitai. 
Chule Pa chu Chapan achoiat a, chule nang hon thil jouse ipi hijongleh Keima min'a nathum chanu chu kabol peh ding nahiuve. 
Chule Keima min'a nathum dingu chu ipi hijongleh ka bolpeh ding nahiuve!” ati. 
“Keima nei ngailut uleh ka thupeh ho hi nanit dingu ahi. 
Keima Pa henga taoving ting chuleh aman nangho naumpi jing dingu Lhamonsah Lhagao napeh dingu ahi. 
Ama chu Lhagao Theng, thudih lampia napui uva ahi; vannoiyin ama chu asang theipoi; ijeh inem itile ama chu mu jong amu pouvin, he jong ahe pouve; nang hon vang naheuve, ajeh chu ama nangho sunga chenga ahi, um jong hung um ding ahi. 
Chule keiman nangho avetup ding beiyin na dalha pouvinge; keima naheng uva hung kit ding kahi. 
Vannoi mihon phat chomkhat jou le eimu tahlou dingu ahi, ahinla nang hon vang neimu dingu ahinai; Ajeh chu keima kahing jinge, chule nangho jong nahin dingu ahi. 
Keima hinna a ka thodoh kit teng, nang hon nahet chen diu ahi; keima Pa a kaum'a chule nangho keima a naum diu chule keima nangho a kaum ding ahi. 
Koi hijongleh ka thupeh ho neiya chule nit chu eingailua ahi; chule keima eingailua chu ka Pan angailut ding; chule keiman ama chu ka ngailut a ahenga kaki phon ding ahi,” ati. 
Judas (Iscariot hilou, seijui achom khatpa) chun ajah a, “Pakai, ibol'a nangma keiho henga naki phon'a vannoi mi henga naki phon louding ham?” ati. 
Jesun adonbut in ajah a, “Eingailu jouse chun kathu anit ding, chule ka Pan amaho chu angailut ding, chule aheng uva kahunga amaho chu kachenpi ding ahi, 
Chule koi hijongleh keima eingailu lou chun kathu jong anitpoi; chule nathu jah uhi keiya ahipoi, eisola Pa a chu ahi bouve. 
Keiman nangho kaumpi pet a thu hicheng hi ka seipeh nahiuve. 
Ahinla Pan keima khel'a naki thopi dingu Lhagao Theng ahinsol teng, Ama chun thil jouse nahetsah dingu chule thil jouse ka seidoh ho jouse avel'a nagel dohsah kit dingu ahi.” 
“Lungmon ka dalhah peh nahiuve; kalungmon kapeh nahiuve; kapeh hi vannoi mi peh bang ahipoi; nalungu donsah hih-un, kicha jong kicha hih-un. 
Naheng uva thu kana seiho geldoh jing uvin ajeh chu keima kache mang ding ahin, ahin naheng uva kahung kit ding ahi; chule keima nei ngailut mong ule kei sanga laljo Pa kom'a kache ding hi nang hon nei kipapi joh dingu ahi. 
Hiche ho hi naheng uva ka seipeh masat u ahi, Ajeh chu thil ho hi ahungso phat tengleh nang hon na tahsan theina dingu ahi.” 
“Nangho toh kihou limna ding phat kanei lhomtai, ajeh chu vannoi leiset chunga vaihompa ahung nai tai, aman ka chungah tha aneipoi. 
Amavang Pan keiya konna angaichat chu kabol ding ahi, chutile vannoi in Pa ngailutna hi ahin hetthei dingu ahi, hung uvin eiho che jengu hite.”
