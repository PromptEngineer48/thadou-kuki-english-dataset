John.
18.
Jesun thu hicheng chu aseijin apotdoh in Kidron vagal a hon umna a chun aseijui ho toh agalkaiyun, chuin honsunga chun ama le aseijui ho alut tauve. 
Jesu pedoh a pang Judas jong chun hilai mun chu ahet chen mama ahi, Ijeh inem itile Pakai Jesun aseijui te chu phat seh a ana pui jing ahi. 
Chutah in Judas chun sepaihon chule thempu pipuiho le Pharisee hoa konna sottol leho aloiyin, meivah, meisel, chuleh hem manchah athouvin hiche kom mun chu ahunglhung tauve. 
Jesun achunga thil hunglhung ho jouse chu ahesoh keiyin ahi, hichun amaho kom lang chu ajonphei in adongtai, “Koi nahol'u ham?” ati. 
Ama hon adonbut un, “Nazareth khoa konna hungkon Jesu chu kahol'u ahi,” atiuvin ahi. Jesun adonbut in, “Ama chu kahi,” ati. (Judas, apedoh a pangpa chu, amaho lah a chu dinga ahi). 
Jesun, “Keima hi kahi,” ati chun amaho jouse chu anung chon'un tol'a alhu jengun ahi. 
Hichun avel'in adongkit in, “Nanghon koi nahol'u ham?” ati. Chuin ama hon adonbut'un, “Nazareth khoa konna hung Jesu chu kahol'u ahi,” atiuve. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Keiman ka seipeh nahiuve, amapa chu keima hi kahi, keima neihol'u ahijeh chun midang ho hi soldoh tauvin,” ati. 
“Nangman keima neipeh chengse chu khatcha jong kaman sahpoi, tia asei chu guilhunna dinga chutia ahi.” 
Hichun Simon Peter in achemjam asatdoh in Thempu Chungnung sohpa asat le ajetlam abilkol asatlha tai, sohpa min chu Malchus ahi. 
Ahinla Jesun Peter jah a, “Na chemjam chu apai sunga koilut kit in, Pan eipeh gentheina khon chu don louva kaum ding ham,” ati. 
Hichun sepai ho le apipui pau toh chuleh Juda ho sottol lehon Jesu chu aman un akan tauvin ahi. 
Annas henga apui masauve; Ijeh inem itile ama chu chuche kuma chu Thempu Chungnung pen'a pang Caiaphas nupa pa ahi. 
Hiche Caiaphas hi ahi, “Miho jouse dinga mikhat athi aphajoi,” tia Juda ho ana thumop chu. 
Simon Peter chun, seijui dang ho bangin, Jesu chu anung ajui tan ahi. Seijui ho lah a khat chu Thempu pipuiho toh ana kihet jeh chun Thempu pipu pa umna gei chu Jesu toh ana lutsah un ahi. 
Ahin Peter vang chu polanga phat chomkhat aumin, hichun seijui ho lah a khat thempu pipuipa toh kihepa chun kelkot ngah a pang numeinu kom'a phalna athumin hichun Peter insunga apuilut tai. 
Hiche numeinu chun Peter chu thudoh aneijin, “Nangma hi hiche pa seijui ho khat nahi lou ham? Nangma nahi,” ati. Ahin Peter in adonbut in, “Kahipoi,” ati. 
Hiche kom'a chu sohho leh sottol le hon adapjeh a mei akitiuva dinmun'a mei aoiyu ahin, Peter jong adingin mei chu aoiyin ahi. 
Hikom insung langa chun Thempupan Jesu chu thudoh aneijin, anungjui ho ipi ana seipeh a chuleh ipi ana hilchah em tichu adong tan ahi. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Mipi hon ahesoh keiye ipi kana hilchah, chule Hou-In a jong aphat jouse in mipi ho kom'a kana seipeh jinge, guhthima kana seipeh imacha aumpoi. 
Ibol'a nangman hiche thudoh ho hi neidoh ham? ka thusei ngai ho le jaho chu nadoh theilou ham? Ajeh chu ama hon ka thuhil jouse chu ahe sohkei uve,” ati. 
Hichun Jesun thu hicheng chu aseijin ahile akom'a Santri ding ho lah a khat in Jesu ngeiphe abengin, “Thempu Chungnung hitia chu kidonbut ngaiyem?” ati. 
Hichun Jesun adonbut in, “Keiman kasei khel aum le neimu sah un, ahinla thutah kasei doh jeh a chu ibol'a neivoh uham?” ati. 
Hichun Annas chun Jesu chu ahentup in Caiaphas Thempu Chungnung heng lama asoltai. 
Hiche phat dontah a chu Simon Peter chun dap asah jeh a mei aki oi ahin, ama hon adongkit un, “Nangma hi amaho lah a khat chu nahilou ham?” atiuve. Ahin Peter in adonbut in, “Keima kahipoi,” ati. 
Ahinla Thempu Chungnung sohpa Simon Peter in abilkol asatlhah pehpa inko khat in ajah a, “Keiman hon a chu nangma amato kamu nahi lou ham?” atin ahi. 
Hichun avel'in Peter in aseilep kit in, chule hetman louvin Ahchal akhong tai. 
Chutah chun ama hon Caiaphas in a kon'in gamvaipo pa inlama Jesu akai tauve; hichu jingkah ahina laiye; ahivangin Kalchuh Kut an aneh theina dingun, akisuhboh louna dingun gamvaipo inpia chun amaho alut pouve. 
Hichun Pilate apotdoh in, amaho chu aga kimupin thudoh aneijin, “Hiche pa hin ipi abolkhel atoh khel um hitam?” ati. 
Hichun ama hon adonbut un, “Amapa khang-gilou ahilouleh nangma kom'a kahin lhut lou dingu ahi,” atiuve. 
Hichun Pilate in adonbut in, “Hiche chu ahitah le, nang hon nungpui tauvin, chuleh nangho dan dungjuijin kitan lhah tauvin,” ati. Hiche phat chun Judah hon adonbut un, “Keima ho Danthu dungjuijin koima thana ding tha kanei pouve,” atiuvin ahi. 
(Hiche hin avetsah chu, Jesun athi ding dan ana seidoh masah chu guilhunna ahi). 
Hichun Pilate insung dan'ah alut in, Jesu chu akouvin, thudoh aneijin, “Nangma Juda te lengpa chu nahim?” ati. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Hiche thudoh ho hi nangma chama kon ham, ahilou le mi khat touvin keima chung chang n aseipeh ham?” ati. 
Hichun Pilate in adonbut kit in, “Kei hi Juda mi kahi ham? Na mite le thempu pipui hon kakhut a eipeh nahi chu, ipi na bolset baham?” ati. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Keima Lenggam hi hiche vannoi a kon ahipoi. Hiche vannoi a kon ana hitaleh keima dingin kaseijui hon gal eisat peh intin, Juda te khut a peh doh a kaum louding ahi. Ahinla keima lenggam hi hiche vannoi a kon ahipoi,” ati. 
Hichun Pilate in aseikit in, “Ahileh nangma hi lengpa nahim?” atile Jesun adonbut in, “Leng kahi chu nasei khai, Thutah thu hettohsah dinga hung penga, hiche dinga hi vannoiya hunga kahi. Thutah hechan chun thutah ahin photchen diu ahi” ati. 
Hichun Pilate in adonbut in, “Thutah chu ipi ham?” Hichun Pilate chu Juda mipi holah a aga potdoh kit in, ajah uva aseitai, “Jesu hi themmona neilou ahi, ati. 
Ahivangin nang hon Kalchuh Kut le mikhat nakilha doh peh dingu chonna ngai khat naneiyui; hijeh chun Juda lengpa hi lhadoh peh leu veng nalungu ham?” ati. 
Ahinla ama hon asam'un, “Hiche pa hi hida hen, Barabbas joh,” atin asam tauve. (Barabbas chu doudal natong ahi).
