John.
19.
Hichun Pilate in Jesu chu mol'a jep dingin apedoh tai. 
Chuin sepai hon ling lukhuh apheh un, Pakai chu akhuh peh tauvin ahi. Chuleh ama hon ponsandup asilpeh un ahi. 
Chuin akom'a ahungun, “Juda te lengpa chibai,” atiuvin, totnopnan aneiyun chuleh abengphe abeh peh un ahi. 
Chuin Pilate agapot kit in, ajah uva, “Veuvin akhan gitlouna ima kamu lou nahetna ding uva ama naheng uva kahin puidoh ahi,” ati. 
Hichun Jesu chu ling lukhuh khuh le ponsandup kisil pum chun ahung potdoh in, chuin Pilate in ajah uva, “Veuvin hiche mipa hi!” ati. 
Hichun thempu pipuiho leh Hou-In ngah a pang sepai hon ama chu amu phat un, “Thingpel'a khenbeh-in, thingpel'a khenbeh-in,” tin asam tauve. Ahin Pilate in adonbut in, “Nanghon natahsa tah in kai unlang thingpel'a khenbeh tauvin, keiman vang Jesu hi amona kamu dohpoi,” ati. 
Juda te lamkai hon adonbut uvin, “Keiho danpi dungjuiya ama hi athi dinga lomtah ahi, ijeh inem itile ama tah Pathen Chapa akihisah jeh chun,” atiuve. 
Hichun Pilate in hiche thu ajah phat in, atil'a sangin akicha cheh tan; 
Hichun Pilate chu inpi sunga alut kit in Jesu jah a, “Nangma hoiya hung kon nahim?” ati. Ahivangin Jesun adonbut pon ahi. 
Hijeh chun Pilate in ajah a, “Nei houpi louding ham? Nangma lhadohna ding jong chuleh thingpel a khetbehna ding jong thu kanei hi nahet lou ham?” ati. 
Hichun Jesun aseitai, “Nangman keima chunga imacha tha nanei na ding aumpoi, amavang chunglam akon tilouvin, ati. Hiti ahijeh a chu keima eipedoh a pangpa chonsetna achungnungpen e,” ati. 
Hichun Pilate in Jesu chu lhadoh dingin agong tan, ahinla Judah hon asam uvin, “Nangman hiche pa hi na lhadoh leh Caesar jol nahilou ahi; ama lenga kisem chan chu Caesar douna a thusei ahi,” atiuve. 
Pilate in thu chucheng ajah phat chun Jesu chu ahin puidoh in; chuin Pilate chu song lhongnal atiu (Hebrew paova Gabbatha) hichu thutanna a touna chunga chun atou tan ahi. 
Hichu Kalchuh Kut dinga kigotni, nidan gup dontah ahi; chuin Juda ho jah a, “Veuvin, na lengpau hi!” ati. 
Hijeh chun amaho a-aosot jengun, “Sumangin, sumangin, thingpel'ah khenbeh-in!” atiuvin ahileh, Pilate in ajah uva, “Nalengpau thingpel'a ka khetbeh ding ham?” atileh; thempu pipui hon adonbut un, “Keihon Caesar tailou leng dang kanei pouve,” atiuvin ahi. 
Hichun Pilate in Jesu khetbeh na dingin amaho kom'a apedohtai, hichun Juda ten Jesu chu apuidoh tauvin ahi. 
Hichun ama hon Jesu chu akaiyun, chuin a thingpel akiput in Lugu mun (Hebrew paova Golgotha) atiu mun chu jonin achetan ahi. 
Chuleh hiche mun'a chun Pakai Jesu chu thingpel chung'ah akhenbeh tauvin ahi, chuleh midang pasal ni jong asih langto gel'a ama toh akhetbeh thauvin, Jesu chu amani laitah a akikhenbeh un ahi. 
Chuin Pilate in thingpel chunga simdoh ding atah in hichu, “Nazareth Jesu, Juda te lengpa,” tin atahdoh in ahi. 
Chuleh Jesu akhetbeh na mun chu khopi nai ahijeh chun, hiche min chu Juda ho tamtah-in asimun; hichu Hebrew pao, Latin pao, chuleh Greek paova kisun ahi. 
Hichun thempu pipui hon Pilate henga alungkim lounau aga lhut un, hiche kheldohna dingin, “Juda te lengpa” khel'a “Aman aseijin, ‘Keima hi Juda te Lengpa kahi,’” tijoh a khel dingin asei tauve. 
Ahin Pilate in adonbut in, “Hithei ponte, ajeh chu keiman kana jihdoh sa chu kajih doh ahitai,” ati. 
Chule Rome sepai hon Jesu akhetbeh uchun, aponsil alauvin, hal li asouvin sepai khat hal khat cheh in achang'un; chule asangkhol jong chu alauvin; asangkhol chu khuilou ahin, alua pat ato geiya kikhong lhungkei ahi. 
Hichun ama hon aseiyun, “Atel tel'a bokeh sangin leh sangu hitin, vang vetaote, koi chang ding ham” atiuve; hiche hi Pathen thua kijih guilhun na dinga kisei chu ahi. Hichun ama hon von chu alehsang un vangvet nan aneiyun, hiti chun anabol'un ahi. 
Hiche jeh chun sepai hon hiti chun hicheng chu abol tauve, Ahin Jesu thingpel kom'a chun anu toh, anu sopinu Mary (Clopas Inneipi) toh, Mary Magdalene ading jingun ahi. 
Hichun Jesun anu le angailut aseijui chu akom'a adin amu phat in anu jah a, “Ngailut umtah numei, ven nachapa chu,” ati. 
Chuin Jesun hiche aseijui pa jah a aseijin, “Hiche hi nanu ahi,” atin hichun hiche aseijui pa chun Jesu nu Mariam chu a-inlam'a apui tan ahi. 
Hiche jou chun Jesun thil ijakai akichai soh chu ahen, Pathen Lekhabua thu chu aguilhun soh thei nan, “Kadang achah e,” ati. 
Chuleh chukom'a chu belkhat a theitwi thuh kitung ahin, chule twichop phon a theitwi thuh dimset in hyssop molleh a atah un akam toh asuto uvin ahi. 
Chuin Jesun theitwi thuh chu adonin, “Akichaitai!” ati. Chuin akaikun alhagao alhadoh tan ahi. 
Chuin kiginni ahijeh chun cholngahni a thilong ho chu thingpel'a aumden louna dingin (cholngah ni chula niloupi ni ahin), Judah hon Pilate henga akenggu heboh ding leh along galah mangna dingin athumun ahi. 
Hichun Rome sepai ho ahungun mini kikhai Jesu toh kikhenbeh teni keng chu ahehbong tauvin ahi. 
Amavang Pakai Jesu kom ahin phah uchun, ama chu ana thitan ahi, hiphat chun Jesu keng vang chu aheh bong tapouve. 
Hitia aum vang chun sepai ho khat in Jesu nah chutengcha a asut le apet pet chun thisan le twisih ahung pot tan ahi. 
Chule amupa tah chun a hettohsah ahi; chule ama thu hettohsah chu tahbeh ahi; chuleh aman atahbeh asei ahi chu ahei; nang hon jong na tahsan theina dingu ahi. 
Hiche ho jouse hi Pathen thua ana kisei doh banga, “Agu khatcha jong kihehbong louding ahi,” ati chu guilhunna dinga thil hicheng chu soh a ahitai. 
Chule “ama hon tengcha a asutpau chu avet dingu ahi” kiti toh. 
Chuin hicheng jou chun Arimathea Joseph, Jesu seijui khat, amavang Juda ho gin jeh a guhthima hia ahin, ama chun Jesu long chu pohdoh dingin Pilate henga aga thum'in ahileh, Pilate in jong ana phal'e. Hijeh chun ama ahungin Jesu long chu apo mangtai. 
Chule Arimathea Joseph toh hung khom pa chu Nicodemus ahin, ama hi Jesu toh kimuto dinga jan muthim'a hungpa chu ahi. Nicodemus chun Myrrh leh Aloes thao namtui kihal, sel som sagi le nga a gih ahin choiyin ahi. 
Hichun ama hon Jesu long chu alauvin Juda te chonna ngai bangin lounamtui toh tupat ponnemin atom tauvin ahi. 
Chuleh thingpel'a aki khetbeh nau mun'a chu hon khat uma ahin, hon a chu koima kivuina khah louhel lhan thah khat uma ahi. 
Hijeh chun Juda ho kiginni ahijeh chun lhan chu akomcha ahiman chun chukom'a chun Jesu chu avui tauvin ahi.
