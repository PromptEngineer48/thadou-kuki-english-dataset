John.
20.
Chuin hapta kipatna nimasani jingkah matah ahunglhung tan, kho jong vah chen loulai ahin, Mary Magdalene chu lhan a chun agachen ahileh song chu lhan a konna tahdohsa aum chu agamui. 
Hichun Mary Magdalene chu alhai in Simon Peter leh Pakaijin angailut seijui pa henga hitin thu aga lhuttai, “Pakai longdamsa chu ama hon apomang tauvin, chule lhan khuh akon'a hoilanga akoiyu ham akihe tapoi” agatin ahi. 
Hiche jeh chun Peter le seijui khatpa chu lhan khuh lam jonin ache lhontai. 
Hichun seijui teni chu hatah in lhan lam jon'in akilhaitet lhon'in ahile seijui khat pa chun Peter akhokhel in lhan khuh apha masa tan ahi. 
Ama akunin asung avet le tupat ponnem ho um chu amun, ahivangin asunga alutpoi. 
Hichun Simon Peter chu ahunglhung tan, chuin lhan sunga chun alut tai; chuin tupat ponnem ho amu'n chuleh, 
aluchunga um ultheh pon chu tupat ponnem ho chutoh umtha louva akijel'a atuma ana um aga mun ahi. 
Hichun seijui alhung masajo pa jong chu lhan khuh sunga aga sunglhan, hichu amun atahsan tai. 
Hiche masangsea chu ama hon Pathen thu a kiseidoh “Pakai Jesu chu thi ding chuleh hung thoudoh kit ding ahi” tichu ana tahsan theilou u ahi. 
Hichun ama hon Inlam ahin jon tauvin ahi. 
Mary vang chu lhan khuh pam'a chun adingin akap in aka pum chun lhan khuh sung chu aga ven ahi. 
Hichun aban a bang vantil ni Jesu long alupsah nauva chu khat alu-lhuna atouva khat akeng todop'a atou amui. 
“Ngailut umtah numei nangma ibol'a kap nahim?” tin vantil teni chun ahin dongin ahi. Hichun amanun adonbut in, “Ajeh chu ama hon Ka Pakai apomang tauvin, chuleh alongdamsa chu hoiya akoiyu keiman kahe tapoi,” ati. 
Chutia aseijou chun ama akinung heiyin ahileh, Jesu adin amun, ahin Jesu ahi chu ahepoi. 
Jesun ahin dong'in, “Ngailut umtah numei, ibol'a kap nahim?” ati. Amanun honlei ngahpa ahisah in, “Pu, hoilaiya nakoi hitam Ka Pakai long chu, nei seipeh in keiman gapomang ing kate,” ati. 
“Mary!” tin Jesun ahin koutai. Mary chu Pakai henglam a ahung kihei in akap in, “Rabboni,” ati (hichu Hebrew paova, “houhil,” tina ahi). 
Hichun Jesun ajah a, “Nei tongkha hih in; ijeh inem itile keima Pa henga chun kakaltou hih laiye; ahin chen lang kasopi ho jah a gaseijin, 'Ka-Pa, nangho Pa, Ka Pathen nangho Pathen henga kakal touve,'” ati. 
Mary Magdalene chun seijui ho akimupin, hitin aseiye, “Keiman Pakai chu kamutai,” ati. Chuin amanu chun athusei ho chu aseipeh tai. 
Nikho kipat ni nilhah chun aseijui te Judah ho ginna jeh a kot kikha khuma um ahiuvin, hichun Jesu ahungin amaho lah a ahung dingin ajah uva, “Nachung uva lungmon umhen,” ati. 
Aman hiche chu asei jouvin amaho chu akhut thihkil maha le anah tenga kisutna maha avetsah tan ahi. Hichun ama hon Pakai amu u chun seijui te chu akipah lheh jengun ahi. 
Hichun Pakaijin avel'in ahoulimpi kit in, “Nachung uva lungmon umhen, chuleh Pan keima eisol banga nangho kasol nahiuve,” ati. 
Hichun Pakaijin aseijui ho chu ahu ahai khum in, “Lhagao Theng kisan un. 
Nanghon koitobang ham khat chu achonsetna na ngaidam uva ahileh kingaidam ahi tauve, ahin nang hon na ngaidam louleh amaho chu kingaidam lou ahi jenge,” ati. 
Seijui som le ni ho lah a khat Thomas (Didymus atiu) vang chu Jesu hung kilah a chu amaho lah a ana umlou ahi. 
ama hon aseipeh un, “Keihon Pakai chu kamu tauve,” atiuvin ahi. Ahivangin Thomas chun, “Keiman akhut phanga thingkil maha kamua kakhut junga kakhoiya, anah kakhut a katham kahsen keiman tahsan ponge,” ati. 
Niget jouvin seijui te insunga aumkhom pettah un, hiche a chu Thomas jong um'ah ahi. Aumnau in chu kot kikhah sohkeiya ahi, hinlah hetmanlou kah in, amasa a bangin, Jesu alah uva ana dingin ahi. “Chamnan nalon khum'u hen,” ati. 
Hichun Thomas jah a, “Na khutjung in hinkhoiyin, kakhut teni hi ven; chuleh nakhut hin lhaangin lang kanah tham'in; chuleh gingcha lel tahih in, tahsanin!” ati. 
“Ka Pakai le Ka Pathen!” tin Thomas chun ahin phongdoh tai. 
Chuin Jesun aseipeh in, “Nangman neimu jeh a nei tahsan ahi. Mulouva tahsan ho anunnomui,” ati. 
Hiti tah a chu Jesun aseijui te angsunga melchihna kidang tamtah abol ahin, hiti ho chu hiche lekhabu sunga hin akisunlut poi. 
Ahinla nang hon Jesu chu Messiah, Pathen Chapa, ahi chu na tahsan uva chule na tahsan vang uva ama min a hinna nanei theina ding uva hichengse hi hiche lekhabua hi kisun'ah ahi bouve.
