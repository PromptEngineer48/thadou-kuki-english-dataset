John.
21.
Hichun phat chomkhat jouvin Jesu chu seijui ho toh Galilee dillen panga akimuto kit un, hiti hin ana um'in ahi. 
Simon Peter le Thomas (Didymus atiu) chutoh, Galilee gam'a Cana khoa mi Nathanael toh, Zebedee chapa teni toh chuleh seijui dang ni toh umkhom'a ahiuvin; 
Simon Peter in, “Keima ngaman in chenge,” ati. Hichun ama hon ajah a, “Keiho jong kahung dingu ahi atiuvin ahi.” Chuin acheuvin kong sung khat ah alut tauve, ahin hiche jan chun imacha aman pouvin ahi. 
Jingkah matah in Jesu chu vapam'a adin seijui hon amu uvin, hinlah ama chu koi ahi ageldoh pouve. 
Hichun Pakaijin amaho chu akouvin, “Sopite ho, nga ijat namat tah uvem?” ati. Ahin ama hon adonbut un, “Imacha kaman pouve,” atiuvin ahi. 
Hichun aman, “Nalen'u chu kong jetlam'a gaseplha temun, chule themkhat namat dingu ahi!” ati. Hichun ama hon aseu le nga atam'a tam ahijeh chun akaidoh jou tapouve. 
Hichun Jesun angailut seijui chun Peter jah a, “Pakai ahi,” ati. Hichu Peter in Pakai ahi ati chu ajah phat in asangkhol aki-ah loiyin, (asagoh keova um ahin) dillen a chun achom lhan chule vapam lam chu ajon'in ahi. 
Adangse vang chu kong sunga atouvun, len chu vapam langa kaidoh ding agong'un, ajeh chu len a chu nga dimset ahin chule amaho vapam alhunna dingu chu tong jani tobang ache beu ngaiyah ahi. 
Amaho vapam alhun uchun songmeihol meiya kitih le achunga nga kikoi chuleh changlhah, jing-an a neh ding, amu uvin ahi. 
Jesun amaho jah a, “Namatsau nga chu themkhat hinchoiyun,” ati. 
Hichun Simon Peter kong sunga alut in len chu vapam langa akaidoh in ngalen jakhat le som nga le thum dimset len chu agakaiyin, nga achuti tam vang chun alen chu a-eh pon ahi. 
Hiphat chun Jesun, “Tun nangho hungun lang jing an hung kineh tauvin,” ati. Ahinla aseijui ho lah a chun khatcha beh in jong, “Nang koi nahi ham?” tin adong ngam pouve. Ama hon Pakai ahi chu ahesoh keiyun ahi. 
Hichun Pakaijin seijui ho chu changlhah le nga anehsah in avahva sohkei tai. 
Hichu athia konna athodoh nunga aseijui te henga akilah athumvei na ahi. 
Jing-an anehjou phat'un, Jesun Simon Peter adongin, “John chapa Simon, nangman hiche ho jouse sanga hi nei ngailut joh em?” ati. Peter in adonbut in, henge Pakai keiman kangailut nangman nahe nai.” Jesun ajah a, “Ka kelngoi ho vah in,” ati. 
Anivei nan ajah a, “John chapa Simon, nangman nei ngailut em?” atikit tai. Aman ajah a, “Henge Pakai ka-ngailut nangman nahenai,” ati. Hichun Jesun ajah a, “Ka keltah ho chingtup in,” ati. 
Jesun athumvei nan adongkit in, “John chapa Simon, nangman nei ngailut em?” ati. Thumvei jen, “Nei ngailut em?” atijeh chun Peter chu alungthim anommo lheh tan, ahin Peter in adonbut in, “Pakai, nangman ijakai nahe sohkeiye, keiman nangma ka ngailutna jouse nangman nahenai,” ati. Hichun Jesun ajah a, “Ka keltah ho vah in,” ati. 
“Tahbeh a ka seipeh nahi, na khangdon lai chun nangma deibang tah in navon nakivon theijin, chuleh nangma nop nopna lama nache theiye, ahinla nakum ahungtam tan chujongleh nahung pilhing tan, hijeh a chu nakhut ho nahin jaldoh a midang koi tobangin navon nahin vonpeh ding chuleh nache nom louna lam lama napui dingu ahi,” ati. 
Hichu ama iti thia athi ding Pathen achoiat ding chu hetsahna a ati ahi. Chuin hiche chu asei jouvin ajah a, “Kanung hin jui in,” ati. 
Peter akinung heijin avet le anunglam uva–Jesu ngailut seijui, jan anneh pet a Jesu henglam a akingailhua chule “Pakai koiham nangma na pedoh dinga chu,” tia dongpa chu amun ahi. 
Peter in Jesu chu thudoh aneijin, “Pakai hiche pa chula?” ati. 
Jesun adonbut in, “Keima kahung kinung lekit kah a anaum dinga phalna kapeh chu, nangma a dinga ipi aboina uma ham? Nangman keima neihin juitan,” ati. 
Hichun hiche asei chu, hiche seijui chu thilou ding ahi, tin sopite lah a athang tan ahi; ahinla Jesun ama jah a chu, “Ama chu thilou ding ahi, ati ahipoi. Kahung kahsen umhen, tileng hichu nangman ipi ding natim?” ati ahi bouve. 
Hicheng thu hettohsah a chule hicheng thusun a hi hiche seijui chu ahin; ama thu hettohsah chu tahbeh ahi, ti kaheuve. 
Chuleh hicheng tailouvin jong Jesun thil kidang tamtah anabol aum'e; hiti ho chu ka geldan in, lekhabun ana kisun sohkei henlang hileh, lekhabua kisun chengse chu vannoi leiset hin dolponte kati.
