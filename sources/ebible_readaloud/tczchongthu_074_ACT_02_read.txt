Acts.
2.
Pentecost nikho chun tahsan chate jouse chu munkhat a ana umkhomun ahi. 
Chuin phulou helouvin van'a kon'in hui hattah gin tobang ahung ging in, atoukhom nau in chu ahung dimset jeng tan ahi. 
Chuin meikong toh kilou ahilou le meikong banga kong lei ahung kilah in amaho jouse chunga chun achutan ahi. 
chuin amaho jouse chu Lhagao Theng adimsoh tauvin ahileh Lhaogao Theng in apeh bangun ama hon paochom paochom in thu aseipan tauvin ahi. 
Hiche pet a chu vannoi gamtin'a konna hungkhom Judah hougut ho jong Jerusalem'a um'u ahi. 
Chuin chuche gin chu ajah phat un mipi ajapha jouse chu ahung khomun ahileh tahsan chate thusei chu ama ama pao cheh a ajah jeh uchun amaho alung kichih lahlah jengun ahi. 
Chuin amaho chun kidang asauvin, “Hiche hi iti hithei ham?” tin adatmo lheh jengun ahi. “Hiche mite hi abonchauva Galilee mi ahiuvin, 
ahinlah eihon eima eima penna gam cheh paovin ijauve! 
Eiho hila Parthia mi, Mede mi, Elam mi ihiuvin, abang Mesopotamia gam'a cheng, Judah gam le Cappadocia gam'a cheng, Pontus gam'a le Asia gam'a cheng, 
Phrygia le Pamphylia gam'a cheng, Egypt gam'a cheng chule Cyrene gam vel'a, Libya gam'a cheng ihiuvin; abang Rome a konna hung kholjin, Judah chona chonho, 
chule Crete mi le Arab gam mi ihiuvin; ahivangin eima pao cheh a Pathen thilbol loupi thu aseiyu thakhat jah in ijauve,” atiuvin ahi. 
Amaho chun kidang asauvin adatmo lheh jengun “Itidan ahidem,” tin khat le khat akidongto tauvin ahi. 
Ahivangin mipi lah a konkhat chun ajontaiyun, “Hite ho hi lengpitwi kham ahiuve,” atiuvin ahi. 
Chuin Peter chu solchah dang som le khat chutoh adingdoh in mipi ho kom'a chun aw sangtah in asamin ajah uva, “Phatechan ngaiyuvin, Judah mite le Jerusalem'a cheng mite! Hiche thu hi nang hon hekhel hih un, 
Nangho ngaito banga hite ho hi lengpitwi kham ahi pouve, ajeh chu tuhi jingkah nidan ko ahinalai bouve. 
Ahin hiche namu uhi Joel themgaopan ana phondohsa guilhunna ahi bouve: 
‘Pathen'in’ ninunung lam le hiti ding ahi, mijouse chunga Ka Lhagao kabuhlhah ding ahi. Na chapa teu le na chanu teuvin gaothu asei dingu, chule na khangdong ho'uvin mu aneiyuva, chule na Upa ho'uvin mang anei dingu ahi. 
Hiche niteng chule kasoh pasalte le kasoh numeite chunga ka-Lhagao kabuhlhah ding ama hon gaothu asei dingu ahi. 
chuleh van'a thil kidang tampi kasosah ding, leiset chunga melchihna tampi-thisan, meikong le meikhu kamusah ding ahi. 
Pakai hungkitna nikho loupitah ahung lhun masanga, Nisa hung thim ding, lha jong thisan soh ding ahi.” 
Ahinlah koi hijongleh Pakai min kou jouse sohcha ding ahi. 
“Nangho Israel mite, ngaiyuvin! Pathen'in Nazareth Jesu chu nangho lah a amanchah a bolmo kidang, datmo umho, chule melchihna ho abola konna chu aphotchetsah ahin, hichu nang hon nahet u ahi. 
Ahin Pathen'in ahet masat le agontoh masat bangin Jesu chu pehdoh in aum tan ahileh nang hon dan neilou chidang namdang te toh kithon thingpel a nakhenbeh un nathat tauvin ahi.” 
Ahin Pathen'in Ama chu kichat umtah thina a kon'in alha-ong in hinna apekit tan ahi ajeh chu thina in atuhden hoi ahilou jeh chun. 
Leng David in Ama thudol aseijin, “Keiman Pakai chu kamaikoh a kamu jinge; keima kalonle louding ahi ajeh chu kajetlama ama aum jinge. 
Hijeh chun kalung akipah in, chule kalei athanome; chuche kalval chun katahsa jong kinem'a cholnga ding ahi; 
Ajeh chu nangman kalhagao mithiho lah a na dalhah louding, namitheng monmangna namusah louding ahi. 
Nangman keima hinna lampi neihetsah ahitan, namaisoa kipana neidip ding ahi, ati.” 
“Sopi te ho, hiche thu hi ngaito temuvin, Ipu-u David in ama chung chang thu asei ahilou dan kicheh tah in iheuve, ajeh chu ama athi in, akivuina lhanmun jeng jong tugeiyin eiholah a aumin ahi. 
Ahin ama themgao ahijeh chun, Pathen'in Ama taiguiya pengkhat alaltouna a atousah ding, kihahsela athutep chu ahen, 
Chutia chu khonung thu ahet masah jeh chun Messiah thokit thu aseijin, ‘Thikhol khul'ah dalhah denin aumpoi, alongin jong monmangna amupoi,’ ati. 
Pathen'in Jesu chu thina a kon'in akaithouvin, keiho jouse hi ahettoh kahiuve. 
Tun Ama van'a Pathen khut jetlama choisangin aumin, athutepsa Lhagao Theng chu a-Pa a kon'in amun, hichu ahin bunglhan, tua namu u najah uhi ahi. 
David chu ama tah van'a akaltou lou vangin hitin aseiye, ‘Pakai chun ka Pakai jah a, jabolna mun kakhut jetlam'a tou jingin, 
Namelma te kasuhnem a, nakeng chotpha a kaneisah masang sen,’ ati. 
Chuti ahijeh chun Israel chate jousen heuvin, Pathen'in hiche Jesu, thingpel chunga na khetbeh pau chu Pakai le Messiah dinga atundoh ahitai!” 
Peter thusei chun alungthim'u asuntan ahile ama hon Peter le seijui dang ho kom'a chun, “Sopi te ho keihon ipi kabol dingu ham?” atiuvin ahi. 
Peter in adonbut in, “Na chonset lungheiyun lang Pathen lama hung kiheiyun, chule na chonset nau ngaidama aum jeh in Jesu Christa minin twiya baptize changun, chutengleh Lhagao Theng thilpeh nachan dingu ahi. 
Hiche thutep hi nangho le nachateu ding, chule chidang namdang te ding le, Pakai I-Pathen uvin akou mite jouse dinga jong ahi,” ati. 
Chule Peter in thu tampi asei jomjing nalaiyin, amaho chu atilkhouvin, “Hiche khang gilou mite a kon hin kihuhdoh un,” atin ahi. 
Hiche nikho a Peter thusei tahsanho jouse chu twija baptize achangun, hiche nikho chun Houbung mi sangthum ana kibe in ahi. 
Tahsan chate jouse chun solchah ho thuhil jong, kiloikhomna jong, neh leh chah kihopkhomna le Pakai anjon a jong, taokhomna a jong gunchu tah in ana pangdet jingun ahi. 
Chuin alungthim sung uva kichatna nasatah ahung lhungin, solchah hon jong melchihna le thil kidang tamtah abol tauve. 
chuin tahsan chate jouse chu munkhat ah aumkhom un athilnei jouseu chu neilhaangin ana neikhom uvin ahi. 
Anei agou u ajoh uvin asum chu angaicha jouse toh ana kihom jiuvin ahi. 
Niseh in Hou-In'a Pathen ahoukhom jiuvin, Pakai anjon jong dingin in'ah akikhom jiuvin, chule kipah tah le hongphal tah in an ana nekhom jiuvin ahi. 
Abonchauva Pathen avahchoi jing laiyu le mijouse lungdeiya thanom tah a aum jing laiyun, aniseh in Pathen'in huhhing'a um ho chu amaho lah a chun abe jing tan ahi.
