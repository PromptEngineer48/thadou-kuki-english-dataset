Acts.
5.
Ahin Ananias kiti mipa khat ana um'in ama chun ajinu Sapphira to agou themkhat ajoh lhon'in, 
Asum kehkhat chu solchah ho henga ahin choi lhon'in, abona kahin choi lhon ahitai ati lhon'in adangse chu ajinu toh kinopto in akikoi den lhon tan ahi. 
Chuin Peter chun aseijin, “Ananias ipi dinga nalungthim chu Satan nalodimsah hitam? Lhagao Theng najoulhep in nasum akehkhat nanga dingin nakikoi tai. 
Nagouthil chu nanga ahi, joh ding le joh louding jong nangma thu ahi, najoh jouva jong asum chu nanga ahin, pehdoh ding le pehdoh louding jong nangma thu ahi. Ipi dinga hitobang hi nabol hitam? Hiche hi keiho nei joubol nau ahipon Pathen najou bolna joh ahi” ati. 
Hiche thu chu Ananias in ajah jah chun lhonglaiya chun alhun athitai, chuin hiche thu ajapha jouse chu akicha thei lheh tauvin ahi. 
Chuin khangdong phabep ahungdoh un ponin atomun vui dingin apodoh tauvin ahi. 
Nidan thum vel che jouvin, thilsoh imacha hepha louvin ajinu chu ahungin, 
Chuin Peter in adongin, “Hiche sum hi najipa toh nagam mana namu lhon chu hitam?” atileh amanun jong “Henge hichu kagam man lhon ahi,” ati. 
Chuin Peter in aseijin, “Ipi dinga nani tah lhon'a Pakai Lhagao napatep lhona hitobang thil phalou hi nabol lhon hitam? Najipa vuiya che gollhang hon kotphung ahinpha tauvin ahi, chule nang jong naputdoh diu ahitai,” ati. 
Apettah chun lhonglaiya chun alhun athipai tan ahi. Gollhang ho ahunglut un ahileh amanu athisa anaum chu amu uvin chuin apodoh paiyun ajipa kivuina kom'a chun aga vui tauvin ahi. 
Chuin Houbung pumpi le athu japha jouse chunga kichatna lentah achutan ahi. 
Chuin solchah ho chun miholah a chun melchihna le thil kidang tampi abolun, tahsan chate jouse Hou-In a, Solomon kempum kiti mun'a chun niseh in akikhom jing tauvin ahi. 
Ahin mipi hon amaho chu amusang lheh vangun alah uva jaoding chu angam pouvin ahi. 
Hijeng jong le tahsan chate chu Pakai henga hung akibe jing jengun, numei le pasal akibe jingun ahi. 
Solchah ho natoh jeh chun miho chun Peter avale kom le alebeh achung uva achuh khah na ding tin damlou ho jouse chu lamlen dunga ahin podoh uvin lupna le pheh chunga alupsah un ahi. 
Jerusalem kimvela khopi hoa kon chun mipi ahung khomun damlou ho le lhagao boh vop ho ahin puiyun ahileh abonchauvin adam sohkei uvin ahi. 
Chuin Thempu Chungnung leh aloite Sadducee ho chu thangset nan adimset un ahi. 
Chuin solchah ho chu amanun songkul'a chun ahen tauvin ahi. 
Ahin janin Pakai Lhagao ahungin, akihennau songkul kot chu ahongdoh in solchah ho chu apuidoh in, 
“Hou-In a cheuvin lang miho kom'a hiche hinna thu hi gaseipeh tauvin,” ati. 
Chuin ajing khovah in solchah ho chu Hou-In'a alut un, Pakai Lhagaovin akom uva aseipeh bangun ahil tauvin ahi. Chuin Thempu Chungnung chu atohkhompi chengtoh ahungin, Israel te lah a a upahou le hou vaipoloi ho chu akou khomun, chuin solchah ho chu achungthu u kholchen dingin songkul'a kon'in aga puidoh sah un ahi. 
Hou-In ngah a pang sottol leho chu songkul'a ahungun ahileh, songkul'a chun koima anaum tapon ahi, chuin amaho akile uvin houvaipo ho henga chun thil umchan chu aseiyun, 
“Songkul kot chu kikhah detchet a angah ho chu apama ding ahiuvin, ahin songkul kot chu ahon uva ahile asunga chun koima ana umpon ahi!” atiuve. 
Chuin Hou-In ngahho lah a lamkaipa le thempu pipuho chu alungboi lheh jengun, ipi khat ahungsoh tadem tin adatmo lheh jengun ahi. 
Chuin mikhat ahungin kidang satah in, “Songkul'a nakhum u miho chu Hou-In'a ading uvin miho thu ahil uve,” ahung tin ahi. 
Chuin Hou-In ngah a pang sepai lamkaipa leh asepai te chu acheuvin solchah ho chu aga manun, ahin ajokhat le mipi ho chun songin eisep unte tin boina sem louvin oltah in agabol'un ahi. 
Chuin solchah ho chu hou vaipoloi angsunga ahin puilut un, amaho chu Thempu Chungnung in thu adongun, 
“Hiche mipa min chun thu hilkit tahih un tia hakhama kakham nahilou uham? Ahinlah nathuhil'u Jerusalem nadip soh un hiche mipa thina chung changa hin keiho neimoso go uve,” atin ahi. 
Ahin Peter le solchah ho chun adonbut un, “Keihon mihem thuneiho sanga Pathen thu kangai joh dingu ahi. 
Ipu ipateu Pathen chun thingpel chunga na khaisang uva nathau Jesu chu thina a konna akaithou kit ahi. 
Ahin ama chu Pathen'in Pakai leh Huhhingpu dinga achoisanga akhut jetlama atousah ahitai. Chule hiche hi Israel chate chonset lungheina ding le ngaidam channa dinga abol ahi. 
Keiho hi hiche thudol hettohsah chu kahiuvin, chuleh athua nungho dinga Pathen'in apeh Lhagao Theng jong chu ahi,” atiuvin ahi. 
Chuin hiche kisei ajah phat un hou vaipoloi ho chu alunghang doh jah un tha ding lung agel tauvin ahi. 
Ahin hou vaipoloi lah a mikhat, Pharisee mi amin Gamaliel, amapa chu hou danthu them le mijousen ajabol mikhat ahin, ading doh in miho chu chomkhat pam langa soldoh dingin thu apen ahi. 
Chuin aman aloite henga chun aseijin, “Nangho Israel mite, hite ho chunga bol ding nagot uhi chingtheiyun! 
Tu masang chun Theudas kiti mikhat ana um'in, ama chu milen khat bangin ana um'in ahile mihem jali valin anung ana jui un ahi, ahin ama chu ana that un ahileh anungjui ho chu lamchom cheh a ana vah cheh gamun, abol atoh hou jouse imalou asohtan ahi. 
Amapa jou chun hichu min kisut lai ahin, Galilee mi Judas kiti mikhat anaum kit in ama jong chun anungjui mitampi ana neiyin, ahin athat kit un ahile anungjui ho jouse chu ana kithecheh kit tauvin ahi. 
Chuti ahijeh chun hiche mite hi ima lohih un, moh lha-ong uvin, ajeh chu ama hon angaito uva abolgot uhi amaho lungchama ahile chomkhat jou le hung mang pai ding ahi. 
Ahin Pathen a konna ahileh na suhmang joulou dingu, nangho le nangho Pathen toh kidoubep nahilo dingu ahi!” ati. 
Chuin ama thumop chun akilol tauvin, solchah ho chu akouvun ajep uvin, chujouvin Jesu min'a thu aseikit tahlouna dingun agihsalun alha tauvin ahi. 
Chuin solchah ho chu hou vaipoloi angsunga kon chun apotdoh un, Jesu min'a suhgima aum jeh uchun akipah lheh jengun ahi. 
chule aniseh in Hou-In le ainson tin'ah “Jesu chu Messiah ahi,” tichu ahil'un asamphong jom jingun ahi.
