Acts.
6.
Ahin aniseh a atahsan akibe jing phat in alah uva phunnoina le kilunglhaito louna aumdoh tan, hichun Greek pao tho atahsan ho chun Hebrew pao tho atahsan hon niseh a neh le chah kihopna a meithaiho akhohsah pouve tin aphunnoi tauvin ahi. 
Chuin solchah som le ni ho chun atahsan ho chu abonchauvin akoukhom uvin, “Keiho solchah ho hin Pathen thuhilna a phat kaman diu ahin neh le chah a hi kaboi louding dol u ahi. 
Hijeh chun sopi te ho, nang hon jaumtah, lhagao le chihna a dim mi sagi lhengdoh uvin lang amaho chu neh leh chah a mo kapohsah dingu ahi. 
Chutileh keiho solchah ho hin taona le Pathen thuhil'a phat kaman thei dingu ahi,” atiuvin ahi. 
Abonchauvin hiche lunggel chu pha asauvin chuin, Stephen (tahsanna le lhagaova dim mipa), Philip, Procorus, Nicanor, Timon, Parmenas, le Antioch mi Nicolas (Judah te tahsana sang masaloi) ho chu hiche panmun loding chun alhengdoh tauvin ahi. 
Chuin hiche mi sagi kilhengdoh ho chu solchah ho henga ahin puilut uvin ahileh solchah hon achung uva khut angamun ataopeh tauvin ahi. 
Chuti chun Pathen thu chu amachal jingin atahsan mi hatah in akibe jingin, chule Juda te thempuho jong tamtah ajau uvin ahi. 
Chuin Stephen chun, Pathen lungsetna le thaneina dimset in thil kidang le melchihna tampi mipi ho masanga abol in ahi. 
Ahin nikhat chu chamlhat miho kikhop khomna a kon'in mi phabep, amaho chu Cyrene, Alexandria, Cilicia, le Asia gam'a konna Judah mi ahiuvin amaho chu ahungun Stephen chu ahung kinelpi tauvin ahi. 
Ahin Stephen in chihna le Lhagao dimset puma aseidoh thucheng anga chun koima adingjou pouvin ahi. 
Chuin ama hon mipi themkhat atildoh un amaho kom'a chun Stephen in “Mose le Pathen ataitom,” thu kajauve atiuvin ahi. 
Hiche asei uchun mipi chu alung handoh sah-uvin, chuin upaho leh houdan thuhil ho chun Stephen chu aman uvin vaipoloi anga ahin puilut un ahi. 
Chuin jouthu hettohsahna asemun, “Hiche mipa hin Hou-In theng douna le Mose dan dounan thu aseijin, 
Chule Nazareth Jesu chun Hou-In hi ahin suhchim ding chule Mose'n eipeh u dan jong akhel ding ahi” tia asei kajauve, atiuvin ahi. 
Hiche pettah chun vaipo loiho jouse chun Stephen chu ave sohkeiyun ahi, ajeh chu Stephen maiso chu vantil ho mai banga vahset ahi.
