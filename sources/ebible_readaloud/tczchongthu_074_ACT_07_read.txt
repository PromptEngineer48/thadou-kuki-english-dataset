Acts.
7.
Chuin Thempu Chungnung chun Stephen chu adongin, “Hiche hehna thu ho hi dih em?” ati. 
Chuin Stephen in adonbut in, “Sopite le Pate ho ngaiyuvin, Ipuluiyu Abraham Haran a achen masang, Mesopotamia gam'a aum laiyin Loupi Pathen chu ahenga ana kilah in, 
Hitin ana seiyin ahi, ‘na penna gam leiset le na insungmi hin dalhan lang, keiman kavetsah ding gam leiset a hungin’” ati. 
Chuin Abraham'in Chaldea gam ahin dalhan apa thikah in Haran a ana chengin, chujouvin tua na chennau gamsunga hin ahin puilut in ahi. 
Ahinlah Pathen'in akengto ngapna ding kham gambeh neocha jong ana pepoi. Ahin Abraham'in chapa anei loulai vang'in nikhat le ama le ason achilhah ten hiche gam hi alo dingu ahi tin Pathen'in ana thutep in ahi. 
Chuleh Pathen'in aseikit in nason na chilhate jong migama kum jali sohchang le bolgima um dingu ahi 
Ahin soh a kai nam mite chunga keiman thu katan ding, achaina le hiche gam'a hi hung kile kit uva keima eihou kit dingu ahi ati. 
Chule Pathen'in jong Abraham chu cheptan a kitepna thu anapen, chuin Abraham'in achapa Isaac ahin phat in aniget nin chep anatan in, chule hiche kitepna chu Isaac in jong Jacob ahina, chuleh Jacob in apului u som le ni alhin chan geiyin jong ahin boljom jingun ahi. 
Chuin hiche puluiho chu asopi pau Joseph chunga ana thangthip uvin, hijeh chun Egypt gam'a soh dingin ana johdoh uvin ahi. Ahin Pathen'in ana umpi jingin, 
Gentheina ho jouse a kon'in ana huhdoh in Egypt lengpa Pharaoh angsunga lungsetna anapen ahi. Chule Pathen'in Joseph chu kidangtah chihna anapen hijeh chun Pharaoh in Joseph chu Egypt gam pumpia vaipon ana pansah in chuleh aleng inpia jong mopon ana pansah in ahi. 
Ahin Egypt gam le Canaan gamsunga kel ahunglha tan ahileh gentheina sangtah ahunglhung tan hijeh chun ipuilui teu jong neh ding nei louvin anaum un ahi. 
Chuin Jacob in Egypt gamsunga neh aum nalaiye ti ahet phat in achate ipuilui teu chu neh cho dingin asolin ahi. 
Anivei channa a anache uchun Joseph chu asopite henga ana kiphongin chuin asopite chu Pharaoh henga ana puilut in ahi. 
Chuin Joseph in mi asolin apa Jacob le a insung mite somsagi le nga ho chu ahenga dingin aga kousah tan ahi. 
Chuin Jacob chu Egypt gam'a anache in athitan ahi chule ipului teu jong anathi tauvin ahi. 
Chun alongdamsau chu Shechem a ahin pouvin Abraham'in Hamor chapa te kom'a ana chohna lhanmun'a chun avui tauvin ahi. 
Pathen'in Abraham kom'a athutep guilhunna ding phat anaitah jeh chun miho chu Egypt gam'a chun hatah in apungun. 
Ahin Joseph hephalou lengthah khat Egypt lengvaipoa ahung pan phat in, 
Hiche lengpa chun imiteu chu duha in abolin asugentheiyin chule achasen ho'u chu athina dingin anu le apa teu chu hunam'in ana paidoh sah in ahi. 
Hiche phat laitah chun Pathen mitvet a chapang hoitah Mose chu ana pengin, anu le apan inmun'ah lhathum hoitah in anasin lhon'in ahi. 
Chuin anu le apan Mose chu apaidoh lhon tan ahileh Pharaoh chanun ana kimudoh in ama cha bang bangin ahin vahkhang tan ahi. 
Chuin Mose chu Egypt gamsung chihna ho jouse ahilun ahile ama chu thusei le natoh a thahat tah a hung hitan ahi. 
Chuin Mose chu kum somli ahung hi phat hin nikhat chu asopite Israel te chu aga vetan ahile, 
Egypt mipa khat in Israel mikhat alamlou tah a abol chu amun chuin Mose chu ahungin Israel mipa chu ahuh in Egypt mipa chu athat tan ahi. 
Mose chun hiche Israel mite chun Pathen'in keiho huhdoh dinga eihin solpeh ahi ti geldoh nauvinte tia anagel vang chun Israel mite chun ana geldoh pouvin ahi. 
Chuin ajing nikhon amite agavet a ahileh, Israel mini ana kina lhon'in hijeh chun aman jong chamna nasem kigon hiche miteni kom'a chun “Nangni sopi khat nahi lhon lou ham? Ipi dinga nakinah lhon ham?” atin ahile, 
mikhat pa chun Mose chu ahin sondoh in, kon keiho chunga vaihom le thutana napan sah ham? tin ahin dongin, 
“Janhia Egypt mipa khat natha banga chu keima jong neitha got ham?” atin ahi. 
Chuin Mose’n hichu ajah phat chun, Egypt gam'a kon'in ajamin Midian gam apemin hiche mun'a chun chapa ni ahingin ahi. 
Chuin kum somli jouvin Vantil hamboh meikongin Mose chu Sinai mol toh kinai gamthip a chun ana kimupi in, 
Chuin Mose’n hichu amun athilmu chu kidang asah in, achenaiya agavet kigot le Pakai awgin chun ahin kouvin, 
“Keima napului teu Pathen kahi, Abraham, Isaac, Jacob Pathen kahi,” ahin tin ahile Mose jong akicha behseh jengin ave ngam tapon ahi. 
Chuin Pathen'in aseijin, “Na kengchot sutlhan ajeh chu na dinna mun chu muntheng ahi. 
Egypt gam'a kamite gentheina kamu tan, akanau aw kajan ahuhdoh dinga hung kumlha kahitai, hijeh chun tun keiman nangma hi Egypt gam'a ka nungsol kit nahi,” ati. 
Chuin “Kon keiho chunga vaihom le thutana napan sah ham?” tia asanlou pau chu, Pathen'in hamboh lah a konna akimusah vantil jal chun vaipo ding le thutan dingin Pathen'in Mose chu alesol kit in ahile. 
Mose'n thil kidang le melchihna tamtah pumin Israel te chu Egypt gam'a kon'in ahin puidoh in twipisan le gamthip chu kum somli sungin ahung galkaiyun ahi. 
“Chuin Mose ama tah in Israel te kom'a anaseijin, Pathen'in keima tobang themgao khat nangho lah a konna asodoh sah ding ahi,” ati. 
Mose chu gamthip a ipului teu toh Pathen mite kikhop khomna a anaum khomin, Sinai Molchunga valtil in thu aseipeh chun, khonunga eiho jouse pehson dingin hinna thu eina lahpeh uvin ahi. 
Ahinlah Ipului teu chun Mose thusei ana ngai pouvin Egypt gam'a kinung le ding ana deijo un ahi. 
Ipuilui teu chun Aaron kom'a aseiyun, “Eilamkai thei dingu pathen lim themkhat nei sempeh un ajeh chu Egypt gam'a konna eihin puidoh pau Mose hi iti hitam kahe tapouve,” atiuvin ahi. 
Chuin bongnou toh kilou milim khat asem uvin, hiche milim henga chun kilhaina gantha asem uvin, athilsem uchu akipapi uvin ahi. 
Chuin amahoa kon'in Pathen akihei mangin, Van'a ahsiho chu a pathen'u hi dingin adalha tan ahi! Chuin themgao lekhabun asei “Vo Israel kum somli gamthip a natham laiyuva nahin choiyu kilhaina gantha le thilto ho chu keima henga ding ham? 
Ahipoi, nang hon Molech ponbuh le pathen Rephan ahsi hou dinga alim na semthu u chu napo le ji bouvui, hijeh a chu nangho Babylon gamsung chan a soh a kasol ding nahiuve,” ati. 
Ipului teu chun gamthip noija chun Houbuh chu ana pole un Pathen'in Mose henga ana musah lim bang bang chun ana tungdoh ji un ahi. 
Kum phabep jouvin Joshua lamkaina a ipului teuvin hiche gam'a konna Pathen'in ana nodoh ho khopi mun analo uchun Pathen Houbuh chu ana polut un Leng David khang chanin ana um'in ahi. 
David chu Pathen mitmun deium ana chang'in, a-itih a Jacob Pathen chenna ding Hou-In sahna dingin ana thumin ahi. 
Ahin hiche Hou-In chu Solomon in ana tungdoh in ahi. 
Hijeng jong le hiche khut a kisa Hou-In a chun Chungnungpen Pathen chu achengpoi tin, Themgaovin aseiye. 
“Van ka laltouna ahin leiset kakengto ngapna ahi, chutobang chenna ding inpha chu nei sahpeh thei ding ham?” tin Pakai chun adongin ahi. 
Van le leiset ania kakhut a kasem hilou ham? tia asei banga chu. 
“Nangho mi louchalte, thutah a dinga alungthim le ana ngong te ho, a imatih chana Lhagao Theng na doudalu hilou ham? Hichu na pului teuvin ana bola nang hon jong nabolu ahi. 
Napului teuvin ana bolgim lou themgao khat umem? Chondih tah Messiah hung ding thu ana sei masaho chu ana that un ama tah jong na matsah un na that un ahi. 
Chule Vantil a konna nalah son u Pathen danthu jeng jong henasa san nanit pouvin ahi,” ati. 
Stephen in athemmo nau thu asei chun Juda te lamkai ho chu alunghan sah lheh jengun chuin ama chu akhuttum ajel khumun ahi. 
Ahin Lhagao Theng dimset in Stephen chun vanlam aven ahileh Pathen loupina le Jesu Pathen khut jetlam jabolna mun'a adin chu amun ahi. 
Chuin aman amaho jah a, “Ven vankot akihona chule Pathen khut jetlam jabolna mun'a mihem chapa adin kamui,” ati. 
Chuin ama hon akhut un anakol akisip un apengjah un ama chu anokhum jengun, 
Khopi pamlanga akaidoh un songin asep un, chuin ama themmo chansah ho chun avon chu asut un Saul kiti khangdongpa kengbul'a chun akoiyun ahi. 
Chuin songa ase pettah un Stephen chu ataovin, “Pakai Ka lhagao lan,” ati. 
Chuin abohkhup in apengjah jengin, “Pakai abol ho jouseu hi amaho chonset in simpeh hih-in,” atin hiti chu athitan ahi.
