Acts.
9.
Ahin Saul vang chu Pakai nungjuiho gihsal le that dingin ahai hai nalaiyin, hijeh chun Thempu Chungnung henga achen, 
Damascus khopia kikhopna in hoa dingin lekha agathumin, chule koi hijongleh hiche lampi tahsanho jouse chu numei hihen, pasal hijongleh akana Jerusalem ahin lhutna dingin kithopina agathumin ahi. 
Hiche tupna nei puma ache laiyin Damascus khopi anaitah chun van'a kon'in phulou helouvin vah in akimvel ahin salin, 
Chuin ama tol'a alhun chule “Saul! Saul! Ipi dinga nei bolset ham?” tia kisam aw khat ajan, 
Chuin Saul in jong “Pakai nang koi nahim?” tin adong tan ahi. Chuin aw chun ahin donbut in “Keima Jesu, nabolset jingpa chu kahi! 
Tun kipatdoh in khopi sunga khun lut in nabol ding ho naki seipeh ding ahi,” ati. 
Chuin Saul toh kilhon khom ho chu paopoh loubeh in aum tauvin ahi, ajeh chu Saul kom'a thusei aw chu ajauvin ahin koima la amu pouvin ahi. 
Chuin Saul chu tol'a konna ahung dindoh a amit ahasah le kho amu tapon hijeh chun alhon khompi hon akhut in akaiyun Damascus khopi sung alut tauvin ahi. 
Chuin khopi sunga chun nithum sungin an nelou twidon louvin aumden tan ahi. 
Chuin Damascus khopi sunga tahsan mikhat amin Ananias kitipa chu Pakaijin akimusah in “Ananias!” tin akouvin ahileh amapan, “Hikom'a kaumnai Pakai!” atin ahi. 
Chuin Pakaijin adonbut in “Na masanga lampi jangkeiya khun chenlang Judas in'a khun galut tan, chule insunga khu nalut tengleh Tarsus gammi Saul kitipa chu gahol tan ajeh chu ama kaheng'a atao pettah ahi. 
Chule hiche pa khun Ananias kiti mikhat ahenga hunga aluchunga khut angap a chutengleh kho amukit ding khu kamusah ahi,” ati. 
Ahivangin Ananias in adonbut in “Pakai hiche mipa hin Jerusalem'a tahsan chate kichat umtah a abol genthei thu mi tamtah a konna kajah ahitai, 
chule hoilai mun hijongleh nangma min kouho jouse amat a songkul'a akhum theina dinga thempu pipui hon thaneina apeh u ahitai,” ati. 
Ahin Pakaijin aseijin, “Chen, ajeh chu Saul khu chidang namdang te le lengho chuleh Israel te henga kathu lhangsam dinga ka lhendoh ahitai, 
Chule tua hi kamin jal'a itobang genthei athoh ding ham kavetsah ding ahi,” ati. 
Chuin Ananias chu achen ahileh Saul chu amutan hichun aluchunga akhut angamin aseitai, “Sopi Saul, kho namu kitna ding le Lhagao Thenga na dimsetna dinga lampia nakimu sah Pakai Jesu chun nahenga eihin sol ahi,” ati. 
Chuin apettah chun lip tobang thil khat Saul mit a kon chun ahung kilehlhan ahileh Saul chun kho amutan ahi. Chutah chun Saul chu akipatdoh in baptize achang tan ahi. 
Chujouvin Saul chun an themkhat anen ahileh athahat doh kittan, chuin Damascus khopi sunga tahsan chate kom'a chun nikho phabep aum den tan ahi. 
Chule apettah chun Jesu thudol chu asamphong pantan kikhopna in tin'ah, “Jesu chu Pathen Chapa ahi,” tin asam le tan ahi. 
Chuin athu lhangsap japha jousen kidang asauvin, “Hiche pa hi Jerusalem'a Jesu seijui ho suhmang ding gopa chu hilou ham?” atiuvin ahi. Chule “Hikom'a hi seijui ho man ding le kanchoma thempu ho henga kai dinga hung hilou ham?” atiuvin ahi 
Saul thulhangsap chu ahung thahat cheh cheh jeng tan ahileh Damascus khopia um Juda mite chun Jesu chu Messiah ahi ti avetsahna apeh ho chu aneljou tapouvin ahi. 
Chuche phat chomkhat jou chun Judah mite phabep khat ahung khomun ama chu tha ding agouvin, 
Ama chu thateina dingin khopi kelkot dunga sun le jan in achanglhi jingun, ahin mihon tha ding agonu thu chu aseipeh un, 
Hijeh chun seijui ho mi phabep khat chun jankah in Saul chu boma akhumun khopi bang onglai khat ah khaovin akhai lha tauvin ahi. 
Chuin Saul chu Jerusalem ahung lhunin, seijui ho kimupi ding agon ahileh amaho abonun akichauvin ahi ajeh chu seijui dihtah ahi ding chu atahsan lou u ahi! 
Chuin Barnabas chun Saul chu solchah ho henga apuilut in Damascus lampia Saul in Pakai akimupi thu le Pakaijin Saul henga asei thu ho abonchan solchah ho henga aseipeh in chule Damascus khopia Saul in Jesu min'a hangsan tah a thu asei jong chu aseipeh in ahi. 
Chuin Saul chu solchah ho chutoh aum khom tan, Jerusalem kimvela avahle un hangsan tah in Pakai minin thu alhangsam tan ahi. 
Chuin Greek pao tho Juda te toh akinil un ahile Juda miho chun tha ding agouvin ahi. 
Seijui hon chuche thu ajah phat un Caesarea lama apuisuh un achenna Tarsus lama asol mang tauvin ahi. 
Chuin Judea gam le Galilee gamsung chule Samaria gamsung pumpia Houbung chu mongtah in aum tauvin chule seijui hon Pakai agin jeh u chun athahat cheh cheh uvin ahi. Chule Lhagao Theng in aumpi jeh u chun seijui jong akibe jingin ahi. 
Chujongleh Peter chu munkhat a kon'in munkhat ah avahle len chuin Lydda khopia seijui ho vil dingin ahung kumsuh in ahile, 
Hikom'a chun Aeneas kiti mikhat langthia kumget jen jalkhun a lumpa chu aga mudoh tan, 
Chuin Peter in ajah a “Aeneas, Jesu Christan na damsah ahitai! Thouvin lang nalupna kijeldoh tan,” ati. Chupet tah chun ama adamtan ahi. 
Chuin Lydda le Sharon khopi sunga mijouse chun Aeneas in lam ajot a avale chu amu uvin, abonchauvin Pakai lamah ahung kihei tauvin ahi. 
Chuin Joppa atiu mun a chun seijui mikhat Tabitha (Greek paova Dorcas) atiu chu aumin, amanu chun midang dingin thilpha abol jingin, genthei ho akithopi jingin ahi. 
Amanu chu ahung dammo in athitan ahileh atahsa chu asiltheng uvin vui dingin indan chungnunga chun alupsah tauvin ahi. 
Ahivangin seijui ho chun Peter chu Lydda kom'a aum'e tithu ajauvin hijeh chun mini asol uvin, Peter henga “Lungset tah in aganna thei penin hungin,” tin aga kousah tauvin ahi. 
Chuin Peter chu akouva che teni chutoh ahung kile khomin ahileh ahung lhun lhun chun ama hon Peter chu indan chungnunga chun apuitouvun ahileh insunga chun meithai ho adimset uvin kap pumin a sangkhol hou le von dang Dorcas in adam laiya ana bolpeh hou chu avetsah un ahi. 
Ahivangin Peter chun abonchauvin insunga kon chun asoldoh in chuin ama adilsun atahsa alupsah u chu aven “Thoudoh in Tabitha,” tin atao tan ahileh amit ahung hahdoh in, chuin amanun Peter amu phat in ahung toudoh tan ahi. 
Chuin aman akhut in akai dingin chule meithai ho le seijui ho chu abonchan akoulut in amanu chu ahingin apetan ahi. 
Hiche thu chu khopi sunga akithang soh tan ahileh mi tamtah in Pakai atahsan tan ahi. 
Chule Peter chu Joppa mun'a savun sem Simon in'ah nisottah aumden tan ahi.
