Acts.
10.
Rome te sepai lamkai Cornelius atiu mikhat Caesarea ah ana chengin, ama chu Italia loi atiu sepai jalamkai ahi. 
Amapa chu Pathen ngaisah tah le a insung mijouse toh Pathen gingmi ahin; migenthei ho hongphal tah a pejing le Pathen henga tao jing mi ahi. 
Nikhat nilhah lam nidan thum donin mu aneijin athilmu achun Pathen vantil khat ahenga ahungin “Cornelius!” ahung tin ahi. 
Chuin Cornelius chun kicha tah in aven, “Ipi ham Pakai,” tin adong tan ahi. Chule Vantil chun ahin donbut in, “Nataona le migenthei ho dinga nathilpeh ho chu Pathen'in thilto bangin nasanpeh tai! 
Tun nangman mi themkhat solin lang Joppa a um Simon Peter kitipa khu gakousah tan. 
Ama twikhanglen panga Simon kiti savun sem mikhat in'ah aum'e,” ati. 
Chuin vantil in adalhah lhah in Cornelius chun a insunga lhacha ni le akinbol a pang jing Pathen ngaisah tah sepai ho lah a khat akouve. 
Thilsoh jouse aseipeh jouvin amaho chu Joppa ah asol tan ahi. 
Ajing nikho in Cornelius thupo ho khopi sung alhun ding konun Peter chu indan chunga tao dingin ahung kaldoh in hichu sunkim don ahi. 
Chule ama chu agilkel in, ahivangin aneh ding an asem laitah un ama agaochangin, 
Chuin vankot akihona lekha pheng lentah tobang khat aning lia ahung kikhai lhah amun, 
Chuin hiche lekha pheng lentah tobang sunga chun ganhing namkim le tol'a vah ganhing chule chunga leng vacha ho aumun, 
Chuin aw khat in ahenga ahin seiyin, “Peter, kipatdoh inlang chule kithan lang nen,” ati. 
Chuin Peter in adonbut in, “Ahipoi Pakai, ajeh chu imatih chanin keiman Juda te dan thu dungjuiya sathenglou le saboh ka nekhapoi,” ati. 
Ahivangin aw chun ahinsei kit in, “Pathen'in asuhthengsa chu athenglou tidan,” ati. 
chuti chun thumvei jen kibang chet in mu aneijin chujouvin hetmanlou kah in lekha pheng lentah tobang chu van'a khai touvin aum tan ahi. 
Chuin Peter chun gaova athilmu chu ipi kiseina ahidem tia lunggim tah a ana um laitah in Cornelius in ahin sol miho chun Simon in chu ahin holdoh un, amaho chu kelkot ah ana ding uvin, 
Amaho chun hiche laiya hi Simon Peter kiti mikhat cheng em tin adongun ahi. 
Chuin Peter in gaova athilmu chu ipi ahidem tia ana gel jing laiyin Lhagao Thengin ahenga thu ahin seiyin, “Mithum tah in nangma nahol'e, 
Kipatdoh in, kumsuh inlang amaho toh kilhon ding khu thase hihbeh in, chule lungkham hih in ajeh chu keiman amaho khu kahin sol ahi,” ati. 
Chuin Peter akum suh in amaho kom'a “Nahol u mipa chu keima kahi, ipi dinga hung nahiuvem?” tin adong tan ahile, 
ama hon adonbut-un, “Keiho hi Cornelius kiti Pathen gingtah mi chule Juda ten ajabol u Rome sepai jalamkai pa chun eihin sol u ahin, chule nangma thusei hi a insung mite toh angai theina dinga vantil theng khat in thu apeh bang banga nakouva hung kahiuve,” atiuvin ahi. 
Chuin Peter in amaho chu akoulut in a in'ah jankhat agesah in, ajingin Joppa a konna sopi themkhat toh akilhon tauvin ahi. 
Ajing nikhon amaho Caesarea ahunglhung tauvin ahile Cornelius in a insung mite le aloi agol ho ana koukhomin ana ngah tauvin ahi. 
Chuin Peter insunga alut in ahile Cornelius chu akengbula abohkhup in chibai abohtan ahi. 
Ahinlah Peter chun akai dingin, “Dingdoh in keijong nang banga mihem khat kahi bouve!” ati. 
Chuti chun amani akihoulim lim lhon'in insunga mitampi kikhop khomna achun alut lhon'in, 
Chuin Peter in amaho henga chun, “Judah mikhat hitia chidang namdang te in'a alut hi Judah te chonna dan toh kitoh lou ahi naheuve, Ahin Pathen'in koima aboh le athenglou tia kagel louna dinga eimusah ahitai. 
Hijeh a chu lungthoi louhel a eihin kou banga kahung jeng ahi. Tun ipi dinga neihin kou ham seitan,” ati. 
Chuin Cornelius in adonbut in, “Nili masang, tuphat chet nilhah lang nidan thum donin ka insunga kataovin ahileh, hetmanlou kah in ka masang laitah a ponbanga kivon mikhat ka anga ahung dingin; 
Hitin ahin seiyin ahi, ‘Cornelius Pathen'in nataona ajatan genthei ho dinga na thilpha bol jong amutai! 
Tun nangman Joppa a mi solin lang, Simon Peter kiti mipa khu gakousah tan. Ama twikhanglen panga Simon kiti savun sempa in'ah aum'e’ ati. 
Hijeh a chu nangma kouva mi kahin sol ahin, nahung hi apha lheh jenge, chule tun Pakaijin thu napehho ngai dingin hikom'a Pathen angsunga hin ibon chauvin iki khom tauve,” ati. 
Chuin Peter in adonbut in, “Pathen'in mi lungset tum aneipoi ti kichen tah in kamutai; 
Ahin chitin namtin lah a koi hijongleh Ama ginga thildih bol ho chu ama dinga lahthei ahi. 
Chule hiche hi Israel chatea dinga Kipana Thupha ahi–Jesu Christa mijouse Pakai vanga Pathen toh kichamna aum hi. 
John in twilut thudol ahin lhangsap pat a pat Galilee a kipan Judea gam pumpia ipi thilsoh em nang hon naheuve. 
Chule Pathen'in Nazareth Jesu chu Lhagao Theng le thahatna a thao anua chule Jesu avalea thilpha abol ho leh Pathen'in aumpi jal'a satan suhgima umho aboldam chu nang hon naheuve. 
Chule keiho solchah ho hi Judah gam pumpi le Jerusalem'a athilbol ho jouse hettohsah kahiuve. Ama chu thingpel chunga akhaisang un athat tauve. 
Ahin Pathen'in ama chu anithum nin akaithouvin aki phondohsah tan ahi. 
Hichu mijouse henga hilouvin, athu hettohsah dinga Pathen'in ana lhendohsa, athodoh jouva ama toh nekhom le donkhom keiho henga kiphong ahi. 
Chule Jesu chu mihing le mithi ho jouse thutan ahi ti thudol hi hille ding le hettohsah dinga keiho hi thu eipeh u ahi. 
Ama tahsan ho jouse Amin jal a achonset nau kingaidam ding ahi tia themgao ho jousen a hettohsah u chu ama chu ahi,” ati. 
Chuin Peter in thu hicheng asei jing laiyin athu ngaiho jouse chunga Lhagao Theng ahung chutan ahile, 
Peter toh hung khom Judah mi atahsan ho chun chidang namdang te chunga jong Lhagao Theng akipeh chu kidang asalheh jengun ahi. 
Ijeh-inem itile ama hon paochom paochoma thu aseiyuva Pathen avahchoi uchu ajah u ahi. 
Chuin Peter in adong un, “Eiho banga Lhagao Theng changa hite ho baptize chan louna dinga twi kham theiya koi umam?” ati. 
Chuin aman Jesu Christa min'a baptize chang dingin amaho chu thu apetan, chujouvin Cornelius in amaho henga nikho phabep um dingin alhotan ahi.
