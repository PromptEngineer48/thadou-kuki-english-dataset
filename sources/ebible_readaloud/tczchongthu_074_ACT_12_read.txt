Acts.
12.
Chuin hiche phatlai chun Leng Herod Agrippa chun Houbung sunga seijui phabep ho chu ahin sugim pan tan ahi. 
Chuti chun John sopipa solchah Jacob chu chemjamin ana that tan ahi. 
Hiche thilsoh hin Judah te akipasah chu Herod in amu phat in Peter jong chu aman in, (Hiche thilsoh hi Kalchuh Kut kimanlai ahi). 
Chuin ama chu Songkul'a akhum tan angah dingin Sepai lili a kiloikhom loili akoiyin ahi. Herod in Kalchuh Kut kichai tengleh mipi anga Peter chu dinsah dinga lung angaito ahi. 
Ahin Peter songkul'a aum phat chun Houbung mite chu lunglut tah in ama dingin atao tauvin ahi. 
Chuin mipi anga dinsah dinga agot jan chun Peter chu sepai ni kikah a thihkhao ni a kihen a ihmu ahin; angah ho jong chu kotbula dingah ahiuvin ahi. 
Chuin hetmanlou kah in vah hattah khat in aumna mun chu ahin salvah in chule Pakai vantil chu Peter angsunga ahung dingin, chuin Vantil chun Peter pangpeh abengin “Thoudoh loiyin,” tin asukhang tan ahile akihenna thihkhao jong chu akilham tan ahi. 
Chuin Vantil chun Peter jah a “Kivondoh loiyin lang na kengchot kisen,” asei bangin Peter in aboltan ahile Vantil chun “Napon kisil inlang kanung hin juijin,” tin thu apetan ahi. 
Chuin Peter in aumna mun chu adalhan Vantil nung chu ajui tan ahi. Ahin Peter in thilsoh jouse chu gaochanga munei bangin akigelin atah tah ahilam ahedoh pon ahi. 
Chuin amanin kot ngah masa le anina akhel phat lhon'in khopi sung lam jotna a thih kot phung chu agei lhon'in, kot chu amani dingin achamin akihong jengin, chuin amani achedoh lhon'in khosung lamlen khat ajot lhung lhon'in ahile, hichun Vantil chun ama adalha pai tan ahi. 
Chuin Peter in kho ahin hedoh tan, “Thilsoh chu atah ahi! Pakaijin a Vantil ahin solin Herod lengpa leh Judah lamkai hon kachunga abolgot u thilsea kon'in eihuhdoh e,” ati. 
Chuin aman thilsoh ho ahin hetdoh phat in John Mark nu Mary in'a achetan ahile mi tamtah ana taokhomun, 
Chuin aman kot chu akiuvin ahile soh numei Rhoda kiti nu chu kot hong dingin ahungin ahi. 
Chuin numei nu chun Peter awso ahi ahetdoh phat chun kot jong hong louvin insunga anung lhailut in miho jah a chun “Peter kotbula adinge!” agatin ahi. 
Chuin miho chun amanu jah a “Angol thim nahim,” atiuve, ahin amanu chun ahi mong monge ati phat chun amaho chun akhutile “Avantil hinah ta,” atiuvin ahi. 
Alangkhat ah Peter in kot chu akiu kiu jengin ahi. Achainakeiya ama hon kot chu ahona avet ule Peter chu amu phat un kidang asa lheh jengun ahi. 
Chuin Peter in amaho chu thipbeh a um dingin akhut in avetsah in, amaho kom'a chun Pakaijin kidang tah a songkul'a konna ahin puidoh dan thusim chu aseipeh tan ahi. Chule “Hiche thu chu Jacob le asopiho seipeh un,” atin ama hikom'a kon chun munchoma achetan ahi. 
Chuin kho ahung vah phat in sepai ho chun i-atileh Peter chu songkul'a konna potdoh thei ham tin alungdong lheh jeng uvin ahi. 
Chuin Herod lengpan sepai ho kom'a chun Peter chu phatecha aholna dingun thu apen ahinlah amu dehlou phat un, songkul ngah a pang sepai ho chu thudoh aneijin ajonan amaho chu athat tan ahi. Chujou chun Herod lengpa jong Judah gam dalhan phat chomkhat um dingin Caesarea ajon tan ahi. 
Tun Herod lengpa chu Tyre le Sidon mite chunga alunghang lheh jeng tan ahileh ama hon thalheng in mi asol uvin amaho chun Herod lengpa noiya natong Blastus chu athujo jouvun Herod lengpa toh chamna sem dingin aga thum tauvin ahi. Ajeh chu Tyre le Sidon gamsunga ding neh le chah hi Herod lengpa gamsunga konna hungji ahi. 
Chuin Herod in athum nau chu asanpeh in akitep nikhou ahung lhunin Herod in aleng von ahin kivonin, aleng touna a atouvin amaho kom'a chun thu asei tan ahi. 
Chuin mipi ho chun “Hiche hi mihem awso ahipoi, Pathen aw ahi,” atiuvin hasap in asam tauvin ahi. 
Chuin mipi hon chutia asap pettah u chun Herod in Pathen loupina peh sangin ama akiloupi sah tan ahile Pakai vantil in Herod chunga natna ahin lhunsah tan, chuti chun lungin anen athitan ahi. 
Alangkhat ah Pathen thu chu akithe jal jingin seijui apung jingin ahi. 
Chuin Barnabas le Saul in Jerusalem'a akin lhon achai lhon phat in John Mark apui lhon'in akile tauvin ahi.
