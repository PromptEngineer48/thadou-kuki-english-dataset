Acts.
20.
Chuin mipi sousang na chu athip phat in Paul in seijui ho chu agakousah in atilkhou jouvin, damselun tin akikhen pin ama Macedonia lamah achetan ahi. 
Chuche gamsunga ajotpa na khopi jousea seijui ho atilkhou peh in chuin Greece khopi chanin achesuh tan ahi. 
Chuin hiche khopia chun lhathum sung aumin, Syria gam lama konga kinung tol ding angaiton ahile Juda mi phabep in tha ding agot u ahetdoh phat in Macedonia lang joh jotpa dingin akile kit tan ahi 
Ama chu mi phabep toh alhon'in ahi. Amaho chu: Berea a kon Pyrrhus chapa Sopater, Thessalonica a kon Aristarchus le Secundus, Derbe a kon Gaius, Timothy, chule Asia gam'a konna Tychicus le Trophimus ahiuve. 
Amaho chu ana masauvin Troas mun'a eina ngah un ahi. 
Chuin Kalchuh Kut kichaijin Macedonia gam'a Philippi khopia kon'in konga katouvun ni nga jouvin Troas mun'a amaho heng kalhung uvin, chuin hiche mun achun hapta khat kaumun ahi. 
Chuin nimasa nin seijui ho toh Pakai Anjon jong dingin kakikhomun hichea chun Paul in amaho henga thu aseijin, ajinga ama chekit ding ahitah jeh chun jankim chan ahoulimin ahi. 
Hiche kakikhop nau indan chungnunga chu meivah tamtah kide ahi. 
Chuin Paul in phat sottah thu asei jeh chun bangkot kom'a tou gollhang chapang khat amin Eutychus atiu chu aihmutso beh seh jeh chun aihmulhu tan ahile bangkot a kon chun in danthum a sanga chun akileh lhan noilama athisa in aga muvin ahi. 
Chuin Paul akumsuh in abungin akhut in hiche gollhang chapang pa chu apomin, “lungdong hih un ahing nalaiye” ati. 
Chuin amaho indan chungnunga akaltou kit un Pakai Anjon changlhah aneuvin ahi. Hiti chun Paul in thusei abanjom kit in khovah lhah phat in aman adalha tauvin ahi. 
Alangkhat ah gollhang chapang pa jong chu aki suhkhahna beihel in inlam'a akilen ahileh mijouse chu alungmong lheh jengun ahi. 
Chuin Paul chu Assos chanin keng lamah ana chepeh in, keiho konga kacheuva chule ama kalahpana diuva agontoh ahi. 
Chuin hiche mun'a kon chun ama jong keiho toh konga katou khomun Mitylene chan ka cheuvin ahi. 
Ajingin Kios twikol ka khokhel un, akit in Samos twikol kajot paikit un chule nikhat jouvin Miletus twikol kalhung uvin ahi. 
Chuin Asia gamsunga aumsot nomlou phat in Paul in konga touva Ephesus khopi jotpai ding lung angaito tan ahi. Kinotah a cheloiya ahithei le Pentecost masanga Jerusalem lhun tei ding ati ahi. 
Ahin Miletus kalhun un Ephesus Houbung a um upaho henga ahung kimupi diuvin thu athot tan ahi. 
Chuin amaho ahung lhun phat un aman “Nanghon naheuve, Asia gamsung kahung chotlut nikhoa pat tuni chanin, 
Kineosah tah le mitlhi pumin Pakai na katongin, Juda te a konna gimbolna tamtah jong kathoh in ahi. 
Chule nangho jah ding imacha imlouvin, japi lah le intin'ah kahil leuvin ahi. 
Juda te le Greek te henga thukhat seh hichu, chonsetna a kon lungheiya chule Pathen lama kiheiya, chule Pakai Jesu tahsan ding thu bou chu kasei jing ahi. 
Tun keima Lhagaovin Jerusalem'a che dingin eisom tan, ipin einga ham jong kahepoi. 
Khopi tin a Lhagao Theng in eihetsah bangin thohgimna le songkul tanna tailou ima kahepoi. 
Ahin ka hinkho hi Pakai Jesun eingansena banga midang te henga Pathen mi ngailutna kidangtah Kipana Thupha sap phongna natohna a ka manchah loule thil dang amanna ding kahepoi. 
Chule tunia Pathen Lenggam thudol ka seipeh miho hin keima eimukit tah lou diu ahi. 
Tu nikhoa hi ka phondoh ahi, keiman kitah tah a ka seipeh nahiuvin nalah uva koi tobang khat tonsot thina toh aum khah a ahile keima thepmo ahi tapoi. 
Ajeh chu Pathen'in nangho hetsah dinga adeisah jouse imlouhel a ka seipeh nahi tauve. 
Chule tun, chingtheiyun, nang hon Pathen kelngoi hon, athisan'a achohdoh houbung, chu vah unlang chingtup un, hiche dinga chu Lhagao Theng in upa a napansah u ahi. 
Ajeh chu kadalhah tengu le nangho lah a konna ngeihang tobang thuhillhem hunglut a kelngoi ho hi khatcha ahin hoilou ding kahei. 
Chule nangho lah jenga jong hi mi phabep in amaho thu ahinjop a thutah chu akaleh a ahin hil dingu ahi. 
Hijeh chun nangho kiging phauvin! Kumthum sunga asun ajana mitlhi tamtah puma kaumpiu chu geldoh jingun. 
Chule tunia hi keiman nangho hi Pathen le ami ngailutna thu alhenchomte toh goulo khom dinga natungdoh theiyu thua chu kanganse nahiuve. 
Keiman koima dangka le Sana chule nihchen ponsil hoi ka otcha poi. 
Ajeh chu kakhut a konna keima ngaichat le kaum khompi ho ngaichat ding katoh a kakivah ahi. 
Chule na hatoh a toh'a migenthei ho kithopi ding dan keima tah hinkhoa namu u ahitai. Pakai Jesun ‘Akisan ho sanga apeho nunnom jo ahi’ ati chu geldoh jingun.” 
Chuin thu asei jouvin adilsun amaho toh ataokhomun ahi. 
Amaho abonchauvin akap un akikoiyun damselin, tin akikhen tauvin ahi. 
Neimu kit tah lou diu ahi ati jeh chun amaho abonchauvin lungngui tah in ama chu kong mun'a agathah tauvin ahi.
