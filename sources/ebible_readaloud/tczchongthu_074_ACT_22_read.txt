Acts.
22.
Chuin Paul in, “Sopite le jaumtah pate ho, kakihkonna thu kasei hi ngaiyuvin,” 
Amaho paova thu asei chu ajah phat un mipi ho chu athipchet jengun, 
Chuin Paul in, “Keima Tarsus gam'a Cilicia khopia peng Juda mi kahin, chule chihna thepna lama Jerusalem'a Gamaliel noiya hung seilen kahi. Ama noiya hung seilen khat kahi jeh chun Juda te dan le chonna ho phatecha hung kijil kahi. Tunia nangho jouse banga hi Pathen a dinga tompan tah mi kana hin ahi. 
Chule anungjui ho ka sugentheiyin, abang kathat jin, chule numei pasal kamanin songkul'ah kakhum jin ahi. 
Thempu Chungnung le kivaihomna upahon hiche hi eihetpeh u ahi. Ajeh chu Damascus a um sopi Juda te henga kahunga Christian ho kamat a suhgenthei dinga Jerusalem'a kahin kaina dinga amaho kom'a phalna lekha kahin kichoi ahi.” 
Ahin Damascus khopi lut ding kon lampi laitah ah, hichu sunkim ahin, hichun van'a kon'in vah hattah khat in kakimvel ahin kho tan, 
Keijong tol'a kalhu jengin ahile aw khat kajan, “Saul, Saul, ipi dinga nei bolset jing ham?” ahin tin ahi. 
Chuin ken jong, “Nang koi nahim?” kati. Chuin aw chun eihin donbut in 'Keima nabolset jing Nazareth Jesu chu kahi' ahin tin ahi. 
Chuin kakilhonpi miho chun vah chu amuvin, ahin kakom'a thusei aw chun ipi asei ham ahe pouve. 
Hichun “Ipi kabol ding ham Pakai” tin kadongin ahile, Pakaijin eihin donbut in 'Thoudoh inlang Damascus khosunga khun lut in chuteng ipi nabol ding nakisei peh ding ahi,' ati. 
Vah hattah jeh a kamit in kho amutah lou jeh in kalhonpi ho chun kakhut in eikaiyun khopi sunga eikailut un ahi. 
Hichea chun Ananias ana chengin ama chu Pathen gingtah mi ahin Juda te dan juithem tah le Damascus khopia um Juda te jousen angaisang tah mi khat ahi. 
Ama chu kakom'a ahung dingin, “Sopipa Saul, kho mukit tan atin ahile apettah chun ama chu kamu tan ahi.” 
Chuin aman, “Ipu ipateu Pathen chun alunggot lam heding le, dihtah pa mua, chule athu ngai dinga nalhen ahitai” ati. 
Ajeh chu nangman namu ho leh najah ho jouse chu nasei phong le-a ama thu hettohsah a napan ding ahitai. 
Ipi na khongai nahlai ham? Kipatdoh in lang baptize chang tan, Pakai min kouvin lang na chonset soplhan eiti. 
Chuin Jerusalem kahung kile chun Hou-In ah dilsun kataovin ahile, 
Gaovin Jesu kamun aman, 'Kipat loiyin! Jerusalem'a kon'in jamdoh in ajeh chu hiche laiya miho hin keima thudol'a nathusei asanlou dingu ahi' ati. 
Ahin keiman kaseijin, “Pakai kikhopna in tin a seijuiho kasuh gentheiya songkul'a kakhum jong ahe sohkei uve” 
Chule nasohpa Stephen aselih pet uva asangkhol sutlhah hou akoinau kom'a ding jong chu kana hinai, kati. 
Ahin Pakaijin, 'Chen, gamla tah a chidang namdang te lah a kasol ding nahi!' ati. 
Hichun mipi hon chuche Paul thusei chu ajah phat un asam uvin, “Hitobang mi chu delmang un, ahing dinga jong lom ahipoi!” atiuvin ahi. 
Chuin mipi chun asangkhol u asut un, vanlama vutvai atheuvin ahi. 
Chuin Sepai vaipopa chun, Paul chu inpi sunga apuilut sah in, mipi achunga chubang tah a alunghan nau ajeh seidoh sah ding ngaito nan jepsah dingin thu apetan ahi. 
Chuin Paul chu jep dinga ahin kan phat un aman akom'a ding sepai lamkaipa henga chun, “Rome mikhat athemmona khol masa louva jep ding chu dan in aphal hinam?” tin adongin ahi. 
Chuin sepai lamkai pan chuche ajah phat chun sepai vaipopa kom'a achen, “Ipi bol nahim? Ama hi Rome mi hibouva hila!” atin ahi. 
Chuin sepai vaipopa chu Paul henga ahungin, “Nangma Rome mi nahim? seiyin,” tin adong tan ahile Paul in jong “Kahinai,” ati. 
Chuin sepai vaipopa chun aseijin “Kei jong sum tampi kapeh jouva Rome mihina chang kahi bouve,” atin ahile Paul in “Keivang kapen apat a Rome mi a peng kahi,” ati. 
Chuin Paul in Rome mi kahi ati ajahdoh phat in thudong dinga kigong sepai ho chu gang tah in apotdoh tauvin ahi, chule sepai vaipopa jong chun chutobang thupeh anei jeh chun akicha lheh jengin ahi. 
Ajing nikhon Paul chung changa boina thu hetdohna dingin sepai vaipopa chun Juda te kivaihom na a lamkaiho leh thempu ho chu hungkhom dingin thu apen, chule amaho masanga ding dingin Paul chu ana lhadoh tan ahi.
