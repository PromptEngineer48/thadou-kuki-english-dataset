Acts.
23.
Chuin Paul in houvaipo ho chu ave changmel in, “Sopi te ho, Pathen angsunga se le pha hetkhen na kicheh tah neiya hingjing kahi!” 
Chuin Thempu Chungnung Ananias chun Paul toh kinaiya umho kom'a chun amuhsom abehpeh dingun thu apen ahi. 
Ahin Paul in, “Phat louna a dim miphalhem pa! Pathen'in nangma nabeh ding ahi, itobang thutan mi nahiya nangman dan napal keh a chutobang a eibeng dinga mi thu napeh ham?” ati. 
Chuin Paul kimvela ding ho chun, “Pathen Thempu Chungnung chu na housal ngam ham?” atiuvin ahi. 
Paul in adonbut un, “Sopi te ho, neingai damun, Thempu Chungnung ahilam kana hetlou ahi, ajeh chu Pathen Lekhabun ‘nalamkai te chunga thuse nasei louding ahi’ tin akisune” ati. 
Chuin Paul in hou vaipoloi ho kikhopna a chu Sadducee ho leh Pharisee ho kihal ahiu ahet doh phat chun, hatah in asamin, “Sopi te ho, kapu kapate khanga pat a Pharisee mi kahin! Thokit thudol'a kinepna kanei jeh a kachung thu kitan ahi!” atin ahi. 
Hijeh chun Pharisee ho le Sadducee ho chu hopnin aki homkhen tauvin ahi. 
Ajeh chu Sadducee ho chun mihem thokitna, vantil, chule lhagao um thudol atahsan lou u ahi, ahin Pharisee ho vang chun aboncha a hi atahsan u ahi. 
Chuin akah uva boina asousang tan, houdan thuhil'a pang Pharisee phabep chun thunei tah in, “Hiche pa chunga hin thildih lou ima aumpoi, lhagao ahilou le vantil in ahenga thu asei hitei ding ahi,” atiuvin ahi. 
Chuin akah uva kitomona asan cheh cheh phat in sepai vaipopa chun Paul chu kehni sokhauvin te tin, sepai ho chu asol'in Paul chu hunam in aga huhdoh sah in kulpi sunga apuilut sah tan ahi. 
Hiche ni jan chun Paul henga Pakai akilah in, “Paul, lungneo hih in. Hiche Jerusalem'a keima nei phondoh banga hi, Rome a jong Kipana Thupha hettohsah a napan ding ahi” ati. 
Ajing jingkah in Juda miho phabep akiloiyun Paul itha tokah uva an ineh lou diu ahi tin akihahsel tauvin ahi. 
Chutia kigahna sem chu abonun mi somli aval uvin ahi. 
Chuin amaho thempulen ho leh upaho henga acheuvin aga seiyuvin, “Paul katha tokah uva ima nelou dinga kihahsel kahiuve,” aga tiuvin ahi. 
“Hijeh chun nangho vaipo hon sepai vaipopa henga gaseiyun lang Paul chu achung chang thudol kholchet be dingin houvaipo ho anga hin nung puikit in tin gathum uvin, chutengleh keihon ama chu lamkah a katha diu ahi,” atiuvin ahi. 
Ahin hiche tohgon chu Paul sopinu chapan ajah lhih phat in kulpi sunga alhailut jelin Paul henga agasei tan ahi. 
Chuin Paul in Rome sepai jalamkai khat akouvin, “Hiche khangdong pa hi sepai vaipi popa henga puilut uvin ajeh chu sei ding thupitah khat aneiye,” ati. 
Chuin Sepai jalamkai pa chun aseijin, “Songkul'a um Paul chun eikouvin, hiche khangdong pa hin sepai vaipipo henga seiding thupitah aneiye atin eihin puilut sah ahi,” atin ahi. 
Chuin sepai vaipipo chun akhut in atuh in, “Ipi ham nasei nom chu?” tin adong tan ahi. 
Chuin Paul sopinu chapa chun, “Jing nikho tengleh Juda mi phabep in hou vaipoloi anga dinsah kit dinga nahenga konna achungthu kholbe ding kisa a Paul ahung puidoh got diu ahi. 
Ahin soldoh hih in ajeh chu mi somli valin lamkah a chanlhih a chuleh atha tokah uva ima nelou dingin akihahsel uve, chule tua hi nangma thulhuh bou angau ahitai,” tin asei tan ahi. 
Chuin sepai vaipipo chun khangdong pa henga, “Keima kom'a hiche thu nahung sei hi koima hetsah hih in,” tin agihsal tan ahi. 
Chuin sepai vaipipo chun sepai jalamkai ni akouvin “Tujan nidan ko tengleh Caesarea jon dingin sepai jani gotsan koi lhon'in, chule tengchoi sepai jani le sakol sepai somsagi jong gong tup lhon'in. 
Chule Paul touna ding sakol khat jong gong lhon'in lang gamvaipo Felix henga kitup selin gathah un,” atin ahi. 
Chuin gamvaipo ding chun lekha khat ajih in, 
“Jaumtah gamvaipo Felix, Claudius Lysias a kon'in salam!” tin athot in ahi. 
“Hiche pa hi Juda ten abulhu uva tha dinga anagot pettah un sepai hotoh kagalut un, Rome mi ahi kahetdoh phat in bitkeiyin kahin huhdoh e. 
Chuin ahehset nau hetdohna dingin hou vaipoloi angsunga kadinsah in ahileh, 
Aheh nau hi amaho houthu toh kisai ahi kahedoh tan, ima songkul tanna ding le thana ding kamupon ahi. 
Ahin tha dinga agot u kahet doh phat a apet pet a nahenga kahin sol ahitai, chule ahehse ho jong nangma henga hung dingin ka seipeh tauve,” ati. 
Thupeh dungjuijin sepai hon Paul chu Antipatris heng alhut un ahi. 
Ajing jingkah in amaho ahung kinung leuvin ahin sakol sepai hon Paul chu Caesarea chan aga thah un ahi. 
Chuin Caesarea alhun un Paul le Lekhathot chu gamvaipo Felix henga apelut tauvin ahi. 
Chuin lekhathot chu asim jouvin Paul chu “hoilai gamkai mi nahim” tin adongin ahileh Paul in “Cilicia,” ati. 
Chuin gamvaipo chun, “Na hehse ho ahung lhun teng nachung thu kakhol ding ahi,” atin chujouvin gamvaipo chun Herod inpia songkul'a koi dingin thu apetan ahi.
