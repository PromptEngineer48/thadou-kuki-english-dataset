Acts.
24.
Chuin ni nga jouvin gamvaipo henga Paul ahehset nau thusei dingin Thempu Chungnung Ananias le Juda te upa phabep chule Tertullus kiti minelkal them chu ahunglhung tauvin ahi. 
Chuin Paul chu ahin puilut uvin ahile Tertullus chun gamvaipo henga Paul hehna thu hitin ahin seitan ahi: “Nangma vaihom themna le nangma jal'a keiho Juda te hi phat sottah lungmonga um kahiuve.” 
“O Lal chungnung, hiche ho jeh hin keiho kakipah lheh jeng uve. 
Ahin nangma kalungjin sah nompoi hijeh chun nei lungset inlang phat chom chakhat neingai peh in tia kahung tao ahi. 
Hiche pa hi phatseh a Juda te umna jousea boina semjing kei mi ahi kamudoh un, chule thuhillhem Nazareth Jesu chon a chon ho lamkai pipu ahi ti kamudoh uve. 
Chukit jong le kamat uva chu aman Hou-In jong suhboh ding agot ahi. 
Keihon keiho dan dungjuiya athu katan got u ahin, hinlah sepai jalamkai Lysias in thahat san'a kakhut uva konna ahung puidoh ahi. 
Kaheh nau hi nangma tah in dong lechun adihtah chu namat doh ding ahi,” atin ahi. 
Chuin Judah mi adang ho jong chun Tertullus in asei jouse adih ahi atiuvin ahi. 
Chuin gamvaipo chun Paul jong thu asei sah in, chuin Paul in, “Pu, kum tamtah Juda te chunga thutan'a hung pang nahi kahen, hijeh chun na masanga kipah tah in kaki hkonna thu seiying kate. 
Jerusalem'a Pathen hou dinga kahung lhun jouva pat ni som le ni jong lhing loulai ahi hi nahetdoh jeng ding ahi. 
Eihehse ho hin Hou-In'a mitoh kakinel na jong amu naisai lou diu ahin, kikhopna in ahilou le khopi lamlhong lah a boina kasem jong amukhah lou dingu ahi. 
Hiche mihon eihehset nau hi khat cha aphotchet na apeh theilou dingu ahi.” 
Ahin hou dihlou lamkai ahi atiu vang chu kanom'e, ajeh chu kapu kapa teuvin ana hou Pathen chu kahou ahin, Juda te dan le themgao lekhabua kisun jouse dihtah a katahsan ahi. 
Amaho kinepna banga keijong Pathen'in nikhat le midih le midihlou ahin kaithou kit ding ahi tia kinem kahi. 
Hiche jeh a hi mijouse le Pathen anga dihtah a hinkho man ding kagot jing ahi. 
Chuin kum tamtah jouvin kamite kithopina sum peding le Pathen kom'a kilhaina bol dingin Jerusalem'a kahung kilen ahileh, 
Hou-In sunga kauma ka kisuhtheng chu eihehse hon amu uvin, hiche pet a chu kavel'a mipi jong umlou ahin boina ima jong aumpon ahi. 
Ahin Asia gamsunga konna Judaho mi phabep umu ahi, chule amaho chu kei hehsetna ding hola um jing'u ahi! 
Juda te hou vaipoloi hon kachunga ipi chonsetna amu uham hiche miho hi dongin, 
Thilkhat bouseh “thokit thudol katahsan jeh a tunia na angsung uva kachung thu kikhol ahi,” tia kasap tailou adang ima namu lou dingu ahi. 
Chuche tahsanna lampi thudol hethem tah ahitoh kilhon'in, Felix chun phat asuh saobe in, “Vaipipo Lysias hung lhun kah in khongaiyun, ama ahung tengleh nathuu ngaito chen nange,” ati. 
Chuin sepai jalamkaipa kom'a chun, “Paul chu songkul'a koiyun lang ahin aloi agol hon avila angaichat ho abolpeh theina dingun phalna peuvin,” ati. 
Nichomkhat jouvin Felix chu ajinu Juda numei Drusilla toh ahung kit in, chuin Paul chu aga kousah lhon'in Paul in Jesu Christa tahsan thudol asei chu angai lhon'in ahi. 
Chuin Paul in chondihna le eima chang kitim hat chule khonunga hunglhung ding thutanna thu asei ajah phat chun Felix chu ahung kicha tan, “Tun chen lang, phat gomkom aum tengleh kahin koukit ding nahi,” tin asoldoh tan ahi. 
Chujongleh Paul chun sum eithimpeh khante tin, akouloi loijin ahoupi jing jin ahi. 
Chutia kumni ache jouvin, Porcius Festus in Felix panmun chu alotan, chule Felix in Juda te lung ahol jeh chun Paul chu songkul'a akhum in adalha tan ahi.
