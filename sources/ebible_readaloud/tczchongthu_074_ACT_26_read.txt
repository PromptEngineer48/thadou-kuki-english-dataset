Acts.
26.
Chujou chun, Agrippa in Paul jah ah, “Nakihkonna thu phong theijin nate,” ati. Hichun Paul in akhut alhaang doh in akihkonna thu aseitai. 
“Kakipah lheh jeng e, Leng Agrippa, tunia Juda lamkai hon themmo eichen nau chung changa kakihkonna thu ja ah nang bou nahi. 
Ajeh chu Judah te kivaipoh dol le akinel baotam nau hethem ah nahi kahen, hijeh chun ka thusei hi lungneng tah in nei ngaipeh in.” 
Keima ka chapan laija pat a kanam mite lah le Jerusalem a kon hung seilen a, Juda nam chon le khan kichuh themsa kahi hi Juda lamkai hon ahet sohkeiyu ahi. 
Ama hon aseinom ule, atil apat a ahet jing sau Pharisee mi, hou thudol'a chondan kikhel tah kahi aheuve. 
Chule tua hi kapu kapa teu henga Pathen tepsa hung guilhung ding kakinepna thudol'a, kachung thu khol dia hikom'a ding ah kahi. 
Adihtah a seidin, Israel phungsom le phung nin sun le jan a anahou jingu ahi. Vo Lengpa hiche kinepna thudol'a Juda ten eiheh-u ahi bouve. 
Ipi jeh a Pathen'in mithisa athodoh sah thei thudol hi tahsan lel nahiuvem? 
Keima jeng jong Nazareth Jesu min douna a thil ijakai bol ding kana ngaitosa ahi. 
Atahbeh in jong Jerusalem'a kana bol in, Thempu pipuiho a kon'in thuneina kalan, mitheng tampi songkul'ah kana khum in chule atha jengu jong chu pha kasan ahi. 
Phat atamjon hou thudol'a akikhop nau intin'a jong talent kamat sah jin, Jesu atahsan nau taitom sah in hoise tah in kana douvin, gamdang miho khopi dung geijin kana nocheh jin ahi. 
Nikhat hi katoh ban tong jom din Thempu pipuiho thuneina le thusah kengin Damascus jonin kachen ahi. 
Sunkim dontah in, vo Lengpa, lampia chun van'a kon nisa sanga vahjo vah khat in kei le kalhonpi ho eihin kho'uvin ahile. 
Kabon chauvin kalhu soh tauvin ahi, hichun, Aramaic paovin kajah a, “Saul, Saul, ibola nangin nei bolset jing ham? Neidou jing hi nanga din aphat chomna aumpoi,” eiti. 
Hichun keiman, “Nang koi nahim, Pakai?” kati. “Aman eihin donbut in, ‘nabol set jing Jesu chu kahi. 
Tun nakeng in pansan dingin, nangma leiset kolkim vel'a kathu hettohsah le kalhacha a pang ding, chule nathil mu chengse le kahin musah kit ding chengse hettohsah a pansah dinga hung kilah kahi 
Chule kasolna chidang namdang te lah le nanam mite lah a konna kahuhdoh jing ding nahi, 
Amit'u hasah a, muthim lah a konna lungheiya khovah ahin mun'a thei diu le satan thaneina a konna Pathen henga ahung kiheina thei diu, chule chonset ngaidamna achan theina diu le tahsan jal a Pathen mite ahi theina diuva kasol ding nahi,’ eiti. 
Chule, Leng Agrippa, vanlam'a kon themgao thilmu kamu thua nung ah kahi. 
Amasan Damascus a umho henga kahil lhanglen, chujouvin Jerusalem le Juda gamkol sung chukit le chidang namdang ho hengah, chonset kisih a Pathen lam ahin belna diu le alunghei utoh lhon a thilpha abolna diuvin kahil lhanglei. 
Hiche thu kahil lhang le jeh a hi Juda hon Hou-In sunga eimat uva, tha ding eigot u ahi. 
Ahin tuphat chana Pathen venbitna kachan jeh in mijouse, aneo kom hihen alenkom hijongleh hettohsah in kapang jing e. Thudang ima kahilpoi Themgao ho leh Mose’n hung guilhung dinga aseiyu– 
Christan hesoh genthei athoh a, thina a konna thoudoh masapen a pang ding, chuleh Juda te le chidang namdang ho henga Pathen vah thudol aphondoh ding je kasei ahi bouve,” ati. 
Photlot louvin Festus chu ahung pengjah jengin, “Paul angol ah nahitai; na lekha thepnan na ngolsah ahitai,” ati. 
Hichun Paul in adonbut in, “Angol a kahipoi, vo Lalchungnung Festus, kicheh tah a thudih kasei joh ahi bouve. 
Chule Agrippa Lengpan jong ahei. Ijeh inem itile aning koi a kibol ahilou jeh in aman hitobang ho hi ahesoh keiye ti kichen tah in kahei, hijeh a chu hangsan tah a kasei ahi,” ati. 
Leng Agrippa, themgao ho na tahsan em? Natahsan e ti kahei, ati. 
Hichun Agrippa in, “Kintah a Christian hisah jeng thei dia nei gel ham?” ati. 
Chuin Paul in adonbut in, “Gang henlang gang hih jong le, nang le hiva mipi um jouse hi, thihkhaova kakihenna ho tilouvin keima tobang hisohkei ule tin, Pathen hengah kataove,” ati. 
Hichun Lengpa, Gamvaipo, Bernice chule adang ho se adingdoh un adalha tauve. 
Apotdoh uva akihou nau le akinopto nau chun “Hiche mipa hin thina le songkul tanna thei chan gei ima bolset aneipoi,” atiuve. 
Chule Agrippa in Festus jah a, “Aman Caesar henga dinga ngehna ana bollou hile lhadoh thei ahitai,” ati.
