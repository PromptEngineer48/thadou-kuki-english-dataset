Acts.
27.
Chuin aphat ahung lhun phat in Italy che dingin konga katou tauvin ahi. Paul le songkul atan khompi mi phabep chu Rome sepai lamkai ho lah a Julius atiu khut a chun apedoh tauvin ahi. 
Chule Macedonia mi Thessalonica a konna Aristarchus jaonan, Asia gamsung sah-lhumlam a Adramyttium kong ngahmun'a kon'in konga kache tauvin ahi. Chule hiche kong chu Asia gamsung muntin'a kingapa dinga kigong ahi. 
Chuin ajinga Sidon kong ngahmun kalhunun, Julius chun ngailut tah a Paul chu twipanga aloiho vilthei dinga phalna apeh jeh chun achen ahileh aloihon angaichat ho jouse ahin kithopi uvin ahi. 
Chuin hichea konna twi chunga kachelai tah un huipi hattah khat in keiho lam nga-a einun den phat un kache jom thei tapouvin, hijeh chun Cyprus twikol sahlam twipi panga kache peh tauvin ahi. 
Hiti chun Cilicia le Pamphylia twipi kahopaiyun, Lycia gamsunga Myra mun'ah kaki ngauvin ahi. 
Chuin hikom'a chun sepai jalamkai pan Alexandria a konna Egypt te kong Italy jon ding khat ahin mu phat in hiche konga chun eiheng doh tauvin ahi. 
Chuin nikho tamtah ol olin kakitolun hahsatah in Cnidus kanai ham-ham'un, huiyin eilenan jeh un Crete twikol pang panga kakitolun, Salmone jon kajot un ahi. 
Twi pamchin china hahsatah in kacheuvin, nahchangsalin Lasea khopi muntoh kinaicha a um Kongkai Munhoi atiu chu kalhung tauvin ahi. 
Chuin phat tampi kamansah jouvun, konga chejom ding la aphat toh kitoh a toset toding kichat aum jeh chun, Paul in kongtol ho akimupin, 
“Sopi te ho, iche jom uva ahile, kongkeh thei, thilkeo ho jong mangthei, chule ihinkhou jeng jong ichan theiyu ahi,” tin aseitan ahi. 
Ahivangin songkul tangho chunga mopo lamkai pa chun, Paul thusei sangin Kong neipa le kongtol ho thusei joh chu angaiyin ahi. 
Chuin Kongkai Munhoi chu phalbia umna dinga ahahsat jep jeh chun, konga natong ho atamjo chun Crete twipi panga Phoenix mun lhuntei ding hikom'a chu phalbi man ding adeisah un ahi. Phoenix mun chu kongkaimun nomtah ahin sah-lhum le lhang-lhum angatto ahi. 
Chuin lhanglama kon'in hui ol olin ahung nung pan tan ahileh kongtol hon kong chethei nante tin khao alhauvin Crete vapang panga atol tauvin ahi. 
Ahivangin chomkhat jouvin hui chedan ahung kikhelin twikhanglen'a kon'in huipi le gopi hattah (solam hui) atiu chu ahung nungin twipi langa einuh lut tauvin ahi. 
Chuin kongtol hon kong chu ahei joulou phat uvin, huilah a chun kong chu amoh lha tauvin ahi. 
Chuin Cauda atiu twikol neocha pang panga chun kakitol uvin anah nah in hahsatah in kongneo khat kaman ham-ham'un ahi. 
Chuin kongtol hon hichu akhaidoh un ahunan amang un, khaovin kong noilamah akanbeh tauve; chuin ajole Syrtis kilenglut get inte ti akichauvin pondal kikhaina ho akainem uvin ahile eimoh lhoh lhoh jeng tauve. 
Chuin huipi chun eisuh gim behseh jeh u chun ajing nikhon thil apaidoh pan tauvin ahi. 
Anithum nin amaho tah in akhuttah in kong manchah ho chu apai tauvin ahi. 
Chuin tijat umtah huipi hattah a nikho tamtah anun a nisa le ahsi ho eisalvah u beihela aum phat chun kihing inte tia kinepna jong aum tapon ahi. 
Chuin koima an nelouva nitamtah aum phat un Paul in mipi ho chu akoukhom un ajah uva, “Vo miho, kasei chu ngaiyuvin lang Crete chu dalhah da leuchun hitobang settona leh manthahna hi itolou dingu ahi. 
Ahin tunjong lungneu hih un, kong hi twiya lhalut jong le koiman nahin nau nachan lou dingu ahi. 
Ajeh chu janlhah in kahou le alhachana katoh jing ka Pathen vantil khat kaheng'a ahung dingin, 
Chule hitin aseijin ahi, kicha hih in Paul ajeh chu iti jong le nangma Caesar angsunga nadin teitei ding ahi, chule konga natoupi ho jouse toh nahoidoh sohkei dingu ahi. 
Hijeh chun kicha hih un! Ka Pathen'in asei tobang hi ding ahi ti katahsan'e. 
Ahin Twikol khat a vang ikongu seden tei ding ahi,” ati. 
Chuin Adria twikhanglen'a huipi hattah in eilhoh le le nau nisom le janli lhinni jankhangkimin kongtol hon kong chu tol toh akinai tai ti aging chauvin, 
Hijeh chun twi chu ateuvin ahile feet jakhat le somni bep in athuh in, chuin chomkhat jouvin atekit-un ahile feet somko bep ahitan ahi. 
Hiche phat chun twipanga songho kitat khading ahitai tin akichauvin, kong nunglama chun pondal li asepdoh un, ataovun khovah angah tauvin ahi. 
Chuin kongtol ho chu kongmai lama pondal khailha ding bang akisauvin, kong chu dalhan kongneova jam ding angaito tauvin ahi. 
Chuin Paul in Sepai jalamkaipa le asepai ho jah a “Kongtol ho kong sunga aumlou ule nangho khatcha sohcha pouvin nate,” ati. 
Chuin Sepai hon kongneo khao chu asat tanun alha mang tauve. 
Chuin ajing kho ahungvah phat in Paul in miho chu anne dingin asolin, “Lungkham tah a an le twi natoh khah lounau haptani ahitai.” 
“Tun lungset tah in nangho phatchomna dingin an netauvin ajeh chu nasam'u jangkhat jeng jong mangthah louding ahi.” ati. 
Chuin changlhah phabep alan Pathen athangvah in aheh balbal'in anetan ahi. 
Hichun midang ho jong ahung lunglen doh un ama hon jong an anetauvin ahi. 
Kabon chauva konga tou chu mi jani le somsagi le gup kahiuve. 
Chuin konga touho jousen an aneh jou phat un kong chu asujaang un, asunga um suhlou chang ho chu aseplha tauvin ahi. 
Chuin jingkah kho ahung vah phat in, aumnau vapang chu hoilai ahi ahe tapouvin ahi. Ahin twininglutna vapang khat amudoh un, ahithei le kong huhna a chu hilaimun a chu tollut ding tin angaito tauvin ahi. 
Chuin thihhat ho alha lhauvin, twikhanglen'a adalha tauve. Chuin chuche toh thakhat in kongkhe kanna chu alham lhauvin, hui chena lama pondal atungdoh un, vapam lam chu ajon tauve. 
Chuin twi kinong ni kisuhtona kalutkha tauvin ahile, kong manghem chu tol'a akiphut dentai. Chuin kong mailam chu long louhelin aohdentan, kong meilam chu twi kinong chun hatah in ahin sutan ahile kong chu akeh pantan ahi. 
Chuin sepai ho chun songkul tangho chu twi aje galkaiyuva asochat louna dingun tha ding agouvin ahi. 
Ahivangin sepai jalamkai chun Paul chu ahing hoinom jeh chun ajadan, twi japthei theiyin anajap galkai masat dingun thu apetan ahi. 
Chule adang twijap theilou ho chu kong sunga um thingpeh ham ahilou le kongse hel hoa chu kitol dingin thu apen ahile, abonchauvin twipanga bitkeiyin asohchauvin ahi.
