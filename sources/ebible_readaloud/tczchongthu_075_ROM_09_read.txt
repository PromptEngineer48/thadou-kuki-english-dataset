Rome.
9.
Keiman thudih kasei hi Christa ahetoh ahi. Ase le apha hetna kaneijin chule Lhagao Theng aphotchetna ahi. 
Kalungthim hi hatah in lunghemna le lung kicholdo theilou gimnan kadim e. 
Ajeh chu kamite Judah mite kasopiho dingin, keima Christa a konna a-itih a gaosap chang kahinom'e; hichun amaho ahuhhing jou ding hileh kati ahi. 
Amaho Israel mite ahiuvin, Pathen cha lamthu hina changah ahiuvin, Pathen'in aloupina amaho heng alhut ahitai. Aman amaho to kitepna aneiya chule a Danthu apehdoh ahiuve. Aman amaho chu ahou theina ding uva michonpha a asem ahin, chule athutep kidangtah ahin pehdoh ahiye. 
Amaho hi Abraham, Isaac chule Jacob son le chilhah te ahiuvin, chule Christa ama tah chu mihem lamdola sei dingin Israel mi anahi, chule Ama Pathen ahin, thil jouse chunga vaihoma ahin, chule tonsot a vahchoi dinga kilom ahi. 
Chuti ahileh aphai, Pathen'in Israel te chu athutep lamdola abol pehlou got hitam? Ahipoi, Israel nam sunga peng jouse chu abon uva Pathen mite ahideh pouve. 
Abraham chilhah himan'a Abraham chate tia kisim jenga ahipoi. Ajeh chu Pathen thun Isaac a na phunggui kisut ding ahi ati chun, Abraham'in chapa dang anei henasa asei ahi. 
Hiche hi, tahsalam a Abraham'in ahindoh chate jouse chu Pathen chate ahiuve tia seina ding aumpoi. Thutep chate bou chu Abraham chate tia kisima ahije. 
Ajeh chu Pathen'in athutep, “Khovei tuphat tengleh keima kahung kile kit ding Sarah chun chapa khat ahin ding ahi,” ati. 
Hiche achapa chu ipuluiyu Isaac ahi. Aman Rebekah akichenpi chun mi-ni apengkop in ahi. 
Hinlah amani ahung penlhah masang lhon'in, thilpha ahilouleh thilse abol masang lhon'in, Pathen'a kon'a thulhut amu ahi (Hiche thulhunga konna chu Pathen'in Ama lunggon dungjuiya akouji ahi, tihi ahi. 
Amaho phat le set jeh jong ahideh poi ti het ahi). Amanu kom'a, “Nachapa atahpa chu anaopa kinbol'a hung pang ding ahi,” ati. 
Pathen thun, “Keiman Jacob kangailun, hinla Esau chu kapampai ahi,” ati. 
Chuti ahileh, eihon Pathen chu athilbol adihpoi itiuvem? Ahideh poi! 
Ajeh chu Pathen'in Mose jah a, “Keiman ka khotona'a kalhendoh penpen chunga kalhut ding, chule kahepina chu kalhendoh pen chunga kalhut ding ahi,” ati. 
Hiti ahijeh chun, Pathen khotona chu Ama kigel lhah thuthu hiya ahi. Eihon ilhen theiju jong hilou, toh a itohdoh theiju jong ahidehpoi. 
Ajeh chu Pathen Lekhabun, anaseisa chu Pathen'in Pharaoh jah a, “Keiman ka thahatna na chunga katahlang na dinga keiman nangma kalhen nahin chule kaminthanna leiset pumpia kathejal ding ahi,” tia ana seipehsa ahi. 
Hijeh a chu, Pathen'in ami khotona chu mi abangkhat chunga avetsah a chule aman midang lungsung chu asuhtah a asei angaipeh lou ahi. 
Chuti ahileh aphai, nangman, “Iti dan'a athu jalou ho chu a oimo hintem? Aman amaho abolsah chan chu abol'u hijeng louding ham?” tin nate. 
Ahipoi, hichu seihih in. Nangma mihem maimai in Pathen nanel ngam ham? Thilsem khat chun asempa jah a chu, “Ipi dinga hitobang bep a hi neisem ham?” tia asei ngaijem! 
Bel dengpa chun leiset a kon'a bel ho asem tengleh ama chun aleiset leingan chu aphabep khat ajem dinga asem'a chule phabep khat loi chu nen koina a jong asemthei hilou ham? 
Hitobangma chun, Pathen'in jong alunghan le athahat vetsahna dingin thaneina aneijin, chule alunghanna chuhna ho chunga jong thohhat tah a anga a amahthahna diu gonsa akoipeh ahitai. 
Hiche abol hi aloupina ho dungjuiya, aloupina tahlangna dinga mi akhotona chu kilangdoh cheh dinga agon ahi. 
Chule eiho Aman alhendoh ho lah a hi Judah mite leh chidang namdang ho chunga hijongleh eiho jong ijao uve. 
Chidang namdang te chung chang thua Pathen'in Hosea Themgao henga gaova aseidohsa, “Kamite hilouho chu keiman kamite tia tu apat kakou ding ahitai, chule keiman kangailut louho chu kangailut ding ahitai. 
Chule amaho kamite nahi pouve kitina mun'a chu Pathen Hing chate kiti ding ahiuve. 
Chule Israel chung chang thua Themgao Isaiah chun ahin samdoh in, Israel mite chu twikhanglen panga neldi jat tam'in tam jongleh, amoh them bou sohcha ding ahi,” ati. 
Ajeh chu Pakaijin leiset chunga thutanna gangtah le achainapen a dinga ahinbol ding ahi. 
Chule Isaiah chun mun danga hitima chun aseijin, “Ven sopite Pakai chun ichateu themkhat hi ahoidoh lou hileh eiho hi Sodom banga eikithat chaihel uvin tin, Gomorrah banga eisuhmang dingu ahi,” ati. 
Hiche ho jouse hi ipi kiseina hintem? Chidang namdang te Pathen chondan juilou ahivangun, amaho chu Pathen lungtoh dia kisema ahi tauve. Chule tahsan hin hiche hi ahin hitheisah ahi. 
Hinlah Israel mite Pathen to kilunghet themna dinga kihabol ho jeng jong Danthu ajui jeng vangun alolhing jou pouve. 
Ipi jeh a lolhinglou hiuvem? Ajeh chu ama hon Pathen to kilunghet themna dinga Ama tahsan sangin Danthu ajuiyuve. Ama ho alamlhah nauva songpia kipal ahi tauve. 
Pathen Lekhabun amaho gihsalna ana neijin, ajah uva, “Keiman Jerusalem'a songpi khat kakoiyin, hichun miho akipalsah e, amaho lhuh kamna songpi khat ahi. Hinla koi hileh Ama tahsan chan chu jachatna a chulou ding ahi,” ati.
