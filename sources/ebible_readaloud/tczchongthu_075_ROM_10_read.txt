Rome.
10.
Kasopi deitahte ho, kalunga kagelkhoh jing le Pathen'a kataona hi Israel mite huhhing'a aumna dingu hi ahi. 
Ama hon Pathen'a thanopna aneinau chu ipi ahi kahei, hinla hichu hetkhenna beiya kipehna nei ahiuve. 
Ajeh chu ama hon Pathen'in miho chu Ama a dinga athemchan thudol hi ahethei pouve. Pathen tohgon chu ama hon asan nomlou jeh uvin, amaho lung ngaito juiya Pathen themchanna mu ding chu alunggon'u ahi. 
Ajeh chu Christa chun Danthu kipehlona ajeh chu ana suh bulhitsa ahitai. Hiche jal'a chu Ama tahsan jouse chu Pathen themchanna asodoh ahitai. 
Ajeh chu Mose chun Danthua mikhat themchanna dinga athupehsa chu nit ding ahi, ati. 
Hinlah tahsan jal'a Pathen themchan theina ding chun, “Nangman na lungthima koi van'a gakal ding ham (Christa chu van'a konna agapuilhah ding kiseina ahi). 
'Chule koi chu mithiho lah a gakumsuh ding ham?' (Christa chu hinsah kitna dingin) tihih un. 
Tahbeh mong'in, hitin ati. Athupoh chu ahung nailheh e; hichu na kamsung le na lungsunga aum'e.” Chule chuche thupeh chu keihon kalhangsap u tahsan thudol chu ahi. 
Nangman nakam'a Jesu chu Pakai ahi tia na phondoh a chule Pathen'in Ama chu athilah a kon'in akaithou tai ti nalung sunga na tahsan tah a ahileh huhhing'a um nahi ding ahitai. 
Ajeh chu nalung sunga na tahsan chun Pathen themchanna chu nachan sah ahitai, chule na phondoh chun nangma huhhing'a naum sah ahitai. 
Pathen Lekhabun eiseipeh u dungjuijin, “Koi hijongleh ama tahsan chan chu jachatna a chulou ding ahi” ati. 
Hiche thudol'a hin Judah mite le chidang namdang ho thakhat ahitai. Ama hon Pakai khat aneikhom'un, Ama kou jouse chu lungsetna bulhingset apei. 
Ajeh chu koi hileh Pakai min'a tao chan chu huhhing'a um ding ahi. 
Hinlah ama hon Ama atahsanlou uleh a huhhing theina dinga iti akou thei dingu ham? Chule Ama thudol anajah khah lou u ahileh iti ahin tahsan thei dingu ham? Chule koiham khat in aseipeh louva ahileh ama hon iti achung chang thu ajah thei dingu ham? 
Chule akisol aum louhel a ahileh, koipen gachea amaho thu agaseipeh dingu ham? Hiche jeh a chu Pathen thubun, “Kipana Thupha poa vahle ho keng iti hoiya hoi hitam,” ati ahi. 
Hinlah amichang cheh in Kipana Thupha chu ana sang pouve, Ajeh chu Isaiah Themgaovin, “Pakai kathupoh u tahsan'a koi ham?” anati. 
Hiti chun, tahsan chu thujah a kon ahin, hichu Christa chung chang thujah a kon ahi. 
Hinlah ken kadongin, “Israel miten thu hunglhung chu ajah tahbeh u hinam?” Henge, aja nauve. “Thu hunglhung chu leiset kolkimvel'a akithe chansoh in, chule thu chu vannoi mun jousea alhungsoh e.” 
Hinlah ken kadong'in, “Israel mipi ten ahet khen mong'u hinam?” kati'e. Henge, aheuve, ajeh chu Mose phatlai jeng jong chun Pathen'in aseijin, “Keiman nangho namkhat hiloulai ho jong miho thangset sah dinga ngolnaa chu ka lunghan sah ding ahi,” ati. 
Chule khonungin Isaiah chun, lung ngam tah in Pathen'a kon'in hitin aseiye, “Keiman kamite kamudoh ding ho chu eina helou miho hi ding ahiuve. Keima eidonlou beh ho chu keiman kaki mudoh sah ding ahi,” ati. 
“Hinlah, Israel chung chang thua, Pathen'in aseijin, nilhum keiyin keiman amaho chunga kakhut kajah in, hinla amaho seingai louleh engset hatte ahiuve,” ati.
