Rome.
12.
Chule hiti chun sopi deitahte, kahin ngeh nahiuve, Pathen'in ijakai nabolpeh tah jeh uvin Ama din natahsau chu peuvin. Hichu ahing chule athengsel kilhaina–Pathen santhei dol tah hihen. Hiche hi adihlam'a Ama houtheina chu ahi. 
Nanghon tulai vannoi chonna le umchan khosahna ho jom hih un, ahin Pathen'in mithah hina lungthah napeu henlang nalunggel ngaito nau khel hen. Chutengleh Pathen'in nangho dinga apha le lunglhaina chule chamkimna nagonpeh u chu nahin het ding'u ahi. 
Pathen'in chanpha le thuneina eipeh jal a hiche thua hi kahilchah nahiuve: Nangho nahina tahbeh u kalval'in phajon kigel hih un. Nangho tah nakigel nauva Pathen'in tahsan eipeh dung juiyun ahibep chan kigel'un. 
Itahsa uvin tibah tampi anei cheh in chule tibah khat cheh in panna anei cheh un ahi. 
Hitobangma chun Christa tipum jong chutima ahi. Eiho tipum khat chung'a tibah tampi ihiu ahin, chule eiho iboncha uva khat cheh hi khat tibah ihiu ahi. 
Alungsetna a Pathen'in thilpeh jatchom chom cheh eipeh u ahin, hichu phatah a ibol theina dingu ahi. Hijeh a chu Pathen'in gao thusei theina napeh a ahileh, Pathen'in napeh tahsan nanei dungjuijin seijin. 
Lhagao thilpeh nachan chu midang kithopina dinga ahileh, phatah in kithopinan bol'in. Nangma mihil'a napan leh phatah in hil'in. 
Lhagao thilpeh nachan chu midang tilkhouna ahileh, tilkhouvin; hongphalna lama thilpeh nachan ahileh hongphal tah in pedoh in. Pathen'in lamkai hina boltheina napeh leh, namopohna chu gelkhohna neijin. Chule nangma milungset themna thilpeh nachan'a ahileh kipah tah in bol'in. 
Midang chu angailu tobang bep in umhih un, ngailu tahbeh uvin. Adihlou chu thetbol'un, thutah chu kolchah kheh un. 
Nangho khat le khat kingailutna dihtah in kihepito them'un, chule khat le khat kijabolna thanop piuvin. 
Thase hihbeh un, ahin natoh pontho uvin chule Pakai kinbol chu thanop nan neiyun. 
Ama a ikinepna uva kipah thanom'un. Hahsatna a thohhat un, chule taojom jing'un. 
Pathen mite ngaichatna a kithopina neiyun. Jindot them dan kichuh jing uvin. 
Nabolse teu chu phatthei boh un. Amaho chu gaosap hih un, Pathen'in phatthei abohna dingin taopeh un. 
Akipahho kipapi uvin, chule akap ho kaapi uvin. 
Khat le khat kitoh del'in cheng khomun. Milham hotoh kiloikhomna neina dinga kiletsah hoitho bol hih un. Chule ijakai hesoh keiyin kigel hih un. 
Miphalou chu aphalou cheh a lethuh hih un, nangho ja umtah nahiu chu mijousen amuna dingin bolun. 
Nabol jouseu chu amichang cheh to kichamna dingin bolun. 
Gol le paite, phulah bol hih un, Pathen dihtah lunghanna chu otdoh jouvin. Ajeh chu Pathen thubu in, “Keiman phu kalah ding ahi. Keiman amaho chu lethuh nange” ati. 
Chusangin, “Namelma teu chu agil akelleh vahvaset un, amaho adang'u achah leh adon ding peuvin. Hitia chu nabol uva ahileh alujang uva mei-am setkhom peh nahi dingu ahi.” 
Setna chun najou hih uhen, hinla setna chu thilpha in jou jouvin.
