Corinth Mite Henga Paul Lekhathot Khatna.
1.
Hiche lekhathot hi Pathen lung cham'a kilhengdoh Jesu Christa solchah Paul le isopiu Sosthenes a kon ahi. 
Keiman Pathen houbung Corinth a um nangho Pathen'in amitheng te hi dinga akouho henga kahin sut ahi. Aman Christa Jesu jal'a athenga nasem'u ahi, mun jousea Christian ho jouse, I-Pakaiyu Jesu Christa, amaho le eiho dinga Pakai, min hin kouho jouse abolna banga. 
I-Pau Pathen le Pakai Jesu Christa chun nangho lungset le cham lungmon napeu hen. 
Nangho tua hi Christa Jesu a nahi jalun chule lungsetna thilpeh napeh jalun keiman ka Pathen chu ka thangvah jingin ahi. 
Ama a konna na houbung uhi Pathen'in imalam jousea nahaosah u ahin, thusei thepna le hetthemna jouse hi naneiyu ahi. 
Hiche hin keiman Christa chung thu ka seipeh uhi adih e ti aphotchen ahi. 
Nanghon tua lhagao thilpeh tintang na ngaichat ho'u chu I-Pakaiyu Jesu Christa hungkitna ding kinem'a ngah a nahi jeh uva naneiyu ahitai. 
Aman achaina geiya nahatsah uva, I-Pakaiyu Jesu Christa ahung niteng nolnabei hinkho naman theina dingu ahi. 
Pathen hi tahsan umtah ahina jal'a Aman abol dinga asei chu thon louhel a ahinbol ding ahin, chule Ama chun Achapa Pakai Jesu a chan neikhom dinga na ngeh u ahitai. 
Kasopi deitahte, I-Pakaiyu Jesu Christa thuneina a kangeh nom nau chu nangho khat le khat kilungtoh tah in umun. Houbunga kibung khenna umhih hen. Chusang'in lungkhat kisauvin, lunggel le lungtup kithakhat hiuvin. 
Ajeh chu kasopi deitahte, Chloe insung mite phabep in kitohmoa naum u thu chu kajah a ahung seiyuve. 
Nangho lah a mi phabep in, “Keima Paul nungjui kahi,” abangkhat loiyin, “Apollos nung kajuiye,” ahilouleh, “Peter nungjui kahi,” ahilouleh “Christa bou kajui ahi,” natiuvin ahi. 
Christa chu loituma achom cheh a nakhen'u hitam? Keima Paul hi nangho dinga kikhenbeh a kahim? Nalah uva Paul min'a baptize kibol naum uvem? Naum hih nauve! 
Nalah uva Crispus le Gaius tailou khatcha baptize kana bollou jeh in Pathen ka thangvah e. 
Ajeh iham itileh tua hi koima chan kamin'in baptize einabol'e eiti theilou dingu ahitai. 
(Oh ahinai, keiman Stephenas insung pumpi jong chu baptize kanabol nai, ahinlah koima dang baptize kabolbe kahepoi). 
Ajeh chu Christan keima baptize bol le dinga eisol ahipoi, Kipana Thupha seiphong le dinga bou eisol ahi. Christa thingpel chun athaneina aman louna dinga chihna thudol asei kahilou ahi. 
Ajeh chu thingpel thu hi mahthahna lam mano ho dinga ngol hoitah thu ahi. Hinlah eiho huhhingna chang ho dinga Pathen thahatna ahi. 
Pathen Thubun aseima bangin, “Keiman miching ho chihna chu kasuhbei ding, chule milungching chihna chu imacha hilouva kakoi ding ahi” ati. 
Hiti ahijeh chun, chihgilna neiho, lekhathem ho, chule vannoi lungthim themna a minelkal hat ho iti tauvintem? Pathen'in hiche vannoi chihna chu angola amu ahi bouve. 
Pathen'in achihna a vannoiyin mihem chihnaa Ama ahetdoh lou dingu ahi ti amu jeh a, angol ho thusei banga ka thuseiyu hin atahsan ho ahuhhingna dinga amanchah ahi. 
Van'a konna melchihna thum Judah nam mite dinga hichu ngol thu ahin, chule Greek mite, mihem chihnaa hol ho dinga jong ngol thubuh ahi. 
Hitia hi keihon Christa kikhetbeh thu kaseiyu hi Judah nam mite lunghan kondohna ahung hin chule chidang namdang hon panna bei thu kisei ahi bouve, atiuvin ahi. 
Hinlah Pathen'in huhhing dinga akou ho dingin vang, Judah mi hihen chidang namdang hijongleh, Christa chu Pathen thahatna le Pathen chihna ahigel e. 
Hiche Pathen'in ngol tah a tohgon anei hi mihem achingpen tohgon sangin jong achingjon chule Pathen thalhahsamna chu mihem thahatna pen sangin jong ahatjo nalaiye. 
Kasopi deitahte ho geldoh temun, Pathen'in nanakou pet u chun hiche vannoi lamdol a aching'ah ahilouleh athaneiyah ahilouleh ahaovah naum pouve. 
Chusangin, vannoiyin angol'a agel ho'u chu Pathen'in ching kisaho jachatsahna dinga alhendoh ahi. Chule Aman thaneilou ho jong chu thanei tah a kigel ho jachatsahna a alhendoh ahi. 
Pathen'in hiche vannoiyin anahsahmo ho, imacha louva agel ho, chu alhendoh a, vannoiyin thupitah a agel ho imacha hilou sosah dinga manchah dinga alhendoh ahi. 
Akhonna keiya, koimacha Pathen angsunga akiletsah hoitho umlouna ding ahi. 
Pathen'in Christa Jesu toh nagop khomu ahitai. Iphatchompina ding uva Pathen'in chihna tahbeh chu Ama ahisah ahitai. Christan eiho Pathen toh isuhchamu ahitah jeh in, Aman eiho athenga chule athengsel a eisem uva, chule chonset a konna eilha ongtholu ahitai. 
Hiche jeh a chu Pathen Thubun asei, “Nangma nakison noma ahileh Pakai chungthua bou kisongin,” ati ahi.
