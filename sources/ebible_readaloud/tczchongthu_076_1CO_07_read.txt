1 Corinth.
7.
Tun na lekhathot uva neihin thudoh u thudol'a, henge, jonthanhoiya kon'a kikangse hi thilpha ahi. 
Hinlah hitilom'a jonthanhoina ahat tah jeh hin, pasal in ama amaji kinei cheh henlang, chule numeiyin jong ama ama pasal ji'n nei cheh uhen. 
Jipa chun ajinun pasal'a ngaichat anei chu bulhit peh hen, chule jinu jong chun numeiya ajipa lung ngaichat chu bulhit peh hen. 
Ajinu chun atahsa chunga thuneina chu ajipa pehen, chule ajipa jong chun ajinu chu atahsa chunga thuneina chu pehen. 
Nangho naniuva kigel lhahna nanei uva, nupa hina a kitimat louva uma taona'a kipeh theina dinga khat le khat kingaichat tona phat chomkhat khongai ding tina ahingal louva ahileh, nupa kilung ngaichat tona jahda bol hih un. Nangho kitimlelna neijeh a Satan in na lheplhah louna ding uva chomkhat jouleh nahung umkhom kittheina dingu kati ahi. 
Hiche kasei hi gelkhoh pehna jeh a bou ahin, thupeh kapeh nahi pouve. 
Hinlah amichang cheh hi kei tobangin achangin um cheh leu kati. Hinla amichang cheh hi Pathen a kon thilpeh chang cheh a ahin, khat ahilouleh achom changa ahiuve. 
Hitia hi keiman jineilou laiho le meithai ho jah a kasei ahi-keima tobanga jinei louva um hi phajoa ahi. 
Hinlah amaho tah akitim joulou diuva ahileh jineijo dingu ahi. Angaicha gunset a um sanga jinei chu phajoa ahitai. 
Ahin ajineisaho dingin, keiman thupeh keiya konna hilou Pakaiya konna thupeh chu kaneiye: Jinu khat chun ajipa adalhah louding ahiye. 
Hinlah aman ajipa chu adalhah tah a ahileh, amanu chu achangin umjeng hen ahilouleh amapa chuto kihoucham kit hen. Chule ajipa chun ajinu adalhah louding ahi. 
Tun nangho adang chengse henga Pakaiya kon hilouvin keiman nakom uva hinsei inge. Christian pasal khat in atahsanlou numei khat ji a aneiya chule amanu chu aumden a achenkhompi noma ahileh, apasal chun ajinu chu adalhah louding ahi. 
Chule Christian numei khat chun jipa atahsanlou neihen lang chule amapa chun ajinu chu umdenpi nom taleh, amanu chun apasal chu adalhah louding ahi. 
Ajeh chu Christian jinua pang chun ajineina chu atheng ahisah ahitai, chule Christian jipa a pang chun ajineina chu atheng ahisah ahitai. Chutilou hileh nachateu chu thenglou diu ahin, hinla tun amaho atheng uve. 
(Hinlah jipa ahilouleh jinu chu atahsanlou ahia adalhah teitei got leh sol jengin. Hitobang thua hin, Christian ajipa ahilouleh ajinu hijongleh khat joh chunga amopo tapoi, ajeh chu Pathen'in lungmonga khosa dinga nakouvu ahi). 
Numei hon nageldoh diu chu nangho jal'a najipa teu chu huhhing'a naumsah dingu, chule pasal hon jong nangho jal'a najiteu chu huhhing'a naum sah diu ahi geldoh un. 
Nangho khat cheh hi Pakaijin nakoi nau bangtah a naum dingu ahi, chule Pathen'in nakouna masah a chun um cheh un. Hiche hi houbung jouse henga ka dan sempeh ahiye. 
Avetsahnan, mikhat chu atahsan masanga chep kitan chu akalea hei ding ahideh poi. Chule mikhat chu atahsan pet a chep kitanlou ahile tununga chep atan ngailou ahi. 
Ajeh chu mikhat chu cheptan hen, tan hih jongleh hiche hin ima ajatchom sahna aumpoi. Athupi pen chu Pathen Thupeh ho jui hi ahi. 
Ahimongin, nangho khat cheh hi Pathen'in nakoupet uva nahi nauva chu naum dingu ahi. 
Nangho soh a um nahiuvem? Hichun nalung himosah dauhen, hinlah nang hon ontholna ding phat kijen naneiyuleh, hichu kilah un. 
Chule geldoh un, Pakaijin nakou pet uva chu soh a um nahiu leh, tua hi Pakaiya chamlhat nahiu ahitai. Chule Pakaijin nakou pet uva chu chamlhat nahiu leh tua hi Christa a soh nahiu ahitai. 
Pathen'in nangho dia aman tampi apeh ahitai, hijeh chun vannoiyin soh nachan sah dauhen. 
Sopite nangho khat cheh hi Pathen'in nakou masah pet uva nahi nauva chu naum cheh dingu ahi. 
Tun numei khangdong jinei loulai ho thua neidoh u hi, amaho dingin Pakaiya kon'in thupeh kamupoi. Hinla Pakaijin ami lungsetna a tahsan theiya kilom chihna chu eipen, chule hiche hi nangho toh chan kanei khompi ding nahiuve. 
Tupet hahsatna jal'a hi, nahina bang uva naum dingu phapen in kagel'e. 
Nangman jinu nanei leh kikhenpi ding lunggel neihih in. Jinei loulaiya naum leh, jinei tei dingin hol hih in. 
Hinlah nangin ji chu naneiya ahileh, hichu chonset ahipoi. Chule numei khangdong chun ji aneiya ahileh, hichu chonse ahipoi. Hijeng jongleh, tuphat a hi jineiho chun boina ato dingu ahi, chule keiman hiche boina thua hi ka ontholsah got nahiuve. 
Tun sopi te ho, hiche hi kahinsei nome: Phat um nalai hi achom behseh e. Hijeh hin, jinei pasal hon ajineiyu hi akisahboi nau pipen hisah dauhen. 
Akap ho ahilouleh akipah ho ahilouleh thilcho ho chu akanao ahilouleh akipanau ahilouleh athil neiyuva kipum phumlut dauhen. 
Vannoi thil mangcha ho chu, chehoa chun kipum ngaplhah dauhen. Ajeh iham itileh hiche vannoi thil ho hi ihetma bang uva, hung mangthah ding ahiye. 
Keiman hiche hinkho gelkhohna a konna hi na onthol dingu kadei ahi. Jineilou pasal chun aphat le alunggel chu Pakai iti lunglhaiya, ana iti atoh ding angaito ahi. 
Hinlah jinei pasal chun leiset a amopohnaho iti asuh bulhit a chule ajinu iti alunglhai ding ham ti agel ahi. 
Alunglutna kihomkhen'a ahitai. Hitobangma a chu jinei talou numei ahilouleh jinei khalou nu chu Pakaiya kipedoh a atahsa le alhagao thengsel'a akoi thei ahi. Hinla jineisa numei chu leiset lamdol'a amopohna agelkhoh a chule ajipa alunglhai got ding ahi. 
Hiche kasei hi na phatchomna dingu kati ahin, nangho imacha a jahdana kaneina aumpoi. 
Hinlah pasal khat in numei khat kichenpi dinga akiteppia alam louva bola akigela chule atahsa lung ngaichat in athujo beh tah a ahileh, aman agon bangin kichenpi jenghen. Hichu chonset ahipoi. 
Hinlah ama dettah a apan'a jineilou dinga akigel lhah a chule jineina dinga kinona aumdeh louva, chule ama kitim jou dinga akigela ahileh, jinei louva aum nahlai chu apha ahi. 
Hiti chun ajihol kichenpia chun thilpha abol ahi, chule ajinei pailou chun abol phat cheh ahije. 
Jinu chu ajipa hin laisea ajipa thunoiya uma ahi. Ahin ajipa athia ahileh, aman akichenpi nom nomto kicheng thei ahi, hinla hichu Pakai ngailutna jal hi ding ahi. 
Hinlah ama dinga jinei louva aum chu phajo ding ahi, chule keiman hiche thu kahin sei hi Pathen Lhagao thumop a konna seiyin kakigelin ahi.
