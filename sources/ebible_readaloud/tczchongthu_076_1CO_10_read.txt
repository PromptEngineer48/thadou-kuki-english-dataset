1 Corinth.
10.
Kasopi te ho, nang hon masangpeh a gamthip a ipu ipateu anaum'u thudol chu nasuhmil dingu kanompoi. Amaho jouse chu achung uva um meibolin apuiyun, chule abonchauvin Twipisan chu tolgolai jot in ajot jeng uve. 
Mose nungjui ahijal uvin meibol sunga le twipi sunga chun abonun baptize achangun ahi. 
Amaho jouse chun lhagao an thakhat chu ane cheh uvin, 
Chule amaho jouse chun Lhagao twi khatma chu adon khomun ahi. Ajeh chu ama hon akilhonpiu Lhagao songpima a kipat a chu adonu ahin, chule hiche songpi chu Christa ahi. 
Hijongleh Pathen chu amaho atamjo chunga alunglhai pon, chule atahsau chu gamthip ah athethang peh in ahi. 
Hichengse ana sodoh hi eiho dinga kichih theina ding ahi, hiche hi amaho tobanga thilse ngaichatna inei louna dingu ahije. 
Ahilouleh amaho lah a milim hou aum bang uva ium louna dingu ahi. Pathen Thubun asei dungjuijin, “Mipiho chun kivahva na golnop anneh le don abol'un, chule amaho chu milim houna a kipah in, akipah thanomun ahi.” 
Amaho phabep chun jonthanhoi abol jeh uva mi sang somni le sangthum chu nikhat a athilo tobangu chun jonthanhoi bolpou hite. 
Ahilou jongleh Christa chu patep pou hite, amaho lah a phabep in achutibol uvin chule gul in achulih uvin ahi. 
Chule phunset bolpou hite, alah uva phabep aphunset jeh uvin, mithat vantil in amaho chu asuhmang lo'uvin ahi. 
Hichengse achung uva ana sodoh chu eiho dinga vetsahna a soh a ahi. Amaho thudol kisun hi eiho khang kichai kom'a ho kichih theina dinga ahi. 
Nangma adingdet a nakigel a ahileh, nalhuh louna dingin ching theijin. 
Na hinkhoa lhepna nato ho hi midangin atoho toh akikhehna aum dehpoi. Chule Pathen tahsan umtah ahin, nangman nadinpi joulou ding kalval chu Aman na chunga achuh ding phal ponte. Nangman lhepna chu nato tengleh, Aman nathoh jouva naki huhdohna ding lampi na gonpeh ding ahi. 
Hiti chun, kasopi deitahte, milim houna a kon'in jamdoh un. 
Nangho lung geltoh thei mi nahiuve. Keiman kasei hi adih ahi tia na tahsan uva ahileh nangho kigel phauvin. 
Eihon Pakai Anjon kibolna a khon'a phatthei chu iboh tengu leh, eihon Christa thisan a chu chan neiya ihilou uham? 
Chule eiho mi tamtah ihivang uvin, eiho iboncha uva changlhah lum khat a chu nekhoma ihiuvin, hichun eiho tipum khat ihiuve ti vetsahna ahi. 
Israel mipite chu gelun, amaho chun maicham a kikatdoh ho chu lungkhat tah a anehkhom jiu hilou ham? 
Ipi seinom a kahi dem? Keiman milim houna a kikatdoh anneh chu ipi vetsahna ahi katina hintem? Ahilouleh milim kisem thu ho hi pathen ho ahiuve katina ham? 
Ahipoi, hichu ahi naisaipoi. Hiche avetsahna thil kitoh ho hi lhagao boh ho kom'a kibol'a ahin, Pathen a kikatdoh ahipoi. Chule nangho lhagao boh hotoh najao kop dingu kanompoi. 
Nanghon Pakai khon'a le lhagao boh ho khon'a chu nadon thei dehlou dingu ahi. Pakai dokhang le lhagao boh dokhanga nanehkop thei dehlou dingu ahi. 
Pakai chu thangthip sahna ikondohsah u hingap a ham? Ama sanga ihat joh uva nagelu hitam? 
Nangin “Keiman ipi hijongleh kabol ding phal ahi” nati, hinla ijakai nangma dinga phatchomna ahipoi. Nangin, “Ipi hijongleh kabol dia phatsah ahi” nati, hinla ima jouse aphachoma ahidehpoi. 
Nangma ding seh a phatna ding chu gelkhoh hih in, hinla midang phatna ding gelkhoh un. 
Hitia chu se le pha hetna a thudoh bol louvin kailhanga sa kijoh tapou khu naneh thei ahi. 
Ajeh iham itileh leiset hi Pakaiya ahin, chule asunga um jouse jong hi Ama a ahi. 
Atahsan lou koi ham khat in in'a nilhah an nedinga nakouva ahileh temna namu chu nanop a ahileh sanpeh jengin. Na angsunga aluichan chu se le pha hetna thua ima dohna neilouvin sa chu nejengin. 
(Hinlah khat touvin, hiche sa hi milim houna'a kikatdoh a ahitai, atia ahileh na seipehpa se le pha hetna jal jeh chun neh din gellhahna nei hih in). 
Mi koiham khat in agel ding jeh man a chu kachamlhatna imalou soh ding ham? 
Keiman anneh jeh a chu Pathen ka thangvah theiya chule hichu kanopsahpia ahileh, ipi jeh a anneh mana keima eiki oimo ding ham? 
Hijeh chun nang hon naneh u hihen nadon'u hijongleh, ahilouleh ipi nabol'u hijongleh, abonchan Pathen loupina dingin bol'un. 
Judah mite hihen lang, ahilouleh chidang namdang ho hijongleh, ahilouleh Pathen houbung hijongleh lungthoi sah hih un. 
Kei jengin jong kabollam jousea hi mitin lunglhaisahna ding kabol ahi. Keima dinga aphat pen mana kabol ahipoi, mitampi huhhing'a aum theina dinga midang dinga aphapen chu kabol joh ahi.
