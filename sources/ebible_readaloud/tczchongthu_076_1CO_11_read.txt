1 Corinth.
11.
Chule keima Christa dungsun kahi bangin, nangho jong keima dungsun hiuvin. 
Nanghon nalung uvah keima neigel jingun, chule naheng uva ka thuhil u thu chu najuiyuve, ti kahet hin keima kakipah lheh in ahi. 
Hinlah nang hon thil khat nahet dingu keiman kadei chu: Pasal chu Christa lama mopoa ahin, numei chu ajipa henga mopoa ahi, chule Christa chu Pathen henga mopoa ahi. 
Pasal in ataopet ahilouleh lhagao thu aseipet a alu akhua ahileh Christa ajalou ahi. 
Hinlah numeiyin alu khu louva ataova, ahilouleh Lhagao thu aseidoh a ahileh ajipa chu ajalou ahin, hijeh a chu hiche hi alu kivoutol tobang ahitai. 
Ahin amanu hin alu pon'a akhukhum nom louleh, aman asam jouse atan lhah ding dol ahitai! Ahin numei dinga asam tan lhah ahilouleh asam vomui chu jachat um ahijeh a, amanu chun ponlu khu chu akiken ding ahi. 
Pasal in Pathen ahoupet a alua ima akoilou ding ahi, ajeh chu pasal chu Pathen lima kisema chule Pathen loupina sulanga ahi. Chule numeiyin Pasal loupina asuhlang hiya ahi. 
Ajeh iham itileh mimasa chu numeiya konna ahipon, hinla numei masa chu pasala kon ahi. 
Chule pasal chu numei dinga kisema ahipon, ahin numei chu pasal dinga kisema ahi. 
Hijeh a chu numeiyin thuneina noiya um kahi ti vetsahna a, alua ponkhu akivon ding ahi, ajeh chu vantil ten avet jingu ahi. 
Hinlah Pakai mite lah a, numeiho chu pasala konna chamlhat ahilou tobangin, pasal ho jong numeiya konna chamlhat ahi pouve. 
Ajeh iham itileh numei masa chu pasala konna hung uma ahijeng vang'in, pasal dang khat khat cheh hi numei khat a konna hung peng doh a ahi cheh uve. 
Nangho cheh dingin thu kitanun. Numei hin alu pon kikhu louva mipi lah a Pathen henga atao ding chu dih a hinam? 
Pasal in sam asaova aput ding chu ama dinga kilom louva hilou ham? 
Chule numei dinga asamsao hi akiletsahpi le athanoppi hilou ham? Ajeh chu hichu amanu dinga khukhum dinga kipea ahi. 
Hinlah hiche thudol'a hi khattou anelkal nom aum leh, eihon hiche tailou hi chonna ding dang inei pouve, chule Pathen houbung dang hon jong anei deh pouve, ti hi kaseidoh nom ahi. 
Ahivangin tun anoiya kithuhilna hoa hin, nangho ka pahcha thei pouve. Ajeh chu nangho nakimuto tengu leh thilpha sanga aphalou ding joh boldoh a hileu chun akilouve. 
Amasan, houbunga nakikhop khom tengu leh nalah uva kibung khenna aum'e tithu kajan, chule hichu ichan hamin katahsanin ahi. 
Hinlah, ahinai, nalah uva kibung khenna soh mong ding ahinai chutia chu nangho adih a umho hetdoh a na umna ding uva ahi! 
Nangho naki khop khom tengu leh, nangho Pakai Anjkonna'ah na lunglut tahtah pouve. 
Ajeh iham itileh nalah uva mi phabep chu midang to nehkhom ding golouvin nangma an cheh nakineh pai jeng uve. Hiti chun, abang khat gilkel in aum un, adang phabep akhamji tauve. 
Ipi ham? Nehna le donna dinga nangma nangma in cheh neiya nahi lou uham? Ahilouleh nang hon Pathen houbung chu imacha hilou sosah ding goa chule avaichaho jachatsah goa nahiuvem? Keiman ipi kasei ding hitam? Nangho ka pachat diu nanop uvem? Hichung chang thua hin napahcha hih uvinge! 
Ajeh chu, Pakai Ama tah a konna kalahson chu nangho kapehson kit nahiuve. Pakai chu mat dinga pehdoh a aum jan chun, Pakai chun changlhah phabep ala in, 
Hiche a chun Pathen athangvah in ahi. Chuphat in Aman ahehbal bal'in chuin aseijin, “Hiche hi nangho dinga kipedoh katahsa ahi. Keima neigeldoh jingna dingun hiche hi bol'un,” ati. 
Hitobangma chun an aneh jouvun, Aman khon'a lengpitwi chu ala in, aseijin, “Hiche khon hi Pathen le Amite kitepna thah ahi–kathisan a kitepna suhdetna chu ahi. Hiche hi nadon laiseuvin keima geldohna in bol'un” ati. 
Ajeh chu nang hon hiche changlhah hi naneh chanseuva chule hiche khon'a hi nadonlai chanseuva nang hon Pakai thina chu ahung kahsea na phondoh u ahi. 
Hiti chun, koi hijongleh lomlouhel'a hiche changlhah chu nea, ahilouleh hiche Pakai khon'a chu don'ah aum leh Pakai tahsa le thisana chu themmo chang ding ahi. 
Hiche jeh a chu changlhah chu naneh masang ule khon'a chu nadon masang uva nangma tah kivetchilna nanei dingu ahiye. 
Ajeh chu Christa tahsa chu naneh masangu, ahilouleh nadon masang uva Christa tahsa chu najabol louva ahileh, naneh le nadon a chu Pathen thutanna nangma chung tah a nakilhut hiya ahi. 
Hiche jeh a chu nangho mi tamtah thalhoma chule naa chule phabepset jong thia nahi tauve. 
Hinlah eimaho tah kikholtoh a hileuhen, Pathen'in hitia hi ichung uva thutanna anei louding ahi. 
Amavang Pakaijin ithu u atan'a thununna noiya eikoiyu tengleh, vannoi toh themmo ichantha lou diu ahi. 
Hiti chun kasopi te, nang hon Pakai Jan Anjon bol dinga nakikhop ji tengu leh, khat le khat kingahto uvin. 
Agilkel tahbeh monga nahiu leh, in'ah kineh jengun, chuti henlang hileh nakikhop khom tengu leh nangma chunga thutanna chu nakiloilut louding ahi.
