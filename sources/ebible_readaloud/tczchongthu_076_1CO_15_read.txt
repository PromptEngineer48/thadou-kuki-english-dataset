1 Corinth.
15.
Kasopi deitahte, tumasanga naheng uva Kipana Thupha kahung lhangsap thu chu keiman nahin geldoh sahkit uvinge. Chupet chun nasang uvin chule tugeiyin nading det jing nauvin ahi. 
Keiman najah uva ka seipeh u thu chu na tahsan jom jing uva ahileh hiche Kipana Thupha chun nahuhhingu ahi–apat a pat a adihlou thu chu tahsana nahilou pouva ahileh. 
Keiman naheng uva akhohpen tah keima henga ana kipe chu kahin pehson nahiuve–Christa chu Pathen Thubun asei dungjuijin ichonset jal'un athin ahi. 
Ama chu akivuiyin, chule Pathen Thubun asei dungjuijin, ani-thum nin athoudohtai. 
Ama chu Peter in amun chujouvin somle ni ho chun amu uve. 
Chujouvin, khatveiyin anung jui mi janga val hon khatvei mun amuvin ahi, amaholah a atamjo ahing nalaiyun, hinla phabep chu athi tauve. 
Chupet in Jacob in ama chu amun chujouvin solchah jousen amu uve. 
Achaina keiyin, aphat louva peng bangin, keiman jong Ama chu kamun ahi. 
Ajeh chu keima solchah ho jouse lah a imacha hiloupen kahi. Ahimongin Pathen houbung ana gimbol hesoh a kahi jeh hin solchah ti dinga jong kiloma kahipoi. 
Hinlah tun ipi thu um jongleh kahi ahi, Pathen'in alungsetna chu kachunga asunlhah ahitah jeh hin-chule hichu phatchomna bei ahidehpoi. Ajeh chu keiman solchah dang ho sangin na kaha tohgimjon, hinla hichu keijoh hita louva Pathen'in alungsetnaa keiya na atoh ahijo bouve. 
Hiti chun keiman seiphong teng ahilouleh ama hon ka thusei ma tahsanna nakisanu thudol seiphong kit jonglehu ima chom aumpoi, ajeh chu keihon kabon chauva na tahsan u thudol chu kasei phong khomu ahi. 
Hinlah hiche hi nei seipeh un-Christa chu thinaa kon'in athoutai katiu leh, itidan'a nalah uva phabep in athiho thokitna aumpoi tia nahiu ham? 
Ajeh chu athiho thokitna aumlouva ahileh Christa jong chu kaithouva um dehlou hi ding ahitai. 
Chule Christa chu kaithoulou anahita leh, thu kaseiphong leu hi mohseh hita ding, chule na tahsan 'u jong chu mohseh hi ding ahitai. 
Chule solchah keiho jouse jong hi Pathen lamdola jousei kahi dingu ahitai-ajeh chu keihon Christa chu Pathen'in lhan'a kon'in akaithouve kana tiu ahi. Hinlah thokitna aumlouva ahileh hiche kisei chu dih theilou ding ahitai. 
Chule athiho thokitna chu umlou hitaleh, Christa chu kaithou hilou ding ahitai. 
Chule Christa chu kaithou hilou anahi khah leh, na tahsan u chu panna bei hi ding chule nangho hi na chonset nauvin themmonachanjing nahlai dingu ahiye. 
Hiche chung chang thua hi, Christa tahsana thiho jouse chu mang thah hidiu ahitai. 
Chule Christa itahsanu hi tuhinkhoa ding bou hita leh, vannoiya midang jouse sanga khoto umpen ihi dingu ahitai. 
Hinlah, Christa chu athiho lah a kon'in kaithou ahitah behnai. Ama athiho jouse lah a aga masapen chu ahitai. 
Hitia hi mikhat jal'a vannoi a thina ahunglhun banga chu, athiho lah a konna thokitna hi michom khat jal'a hi ding ahi. 
Eiho iboncha uva Adama hungkon ihijeh uva ithi sohkei dingu ahi tobanga, Christaa hiho jouse chu hinkho thah kipe ding ahi. 
Hinlah hiche thokitna thudol'a hin akigontoh dol aum'e. Christa chu ga kiloh masapen ahi, abana Christaa thiho jouse chu Ama ahungkit tengleh kaithouva um ding ahitai. 
Aman vaipoho le athunei ho chule athanei ho chu asuhmang soh jou tengleh, Lenggam chu Pa Pathen kom'a apehdoh a chujou tah leh achaina hung hi ding ahi. 
Ajeh chu Christa hin Amelmate jouse chu akengto noiya akoi tokahsea vai ahom tei ding ahi. 
Chule melma anunungpen akisumang ding chu thina ahi. 
Ajeh chu Pathen Thubun aseijin, “Pathen'in ijakai Ama thunoiya akoi sohkei ahitai,” ati. *(Hichea, “ijakai Ama thuneina noiya akoi” kiti hin Pathen Ama akijaosah thapoi, Ama chun Christa hi Athuneina apehdoh joh ahi bouve). 
Aman ijakai athunoiya akoi sohkei tengleh, Chapa Ama tah jong chu Pathen thunoiya kikhum lut intin, Pathen Achapa ijakai chunga thuneina pepa chu, ijakai a achungvuma imalam jousea athunei hi ding ahitai. 
Athisaho kaithouva umlou ding ahia ahileh, thi jal'a baptize chanlona ajeh ipiba hitantem? Athiho chu nikhat le kaithou ding ahilouleh ipi bol ngaiya ham? 
Chule ipi dinga aphat phat a keiho hin kahinkhou hi hahsatna kathoh jiu hintem? 
Ajeh chu kasopi te ho, kihahselna theijin keiman anitinin thina kakimaitopi jinge. I-Pakaiyu Christa Jesun nanghoa anatoh hi keima kakiletsahna tahbeh chu ahi. 
Chule gamsaho toh (Ephesus khomite toh) kakidel lona ipi phatchomna hintem? Athisaho thokitna umlou ding ahia ahileh? Chule thokitna aum louva ahileh, “Eiho kivah'u hitin, donu hite, ajeh chu ajinga kithi tading chu!” itidiu ahitai. 
Chutobanga thusei ho chu kingol bolsah hih un, ajeh chu, “kivoppi phalouvin chon chan phalou asoji,” kitia ahi. 
Adih chu ipipen ahi geltoh uvin lang, chule chonse tahih un. Ajeh chu hiche hi naja chatna ding uva kasei doh ahi, nangho mi phabep Pathen he tahbeh lou nahiuve. 
Hinlah khat touvin, “Mithiho itobanga thoudoh dingu ham? Itobang tahsa pum anei dingu ham?” tin dongun te. 
Itobang ngol hoi thudoh hitam! Nangman muchi tol'a natu chu athi kahsen ahung kehdoh theipoi. 
Chule nangman leiset a nakoi chu suhlou chang mu hihen ipi pen chu natulut hita jongleh aphung ahung khang ding chu ahi hih laiye. 
Chutengleh Pathen'in ahin hisah nom ding vouthah chu ahinpeh ding ahi. Muchi khat cheh a konna chu thingphung chom chom hung khang touva ahi. 
Chutobangma a chu tahsa pum jong jatchom chom ahi, mihem tahsa pum le ganhingho tahsa pum, vacha ho tahsa pum, chule ajat chombeh nga tahsa pum geiya uma ahi. 
Leiset a tipumho aum tobangin, Van'a jong tipumho aumin ahi. Van'a tahsapum loupina chu leiset a tahsapum loupina toh ajatchomin ahi. 
Nisa hin loupina jatkhat aneijin, lha le ahsi hon jong khat cheh hina ma anei cheh uve. Ahsiho jeng jong khat khat hi aloupinau ajatchom cheh in ahi. 
Mithiho thokitna jong chutobangma chu ahi. Leiset a itahsau hi ithi tengu leh leinoiya iphumlut jiuvin, hinla a-itih a hingjing dinga hung thoudoh ding ahiuve. 
Itahsau hi abong abaija kiphumlut a ahin, hinla hiche ho hi loupina a hung thoudoh ding ahiuve. Amaho hi thanei louva kivuilut ah ahin, hinla thahat a hung thoudoh ding ahiuve. 
Amaho hi angaiya mihem vouphea kivuiya ahiuvin, hinla amaho hi lhagaolam vouphea hung thoudoh ding ahiuve. Ajeh chu angaidol'a tahsa vouphe aum tobanga chu lhagaolam dola vouphe aumin ahi. 
Pathen Thubun, “Mimasa Adam chu hinna nei mihem ahung hi,” tin eiseipeh uvin ahi. Hinlah Adam nunungpa chu, Christa kiseina, hinna pea Lhagao ahi. 
Amasa tahsa tipumho hung um masaa chujouva lhagaova tipumho chu khonunga hung uma ahi. 
Mimasa Adam chu leiset leivuiya konna kisema ahin, mihem aninapa Christa chu Van'a konna hung'ah ahi. 
Leiset mite hi leiset mihem chuto akilou uvin, chule van'a mite chu van'a hungkonpa tobang ahiuve. 
Tua hi eiho leiset a mihempa tobang chu ihiuvin, phat khat le van'a hungkonpa tobang chu ihung hi dingu ahi. 
Kasopi deitahte ho, keiman kasei hi, itahsapheu hin Pathen Lenggam alotheipoi. Hiche thi thei tahsaphe hin tonsot a um ding gou alo theipoi. 
Hinlah keiman thuguh kidangtah na phondoh peh uvinge. Eiho jouse iboncha uva ithi sohkei dingu hilou, hinla iboncha uva vouso kikhel ding ihiuve. 
Hichu phulou helouva, mitphetkah sunga sumkon nunungpen chu hung kimut ging ding ahi. Ajeh chu sumkon chu ahung kimut ging tengleh, Pakaiya ana thisaho chu vou kikhel a thoudoh diu ahi. Chule eiho ahing chengse ivouvu kikhel ding tonsot a hing ding ihitauve. 
Ajeh chu athi ding itahsau hi thilouhel ding tahsa vouva kikhel ding, athi thei itahsau hi athi thei talou tahsa voupua kikhel ding ahitai. 
Chuteng, athi ding tahsa ineihou hi athi talou ding tahsahoa ahung kikhel tengleh, hiche Pathen Thubu in asei, “Thina chu jonain avallhum tai” 
“Vo thina, hoiba ham namijo na? Vo thina, hoiba ham namide na?” kiti chu guilhung ding ahi. 
Ajeh chu chonset hi thina a dinga midep a panga ahi, chule Danthu hin chonset thaneina apeh ahi. 
Hinlah Pathen thangvah in umhen! Aman chonset le thina jo jou theina I-Pakaiyu Jesu Christa jal'in eipeuvin ahi. 
Hijeh chun, kasopi deitahte ho, hat un chule suhlong thei louvin pangun. Pakaiya dingin thanom tah in tong jingun, ajeh chu na thilbol u chu Pakaiya dingin phatchomna bei imacha aumpoi ti naheuve.
