2 Corinth.
11.
Mingol tobanga kaumna a jong nei hetthem diu kakinem e. Henge, nangho tah in nei hethem un. 
Keima nangho dia katom ngaina hi Pathen tomngaina jal jeh ahi. Keiman nangho jipa khat, Christa mou theng hi dinga kalhendoh nahiuve. 
Hinlah gulin duha pan a Eve alheplhah tobang chun Christa lama lung thengsel le lungthim kehlou beh a napan nau chu suhboh in umkhante ti katijat peh nahiuve. 
Mi khat touvin, keiho hung seiphong Jesu hilou achom dang khat thu joh ahung seiya, ahilouleh Lhagao jatchom, nakisan u tobang hilou, ahung seiphonga, ahilouleh na tahsan sau sanga jatchom Kipana Thupha ahungsei jongleh akisei tapou namoh tahsan kigot uve. 
Hinlah chutobang thuhil choi solchah achungnunga kiheho sanga noinung jon keima chang kakigel hih e. 
Keima hi thusei kijil them tah chu hi hih nange, hinla ipi kasei kakihet nai. Hiche hi tun hethem tauvin nate tin kagel'e, ajeh chu avel vel'a iphotchen u ahitai. 
Keima kaki neosah a chule nanghoa konna lemukit ding kinem louhel'a Pathen Kipana Thupha seiya naheng uva kahunga, nangho kajabolna hi bolkhel kahi hitam? 
Keiman houbung dang hoa konna athil tokhom'u chu kakhop khoma nangho henga manbeiya lhacha natoh kanei thei ding kati ahi. 
Chule keima nangho to kaumkhom a chule kihinsona ding imacha kanei loupet in jong koima sum lamah pohgih kapohsah pon ahi. Ajeh chu Macedonia a konna hung sopiho chun kangaichat jouse eihin pohpeh tauve. Hiti chun keiman nangho dingin pohgih kapohsah khapouvin chule kapohsah louhel ding nahiuve. 
Christa a thudih chu keiya aum jing akicheh laisea, hiche thudol'a kaki skonna hi Greece pumpia koiman eikham thei louding ahi. 
Ipi dia em? Nangho kangailut loujeh uham? Keiman kangailut nahiu Pathen'in ahenai. 
Hinlah keiman kanabol jing chu kabol jom ding ahi. Hiche hin ama hon kisonpina ding phat kijen ahol uva anatoh u chu keiho natoh tobang bep ahi atiu chu abei theina ding hiya ahi. 
Hiche miho hi solchah lhemho chu ahiuve. Amaho hi duha thuhtah a natong ho Christa solchah ho a kisei ahiuve. 
Hinlah keiman kadatmopoi. Satan jeng jong chu avah tah vantil tobanga kisoh thei ahi. 
Hitia chu asohte ho jong chu chonphatna sohtea kisem thei ahiuve. Achaina tengleh athil gilou boldoh hou jeh a gimbolna chang ding ahiuve. 
Kasei kit ahi, hitia thu kasei hi keima angolin neigel hih un. Hinla hitia chu neigelu ahijongleh, keima angol tobanga kaumpet in jong, mingol tobanga nagel nau pumin, kasei ngaiyuvin. 
Hitobang kiskonna hi Pakaiya kon ahipon, hinla mingol thuseiya kasei ahijoi. 
Midang hon bon tahsa mihem hina a atohdoh hou akison piuva ahileh, ken jong kaki skonna seiyinge. 
Nangho chingtah a kigel'a nahiu hilou ham, hinla nang hon angol ho chon chan na hethem nauve. 
Nangho hin koiham khat touvin sohchang bol'a nabol uva, nanei jouseu akilah uva, nanghoa phatchomna akimu uva, ima jouse athu uva aheiyuva, chule namaiyu abeh gei uvin jong na hethem theiyuvin ahi. 
Chutobang bol dinga keiho thalhom val kahiu hi ka jachat piuve tia kasei ahi! Hinla ipilam dol'a kiskonna anei nau hijongleh–mingol banga kaseikit ahi–keijong chutobanga kisong thei kahi. 
Amaho Hebrew mi hiuvem? Keima jong chutima kahi! Ama ho Israel mi hiuvem? Keima jong hitima chu kahi! Amaho Abraham chilhah hiuvem? Keima jong hiti chu kahi! 
Amaho Christa soh hiuvem? Mingol thusei bang bep kahi ding kakihet e, hinla keiman kaha jen jocheh in ahi. Keiman kaha tohgim cheh in, songkul'a eikikhum matchet in, simjoulou khop in eikijep in, chule thina avel vellin kaki maito pin ahi. 
Munchom chom Juda nam lamkai hon nga-veijen khaohol a jepna somthum le ko vei cheh einavo uvin ahi. 
Keima hi thihjol ah thumvei jen eina vouvin ahi. Khatvei songa eikisep in, thumveijen kongke katoh in, khatvei chu nikhat le jankhat twikhanglen'a kaumdenin ahi. 
Keima lamsaotah tah kana kholjinin ahi. Keiman vadung hoa le gucha michomho lah a vangsetna katoh in ahi. Keiman kamite, Judah nam'a kon le chidang namdang ho lah a kon'in vangsetna katoh in ahi. Atahsan kahi tia ahia atahbeh ahilou jeh uvin amahoa kon'in tosetna katoh in ahi. 
Keima ihmumon, na katong gimin, chule kathoh gimin ahi. Keiman dapthoh in kithing thingin eisulum jouding ponbeiyin kaumin ahi. 
Chuti chun, hiche ho jouse kalvalin, keiman houbung ho jouse dingin gelkhohna pohgih kaneijin ahi. 
Koiham athalhom keiman athalhomna chu kapohhu pi nomlou? Koi ham akipui lamvai keiman kalunghanpi lou? 
Keima kisong ding hileng, keima ichan gei loma thalhoma kahim ti thil hoa bou kisong ding kahi. 
Pathen, I-Pakaiyu Jesu Pa, Ama chu tonsot a vahchoi jing dinga kilomtah ahin, keiman jou kaseipoi ti ahenai. 
Keima Damascus a kaum chun, Leng Aretas in Gamvaipoa apansah pa chun keima matdohna dingin khopi kotpi hoa chun angah akoiyin ahi. 
Amaa konna kaki huhdoh theina dingin keima chu khopi bang kotneova konna bom'a khailhah tho kana hi.
