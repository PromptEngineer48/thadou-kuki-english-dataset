Galatia Mite Henga Paul Lekhathot.
1.
Hiche lekhathot hi solchah Paul, mihem lhen le mihem thuneina vanga hilou, Jesu Christa Ama tah le Jesu athia konna kaithouva Pa Pathen pansah pa, a kon ahi. 
Hikom'a sopi te ho jouse panna a Galatia houbung ho henga lemna thu kahin thot nahiuve. 
I-Pau Pathen le Pakai Jesu Christa chun lungset le cham-lungmon napeuhen. 
Ichennau vannoi gitlouna a konna huhdoh ichan theina ding uvin, Pa Pathen lunggon dungjuijin, Jesun ahinna eiho chonset jal'in ahungpe tan ahi. 
Atonsot tonsot a dingin loupina chu Pathen a hijing tahen! Amen. 
Nangho Christa ngailutna khotona a konna, Ama a dinga nakoupau Pathen a konna nakihei mang loi jengu hi kahin datmo lheh jeng nahiuvin ahi. Nangho hin lampi achombeh najuiyu ahitai. 
Hinlah Kipana Thupha tahbeh chu ahi tapoi. Christa chung chang thua thudih heikoi hon nangho na ngol bolu ahitai. 
Keihon nangho henga Pathen Thu kahung seiphongu kalvala Kipana Thupha chom dang khat seiya auma ahileh keiho hiuving ting ahilouleh van'a konna vantil khattou hijongleh Pathen gaosapna achunga umhen. 
Keihon kaseisau kavelsei kit ahi. Nanghon nanajopsau kalvala Kipana Thupha achom seiphonga aum leh ama chu gaosap chang hen. 
Mitmua miho seiphat hol a kahipoi, Pathen a kon joh chu kahol ahi. Miho lunglhaisah ding chu kalunggon ana hitaleh keima Christa soh hilou kahi ding ahitai. 
Sopi deitahte, keiman nangho kahetchen sah nomu chu Kipana Thupha thudol kaseiphong hi mihem lunggel ngaitoa kingam'a ahipoi. 
Keiman kathupohle hi mihem a kon ahipon, koiman eihil ahipoi. Chusanga, keiman Jesu Christan kaheng'a aphondoh hoa konna bou kamu ahiye. 
Juda mite hou lama itobang loma katompana Pathen houbung chu keiman kana bolset hitam ti naheuve. Keiman hichu suhmanghel kana got ahi. 
Kapu kapaten achepiu houlam chonna lamdol a keima Judah mi kachan khompi ho sanga gamchenga chungnung joa kana hi ahi. 
Hinlah keima kapen masang jenga jong, Pathen'in eilhendoh a, chule Alungsetna kidangtah a chu eikou ahi. Ajeh chu hichun Ama alung lhaisah ahi. 
Keiman Kipana Thupha Jesu thu chu chidang namdang ho kom'a ka phondohna dinga Achapa chu kaheng'a aphondoh ahi. Hichu ahung hi phat in, keiman mihem khattou to kihoutohna dingin kakimoh no jengpon ahi. 
Chule keima masanga solchah himasaho to kihoutohna dingin Jerusalem'a kakaltou dehpoi. Chusangin, keima Arabia gam lama kache mangin chule khonungin Damascus khopia kahung kinungle kit in ahi. 
Chujou kum thum alhinin, Peter to kiheto dingin Jerusalem'a kaga chetouvin, chule amato chun ni som le nga kaum khomin ahi. 
Hiche pet a chu seijui dang kakimupi khat chu Pakai sopipa Jacob chu ahi bouve. 
Keiman Pathen masanga naheng uva kahin jih a ka phondoh ho hi jou apangpoi. 
Hiche kagavil jou chun keima sahlam Syria le Cilicia gamsunga kagalut in ahi. 
Chule hiche pet a chu Judea gam'a houbung ho chun keima eina hetchen loulaiyu ahi. 
Ama hon miho seinaa ahet u chu, “Eiho eigimbol hesoh pau chun asuhmang got tahsan chu tun aseiphong tai” tin aseiyuve. 
Chule ama hon keima jal'in Pathen avahchoiyun ahi.
