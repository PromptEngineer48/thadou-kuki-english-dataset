Galatia.
4.
Hiti lam hin gel uvin. Mipa khat chu athia chule achate neo nalai ho dinga goulo ding adalhah peh tah a ahijongleh, hiche chapang ho chu apau nei jouse chu chang diu hinama jongleh, ahung letdoh kahse uva chu soh ho sanga achungnun johnau ima chom aum deh pouve. 
Amaho chu apauvin ateppeh u kum alhin kahse uva aching hoiyuva pangho thu thua chu nung ding ahiuve. 
Eiho dinmun jong Christa ahung masanga chutobangma chu inahiu ahi. Eiho hi chapang ho banga chu hiche vannoiya hi lhagao thubul ho thudol'a soh changa inahiuve. 
Hinlah aphat chet chu ahung hi phat in, Pathen'in Achapa chu numeiya konna hungpeng din, danthu noiya kipelut din ahinsol tan ahi. 
Eiho Dan thua sohchangsa ho hi achatea eisem uva, chamlhat ihina dingun, Pathen'in Achapa ahinsol tai. 
Chule eiho achate ihitah jeh uvin, Pathen'in achapa Lhagao chu ilungsung uva ahinsolin, Abba Pa tia koutheina einei sah tauve. 
Tun nang chu soh nahi tapon, Pathen cha tahbeh nahi ahitai. Chule nangma acha nahitah jeh a, Pathen'in agoulo dinga nasem ahitai. 
Chidang namdang nang hon Pathen nahet masang uva, auma jong hilou pathen ho soh a chu napanu anahi. 
Hiti chun tun Pathen nahe tauvin (ahilou le Pathen het nahi tauve tileng), iti dana chu nangho avel'a hiche vannoiya lhinlelna le lhagao thubul ho panna bei thudol'a sohchang kit dinga naki pehlut dingu ham? 
Nanghon Pathen lunglhaisahna dinga nikho ho ahilou le lhaho ahilou le kum kihei chin ahilou le kum ho geiya nanit u ahi. 
Nangho katijat peh uhi, ijemtia nangho chunga katohgim nau jouse hi mohseh hibep a hitam tihi ahi. 
Sopi deitahte, kangeh nomnau chu nangho hiche ho a konna na chamlhatna ding uva, keima tah jong nangho chidang namdang te banga kaum hi, hiche danho a kon'in keima bang hin ongtholun. Keiman nangho henga Pathen thu kahung seiphong masat chun alam louvin neibolse pouve. 
Keiman naheng uva Kipana Thupha kahin poh chun keima kana dampoi ti na geldoh jing diu ka gingchai. 
Ahin ka dinmun umdan chun neisan louna ding uva lhep patepna nato jeng vangun, nang hon nei nahsahmo bol pouvin ahilouleh keiya kon'in nakihei mang pouve. Chuti louvin neina sang uvin, nang hon neina lhunsah uvin, chule Pathen vantil khat bangin ahilouleh Christa Jesu Ama tah nasan diu bangtah chun neibol phauvin ahi. 
Chuche pet a nakipa nau le na thanop nau lungthim chu tua hoilamba hitam? Hithei bon henlang hileh namit u chu nakaldoh uva keima neipeh jeng thei diuva ka ginchat nahiuvin ahi. 
Tua hi keiman thutah ka seipeh jeh uva namelma uva kapan hitam? 
Chuche thuhil dihlou ho chun nangho lung lhaisahna dingin akiha bol uvin, hinla alunggon'u apha ahipoi. Amaho chun nangho keima a konna nachom khen jeng got uhi, nang hon amaho bou najuina dingu agel jeh u ahi. 
Mi khat touvin nangho dinga thilpha bolnomna aneiya ahileh, apha jenge; keiman nangho kaumpi pet jeng bou hilouvin aman boljing hen. 
Vo kacha deitahte! Keiman nangho dinga naosonat thohlel banga kathoh gim kit hi nanghoa Christa lim asodoh kahsea ding hiya ahi. 
Keima hi nangho toh tupet a kaum khom hileh kati lheh e, chule kapao awcheng kakhel nom e. Hinlah hitobanga kigamlatna hin ipi lamsanga kakithopi thei dingu kahe jengpoi. 
Danthu noiya umnom hon nei seipeh un, Danthun ipi mong asei nahet chet mong hinam? 
Pathen Lekhabun aseiye: Abraham'in chapa ni aneijin, khat chu sohnu akon ahin chule khat chu numei ongthol nua kon ahi. 
Sohnu chapa chu mihem lungtup a Pathen thutep suh bulhit gotna ahi. Hinla aji tahbeh nu chapa chu Pathen thutep dungjuiya guilhungah ahi. 
Hiche numei teni hi Pathen kitepna ni vetsahna ahi lhone. Numei masanu Hagar hi Sinai Mol'a konna Danthua asohchan nau chu vetsahna ahi. 
Chule tun Jerusalem hi Arabia gam'a Sinai Mol tobang hiya ahi, ajeh chu ama le achate Danthua soh changin aumun ahi. 
Hinlah numei chom dang nu, Sarah chu Van Jerusalem vetsahna ahi. Ama numei ongthol ahin, eiho nu ahi. 
Isaiah themgaovin, “Vo chaneilou numei kipah thanomin, cha hing khalouhel'a, naosonat thoh khalouhelnu, kipah tah in awthong jejun samin! Ajeh chu numei chahinglou nun ajipato umkhomnu sangin chate ahaojo tai,” ati. 
Chule kasopi te, nangho Isaac banga chu thutep chate nahiu ahi. 
Hinlah tun nang hon danthu najui dingu deisah ho chun, Ishmael-mihem kibol hat a hungpeng cha chun, Isaac–Lhagaova hungpeng cha chu agimbol tobanga nagimbol'u ahi. 
Hinlah Pathen Thubun hiche thua hi ipi asei em? “Sohnu le acha chu nodoh tha tan, ajeh iham itileh soh numei cha chun ongtholnu cha chuto gou lokhom thei ponte” ati. 
Hijeh chun sopi deitahte, eiho hi sohnu cha ihipouve, ongtholnu chate joh ihiu ahiye.
