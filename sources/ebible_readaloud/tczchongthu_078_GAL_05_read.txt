Galatia.
5.
Hitia hi Christa chun tahbeh monga eichamlhat sah u ahitai. Tun chamlhat nahiu chu photchen nan umden un, chule Danthua sohchanna a kihen ohkit tahih un. 
Ngaiyun! Keima Paul in hiche hi ka seipeh nahiuve. Nanghon cheptan chu Pathen to kilungtona dinga kiphamoa nagel jing uva ahileh Christa chu nangho dinga ima phatchomna hita louding ahi. 
Keiman velseikit inge, nang hon cheptan'a Pathen lung lhaisah nagot nahlai uva ahileh, nang hon Mose Danbua kisun jouse chonna dan ho najui diu ngai nalai tina ahi. 
Ajeh iham itileh Danthu juiya Pathen lungto ding nana got u ahileh nangho Christa a konna satlhah nahiu ahitai! Pathen lungset chan'a konna pullha nahiu ahitai. 
Hinlah Lhagaova hing'ah eihon Pathen'in chonphatna eithutep u chu tahsan a thanoma ngah jinga um ihiuve. 
Ajeh iham itileh eihon Christa Jesu a itahsanu ingap lhah uva chu cheptan ahilou le cheptan louvin ima phatchomna aum tapoi. Athupi pen chu ngailutna a kiphongdoh tahsan hi ahi. 
Nalhaitet jaonau aphanai. Thutah nung najui nauva konna koi ham nanunghei kit uvah? 
Pathen hilou beh ding ahi, ajeh chu Aman chamlhatna dinga nakouvu ahi. 
Hiche thuhil dihlou hi chol neocha changbong lah a kilhah tobang ahi! 
Pakaijin thuhil dihlouva konna nahonbit dingu katahsan in ahi. Nalung nohphah sah uva chu Pathen'in koipen hijongleh achung thu tanhen. 
Kasopi deitahte, keiman tugei hin nangho chep natan diu ahi tileng–mi phabep in kaseijin alauvin–chuti ahileh ipi jeh a tugeiya hi keima eikigimbol hesoh hintem? Christa thingpel jal a huhhingna thu seiphonga panglou hileng koima alungthoiya um louding ahi. 
Chuche boina sodoh ho, cheptan sah a imacha hilou nasogo hou chu amaho tah imacha hilou kisoh ule kati lheh jenge. 
Ajeh iham itileh kasopi te ho, nangho chamlhat hinkho mang dinga kikou nahiuve. Hinlah na chamlhat nau chu ngailutnaa khat le khat kijentonan mang jouvin. 
Ajeh iham itileh Dan pumpi chu hiche thupeh khat a hi kingam'a ahi, hichu “Nangma naki ngailut bangin naheng nakom ngailun.” 
Hinlah nangho aphatseh a nakipeto uva chule kivallhumto sao saova uma nahiu leh kihongpha uvin, khat le khat kisuhmang tona theiya ching theiyun. 
Hiti ahijeh in Lhagao Thengin nahinkhou kaihoi hen kati ahi. Hiti chu hileh nang hon na chonset nomnau lung ngaichat chu nabollou dingu ahitai. 
Chonset nomna lungchang in thilse abolnomin, hichu Lhagaovin abolnom chuto kikala ahi. Chule Lhagao chun chonset nomna lung ngaichat to kikal ngaichatna eipeh u ahitai. Hiche teni hi kidou jing jenga ahijeh a nangman nalunggot apha jong chu nabol theilou ahi. 
Hinlah nangma Lhagao chun nathunun tengleh nangma Mose Danthua chu na mopohna um louva ahitai. 
Nangho na chonset lung ngaichat thua nanun tengu leh, hichea kon chun: kingaichat tona lama chon hoilou, then louna, lungguh a chonset nopsah ngaichatna, 
Milim houna, doichoina, kihotna, kinahna, kimit thipna, kilunghan khum tona, chang phatchom holna, kidouna, kibung khenna, 
kimit thipna, jukhamna, eltol golnop bol, chule hitobang chonset adang ho jong ahi. Kana seisa bangin naseipeh kit uvinge, koi hitaleh hitobanga hing nalai ho chun Pathen Lenggam alo louding ahi. 
Hinlah Lhagao Theng chun hitobang gaho hi ihinkho uva ahin sodoh ding ahi: ngailut, kipa, cham-lungmon, thohhat, lungset them, phatna, tahsan um hina, 
lungneng, chule kitim them hichengse hi ahi. Hiche ho chunga Danthun thaneina aneipoi. 
Christa Jesu a hiho dingin tahsa lungtup ho leh chonset lung ngaichat ho chu athingpela akhetbeh tha ahitai. 
Eiho Lhagaova hinga ihitauvin, ihinkho uvah imalam jousen Lhagao kaihoina hinkho chu mangu hite. 
Eiho kihisah val pouhite, ahilouleh khat le khat lungphatmo sah ahilouleh khat le khat kithangsetto pou hite.
