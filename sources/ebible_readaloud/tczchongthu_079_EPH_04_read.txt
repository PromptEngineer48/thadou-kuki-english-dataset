Ephesus.
4.
Hiche jeh chun, Pakai kin bolna a sohchang keiman, Pathen'in nakouvu ahitah jeh in, nakikou nau totoh in chonun tia nakom uva katao ahi. 
Phatseh in kineosah un chule lungneng un. Khat chu khat chungah thohhat un, na ngailut nau jaljeh in khat in khat suhkhelna a kihepito cheh un. 
Lhagaova pumkhat hina dingin kiseh un, khat le khat chamna in kikan khomun. 
Ajeh chu nangho hi khonunga loupina kinem'a kikou nahi bangun, tipum khat ahin chule Lhagao jong khat ahi. 
Pakai khat, tahsan khat chule baptize chan khat bou ahi, 
chule Pathen khat le Pa chu ima jouse chung vum'a uma ahin, chule ima jousea uma ahiye. 
Hiti chu hijongleh, Aman eiho khat cheh hi manbei thilpeh aguntah chu, Christa hong phalna jal in, eipe cheh uvin ahi. 
Hijeh chun Pathen Thubun aseijin, “Ama achung sang lama akaltou chun, sohchang ho apuiyin chule Amite chu manbei thilpeh ho ape in ahi,” akiti. 
Melchih in, “Ama akaltou chun” kiti hi hiche hin Christa chu vannoi anoinung lama gakumsuh a ahi tina jong ahi. 
Chule Ama agakumsuh a chu vanho jouse sanga jong sang joa kaltouva ahi, hitia chu Aman vannoi pumpi hi Ama tah in adimset sah ahi. 
Tun hiche ho hi Christa in a houbung dinga manbei thilpeh a apeh ahi: Solchah ho, Themgao ho, Kipana Thupha lhangsam ho, Chinghoi Kaihoi ho chule Mihil ho ahiuve. 
Amaho amopoh nau chu Pathen mite chu Pathen natohna a pang ding chule Christa tipum, houbung chu sahdohna dinga ahi. 
Hiche hi eiho iboncha uva chutobang tahsanna a kipumkhat a chule Pathen chapa hetna lama Pakaiya ipilhin theina ding uva, Christa a idim uva chule chamkimna a ichejompeh theina dingu ahi. 
Chutengleh eiho chapang ho banga pilhing louva iumlou dingu ahitai. Eiho hi thuhil thah hui hung nung tobang ahung ho chun eimut lonle le lou dingu ahitai. Eiho hi mihon jouva eihin duha bol tengu leh thutah banga chingthei tah a asei dingu ahi. 
Chusangin, eihon thutah chu ngailutna neiyin seiyu hite, imalam jousea khangtouva Christa (Houbung Luchang), Atipum (Christa tahsa) dinga ihi theina dingu ahi. 
Aman atipumpi chu chamkim keiya asuhto sohkei ahi. Tibah hon ama ama kin hoitah a akibol cheh uva ahileh hiche hin tibah dang jong akhantou sah ahi, hitia chu tipumpi chu damthei le khangtouva chule ngailutna dimset hiji ahi. 
Pakai thuneina a hiche hi kasei ahi: chidang namdang ho bolngai bangin bol tahih un, ajeh chu amaho tah kinepna beiya lungnoh phah bep ahiuve. 
Alunggelu muthim lhangkhal kiheh ahin, amaho Pathen'in hinkho apehna uva kon'in akihei mangun, Pathen'in amaho apeh u lungthim chu akhah chah un chule Ama dounan alungu atahsah cheh un ahi. 
Ama hon jachat lam jong ahepouve. Amaho tahsa lung ngaichat nan ahing uvin chule thenlouna lam jousea akipeuvin ahi. 
Hinlah hichu Christaa konna nakihilu ahipoi. 
Nanghon Jesu thu najah u ahitan, chule Amaa konna thutah chu kihil'a nahi tauve. 
Na chonset luiyu chonna le masang na hinkhou thenlouna lung ngaichat le duha lhahna chu pailha uvin. 
Chusangin, Lhagao chun nalunggel ule na ngaito nau suthahjo hen. 
Na chonna u athah Pathen lim tobang pu–chonpha tahbeh le atheng chun kivonun. 
Hijeh chun jousei pailha tauvin. I-heng le ikomho henga thutah chu seitau hite, ajeh chu eiho jouse hi tipum khat a chu jaova ihi sohkei uve. 
Chule chonset sodohna lunghan chun na thunun da tauhen. Nalunghan pet pumin nisa kilhum khum sah hih un. 
Ajeh chu lunghanna hin Diabol dinga pansatna apeh ahi. 
Gucha nahi leh, guhchat ngajengin. Chusang chun, nakhut teni chu thilpha hatoh nan mangchan, chujou teng angaicha ho chu hongphal tah in pen. 
Pao ngeilou le thuphalou seihih in. Nasei chasun chu apha le aphachom ding hihen, hiti chun nathusei ngaiho dinga kitilkhouna sohdoh hen. 
Na hinkho mandan u chu Pathen Lhagao Theng lung himosahna hida hen. Aman Amaa mong monga lhatdamna nikhoa nangho huhhingna channa ding uva na melchih u ahitai. 
Lungdam louna jouse, lungsat, lunghan, thungailou sei, chule seiset oimona, chule chonchan phalou jouse chu paimang un. 
Chusangin, khat le khat kilungsetto cheh un, lungneng un, khat le khat kingaidam touvin, Christa in nangho na ngaidam bang uvin ngaidamun.
