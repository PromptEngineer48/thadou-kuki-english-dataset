Ephesus.
5.
Hijeh chun imalam jousea Pathen umchan jomun, ajeh chu nangho Ama deilhen chate nahiuve. 
Ngailutna dimset hinkho mang uvin, Christa in avetsahna nung chu juiyun. Aman eingailut uva, chule Ama tah hung kipedoh a Pathen a dinga gimnam tuiya kipedoh a ahitai. 
Nalah uva jonthanhoina, thenlouna, ahilouleh chipset umda hen. Hitobang chonsetna ho hin Pathen mite lah a mun aneipoi. 
Phatchomna bei thusim seiho, ngol-hoi thusei, alam louva totnaho-hiche ho hi nangho hina ahipoi. Chusangin, Pathen thangvahna sohjo hen. 
Nahet chet diuva pha chu–jonthanhoi, thenlouna, ahilouleh michipse ho Christa le Pathen Lenggam lut louding ahiuve. Ajeh chu michipse kiti hi milim hou ahin, hiche vannoi thil ho hi pathen a anei ahitai. 
Hiche chonset hoa kithem chan ho duhan lhahih hel un, ajeh chu Pathen lunghan chu Ama seingailou ho jouse chunga chu ding ahi. 
Hiche mihon thil abol nauva hin jao hihbeh un. 
Ajeh chu nangho khatvei chu muthim lhangkhal kiheh leu lah a uma nana hiuvin, hinla tun nang hon Pakaiya konna vah chu naneiyu ahitai. Hijeh chun vah mite hin'in hing uvin. 
Ajeh chu hiche vah nasung uva konna hungvah doh chun apha le adih chule thutah ahin hindoh sah ding ahi. 
Pakai lunglhaina ipi ahi chu chingthei tah in holdoh un. 
Phatchomna bei agiloupa le muthim natoh hoa chun pan lahih un, chusangin sosal jouvin. 
Pathen ngaisah lou hon guhthima athilbol hou hi sei dinga jong jachat umtah ahi. 
Hinlah amaho lungput phalou chu achungu vah'in ahinsal tengleh kilang doh ding ahi. 
Ajeh chu vah in ijakai atahlang ding ahi. Hiche jeh a hi hitia hi kiseiya ahi, “Vo ihmumil, athilah a kon'in thoudoh in, chule Christa in nangma na salvah ding ahi.” 
Hijeh chun itobang hinkho mang nahiu ching theiyun. Mingol ho tobangin hinkho mang hih un, achingho tobangin hingjo uvin. 
Tulai nikho phalou lah a hin phat kijenpet cha hi mang themun. 
Lung geltoh louvin chon hih un, hinla Pakaijin ipi nabol ding uva adei chu hedoh un. 
Lengpitwi donkham hih un, ajeh chu hichun na hinkhou amah thah sah ding ahi. Chusangin, Lhagao Thenga dimset un. 
Vahchoina laho le Pathen houna la chule lhagaolam laho nangma ho lah a sauvin, chule na lunggilun Pakaiya tumging to thon sauvin. 
Chule imalam jousea Pa Pathen chu I-Pakaiyu Jesu Christa minin thangvah un. 
Chule akalvalin, Christa jana jal in khat chu khat thua kitulut un. 
Nute ho, Pakai thua nung tobangin najipa teu thu thun nungun. 
Ajeh chu jipa khat chu Christa Houbung luchang ahi tobanga jipa chu ajinu dinga aluchang ahi. Ama hi Atahsa pum, hichu Houbung Huhhingpu chu ahije. 
Houbung Christa thua nung ahi bangin, hitobangma chun nute ho jong najipa teu thunoiya imalam jousen umun. 
Chule nangho pate hon, Christa in Houbung angailut tobang chun, najinu teu chu ngailu uvin. Aman amanu dinga ahinkho apeh a 
amanu chu athen'a chule angimsel na dinga Pathen Thu le baptize kibol'a konna asilngim ahi. 
Hiche aman abol hi amanu hi Amaa dinga loupi tah Houbung chu ahia anenlai, ahilouleh akohlai, ahilouleh adang nolna thei mun umlouna dinga abol ahi. Chusanga, amanu hi atheng le nolnabei hi ding ahije. 
Hitobangma chu, jipa te hon jong ajinu teu chu atahsau banga angailut dingu ahi. Ajeh chu mihem khat ajinu ngailua chun ama tah kingailua ahi ti avetsah e. 
Christa in a houbung avettup tobang chun koiman ama tahsa ahotbol ngaipoi, chusangin avah jingin chule avetup jinge. 
Chule eiho Ama tipuma atibah ho ihiuve. 
Pathen Thubun asei tobangin, “Pasal in apa le anu adalhah a, chule ajinua beh ding ahi, chule ani lhona khat a kigom khom lhon ahitai.” 
Hichea hi thuguh kidang tah uma ahi, hinla hichea hi Christa le houbung khat ahina vetsahna ahi. 
Hijeh a hi avel'a kasei ahi, pasal khat cheh in ama tah akingailut tobanga ajinu chu angailut tei ding ahi, chule jinu chun ajipa chu aja ding ahi.
