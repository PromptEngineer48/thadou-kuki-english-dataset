Ephesus.
6.
Chate ho, Pakaiya nahi tah jeh uvin nanu le napa teu thun nungun, ajeh chu hiche hi thil dih bol ding dol chu ahi. 
Napa le nanu jabolin. Hiche hi thutepto kigoma thupeh amasa ahi. 
Nangman napa le nanu najabol a ahileh, “nangma dinga thil ijakai hoi le pha hung hi ding, chule leiset chunga hi na hinkho sot ding ahi.” 
Pate ho, nachateu chu sasahna nanei nauva konna lunghan sahna bol hih un. Chusangin, Pakaiya konna thununna le thuhilnaa seilet sah jouvin. 
Soh ho, leiset a na pakai teu phatah a jabolna le ginna nei pumin athu uva nungun. Nanghon Christa najen ding bangtah u chun jenun. 
Amaho chun na vet-vet pet bouva hilouvin, phat jousen lunglhaisah un. Christaa sohchang nahi jal un, Pathen lunglam chu nalung pumpi uvin bol un. 
Halpel in na tongun, miho dinga hilouvin Pakaiya dinga natong hijouvin. 
Geldoh un, Pakaijin thilpha bol jeh a, eiho khat khat cheh hi tohman eipeh cheh dingu ahi, soh hihen, achamlhat ah hijongleh, 
Hitobangma chun pakaiho, nasoh teu chu hitima chun bol un. Amaho chu hutum-gihsal bol hih un, nang hon nani lhona van'a Pakai khat naneiyu ahi geldoh un chule Ama chun koima maipha avepoi. 
Achaina keiyin: Pakaiya chule athahat thilbol theinaa chun hat un, 
Pathen galvon ho jouse kivonun hitia chu Diabol duha tintang ho nanan jou sohkeina diu ahi. 
Ajeh chu eiho tahsaphe le thisanpua melma to chun kidouva ihipouve, hinla agilou vaipo hotoh chule mutheilou vannoiya athuneiho toh, hiche vannoi muthim lah a hattah a thanei vaipo hotoh, chule vanmun hoa lhagao gilou engse hotoh kidouva ihiuve. 
Hijeh a chu, nikho phalounia melma chu na doujou theiyuva, na lethuh jouna dingun Pathen galvon chengse panun. Chujongleh gal akichai jouva jong nangho na dindet jom jingna dingu ahi. 
Nadin nauva chun pang det un, thutah konggah in kigah un, chule tipum chu Pathen chonphatna galvon in kivonun. 
Kipana Thupha a konna bulhingset a kigosadema naum theina dingun chamna chu kengchot in kiseuvin. 
Hicheng jouse chungvuma chun, Diabol meikou thalchangho chu suhmitna dingin tahsan lumdal chu dom sangun. 
Huhhingna chu lukhuh in neiyun, chule Lhagao chemjam Pathen Thu chu panun. 
Lhagaovin phat bih nei jousea le thilsoh jousea tao jingun. Limgeh chan um jingun chule muntin'a mitheng te ho jouse dingin na taona uva pangdet jingun. 
Chule keima dingin jong taovun. Pathen'in aphat phat cha thu ho eipeh a ngamtah a Pathen thuguh Kipana Thupha alunggon chu Judah mite le chidang namdang ho jouse ding ahi hi ka hilchet theina dingin. 
Keima Pathen palai kahi naa, hiche thu kaseiphong jeh a thihkhaova kihena kahi. Hiti ahijeh chun, Ama dinga kaseiphong dingdola lung ngamtah a kasei jing theina dingin nei taopeh un. 
Tychicus hin keiman ipi kabola chule itobanga kimanchahna kanei ahi thudol bulhing keiya na seipeh dingu ahinai. Ama hi Pakai natohnaa sopi deitah le kithopi pha tahsan umtah ahi. 
Ama naheng uva dinga kahinsol lona ajeh hi-nangho tilkhou ding le keiho ikatiu hetsah ding kagelkhoh u ahi. 
Kasopi deitahte ho, cham-lungmon konna in naumpiu hen, chule Pa Pathen le Pakai Jesu Christa chun ngailutna le kitahna napeuhen. 
Pathen milungsetna chu tonsot in I-Pakaiyu Jesu Christa ngailuho jouse chunga umhen.
