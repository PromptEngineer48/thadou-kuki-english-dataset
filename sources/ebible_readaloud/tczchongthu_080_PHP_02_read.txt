Hiti ahijeh chun Christaa hina jal a kitilkhouna um nam? Angailutna a konna lhamkonna um nam? Lhagaova kiloikhomna um nam? Lung nemna le kikhotona um nam? 
Chuti ahileh nang hon nei kipasah tahbeh uvin, chuti chun lung pumpia thukhat kisauvin, khat in khat ngailu uvin, chule lunggel kithakhat neiyin chule doi kithakhat hiuvin. 
Chang kilunggel bol hih in, midang pachat hol hih in. Kineosah in, nangma sanga midang chu aphajon gelin. 
Na phatchomna ding bou velouvin, midang phatchomna ding jong ven. 
Christa Jesun anei lunggel ngaito tobangma chu nang hon jong neiyuvin. 
Ama Pathen ahi jeng vang'in, Pathen toh kibah a, kolchah den chu abolpoi. 
Chusangin, Pathen ahina dinmun chu alhakangin, soh hina a kingainemna aneijin chule mihem ahung kiso chun mihem pen ngaiyin ahung pengin, 
Pathen seingaina jal'in Ama akineosah jengin chule thingpel'a migilou thina dinmun akilhen tai. 
Hijeh chun, Pathen'in jabolna sangpen chan gei chun achoisangin chule min dang jouse lah a min chungnung pen chu Ama apeh ahitai. 
Hitia chu Jesu min'a van mun'a, chule leiset chunga, chule leinoiya hijongleh khupnei jouse dilsu diu, 
Chule paonei jousen Jesu Christa hi Pa Pathen loupina dinga Pakai tia aphondoh cheh dingu ahi. 
Kangaitah te ho, keiman nangho kaumpi pet uva kahilnaho nangai bangu chun tu kaumlou pet dehset hin Pathen chu jana lentah le kichatna naneiyu photchetnan, huhhingna chu nahinkho uvah anatoh in sodoh un. 
Ajeh chu Pathen nasung uva natonga chun alunglhaina tohdohna dinga ngaichatna le tohdoh theina tha napeuhen. 
Nabol chasun'u chu phunchelna le nelkalna jaotha louvin bolun. 
Hitia chu koima chan Pathen chate nahi nauva nalhep lhah louna dingun hiche khang gitlouna le vahkoi mite lah a vannoiya hinna vah chu vahdohsah un. 
Hinna thu chu tuhchah un hitia chu Christa ahung niteng leh keima kilhaitetna a kajao hi mohjenseh a lhai kahilou, mohseh a tonggima kahilou chu kisonpia kanei theina ding ahi. 
Hinlah keiman ka hinkho hi Pathen a dia kilhaina a twisun a kasunlhah ding hijongleh kipah ingting nanghoa tahsan umtah a Pathen a nakikatdoh tobangu hi ding ahi. Keiman hiche kipana chu chan neithei nahi cheh dingu kadei peh nahiuve. 
Henge, nangho kipah unlang, keiman jong nakipa huvinge. 
Pakai Jesu lunglhaina ahileh Timothy hi nangho vila hinsol ding kati ahi. Chuteng chuleh nangho iti naumu aman eihil a kon'in kei dingin jong kisuhhalna hinte. 
Ajeh chu nangho phatna ding lungdihpua lunggel ngaitona nei Timothy tobang nagel khoh diu kahe hih e. 
Midang ho hin ama ama phattouna ding kigel khoh'ah ahiuvin Jesu Christa lam thu chu agelkhoh pouve. 
Hinlah Timothy hin ama tah ahinan photchenna aneijin, hiti hin chapa le apa tobangin Kipana Thupha seiphongna a keima toh tongkhom ahi ti nang hon naheuve. 
Hilai mun'a kachung thu ipi aso aphotchetna aum tengleh ama nangho henga hinsol ding kati ahi. 
Chule Pakaiya kakiskonna chu nangho to kimuto vah ding hi kakineme. 
Tutadia dingin Epaphroditus naheng uva hinsol ding kangaito ahi. Ama hi sopi dihtah tohkhompi ahi. 
Aman na munom lheh jeng uvin naheng uva kahinsol e. Chule ana dammo thudol najah uchu phamo asa lheh jenge. 
Tahbeh in ama ana dammon chule thidehchet anahi'e. Hinla Pathen mikhotona chu achangin chule keima eikhoto dehset ahi. Ajeh chu keima lungkhamna chung chona lungkhamna kaneibe louna ding hiya ahi. 
Hijeh a chu ama nangho kom'a hinsol ding kagel khoh ahi. Ajeh chu nang hon ama namu tengu leh nakipa uva chule nangho kalung olmopeh tahlouna ding ahiye. 
Christian kingailutna chun kipah thanom tah in ama ana lem uvin chule hitobang mihi jabolna nei chu thilpha ahi. 
Ajeh iham itileh Christa natohna a ahinkho ahahsatna ato ahi. Chule nang hon mun gamla tah a konna nabol theilou diu jeh a nangho thalhenga keima dinga aman eibolpeh ahi.
