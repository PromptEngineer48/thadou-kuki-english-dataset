Philippi.
3.
Kasopi ngaitah te ho, ipi chu hita jongleh Pakaiya kipah thanom'un. Hitiho sei ding hi kachimpoi, chule hiche hi na tahsan u honbitna ding ahijoi. 
Chuche uicha ho lah a chun chingtheiyun–kihongpha uvin, hitobang miho chun thilse abolun, hitobang miho chun huhhing channa dinga chep natan diu ahi atiuve. 
Ajeh iham itileh eiho Pathen Lhagaova kona Pathen hou cheptan tahbeh ihiuvin, Jesu Christa natoh chaisa a chu kingamdet a, tahsalama kiskonna neilou ihiuve. 
Keima tahsalam boltheina jeh a kisong jeng jongleng kisong thei kahi. Tahbeh mongin midangin tahsa lama kiskonna anei thei le keiman kison cheh theina kaneiye. 
Keima ni-get nia cheptan kahin, Israel mihina pen-gui chu kaneijin, chule Benjamin phunga kon Hebrew mi atahbeh chu kahi. Juda te Dan juina lama hakattah Pharisee ho lah a mi kahi; 
Keima katom ngai behseh jeh in houbung chu hoise tah in kana gimbol in, chule chonphat lamdol a keima danthua nolnabeiya jui bukima kahi. 
Keiman hiche ho hi atila chu manlutah a kana ngaisang anahi, hinlah tun keiman Christan anatoh chaisa jal'in hiho chu mohseh in kageltai. 
Christa Jesu chu Pakai ahi tia kahet a pat in hiho jouse chu panna bei bep in kagel tai. Keiman Christa chu kaphatchompi theina dinga thil adang jouse chu nen kikholkhom tobang bep a kangaitoa kanungsun ahitai. 
Danthu juina a keima chonphatna chu hitalouva Ama toh thakhat kahia hitia chu keima Christa tahsan jal a chonpha eikisima ahitai. Ajeh chu tahsan jal'a Pathen'in eiho chonpha a eisemu ahitai. 
Keiman Christa chu kahet nomin, agimthohna chu thoh khoma, athina chu chankhompi kahi theina ding chule Ama thinaa konna kaithoupa thahat thilbol theina chu kachan theina ding kati ahi. 
Hitia chu ipi iti jongleh keiman thinaa konna thokitna chu kachan tei ding ahi. 
Keiman hiche ho chu kaneitai kati ahipoi, ahilouleh keima chamkimna chu chang kahitai katipoi. Hinlah keima Christa Jesun einei banga hi keiman jong hiche chamkimna chu kaneina tei ding kadel ahi. 
Kasopi te ho, keiman hichu kanei hih laiye, hinla hiche thil khat bou hi kagut ahi. Keiman achesa thil chu kasumilin, kamasanga ipi um ham ti kagel khoh in ahi. 
Keiman kilhaitetna chu achaina geiya lhai lhunga chule Pathen'in Christa Jesu a eikou nau van kipaman sanna ding mun chu hadel'a del kahi. 
Lhagaova apilhing chengsen hiche hoa hin kinoptou hite. Ipiham jeh a lungkim louna nei aum le, hijong chu Pathen'in na seichet peh tauvinte. 
Hinlah eihon imaskonnau chan geiya hi ituhchah diu angaiye. 
Sopi deitahte ho, keima umchan tobang hin nangho jong um uvin, chule keima ho nung juiya pangho um chan'a kon'in kihil uvin. 
Ijeh inem itileh masanga kana seipeh matchet jiu bangun chule tua jong kamit a kamitlhi puma ka seipeh u ahi, amaho chu achonna u chun Christa Thingpel melmaho ahiuve ti aphotchen u ahi. 
Amaho chun mahthahna lam amano uve. Amaho atumpiu chu a pathen'u ahi, Akithang-at nau chu jachat akimun'a diu, amaho chun tu hinkhoa ding bou agelu ahi. 
Ahinlah eiho Pakai Jesu Christa chenna van gam mite ihiuve. Chule eiho huhhingpu ahungkitna ding chu kinem'a ngah jinga ihiuve. 
Aman itahsau thi thei hi alah a, Amaa banga loupina vouphea akhel ding, Aman thil ijakai athuneina thunoiya akoi banga athua ahin nunsah ding ahi.
