Philippi.
4.
Hijeh chun kasopi deitahte ho, Pakaiyah umdet jingun. Kasopi deitahte ho keiman nangho kangailu uvin chule kamunom uve, ajeh chu nangho hi kakipana le kanatohna ka lallukhuh nahiuve. 
Tun keiman Euodia le Syntyche hengah, Pakaiya nahi lhon jeh in nakitomona lhon chu umsah lhon tahih in tia kangeh lhon nahi. 
Chule nangma katohkhompi dihtah, nangman hiche numei teni hi kithopin, ajeh chu amani hi midang Kipana Thupha seiphong pehnaa gunchutah a pang lhon'ah ahi. Amani hi Clement to chule Hinna Lekhabua amin kisun dang hoto tongkhom'a ahiuve. 
Pakaiya kipahtah in um jingun. Avel'in tikit inge–Kipahtah in um jingun. 
Amitakip in naum nauva nabol jouseuva lunggel toh them nahiu hehen. Pakai hungloi tading ahi geldoh un. 
Imacha lung olmona neihih un, chusangin, imalam jousea dingin taovun. Na ngaichat chu Pathen henga seiyun, chule athil boldohsaho jal in thangvahna peuvin. 
Chuti henlang hileh Pathen a cham-lungmon konna, ijakai hetna a khel sohkeiya chun, nalungu le nalungthimu Christa Jesu a nahi jal uva chu napeh dingu ahi. 
Chule tun, kasopi deitahte ho, achaina keiyin-akitah lam, ja-umdol lam, adihlam, athenglam, lungset um le pachat umlam, aphalam le pachat thei lama auma ahileh hitiho hi lunggel un. 
Nanghon keiya kon a nakihilsa hou, na sansa ho-u chule keima a kon a najahsa hou le namusa hou nitin in juiyun. Chuteng cham-lungmon Pathen chun naumpi dingu ahi. 
Nanghon keima nei gelkhoh kitnau jeh a Pakai iti kavahchoi hitam! Nanghon keima neigelkhoh jing uve ti kahei, hinla nang hon chutia kithopina thei phat kijen namulouvu ahi. 
Hichu kangaichat umjeh a kasei ahipoi, ajeh chu keiman kanei khama chu lung lhaiya umje kahet ahi. 
Keiman imacha nei louva um ahilouleh ijakai neiya umje kahen ahi. Itobang phat hunglhung dinga jong, oivaset ahilouleh keltah a umje jong chule ninglhingset hihen lhomcha hijongleh hiche thuguh chu kahet ahitai. 
Ajeh chu keima thahat eipea Christa jal a chu ijakai bolthei kahi. 
Hitima chun, nang hon tupet kahahsatna a nei panhu uhi nabol phalheh jeng uve. 
Philippia a um nang hon naheng uva Kipana Thupha kahin pohpeh uva chu, nahetma bang uvin, sum le paiya eikithopia nangho bou nahiuvin chule Macedonia a konna kaga kholjin patkit chun koima houbung in achutibol pouvin ahi. 
Thessalonica kho a kaumpet jengin jong kithopina khatvei seh hilouvin avel in neihin thot uvin ahi. 
Keiman hiche kasei hi nanghoa thilpeh chu ka ngaichat ahipoi. Chusangin, lungset them nahijal uva kipaman nasan diu chu kadei joh ahi. 
Tupet in ka ngaichat jouse kaneiye-chukalval in Epaphroditus khut a na thilpeh u chun ninglhingset in einei sah e. Hiche ho chu kilhaina gimnamtui kihal Pathen a dinga santhei le lunglhai umtah ahi. 
Chule hiche Pathen, keima eigelkhohpeh a chun, aloupinaa Christa Jesu a ahao dungjuijin na ngaichat jouseu chu pebukim nauvin nate. 
Tun I-Pau Pathen'a chun loupina jouse atonsot atonsot in um jing hen! Amen. 
Pathen mitheng te jouse, Christa Jesu a hiho jouse chu keima a kon'in salam anape uvin. Sopite kaumpi chengsen salam nahin peuvin ahi. 
Chule Pathen mitheng te adang dang ho jousen salam nahin thot uve, adehset in Caesar insung mitea kon'in salam napeuve. 
Pakai Jesu Christa lungsetna chun nalhagaovu umpi hen.
