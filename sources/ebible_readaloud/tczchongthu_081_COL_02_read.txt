Colossae.
2.
Keiman nangho le Laodicea a um houbungho chule tahsaa kakimupi loulaiho chengse dinga nasatah a kapohnat peh uhi nahet dingu kalunggot ahi. 
Keiman amaho hi alung uva lhamkonna aum theina dinga amaho hi tilkhouva chule ngailutnaa akikandet uva, hitia hi ama hon Pathen lunggon thuguh Christa Ama tah a um chu ahetdoh theina dingu kangaito ahi. 
Ama a chu chihna le hetna gou aboncha kisela ahi. 
Hiche ka seipeh uhi koima chan duha pana nangho mohthua na lheplhah louna dingu ahi. 
Ajeh chu keiman tahsa a nalah uva kaumlou vang'in, lhagaovin kaumpi uve. Chule keiman na chondih ule Christaa na tahsan det u thu chu kipahtah in kave jinge. 
Chule tun, nang hon Christa Jesu chu Pakaiya nakisan bangu chun, Ama chu juijomun. 
Nakihilsa bang uvin, Ama a jungkho uvin lang, thutah a chun kisahdoh un, chule thangvahna dimletset in hing uvin. 
Mihem lunggel leh hiche vannoi lhagao thuneihoa hungkon chihgut lekhathemna le duha lheplhah a naum louna dingun chingtheiyun, hichu Christa a kon ahipoi. 
Ajeh chu Christa mihem tahsaa chu Pathen hina bulhingset a chenga ahi. 
Chule nangho hichea chu kisuchamkima nahiuve, ama chu piput le thuneina jouse luchang ahi. 
Nangho Christa henga nahung uva chu aman lhagaova chep na tanpeh u ahitai, hinla hichu tahsaa cheptan ngai chu hita louvin, chonna ngai chonset nomna chu atan lhah ahi bouve. 
Ajeh chu baptize nachan uva chu Christa toh kivuitha nahi tauve. Chule nang hon Christa kaithoupa Pathen thahat chu na tahsan jal uva Ama toh hinkho thah a kaithouva uma nahi tauve. 
Nachonset nau le chonset nomna ho chu paidoh a um ahiloulai jeh a nangho athia nanahiu ahi. Pathen'in ichonset nau angaidam tahjeh a Christa to eihin khomsah u ahitai. 
Aman eiho dounaa themmo chansahna bat lekhabua kijih chu akah a, chule thingpel'a akhetbeh tah jeh a alahmang ahitai. 
Hiti chun aman lhagao lama vaipoho leh thuneiho chu athaneinau alahpeh a, thingpel'a agaljona jal'a chu amaho chu lhangphonga maimoh a alha ahitai. 
Hijeh chun nangho naneh u ahilou le nadonu ahilou le nikho khat achoma nitlou jeh ahilou le lhathah nikho nit ahilouleh choldo nikho thua koiman na oimo dauhen. 
Ajeh iham itileh cheho chu atahbeh hunglhung ding vetsahna ho ahi bouvin, hinla Christa chu atahbeh ahung hitai. 
Koima kineosah lhema le vantil ho houna thua kipui mangsah hih un. Hitobang mi chu athilmu u thudol akip akoiyin aseiyun, amaho chun lhagao lam lunggel anei pouvin, tahsaa thaset lunggelin ahoitho sah uvin, 
Amaho chu aluchang Christa to kijopmatlou ahiuve. Ajeh chu Aman tipumpia agu kisuhtona le kikilmat tona chu akhut a aloikhoma Pathen'in akhansah dungjuiya aumsah ahi. 
Nangho Christa to thithaa nahitah uleh, hiche vannoi lhagao thaneinaa konna na chamlhat sah u ahitai. Chuti ahileh ipi itia vannoiya hing nalai banga chu vannoi chonna dan kihilna, 
tham hih in, tep hih in, tuh hih in kitiho najui nahlaiyu hintem? 
Hiche kipuina dan ho hi mihem thupeh le thuhil ahin, ahung kimanchah toh lhon a mangthai ding thil ahi bouve. 
Hiche chonna dan kijuiho hi chihtoh a ahi, hiche hoa hin eimatah lungcham a hou, eima tah kingaitoma kingainemna chule tahsa kithununna angaiyin ahi. Hinla hiche ho hin mikhat lung ngaichat phalou jona apechompoi.
