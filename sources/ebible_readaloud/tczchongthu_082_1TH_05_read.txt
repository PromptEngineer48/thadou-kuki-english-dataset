1 Thessalonica.
5.
Tun kasopi deitahte ho, hicheng kisei jouse hi iti hungsoh a chule itih le hung hi ding ham tithua nangho kom'a kahin jih mongu angaipoi. 
Ajeh chu na ginchat lou pettah uva Pakai hung kilekit nikho chu, jan'a gucha tobanga hung ding ahi. 
Mihon “Ijakaiya chamna le bitna aum'e” tia asei laitah uleh numei naosonat thohgim tobanga, jana gucha hung banga achung uva manthahna chu hunglhung ding ahi. Chule hoidohna um thei louding ahitai. 
Hinlah sopi deitahte, nangho hiche thudol'a hi ima helouva uma nahi pouve, chule Pakai nikho chu gucha tobanga ahung ding chu nang hon nadatmo diu ahipoi. 
Ijeh inem itileh nangho na boncha uvin vah le sunlai chate nahiuvin, eiho muthim le janlai chate ihipouve. 
Hiti ahijeh chun kihong phauvin, midang ho bangin ihmu hih un. Kigingin um unlang chule lung limgeh in umun. 
Jan phat le miho ihmu jiuva chule judon hon ju adonkham jiu phat ahi. 
Hinlah eiho lung limgeh in vah a umu hitin, tahsan le ngailut lum pan'u hite, chule huhhingna lukhuh chu lungngam tah in kikhuh uhite. 
Ajeh iham itileh Pathen'in eiho chunga alunghanna abuhlhah khum ding hijo louva, I-Pakaiyu Jesu Christa jal'a huhhingna chang dinga eilhendoh joh u ahi. 
Hiti chun Christa ahung kile tengleh eiho ithiu hihen ahilouleh hing jong leuhen, Ama eiho dinga athi jal a chu a itih a dia Amato i-umkhom jing dingu ahitai. 
Hiti chun nabol ngaisau tobang chun, khat le khat kitilkhouto uvinlang chule kisadoh ton khat le khat umun. 
Sopi deitahte ho, Pakai natohnaa nalamkai hou chu jabolun. Ama hon nalah uva atong gim'un, chule lhagao lama nakaihoi uva panga ahiuve. 
Amaho chu jana lentah peuvin lang chule anatoh jal un lunggilin ngailu uvin. Chule kichamdelin khat le khat cheng khomun. 
Sopite ho, kahin ngeh nau chu mithase ho chu gihsal un. Akichaseho tilkhouvun. Atha lhasam ho phaten khohsah in vesuiyun. Amitakip chunga thohhat un. 
Koiman ase chu asea alethuh louna dingin vephauvin, chusangin khat in khat chunga chule mijouse chunga thilpha boljing ding gouvin. 
Phatseh in kipah thanomun. 
Taona man ngahih helun. 
Itobang phat in naphah jong le thangvahna nei jingun, ijeh ham itileh Jesu Christa a hia nangho dinga Pathen lunglam ahi. 
Lhagao Theng chu sumit hih un. 
Gaothuseiho nahsahmoa dai chatvai bol hih un. 
Hinlah akisei jouse patep cheh un. Apha chu kolchah un. 
Gitlouna chichom choma kon'in kihei mangun. 
Tun cham-lungmon konna Pathen chun imalam jousea na thensah uhen, chule na lhagaovu le na hinnau chule natahsau chu I-Pakaiyu Jesu Christa ahung masangsen nolna beiyin koihen. 
Nakou Pau chu tahsan umtah ahijeh in, Pathen'in hichu ahin molso ding ahi. 
Sopi deitahte ho, keiho dingin hung taovun. 
Sopite ho jouse chu Christian kingailutna in chibai bolun. 
Keiman Pakai min'a thu kapeh nahiuve, hiche lekhathot hi sopite jouse henga kisimdoh hen. 
I-Pakaiyu Jesu Christa lungsetna in na umpiu hen.
