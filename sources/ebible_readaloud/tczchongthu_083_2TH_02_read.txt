2 Thessalonica.
2.
Sopi deitahte, tun keihon I-Pakaiyu Jesu Christa hungna ding thu le Ama kom'a ikikhop khom dingu thudol'a seichetna hinnei uvinge. 
Pakai nikho chu ahunglhung'ah ahitai tiho jeh in nangho bailamtah in suhlongin ahilouleh lung tijan umhih un. Amaho chun lhagaova vetsahna eikineiye, phondohna eikipei ahilouleh keihoa kon'in lekhathot kaneiye tijonglehu tahsan hih un. 
Ama hon ipi thu aseiyun nathujo dauhen. Ajeh chu hiche nikho chu ahunglhun masanga Pathen douna nasatah hunglhung ding chule dan beipa ___ manthaina mihempa kiti chu hung kilangdoh ding ahi. 
Ama kichoisang ding, chule mihon pathen atiuva aumsah u tapou chu ajahda ding chule ama tah Pathen kahi tia hung ki Pathen sah ding ahi. Ama Pathen Hou-In'a lut a, keima Pathen kahi ahungti ding ahi. 
Keiman nangho kaumpi pet uva hiche thu ho jouse hi ka seipehsau chu nageldoh lou u hitam? 
Chule nang hon ama chu ipin atuh tang denham ti naheuve, ajeh iham itileh ama aphat lhin tengleh bou hung kilang doh ding ahi. 
Ajeh chu hiche danbeipa hin guhthima na atoh pat ahitai chule atuhtangpa hi lampia konna akiheidoh masangsea hitia hi umthim ding ahi. 
Chujoutah leh mihem dan neiloupa hung kiphong doh ding, hinla Pakai Jesu chun akama ahaidoh hua chu amapa atha ding chule ahungna aloupina chun asuhmang ding ahitai. 
Hiche pa hin Satan natoh ama thaneina hilou chu ahinpat ding chule melchihna ho le thil kidang dang boldoh theina tha anei ding ahi. 
Aman mahthahna lampia cheho chu duha lheplhahna pana angolbol ding ahi, ajeh chu ama hon huhhing achan theina ding uva ngailutna le thutah chu akisan nomlouvu ahi. 
Hitia chu Pathen'in amaho chu hatah a lheplhahna a achuhsah ding, chule hiche jouthu ho chu ama hon atahsan dingu ahi. 
Chutengleh ama hon thutah chu tahsan tah sanga agilou chu athanoppi jeh uva themmo chang diu ahi. 
Sopite, keiho dingin vang nangho Pakai ngailut ho jal a bou Pathen chu thangvahna kapeh dingu tailou adang kahe pouve. 
Aman huhhing'a naumna ding uva nakouvu ahi thua keihon Kipana Thupha ka seipeh nahiuvin; tua hi nang hon I-Pakaiyu Jesu Christa loupina a chu chan nanei khom theiyu ahitai. 
Hiche ho jouse gel pumin, sopi deitahte, keihon nangho tahsa a kaumpi pet u hihenlang ahilouleh lekhathot a kon hijongleh nangho dingdet un lang chule ka thuhil u chu kolchah jing uvin. 
Tun I-Pakaiyu Jesu Christa Ama tah le eingailu uvah I-Pau Pathen le alungsetna jal a tonsot lhamkonna chule kinepna kidangtah eipeuva chun, 
Thilpha nabolna le naseinau jousea nalhamonu henlang chule nahat dohsah tauhen.
