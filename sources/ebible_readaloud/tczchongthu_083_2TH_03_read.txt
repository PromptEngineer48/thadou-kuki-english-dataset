2 Thessalonica.
3.
Achainakeiyin, sopi deitahte, keiho dingin hung taovun tia kangeh nahiuve. Nangho henga anahung tobanga chu Pakai thuhil hi alhunna chan a gangtah a akithejala chule jabol'a aum theina dingin taovun. 
Chujongleh amitakip tahsan nei ahilou jeh uvin, keiho migilou le miphalou hoa konna huhdoh kachan theina ding uvin taovun. 
Hinlah Pakai hi tahsan umtah ahin, Aman na thahat sah uvin tin, chule agiloupa a kon'in nahuhbit nauvinte. 
Chule keiho Pakaiya kakiskonnau chu–nang hon kathupeh u chu nabol uva chule naboljom dingu ahi ti kakisonpiu nahiuve. 
Pakaijin nalungthimu Pathen ngailutna hetkhenna le phondohna bukim keiyin chule Christa a konna thohhatna lama napuiyu hen. 
Chule tun, sopi deitahte, Pakai Jesu min'a hiche thupeh hi kapeh nahiuve: tahsan nei sopiho jouse thasea hinkho mangho chule keihoa konna ka thuhil u juilou hoa kon'in kikhindoh un. 
Ajeh chu nang hon keiho umchan najom diu adol ahi. Keiho nangho toh kaumkhom laiyun kathase khapouve. 
Keihon kaneh manu pelouvin koima an kane pouve. Keihon koima kapohgih sah louna ding uvin sun le jan in katong gim uve. 
Keihon neivah ding uva thupeh kapeh thei nahiuve, hinla keihon najui ding uvin vetsahna kapejo bou uve. 
Keihon nangho kaumpi pet jeng uva jong, hiche thupeh hi kapeh u nahi, “Natong nomlou hon neh ding kimu louding ahiuve,” tihi. 
Ahijeng vang'in nangho phabep thasetah in hinkho namang uvin, natoh nanom pouve chule midang natohna chunga kikum naum uve. 
Keihon hitobang miho chu thu kapeuvin, chule Pakai Jesu Christa minin amaho chu kangeh uve, amaho chun ageltoh uva chule akihinsona diuva na atoh diu hi ahi. 
Sopi deitahte, nangho lah a adangse dinga kasei chu, thilpha bolnaa chim hihbeh uvin. 
Hiche lekhathot a keiho seingailou ho chu melchih uvin, amahoa kon'in kichom khindoh leu chun amaho jahcha tauvinte. 
Amaho chu melma ho gelin gel hih un, hinla sopi nabolna bangun sosalun. 
Tun cham-lungmon konna Pathen chun cham-lungmon imatih chanin chule phat jousen nape jingu hen. Pakaijin naumpi jingu hen. 
Hiche hi keima Paul in ka khuttah a kajih chibai bohna ahi. Hitia keima tah in lekhathot kajihna ho photchenna a hitia hi kabolji ahi. 
I-Pakaiyu Jesu Christa milungsetna chun na boncha uvin naumpi tauhen.
