1 Timothy.
2.
Amasapen in mijouse dinga tao dingin kangeh un ahi. Pathen hepina achan theina dingun thum'un, chuleh amaho dingin thangvahna peuvin. 
Lengho leh thuneina mun a umho dingin taovun, hitia chu Pathen jana neipuma lungmong tah le chamdel a hinkho iman theina diuvin. 
Ijeh-inem itileh hichu thilphatah eihuhhingpau Pathen lahthei doltah ahi. 
Aman mijouse hi huhhing'a aum theina ding leh thutah hetna ahin neithei diu hi eideipeh u ahi. 
Ajeh chu Pathen khat seh leh mihemte kikah a chamna eisempeh theiyuvah palai khat seh Jesu Christa aumin ahi. 
Aman mijouse dingin ahinna mangchan chamlhatna eichohdoh peh tauvin ahi. Hiche hi Pathen'in vannoi mite aphatcha ahetsah nom chu ahi. 
Chule hiche tahsan thu leh thutah thu hi chidang namdang te lah a hilchen ding leh seiphong dinga keima hi solchah a eikilheng doh ahitai. Keiman kaseival poi thutah bou kasei ahi. 
Pathen houkhomna mun jousea, mihon lunghanna le kinel baotam nei louva Pathen lama akhuttheng adop uva taona aman diu kadeiyin ahi. 
Chuleh numei ho hi adolbep chan kijemu henlang kiphalam khen chan umuhen. Akivon diu bep chan kivon u henlang, asampheh dan uvah chingtheiyuhen, sana le dangka mantam ahilouleh thil manlutah kivona kilelahna neida uhen. 
Ajeh chu Pathen ngaisah kahi ti numeiho chu achonphat nauvin kijemjo leu aphai. 
Numei hon kangkhencha leh ginggeh cha a um kithemchuh uhen. 
Numei ho hi pasal ho chunga thunei ding leh thuhil dingin thu kapepoi, thipkhen chan thu ngaileu kati. 
Ajeh chu Pathen'in Adam asem masan chujouvin Eve asemin ahi. 
Chuleh Satan in alheplhah chu Adam ahipon, numeinu joh chu ana lheplhah a chonset hunglut ahi. 
Ahinlah numeiho hi tahsanna le ngailutna a, thenna le chonnema aum jing leh acha hin uva konna huhhing'a um ding ahiuve.
