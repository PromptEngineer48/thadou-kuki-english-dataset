1 Timothy.
3.
Hiche hi tahsan thei ding doltah a kisei ahi. “Khat touvin Houbung Upa hi ding natoh anop leh ama chun ja umtah panmun adei ahi.” 
Hijeh chun Houbung Upa ding chu ja umtah nolnabei hinkho mang hi ding ahi. Aji ang'a kitah ding, ama le ama kitimjou mi hi ding, chingthei tah a hing hi ding, chule minphatna nei hi ding ahi. Jindot them chule mihil them hi ding ahi. 
Ama chu julopmi ahilou le agilou hilou ding ahi. Ama chu milungneng hi ding, mitoh kinahat hilou ding, chule sum ngailu lou mi hi ding ahi. 
A-insung kipohtup jou mi, achaten ajathei le athua nunsah jou mi hi ding ahi. 
Ajeh chu a-insung akivettup joulou leh Pathen houbung chu iti avettup jou ding ham? 
Houbung Upa ding chu tahsan thah hilou ding ahi, ajeh chu hung kiletsah intin Diabolin asuhlhuh ding ahi. 
Chujongleh, pam a miho seiphat changmi hi ding ahi, achuti louleh miseiset achanna a kon chun Diabol thangkolah ohkhaget inte. 
Chutobang ma chun deacon panmun loding ho jong chu ja-um mi chuleh kitahna neimi hi ding, ju a lopmi hilou ding, sum a kitahlou mi hilou ding ahi. 
Tahsan thugil tua kiphongdoh thudol'a kipeh thengna nei, chule lungthengsel a hing mi hidiu ahi. 
Amaho hi deacon a akipansah masanga hoitah a kholchil masat ding, alhin uva ahileh deacon panmun a pang ding ahiuve. 
Hitobangma a chu ajiteu jong ja um mi hi ding, miseiset hat hilou ding, ama le ama kitimjoumi hi ding chuleh anatohna jousea jong kitahna neimi hi ding ahiuve. 
Deacon ho chu jikhat neimi hi ding chuleh achateu leh a insung kivetsuijou mi hi ding, 
Deacon natohna a lolhing tah mi chun mipia kon a jana chang diu hichun Jesu Christa atahsan nau toh tahsan aum nau asuhpung ding ahi. 
Nakom u hunglhun vah ding kinepna kanei jing vang'in tun hiche ho hi kahin sun e. 
Ijemtia keima kahunggei khah leh Pathen insung mite iti chon diu ham ti nahet theina dinga kati ahi. Hiche hi hingjing Pathen houbung ahin, hichu thutah kingapna khombul ahi. 
Kinelna beihel in, hiche hi itahsan nau thugil loupitah chu ahi. Christa chu mihem a hung kilanga Lhagaovin atahlanga vantil hon amuu ahi. Ama thu hi chitin namtin lah a akiphong jal in vannoi miten atahsan un chuleh loupitah in van ah lahtouvin aumtai.
