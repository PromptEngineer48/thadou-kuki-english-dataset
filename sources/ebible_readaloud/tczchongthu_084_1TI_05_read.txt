1 Timothy.
5.
Upa tehseho chu phoh hih-in, napa tobangin jana neitah in jolthon, khangdong pasal ho chu napenpi nasopitah tobangin kihoupin, 
Nupi tehseho chu nanu tobangin gel inlang, na sanga dongjo numeiho chu napenpi nasopinu tobangin lungtheng tah in kaihoiyin. 
Ajen ding koimacha neilou meithai ho chu jana neitah in kaihoiyin, 
Ahinlah chate le tute nei ahiu leh amopoh nau masapen chu a insunga Pathen mikhotona apohdoh dingu, ahing uvah anu le apateu phatna ngailutna a alethuh diu ahi. Hichu Pathen lung lhaina ahi. 
Amavang leiset chunga a insungmi um talou meithai dihtah ho chun Pathen a akingapnau ngaplhahna anei theiyun, Pathen'in apanpina diuvin sun le jan in atao jingun ahi. 
Ahinlah meithai nopsahna hinkhoa kimangcha jingho vang ahinlai sunga jong athisa ahiuve. 
Amaho chu miseiset achan louna diuva hiche thupeh ho hi napeh ding ahi. 
Ahinlah koi hileh ama sopite ding khohsahna neilou adeh deh a ama insung mite ding khohsah louho chun tahsan dihtah chu apaidoh u ahitan, hitobang ho chu atahsan louho sanga khohjo ahiuve. 
Kithopi dinga amin kikhum meithaiho chu alhompen a kum somgup hi ding, ajipa anga kitahna ana neimi hi ding ahi. 
Ama chu achate ahin khoukhahna a athilpha bol jeh a mijouse jana changmi hi ding, maljin khoto, tahsan kibahpiho kisunem tah a jenmi hi ding, hahsatna tohho kithopi nommi hi ding ahi. 
Khangdong meithaiho vang panglou diu ahi, ajeh chu Christa dinga akipehnau sanga atahsau ngaichatna chu hatjo ding jinei nom kit diu ahi. 
Hiche chun kitepna aneisau chu asuhkeh a themmona chang diu ahi. 
Chuleh amaho chun thaset hinpan untin intin a vahle untin, thuchavai seina a kipeuvin tin, mi natohna a gakikum lediu chuleh akinlouva thuseina a gakamjao lediu ahi. 
Hijeh chun hitobang khangdong meithai ho hi ajinei kit uva cha anei diu, inchen akisem diu ka phatsah ahi. Chutileh melma chun amaho chu aseisetna diu agomkom aneilou diu ahi. 
Ajeh chu amaho lah a phabep khat hi ache lengun, Satan nung ajui tauve tihin eitijat sah in ahi. 
Tahsan cha numei khat chun sopi meithai aneiya ahileh amaho chu phatah in kivetsui henlang mopohna houbung chunga ngapda hen. Chutileh houbungin achangseh a cheng meithaiho phatah a avetsui thei dingu ahi. 
Houbung Upa akin phatah a tongho adehset a thupha lhangsapna le thuhilna a hapanho chu jana peh ding chuleh atohman jong phatah a peh ding ahi. 
Ajeh chu Pathen thun, “Bongchal changchila chu amuh kikan hihhen chujongleh mun dang khat a chun, natonga chun atohman asan dinga lomtah ahi!” ati. 
Houbung lhacha khat chunga hehna aum leh hettohsah mini, mithum aumlou leh lahih in. 
Adang hon kichatna anei theina dingin achonseho chu mipi angsunga sosal'in. 
Pathen leh Jesu Christa chuleh vantil sangpen ho mitmua hiche thu hi kapeh nahi, hiche thupeh ho hi langneina leh sihneina koimacha kom'a neilouvin mangchan. 
Houbung sunga Pakai lhacha napansah ding tengleh kino hihbeh in. Midang chonsetna a kinunat hih in, nang le nang kisuthengin. 
Twisih jengseh donhih in, naveiset jeh leh nagilpi damlouna jeh in lengpitwi themkhat khat anamang jitan. 
Mi phabep chonsetna hi mijouse het in akilangin, suhdih thei ahi. Ahinla mi phabep chonsetna hi khonung le bou hung kilang ding loi aum'e ti sumil hih in. 
Chutobang chun mi phabep thilpha bol hi mipi het in akilangdoh theiyin, chuleh mi phabep in thilpha abol hi phat khat le bou hung kilang doh ding ahi.
