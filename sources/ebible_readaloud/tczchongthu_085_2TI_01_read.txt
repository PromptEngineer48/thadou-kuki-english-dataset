Timothy Henga Paul Lekhathot Nina.
1.
Hiche hi Jesu Christa solchah hi dinga Pathen lunglhaina a lhendoh a um Paul lekhathot ahi. Keima Christa Jesu tahsan a kon hinna athutep seiphong dinga eikisol'ah ahi. 
Ka ngailuttah kachapa Timothy dinga kajih ahi. Pa Pathen le I-Pakaiyu Jesu Christa ngailutna le khotona chuleh lungmon konna chun naumpi hen. 
Timothy, kapu kapaten apom banga se le pha hetna lunggel thengtah puma kapom Pathen chu nang jal'in ka thangvah e. Asun ajanin kataona a nangma kageldoh jinge. 
Ikikhen a na mitlhilon chu kageldoh in, nangma kamukit nom lheh jenge. Chule iumkhom kit tengleh keima kipana a dimset ding kahitai. 
Natahsan dihtah chu kageldoh e, ajeh chu napi Lois leh nanu Eunice a ana um dim masa tahsan chu nachan khompi ahi. Chule hiche tahsan chu nang'ah dettah in aum jinge ti kahen ahi. 
Hiche jeh a chu, na chunga kakhut kangap a Pathen'in lhagao thilpeh napeh chu nasuhlang jing dinga kahin geldohsah kit nahi. 
Ajeh chu Pathen'in tijatna le meisanna Lhagao eipe pouve, amavang thohhatna, ngailutna chule kitim themna joh eipeuve. 
I-Pakaiyu chung thu midang seipeh ding jahcha hihbeh in, chuleh ama jeh a songkul katan jeh hin keima jong nei jachatpi hih in. Pathen'in napeh thahatna chu kengin lang hiche Kipana Thupha jeh a hi thohgimna toh dingin kigosan um'in. 
Ajeh chu Pathen'in eihuhdoh uva hinkho thenga hing dinga eikouvu ahi. Hiche hi eiho ichan diu lom a eibolpeh u ahipon, amavang phat kipat masangpeh a ama tohgon dungjuiya abol ahi bouve, amikhotona Jesu Christa a konna eivetsah nom jal u ahi. 
Tun eihuhhingpau Jesu Christa ahung kilah chun hiche ho hi eihet chetsah u ahitai. Aman thina thahat chu asumangin athupha hi mangchan thi thei louna leh hinna lampi chu eisalvah peh tauvin ahi. 
Chule Pathen'in hiche Kipana Thupha hi hillhang le dinga solchah khat a eilhendoh ahitai. 
Hiche jeh a chu, keiman hiche songkul'a hi kathohgim ahi. Ahinlah keima kajah achapoi, ajeh chu keiman katahsan chu kahe chenin chuleh ama ahungkit kah a achunga kakingap lhahna dungjuiya hi eikaihoi jing ding ahi ti katahsanin ahi. 
Keima a konna nahet thuhil dihtah Jesu Christa a nanei tahsan le ngailutnaa kisemdoh chu chepin. 
Isung uva chengah Lhagao Theng thaneina a kon chun nangman nakingansena thutah manlutah chu chingthei tah in kichin tup in. 
Nahet ngaima bangin Asia gamkaiya konna hungho jouse Phygelus le Hermogenes chan gei chun eidalhagam tauvin ahi. 
Pakaijin ahepina giltah chun Onesiphorus leh a insung mite umpi hen ajeh chu aman eihung vil in eihung tilkhou jinge. Kabul put jong chu aman eijachatpi pon ahi. 
Ama Rome a ahung chun eihung mudoh kahsen muntin'ah eihol len ahi. 
Pakai chu ahung kilekit nileh angailutna giltah chun umpi hen, chuleh Ephesus mun a chu itobang tah a phachom'ah hitam nangman nahesoh nai.
