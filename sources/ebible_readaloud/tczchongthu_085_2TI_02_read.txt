2 Timothy.
2.
Timothy kangai luttah kachapa, Jesu Christa a um Pathen'in napeh hiche lungsetna a chun hat in. 
Tahsan theitah hettohsah tamtah in asuhdet ka thuhil ho chu nangman najatan ahi. Tun nangin hiche thutah ho hi tahsan thei miho chu hilin, chutileh ama hon midang ho kom'a apehdoh son diu ahi. 
Jesu Christa sepai phatah bangin ahahsa kathoh hi nei thohpin. 
Sepai galkap pet koima leiset hinkho thil ho dingin akisalelpoi, ajeh chu hiche chun sepaiya apansahpa alunglhai sah thei pon ahi. 
Chuleh golseh kitetna ajong koiman dan juilouvin kipaman asang ngaipoi. 
Chule natong hat loubolmi jong atohga nemasapen a pang ding dol ahi. 
Kathusei hi hoitah in geltoh them in, Pakaijin hiche ho hi nahetthem sah ding ahi. 
Jesu Christa hi leng David chilhah ahin, athia konna kaithouva um ahi tia ka thuhil hi phat tinin geldoh jingin. 
Chuleh hiche Kipana Thupha thohgimna ho hi kato ahi. Amavang Pathen thu hi akikham tang thei dehpoi. 
Pathen'in alhendoh ho dinga huhhingna le Christa a tonsot loupina ahin pohlut ding ahiphot le itobang thohgimna hijongleh thoh ding kanom in ahi. 
Hiche hi tahsan umtah thuhil ahi: Ama toh ithikhom uleh ama toh ihinkhom diu ahi. 
Ama toh ithohgim khom uleh ama toh ivaihom khom diu ahi, ama iseilep uleh aman jong eiseilep diu ahi. 
Ahinlah eihon kitahna neihih jong leuhen ama chu kitah jing ahi, ijeh inem itileh ama chu ama leh ama kiseilep theilou ahi. 
Nangman hiche thu ho hi mijouse geldohsah in, chule phatchomna bei mohthu pouva akinel baotam louna dingun Pathen anga hilchah in. Ajeh chu hitobang kinelna ho chun ajaho lungthim asuhset ding ahi. 
Pathen lunglhaina nachan theina dingin ama a dingin kiphal thengin, gunchun Pathen thu hi jachatna beihelin adihna bangtah in hilchenin lang ponpanin. 
Pathen nahsah louna thu phachomlou ngolkai thu kihoulimna a kon'in kikangse in, 
Hitobang thuphalou ho hi tahsa a hivei phalou tobanga, Hymenaeus le Philetus chunga soh tobanga chu, kithejal ji ahi. 
Amaho hi thutah a konna lamvai ahiuvin, mithiho chu thoudohsa ahi tauve atiuvin mi phabep khat tahsanna lama kon'in alehei tauvin ahi. 
Ahinlah Pathen thudih hi khombul kiphut detchet bangin adingdet jingin achungah hiche thu hi akijih in ahi, “Pakaijin amaho chu koikoi ahi ahen chuleh Pakai mite chu thilsea kon'in akihei mang jiuve,” akiti. 
Mihao insunga chun sana le dangka a kisem thilkeo ngen aumpon thing le leibel jong aumin, amantamho chu kin thupia kimangcha ding ahin, aman lhomho chu nitina manchah hi ding ahiuve. 
Nangma na kisuhtheng jinga ahileh kinloupia manchah thei thilkeo thupi chu nahi ding ahi. Na hinkho chu theng ding Pakaijin thilphachoma amanchah dinga kigosa jing chu nahi ahi. 
Khandonlai lung ngaichat ho jousea kon'in jammangin, amavang chonphatna, kitahna, ngailutna le chamna chu hadel in, lungthengsel a Pakai kom beljingho toh kiloikhomin, 
Avel in seikit inge, ngol hoitah, ima hetoh louva kinel baotamna hoa konchun kikangse doh in, hichun kinahna asodoh sah jin ahi. 
Chuleh Pakai lhacha chun mitoh kinah abol louding, amavang mijouse angailut ding, mihil them hi ding, chuleh mihahsa ho chu adepthem ding ahi. 
Thutah doudal ho chu kangkhen chan hilthem in ajeh chu amaho lungthim chu Pathen'in aleheiya thutah chu ahin hetthem thei diu ahi. 
Hiteng chuleh amaho hung khangdoh untin, Diabol thangkol a konna hoidoh thei diu ahi. Ajeh chu aman amaho chu adei deiya abolthei dinga gal hinga anamat ahitai.
