Titus Henga Paul Lekhathot.
1.
Hiche Lekhathot hi Pathen soh chule Jesu Christa solchah, Paul'a kon ahi. Keima Pathen'in alhendohsa ho lah a tahsan thudol seiphong ding le Pathen mite hindan ahetthem theina diu thutah chu hil dinga eikisol ahi. 
Chuche thutah, jou seitheilou Pathen'in vannoi bul kiphudoh masanga ana tepsa, chun tonsot hinna aneiyu chu kiskonna apeh ahi. 
Chule tua aphat alhintah a hiche thu hi aman ahin phondoh a, keihon mijouse lah a kasei phongleu ahi. Hiche hi eihuhhing uva Pathen a konna thupeh, ama natong dinga keima eikinganse ahi. 
Keiman, kakikoppi tahsanna jal'a kachapa dihtah, Titus henga kasut ahi. Pa Pathen chule eihuhhing uva Christa Jesun chamna le lungmon nape tahen. 
Keiman nangma Crete twikol a ka dalhah chu, inatoh lhon nasuh bulhit a chule kahilna banga khopi tinchenga upaho na pansahna dinga kadalhah nahi. 
Upa khat chun nolnabei hinkho aman ding ahi. A inneipi dinga kitahna neimi hi ding, chule achate chu atahsan chon chatvaina lam le thua nunlouna lama seiset changlou hi ding ahi. 
Upa khat chu Pathen insunga gouching ahi, hijeh a chu nolna beiya hinkho aman ding ahi. Ama chu kichamsah, lungsabai, ju a loplou hi ding, alam louva sum ngailu mi hilou ding ahi. 
Maljin phatbol mi, a-in'a jin alhun le kipah, chule Pathen ginga, chondihtah a hinkho mang mi hi ding, thilpha ngailu, lunglimgeh, chondih, amachang kitimthei, Pathen ging mi hi ding ahi. 
Tahsan umtah thu ana kihilna ho detchet a tuhchah mi hitei ding ahi; chutileh aman midang ho chu kihilna hoitah a atilkhou thei ding chule minelkal hat ho jong athemmo nau akomu peh thei ding ahi. 
Ajeh chu adoudal ah mi tamtah panna bei thuseiya thanom chule milhemlha tamtah aum'e. Hiche hi ahideh a cheptan vanga sochatna um ahi tia seiho lah a aum nom e. 
Amaho chu suhthip ding ahiuve, ajeh chu athuhil dihlou uva kon chun insung tamtah apuimangun ahi. Chule hiche athilbol u chu sum ngailut jeh a bou abol'u ahi. 
Amaho lah jenga jong, mi khat, Crete a konna themgao khat in amaho thudol hitin aseijin, “Crete miho hi abon'uva mijou, gam sahang, chule mithase nehlop ahiuve” ati. 
Hiche hi adihtah mong ahi. Chuti ahijeh chun amaho hi tahsan kicheh mi ahina dingun hatah in phohsal in. 
Thutah a konna mi puikon thei, Juda ho moh thusim le miho thupeh ah lunglut dauhen. 
Alungtheng dinga ima jouse theng sohkei ahi, ahinlah akisuboh atahsanlou ho ding vanga athenga khatcha umlou ahi, ajeh chu alunggel ule se le pha hetna alungsungu bohji a ahi. 
Hitobang miho chun akamsung uva Pathen kahenai tin aphongdoh nauvin, ahinlah ahinkho mandan uvin aseilep un ahi. Amaho chu thet umtah, thungailou ahiuvin, thilpha bolna lama panna bei ahiuve.
