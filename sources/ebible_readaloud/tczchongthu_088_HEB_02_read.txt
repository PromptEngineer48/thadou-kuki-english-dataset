2.
Hiti ahijeh a chu eihon ithujah u thutah hi chingthei tah a ingai diu ahi, achuti louva ahileh hichea konna hi ikiheimang theiyu ahi. 
Ajeh chu Pathen'in vantil hoa konna apehdoh Athu chu adingdet jingin, chule dan palkeh le seingai louho chu atotoh in engbolna achang jiuvin ahi. 
Hiti ahijeh achu hiche Huhhingna loupitah, amasatah a Pakai Jesu Christa ama tah in aphondoh chule chujouva Athusei jahon eiho henga ahung phondohson u, hi isahlouva ikoi uleh, ahoidoh dinga iti ikigel theiyu ahidem? 
Chule Pathen'in Athusei chu aphudetna dinga melchihna ho le thil kidang ho chule bolmo kidang jatchom chom ho chule Lhagao thilpeh, aman alhendoh phat phat a, apeh ahi. 
Chule akalvalin, tua iseiyu ahunglhung ding vannoi jong vantil ho thuneina ding ahipoi. 
Ajeh chu mun khat ah Pathen Thubun aseijin, “Mihem hi ipi hiya nangman na gelkhoh a, ahilouleh Mihem Chapa hi ipi hiya nakhohsah ham? 
Ahijeng vang'in nangman vantil ho sangin noi nung jon nasemin chule amaho hi loupina le jabolnan lallukhuh nakhuh sah in ahi. 
Nangman thil jouse chungah thuneina napetan ahi” ati. Tua “thil jouse” kitina a hi, imacha ajaolou umlou ahi. Hinla imacha amaho thuneina noiya akikoi akimudoh hih laiye. 
Eihon imupenu chu Jesu, vantil ho sanga panmun neojep a kikoi chu ahin, chule Aman eiho dinga thina ahung thoh ahitah jeh in, Ama hi tun “loupina lallukhuh khuhpehna le jabolna achangtan ahi.” Ahi, Pathen lungset in, Jesun mitin dingin thina chu ahung tepkha tan ahi. 
Pathen'in, chate tamtah loupina a apuilut theina dinga ama dinga alhena chule ama jala ijakai asem ahi. 
Hiti chun tun Jesu le aman athenga asemho dingin Pa kibang aneiyu ahitai. Hijeh a chu Jesu chun asopite tia akou ding chu jahcha louva ahi. 
Ajeh iham itileh aman Pathen kom'a, “Keiman kasopi te kom'a namin ka phondoh ding, Mihonpi kikhop khomna a keiman kavahchoi ding nahi,” ati. 
Aman jong aseijin, “Keiman katahsan achunga kangap ding ahi,” hichu, “Keima le kachate Pathen'in eipeh ahitai,” ati. 
Ajeh chu Pathen chate chu mihemho ahiuvin–tahsa le thisana kisem ahiuvin–Chapa jong chu tahsaphe le thisan pu ahung hitai. Ajeh chu mihemte ahijal a bou chu thi thei ding ahin, chule athi jal a bou thina thaneina neipa Diabol thaneina chu ajo ding hiya ahi. 
Hichea konna bou thina kichatna a hinkho mang sohchang ho chamlhatna apeh thei ding hiya ahi. 
Chapa chu vantil ho kithopi dinga hunga ahipoi tijong iheuvin ahi. Abraham chilhah ho panhu dinga hunga ahi. 
Hiche jeh a chu, Ama tah jong chu imalam jousea eiho, asopite, tobang ahi theina dinga sem ngai ahi, chutia chu ama Pathen masanga lungset them le tahsan umtah Thempu Chungnung ahi theina dinga ahi. Chujeh a chu aman miho chonset pomang dinga kilhaina abol thei bou ahi. 
Aman thohhahsa athoh lhumkeiya chule patepna ho athoh tah jeh a, eihon patepna ichan tengu jongleh aman eipanpi theiyu ahi.
