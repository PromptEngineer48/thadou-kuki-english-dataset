4.
Acholdona a lut ding thua Pathen kitepna tuchanin ading jing nalaiye. Hijeh a chu, nalah uva mi phabep khattou chuche thutep chu tepkhalou ding naum khah diu kichatna a eiho kichatah le kithing puma ium diu ahi. 
Ijeh inem itileh hiche Kipana Thupha Pathen'in kicholdona a asem hi-amaho kom'a akiphongdoh ahitah banga eiho kom'a jong kiphongdoh a ahitai. Hinla ama hon aphatchompi pouve, ajeh chu ama hon Pathen sei ngaiho tahsan chu akikoppi pouve. 
Ajeh chu eiho atahsan ho hi acholngahna a lutthei ihiuve. Hinla adang ho dingin vang Pathen'in, “Kalung hanna a kahahsel ahi: Amaho kacholdona a lutlouhel ding ahiuve,” ati. Hiche cholngahna hi vannoi asem laiya pat a gotsa akoi ahivangin. 
Hiche hi gotsa koi ahi ti ihetnau ajeh chu Pathen Thubu munkhat ah nisagi thudol hiti hin akisune, “Nisagi nin Pathen anatohna jousea kon'in akicholdo tai.” 
Hinlah munchom khatma ah Pathen'in, “Amaho ka choldona mun'a lutlouhel dingu ahi,” ati. 
Hiti chun Pathen choldona chu miho luttheina dingin aum'e, hinla hiche Kipana Thupha jamasaho chun Pathen sei angailou jeh uchun alut thei tapouve. 
Hiti chun Pathen'in acholdona a luttheina dingin phat chomkhat agonpeh in, chule hiche phat chu tuni hi ahi. Pathen'in hiche hi David a kon'in khonungin ana phongdoh in, “Tunia Ama aw najah tengleh, nalung tahsah hih un,” atikit in ahi. 
Tun Joshua chun hiche choldona hi ana neisah ahitah leh, Pathen'in choldona nikho chom hunglhung nalai ding aseitah louding ahi. 
Hijeh a chu, Pathen mite dinga choldona chombeh khat nga jing ding um nalai ahi. 
Ijeh ham itileh Pathen'in vannoi asem jouva acholdo banga chu, Pathen cholngahnaa lut ho chu atohgim nauva konna choldoa ahi tauve. 
Hiti ahijeh chun ijochanun chu che cholngahna a chu lutding kiseh u hite. Hinla eihon Israel mite banga Pathen sei ingailou uleh ilhuh diu ahi. 
Ijeh ham itileh Pathen Thu hi ahinga le thanei tah ahi. Hemto chemjam ahempen sanga jong hemjoa ahin, hinna le lhagao chule gu kisuhtona le lhin jong sukhen thei ahi. 
Thilsem jouse lah a imacha Pathen a konna kisel thei aumpoi. Ijakai amitmua sagohkeo chule kihongsa ahi, chule Ama ichonna u jouse sutoh dingpa chu hiya ahi. 
Hiti chun eihon Thempu Chungnung, Pathen Chapa Jesu, van mun galut ah, chu ineiyu ahitah jeh in, Ama itahsan'u hi kolchah jingu hite. 
Hiche ithempu Chungnung uhi ilhahsamnau hethem tah ahi, ajeh chu Aman eiho banga patepna jouse ato ahin, ahivangin chonse lou ahiye. 
Hiti ahijeh chun eilungset uva I-Pathen'u Laltouna a chun lung ngamtah in hungu hite. Chukom'a chu eihon hepina le ingaichat khoh dehset tengu leh lungsetna panpina imu diu ahi.
