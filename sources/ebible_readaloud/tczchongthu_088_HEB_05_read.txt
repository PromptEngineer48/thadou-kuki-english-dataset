Hebrew.
5.
Thempu Chungnung chu midang thalhenga Pathen kom'a galutji ahi. Aman amaho thilto chu Pathen kom'a apeh a chule achonset jeh uva maichama thil atou chu akatdohji ahi. 
Chule Ama hi ima helou le seingailou ho jeng jong lungneng tah a bolthei ahi, ajeh chu Ama tah jong hiche lhahsamna thunoiya chu kipelut a ahi. 
Hiche jeh a chu Ama le midang ho chonsetna ho jeh a chu kilhaina abolteiji ahi. 
Chule mi koima Thempu Chungnung chu jabolna tah achan jeh a ahinom mana moh hi jeng thei ahidehpoi. Ama chu Aaron tobanga hiche natohna dinga hi Pathen kouna chang mi hi ding ahiye. 
Hiti ahijeh a Christa jeng jong chu Thempu Chungnung hina na atoh jeng vang chun Ama tah kichoian deh louva ahi. Ama chu Pathen deilhen changa ahin, ajah a hitia hi asei ahi, “Nangma ka Chapa nahi. Keima tunia na Pa kahung hi ahitai.” 
Chule munchom khat ah, Pathen'in ajah a aseijin, “Nangma Melchizedek themputna tobanga tonsot thempu nahi,” ati. 
Jesu chu leiset a anaum laiyin, Aman taona le thumna aneijin hapen in apengin kana le mitlhi tothon thinaa konna ahuhdoh theipa henga chun ataove. Chule Aman Pathen agin lheh jeh chun Pathen'in ataona ho chu angaipeh tai. 
Jesu chu Pathen Chapa ahijeng vang'in, Aman athohgimna a chun sei angaiyin ahi. 
Hiti hin, Pathen'in Thempu Chungnung hina chamkim tah in asemin, chule asei ngai jouse dingin tonsot hinna bulpin asemtai. 
Chule Pathen'in Ama hi Melchizedek banga Thempu Chungnunga apehdoh ahitai. 
Keihon hiche thudol hi tampi kaseibe nomun, hinla hilchet ding ahahsai, adeh in nangho lhagaolam dol ah nalungmo un chule na ngainom diu chu ginchat aumpoi. 
Tahsan cha nahung hinau sotpi ahung hitan, tua hi midang hilthei ding nahiu ahitai. Chusangin, nang hon midang khat in Pathen Thubul lam nahil diu na ngaicha nalaiyuve. Nangho hi naosen noitwi ngaicha nalai chule anham nethei loulai tobang nahiuve. 
Ijeh ham itileh noichep nalai chu naosen hinalai bep ahin, chule thilpha iti bol ding ham chu ahet loulai ahi. 
An ham chu akikhanlhit hoa ding ahi, amaho chun akithemchuh uva konna adih le adihlou hetkhenna aneiyu ahitai.
