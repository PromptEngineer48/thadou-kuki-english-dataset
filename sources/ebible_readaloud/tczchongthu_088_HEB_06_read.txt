Hebrew.
6.
Hijeh chun eihon Christa thudol thubul lam avel vel'a kihilna ngatau hite. Chusangin masotjou hitin hetna lam'ah pilhin ding gou hite. Tahbeh mongin eihon thilse bolnaa konna lunghei chule Pathen a itahsan nau ngaplhah thudol'a thubul lam kithah pat kitna ingaicha tapouve. 
Baptize chan thudol, khutngap thudol, mithi thokitna, chule tonsot thutanna thudol'a kihilbena na ngaichat u ahi tapoi. 
Chule hitia hi Pathen phatsah ahileh, eihon aban hetbena lam imasot dingu ahiye. 
Ijeh inem itileh khatvei lhagao mitvahna neisa–vanlam thilpha ho hetna nei chule Lhagao Theng chankhompi hitasa ho, 
Pathen thu aphatna ho tepkha a chule ahunglhung ding khangho um ding dol hetna neitasa, 
chujouva Pathen a konna kiheimang ho chu: Pathen Chapa sang nomlou, Ama chu thingpel to avel'a akilbeh kit ahin, chule mihonpi lah a jachatsahna a atahlah ahitah jeh chun akisih kitna ding uva hin lepui chu thil hahsa tah ahi. 
Van'a kon go hunglha chun leiset achap nouvin chule loubol mi dingin Pathen phattheina gapha ahin sodoh in ahi. 
Hinlah lou chun lingphung le nangkai hinsodoh henlang hileh, panna bei hi ding ahitai. Loubolpa chun hiche lou chu themmo chanpai intin chule ahalvam jeng ding ahitai. 
Sopi deitahte, keihon hiti hin sei jengu jongleng, nangho chung chang thu a kisei ahipoi ti kaheuve. Keihon katahsanu chu aphajo, nangho huhhing channa thudol'a hinte tihi ahi. 
Ijeh ham itileh Pathen chondih louva ahipoi. Aman nang hon ichan geiya ama dinga kisa lunggima nahiu chule nang hon tugeija naboljom'u atahsan dang ho nakhohsah nau navetsah u chu asuhmil louding ahi. 
Kagel khoh tah u chu nahinkhou umsungin atahsan midang ho chu ngailu jomun, hichun nakinep nau chu atah hung himong ding ahinai ti aphotchet teina ding ahije. 
Chutileh nangho lhagao lama amol le ima khohsalou nahilou dingu ahitai. Chusanga Pathen thutep loding ho tahsan le thohhat umchan nungjuiya nahi dingu ahi. 
Vetsahnan, Pathen'in Abraham kom'a thutep ana neiye. Ama sanga hettohsah lenjo dang aum tahlou jeh in Pathen chu Ama tah minin akihahselin, 
“Keiman thon louhel a phatthei kaboh ding, chule keiman na chilhah ho chu simjou louva katamsah ding ahi,” ati. 
Chuti chun Abraham'in thohhat tah in angah in, chule Pathen thutep chu achangtai. 
Hitia hi mihon kihahselna aneiji tengu leh amaho sanga lenjoa chun akihahsel jiuvin chule hiche kihahselna chu kidohna ngai louvin akiphutdet jin ahi. 
Pathen Ama tah jong hin athutep chang hon alung akhel kit louna photchen nan, kihahselna achun ama jong aumin ahi. 
Hitia hi Pathen'in Athutep le akihahselna chu apehdoh gel ahije. Hiche teni hi khelkit thei ahi tapoi, ajeh chu Pathen joutheilou ahije. Hijeh a hi, Ama henga kipelut a kisel ho chun kinepna imasang uva um chu ituhchah jing uva ahileh kiskonna phatah ineitheiyu ahi. 
Hiche kinepna hi ilhagao ding uva kingap detna a phatah le tahsan umtah ahin, hichun pondal noiya Pathen muntheng sunga eipuilut theiyu ahi. 
Jesu chu chuche mun'a eiho dinga analut a eiho dinga tonsot Thempu Chungnung Melchizedek dinmun a uma ahitai.
