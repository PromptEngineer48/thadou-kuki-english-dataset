Hebrew.
13.
Sopi hina a khat le khat kingailut tona neijom jingun. 
Nanghon kholjin hungho chu phatbol ding sumil hih un, ijeh ham itileh hitia bol a vantil ho het louva lhunsah khah aumun ahi! 
Songkul'a nangho tah aumsa bangin kigelun lang, songkul'a umho chu geldoh un. Nanghon natahsa uva gimbol natna ama hon athoh u nangho tah in nathoh sau ahi tobangin, alam louva kibol ho jong chu geldoh un. 
Imalam jousea kichen hi jabolun, chule kichenna a kitahna hi khat in khat chunga neiyun. Pathen'in chonthanghoi bol ho le jonbol ho thu atan ding ahi. 
Sum ngailu hih un; nanei chung'ah lungkimna neiyun. Ijeh ham itileh Pathen'in aseisa ahitai, “Keiman nangma kalha louhel ding nahi. Keiman nangma kachanglha louhel ding nahi,” ati. 
Hijeh a chu eihon lungngam tah a, “Pakaijin eikithopin, hijeh a keima kicha louding kahi. Miho bep in ka chunga ipi abol diu ham?” tia isei theiyu ahi. 
Na lamkaiyu Pathen Thu nahil hou chu geldoh un. Ahinkho uva thilpha ahin sodoh hou chu gelun, chule atahsan u vetton thei chu juiyun. 
Jesu Christa hi janhi, tuni chule a-itih a ahina abang ahi jinge. 
Hijeh chun thuhil lamdang le gotchom'ah lunglut hih helun. Na thahat nau hi Pathen lungsetnaa hungkon ahi bouvin, anneh thudol'a chonna hoa kon ahipoi, hiche thua kisa lunggim hon ima phatchomna anei pouve. 
Eihon maicham khat, thempu hon Houbuh sunga nehtheina thaneina aneilouvu, ineiyun ahi. 
Chonna lui dungjuiya Thempu Chungnung chun gancha thisan chu Munthenga chonset maicham thiltoa ahin choilut ahin, chule gancha ho tahsa pumho chu khopi pamlama ahalvam jiu ahi. 
Hitobangma a jong chu Jesu chun khopi kotpi polama chu gim athoh a chule thia athisan'a amite athensah ahi. 
Hiti chun eiho Ama henga kulpi pam lama chun gachedoh uhitin, chule Aman jachatna apoh chu gapoh hu-ute. 
Ijeh ham itileh hiche vannoiya hi indet aumden ding inei pouve; eihon ahunglhung dingin inga nahlaiyu ahi. 
Hiche jeh chun Jesu vanga Pathen vahchoi jingna eihon Amin'a ikitahnau phondohna kilhaina neijom jingu hite. 
Chule thilpha bol le ngaichatna neiho to chan neikhom ding sumil hih un. Hitobang kilhaina ho hi Pathen lunglhaina ahi. 
Lhagaova nalamkai hou thun nungun, chule ipi aseiyu bol un. Amaho hi nalhagaovu honbitna a natong ahiuvin, chule amaho Pathen a mopoa ahiuve. Amaho chun kipahtah a abol theina dingu le lunghimo puma abol louna ding uvin, ajeh phatah in peuvin. Hichu nangho dinga phatchomna hilouhel ding ahi. 
Keiho dingin taovun, ijeh ham itileh se le pha hetna kaneiyu hi akicheh in chule kabolna lam jouseuvah jana neitah in hinkho kaman nom uvin ahi. 
Chule adeh a keima nangho kom agangthei pena kahung theina dingin taovun. 
Tun cham-lungmon konna Pathen, I-Pakaiyu Jesu, kelngoiho Achingpipa, thina a konna kaithoupa, athisan a tonsot kitepna namdet nom pa chun– 
Na ngaichat nau jousea alunggot bolna dingin na bulhin sah uhen. Ama chun Jesu Christa thuneina jal in, nachung uva thilpha lam jousea Ama lunglhaina dingin sodoh hen. Loupina jouse tonsot atonsot in Amaa hijing hen! Amen. 
Kasopi deitahte, kangeh nahiuve, hiche tilkhouna chomcha kajih hi gelkhoh uvin. 
Keiman nahet dingu kadei chu isopiu Timothy chu songkul'a kon'in ahin lhadoh tauve. Ama hikom'a ahungvah a ahileh nangho to kimuto dinga kahin lhonpi ding ahi. 
Chuche mun a na lamkaihou le tahsannei ho jouse ana lemun. Italy a tahsan neihon lemna nahin thot ui. 
Pathen milungsetna chu na boncha uva chunga umhen. Amen.
