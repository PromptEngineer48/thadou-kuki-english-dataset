Jacob.
3.
Sopi deitahte, mitampi hi houbunga mihilin pang hih un, ajeh chu ahila pangho chunga thutanna chu gihjo cheh ding ahi. 
Ahimongin, eiho jousen suhkhel tampi inei jiuve. Ajeh chu eihon ileiyu kithunun jou leuhen, eiho chamkim ihi dingu ahi chule thil danga jong kitim jou ihi dingu ahi. 
Eihon sakol lentah khu idei deina uva ichesah theina ding uvin akamdal ikoipeh uvin ahi. 
Chule kong innei lenpitah jong chu ahehna neocha a chu atolpan achesah nomna lam lama aheiya, hui hat jongleh athunun ahi. 
Hitobang maa chu lei hi thil neocha thil thupi tahtah ho seithei hiya ahi. Ahin mei-am neocha khat in gammang lentah akah lhah jeng thei chu ahi. 
Chule leigui hi mei kong tobang ahi. Hiche hi vannoi gitlouna dimset, natahsa pumpi suboh ahi. Hiche hin na hinkho pumpi meiya ahal thei, ajeh chu hiche hin meidil meikou chu akahsah ahi. 
Miho hin ganhing jouse, vacha ho, a-op a kithol ho chule ngaho avah ngoi thei cheh e, 
Hinlah koiman lei hi avah ngoi joupoi. Hiche hi kicholdo theilou le gilou, thina thei gu dimset ahi. 
Khatvei kom le hiche hin I-Pakaiyu le Pa chu avahchoiyin, chule khatvei kom le Pathen lima kisem chu agaosap jin ahi. 
Chutia chu kamhom khat ma a konna chu phatthei bohna le gaosapna hung kisunglha khom jia ahi. 
Twinah khat a konna chu twi thengsel le twi kha putdoh khom ngaiyem? 
Theichang phungin olive ga asodoh theija ahilouleh lengpi guiyin theichang ga asodoh theiyem? Ahipoi, nangman twitheng chu chitwiya kon'in na thal doh theipoi. 
Nangma nachih a chule Pathen lamlhah ho chu nahet a ahileh, jabolna lama hinkho naman chu aphot chenna hihen chule chihna a konna natohpha kineosahna hin sodoh hen. 
Hinlah nang hon kithangthip tona nanei uva chule nalung uva chang phatchomna hollea nahiu leh, thutah lekhuna a kiletsahna pum le jouvin thu seihih un. 
Ajeh chu kithangthip le chang phatchomna holna ho hi Pathen'a hung kon chihna chu ahipoi. Hiti ho hi leiset thudol, lhagao thu hilou chule lhagao gilouva kon joh ahi. 
Ajeh chu, kimit thipna le chang phatchomna umna mun a chu phatlouna jouse le kitomona namu ding ahi. 
Hinlah, chunga konna chihna hi amasapen a atheng ahi. Chujongleh chamna ngaichatna, phat jousea lungneng, chule midang thua jong nung nom tina ahi. Khotona dimset le thilpha bolna asodoh in ahi. Koima lama langkaina aneipon chule lungdih pun aum jinge. 
Chule kicham ding deiho dinga chamna muchi kitu ding chule chonphatna tohga chu amu ding ahitai.
