Jacob.
4.
Nalah uva kinah le kidel ho hi ipia kon ham? Nasung uva ngaichat dihlou ho chu kidouva hilou ham? 
Nanei louvu chu na deiyun, hichu mutei na gongun chutia na gotna lamun nathat jiuve. Midang thil nei nathangse uvin, hinla na neithei pouvin, hiti chun nang hon gal nasat un amahoa konna lahdohpeh ding nago uve. Ahijeng vang'in, nang hon nadeiyu chu nanei thei pouve, Pathen kom'a nathum loujeh u ahi. 
Chule nathum jeng vangun namu deh pouve, ajeh chu nalung gonu chu abona dihlou ahi-nanghon nopsahna ding bou nadei jeh u ahi. 
Nangho jonbolte ho! Vannoi toh kigolvop theina hin Pathen melma a nasemu ahi geldoh lou nahiuvem? Seikit inge: Nangho Vannoi toh naki golvop uva ahileh, Pathen melma a kisema nahiu ahitai. 
Pathen Thubun Pathen Lhagao isung uva cheng hin thangsetna a eidip dimu ahi kiti hi ipi nagel uvem? 
Hinlah Ama hin hitobang lung gitlou ngaichatna idou jouna ding uva lungsetna eichan cheh joh u ahi. Pathen Thubun, “Pathen'in akiletsah ho adouvin, akineosah ho akhoto'e,” ati. 
Hiti chun, Pathen masanga kineosah un. Diabol chu douvun chutileh nanghoa kon'in jamdoh tante. 
Pathen kom nailut un, chutileh Pathen'in nahin nailut dingu ahi. Nakhut u sutheng un, michonse nangho; nalungu sutheng un, ajeh chu nangho Pathen le vannoi kikah ah nalungthimu akikehsotai. 
Na thilbol chung uva kisihna mitlhi longdoh hen. Lungkhamna le lunghemna tahbeh soh hen. Nuina sangin kana, chule kipana sangin lhasetna sohhen. 
Pakai masanga kineosah un, chuteng Aman jabolna a na dopsang dingu ahi. 
Kasopi deitahte, khat in khat dounan thuse seihih un. Nangho naki oimoto uva chule khat in khat chung thu natan u ahileh Pathen danthu chu oimoa chule athutan a napanu ahitai. Hinla nabol diu dol chu danthu najui diu bou ahin, athutan ding nahilou u ahi. 
Danthu pea pang Pathen bou chu Thutanpa ahi. Ama bou chu huhhing thei ahilouleh sumang thei hiya ahi. Achutileh, nangman naheng nakom chunga thutan theina ipi thu naneiyem? 
Tun veuvin, “Tuni ahilouleh jing tengleh khopi khat a gacheu hitin chule chuche mun a chun kum khat gaum uhite. Chumun a chun sumkol gakiveiyu hitin chule sumpung hol uhite,” natiuve. 
Jing nikhoa itobang hinkho naman diu nahet uvem? Nahin khou chu jingkah twimei tobang ahi-hikom'a chomkhat uma, chujou le chemang tobang ahi. 
Gahsei dia pha chu, “Pakaijin eidei peh u ahileh eiho hingu hitin chule hiche ahilouleh khuche bol uhite,” tiuvin. 
Achuti louva na tohgon uva naki letsahpi uva ahileh hitobang kiletsah a thusei chu phalouva ahi. 
Geldoh un, nang hon nabol dingu chu ipi ahi henasa'a nabol lou u chu chonset ahi.
