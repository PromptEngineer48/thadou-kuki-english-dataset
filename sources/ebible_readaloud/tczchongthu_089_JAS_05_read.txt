Jacob.
5.
Hin veuvin, nangho mihao hon: Kap un chule lunghem pumin maole uvin, ajeh chu tijat um hahsatna ho jouse na masang uva aum'e. 
Nagou thil hou amonlha gamtai, chule napon hoitah tah hou jong nget in ane heng gamtai. 
Na sana hou le na dangka hou panna bei asoh gamtai. Na gou thil hou na sahlut u chun na tahsau chu meikah banga aneh mang ding ahi. Hiche gou nakhol khomu chu thutan nikhoa dinga nangho douna a hung pang ding ahi. 
Ajeh chu ngaiyun! Na loulhou atohman na lhimlhah peh hou ka awgin ngaiyun. Atohman napehlou hou chun nangho dounan kho ahin samui. Ajeh chu nalou uva tohman napehlou hou kana aw chu Van Pakai sepai te nakol sunga ahunglhung tai. 
Nanghon leiset chunga hi nomsatah a nahinkhou namanu ahin, na ngaichat chasunu chu nasuh bulhit u ahi. Nangho nakivah thaovu hi nakithana diu nikhoa dinga nahi tauve. 
Nanghon suhkhel neilou ho chu themmo nachan uva chule natha vangun, amaho chun na lethuh pouve. 
Sopi deitahte, Pakai hung kilena ding na-nga nauva thohhat un. Loubol mipan thohhat tah a goju masa le goju nunung angaji khu ngaito uvin. Amaho chun chang amin'a chang atphat manlu tah chu kinem tah in angah uve. 
Nangho jong thohhat un, lungdet un, ajeh chu Pakai hung na ding anaitai. 
Sopite, khat le khat kiphun lou jenun, achuti louleh themmo changin umget uvin nate. Ajeh chu veuvin-Thutanpa chu kot a adinge! 
Kasopi deitahte, thohhatna thudol'a avetsahpi dingin, Pakai min'a thusei themgao ho veuvin. 
Thohgimna thoh jouho eihon jabolna lentah ineiyuve. Vetsahnan, nang hon Job thohhat chu nana heuve. Ajonaa Pakaijin ichan geiya akhoto hitam, ajeh chu Pakai hi lungset dimset le khoto them ahi. 
Ahin adehset in, kasopi te ho, van panin ahilouleh lei panin, ahilouleh ipi pen min hijongleh kihahsel hihbeh un. 
Nalah uva hahsatna thoh um'em? Natao jing diu ahiye. Chule thangvahna ding ajeh neihon Pakai chu avahchoi jing diu ahi. 
Nalah uva ana damlou um'em? Nanghon houbung upaho ahung uva, nachung uva Pakai min'a thao anu uva chule taona aneina ding uva nakou diu ahi. 
Hitobanga tahsan neitah a taona chun ana chu adamsah a, chule Pakaijin nadamsah dingu ahi. Chule nang hon chonsetna chu nana bolu ahileh ngaidam chang ding nahiuve. 
Na damtheina dingun na chonset u khat in khat kom'a phong unlang, chule khat chu khat dingin taovun. Michonpha lung gutten'a ataona chun thaneina lentah aneijin chule kidangtah ahinbol doh e. 
Elijah chu eimaho tobang bep mihem ahin, chuti ahijeng vang'in lunggil tah a gojuh louna dinga ataova ahileh kum thum le akeh leiset a go ajutapon ahi. 
Chutah in ama ataokit in ahileh van'a kon'in go ajun chule leiset in aga asodohtai. 
Kasopi deitahte ho, nalah uva thutah a konna avahmanga naum uva ahin lepuikit ah auma ahileh, 
Koi hita jongleh michonse hin lepuiya chu, hiche mi chu thinaa konna huhdoh ahin chule chonset tampi ngaidamna ahin lhut ding ahi
