1 Peter.
2.
Hiti chun umchan phalou ho, mi lheplhahna jouse, phatlhem, mithang thipna, chule ngainom umlou thusei jouse pailhauvin. 
Naosen sohthah ho bangin, lhagao bongnoi thengsel chu ngaichauvin chutileh lhagao huhhingna a chu na kikhanlhit thei dingu ahi. 
Tun nang hon Pakai phatna chu natekhah u ahitai. 
Nangho Christa, Pathen Hou-In hingjing inning songpi, henga chu hunga nahi tauve. Ama chu miho pampai ahin, ahinlah Pathen'in jana lentah chang dinga alhendoh joh ahitai. 
Chule nangho, song hing ho Pathen'in lhagao Hou-In a dinga asahdoh chu nahiuve. Akalval ipi um'em, nangho Ama dia thempu thengho nahi tauve. Jesu Christa Palai hina a Pathen lunglhaina theiya lhagaova kilhaina-thilto chu bol'ah nahi tauve. 
Pathen Thubun asei dungjuijin, “Keiman Jerusalem'a inning songpi kakoiyin, jabolna lentah dinga kilhengdohsa ahi, chule koi hileh Ama tahsan chan chu lungsetna changlouva umlou beh ding ahi,” ati. 
Tahbeh in, nangho Ama tahsan hon Pathen'in apeh jabolna chu nahetpeh un. Hinlah Ama pampai ho din “Insa hon adeimo uva apampaiyu song chu tun inning sonpin apangtai.” 
Chule “Ama miho kipalsah chu ahin, chule amaho lhuhna songpi chu ahitai.” Amaho akipalnau ajeh chu amaho Pathen thun anung pouve, chule amaho dinga kigong achan diu chu akimu tauve. 
Ahin nangho hitobang chu nahi lou u ahi, ajeh chu nangho lhen tum mite nahiuve. Nangho leng-thempu ho, namtheng, Pathen nei chu nahi tauve. Hijeh a chu, nang hon midang ho Pathen phatna chu navetsah theiju ahitai, ajeh chu Aman muthim lah a konna avah loupi kidangtah a chu napuilut u ahitai. 
“Masanga chu amite hina photchenna neilou nahiu ahin, tua hi nangho Pathen mite nahiu ahitai. Masanga chu khotona ana changlou nahiu ahin, tua hi Pathen khotona chu changa nahi tauve.” 
Deitahte, “Chomkhat a dinga khosa chule gamchom mi” nahiu toh kilhon'a kagihsal nahiuve, nangho nalhagaovu douva pang vannoi lung ngaichat hoa kon'in kikang seuvin. 
Naheng nakom atahsanlou to cheng khom nahiu chu heuvin lang, chingtheiyun. Chujouleh ama hon thil dihlou bol a themmo nachan ujongleh jatheiya naum nau chu mudoh uvin tin, chule Aman vannoi thu atan teng Pathen chu jana apeh dingu ahi. 
Pakai jal'in, mihem thuneina jouse jabol un, Lengpa chu gamneipa ahinaa ja ding ahi, 
ahilouleh thunei aman alhendoh hijongleh jana pen. Ajeh chu lengpa chun thil dihlou bol ho gimbol dinga chule thilpha bol ho jabolna pedinga amaho chu ngansena apeh ahi. 
Ja umtah a hinkho naman diu chu Pathen lunglam ahi, hichun alam louva ngol-hoitah a themmona hin chan hou chu asuhthip ding ahi. 
Ajeh chu nangho chamlhat nahiu ahi, hinlah nangho Pathen sohte nahiuve, hiti ahijeh chun na chamlhat nau chu thilse bolnan neihih un. 
Amitakip jabolun, chule Christian nasopi chu ngailun. Pathen gingin, chule lengpa chu jabol in. 
Chule nangho sohho naputeu thuneina chu jana neitoh thon sang uvin. Ama hon bol'un tia nahilu chu bolun-amaho chu mihepi them le lunggel toh them ahiseh-u hilouvin, mi engse ahiu jongleh bolun. 
Ajeh chu Pathen lunglhaina chu nang hon adih ahi ti nahet'u chu nabol uva chule alam louva nakibol nauva jong thohhat tah a napan dingu ahi. 
Ahinai, thilse boljeh a na thohhat nau chun ima chan ding nanei pouve. Hinlah thilpha bol jeh a gimbolna nathoh uva thohhat tah a napan uva ahileh, Pathen nachung uva lunglhaiyah ahi. 
Ajeh chu Pathen'in Christan nangho dinga gimbol hesoh athoh banga chu gimthohna ding hijongleh thilpha bol dinga nakouvu ahi. Ama navettoh uva najui dingu chule akhonung najui dingu ahiye. 
Ama achonse khapoi, koimacha jong alhemlha khapoi. 
Ama ajumsou jong chun alethuhpoi, agimhesoh thoh pet in jong akithuhpoi. Aman achung chang thu adihtah a thutan jing Pathen khut a angam lhan ahi. 
Ama tah in atahsa chunga ichonsetna hou thingpel chunga chun apoi, hichu eiho chonsetna lama ithiuva chule chonphatna lama ihin theina dingu ahi. Ama kijepna mahahoa chu nang hon damna namu u ahi. 
Nangho masanga chu avah manga nana hiu ahin, hinlah tun na Chingpau, na lhagaovu Kaihoipa henga chu hung kilekit ah nahi tauve.
