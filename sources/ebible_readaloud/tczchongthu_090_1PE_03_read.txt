1 Peter.
3.
Chutima chun, nangho inneipi hon napasal teu thuneina chu kisan uvin. Chutengleh, amaho lah a Kipana Thupha joplou aum tah jongleh, thuchenga seilouva na Pathen ngaisah nau navetsah uchun thu aseijoh ding ahi. Amaho nathujona ding uchu, 
Nanghon thenna le jana hinkho naman'u amu uva kon hi ding ahi. 
Polam hoina khohsah nan sampheh a kijep hoi, thil mantam sana khi-o ahilouleh ponsil hoi hoa kijep ding gelkhoh hih un. 
Nangho sunggila konna hoina, chultheilou lungneng le thipkhen cha um, hichea chu nakijep joh dingu ahi, hichu Pathen a dinga manlutah ahi. 
Hiche hi malai numei theng masa ho amaho tah akijephoi nau chu anahi. Ama hon Pathen atahsanun chule a inneipu teu thuneina chu ana kisan u ahi. 
Avetsahnan, Sarah chu a inneipu Abraham thun anungin, chule a inneipu chu ka-pakai tin akouve. Na inneipu teuvin ipi ahinbol dingu kichatna neilouhel'a nang hon thilpha chu nabol uva ahileh ama chanute nahiu ahi. 
Hitobangma chun, pasal nang hon na inneipi teu jabolun. Na inneipi teu chuto umkhom'a nahiu toh kilhon'in hetkhen themna neijin bolun. Ama nangho sanga thalhomjo hithei nante, hinlah Pathen'in hinkho thah a hinkho naman khompi dinga napeh u chu ahi. Natao nau adal louna dingin amaho chu nabolna ding dol a chun bolun. 
Achainakeiyin, nangho jouse lungkhat kisauvin. Khat le khat kigelkhohto uvin. Sopi hina in kingailu touvin. Lungneng unlang chule kineosah cheh un. 
Setna chu asen lethuh hih un. Mihon najumso tengu leh jumso kitna ding lung gel hih un. Hisang chun phattheiboh jo uvin. Hichu Pathen'in nabol dinga nakouna chu ahi, chule Aman hiche jal a chu phatthei naboh ding ahi. 
Ajeh chu, Pathen Thubun asei chu, “Nangman hinkho chu nakipapia, nikho kipa um tampi namu noma ahileh, na leigui chu thilse seinaa kon'in kihuh tup in, thilpha bolin chule namuh chu jou seina a kon'in kikangsen. 
Thil phalouva kon'in kihei mangin, chule thilpha chu bol in. 
Thildih bol ho chu Pakaijin amit a avetsui ahi, chule ataonau ngaina in anakol asung jinge. Hinlah Pakai chun thilse bol ho dounaa amai angat jing ahi.” 
Tun, nang hon thilpha nabol nom uva ahileh koiham nasugim ding uva? 
Thildih boljeh a hahsatna nato tah uva ahijongleh, Pathen'in hichea chu na lethuh ding ahi. Hijeh chun, amaho gihsal jeh chun lung himo ahilouleh kichat bol hih un. 
Chusanga nangman Christa chu nahinkhoa Pakaiya nanei ding hiya ahi. Chule koiham khat in na Christian hina a na kinepna thudol chu nadoh a ahileh hilchenin. 
Hinlah hiche hi lungneng le jatheiyin bolin. Se le pha nahetna chu athengin koiyin. Chua chu mihon nangho douna a thu aseiyuva ahileh ama hon Christa a nahijeh uva hinkho phatah naman chu amu tengu leh jahcha dingu ahi. 
Geldoh in, thil phalou boljeh a gimthoh sanga chu, thilpha bol jeh a gimthoh chu Pathen deilam ahijoi. 
Christa chun a-itih a dinga khatvei athohgim ahi. Ama achonse khapoi, hinlah ama Pathen kom'a chu bitkeicha a lhun theina dinga chonseho jeh a thia ahi. Aman tahsa tah a thina ato ahin, hinlah Ama Lhagaova kaithouva uma hingdoh kit ahi. 
Hiti chun, Ama gachea lhagao songkul'a kihenho kom'a aga seiphong ahitai. 
Amaho hi masanglai peh a Noah chun kong anasui laiya Pathen'in thohhat tah a anga laiya Pathen thua nung louva ahiuve. Hiche tijat umtah twisanglet a lhahlhumna dinga konna chu mi get bou sohchaa ahiuve. 
Chule hiche twi chu baptize chan vetsahna ahi, hichun tua hi na huhhingu ahi, tipuma anen pailhahna hilouva, Pathen lama se le pha hetna kichehna ding ahi. Jesu Christa thodoh kit jal a hichun na atoh ahiye. 
Tun Christa chu van'a gachea ahitan, Ama Pathen bantah a jabol channa mun a touva ahitai, chule Vantil te le athuneiho chule thaneiho jousen Ama thuneina asangun ahi.
