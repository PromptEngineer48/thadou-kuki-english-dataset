1 Peter.
5.
Chule tun, houbung hoa Upa ho henga, keima jong Upa khat hina a Christa gimthohna a chan kachan khompi hina a kathumop nom nahiuve. Chule, keima jong, Ama loupina a chu vannoiya ahung kilah teng chuleh aloupina chu chang khom ding kahi. Upat nachan khompiu keiman naheng uva kahin ngeh nahiuve: 
Pathen'in chin dinga na nganseu kelngoi hon chengse chu chingun. Lungcham tah in, lunghimo louhelin bolun-nang hon ipi phatchomna kaki mudoh theidem ti hilouvin, Pathen na natoh nom joh jeh uvin bol jouvin. 
Na kinganse hou chunga kipiputsahna bol louvin, na umchan hoi chu vetsah in puihoi jouvin. 
Chule Kelngoiching Pipu ahung kilah teng chuleh, nang hon a-itih a abeitih umlou loupina le jabolna Lallukhuh chu nachan dingu ahi. 
Hitobangma chun, nangho khangdong hon jong Upa vaihom ho thuneina chu noppeh un. Chule nangho na boncha uvin, khat in khat kineosah pumin jenun, ajeh chu “Pathen'in akiletsah chu adouvin, akineosah mi vang chu alung lhaina ahi.” 
Hiti chun nangho Pathen thahat tah thaneina noiya umun chule aman aphatcha jabolna toh thoa nachoisang dingu ahi. 
Nalung khamna hou le nagelkhoh hou chu Pathen a peuvin, ijeh inem itileh aman na chungchang thu na gelkhoh peh nai. 
Kichih theiyuvin! Namelmau pipu, Diabol jeh chun kihong phauvin. Ama keipi bahkai kitum tobangin vallhum ding mi ahol in ahi. 
Ama dounaa ding det un, chule na tahsan nauva chun hat un. Vannoi pumpia nasopiu Christian ho chu nangho banga thohgimna ato cheh u ahi. 
Pathen'in amikhoto themna Christa Jesu jal a tonsot a loupina chu chang dinga nakouvu ahitai. 
Amaa thaneina chu tonsot tonsot in hijing hen! Amen. 
Tahsan umtah sopi Silas, nangho kom'a seiphatna kapeh, ama kithopina jal a hiche lekhathot chomcha hi naheng uva dinga kahin jih ahi. Nakom uva dinga kahin jihlona ajeh chu nang hon tua hi Pathen lungset nachan tahbeh u ahi tia tilkhouna le photchetsahna a ding ahi. Hiche lungsetna a hin ding det un. 
Hikom Rome mun'a na-houbung chanpiu le ka chapa Mark in jong chibai nahin peuve. 
Christian kingailutna chun khat in khat chibai kibohto uvin. Christa Jesu a umho jouse cham-lungmon konna in na umpiu hen.
