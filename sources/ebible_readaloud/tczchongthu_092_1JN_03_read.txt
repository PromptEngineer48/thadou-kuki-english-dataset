1 John.
3.
Veuvin, Van'a um I-Pauvin ichan geiya eingailut u hitam? Ajeh chu Aman Achate tin eikou tauve–chule eiho jong ihi tahbeh u ahi! Hinlah hiche hi vannoiya hihon Pathen Chate ihiu hi ahepha pouve, ajeh chu ama hon Ama ahetlou jeh u ahi. 
Ngaitahte ho–eiho Pathen chate ihiu ahitai. Chule ipi ihi dingu tun akilangdoh hih laiye. Hinlah eihon ihet u chu Christa ahung kilah teng chuleh eihon Ama ahina tahbeh a imu dingu ahitai. 
Chule Ama hung ding kinem'a ngah jingho chu Ama athen banga amaho jouse jong thenga um ding ahiuve. 
Koi hileh chonset bol chun Pathen Danthu apalkeh ahi, ajeh chu chonset jouse hi Pathen Danthu toh kikal ahi. 
Koi hileh Ama toh hingkhom chu chonse jom louding ahiuve. Hinlah chonset boljom jing mi chu Ama heloulai ahilouleh Ama koi ahi hechen lou ahi. 
Koi hileh Amaa um jom jing mi chu chonse louding ahi. Hinlah achonse jom jing chu koi hileh Ama chu heloulai ahilouleh hetkhenna neiloulai ahi. 
Cha deitahte, hiche chung chang thu ah koiman nalhep lhah dauhen: Mihon adih tia ahol tengu leh, Christa chu chondih ahi banga amaho chondih a ahiuve ti avetsah ahi. 
Hinlah miho chu achonset jom jing tengu leh amaho chu diabol a ahiuve ti avetsah ahi. Ama chu atil abula pat a ana chonse peh ahi. Hinlah diabol natoh ho sumang dinga Pathen Chapa hung anahi. 
Pathen insung mia peng ho chun chonset aboljom jing pouve, Pathen a hinna chu amahoa um ahijeh ahi. 
Hiti chun, tun koiho hi Pathen chate hiuva chule koiho chu diabol chate hiuvem ti iseithei tauve. Koi hileh chonphatna a hinglou ho chule midang tahsan chankhompi ngailulou ho chu Pathen a ahi pouve. 
Hiche hi atil abula pat a nana jahsau chu ahi: Eiho khat le khat ikingailut dingu hi ahi. 
Eiho Cain tobanga agilouva ihipouve, ama chun asopipa anatha ahi: Ipi jeh a atha hintem? Ajeh chu Cain chun aphalou abola chule asopipa chun chonphat abol jeh ahi. 
Hijeh chun, sopi deitahte, vannoiyin nahot uva ahileh datmo hih un! 
Eihon Christian isopihou ingailut u ahileh, eiho thinaa konna hinna a galkai ihitauve. Hinlah ngailutna neilou chu athisa ahi. 
Koi hileh sopi michom khat hotbola chu alungsunga tolthat ahitai chule tolthat ho chun asung uva tonsot hinna anei pouve. 
Eihon ngailutna atahbeh ipiham ti iheuve, ajeh chu Jesun eiho dinga ahinna chu ahung pehdohsa ahitai. 
Koiham khat chun sum neihen lang sopinu ahilouleh sopipan angaichat he henlang, hinlah khotona nei tahih leh–hiche pa chu Pathen ngailutna a iti um thei ding ham? 
Kacha deitahte, eiho khat le khat ikingailu uve tin sei maimai pouhite, atahbeh chu ichonna uva vetsah u hite. 
Ichonna uvin thutah a hinga ihiu avetsah ding ahi, hitia chu Pathen masanga idin tengu leh lungngam tah a ium dingu ahi. 
Ikithemmo channau aum jeng jongleh Pathen chu eimaho lunggel sanga lenjoa ahijeh in ijakai Aman ahet ahinai. 
Kagol ngaitahte, eima hon kithemmo channa inei louva ahileh, Pathen kom chu lungngam tah a inailut theiyu ahi. 
Chule ipi hijeng jongleh ithum chan u chu Amaa konna imu thei dingu ahi, ajeh chu eihon Ama sei ingai uvin, chule Ama lunglhaina chu ibolun ahi. 
Chule hiche hi Ama thupeh ho ahi: Eihon Achapa Jesu Christa itahsan uva chule Aman eithupeh bang uva eiho khat le khat ikingailut dingu ahije. 
Pathen Thupeh ho nit a juiho chu: Ama toh kiloikhom ahiuvin, chule Ama jong amahoa chu uma ahi. Chule eihon eihoa Ama aum'e ti iheuve, ajeh chu Aman Lhagao eipeh u chu isung uva achengin ahi.
