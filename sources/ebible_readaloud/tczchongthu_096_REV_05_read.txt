Revelation.
5.
Chutah in keiman laltouna a toupan akhut jetlam'a lekhajol khat achoi chu kamun ahi. Lekhajol chu asung lam le apolam kijih gel ahin, chule hichu sinkhana sagin mohor akinam khum in ahi. 
Chuin keiman vantil hattah khat aw sangtah a kho asap kajai: “Koiham hiche lekhajol sinkhana kinam khum hi ahong dinga kiloma chu?” ati. 
Hinlah van'a hihen, ahilouleh lei chunga hihen chule leinoiya hijongleh lekhajol chu ahong thei le asim thei ding akimu tapoi. 
Chuphat in keima hakan kakaptai, ajeh chu lekhajol chu ahong ding le asim dingah koima akimu tapon ahi. 
Hinlah upa somni le li holah a khat chun kajah a aseijin, “Kap tahih in! Ven, Judah phunga Keipi Bahkai, David Laltouna a tou dingpa chun jona aneitai. Ama hi lekhajol hong dinga le a sinkhana sagi lhamlha dinga kiloma ahi,” ati. 
Chuphat in keiman Kelngoinou khat akithatsa lheh lheh chu Laltouna le hinna nei liho kahlah a chule upa somni le li ho kahlah a adin chu kamun ahi. Ama chun ki-sagi aneijin, chule vannoi muntin'a kisoldoh a Pathen Lhagao sagi, mit-sagi jong neiya chu ahi. 
Ama chu ahung pheiyin chuin Laltouna a toupa khut jet lama kon chun lekhajol chu aladoh in ahi. 
Chuin aman lekhajol chu alah phat in, hinna nei-liho chule upa somni le liho chu Kelngoinou masanga chun akun cheh tauve. Khat khat cheh in akhut uva selangdah, chule sana khon gimnamtui dimset a kikoi, Pathen mite taona chu akichoi cheh un ahi. 
Chule amaho chun lathah khat hiche thucheng ho hin asauve: “Nangma lekhajol chu la a chule asinkhana chu lhamlha dinga kiloma nahi. Ajeh chu nangma akithatsa nahin chule nathisanin mitampi chu–phungtina kon, paotina kon chule mijouse le namtina kon'in Pathen a dingin na lhatdam tai. 
Chule nangman amaho chu I-Pathen uva dingin Lenggam thempu hoa nahisah in, chule ama hon leiset chunga vai ahom dingu ahi.” 
Chuin kavekit in ahileh keiman mi asangsang, simsenlou laltouna kimvela um Vantil te ho le sahem-hing ho chule upaho awgin chu kajan ahi. 
Chule amaho chun awchoh jejun la asauvin, “Akithatsa Kelngoinou chu–thaneina le haona ho chule chihna le thahatna chule jabolna le loupina chule phattheina chang dinga lom ahi.” 
Chuin keiman van'a le lei chunga chule leinoiya le twikhanglen'a hinna nei jousen vahchoila asah u kajan, “Phattheina le jabolna chule loupina chule thilbol thei thahatna chu Laltouna a toupa chung'ah chule Kelngoinou chung ah atonsot tonsot in umhen” atiuve. 
Chule hinna nei liho chun “Amen!” tin aseiyuve. Chuin upa somni le liho chu abohkhup un chuin Kelngoinou chu ahou tauvin ahi.
