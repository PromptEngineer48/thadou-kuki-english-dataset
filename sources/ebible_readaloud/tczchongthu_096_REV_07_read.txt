Revelation.
7.
Chujouvin keiman vantil li leiset kolning-li a adin'u kamun, ama hon huinun-li chu leiset chunga anunlouna ding uvin atuhtangun ahi. Thingphung hoa thingna khat jong akiling pon, chule twikhanglen jong phatvet mai bangin ajolsel jenge. 
Chule keiman vantil chomkhat solama konna ahunga Pathen hing mohor achoi chu kamun ahi. Chule ama chun hiche vantil-li, leiset chung le twikhanglen suhset theina thaneina kipeho jah a asamin, 
“Keihon Pathen mohor chu asohte ho chalpanga kanampeh masang seuvin leiset ahilouleh twikhanglen ahilouleh thing ho susehih un,” ahinti-e. 
Chule keiman mi ijat chu Pathen mohor kinam khum hiuvem–Israel nam sunga konna mi sang jakhat le sang somli le sangli ahiuve, ti kajan ahi. 
Judah phunga konna mi sangsom le sangni, Reuben phunga konna mi sangsom le sangni, Gad phunga konna mi sangsom le sangni; 
Asher phunga konna mi sangsom le sangni, Naphtali phunga konna mi sangsom le sangni, Manasseh phunga konnami sangsom le sangni; 
Simeon phunga konna mi sangsom le sangni, Levi phunga konna mi sangsom le sangni, Issachar phunga konna mi sangsom le sangni; 
Zebulun phunga konna mi sangsom le sangni, Joseph phunga konna mi sangsom le sangni, Benjamin phunga konna mi sangsom le sangni ahiuve. 
Hiche jou chun keiman mi atama tam, sima simjou ding hilou chitin namtina kon, phungtina kon, mitina kon chule paotin a konna laltouna mai chule Kelngoinou anga adin uchu kamui. Amaho chun ponbang jeng akivonun, chule akhut uva lusu-bah akichoi cheh uvin ahi. 
Chule amaho chun aw choh jejun asam uvin, “Huhhingna chu laltouna a toupa I-Pathen uva kon le Kelngoinou a kon ahi,” atiuve. 
Chule vantil ho jouse chu laltouna kimvela, chule upaho kimvela, chule hinna nei-liho kimvela chun ading uvin ahi. Chule amaho laltouna angsunga chun alhu uvin amaiyu tol'a asulut un chule Pathen chu ahouvun ahi. 
Ama hon avahchoiyun, “Amen! Phattheina le loupina le chihna le thangvahna le jabolna le thaneina chule thahatna chu I-Pathen uva a-imatih chanin hijing hen, Amen” tin asam uve. 
Chuphat in vantil ho lah a khat chun kajah a, “Hiche abang jenga kivon ho hi koiho hiuvem? Hoiya hungkon hiuva ham?” eiti. 
Chuin keiman ajah a, “Pu, nangman nahenai” tin kaseiye. Chuphat in aman kajah a, “Amaho hi thohhahsa phat sunga thi ho ahiuve. Amaho hin aponsil'u chu kelngoinou thisana akisop ngim uva chule bangleh ah ahitai.” 
“Hijeh a chu amaho hi Pathen laltouna masanga ding uva olmo louva sun le jana Hou-In sunga chu kicholdo man louva Pathen ajenu ahi. Chule laltouna a toupa chun amaho aumna diu apeh ding ahi.” 
Amaho gilkel kit talou dingu chule dangchah kit talouhel dingu ahi; nisa sat in jong amaho akahkhah tahlouhel dingu ahi. 
Ajeh chu laltouna a toupa chu amaho kelngoi chin'a aching dingpa hi ding ahi. Aman amaho chu hintwi lonna mun'a apuilut ding chule Pathen'in amit uva konna amitlhiu atheh hulpeh dingu ahi.
