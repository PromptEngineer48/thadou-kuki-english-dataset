Revelation.
17.
Vantil sagi sumlheng choi ho lah a khat chu kaheng'a ahungin, chuin eihouvin, “Neihin juijin, chutileh keiman noti lentah, twi tamtah chunga vaihomnu chunga thutanna hunglhung ding chu navetsah inge. 
Amanu to chun vannoi leiset lengho jong uva, chule hiche vannoi leiset mite chu jonthanhoiya akhamnau leh jua amanu toh jong uva ahi,” ati. 
Hiti chun vantil chun keima Lhagaova gamthip mun'a eipuiyin ahi. Keiman chuche mun'a numei khat chu sahem santhip, lu sagi neiya chule ki som nei, chule Pathen taitomna dimset kijihna chunga atou chu kamui. 
Numeinu chun ponsandup le asanthip akisilin chule ahoiya hoi, sana le khisong mantam chule twikohchanga akijem in ahi. Amanu hin sana a kisem jukhon, jamo thu kisun dimset chule ajonthanhoiya athenlouna ho chu akhut a akichoiyin ahi. 
Chule achalpanga chun min kidang le datmo umtah kisun: “Babylon khopi lentah, vannoi leiset a numei kijoh jouse le jamo thusei dimset nu,” akitin ahi. 
Keiman amanu akham'a Jesu thu hettohsah a pang ho Pathen mitheng te thisan adonkham a aum pet chu kamun ahi. Kadatmo behseh in amanu chu kave vejengin ahi. 
Vantil chun, “Ipi dinga nachuti datmo ham?” Keiman hiche numeinu le alu sagi chule aki som nei, sahem chunga atou thuguh khu naseipeh inge. 
Sahem namu chu phat khatna ana-uma ahi, hinlah tua um talou ahi. Ajeh chu ama hi lei-ong amongbeiya kon'a hung potdoh kit ding, chule tonsot mahthahna a chelut ding ahi. Chule hiche vannoi leiset mite, vannoi leiset kisem masanga pat a amin'u Hinna Lekhabua kisunlou jouse chun, sahem thisa ahung hindoh kit chu adatmo lheh diu ahi. 
Hichea hin hetkhenna lunggel nei angaina akimudoh e: Sahem alu sagi chu numeinu vaipohna, mol sagi ho vaihomtu ahi. Amaho hi leng sagi jong vetsahna ahiuve. 
Leng nga ho chu alhusa ahiuvin, agupna pa tua hi vaipo ahi, chule asagina chu hung nalai ding, hinlah avaipohna chomcha bou hi ding ahi. 
Sahem asanthip anaumsa, ahia umjom talou chu leng aget channa ahi. Ama hi sahem sagi tobangma ahin, chule ama jong mahthahna lam manoa ahi. 
Sahem aki som ho chu leng som athaneinau pan'a vaihomna nei loulai ahiuve. Amaho chu sahem toh phat chomkhat vai ahom khom diu ahi. 
Amaho hi abonchauva ama chu athanei nau pecheh dinga kinopto diu ahi. 
“Amaho kitho khom uva Kelngoinou chu kisatpi dinga kondoh diu, hinlah Kelngoinou chun amaho chu ajo ding ahi, ajeh chu Ama pakai jouse PAKAI le leng jouse LENGPA ahi. Chule Aman akouva, alhendoh a chule tahsan umho chu Ama toh umkhom diu ahi,” ati. 
Chuphat in vantil chun kajah a, “Notinu vaipohna twi tamtah kiti hin chitin namtin le paotin'a kon mihonpi chu kiseina ahi.” 
Sahem asanthip le aki som ho chun abonchauvin notinu chu athetbol tauve. Ama hon amanu hi sagoh keova alha diu, asa aneh diu, chule atibah anehmoh chengseu chu meiya ahalvam diu ahi. 
Ajeh iham itileh Pathen'in alunggel uva tohgon, asuh bulhit thei ding hou akhumpeh ding ahi. Amaho chun sahem santhip chu athanei nau pecheh dinga kinopto diu, chule hitia chu Pathen thu chu suh bulhit a um ding ahi. 
Chule hiche numeinu nangman gaova namu hi, vannoi lengho chunga vaipoa khopi chu ahi.
