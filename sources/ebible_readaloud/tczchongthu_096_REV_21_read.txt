Revelation.
21.
Chujouvin keiman Van thah le leithah kamui, ijeh inem itileh vanlui le leilui chu manga ahitai. Chule twikhanglen jong chu chemanga ahitai. 
Chule keiman khopi theng Jerusalem thah chu van Pathen a kon'a mou aji dingpa hoitah a akivon'a ahung kumlhah kamun ahi. 
Keiman laltouna a kon'a aw hung kisam gin kajan, aseijin, “Ven, Pathen chenna in chu mihem lah ahung hitai! Ama amaho toh chengkhom intin, chule amaho chu Ama mite hi ding ahiuve. Pathen Ama tah chun amaho aumpi diu ahi. 
Aman amit uva kon'a amitlhiu atheh ngimpeh ding, chule thina jong umkit talou ding ahilouleh lungkhamna ahilouleh kana ahilouleh natna um talou ding ahi. Hicheng jouse hi a-itih a um talou ding ahitai,” ati. 
Chule laltouna a toupa chun aseijin, “Ven, keiman ijakai kathah semtai!” ati. Chule ajou chun aman kajah a, “Hiche hi jihdoh in, ijeh ham itileh keiman najah a kasei hi tahsan umtah le adih ahi,” ati. 
Chule ama chun hiti hin aseijin, “Akichaiya ahitai! Keima Alpha le Omega kahi, Akipatna le akichaina chu! Adangchah jouse chu keiman Hinna twi nah'a kon'a twi chu manbeiyin penge.” 
Jona neiho chu hiche phattheina ho hi chang diu ahi, chule keima Amaho din Apau hing ting chule amaho kachate hi ding ahiuve. 
Hinlah akichase ho, atahsanlou ho, akisuboh ho, tolthat ho, jonbol ho, doichoi ho, milim hou ho chule mijou ho, amaho jouse chan ding chu meidil al-lenglung meikou mittheilou lhummei chu hi ding ahi. Hiche hi thi nina ahi. 
Chuphat in sumlheng sagi gamna hoise sagi nunungpen choi vantil sagi ho lah a khat chu ahungin, chule kajah a aseijin, “Hungin keito che hite! Keiman kelngoinou jidingnu, Mounu chu navetsah inge,” eiti. 
Hiti chun aman Lhagaovin keima molsang alentah khat mun'a eipuiyin, chule aman khopi theng, Jerusalem chu Pathen'a kon'a van'a pat ahung kumlhah eivetsah in ahi. 
Hichu Pathen loupina toh ahungvah doh in chule songmantam tobangin aemsel in jasper songmantam crystal thenin athengselin ahi. 
Khopi kulpi bang chu alenin chule asangin, kelkot som le ni aum in, vantil som le ni ho chun angau ahi. Chule Israel phungsom le ni ho min chu akelkot a chun akisunin ahi. 
Khopi solam, sahlam, lhanglam chule lhumlam jousea chun kelkot thum cheh aumin ahi. 
Khopi kulpi pal chun khombul som le ni aneijin, chule achunga chun Kelngoinou solchah som le ni ho min akisunin ahi. 
Vantil kaheng'a thuseiya chun khopi le akelkot ho, chule abang chu tena dingin akhut ah sana a kisem thiltena mollhon achoiyin ahi. 
Aman atetoh jouvin khopi chu aningli ahin, chule aletdan chu asan chan ma ahi. Chule khopi chu adung le avai chule atun kibang ahin, mile sang khat le jali cheh ahi. 
Chujouvin aman kulpi bang chu atetoh a ahileh feet jani le som le gup in asan ahi (hichu mihem tetohna dungjuiya vantil in atetoh ahi). 
Kulpi bang chu jasper songa kisem ahin, chule khopi chu sana theng haithengsel then'a theng ahi. 
Khopi kulpi bang chu khombul songa songmantam som le nia kisem ahin, khatna Jasper ahin, anina Sapphire, athumna Agate, alina Emerald, 
angana Onyx, agupna Carnelian, asagina Quartz, agetna Beryl, akona Topaz, asomna Chrysoprase, asom le khatna Jacinth, asom le nina Amethyst, hichengse hi aboncha khi song jeng ahi. 
Kot som le ni ho chu twikohchanga kisem ahin, kot khat cheh chu twikoh chang khat cheh a kon ahi! Chule alamlen chu sana thengsel ahin, haithengsel then a thengsel ahi. 
Keiman khopia chun Hou-In kamupoi, ajeh chu Pakai Pathen Hatchungnung chule Kelngoinou chu a Hou-In hiya ahi. 
Chule khopi chun nisavah ahilouleh lhavah jong angai tapoi, ijeh ham itileh Pathen loupina chun khopi chu asalvah jenga chule Kelngoinou chu vah a pang jeng ahi. 
Chitin namtin chu avah noiya vahle ding, chule vannoi leiset lengho khopi sunga chu aloupinau pum'a lut diu ahi. 
Akelkot hou chu kikhatih umlou ding ahitai, ijeh inem itileh jan umlou ding ahitai. 
Chule chitin namtin ho chun aloupinau le jabol achannau chu khopi sunga chu ahin pohlut diu ahi. 
Chuche mun'a chu imacha aphalouva, jachat umtah milim houna le lungthen louna ho imacha lut talou ding, Kelngoinou Hinna Lekhabua kisunho chengse bou chu lut ding ahi.
